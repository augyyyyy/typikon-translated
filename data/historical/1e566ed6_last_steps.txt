### Key Distinction Proximity Summary
* **Service Books:** Spoken prayers/hymns are in standard Roman text (read aloud), while rubrics (actions) are in *italics*, smaller fonts, or red print.
* **The Typikon:** Because a Typikon is *exclusively* a book of rubrics, printing all rubrics in italics would make ~90% of the book italicized, rendering it unreadable. Instead, the narrative rubrics/instructions remain in standard Roman text (structured in lists), while spoken dialogues are blockquoted, and hymn incipits (*"Lord, I have cried"*), tones, and book names (*Octoechos*) are italicized or quoted.
