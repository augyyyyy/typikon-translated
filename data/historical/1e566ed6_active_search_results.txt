Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md | Line 1962: This Sunday can fall on one of seven days, starting from 18 to 24 December. But since 20 December the Forefeast of the Nativity of Christ begins, this Sunday can fall either outside, or in the middle of the Forefeast. Furthermore, if this Sunday falls on the last day of the Forefeast, that is 24 December, on the very Eve of the Nativity of Christ, then it has a separate rule. Therefore this Sunday has three separate rules: one for outside the Forefeast, that is on 18 and 19 December; the second for in the middle of the Forefeast, with the exception of the Eve, that is 20, 21, 22 or 23 December; the third for the Eve, that is for 24 December, as we present.
Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md | Line 2078: Nothing special, except the Liturgy of Basil the Great with Vespers of the Feast on the Eve of the Nativity and Theophany, if they fall on one of the five fast days, from Monday to Friday. For if they fall on Saturday or on Sunday, then on the Eve there will be the Liturgy of Chrysostom, at its proper time without Vespers, and afterwards - Vespers of the Feast, at its proper time. On the Feast itself - Liturgy of Basil the Great without Vespers. On Friday, before the Eve, to which the Royal Hours are transferred, the typikon gives no Liturgy, this is adhered to also by the Lviv Synod[^307].

Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md | Line 2115: After the concluding prayer of the 9th Hour we immediately read the Typika just as in the Horologion, however, without the introductory verse that is before the first psalm, without the stichera on the "Beatitudes", without the Apostle-Gospel; also, if there be Liturgy, then also without the "Creed" and without "Blessed be the name of the Lord", and without the following 33rd Psalm, for all this will be taken at the Liturgy. When the Royal Hours are transferred back, to Friday, then since then there will be no Liturgy, here both the "Creed" and "Blessed be the name of the Lord" with the 33rd Psalm is read. Instead of the kontakia of the temple, of the day and others, one Kontakion of the Eve is taken. Here is the detailed rule for you.
