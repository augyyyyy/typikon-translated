O venerable mother Euphrosyne, worthy of marvel, desiring the true joy, thou didst walk the path mediating it: for thou didst exchange riches for much poverty; a carnal betrothed for Him Who lives unto the ages; corruptible food for abstinence; rest for painful afflictions in ascetic struggle; life in the world for otherworldly life: which also thou hast obtained with the wise virgins,[^2144] having kept thy lamp unquenched, and being accounted worthy of the bridal chamber as a virgin, as a bride of Christ, O all-praised one.

Having dwelt in the midst of men with a perfect mind,[^2146] how didst thou pass through the midst of fire unburned?
