[^14]: Slavonic: *правосла́внаго короля̀* ("the Orthodox King"), standard titulature in Pochaiv Lavra printings under the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth referring to the ruling Crown of Poland (King Augustus III, r. 1733–1763).
[^15]: An allusion to the traditional hagiographical account of St. Symeon the Stylite, who according to his Life by Theodoret of Cyrrhus and Antonius spent the entire forty days of Great Lent without tasting any food or water, receiving only the Holy Mysteries on Pascha or at the midpoint of the Fast.
[^16]: This rubrical directive specifies that the Biblical Canticle of the Theotokos (*Magnificat* / *My soul doth magnify the Lord*) with its refrain *More honorable than the Cherubim* is sung at the 9th Ode unless the day is a Feast of the Lord, even on days with the Great Doxology or the patronal feast of a temple saint.
