# MONTH OF SEPTEMBER
*September 1. Beginning of the Indiction, That Is, the New Year:*

*On the 1st day, the Beginning of the Indiction, that is, the New Year; and the memory of our venerable father Symeon the Stylite and Archimandrite; and of his mother Martha; and the Synaxis of the Most Holy Mother of God in Miasena;[^5] and of the holy martyr Aeithalas; and of the holy forty women martyrs; and of the holy deacon Ammon, their teacher; and of the holy martyrs Callista, Evodius, and Hermogenes, brothers of the same mother; and the memory of righteous Joshua, son of Nun.*

*At Vespers, "Blessed is the man," the 1st antiphon. If it be on a Saturday, we recite the whole kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," we set 10 verses, and sing 3 stichera of the Indiction:*

*Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the Heavenly Ranks" (Небныхъ чинѡвъ):*

Having learned the prayer of the personal and divine teaching of Christ, let us cry daily unto the Creator: Our Father, Who dwellest in the heavens, give us our daily bread, overlooking our transgressions.

Thou Who of old didst write the tablets on Mount Sinai, Thyself also now in the city of Nazareth according to the flesh, didst receive the prophetic book to read, O Christ God; and having closed it, Thou didst teach the people that the Scripture concerning Thee was fulfilled.

Even as of old the bodies of the Hebrews who in truth were not obedient unto Thee, the Master of all, fell worthily in the wilderness: so also now, as the Psalmist says, scatter by Hades the bones of the impious and infidel Agarenes, O Christ.[^6]

*Other stichera of the Venerable One, Tone 5, Podoben: "O Venerable Father" (Преподобне ѻтче):*

O venerable father, if it were possible for the pillar to speak, it would not cease crying aloud of thy pains, thy labors, and thy lamentations;


=== LEAF p4 === (Fol. 1v)
But it was rather borne by thee than bore thee, watered like a tree by thy tears. The Angels were amazed, men marveled, and demons trembled at thy patience, O venerable Symeon; pray that our souls may be saved.

O venerable father, by the power of the Holy Spirit, imitating thy Master, thou didst ascend the pillar as upon a Cross. But He tore up the handwriting of debt against all, while thou didst destroy the assaults of passions; He was as a sheep, but thou as a slaughter; He ascended the Cross, and thou the pillar: O venerable Symeon, pray that our souls may be saved.

O venerable father, having bedewed thy body with tears and cleansed thy soul, thou didst ascend to the height of virtues, having thy pillar as a sensible dwelling, but as thy spiritual and heavenly city the Zion on high. Thou didst amaze the Angels, terrify men, and put demons to shame by thy virtues, O venerable Symeon; pray that our souls may be saved.

*Other stichera of the Saint, Tone 2. Idiomela:*

From a good root, a good fruit sprouted forth: the consecrated Symeon from infancy was nourished by grace rather than by milk. And having elevated his body upon a stone, and exalted his mind toward God, he built a heavenly habitation by his virtues; and walking in choir with the divine powers, he became the dwelling of Christ, the God and Savior of our souls.

Thy memory abides unto ages, O venerable father Symeon, and the meekness of thy heart, O blessed servant of Christ; for though thou hast departed from us in the flesh, O good one, yet hast thou not withdrawn from us in spirit, standing in love before God and dancing in choir with the Angels in the heavens, with whom do thou pray that our souls may be saved.

The reliquary of thy relics, O all-praised father, pours forth healings, and thy holy soul, joined with the Angels, worthily rejoices. Having boldness before the Lord, O venerable one, and dancing in choir with the bodiless powers in the heavens, with whom do thou pray that our souls may be saved.

Thou didst love the highest philosophy, O God-bearer, and didst become outside the world, living higher than visible things; and thou didst divinely show thyself an undefiled mirror of God; and being ever united unto the Light, thou didst receive light, and didst obtain a most radiant end, O blessed one. Pray for our souls, O wise Symeon.

*Glory... Tone 6. By Germanus:*

Divine grace overshadows the shrine of thy relics, O consecrated Symeon; therefore, running unto the fragrance of the myrrh of thy miracles, we draw forth healing of diseases. But O venerable father, pray unto Christ God for our souls.

*Both now... Of the Indiction, the same Tone. By Byzantios:*

O Thou Who art united with the Holy Spirit, unoriginate Word and Son, Maker and Creator of all things visible and invisible: bless the crown of the year, preserving in peace the assemblies of the Orthodox, by the prayers of the Mother of God and of all Thy saints.

*Then the Entrance, the Prokeimenon of the day, and the Readings.*  
*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 61):*[^7]

The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because He hath anointed Me; He hath sent Me to preach good tidings unto the poor, to heal the broken in heart, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and recovery of sight to the blind; to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord, and the day of recompense of our God; to comfort all that mourn; to give unto the mourners of Zion glory instead of ashes, the oil of gladness to the mourners, the garment of glory instead of the spirit of despair; and they shall be called generations of righteousness, the planting of the Lord for glory. And they shall build the old waste places, they shall raise up the former desolations, and they shall renew the desert cities, waste from generation to generation. And strangers shall come and feed your sheep, and aliens shall be your plowmen and your vinedressers. But ye shall be named priests of the Lord, ministers of your God; it shall be said unto you: Ye shall eat the riches of the Gentiles, and in their wealth shall ye be admired. Instead of your double shame, and instead of confusion, their portion shall rejoice; therefore in their land they shall possess the double, and everlasting joy shall be upon their head. For I am the Lord Who loves righteousness, and hates robbery of injustice; and I will give their labor unto the righteous, and I will make an everlasting covenant with them. And their seed shall be known among the Gentiles, and their offspring in the midst of the peoples; every one that sees them shall acknowledge them, that these are the seed blessed by God. And with gladness they shall rejoice in the Lord.

*Reading from the Book of Leviticus (Chapter 26):*[^8]

The Lord spoke unto the children of Israel, saying: If ye walk in My ordinances, and keep My commandments, and do them: then I will give you rain in its season, and the land shall yield its increase, and the trees...

=== LEAF p6 === (Fol. 2v)
...of the field shall yield their fruit. And your threshing shall overtake the vintage, and the vintage shall overtake the sowing; and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell safely in your land. And I will give peace in your land, and ye shall sleep, and none shall make you afraid; and I will destroy the wild beasts out of your land, and the sword shall not go through your land. And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you slain. And five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall put ten thousands to flight; and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword. And I will look upon you, and bless you, and make you increase, and multiply you, and establish My covenant with you. And ye shall eat the old store, and the old of the old, and clear out the old because of the new. And I will set My covenant among you, and My soul shall not abhor you. And I will walk among you, and will be your God, and ye shall be My people. But if ye will not give ear unto Me, and will not do these My commandments; but disobey them, and if your soul loathe My judgments, so that ye will not do all My commandments, and so that ye break My covenant: I also will do this unto you: I will bring upon you scarcity, and ye shall sow your seed in vain, and your enemies shall eat your labors. And I will set My face against you, and ye shall fall before your enemies, and they that hate you shall chase you, and ye shall flee when none pursues you. And I will break the pride of your power; and I will make your heaven as iron, and your earth as brass. And your strength shall be spent in vain, and your land shall not yield her seed, neither shall the trees of the field yield their fruit. And I will send wild beasts of the earth upon you, which shall devour you, and destroy your cattle, and make you few in number, and the coming sword shall destroy you. And your land shall be desolate, and your courts shall be waste; because ye walk contrary unto Me, I also will walk contrary unto you in fury: says the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel.

*For the Venerable One:*  
*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Chapter 4):*[^9]

Though the righteous happen to die early, yet shall he be in rest; for honorable age is not that which consists in length of time, nor is it measured by number of years. But wisdom is the gray hair unto men, and an unspotted life is old age. He pleased God, and was beloved of Him; so that living among sinners he was translated, he was caught away, lest wickedness should alter his understanding, or deceit beguile his soul.


=== LEAF p7 === (Fol. 3r)
For the fascination of wickedness darkens that which is good, and the wandering of desire perverts the innocent mind. Being made perfect in a short time, he fulfilled long years; for his soul pleased the Lord, therefore hasted He to take him away from among the wicked. And the people saw it, and understood it not, neither laid they this up in their minds: that His grace and mercy is with His venerable ones, and that He hath visited His chosen.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Indiction, Tone 1. Idiomela. By John the Monk:*

The beginning of the year has arrived, summoning us to honor those who have shone forth therein: Callista, Evodius, and Hermogenes, brothers of one mother and sufferers; Symeon equal to the Angels; and Joshua son of Nun; and the seven youths in Ephesus; and the choir of the forty-two holy women. Partaking in their memory, O feast-lovers, let us cry aloud in piety: O Lord, bless the works of Thy hands, and grant that we may worthily pass through the cycle of the year.

*Verse: Praise is due unto Thee, O God, in Zion, and unto Thee shall the vow be performed in Jerusalem [LXX Ps. 64:2].*

O Christ our God, Who by wisdom hast made all things, and brought them from non-existence into being: bless the crown of the year, and preserve our cities unharmed; and make glad our right-believing King by Thy power, granting him victories over adversaries, for the sake of the Mother of God, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Tone 2. Verse: We shall be filled with the good things of Thy house; holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness [LXX Ps. 64:5b–c].*  
*By Cyprian:*

Wonderful art Thou, O God, and marvelous are Thy works, and Thy paths are past finding out; for Thou art the Wisdom of God, and perfect Hypostasis, and Power: co-unoriginate and co-eternal, and by Thy creative almighty power Thou didst come into the world, thereby to adorn Thy creation, ineffably born of a Mother that knew not man, without changing Thy Godhead, ordaining seasons and years unchangeably for our salvation. Therefore we cry unto Thee: O Good Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Thou shalt bless the crown of the year of Thy goodness, O Lord [LXX Ps. 64:12].*  
*By Damascene:*

O Thou Who in wisdom hast fashioned all things, eternal Word of the Father, and Who by Thine almighty word hast framed every creature: bless the crown of the year of Thy goodness, and cast down heresies for the sake of the Mother of God, as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind.

*Glory... Of the Saint, Tone 5:*

O venerable father, thou didst finely find a ladder whereby thou didst ascend to the height, which Elijah found as a fiery chariot; yet he left no ascent for others, but thou even after death hast thy pillar: O heavenly man, earthly angel, sleepless light of the universe, venerable Symeon, pray that our souls may be saved.

*Both now... Of the Indiction, Tone 5. By John the Monk:*

O King Who art ever and abidest unto endless ages, receive the supplication of sinners asking for salvation; and grant, O Lover of mankind, fertility unto Thy land, bestowing seasonable weather; fight alongside our right-believing King against godless barbarians, even as of old Thou didst for David, for they have entered into Thine habitations and defiled the all-holy place, O Savior; but do Thou grant victories, O Christ God, by the prayers of the Mother of God: for Thou art the victory and boast of the Orthodox.

*Troparion of the Indiction, Tone 2:*

O Creator of all creation, Who hast placed the times and seasons in Thine own power: bless the crown of the year of Thy goodness, O Lord, preserving in peace the King and Thy city, by the prayers of the Mother of God, and save us.

*Glory... Of the Venerable One, Tone 1:*

Thou wast a pillar of endurance, rivaling the Forefathers, O venerable one: Job in sufferings, Joseph in temptations, and the life of the bodiless powers while in the body, O Symeon our father; pray unto Christ God that our souls may be saved.

*Both now... To the Mother of God, Tone 1:*

Rejoice, O full of grace, Virgin Mother of God, haven and intercession of the human race; for from thee was incarnate the Redeemer of the world: for thou alone art Mother and Virgin, ever-blessed and all-glorified; pray unto Christ God to grant peace unto all the universe.

*Glory... Of the Venerable One, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and the Word" (Премудрости):*

Having directed thy mind to higher things, O wise one, and followed Christ unto the end, thou didst not in mind return unto the world, O God-wise one; by self-mastery and labors having put passions to death, thou didst prepare a temple for thy Lord. Therefore thou didst receive the recompense of gifts, to heal the diseased and drive away spirits, O most venerable Symeon; pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who feast with love thy holy memory.

*Both now... Of the Indiction: "As unto the Creator of all..." (see fol. 3v).[^10]*  
*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and the Word" (Премудрости и Слова):*

Having divinely enriched thy soul with self-mastery, labors, and prayers, thou didst become a partaker with the martyrs, O blessed one; and in truth thou didst receive the gift of miracles to heal the diseases of those who faithfully honor thee. Therefore, also driving away the demon hosts by the grace given thee from on high, pray for us, O most blessed Symeon; pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who feast with love thy holy memory.

*Glory... Tone 4, Podoben: "Speedily Anticipate" (Скоро предвари):*

Thou didst attain unto the contest of martyrdom, faithfully proclaiming Christ, O Callista, with thy two brothers; for nurtured by desire for our God, thou didst make them in truth spiritual vessels of the Church of Christ. Therefore also thou wast united with them, O martyr, in the life on high.

*Both now... Theotokion:*

Speedily receive our supplications, O Sovereign Lady, and bring them unto thy Son and God, O all-pure Lady; dispel the tribulations of those who run unto thee; dispel the plots and insolence, O Virgin, of those who now take up arms against thy servants.

*(If it be a temple of Venerable Symeon.) After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn of the Venerable One, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and the Word" (Премудрости):*

Thou didst surpass in faith, O wise father, and having despised all temporal things, thou didst follow Christ by the power of the Spirit. By self-mastery thou didst waste away thy body, O venerable one, ever foreseeing the heavenly glory. Therefore also thou didst find the ladder of the pillar for a divine ascent, answering to thy desire, O most sacred Symeon; pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who feast with love thy holy memory.

*Glory... Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-Unoriginate" (Собезначалное):*

By self-mastery thou didst adorn thy life, and having put thy body to death, thou didst turn to naught the assaults of the enemy, O blessed father; and thou hast departed unto God into everlasting life, as a worthy heir. Therefore cease not to pray, that our souls may find mercy.

*Both now... Theotokion:*

O Most Holy Virgin, have mercy upon us who flee with faith unto thee, compassionate one, and who ask for thy fervent protection; for thou art able as good to save all, being the Mother of God the Most High, ever entreating Him with thy maternal supplications, O full of the grace of God.

*The 1st Antiphon of the Songs of Ascents, Tone 4. Prokeimenon, Gospel, and Sticheron of the Temple. Then Psalm 50. Canons, 3: of the Indiction, Tone 1, with the Heirmos on 6, the composition of the Lord John the Monk; the Heirmos twice, the Troparia on 4.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Let us all people sing a song of victory unto Him Who delivered Israel from bitter bondage to Pharaoh, and led them dryshod through the depth of the sea: for He is glorified.

*Troparia:*

Let us all sing a song of victory unto Christ, by Whom all things were framed, and in Whom they were made indivisible, as unto the hypostatic Word born of the unoriginate Father: for He is glorified.

Let us all sing a song of victory unto Christ, Who by the Father's good pleasure appeared from the Virgin, and proclaimed the acceptable year of the Lord for our redemption: for He is glorified.

Having come to Nazareth, the Giver of the Law taught on the Sabbath day, ordaining for the Hebrews His ineffable coming, whereby as compassionate He saves our race.

*Theotokion:*

Hymning the all-marvelous Maiden, who caused Christ to shine forth unto the universe and filled all things with the joy of eternal life, let us all faithful praise her: for He is glorified.
