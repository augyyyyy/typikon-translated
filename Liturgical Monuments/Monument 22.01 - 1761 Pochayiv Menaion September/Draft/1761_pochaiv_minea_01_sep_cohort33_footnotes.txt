[^2196]: The acrostic in Church Slavonic is: *Сы́на гро́мова христокра́снѡ пою̀* ("I sing of the Son of Thunder in Christ-befitting beauty"), corresponding to the Greek iambic acrostic: Υἱὸν βροντῆς χριστοτερπῶς ᾄσομαι ("I shall sing of the Son of Thunder pleasingly to Christ"), composed by St. Theophanes Graptos.
[^2197]: The Slavonic acrostic is: *Моле́нїа бж҃е́ственномꙋ таи́нникꙋ несꙋ̀* ("I offer supplications to the divine initiate of mysteries"), corresponding to the Greek: Μέλπω σε θεῖον μύστην ("I hymn thee, O divine initiate of mysteries"), composed by St. Joseph the Hymnographer.
[^2198]: Although rubricated as *И́нъ. Ірмо́съ то́йже* ("Another. The same Heirmos"), this stanza is the first troparion of the apostle, concluding with the refrain of the Heirmos (*въ не́йже оу҆тверди́ся моѐ се́рдце*).
[^2199]: The Church Slavonic phrase *исходя́ща из̾ безнача́лнагѡ Ѻ҆ц҃а̀: и почива́юща въ Сн҃ѣ неисхо́днѡ* ("proceeding from the unoriginate Father and abiding in the Son unceasingly") represents the classical patristic pneumatological formula of St. John of Damascus (*De Fide Orthodoxa* I.8) and St. Tarasius of Constantinople, affirming the Father as the sole eternal cause (*aitia*) while asserting the eternal resting and shining forth of the Spirit through the Son.
