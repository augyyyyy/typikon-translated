# EXAPOSTILARIA AND EVANGELICAL STICHERA

*The Resurrectional Morning Gospels, with the Resurrectional Exapostilaria and Evangelical Stichera.*[^11002]  
*(The Morning Stichera are the work of Emperor Leo the Wise; and the Exapostilaria of his son, Emperor Constantine.)*[^11003]

*Resurrectional Gospel 1. From Matthew, Pericope 116 [Matt. 28:16-20]:*[^11004]  
At that time, the eleven disciples went into Galilee, into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them. And when they saw Him, they worshipped Him; but some doubted. And Jesus came and spoke unto them, saying: All power is given unto Me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even unto the end of the age. Amen.[^11005]

*Exapostilarion 1:*  
With the disciples let us ascend the mountain of Galilee, in faith to behold Christ, saying that He has received authority over heavenly and earthly things; let us learn how He teaches to baptize all nations in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, and to abide with the initiates of His mysteries, as He promised, even unto the end of the age.[^11006]

*Theotokion:*  
With the disciples thou didst rejoice, O Theotokos Virgin, for thou didst behold Christ risen from the tomb on the third day as He said; unto whom He also appeared, teaching and revealing better things, and commanding to baptize in the Father, and the Son, and the Spirit, so that we may believe in His rising, and glorify thee, O Maiden.[^11007]

*Evangelical Sticheron 1, Tone 1:*  
When the disciples went up into the mountain for the earthly ascension, the Lord stood before them; and having worshipped Him, and learned of the authority given Him everywhere, they were sent into all under heaven to preach the resurrection from the dead, and the ascension into heaven; with whom also the truthful Christ God, the Savior of our souls, promised to abide unto the ages.[^11008]

*Resurrectional Gospel 2. From Mark, Pericope 70 [Mark 16:1-8]:*[^11009]

=== LEAF p995 ===
...the first day of the week, they came unto the tomb at the rising of the sun. And they said among themselves: Who shall roll us away the stone from the door of the tomb? And when they looked, they saw that the stone was rolled away: for it was very great. And entering into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, clothed in a long white garment; and they were affrighted. And he says unto them: Be not affrighted: Ye seek Jesus of Nazareth, Who was crucified: He is risen; He is not here: behold the place where they laid Him. But go your way, tell His disciples and Peter that He goes before you into Galilee: there shall ye see Him, as He said unto you. *Exapostilarion 2:*  
Seeing the stone rolled away, the myrrh-bearing women rejoiced; for they saw a young man sitting in the tomb, and he said unto them: Behold, Christ is risen; say unto the disciples with Peter: hasten unto the mountain of Galilee, there shall He appear unto you, as He foretold unto His friends.[^11012]

*Theotokion:*  
An angel indeed brought unto the Virgin the "Rejoice" before Thy conception, O Christ; and an angel rolled away the stone of Thy tomb: the former instead of sorrow bringing tokens of ineffable joy, and the latter instead of death proclaiming and magnifying the Giver of life, and speaking of the Resurrection unto the women and the initiates of the mysteries.[^11013]

*Evangelical Sticheron 2, Tone 2:*  
Unto the women who came with Mary bringing myrrh, and were at a loss how they might obtain their desire, the stone appeared taken away, and a divine young man assuaged the turmoil of their souls: for Jesus the Lord is risen, says he; therefore preach ye unto His preachers, the disciples, to hasten unto Galilee, and to behold Him risen from the dead, as Giver of life and Lord.[^11014]

*Resurrectional Gospel 3. From Mark, Pericope 71 [Mark 16:9-20]:*[^11015]

*Theotokion:*  
The Sun that has shone forth this day from the tomb as a bridegroom from his chamber, having despoiled hades and abolished death: by the prayers of her that bare Thee, send down light upon us, light enlightening hearts and souls, light guiding all to walk in the paths of Thy commandments and in the way of peace.[^11020]

*Evangelical Sticheron 3, Tone 3:*

*Theotokion:*  
Saying "Rejoice," Thou didst turn the sorrow of the forebears into joy, O Lord, bringing the joy of Thy resurrection into the world; by her that bare Thee, O Giver of life, send down therefore light enlightening hearts, the light of Thy compassions, that we may cry unto Thee: O Lover of mankind, God-Man, glory to Thy resurrection.[^11027]

*Evangelical Sticheron 4, Tone 4:*
