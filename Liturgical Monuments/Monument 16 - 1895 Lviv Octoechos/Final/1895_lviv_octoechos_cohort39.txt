*Verse: I will remember thy name in every generation and generation.*[^3647]
Save those who piously worship thy birthgiving from every assault of the enemy, O all-immaculate Mother of God, as the protectress of our race. Having now obtained thy help, we have all gained a refuge and confirmation, and a mediatress unto the Master Christ God: Him do thou entreat, we pray, to grant peace unto the world, and remission of transgressions unto those who flee unto thy protection.[^3648]

*Verse: Give ear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear.*[^3649]
Behold, I uncover unto thee the stripes of my soul, and show unto thee the wretched bruises which robbers have inflicted upon me through subtle thoughts and demonic rebellions, O all-immaculate Lady! Having given birth to the Physician of souls and bodies, grant me healing through the removal of these, pouring out the oil of thy tenderheartedness and goodness, that I may worthily receive salvation and deliverance.[^3650]

*Verse: The virgins that follow after her shall be brought unto the King.*[^3651]
I, wretched one, will not hide my misery, for I have acquired all things that God hates, having defiled my flesh and my spirit and my mental thoughts with unfitting deeds and foul words; with my tongue I judge those who sin, but I myself do more grievous things. O Mother of God, grant me amendment of all these things, that having sobered up from my evil habits, I may fall down and weep over those things which I have miserably committed in the conduct of my life.[^3652]

*Glory... both now... Theotokion:*
The Son Who with the Father and the Spirit is glorified in the highest by the Seraphim, wishing to renew again the first-created man, emptied Himself entirely in an ineffable manner into thy womb, O all-hymned Theotokos; and having shone forth from thee, He enlightened the whole world by His Godhead, delivered it from the madness of idols, deified it through Himself, and led mankind up into the heavens: Christ God and the Savior of our souls.[^3653]

*Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart. Trisagion. Troparion: The radiant proclamation of the Resurrection. Theotokion: The mystery hidden from all ages. Litany and dismissal.*[^3654]

Angels and men, O Savior, sing of Thy third-day rising, whereby the ends of the universe were illumined, and we all were delivered from the bondage of the enemy, crying: O life-creating, almighty Savior, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind.[^3659]

The gates of brass didst Thou shatter, and the iron bars didst Thou crush, O Christ God, and didst raise up fallen mankind; therefore with one accord we cry: O Lord Who didst rise from the dead, glory to Thee.[^3660]
