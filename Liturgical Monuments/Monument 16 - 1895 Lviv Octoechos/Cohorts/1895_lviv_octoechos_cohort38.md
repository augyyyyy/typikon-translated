The gates of hades didst Thou shatter, O Lord, and by Thy death Thou didst destroy the kingdom of death; and Thou didst free the human race from corruption, granting life and incorruption unto the world, and great mercy.[^3643]

A great and most glorious wonder was wrought in thee! And who is able to declare the mighty acts of the Lord that were accomplished in thee, O Theotokos? For who from the ages hath heard of a virgin nursing with breasts? and of a mother appearing on earth without a husband? and bearing in her arms her Creator as an Infant?
