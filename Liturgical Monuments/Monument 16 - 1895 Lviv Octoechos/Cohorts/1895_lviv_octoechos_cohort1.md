# TONE 1

*[Engraved Headpiece: An ornamental border depicting the Prophet Moses, King David, the Prophet Isaiah, Christ the Good Shepherd, the Most Pure Virgin Theotokos with the Christ Child, and Saint John Chrysostom]*[^15]

**THE BEGINNING, WITH GOD MOST HOLY, OF THE OCTOECHOS, THAT IS, THE BOOK OF EIGHT TONES.**

*On Saturday evening, at Little Vespers:*[^16]

*On "Lord, I have cried," we set 4 verses, and we sing 3 Resurrectional stichera, repeating the sticheron of the first verse.*[^17]

*Tone 1:*

*(The work of our venerable father John Damascene)*[^18]

*Resurrectional Sticheron 1:* Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins; for Thou alone art He Who hath manifested the Resurrection unto the world.

*Resurrectional Sticheron 2:* Encompass Sion, ye peoples, and embrace her, and give glory within her unto Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered us from our iniquities.[^19]

*Resurrectional Sticheron 3:* Come, ye peoples, let us sing and worship Christ, glorifying His Resurrection from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered the world from the deceit of the enemy.

*Glory... Both now...*

*Theotokion Dogmatikon, in the same Tone:*

=== LEAF p4 ===

*Theotokion Dogmatikon (continued):* Today is a festival of virginity, brethren! Let creation leap for joy, let mankind dance; for the holy Theotokos hath summoned us, the undefiled treasury of virginity, the spiritual paradise of the Second Adam, the storehouse of the union of the two natures, the festival of the saving reconciliation, the bridal chamber wherein the Word truly espoused the flesh, the light cloud that bore in the body Him Who is above the Cherubim. By her prayers, O Christ God, save our souls.

*Then: "O Joyful Light." Prokimenon: "The Lord is King," with its verses [LXX Ps. 92:1];*[^20] *and after "Vouchsafe, O Lord," the priest says no litany, but we sing the Resurrectional sticheron at the Aposticha, in Tone 1:*

*Resurrectional Aposticha Sticheron:* By Thy Passion, O Christ, we have been set free from passions, and by Thy Resurrection we have been delivered from corruption; O Lord, glory to Thee.

*Other Stichera to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*

*Verse: I will remember thy name in every generation and generation [LXX Ps. 44:18].*[^21]

*Sticheron 1:* Preeminently glorified art thou throughout generations of generations, O Virgin Maiden Mother, Theotokos Mary, protectress of the world, having in the flesh given birth to the Son of the unoriginate Father, truly co-eternal with the Spirit; entreat Him that we may be saved.

*Verse: Give ear, O daughter, and behold, and incline thine ear [LXX Ps. 44:11].*[^22]

*Sticheron 2:* Held fast by unexpected afflictions, O pure one, having thee as our sole intercessor, O Virgin, we cry aloud in thanksgiving: Save us, O all-holy Bride of God; for thou art the refuge of the world, and the protection of our race.

*Verse: The rich among the people shall pray before thy face [LXX Ps. 44:13].*[^23]

*Sticheron 3:* The world was renewed at thy childbirth, O maiden Theotokos, salvation of the faithful and unslumbering intercessor for those who piously pray to thee, O all-pure one! Cease not fervently praying for all who sing thy praise.

*Glory... Both now...*

*Dogmatikon, in the same Tone:*

*Theotokion Dogmatikon:* A cloud of the co-eternal Light did the Prophet call thee, O Virgin; for descending from thee like rain upon the fleece [LXX Ps. 71:6],[^24] the Father's Word, and having shone forth from thee, enlightened the world and destroyed deceit, Christ our God. Praying fervently unto Him, O most holy one, we pray: Cease not to intercede for us who confess thee to be the true Theotokos.

*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart." Trisagion Prayers. After "Our Father:" The Resurrectional troparion (see fol. 5);*[^25] *Glory... Both now... its Theotokion. The litany: "Have mercy on us, O God," and the Dismissal.*

*On Saturday at Great Vespers:*[^26]

*After the Initial Psalm, the usual kathisma of the Psalter.*[^27]

*On "Lord, I have cried," we set 10 verses, and we sing 3 Resurrectional stichera, 4 Eastern stichera, and 3 or 6 from the Menaion. If a celebrated saint occurs, Glory... from the Menaion; Both now... Theotokion 1 of the Tone.*[^28]

*Resurrectional Stichera of the Octoechos, Tone 1:*

*Resurrectional Sticheron 1:* Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins, for Thou alone art He Who hath manifested the Resurrection unto the world.

*Resurrectional Sticheron 2:* Encompass Sion, ye peoples, and embrace her, and give glory within her unto Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered us from our iniquities.

*Resurrectional Sticheron 3:* Come, ye peoples, let us sing and worship Christ, glorifying His Resurrection from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered the world from the deceit of the enemy.

*Other Eastern Stichera, Tone 1:*[^29]

*Eastern Sticheron 1:* Rejoice, ye heavens; trumpet forth, ye foundations of the earth; cry out in rejoicing, ye mountains [LXX Isa. 44:23, 49:13]![^30] For behold, Emmanuel hath nailed our sins to the Cross, and giving life, He hath slain death, having raised up Adam, as the Lover of mankind.

*Eastern Sticheron 2:* Let us sing unto Him Who was willingly crucified in the flesh for our sake, Who suffered, and was buried, and rose from the dead, saying: Establish Thy Church in Orthodoxy, O Christ, and bring peace unto our life, as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind.

*Eastern Sticheron 3:* Standing before Thy life-receiving tomb, we unworthy ones offer praise unto Thine ineffable lovingkindness, O Christ our God; for Thou, the Sinless One, didst accept the Cross and death, that Thou mightest grant resurrection unto the world, as the Lover of mankind.

*Eastern Sticheron 4:* Let us praise the Word, co-unoriginate and co-eternal with the Father, Who came forth from the virginal

=== LEAF p6 ===

*Eastern Sticheron 4 (continued):* womb ineffably, and willingly accepted the Cross and death for our sake, and rose in glory, saying: O Giver of life, Lord, glory to Thee, Savior of our souls.

*Glory... from the Menaion; Both now... Theotokion:*

*Theotokion Dogmatikon:* Let us praise the Virgin Mary, the glory of all the world, who blossomed forth from men and gave birth to the Master, the heavenly gate, the hymn of the bodiless powers, and the beauty of the faithful; for she was revealed as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she destroyed the barrier of enmity, brought in peace, and opened the kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the anchor of faith, we have as our champion the Lord Who was born of her. Be of good courage therefore, be of good courage, O people of God; for He shall defeat the enemies, as He is all-powerful.

*The Entrance. "O Joyful Light." After the Entrance, the daily Prokimenon:*

*Tone 6: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty [LXX Ps. 92:1].*[^31]

*Verse 1: The Lord is robed in strength, and He hath girded Himself [LXX Ps. 92:1].*[^32]

*Verse 2: For He hath established the world, so that it shall not be moved [LXX Ps. 92:1].*[^33]

*Verse 3: Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days [LXX Ps. 92:5].*[^34]

*Then the usual litany. "Vouchsafe, O Lord." "Let us complete our evening prayer," and the rest.*

*At the Aposticha:*

*Resurrectional Sticheron, Tone 1:*

*Resurrectional Sticheron:* By Thy Passion, O Christ, we have been set free from passions, and by Thy Resurrection we have been delivered from corruption. O Lord, glory to Thee.

*Other Stichera in Alphabetical Order.*[^35] *Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty [LXX Ps. 92:1].*

*Alphabetical Sticheron 1:* Let creation rejoice, let the heavens be glad, let the nations clap their hands with gladness; for Christ our Savior hath nailed our sins to the Cross, and having slain death, hath bestowed life upon us, having raised up all-father Adam, as the Lover of mankind.

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it shall not be moved [LXX Ps. 92:1].*

*Alphabetical Sticheron 2:* Being King of heaven and earth, O Incomprehensible One, Thou wast willingly crucified in Thy love for mankind. When Hades met Thee below, it was embittered, and receiving the souls of the righteous, they rejoiced; and Adam, seeing Thee, the Creator, in the nethermost regions, arose. O marvel! How did the Life of all taste death? But as He willed, He enlightened the world that cries aloud and saith: Risen from the dead, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days [LXX Ps. 92:5].*

*Alphabetical Sticheron 3:* Bearing myrrh, the myrrh-bearing women with haste and lamentation reached Thy tomb; and not finding Thy most pure body, yet learning from the Angel of the new and glorious miracle, they said unto the Apostles: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Glory... from the Menaion, if there is one; but if not, Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

*Theotokion:* Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled [Isa. 7:14];[^36] for as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and after childbirth thou didst remain even as before childbirth; for He Who was born was God, and therefore He re-fashioned the nature of things. But O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants, which are offered unto thee in thy temple; but as thou bearest the Compassionate One in thine arms, have pity upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart." Trisagion Prayers. After "Our Father:"*

*Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 1:*

*Resurrectional Troparion:* While the stone was sealed by the Jews and the soldiers were guarding Thy most pure body, Thou didst arise on the third day, O Savior, granting life unto the world; therefore the heavenly powers cried aloud unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O Christ; glory to Thy kingdom; glory to Thy dispensation, O Thou Who alone art the Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*

*Theotokion:* When Gabriel proclaimed unto thee, "Hail!" O Virgin, at his voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, even as righteous David said [LXX Ps. 131:8].[^37] Thou wast revealed as more spacious than the heavens, having borne Thy Creator. Glory to Him Who made His abode within thee; glory to Him Who came forth from thee; glory to Him Who hath set us free through thy childbirth!

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos:*[^43]

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:* Israel, delivered from bitter bondage, crossed the impassable sea as dry land. Beholding the enemy drowned, it sang a hymn unto God as Benefactor, Who performs wonders with an outstretched arm; for He hath been glorified.

*Refrain: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion 1:* The angelic ranks were struck with awe in fear, O Sovereign Lady, praising thee; and every intellect sings of thee as Mother of the Creator because of goodness; for having given birth to Christ, thou didst surpass every rank of praise.

*Troparion 2:* Tormented by grievous tribulations and harassed by foes, wretch that I am, I call out with tears: Stretch forth thy hand unto me from on high, O all-abundant one, delivering me, and vouchsafe that I may live without peril through thy prayers. Glory...

*Troparion 3:* Heal the secret transgressions of my soul with the medicine of mercy, and calm the surging of the flesh, O Theotokos! And turning back the spears and arrows of the enemies, pierce them firmly into their own hearts. Both now...

*Troparion 4:* The virginal womb consumed the ancient, murderous pasture by giving birth to Christ; therefore all creation now rejoices, O all-pure one, as having been brought to life, and in unison sings praises unto thy Son and God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* Let not the mortal boast in his own wisdom and riches [LXX 1 Reg. 2:10; Jer. 9:23],[^44] but in the faith of the Lord, orthodoxly crying unto Christ God and singing ever: Establish me upon the rock of Thy commandments, O Master.

*Refrain: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion 1:* Sleeping once on his journey, great Jacob marveled as he beheld the angels descending from on high unto the earth upon a ladder, O Virgin [Gen. 28:12];[^45] and awakening, he clearly depicted thee as the gate of heaven.

*Troparion 2:* Cast into tribulations by a temporary restraint, wretch that I am, and tormented by tempestuous perils, woe is me, I cry: O thou who didst give birth to God and hast exalted our horn, save me by thy prayers. Glory...

*Troparion 3:* Stretching forth a mighty hand from heaven, O Christ, King over all visible and intelligible things, my Jesus, put the heads of the enemies under the feet of those who faithfully proclaim the Theotokos to be Thy Mother. Both now...

*Troparion 4:* Having of old been purified by the coal of the Spirit, Isaiah clearly cried out that the Son was to be born of thy womb, O all-abundant Virgin,

*Troparion 4 (continued):* to be born; Whom in the latter times, for my sake, without a husband thou didst bear.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* Of old Habakkuk heard Thy marvelous report, O Christ, and cried aloud with fear: God shall come from Theman, and the Holy One from the dense overshadowed mountain, to save His anointed [LXX Hab. 3:2-3, 13].[^46] Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Refrain: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion 1:* Forefiguring thee as the one who among women is fair and all-honorable, coming up as from the wilderness [Cant. 3:6, 8:5][^47] and bearing the Branch Christ on thine arm [Isa. 11:1],[^48] O Mother of God, the prince of myrrh cried aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion 2:* Incline Thine ear unto me, O good one, and behold my affliction and the multiplication of perils; for having stretched the soul's eye toward thee, O Lady, and bowing my knees with tears, I now entreat thee, crying aloud: Put an end unto the turmoil of my tribulations. Glory...

*Troparion 3:* Knowing thee to be an invincible wall, drawn by supplication, I thy servant now flee unto thee; and I account the arrows of the foes as infantile and ineffective, O all-abundant one! Therefore, rejoicing, I cry aloud: Glory to thy childbirth, O Mother of God!

*Both now...*

*Troparion 4:* The power of the Most High overshadowed thee, O Virgin, by the descent of the Divine Spirit [Luke 1:35];[^49] and then beyond nature, the Lord of all things, having given life to flesh and soul, united it unto Himself, remaining in the same nature.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* Shine forth Thy never-setting light, O Christ, into the hearts of those who faithfully sing of Thee, granting us peace which surpasses all understanding [Phil. 4:7],[^50] that running from the night of ignorance unto the day by Thy light, we may give glory unto Thee, O Lover of mankind.

*Refrain: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion 1:* Having of old foreseen thee as the unhewn mountain, O all-lauded one, Daniel cried aloud manifestly of the precious stone, Christ the Savior of the world, Who was cut from thee [Dan. 2:34, 45];[^51] and honoring Him now, we the faithful praise thee, O Virgin Bride of God.

*Troparion 2:* Fallen, wretch that I am, into many tribulations, praying with anguish and weeping of heart, shamelessly I, thy servant, cry aloud: Deliver me, O Theotokos, from the troubles that encompass my lowliness, and fill me with gladness. Glory...

*Troparion 3:* Calm the fierce abyss of my passions with thy mighty prayer, O good one; for thou who didst give birth to Christ apart from passions,

*Troparion 3 (continued):* that living the remainder of my life in tranquility of soul, I may sing thy praise in hymns.

*Troparion 4:* How bearest thou God upon thine arm? Tell us; and how givest thou suck unto Him Who holds all things in His hand? O all-blessed Virgin! "I remain pure" (she said) "even after childbirth, having given birth to Christ God, taking away the debt of Adam and of the ancestral mother."

*Troparion 3:* Wretch that I am, I have fallen into the pit of destruction, and many beasts surround me. But O Lady, by thy prayers turn away the stones and preserve thy servant unharmed; for thou didst bear in thy womb Christ, the chief Cornerstone [LXX Ps. 117:22; Eph. 2:20].[^53] Both now...
