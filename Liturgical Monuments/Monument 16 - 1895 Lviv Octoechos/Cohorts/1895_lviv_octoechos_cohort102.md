# GENERAL PARASTAS (OFFICE FOR THE DEPARTED)

*Instructions.*[^11160]  
*Instruction on the Service of the Whole Week,*[^11161]  
*how the Octoechos is sung with the Menaion.*[^11162]  

It is meet to know: if a Saint having a vigil occurs on Sunday: at Great Vespers on Saturday, Resurrection Stichera 6, Anatolian 1, and to the Saint 6; Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Tone. In like manner it is sung on forefeasts and afterfeasts with a Saint, or when two Saints occur. At the Aposticha we always sing the Resurrectional Stichera, except for the Temple. Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion in the same Tone. On Sunday at Matins, after the Kathismata, Resurrection Sessional Hymns, Polyeleos to the Saint, and his Sessional Hymns all in order, except the Theotokia, only after Glory: Both now: Theotokion. Graduals of the Tone. Sunday Gospel, except for the Temple. Resurrection Canon with Heirmos to 4, of the Theotokos to 2, and to the Saint to 8. Thus it is sung if two Saints occur, or a forefeast or afterfeast with a Saint. But if a Saint is celebrated to 6: the Cross-Resurrection Canon is omitted, and we sing the Resurrection Canon to 4, of the Theotokos to 2, and to the Saint to 8. But if a Saint is celebrated to 4: the Resurrection Canon with Heirmos is sung to 4, the Cross-Resurrection to 3, of the Theotokos to 3, and in the Menaion to 4. The Resurrection Kontakion after Ode 6 always takes precedence, except for a feast and the Temple. The rest as indicated in the Typikon.[^11163]

On Sunday evening we say as indicated in the Octoechos, only at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera in the Octoechos 3, then to the Saint 3; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion. If not: Glory: Both now: Theotokion. Then the Prokeimenon of the day, and the rest. At the Aposticha in the Octoechos, Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion. If not: Glory: Both now: Theotokion. If to the Saint in the Typikon to 6: then in the Octoechos the evening Stichera are omitted, and we sing Stichera to the Saint to 6; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion. If not: Glory: Both now: Theotokion. But if it be for two Saints, and each has their own Stichera, likewise we say to them 3 Stichera each, and we say Glory likewise if there be one for either Saint; Both now: Theotokion. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: Theotokion. After "Now lettest Thou depart" and after the Trisagion, we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Daily Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion. If there are two Saints and they have two Troparia: we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Troparion to the other Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the lesser, according to the Tone of the other Troparion.[^11164]

If there be a forefeast or an afterfeast: at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera of the feast 3, and to the Saint 3; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: of the feast. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: of the feast. At the Aposticha, of the feast; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: of the feast. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: of the feast. After "Now lettest Thou depart" and after the Trisagion, we say the Troparion to the Saint, if there is one; Glory: Both now: of the feast. If not, then we say one Troparion of the feast; and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast and of a Saint who has a Polyeleos or Vigil: we sing evening and morning and at the Liturgy as indicated in the Typikon in order. Compline we sing according to the custom. After "It is truly meet" and after the Trisagion, we say the Troparion of the Temple. If it be the Temple of a Saint, we-[^11165]

=== LEAF p1017 ===
-say the Troparion of the day, and thereafter of the Temple of the Saint; then the general Troparion: "O God of our fathers"; thereafter: "Thou Who in all the world"; then Glory: "With the Saints give rest"; Both now: Theotokion: "Through the prayers, O Lord, of all the Saints." If it be a Temple of Christ, on Tuesday at Compline, likewise also on Thursday, we do not say the Troparion of the Temple during the entire year, because the Troparion of the day is said: "Save, O Lord, Thy people"; thereafter to the Temple of the Theotokos or of the Saint, then the general ones, as was also indicated before. Thus we say on the other days also, except Saturdays and Sundays, and forefeasts and afterfeasts. If it occur for a Saint with a Polyeleos, then after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion to the Saint, but the daily Troparia and to the Temple which were indicated, we then do not say. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, then after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion of the feast alone. If it be for a Saint with a Polyeleos or with a Vigil, after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Kontakion of the feast, and the rest as usual, even unto the Dismissal.[^11166]

Midnight Office we sing according to the custom, as indicated in the Typikon; only if there be a Master’s [Dominical] Feast, or a Feast of the Theotokos—which Feasts of the Theotokos are numbered among the Master's Feasts—then at the Midnight Office after the first Trisagion, instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," we say the Troparion of the feast alone; and after the second Trisagion we say the Kontakion of the feast alone, and thereafter "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal; but the prayers "Remember, O Lord, in hope" on those feasts we do not say. On Lazarus Saturday, and on Mid-Pentecost, and on the morrow of Pentecost, and on the morrow of the Nativity of Christ at the Synaxis of the Theotokos, and on the morrow of Theophany at the Synaxis of John the Forerunner, we say likewise at the Midnight Office. And at the Apodosis of Pascha, at the Midnight Office we say: after the first Trisagion, instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," the Resurrection Troparion: "The Word co-unoriginate"; Glory: Both now: its Theotokion. After the second Trisagion, instead of "Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good," we say the Kontakion of Pascha: "Though Thou didst descend into the tomb"; thereafter, "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal; but the prayers "Remember, O Lord" we do not say. If it occur for the Temple of a Saint: instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," we say the Troparion of the Temple; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint; and after the second Trisagion, instead of "Remember, O Lord," we say the Kontakion of the Temple alone. Thereafter, "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal. If there be the Feast of the Nativity of John the Forerunner, or the Beheading, or of the Chief Apostles Peter and Paul: at the Midnight Office we say after the first Trisagion, instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint; after the second Trisagion, instead of "Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good," we say the Kontakion to the Saint; thereafter, "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal; but the prayers "Remember, O Lord, in hope" we do not say.[^11167]

On Monday we sing Matins according to the custom, as indicated, only at "God is the Lord": the Troparion to the Saint twice; Glory: Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint, from the lesser Theotokia. But if there be no Troparion to the Saint in order, then we say the general Troparion to the Saint according to his hierarchic rank or monastic venerable dignity, twice; Glory: Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, then we say the Troparion of the feast twice; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: of the feast.[^11168]

The Penitential Canon we sing with the Heirmos to 6, and of the Bodiless Powers to 4, and of the Saint from the Menaion to 4. But if the Saint is sung to 6, then both Martyria in the Octoechos are omitted, and we sing to the Saint to 6. But if a Polyeleos of the Saint is sung, then the Octoechos is not sung, but we sing the Canon to the Theotokos—or if there be a forefeast or afterfeast—with the Heirmos to 6 (the Heirmos twice, troparia to 4), and to the Saint to 8. Thus indeed throughout the whole week we sing to a Saint with a Polyeleos the Canon to 8. If two Saints occur, the Canon of the Bodiless Powers is omitted, and we sing the Penitential Canon with the Heirmos to 6, and to the Saints to 4 verses each. On Tuesday at Matins: the Penitential Canon with the Heirmos to 6, and to the Forerunner to 4, and of the Saint from the Menaion to 4. But if the Saint is sung to 6, both Martyria in the Octoechos are omitted, and we sing to the Saint to 6. The Polyeleos is according to what was prescribed above. The same happens if there are two Saints: their Canons to 4 verses each. On Wednesday at Matins: the Canon of the Cross with the Heirmos to 6, and of the Theotokos to 4, and of the Saint to 4. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, we say instead of the Theotokion: Both now: of the feast. If it be to the Saint to 6, we say the Sessional Hymn to him twice; Glory: Both now: of the feast. If it be for a Saint with a Polyeleos or with a Vigil, then we say the Kontakion and Ikos to the Saint. *Instruction on the Saturday Service,*  
*when we sing "God is the Lord":*[^11179]  

On Friday evening at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera in the Menaion to the Saint to 6; Glory: to the Saint, if he has one; if not, Glory: Both now: 1st Theotokion according to the occurring Tone. At the Aposticha in the Octoechos, three Martyria which are written at "Lord, I have cried," omitting the 1st; and refrain: "Unto Thee have I lifted up mine eyes" [Ps. 122:1 LXX]; and second: "Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us" [Ps. 122:3 LXX]; Glory: to the Saint, if he has one; if not, Glory: Both now: Theotokion in the Octoechos. After "Our Father": Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion of the occurring Tone; litany, and dismissal. At Compline on Friday after "It is truly meet," after the Trisagion: Troparion to the Temple of Christ and to the Temple of the Theotokos; and to the Temple of a male Saint or female Saint on Friday the Troparion is not said. Then: "Apostles, Martyrs"; Glory: "With the Saints give rest"; Both now: "As first-fruits." If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, we say the Kontakion of the feast. If it be for a Saint with Doxology, we say the Kontakion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: of the feast. Midnight Office we sing according to the custom; only if there be a Master’s [Dominical] Feast, or if the Theotokos is sung with Apodosis, or the Nativity of the Forerunner, or the Beheading, or Peter and Paul, or the Temple of the Saint, and on the Synaxis of the Theotokos, and on the Synaxis of John the Forerunner, we say after the first Trisagion the Troparion, and after the second Trisagion the Kontakion, as was indicated on Monday. At Matins, at "God is the Lord": Troparion to the Saint twice; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint, and not of the occurring Tone, because the Resurrection Theotokion of the Tone is said after the 1st Sessional Hymn at: Both now. But if the Saint has a Polyeleos, then we say at "God is the Lord": after the Troparion of the Saint, the Resurrection Theotokion of the Tone. If on Saturday there be a forefeast or afterfeast, at "God is the Lord": we say the Troparion of the feast twice; Glory: Troparion to the Saint,-[^11180]
