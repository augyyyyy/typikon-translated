## Tuesday: The Holy Forerunner[^4100]

*On Monday Evening:*[^4101]
*On "Lord, I have cried," Penitential Stichera, Tone 4.*
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Being called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*[^4102]

Emulating the Canaanite woman, O my soul, draw near unto Christ from behind, and cry out frequently: Have mercy on me, O Master! For I have a child—my disorderly flesh—that is grievously demonized; drive away the burning inflammation from it, I pray Thee, and having assuaged its unruly leapings, make it dead through Thy fear, through the prayers of her who conceived and bore Thee in all honor, O Christ, and of all the saints, O Benefactor plenteous in mercy.[^4103]

When the Ninevites once had sinned, Thou didst send Jonah, O Christ, to preach unto them; and they, having repented, turned Thy wrath into compassion, being delivered from destructive anger. Therefore, O Lover of mankind, send down upon me also, unworthy though I be, Thy sovereign help, that I may turn away from my countless sins, and be guided into the path of repentance, and weeping, sighing bitterly, be delivered from my many transgressions by Thy mercy.[^4104]

Thou Who camest into the world to save men that sin, and to call them unto repentance, as Thou art tender-hearted: have compassion, O Compassionate One, on me who have angered Thee more than all, and save me for the sake of Thy goodness; direct me into the path of repentance, and grant me a thought of compunction, establishing my heart humble, simple, meek, and guileless, O my Savior, by Thy goodness, as Thou art tender-hearted.[^4105]
