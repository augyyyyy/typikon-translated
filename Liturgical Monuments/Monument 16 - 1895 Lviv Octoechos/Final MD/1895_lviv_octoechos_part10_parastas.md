# PART 10 — GENERAL PARASTAS: OFFICE FOR THE DEPARTED, MEMORIAL CANON, SECRET PRAYERS, KOLYVO RUBRIC, AND COLOPHON

*Instructions.*[^11160]  
*Instruction on the Service of the Whole Week,*[^11161]  
*how the Octoechos is sung with the Menaion.*[^11162]  

It is meet to know: if a Saint having a vigil occurs on Sunday: at Great Vespers on Saturday, Resurrection Stichera 6, Anatolian 1, and to the Saint 6; Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Tone. In like manner it is sung on forefeasts and afterfeasts with a Saint, or when two Saints occur. At the Aposticha we always sing the Resurrectional Stichera, except for the Temple. Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion in the same Tone. On Sunday at Matins, after the Kathismata, Resurrection Sessional Hymns, Polyeleos to the Saint, and his Sessional Hymns all in order, except the Theotokia, only after Glory: Both now: Theotokion. Graduals of the Tone. Sunday Gospel, except for the Temple. Resurrection Canon with Heirmos to 4, of the Theotokos to 2, and to the Saint to 8. Thus it is sung if two Saints occur, or a forefeast or afterfeast with a Saint. But if a Saint is celebrated to 6: the Cross-Resurrection Canon is omitted, and we sing the Resurrection Canon to 4, of the Theotokos to 2, and to the Saint to 8. But if a Saint is celebrated to 4: the Resurrection Canon with Heirmos is sung to 4, the Cross-Resurrection to 3, of the Theotokos to 3, and in the Menaion to 4. The Resurrection Kontakion after Ode 6 always takes precedence, except for a feast and the Temple. The rest as indicated in the Typikon.[^11163]

On Sunday evening we say as indicated in the Octoechos, only at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera in the Octoechos 3, then to the Saint 3; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion. If not: Glory: Both now: Theotokion. Then the Prokeimenon of the day, and the rest. At the Aposticha in the Octoechos, Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion. If not: Glory: Both now: Theotokion. If to the Saint in the Typikon to 6: then in the Octoechos the evening Stichera are omitted, and we sing Stichera to the Saint to 6; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion. If not: Glory: Both now: Theotokion. But if it be for two Saints, and each has their own Stichera, likewise we say to them 3 Stichera each, and we say Glory likewise if there be one for either Saint; Both now: Theotokion. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: Theotokion. After "Now lettest Thou depart" and after the Trisagion, we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Daily Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion. If there are two Saints and they have two Troparia: we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Troparion to the other Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the lesser, according to the Tone of the other Troparion.[^11164]

If there be a forefeast or an afterfeast: at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera of the feast 3, and to the Saint 3; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: of the feast. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: of the feast. At the Aposticha, of the feast; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: of the feast. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: of the feast. After "Now lettest Thou depart" and after the Trisagion, we say the Troparion to the Saint, if there is one; Glory: Both now: of the feast. If not, then we say one Troparion of the feast; and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast and of a Saint who has a Polyeleos or Vigil: we sing evening and morning and at the Liturgy as indicated in the Typikon in order. Compline we sing according to the custom. After "It is truly meet" and after the Trisagion, we say the Troparion of the Temple. If it be the Temple of a Saint, we-[^11165] -say the Troparion of the day, and thereafter of the Temple of the Saint; then the general Troparion: "O God of our fathers"; thereafter: "Thou Who in all the world"; then Glory: "With the Saints give rest"; Both now: Theotokion: "Through the prayers, O Lord, of all the Saints." If it be a Temple of Christ, on Tuesday at Compline, likewise also on Thursday, we do not say the Troparion of the Temple during the entire year, because the Troparion of the day is said: "Save, O Lord, Thy people"; thereafter to the Temple of the Theotokos or of the Saint, then the general ones, as was also indicated before. Thus we say on the other days also, except Saturdays and Sundays, and forefeasts and afterfeasts. If it occur for a Saint with a Polyeleos, then after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion to the Saint, but the daily Troparia and to the Temple which were indicated, we then do not say. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, then after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion of the feast alone. If it be for a Saint with a Polyeleos or with a Vigil, after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Kontakion of the feast, and the rest as usual, even unto the Dismissal.[^11166]

Midnight Office we sing according to the custom, as indicated in the Typikon; only if there be a Master’s [Dominical] Feast, or a Feast of the Theotokos—which Feasts of the Theotokos are numbered among the Master's Feasts—then at the Midnight Office after the first Trisagion, instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," we say the Troparion of the feast alone; and after the second Trisagion we say the Kontakion of the feast alone, and thereafter "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal; but the prayers "Remember, O Lord, in hope" on those feasts we do not say. On Lazarus Saturday, and on Mid-Pentecost, and on the morrow of Pentecost, and on the morrow of the Nativity of Christ at the Synaxis of the Theotokos, and on the morrow of Theophany at the Synaxis of John the Forerunner, we say likewise at the Midnight Office. And at the Apodosis of Pascha, at the Midnight Office we say: after the first Trisagion, instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," the Resurrection Troparion: "The Word co-unoriginate"; Glory: Both now: its Theotokion. After the second Trisagion, instead of "Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good," we say the Kontakion of Pascha: "Though Thou didst descend into the tomb"; thereafter, "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal; but the prayers "Remember, O Lord" we do not say. If it occur for the Temple of a Saint: instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," we say the Troparion of the Temple; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint; and after the second Trisagion, instead of "Remember, O Lord," we say the Kontakion of the Temple alone. Thereafter, "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal. If there be the Feast of the Nativity of John the Forerunner, or the Beheading, or of the Chief Apostles Peter and Paul: at the Midnight Office we say after the first Trisagion, instead of "Behold, the Bridegroom comes," the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint; after the second Trisagion, instead of "Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good," we say the Kontakion to the Saint; thereafter, "Lord, have mercy," 12 times, and the Dismissal; but the prayers "Remember, O Lord, in hope" we do not say.[^11167]

On Monday we sing Matins according to the custom, as indicated, only at "God is the Lord": the Troparion to the Saint twice; Glory: Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint, from the lesser Theotokia. But if there be no Troparion to the Saint in order, then we say the general Troparion to the Saint according to his hierarchic rank or monastic venerable dignity, twice; Glory: Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, then we say the Troparion of the feast twice; Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: of the feast.[^11168]

If there be no Troparion to the Saint, then we say the Troparion of the feast twice; Glory: Both now: the same Troparion of the feast. The Penitential Canon we sing with the Heirmos to 6, and of the Bodiless Powers to 4, and of the Saint from the Menaion to 4. But if the Saint is sung to 6, then both Martyria in the Octoechos are omitted, and we sing to the Saint to 6. But if a Polyeleos of the Saint is sung, then the Octoechos is not sung, but we sing the Canon to the Theotokos—or if there be a forefeast or afterfeast—with the Heirmos to 6 (the Heirmos twice, troparia to 4), and to the Saint to 8. Thus indeed throughout the whole week we sing to a Saint with a Polyeleos the Canon to 8. If two Saints occur, the Canon of the Bodiless Powers is omitted, and we sing the Penitential Canon with the Heirmos to 6, and to the Saints to 4 verses each. On Tuesday at Matins: the Penitential Canon with the Heirmos to 6, and to the Forerunner to 4, and of the Saint from the Menaion to 4. But if the Saint is sung to 6, both Martyria in the Octoechos are omitted, and we sing to the Saint to 6. The Polyeleos is according to what was prescribed above. The same happens if there are two Saints: their Canons to 4 verses each. On Wednesday at Matins: the Canon of the Cross with the Heirmos to 6, and of the Theotokos to 4, and of the Saint to 4. But if the Saint is sung to 6, both Martyria in the Octoechos are omitted, and the Canon of the Cross is sung with the Heirmos to 4, of the Theotokos to 4, and to the Saint to 6. If there are two Saints, the Canon of the Theotokos in the Octoechos is omitted, and we sing to the Saints to 8. On Thursday at Matins: we sing the Canon of the Holy Apostles with the Heirmos to 6, and of Saint Nicholas to 4, and of the Saint from the Menaion to 4. But if the Saint from the Menaion is sung to 6: we sing the Canon of the Apostles with the Heirmos and with the Theotokia to 5, and of Saint Nicholas with the Theotokion to 3, and of the Menaion to 6. If there are two Saints: we sing the Canon to the Apostles with the Heirmos to 6, and to the Saints to 8. On Friday we sing as was declared for Wednesday, without change.[^11169]

After Ode 3, Sessional Hymn to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Theotokion. If there be two Saints, and they have two Kontakia and two Sessional Hymns: we say first the Kontakion to the Saint (if there is one) and Ikos, then the Sessional Hymn to the same; Glory: Sessional Hymn to the other Saint; Both now: Theotokion; and to the other Saint we say the Kontakion and Ikos after Ode 6. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, we say instead of the Theotokion: Both now: of the feast. If it be to the Saint to 6, we say the Sessional Hymn to him twice; Glory: Both now: of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion and Ikos to the Saint, if there is one; but if not, then we say the general Kontakion to the Saint according to his hierarchic rank or monastic venerable dignity. If it be for a Saint with a Polyeleos or with a Vigil, then we say the Kontakion and Ikos to the Saint. But during a forefeast or afterfeast we say the Kontakion and Ikos in this manner: after Ode 3, Kontakion and Ikos of the feast, then Sessional Hymn to the Saint twice; Glory: Both now: Sessional Hymn of the feast; and the rest in order, as indicated in the Typikon.[^11170]

*Concerning the Photagogica [Exapostilaria],*  
*how they are said on weekdays apart from Sunday.*[^11171]  
Photagogicon in the Octoechos; Glory: Both now: its Theotokion. But if there be a Photagogicon of the Saint in the Menaion: say it at Glory, after the daily Pho-[^11172] -tagogicon; Both now: its Theotokion. But if there be two Saints in the Menaion, and two Photagogica: we say the 1st in the Octoechos, thereafter of the Saint; Glory: of the other Saint; Both now: its Theotokion. But if it be Wednesday or Friday: Both now: Stavrotheotokion in the Octoechos. On Saturday, if the Saint of the day has one: after Ode 9 we say the Photagogicon of the Saint before the Menaion, then Glory: in the Octoechos; Both now: its Theotokion. Thus are the Photagogica said when the Octoechos is sung with the Menaion.[^11173]

At the Praises [Lauds] to the Saint, if there be any, and as many as indicated in the Typikon; Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Saint; thereafter we say: "Glory to Thee Who hast shown us the light." But if there be none to the Saint at the Praises, then we say: "To Thee glory is due."[^11174]

At the Aposticha in the Octoechos, Glory: to the Saint, if there is one; Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Saint. If not, then we say Glory: Both now: in the Octoechos. If there be a verse with a refrain to the Saint at Matins, then we say at the Aposticha in the Octoechos, with the verses: "We were filled in the morning with Thy mercy, O Lord" [Ps. 89:14 LXX]; second: "And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us" [Ps. 89:17 LXX]. Then we say the refrain to the Saint which occurs, and the verse, and Glory: to him; Both now: Theotokion. And at Vespers at the Aposticha we say likewise. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, when the Octoechos is not sung: then at the Aposticha we say of the feast with the verses. If there be a verse with a refrain to the Saint, then after the festal verses, before the festal Glory, we say the verse to the Saint with the refrain; Glory: to the Saint; Both now: of the feast. And at Vespers at the Aposticha we say likewise. After "It is good to give praise to the Lord": after the Trisagion, Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, then we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: of the feast. If there be no Troparion to the Saint, then we say one Troparion of the feast. If there be no forefeast or afterfeast, we say the general Troparion to the Saint according to his hierarchic rank or monastic venerable dignity; Glory: Both now: Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion.[^11175]

At the First Hour, Glory: Troparion to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion to the Saint. If for two Saints there are two Troparia, then we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Troparion to the other Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion to the first Saint. At the Third Hour and at the others we say likewise, and we alternate the Kontakia. If a forefeast or afterfeast occurs, we say first the Troparion of the feast, then Glory: Troparion to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion of the feast invariably at all the Hours. If it be also to the Saint to 6, do thus every day. If there be no Troparion to the Saint, or to two Saints, nor a Kontakion, if there be no forefeast or afterfeast, then we say at the Hours the general Troparion and Kontakion to the Saint according to his hierarchic rank or monastic venerable dignity; after "Pardon, remit": after the Trisagion we say the Kontakion first to the Temple of Christ, then of the day, and to the Temple of the Saint, and-[^11176] -to the Saint of the day, if there is one; Glory: "With the Saints give rest"; Both now: Kontakion to the Temple of the Theotokos. If it be not the Temple of the Theotokos, then we say: Both now: "Protection of Christians." If it be a forefeast or afterfeast of Christ, we say first the Kontakion of the feast of Christ, and the rest in order; but the Kontakion of the Temple of Christ we do not say. Thus we say if the Liturgy is not celebrated.[^11177]

At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes in the Octoechos to 6. If there be an Ode to the Saint which the Typikon indicates, then we say in the Octoechos to 4, and the Ode to the Saint to 4. If there be two Saints, and the Typikon indicates an Ode to each, then the Octoechos is omitted. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, then also we say as the Typikon indicates. After the Entrance we say Troparia and Kontakia as the Typikon declares. And during a forefeast and afterfeast at the Liturgy the Troparion and Kontakion of the day are not said until the Apodosis, because the Octoechos is not sung. Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Verse of the day, and as indicated in the Typikon. In like manner we sing on the other days also, except Saturdays and Sundays.[^11178]

*Instruction on the Saturday Service,*  
*when we sing "God is the Lord":*[^11179]  

On Friday evening at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera in the Menaion to the Saint to 6; Glory: to the Saint, if he has one; if not, Glory: Both now: 1st Theotokion according to the occurring Tone. At the Aposticha in the Octoechos, three Martyria which are written at "Lord, I have cried," omitting the 1st; and refrain: "Unto Thee have I lifted up mine eyes" [Ps. 122:1 LXX]; and second: "Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us" [Ps. 122:3 LXX]; Glory: to the Saint, if he has one; if not, Glory: Both now: Theotokion in the Octoechos. After "Our Father": Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion of the occurring Tone; litany, and dismissal. At Compline on Friday after "It is truly meet," after the Trisagion: Troparion to the Temple of Christ and to the Temple of the Theotokos; and to the Temple of a male Saint or female Saint on Friday the Troparion is not said. Then: "Apostles, Martyrs"; Glory: "With the Saints give rest"; Both now: "As first-fruits." If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, we say the Kontakion of the feast. If it be for a Saint with Doxology, we say the Kontakion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: of the feast. Midnight Office we sing according to the custom; only if there be a Master’s [Dominical] Feast, or if the Theotokos is sung with Apodosis, or the Nativity of the Forerunner, or the Beheading, or Peter and Paul, or the Temple of the Saint, and on the Synaxis of the Theotokos, and on the Synaxis of John the Forerunner, we say after the first Trisagion the Troparion, and after the second Trisagion the Kontakion, as was indicated on Monday. At Matins, at "God is the Lord": Troparion to the Saint twice; Glory: Both now: Resurrection Theotokion according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint, and not of the occurring Tone, because the Resurrection Theotokion of the Tone is said after the 1st Sessional Hymn at: Both now. But if the Saint has a Polyeleos, then we say at "God is the Lord": after the Troparion of the Saint, the Resurrection Theotokion of the Tone. If on Saturday there be a forefeast or afterfeast, at "God is the Lord": we say the Troparion of the feast twice; Glory: Troparion to the Saint,-[^11180] -Both now: of the feast. If there be no Troparion to the Saint, then we say the Troparion of the feast twice; Glory: Both now: the same. Then we say Kathisma 16. After the Kathismata, litany, and Sessional Hymns in the Octoechos, two Martyria, the 1st and the 2nd; Glory: Both now: their Resurrection Theotokion, and reading; thereafter the Evlogetaria [Amomos / Psalm 118]. After the Evlogetaria, litany, and Sessional Hymns in the Octoechos, Martyria, the 3rd and the 4th, [and] the Sessional Hymn of Repose [Pokoin]; Glory: Both now: Theotokion, and reading; Psalm 50. And we sing the Canons; on Saturday the Saint of the Menaion always takes precedence. We sing the Canon of the Saint of the Menaion with the Heirmos to 6, then to the Saint of whom the Temple is [dedicated] to 4, and in the Octoechos the Martyr Canon to 4. But if there are two Saints in the Menaion, we sing to the one the Canon with the Heirmos to 6, and to the other to 4, and the Martyr Canon of the Octoechos to 4; but the Canon of the Temple is omitted. But if it be the Temple of Christ or of the Theotokos, we sing first the Canon of the Temple with the Heirmos to 6 (the Heirmos twice, troparia to 4), and of the Saint to 4, and in the Octoechos the Martyr Canon to 4. But if the Saint is celebrated to 6, we sing of the Temple with the Heirmos to 4, and of the Saint to 6, and in the Octoechos to 4.[^11181]

On whatever Saturday a Doxology occurs: at Matins, the Canon of the Temple—if it is of Christ or of the Theotokos—with the Heirmos to 6 (the Heirmos twice, troparia to 4), and of the Saint to 8. After Ode 3, Sessional Hymn to the daily Saint; Glory: Both now: its Theotokion. After Ode 6, Kontakion to the Saint, and Ikos. But if there is no Troparion and Kontakion to the Saint, then we say the general Troparion to the Saint according to his hierarchic rank or monastic venerable dignity, in the general troparia and kontakia. After Ode 9, Photagogicon [Exapostilarion] first to the Saint; then Glory: in the Octoechos; Both now: its Theotokion. If not: Photagogicon in the Octoechos: "Thou Who [rulest] over the dead"; Glory: Both now: "In thee we make our boast." If there be a Doxology to the Saint on Saturday, we sing the Photagogicon to the Saint twice; Glory: Both now: its Theotokion. If there are no Stichera to the Saint at the Praises [Lauds], we say: "To Thee glory is due," and "Glory to Thee Who hast shown us the light." And in the Octoechos we do not sing Stichera at the Praises, but we sing them at the Aposticha. But if we sing Stichera to the Saint at the Praises, we do not say that verse: "To Thee glory is due," but after the Stichera we say: "Glory to Thee Who hast shown us the light." Thereafter: "Let us complete our morning prayer"; and at the Aposticha, in the Octoechos, the three Martyria which are written at the Praises. And we say the customary refrains: the 1st: "We were filled in the morning with Thy mercy, O Lord" [Ps. 89:14 LXX]; the 2nd: "And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us" [Ps. 89:17 LXX]; and the 4th, of repose [pokoin], we omit. Glory: to the Saint, if he has one; but if not, Glory: Both now: their Theotokion. But the Aposticha in the Octoechos we omit when we sing on Saturday: "God is the Lord." After "It is good to give praise to the Lord" and after the Trisagion: Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: Morning Theotokion, according to the Tone of the Troparion of the Saint. If there be a forefeast or afterfeast, we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Both now: of the feast. If there be two Saints in a forefeast or afterfeast, and they have two Troparia, then we say the Troparion to the Saint; Glory: Troparion to the other Saint; Both now: of the feast. If there be no Troparion to the Saint, then we say one Troparion of the feast. Then the litany, and the First Hour. At the Third Hour we say,-[^11182] -Glory: Troparion to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion to the Saint; if there be a forefeast or afterfeast, we say the Troparion of the feast; Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion of the feast. If there are two Saints, and they have two Troparia, we say the Troparion of the feast; Glory: to the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion of the feast. At the Sixth Hour, Troparion of the feast; Glory: Troparion to the other Saint, and the Kontakion of the feast at all the Hours without change, except for a great Saint with a Polyeleos; for then we alternate the Kontakion. If there be no Troparion to the Saint, then we say the Troparion and Kontakion of the feast, and thereafter the Dismissal. Then the customary Litiya. At the Liturgy (the Beatitudes in the Octoechos to 6. But if the Saint has an Ode, we say first to the Saint Ode 3, with the Heirmos to 4, and in the Octoechos to 4.) Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, and Gospel, and Communion Verses, first to the Saint, and then of the day. If to the Saint there is no Epistle, then Prokeimenon, and Epistle, Alleluia, and Gospel, and Communion Verse of the day, and for repose. But from the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee until the Sunday of All Saints, Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Verse first of the Saturday, and then to the Saint. If to the Saint there is no Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Verse, then for repose.[^11183]

*Instruction concerning the Temple of Christ’s Resurrection.*[^11184]  
Where there is a Temple of Christ’s Resurrection, we sing on Saturday at Matins the Resurrection Canon, the Heirmos twice, the troparia to 4, and we sing thus: two Resurrection troparia, and one Cross-Resurrection, and one Theotokion, and to the Saint in the Menaion to 4, and in the Octoechos to 4. But if the Canon to the Saint is to 6, then we sing the Resurrection Canon with the Heirmos to 4, the Heirmos once, and to the Saint in the Menaion to 6, and in the Octoechos to 4. Another Typikon: the Resurrection Canon with the Heirmos to three, without the Theotokion, the Heirmos once, and two Cross-Resurrection, and one Theotokion, and in the Menaion to 4, and in the Octoechos to 4.  
If a forefeast or afterfeast of Master’s [Dominical] feasts or of the Theotokos occurs on Saturday, and on the other days of the week, except Sunday, likewise on the Deposition of Her Robe in Blachernae, and on the Deposition of the Precious Sash, the Octoechos is not sung, except for the Dogmatikon at "Lord, I have cried" on Friday evening; but the entire service is sung of the forefeast or afterfeast together with the Saint, as the Typikon declares.[^11185]

*When there is Alleluia on Saturday.*[^11186]  
On Friday at Vespers, after the customary psalmody, at "Lord, I have cried": Stichera in the Menaion to the Saint 3, and in the Octoechos, Martyria 2, 3, 4, but the first is omitted. Glory: Both now: Theotokion of the Tone. "O Gladsome Light," and the rest. At the Aposticha, Martyr Sticheron 1, and of repose 2. Glory: Both now: Theotokion of the Tone. Then, "Now lettest Thou depart"; after the Trisagion, Troparion, Tone 2: "Apostles, Prophets, and Martyrs"; Glory: "Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good"; Both now: Theotokion: "O holy Mother"; litany and dismissal.  
On Saturday at Matins, as usual, after the Six Psalms, litany; then instead of:-[^11187] -"God is the Lord": we sing Alleluia in Tone 2, thrice. Verse 1: "From night-time my spirit watches early for Thee, O God" [Isa. 26:9 LXX]; and the other verses. Then the Troparion, Tone 2: "Apostles, Prophets, and Martyrs," twice; Glory: "Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good"; Both now: Theotokion: "O holy Mother." After Kathisma 1: "The Lord said unto my Lord" [Ps. 109:1 LXX]: Sessional Hymn in the Octoechos, and reading. And the Evlogetaria [Amomos / Psalm 118] we sing, dividing the Kathisma into two stases with refrains.[^11188]

After the 1st stasis we do not say: "Glory: Both now:"; but we sing at the end: "Unless Thy law had been my meditation" [Ps. 118:92 LXX]; and the remaining 3 [verses], and the Deacon (or Priest) makes this Litany:  
**Deacon:** Again and again in peace, let us pray to the Lord.  
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.  
**Deacon:** Again we pray for the blessed repose of the servants of God who from the beginning of the world have fallen asleep: the most holy ecumenical Hierarchs, Right Reverend Metropolitans, God-loving Archbishops and Bishops, venerable Archimandrites and Igumens, and the entire Priestly and Monastic Order, the right-believing Emperors and Empresses, Czars and Czarinas, Princes and Princesses, the blessed and ever-memorable founders and benefactors of this holy Temple (of this holy Monastery), parents and kinsfolk (of our fathers and brethren) lying here, inscribed here, and Orthodox Christians everywhere, of whom we make remembrance; (This is said when universal commemoration is made for the departed.) and that every transgression of theirs, voluntary and involuntary, may be pardoned them.  
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy (40 times).  
**Deacon:** That the Lord our God may establish their souls in a place of light, in a place of green pasture, in a place of refreshment, in the bosom of Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, where all the Saints and Righteous take their rest.  
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.  
**Deacon:** The mercies of God, the heavenly kingdom, and the remission of their sins, let us entreat of Christ, the Immortal King and our God.  
**Choir:** Grant, O Lord.  
**Deacon:** Let us pray to the Lord.  
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.  
*The Priest reads the Prayer secretly:* "O God of spirits and of all flesh," *unto the end; thereafter the Exclamation:*  
**Priest:** For Thou art the resurrection, the life, and the repose of Thy servants who from the beginning of the world have fallen asleep: the most holy ecumenical Hierarchs, Right Reverend Metropolitans, God-loving Archbishops and Bishops, venerable Archimandrites and Igumens, and all those of the Priestly and Monastic Order, right-believing Emperors and Empresses, Czars and Czarinas, Princes and Princesses, the blessed and ever-memorable founders of this holy Temple (of this holy Monastery, of our fathers and brethren), parents and kinsfolk lying here, inscribed here, and Orthodox Christians everywhere, O Christ our God; and unto Thee do we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father, and with Thy most holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages.  
**Choir:** Amen.[^11189]

*Then we sing the second stasis:* "I am Thine, save me" [Ps. 118:94 LXX]; *with the refrain:* "Give rest, O Lord, to the souls of Thy servants." *And some chant the refrain:* "O Savior, save the souls of Thy servants." *And at the end we say:* "My soul shall live, and shall praise Thee" [Ps. 118:175 LXX]. *Then:* "Blessed art Thou, O Lord" [Ps. 118:12 LXX]; *and we sing the Funeral Troparia:* "The choir of the Saints hath found the fountain," *and the rest; and after them the aforesaid diaconal petitions of repose,-*[^11190] -repose; and the Priest reads the Diptychs [Pomyannyk] and the Prayer secretly: "O God of spirits and of all flesh"; and the Choir sings: "Lord, have mercy" (thrice). After the Exclamation, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5: "Give rest, O our Savior"; Glory: Both now: Theotokion: "Thou Who didst shine forth from the Virgin"; then Psalm 50: "Have mercy upon me, O God." The Canon first in the Menaion with the Heirmos to 6, and to the Saint of whom the Temple is [dedicated] to 4, and in the Octoechos to 4, Canon 1. But where there is a Temple of the Lord or of the Theotokos: the Canon first of the Temple with the Heirmos to 6, then in the Menaion and in the Octoechos. After Ode 3, Sessional Hymn in the Menaion to the Saint; after Ode 6, Kontakion, Tone 8: "With the Saints give rest," and the Ikos. After Ode 9, Photagogicon [Exapostilarion] of the day. At the Praises [Lauds], Martyr Stichera 4, and of repose; Glory: Both now: Theotokion. At the Aposticha, prosomoia Stichera of repose; Glory: Both now: Theotokion. After "It is good to give praise to the Lord": Trisagion; the above-mentioned Troparia; litany, and dismissal. First Hour, and procession into the Narthex, and the complete dismissal. At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes in the Octoechos to 6. But if on Saturday a forefeast or afterfeast occurs with the Octoechos, that of repose is not sung until the Apodosis of the feast.[^11191]

*Instruction, how it is proper to sing the Parastas for deceased orthodox Christians.*[^11192]

On Friday evening, after the Dismissal of Vespers, the Priest and the Deacon, having entered into the Altar, vest: the Priest in epitrachelion and phelonion, and the Deacon in sticharion and orarion. And having come forth and taken their stand according to custom before the Tetrapod,[^11193] upon which is a dish with kolyvo or kutia, that is, with boiled wheat mixed with honey: the Priest, having taken the censer with incense, censes over the kutia, having said:  
**Priest:** Blessed is our God...  
*And we say the Trisagion; and after "Our Father," and after the Exclamation:*  
**Reader:** Lord, have mercy (12 times); Glory: Both now: Come, let us worship (thrice); Psalm 90: "He that dwells in the aid of the Most High" [Ps. 90 LXX]; and at the end of it: Glory: Both now: Alleluia (thrice).  
*And there is the Litany of Repose:*  
**Deacon:** In peace let us pray to the Lord.  
For the peace from above, and the salvation of our souls, let us pray to the Lord.  
For the peace of the whole world, the good estate of the holy churches of God, and the union of all, let us pray to the Lord.  
For this holy Temple, and for those that with faith, reverence, and the fear of God enter therein, let us pray to the Lord.  
For the remission of the sins of the servants of God who from the beginning of the world have fallen asleep in blessed memory, the most holy ecumenical [Hierarchs]: (and the rest, as in the litany written above on Saturday at Matins, if the general Parastas is sung, as on the designated Memorial Saturdays). But if at any other time, then to say thus:  
For the remission of the sins of the fallen-asleep servants of God, the blessed and ever-memorable founders of this holy Temple (or of this holy Monastery), parents and kinsfolk (and monks say: Of our fathers and brethren) lying here and inscribed here, and Orthodox Christians everywhere, and those now remembered *(Names)*, and for their blessed repose, let us pray to the Lord.  
That every transgression of theirs, voluntary and involuntary, may be pardoned them, let us pray to the Lord.  
That He may establish their souls in the land of the li-[^11194] -ving, in a place of light, where all the Saints and Righteous take their rest, let us pray to the Lord.
**Deacon:** That they may stand uncondemned before the dread judgment seat of Christ, let us pray to the Lord.  
That they may inherit the immortal kingdom that is in the heavens, let us pray to the Lord.  
That they may be partakers of the unceasing gladness of the Saints from the beginning of the world, let us pray to the Lord.  
For those who weep and are in pain, and that they may have consolation in Christ, let us pray to the Lord.  
That we may be delivered...  
Help us, save us...  
Calling to remembrance our most holy, pure, most blessed, glorious Lady, Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary, with all the Saints, let us commit ourselves and one another, and our whole life unto Christ our God.  
**Choir:** To Thee, O Lord.  
**Priest:** For Thou art the resurrection, and the life, and the repose of Thy fallen-asleep servants, the blessed and ever-memorable founders of this holy Temple (or: of this holy Monastery, of our fathers and brethren), our parents and kinsfolk, and those lying here, inscribed here, and Orthodox Christians everywhere, and those now remembered *(Names)*, O Christ our God; and unto Thee do we send up glory... and the rest.  
*Then:* Alleluia (thrice), *in Tone 8. And we say these verses:*  
*Verse 1:* Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord [Ps. 64:5 LXX].  
*Verse 2:* And their remembrance is unto generation and generation [Ps. 101:13 LXX].  
*Verse 3:* Their souls shall dwell in prosperity [Ps. 24:13 LXX].  
*Troparion, Tone 8:* "Who by the depth of wisdom," *twice; Glory: Both now: Theotokion:* "In thee we have a wall and a haven."[^11195]

*After this, the Kathisma:* "Blessed are the blameless" [Ps. 118:1 LXX]: *we sing these in Tone 5 with refrains:* "Blessed art Thou, O Lord" [Ps. 118:12 LXX]; *and some sing:* "Remember, O Lord, the souls of Thy servants"; *the first Choir beginning the 1st verse, the second Choir the 2nd verse, and the rest of the verses in order; and we divide them into two stases. And after the first stasis we do not say: "Glory: Both now:"; but immediately this verse:* "Unless Thy law had been my meditation" [Ps. 118:92 LXX], *thrice.*  
*Then the Deacon, or the Priest himself, the Litany:*  
**Deacon:** Again and again in peace, let us pray to the Lord.  
Again we pray for the repose of the fallen-asleep, ever-memorable servants of God *(Names)*. (If it be a universal commemoration, the Litany is as was prescribed above on Saturday at Matins. But if a non-universal Pannikhida is sung, we say thus:) Again we pray for the repose of the fallen-asleep servants of God, the blessed and ever-memorable founders of this holy Temple (of this holy Monastery, of our fathers and brethren), our parents and kinsfolk, lying here, inscribed here, and Orthodox Christians everywhere, and the servants of God *(Names)*, of whom we make remembrance, and that every transgression of theirs, voluntary and involuntary, may be pardoned them.  
"That the Lord our God...": "The mercies of God, the kingdom...": seek there. And the Clerics: "Lord, have mercy," until the Priest recites the Prayer secretly: "O God of spirits and of all flesh"; Exclamation: "For Thou art the resurrection, and the life, and the repose," and the rest unto the end.  
*Then the second Choir begins:* "I am Thine, save me" [Ps. 118:94 LXX]. *Refrain:* "Give rest, O Lord, to the souls of Thy servants." *And some chant:* "O Savior, save the souls of Thy servants"; *and the other verses in order. And at the end we sing the verse:* "My soul shall live, and shall praise Thee" [Ps. 118:175 LXX], *thrice, unto the end.*  
*Then the Funeral Troparia, in Tone 5:*[^11196]

*Refrain:* Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes [Ps. 118:12 LXX].  
*Troparion:* The choir of the Saints hath found the fountain of life and the portal of Paradise. May I also find the way through repentance: I am the lost sheep; recall me, O Savior, and save me.[^11197]

*Refrain:* Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes.  
*Troparion:* Thou Who of old didst fashion me out of nothingness with Thy palm, and didst honor me with Thine image divine, but by transgression of the commandment didst return me again to the earth whence I was taken: lead me back unto that which is according to Thy likeness, to be refashioned in the ancient beauty.[^11198]

*Refrain:* Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes.  
*Troparion:* I am the image of Thine ineffable glory, though I also bear the wounds of transgressions. Have compassion upon Thine own creature, O Master, and cleanse me through Thy tender mercy, and grant unto me the longed-for homeland, making me once again a citizen of Paradise.[^11199]

*Refrain:* Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes.  
*Troparion:* Ye who walked in the narrow and sorrowful way, all who in life took up the cross as a yoke, and followed Me by faith: come, take your delight in the honors and the heavenly crowns which I have prepared for you.[^11200]

*Refrain:* Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes.  
*Troparion:* Ye who preached the Lamb of God and were slain even as lambs, and have passed over unto the unending and ever-existing life, O holy Martyrs: fervently entreat Him to grant us remission of our debts.[^11201]

*Refrain:* Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes.  
*Troparion:* Give rest, O God, to Thy servants, and establish them in Paradise, where the choirs of the Saints, O Lord, and the Righteous shine as stars; give rest to Thy fallen-asleep servants, overlooking all their transgressions.[^11202]

*Glory:*  
*Triadikon:* The tri-radiant radiance of the one Godhead devoutly do we hymn, crying aloud: Holy art Thou, O unoriginate Father, co-unoriginate Son, and divine Spirit; enlighten us who by faith worship Thee, and snatch us from the eternal fire.[^11203]

*Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* Rejoice, O Pure One, who in the flesh didst bear God for the salvation of all,-[^11204] -through whom the human race hath found salvation; through thee may we find Paradise, O pure, blessed Theotokos.
Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia: Glory to Thee, O God (thrice).  
*Thereafter the above-said Litany for the departed:* "Again and again..." *And the Priest commemorates the departed in order. And the Clerics:* "Lord, have mercy." *And after the Exclamation, we sing the Troparia:*  
*Tone 5:*  
*Troparion:* Give rest, O our Savior, unto Thy servants with the Righteous, and establish them in Thy courts, as it is written, overlooking as Thou art good their transgressions voluntary and involuntary, and all those done in knowledge and in ignorance, O Lover of mankind.[^11205]  
*Glory: Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* O Christ God, Who didst shine forth to the world from the Virgin, and through her hast revealed us as sons of light, have mercy upon us.[^11206]  
*Then Psalm 50:* "Have mercy upon me, O God" [Ps. 50 LXX]. *Thereafter the Canon in the Octoechos for the departed, directly according to the Tone, to 4. After Ode 3, the diaconal petitions for the departed, as was said before, and the commemoration by the Priest; and after the Exclamation we sing:*  
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6:*  
Truly all things are vanity, and life is but a shadow and a dream; for in vain is every earth-born man disquieted, even as the Scripture hath said: when we have gained the world, then do we take up our dwelling in the grave, where kings and beggars are together. Therefore, O Christ God, give rest to those who have passed away, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.[^11207]  
*Glory: Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* O all-holy Theotokos, in the time of my life forsake me not; commit me not unto human protection, but do Thou Thyself help and have mercy upon me.[^11208]  
*After Ode 6, the above-mentioned diaconal petitions for the departed, and the Commemoration by the Priest. After the Exclamation:*  
*Kontakion, Tone 8:*  
With the Saints give rest, O Christ, to the souls of Thy servants, where there is neither sickness, nor sorrow, nor sighing, but life unending.[^11209]  
*Ikos:*  
Thou alone art immortal, Who didst fashion and make man; for we earthly beings were fashioned of the earth, and unto the same earth shall we go, as Thou didst command, Who didst create me and say unto me: "Earth thou art, and unto earth shalt thou return" [Gen. 3:19 LXX]; whither all we mortals go, making our funeral wailing the song: Alleluia (thrice).[^11210]

*Then after Ode 9, instead of "It is truly meet": we sing the Heirmos of Ode 9. After this, the Trisagion; after "Our Father": we sing these troparia:*  
*Tone 4:*  
*Troparion:* With the spirits of the Righteous made perfect, give rest to the souls of Thy servants, O Savior, preserving them in the blessed life which is with Thee, O Lover of mankind.[^11211]  
*Troparion:* In Thy resting place, O Lord, where all Thy Saints take their rest, give rest also to the souls of Thy servants, for Thou alone art the Lover of mankind.[^11212]  
*Glory:*  
*Troparion:* Thou art the God Who descended into Hades and loosed the bonds of the fettered: do Thou Thyself give rest also to the souls of Thy servants.[^11213]  
*Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* O Virgin, alone pure and immaculate, who didst bear God without seed: intercede for the salvation of their souls.[^11214]

*Then the Litany:*  
**Deacon:** Have mercy upon us, O God, according to Thy great mercy...  
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy (thrice).  
**Deacon:** Again we pray for the repose of the blessed memory... And the rest in order, as was prescribed above.  
*And the Priest the Prayer secretly (and at the conclusion of the Parastas aloud):*  
**Priest:** O God of spirits and of all flesh, Who hast trampled down death, and overthrown the devil, and given life unto Thy world: do Thou Thyself, O Lord, give rest to the souls of Thy fallen-asleep servants, in a place of light, in a place of green pasture, in a place of refreshment, whence pain, sorrow, and sighing have fled away; and every sin committed by them in word, or deed, or thought, as a good God and Lover of mankind do Thou forgive: for there is no man who shall live and not sin; for Thou alone art without sin, Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and Thy word is truth.  
*Exclamation:* For Thou art the resurrection and the life...  
*Then the Priest:* Wisdom!  
**Choir:** More honorable than the Cherubim...  
**Priest:** Glory to Thee, O Christ God, our hope, glory to Thee.  
**Choir:** Glory: Both now: Lord, have mercy (thrice). Give the blessing.  
*The Priest, the Dismissal:*  
**Priest:** May He Who rose from the dead,  
Christ our true God, Who rules over the living and the dead, through the intercessions of His all-pure Mother, of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles, of our venerable and God-bearing-[^11215] -Fathers, and of all the Saints, establish the souls of His servants who have passed away from us in the tents of the Righteous, give them rest in the bosom of Abraham, number them among the Righteous, and have mercy upon us and save us, as He is good and the Lover of mankind.
**Choir:** Amen.  
*Then the Deacon or the Priest says:*  
**Deacon:** In blessed falling-asleep, eternal rest grant, O Lord, to Thy fallen-asleep servants *(Names)*, and make their memory to be eternal.  
**Choir:** Memory eternal (thrice).[^11216]
