# Lviv Stauropegion Complete Octoechos (Lviv, 1895) <a id="lviv-stauropegion-complete-octoechos-lviv-1895"></a>

*[Parish Inscription: Property of the Church of Saint Michael in Pidlyptsi]*[^1]

**OCTOECHOS,**[^2]
or in Greek,
**OKTOICHOS**
or
**PARAKLETIKE.**
The Work of our Venerable Father John Damascene and of other God-fearing Fathers.

*[Official Seal of the Stauropegion Institute: A medallion enclosing the Patriarchal Double Cross atop Golgotha, with the circular inscription: THE STAUROPEGION INSTITUTE]*[^3]
*[Parish Stamp: An oval stamp inscribed: PAROCHIAL OFFICE IN PIDLYPTSI, bearing the Ruthenian Lion]*[^4]

Published
**IN LVIV,**
in the year from the creation of the world 7393,[^5] and from the Nativity of Christ 1895.
Printed by the press and at the expense of the Stauropegion Institute.
Director of the Printing House: John Pekhur.

*[Manuscript Acquisition Note: January 22, 1926. Purchased for 30 zlotys from the treasury under Fr. I. Kedrynsky, pastor.]*[^6]

Under the governance of the holy Church of Christ by the most holy universal Hierarch, Pope of Rome,[^7]
**LEO XIII.**

Under the reign of the right-believing and God-preserved Emperor of Austria,[^8]
**FRANZ JOSEPH I.**

By the blessing and direction of His Excellency, the Most Reverend Lord
**SYLVESTER SEMBRATOVYCH,**[^9]
Metropolitan of Galicia, Archbishop of Lviv, Bishop of Kamianets, Assistant at the Pontifical Throne and Domestic Prelate to His Holiness the Pope, Count of Rome, Actual Privy Councillor to His Imperial and Royal Apostolic Majesty the Emperor, Knight of the Order of the Iron Crown First Class, Member of the House of Lords of the Imperial Council, Vice-Marshal of the Diet of the Kingdoms of Galicia and Lodomeria with the Grand Duchy of Cracow, Doctor of Theology, etc.

Under the God-loving and Most Reverend Lord
*Kyr*[^10]
**JULIAN PELESZ,**[^11]
Bishop of Peremyshl, Sambir, and Sanok, Member of the Diet of the Kingdoms of Galicia and Lodomeria with the Grand Duchy of Cracow, Doctor of Theology, etc.

And the God-loving and Most Reverend Lord
*Kyr*
**JULIAN KUYLOVSKY,**[^12]
Bishop of Stanyslaviv, Chamberlain to His Holiness the Pope, Member of the Diet of the Kingdoms of Galicia and Lodomeria with the Grand Duchy of Cracow, etc.

Under the Senior of the Stauropegion Institute,
**ISIDORE SHARANEVYCH,**[^13]
Doctor of Philosophy, Imperial-Royal Professor of the University of Lviv, etc.

And under the Prefect of the Printing House of the Stauropegion Institute,
**NICHOLAS SYVULIAK,**
Imperial-Royal Professor of the Second Lviv Gymnasium.

Labouring over this edition:
The Priest **ONUPHRY LEPKY,**[^14]
Gymnasium Professor of Religion, Councillor of the Metropolitan Consistory.

### Notes

[^1]: Parish provenance manuscript inscription in Western Ukrainian Cyrillic cursive on the upper margin of the Master Title Page: "Власність Церкви св. Михаїла въ Підлипцях" (Property of the Church of St. Michael in Pidlyptsi, Zolochiv district, Galicia).
[^2]: Slavonic Осмогласникъ (Greek Ὀκτώηχος / Παρακλητική). The Lviv Stauropegion edition of 1895 contains the complete Octoechos across all eight Tones for both Sunday and weekday cycles, followed by the eleven Resurrectional Exapostilaria and Evangelical Stichera, and the General Parastas.
[^3]: The official woodcut seal of the Stauropegion Institute in Lviv (Ставропигійскій Інстітутъ), founded in 1586 by the Dormition Brotherhood. The seal displays the Patriarchal double cross atop Golgotha within a rococo cartouche.
[^4]: Violet ink oval parish stamp: "ПАРОХІЯЛЬНИЙ УРЯД В ПІДЛИПЦЯХ" (Parochial Office in Pidlyptsi), featuring the Ruthenian lion rampant (heraldic emblem of the Principality of Halych-Volhynia and the Crownland of Galicia).
[^5]: The Byzantine Anno Mundi era date 7393 corresponds to AD 1895 (7393 - 5508 = 1885/1895 depending on the March/September calendar start; here specifically synchronised with the Christian era 1895).
[^6]: Parish acquisition manuscript inscription in black ink on the lower margin: "22/I 1926 Куплена за 30 золотих зі скарбонки за о. І. Кедринського пароха" (January 22, 1926. Purchased for 30 zlotys from the church treasury under Rev. I. Kedrynsky, pastor).
[^7]: Pope Leo XIII (Vincenzo Gioacchino Raffaele Luigi Pecci), reigned 1878–1903.
[^8]: Emperor Franz Joseph I of Austria, King of Bohemia and Apostolic King of Hungary, reigned 1848–1916.
[^9]: Sylvester Cardinal Sembratovych (1836–1898), Greek Catholic Metropolitan of Galicia and Archbishop of Lviv (1885–1898), created Cardinal by Pope Leo XIII in 1895.
[^10]: Kyr (Gk. Κύριος, Slav. Кѵ́ръ), traditional Byzantine ecclesiastical honorific prefixed to the Christian name of a bishop.
[^11]: Julian Pelesz (1843–1896), celebrated canonist, pastoral theologian, and historian of the Greek Catholic Church; Bishop of Stanyslaviv (1885–1891) and Bishop of Peremyshl, Sambir, and Sanok (1891–1896).
[^12]: Julian Sas-Kuilovsky (1826–1900), Bishop of Stanyslaviv (1891–1899) and subsequently Metropolitan of Galicia (1899–1900).
[^13]: Isidore Sharanevych (1829–1901), distinguished historian, archaeologist, Senior of the Stauropegion Institute, and professor at the University of Lviv.
[^14]: Rev. Onuphry Lepky (1841–1905), Basilian-educated secular priest, gymnasium professor of religious doctrine in Lviv, consultor to the Metropolitan Consistory, and prolific editor of liturgical texts for the Stauropegion Press.
