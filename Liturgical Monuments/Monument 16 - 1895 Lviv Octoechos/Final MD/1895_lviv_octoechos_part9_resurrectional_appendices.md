# PART 09 — RESURRECTIONAL EXAPOSTILARIA AND ELEVEN EVANGELICAL STICHERA (EOTHINA)

## EXAPOSTILARIA AND EVANGELICAL STICHERA

*The Resurrectional Morning Gospels, with the Resurrectional Exapostilaria and Evangelical Stichera.*[^11002]  
*(The Morning Stichera are the work of Emperor Leo the Wise; and the Exapostilaria of his son, Emperor Constantine.)*[^11003]

*Resurrectional Gospel 1. From Matthew, Pericope 116 [Matt. 28:16-20]:*[^11004]  
At that time, the eleven disciples went into Galilee, into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them. And when they saw Him, they worshipped Him; but some doubted. And Jesus came and spoke unto them, saying: All power is given unto Me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even unto the end of the age. Amen.[^11005]

*Exapostilarion 1:*  
With the disciples let us ascend the mountain of Galilee, in faith to behold Christ, saying that He has received authority over heavenly and earthly things; let us learn how He teaches to baptize all nations in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, and to abide with the initiates of His mysteries, as He promised, even unto the end of the age.[^11006]

*Theotokion:*  
With the disciples thou didst rejoice, O Theotokos Virgin, for thou didst behold Christ risen from the tomb on the third day as He said; unto whom He also appeared, teaching and revealing better things, and commanding to baptize in the Father, and the Son, and the Spirit, so that we may believe in His rising, and glorify thee, O Maiden.[^11007]

*Evangelical Sticheron 1, Tone 1:*  
When the disciples went up into the mountain for the earthly ascension, the Lord stood before them; and having worshipped Him, and learned of the authority given Him everywhere, they were sent into all under heaven to preach the resurrection from the dead, and the ascension into heaven; with whom also the truthful Christ God, the Savior of our souls, promised to abide unto the ages.[^11008]

*Resurrectional Gospel 2. From Mark, Pericope 70 [Mark 16:1-8]:*[^11009]  
At that time, when the Sabbath was past, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome, had bought sweet spices, that they might come and anoint Jesus. And very early in the morning, on...[^11010]

...the first day of the week, they came unto the tomb at the rising of the sun. And they said among themselves: Who shall roll us away the stone from the door of the tomb? And when they looked, they saw that the stone was rolled away: for it was very great. And entering into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, clothed in a long white garment; and they were affrighted. And he says unto them: Be not affrighted: Ye seek Jesus of Nazareth, Who was crucified: He is risen; He is not here: behold the place where they laid Him. But go your way, tell His disciples and Peter that He goes before you into Galilee: there shall ye see Him, as He said unto you. And they went out quickly, and fled from the tomb; for they trembled and were amazed: neither said they any thing to any man; for they were afraid.[^11011]

*Exapostilarion 2:*  
Seeing the stone rolled away, the myrrh-bearing women rejoiced; for they saw a young man sitting in the tomb, and he said unto them: Behold, Christ is risen; say unto the disciples with Peter: hasten unto the mountain of Galilee, there shall He appear unto you, as He foretold unto His friends.[^11012]

*Theotokion:*  
An angel indeed brought unto the Virgin the "Rejoice" before Thy conception, O Christ; and an angel rolled away the stone of Thy tomb: the former instead of sorrow bringing tokens of ineffable joy, and the latter instead of death proclaiming and magnifying the Giver of life, and speaking of the Resurrection unto the women and the initiates of the mysteries.[^11013]

*Evangelical Sticheron 2, Tone 2:*  
Unto the women who came with Mary bringing myrrh, and were at a loss how they might obtain their desire, the stone appeared taken away, and a divine young man assuaged the turmoil of their souls: for Jesus the Lord is risen, says he; therefore preach ye unto His preachers, the disciples, to hasten unto Galilee, and to behold Him risen from the dead, as Giver of life and Lord.[^11014]

*Resurrectional Gospel 3. From Mark, Pericope 71 [Mark 16:9-20]:*[^11015]  
At that time, when Jesus was risen early the first day of the week,[^11016] He appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom He had cast seven devils. And she went and told them that had been with Him, as they mourned and wept. And they, when they had heard that He was alive, and had been seen of her, believed not. After that He appeared in another form unto two of them, as they walked, and went into the country. And they went and told it unto the residue: neither believed they them. Afterward He appeared unto the eleven as they sat at meat, and upbraided them with their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they believed not them which had seen Him...[^11017]

...after He was risen. And He said unto them: Go ye into all the world, and preach the Gospel to every creature. He that believes and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believes not shall be condemned. And these signs shall follow them that believe: In My name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues; they shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover. So then after the Lord had spoken unto them, He was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God. And they went forth, and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word with signs following. Amen.[^11018]

*Exapostilarion 3:*  
Let no one disbelieve that Christ is risen; for He appeared unto Mary, and afterward was seen of two walking into the country; and again He appeared unto the eleven initiates of His mysteries as they sat at meat, whom having sent forth to baptize, He was received up into heaven, whence also He had descended, confirming the preaching by multitudes of signs.[^11019]

*Theotokion:*  
The Sun that has shone forth this day from the tomb as a bridegroom from his chamber, having despoiled hades and abolished death: by the prayers of her that bare Thee, send down light upon us, light enlightening hearts and souls, light guiding all to walk in the paths of Thy commandments and in the way of peace.[^11020]

*Evangelical Sticheron 3, Tone 3:*  
When Mary Magdalene announced the Savior's resurrection from the dead and His appearance, the disbelieving disciples were upbraided for their hardness of heart; but being armed with signs and wonders, they were sent forth to preach. And Thou indeed, O Lord, didst ascend unto the Father, the primal Light; while they preached the word everywhere, confirming it by miracles. Therefore, being enlightened by them, we glorify Thy resurrection from the dead, O Lord and Lover of mankind.[^11021]

*Resurrectional Gospel 4. From Luke, Pericope 112 [Luke 24:1-12]:*[^11022]  
At that time, on the first day of the week,[^11023] very early in the morning, the women came unto the tomb, bringing the spices which they had prepared, and certain others with them. And they found the stone rolled away from the tomb. And they entered in, and found not the body of the Lord Jesus. And it came to pass, as they were much perplexed thereabout, behold, two men stood by them in shining garments: and as they were afraid, and bowed down their faces to the earth, they said unto them: Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen...[^11024]

...remember how He spoke unto you when He was yet in Galilee, saying: The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again. And they remembered His words, and returned from the tomb, and told all these things unto the eleven, and to all the rest. It was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and other women that were with them, which told these things unto the Apostles. And their words seemed to them as idle tales, and they believed them not. Then arose Peter, and ran unto the tomb; and stooping down, he beheld the linen clothes laid by themselves, and departed, wondering in himself at that which was come to pass.[^11025]

*Exapostilarion 4:*  
Shining with virtues, let us behold the men in shining garments standing in the life-bearing tomb, while the myrrh-bearing women bowed their faces to the earth; let us learn of the Resurrection of the Ruler of the heavens, and run with Peter unto the tomb of life, and having marveled at that which was done, let us remain to see Christ.[^11026]

*Theotokion:*  
Saying "Rejoice," Thou didst turn the sorrow of the forebears into joy, O Lord, bringing the joy of Thy resurrection into the world; by her that bare Thee, O Giver of life, send down therefore light enlightening hearts, the light of Thy compassions, that we may cry unto Thee: O Lover of mankind, God-Man, glory to Thy resurrection.[^11027]

*Evangelical Sticheron 4, Tone 4:*  
It was very early in the morning, and the women came unto Thy tomb, O Christ; but the body was not found which they desired. Therefore as they were perplexed, those who stood by them in shining garments said: Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is risen, as He foretold; why remember ye not His words? And believing them, they proclaimed what they had seen; but their glad tidings seemed as idle tales: so dull of heart were the disciples still. But Peter ran, and having seen, glorified in himself Thy wonders.[^11028]

*Resurrectional Gospel 5. From Luke, Pericope 113 [Luke 24:12-35]:*[^11029]  
At that time, Peter arose, and ran unto the tomb; and stooping down, he beheld the linen clothes laid by themselves, and departed, wondering in himself at that which was come to pass. And, behold, two of them went that same day to a village called Emmaus, which was from Jerusalem about threescore furlongs. And they talked together of all these things which had happened. And it came to pass...[^11030]

...that, while they communed together and reasoned, Jesus Himself drew near, and went with them. But their eyes were holden that they should not know Him. And He said unto them: What manner of communications are these that ye have one to another, as ye walk, and are sad? And the one of them, whose name was Cleopas, answering said unto Him: Art Thou only a stranger in Jerusalem, and hast not known the things which are come to pass there in these days? And He said unto them: What things? And they said unto Him: Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, which was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people: and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered Him to be condemned to death, and have crucified Him. But we trusted that it had been He which should have redeemed Israel: and beside all this, to day is the third day since these things were done. Yea, and certain women also of our company made us astonished, which were early at the tomb; and when they found not His body, they came, saying, that they had also seen a vision of angels, which said that He was alive. And certain of them which were with us went to the tomb, and found it even so as the women had said: but Him they saw not. Then He said unto them: O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken: Ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and to enter into His glory? And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, He expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning Himself. And they drew nigh unto the village, whither they went: and He made as though He would have gone further. But they constrained Him, saying: Abide with us: for it is toward evening, and the day is far spent. And He went in to tarry with them. And it came to pass, as He sat at meat with them, He took bread, and blessed it, and brake, and gave to them. And their eyes were opened, and they knew Him; and He vanished out of their sight. And they said one to another: Did not our heart burn within us, while He talked with us by the way, and while He opened to us the scriptures? And they rose up the same hour, and returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and them that were with them, saying: The Lord is risen indeed, and has appeared to Simon. And they told what things were done in the way, and how He was known of them in breaking of bread.[^11031]

*Exapostilarion 5:*  
Christ, the Life and the Way, having risen from the dead, journeyed with Cleopas and Luke, unto whom also He was made known in Emmaus in the breaking of bread; whose souls and hearts were burning when He talked with them by the way, and expounded the scriptures concerning that which He endured. With them let us cry: He is risen, and has appeared also unto Peter.[^11032]

*Theotokion:*  
I sing of Thine unnumbered mercy, O my Creator, for Thou didst empty Thyself to bear and save afflicted human nature; and being God, Thou wast pleased to become as I am from the pure Maiden of God, and to descend even unto hades, willing that I should be saved, through the prayers of her that bare Thee, O All-Compassionate Master.[^11033]

*Evangelical Sticheron 5, Tone 5:*  
O how all-wise are Thy judgments, O Christ! How unto Peter indeed by the winding-sheets alone didst Thou grant to understand Thy resurrection? And journeying with Luke and Cleopas, Thou didst converse; and conversing, didst not immediately reveal Thyself? Therefore also Thou wast reproached as being the only stranger in Jerusalem who took no part in their counsel unto the end. But Thou Who dispensest all things for the benefit of creation didst also open the prophecies concerning Thyself, and in the blessing of the bread Thou wast recognized by them; whose hearts even before that burned unto the knowledge of Thee, who also unto the assembled disciples now clearly preached Thy resurrection; through whom have mercy upon us.[^11034]

*Resurrectional Gospel 6. From Luke, Pericope 114 [Luke 24:36-53]:*[^11035]  
At that time, Jesus having risen from the dead stood in the midst of His disciples, and says unto them: Peace be unto you. But they were terrified and affrighted, and supposed that they had seen a spirit. And He said unto them: Why are ye troubled? and why do thoughts arise in your hearts? Behold My hands and My feet, that it is I Myself: handle Me, and see; for a spirit has not flesh and bones, as ye see Me have. And when He had thus spoken, He showed them His hands and His feet. And while they yet believed not for joy, and wondered, He said unto them: Have ye here any meat? And they gave Him a piece of a broiled fish, and of an honeycomb. And He took it, and did eat before them. And He said unto them: These are the words which I spoke unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning Me. Then opened He their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures, and said unto them: Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day: and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in His name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem. And ye are witnesses of these things. And, behold, I send the promise of My Father upon you: but tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high. And He led them out as far as to Bethany, and He lifted up His hands, and blessed them. And it came to pass, while He blessed them, He was parted from them, and carried up into heaven. And they worshipped Him, and returned to Jerusalem with great joy: and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.[^11036]

*Exapostilarion 6:*  
Showing that Thou art man in essence, O Savior, having risen from the tomb Thou didst partake of food, and standing in the midst didst teach to preach repentance; immediately...[^11037]

...also Thou wast taken up unto the heavenly Father, and didst promise to send the Comforter unto the disciples, O Most-Divine God-Man: glory to Thy resurrection.[^11038]

*Theotokion:*  
The Creator of creation, and God of all, took human flesh from thy most pure blood, O all-holy Virgin, and remade all my corrupted nature, leaving thee after childbearing again even as thou wast before childbearing. Therefore we all in faith praise thee, crying: Rejoice, Sovereign Lady of the world.[^11039]

*Evangelical Sticheron 6, Tone 6:*  
Thou art the true Peace, O Christ; giving Thy divine peace unto men, after Thy rising unto the disciples, Thou didst show them affrighted, supposing they saw a spirit; but Thou didst calm the turmoil of their souls, showing Thy hands and Thy feet. Yet while they still disbelieved, by partaking of food, and by reminding them of Thy teachings, Thou didst open their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures: unto whom having also promised the Father's promise, and having blessed them, Thou wast parted unto heaven. Therefore with them we worship Thee: O Lord, glory to Thee.[^11040]

*Resurrectional Gospel 7. From John, Pericope 63 [John 20:1-10]:*[^11041]  
At that time, on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene comes early, when it was yet dark, unto the tomb, and sees the stone taken away from the tomb. Then she runs, and comes to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple, whom Jesus loved, and says unto them: They have taken away the Lord out of the tomb, and we know not where they have laid Him. Peter therefore went forth, and that other disciple, and came to the tomb. So they ran both together: and the other disciple outran Peter, and came first to the tomb. And stooping down, he saw the linen clothes lying; yet went he not in. Then came Simon Peter following him, and entered into the tomb, and saw the linen clothes lie, and the napkin, that was about His head, not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself. Then entered also that other disciple, who came first to the tomb, and he saw, and believed. For as yet they knew not the scripture, that He must rise again from the dead. Then the disciples went away again unto their own home.[^11042]

*Exapostilarion 7:*  
Mary having said that they had taken away the Lord, Simon Peter and the other initiate of Christ's mysteries whom He loved ran unto the tomb; and they ran both together, and found the linen clothes...[^11043]

...lying within, and the napkin that was about His head apart from them. Therefore again they held their peace, until they beheld Christ.[^11044]

*Theotokion:*  
Great and most glorious things hast Thou wrought for my sake, O my Christ Many-merciful: for of the Virgin Maiden Thou wast born ineffably, and didst submit unto the Cross, and having endured death, Thou didst rise in glory, and set our nature free from death. Glory, O Christ, to Thy glory; glory to Thy power.[^11045]

*Evangelical Sticheron 7, Tone 7:*  
Lo, it is dark and early morning; and why, O Mary, standest thou at the tomb, having much darkness in thy mind? in which thou inquirest where Jesus is laid; but behold the disciples running together, how by the linen clothes and the napkin they discovered the Resurrection, and remembered the scriptures concerning these things. With whom and through whom we also having believed, sing praises unto Thee, Christ the Giver of life.[^11046]

*Resurrectional Gospel 8. From John, Pericope 64 [John 20:11-18]:*[^11047]  
At that time, Mary stood without at the tomb weeping: and as she wept, she stooped down, and looked into the tomb, and sees two angels in white sitting, the one at the head, and the other at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain. And they say unto her: Woman, why weepest thou? She says unto them: Because they have taken away my Lord, and I know not where they have laid Him. And when she had thus said, she turned herself back, and saw Jesus standing, and knew not that it was Jesus. Jesus says unto her: Woman, why weepest thou? whom seekest thou? She, supposing Him to be the gardener, says unto Him: Sir, if Thou have borne Him hence, tell me where Thou hast laid Him, and I will take Him away. Jesus says unto her: Mary. She turned herself, and says unto Him: Rabboni; which is to say, Master. Jesus says unto her: Touch Me not; for I am not yet ascended to My Father: but go to My brethren, and say unto them, I ascend unto My Father, and your Father; and to My God, and your God. Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that He had spoken these things unto her.[^11048]

*Exapostilarion 8:*  
Having seen two angels within the tomb, Mary was amazed, and not knowing Christ, as of a gardener she inquired: Sir, where hast Thou laid the Body of my Jesus? But recognizing Him by His calling her to be the Savior Himself, she heard: Touch Me not; I depart unto the Father, say thou unto My brethren.[^11049]

*Theotokion:*  
One of the Trinity hast thou borne, O Maiden, ineffably being in two natures, and twofold in operation, and one in hypostasis. Him therefore pray thou ever, for them that worship in faith, to be delivered from every assault of the enemy; for we all now flee unto thee, O Sovereign Lady Theotokos.[^11050]

*Evangelical Sticheron 8, Tone 8:*  
Mary's tears are not shed warmly in vain: for lo, she was vouchsafed both the teaching Angels, and the sight of Jesus Himself; yet she still minded earthly things, as a weak woman; therefore also she is sent away not to touch Christ, but yet is sent as a herald unto Thy disciples, unto whom she declared the glad tidings, proclaiming the ascent unto the Father's portion. With her vouchsafe us also of Thine appearance, O Master Lord.[^11051]

*Resurrectional Gospel 9. From John, Pericope 65 [John 20:19-31]:*[^11052]  
Then the same day at evening, being the first day of the week,[^11053] when the doors were shut where the disciples were assembled for fear of the Jews, came Jesus and stood in the midst, and says unto them: Peace be unto you. And when He had so said, He showed unto them His hands and His feet and His side. Then were the disciples glad, when they saw the Lord. Then said Jesus to them again: Peace be unto you: as My Father has sent Me, even so send I you. And when He had said this, He breathed on them, and says unto them: Receive ye the Holy Spirit: whose soever sins ye remit, they are remitted unto them; and whose soever sins ye retain, they are retained. But Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came. The other disciples therefore said unto him: We have seen the Lord. But he said unto them: Except I shall see in His hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the print of the nails, and thrust my hand into His side, I will not believe. And after eight days again His disciples were within, and Thomas with them: then came Jesus, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst, and said: Peace be unto you. Then says He to Thomas: Reach hither thy finger, and behold My hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into My side: and be not faithless, but believing. And Thomas answered and said unto Him: My Lord and my God. Jesus says unto him: Thomas, because thou hast seen Me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed. And many other signs truly did Jesus in the presence of His disciples, which are not written in this book: but these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through His name.[^11054]

*Exapostilarion 9:*  
The doors being shut, O Master, when Thou didst enter in, Thou didst fill the Apostles with the All-Holy Spirit, having peacefully breathed upon them, unto whom Thou didst say to bind and loose sins; and after eight days Thou didst show Thy side and hands unto Thomas. With whom we cry: Thou art Lord and God.[^11055]

*Theotokion:*  
When thou didst behold thy Son risen on the third day from the tomb, O Bride of God, all-holy Virgin, thou didst put away all the sorrow which thou didst endure as a Mother when thou beheldest Him suffering; and filled with joy, with His disciples thou praisest and singest unto Him. Therefore save those who now confess thee to be the Theotokos.[^11056]

*Evangelical Sticheron 9, Tone 5:*  
As in the latter years, at evening on the first day of the week, Thou didst stand before Thy friends, O Christ; and by wonder Thou didst confirm wonder: by the shut entrance of the doors, Thy resurrection from the dead. But Thou didst fill the disciples with joy, and didst impart unto them the Holy Spirit, and didst bestow authority to remit sins; and Thou didst not leave Thomas to be drowned in the tempest of unbelief. Therefore grant unto us also true understanding and remission of transgressions, O Tenderhearted Lord.[^11057]

*Resurrectional Gospel 10. From John, Pericope 66 [John 21:1-14]:*[^11058]  
At that time, Jesus showed Himself again to the disciples (having risen from the dead), at the Sea of Tiberias; and on this wise showed He Himself. There were together Simon Peter, and Thomas called Didymus, and Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two other of His disciples. Simon Peter says unto them: I go a fishing. They say unto him: We also go with thee. They went forth, and entered into a ship immediately; and that night they caught nothing. But when the morning was now come, Jesus stood on the shore: but the disciples knew not that it was Jesus. Then Jesus says unto them: Children, have ye any meat? They answered Him: No. And He said unto them: Cast the net on the right side of the ship, and ye shall find. They cast therefore, and now they were not able to draw it for the multitude of fishes. Therefore that disciple whom Jesus loved says unto Peter: It is the Lord. Now when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he girt his fisher's coat unto him, (for he was naked,) and did cast himself into the sea. And the other disciples came in a little ship; (for they were not far from land, but as it were two hundred cubits,) dragging the net with fishes.[^11059]

As soon then as they were come to land, they saw a fire of coals there, and fish laid thereon, and bread. Jesus says unto them: Bring of the fish which ye have now caught. Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to land full of great fishes, an hundred and fifty and three: and for all there were so many, yet was not the net broken. Jesus says unto them: Come and dine. And none of the disciples durst ask Him: Who art Thou? knowing that it was the Lord. Jesus then comes, and takes bread, and gives them, and fish likewise. This is now the third time that Jesus showed Himself to His disciples, after that He was risen from the dead.[^11060]

*Exapostilarion 10:*  
The Sea of Tiberias of old had Nathanael with Peter, together with the children of Zebedee, and two others, and Thomas, at fishing: who, having cast on the right side at Christ's command, drew out a multitude of fishes; Whom Peter having recognized, swam unto Him; unto whom He appeared for the third time, and showed bread, and fish upon coals.[^11061]

*Theotokion:*  
Pray the Lord Who rose on the third day from the tomb, O Virgin, for them that sing of thee and with love call thee blessed; for we all have thee as a saving refuge and an intercessor before Him; for we are thy heritage and servants, O Theotokos, and unto thy protection we all look.[^11062]

*Evangelical Sticheron 10, Tone 6:*  
After Thy descent into hades and Thy resurrection from the dead, grieving as was meet over Thy parting, O Christ, the disciples turned unto work, and again there were ships and nets, and catch nowhere; but Thou, O Savior, having appeared as Master of all, dost command to cast the nets on the right side. And the word was deed straightway, and the multitude of fishes was great, and a strange supper was prepared upon the land; of which when Thy disciples then partook, vouchsafe us also now spiritually to delight therein, O Lover of mankind.[^11063]

*Resurrectional Gospel 11. From John, Pericope 67 [John 21:15-25]:*[^11064]  
At that time, Jesus showed Himself unto His disciples, having risen from the dead, and says to Simon Peter: Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou Me more than these? He says unto Him: Yea, Lord; Thou knowest that I love Thee. He says unto him: Feed My lambs. He says to him again the second time: Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou Me? He says unto Him: Yea, Lord; Thou knowest that...[^11065]

...I love Thee. He says unto him: Feed My sheep. He says unto him the third time: Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou Me? Peter was grieved because He said unto him the third time: Lovest thou Me? And he said unto Him: Lord, Thou knowest all things; Thou knowest that I love Thee. Jesus says unto him: Feed My sheep. Truly, truly, I say unto thee: When thou wast young, thou girdedst thyself, and walkedst whither thou wouldest: but when thou shalt be old, thou shalt stretch forth thy hands, and another shall gird thee, and carry thee whither thou wouldest not. This spoke He, signifying by what death he should glorify God. And when He had spoken this, He says unto him: Follow Me. Then Peter, turning about, sees the disciple whom Jesus loved following; who also leaned on His breast at supper, and said: Lord, who is he that betrays Thee? Peter seeing him says to Jesus: Lord, and what shall this man do? Jesus says unto him: If I will that he tarry till I come, what is that to thee? follow thou Me. Then went this saying abroad among the brethren, that that disciple should not die: yet Jesus said not unto him, He shall not die; but, If I will that he tarry till I come, what is that to thee? This is the disciple who testifies of these things, and wrote these things: and we know that his testimony is true. And there are also many other things which Jesus did, the which, if they should be written every one, I suppose that even the world itself could not contain the books that should be written. Amen.[^11066]

*Exapostilarion 11:*  
After His divine rising, the Lord, having thrice asked Peter: Lovest thou Me? appointed him chief shepherd of His sheep; who seeing him whom Jesus loved following after, asked the Master: And what of this man? If I will, He said, that he remain until I come again, what is that to thee, friend Peter?[^11067]

*Theotokion:*  
O fearful mystery! O most glorious wonder! By death, death has been utterly destroyed. Who therefore will not praise, and who will not worship Thy resurrection, O Word, and the Theotokos who purely bare Thee in the flesh? By whose prayers deliver all from Gehenna.[^11068]

*Evangelical Sticheron 11, Tone 8:*  
Manifesting Thyself unto Thy disciples after the Resurrection, O Savior, Thou gavest unto Simon the pasturing of sheep, as a reward of love, seeking the care of pasturing; therefore also Thou saidst: If thou lovest Me, Peter, feed My lambs, feed My sheep. And he straightway showing friendliness, asked concerning the other disciple. By their prayers, O Christ, preserve Thy flock from the wolves that destroy it.[^11069]

*On all Sundays before the Doxology, at the Praises, the Evangelical Sticheron; and both now: Theotokion, Tone 2:*[^11070]  
Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos: for through Him Who was incarnate of thee, hades was taken captive, Adam was recalled, the curse was annulled, Eve was set free, death was put to death, and we were made alive. Therefore singing praises we cry aloud: Blessed is Christ our God, Who was thus well-pleased; glory to Thee.[^11071]

*Resurrectional Troparia sung after the Doxology on all Sundays:*[^11072]  
*When in the Octoechos it is Tone 1, 3, 5, 7:*  
Today salvation is come unto the world; let us sing unto Him Who rose from the tomb, the Author of our life; for having destroyed death by death, He has given us the victory and great mercy.[^11073]

*And when it is Tone 2, 4, 6, 8:*  
Having risen from the tomb, and burst the bonds of hades, Thou didst loose the condemnation of death, O Lord, delivering all from the snares of the enemy; manifesting Thyself unto Thine Apostles, Thou didst send them forth unto the preaching, and through them didst grant Thy peace unto the universe, O only Many-merciful One.[^11074]

*Exapostilaria, that is, Daily Photagogica for the Whole Week.*[^11075]  
*On Monday:*  
*Photagogicon of the Angels:*  
*Tone 2, Podoben: "Give ear, ye women" (Жены̀ оу҆слы́шите):*  
Archangels and Angels, Principalities and Dominions, Powers and Authorities, Thrones and many-eyed Cherubim, and six-winged Seraphim: pray for us, that we may be delivered from perils and the unquenchable fire.[^11076]

*Or this one:*  
*Tone 3, Podoben: "Thou Who didst adorn heaven with stars" (Не́бо ѕвѣзда́ми):*  
Thou Who didst adorn heaven with stars as God, and by Thine Angels didst enlighten all the earth, O Creator of all: save them that sing praises unto Thee.[^11077]

*Theotokion:*  
Thou art my protectress, O Most Pure One, and a strong rampart, and intercessor for the world; and unto thee...[^11078]

...I fall down crying: deliver me from cruel things, and snatch me from eternal fire, O Virgin, who place my hope in thee.[^11079]

*On Tuesday:*  
*Photagogicon of the Forerunner John:*  
O most glorious prophet and forerunner of grace, who didst preach the repentance of salvation unto all, and show forth the Lamb that takes away the sins of the world, O blessed one: pray Him even now to have mercy upon the world.[^11080]

*Or this one:*  
John the Forerunner and Baptist of the Savior, prophet among prophets, nursling of the desert, and offspring of Elizabeth, let us all praise.[^11081]

*Theotokion:*  
Thee did all the prophets foretell, O Virgin; David called thee mountain, and ark, and lampstand, and ladder, and bridge, and urn bearing manna, and budded rod. And we honor thee worthily as the Theotokos.[^11082]

*On Wednesday:*  
*Photagogicon of the Cross:*  
*Tone 2, Podoben: "Give ear, ye women" (Жены̀ оу҆слы́шите):*  
An invincible weapon and confirmation of faith, the divine Cross do we hymn, and worship in faith: great refuge, boast of Christians, guardian of the Orthodox, glory of passion-bearers, worthily do we praise thee.[^11083]

*Or this one:*  
The Cross is the guardian of the whole universe, the Cross is the beauty of the Church, the Cross is the might of kings, the Cross is the glory of Angels and the wounding of demons.[^11084]

*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Standing by the Cross, the Most Pure One, and seeing her Son crucified upon the Tree lifeless, lamented, crying out as a mother with tears, and saying: What is this most glorious thing? that the Creator of creation submits to be nailed upon the Cross?[^11085]

*On Thursday:*  
*Photagogicon of the Apostles:*  
Peter and Paul let us all, Andrew, James, Bartholomew, and Matthew, James, Philip, Thomas, and Simon, Luke, and John, and Mark: the Apostles of the Lord let us faithfully praise.[^11086]

*Or this one:*  
*Tone 2, Podoben: "Give ear, ye women" (Жены̀ оу҆слы́шите):*  
Having run into all under the sun, ye truly preached Christ's holy incarnation from the Virgin, from...[^11087]

...delusion turning the nations, and enlightening them, and teaching all to venerate the Holy Trinity, O Apostles of the Savior.[^11088]

*Or another:*  
*Tone 3, Podoben: "Thou Who didst adorn heaven with stars" (Не́бо ѕвѣзда́ми):*  
O holy Twelve, glory of the Apostles: with honorable Nicholas and the Hierarch of Myra, unceasingly pray the Savior for us.[^11089]

*Theotokion:*  
O Virgin unwedded, unceasingly pray Christ with the wise Apostles, and with the multitude of Martyrs, and with wise Nicholas: that they who place their hope in thee may be delivered from every peril, O Maiden.[^11090]

*On Friday:*  
*Photagogicon of the Cross:*  
Rejoice, O most holy Cross, unconquerable rampart of the world, refuge of the sorrowing, and guardian of them that are in distress: victory of kings, protection of warriors, and conquest of barbarians, crown of the Apostles, and salvation of the faithful.[^11091]

*Or another:*  
The Cross is the guardian of the whole [universe]: (see Wednesday).[^11092]

*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Beholding Thee nailed upon the Cross, the Most Pure One, weeping and lamenting, cried out: How hast Thou set, O Sun of glory, O Pre-eternal Light? How art Thou nailed upon the Cross, and pierced in the side with a spear, O my Son and my God, for the sake of human salvation?[^11093]

*On Saturday:*  
*Photagogicon of All Saints:*  
Most glorious prophets, blessed Apostles, righteous patriarchs, sole solemnity of hierarchs, assembly of venerable and righteous ones and holy women: all ye holy martyrs, with the Mother of God pray the Lord to have mercy upon your servants.[^11094]

*Or this one:*  
Thou Who didst adorn heaven with stars as God, and by Thy saints didst enlighten all the earth, O Creator of all: save them that sing praises unto Thee.[^11095]

O Thou Who rulest over both the dead and the living as God: give rest unto Thy servants in the habitations of the elect; for though they sinned, O Savior, yet they departed not from Thee.[^11096]

*Theotokion:*  
Sweetness of Angels, joy of the sorrowing, protectress of Christians, O Virgin Mother of the Lord: intercede for us, and deliver us from eternal torments.[^11097]

*Daily Triadica.*[^11098]  
*If there is Alleluia or the Great Forty Days [Lent], at Matins, after the exclamation of the Great Litany, instead of "God is the Lord," we sing Alleluia, thrice, in the Tone of the Octoechos, and these verses are said:*[^11099]  
*Verse 1: My spirit wakes early from the night unto Thee, O God, for Thy commandments are a light upon the earth. [Isa. 26:9 LXX]*[^11100]  
*Verse 2: Learn righteousness, ye that dwell upon the earth. [Isa. 26:9 LXX]*[^11101]  
*Verse 3: Envy shall take hold of an untaught people. [Isa. 26:11 LXX]*[^11102]  
*Verse 4: Bring evils upon them, O Lord, bring evils upon the glorious ones of the earth. [Isa. 26:15 LXX]*[^11103]  
*And after each verse Alleluia is sung, thrice. And after the Alleluia we sing the Triadica of the Tone, each once:*[^11104]

*Tone 1:*  
By bodily images of the bodiless Powers, being led up in mind unto that which is spiritual and immaterial, and by the thrice-holy hymn receiving the radiance of the three-hypostatic Godhead, with the Cherubim let us cry aloud unto the one God: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11105]

*Glory:*  
With all the heavenly Hosts, with the Cherubim let us cry aloud unto Him Who is in the highest, sending up thrice-holy praise: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11106]

*Both now:*  
Having risen from sleep, we fall down before Thee, O Good One, and with angelic song we cry unto Thee, O Mighty One: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11107]

*(It is meet to say at the conclusion of the first Triadicon in all eight Tones:)*[^11108]  
*On Monday: Through the intercession of Thy Bodiless Powers, have mercy upon us.*  
*On Tuesday: Through the prayers of Thy Forerunner, have mercy upon us.*  
*On Wednesday and Friday: By the power of Thy Cross, preserve us, O Lord.*  
*On Thursday: Through the prayers of Thine Apostles and the Hierarch Nicholas, have mercy upon us.*

*Tone 2:*  
Imitating the hosts on high upon the earth, we offer the song of victory unto Thee, O Good One: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11109]

*Glory:*  
Uncreated Nature, Creator of all: open Thou our lips, that we may declare Thy praise, crying: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11110]

*Both now:*  
From the bed and sleep Thou hast raised me up, O Lord: enlighten my mind and heart, and open Thou my lips, that I may sing of Thee, O Holy Trinity: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11111]

*Tone 4:*[^11112]  
O Trinity consubstantial and undivided, Monad three-hypostatic and co-eternal: unto Thee as God we cry the angelic song: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11113]

*Glory:*  
The unoriginate Father, the co-unoriginate Son, the co-eternal Spirit, one Godhead: daring with the Cherubim to glorify, we say: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11114]

*Both now:*  
Suddenly the Judge shall come, and each one's deeds shall be laid bare; but with fear let us cry at midnight: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11115]

*Tone 5:*  
Daring, as mortals, to offer the hymn of Thy spiritual servants, we say: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11116]

*Glory:*  
As the Angelic ranks now in heaven, and the companies of men with fear upon earth, we offer the song of victory unto Thee, O Good One: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11117]

*Both now:*  
Daring to hymn with the Cherubim Thine unoriginate Father, and Thee, O Christ God, and Thy Most-holy Spirit, we say: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11118]

*Tone 5:*[^11119]  
It is the time for singing, and the hour for prayer: let us diligently cry aloud unto the one God: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11120]

*Glory:*  
Daring to form the likeness of Thy spiritual armies, O unoriginate Trinity, with unworthy lips we cry unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11121]

*Both now:*  
Thou Who wast contained in the virginal womb, and yet wast not separated from the Father’s bosom, receive us also with the Angels, O Christ God, as we cry unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11122]

*Tone 6:*  
The Cherubim standing before Thee with fear, and the Seraphim dreading with trembling, offer the thrice-holy hymn with unceasing voice; with them also we sinners cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11123]

*Glory:*  
With bodiless lips and unceasing glorifications, the six-winged ones cry unto Thee the thrice-holy hymn, O our God; and we who are upon earth with unworthy lips send up praise unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11124]

*Both now:*  
We glorify the Godhead of the Trinity in Unity with unconfused union, and we cry aloud the angelic song: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11125]

*Tone 7:*  
Hymned with the Cherubim by the power on high, and worshipped with the Angels in divine glory, receive us also who are upon earth, with un-[^11126] -worthy lips sending up praises unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11127]

*Glory:*  
Having cast off sloth as sleep, O my soul, show forth amendment for praising the Judge, and with fear cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11128]

*Both now:*  
Sending up Seraphic thrice-holy praise unto the unapproachable Godhead in the Trinity in Unity, with fear let us cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11129]

*Tone 8:*  
Having our hearts on high, let us imitate the Angelic order, and with fear let us fall down before the Judge, crying aloud the song of victory: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God:[^11130]

*Glory:*  
The Cherubim daring not to gaze upon Thee, flying, cry with shouting the divine song of thrice-holy voice; with them also we sinners cry unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: through the prayers of all Thy Saints have mercy upon us.[^11131]

*Both now:*  
Bowed down by the multitude of our transgressions, and daring not to look up unto the height of heaven, having bent down soul and body, with the Angels we cry a song unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: through the Theotokos have mercy upon us.[^11132]
