# Master Corpus of Eastern Liturgical Law, Service Books & Typika
## High-ROI Wave-Prioritized Transmission Chain for the Translation & Typikon Coded Spokes

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Executive Architecture & High-ROI Wave Prioritization Mandate**  
> This specification codifies the **Master Liturgical Translation Canon** of the Byzantine-Ruthenian (Galician and Kyivan) liturgical tradition, spanning 1,200 years of liturgical history (8th century to 2010 AD).  
> Staff-notated musical score books (*1894 Dolnytsky Hlasopisnets*, *1902 Polotniuk Napivnyk Tserkovnyj*, and *1790 Pochayiv Bohohlasnyk*) are assigned exclusively to the **Chant Indexer / Kyivan Musicology** spokes and pruned from this textual translation queue.  
> Following the sealing of **Monuments 0–6** and the ongoing execution of **Monument 7** (`1895_dmitrievsky_vol1`), the translation queue from **Monument 8 onward** is structured into **Four Intelligent High-ROI Waves** prioritizing Galician and Kyivan Service Books (Octoechos, Lenten Triodion, Paschal Pentecostarion, Festal Anthologia, Horologion, Menaia) and Primary Typika ahead of large Sacerdotal Euchologia, Modern Rome Ordines/Liturgika, Muscovite *Oko Tserkovnoe* codices, Monastic Legislation, and Comparative Rites.
