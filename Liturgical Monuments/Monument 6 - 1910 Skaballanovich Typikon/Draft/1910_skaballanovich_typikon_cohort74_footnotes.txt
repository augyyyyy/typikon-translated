[^2373]: The Liturgical Apostle has, in addition to the division into chapters and pericopes [*zachalá*], special sections numbering 72, in the margins before which are placed indications of the days of the week: "Sunday", "Monday"... This division was made for cell reading, as is explained in the MS of the Moscow Synodal Library No. 51. Into these sections are inserted also the "narratives" [*skazaniya*] concerning the origin of the epistles. To use this division for the present readings (Nikolsky, K., Archpriest, *Posobie k izucheniyu ustava bogosluzheniya* [Manual for the Study of the Typikon of Divine Worship], 230) is impossible, if only in view of the discrepancy between the number of sections and the Sunday vigils at which such readings are appointed, which number fewer than 50.
