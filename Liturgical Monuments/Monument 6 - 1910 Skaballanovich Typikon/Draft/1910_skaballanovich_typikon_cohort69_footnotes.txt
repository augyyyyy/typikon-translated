Thus were called the gravediggers [fossarii / kopiatēs], of whom Constantine the Great constituted a special corporation, exempt from taxes and subject unto ecclesiastical authority, consequently, a certain order of clergy. It is scarcely probable that they are meant here and placed prior unto the singers because they stood higher than them (Veniamin (Krasnopevkov-Rumovsky), Archbishop, *Novaya Skrizhal'* [The New Tablet], in 4 parts, St. Petersburg, 1858, p. 26); rather, here are meant the manual laborers of the monastery, and they are placed higher than the singers on account of the arduousness of their service.
