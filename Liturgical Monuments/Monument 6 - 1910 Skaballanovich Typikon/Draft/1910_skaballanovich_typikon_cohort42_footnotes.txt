[^1830]: This office, which replaced the order for the kneading of dough of the Studite-Alexis Typikon (see above, p. 426) and was retained by certain Slavic MSS, consisted in the following: After Matins and the First Hour, if leavening of bread takes place in the bakery, the serving priest proceeds thither in epitrachelion with the censer and preceded by a candle, as for a Litiya, with the chanting of the Litiya sticheron of the temple with its Theotokion, accompanied by the brethren; in the bakery a Little Litany takes place and the prayer for the blessing of loaves, as at the Vigil (only instead of "these loaves," there is said: "this kneaded dough"); holy water (*hagiasma*) is poured into the leaven and the dismissal is given: "May Christ our true God, through the intercessions of His all-immaculate Mother and of all the saints, grant a heavenly reward unto our fathers and brethren who labor in this monastery, and have mercy upon us..."
