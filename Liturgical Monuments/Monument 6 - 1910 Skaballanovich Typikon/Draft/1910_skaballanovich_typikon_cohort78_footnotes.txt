[^2436]: In the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra, by tradition, presumably from the dominance of the Studite Typikon and other recensions of the Octoechos, they sing different Sunday Theotokia compared with those placed in the present Octoechos. Here they are. Tone 1 after Kathisma 2: "Having conceived unconsumed the Divine fire and given birth without seed unto the Fountain of life, the Lord, O full of grace, Theotokos, save them that magnify thee." Tone 2 after Kathisma 2: "Concealed from the laws of nature, thou hast united virginity unto Divine childbearing; for thou alone hast given birth unto Him Who was begotten of the Father before thee without mother, therefore thee, O Theotokos, do we magnify." Tone 3 after Kathisma 1: model (*podoben*), like the sessional hymn, **"Today the Virgin"** (*Deva dnes'*): "The heavenly hosts rejoiced with love and the earthly trembled with fear, when the most pure voice came upon thee, O Theotokos; for a single triumph shone forth for both, when it delivered the first-formed from death." Tone 4 after Kathisma 1: "Thee do we magnify, O Theotokos, crying aloud: Thou art the mountain wherefrom ineffably was cut a stone, which shattered the gates of Hades." Tone 5 after Kathisma 1: "A wondrous miracle of conception and an ineffable manner of birth was made known in thee, O Pure Virgin; it astonisheth my mind and terrifieth my thought; thy glory, O Theotokos, is spread abroad unto all, for the salvation of our souls." In Tones 4 and 5 the Theotokia of the 1st Kathisma are transferred unto the 2nd. In Tones 6 and 7 the customary Theotokia are transposed. Tone 8 after Kathisma 1: "Rejoice, thou that through the Angel receivedst the joy of the world; rejoice, thou that didst bear thy Creator and Lord; rejoice, thou that wast vouchsafed to be the Mother of God." **"All of creation rejoiceth in thee"** (*O Tebe raduetsya*) is model (*podoben*) to the 1st sessional hymn. After each kathisma there is one sessional hymn, sung once: for Tones 1–6 the present 1st and 2nd after the 1st Kathisma; for Tone 7 the first after the 1st and 2nd Kathismata, but transposed; for Tone 8 the same without transposition (*Notny Obikhod of the Kyiv-Pechersk Dormition Lavra*, Part 1: *All-Night Vigil, Score*, Kyiv, 1910, pp. 104ff.).
