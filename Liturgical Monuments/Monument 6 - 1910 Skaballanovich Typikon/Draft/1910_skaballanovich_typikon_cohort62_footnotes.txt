In the Persian-Nestorian Liturgy attributed unto the Apostle Thaddaeus, before the prayer for the hallowing of the Gifts, the deacon says: "In your minds pray for peace with us"; before communion: "Let us pray for our peace among ourselves"; after communion—the same (*Sobranie drevnikh liturgiy* [Collection of Ancient Liturgies], St. Petersburg, 1874–1878, IV, pp. 22, 30, 36). The choir responds: "My peace I give unto you" thrice with the refrain of the lesser doxology, and then the priest says: "Give the kiss of love and peace, that ye may be prepared for the Holy Mysteries of God" (ibid., IV, pp. 106, 144).
