The tracing of the cross upon the forehead with a finger, as the simplest form of the sign of the cross, must have been, as is evident from the present passage, its primitive form (now preserved in the Roman Catholic Mass: by the priest before the reading of the Gospel). Of this form St. John Chrysostom likewise maketh mention (*Homily on Christ Being God*); yet unto him the sign of the cross upon the lips and breast is already known (*Homilies on Matthew*, Hom. 87 [88]), as likewise unto Blessed Jerome (*Funeral Oration on Paula*, Ep. 108 [27]).
