How consistently and strictly the Typikon enforces this principle of combining other kanons with the Sunday kanon is shown by the circumstance that even when a Great Feast of the Theotokos having a kanon to 16 coincides with Sunday, Sunday Matins does not increase its kanon by a single verse, and the festal kanon for this reason is halved and chanted only to 8.
