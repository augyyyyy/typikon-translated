"After Pascha until Pentecost on Fridays they take refreshment (*reficiendum*) once, and after the 12th (hour) there are six *missae*, that is, 18 readings must be pronounced from memory and then 18 psalms, 3 antiphons; after the nocturn there must be 3 *missae* from the book until daybreak." This obscure passage of Caesarius presumably prescribes throughout the entire period of Pentecost on Friday evenings before Saturday especially long vigils from 6 p.m. Friday without supper until the very dawn of Saturday (cf. in Cassian, above p. 162), whereby the customary vigil of 18 psalms was preceded by 18 readings and followed by yet 3 readings. All this is in the *Regula ad virgines*, § 66. Caesarius describes the night service more briefly in the *Regula ad monachos*: "The vigils from October until Pascha comprise two nocturns and three *missae* (readings): at one *missa* a brother reads three folios and they pray; he reads another three folios and arises, say an antiphon, a responsory, and another antiphon; the antiphons being according unto the order of the Psalter; at the 1st *missa* the reading is concerning the resurrection and at it all stand" (canon 21).
