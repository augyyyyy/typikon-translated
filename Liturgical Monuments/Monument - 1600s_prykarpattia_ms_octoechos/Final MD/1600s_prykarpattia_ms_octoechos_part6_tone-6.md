# TONE 6

## TONE 6 [^107]

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 6)

[f. 89A] *On Saturday Evening at Great Vespers:*

*On "Lord, I have cried," Resurrection Stichera, Tone 6:*

Having victory over Hades, O Christ, Thou didst ascend upon the Cross, that Thou mightest raise up together with Thyself those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death, free among the dead, pouring forth life from Thine own light: O almighty Savior, have mercy upon us!

*Sticheron:*

Today Christ, having trampled down death, is risen even as He said, and granted joy unto the world, that we all crying out might say this hymn: O Fountain of life, O unapproachable Light, almighty Savior, have mercy upon us!

*Sticheron:*

Whither shall we sinners flee from Thee, O Lord, Who art in all creation? In heaven Thou Thyself dwellest; in Hades Thou didst trample down death; into the depths of the sea, even there is Thy hand, O Master. Unto Thee we flee, and falling down before Thee we pray: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, have mercy upon us!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian [Eastern], the same Tone:*

In Thy Cross [f. 89A] do we boast, O Christ, and Thy Resurrection do we hymn and glorify; for Thou art our God, besides Thee we know no other!

*Sticheron:*

Glory to Thy power, O Lord, for Thou didst abolish him that had the dominion of death, and hast renewed us by Thy Cross, granting unto us life and incorruption!

*Sticheron:*

Ever blessing the Lord, we hymn His Resurrection; for having endured the Crucifixion, by death He destroyed death!

*Sticheron:*

Thy burial, O Master, having shattered and torn asunder the bonds of Hades, and Thy Resurrection from the dead enlightened the world: O Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion [Dogmatikon], Tone 6:*

Who does not call thee blessed, O all-holy Virgin? Or who will not sing of thy most pure childbirth? For the only-begotten Son, Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the Same came forth from thee, pure one, having ineffably become incarnate, being God by nature, and [having become man] by nature for our sake; not divided into two persons, but known in two natures without confusion. Him beseech, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may be saved!

[f. 90A] *At the Aposticha, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 6:* [^108]

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, the Angels hymn in the heavens: vouchsafe also us on earth to hymn and glorify Thee with a pure heart!

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

Having shattered the brazen gates and smashed the bars of Hades, as God almighty Thou didst raise up the fallen human race; therefore we also cry out with one accord: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

Desiring to set us upright from the ancient corruption, O Christ, Thou wast nailed to the Cross and placed in the tomb; Whom the Myrrh-bearing women sought with tears, and weeping said: Woe unto us, O Savior of all! How didst Thou accept to dwell in a tomb? And having dwelt therein willingly, how wast Thou stolen away? How wast Thou moved? What place hid Thy life-bearing Body? But, O Master, appear unto us as Thou didst promise, and assuage our tearful lamentation! And as they wept, an Angel cried unto them: Cease from weeping, and say unto the Apostles that the Lord is risen, granting cleansing and great mercy unto the world!

*Verse:*

Thou wast crucified...

[f. 90A] *Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

Thou wast crucified as Thou didst will, O Christ, and by Thy burial Thou didst take death captive; on the third day Thou didst arise as God with glory, granting eternal life and great mercy unto the world!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion, Tone 6:*

My Creator and Deliverer, Christ the Lord, having come forth from thy womb, O all-pure one, and having clothed Himself in me, delivered Adam from the first curse; therefore unto thee, O all-pure one, as truly the Mother of God and Virgin, we cry unceasingly with the Angel's voice: Rejoice, O Lady, protectress, and veil, and salvation of our souls!

*Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart. Trisagion. Our Father:*

*Troparion, Tone 6:*

The angelic hosts were upon Thy tomb, and the guards became as dead; and Mary stood at the tomb, seeking Thy most pure Body. Having despoiled Hades, and not being tempted by it, Thou didst meet the Virgin, granting life: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

He Who called His Mother blessed, came unto the Passion by voluntary counsel, shining forth upon the Cross, seeking to search out Adam, and saying unto the Angels: Rejoice with Me, (fol. 91)[^109] for the lost coin has been found! O Thou Who hast wisely ordered all things, Lord, glory to Thee!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 6)

*On Sunday at Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity. The acrostic whereof is: A six-fold song I offer unto Thee, O Godhead:*

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos: Traversing the deep on foot as on dry land...*

*Troparion:*

We hymn the three God-originating Hypostases of the unitary essence, the unchangeable Form of the good God Who loves mankind, Who grants us cleansing of transgressions!

*Troparion:*

O supersubstantial Lord, One and tri-radiant in personal characters, existing in one Godhead: grant us understanding, and vouchsafe us Thy divine radiance!

*Troparion:*

Adorning as a bride the Church from the Gentiles, Paul taught her to worship Thee, the one three-hypostatic God, from Whom, and through Whom, and in Whom all things were made!

*Theotokion:*

From thy womb came forth the noetic Sun, O Mother of God, illuminating us with the rays of the tri-radiant Godhead [f. 90A] Whom hymning, we piously call thee blessed!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: There is none holy as Thou, O Lord...*

*Troparion:*

O tri-radiant God, Who hast adorned the heavenly ranks, Thou didst appoint them to sing unto Thee with thrice-holy voices; with them receive also us who hymn Thy goodness!

*Troparion:*

Hymning the one unchangeable, co-formal, unitary Trinitarian Principle of the Godhead, we fervently pray: Send down now unto us forgiveness of our many sins!

*Troparion:*

O unoriginate Mind, the Father, O co-formal Word of God, and O divine, good, and upright Spirit: preserve those who faithfully hymn Thy dominion, as Thou art compassionate!

*Theotokion:*

My God, having become man according to essence in thy womb, O pure one, destroyed the pasture of corruption, and alone set our forebears free from the former condemnation!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6.*

*Podoben: "Fulfilling the dispensation for our sake" ("Еже ѡ насъ" (Исполнивъ смотрѣніе)):*

O Master God, look down from heaven and behold our lowliness, as Thou art generous, and have compassion, O all-good Lover of mankind; for we have no hope from anywhere of receiving forgiveness for the evil things wherein we have sinned: therefore be Thou with us, and none shall be against us!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

[f. 92A] O pure Lady, look down, O Mother of God, behold the painfulness of our wounds, and take pity, O all-pure one, and heal the burning of conscience, bedewing it with thy mercy, and saying unto thy servants: I am with you, and none shall be against you! [^110]

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Christ is my power, God and Lord!" the...*

*Troparion:*

O tri-radiant Unity, Thou Who exaltest the mind: lift up swiftly the soul and heart of Thy singers, and vouchsafe them Thy radiance and brightness!

*Troparion:*

Turn us and transform us from every malice unto virtue, O only Uncircumscribed and Unchangeable One, and enlighten us with Thy rays!

*Troparion:*

Having conceived before, Thou didst wisely establish the ranks of Angels as servants of Thy goodness, O three-hypostatic Unity; with them receive also my praise!

*Theotokion:*

He Who by nature is uncreated, ever-existing God, taking upon Himself created human nature, formed it again in thy holy womb, O Mother of God, Ever-Virgin!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: With Thy Divine light, O Good One, illumine...*

*Troparion:*

Contemplating the God-originating nature, providential and saving of all, which is the Master, tri-radiant and yet [f. 92A] One: unto Thee we rise early, asking forgiveness of our transgressions!

*Troparion:*

O unoriginate God the Father, and co-ever-existing Son, and Holy Spirit: O monarchic Trinity, establish Thy singers, and deliver them from every temptation and tribulation!

*Troparion:*

Guide me by Thy beneficent rays, and lead me ever unto the well-pleasing of Thy three-hypostatic Godhead, O Sun of glory, and make me a partaker of Thy divine Kingdom!

*Theotokion:*

O unchangeable Word of God, Who upholdest and preservest all things by Thine almighty hand: protect and preserve those who glorify Thee, through the prayers of the Mother of God who bore Thee!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Being swallowed up by the abyss of sins...*

*Troparion:*

Grant wisdom and understanding, O tri-radiant Principle of the Godhead, unto Thy singers, and vouchsafe that all may be illumined with the rays of the beauty of Thy beneficent goodness! *(Twice)*

*Glory:*

O Light indivisible according to essence, tri-radiant, almighty, unapproachable: illuminate the hearts of those who faithfully praise Thy dominion, and wing them unto divine love!

*Both now:*

[f. 93A] In thee, O Ever-Virgin, the Sustainer and Lord of all openly made His abode, and taught mankind to bow down before the one tri-radiant form of the Godhead! [^111]

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6.*

*Podoben: "Fulfilling the dispensation for our sake" ("Еже ѡ насъ" (Исполнивъ смотрѣніе)):*

O Father and Son, with the Holy Spirit: look upon us who worship Thee in faith and glorify Thy dominion, O compassionate One, with our earth-born clay; for we know no other besides Thee: and cry Thou unto those who hymn Thee: I am with you, and none shall be against you!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

Look upon us, O all-hymned Mother of God, shine forth enlightenment upon darkened hearts, and illumine thy flock, O all-pure one; for as much as thou wilt, thou art able to do, as thou art the Mother of thy Creator: and cry unto those who pray unto thee: I am with you, and none shall be against you!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The Angel made the furnace sprinkle dew for...*

*Troparion:*

Establish in me a steadfast mind, O Unity tri-radiant in personal characters, so as to keep and observe Thy divine commandments, and ever to sing unto Thee with faith: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

As Thou art hymned through identity of nature, O ineffably unitary God, yet bearing the number of a Trinity in Persons: preserve [f. 93A] us all from diverse temptations and assaults!

*Glory:*

As co-natural and co-ever-existing we glorify Thee, the one God according to essence, O Trinity, clearly presenting what is distinct in unconfused hypostatic properties simply in an unchangeable Form!

*Both now:*

The supersubstantial God accepted our mixture openly from thy pure womb, O all-pure one, in His love for mankind, and taught all to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: From the flame Thou didst pour forth dew...*

*Troparion:*

Grant me swiftly cleansing of transgressions and deliverance from multiform passions, O co-formal Trinity, three-hypostatic Unity: that I may glorify Thee unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Known as rich in mercy, since Thou art a merciful God: have mercy upon all, O tri-radiant Unity and all-good Trinity, who doxologize Thy majesty!

*Glory:*

From the Light, the ever-existing Father, the co-ever-existing Light, the begotten Word, with the Light, the proceeding Spirit, do we glorify in faith, and supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Both now:*

Thou didst bring forth the Physician of mankind, O all-pure one, the almighty Word, (fol. 94)[^112] Christ the Lord, Who heals the ancestral wounds of all who supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: It is impossible for men to see God...*

*Troparion:*

The Cherubic ranks, O Master, unable to gaze upon the beauty of Thy glory, covering themselves with their wings, unceasingly cry out the Trinitarian hymn, glorifying the three-hypostatic dominion of Thy unitary Godhead!

*Troparion:*

Grant Thy rays, O unsetting Sun, unto the hearts of Thy servants: and enlighten their souls, and deliver them from many transgressions, O only All-merciful and Three-hypostatic One, and vouchsafe us Thine incorruptible life!

*Troparion:*

O true Godhead, the Light equally honorable, tri-solar, and light-creating: illuminate those who hymn Thee in faith, and deliver them from dark wickedness, and vouchsafe them Thy most radiant mansions, as Thou art supremely good!

*Theotokion:*

Thy Son wisely created man of old, O Virgin, and through thee He renewed him when corrupted, O all-hymned one, and filled with the unwaning rays of divine light all who in faith glorify thee as the true Mother of God!

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 6)

[f. 93A] *On Sunday at Matins, after the Six Psalms, at "God is the Lord," Troparion, Tone 6: "The angelic hosts were upon Thy tomb." Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

*After the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns, Tone 6:*

The tomb being opened and Hades weeping, Mary cried unto the hidden Apostles: Come forth, ye workers of the vineyard, preach the word of the Resurrection! The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Sessional Hymn:*

Life lay in the tomb, and with a seal the stone was sealed; as a sleeping king the soldiers guarded Christ, and having struck His enemies with blindness, the Lord is risen!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

O Mother of God, Ever-Virgin, pray Thy Son, Christ our God, Who was willingly nailed unto the Cross and is risen from the dead, that our souls may be saved!

*After the 2nd Stichology, Sessional Hymns, Tone 6:*

O Lord, Mary Magdalene, standing before Thy tomb, wept, crying aloud, and supposing Thee to be the gardener, said: Where hast Thou laid the eternal Life? Where hast Thou hidden Him Who sits upon the Cherubic throne? For those who guarded Him became as dead from fear. Either give me my Lord, or cry out with me: (fol. 95)[^113] O Thou Who wast among the dead and hast raised the dead, glory to Thee!

*Glory:*

Jonah prefigured Thy tomb, and Simeon makes known Thy divine rising, O immortal Lord; for Thou didst descend into the tomb as dead, O Thou Who didst destroy the gates of Hades, and Thou didst arise without corruption as Master, for the salvation of the world, O Christ our God, Who hast enlightened those who were in darkness!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Gideon prefigured the conception, and David tells of thy childbirth, O Mother of God; for like rain upon the fleece, the Word descended into thy womb, and thou didst bud forth without seed, O holy earth, the salvation of the world, Christ our God, O full of grace!

*Hypakoe, Tone 6:*

By Thy voluntary and life-creating death, O Christ, having shattered the gates of Hades as God, Thou didst open the ancient Paradise unto us, and having risen from the dead, Thou didst deliver our life from corruption!

*Then the Songs of Ascents [Anabathmoi], Antiphon 1, Tone 6:*

*Verse 1:*

Unto heaven do I lift up mine eyes, unto Thee, O Word: have compassion upon me, that I may live unto Thee!

*Verse 2:*

Have mercy upon us who are despised, fashioning us as vessels well-fitted for Thee, O Word!

[f. 93A] *Glory:*

In the Holy Spirit is the cause of all salvation; if upon anyone He breathes worthily, quickly does He lift him up from earthly things, wings him, makes him grow, and establishes him on high!

*Both now:*

[Through the Holy Spirit...]

*Antiphon 2:*

*Verse 1:*

If the Lord had not been among us, not one of us would have been able to withstand the enemy's warfare; for they that conquer are exalted from hence!

*Verse 2:*

Let not my soul be seized by their teeth like a bird, O Word: woe is me! how shall I escape the enemy, being a lover of sin?

*Glory, both now:*

Through the Holy Spirit is deification unto all, good will, understanding, peace, and blessing; for He works equally with the Father and the Word!

*Antiphon 3:*

*Verse 1:*

They that trust in the Lord are fearful unto enemies and marvelous unto all; for they look on high!

*Verse 2:*

Unto iniquities the righteous lot will not stretch out its hands, having Thee as Helper, O Savior!

*Glory, both now:*

Of the Holy Spirit is the dominion over all, Whom the hosts on high worship, together with every breath below!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 6:*

O Lord, stir up Thy power, and come to save us!

*Verse: Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, Thou Who leadest Joseph like a sheep!*

Let every breath praise the Lord. The Matins Resurrection Gospel.

[f. 96A] *Resurrectional Canon, Tone 6. Ode 1. Heirmos:* [^114]

Traversing the deep on foot as on dry land, Israel beheld the pursuer Pharaoh drowning, and cried out: Let us sing a song of victory unto God!

*Troparion:*

With outstretched palms upon the Cross, O good Jesus, Thou didst fulfill the Father's good pleasure in all things; therefore a song of victory we all sing unto Thee!

*Troparion:*

In fear, as a maidservant, death, having been commanded, drew near unto Thee, the Master of life, Who through it grantest us endless life and resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

Having received Thine own Creator, as He Himself willed, ineffably incarnate from thy seedless womb, O pure one, thou didst truly appear as the Lady of creation!

*Another Canon [to the Theotokos]. Heirmos: By the wave of the sea...*

*Troparion:*

In fear the gates of death open unto Thee, and the eternal bars are torn asunder; for by Thy venerable descent the dead of old arose, thankfully hymning Thy Resurrection, O Christ!

[f. 96A] *Troparion:*

Thou wast laid in the lowest pit, O Christ, having become dead, and Thou didst slay the slayer, and didst deliver the prisoners in Hades who glorify Thy venerable Resurrection, O Master!

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, light-bearing tabernacle and candlestick, church and table, and holy mountain! Rejoice, palace of glory and throne of our God! Rejoice, unassailable rampart unto us who sing praise!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: As on dry land...*

*Troparion:*

Having partaken of the pleasant garden of disobedience, Eve brought in the curse; but this hast thou loosed, O pure one, having given birth to the Author of blessing, Christ!

*Troparion:*

Having given birth to Christ, the Pearl of divine lightnings: disperse the mist of my passions and the turmoil of my transgressions, O pure one, by the light of thy brightness!

*Troparion:*

With prophetic eyes Jacob secretly foresaw the expectation of the nations, God incarnate from thee, delivering us through thine intercession!

*Troparion:*

The rulers from the tribe of Judah having failed, O all-pure one, Thy Son and God came forth, a Leader unto the ends of the earth, and now reigns in truth!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established them upon the rock of Thy confession!

[f. 97A] *Troparion:* [^115]

Beholding God crucified in the flesh, creation dissolved in fear; yet by the almighty hand of Him Who was crucified for our sake, it was firmly held together.

*Troparion:*

Shattered by death, wretched death lies breathless; for unable to endure the divine assault of Life, the strong one is slain, and resurrection is granted unto all!

*Theotokion:*

The wonder of thy divine childbirth, O pure one, surpasses every order of nature; for beyond nature thou didst conceive God in thy womb, and having given birth, thou remainest ever a Virgin!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: Hanging Thee upon the waters...*

*Troparion:*

Confessing Thee Who didst arise on the third day and appeared unto the Myrrh-bearing women, O Savior, and said unto them: "Rejoice!", we say: There is none holy save Thee, O Lord, Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Glory to Thy rising, O our Savior, for Thou hast snatched us from Hades, corruption, and death, as Thou art almighty; and singing we say: There is none holy save Thee, O Lord, Lover of mankind! *(Twice)*

*Troparion:*

[Confessing] the Word incarnate from the holy Child of God [f. 97A] incarnate Word from the holy Child of God, and Him Who saved fallen Adam, the first-formed, from deception, we gloriously hymn: There is none holy save Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: There is none holy as the Lord...*

*Troparion:*

Having ineffably received my corruptible and mortal flesh from thy womb, O all-venerable Mother of God, the Master, having clothed it in incorruption, bound it eternally unto Himself!

*Troparion:*

Beholding God incarnate from thee, O Virgin, the ranks of Angels were struck with fear, and as the Mother of God, they glorify thee with unceasing hymns!

*Troparion:*

The Prophet Daniel was struck with awe, beholding thee as the noetic mountain, from whence a stone was cut without hands, which mightily crushed the demonic altars, O Mother of God!

*Theotokion:*

No human word nor tongue can worthily praise thee, O Virgin; for from thee without seed, Christ the Giver of life was well-pleased to become incarnate, O all-pure one!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Christ is my power, God and Lord, the venerable Church divinely cries aloud, keeping festival in the Lord with a pure mind!

*Troparion:*

The Tree of true life has blossomed forth, O Christ, for the Cross was set up; and being watered with blood and water from Thy corruptless side, it budded forth life unto us!

[f. 98A] *Troparion:* [^116]

No longer does the serpent falsely suggest deification unto me; for Christ, the Deifier of human nature, has now unhindered opened unto me the path of life!

*Theotokion:*

Truly ineffable and incomprehensible are the mysteries of thy divinely-fitting childbirth, O Mother of God, both on earth and in heaven, O Ever-Virgin!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: Foreseeing Thy divine emptying upon the Cross...*

*Troparion:*

Co-existing divinely with the Father on high, Thou wast bodily nailed unto the Tree for our sake, loosing the curse that was in Paradise, as Thou art almighty!

*Troparion:*

The all-pure flesh of the Redeemer lying in the tomb was not corrupted, but as almighty He shattered the bars of Hades, and arose in glory on the third day, as God! *(Twice)*

*Troparion:*

Having risen from the dead, our Savior cried out unto the Myrrh-bearing women, saying: "Rejoice!", and called unto them: Go, announce the good tidings unto My Apostles!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: Christ is my power...*

*Troparion:*

The assembly gathered through thee hymns thee, O all-pure and all-spotless one, and piously singing we cry aloud: Blessed art thou, O Ever-Virgin, who hast given birth to God!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst give birth to the unsetting Light, O Virgin, shining in the flesh unto those who are in the darkness of life, O all-blessed one, and [f. 98A] unto those who hymn thee, thou hast poured forth joy, O Ever-Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Grace has blossomed forth, the Law has ceased through thee, O all-hymned one; for thou, O pure one, didst give birth to the Lord Who grants us forgiveness forever, O Virgin!

*Troparion:*

The tasting of the garden showed mankind to be dead; but the Tree of Life that appeared from thee, O all-pure one, raised them up, and made them heirs of the delight of Paradise!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

With Thy divine light, O Good One, enlighten with love the souls of those who rise early unto Thee, I pray, that they may behold Thee, the Word of God, the true God, drawing them forth from the darkness of sin!

*Troparion:*

The Cherubim now yields unto me, and the flaming sword turns its back before me, O Master, having beheld Thee, the Word of God, the true God, making a way into Paradise for the Thief!

*Troparion:*

No longer do I fear the return unto the earth, O Master Christ; for from the earth Thou didst lead me up, who was forgotten, because of Thy great compassion, unto the height of incorruption by Thy Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

Save those who confess thee from the soul to be the Mother of God, O good Lady of the world; for we have gained thee as an unconquerable intercessor, the true Theotokos!

[f. 99A] *Another Canon. Heirmos: With Thy Divine light, O Good One, illumine...* [^117]

*Troparion:*

Out of compassion, O Good One, having taken upon Thyself our form, Thou wast willingly nailed unto the Cross for my sake, who had formerly rejected Thy holy commandment through eating; and Thou diest as man, granting me life!

*Troparion:*

Beholding Thee upon the throne as God, and among the dead reckoned as dead, wretched Hades vomited forth those whom it formerly held; and on the third day, O Mighty One, Thou didst co-resurrect fallen nature, and Thou Thyself didst enlighten the whole world, O Christ, as Thou art merciful!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst not suffer corruption in thy womb, having received a virginal childbirth, but again as before thou didst remain, O unwedded Mother of God; therefore pray fervently for thy flock that glorifies thee, that we may be delivered from temptations and perils!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: Shining forth brightly in the morning...*

*Troparion:*

Having shone forth brightly in the morning, a divine dwelling was prepared for the Master, O all-hymned one; for thou alone didst appear as the Mother of God, O pure one, having carried Him in thine arms as an infant!

*Troparion:*

Possessing noetic beauty, fair above all in thy soul, thou wast called the Bride of God, sealed [f. 99A] with virginity, O pure one, and illuminating the world with the light of purity!

*Troparion:*

Let the assembly of the impious lament, who do not openly confess thee as the pure Mother of God; for thou hast appeared unto us as the gate of divine light, driving away the darkness of sins!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Beholding the sea of life surging with the storm of temptations, and fleeing unto Thy calm haven, I cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O Greatly-merciful One!

*Troparion:*

Nailed with nails, O Master, Thou didst wipe away the curse that was against us; and pierced with a spear in the side, tearing up the handwriting of Adam, Thou didst deliver the world!

*Troparion:*

Adam was brought down, tripped by deceit unto the abyss of Hades; but as God and merciful, Thou didst descend in search of human nature, and taking it upon Thy shoulders, didst raise it up with Thyself!

*Theotokion:*

O all-pure Lady, who didst give birth to the Lord, the Helmsman of the universe: calm the unstable and fierce tumult of my passions, and grant stillness unto my heart!

[f. 100A] *Another Canon. Heirmos: Jonah was seized, yet not held fast...* [^118]

*Troparion:*

How Thou didst endure wounds, insults, and spitting, O Word! Thou didst willingly suffer in the flesh as man, being crucified upon the Tree between two Thieves, and Thou didst save our nature from corruption, as Thou art almighty!

*Troparion:*

Thou wast laid below in the tomb for my sake, O Savior, as dead, and Thou didst shine forth light unto those held in darkness and the shadow of death, and didst shine forth on the third day; and meeting the Myrrh-bearing women, Thou didst cry aloud unto them: Truly rejoice, and tell further unto My Apostles!

*Theotokion:*

O Virgin, favored Mother and Bride of God, who without knowing man didst receive in thy womb and bear incarnate Him Who was begotten of the unoriginate Father before the ages: pray Him on behalf of us all, that He send down the multitude of His compassions unto us!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: Beholding the sea of life...*

*Troparion:*

Great Moses among the Prophets prefigured thee as the Ark, the Table, the Candlestick, and the Urn, figuratively signifying the incarnation of the Most High from thee, O Virgin Mother!

*Troparion:*

Death is slain, and the corruption of Adam's condemnation is abolished [f. 100A] O Lady, having clashed against Thy fruit; for thou didst bear Life, delivering from corruption those who hymn thee!

*Troparion:*

The Law grew weak and the shadow passed away, when grace beyond mind and understanding appeared unto me through the birth of God and the Savior from thee, O Virgin, much-hymned one!

*Kontakion, Tone 6:*

With His life-originating palm, Christ God, the Giver of life, having raised up all the dead from the dark valleys, granted resurrection unto the human race; for He is the Savior of all, the resurrection and life, and God of all!

*Ikos:*

Thy Cross and burial, O Giver of life, we the faithful hymn and venerate, for Thou hast bound Hades, O Immortal One, as almighty God, and hast raised the dead with Thyself, and shattered the gates of death, and cast down the dominion of Hades as God! Therefore we earth-born doxologize Thee with love, Who art risen, and hast cast down the all-destructive power of the enemy, and raised up all who believe in Thee, and delivered the world from the serpent's arrows and from the enemy's deception, as Thou art God of all!

[f. 101A] *Ode 7. Heirmos:* [^119]

The Angel made the furnace bedewing for the holy children, while the command of God consumed the Chaldeans, persuading the tyrant to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Lamenting at Thy Passion, the sun clothed itself in gloom, and by day throughout all the earth the light was darkened, O Master, singing unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

The nethermost regions clothed themselves in light at Thy descent, O Christ; and the Forefather appeared full of gladness, dancing and leaping for joy, crying: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Hymning the Trinity ruling over all, the three-hypostatic Nature, the boundless goodness, and the abyss of light, let us the faithful cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: Ineffable wonder...*

*Troparion:*

The lawless children of the impious crucified Thee, the Savior, at the Place of the Skull; Who didst shatter the brazen gates and bars for our salvation, as we sing: Blessed art Thou, O God our Redeemer!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst destroy the dominion of death and didst arise, granting resurrection unto those who truly glorify Thee as Lord, and in orthodox faith cry unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, O God our Redeemer! *(Twice)*

[f. 101A] *Theotokion:*

Thou didst give birth to One of the Trinity, O all-pure one, Who appeared upon earth in two natures out of mercy; Whom pray unceasingly to save our souls!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The furnace did not burn the three youths...*

*Troparion:*

The furnace did not burn the three youths, prefiguring thy childbirth; for the divine fire did not consume thee, but took up its abode in thee, and taught all to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

The ends of the earth bless thee, O all-venerable Mother, as thou didst foretell; illumined by the radiant rays of thy light, and singing in grace, they cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

The all-evil serpent plunged his destructive fangs into me; but these Thy Son shattered, O Mother of God, granting us strength to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

Thou alone becamest the propitiation of our nature, O divinely-blessed one; for carrying in thine arms God Who sits upon the Cherubic shoulders, thou criest aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

From the flame Thou didst pour forth dew for the holy ones, and didst consume the sacrifice of the righteous one with water; for Thou doest all things, O Christ, whatsoever Thou willest, and we supremely exalt Thee unto all ages!

[f. 102A] *Troparion:* [^120]

The Jewish people, who of old were murderers of the Prophets, envy now made murderers of God, lifting Thee upon the Cross, O Word of God; Whom we supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Without leaving the vault of heaven, and descending into Hades, Thou didst raise up entirely the human being lying in the dungheap, O Christ, who supremely exalts Thee unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

The one Nature of the Trinity, of unreckonable power, surpassing every word, nature, and thought, we ever preach in orthodox manner and supremely exalt unto the ages!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: Be astonished, O heaven...*

*Troparion:*

Every ear was astonished at how the Most High willingly descended unto the earth, shattered the power of Hades by His Crucifixion and burial, and raised all to cry: Bless Him, ye children; praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye peoples, unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

O Thine ineffable love for mankind, O Christ, and unspeakable goodness! For seeing me perishing in the prison of Hades, having endured the Passion Thou didst snatch me away; therefore we bless Thee, the Master of all, and supremely exalt Thee unto the ages!

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, Gate of God! Rejoice, rampart of the faithful [f. 102A] Rejoice, Unwedded Mary! Rejoice, all-pure Mother of God! Rejoice, thou who alone didst bear God for the earth-born, unto Whom we cry: Bless Him, ye children; praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye peoples, unto all ages!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: In a vesture of gold...*

*Troparion:*

Having adorned thee with a vesture of gold like a Queen, illumining thee with the ray of the Spirit, Thy Son set thee at His right hand, O all-pure one; Whom supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

He Who established the world by His sole will borrowed flesh from thy most pure womb, desiring to fashion it again from above; Whom supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

By the union of the Word with me, man, thou becamest a divine dwelling, O all-pure one, shining brightly with the splendor of virginity; therefore we hymn thee unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

The golden candlestick prefigured thee, who ineffably received the unapproachable Light that enlightens all things with its understanding; therefore we hymn thee, O pure one, unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

It is not possible for man to see God, upon Whom the ranks of Angels dare not gaze; but through thee, O all-pure one, the Word was manifested unto us incarnate, Whom magnifying, together with the heavenly hosts we call thee blessed!

[f. 103A] *Troparion:* [^121]

Thou didst remain unpartaker of passions, O Word of God, though sharing in sufferings in the flesh; but Thou loosest man from passions, having become subject to passions through Thy Passion, O our Savior, for Thou alone art passionless and almighty!

*Troparion:*

Having accepted mortal corruption, Thou didst deliver our nature from corruption, Thy body remaining untasting thereof; and Thy life-creating and divine soul, O Master, was not abandoned in Hades, but rising as from sleep, Thou didst raise us up with Thyself!

*Troparion:*

God the Father, and the co-unoriginate Son, all of us earth-born glorify with pure lips; and honoring the ineffable and all-glorious power of the All-holy Spirit, we confess the one almighty, indivisible Trinity!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: Weep not for Me, O Mother...*

*Troparion:*

Though Thou didst descend into the tomb as dead, O Giver of life, yet Thou didst tear asunder the power of Hades, O Christ, and didst raise with Thyself the dead whom it had formerly swallowed; and as God Thou didst grant resurrection unto all who magnify Thee in faith and love!

*Troparion:*

Let creation rejoice, and let all the earth-born be glad; for Hades, the enemy, has been despoiled! Christ is risen, and the first-formed arose with Him in glory, being delivered from corruption; unto the Myrrh-bearing women [f. 103A] the Angel cries: Let creation rejoice, and let it blossom like a lily! For Christ is risen from the dead as God; where is thy victory, O Hades? He has cast thee down to the earth, having exalted our horn, as He is compassionate!

*Theotokion:*

Creation rejoices with thy childbirth, O Virgin; for Bethlehem opened Eden unto us, and enjoying the Tree of Life in faith, we cry fervently: Fulfill our petitions, O Lady!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: It is not possible for man to see God...*

*Troparion:*

A Star shining with the rays of the Godhead from Jacob shone forth unto those held by darkness; for through thee, O all-holy one, Christ God the Word was incarnate, by Whom being enlightened, with the heavenly hosts we call thee blessed!

*Troparion:*

Strengthened by thy power and grace, I have fervently offered a hymn from the heart unto thee; do thou receive it, O pure Virgin, granting in return thy radiant grace from incorruptible treasures, O divinely-blessed one!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst appear openly as a divinely-woven loom, whereon the Word wove the vesture of the flesh, deifying my form, O Virgin; and clothed therein, He saved all who with a pure mind magnify thee!

[f. 104A] *Theotokion:* [^122]

Resurrection has now been granted unto the dead by thine ineffable and unspeakable childbirth, O all-venerable Mother of God; for Life, clothed in the flesh from thee, has shone forth unto all, and openly destroyed the infirmity of death!

*On Sunday at the Praises, Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron:*

Thy Cross, O Lord, is life and resurrection unto Thy people; and hoping thereon, we sing unto Thee, our risen God: Have mercy upon us!

*Sticheron:*

Thy burial, O Master, opened Paradise unto mankind, and being delivered from corruption, we sing unto Thee, our risen God: Have mercy upon us!

*Sticheron:*

With the Father and the Spirit let us hymn Christ Who is risen from the dead, and cry unto Him: Thou art our life and resurrection, have mercy upon us!

*Sticheron:*

On the third day Thou didst arise from the tomb, O Christ, as it is written, and didst raise our Forefather with Thyself; therefore the human race glorifies Thee, and hymns Thy Resurrection!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian, Same Tone:*

*Sticheron:*

O Lord, great and fearful is the mystery of Thy Resurrection; for in such manner didst Thou come forth from the tomb [f. 104A] like a Bridegroom from His chamber, having shattered death by death that Thou mightest set Adam free; therefore in heaven the Angels dance, and on earth mankind glorifies Thy tender mercy shown unto us, O Lover of mankind!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

O lawless Judeans, where are the seals and the silver coins that ye gave unto the soldiers? The treasure was not stolen, but is risen as mighty; ye yourselves were put to shame, having rejected Christ, the Lord of glory, Who suffered and was buried, and is risen from the dead: worship Him!

*Sticheron:*

Though the tomb was sealed, how were ye robbed, O Judeans, having set a guard and placed signs? While the doors were shut, the King came forth: either produce Him as dead, or worship Him as God, singing with us: Glory to Thy Cross, O Lord, and to Thy Resurrection!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

Reaching Thy life-receiving tomb early in the morning with weeping, O Lord, and bearing sweet spices, the Myrrh-bearing women sought to anoint Thy most pure body; but they found a radiant Angel sitting upon the stone, and speaking unto them [^123] [f. 105A] saying: Why weep ye for Him Who poured forth life unto the world from His side? Why seek ye the Immortal One in the tomb as dead? Run rather, announce unto His disciples His glorious Resurrection and worldwide joy; through which do Thou Who hast enlightened us also grant cleansing and great mercy!

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 6)

*At the Divine Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 6:*

*Troparion:*

Remember me, O God my Savior, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom, and save me as the only Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Adam was deceived by a tree, but by the Tree of the Cross Thou didst again save the Thief who cried: Remember me, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

Let us hymn Christ our God, Who was willingly crucified and buried, and arose in glory on the third day; Who shattered the gates and bars of Hades, O Giver of life, and raised up all who were in Hades, crying: O compassionate Savior, glory to Thy rising!

*Troparion:*

[He Who arose from the tomb on the third day, having foretold His rising] before the Passion, [...] let us all harmoniously sing praise [unto Him]!

[f. 105A] *Troparion:*

Having appeared as the light of the world, the Apostles drove away all the darkness of deception by the divine rays of their teachings!

*Glory:*

We doxologize the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, saying: O Holy Trinity, save our souls!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Rejoice, gate opened unto God, Who ineffably took up His abode in thee and came forth from thee, O Ever-Virgin!

*End of the Sixth Tone.*
