# TONE 3 (fol. 37)[^55]

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 3)

*On Saturday Evening at Great Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 3:*

By Thy Cross, O Christ Savior, the dominion of death was overthrown and the deception of the devil was brought to naught; and the human race, being saved by faith, ever brings a hymn unto Thee.

*Sticheron:*

All things were enlightened by Thy Resurrection, O Lord, and Paradise was opened once again; and all creation, praising Thee, ever brings a song unto Thee.

*Sticheron:*

I glorify the power of the Father and of the Son, and I hymn the sovereignty of the Holy Spirit: undivided, uncreated Godhead, Consubstantial Trinity, reigning unto ages of ages.

*Sticheron:*

We worship Thy precious Cross, O Christ, and we hymn and glorify Thy Resurrection; for by Thy wound we all were healed.

[Saturday.]

*Sticheron:*

We hymn the Savior Who was incarnate of the Virgin; for our sake He was crucified, and on the third day He arose, granting us great mercy.

*Sticheron:*

Having descended unto those in Hades, Christ proclaimed glad tidings, saying: Take courage; now have I conquered! I am the Resurrection; I lead you forth, having shattered the gates of death.

*Sticheron:*

Standing unworthily in Thy most honorable house, we send up an evening hymn, crying out from the depth, O Christ God, Who hast enlightened the world by Thy three-day Resurrection: snatch Thy people from the hand of the enemies, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

How should we not marvel at thy theandric childbearing, O Most Pure Lady? For without having accepted the trial of man, O All-Blameless One, thou didst give birth in the flesh without a father unto the Son Who was born without a mother of the Father before the ages, in no way undergoing change, or confusion, or division, but preserving intact the property of each nature. Therefore, O Virgin Mother, Sovereign Lady, entreat Him that our souls may be saved, who in orthodox manner confess thee to be the Mother of God.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 3:* (fol. 38)[^56]

O Christ, Who by Thy Passion didst darken the sun, and by the light of Thy Resurrection didst enlighten all things: accept our evening hymn, O Lover of mankind!

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

Thy life-giving arising, O Lord, enlightened the whole universe, and summoned His corrupted creation. Therefore, being delivered from the curse of Adam, we all cry aloud: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

Being God unchangeable, suffering in the flesh Thou wast changed; Whom creation, not enduring to behold hanging, was bowed down with fear, and groaning, it hymned Thy long-suffering. Having descended into Hades, Thou didst arise on the third day, granting life unto the world and great mercy.

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

That Thou mightest deliver our race from death, O Christ, Thou didst endure death; and having arisen on the third day from the dead, Thou didst raise up with Thyself those who acknowledge Thee as God; and Thou didst enlighten the world: Lord, glory to Thee!

[Saturday, Tone 3.]

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

Without seed from the Divine Spirit, and by the Father's good pleasure, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who was of the Father without a mother before the ages; for our sake and of thee, having come to be without a father, thou didst bear Him in the flesh, and thou didst nourish the Infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray that our souls may be delivered from tribulations.

*Now Thou dismissest. Holy God. After Our Father:*

*Troparion, Tone 3:*

Let the heavens be glad, and let the earthly rejoice; for the Lord hath wrought might with His arm: He trampled down death by death, He became the Firstborn of the dead, He delivered us from the belly of Hades, and granted great mercy unto the world!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, we hymn, O Virgin Mother of God; for having accepted the Passion of the Cross in the flesh taken from thee, Thy Son and our God delivered us from corruption, as Lover of mankind.

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 3)

*At the Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy Trinity. Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos: He Who of old by divine beckoning...* (fol. 39)[^57]

*Troparion:*

Incomprehensible, Sole Sovereign, and One Tri-hypostatic Godhead: deem me worthy of Thy Tri-radiant Light, that I may hymn Thee Who art unceasingly sung with thrice-holy hymns by angelic mouths!

*Troparion:*

All the immaterial ranks sacredly sing of Thee as the sole Tri-radiant Creative Cause, the Supreme Light; Which human multitudes also harmoniously sing of, and faithfully glorify with earthen mouths.

*Glory:*

The theologians fittingly and compositely call Thee Father, and Word, and Spirit: designating the Son impassibly from the Unbegotten Father, and together the procession of the Divine Spirit, O God, Sole Sovereign over all!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

As Lover of mankind by nature, taking human substance...

[Sunday.]

...having assumed human nature, O Divine Word, Thou didst shine forth as the Tri-hypostatic, Supreme-Sovereign Light, having shown forth the undivided Godhead, exceedingly glorified unto all, through the Pure Virgin who bore Thee.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: He Who brought all things out of nothing...*

Bidding Elijah of old to arrange the water upon the logs, He figuratively revealed unto all the Tri-hypostatic constitution of the Sole Godhead's Dominion.

*Troparion:*

The corruptible nature of earth-born mortals hymns Thee, the One and Tri-radiant, unchangeable Creator; and I cry unto Thee, O Master: Deliver me from every sudden destruction, and save me!

*Glory:*

Clearly proclaiming through prophetic words, and through the all-wise Apostles, preachers of the faith: we, the faithful, glorify Thee, the Equally-Effective Trinity, O God of all!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

From the high Throne the Word came down to make man higher, as Lover of mankind, through thee, O Most Pure Lady; and the Tri-solar Light shone forth upon all.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:*

O Undivided and Inseparable Trinity, have mercy upon us who worship Thee, and who piously hymn and unceasingly call upon Thee: save those whom Thou hast gathered together in Thy holy Church because of mercy, O Mother, to glorify Thy dominion; for as Thou art merciful, Thou knowest the weakness of our heart (fol. 40).[^58]

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

I am completely changed through mine intemperance, and through my fearlessness, foolish, senseless, accursed in lack of feeling: I tremble not before the day at which all things visible and invisible tremble. O Lady, help me and destroy the perplexity of my heart; for thou thyself art the hope and protectress unto thy servants!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Thou hast set firm love toward us: the...*

*Troparion:*

A two-fold branch sprang forth from the Father as from a root: the upright Son and the Spirit, consubstantial offshoots, divinely planted and co-unoriginate flowers, so that the Godhead is of Three Lights.

*Troparion:*

The assemblies of the noetic beings unceasingly hymn Thee, the Inscrutable God; with whom we also glorify Thee, saying: O Supremely Essential Trinity, save...

[Sunday.]

...save Thy servants, as Lover of mankind!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Thou didst inflame us with Thy love, O Abundantly Merciful Word of God, Who for our sake wast incarnate without change, and mystically didst declare the Tri-radiant One Godhead; therefore we glorify Thee!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: As Isaiah figuratively beheld upon a throne...*

*Troparion:*

As Isaiah figuratively beheld God, the One Sovereign, glorified in Three Persons with most honorable voices, a Seraph was straightway sent to proclaim the Tri-radiant Essence and the Tri-solar Monad.

*Glory:*

O Tri-solar Monad, Who didst establish the nature of all things invisible and visible out of non-being before: deliver from all temptations those who faithfully hymn Thee as the One God, and deem them worthy of Thy glory!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Having become the light-bearing and honorable bridal-chamber unto God, O Virgin, we hymn thee with love and bless thee; for of thee Christ was born in two natures, with dual wills, Who is One of the Trinity and the Lord existing in glory!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: The uttermost abyss of sins hath encompassed me...* (fol. 41)[^59]

*Troparion:*

Being a sojourner, Abraham was deemed worthy to receive figuratively, in the likeness of the Lord, the Supremely Essential One in Three Hypostases, under the forms of men.

*Glory:*

Direct the hearts of Thy servants unto the Unapproachable Light, O Tri-solar Lord, and grant the radiance of Thy glory unto our souls, that we may be illumined by Thine ineffable beauty!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Open unto me the gates of the Light Who was born of thy womb, O Most Pure Lady, that I may behold the Tri-radiant beam of the Godhead, and glorify thee, the All-Radiant Lady!

*Kontakion, Tone 3:*

When Thou comest, O Master, upon the earth to judge all whom Thou didst fashion from the earth according to Thine image: have compassion as Merciful One, and deliver all from Thy righteous judgment, and send us not into the fire of Gehenna, as Thou alone art Christ, the Abundantly Merciful!

*Ikos:*

Groaning...

[Sunday.]

...Grant me groaning, O Virgin, even as unto the Publican, and tears like the harlot, that offering these unto thee I may cry aloud: Have mercy on me, O All-Merciful Lady, and destroy my perplexity by thy grace, pouring down upon me a rain of compunction; for unto thee have I fled, O Most Pure Lady!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: As of old Thou didst bedew the three...*

*Troparion:*

Show me forth as a temple of Thy Tri-radiant, all-luminous Godhead, O Master, higher than grievous sinful darkness and passions! By Thy light-giving radiances: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

We proclaim one appearance of Thy Godhead in Three Hypostatic and distinct properties: Father, Son, and Spirit, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Glory:*

God appeared unto Abraham in three hypostases of old by the Oak of Mamre, granting Isaac as reward for hospitality through mercy; Whom we also now glorify as the God of our fathers.

*Both now. Theotokion:*

The Creator appeared on earth, having become man in God-befitting manner from thy virginal and most pure womb, and He renewed us: O blessed, most pure Mother of God, All-Pure Lady! (fol. 42)[^60]

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Gathered together in the unenduring fire, yet unharmed...*

*Troparion:*

From the Unoriginate Father, as from a root, the Word and the Spirit, co-unoriginate existing by nature, as offshoots of the Supremely Essential Godhead, showed forth the one glory and power of the Trinity; Which all of us, the faithful, hymn unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

By Thy splendors Thou directest the heavenly ranks to hymn unceasingly with thrice-holy divine songs: O Father, Word of the same form, and Spirit, the Tri-radiant and equally-potent dominion; therefore we hymn Thee unto the ages.

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Beholding thy childbearing from afar, the prophetic utterances praised it, O Mother of God, as having been born without seed and beyond nature, O Lady; and harmoniously we hymn Him as King, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A new wonder and God-befitting: for through the...*

*Troparion:*

Manifestly desiring to behold the Tri-radiant glory, the God-beholding ranks of the bodiless hosts with immaterial wings...

[Sunday.]

...desire to ascend on high; but they veil themselves exceedingly with the unapproachable Light, and unceasingly cry out a hymn; with them harmoniously, O Sole Trinity, we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:*

With insatiable love, we who are upon earth, having received from Thee a noetic and rational soul, hymn Thee as God the Almighty, the Sole Essence in truth, and Tri-hypostatic Persons from the whole heart; therefore, O Compassionate One, as Abundantly Merciful, have compassion upon us!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

Show me forth as a light-bearing temple of Thy Sole Sovereign and Tri-radiant Godhead: cleanly to serve Thee, the Creator of all, and noetically to behold Thine ineffable glory, through the prayers of the only Mother of God, whom worthily, as exceedingly glorious, we magnify!

*After this we sing: It is truly meet. [End of Midnight Office]*

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 3)

*On Sunday at Matins, after "God is the Lord": on 8: Tone 3. Troparion: Let the heavens be glad... (see above). Glory, both now. Theotokion.*

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:* (fol. 43)[^61]

Christ is risen from the dead, the First-fruits of those who have fallen asleep: the Firstborn of creation and the Maker of all things that have come to be; and He renewed in Himself the corrupted nature of our race. No longer dost thou have dominion, O death; for the Master of all hath overthrown thy power!

*Glory:*

Adoring the unchangeable Godhead and Thy voluntary Passion, O Lord, Hades, terrified within himself, lamented, saying: I tremble at the flesh of incorruptible structure; I behold the Invisible One mystically warring against me! Therefore those whom I hold also cry aloud: Glory, O Christ, to Thy Resurrection!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

The heavenly beings rejoiced with love, and the earthly were terrified with trembling, when the most pure voice came upon thee, O Mother of God; for one triumph shone forth upon both when the Bodiless One brought joy unto thee. Therefore with the Angel we cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Pure Lady, Dwelling-place of God!

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:*

Having tasted death in the flesh, O Lord, by His arising He cut short the bitterness of death, and strengthened man again, recalling the overcoming of the first curse...

[Sunday.]

...O Defender of our life, Christ God, glory to Thee!

*Glory:*

We, the faithful, praise the incomprehensible Crucifixion and ineffable Resurrection, an unutterable mystery; for today death and Hades were despoiled, and the human race was robed in incorruption. Therefore also thankfully we cry aloud: Glory, O Christ, to Thy Resurrection!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

The Incomprehensible and Uncircumscribed One, consubstantial with the Father and the Spirit, thou didst mystically contain within thy womb, O Mother of God; we have come to know one and unconfused Godhead of the Trinity, to glorify thy childbearing in the world. Therefore also thankfully we cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Full of Grace!

*Hypakoe, Tone 3:*

Astonishing by his appearance, refreshing with words, the shining Angel said unto the myrrh-bearing women: Why seek ye the Living One in the tomb? He is risen, Who emptied the tombs! Understand the unchangeable Changer of corruption; say unto God: How awesome are Thy deeds, for Thou hast saved the human race!

*Antiphon 1, Tone 3:* (fol. 44)[^62]

Thou didst deliver the captivity of Zion from Babylon; draw me also from passions unto life, O Word!

*Verse:*

They that sow in the South with divine tears shall reap sheaves of ever-living joy.

*Glory, both now:*

Unto the Holy Spirit belongs every thanksgiving: even as unto the Father and the Son, He co-shines; in Him all things live and move.

*Antiphon 2:*

Unless the Lord build the house of virtues, in vain do we labor; and if He protect the soul, no one shall overthrow our city.

*Verse:*

Through the Spirit, the saints are ever adopted sons of the fruit of the womb unto Thee, O Christ, even as unto the Father.

*Glory, both now:*

Through the Holy Spirit all holiness and wisdom are foreseen; for He gives essence unto every creature. Let us serve Him, for He is God, even as the Father and the Word!

*Antiphon 3:*

Blessed are they that fear the Lord, walking in the way of the commandments; for they shall eat of all the fruit that gives life.

[Sunday.]

*Verse:*

Be glad around thy table, beholding thy offspring bearing branches of good deeds unto the Chief Shepherd!

*Glory, both now:*

Through the Holy Spirit comes every richness of glory; from Him is grace and life unto every creature; for He is hymned together with the Father and with the Word.

*Prokeimenon, Tone 3:*

Say among the heathen that the Lord reigns; for He hath established the world, which shall not be moved.

*Verse: O sing unto the Lord a new song.*

*Let every breath praise the Lord. Verse: Praise God in His saints.*

*Sunday Matins Gospel. Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ.*

*Canon of the Resurrection. Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos: He Who of old by divine beckoning gathered the waters into one gathering...*

He Who of old by divine beckoning gathered the waters into one gathering, and divided the sea for the people of Israel: this is our God, exceedingly glorified; unto Him alone let us sing, for He hath been glorified!

*Troparion:*

He Who condemned the earth, unto the transgressor afterward to bring forth the fruit of thorns: this is our God, Who received in the flesh a crown of thorns from the hands of the lawless; He dissolved the curse, for He hath been glorified! (fol. 45)[^63]

*Troparion:*

The Victor and Vanquisher of death, having appeared as One Who feared death; for having assumed animate, passible flesh, this is our God; and having wrestled with the tormentor, He resurrected all with Himself, for He hath been glorified!

*Theotokion:*

All nations glorify thee as the true Mother of God, who didst bear without seed; for having descended into thy sanctified womb, this is our God, Who was substantialized for our sake, and was born of thee as God and Man!

*Another Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection. Tone 3. Heirmos: The same:*

Let us, the people, sing a new song unto God Who wrought: the human race, enslaved by the sin-loving tormentor, Christ redeemed with His Divine Blood, and having deified it, He renewed it, for He hath been glorified!

*Troparion:*

Even as mortal, Christ willed to taste of death, He Who is the Treasury of life; and being immortal by nature, He brought the dead to life, for He hath been glorified!

*Another Canon, to the Most Holy Theotokos. Heirmos: [The same]*

[Sunday.]

*Theotokion:*

Every nature of heavenly beings bends the knee unto Him Who was incarnate of thee, O Virgin, and together with earthly beings, fittingly those under the earth, for He hath been glorified!

*Theotokion:*

At the reconciliation that was toward thee: for as God, the begrudgeless Giver of good things, having given the Divine Spirit unto us, He assumed flesh of thee, O Maiden, for He hath been glorified!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: He Who brought all things out of nothing...*

He Who brought all things out of non-being by the Word, which are fashioned and perfected by the Spirit: O Almighty Master, establish me in Thy love!

*Troparion:*

By Thy Cross the ungodly one was put to shame; for having made a pit which he dug, he fell into it; but in Thine arising, O Christ, Thou didst raise the horn of the humble.

*Troparion:*

The proclamation of godliness unto the nations covered the sea like water, O Lover of mankind; for having arisen from the tomb, Thou didst reveal the Tri-hypostatic Light!

*Theotokion:*

Glorious things were spoken of thee, O animate City of Him Who reigns ever; for through thee, O Lady, God dwelt with those on earth!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]:* (fol. 46)[^64]

...Thou didst crush the [strength] of the enemies; the shield Thou didst wipe away by Thy might, O Master Christ, our confirmation: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Thou, O Most Pure Cross, hast been shown forth as purity against idolatrous impurities; for Jesus, the Supremely Divine, stretched forth His hands upon thee!

*Troparion:*

That all of us, the faithful, may worship Thee, O Life-Giving Tomb; for Christ, truly our God, was buried in Thee and arose!

*Another Canon, to the Most Holy Theotokos. Heirmos:*

A Rod from the root of Jesse, sprouting forth prophetically, O Virgin: Christ blossomed forth as a flower for us from thee: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

That Thou mightest make earth-born mortals partakers of the Divine Nature: Thou, having become poor, didst assume our flesh from the Virgin: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Thou hast set firm love toward us: the...*

Thou hast placed steadfast love toward us, O Lord; for for our sake Thou didst give Thine Only-Begotten Son unto death; therefore we cry unto Thee thankfully: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst mercifully accept wounds and stripes, O Christ, enduring the outrage of being struck on the cheeks, and patiently bearing spitting, through which...

[Sunday.]

...whereby Thou didst effect salvation for me: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Having a mortal body, Thou, the Life, didst partake of death: for the sake of the passion of the poor and the groaning of the needy; and having corrupted the corruptor, O Supremely Glorified One, Thou didst raise up all with Thyself, for Thou art glorified!

*Troparion:*

Remember, O Christ, the flock which Thou didst acquire by Thy Passion: accepting the prayerful entreaties of Thy supremely glorified Mother; and having visited the afflicted, deliver it by Thy power, O Lord!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Strange and ineffable is Thy birth...*

*Troparion:*

O Lover of mankind, Who didst fashion man according to Thine own image: when he was put to death on account of the sin of transgression, being crucified upon the Place of the Skull, Thou didst save him.

*Troparion:*

The dead whom death had devoured it surrendered, and Thou didst destroy the corrupting kingdom of Hades when Thou didst arise from the tomb, O Lord.

*Theotokion:*

O Pure Mary, Golden Censer: without composition, as One of the Trinity having descended into thee, God the Word, having become incarnate, filled the world with sweet fragrance.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]:*

O Master Who didst set the mountains by the measure of divine wisdom: Thou, the Stone cut without hands from the Virgin, [didst appear]: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:* (fol. 47)[^65]

Thou didst heal our diseased nature, O Master, having joined whole that which was grieving [taken] from the Virgin unto Thy precious Divinity, O Word.

*Troparion:*

Thou art my portion, O Lord, and desired inheritance, O Word, Who hast joined me from the Virgin unto Thine own Hypostasis, having become substantial in the flesh.

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Unto Thee do I rise early in the...*

Unto Thee I rise early in the morning, O Creator of all, which surpasses every mind of the world; for Thy commandments are light: guide me therein.

*Troparion:*

The All-Seeing One and He Who judges all the earth righteously, being delivered unto an unrighteous judge by Hebrew envy, Thou didst deliver ancient Adam from condemnation.

*Troparion:*

Grant Thy peace unto Thy churches, O Christ, by the unconquerable power of Thy Cross, O Thou Who didst arise from the dead, and save our souls.

*Theotokion:*

O Holy Tabernacle and more spacious than the heavens, as having received the everywhere-incomprehensible Creator, the Word of God, thou alone wast shown forth, O Ever-Virgin.

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: He Who was invisible on earth was seen...*

*Troparion:*

Pierced in Thy side by a spear, O my Christ...

[Sunday.]

...having been pierced, Thou didst deliver from the curse her that was created from man's rib, who had become the mediatress of destruction unto all men.

*Troparion:*

Being equal to the Father in essence, O Christ our Savior, Thou didst raise from the dead the sacred temple of Thine immaculate and all-honorable Body.

*Theotokion:*

The Word of God, Thy Son, O Virgin, the Word of God, is the Creator of first-formed Adam, uncreated; even though He created for Himself an animate flesh from thee.

*Theotokion:*

Equal to the Father is Thy Son, O Virgin, the Word of God: a perfect Hypostasis in two natures, Jesus, Lord and God, perfect [God] and man.

*Ode 6. Heirmos: The uttermost abyss of sins hath surrounded me...*

The uttermost abyss of sins hath surrounded me, and my spirit fails; but having stretched forth Thine exalted arm, O Master, save me, O Pilot, as Thou didst Peter.

*Troparion:*

An abyss of mercy and compassions hath surrounded me by Thy compassionate condescension; for having become incarnate, O Master, and having been in the form of a servant, Thou didst deify it, and didst glorify it with Thyself.

*Troparion:*

The slayer suffered slaying when he beheld him that was slain made alive by Thy Resurrection...

*Troparion:* (fol. 48)[^66]

...Resurrection: these, O Christ, are the tokens and the victories of Thine immaculate Passions.

*Theotokion:*

O Most Pure Lady, who alone beyond understanding didst mediate between the Creator and men: pray Thy Son that He may be merciful unto Thy sinful servants, and a champion.

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: The dwelling of Jonah in the nethermost parts of Hades...*

The dwelling of Jonah in the nethermost parts of Hades was a figurative image: bring up my life from corruption, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Having been tried by Thy wounds, wounded by Hades, Thou didst raise him up by the Passion of the Cross; therefore I cry unto Thee: Bring up my life from corruption, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

The gates of Hades open unto Thee in fear, O Christ, and the vessels of the enemy are plundered; therefore the women met Thee, receiving joy instead of sorrow.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]:*

He Who is inseparable in form is conformed unto that which is ours from the incorrupt Virgin; having become [man] in form and in matter, He was not changed in His Divinity, [becoming] man.

*Theotokion:*

From the abysses of sins and the tempest of passions, O Most Pure Lady, deliver me; for thou art a harbor and an abyss of wonders unto those who hasten unto thee with faith.

*Kontakion, Tone 3:*

[Sunday.]

*Kontakion, Tone 3:*

Thou didst arise today from the tomb, O Merciful One, and didst lead us forth from the gates of death; today Adam dances and Eve rejoices, and together the Prophets with the Patriarchs unceasingly hymn the divine dominion of Thy might.

*Ikos:*

Let heaven and earth dance today, and let them hymn Christ God with one mind, for He hath raised the prisoners from the tombs; all creation rejoices together, offering worthy hymns unto the Creator of all and our Redeemer, for having today led men forth from Hades as the Giver of life, He carried them up to heaven with Himself, and cast down the haughty liftings of the enemy, and smashes the gates of Hades by the divine dominion of His power.

*Ode 7. Heirmos: As of old Thou didst bedew the three...*

As of old Thou didst refresh the three pious youths in the Chaldean flame, illuminating them with the bright fire of the Godhead, as they cried: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

The bright temple veil was torn at the crucifixion of the Creator, revealing unto the faithful the truth hidden in the Scripture, as they cry: Blessed art Thou...

*Troparion:* (fol. 49)[^67]

...crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

When Thy side was pierced, O Christ, a drop of the God-flowing, life-creating Blood, dripping down upon the earth by Thy dispensation, renewed those who are from the earth, as they cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Let us glorify the Good Spirit together with the Father, and with the Only-Begotten Son, we the faithful honoring one Principle and one Godhead in Three [Persons], crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: The proud tormentor became a plaything unto the youths...*

The proud tormentor became a plaything unto the youths; for trampling upon the sevenfold flame like dust, they sang: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Seeing not simply a man hanging upon the Cross, but God incarnate, the sun was darkened; unto Whom we sing: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Having received Him Who is mighty in Godhead, the Giver of incorruption, fearful Hades vomited forth the souls of the righteous, who cry: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast shown forth as a priceless treasure of blessing, O Most Pure Lady, unto those who with pure heart confess thee to be the Mother of God; for of thee was incarnate the God of our fathers.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]:*

[Sunday.]

...The Lord of glory, Who holds dominion over the heavenly hosts, Who sits with the Father, is carried by virginal hands: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Death reigned; but partaking of it from the Virgin, Thou didst destroy it, having become God-composed flesh: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

We all have acknowledged thee who didst bear God to be the Mother of God; for thou didst bear of thee One of the Trinity Who was incarnate: Blessed, O Most Pure Lady, is the fruit of thy womb!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Gathered together in the unenduring fire, yet unharmed...*

United in the unendurable fire, the youths who stood for piety, unharmed by the flame, sang the divine hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him supremely unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

The bright splendor of the temple was rent when Thy Cross was planted on the Place of the Skull, and creation bowed down in fear, singing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him supremely unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst arise from the tomb, O Christ, and by divine power Thou didst set upright him who had fallen by the deception of the tree, as they cry and say: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him supremely unto all ages!

*Theotokion:* (fol. 50)[^68]

Thou wast revealed as the Temple of God, an animate dwelling and Ark; for, O Mother of God, Most Pure Lady, thou didst reconcile the Creator unto men; and worthily, all ye works, we hymn and exalt thee supremely unto all ages!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: The God-bearing youths extinguished the material fire's flame...*

The God-bearing youths extinguished the material fire's flame in immaterial manner, and sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him supremely unto ages!

*Troparion:*

The impassible Word, impassible indeed in Godhead, yet God suffers in the flesh; unto Whom also we sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, sing and exalt Him supremely unto ages!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst fall asleep as mortal, and didst arise as immortal, O Savior, and Thou dost save from death those who sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, sing and exalt Him supremely unto ages!

*Troparion:*

Let us piously serve the Tri-hypostatic Godhead, united ineffably, and let us sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, sing and exalt Him supremely unto ages!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]:*

As Mother, thou didst surpass the noetic ranks...

[Sunday.]

...and having come near unto God: We bless thy childbearing, O Blessed Virgin, and exalt [thee] supremely unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst show forth natural beauty most excellent, flesh radiant with Godhead: We bless thy childbearing, O Blessed Virgin, and exalt [thee] supremely unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A new wonder and God-befitting: for through the...*

A new and divinely fitting wonder: the virginal door being shut, the Lord manifestly passes through; naked at His entering, and God appeared bearing flesh at His coming forth, and the door remains shut: her unceasingly as Mother of God we magnify.

*Troparion:*

Awesome it is to behold Thee, the Creator, lifted up upon the Tree, O Word of God: God suffering in the flesh for His servants, and lying breathless in the tomb, yet releasing the dead from Hades; therefore, O Christ, as Almighty we magnify Thee.

*Troparion:*

From mortal corruption Thou didst save the Forefather, O Christ, being laid dead in the tomb; and Thou didst cause life to blossom forth, having raised the dead; by Thy hand Thou didst lead corrupt nature unto the light, clothing it in divine incorruption; therefore Thee, the Light of the Ever-Living Spring, we magnify.

*Theotokion:* (fol. 51)[^69]

A receptacle of Light did He show thee forth, O Pure One: God, Who was ineffably and unchangeably incarnate of thee, having lifted up Adam's essence upon the Cross, having become the Firstborn from the dead; and He abides in the highest, together glorified with the Father and the Divine Spirit.

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Wounded by the sweet arrow of thy most pure childbearing, O Pure One...*

Wounded by the sweet arrow of thy most pure childbearing, O Pure One, and marveling at thy most amiable beauty, with angelic hymns worthily as Mother of God we magnify thee.

*Troparion:*

Honor unto men, [delivering] all from ignominious death, Thou didst pour forth, having tasted it by Thy crucifixion, O Savior; unto me, mortal by nature, as God Thou didst grant incorruption, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst save me, having arisen from the tomb, O Christ, and from torment, and didst lead me unto Thy Father and Begetter, and didst seat me at His right hand: O tender compassion of Thy mercy, O Lord!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos], the same:*

Full satiation of thy praises, O Virgin, can never be for the pious faithful; for receiving desire upon desire, ever divine and spiritual, as Mother of God we magnify thee.

[Sunday.]

*Troparion:*

Thou hast given us an unashamed intercessor, her who bore Thee, O Christ; by her entreaties, being merciful, Thou dost give us the Spirit, the Giver of grace, proceeding from the Father through Thee.

*Resurrectional Exapostilarion.*

*At the Praises, Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 3:*

Come, all ye nations, understand the power of the awesome mystery: for Christ our Savior, the Word Who was in the beginning, was crucified for our sake, and willingly was buried, and arose from the dead to save all things: let us worship Him!

*Sticheron:*

Thy guards recounted all the wonders, O Lord; but the council of vanity filled their right hands with a bribe, thinking to conceal Thy Resurrection, which the world glorifies: Have mercy on us!

*Sticheron:*

All things were filled with joy, having received the proof of the Resurrection; for Mary Magdalene came unto the tomb, and found an Angel sitting upon the stone, shining in his raiment and saying: Why seek ye the Living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen, as He said, going before you into Galilee!

*Sticheron:* (fol. 52)[^70]

In Thy light, O Master, we shall see light, O Lover of mankind; for Thou didst arise from the dead, granting salvation unto the human race, that all creation may hymn Thee, the only Sinless One: Have mercy on us!

*Eastern Stichera, Tone 3:*

*Sticheron:*

The Myrrh-bearing women brought a morning hymn with tears, O Lord; for having sweet-smelling fragrances, they reached Thy tomb, striving to anoint Thine immaculate Body; but the Angel sitting upon the stone brought them good tidings: Why seek ye the Living among the dead? For having despoiled death, He arose as God, granting great mercy unto all!

*Sticheron:*

An Angel shining upon Thy life-giving tomb said unto the Myrrh-bearing women: The Redeemer has emptied the tombs, has overthrown Hades, and arose on the third day, as the only God and Almighty!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

*Sticheron:*

Coming unto the tomb to seek Thee on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene, not having found [Thee]...

[Sunday.]

...grieving with weeping, cried aloud: Woe is me, O my Savior! He has been stolen away, O King of all! And a pair of life-bearing Angels within the tomb cried aloud: Why weepest thou, O woman? I weep, she said, because they have taken away my Lord from the tomb, and I know not where they have laid Him. And turning back, when she beheld Thee, she immediately cried out: My Lord and my God, glory to Thee!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron:*

The Jews shut up Life in the tomb, but the thief opened Paradise with his tongue, crying out and saying: He Who is crucified with me and for my sake hung with me upon the Tree, and is revealed sitting upon the Throne with the Father: He is Christ our Savior, having great mercy!

*Glory: Evangelical Morning Sticheron. Both now. Theotokion: Most blessed art thou...*

*At the Liturgy, Beatitudes, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:*

Adam our forefather, who transgressed Thy commandment, O Christ, Thou didst cast out of Paradise; but the thief, O Merciful One, who confessed Thee upon the Cross, Thou didst settle therein, as he cried: Remember me, O Savior, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:* (fol. 53)[^71]

...mercifully, who confessed Thee upon the Cross, Thou didst settle therein, as he cried: Remember me, O Savior, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

When Thou wast crucified, O Christ, all creation was changed, seeing its Master in human nature; therefore the thief cried out unto Thee: Remember me when Thou comest in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

When we had sinned, Thou didst condemn us with the curse of death, O Giver of life and Lord; yet in Thy Body, O Sinless Master, having suffered, Thou didst make the dead man alive, as he cries: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

Having arisen from the dead, Thou didst raise us from passions by Thy Resurrection, O Lord, and Thou didst destroy all the power of death, O Savior; therefore with faith we cry unto Thee: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

Unto the Myrrh-bearing women Thou didst first appear, having arisen from the dead, O Savior, crying aloud: Rejoice! and through them [didst bid them] announce Thine arising unto Thy friends, O Christ; therefore: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

On the mountain Moses, having stretched out his hands, prefigured the Cross, overcoming Amalek; [do Thou, O Christ Savior, raise us up who have been cast down by his snares, as]...

[Sunday.]

...we faithfully cry: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*Glory. Troas [Trinity Hymn]:*

Unto the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit let us, the faithful, bow down: the uncreated Trinity, in three Persons and in one Godhead, Who is ever faithfully glorified by the Bodiless Hosts in thrice-holy sanctifications.

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Rejoice, O Door of God, through which the Creator passed, having become incarnate, preserving thee sealed! Rejoice, O Light Cloud, carrying Christ, the Divine Rain! Rejoice, O Ladder and Heavenly Throne! Rejoice, O Mountain of God, honorable, fertile, and unquarried!
