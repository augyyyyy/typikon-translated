## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 1)

*The Beginning of Tone 1, or the Octoechos.*

*On Saturday evening at "Lord, I have cried," we set the verses...*

*Sticheron:*

Receive our evening prayers, O Holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins; for Thou alone art He Who showed forth the Resurrection unto the world.

*Sticheron:*

Encompass Zion, O ye people, and surround her, and give glory within her unto Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God...

...Who delivered us from our iniquities.

*Sticheron:*

Come, ye people, let us hymn and worship Christ, glorifying His Resurrection from the dead; for He is our God, Who delivered the world from the deceit of the enemy.

*Other Stichera, Eastern, Tone 1:*

*Sticheron:*

Be glad, O ye heavens; sound the trumpet, ye foundations of the earth; cry aloud, ye mountains, with gladness! For behold, Emmanuel nailed our sins to the Cross, and giving life, He put death to death, raising Adam, as the Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:*

Him Who of His own will was crucified in the flesh for our sake, Who suffered and was buried, and rose from the dead, let us hymn, saying: Confirm Thy Church in Orthodoxy, O Christ, and make our life peaceful, as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron (fol. 2):*[^4]

Standing before Thy life-receiving Tomb, we, the unworthy, offer doxology unto Thine unutterable loving-kindness, O Christ our God; for Thou, the Sinless One, didst accept the Cross and death of Thine own will for our sake, and didst rise in glory, that Thou mightest grant resurrection unto the world, as the Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:*

The Word Who is equally without beginning and co-eternal with the Father, Who came forth ineffably from the Virginal womb, and of His own will accepted the Cross and death for our sake, and rose in glory, let us hymn, saying: O Giver of life, Lord, glory to Thee, the Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Now and ever... Dogmatic Theotokion (Incipit):*

The glory of the whole world, who blossomed forth from mankind...

[Saturday.]

...who bore the Master, the Heavenly Gate, let us hymn Mary the Virgin, the song of the Bodiless Powers and the adornment of the faithful! For she was revealed as Heaven and the Temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the barrier of enmity, brought in peace and opened the Kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the confirmation of our faith, we have a Champion, the Lord Who was born of her. Be of good courage, therefore, be of good courage, O people of God; for He will conquer the enemies, as He is almighty.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1:*

*Sticheron:*

By Thy Passion, O Christ, we have been set free from passions; and by Thy Resurrection we have been delivered from corruption. O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

*Sticheron:*

Let creation rejoice, let the heavens be glad, let the nations clap their hands with mutual rejoicing! For Christ our Savior nailed our sins to the Cross, and putting death to death, He bestowed life upon us, raising the fallen Adam with all his race, as the Lover of mankind.

*Verse: For He established the world, which shall not be moved.*

*Sticheron (fol. 3):*[^5]

O Incomprehensible King of Heaven and earth, of Thine own will Thou wast crucified for love of mankind! Hades, encountering Thee below, was embittered, while the souls of the righteous, receiving Thee, rejoiced; and Adam, beholding Thee, O Creator, in the nethermost parts, arose. O the marvel! How did He Who is the Life of all taste of death? But as He willed, He enlightened the world, which cries and says: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

*Sticheron:*

The Myrrh-bearing Women, carrying myrrh with haste and weeping, reached Thy Tomb; and not finding Thine immaculate Body, but learning from the Angel of the new and most glorious miracle, they said unto the Apostles: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:*

Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and after giving birth thou didst remain as before birth. For He Who was born was God, and therefore He transformed nature anew. But, O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple, but as the Mother of the Compassionate One...

[Saturday.]

...bearing Him in thine arms, have compassion on thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*Troparion, Tone 1:*

While the stone was sealed by the Jews and the soldiers were guarding Thine immaculate Body, Thou didst rise on the third day, O Savior, granting life unto the world. Therefore, the Heavenly Powers cried aloud unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O Christ! Glory to Thy Kingdom! Glory to Thy dispensation, O Thou Who alone art the Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*

When Gabriel proclaimed unto thee, O Virgin, "Rejoice!", with the voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, as the righteous David said. Thou wast revealed more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who dwelt within thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who set us free through thy childbearing!

*Rubric: These same Troparia are sung also on Sunday at Matins at "God is the Lord."*

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 1)

*On Sunday at the Midnight Office, the Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity (fol. 4):*[^6]

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Thy victorious right hand in God-befitting manner...[^7]

*Troparion:*

The Seraphim unceasingly glorify the One Tri-Hypostatic Beginning: unoriginate, co-eternal, Creator of all things, incomprehensible, O Good One; and every tongue faithfully honors Thee with hymns.

*Troparion:*

That Thou mightest reveal Thine only, tri-radiant Godhead unto men, having fashioned man before, Thou didst form him according to Thine image, giving him mind, and word, and spirit, as the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Showing forth from on high the single dominion of the Divine Sovereignty in Three Hypostases, Thou, O Father, didst say to Thine equally creative Son and Spirit: Come, let Us go down and confound their tongues.

*Troparion:*

The unbegotten Father is figurative mind, named most wise; while the Word, co-unoriginate, is the consubstantial Son; and the Holy Spirit, Who fashioned the incarnation of the Word...

[Sunday.]

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

O Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human nature...[^8]

*Troparion:*

Of old, visibly appearing unto Abraham as three hypostases, yet one in the nature of the Godhead, Thou didst prefigure the truest theology; and we faithfully hymn Thee, the Single-Origin God, Tri-Radiant.

*Troparion:*

Born of Thee in a God-befitting manner, ineffably, O Father, the Son, Light of Light, shone forth immutably, and the Holy Spirit, Divine Light, proceeded; and we faithfully worship and glorify the Three Hypostases, the splendors of the One Godhead.

*Troparion:*

The Trinity in Unity is praised supernaturally, ineffably, beyond understanding, by the noetic beings, who cry out with unceasing praise in thrice-holy voices, with whom the Tri-Hypostatic Lord is harmoniously hymned by us also.

*Theotokion:*

From thee in time, without seed, came forth the One Who is above time; the Invisible One, having become like unto us, taught us the one nature and Kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Spirit, O Mother of God; therefore, we glorify thee.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 1.*

*Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гробъ твои):* [^9]

O All-Effecting Trinity and All-Good Unity, receive the supplications of those who unceasingly cry with clay lips: Holy, Holy, Holy, sung in the manner of the Seraphim! O Giver of life, save and have compassion on those who cry out in Thy holy Church: Lord, glory to Thee!

*Theotokion:*

Have mercy on us who cry unto thee, O Most Holy, Pure One, who cry night and day: Cleanse our transgressions, for thou hast a Merciful One Who hears thy supplications; encompass us, therefore, with a motherly prayer.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Mount overshadowed by the grace of God...[^10]

*Troparion:*

Shine upon us, O Tri-Radiant Divine Sovereignty, with the splendors of Thy beneficent illuminations into the eyes of our hearts, to envision the beauty of Thy Divine brightness that is beyond the mind, and of Thy light-giving and sweet communion!

*Troparion:*

In the beginning Thou didst establish the heavens, O Lord, and all their host by Thy all-creating Word, and by the Spirit of Thy mouth, Who is consubstantial; with Whom Thou rulest over all things by Thy tri-radiant, single-origin Godhead.

*Troparion:*

Having created us according to Thine image and likeness...

[Sunday.]

...O Sovereign and All-Effecting Trinity, Unconfused Unity: give us understanding and enlighten us, that we may do Thy holy and good will in strength and contrition.

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear One of the Trinity, O Most Pure Lady, the Most Sovereign Son, Who was incarnate for our sake of thee, illuminating earthborn mortals with the unwaning and holy light of the Tri-Radiant Godhead.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

By the illumination of the brilliance of Thy coming...[^11]

*Troparion:*

O Trinity, most single in sovereignty, Who wast well pleased that the first angelic creation should be illuminated without mediation by the unapproachable rays of Thy beauty: enlighten with Thine illuminations those who hymn Thee in Orthodoxy!

*Troparion:*

Now human nature, which Thou didst bring into being out of goodness, hymns Thee, the Single Nature, the Tri-Radiant Divine Sovereignty, asking deliverance from transgressions, and assaults, and tribulations, and sorrows.

*Troparion (fol. 6):*[^12]

Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit: one Nature and Godhead do we, the faithful, glorify, divided yet undivided, of the One God of visible and invisible creation.

*Theotokion:*

All the prophetic sayings prefigured thy childbearing, O Most Pure Lady: ineffable and inexplicable; whereby we came to know, being mystically taught, of the single and tri-radiant Godhead.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The lowest abyss has encompassed us...[^13]

*Troparion:*

Possessing equal power, O Supersubstantial Trinity, in identity of will, Thou art a simple and undivided Unity; do Thou, therefore, preserve us by Thy power!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst establish all the ages by Thy will from non-being, as Thou art good, O Incomprehensible Trinity; then Thou didst also create man; but now deliver us from every tribulation!

*Troparion:*

O Holy Trinity, without beginning and enthroned together, Father, Son, and Spirit, Who givest life to all, sanctifiest all, and savest all by one power: for the Cherubim glorify Thee, and we, the unworthy, render praise unto Thee!

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast a temple unto the Unwaning Sun...

[Sunday.]

...unto Him Who created and in all His power set in order the great luminaries, O Most Pure Virgin, Bride of God; but even now deliver me from passions and torment.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 1.*

*Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*

Falling down before Thee in faith, we cry out from the heart: Have mercy on us, Thine unprofitable servants, O All-Good Trinity, Father and Son with the Spirit; and show compassion, O All-Merciful, toward the flock which Thou hast wisely gathered, which unceasingly glorifies Thy goodness.

*Theotokion:*

O Virgin, pray to Him Who was ineffably born of thee, that He may lighten our hearts, weighed down in the depth of despair by transgressions, and lead us up from grievous despondency to glorify thee, O All-Merciful Lady, the only hope of thy servants.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Considering thee, O Mother of God, as a mystical furnace...[^15]

*Troparion:*

O Word of God, consubstantial radiance of God the Almighty, even as Thou didst promise, make Thine all-beneficent abode in me, as Thou art tenderly compassionate, with Thy Father and the Spirit, and show me to be terrible unto demons and passions.

*Troparion (fol. 7):*[^14]

That Thou mightest show the beginning of Thy tender-heartedness toward us, O Master, having sent Thy Son unto our lowliness, Thou didst reform us unto our first brightness; but even now give me understanding through Thy Divine Spirit.

*Troparion:*

By three hallowings we glorify the One Godhead: the Unoriginate Father, the Co-Enthroned Son, and the Spirit, in one dominion, and faithfully cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

He Who is borne upon the Cherubic Throne, the King of all, dwelt within thy virginal womb, O Most Pure Lady, delivering all from corruption, as the Lover of mankind; but even now preserve me by thy supplications.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

A marvelous wonder: the furnace prefiguring the dew-giver...[^16]

*Troparion:*

By Thine all-beneficent nod, O Lord of all, Tri-Hypostatic and Almighty, Thou didst stretch out the heavens like a skin, and didst also establish the depth of the earth by Thine all-powerful fist; therefore strengthen also Thy servants in love and faith toward Thee, O Lover of mankind, that we may glorify Thee with longing unto the ages.

*Troparion:*

Enlighten with Thy Divine-origin Light those who hymn...

[Sunday.]

...Thee, the Tri-Radiant Light in Persons, yet Single in Essence, ever to gaze upon Thy light-giving rays, whereby I may be filled with Thy sweet, light-giving, and bountiful glory, and I faithfully exalt Thee unto the ages.

*Troparion:*

Receive praise and honor from our incomprehensible mind, O Most Perfect Trinity, enlightening all things, and illuminating the mind with dominion and power; for the heavenly powers glorify Thee, and we, earthborn mortals, unceasingly cry aloud and exalt Thee unto the ages.

*Theotokion:*

Thy Son, O Most Pure Mother of God, having assumed human nature immutable, by the abundance of His goodness, and delivered it from ancient corruption, ascended into the heavens; unto Whom we also sing in thanksgiving: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The image of thy pure childbearing...[^18]

*Troparion:*

Save Thy servants, O Savior of creation, both sensible and intelligible, from the snare and malice of the enemy, O Most Holy, Consubstantial Trinity, and keep Thy flock ever unharmed!

*Troparion (fol. 8):*[^17]

That Thou mightest show the unsearchable depth of Thine essential goodness, Thou hast given saving promises unto Thy servants, O Tri-Radiant and Single-Originate Almighty God; which vouchsafe to fulfill!

*Troparion:*

Look upon our supplications, Thou Who art One God in Three Divine-Origin Hypostases, truly believed in, and grant comfort unto Thy servants,[^19] through the prayers of the Most Pure and All-Hymned Mother of God!

*Theotokion:*

He could not endure to behold earthly nature fallen into corruption through the envy of the enemy by the disobedience of the First-Formed; but bowing the heavens, He came down without parting from the Godhead, and was incarnate of the Virgin without a father, that He might deify earthborn mortals.

*Then the Refrain: It is truly meet.*

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 1)

*On Sunday at Matins, after the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

While the soldiers were guarding Thy Tomb, O Savior, they became as dead from the lightning brilliance of the appearing Angel, who proclaimed the Resurrection unto the women. We glorify Thee, the Destroyer of corruption; we fall down before Thee...

[Sunday.]

...Who art risen from the tomb, and our only God.

*Glory:*

Thou wast nailed to the Cross of Thine own will, O Compassionate One, and laid in the Tomb as one dead, O Giver of life; Thou didst mightily crush the dominion of death, for the gatekeepers of Hades trembled before Thee; and Thou didst raise with Thyself those dead from the ages, as the only Lover of mankind.

*Now and ever. Theotokion:*

We all know thee to be the Mother of God, truly revealed as Virgin even after childbearing, who flee unto thy goodness with love; for we sinners possess thee as a mediatress, and we have acquired thee as salvation in afflictions, the only All-Immaculate Lady.

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

The women went early in the morning to the Tomb, and seeing the appearance of the Angel, they trembled; the Tomb shone forth life, and the marvel amazed them; therefore going unto the Disciples, they proclaimed the rising: Christ has overthrown Hades, as He alone is mighty and strong, and He has raised all the corrupt with Himself, destroying the fear of condemnation by the power of the Cross.

*Glory (fol. 9):*[^20]

Thou wast nailed to the Cross, O Life of all, and numbered among the dead, O Immortal Lord; Thou wast in the heart of the earth three days, O Savior, and didst raise Adam from corruption. Therefore the Heavenly Powers cried aloud unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy Passion, O Christ; glory to Thy Resurrection; glory to Thy descent, O only Lover of mankind!

*Now and ever. Theotokion:*

Having conceived without being burned the Divine Fire, and given birth without seed unto the Lord, the Fountain of life, O Full of Grace, Mother of God: save those who magnify thee!

*Hypakoe, Tone 1:*

The repentance of the thief opened Paradise, and the lamentation of the Myrrh-bearing Women proclaimed joy: that Thou art risen, O Christ God, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Then the Gradual Antiphons.*

*Antiphon 1, Tone 1:*

When I am in sorrow, give ear unto my afflictions, O Lord, as I cry unto Thee.

For those of the desert, divine longing is unceasing, since they are outside the vain world.

*Glory... Now and ever:*

To the Holy Spirit belong honor and glory, even as it is fitting unto the Father...

[Sunday.]

...together with the Son; therefore let us hymn the single dominion of the Trinity.

*Antiphon 2:*

Unto the mountains of Thy laws has the law of virtues lifted me up; enlighten me, O God, that I may hymn Thee.

Receiving me with Thy right hand, O Word, keep and preserve me, lest the fire of sin burn me up.

*Glory... Now and ever:*

By the Holy Spirit every creature is renewed, running back unto the first state; for He is equally powerful with the Father and the Word.

*Antiphon 3:*

Over those who said unto me: Let us go into the courts of the Lord, my spirit was glad, and my heart rejoiced.

In the house of David there is great fear; for thrones being set there, all the tribes and nations of the earth shall be judged.

*Glory... Now and ever:*

To the Holy Spirit belong honor and worship, glory and dominion, even as it is worthy of the Father, and fitting to be offered unto the Son; for the Trinity is a Unity in essence, but not in persons.

*Prokeimenon (fol. 10), Tone 1:*[^21]

Now will I arise, saith the Lord; I will set Myself in salvation, I will deal openly therein.

*Verse: The words of the Lord are pure words.*

*Let every breath praise the Lord. Matins Gospel. The Resurrection of Christ. Psalm 50.*[^22]

*The Resurrection Canon on 4; the Cross-Resurrection Canon on 4; that of the Mother of God on 3; and in the Menaion on 4. If a celebrated saint, on 6.*

*Resurrection Canon.*

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

Thy victorious right hand has been glorified in strength in a manner befitting God; for it, O Immortal One, being all-powerful, shattered the adversaries, making a way for the Israelites through the deep.

*Glory to Thy holy Resurrection, O Lord:*

He Who with all-pure hands fashioned me in divine beneficence from the dust in the beginning, stretched out His hands upon the Cross, calling up from the earth my corruptible body, which Thou didst receive from the Virgin.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst submit to mortification for my sake, and didst deliver Thy soul unto death, O Thou Who by Divine inbreathing didst place a soul in me; and loosing me from eternal bonds and raising me with Thyself, Thou didst glorify me with incorruption.

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, O Fountain of grace; rejoice, O Ladder...

[Sunday.]

...and Heavenly Gate! Rejoice, O Candelabrum and Golden Jar, and Uncut Mountain, who didst bear Christ the Giver of life unto the world!

*Canon of the Cross and Resurrection, Same Tone. Ode 1:*

*Troparion:*

Christ deifies me, becoming incarnate; Christ exalts me, humbling Himself; Christ makes me impassible, as the Giver of life suffers in the nature of flesh; therefore I sing a song of thanksgiving: For He is glorified!

*Troparion:*

Christ exalts me, being crucified; Christ raises me with Himself, being put to death; Christ grants me life; therefore with gladness, clapping our hands, I sing unto the Savior a song of victory: For He is glorified!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst conceive God, O Virgin, and didst bear Him in virginity, incarnate of thee, O Most Pure Lady: the Only-Begotten Son, One in Hypostasis, yet known in two essences: For He is glorified!

*Another Canon, to the Most Holy Mother of God:*

*Troparion:*

What worthy song can our weakness offer thee, save that of joyful greeting, which Gabriel mystically taught us: Rejoice, O Virgin Mother of God, Unwedded Mother! (fol. 11)[^23]

*Troparion:*

Unto the Ever-Virgin and Mother of the King of the heavenly hosts, from a pure heart let us, the faithful, spiritually cry aloud: Rejoice, O Virgin Mother of God, Unwedded Mother!

*Troparion:*

O All-Pure Lady, unto the ineffable abyss of thine incomprehensible childbearing, with unhesitating faith we purely offer thee praise: Rejoice, O Virgin Mother of God, Unwedded Mother!

*Katavasia: I will open my mouth...*[^24]

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou alone, Who knowest the weakness of human nature, and didst mercifully clothe Thyself in it: gird me with power to cry unto Thee: Holy art Thou, the animate temple of Thine all-pure glory, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Being God, O Good One, Thou didst have compassion upon me who was fallen, and wast well pleased to descend unto me; and by Crucifixion Thou didst exalt me, that I may cry unto Thee: Holy art Thou, the Lord of glory, unsearchable in goodness!

*Troparion:*

Being Substantial Life, O Christ, and having clothed Thyself in me who was corrupted, as the Merciful God: descending into the dust of death, O Master, Thou didst destroy the dominion of death, and rising as dead on the third day...

[Sunday.]

...Thou didst raise me, and didst clothe me in incorruption.

*Theotokion:*

Having conceived Fire in thy womb through the Holy Spirit, O Virgin, thou didst remain unconsumed; for the Bush manifestly prefigured thee to Moses the Lawgiver, burning yet unconsumed, as thou didst receive the Insupportable Fire.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Unto Him Who took upon His shoulders the straying sheep, and by the Tree cast down its sin, unto Christ God let us cry aloud: O Thou Who hast exalted our horn, holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Unto Him Who brought up from Hades the Great Shepherd, Christ, and through His pastorship wisely shepherded the nations through the Apostles, let us minister in truth and in the Holy Spirit.

*Theotokion:*

Unto the Son Who of His own will was incarnate without seed of the Virgin, and Who by Divine power preserved the Pure Virgin even after childbearing, unto God Who is over all let us cry aloud: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

We truly call thee a light cloud, O Virgin, following the prophetic sayings; for the Lord came upon thee to destroy the Egyptian deceptions of hand-made idols, and to enlighten those who serve them. (fol. 12)[^25]

*Troparion:*

The prophetic choir truly named thee a sealed fountain and a shut gate, hymning the wonders of thy virginity, clearly describing the signs for us, which He preserved even after childbearing.

*Troparion:*

Gabriel, having been vouchsafed to see the Supersubstantial Mind as far as possible, brought unto thee the joyful greeting, O All-Immaculate Virgin, clearly announcing the conception of the Word and proclaiming His ineffable birth.

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Having perceived with foresighted eyes the mountain overshadowed by Divine grace, Habakkuk proclaimed the Holy One of Israel about to come forth from thee, for our salvation and renewal.

*Troparion:*

Who is this Savior coming forth from Edom, wearing a crown of thorns, having His garments dyed red, hanging upon the Tree? He is the Holy One of Israel, for our salvation and renewal.

*Troparion:*

Behold, ye disobedient people, and be ashamed...

[Sunday.]

...for Him Whom ye madly asked of Pilate to lift upon the Cross as an evildoer, destroying the power of death, arose from the tomb in a manner befitting God.

*Theotokion:*

We know thee to be the Tree of Life, O Virgin; for no mortal fruit for food grew forth from thee unto men, but the enjoyment of life eternal, for our salvation and renewal.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Who is this beautiful One coming from Edom, and His garments dyed red from the vineyard of Bosor? Beautiful as God, and as man, wearing a garment reddened with the Blood of His flesh; unto Whom we, the faithful, sing: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Christ, having appeared as the High Priest of good things to come, destroyed our sin, and having shown a strange way by His own Blood, entered into the greater and more perfect Tabernacle, having become our Forerunner.

*Troparion:*

Even as Thou didst say, O Christ, on the third day Thou didst rise from the tomb, which was truly accomplished; for as Creator Thou didst renew the destroyed temple of Thy body; and Thou wast revealed as incorruptible God, and incorruptible man. (fol. 13)[^26]

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst release the ancient debt of Eve, O All-Hymned Lady, through the New Adam Who appeared for our sake; having united in thee through the honorable conception an intelligible, animate flesh, Christ came forth from thee, One Lord in both natures.

*Another Troparion, to the Most Holy Theotokos:*

Hear wonders, O Heaven, and give ear, O Earth! For the daughter of the earthly, fallen Adam was called Mother of God, and parent unto her Creator, for our salvation and renewal.

*Troparion:*

We hymn thy great and awesome Mystery; for hiding Himself from the heavenly ranks, He Who is ever-existing came down upon thee like rain upon the fleece, O All-Hymned Lady, for our salvation and renewal.

*Troparion:*

Holy of holies, O All-Hymned Mother of God, the expectation of the nations and salvation of the faithful: from thee shone forth the Redeemer, and Giver of life, and Lord; Whom implore to save thy servants.

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 5. Heirmos:*

O Christ, Who didst enlighten the ends of the world with the brilliance of Thy coming, and didst make them radiant by Thy Cross: enlighten the hearts...

[Sunday.]

...with the light of Thy divine knowledge, of those who hymn Thee in Orthodoxy.

*Troparion:*

The Jews put to death the Great Shepherd of the sheep and the Lord by the tree of the Cross; but He Himself delivered the dead buried in Hades from the dominion of death.

*Troparion:*

Having announced peace by Thy Cross, Thou didst proclaim remission unto captives, O my Savior; and Thou didst put to shame him who held dominion, O Christ, showing him naked and destitute by Thy Divine rising.

*Theotokion:*

Despise not the petitions of those who ask in faith, O All-Hymned Lady, but receive them and bring them unto thy Son, O Most Pure Lady, the only Benefactor God; for we have acquired thee as our mediatress.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

O the depth of the riches and wisdom of God! The Lord, surpassing all things, delivered us from their craftiness; for having suffered of His own will in the weakness of the flesh, by His own might He gave life, and rose as dead.

*Troparion:*

Being God, He is united unto flesh for our sake, is crucified, dies, is buried, rises again, and ascends brightly in His flesh, Christ unto the Father, with Which He will come and save those who have piously served Him. (fol. 14)[^27]

*Theotokion:*

Holy of holies, O Pure Virgin, thou didst bear the Holy of holies, Christ the Redeemer, Who sanctifies all; therefore we proclaim thee Queen and Sovereign Lady of all, as the Mother of the Creator of creatures.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

The heavenly Hosts are glad, beholding thee; and with them the assemblies of mankind rejoice; they are joined together through thy childbearing, O Virgin Mother of God, which we worthily glorify.

*Troparion:*

Let every human tongue and thought be moved to the praise of the truly human adornment; for the Virgin has come, clearly glorifying those who hymn her marvels in faith.

*Troparion:*

Glorified is the song and praise of the all-wise offered unto the Virgin and Mother of God; for she was the temple of the most blessed glory, which we worthily glorify.

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The lowest abyss has encompassed us, and there is none to deliver; we were accounted as sheep for the slaughter; save...

[Sunday.]

...Thy people, O our God; for Thou art the strength of the weak.

*Troparion:*

Through the sin of the first-formed man, O Lord, we were grievously wounded; but we were healed by Thy wound, whereby Thou wast wounded for our sake, O Christ; for Thou art the strength and restoration of the weak.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst bring up from Hades, O Lord, having slain the all-devouring whale by Thine all-powerful might, casting down his power; for Thou art Life and Light and Resurrection.

*Theotokion:*

The forefathers of our race rejoice in thee, O Most Pure Virgin, having received Eden again through thee, which they had lost through transgression; for thou art pure, both before childbearing and after childbearing.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Being impassible and immaterial Mind, Christ God was joined unto human mind, mediating by the Divine nature and virginity of the flesh; and being immutable, He was wholly united unto all of me, that being crucified He might grant salvation unto all of me who was fallen.

*Troparion:*

Adam was deceived and fell, and was crushed, having of old been lied to by the hope of deification; but he rises again, being deified by union with the Word, and through His Passion receives impassibility, glorified as Son upon the Throne, sitting with the Father and the Spirit.

*Theotokion (fol. 15):*[^28]

Without departing from the bosom of His Unoriginate Father, He takes up His abode in the bosom of the Pure Maiden, and He Who was before without mother becomes incarnate without father, God reigning in righteousness: His awesome generation is without genealogy and ineffable.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

The heavenly orders stand before thy childbearing as servants, worthily marveling at thy seedless childbearing, O Ever-Virgin; for thou art pure, both before childbearing and after childbearing.

*Troparion:*

The Word, Who was formerly bodiless, was incarnate of thee, O Most Pure Lady, He Who makes all things by His will, Who brought the incorporeal hosts from non-being, as He is all-powerful.

*Troparion:*

The enemy was slain by thy life-bearing Fruit, O Full of Divine Grace, and cursed Hades was trampled down, and we who were in bonds were set free; therefore I cry aloud: Loose the passions of my heart!

*Kontakion, Tone 1:*

As God Thou didst rise from the tomb in glory, and didst raise the world with Thyself; and human nature hymns Thee as God, and death has vanished; and Adam dances for joy, O Master, and Eve, now delivered from bonds...

[Sunday.]

...rejoices, crying aloud: Thou art He Who grantest resurrection unto all, O Christ!

*Ikos:*

Let us hymn Him Who arose on the third day as Almighty God, Who shattered the gates of Hades, and raised from the tomb those dead from the ages, and appeared unto the Myrrh-bearing Women, even as He was well pleased, He Who first said "Rejoice!" unto the women,[^30] and proclaims joy unto the Apostles, as the only Giver of life. Therefore with faith the women announce the tokens of victory unto the Disciples, and Hades groans, and death laments, and the world is glad, and all rejoice together with them in the Resurrection of Christ: For Thou, O Christ, grantest resurrection unto all!

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 7. Heirmos:*

We, the faithful, understand thee to be a mystical furnace, O Mother of God; for even as the Exalted One saved the three youths, He renewed all of me, man, in thy womb, the God of our fathers Who is praised and exceedingly glorified.

*Troparion:*

The earth was afraid, the sun hid itself, and the light grew dark; the divine veil of the Temple was rent in twain, and the rocks were split; for the Righteous One hung upon the Cross: the God of our fathers Who is praised and exceedingly glorified.

*Troparion (fol. 16):*[^29]

Thou didst become as one helpless, and wast wounded among the dead of Thine own will for our sake, O Exalted One; Thou didst free all, and by Thy mighty hand didst raise them with Thyself: the God of our fathers Who is praised and exceedingly glorified.

*Theotokion:*

We name thee the all-pure Golden Jar, the Table of the Divine Bread of life, O Pure Lady, the place of sanctification, the exalted Throne whereon God has rested, Who is all-praised and exalted unto the ages.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Of old the earth was cursed, having been stained with the blood of Abel by a brother-slaying hand; but sprinkled with Thy God-shed Blood, it is blessed, and dancing it cries aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Let the Jewish God-opposing people weep for the daring murder of Christ; but let the nations be glad, and let them clap their hands and cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Behold, shining upon the Myrrh-bearing Women, the Angel cried aloud of the Resurrection of Christ: Come and see the signs, the Plashchanytsia and the Tomb, and cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

[Sunday.]

...O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

Being nailed of Thine own will unto the Tree of the Cross for our sake, O Lover of mankind, Thou didst tear up the handwriting of forefather Adam; therefore with him we all unceasingly sing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos): We, the faithful, understand thee to be a mystical furnace...*

*Troparion:*

Jacob prophetically perceived thee to be the Ladder, O Mother of God; for through thee the Exalted One appeared upon earth and lived among men, as He was well pleased: the God of our fathers Who is praised and exceedingly glorified.

*Troparion:*

Rejoice, O Pure Lady! From thee came forth the Shepherd, Who truly clothed Himself in Adam's skin: into all of me, man, for His blameless compassion: the God of our fathers Who is praised and exceedingly glorified.

*Troparion:*

Rejoice, O fountain of ever-living water! Rejoice, delight-filled paradise! Rejoice, wall of the faithful! Rejoice, unwedded one! Rejoice, joy of all the world, for whose sake the God of our fathers Who is praised and exceedingly glorified shone forth upon us!

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 8. Heirmos:*

In the furnace the children of Israel, as in a crucible, shone with the beauty of piety purer than gold (fol. 17),[^31] saying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

O Word of God, Who by Thy will dost make and transform all things, turning the shadow of death into life eternal by Thy Passion: unto Thee all works unceasingly sing as God, and exceedingly exalt Thee unto all ages.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst destroy the affliction and misery in the gates and strongholds of Hades, O Christ, rising from the tomb on the third day; unto Thee all works unceasingly sing as God, and exceedingly exalt Thee unto all ages.

*Theotokion:*

Let us hymn Him Who shone forth without seed and beyond nature, having borne the Divine, precious Pearl, Christ, saying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Come, ye peoples, let us worship the place whereon the most pure feet stood, and where the Divine, life-creating hands of Christ were stretched forth upon the Tree for the salvation of all men; and standing around the life-giving Tomb, let us sing: Let every creature bless the Lord, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

The lawless slander of the God-killing Jews was put to shame; for Him Whom they called a deceiver...

[Sunday.]

...arose as mighty, laughing to scorn the foolish seals; therefore rejoicing we sing: Let every creature bless the Lord, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Trinity Troparion:*

The purest Seraphim, theologizing in three sanctifications, and with fear servilely glorifying in glory the One Lordship, the Tri-hypostatic Divinity; with them we also piously sing: Let every creature bless the Lord, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

Of old Aaron's rod prefigured thee beyond nature, O Virgin; for thou alone didst bear without union with man Him Who adorned the earth with flowers; therefore rejoicing, we, the faithful, truly glorify thee as Mother of God, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

The light-bearing bridal chamber, from which the Master of all, Christ, came forth as a Bridegroom, let us all hymn, crying aloud: All ye works of the Lord, we bless thee, O Virgin, and exceedingly exalt thee unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Rejoice, O Throne of God! Rejoice, wall of the faithful, from whom Christ the Light shone upon those in darkness, who call thee blessed, and cry aloud: All ye works of the Lord (fol. 18),[^32] sing to the Lord and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Having borne the Lord, the Cause of salvation for us, entreat Him for all who diligently cry aloud, O All-Hymned Virgin: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The burning bush showed forth unconsumed an image of thy pure childbearing; and now we pray thee to quench the furnace of temptations raging against us, that we may unceasingly magnify thee, O Mother of God.

*Troparion:*

O how the lawless and disobedient people, having taken crafty counsel, justified the unrighteous and impious, yet condemned to the Tree the Righteous One, the Lord of glory, Whom we worthily magnify!

*Troparion:*

O Savior, blameless Lamb, Who takest away the sins of the world: Thee we glorify, Who didst rise on the third day, with Thy Father and Divine Spirit, and theologizing the Lord of glory, we magnify Him!

*Theotokion:*

Save Thy people, O Lord, whom Thou didst acquire by Thy precious Blood, granting the king strength against enemies, and grant peace unto Thy churches, O Lover of mankind, through the prayers of the Mother of God.

[Sunday.]

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Thy Cross was glorified by Thine ineffable power, O Lord; for Thy weakness appeared greater than all strength, whereby the mighty were cast down unto the earth, and the poor are exalted unto heaven.

*Troparion:*

Our vile death was put to death by the Resurrection from the dead; for Thou didst appear, O Christ, reigning over those in Hades; therefore, singing of Thee as Life and Resurrection, hypostatic Light, we magnify Thee.

*Trinity Troparion:*

The Unoriginate and uncircumscribed Nature is known in three hypostases of Divine origin, One Godhead: in Father, and Son, and Spirit; hoping in Whom the godly-minded king is saved.

*Theotokion:*

Ineffable is the Mystery of the Virgin; for she was revealed as Heaven and Cherubic Throne, and the light-bearing bridal chamber of Christ God the Almighty; whom we piously magnify as Mother of God.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

Every law of praise is overcome, O Most Pure Lady, by the greatness of thy glory; yet, O Sovereign Lady, from thy servants who are unworthy, who bring unto thee of good zeal, receive with goodness, O Mother of God, the song of praise unto thee.

*Troparion:*

From the root of the prophet and forefather of God, David, thou didst blossom forth (fol. 19),[^33] O Virgin; but David also hast thou truly glorified, having borne the forenamed Lord of glory, Whom we worthily magnify.

*Troparion:*

O the marvels of thy deeds surpassing the mind! For thou alone under the sun, O Virgin, hast given all men to understand the newest wonder of thine incomprehensible childbearing, O All-Venerable Lady, for whose sake we all magnify thee.

*At the Praises, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 1:*

We hymn Thy saving Passion, O Christ, and glorify Thy Resurrection.

O Thou Who didst endure the Crucifixion, and abolish death, and rise from the dead: give peace unto our life, O Lord, as Thou alone art almighty.

O Thou Who didst despoil Hades, and didst raise man by Thy Resurrection, O Christ: vouchsafe us with a pure heart to hymn and glorify Thee.

Glorifying Thy God-befitting dispensation, we cry aloud unto Thee, O Christ: Born of the Virgin, yet unseparated from the Father; Thou didst suffer as man, and of Thine own will didst endure the Crucifixion; and Thou didst rise from the Tomb as one coming forth from a bridal chamber, that Thou mightest save the world: O Lord, glory to Thee!

[Sunday.]

*Other Stichera, Anatolian, in the same Tone:*

When Thou wast nailed unto the Tree of the Cross, then was the dominion of the enemy put to death; creation was shaken with fear of Thee, and Hades was despoiled by Thy might; Thou didst raise the dead from the tombs, and didst open Paradise unto the Thief: O Christ our God, glory to Thee!

Lamenting, having reached Thy Tomb in haste, the honorable women found the Tomb opened; and having learned from the Angel the new and all-glorious marvel, they announced unto the Apostles that the Lord was risen, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor for ever (Ps. 9:33):*

We worship the Divine wounds of Thy Passions, O Christ God, and the sovereign priestly ministration in Zion, which in the end of the ages was made manifest of God; for unto those sleeping in darkness the Sun of righteousness shone forth, guiding them unto the unsetting radiance: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will tell of all Thy wonders (Ps. 9:2):*

Hear, ye mutinous Hebrew race: Where are they who came unto Pilate (fol. 20)?[^34] Say, ye soldier guards: Where are the seals of the tomb? Where was He Who was buried removed? Where was He sold Who was not sold? How was the treasure stolen? Why do ye slander the Savior's rising, ye lawless Jews? He Who is free among the dead is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory, both now. Evangelical Sticheron.*

*Now dismissest Thou. Theotokion: Most blessed art thou.*

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 1)

*At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 1:*

Through food the enemy led Adam out of Paradise, but by the Cross Christ brought the Thief back into it, who cried aloud: Remember me when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

I worship Thy Passions, O Christ, and glorify Thy Resurrection; and with Adam and the Thief, with a radiant voice I cry unto Thee: Remember me, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

Thou wast crucified, O Sinless One, and wast laid in the Tomb of Thine own will; but rising as God, Thou didst raise with Thyself the first-formed man, who cries aloud: Remember me when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

The bodily temple, O Savior, by Thy three-day burial...[^35]

[Sunday.]

...Thou didst raise with Thyself, O Christ God: Remember me as I cry aloud when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

The Myrrh-bearing women came weeping unto Thy Tomb, O Christ God, very early in the morning, and found an Angel sitting in white raiment, who cried aloud: "Why seek ye? Christ is risen, weep no more!"

Thine Apostles, O Lord, came in haste unto the mountain where Thou hadst commanded them; and having seen Thee, they worshipped, whom Thou didst also send forth unto the nations to preach and to baptize.

*Glory:*

Let us worship the Father, and let us glorify the Son, and let all we, the faithful, hymn the All-Holy Spirit, calling aloud and saying: O All-Holy Trinity, save us all!

*Both now:*

Thy people offer Thy Mother unto Thee in prayer, O Christ; through her intercessions grant us Thy bounties, O Good One, that we may glorify Thee Who didst shine forth unto us from the Tomb.[^36]
