# EXAPOSTILARIA AND EVANGELICAL STICHERA
*Resurrectional Exapostilaria and Evangelical Stichera. The Work of Emperor Leo.*

*Exapostilarion 1:*
With the disciples let us go up into the mountain of Galilee with faith to behold Christ, Who says that He has received authority over things above and things below; let us learn how He teaches to baptize all nations in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, and to abide with the initiates of His mysteries, even as He promised, unto the end of the age.

*Theotokion:*
Thou didst rejoice with the disciples, O Virgin Mother of God, for thou didst behold Christ risen from the tomb on the third day, even as He said; unto whom also He appeared, teaching and revealing greater things, and commanding to baptize in the Father, and the Son, and the Spirit: that we may believe in His rising, and glorify thee, O Maiden.

*Glory... Evangelical Sticheron 1, Tone 1:*
When the disciples were going up into the mountain for the earthly ascension, the Lord stood before them, and they worshipped Him; and having been taught of the authority given Him everywhere, they were sent into all under heaven to preach the resurrection from the dead...

[Sunday.]

*Exapostilarion 2:*

*Theotokion:*

*Theotokion:*
O Sun that hast shone forth from the tomb today as a Bridegroom from his chamber, and having despoiled hades and abolished death: by the prayers of her that bore Thee send down light upon us, light enlightening our hearts, light directing all to walk in the paths of Thy commandments and in the way of peace.
