# TONE 4 (fol. 54) [^72]

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 4)

*On Saturday Evening at Great Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron:*

Ceaselessly bowing down before Thy life-giving Cross, O Christ, we glorify Thy three-day Resurrection; for thereby, O Almighty One, Thou didst renew corrupted human nature, and didst renew for us the ascent into heaven, as the only Good One and Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:*

Thou didst loose the penalty of the tree of disobedience, O Savior, being willingly nailed to the tree of the Cross; and having descended into Hades, O Mighty One, as God Thou didst burst the bonds of death asunder; therefore we worship Thine arising from the dead, crying out with joy: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Thou didst shatter the gates of Hades, O Lord, and by Thy death Thou didst destroy the kingdom of death; and Thou didst set the human race free from corruption [p59LA] granting life and incorruption unto the world, and great mercy.

*Other Stichera, Eastern [by Anatolius]:*

*Sticheron:*

Come, O ye peoples, let us hymn the Savior's three-day arising, whereby we were delivered from the unbreakable bonds of Hades, and we all received incorruption and life, crying aloud: O Thou Who wast crucified and buried, and didst arise, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:*

Angels and men, O Savior, hymn Thy three-day arising, whereby the ends of the universe were enlightened, and we all were delivered from the servitude of the enemy, crying aloud: O Life-Creating, Almighty Savior, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:*

Thou didst shatter the brazen gates and didst crush the iron bars, O Christ God, and didst raise up fallen human race; therefore with one accord we cry unto Thee: O Thou Who didst arise from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Lord, Thy birth from the Father is timeless and co-eternal; and Thine incarnation from the Virgin is ineffable unto men and unutterable; and Thy descent into Hades is fearful unto the devil and his angels; for having trampled down death, Thou didst arise on the third day...

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:* (fol. 55)[^73]

...granting incorruption unto men and great mercy.

David, the Prophet and Forefather of God, on account of thee melodiously proclaimed of thee unto Him Who hath done magnificent things for thee: The Queen stood at Thy right hand; for God showed thee forth as Mother and Mediatress of life, Who was pleased to become man from thee without a father, that He might renew His own image corrupted by passions, and having found the lost sheep that had wandered upon the mountain, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and by His own will unite it with the heavenly hosts, and, O Mother of God, might save the world: Christ, Who hath great and rich mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron:*

Ascending the Cross, O Lord, Thou didst wipe away our ancestral curse; and descending into Hades, Thou didst set free the eternal prisoners, granting incorruption unto the human race; therefore, singing, we glorify Thy life-creating and saving Resurrection.

*Other Stichera, by Alphabet, Tone 4:*

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

*Sticheron:*

Hanging upon the Tree, O only Mighty One, all creation [p60LA] Thou didst shake, O only Mighty One; and being laid in the tomb, Thou didst raise up those dwelling in the tombs, granting incorruption and life unto the human race; therefore, singing, we glorify Thy three-day arising.

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

*Sticheron:*

The lawless people, having delivered Thee unto Pilate, O Christ, condemned Thee to be crucified, showing themselves ungrateful toward their Benefactor; but willingly having endured burial by Thine own authority, Thou didst arise on the third day as God, granting us endless life and great mercy.

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

*Sticheron:*

The women with tears reached the tomb, seeking Thee; but not having found Thee, weeping with lamentation they cried out, saying: Woe is us, O Savior and King of all! How wast Thou stolen away? What place holds Thy life-bearing Body? And the Angel answered them, saying: Weep not, but go and proclaim that the Lord is risen, granting us joy, as the only Compassionate One!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

Incline Thyself unto the entreaty of thy servants...

...O All-Blameless Lady, calming the grievous uprisings against us, delivering us from every affliction; for thee alone have we as a firm and sure confirmation, and we have acquired thy protection: let us not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady, who call upon thee! Hasten unto the supplication of those who faithfully cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, Helper of all, Joy, and Protection, and Salvation of our souls!

*Dismissal Troparion of the Resurrection, Tone 4:* (fol. 56)[^74]

Having learned the joyful proclamation of the Resurrection from the Angel, and cast off the ancestral condemnation, the women disciples of the Lord boasted unto the Apostles, saying: Death has been overthrown, and Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto the Angels was revealed unto those on earth through thee, O Mother of God: God being incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised the first-formed man, He saved our souls from death.

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 4)

[p61LA] *On Sunday at the Midnight Office, Canon to the Most Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, the work of the most honorable Gregory; the fourth song [Tone 4], the work of Mitrophanes.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos: Traversing the deep of the Red Sea with dryshod feet...*

*Troparion:*

Let us glorify the God-Originating Hypostasis in Three Persons, co-everlasting, co-enthroned, entreating Whom we say: Save those who praise Thee with faith!

*Troparion:*

The Son was anointed by the Father with the Spirit of joy, with the beneficent oil; and He became man, and taught the three Hypostases of the one Godhead.

*Troparion:*

The Seraphim, not enduring to gaze upon the beauty of Thine unapproachable glory, O Tri-Solar Unity, cover themselves with their wings, and with thrice-holy hymns unceasingly praise Thee.

*Theotokion:*

Ineffably didst thou bear the Creator of all, O Most Pure Lady, Who delivers men from the ancient curse; and saved by thee, through thee we have known the one Tri-hypostatic God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Not in wisdom, nor in power, nor in wealth...*

*Troparion:* (fol. 57)[^75]

Having sent down from on high the Comforter from the Father unto Thine holy Apostles of old, O Christ, Thou didst reveal the one Tri-Solar Nature.

*Troparion:*

When Thou didst appear of old unto the Patriarch Abraham in the form of men, O immutable Trinitarian Unity, Thou didst show forth [the mystery] of Thy goodness and Lordship; Thou Who art believed in as the one God in three forms, visibly uncircumscribed, incomprehensible unto all: deliver our souls from every affliction!

*Theotokion:*

Guided by the all-wise teachings of thy Son, we glorify the unique and tri-radiant Godhead, and we bless thee, O Ever-Virgin.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4 [after Ode 3]:*

O Tri-Solar, uncreated, and consubstantial Unity, Tri-hypostatic and incomprehensible: take pity upon Thy servants; save us from perils, as Thou art merciful; for we have acquired Thee, O Lord, as Redeemer and Master, crying: Be merciful unto us!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

We hymn thee, O Bride of God, Mother of Christ God, praising [p62LA] thy birthgiving, incomprehensible, whereby we were delivered from the devil's deceit and from all assaults, O Sovereign Lady, Mother of God; therefore with faith we cry unto thee: Have mercy upon thy flock, O only Supremely Hymned One!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Seated in glory upon the throne of the Godhead...*

*Troparion:*

The super-essential Trinity in the Unity of the Godhead and Lordship, with the Seraphim we glorify Thee as indivisible in Nature, as unapproachable, as co-equal in glory, O incomprehensible God!

*Troparion:*

Incomprehensibly divided in Persons of the Godhead, yet united together in dominion in one Lordship, boundless, one, uncircumscribed, we hymn Thee, the Creator of all creation.

*Troparion:*

The unoriginate Mind ineffably begot the Word, and the Divine Spirit is co-equal in power; and on this account we proclaim the consubstantial Trinity, the God and Master of all things.

*Theotokion:*

Being seen in figures by the ancients, the Word pre-announced His incarnation from thee, O Virgin; yet in these latter times He appeared unto men, truly revealing the Tri-hypostatic Monarchy.

*Ode 5. Heirmos: All things marvelled at thy divine [memory]...*

*Troparion:* (fol. 58)[^76]

Having understood through faith the all-effecting Godhead, we hymn indeed one unapproachable Essence, yet three Hypostases, life-originating and equal: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, a co-everlasting existence.

*Troparion:*

O Tri-Solar Light, unique Godhead, uncreated Nature, and light-producing Fountain of every light-giving ray: shine forth upon me Thine essential Light, that I may contemplate Thine ineffable beauty!

*Troparion:*

As unto the only existing Creator of all, the all-wise Sustainer and Helmsman, and truly the Giver of life, faithfully we cry unto Thee: O Tri-Solar Master, preserve those who hymn Thee!

*Theotokion:*

Desiring to give life unto man who had grown old and corrupt of old, whom for the sake of goodness Thy Son created, O Virgin, and showing forth the image of the divine form, He became man from thee, and proclaimed the one Godhead in three Persons.

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Crying aloud, he prefigured the three-day burial...*

[p63LA] *Troparion:*

...The Father appeared, proclaiming the Sonship; and the Spirit was seen as Christ was baptized; on this account we glorify the one and Trinitarian Godhead.

*Troparion:*

When Isaiah beheld Thee seated upon a high throne, praised with thrice-holy voices, he understood the Trinitarian Hypostasis of the one Godhead.

*Troparion:*

Show forth a lifted-up heart also in Thy servants, O exalted King, Tri-hypostatic [God], that we may clearly gaze upon the radiance of Thy glory.

*Theotokion:*

I have sinned, yet I have not departed from Thee, wretched one that I am; have compassion upon me, O Tri-hypostatic, one, honorable Godhead, and deliver me from grievous condemnation through the entreaties of the Maiden of God!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4 [after Ode 6]:*

O incomprehensible and unapproachable God, Who art one in Nature, good and merciful: Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit! Preserve and sanctify those who cry out with faith in Thine holy church by night and by day, and deliver us not in any way unto the enemies who seek our destruction!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

We shall never cease, O Ever-Virgin, gratefully to hymn [^77] [f. 59A] from the heart, we thy servants, thy mercies, O Sovereign Lady, calling out and saying: O Most Holy Virgin, go before us, and deliver us from invisible enemies, and from perils, and from every threatening; for thou art our Protection!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: In the fiery furnace the Persian children of...*

*Troparion:*

Imitating the heavenly natures established in rank, and the noetic orders, all we earthborn of the Orthodox glorify one Godhead in three Hypostases equal in action.

*Troparion:*

The eyes of the holy Prophets of old figuratively made Thee known as the only Creator of all the ages, the ineffable God and Lord, in three God-originating Hypostases.

*Theotokion:*

The Word, invisible by essence and all-effecting, Thou didst appear unto men, becoming man from the pure Mother of God, calling man unto communion with Thy Godhead.

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Daniel, stretching forth his hands, [stopped] the gaping mouths of the lions...*

*Troparion:*

O unique and tri-radiant Light, unoriginate Essence, unfathomable Beauty: make Thine abode in my heart, and a radiant temple of Thy Godhead [f. 59A] show me forth radiant and pure, as I cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him supremely unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

From my diverse passions, O indivisible Trinity, unconfused Unity, and from the darkening of transgressions deliver me, and illumine me with Thy divine rays, that I may reflect Thy glory and hymn Thee, the Lord of glory!

*Troparion:*

The Father is indeed the unbegotten Mind, and the Word is of like form, and the Spirit is co-enthroned; one Essence, Power, and Existence: O super-essential, ineffable, majestic Trinity and Unity, preserve Thy flock by the intercessions of the Mother of God, as the only Lover of mankind!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Let all earthborn leap for joy...*

*Troparion:*

Unto Thee now I move all my heart and mind, and the intentions of my whole soul and body, unto my Creator and Redeemer, O tri-radiant Monarchy; and I cry unto Thee: Save me, Thy servant, from perils and all afflictions!

*Troparion:*

Lift up our mind and thought unto Thee on high; illumine us with Thine immaculate splendors, O Father, Word, and Comforter, Who dwellest in light unapproachable [^78] [f. 60A] O Sun of glory and Holder of light, that we may ever glorify Thee, the one Principle, the Tri-hypostatic God!

*Theotokion:*

Save those who believe in Thee, O Lord, and who proclaim the unoriginate, co-everlasting one Essence, and the three God-originating, conformed Persons of Thy Lordship, and make them worthy of divine glory, by the entreaties of the pure Mother of God!

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 4)

*On Sunday at Matins, on "God is the Lord," Troparion of the Resurrection. Sessional hymns of the Resurrection, and the canons of the Octoechos to 8, and we also sing other Theotokia.*

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymn of the Resurrection, Tone 4:*

Gazing upon the entrance of the tomb, and not enduring the angelic flame, the Myrrh-bearing women marvelled with trembling, saying: Has He been stolen away, Who opened Paradise unto the thief? Or is He risen, Who even before His Passion proclaimed His arising? Truly Christ is risen, granting life and resurrection unto those in Hades!

*Another Sessional Hymn:*

Thou didst arise as immortal from Hades, O Savior; Thou didst raise up Thy world from bonds; by Thy Resurrection, O Christ our God, Thou didst crush with Thy strength the [f. 60A] dominion; Thou didst show forth the Resurrection unto all, O Merciful One; therefore we glorify Thee, O only Lover of mankind.

*Theotokion:*

We magnify thee, O Mother of God, crying: Thou art the Mountain from which the Stone was ineffably cut without hands, and crushed the gates of Hades.

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn of the Resurrection, Tone 4:*

By Thine own free counsel Thou didst endure crucifixion, O Savior, and mortal men laid Thee in a new tomb, Who by a word didst establish the ends [of the earth]; therefore the stranger was bound, death was grievously despoiled, and all those in Hades cried out unto Thy life-bearing arising: Christ the Giver of life is risen, abiding unto the ages!

*Another Sessional Hymn:*

Gabriel descended from the heights of heaven, and approaching the stone where the Rock of life lay, appearing in radiant white, he cried unto the weeping women: Cease from the cry of lamentation, ye who possess ever tender compassion; for Whom ye seek weeping, be of good courage, for He is truly risen; therefore cry out unto the Apostles that the Lord is risen!

*Theotokion:*

Joseph marvelled, beholding that which was beyond nature [^79] [f. 61A] and brought to mind the rain upon the fleece, in thy seedless conception, O Mother of God; the bush unconsumed by fire, Aaron's rod that blossomed; and thy betrothed and guardian, bearing witness, cried unto the priests: A Virgin bears a Child, and after childbirth remains a Virgin still!

*After the Evlogetaria, Hypakoe, Tone 4:*

Going before, the Myrrh-bearing women proclaimed Thy glorious arising unto the Apostles, O Christ: that Thou art risen as God, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Anabathmoi, Antiphon 1, Tone 4:*

From my youth many passions war against me; but do Thou Thyself help and save me, O my Savior.

Ye that hate Zion, be put to shame by the Lord; for ye shall be dried up by fire as grass.

By the Holy Spirit every soul is given life, and is exalted in purity; it is illumined by the Trinitarian Unity in sacred mystery.

*Antiphon 2:*

I have cried unto Thee fervently, O Lord, from the depth of my soul; and let Thine ears be attentive unto me [f. 61A] [even] Thine divine ears.

Everyone who possesses hope in the Lord is higher than all who grieve.

By the Holy Spirit streams of grace gush forth, watering all creation unto life.

*Antiphon 3:*

Let my heart be lifted up unto Thee, O Word, and let nothing of the world's beauties entice me unto weakness.

As anyone has love toward his mother, a warmer love ought we to have toward the Lord.

By the Holy Spirit is the wealth of divine knowledge, contemplation, and wisdom; for in Him the Word reveals all the Father's decrees.

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*

Arise, O Lord, help us, and deliver us for Thy name's sake!

*Verse: O God, with our ears have we heard...*

*Let every breath praise the Lord.*

*Glory: Through the prayers of the holy Apostles... Both now: Through the prayers of the Most Pure Lady...*

*Canon of the Resurrection, Tone 4.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos:* (fol. 62)[^80]

Traversing the deep of the Red Sea with dryshod feet, Israel of old, by Moses' hands stretched out in the sign of the Cross, conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Troparion:*

Thou wast lifted up upon the immaculate Tree of the Cross, setting upright our fall, and healing ancient total destruction, O Master, for Thou art good and almighty.

*Troparion:*

In the tomb bodily, in Hades with the soul as God, in Paradise with the thief, and on the throne wast Thou, O Christ, with the Father and the Spirit, filling all things, Thyself uncircumscribed.

*Theotokion:*

Seedlessly, by the Father's will and from the Divine Spirit, having conceived the Son of God, thou didst bear Him in the flesh, Who is from the Father without mother, and for our sake from thee without father.

*Second Canon [Cross-Resurrection], Heirmos: I shall open my mouth...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst heal the crushing of mankind, O Lord; by Thy divine Blood Thou didst renew it, and didst crush him that was mighty in strength, who of old had crushed Thy creation.

*Troparion:*

Arising came unto the dead on account of Thy putting to death; for strength was taken away from death when it contended with eternal Life, which reigns over all in God incarnate...

[f. 61A] *Theotokion:*

...Fair beyond the heavenly hosts was Thine animate divine temple, having borne in the womb, O Virgin, the holy Mountain of our God.

*Another Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos, Heirmos: The captains of three ranks...*

*Troparion:*

Peoples shook, nations were troubled, and mighty kingdoms bowed down, O Pure One, from the fear of thy childbearing; for my King came, cast down the tormentor, and delivered the world from corruption.

*Troparion:*

Showing forth His dwelling on high, coming down unto men, O Christ, Thou didst sanctify and reveal it unshakable; for she alone after childbirth preserved the virginal vessel, having borne the Creator.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying: Thou art my strength, O Lord, my refuge and my firm support!*

*Troparion:*

The Tree of life, the noetic true Vine, hangs upon the Tree, pouring forth incorruption unto all.

*Troparion:*

How great, how fearful! having overthrown the haughtiness of Hades, and as incorrupt God, He is now bodily risen!

*Theotokion:*

Thou alone for those on earth wast shown to be the Mediatress of supernatural goods, O Mother of God; therefore we bring unto thee "Rejoice!"

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Establish thy singers, O Mother of God...* (fol. 63)[^81]

*Troparion:*

The serpent full of poison drove his teeth into me, O Savior; but Thou, O Almighty Lord, didst shatter them with the nails of Thy hands; for there is none holy among the saints save Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Being willingly seen dead in the tomb, O Lover of mankind and Giver of life, Thou didst fling wide the gates of Hades unto the souls from the ages; for there is none holy among the saints save Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*

An unplowed furrow wast thou revealed, having brought forth the living Ear of grain that mediates immortality unto all that partake of Him Who is Holy among the saints, resting in holiness.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: He descended from on high...*

*Troparion:*

The essence of earthborn is cleansed, being joined through thee unto the unendurable Divine Fire; as upon embers, O Most Pure Virgin, the Bread was baked within thee, by Him Who preserved thee unharmed.

*Troparion:*

Who is this that is truly so close unto God, having surpassed all the angelic orders, alone by the beauty of virginity, shining as Mother of the Almighty?

[f. 61A] *Ode 4. Heirmos: Beholding Thee, the Sun of righteousness, lifted up...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst ascend the Cross, healing my passions by the passion of Thine immaculate flesh, wherewith Thou didst willingly clothe Thyself; therefore we cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Death, tasting of Thy sinless, life-creating Body, was deservedly put to death; and we cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Theotokion:*

Without bridegroom didst thou bear, O Virgin, and after childbearing thou wast shown to be a virgin still; therefore with unceasing voices, with unhesitating faith we cry unto thee, O Sovereign Lady: Rejoice!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Perceiving the inscrutable divine counsel...*

*Troparion:*

Israel, which had received the law, knew Thee not to be God Who had given the law, O Christ; but transgressing the law, like a lawless one they nailed Thee to the Cross, they that were unworthy of the giving of the law.

*Troparion:*

Thy deified soul, O Savior, despoiled the treasures of Hades, and raised the souls from the ages; [^82] [f. 64A] and Thy life-bearing Body poured forth incorruption unto all.

*Theotokion:*

Thee, the truly only Ever-Virgin Mother of God, we glorify, whom the unconsumed burning bush prefigured unto Moses the God-beholder, O Most Pure Lady, having joined with fire.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos], the same:*

*Troparion:*

He lived among men; He Who is invisible was seen in the form of incomprehensible Godhead; and taking form from thee, O Virgin, He delivers from the stranger those who acknowledge thee as the pure Mother of God.

*Troparion:*

The Virgin received materially the Immaterial One in the midst of matter, Who became an Infant from her; therefore in two natures is one known: God bearing flesh, and earthly, yet above nature.

*Theotokion:*

He Who dwelt in thee, the Virgin, preserved thee a virgin after childbirth; and born seedlessly, the Word and God remained, and in childbearing preserved thee a Virgin, as Master and Creator of all creation.

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Thou, my Lord, art come as a light into the world: a holy light turning from gloomy ignorance those who hymn Thee with faith.*

*Troparion:*

Thou, O Lord, didst mercifully come down unto the earth; Thou didst lift up fallen human nature [f. 64A] being lifted up upon the Tree.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst take away from me the condemnation of transgression, O Christ; Thou didst loose the pangs of death, O Compassionate One, by Thy divine Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

Thee do we set forth as an invincible weapon against enemies; thee have we acquired as firm support and hope of our salvation, O Bride of God!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: All things marvelled at thy divine [memory]...*

*Troparion:*

Abyssal Hades received Thee wholly with its jaws; for seeing Thee nailed to the Cross, pierced by a spear, breathless, the simple one supposed the living God to be a mere man; but having tried the strength of Thy Godhead, he understood.

*Troparion:*

The tomb and Hades, having divided the destroyed temple of Thy Body, O Lover of mankind, are both unwillingly judged and despoiled: the one indeed sending forth the souls of Thy saints, and the other their bodies unto immortality.

*Theotokion:*

Behold, now is the prophetic utterance fulfilled; for thou, O unwedded Virgin, didst receive in thy womb God Who is over all, and didst bear the timeless Son, Who grants peace unto all that hymn thee.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: Now shall I arise, [the Lord said]...*

*Troparion:* (fol. 65)[^83]

The Son of God, having dwelt within thee, O Pure One, made thee a house of glory, the holy Mountain of God, bride, bridal chamber, temple of sanctification, and delightful, everlasting Paradise for us.

*Troparion:*

From the virginal blood Thou didst take flesh, O Christ, seedless, immaculate, and hypostatic, endowed with word and mind, animate, active and endowed with will, sovereign and self-determining.

*Theotokion:*

The virginal womb put to shame the understanding of tyrants; for the Infant examined with His hand the soul-destroying bite of the asp, and cast down the proud apostate, subduing him under the feet of the faithful.

*Ode 6. Heirmos: I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries out unto Thee, having been cleansed from the blood of demons by the Blood that flowed in mercy from Thy side.*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst ascend the Cross, girded with power, and grappling with the tormentor as God, Thou didst cast him down from on high; and Adam Thou didst raise up by Thine invincible power.

*Troparion:*

Radiantly beautiful, Thou didst arise from the tomb, O Christ, and didst scatter all Thine enemies by Thy divine [f. 64A] power; and as God Thou didst fill all things with joy.

*Theotokion:*

O wonder, of all wonders the newest! For the Virgin, having conceived without knowing man, unconfinedly contained in her womb Him Who holds all things.

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: I came into the depths [of the sea]...*

*Troparion:*

Hades opened its throat and swallowed me, and the senseless one expanded its soul; but Christ having descended, bring up my life, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Death perished by death; for He that had died arose, granting incorruption unto me; and the Immortal One appeared unto us, proclaiming joy.

*Theotokion:*

Thy pure womb was revealed as the receptacle of the unendurable Godhead, O Mother of God, upon Whom the heavenly ranks cannot gaze without fear.

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos], the same:*

*Troparion:*

Of old the serpent deceived me and put me to death through my foremother Eve; but now, O Pure One, He Who created me called me back from corruption through thee.

*Theotokion:*

An abyss of abysses, of ineffable tender compassion, did He show thee forth, O chosen Maiden of wonders; for from thee Christ, the Pearl, shone forth with the lightning of the Godhead.

*Kontakion, Tone 4.*

*Podoben: "Thou hast appeared today" (Явился еси):* [^84]

My Savior and Redeemer, as God, having raised the earthborn from bonds from the tomb, and crushed the gates of Hades, arose on the third day as Master!

*Ikos:*

The Giver of life, Christ, Who arose from the dead on the third day from the tomb, and today crushed the gates of death by His power, and put Hades to death, and blunted the sting of death, and set Adam free with Eve: let all us earthborn hymn, eagerly crying out praise! For He, as the only mighty God and Master, arose on the third day!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: In the furnace the Abrahamite children, burned by the desire of piety rather than by the Persian flame, cried out: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!*

*Troparion:*

Unto incorruption was mankind recalled, washed by the divine Blood of Christ, gratefully singing: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

As life-bearing, as truly fairer than Paradise, and more radiant than any royal chamber, O Christ, was Thy tomb revealed: the fountain of our resurrection! [f. 64A] divine and holy dwelling of the Most High! Rejoice, for through thee joy is given, O Mother of God, unto those who cry unto thee: Blessed art thou among women, all-immaculate Lady!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: He Who bears all things...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst humble, being lifted up upon the Tree, the lofty eye and the exalted brow, casting them down unto the earth, and Thou didst save man! O supremely hymned Lord and God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

By Thy power, Thou didst exalt our horn, unto us who give ear unto Thee, Who didst arise from the dead and in Thy great love for mankind didst empty the wealth of Hades! O supremely hymned Lord and God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

Following the divine decrees, we glorify the one Godhead as in three Lights, unconfused, indivisible, an everlasting flame enlightening all creation that cries: O supremely hymned Lord and God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: The three youths in Babylon...*

*Troparion:*

Virginal love draws me unto singing, the fire which is in my heart, to cry unto the Mother: Rejoice, blessed one, the Lord of hosts is with thee!

*Troparion:*

Thou wast revealed higher than creation, having borne the Creator (fol. 67)[^85] and Lord; therefore I cry unto thee: O blessed Mother of God, the Lord of hosts is with thee!

*Triadikon:*

Honoring Thee as one Lordship in three illuminations, indivisibly I hymn the Tri-hypostatic Nature: O Blessed One, to praise Thee Who governest all things!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Daniel, stretching out his hands, stopped the open mouths of the lions in the pit; and the Children, lovers of piety, girded about with virtue, quenched the power of the fire, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!*

*Troparion:*

Stretching out Thy hands upon the Cross, Thou didst gather all the nations unto Thyself, and as Master Thou didst reveal one Church, earthly and heavenly, singing unto Thee, in harmony crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

White-robed appeared the Angel unto the women, flashing with the inaccessible light of the Resurrection: Why do ye seek the Living One in the tomb as dead? he cried; truly Christ is risen, unto Whom we cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

Thou alone in all generations, O Most Pure Virgin, wast shown to be the Mother [f. 64A] of God; thou becamest the dwelling of the Godhead, all-immaculate one, unconsumed by the fire of the unapproachable Light; therefore all of us bless thee, O Mary, Bride of God!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: The godly youths in the furnace...*

*Troparion:*

Beholding Thine unjust slaying, creation darkened and lamented; for as the earth was shaken, the sun was clothed in darkness as in a crimson robe; but we sing of Thee, O Christ, and supremely exalt Thee unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Descending unto me even unto Hades, and making the Resurrection a way for all, Thou didst ascend again, taking me upon Thy shoulders, and didst bring me unto the Father; therefore I cry unto Thee: Works, sing the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Triadikon:*

We glorify the first Mind and Cause of all, the Father alone, Who has no cause; and the Word Who is co-unoriginate, and the Comforter Spirit, the one God of all, bowing down before the equally unoriginate Trinity, and supremely exalting It unto all ages!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: Almighty Redeemer of all...*

*Troparion:*

He Who fashioned the frame of Adam is incarnate from thy virginity, the Lord of all, Whom hymning (fol. 68)[^86] we cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

In the tent Abraham beheld the mystery concerning thee, O Mother of God; for he received thy bodiless Son, singing: All works, bless the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Triadikon:*

The prefiguration of thy virginity saved those equal in number to the Trinity; for in virginal bodies they trampled the flame, O Maiden, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A cornerstone uncut by hands was cut out from thee, an uncut mountain, O Virgin, even Christ, Who joined together the separated natures; therefore rejoicing, we magnify thee, O Mother of God!*

*Troparion:*

Whole as I was, Thou didst wholly assume me in unconfused union, granting salvation unto my whole being, O my God, through Thy Passion, which Thou didst endure bodily upon the Cross, by reason of Thy manifold tender compassion!

*Troparion:*

Thy Disciples, beholding Thy tomb opened and the God-bearing Shroud [Plashchanytsia] emptied by Thy Resurrection, with the Angel cried aloud: Truly the Lord is risen [f. 64A] *Triadikon:*

...Bowing down before the unity of the divine essence, yet Three Persons of the Trinity, in unconfused Hypostases, all we faithful now piously magnifying glorify the equal in power and equally honored!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: The Savior, born of earth...*

*Troparion:*

Deceitfully creeping, the serpent dragged me captive from Eden; but the Almighty Lord, crushing the adversary upon the firm Rock of Cranium [Calvary] as if an infant, through the Tree of the Cross opened unto me an entrance into the sweetness [of Paradise]!

*Troparion:*

Having now laid waste the strong fortresses of the enemy, and plundered his wealth by Thine all-powerful hand, Thou didst raise me up with Thyself, O Christ, from the depths of Hades; and him that of old boasted immeasurably, Thou didst show to be an object of derision and mockery!

*Troparion:*

Come, visiting the affliction of Thy poor people, and by Thy merciful and mighty right hand strengthen the Cross-bearing King against the boastful enemies, and protect Thy choice heritage, as Thou art the Lover of mankind!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: The mystery of God, O Most Pure One...*

*Troparion:* (fol. 69)[^87]

We behold thee, O Most Pure One, as a lily adorned with a vesture dyed in the purple of the Divine Spirit, shining in the midst of thorns and filling with fragrance those who sincerely magnify thee!

*Troparion:*

Having received corruptible earthly nature from thine all-immaculate womb, the Incorruptible One showed it forth incorruptible within Himself, by reason of His tender compassion; therefore we magnify thee as the Mother of God!

*Troparion:*

O thou that rulest over all creation, grant unto thy people victorious triumph, overthrowing the adversary, O Reconciler of the Church, that we may magnify thee as the Mother of God!

*At the Praises, Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron 1:*

O almighty Lord, Who didst endure crucifixion and death, and didst arise from the dead: we glorify Thy Resurrection!

*Sticheron 2:*

By Thy Cross, O Christ, Thou didst deliver us from the ancient curse; and by Thy death Thou didst bring to naught the devil who tormented our nature; and by Thine arising Thou didst fill all things with joy; therefore we cry unto Thee: O Thou Who didst arise from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

[f. 64A] *Sticheron 3:*

By Thy Cross, O Christ our Savior, guide us unto Thy truth, and deliver us from the snares of the enemy, O Thou Who didst arise from the dead: raise us up who have fallen into sin, stretching out Thy hand, O Lord, Lover of mankind, by the entreaties of Thy saints!

*Sticheron 4:*

Without departing from the Father's bosom, O only-begotten Word of God, Thou didst come down unto the earth for the sake of Thy love for mankind, having become man without change; and Thou didst endure crucifixion and death in the flesh, while remaining passionless in Thy Godhead; and having arisen from the dead, Thou didst grant immortality unto the human race, as Thou alone art almighty!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian [Eastern], the same Tone:*

*Sticheron 5:*

Thou didst submit unto death in the flesh, mediating immortality for us, O Savior; and Thou didst take up Thine abode in the tomb, that Thou mightest free us from Hades, raising us up with Thyself; Thou didst suffer indeed as man, but Thou didst arise as God; therefore we cry: Glory to Thee, O Lord, Giver of life, only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 6:*

The rocks split asunder, O Savior, when Thy Cross was planted upon Calvary; the gatekeepers of Hades were terrified when Thou wast laid in the tomb as dead (fol. 70)[^88]; for Thou didst bring to naught the power of death, and Thou didst grant incorruption unto all the dead by Thy Resurrection, O Christ, Giver of life, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

*Sticheron 7:*

The women desired to see Thy Resurrection, O Christ God; Mary Magdalene went beforehand and came, and found the stone rolled away from the tomb, and an Angel sitting and saying: Why do ye seek the Living One with the dead? He is risen as God, that He might save all things!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron 8:*

Where is Jesus, Whom ye were appointed to guard? Say, O Jews! Where is He Whom ye laid in the tomb, having sealed the stone? Give up the dead man, ye who have rejected Life! Give up Him that was buried, or believe in Him that is risen! Even if ye hold your peace, the stones shall cry out the Lord's rising, especially that rolled away from the tomb! Great is Thy mercy, great is the mystery of Thy dispensation: O our Savior, glory to Thee!

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 4)

[f. 64A] *At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 4:*

*Troparion 1:*

On account of the tree was Adam banished from Paradise, but through the Tree of the Cross the thief took up his dwelling in Paradise; for the one by tasting rejected the commandment of Him that created him, while the other, crucified together with Thee, confessed Thee as the hidden God, crying: Remember me in Thy Kingdom!

*Verse: Remember us, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom.*

*Troparion 2:*

Having all of us the Cross of Christ in our minds, let us hasten to mortify worldly pleasures, making our soul a church unto the Holy Spirit, sending up the voice of the virtuous thief unto Him, we faithful: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*Verse: Remember us, O Master, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom.*

*Troparion 3:*

Let us faithful entreat Him Who arose from the dead, and plundered the dominion of Hades, and was seen by the Myrrh-bearing Women, saying: Rejoice! that He deliver our souls from corruption, ever crying unto Him with the voice of the wise thief: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*Verse: Remember us, O Holy One, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom.*

*Troparion 4:*

Unto Thy tomb, O Christ, the Myrrh-bearing Women came to anoint Thee, and finding not, O Good One, Thy life-bearing (fol. 71)[^89] and most pure Body, they were at a loss; weeping with tears, they sought Thee, but having seen Thee, O Master, their beloved One, they cried unto Thee: Remember us in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion 5:*

Truly Christ is risen, and the tomb bears witness, and unto you, O transgressors of the Law: for leaving His grave-clothes, He arose on the third day; the stone was sealed, and the guards stood before the tomb; Hades was plundered, death was slain, Christ was not stolen: believe with us in His Resurrection!

*Troparion 6:*

Having come unto the Disciples, and granting peace unto them, and assuring the doubting Thomas by the showing of Thy wounds: Thou didst raise up all the ends [of the earth], O Lord, to glorify Thee; and being now entreated, save those who cry, O Master: Remember us in Thy Kingdom!

*Glory:*

The Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, let all we faithful with one mind pray worthily to doxologize: the Unity of the Godhead existing in Three Persons, abiding unconfused, simple, indivisible, and unapproachable, whereby we are delivered [f. 64A] from fiery torment!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Thy Mother, O Christ, who bore Thee in the flesh seedlessly, and truly remained an incorrupt Virgin after childbearing: her we bring unto Thee in prayer, O exceedingly merciful Master, to grant forgiveness of sins unto those who ever cry unto Thee: Remember us also in Thy Kingdom!

*End of the Fourth Tone.*
