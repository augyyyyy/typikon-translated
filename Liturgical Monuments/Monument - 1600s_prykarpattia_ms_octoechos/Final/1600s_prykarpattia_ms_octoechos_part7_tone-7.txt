# TONE 7

## TONE 7 [^124]

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 7)

[f. 106A] *On Saturday Evening at Great Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 7:*

*Sticheron:*

Come, let us rejoice in the Lord, Who shattered the dominion of death and enlightened the human race, crying with the bodiless hosts: O our Creator and Savior, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Thou didst endure the Crucifixion and burial for our sake, O Savior; and by death as God Thou didst slay death. Therefore we worship Thy third-day Resurrection: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Beholding the Resurrection of the Creator, the Apostles marveled, singing angelic praise: this is the glory of the Church, this is the wealth of the Kingdom! O Thou Who didst suffer for our sake: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian, Same Tone:*

*Sticheron:*

Though Thou wast seized, O Christ, by lawless men, yet Thou art my God, and I am not ashamed [f. 106A] I was scourged on the back, and I do not deny it; I was nailed to the Cross, and I do not conceal it; I boast in Thy Resurrection. For Thy death is my life, O almighty Lover of mankind: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Fulfilling David's prophecy, O Christ, Thou didst reveal Thy majesty in Zion unto Thy disciples, showing Thyself praiseworthy and ever glorified together with the Father and the Holy Spirit: first indeed bodiless as the Word, but later incarnate for our sake as man, and risen with power as the Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:*

Thou didst descend into Hades, O Christ, as Thou didst will; and Thou didst overthrow death as God and Master, and didst arise on the third day, raising Adam with Thyself from the bonds and corruption of Hades, as he cried out, saying: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:*

Thou wast laid in the tomb as sleeping, O Lord; and Thou didst arise on the third day as mighty in strength, raising Adam with Thyself from the corruption of mortals, as almighty God!

*Glory. Both now. Theotokion:*

[f. 107A] Thou wast known as a Mother above nature, O Mother of God, and didst remain a Virgin above word and understanding; and the tongue cannot tell the wonder of thy childbirth! Glorious indeed is thy conception, O pure one, and incomprehensible is the manner of thy giving birth; for where God wills, the order of nature is overcome! Therefore, knowing thee all as the Mother of God, we pray thee fervently: intercede that our souls may be saved! [^125]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 7:*

*Sticheron:*

Risen from the tomb, O Savior of the world, Thou didst raise man with Thyself together with Thy flesh: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

Come, let us worship Him Who arose from the dead and enlightened all things; for from the tyranny of Hades He set us free by His third-day Resurrection, and granted us life and great mercy!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

Descending into Hades, O Christ, Thou didst despoil death, and rising on the third day, Thou didst raise us with Thyself, who glorify Thine almighty rising: O Lord, Lover of mankind!

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

Fearful didst Thou appear, O Lord, lying in the tomb as sleeping; but rising on the third day as mighty, Thou didst raise Adam with Thyself, who cried: Glory to Thy Resurrection [f. 107A] unto Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind! Glory. Both now.

*Theotokion:*

Fleeing under thy protection, O Lady, all of us earth-born cry aloud unto thee: O Mother of God, our hope, deliver us from our boundless transgressions, and save our souls! *Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart. Trisagion through Our Father.*

*Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 7:*

Thou didst destroy death by Thy Cross, and didst open Paradise unto the Thief; Thou didst transform the lamentation of the Myrrh-bearing women, and didst command the Apostles to preach that Thou art risen, O Christ God, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory. Both now. Theotokion:*

As the treasure of our Resurrection, O all-hymned one, lead up from the pit and depth of transgressions those who trust in thee; for having given birth to our Salvation, thou hast saved those guilty of sin: who before childbirth wast a Virgin, and in childbirth a Virgin, and after childbirth art still a Virgin!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 7)

*On Sunday at the Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity.*

*Ode 1, Tone 7. Heirmos: By Thy nodding upon earth, the form was transformed...*

*Troparion:*

Open my intelligible lips of the heart and material lips unto Thy praise, O thrice-radiant single Godhead of all, that I may sing a light-bestowing hymn of thanksgiving unto Thee!

[f. 108A] *Troparion:* [^126]

That Thou mightest show the exceeding abundance of Thy goodness, Thou didst create man, O Trinity of unassailable power, only as an earthen image of Thy primordial Masterhood, O Creator!

*Troparion:*

The unoriginate Father having begotten the co-everlasting Word, and the co-unoriginate Spirit having shone forth: grant us to worship the one God, identical in essence and three-hypostatic!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst appear unto Moses at the bush, O Word of God, as a cleansing fire that consumed it not at all, prefiguring Thy fleshly incarnation from the Virgin, wherewith Thou didst re-fashion mankind!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: In the beginning, the heavens by Thine almighty word...*

*Troparion:*

Hymning Thee, the Master, as single, thrice-shining, and all-creating, we ask deliverance from sins and trials, O God of unassailable power: do not overlook those who glorify Thy goodness in faith!

*Troparion:*

A shoot sprouted forth from the Father, as from an unoriginate root, God the Word, equal in power with the co-sprouting [f. 108A] and the divine Spirit; and therefore we the faithful glorify the Trinity in Persons, the one Lordship!

*Troparion:*

Equally glorified and co-sprouting, we the faithful all doxologize Thee, the three-hypostatic Nature indivisibly and divisibly, the monarchical Trinity; and bowing down, we ask forgiveness of transgressions!

*Theotokion:*

Inalterably Thou wast made like unto men in all things, O Word of God, openly coming forth from the pure Maiden; and Thou didst show unto all the thrice-shining and single Godhead, of inalterable Hypostases in essence!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

Have mercy upon Thy servants who have sinned, O Holy Trinity, and receive those who repent unto Thee, O Compassionate One, and vouchsafe forgiveness!

*Glory. Both now. Theotokion:*

Assuage our souls afflicted with sins, O all-holy Mother of God; deliver from transgressions those who hymn thee, O Bride of God!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

All-holding, thrice-radiant Unity, divine Principle and Savior of all: protect now Thy singers, and save them from sorrow, passions, and every affliction!

*Troparion:*

Through symbolic sayings of Thine incomprehensible [^127] [f. 109A] thrice-radiant Godhead, at a loss we sing unto Thee, O Lover of mankind, O Lord, and we glorify Thy power!

*Troparion:*

On earth as in heaven, together with the bodiless choirs, O Unity and Trinity, we divide Thee indivisibly, and with love we glorify Thee, as doing good unto all existing things!

*Theotokion:*

Without departing from the Father's glory, Thou didst willingly descend unto our lowliness: incarnate, O supersubstantial One, Thou didst raise us up unto divine glory, as Thou art compassionate!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Night is without light unto the unbelieving, O...*

*Troparion:*

O light-originating Nature, Trinity in hypostatic forms, and Unity in counsel, glory, and honor: establish us in Thy love!

*Troparion:*

Glorifying the Mind, the Word, and the Spirit, one divine-principled and thrice-solar Nature, we pray to be snatched away from trials and all afflictions!

*Theotokion:*

Taking form in human nature from the holy Virgin, O Word of God, Thou didst teach us to hymn the Trinity in Unity, of one form and co-enthroned!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Sailing in the surge of the billows of...*

*Troparion:*

The co-glorified dominion, and the single-principled [f. 109A] divine Principle: existing in three Hypostases, without confusion with one another we glorify, possessing difference only in the manner of subsistence of each personal property!

*Troparion:*

The intelligible ranks of angelic splendors praise Thee, the thrice-solar divine Principle; with whom also we with earthen lips, as the Creator of all things, hymn and faithfully glorify as one!

*Theotokion:*

By an incomprehensible word, the Word begotten of the Father the Sun, another Sun before the ages, shone forth at the end of times from the Virgin, and proclaimed the one incomprehensible God in three Persons!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

O consubstantial Trinity, three-hypostatic Unity: have mercy upon those whom Thou didst create, O Immortal One, burning up the malice of sinners, and enlightening the hearts of those who hymn Thy compassion: O our God, glory to Thee!

*Glory. Both now. Theotokion:*

Brighter than fire, more efficacious than light is the mercy of thy goodness, O Lady, burning up the sins of mankind, and bedewing the thoughts of those who praise thy wonders, O most immaculate Mother of God!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The Children of old showed the fiery furnace...*

[f. 110A] *Troparion:* [^128]

Ever shining with light-beaming flashes, O thrice-radiant God, unapproachable and supersubstantial Unity: save those who piously believe in Thee, O Master, and bow down before Thee!

*Troparion:*

Obeying the sayings of the divine Prophets, Thee alone as God we glorify in every way in three Persons, crying thus: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

With earthen lips together with the immaterial ranks we sing hymns unto Thee, O Holy Trinity, in unity of essence, crying: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

That He Who fashioned Adam might renew him again, He openly became man from thee, bringing mankind to life, who cry thus: Blessed, O all-pure one, is the fruit of thy womb!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Unconsumed by fire, communicating at Sinai...*

*Troparion:*

Vouchsafe the hearts of Thy singers to be enlightened with the light-bestowing rays of the thrice-shining Sun, and grant now to all who hymn Thy majesty in similar faith, ever to behold Thy beauty as far as is possible, O Unity-Trinity, unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Thou holdest all things, O Trinitarian and single [f. 110A] unoriginate Lordship; and Thou rulest both heaven and earth. Therefore, drawn ever by Thy love, preserve Thy singers to sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; hymn and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

By Thy grace make me a temple of Thy thrice-radiant dawn, O Lover of mankind; and show me unapproachable in participation and communion unto invisible enemies and fleshly passions, O Master, my single-principled God and Lord of glory, to hymn Thee, O God, unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

The divine-principled Light, shining forth from thy womb, O most pure Mother of God, illumined the whole world with thrice-solar light, and revealed the earth as another heaven, singing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; hymn and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A Mother unto God and a Virgin, giving birth and remaining a virgin...*

*Troparion:*

We earthen ones are unable worthily to praise and hymn Thee in elevated words, Whom the Seraphim on high unceasingly hymn; yet daring in every way as our Master, we magnify Thee as our most loving God!

*Troparion:*

Deliver from bodily diseases and spiritual attach- [^129] [f. 111A] -ments Thy faithful singers, O single, indivisible Trinity, and grant them to be preserved unharmed, delivering them from every worldly calamity!

*Troparion:*

O equal in power, divine-principled, thrice-radiant, almighty Dominion, inalterable beauty, substantial Goodness: grant remission of transgressions unto Thy servants, and deliverance from trials and passions!

*Theotokion:*

Having taken mind and soul and fleshly commingling from thy most pure womb, O Mother of God, God the Word in truth appeared as man, and openly revealed man as a partaker of the divine nature!

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 7)

*On Sunday at Matins, on "God is the Lord," Troparion, Tone 7: Thou didst destroy [death] by Thy Cross... Glory. Both now. Theotokion.*

*After the First Stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

While the tomb was sealed, Thou didst shine forth Life from the tomb, O Christ God; and while the doors were shut, Thou didst stand before the disciples, renewing an upright Spirit in us through them according to Thy great mercy!

*Glory:*

He Who by His three-day burial despoiled death, and by His life-bearing rising raised up corrupt man, O Christ God, as [f. 111A] the Lover of mankind: glory to Thee!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

As thou hast tender mercy for our help, look upon those on earth, upon helpless people; have mercy, O blessed Mother of God, remain praying that we perish not grievously; as thou art compassionate, pray the merciful God that our souls may be saved, O most holy Virgin!

*After the Second Stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

Life lay in the tomb, and seals lay upon the stone; as a sleeping King soldiers watched Christ, and Angels glorified Him as immortal God; and the women cried aloud: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory:*

Unto the tomb ran the women with tears, bearing sweet spices; and while soldiers were guarding the King of all, they said among themselves: Who will roll away the stone for us? The Angel of Great Counsel is risen, trampling down death: O almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Unceasingly pray Him Who was crucified for our sake, Christ our God, and Who cast down the dominion of death, O Virgin Mother of God, that He may save our souls!

*Hypakoe, Tone 7:*

[f. 112A] Thou Who didst take our form and endure the Crucifixion in the flesh: save us by Thy Resurrection, O Christ God, as the Lover of mankind! [^130]

*The Hymns of Degrees, Tone 7. Antiphon 1:*

*Troparion:*

Turning back the captivity of Zion from deception, quicken me also, O Savior, delivering me from the slavery of passions!

*Troparion:*

They that sow in the south with tears the sorrows of fasting, shall reap joyous sheaves of ever-living nourishment!

*Troparion:*

Through the Holy Spirit is the fountain of divine treasures, from Whom comes wisdom, understanding, and fear: unto Him be praise and glory, honor and dominion!

*Antiphon 2:*

*Troparion:*

Except the Lord build the house of the soul, in vain do we labor; for without Him neither deed nor word is accomplished!

*Troparion:*

Of the fruit of the womb, the saints, moved by the Spirit, sprout forth paternal traditions of adoption!

*Troparion:*

Through the Holy Spirit all things have their being; for before all He is God, the Lordship of all, unapproachable Light, and the Life of all!

*Antiphon 3:*

[f. 112A] *Troparion:*

They that fear the Lord, having found the way of life, now and ever are blessed with incorruptible glory!

*Troparion:*

Beholding thy offspring round about thy table like shoots, rejoice and be glad, bringing them unto Christ, the Chief Shepherd!

*Troparion:*

Through the Holy Spirit is a great depth of gifts, the wealth of glory, a great depth of judgments; for He is equally glorified with the Father and the Son, and co-worshipped!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 7: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up, forget not Thy poor unto the end. Verse: I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart.*

*Let every breath praise the Lord. Matins Resurrection Gospel. Psalm 50.*

*Resurrection Canon, Tone 7.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

By Thy nodding upon earth, the form was transformed, the nature of water which formerly flowed freely, O Lord; whence Israel passed dry-shod, and sings a song of victory unto Thee!

*Troparion:*

The tyranny of death was condemned by the tree, when Thou, O Lord, wast condemned to an unjust death; whence the prince of darkness, failing to prevail against Thee, was justly cast out!

[f. 113A] *Troparion:* [^131]

Hades drew near unto Thee, and not being able to crush Thy body with its teeth, shattered its own jaws; and Thou, O Savior, having loosed the pangs of death, didst arise on the third day!

*Theotokion:*

The sorrows of our foremother Eve were loosed; for having escaped pangs, without knowing man thou didst give birth; whence knowing thee openly as the Mother of God, O all-pure one, we all glorify thee!

*Another Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection, Same Tone.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos: In the deep He overwhelmed Pharaoh...*

*Troparion:*

Two life-bearing streams the Savior poured forth unto us upon the Cross from His pierced side; let us sing unto Him, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:*

Having lodged in the tomb, and risen on the third day, Christ granted hope of incorruption unto mortals; let us sing unto Him, for He is glorified!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst appear as the only Virgin even after childbirth; for thou didst bear incarnate the Creator of the world: Rejoice! we all cry unto thee.

*Another Canon to the Mother of God.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos: By Thy nod upon earth is fashioned the...*

*Troparion:*

O Virgin who didst bear the Abyss of compassion, enlighten my soul with thy light-bearing radiance, that I may worthily hymn the abyss of thy wonders!

*Troparion:*

Beholding those wounded by the arrow of sin, the Word [f. 113A] beholding them, as Benefactor had compassion; whence ineffably the all-divine One was united, O all-pure one, to the flesh taken from thee!

*Troparion:*

Mankind's nature became subject unto death, corruptible and mortal, O Lady; but having conceived Life, thou didst lead it up from corruption unto life!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

In the beginning having established the heavens by Thine almighty Word, O Lord Savior, and all their host by the all-creating and divine Spirit: establish me upon the immovable rock of Thy confession!

*Troparion:*

Ascending the tree, Thou didst willingly sorrow for our sake, O compassionate Savior, and didst endure wounding that intercedes for reconciliation and salvation unto the faithful; whereby, O Merciful One, we were all reconciled unto Thy Father!

*Troparion:*

Cleansing me from the wound, who was wounded in soul by the serpent's bite, O Christ, Thou didst show light unto me who of old lay in darkness and corruption; for descending into Hades by the Cross, Thou didst raise me with Thyself!

*Theotokion:*

By the prayers of Thy Mother who knew not man, grant peace unto the world [^132] [f. 114A] O Savior, and give victory unto the king over opposing enemies, and vouchsafe those who glorify Thee to partake of Thine ineffable glory!

*Another Canon. Heirmos:*

The flaming sword Thou didst quench with the blood that flowed from Thy side, O Lord, when lawless people nailed Thee to the tree of the Cross; whereby the Thief found Paradise, and I boast in hope of the Cross, that receiving remission of sins for its sake, O Christ, I may hymn Thee!

*Troparion:*

Risen from Thy tomb, O Christ, Thou didst first appear unto the women, and didst loose the sorrows of Eve; and Adam fallen into corruption Thou didst raise up with Thyself as compassionate God: therefore we all hymn Thy rising!

*Theotokion:*

The Lord Whom thou didst receive in a virginal womb, and Whom thou didst bear in the flesh, O pure one: through thy maternal prayers grant Him to be propitious unto us, His servants, as we know thee alone as a haven of compassion towards God!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: The serpent creeping out of Eden...*

*Troparion:*

The serpent creeping out of Eden, having deceived me through desire of deification, cast me down unto the earth; but He Who is merciful by nature and compassionate, had mercy [f. 114A] having compassion, He made me godlike, taking up His abode in thy womb, and became like unto me, O Mother Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O Virgin Mother of God, joy of all; for thou didst bear joy and true gladness unto all the world, which dispels the sorrow of sins, O Bride of God!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst bear eternal Life and Light and Peace unto us, O Mother of God, Virgin, calming through faith and confession of thy grace the ancient warfare of men towards God and the Godhead!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Without leaving the Father's bosom, and descending unto earth, O Christ God, I have heard the mystery of Thy dispensation, and I have glorified Thee, O only Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Offering His back unto wounds, He Who was incarnate from the Virgin, the guiltless Master, is scourged for the servant who sinned, loosing my transgressions!

*Troparion:*

Before the judgment seat, the lawless judge holds Him guilty as the condemned; and He is struck with an earthen hand, Who created man as God, and judges the earth in righteousness!

*Theotokion:*

As truly the Mother of God, pray Thy Creator and Son [^133] [f. 115A] O all-immaculate one, to direct me unto the saving haven of His glorious will!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: The Prophet, beholding the last things...*

*Troparion:*

Knowing no sin, yet for this cause Thou becamest, O Lord, what Thou wast not, taking what was alien unto Thyself, that Thou mightest save the world and slay the tyrant by deceit!

*Troparion:*

Thou wast lifted up upon the Cross, and didst loose the sin of Forefather Adam; on account of which I have heard of Thy power, that Thou didst come to save all Thine anointed ones!

*Theotokion:*

Proceeding from the mountain of the Pharan division, the Virgin was enrolled with the servants, delivering all from slavery, ruling unto the ages: therefore we glorify thee who gavest birth!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Without leaving the Father's bosom...*

*Troparion:*

Appearing all-elect and fair unto God before creation by thy brightness, O all-pure one, enlighten with light-bestowing grace those who hymn thee!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst bear God for mankind, O pure one, incarnate of thy pure blood, delivering from many transgressions those who glorify and honor thee with love, O Virgin Mother!

*Troparion:*

The rational nature celebrates a sacred ministry: shining [f. 115A] forth from thee, O all-hymned one, having now learned the ineffable mystery of thy childbirth, O most blessed one!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Night without light unto the faithless, O Christ, but unto the faithful enlightenment in enjoyment of Thy words; therefore early in the morning do I rise unto Thee, and hymn Thy Godhead!

*Troparion:*

For the sake of Thy servants Thou wast sold, O Christ, and Thou didst endure buffeting upon the cheek, interceding for freedom unto those who sing: Early in the morning do I rise unto Thee, and hymn Thy Godhead!

*Troparion:*

By Thy divine power, O Christ, through fleshly weakness Thou didst cast down the strong one; and Thou didst reveal Him as the conqueror of death, O Savior, for the sake of the Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear God, O pure Mother, divinely incarnate from thee, O all-hymned one; since thou knewest not male seed, but didst give birth from the Holy Spirit!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: O Lord my God, from the night I rise early unto Thee...*

*Troparion:*

When Thou wast reckoned among the lawless, being lifted up upon the Place of the Skull, the luminaries hid themselves, the earth quaked, and the temple veil was rent in twain, revealing the falling away of the Hebrews!

*Troparion:*

Thee Who didst destroy all the power of the tyrant by the might of Thine incomprehensible Godhead, and didst raise the dead [^134] [f. 116A] by Thy Resurrection, we glorify with hymns!

*Theotokion:*

O Mother of the King and God, all-hymned Mother of God: through thy prayers send down cleansing of transgressions unto those who ever praise thee in faith and love with hymns!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Night without light unto the faithless...*

*Troparion:*

Beholding the ladder fixed on high, Jacob was taught the figure of thee who knewest not wedlock; for through thee, O all-venerable Lady, God communicated with mankind!

*Troparion:*

Having now found eternal deliverance through thee, O Virgin, fervently we cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Bride of God! And having rejoiced in thy light, O all-pure one, with hymns we magnify thee!

*Troparion:*

Finding thee alone as a lily among thorns, shining with the brightness of purity and with the lights of virginity, O all-hymned one, the Bridegroom brought thee as a bride!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Sailing in the surge of worldly cares, sinking with the ship through sins, and cast before the soul-destroying beast, like Jonah I cry unto Thee, O Christ: Lead me up from the deadly deep!

[f. 116A] *Troparion:*

The imprisoned in Hades remembered Thee, the souls of the righteous who were left; and they prayed for salvation from Thee, which Thou, O Christ, hast granted by the Cross unto those in the nethermost parts, having come as the Tender-hearted!

*Troparion:*

Looking upon Thy living and not-made-with-hands temple, which was destroyed by sufferings, of old the choir of Apostles despaired; yet beyond hope having worshipped, they proclaimed Thy Resurrection everywhere!

*Theotokion:*

Who among men can declare the image of thine ineffable childbirth, O all-immaculate Virgin, Bride of God, for our sake? For God the uncircumscribed Word was united unto me, and became flesh from thee!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: I cried unto the Lord in my affliction...*

*Troparion:*

Being crucified, the Master shook all the earth, and as good healed its grievous afflictions!

*Troparion:*

Death was destroyed, and Hades was despoiled; for Christ is risen, making a path unto mankind by His Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

O most pure Lady, certain Hope: preserve those who in love hymn thee as the true Mother of God!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Sailing in the surge...*

*Troparion:*

The types of the Law and the prophecies of the Prophets clearly foretold thee, who wast to give birth [^135] [f. 117A] unto the Benefactor of all creation, O pure one, Who in many parts and in diverse manners hath shown goodness unto those who faithfully hymn thee!

*Troparion:*

Adam the first-formed, who became alienated of old by the counsel of the murderer of man, thou hast led back, O Bride of God, into the divine delights of Paradise, having given birth unto Him Who snatched us from transgression!

*Troparion:*

He Who by divine light and creative power framed and upholds all things, came forth from thy womb, O pure one, and shone with divine-originating rays upon those sitting in the darkness of death!

*Kontakion, Tone 7:*

No longer can the dominion of death hold mankind; for Christ came down, shattering and destroying its powers! Hades is bound; the Prophets rejoice with one accord, saying: The Savior hath appeared unto those in faith; come forth, ye faithful, unto the Resurrection!

*Ikos:*

The nethermost parts below trembled today, Hades and death, before the One of the Trinity; the earth shook, and the gatekeepers of Hades, seeing Thee [f. 117A] were terrified! And all creation, rejoicing with the Prophets, sings unto Thee a song of victory, unto our Deliverer God, Who hath now destroyed the power of death! Let us shout aloud and cry out unto Adam and unto those born of Adam: The Tree hath led him in again; come forth, ye faithful, unto the Resurrection!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Of old the Children showed the fiery furnace dew-bearing, hymning the one God and saying: O God of our fathers, supremely exalted, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

By the tree Adam is put to death, having willingly committed disobedience; but by Christ's obedience he is renewed again; for for my sake the Son of God, the supremely glorified, is crucified!

*Troparion:*

All creation hymns Thee, O Christ, risen from the tomb; for Thou, the supremely glorified Light, hast blossomed forth as Life unto those in Hades, the Resurrection of the dead, and unto those in darkness!

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, daughter of corruptible Adam; rejoice, only Bride of God; rejoice, through whom corruption was driven away, for thou didst bear God, Whom implore [^136] [f. 118A] O pure one, that we all may be saved!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: The Children of old showed the fiery furnace...*

*Troparion:*

O Thou Who on the tree of the Cross didst blunt the sting of sin, and didst tear up the handwriting of Adam's transgression by the spear of Thy side: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

O Thou Who wast pierced in the side, and by the sprinkling of divine blood didst cleanse the earth defiled by the blood of idols: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Trinity Hymn:*

Orthodoxly we theologize the indivisible Nature: God the unoriginate Father, and the Word and Spirit of the same dominion, crying: Blessed art Thou, O consubstantial Trinity!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: The Children did not burn in the furnace...*

*Troparion:*

Having thee as an adorned and gilded vessel, O Virgin, Thy Creator and Lord loved thee: the God of our fathers, supremely exalted and supremely glorified!

*Troparion:*

Isaiah of old was cleansed, O Maiden, having received the coal, seeing in figure and symbolically thy divine childbirth, [hymning] the God of our fathers, supremely exalted and supremely glorified!

*Troparion:*

Symbolic types of old [f. 118A] of thy divine childbirth beholding, the divine Prophets, joyfully singing, cried aloud: O God of our fathers, supremely exalted and supremely glorified!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Communing with the fire on Sinai without burning, the bush revealed God unto slow-tongued and heavy-voiced Moses; and the zeal of God showed the three Children unharmed singers in the fire: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Slain for the world, the all-pure spiritual Lamb brought to an end the sacrifices offered according to the Law, cleansing transgressions by His blood as God, of those who ever cry aloud: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Our flesh, which was assumed by the Creator, being incorruptible before the Passion, was made after the Passion and Resurrection untouched by corruption, and renews mortal man, who cries: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Theotokion:*

Thy precious and all-immaculate [giving birth], O Virgin, cleansed the foul and abominable defilement of the world, and became the cause of our reconciliation unto God, O all-pure one; therefore [^137] [f. 119A] thee, O Virgin, do we supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: The only unoriginate...*

*Troparion:*

Him Who willingly endured the Passion, and by His own desire was nailed unto the Cross, and shattered the power of Hades: praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye people, unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Him Who abolished the dominion of death, and rose with glory from the tomb, and saved the human race: praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye people, unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

The only Tender-hearted, the pre-eternal Word, Who in the last times was born of a Virgin, and loosed the ancient curse: praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye people, unto the ages!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Communing with the fire without burning...*

*Troparion:*

With the light of thy childbirth, in marvelous splendor thou didst enlighten the universe, O Birth-giver of God; thou bearest in thine arms the existing God, Who sanctifies the faithful who ever cry: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Piously thy womb hymns thee, O pure one, which held God ineffably incarnate, Who grants unto all the faithful the illumination of the knowledge of God, unto those who ever cry aloud: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

[f. 119A] By the flashings of thy light, make those who hymn thee light-bearing, O Mother Virgin who gavest birth to the Light; for thou wast shown to be the dwelling of Light, shining with light upon those who cry: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Having given birth without experience of corruption, and having lent flesh unto the all-craftsman Word, O Mother who knewest not man, Virgin Mother of God, vessel of the Uncontainable, dwelling-place of thine uncircumscribed Creator: thee do we magnify!

*Troparion:*

Let all the strange-minded who ascribe suffering unto the Godhead be put to shame; for the Lord of glory was crucified in the flesh, yet was not crucified in His divine nature, as in two natures we magnify the One!

*Troparion:*

Let those who do not believe in the resurrection of bodies go unto the tomb of Christ, unto the Teacher: for He was put to death, and rose again, the flesh of the Life-giver, in assurance of the final resurrection, the hope whereof we hold!

*Trinity Hymn:*

Honoring the Trinity not in Godhead, but in Persons; nor the Unity in Persons, but in Godhead: we cut off those who divide It, and we refute again those who dare [^138] [f. 120A] confusion concerning It: Whom we magnify!

*Another Canon. Heirmos: A Mother unto God and a Virgin, giving birth...*

*Troparion:*

Beholding in Christ the fleshly sufferings and the might of the Godhead, let them be ashamed who imagine one single nature; for as man indeed He dies, and as Creator of all He arises!

*Troparion:*

Myrrh unto the dead, but praise unto the Living; tears unto the dying, but a hymn unto the Life of all did ye women bring; and the herald of the Rising cried aloud, announcing the good tidings of Christ's Resurrection!

*Troparion:*

Light from Light, the Father's radiance of glory, shone forth ineffably; as in darkness unto human life Christ dawned, chasing away the gloom: Whom unceasingly we faithful magnify!

*Theotokion:*

I know no other God beside Thee, the Church cries unto Thee, Who hast chosen Thy Bride from the unbelieving nations; grant therefore salvation unto the faithful, O Word, through the prayers of her who bore Thee, as Thou art tender-hearted!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Same Heirmos.*

*Troparion:*

As the Mediatress of eternal joy and gladness hast thou ever appeared unto us, O Virgin Maiden, having borne the Deliverer, unto those who honor Him in truth and the divine Spirit, as God Who delivers!

[f. 120A] *Troparion:*

Singing, David thy forefather, O all-pure one, calls thee the holy divine Ark, which contained God beyond nature: Who sits in the Father's bosom; Whom unceasingly we faithful magnify!

*Troparion:*

Truly thou art higher than all creation, O Maiden, for bodily thou didst bear the Creator of all; therefore as the Mother of the only Master, thou bearest sovereign victory against all!

*At the Praises, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 7:*

*Sticheron:*

Christ is risen from the dead, having broken the bonds of death; announce, O earth, great joy: hymn the glory of God, ye heavens!

*Sticheron:*

Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ, let us worship the holy Lord Jesus, the only sinless One!

*Sticheron:*

We cease not worshipping Christ's Resurrection; for He hath saved us from our iniquities: holy is the Lord Jesus, Who revealed the Resurrection unto the world!

*Sticheron:*

What shall we render unto the Lord for all that He hath rendered unto us? For our sake God is among men; for corrupted nature the Word became flesh and dwelt among us; unto the ungrateful the Benefactor, unto the captives the Liberator, unto those sitting in darkness the Sun of righteousness, upon the Cross the Immortal [^139] [f. 121A] One, in Hades the Light, in death the Life, the Resurrection for the sake of the fallen: unto Whom let us cry aloud: O our God, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera, Eastern:*

*Sticheron:*

The gates of Hades Thou didst shatter, O Lord, and abolish the dominion of death by Thy mighty power; and Thou didst raise up the dead who slept from the ages in darkness, by Thy divine and glorious Resurrection: O our God, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Come, let us rejoice in the Lord, and let us be glad in His Resurrection; for He raised the dead together with Himself from the unbreakable bonds of Hades, and as God granted unto the world eternal life and great mercy!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

*Sticheron:*

Shining brightly, the Angel sat upon the stone of Thy life-receiving tomb, and announced good tidings unto the myrrh-bearing women, saying: The Lord is risen, as He told you before; announce unto His disciples that He goes before you into Galilee, and grants unto the world eternal life and great mercy!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron:*

Why have ye set at nought the Chief Cornerstone, O law-transgressing Jews? Behold, this is [f. 121A] He Whom God placed in Zion: Who poured forth water from the rock in the wilderness, and pours forth immortality unto us from His side; this is the Stone that was cut without male desire from the virginal mountain: the Son of Man coming upon the heavenly clouds unto the Ancient of Days, as Daniel said, and His Kingdom is eternal!

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 7)

*At the Liturgy, The Beatitudes, Tone 7:*

*Troparion:*

Fair was the fruit and good for food that slew me; Christ is the Tree of Life, eating of which I die not, but cry with the Thief: Remember me, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

Thou wast lifted up upon the Cross, O Compassionate One, and didst blot out the handwriting of Adam's ancient sin, and saved all the human race from deception; therefore we hymn Thee, O Benefactor Lord!

*Troparion:*

Laid in the tomb as dead, O Christ, Life of all: Thou didst shatter the bars of Hades, and rising in glory on the third day as mighty, Thou didst enlighten all: glory to Thy rising!

*Troparion:*

By Thy rising Thou didst raise up Adam with Thyself [^140] [f. 122A] O Christ Giver of life, granting the ancient incorruption that was in Paradise; therefore I cry with the Thief: Remember me, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

Risen on the third day from the tomb, the Savior granted life, as He said, peace unto His disciples; and having blessed them, He sent them forth, saying: Bring all into My Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

The evil serpent once poured venom into the ears of Eve; but Christ, on the sharp wood of the Cross, poured forth unto the world the abundance of sweetness; therefore we cry: Remember me who cry out in Thy Kingdom!

*Glory. Trinity Hymn:*

The Father is Light, the Son and Word is Light, the Holy Spirit is Light; yet the three are one Light; for God is one in three Persons indeed, yet in one Nature and Principle, indivisible and unconfused, abiding pre-eternal!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear the Son and the Father's Word in the flesh for our sake, as He Himself knows, O Mother of God; therefore, O Virgin Mother, having been made godlike through thee, we cry: Rejoice! unto thee, the boast of Christians!
