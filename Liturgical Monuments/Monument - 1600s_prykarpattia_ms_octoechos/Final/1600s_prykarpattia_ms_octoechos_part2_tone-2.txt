# TONE 2

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 2)

*On Saturday Evening, at Great Vespers (fol. 21).*[^37]

*On "Lord, I have cried," Resurrection Stichera, Tone 2:*

Come, let us worship God the Word, begotten of the Father before the ages, incarnate of the Virgin Mary; for having endured the Crucifixion, He was committed unto burial as He Himself willed; and rising from the dead, He saved me, the wandering man.

Christ our Savior, being nailed upon the Cross, tore up the handwriting that was against us, and abolished the dominion of death: we worship His third-day rising.

With the Archangels let us hymn the Resurrection of Christ; for He is the Redeemer and Savior of our souls, and in fearful glory and mighty power He shall come again to judge the world which He created.

*Other Stichera, Anatolian, in the same Tone:*

The Angel proclaimed Thee, the Master, Who wast crucified and buried, and said unto the women: "Come and see where the Lord lay; for He is risen, as He said...

[Sunday.]

...as He is almighty; therefore we worship Thee, the only Immortal One: O Life-Giver Christ, remember us!"

By Thy Cross Thou didst abolish the curse of the Tree; by Thy burial Thou didst put to death the dominion of death; and by Thy rising Thou didst enlighten the human race; therefore we cry unto Thee: O Benefactor, Christ our God, glory to Thee!

The gates of death were opened unto Thee for fear, O Lord, and the gatekeepers of Hades, seeing Thee, were terrified; for Thou didst shatter the brazen gates and crush the iron bars; and Thou didst lead us out of darkness and the shadow of death, and didst break our bonds asunder.

Singing a saving hymn, from our mouths we send up praise: Come, all ye, into the house of the Lord, and let us fall down, saying: O Thou Who wast crucified upon the Tree, and didst rise from the dead, and art in the bosom of the Father, cleanse our sins!

*Glory, both now. Dogmatic Theotokion, Tone 2:*

The shadow of the Law passed away when grace came; for as the Bush burned not when set on fire, so a Virgin gave birth, and remained a Virgin: instead of a pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness shone forth (fol. 22);[^38] instead of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls.

*At the Aposticha, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 2:*

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, enlightened the whole universe, and recalled Thine own creation: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty (Ps. 92:1):*

By the Tree, O Savior, Thou didst abolish the curse of the tree, and by Thy burial Thou didst put to death the dominion of death; and Thou didst enlighten our race by Thy rising; therefore we cry unto Thee: O Life-Giver, Christ our God, glory to Thee!

*For He has established the world, which shall not be moved (Ps. 92:1):*

Appearing nailed upon the Cross, O Christ, Thou didst change the beauty of creation; and the soldiers showing inhumanity pierced Thy side with a spear; and the Jews asked to seal the Tomb, not knowing Thy power; but in Thine immeasurable compassion Thou didst accept burial, and didst rise on the third day: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days (Ps. 92:5):*

O Life-Giver Christ, Thou didst endure the voluntary Passion for the sake of the dead; and descending into Hades as mighty...

[Sunday.]

...Thou didst snatch away those who there awaited Thy coming, as one mighty from the ages; and Thou didst grant them to dwell in Paradise instead of Hades; therefore unto us also, who glorify Thy third-day rising, grant cleansing of sins and great mercy.

*Glory, both now. Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 2:*

O new marvel, surpassing all ancient marvels! For who has known a Mother to give birth without a husband, and to bear in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? It is the Word of God Who was born, Whom as an Infant having borne in thine arms, O Most Pure Lady, and possessing maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to entreat Him for those who honor thee, to have compassion and save our souls.

*Resurrection Troparion, Tone 2:*

When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal, then didst Thou put Hades to death with the brilliance of the Godhead; and when Thou didst raise the dead from the nethermost parts, all the Heavenly Powers cried aloud: O Life-Giver, Christ our God, glory to Thee!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion, Tone 2:*

All thy mysteries are beyond comprehension, all are all-glorious, O Mother of God: sealed in purity and preserved in virginity, thou wast acknowledged to be a true Mother, having borne the true God; entreat Him that our souls may be saved.

*On Sunday, at the Midnight Office (fol. 23).*[^39]

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 2)

*Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, Tone 2.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos: He Who submerged of old in the deep...*

*Troparion:*

Let us hymn with songs the Tri-hypostatic and monarchic Nature of the Godhead, saying: Possessing an inexhaustible, substantial abyss of mercy, have compassion and save those who worship Thee, as Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Being the Fountain and Root, the Father as Cause of the co-eternal Godhead in the Son and His Holy Spirit: pour forth the Tri-solar Light into my heart, and by participation illumine me with the Divine-working radiance.

*Troparion:*

O Tri-radiant Unity of Divine origin, destroy utterly the gloom of my sins and passions by Thy radiant rays, and through sweet participations make me a truly pure temple of Thine unapproachable glory.

*Theotokion:*

The ancient corruption of our nature, which had suffered formlessness and slipped toward corruption...

[Sunday.]

...O Most Pure Lady: God the Word, incarnate from thy womb, shone forth in love for mankind, and mystically taught us the Tri-radiant Divine Principle.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Establish me upon the rock of faith: the...*

*Troparion:*

In equality of nature, the Persons glorify Thee with equal honor as Divine Principle: for Life having proceeded from Life, without Thee there is no other Who is our God, and there is none holy besides Thee, O Lord.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst establish the immaterial and heavenly orders as mirrors of Thy goodness, O Trinity, unceasingly to hymn Thee as undivided Monarchy; yet receive also now praise from our mortal lips.

*Troparion:*

Establish upon the rock of faith and broaden with the abyss of Thy love the hearts and minds of Thy servants, O Tri-solar Unity; for Thou art our God, trusting in Whom we are not put to shame.

*Theotokion:*

He Who before brought every creature into substance was substantialized in thy womb by ineffable goodness, O Mother of God; and the Tri-solar Light of the One Godhead and Lordship shone forth unto all.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 2 (fol. 24):*[^40]

O Holy Trinity, have mercy on those whom Thou hast created, and deliver them from the fire and from all condemnation, as Thou canst do all things as a compassionate and abundant-in-mercy God; therefore we fall down before Thee, crying: We have sinned, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

Thy servants are filled with many wounds from the invisible arrows of the destroyer; and lying upon the bed of despair, we cry aloud, O Sovereign Lady: Despise not the prayer of us who flee unto thee in faith!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: I hymn Thee, having heard, O Lord, Thy...*

*Troparion:*

Not even the immaterial angelic ranks are able to comprehend Thee, O Unoriginate Unity and Trinity; yet with mortal tongue we hymn Thy substantial goodness with fear, and faithfully glorify Thee.

*Troparion:*

O Creator of creation, Almighty Lord of human nature: Thou seest now all my weakness, as All-Seeing One; therefore have compassion on Thy servant, and lead me back unto a better life.

*Troparion:*

We hymn the three unconfused Persons of the Primal Unity, possessing Their proper characteristics and divided in Hypostases, yet united and undivided...

[Sunday.]

...in counsel, glory, and Godhead.

*Theotokion:*

The Creator found thee alone manifestly to be a pure and all-blameless human temple, O Ever-Virgin Mother of God; taking up His abode in it, He refashioned human nature, as Lover of mankind.

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Enlightenment of those lying in darkness: as the...*

*Troparion:*

Bestowing peace in every way upon all creation through Thy providence, and stretching forth saving rays: O King of peace, preserve us in Thy peace, for Thou art life and peace unto all things.

*Troparion:*

When Thou didst appear unto Moses in the bush in a fiery vision, O Word of the Father, Thou wast called the Angel, prefiguring Thy coming unto us; whereby Thou didst again announce unto all the tri-hypostatic dominion of the One Divine Origin.

*Troparion:*

O Holy Monarchy and Trinity, preserving the natural and co-eternal glory: vouchsafe those who hymn Thee in Orthodox faith to behold Thine unoriginate glory and the one Tri-solar Radiance.

*Theotokion:*

God the Word, Who substantially upholds all the ages (fol. 25),[^41] was ineffably contained in thy womb, O Virgin Mother, calling men unto the unity of the One Lordship.

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Held fast in the abyss of sin, O...*

*Troparion:*

Mother of the Creator, [entreat Him:] have mercy on those who believe in Thee, O Tri-solar God, and deliver from transgressions, and passions and distresses; save Thy servants.[^42]

*Troparion:*

Through the ineffable abyss of goodness, grant me the light-giving Radiance of Thine inconceivable illumination and Tri-radiant Godhead.

*Theotokion:*

Ineffably, O Virgin, the Most High became man from thee, being clothed in all of man; and illumine me with the Tri-solar Light.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*

O Christ God, only All-Good One, Who didst shed Thy Blood for our sake when suspended upon the Cross by the counsel of Thy Father and the Spirit: therefore taking boldness we cry: Have mercy on Thy flock, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

O Pure Virgin, have mercy on thy servants, and deliver from fire and every torment those who cry night and day...

[Sunday.]

...unto Thy Son, O Most Pure Lady; despise not thy flock. Through the prayers of her who bore Thee, O Christ, [save us]!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: Concerning the golden image on the plain of...*

*Troparion:*

Thou dost ever establish the angelic hosts in immutability, O Lord, Who art One, immutable and tri-hypostatic: establish therefore my heart also ever immutable, warmly to glorify Thee and blessedly to hymn Thee.

*Troparion:*

The fiery ranks of immaterial essences are illuminated by Thy rays, O God of single dominion and Tri-solar One, becoming second lights by adoption: by their radiances and participation reveal me also as light, as Tri-radiant Light-Giver!

*Troparion:*

Illumined by Tri-radiant rays, O All-Holy Trinity in single dominion: preserve Thy singers from adverse temptations, that we may unceasingly glorify Thee: O Father, and Son, and All-Holy Spirit, in One Godhead!

*Theotokion:*

Guiding us and lifting us unto heaven without failing, [save] those who love Thee: Thou Who through ineffable love for mankind didst become man in the Virgin's womb, and having deified man, sittest upon the throne of glory with the Father.

*Ode 8. Heirmos: The children, disregarding the golden image... (fol. 26)*[^43]

*Troparion:*

O Unapproachable Trinity, co-eternal, co-unoriginate, of Divine origin, immutable in all things except the light-bearing hypostatic properties: utterly abolish the evil counsel of adversaries and demonic disturbances, preserving me unharmed ever, O Lord, in all things.

*Troparion:*

All-wisely and all-powerfully, O Incircumscribable Tri-solar Monarchy, Who didst establish the world and dost preserve it by Thy power in all-perfect order: take up Thine abode in my heart, that I may hymn and glorify Thee unceasingly with the angelic choirs unto all ages.

*Troparion:*

O Incomprehensible and Ineffable Wisdom of the Father, Word of God: without changing Thine immutable and Divine nature, Thou didst mercifully take on human nature, and didst teach all men to honor the Single Trinity as Sovereign Principle unto all ages.

*Theotokion:*

Rightness and Wisdom, Which guides all things, loved thee worthily, O All-Venerable and light-bearing Virgin; and God ineffably took up His abode in thee, O Most Pure Lady; hymning whom, we sing, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

[Sunday.]

*Ode 9. Heirmos: God, the Luminary prior to the sun...*

From the Unoriginate Light, the co-unoriginate Son, Light, shone forth, and the consubstantial Light, the Spirit, proceeded: ineffably, in God-befitting manner, confirming the incorruptible birth together with the ineffable procession.

*Troparion:*

Shine forth in the hearts of those who hymn Thee, O Tri-numbered Godhead, with Thy Tri-radiant Light; and grant understanding to discern in all things, and to behold Thy good and perfect will, and to magnify and glorify Thee.

*Troparion:*

Being unreckonable by nature as God, having an unreckonable abyss of compassions: have compassion upon us, as ever before, so also now have compassion upon Thy servants, and deliver us from transgressions, and from tribulations and afflictions.

*Theotokion:*

Save me, O my God, from every temptation and affliction, Thou Who art hymned in Three Persons, ineffably One God and Almighty; and preserve Thy flock through the prayers of the Mother of God.

*After this we sing: It is truly meet. [End of Midnight Office]*

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 2)

*On Sunday at Matins, after "God is the Lord": on 8: Tone 2. Troparion: When Thou didst descend... Glory... Another. Both now. Theotokion: All beyond comprehension... Podoben.* (fol. 27),[^44]

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*

The noble Joseph, taking down Thy Most Pure Body from the Tree, wrapped It in a clean winding-sheet and, having anointed It with spices, laid It closed in a new tomb; but on the third day Thou didst arise, O Lord, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Glory:*

Having willed that the tombstone be sealed, when Thou didst arise, O Lord, Thou didst grant the Rock of faith unto all; Lord, glory to Thee!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Exceedingly glorified art thou, O Mother of God; we sing thy praise! For through the Cross of thy Son, Hades was overthrown and death was put to death; we who were dead have arisen and been deemed worthy of life; we have received Paradise, the ancient delight. Therefore, giving thanks, we glorify Christ our God as mighty and only Abundantly Merciful.

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*

Standing before the tomb, the Angel cried aloud unto the myrrh-bearing women: Myrrh is fitting for the dead, but Christ hath shown Himself a stranger to corruption; rather cry aloud, singing: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory:*

[Sunday.]

The choir of Thy disciples rejoices in unison with the myrrh-bearing women; for we celebrate a common feast with them unto the glory and honor of Thy Resurrection; and through them, O Lord, Lover of mankind, grant great mercy unto Thy people.

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Having transcended natural laws, thou didst unite virginity with divine childbearing; for thou alone didst give birth unto Him Who was timelessly born before thee. Therefore we magnify thee, O Mother of God.

*Hypakoe, Tone 2:*

Going after the Passion unto the tomb to anoint Thy Body, O Christ God, the women beheld Angels and were terrified; for they heard a voice from them: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Then the Degrees, Antiphon 1, Tone 2:*

Unto heaven I lift the eyes of my heart unto Thee, O Savior; save me by Thine illumination!

*Verse:*

Have mercy on us who sin against Thee greatly at every hour, O my Christ, and grant me a way of repentance unto Thee before the end!

*Glory, both now:*

Unto the Holy Spirit it belongs to reign, to sanctify and to move creation; for He is God, consubstantial with the Father and the Word (fol. 28).[^45]

*Antiphon 2:*

If the Lord had not been in us, who could have been preserved whole from the enemy and manslayer together?

*Verse:*

Deliver not Thy servant unto their teeth, O Savior; for my foes move against me like lions!

*Glory, both now:*

Unto the Holy Spirit belongs the origin of life and honor; for as God, by His power He preserves all created things in the Father, together with the Son.

*Antiphon 3:*

They that hope in the Lord are likened unto the holy mountain, which is in no wise shaken by the assaults of the enemy.

*Verse:*

Let not those who live godly stretch forth their hands unto iniquities; for Christ will not leave the rod upon His lot.

*Glory, both now:*

Through the Holy Spirit all wisdom flows forth: from Him is grace unto the Apostles, and through suffering the Martyrs are crowned, and the Prophets behold [mysteries].

*Prokimenon, Tone 2:* Arise, O Lord my God, in the decree...

[Sunday.]

...which Thou hast commanded; and the congregation of the peoples shall surround Thee. Verse: O Lord my God, in Thee have I hoped. Let every breath. The Matins Gospel: "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ." Psalm 50.

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

In the deep of old, the all-armed power of Pharaoh's host was drowned; and the incarnate Word destroyed prevailing sin: exceedingly glorious is the Lord, for gloriously hath He been glorified!

*Troparion:*

The prince of this world, O Good One, unto whom we enrolled ourselves having disobeyed Thy commandment, was condemned by Thy Cross; for assaulting Thee as mortal, he fell from power by Thy dominion, and was revealed as powerless.

*Troparion:*

The Deliverer of the human race and Prince of incorruptible life, having come into the world and by His Resurrection torn asunder the swaddling-bands of death: Whom we all glorify, for gloriously hath He been glorified!

*Theotokion:*

Thou hast shown thyself higher than all visible and invisible creation, O Pure Ever-Virgin; for thou didst bear the Creator, as He was well pleased to become incarnate (fol. 29)[^46] in thy womb: Whom do thou boldly entreat to save our souls.

*Another Canon (Cross-Resurrection). Ode 1, same Tone. Heirmos:*

Walking untrodden, unaccustomed, unwetted paths through the sea, the chosen Israel cried aloud: Unto the Lord let us sing, for gloriously hath He been glorified!

*Troparion:*

Thy Passion in the flesh, O Christ God, became strength unto the weak, resurrection unto the fallen, and incorruption unto the dead; for He hath been glorified!

*Troparion:*

Having had compassion on the fallen image, and raised up the shattered one, God the Creator and Abundantly Merciful was put to death and gave life unto all; for He hath been glorified!

*Theotokion:*

The angelic ranks, rejoicing, minister unto thy supernatural childbearing, O Pure Lady; for thou didst bear their God and Lord.

*Another Canon (Theotokos). Same Tone. Heirmos:*

The immaterial ladder of old and the marvelous congealed path of the sea declared thy childbearing, O Pure Lady, which we all hymn, O Sovereign Lady, for gloriously hath He been glorified!

*Troparion:*

The Power of the Most High, the Perfect Hypostasis, Divine Wisdom, having become incarnate of thee, O Most Pure Lady, drew near unto men; for gloriously hath He been glorified!

[Sunday.]

The Sun of righteousness passed through the impassable, shut gate of thy womb, O Pure Lady, and shone forth unto the world; for gloriously hath He been glorified!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

The wilderness, the barren Church of the Gentiles, hath blossomed as a lily at Thy coming, O Lord; wherein my heart is established.

*Troparion:*

Creation was transformed at Thy Passion, beholding Thee in humble form mocked by the lawless: Thee Who didst establish all things by Thy divine nod.

*Troparion:*

With Thine own hand Thou didst fashion me from the dust according to Thine image, and when I was shattered again into the dust of death by sin, O Christ: descending into Hades, Thou didst raise me up with Thyself.

*Theotokion:*

The angelic ranks were amazed, O Most Pure Lady, and human hearts were terrified at thy childbearing; therefore with faith we honor thee as Mother of God.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

The bow of the mighty is broken by Thy strength, O Christ, and the weak are girded with power.

*Troparion:*

Christ, Who art higher than all, was made a little lower than the angelic nature by the passion of the flesh, being numbered among the dead with the lawless (fol. 30),[^47] shining forth [a sacrifice] in the crowns of glory of Thy Resurrection, O Christ.[^48]

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

He Who is higher than all time, as Creator of time, was willingly fashioned as an infant from thee, O Virgin.

*Troparion:*

Let us hymn the womb more spacious than the heavens, through which Adam dwells rejoicing in the heavens.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Neither an intercessor nor an Angel, but Thou Thyself, O Lord, didst come forth from the Virgin incarnate, and didst save all of me, man; therefore I cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Standing before the judgment seat as condemned, O my God, Thou didst not cry aloud, O Master, pronouncing judgment upon the nations; whereby through Thy Passion, O Christ, Thou didst work salvation for the universe.

*Troparion:*

By Thy Passion, O Christ, the weapons of the enemy have failed; and by Thy descent into Hades the cities of the adversaries were destroyed, and the tormentor's dominion was overthrown.

*Theotokion:*

We, all the faithful, know thee as a haven of salvation and an immovable wall, O Sovereign Lady, Mother of God; for by thy prayers thou deliverest our souls from afflictions.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

[I heard, O Lord...] Having heard of Thy glorious dispensation, O Lord, [the prophet] glorified Thine incomprehensible power, O Merciful One!

[Sunday.]

Beholding Thee nailed unto the Tree, O Christ, she who as a Virgin gave birth unto Thee without pain endured a mother's pangs.

*Troparion:*

Death was conquered, the captive Adam [was freed], and the gates of the all-devouring Hades were shattered; He grants all things unto me beyond nature.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

Behold, the divine mountain of the house of the Lord is manifestly exalted above the powers, being the Mother of God.

*Troparion:*

Apart from natural laws, thou alone, O Virgin, having given birth unto the Master of creation, wast deemed worthy of a divine calling.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou didst become a Mediator between God and men, O Christ God; for through Thee, O Master, from the night of ignorance we have found access unto Thy Father, the Primal Light.

*Troparion:*

Like cedars, O Christ, Thou didst shatter the ragings of the enemies, when Thou wast willingly lifted up in the flesh upon the cypress, the pine, and the cedar, as Thou wast well pleased, O Master.

*Troparion:*

They laid Thee, breathless and dead, in the lowest pit, O Christ; but by Thy wound Thou didst raise up with Thyself (fol. 31)[^49] the wounds of the forgotten dead who were sleeping in the graves.

*Theotokion:*

Entreat Thy Son and Lord, O Pure Virgin, to grant peace unto those who hope in thee, and deliverance unto captives from the assaults of adversaries.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

The Live Coal shown forth unto Isaiah, the Sun, shone forth from the virginal womb, bestowing the illumination of divine knowledge upon those who stray in darkness.

*Troparion:*

Adam, having rejected fasting, tasted of old the death-bearing tree; but the second Adam, having been crucified, consumed his sin.

*Troparion:*

Thou wast passible and mortal in human nature, Who art impassible and immaterial in Godhead; Thou didst strip corruption from that which was dead, O Christ, and didst raise it from the nethermost parts of Hades.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

Ye clouds, drop down the sweetness of rejoicing unto those upon earth; for a Child is given, Who is before the ages: our God, incarnate of the Virgin.

*Troparion:*

Light shone forth unto my life and flesh, and the burden of sin was loosed, when the Most High was incarnate of the Virgin without seed at the end of times.

[Sunday.]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unsearchable abyss of Thy compassions: from corruption, O my God, raise me up!

*Troparion:*

As a malefactor the Righteous One was condemned, and was nailed unto the Tree with the lawless, granting remission unto the guilty through His own Blood.

*Troparion:*

For the sake of one man, the first Adam, death entered the world of old; and for the sake of the one Son of God, the Resurrection hath appeared.

*Theotokion:*

Without knowing man, O Virgin, thou didst give birth, and thou remainest eternally a Virgin, being shown forth as true images of the Godhead of thy Son and God.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Having heard the voice of the words of supplication from a suffering soul, O Master, deliver me from grievous things; for Thou alone art the Cause of our salvation.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst place a Cherub to guard the Tree of life from the fallen; but beholding Thee, the gates opened, and a way was revealed leading the thief unto Paradise.

*Troparion:*

Hades was emptied and overthrown by the death of the One; for the great riches which it gathered into itself, Christ, the One above all, emptied.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):* (fol. 32)[^50]

Human nature, which was enslaved unto sin, O Pure Sovereign Lady, through thee hath obtained freedom; for thy Son, as a Lamb, is slain for all.

*Troparion:*

All men cry aloud unto thee, the true Mother of God: Deliver Thy servants who have provoked anger; for thou alone hast boldness before thy Son!

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*

Thou didst arise from the tomb, O Almighty Savior, and Hades, beholding this wonder, was terrified, and the dead arose; and creation, seeing it, rejoices with Thee, and Adam is glad together; and my world, O Savior, hymns Thee ever.

*Ikos:*

Thou art the Light of the darkened, Thou art the Resurrection of all, and Life unto men; and Thou didst raise all with Thyself, having overthrown the dominion of death, O Savior, and shattered the gates of Hades, O Word; and the dead, beholding this wonder, were amazed, and all creation rejoices together at Thy Resurrection, O Lover of mankind. Therefore we all glorify and hymn Thy descent, and my world, O Savior, hymns Thee ever.

[Sunday.]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The godless decree of the lawless tyrant raised a high flame; but Christ spread a spiritual dew for the pious children: blessed and exceedingly glorified is He, the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

The Master endured not to see man tormented by mortal sin; but having come and become man, He healed him with His Blood: blessed and exceedingly glorified is He, the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Beholding Thee clothed in the garments of vengeance, O Christ, the gatekeepers of Hades were terrified; for [they saw] how Thou didst come to slay the servant of the foolish tyrant: blessed and exceedingly glorified is He, the God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

We understand thee to be holier than the holy, as having alone given birth unto the immutable God, O Undefiled Virgin, Unwedded Mother; for unto all the faithful thou didst pour forth incorruption through thy divine childbearing.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection): The children were shown forth as orators...*

Disobedience in Eden condemned the forefather of old; but He was willingly judged, loosing the transgressions of the transgressors: the Supremely Divine God of our fathers, and exceedingly glorified!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst save him who was wounded by the tongue through the envy of the manslayer (fol. 33),[^51] bitten in Eden by voluntary bites; for by Thy voluntary Passion Thou didst heal him: the Supremely Divine God of our fathers, and exceedingly glorified!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst call me who walked in the shadow of death unto light, having encompassed dark Hades with the radiance of Godhead: the Supremely Divine God of our fathers, and exceedingly glorified!

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

Jacob beheld in the night, as in an enigma, God incarnate; and He appeared from thee in brightness unto those who sing: The Supremely Divine God of our fathers, and exceedingly glorified!

*Troparion:*

The signs in thee showed forth the ineffable union: He wrestled with Jacob, wherewith He was willingly united unto men, O Pure Lady: the Supremely Divine God of our fathers, and exceedingly glorified!

*Troparion:*

Abominable is he who does not proclaim Thee, the One Son of the Virgin, of the All-Hymned Trinity, with unhesitating faith and with the tongue crying aloud: The Supremely Divine God of our fathers, and exceedingly glorified!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The fiery furnace of old in Babylon divided its action by divine command, burning the Chaldeans...

[Sunday.]

...cooling the faithful who sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

Beholding the garment of Thy flesh crimsoned with Thy Blood, O Christ, the angelic ranks marveled with trembling at Thy great longsuffering, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

By Thy rising Thou didst clothe my mortality in immortality, O Merciful One; therefore Thy chosen people, rejoicing, thankfully sing unto Thee, O Christ, crying unto Thee: Death is swallowed up in victory!

*Theotokion:*

Having conceived in thy womb without seed Him Who is inseparable from the Father, and having lived as God-man, thou didst ineffably give birth unto Him, O Most Pure Mother of God; therefore we sing of thee as salvation unto us all.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection): Unto the golden body they did not bow...*

*Troparion:*

He Who is rich in mercy was seen nailed unto the Cross, and was willingly buried, and arose on the third day; and Thou didst deliver all earthly beings, O Lover of mankind, who sing with faith: Let every creature sing to the Lord, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Descending into the nethermost parts to deliver from corruption him whom Thou didst create by Thy divine power, O Christ, Word of God (fol. 34),[^52] and having made him free from corruption, Thou didst make him a partaker of Thine ever-existing glory: that every creature, crying aloud, may sing to the Lord and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

Through thee He was seen upon earth and lived among men, Who is ineffable in grace and power; unto Whom all of us, the faithful, sing, crying aloud: Let hypostatized creation sing to the Lord, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):*

Proclaiming thee in truth as the Mother of God, O Pure Lady, we glorify thee; for thou didst bear the One of the Trinity incarnate, unto Whom with the Father and the Spirit we all sing: Let every creature sing to the Lord, and exceedingly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The Son of the Unoriginate Begetter, God and Lord, incarnate of the Virgin, hath appeared unto us, to illuminate the darkened and to gather together the scattered: therefore we magnify the All-Hymned Mother of God!

*Troparion:*

As planted in Paradise on Cranium, O Savior, the thrice-rich Tree of Thy Most Pure Cross, watered with the divine water and Blood as from a divine...

[Sunday.]

...fountain, Thy side, O Christ, being watered, hath sprouted forth life unto us.

*Troparion:*

Being crucified as Almighty, Thou didst cast down the mighty; and having raised up human nature lying below in the stronghold of Hades, Thou didst seat it upon the Father's Throne: therefore, worshipping Thee Who comest with it, we magnify Thee.

*Trinity Troparion:*

Singing in orthodox manner, we, the faithful, glorify the Tri-numbered Monad, the Consubstantial Trinity, the undivided, Supremely Divine Nature: the Tri-radiant, unwaning Dawn, which hath shone forth upon us as the One Incorruptible Light.

*Another Heirmos (Cross-Resurrection):*

Thou art all desire, Thou art all sweetness, O Word of God, Son of the Virgin, God of gods, Holy Lord of saints; therefore we all magnify Thee together with her who bore Thee!

*Troparion:*

The knees of those in heaven, and on earth, and under the earth bend before Jesus: as unto the Creator, and as unto God Who was crucified in the flesh; and every tongue, theologizing, magnifies Thee.

*Troparion:*

Even as Thou didst snatch from the hands of Hades the bound souls of the mighty, O Master, so now deliver us from the violent hand of the tormentor, that, being delivered, we all may magnify Thee.

*Another Heirmos (Theotokos):* (fol. 35)[^53]

A rod of strength was given unto corruptible nature, the Word of God in thy womb, O Pure Lady, and Thou didst raise up that which had slipped down unto Hades; therefore we magnify thee, O All-Pure Lady, as Mother of God!

*Troparion:*

Accept mercifully the supplication of Thy Mother for our sake, which Thou didst desire, O Master; and let all things be filled with Thy goodness, that we all may magnify Thee as Benefactor.

*At the Praises, Stichera. Samoglasny, Tone 2:*

Let every breath and every creature glorify Thee, O Lord, for through the Cross Thou didst abolish death: that Thou mightest show forth unto the peoples Thy Resurrection from the dead, as the only Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:*

Let the Jews say how the soldiers lost the King Whom they were guarding: why did the stone not preserve the Rock of life? Either let them give up Him Who was buried, or let them worship Him Who is risen, saying with us: Glory to the multitude of Thy compassions; our Savior, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

Rejoice, ye peoples, and be glad! An Angel sitting upon the stone of the tomb, he proclaimed the glad tidings unto us, saying: Christ the Savior of the world is risen from the dead...

[Sunday.]

...and filled all things with fragrance: Rejoice, ye peoples, and be glad!

*Sticheron:*

An Angel indeed brought "Rejoice!" unto the Full of Grace before Thy conception, O Lord; and an Angel also rolled away the stone of Thy glorious tomb at Thy Resurrection: the former declaring emblems of joy instead of sorrow, and the latter proclaiming unto us the Master, the Giver of life, instead of death. Therefore we cry unto Thee, O Benefactor of all, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:*

The women poured out myrrh with tears upon Thy tomb, and their mouths were filled with joy when they said: The Lord is risen!

*Sticheron:*

Let the nations and peoples praise Christ our God, Who willingly endured the Crucifixion for our sake and remained three days in Hades; and let them worship His three-day Resurrection from the dead, whereby the ends of the whole world were enlightened.

*Sticheron:*

Thou wast crucified and buried, O Christ, as Thou didst will; Thou didst despoil death and arise in glory as God and Master, granting unto the world eternal life (fol. 36)[^54] and great mercy!

*Other Stichera [Anatolian]:*

Truly, O lawless ones, by sealing the stone ye deemed us worthy of a greater miracle: the guards have knowledge of it; today He came forth from the tomb, and they said: Say that while we slept the disciples came and stole Him away. But who steals a dead body, especially one that is naked? He arose by His own power as God, leaving His burial wrappings in the tomb. Come and see, ye Jews, how He did not break the seals: He Who trampled down death and granted unending life and great mercy unto the human race!

*On the Beatitudes, Tone 2:*

We bring unto Thee the voice of the thief, and we pray: Remember me, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

We bring Thy Cross for the forgiveness of sins, which Thou didst accept for our sake, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

We worship Thy burial and arising, O Master, through which Thou didst deliver from corruption...

[Sunday.]

...and the world, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

By Thy death, O Lord, Thou didst put death to death; and by Thy Resurrection, O my Savior, Thou hast enlightened the world.

*Troparion:*

Meeting the myrrh-bearing women when Thou didst arise from the tomb, Thou didst bid them proclaim Thine arising unto the disciples.

*Troparion:*

Even as Thou didst raise Adam from the tomb, O Exceedingly Good One, so, as God, by the Tree Thou didst save the world.

*Glory:*

Let us glorify the Father, let us worship the Son, and let all of us, the faithful, hymn the Holy Spirit.

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Rejoice, O Fiery-Throne; rejoice, O Mother unwedded; rejoice, O Virgin who didst bear God unto men!
