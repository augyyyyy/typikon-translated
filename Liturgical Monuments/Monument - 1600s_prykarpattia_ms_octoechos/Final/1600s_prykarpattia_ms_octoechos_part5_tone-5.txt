# TONE 5

## TONE 5 [^90]

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 5)

[f. 72A] *On Saturday evening at Great Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried":*

*Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 5:*

*Sticheron 1:*

By Thy precious Cross, O Christ, Thou didst put the devil to shame; and by Thy Resurrection Thou didst blunt the sting of sin, and didst save us from the gates of death: we glorify Thee, O Only-Begotten One!

*Sticheron 2:*

He Who gives resurrection unto the human race was led as a sheep to the slaughter; the princes of Hades were terrified of Him, and the gates of weeping were lifted up; for Christ, the King of glory, entered in, saying unto those in bonds: Come forth! and unto those in darkness: Reveal yourselves!

*Sticheron 3:*

A great marvel: the Creator of invisible things, having suffered in the flesh for the sake of His love for mankind, arose immortal! Come, families of the nations, let us worship Him [f. 72A] for delivered from deceit by His tender mercy, we have learned to hymn the one God in Three Persons!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian [Eastern], the same Tone:*

*Sticheron 4:*

An evening adoration do we offer unto Thee, the Unwaning Light, Who at the end of the ages, as in a mirror, didst shine forth in the flesh unto the world, and didst descend even unto Hades, and didst dispel the darkness there, and didst show forth the light of the Resurrection unto the nations: O Lord, Giver of light, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 5:*

The Author of our salvation, Christ, let us doxologize; for when He arose from the dead, the world was saved from deceit; the angelic choir rejoices, demonic deceit flees, fallen Adam arises, and the devil is brought to naught!

*Sticheron 6:*

They that were of the guard were instructed by the lawless ones to conceal Christ's Resurrection: Take silver pieces, and say: While we slept, the dead man was stolen from the tomb! Who ever saw, or who ever heard, of a dead man stolen at any time, especially one anointed with myrrh and naked, leaving behind his burial wrappings in the tomb? (fol. 73)[^91] Be not deceived, O Jews! Learn the prophetic utterances, and understand that He is truly the Redeemer of the world, the Almighty One!

*Sticheron 7:*

O Lord, Who didst plunder Hades and trample upon death, our Savior, and didst enlighten the world by Thy precious Cross: have mercy upon us!

*Glory, both now. Dogmatikon Theotokion, Tone 5:*

In the Red Sea an image of the unwedded Bride was prefigured of old: there Moses was the divider of the waters, and here Gabriel was the minister of the wonder; then Israel traversed the deep dryshod, and now the Virgin bore Christ seedlessly; the sea remained impassable after the passage of Israel, and the Immaculate One remained incorrupt after the birth of Emmanuel: O Thou Who art and Who wast before, and hast appeared as man, O God, have mercy upon us!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 5:*

*Sticheron 1:*

Thee, Who wast incarnate, O Christ Savior, and didst not depart from heaven, in voices of songs we magnify; for Thou didst accept the Cross and death for the sake of our race, as the Lord Who loves mankind; having shattered the gates of Hades, Thou didst arise on the third day, saving our souls!

[f. 72A] *Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

*Sticheron 2:*

From Thy pierced side, O Giver of life, Thou didst pour forth streams of remission unto all, life and salvation; and having received death in the flesh, Thou didst grant immortality unto us; and taking up Thine abode in the tomb, Thou didst free us, raising us up with Thyself gloriously as God; therefore we cry: O Lord, Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

*Sticheron 3:*

Strange is Thy crucifixion and descent into Hades, O Lover of mankind; for having plundered it and raised up with Thyself the prisoners of old gloriously as God, Thou didst open Paradise and made them worthy to receive it; therefore grant remission of sins also unto us who glorify Thy third-day arising, making us worthy to dwell in Paradise, as Thou alone art tenderhearted!

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

*Sticheron 4:*

O Thou Who for our sake didst receive death in the flesh, and didst arise on the third day from the dead: heal our fleshly passions, and raise us up from grievous transgressions, O Lover of mankind, and save us!

*Glory, both now. Aposticha Theotokion:* (fol. 74)[^92]

A temple and a gate art thou, a palace and the throne of the King, O all-honored Virgin, from whom my Redeemer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of Righteousness, wishing to enlighten those whom He created according to His image with His hand; therefore, O Supremely Hymned One, having acquired maternal boldness towards Him, pray unceasingly that our souls may be saved!

*Dismissal Troparion, Tone 5:*

The Word, co-unoriginate with the Father and the Spirit, Who was born of the Virgin for our salvation, let us faithful hymn and worship; for He was well-pleased to ascend the Cross in the flesh, and to endure death, and to raise the dead by His glorious Resurrection!

*Glory, both now. Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 5:*

Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, rampart and protection of those who flee unto thee! Rejoice, tempest-free haven and unwedded one, who bore in the flesh thy Creator and Lord: cease not praying for those who hymn and worship thy childbearing!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 5)

[f. 72A] *On Sunday at the Midnight Office, Canon to the Most Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, the work of Mitrophanes.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos: The steed and rider into the Red Sea...*

*Troparion:*

Hymning the dominion of the unitary and tri-solar countenance, we cry: Enlighten our mind, O almighty God, and raise it up, O Master, unto Thine ineffable glory!

*Troparion:*

On high the angelic splendors of noetic beings unceasingly hymn Thee with thrice-holy songs, the threefold Unity and conforming Trinity, super-essential and almighty!

*Troparion:*

Grant unto my soul the divine drink of Thy love, sweet and light-producing, O Trinity, light-originating Unity, and divine cleansing compunction, O Master of all creation, exceedingly merciful!

*Theotokion:*

As rain upon the fleece, noiselessly from heaven did the divine Rain descend into thy womb, O Virgin, and saved all withered human nature, O Most Pure One!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: O Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command...* (fol. 75)[^93]

*Troparion:*

Having endowed noetic beings with reason, Thou didst establish them as unceasing singers of Thy Godhead, O Tri-radiant God and Creator of all; yet receive also the entreaty and supplication of us clay and earthborn, as Thou art tenderhearted!

*Troparion:*

O Thou Who by nature art susceptible to no change, unto us who are healed and who sing, unfathomable Fountain of Thy goodness, grant forgiveness of transgressions and salvation, as Thou art tenderhearted!

*Troparion:*

We glorify the Father, and the Son, and the Spirit in the unchangeable form of the Godhead, Thee, the unitary and tri-radiant Lord of all, even as the Prophets and Apostles were clearly taught by Thee!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst appear unto Moses in the bush as the Angel of Great Counsel of the Almighty, prefiguring Thine incarnation from the Virgin, O Word of God, whereby Thou didst recreate us and lead us up unto heaven!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5 [after Ode 3]:*

Merciful art Thou, O indivisible Trinity, for Thou hast mercy upon [f. 72A] all, as Thou art almighty and all-bountiful, compassionate and exceedingly merciful; therefore we flee unto Thee, weighed down by many sins, crying out: Cleanse Thy servants, and deliver us from every torment!

*Glory, both now. Theotokion:*

O all-holy Virgin, have mercy upon us who run with faith unto thee, the tenderhearted, and who ask for thy warm protection; for as good, thou art able to save all, as being the Mother of God, the Most High, through thy maternal prayers, O ever Grace-bestowed!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Perceiving Thy divine condescension with prophetic insight, O...*

*Troparion:*

Daniel was mystically initiated into the tri-radiant [light] of the one Lordship, having beheld Christ the Judge coming unto the Father, and manifesting the vision of the Spirit!

*Troparion:*

Unto those who hymn Thee with clay lips, the super-essential God, Trinitarian in Hypostases, unitary in nature, vouchsafe angelic glory!

*Troparion:*

One authority and one Lordship in three properties indivisibly we glorify: O Father, and Son, and Spirit, enlighten us, Thy servants!

*Theotokion:* (fol. 76)[^94]

An honorable and overshadowing mountain, which Habakkuk beheld of old, from whence the Holy One came forth, an unapproachable visible birth did he proclaim of thy conception, O Virgin!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with...*

*Troparion:*

O Thou Who out of goodness didst create man and fashion him according to Thine own image: dwell within me, O my tri-radiant God, as Thou art good and tenderhearted!

*Troparion:*

Guide me, O tri-solar Unity, unto the divine paths of salvation, and fill me with Thy radiance, for in nature Thou art God unsearchable in power!

*Troparion:*

O indivisible Light of Thy nature, divided in characters, tri-radiant and unwaning: enlighten my heart with Thy rays!

*Theotokion:*

When of old the Prophet beheld thee, O pure and all-holy one, as a gate facing toward the never-setting Light, he immediately recognized thee as the dwelling-place of God!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Surging with the storm of soul-corrupting passions, O...*

*Troparion:*

Being the Tri-radiant Godhead in Persons, Thou art unitary as conforming and equal in operation [f. 72A] and in extension according to essence.

*Troparion:*

Abundantly did the Prophet make manifest, singing: In Thy light, O Father, we shall see by the Spirit the light of the Son, the one Tri-solar God!

*Troparion:*

An unoriginate Essence in three personal properties, having one authority and dominion; for by Them all creation was established and is renewed!

*Theotokion:*

Deliverance from transgressions and tribulations, O Master God, unitary and tri-radiant, send down unto Thy singers through the supplications of the Mother of God!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*

Perplexed in all things, I have fled unto Thee, the hope of all and the refuge for the sinful and the humble, crying: I have sinned, and I abide in evil, insensible, wretch that I am! Have mercy upon me before the end; convert me and deliver Thy servant from every torment!

*Glory, both now. Triadikon:*

The tri-solar Radiance let us hymn with doxology, and unto the simple Trinity let us now bow down; for It enlightened and had mercy upon us, and snatched all the human race from corruption, delivering the whole world from the delusion of idols, and granted the Kingdom unto us!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The Lord God of our fathers, praised above...* [^95]

*Troparion:*

As Thou hast an abyss of mercy, O Lord, and an unsearchable deep of compassions: have mercy upon those who hymn Thee as the One, the Tri-radiant God of all!

*Troparion:*

Hymning Thee, the incomprehensible, unitary, and tri-radiant God and Lord, we cry unto Thee: Grant cleansing of transgressions unto Thy servants!

*Troparion:*

Honoring the equally unoriginate Hypostases in one dominion, we distinguish, without dividing, the essence: God the Father, and the Son, and the All-Holy Spirit!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst bud forth an Offshoot co-unoriginate with the Father, the Light of the Godhead, an ever-coeternal Stem, O Virgin, Who gives life unto all mankind!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Unto Thee, the Creator of all, the youth...*

*Troparion:*

That Thou mightest clearly reveal of old the Trinitarian Hypostases of the one Lordship, Thou didst appear, O my God, in human form unto Abraham, as he hymned Thy unitary dominion!

*Troparion:*

Vouchsafe me to look upon Thy beneficent rays, O unapproachable Light, O generous Father [f. 72A] and Word, and Spirit, that I may look upon Thy beneficent rays, so as to be well-pleasing unto Thee always, O Lord of all!

*Troparion:*

Holy is God the Father, pre-eternal! Holy also is the Son, begotten of the Father! Holy also is the Life-creating Spirit, proceeding from the Father, and revealed through the Son!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst shine forth unto us from the tri-solar glory the one Christ and Lord, O all-hymned one, mystically teaching all to sing unto the one Godhead in three Persons unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Rejoice, O Isaiah! For a Virgin hath conceived...*

*Troparion:*

Human words cannot worthily hymn Thee, O divine Unity; yet daring according to our strength through faith, O divine co-enthroned Trinity, we offer glory and praise unto Thy dominion!

*Troparion:*

With equal glory the Cherubim and Seraphim praise the one-originating, tri-radiant God with most pure lips; with them receive also us sinners, O Lord, who magnify Thy dominion!

*Troparion:*

Isaiah beheld Thee upon the Cherubic Throne, and the Seraphim standing round about Thee, covering their faces with wings, and crying: Holy, holy, holy art Thou, thrice-holy God, glorified (fol. 78)[^96] in three personal properties!

*Theotokion:*

As pure and immaculate and Virgin, thou didst bear the Son Who delivers us from temptations, the timeless God; pray Him even now to send down upon us remission of transgressions!

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 5)

*On Sunday at Matins, after the sessional hymns: 'God is the Lord', in Tone 5. Troparion: 'The Word, co-unoriginate...'; Glory, both now: 'Rejoice, gate of the Lord...' After the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*

Let us praise the Cross of the Lord, let us honor His holy burial with hymns, and let us supremely glorify His Resurrection; for as God He raised the dead from the graves with Himself, having despoiled the dominion of death and the strength of the devil, and shone forth light unto those in Hades!

*Glory:*

O Lord, after Thy third-day Resurrection and the adoration by the Apostles, Peter cried unto Thee: The women took courage, but I was afraid! The thief confessed Thee as God, but I denied Thee! Wilt Thou then call me still to be a disciple, or wilt Thou show me forth again a fisher of the deep? But receive me in repentance, O God, and save me!

*Both now:*

[f. 72A] *Theotokion:*

Terrifying is the wonder of thy conception, and ineffable the manner of thy childbearing; in thee it was made known, O pure Ever-Virgin! It astounds my mind and strikes awe into my thoughts: thy glory, O Mother of God, extended unto all, unto the salvation of our souls!

*After the 2nd Stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*

O Lord, Thou wast called dead, Who didst put death to death; Thou wast laid in a tomb, Who didst empty the graves! Above, soldiers guarded the tomb; below, Thou didst raise up the dead of all ages! O almighty and incomprehensible Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory:*

O Lord, the transgressors nailed Thee in the midst of the condemned, and pierced Thy side with a spear, O most merciful One; and Thou didst accept burial, Who didst break down the gates of Hades, and didst arise on the third day! The women ran to see Thee, and announced the Resurrection unto the Apostles. Supremely exalted Savior, Whom the Angels hymn: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Both now. Theotokion:*

Rejoice, holy and God-trodden mountain! Rejoice, living and unconsumed bush! Rejoice, only bridge leading the world unto God, that translates (fol. 79)[^97] mortals unto eternal life! Rejoice, incorrupt Maiden who knewest not wedlock, who didst bear the salvation of our souls!

*Hypakoe, Tone 5:*

Astounding the mind by their angelic appearance, and enlightening their souls by the divine Resurrection, the Myrrh-bearers announced the good tidings unto the Apostles: Proclaim among the nations the Resurrection, as the Lord works together with wonders, granting great mercy unto us!

*Anabathmoi, Tone 5. Antiphon 1:*

When I am afflicted, like David I sing unto Thee, O my Savior: Deliver my soul from a deceitful tongue!

*Troparion:*

Blessed is the life of those who dwell in the wilderness, who are borne aloft on wings by divine longing!

*Glory, both now:*

By the Holy Spirit are all things held together, both visible and invisible; for being sovereign, One of the Trinity is He in truth!

*Antiphon 2:*

Unto the mountains let us lift up ourselves, O my soul; thither go, whence help shall come!

*Troparion:*

May Thy right hand, extended unto me also, O Christ, preserve me from every delusion!

*Glory, both now:*

[f. 72A] Unto the Holy Spirit, speaking theology, let us say: Thou art God, life, longing, light, understanding! Thou art goodness, Thou reignest unto the ages!

*Antiphon 3:*

Concerning those who said unto me: "Let us go into the courts of the Lord", filled with great joy, I send up prayers!

*Troparion:*

In the house of David fearful things are wrought; for there is a fire that burns up every defiled mind!

*Glory, both now:*

Unto the Holy Spirit belongs life-originating dignity, from Whom every living creature is animated, as in the Father, together also with the Word!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 5: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up, for Thou reignest unto the ages. Verse: I will confess Thee, O Lord my God, with my whole heart. 'Let every breath praise the Lord.' Verse: 'Praise God in His saints.' Matins Gospel. 'Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ.' Psalm 50. Glory: 'Through the prayers of the holy Apostles...'; 'Jesus having risen...':*

*Canon of the Resurrection, Tone 5:*

*Ode 1. Heirmos: Horse and rider into the Red Sea, He Who shatters wars with a high arm, Christ, cast down; but Israel He saved, as they sang a song of victory.*

*Troparion:* (fol. 80)[^98]

With thorns did the Jewish assembly crown Thee, O Christ, not having preserved maternal love toward Thee, the Benefactor, Who loosest the thorny condemnation of the first father!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst raise me up, who had fallen into the pit, bowing down, O sinless Giver of life; and having without experience of sin endured my foul-smelling corruption, O Christ, Thou didst perfume me with the myrrh of the divine nature!

*Theotokion:*

The curse is loosed, sorrow has ceased; for the blessed and full of grace dawned joy unto the faithful, bearing Christ, the blossom of blessing, unto all ends of the earth!

*Another Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection, the same Tone:*

*Heirmos: Unto God the Savior, Who led the people dryshod in the sea...*

*Troparion:*

Unto Him Who was willingly nailed in the flesh upon the Cross, and by the Tree freed him who fell through the ancient condemnation: unto Him alone let us sing, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:*

Unto Christ Who arose from the tomb from among the dead, and raised up fallen man with Himself, and adorned him by seating him together with the Father: unto Him alone let us sing, for He is glorified!

*Theotokion:*

O most pure Mother of God: pray unceasingly unto God Who was incarnate from thee, yet was not separated from the bosom of the Father, to deliver from every tribulation those whom He created!

[f. 72A] *Another Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos, the same Tone:*

*Heirmos: The steed and rider into the Red Sea...*

*Troparion:*

O pure one, pray Christ, the Light Who took up His abode within thee and enlightened the world with the rays of the Godhead, to enlighten all who sing of thee, O Virgin Mother!

*Troparion:*

Adorned with the beauty of virtues, O full of grace, the all-pure one sings with the radiance of the Spirit of the beneficent majesty of Him Who made all things beautiful!

*Troparion:*

The bush that prefigured thee of old upon Sinai was not consumed, O Maiden, when joined unto the fire; for as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and a Virgin didst remain, beyond understanding, O Bride of God!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: He Who established the earth upon nothing by His command, and suspended its unsupportable weight: upon the immovable rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, establish Thy Church, O only Good One and Lover of mankind!*

*Troparion:*

Gall did they who sucked honey from the rock bring unto Thee, O Christ, Who didst work wonders in the wilderness; and vinegar instead of manna did the ungrateful children of Israel render unto Thee for Thy beneficence!

*Troparion:*

They who of old were overshadowed by the light-bearing cloud (fol. 81)[^99] laid Christ, our Life, in a tomb; but having arisen by Thine own power, Thou didst grant unto all the faithful the radiance of the Spirit secretly overshadowing from on high!

*Theotokion:*

Thou, O Mother of God, without wedlock didst give birth unto Him Who shone forth from the incorrupt Father, without maternal pangs; therefore we orthodoxly proclaim thee as the Mother of God, for thou didst bear the incarnate Word!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: By the power of Thy Cross, O Christ...*

*Troparion:*

Having arisen from the tomb, O Christ, Thou didst deliver from mortal corruption those who hymn Thy voluntary Crucifixion, O Giver of life!

*Troparion:*

The Myrrh-bearers hastened to anoint Thy Body with myrrh, O Christ, and not finding it, they returned, singing of Thy Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

Pray unceasingly, O pure one, unto Him Who came forth from thy loins, to deliver from the delusion of the devil those who hymn thee, the pure Virgin!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: He Who established...*

*Troparion:*

The ladder whereby the Most High came down unto us, to restore our corrupted nature: thou, O pure one, wast visibly seen by all; for through thee the supremely Good One was well pleased to unite Himself unto the world!

*Troparion:*

The mystery foreordained of old, O Virgin, and foreseen before [f. 72A] the ages by God Who knows all things, in these last times took its fulfillment in thy womb, O all-immaculate one, and was made manifest!

*Troparion:*

The condemnation of the ancient curse was loosed through thy mediation, O all-pure Virgin; for the Lord, appearing from thee, poured forth blessing unto all as the supremely Good One, thou only adornment of mankind!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Perceiving Thy divine emptying with foresight, Habakkuk cried out unto Thee with trembling, O Christ: For the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed, art Thou come!*

*Troparion:*

Prefiguring Thy most pure Cross as in an image through the bitter waters of Marah, O Good One, Thou didst sweeten by the Tree that taste of sin which slays!

*Troparion:*

The Cross in exchange for the tree of knowledge, and gall for sweet food didst Thou accept, O my Savior; and for the corruption of death Thou didst pour forth Thy divine Blood!

*Theotokion:*

Without wedlock didst thou conceive incorruptibly in thy womb, and didst give birth before travailing, and after childbirth thou wast preserved a Virgin, having borne God in the flesh!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: I heard the report of the power of the Cross, that paradise was opened...*

*Troparion:* (fol. 82)[^100]

When the Cross was set up on the earth, upon the Place of the Skull, the bars and the eternal gates were shattered, and they cried out: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

When the Savior descended as dead unto the bound, those who were dead from all ages arose with Him, and cried out: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Theotokion:*

A Virgin gave birth, and knew not maternal pangs; yet she is truly Mother, and remained Virgin; singing unto her, we cry aloud: Rejoice, O Mother of God!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: Perceiving Thy divine [emptying]...*

*Troparion:*

With heart and mind, with soul and mouth, I piously confess thee in truth as the Mother of God, O pure one, embracing the fruit of salvation; and they are saved, O Virgin, through thy prayers!

*Troparion:*

He Who created all things out of nothing was well pleased, as the Benefactor, to be created from thee, the pure one, for the salvation of those who hymn thee with faith and love, O all-immaculate Lady!

*Troparion:*

The choirs above the world hymn thy childbirth, O all-immaculate one, rejoicing with them who truly acknowledge thee to be the Mother of God, O undefiled Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Isaiah named thee the rod, from which blossomed forth unto us the fair flower, Christ God, for the salvation of those who flee with faith and love unto thy protection!

[f. 72A] *Ode 5. Heirmos: O Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment, unto Thee I rise early, and unto Thee I cry: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou alone art tenderhearted!*

*Troparion:*

The Lord of glory, in an inglorious form, hangs dishonored upon the Tree of His own will, ineffably providing divine glory for me!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst clothe me in incorruption, O Christ, having tasted mortal corruption incorruptibly in the flesh, and having shone forth from the tomb on the third day!

*Theotokion:*

Having borne for us Christ, our righteousness and redemption, without seed, thou didst set free the nature of our forefather from the curse, O Mother of God!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Rising at dawn we cry unto Thee...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst stretch out Thy hands upon the Tree, O our Savior, calling all unto Thyself, as Thou art the Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Having delivered Adam from captivity, O my Savior, by Thy burial and by Thy Resurrection, Thou didst fill all things with joy!

*Troparion:*

Having arisen from the tomb on the third day, O Giver of life, Thou didst pour forth imperishable immortality unto all!

*Theotokion:*

We hymn thee as Virgin after childbirth, O Mother of God; for thou (fol. 83)[^101] didst bear God the Word in the flesh unto the world!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: O Thou Who clothest Thyself with light...*

*Troparion:*

All the Prophets clearly foretold thee, who wast to be the Mother of God, O all-pure Mother of God; for thou alone wast found completely pure and immaculate!

*Troparion:*

A radiant cloud of living water, showering upon us Christ, the rain of incorruption, do we who are enlightened recognize thee to be, O pure one!

*Troparion:*

Seeing thee entirely fair and immaculate, sealed in purity with virginity, God Who is the only Tenderhearted loved thee and dwelt within thee!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Tossing in a soul-corrupting tempest, O Master Christ: calm the sea of my passions and lead me up from corruption, as Thou art compassionate!*

*Troparion:*

The first father slipped into corruption, O Master Christ, having tasted of the forbidden food; and he was led back unto life by Thy Passion!

*Troparion:*

O Life, Thou didst descend into Hades, O Master Christ, and becoming corruptible unto him who had been corrupted: through incorruption Thou didst pour forth the Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

A Virgin gave birth, and having given birth, remained pure, bearing in her hands Him Who upholds all things, as truly [f. 72A] a Virgin Mother!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: The abyss encompassed me...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst stretch out Thy hands, gathering together from afar the scattered assemblies of Thy nations, O Christ our God, by Thy life-bearing Cross, as Thou art the Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Having despoiled death and shattered the gates of Hades, Adam, who had been bound, was loosed and cried out unto Thee, O Savior: Thy right hand [hath done this], O Lord!

*Theotokion:*

As the unconsumed bush, and the Mountain, and the living Ladder, and the heavenly Gate, we worthily glorify thee, O glorious Mary, the boast of the Orthodox!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: Tossing in a tempest...*

*Troparion:*

He Who is the Cause of all, Who gives being unto all things, taking thee as cause, is incarnate for our sake, O all-immaculate Mother of God!

*Troparion:*

Pouring forth a soul-nourishing fountain of healings unto those who flee with faith unto thy glorious protection, we know thee to be, O all-immaculate Lady!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst bear for us the Giver of life, the Author of salvation, Who grants eternal redemption unto those who truly proclaim thee to be the Mother of God!

*Kontakion, Tone 5:* (fol. 84)[^102]

Unto Hades, O my Savior, didst Thou descend, and having shattered its gates as almighty, Thou didst raise up the dead with Thyself as Creator, and didst break the sting of death; and Adam was delivered from the curse, O Lover of mankind; therefore we all cry: Save us, O Lord!

*Ikos:*

Hearing the words of the Angel, the women put away their lamentation, becoming joyful and trembling, for they beheld a terrifying wonder; and lo, Christ drew near unto them, saying: Rejoice, be of good cheer, I have overcome the world and set free the prisoners! Hasten therefore unto the Disciples, announcing unto them: I go before you into the city of Galilee to preach; therefore we all cry unto Thee: Save us, O Lord!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The Lord of the fathers, Who is supremely exalted, quenched the flame and refreshed the youths, as they sang in unison: Blessed art Thou, O God!*

*Troparion:*

Clothed in flesh, as with bait upon a hook, by Thy divine power Thou didst draw down the serpent, leading up those who cry: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

He Who brought into being the unshakeable foundation of the earth is covered in a tomb according to the flesh, the Incomprehensible One, unto Whom we sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Theotokion:*

[f. 72A] One Hypostasis in two natures, O all-immaculate one, didst thou bear, the incarnate God, unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: In the fiery furnace...*

*Troparion:*

Blessed is the God of our fathers, Who through the Tree of the Cross loosed the delusion of idols!

*Troparion:*

Blessed is the God of our fathers, Who arose from the dead and raised up those who were in Hades with Himself!

*Troparion:*

Blessed is the God of our fathers, Who by His death destroyed the dominion of death, O Christ!

*Theotokion:*

Blessed is the God of our fathers, Who was born of the Virgin and revealed her to be the Mother of God!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: The supremely exalted...*

*Troparion:*

Abiding uncircumscribed and infinite, He was united in hypostasis according to the flesh, as the Tenderhearted One, within thee, the all-holy one: the only blessed God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

The choir in unison glorifies thee as the all-immaculate Bride, O Lady Mother of God, and the Throne of thy Creator, unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

Purified by the Spirit, O Virgin, thou becamest the Mother of the King of all, Who created thee, unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

Saving us, O most pure Mother of God, the Lord was clothed with the garment of the flesh (fol. 85)[^103] from thee, unto Whom we sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Unto Thee, the Creator of all, the youths in the furnace, having woven an all-fair choir, sang: All ye works of the Lord, sing the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst pray concerning the cup of Thy saving Passion, voluntary yet as it were involuntary; for Thou bearest two wills, corresponding unto Thy two natures, O Christ, unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

By Thine all-operative descent, O Christ, stricken Hades cast forth all those who of old had been slain by delusion, who supremely exalt Thee unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

Thee who beyond understanding in godly manner didst bear the Lord by the Word, and remainedst a Virgin: all works bless thee, O Virgin, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Unto Him Who was begotten before [the ages]...*

*Troparion:*

Unto Christ God, Who of His own will stretched out His hands upon the Cross, and destroyed the dominion of death: praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye people, unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Unto Christ God, Who shone forth from the tomb as a bridegroom, and appeared unto the myrrh-bearers, proclaiming joy unto them: praise Him, ye priests; supremely exalt Him, ye people!

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast revealed higher than the Cherubim, O pure Mother of God, having borne in thy womb Him Who is borne upon them, Whom with the bodiless hosts we earthborn hymn with doxology and supremely exalt [f. 72A] unto all ages!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: Unto Thee, the Creator of all...*

*Troparion:*

The voice of our forefather's grief has now ceased, since thou, O Mother of God, didst receive joy; therefore we unceasingly hymn thee, O Virgin, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

The assembly of the bodiless hosts hymns with us thine incomprehensible childbirth, O Virgin, having joined in one choir with love, and supremely exalting Him unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

A clear stream of immortality, O Maiden, came forth from thee, even the Lord of all, washing away the defilement of those who sing with faith and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Truly the divine and light-bearing throne, and the tablets of grace, do we confess thee to be, O Virgin Maiden, as having received the Father's Word, Whom we supremely exalt unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Rejoice, O Isaiah: for the Virgin held in her womb and brought forth a Son, Emmanuel, God and man, Dayspring is His name; Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed, O Virgin!*

*Troparion:*

Taking fallen man unto Thyself, O Master Christ, from virginal loins Thou didst unite Thyself wholly unto him, yet without partaking of sin in any way; and Thou didst set him wholly free from corruption through Thy most pure Passion!

*Troparion:*

By the God-flowing and life-creating Blood poured forth from Thy (fol. 86)[^104] most pure side, O Master, the sacrifice of idols has ceased, and all the earth brings a sacrifice of praise unto Thee!

*Troparion:*

Not a bodiless God, nor again a mere man, came forth from thee, O pure and undefiled Maiden, but perfect Man and truly perfect God, Whom we magnify together with the Father and the Spirit!

*Another Heirmos [Cross-Resurrection]: Thee, the Mother of God beyond mind and word...*

*Troparion:*

Thee Who didst endure the Passion upon the Cross, and didst shatter the power of Hades by death: we faithful orthodoxly magnify!

*Troparion:*

Thee Who didst arise from the tomb on the third day, and didst despoil Hades, and didst enlighten the world: we faithful with one mind magnify!

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, O Mother of God, Mother of Christ God! Pray Him Whom thou didst bear to grant remission of transgressions unto those who sing of thee!

*Another Heirmos [to the Most Holy Theotokos]: Rejoice, O Isaiah...*

*Troparion:*

From thy pure blood flesh was supernaturally curdled for the Creator of all, the only-begotten Son of the Father, without a man and without seed, endowed with mind and animated with a soul, O Mother of God, Ever-Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst stop the irresistible circuit of death [f. 72A] having in truth borne in the flesh beyond mind the eternal Life, Whom touching with bitter lips, Hades was abolished, O most holy Virgin Mother!

*Troparion:*

Sitting upon the throne in Master's state, Thy Son placed thee, O pure one, at His right hand, shining brightly with the gold of divine virtues, rendering honor unto thee as His Mother, O all-immaculate one!

*Troparion:*

Beyond mind is thy childbirth, O Mother of God; for the conception in thee was without a man, and the birth was virginal; for God is He Who was born, Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed, O Virgin!

*At the Praises, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 5:*

O Lord, though the tomb was sealed by transgressors of the law, Thou didst come forth from the tomb even as Thou wast born of the Mother of God. Thine incorporeal Angels knew not how Thou didst take flesh; the soldiers who guarded Thee perceived not when Thou didst arise; for both were sealed to those who would inquire, but the marvels were revealed unto those who bow down in faith before the mystery: which as we hymn, grant unto us joy and great mercy!

*Sticheron:*

O Lord, having shattered the eternal bars and broken the bonds asunder, Thou didst arise from the tomb, leaving behind Thy grave-clothes (fol. 87)[^105] as a witness of Thy true three-day burial, and Thou didst go before into Galilee, O Thou Who wast guarded in the cave. Great is Thy mercy, O incomprehensible Savior: have mercy upon us!

*Sticheron:*

O Lord, the women ran unto the tomb to see Thee, the Christ Who suffered for our sake; and having arrived, they found an Angel sitting upon the stone, rolled away through fear, and he cried unto them, saying: The Lord is risen! Say unto the disciples that He is risen from the dead, saving our souls!

*Sticheron:*

O Lord, even as Thou didst come forth from the sealed tomb, so didst Thou enter, the doors being shut, unto Thy disciples, showing them the bodily passions which Thou didst endure, O long-suffering Savior; as of the seed of David Thou didst endure wounds, and as the Son of God Thou didst set the world free. Great is Thy mercy, O incomprehensible Savior: have mercy upon us!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian [Eastern], the same Tone:*

O Lord, King of the ages and Creator of all, Who for our sake didst accept crucifixion and burial in the flesh, that Thou mightest set us all free from Hades: Thou art our God; besides Thee we know no other!

*Sticheron:*

O Lord, who shall declare Thy supremely radiant marvels, or who shall tell of Thy fearful mysteries? For having become [f. 72A] man for our sake as Thou Thyself didst desire, Thou didst manifest the dominion of Thy power; and by Thy Cross Thou didst open Paradise unto the Thief, and by Thy burial Thou didst shatter the bars of Hades, and by Thy Resurrection Thou didst enrich all things: O compassionate One, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

The Myrrh-bearing women, having reached Thy tomb very early, sought to anoint Thee with myrrh, the immortal Word and God; and having been instructed by the words of the Angel, they returned with joy to proclaim openly unto the Apostles that the Life of all is risen, and Thou grantest cleansing and great mercy unto the world!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

Concerning the God-receiving tomb, the guards said unto the Jews: O your vainly-wise counsel! Ye attempted to guard the Incircumscribable; in vain did ye labor! Wishing to hide the Resurrection of the Crucified, ye clearly revealed it by your vainly-wise assembly! Why do ye again take counsel to conceal that which cannot be hidden? Rather, hear from us and be willing to believe the truth of what has come to pass: a lightning-bearing Angel, (fol. 88)[^106] having descended from heaven, rolled away the stone, through fear of whom we were held fast by deadness; and crying out with a mighty voice unto the wise Myrrh-bearing women, he said: Do ye not behold the slaying of the guards, and the breaking of the seals, and the emptying of Hades? Why do ye seek Him as dead Who abolished the victory of Hades and shattered the sting of death? Go quickly and announce the good tidings of the Resurrection unto the Apostles, crying out without fear: Truly the Lord is risen, having great mercy!

*Glory. Evangelical Sticheron. Both now. Most blessed art thou:*

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 5)

*At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 5:*

The Thief, believing Thee to be God upon the Cross, confessed Thee purely from the heart, O Christ, crying: Remember me, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:*

Unto Him Who from the wood of the Cross made life to blossom forth for our race, having withered the curse that came from the tree: as Savior and Creator let [us sing] with one accord!

*Troparion:*

By Thy death, O Christ, [Thou didst destroy the dominion] of death, and didst raise up together with Thyself those who had [died] from the ages, [as they sing unto Thee as] the true God and our Savior!

[f. 72A] *Troparion:*

Having arrived at Thy tomb, O Christ, the honorable women sought to anoint Thee, the Life-giver, with myrrh; and an Angel appeared unto them, crying: Truly the Lord is risen!

*Troparion:*

When Thou wast crucified, O Christ, between the two condemned Thieves, one indeed blaspheming Thee was condemned righteously, while the other, confessing Thee, went to dwell in Paradise!

*Troparion:*

The utterance of Thine Apostles filled all the earth, O Christ, and drove away the night of godlessness, shining the light of knowledge upon all the faithful!

*Glory:*

We bow down before Thee, O Trinity Unity, unoriginate and equally honorable; and we glorify Thee with the hosts on high, crying out in a Trinitarian manner: Holy art Thou, O unoriginate God!

*Both now:*

Rejoice, O living temple of God, and impassable gate! Rejoice, O unburnt and fiery throne! Rejoice, O Mother of Emmanuel, Christ our God!
