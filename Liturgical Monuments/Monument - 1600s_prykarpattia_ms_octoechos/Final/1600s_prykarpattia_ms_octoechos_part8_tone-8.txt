# TONE 8

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 8)

[f. 122A] *On Saturday Evening, at Great Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Resurrection Stichera, Tone 8:*

*Sticheron:*

An evening hymn and rational service do we offer unto Thee, O Christ: for Thou wast well pleased to have mercy upon us by the Resurrection!

*Sticheron:*

O Lord, Lord, cast us not away from Thy presence: but be well pleased to have mercy upon us by the Resurrection!

*Sticheron:*

Rejoice, O holy Zion, Mother of the Churches, dwelling-place of God: for thou wast the first to receive remission of sins by the Resurrection!

*Other Stichera, Eastern, Same Tone:*

*Sticheron:*

The Word born of God the Father before the ages, and in the last times the Same incarnate of her who knew not wedlock, of His own will enduring the death of the Cross, and saved man who of old was slain, by His Resurrection!

[f. 123A] *Sticheron:* [^141]

Thy Resurrection from the dead do we glorify, O Christ, whereby Thou didst free the race of Adam from the tyranny of Hades, and as God didst grant unto the world eternal life and great mercy!

*Sticheron:*

Glory to Thee, O Christ Savior, only-begotten Son of God, Who wast nailed to the Cross and didst rise from the tomb on the third day!

*Sticheron:*

Thee do we glorify, O Lord, Who for our sake didst willingly endure the Crucifixion; and Thee do we worship, O almighty Savior: cast us not away from Thy presence, but give ear and save us, for the sake of Thy Resurrection, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*

The King of heaven, through love for mankind, appeared upon earth and lived among men; for taking flesh from the pure Virgin and coming forth from her with the assumption [of our nature], He is one Son, twofold in nature, but not in Person; therefore, truly proclaiming Him as perfect God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God: Whom implore, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may have mercy!

[f. 123A] *At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 8:*

*Sticheron:*

Thou didst ascend the Cross, O Jesus, Who camest down from heaven; Thou camest unto death, O immortal Life; unto those in darkness the true Light, unto the fallen the Resurrection of all and Enlightenment: O our Savior, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera, Alphabetical:*

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

*Sticheron:*

Let us glorify Christ, Who rose from the dead: for having assumed soul and body, by His Passion He severed them one from the other; His all-pure soul indeed descending into Hades, which He also despoiled, while in the tomb His holy body saw not corruption: the Deliverer of our souls!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

*Sticheron:*

With psalms and hymns do we glorify Thy Resurrection from the dead, O Christ, whereby Thou didst free us from the tyranny of Hades, and as God didst grant eternal life and great mercy!

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

*Sticheron:*

O incomprehensible Master of all, Creator of heaven and earth, by the sufferings of the Cross Thou didst pour forth passionlessness unto me; and having accepted burial, and risen in glory, Thou didst raise up Adam with Thyself by Thine [^142] [f. 124A] all-powerful hand! Glory to Thy three-day rising, whereby Thou hast granted unto us eternal life and cleansing of sins, as Thou alone art tender-hearted!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

O unwedded Virgin, who didst ineffably conceive God in the flesh, Mother of God the Most High: receive the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, who bestowest upon all cleansing of transgressions, now receiving our supplications: pray that we all may be saved!

*Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart. Trisagion. And after Our Father.*

*Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 8:*

Thou didst descend from on high, O Compassionate One; Thou didst accept three-day burial, that Thou mightest free us from passions: O our Life and Resurrection, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

O Good One, Who for our sake wast born of a Virgin and didst endure the Crucifixion, Who by death didst despoil death, and as God didst manifest the Resurrection: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thine own hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; accept the Mother of God who bore Thee, who intercedes for us; and save, O our Savior, a despairing people!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 8)

[f. 124A] *On Sunday, at the Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, of which the acrostic is: "O Unity, [preserve] the powers of Thy servant." Work of Metrophanes.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos: He submerged the armed Pharaoh, working wonders...*

*Troparion:*

Unto the thrice-sunlike King, and Builder, and Provider in every way, being good alone in nature, and possessing the unique glory of the Godhead, unto God of single principle we fall down, singing the thrice-holy hymn!

*Troparion:*

Divine utterances and prophecies from on high, fulfilling clearly, glorify the divine-originating single Nature, ever-existing, co-unoriginate, in three Persons: Father, and Son, and Spirit: all-powerful, creative!

*Troparion:*

Abraham, having become an initiate of sacred mysteries, sacredly of old received the Creator of all, and God, and Lord, in three figures indeed, rejoicing; and in three Hypostases he recognized the single Dominion!

*Theotokion:*

O thou who knewest not wedlock, thou didst bear Christ, Who for our sake assumed our [^143] [f. 125A] nature, O all-pure one, and remained inalterable in both [natures]: Whom implore unceasingly to grant me remission of sins and deliverance from trials!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: In the beginning, having established the heavens with understanding...*

*Troparion:*

Isaiah, beholding Thee, the unapproachable God and King of glory, upon a high throne, and the Cherubim and Seraphim glorifying Thee with unceasing hymns: [glorified Thee as] the single tri-hypostatic God!

*Troparion:*

Having attained by the wind of understanding and the teachings of the Scriptures the one Word born of the Father as from the Mind, and the Spirit proceeding ineffably: we venerate the one thrice-radiant God!

*Troparion:*

The unbegotten Father, having incorruptibly begotten the Son, the radiance of His essence, Light from Light, appropriately brings forth the co-sprouted Light of the Spirit: all-creating and equally honored!

*Theotokion:*

A pure temple wast thou shown to be, O Virgin Mother Mary, unto Christ Who all-powerfully and all-wisely fashioned all things, and set them in order and upholds them: Whom make propitious unto me by thy maternal prayers!

[f. 125A] *Sessional Hymn, Tone 8.*

*Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Повеленное таинственно (Повеленное)):*

Ye faithful, let us now hymn the power of the thrice-sunlike and precious Godhead, how by a nod alone He framed all things, the supreme angelic choirs, and the lower ecclesiastical hierarchies, to cry: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O all-good God; glory and song be unto Thy dominion!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

O thou who didst bear the inalterable God: my heart, ever growing slothful through sin and indolence by the suggestions of the deceitful one, as good strengthen by thy maternal prayers, that I also may gratefully glorify thee, O Mary Birthgiver of God: have mercy on thy flock which thou hast acquired, O all-immaculate one!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou art my Lord, O Christ, Thou art my strength, Thou art my God; having appeared as the dawn of the Godhead, He dispersed all the gloomy darkness of passions, and the Sun of righteousness shone forth: simple in essence, yet thrice-radiant in Persons: Whom we ever hymn and glorify!

*Troparion:*

Hymned by the mouths of the Seraphim, with earthen lips do we glorify the single and Trinity Lord of glory, crying out in nature and hypostasis: O holy [^144] [f. 126A] King, grant unto Thy servants remission of their diverse transgressions!

*Troparion:*

All-holding unto all existing things, invisible, all-bountiful, tender-hearted, loving mankind, O holy and divine-originating Trinity: forget not me Thy servant unto the end, nor make void the covenant which Thou hast covenanted with Thy servants, for the sake of Thine ineffable mercy!

*Theotokion:*

Finding thee alone, O all-pure one, fair from all ages, the beauty of Jacob, the pre-unoriginate Word dwelt in thee for the sake of tender mercy, and renewed human nature: Whom implore unceasingly to deliver me from every affliction!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy...*

*Troparion:*

We glorify the three co-ever-existing Persons, and one Lord, Thee the divine Nature, simply distinguishing and uniting, and faithfully we cry aloud: O holy Trinity of single origin, deliver Thy servants from afflictions!

*Troparion:*

I weep bitterly for the weakness of my mind: how unwillingly I suffer; an involuntary change in truth! Therefore I cry: O holy, life-originating Trinity, establish me in the state of good things!

*Troparion:*

Weighed down by the slumber of my sin, and having need [f. 126A] unto the sleep of death: as Thou art loving mankind, and all-good, and all-merciful, O holy, divine-originating Trinity, show tender mercy and raise me up!

*Theotokion:*

O Virgin Maiden, all-pure, all-immaculate Mother, full of divine grace: by thy prayers make Thy Son, God, and Lord propitious unto me, and quickly deliver thy servant from passions and transgressions!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine...*

*Troparion:*

Imitating the hierarchies of the heavenly minds, O Trinity transcendent in essence, of single origin over all: with thrice-holy hymns do we glorify Thee with our earthen lips!

*Troparion:*

Unto Him Who created man according to His own image, and all-wisely fashioned all things from non-existence, unto God in three Persons do I bow down, and I honor, sing, and magnify Him!

*Troparion:*

O Almighty God, and only Uncircumscribed One: dwell in me for the sake of Thine ineffable mercy, O thrice-sunlike Master; and enlighten me and grant me understanding, as Thou art tender-hearted!

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast shown to be a temple unto the uncontainable God, O all-pure one: show me also to be a temple unto Him [^145] [f. 127A] of divine graces, O all-holy Mother of God, by thy supplications, and preserve me unharmed!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8.*

*Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Повеленное таинственно (Повеленное)):*

Ye faithful, let us truly hymn with our mouths the unoriginate Father, the co-unoriginate Son, and the divine Spirit, unconfusedly, inalterably, and immutably: the simple, holy, and co-sprouted Trinity, crying aloud with the Angels: Holy art Thou, O Father and Son, with the Spirit, simple and precious: have mercy on those whom Thou hast created according to Thine image, O Master!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

We ever bless thee, O Mother of God, and we magnify thee, and we worship thee, hymning thy birthgiving, O full of grace, crying out unceasingly: Save us, O all-merciful Virgin, as thou art good, in the hour of examination, that we thy servants may not be put to shame!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The fire was ashamed before the condescension of...*

*Troparion:*

By Thine ineffable wisdom and the abyss of goodness, show Thy servant to be freely shown mercy; and now, as of old, deliver me from wickedness, O God, Trinity in Unity, from sins and passions!

*Troparion:*

The Mind is the unbegotten Father, and the Word begotten of Him [f. 127A] and the divine Spirit, proceeding ineffably: O God of single origin, thrice-sunlike, I sing unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, the God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

I have been slain, O all-pure one, made to drink of the poison of sin; and with faith I flee unto thee who didst bear the Author of life: by thy prayers give life unto thy servant, and deliver me from temptations and passions, O thou who alone art pure!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Sevenfold the furnace the Chaldean tyrant fiercely kindled...*

*Troparion:*

Being unwaning Light, thrice-radiant and thrice-sunlike, and of single origin, sovereign, most simple, incomprehensible God, and sovereign Lord: now enlighten my dark and darkened heart, and show it to be radiant and light-bearing, to sing and glorify Thee faithfully unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

With most fiery, most divine wings the Seraphim reverently veil their faces and feet, unable to endure the glory of Thine incomprehensible goodness: O divine-originating, divine-originating, self-originating, all-holy Trinity; yet we also dare to hymn Thee and glorify Thee unto all ages!

[f. 128A] *Troparion:* [^146]

O Lordship without beginning, all-powerful and all-good, perfectly unoriginate, beneficent, and supreme; the Cause having no cause, creative, pre-existing, provident, and saving unto all, Unity in essence and Trinity in Persons: I glorify Thee, my God, faithfully unto ages!

*Theotokion:*

Upon earth shone forth the unwaning Sun through thy virginal birthgiving, O all-pure Lady, and delivered earthly ones from idolatrous and deceitful dark gloom; therefore now also enlighten me with rays of that divine origin, and preserve thy servant!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: The heaven was amazed at this, and the...*

*Troparion:*

The Nature reigning over all and all-creating, pre-originate, timeless, life-originating, tender-hearted, loving mankind, good, Trinity of single origin: Thee do we now glorify, asking remission of sins, peace for the world, and concord for the Churches!

*Troparion:*

One Lordship and thrice-radiant, unique divine origin, thrice-sunlike: accept Thy singers fittingly, and deliver from transgressions and [f. 128A] afflictions, and hardships; and quickly grant peace, in Thy love for mankind, and unity unto the Churches!

*Theotokion:*

Having entered into the virginal womb, O Christ my Savior, Thou wast manifested unto Thy world, as God-man, inalterable, unconfused in truth; and Thou didst promise to be manifestly ever with Thy servants: therefore, by the prayers of her who bore Thee, establish peace unto all Thy flock!

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 8)

*On Sunday at Matins.*

*"God is the Lord..." Troparion, Tone 8: "Thou didst descend from on high, O Compassionate One..." Glory... Both now... Theotokion.*

*After the first Kathisma, Sessional Hymns, Tone 8:*

*Sessional Hymn:*

Thou didst rise from the dead, O Life of all; and a radiant Angel cried unto the women: Cease from tears, announce good tidings unto the Apostles, cry aloud singing: That Christ the Lord is risen, Who was well pleased as God to save the human race!

*Glory... Podoben: "The disciple of wisdom" (Премудрости):*

Having truly risen from the tomb, Thou didst command the venerable women to proclaim the rising unto the Apostles, as it is written; and running swiftly [^147] [f. 129A] Peter stood before the tomb, and seeing light in the tomb, he was astonished; therefore also seeing the linen cloths lying alone, which none could see lying in the night, having believed, he cried aloud: Glory to Thee, O Christ God, for Thou savest all, O our Savior, for Thou art the radiance of the Father! Both now...

*Theotokion:*

Let us hymn the heavenly Gate and Tabernacle, the all-holy Mountain, the radiant Cloud, the heavenly Ladder, the rational Paradise, the deliverance of Eve, the great treasure of the whole world: for salvation was wrought in it for the world, and remission of ancient transgressions; therefore we cry unto thee: Pray to thy Son and God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who piously worship thine all-holy birthgiving!

*After the second Kathisma, Sessional Hymns, Tone 8:*

*Sessional Hymn:*

Men sealed Thy tomb, O Savior, and an Angel rolled away the stone from the doors; and the women, beholding Thee risen from the dead, announced good tidings unto Thy disciples in Zion: That Thou art risen, O Life of all, and the bonds of death are loosed: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory...*

Having brought [f. 129A] burial myrrh, the women heard an angelic voice from the tomb: Cease from tears, and in place of sorrow bring joy; cry aloud singing: That the Lord is risen, Who was well pleased as God to save the human race! Both now...

*Theotokion:*

All creation rejoices in thee, O full of grace: the angelic assembly and the race of man; hallowed temple and rational paradise, virginal boast, of whom God became incarnate and became a Child, Who is our pre-eternal God; for He made thy womb a throne, and made thy body more spacious than the heavens; all creation rejoices in thee, O full of grace: glory to thee!

*Hypakoe, Tone 8:*

The Myrrh-bearers, standing before the life-giving tomb, sought the immortal Master among the dead; and having received the joy of good tidings from the Angel, they announced unto the Apostles: That Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Hymns of Ascent, Tone 8. Antiphon 1:*

From my youth the enemy tempts me, and with pleasures [^148] [f. 130A] burns me; but hoping in Thee, O Lord, I vanquish the enemy!

Let them that hate Zion become as grass before it is plucked up; for Christ shall cut off their necks with the cutting of torments!

*Glory... Both now...*

By the Holy Spirit all things have life: Light from Light, great God; we sing unto Him with the Father and with the Word!

*Antiphon 2:*

I cried unto Thee, O Lord: give ear unto me, incline Thine ear unto me who cry, and purify me before Thou takest me hence!

He that hath hope in the Lord shall not be terrified then, when He shall judge all things with fire and torment!

*Glory... Both now...*

By the Holy Spirit is theology, the Trinity in Unity; for the Father is unoriginate, from Whom the Son sprouted timelessly, and the Spirit, co-enthroned, co-formed, shone forth together from the Father!

*Antiphon 3:*

Let my heart be covered with Thy fear, thinking humbly, lest being lifted up it fall away from Thee, O All-compassionate One!

Whosoever departs [f. 130A] unto his mother earth is straightway dissolved, to receive either torments or honors for what he has lived!

*Glory... Both now...*

By the Holy Spirit, whosoever is godlike beholds and foretells; he works high wonders, singing of one God in three Persons; for though it be thrice-radiant, the Godhead reigns as one Principle!

*Antiphon 4:*

Behold now, what is so good, or what is so fair, as for brethren to dwell together in unity? For concerning this the Lord promised eternal life!

Concerning his clothing, He Who adorns the lilies of the field commands that we ought not to take thought!

*Glory... Both now...*

By the Holy Spirit, through a single Cause, all things are held together in peace-giving manner; for He is God, truly consubstantial with the Father and the Son!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 8:*

The Lord shall reign unto eternity; thy God, O Zion, unto generation and generation!

*Verse: Praise the Lord, O my soul!*

*Let every breath... Praise God in His saints... The Resurrectional Gospel. Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ... Psalm 50, and the rest.*

[f. 131A] *Canon of the Resurrection.* [^149]

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos: He submerged the armed Pharaoh, the rod of Moses working wonders of old, striking in the form of a cross and dividing the sea; but it saved Israel fleeing on foot as a traveler, singing a hymn unto God!*

*Troparion:*

At the all-powerful Godhead of Christ how shall we not marvel? From passions pouring forth passionlessness and incorruption unto all the faithful, and from His holy side trickling an immortal fountain, and life from the tomb unto ever-existing ones!

*Troparion:*

How comely appeared the Angel now unto the women, bearing bright natural images of immaterial purity, and by his appearance proclaiming the light of the Resurrection, crying aloud: The Lord is risen!

*Theotokion:*

Most glorious things were spoken concerning thee in generation of generations, who didst contain the Word of God in thy womb, yet remained pure, O Mary Mother of God; therefore do we all honor thee, our protectress after God!

*Another Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection.*

*Heirmos: Having traversed the water as dry land...*

The gates [f. 131A] of sorrow were lifted up, and the gatekeepers of Hades were terrified, beholding in the nether regions Him Who in the highest is above all nature!

*Troparion:*

The angelic ranks marvelled, beholding fallen human nature, which had been shut up in the nether parts of the earth, seated upon the divine throne!

*Theotokion:*

The angelic and human ranks unceasingly praise thee, O unwedded Mother; for thou didst carry in thine arms as an infant the Creator of these!

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Let us sing unto the Lord...*

*Troparion:*

O all-pure Mother of God, who didst bear beyond nature the incarnate ever-existing and transcendent Word: we hymn thee!

*Troparion:*

A life-bearing cluster of grapes did the Virgin bear, O Christ: dripping the sweetness of salvation upon all the world!

*Troparion:*

The race of Adam, having been led up through thee unto beatitude that is beyond understanding, O Mother of God, worthily glorifies thee!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: In the beginning, having established the heavens with understanding...*

[f. 132A] ...and founded the earth upon the waters: establish me, O Christ, upon the rock of Thy Church; for there is none holy save Thee, O Lover of mankind! [^150]

*Troparion:*

Adam having been condemned through the tasting of sin, by the saving suffering of Thy flesh Thou didst justify, O Christ; for Thou Thyself, being sinless, wast not liable to the trial of death!

*Troparion:*

The light of the Resurrection shone forth unto those sitting in the darkness and shadow of death, even Jesus my God; and having bound the strong one by His Godhead, He despoiled his vessels!

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast shown to be higher than the Cherubim and Seraphim, O Mother of God; for thou alone didst receive the uncontainable God in thy womb, O undefiled one; therefore all we faithful bless thee with hymns, O pure one!

*Another Heirmos: O Lord, Maker of the celestial vault...*

*Troparion:*

Him who at first disobeyed the commandment of the Lord Who truly made me, Thou didst banish, in which he was formed; and having taught obedience by Thyself, Thou didst renew him by the Crucifixion!

*Troparion:*

O Lord, Who by wisdom didst foreknow all things, and by Thy understanding didst establish the nether regions: by Thy descent, O Word of God, Thou didst deign to raise up him who was made according to Thine image!

*Theotokion:*

Having dwelt [f. 132A] bodily in the Virgin, O Lord, Thou wast manifested unto man as it was fitting to see Thee; and her Thou didst show forth as true Queen and helper unto the faithful, O Thou Who alone lovest mankind!

*Another Heirmos: Thou art the establishment of those that flee unto Thee, O Lord...*

*Troparion:*

Grant us help by thy prayers, O all-pure one, repelling the assaults of grievous afflictions; thou wast the setting-aright of our foremother Eve, having borne for the world the Author of life, O Mother of God!

*Troparion:*

Gird me with power, O all-pure one, who in truth didst bear God in the flesh: the hypostatic Power of the Father!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Thou art my strength, O Lord, Thou art my power; Thou art my God, Thou art my rejoicing; Who, without leaving the bosom of the Father, hast visited our poverty; therefore with the Prophet Habakkuk I cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!*

*Troparion:*

When I was at enmity, Thou didst love me exceedingly; by a strange emptying Thou didst descend upon earth, O tender-hearted Savior, not rejecting the reproach of my lowliness; and abiding on the height of Thine all-pure glory, Thou didst glorify him who before was dishonored!

[f. 133A] Who, beholding now, O Master, is not astonished? Sufferings having destroyed death, by the Cross corruption is put to flight, and by death the riches of Hades are despoiled by the divine power of Thee Who wast crucified: a terrifying work, O Lover of mankind! [^151]

*Theotokion:*

Thou art the boast of the faithful, O unwedded one; thou art the protectress, thou art the refuge of Christians, a rampart and a harbor; for unto thy Son thou bearest supplications, O all-immaculate one, and dost save from perils those who with faith and love know thee as the pure Mother of God!

*Another Heirmos: I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation...*

*Troparion:*

The children of the lawless nailed Thee to the Cross, O Christ God, whereby Thou didst save, as Thou art compassionate, those who glorify Thy sufferings!

*Troparion:*

Having risen from the tomb, Thou didst raise up with Thyself all the dead in Hades, and didst enlighten, as Thou art tender-hearted, those who glorify Thy Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

God Whom thou didst bear, O all-pure Mary: Him implore to grant remission of transgressions unto thy servants!

*Another Heirmos, the same:*

*Troparion:*

As the unplowed furrow that sprouted the life-creating Ear of wheat, Who grants life unto the world, O Mother of God, save those who hymn thee!

[f. 133A] *Troparion:*

Enlightened, we all know thee to be the bush, O all-pure one, for thou didst bear the Sun of righteousness, O Ever-Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Grant cleansing unto our negligences, as Thou art sinless, and give peace unto Thy world, O God, by the prayers of her who bore Thee!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy presence, O unwaning Light, and a strange darkness hath covered me, the wretched one? But turn me back, and unto the light of Thy commandments direct my ways, I pray Thee!*

*Troparion:*

Mocked, Thou didst endure to be clothed in a purple robe before Thy Passion, O Savior, healing the shameless nakedness of the first-created man; and naked to be nailed to the Cross, stripping off the garment of slaying, O Christ!

*Troparion:*

From the dust of death, rising, Thou didst rebuild my fallen nature; and Thou didst make it unaging, O Christ, showing it forth again, like a royal image, gleaming with the light of incorruption!

*Theotokion:*

Having maternal boldness toward thy Son, O all-pure one, the kindred care for us [^152] [f. 134A] despise not, we pray, for thee alone do we Christians mercifully offer as cleansing unto the Mother of God!

*Another Heirmos: Enlighten us by Thy commandments, O Lord...*

*Troparion:*

Guide us by the power of Thy Cross, O Christ: for by it we fall down before Thee; grant us Thy peace, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:*

Govern our life now, O all-good Christ our God, who hymn Thy rising, and grant us peace, O Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*

Implore thy Son and our God, O pure one, unwedded Mary all-pure, to send down great mercy upon us, the faithful!

*Another Heirmos: Rising at dawn we cry to Thee, O Lord...*

*Troparion:*

Calm the unstable tempest of my passions, O Virgin, who didst bear God, the Helmsman and Lord!

*Troparion:*

Unto thy birthgiving minister the angelic ranks and human assemblies, O all-pure Mother of God!

*Troparion:*

O Mary, unwedded Mother of God: bring to light the hopes of the enemies, and gladden those who hymn thee!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine...*

[f. 134A] ...and give ear unto me, O God of my salvation!

*Troparion:*

By the tree the author of evil mightily cast me down; but Thou, having been lifted up upon the Tree, didst mightily cast him down, having exposed him by the Cross, and didst raise up the fallen one!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst have compassion on Zion, having shone forth from the tomb; Thou didst make it new instead of old, as Thou art tender-hearted, by Thy divine Blood; and now Thou reignest in it unto the ages, O Christ!

*Theotokion:*

May we be delivered from grievous transgressions by thy prayers, O pure Mother of God; and may we obtain, O all-pure one, the divine radiance of the Son of God Who was ineffably incarnate of thee!

*Another Heirmos: I will pour out my prayer unto the Lord...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst stretch out Thy palms upon the Cross, healing the incontinently outstretched hand of the first-created man in Eden; and of Thine own will having tasted gall, O Christ, Thou didst save as mighty those who glorify Thy sufferings!

*Troparion:*

The Deliverer tasted death of the initial decree, that He might destroy the kingdom of corruption; and having descended into the nether regions, Christ arose [^153] [f. 135A] and saved as mighty those who sing His Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*

Cease not to pray for us, O all-pure Mother of God and Virgin, for thou art the establishment of the faithful, and by hope in thee are we strengthened, and with love we ineffably glorify Him Who became incarnate of thee!

*Epistle to the Romans, Section 88:*

Brethren: Having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through Whom also we have obtained access by faith into this grace wherein we stand, and boast in hope of the glory of God. And not only so, but we boast also in afflictions, knowing that affliction [f. 136A] produces endurance, and endurance approved character, and approved character hope; and hope puts not to shame, because the love of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit Who was given unto us. For while we were yet weak, in due time Christ died for the ungodly. For scarcely for a righteous man will one die; though perhaps for a good man someone might even dare to die. But God proves His love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us. Much more then, having now been justified by His Blood, shall we be saved by Him from wrath. For if, being enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, shall we be saved by His life.

*The Gospel according to Matthew, Section 18:*

The Lord said: The lamp of the body is the eye: if therefore thine eye be simple, thy whole body shall be full of light. But if thine eye be evil, thy whole body shall be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great...

(see fol. 141)[^159]

...is that darkness! No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one and love the other, or else he will hold to the one and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon. Therefore I say unto you: Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more than food, and the body than raiment? Behold the birds of the heaven: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are ye not much better than they? Which of you by taking thought can add one cubit unto his stature? And why take ye thought for raiment? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they toil not, neither do they spin: and yet I say unto you, that even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these. If then God so clothe the grass of the field, which today is, and tomorrow is cast into the oven, shall He not much more clothe you, O ye of little faith? Therefore take no thought, saying: What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed? For after all these things do the Gentiles seek; for your heavenly Father knows that ye have need of all these things. But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and His righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

## THURSDAY EVENING VESPERS (FOR FRIDAY) (TONE 8)

*On Thursday Evening, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Cross, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:*

The forefather, tripped up because of the tree, fell, and received cruel condemnation; but Christ, nailed upon the Tree, loosed it: He renewed the crushed one, and taking him upon His shoulders, made him enthroned together with Himself and the Father with the honorable Spirit!

*Sticheron 2:*

Grant me a thought of compunction, O my Christ, that in waking and in heart I may ever bear the precious sufferings which Thou didst endure in the flesh for our sake, receiving tears and weeping over them, thinking always to stand by Thy Cross, and to behold Thee taken up upon it like a Lamb!

*Sticheron 3:*

The earth was terrified, the air was darkened, beholding Christ God insulted by a servant—He Who dissolves mountains with a rebuke, and by His look alone empties the abysses, closing His eyes upon the Tree, Who with His breath holds everything in the palm of His hand, becoming breathless and dead in the flesh!

*Other Stichera of the Mother of God, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:*

Beholding the Lamb stretched out upon the Tree, between two thieves, the undefiled Ewe-lamb and Virgin: Woe is me [f. 136A] cried she, O my sweetest Child! *Sticheron 2:*

When the Virgin and Thy Mother beheld Thee nailed upon the Cross, O my Christ, by the judgment of the lawless Jews at the Place of the Skull, she cried aloud: Woe is me, O my most beloved Son! All of us have come to know thee alone as all-holy Sovereign Lady, shown forth as a Virgin after childbirth; seeing Him Whom thou didst bear without seed willingly stretch forth His hands nailed upon the Tree, the all-pure one wept and cried aloud: Willingly, O Long-suffering One, dost Thou die, delivering from death all that hymn Thee!

Manifestly didst Thou appear unto man, O Immortal One, Thou didst endure death common to all, and didst show forth the paths of life: deign, as Lover of mankind, to grant forgiveness of sins unto those who have departed, as Master bestowing and granting communion of light.

*Theotokion:*

Thou art the boast of the faithful...

(see fol. 198)[^217]

...O unwedded one, thou art the mediatress, thou art the refuge of Christians, a wall and a haven; unto thy Son dost thou bear supplications, O all-immaculate one, and dost save from calamities those who know thee in faith and love as the pure Mother of God.

*Canon to the Martyrs, Hierarchs, and Venerable.*

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Enlighten us by Thy commandments...*

*Troparion 1:*

The divine martyrs endured bodily wounds, and therefore ever heal our wounds, wounding the demons.

*Troparion 2:*

O holy hierarchs of God, pray with all the venerable ones unto Christ, that He may grant us remission of sins.

*Troparion 3:*

Having mortified the flesh by wounds and asceticism, the venerable women, O Lover of mankind, were accounted worthy of the good things hoped for.

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Having slain death by Thy death, O Immortal One: as Lover of mankind give rest unto Thy faithful servants who have died in hope of life.

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst abolish the condemnation of our forebears, O pure one, having borne in the flesh Jesus, the only Lord Who justifies all.

*Another Canon for the Departed.*

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy...*

*Troparion 1:*

Having compassion, deign to account the departed worthy of Thine ineffable glory and of Thy blessedness which is beyond word, in the dwellings of the saints, where is the delightful voice of those that keep festival in the passionless life, O only Lover of mankind.

*Troparion 2:*

Where are the angelic ranks, where the assemblies of the righteous rejoice [f. 136A] settle Thy servants in the bosom of Abraham, O Savior, and be well-pleased, O Merciful One, that they may stand with boldness before the dread and divine Throne.

*Troparion 3:*

Thou wast revealed as our cleansing, our righteousness, and redemption, and by Thy wound Thou hast healed our infirmities: do Thou therefore, O Bountiful and Good One, appoint those who have departed to dwell in the sweetness of Paradise.

*Theotokion:*

Condescending in mercy, O Compassionate One, Thou didst take upon Thyself the measure of human nature, adorned with divine glory in the highest, taking from virginal loins flesh ensouled and rational, whereby Thou didst destroy death.

*Canon to the Martyrs, Hierarchs, and Venerable.*

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine...*

*Troparion 1:*

By your honorable wounds ye heal spiritual infirmities, and ever remove the festerings of bodies, wounding the hosts of wicked demons, O holy martyrs.

*Troparion 2:*

The choir of the venerable, the assembly of hierarchs, and the divine company of holy women, having suffered steadfastly, inherited the heavenly rewards.

*Troparion 3:*

Having mortified the flesh, the ascetics received life; and having shepherded well the flock of Christ, the most wise hierarchs were accounted worthy of immortal glory after their end.

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Thou Who art life unto the living, O Word, and rest unto the dead...

(see fol. 199)[^218]

...settle Thy departed servants, who have been taken from among us by Thy divine nod, in the bosom of Abraham Thy pleasing servant.

*Theotokion:*

The radiance of the Father was made manifest in thee, O pure one; and having dispelled the darkness of polytheism by the immaterial beams of His Godhead, He enlightened the world.

*Another Canon for the Departed.*

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine...*

*Troparion 1:*

Thy martyrs, O Savior, endured many tortures, their souls having been wounded by Thy love, O Holy One, desiring Thine everlasting glory and sweet communion with Thee.

*Troparion 2:*

Thou didst tear apart the bonds of inimical death, O Savior, and didst resurrect all the prisoners therein, granting life: whereunto, O Benefactor, deign to admit the departed.

*Troparion 3:*

From tears and sighings in hades deliver Thy servants, O Savior; for as the only Compassionate One, Thou wipest away every tear from every face of those who faithfully theologize Thee.

*Theotokion:*

The Creator of nature is fashioned within thy womb; being full as God, He empties Himself, O all-immaculate one; the only Immortal One dies by death for the salvation of all.

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*

With the saints give rest, O Christ, unto the souls of Thy servants, where there is neither sickness, nor sorrow, nor sighing, but life everlasting.

*Ikos:*

Thou alone art immortal, Who hast made and fashioned man [f. 136A] for we mortals were fashioned from the earth, and unto the same earth shall we go, as Thou didst command Who didst fashion me and say unto me: For dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return; whither all we mortals go, making our funeral weeping the song: Alleluia.

*Canon to the Martyrs, Hierarchs, and Venerable.*

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The godly children in Babylon bowed not down unto the golden image, but bedewed in the midst of the fiery furnace, sang a hymn, saying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion 1:*

Having broken down the wall of impiety by your holy bonds, O all-lauded ones: disperse the burden of my sins, as I cry: Martyrs, save me who sing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion 2:*

With the showers of your holy blood ye quench the fire of heresies; and by flame, O most wise hierarchs, ye burn up the deceitful tares of impiety, and enlighten the souls of the faithful.

*Troparion 3:*

Having mortified the flesh through abstinence, the ascetics live after death; and the choir of the prophets and righteous, and the company of women, having suffered, were glorified: by their prayers, O Christ, deliver us from calamities.

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Give rest, O Christ, unto the souls of all who have fallen asleep in hope of life, overlooking their sins committed in this life, according to the multitude of Thy tender mercies, O Compassionate and only Savior, Who art the blessed God of our fathers.

*Theotokion:*

Having received into thy womb...

(see fol. 200)[^219]

...the Word entirely incarnate, as thou art pure: entreat Him, O all-pure one, to cleanse of sins the soul and body of him that flees unto thee and honors thee in faith.

*Another Canon for the Departed.*

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The fire was ashamed before the condescension of...*

*Troparion 1:*

All the desire of the martyrs was extended toward the one Master, being united unto Him in love, and singing: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Troparion 2:*

Thou grantest unto those who have departed in faith the brightness of the Divine Kingdom, bestowing garments of incorruption, as they cry: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Troparion 3:*

Fill with joy and gladness Thy servants whom Thou hast translated, O Bountiful One, having accounted them worthy to call upon Thee and sing unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst loose the curse of Eve, for thou didst pour thyself forth as an all-immaculate virgin, pouring forth a wellspring of blessing unto those who cry: Blessed, O all-pure one, is the fruit of thy womb!

*Canon to the Martyrs, Hierarchs, and Venerable.*

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The God-proclaiming children in the furnace trampled upon the fire and flame, rejoicing and singing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion 1:*

Ye cast down the idol temples while suffering, and fashioned yourselves into divine temples of the Trinity, O passion-bearers of the Lord, companions of the Angels.

[f. 136A] *Troparion 2:*

Thy priests, O Christ, the righteous clothed with righteousness, who are from the ages, now rejoice together, beholding most clearly Thy divine beauty.

*Troparion 3:*

By the holy prayers of Thy Prophets, and of the ever-blessed women, and of the righteous from the ages: grant Thy mercies, O Word, unto Thy world.

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

O Righteous Judge, when Thou shalt come to judge: preserve uncondemned those whom Thou hast taken from among us, overlooking their transgressions, O Master.

*Theotokion:*

Appear, O Maiden, and by thine illumination drive away the dark clouds of my soul, who didst bring forth the Sun of righteousness.

*Another Canon for the Departed.*

*Ode 8. Heirmos: When the musical instruments sounded together...*

*Troparion 1:*

Having passed beyond earthly passions, the true martyrs received heavenly crowns, crying unto Thee unceasingly: Praise ye the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion 2:*

Having descended into the nethermost pit, Thou didst resurrect those that dwell in the tombs by Thy life-originating right hand; and unto Thy servants who have fallen asleep in faith, O Bountiful One, grant rest.

*Troparion 3:*

As Thou art the wellspring of everlasting life, as Thou art the torrent of sweetness: deign that Thy servants who have piously departed unto Thee may sing unto Thee and glorify Thee unto all ages.

*Theotokion:*

O Mary...

(see fol. 201)[^220]

...Virgin Mother of God, who in the flesh didst bear God the Savior unto men: save those who sing of thy childbearing in faith and supremely exalt it unto all ages.

*Canon to the Martyrs, Hierarchs, and Venerable.*

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Thee, the unwedded Mother of God and Virgin, who above understanding didst bear by the word the true God, higher than the most pure powers: with unceasing glorifications do we magnify.

*Troparion 1:*

The martyrs stood before the unrighteous tribunal, by the grace of Christ condemning all unrighteousness, rebuking those held by it, and receiving crowns of righteousness.

*Troparion 2:*

Ye appeared as church rudders, piously steering her ship into the haven of God's commandments, all ye blessed shepherd-hierarchs; therefore as helmsmen do we honor you.

*Troparion 3:*

The assembly of the Prophets and the Venerable entreats Thee; the company of women, O Lord, who suffered most radiantly and shone forth in asceticism, entreats Thee, O Lover of mankind: grant us Thy compassions.

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Of the honor and eternal glory whereof all the saints of Christ were accounted worthy: deign, O Merciful One, that those who have departed from this vain life in faith may partake, by the prayers of Thy saints.

[f. 136A] *Theotokion:*

Holding the fullness of good things in thy hands, O all-immaculate one: fulfill our supplications, the prayers of thy servants; and direct our journeyings toward God, strengthening us to live in virtues.

*Another Canon for the Departed.*

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Every ear trembled at the ineffable...*

*Troparion 1:*

Possessing the invincible and unconquerable strength of Christ, O martyrs: ye brought to naught the godless decree of the torturers, and manifestly were accounted worthy of the Heavenly Kingdom, enlightened by the beams of the Trinity, O praiseworthy ones.

*Troparion 2:*

Bitter hades was destroyed when Thou didst break it down, O Lover of mankind, resurrecting those who from the ages slept therein; and now, as Thou art good, in peace deign to grant Thine unwaning light unto those who have departed unto Thee.

*Troparion 3:*

Thou art all sweetness, O Savior, Thou art all desire and truly insatiable love, Thou art all ineffable comeliness: be well-pleased, therefore, that those who have departed unto Thee may enjoy Thy beauty, and make them worthy of Thy divine goodness.

*Theotokion:*

Save us, O Mother of God, who didst bear Christ my Savior, both God and man: two-fold in nature, but not in hypostasis, only-begotten of the Father, and from thee the first-born of all creation; Whom in two natures we magnify.

(see fol. 202)[^221]

*Exapostilarion to All Saints:*

O most glorious Prophets, blessed Apostles, righteous Patriarchs, unique assembly of Hierarchs, of the Venerable and Righteous, and company of women, all holy martyrs: together with the Mother of God pray the Lord to have mercy on your servants.

*Glory... For the Departed:*

O Thou Who rulest over the dead and the living as God: give rest unto Thy servants in the dwellings of Thine elect; for even though they sinned, O Savior, yet they departed not from Thee.

*Both now... Theotokion:*

O sweetness of Angels and joy of the sorrowing, mediatress of Christians, Virgin Mother of the Lord: protect us and deliver us from eternal torments.

*At the Praises, Stichera to the Martyrs, Tone 8.*

*Podoben: "What shall we call you, O holy ones" (Что вы наречемъ):*

*Sticheron 1:*

What shall we call you, O holy ones? Cherubim: for upon you Christ has rested; Seraphim: for unceasingly ye glorified Him; Angels: for ye turned away from bodies; Powers: ye work in miracles; many are your names and greater still your gifts: pray that our souls may be saved!

*Sticheron 2:*

Greatly did ye contend, O holy ones: having valiantly endured the torments of the lawless, confessing Christ before kings; and having again departed from this life, ye work powers in the world, and heal the sick by your sufferings, O holy ones: pray that our souls may be saved!

[f. 136A] *Sticheron 3:*

O unconquerable martyrs of Christ: having conquered deceit by the power of the Cross, receiving the grace of eternal life: ye feared not the torturers' threatenings; wounded by torments, ye make glad; and now your blood has become healing for our souls: pray that our souls may be saved!

*Sticheron 4:*

Having well clothed yourselves in the breastplate of faith, and armed yourselves with the weapon of the Cross, ye appeared as strong warriors; ye manfully resisted the torturers and cast down the devil's deceit; having become victors, ye were accounted worthy of crowns: pray that our souls may be saved!

*Sticheron 5, for the Departed:*

Thy death, O Lord, was the mediator of immortality; for hadst Thou not been laid in the tomb, Paradise would not have been opened; therefore give rest unto the departed, as Lover of mankind.

*Glory... Tone 8:*

Countless is the torment for those who live prodigally: the gnashing of teeth, and comfortless weeping, the fiery Gehenna, and the outer darkness, and the sleepless worm, hopeless tears, and the unmerciful Judge: therefore before the end we cry aloud, saying: O Master Christ, give rest unto those whom Thou hast chosen with Thine elect!

*Both now... Theotokion:*

O pure Virgin, gate of the Word, Mother of our God: pray that we may be saved!

(see fol. 203)[^222]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera for the Departed, Tone 8.*

*Podoben: "O most marvelous wonder" (О преслав):*

*Sticheron 1:*

Royally didst Thou sign my freedom, O Master, having reddened Thy fingers with crimson wounds and stained them with Thy Blood; therefore with faith I pray Thee: number with Thy first-born and with Thy righteous, to obtain joy, those who have passed over unto Thee, the Compassionate One.

*Verse: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord.*

*Sticheron 2:*

Thou didst minister as man, having slain Thyself as a Lamb; Thou didst offer an oblation unto the Father, delivering man from corruption: appoint therefore, as Lover of mankind, those who have departed to dwell in the land of the living, where streams of sweetness pour forth, and where ever-flowing fountains gush out.

*Verse: Their souls shall dwell among good things.*

*Sticheron 3:*

By the depths of Thine ineffable wisdom Thou settest a boundary unto life, and foreseest the future, and translatest Thy servants unto another life: those whom Thou hast received, therefore, appoint, O Lord, beside the waters of rest, in the brightness of the saints, and where the voice of praise and rejoicing is heard.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Having conceived the unoriginate Word of God and the Father: with thy maternal boldness entreat Him earnestly to appoint thy servants where is the never-dispersing choir [f. 136A] of those that keep festival and praise thee, where are the everlasting brightnesses, and the sweet voice of those that celebrate, O Mother of God.

*It is good to give praise... Trisagion. Our Father. Troparia.*

*At the Divine Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 8:*

*Troparion 1:*

Remember us, O Christ, Savior of the world, even as Thou rememberedst the Thief upon the Tree, and account all worthy, O only Bountiful One, of Thy Heavenly Kingdom.

*Troparion 2:*

Firmly partaking of all afflictions, by your holy wounds, O passion-bearers, ye wounded all the hosts of the demons by divine grace.

*Troparion 3:*

The venerable ones of Christ, and the divine hierarchs, the assembly of the Prophets, and of all the holy righteous, have obtained the heavenly inheritance; let us worthily call them blessed.

*Troparion 4:*

Appoint all who in faith have departed from this life, O God, in the land of the righteous, and show them forth as heirs of Paradise, who sing of Thee in godlike manner.

*Glory... Trinity Hymn:*

Unto Thee, O Trinity, I offer a final hymn: those whom Thou hast translated hence in faith, make worthy of the dwellings of the saints, and have mercy on me, the prodigal.

*Both now... Theotokion:*

Having borne the Fullness of all good things, fulfill our supplications, O Virgin, asking for us remission of sins, and...

(see fol. 204)[^223]

...enlightenment, and great mercy.

*Prokeimenon, Tone 8: Be glad in the Lord, and rejoice, ye righteous. (Ps. 31:11a) [Verse: And shout for joy, all ye that are upright in heart. (Ps. 31:11b)] And for the Departed, Tone 6: Their souls shall dwell among good things. (Ps. 24:13a) [Verse: Unto Thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul. (Ps. 24:1)]*

*The Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Thessalonians, Section 270 (1 Thess. 4:13–17):*

Brethren: I would not have you to be ignorant concerning them which are asleep, that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope. For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, even so them also which sleep in Jesus will God bring with Him. For this we say unto you by the word of the Lord, that we which are alive and remain unto the coming of the Lord shall not prevent them which are asleep. For the Lord Himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first: then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord.

*The Gospel according to John, Section 16 (John 5:24–30):*

The Lord said unto the Jews which came unto Him: Truly, truly, I say unto you [f. 136A] in Himself; and hath given Him authority to execute judgment also, because He is the Son of man. Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear the voice of the Son of God, and shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation. I can of Mine own self do nothing: as I hear, I judge: and My judgment is just; because I seek not Mine own will, but the will of the Father Which hath sent Me.

*Communion Verse: Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous. (Ps. 32:1) Another: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord. (Ps. 64:5a)*

(see fol. 205)[^224]

*Resurrectional Troparia after the Amomos [Evlogitaria], Tone 5, with the Refrain:*

*Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes. (Ps. 118:12)*

*Troparion 1:*

The assembly of the Angels was amazed, beholding Thee numbered among the dead, yet destroying the power of death, O Savior, and raising up Adam with Thyself, and releasing all from hades.

*Troparion 2:*

Why do ye disciples mingle myrrh with compassionate tears? cried the radiant Angel in the tomb unto the myrrh-bearing women: behold the tomb and understand; for the Savior is risen from the tomb!

*Troparion 3:*

Very early the myrrh-bearing women ran unto Thy tomb lamenting; but an Angel stood before them and said: The time for lamentation is ended, weep not, but announce the Resurrection unto the Apostles!

*Troparion 4:*

The myrrh-bearing women, having come with myrrh unto Thy tomb, O Savior, were weeping; but the Angel spoke unto them, saying: Why do ye count the Living among the dead? For as God He is risen from the tomb!

*Glory...:*

Let us worship the Father, and His Son, and [f. 136A] the Holy Trinity in one essence, crying with the Seraphim: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Both now... Theotokion:*

Having given birth unto the Giver of life, O Virgin, thou didst deliver Adam from sin, and didst bestow joy unto Eve in place of sorrow; and He Who was incarnate from thee, God and man, directed toward this [life] those that had fallen away from life. Alleluia, [alleluia, alleluia; glory to Thee, O God] (thrice).

*Resurrectional Troparia, for every Sunday, after the Great Doxology:*

*On Tones 1, 3, 5, and 7, we say this:*

*Troparion:*

Today salvation is come unto the world: let us sing unto Him Who is risen from the tomb and is the Author of our life; for having destroyed death by death, He gave us the victory and great mercy.

*And on Tones 2, 4, 6, and 8, we say this:*

*Troparion:*

Having risen from the tomb and torn asunder the bonds of hades, Thou didst loose the condemnation of death, O Lord, delivering all from the snares of the enemy; and having shown Thyself unto Thine Apostles, Thou didst send them forth unto the preaching, and through them hast granted Thy peace unto the universe, O only Much-Merciful One.
