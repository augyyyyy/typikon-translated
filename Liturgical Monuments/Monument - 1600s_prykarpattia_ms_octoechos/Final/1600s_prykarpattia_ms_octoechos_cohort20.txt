[Saturday.]
*On Saturday at Matins, after the first Kathisma, Sessional Hymns to the Martyrs, Tone 8:*
*Sessional Hymn 1:*
With celestial light creation is enlightened today, even this temple; for in it the angelic hosts rejoice, and with them the choirs of martyrs make glad, in the memory of the passion-bearers: by their prayers send down, O Christ, unto our souls peace and great mercy.

*Glory... Sessional Hymn 2:*
As noetic luminaries were ye revealed, O holy martyrs, unto the world...
