# TONE 5: SATURDAY VESPERS, SUNDAY MATINS, AND WEEKDAY OFFICES

## TONE 5 [^213]

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 5)

[f. 137rA] ON SATURDAY AT LITTLE VESPERS:[^214]

We set 4 verses, and we sing Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 5:

[f. 137rA] By Thy precious Cross, O Christ, Thou didst put the devil to shame; and by Thy Resurrection Thou didst blunt the sting of sin, and didst save us from the gates of death. We glorify Thee, O Only-begotten One!

He Who grants Resurrection unto the human race was led as a sheep to the slaughter; the princes of hades were terrified of Him, and the gates of sorrow were lifted up. For Christ, the King of glory, entered in, saying unto those in bonds: Come forth! and unto those in darkness: Uncover yourselves!

A great wonder! The Creator of the invisible, having suffered in the flesh through love of mankind, the Immortal One is risen! Come, all ye families of the nations, let us worship Him; for having been delivered from deception by His compassion, we have learned to hymn one God in three Hypostases!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Rejoice, noetic door, through which the Chief Illuminator, God, passed! Rejoice, rod of Aaron, that didst blossom forth a flower from the root of Jesse! Rejoice, Table that didst provide the Bread of Life as food [f. 137rB] unto all the human race, O Virgin Pure Mother!

At the Aposticha, Resurrectional Sticheron, Tone 5:

Thee, Who wast incarnate, Christ the Savior, and yet wast not separated from heaven, we magnify with voices of song; for as the Lover of mankind, O Lord, Thou didst accept the Cross and death for our race, and having crushed the gates of hades, Thou didst rise on the third day, saving our souls!

*Other Stichera to the Most-holy Theotokos, Tone 5.*

*Podoben: "Rejoice, O life-bearing Cross" (Ра́дꙋйся живоно):*

*Verse: Virgins shall be brought unto the King after her, her companions shall be brought unto Thee.*

Accept, O tender-hearted Mother of God, this singing from unworthy souls and from our defiled lips, as the widow's two mites, brought thankfully with love. Bestowing great things in return for small by Thy grace, deliver Thy suppliants from every assault and malice, O Lady, dissolving the walls of present approaching affliction; as Thou art wise, calm them by Thy glorious prayers, and [f. 137vA] granting great mercy!

*Verse: The rich among the people shall entreat thy countenance.*

Truly a Cherubic Throne wast Thou revealed, being higher than creation; for having received God the Word, Who desired to renew our form, He dwelt in Thee, O All-pure One, and came forth from Thee clad in flesh, as He is tender-hearted; He accepted the Cross and Passion for our sake, and as God granted the Resurrection. Therefore, knowing that Thou hast reconciled our condemned nature unto the Creator, O Virgin, thankfully we pray: through Thine intercessions may we obtain forgiveness and remission of transgressions!

*Verse: I will remember thy name in every generation and generation.*

My soul having been continually burdened with darkness, O Lady, from the multitude of sins and cruel bonds: I dare not look up unto the height of God. Therefore, prostrate upon the earth...

O Thou Who didst cause the all-merciful God to shine forth from Thy most-pure womb, show forth Thy wonders now ineffably; and lifting up Thy hands unto Thy Son, O All-pure One, send down great mercy upon me who pray!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Who can worthily call Thee, O pure Mother of God? Shall I name Thee the God-receiving Church? Or the Garden of Eden? Or Noah's glorious Ark of salvation? The Dominion of God the King of all? The Holy Nation? The Abode of Christ our God? For Thou wast likened unto the Ark of Moses, having within the cleansing and the blossomed Rod, the Candlestick, the Urn, and the all-golden Censer; unto which every believer flees and asks great mercy!

[f. 138rA] ON SATURDAY AT GREAT VESPERS:[^215]

Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 5.

Work of John of Damascus.

[f. 138rA] By Thy precious Cross, O Christ, Thou didst put the devil to shame; and by Thy Resurrection Thou didst blunt the sting of sin, and didst save us from the gates of death. We glorify Thee, O Only-begotten One!

He Who grants Resurrection unto the human race was led as a sheep to the slaughter; the princes of hades were terrified of Him, and the gates of sorrow were lifted up. For Christ, the King of glory, entered in, saying unto those in bonds: Come forth! and unto those in darkness: Uncover yourselves!

A great wonder! The Creator of the invisible, having suffered in the flesh through love of mankind, the Immortal One is risen! Come, all ye families of the nations, let us worship Him; for having been delivered from deception by His compassion, we have learned to hymn one God in three Hypostases!

[f. 138rB] Other Stichera, Anatolian (Eastern), Tone the same:

We offer evening worship unto Thee, the Unwaning Light, Who at the end of the ages, as in a glass, didst shine forth in the flesh unto the world, and descended even unto hades, and shattered the darkness therein, and showed forth the light of the Resurrection unto the nations. O Light-giver, Lord, glory to Thee!

Let us glorify Christ, the Author of our salvation; for when He was risen from the dead, the world was saved from deception. The angelic choir rejoices, and demonic deceit flees away; fallen Adam is raised, and the devil is brought to naught!

The guards who were instructed by the lawless to conceal the Resurrection of Christ received pieces of silver and said: While we slept, the dead man was stolen from the tomb [f. 138vA] Who hath ever seen, or who hath heard, that a corpse was stolen at any time, especially one anointed with myrrh and naked, leaving behind his grave-clothes in the tomb? Be not deceived, O Jews! Learn the sayings of the Prophets, and understand that He is in truth the Redeemer of the world, and All-powerful!

O Lord, Who didst despoil hades and trample upon death, our Savior, and didst enlighten the world by Thy precious Cross: have mercy on us!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion (Dogmatikon), Tone 5:[^216]

In the Red Sea of old an image of the unwedded Bride was prefigured. There Moses was the divider of the water; here Gabriel was the minister of the wonder. Then Israel traversed the deep dry-shod; now the Virgin hath borne Christ without seed. The sea, after the passage of Israel, remained impassable; the Immaculate One, after the childbearing of Emmanuel, remained incorrupt. O Thou Who art, and Who wast before, and hast appeared as man, O God: have mercy on us!

At the Aposticha, Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 5:

Thee, Who wast incarnate, Christ the Savior, and yet wast not separated from heaven, we magnify with voices of song; for as the Lover of mankind, O Lord, Thou didst accept the Cross and death for our race, and having crushed the gates of hades, Thou didst rise on the third day, saving our souls!

Other Stichera according to the Alphabet:

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

[f. 138vB] When Thy side was pierced, O Life-giver, Thou didst pour forth streams of forgiveness unto all, life and salvation; and having accepted death in the flesh, Thou didst bestow immortality upon us. And taking up dwelling in the tomb, Thou didst set us free, co-resurrecting us with Thyself gloriously, as God. Therefore we cry aloud: O Lover of mankind, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

Strange is Thy Crucifixion, and Thy descent into hades, O Lover of mankind! For having despoiled it, and co-resurrected with Thyself gloriously the ancient prisoners, as God, Thou didst open paradise, and vouchsafed them to enjoy it. Therefore, unto us who glorify Thy three-day rising, grant cleansing of sins, making us dwellers of paradise, as Thou alone art tender-hearted!

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

Thou Who for our sake didst accept suffering in the flesh, and on the third day didst rise from the dead: heal our carnal passions, and raise us up from cruel transgressions, O Lover of mankind, and save us!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Thou art the Temple and the Door, the Palace and the Throne of the King, O all-pure Virgin; from whom my Deliverer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of righteousness, desiring to enlighten those whom He fashioned according to His own image with His own hand. Therefore, O All-hymned One, as Thou hast acquired maternal boldness toward Him: unceasingly pray, that our souls [f. 139rA] may be saved!

Resurrection Troparion, Tone 5:

Let us, the faithful, hymn and worship the Word, co-unoriginate with the Father and the Spirit, Who was born of the Virgin for our salvation; for He was well-pleased to ascend the Cross in the flesh, to endure death, and to resurrect the dead by His glorious Resurrection!

Theotokion:

Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, rampart and protection of those who flee unto Thee! Rejoice, unstormed haven, rejoice, O unwedded Mother, who didst bear in the flesh Thy Creator and God! Pray unceasingly for those who praise and worship Thy childbearing!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 5)

On Sunday at the Midnight Office:[^217]

Canon to the Holy and Life-giving Trinity.

Work of Metrophanes.

Ode 1. Tone 5. Heirmos:

The steed and rider into the Red Sea:

Singing of the light of the single and tri-hypostatic countenance, we pray: Enlighten our mind, O Almighty God, and raise it up unto Thine ineffable glory!

The choir of immaterial angelic beauties unceasingly sings of Thee with thrice-holy hymns: the tri-radiant Unity and the perfect Trinity!

O pre-eternal and all-powerful One, grant unto my soul the paternal drink of Thy sweet, light-giving love, O Trinity, light-originating Unity; and divine, purifying compunction, as Thou art many-merciful unto all creation!

[f. 139rB] As rain upon the fleece, O Virgin, the divine dew descended invisibly into Thy womb from heaven, and saved parched human nature, O All-pure One!

Ode 3. Heirmos:

Thou Who upon nothing didst establish the noetic essences by Thy thought, and didst constitute them as unceasing chanters of Thy Godhead, O Tri-radiant God and Creator of all: receive also from mortals and earthborn beings praise and supplication, as Thou art tender-hearted!

Inaccessible unto any natural change, unto us who are mutable and lost: O unfathomable fountain of Thy goodness, grant remission of transgressions, O three-sunned Sun of glory!

We glorify the Father, the Son, and the Spirit: in the unchangeable countenance of the Godhead, the Trinity, the single and thrice-shining Lord of all; as the Prophets and Apostles were clearly instructed by Thee. Theotokion:

Thou didst appear unto Moses in the bush as the Angel of Great Counsel of the Almighty, prefiguring Thine incarnation from the Virgin, O Word of God, through which Thou didst refashion us, and didst lead us up unto heaven!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5.*

*Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́лно Сло́во):*

Thou art merciful, O undivided Trinity, and Thou hast compassion upon all as Thou art all-powerful, and as Thou art all-compassionate God and many-merciful. Therefore we flee unto Thee, weighed down by many transgressions, crying aloud: Purify Thy servants, deliver from every torment, and save us all!

[f. 139vA] O all-hymned Virgin, have mercy on us who flee with faith unto Thee Who art tender-hearted, and who ask Thy warm intercession; for Thou art able, as Thou art good, to save all, being the Mother of the Most High God, ever offering holy prayers, O Thou full of divine grace!

Ode 4. Heirmos:

Understanding Thy divine condescension with foresight, O Christ, Habbakuk cried aloud unto Thee with trembling: Unto salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed, art Thou come!

Daniel was secretly instructed concerning the one Tri-hypostatic Lord, seeing Christ as Son of Man going unto the Father, and prefiguring the vision of the Spirit!

With clay lips we hymn Thee, the pre-essential God in three Hypostases; vouchsafe us, being of a unified nature, angelic glory: one power and one Lordship in three Persons we indivisibly glorify: O Father, Son, and Spirit, enlighten us Thy servants! Theotokion:

The holy and overshadowed mountain which Habbakuk foresaw of old, from whence the Holy One came forth, foretold the inviolable childbearing of Thy conception, O Virgin!

Ode 5. Heirmos:[^217]

Clothed with light as with a garment, rising early unto Thee I cry aloud: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou alone art compassionate!

Thou Who out of goodness didst fashion man, and didst assemble him according to Thine own image: renew in me, O thrice-holy my God, as Thou art good and tender-hearted!

Do Thou Thyself guide me, O thrice-radiant Unity, unto the divine paths of salvation, and fill me with Thy radiance, as by nature God is unmeasured in power!

To behold the indivisible Light [f. 139vB] of Thy nature, distinguished by hypostatic characteristics, thrice-shining, unwaning: enlighten my heart with Thy rays! Theotokion:

As the Prophet foresaw Thee of old, O Pure and All-holy Virgin, as an open door unto the unwaning light, O Maiden, he recognized the dwelling-place of God!

Ode 6. Heirmos:

Surging with the storm of soul-corrupting passions, O Master Christ, calm the tempestuous sea, and lead me up from corruption, as Thou art compassionate!

O Tri-radiant Unity in three Persons, single in nature, conformed and equal in power, and in essence and will, as the Prophets foresaw, show forth the light of the Son unto Thy light!

O Tri-hypostatic Unity, possessing in three Persons one power and dominion: for by It all creation was constructed, and is renewed! Theotokion:

Deliverance from transgressions and from afflictions, as Thou art God, single and tri-radiant, send down unto Thy singer, through the supplications of the Mother of God!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5.*

*Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезна́чално Слово):*

Have mercy on me, O my Savior, have mercy on me! And reprove me not in Thy wrath, O Merciful One, nor enter into judgment with Thy servants in Thy compassions! For in truth, my countless transgressions appear before us; therefore I cry unto Thee: O Compassionate One, have mercy on me!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

In doubt concerning all things, I flee unto Thee, the hope of all and refuge of sinners and [f. 140rA] the humble, crying: I have sinned, and I remain in evils, unfeeling, wretch that I am! Have mercy on me before the end, convert me, and deliver Thy servant from every torment!

Ode 7. Heirmos:

The Lord God of our fathers, praised above all, extinguished the flame and bedewed the youths who sang with one accord: Blessed art Thou, O God!

Possessing an abyss of mercy, O Lord, and an unfathomable ocean of compassions: have mercy upon us who sing of Thee as the One God!

The thrice-radiant God of all, the sacredly sovereign, single and tri-hypostatic: Thee as God and Lord we hymn; grant remission of transgressions, as with boldness in faith we distinguish, yet divide not, the essence of God!

In the midst Thou didst glorify the Father, the Son, and the All-holy Spirit, co-unoriginate with the Father, the Blossom of the Godhead, co-eternal with the Spirit, granting life unto all mankind through the Virgin!

Ode 8. Heirmos:

Unto Thee, Creator of all, in the furnace, the youths, weaving a world-wide choir, sang: All ye works, chant unto the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

Thou didst appear of old, O my God, unto Abraham in the form of three men, prefiguring the Trinity of the Godhead, as he sang of Thy single dominion!

Vouchsafe me now to look upon Thy beneficent rays, O Unapproachable Light, Father of compassions, Word, and Spirit, that I may render thanks unto Thee, singing unto the Lord of all!

From the Father God is pre-eternal; the Son is the Son born of the Father; and the Holy and Life-giving Spirit proceeds from the Father, being manifest through the Son!

[f. 140rB] Thou didst shine forth unto us from the three-sunned glory the single, all-radiant Christ the Lord, secretly teaching all to hymn one Godhead in three Persons unto the ages!

Ode 9. Heirmos:

Isaiah, dance! A Virgin hath conceived in the womb, and hath borne a Son, Emmanuel, God and man; Orient is His name: Whom magnifying, we call the Virgin blessed!

Human deeds cannot worthily hymn the Sovereign Unity; yet as we are able, daring through faith, O Sovereign, Co-enthroned Trinity, we offer glory and praise unto Thy dominion!

With hypostatic glory, the Cherubim and Seraphim praise the single-origin, tri-radiant God with unceasing mouths; receive also us sinners, O Lord, who magnify Thy dominion!

Three lights today upon the Cherubic Throne, and the Seraphim standing round about Thee, covering their faces with wings, cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy, Thrice-holy God, glorified in Thy three holy Persons! Theotokion:

As pure, immaculate, and Virgin, Thou didst bear the unchanging Son and God, Who delivers us from temptations; entreat Him now to send down remission of transgressions upon us!

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 5)

At Matins, after the Gospel: "God is the Lord and hath appeared unto us" in Tone 5. And we chant the Resurrection Troparion: "The Word co-unoriginate"; Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion: "Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord"; then the usual Kathisma reading of the Psalter [f. 140vA] After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymns: Resurrectional, Tone 5:[^218]

Let us praise the Cross of the Lord, honor His holy Burial with songs, and glorify His Resurrection! For as God He raised the dead from the tombs, having despoiled the dominion of death and the might of the devil, and shone forth light unto those in hades!

Glory:

O Lord, after Thy three-day Resurrection and the worship of the Apostles, Peter cried aloud unto Thee: The women took courage, but I was afraid; the thief confessed Thee as God, but I denied Thee. Wilt Thou then call me still to be Thy disciple, or wilt Thou show me again as a fisher drawing men from the deep? But receive me in repentance, O God, and save me!

Now and ever... Theotokion:

Dread is the wonder of Thy conception, and ineffable the manner of Thy childbearing, made known in Thee, O Pure Ever-Virgin: my mind is astonished, and my thought is seized with awe, at Thy glory, O Theotokos, spread abroad unto all for the salvation of our souls!

After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymns: Resurrectional, Tone 5:

O Lord, Thou wast called dead, Who didst put death to death; Thou wast placed in the tomb, Who didst empty the tombs. Above, the soldiers guarded the tomb; below, Thou didst resurrect the dead from all ages. O Almighty and Incomprehensible Lord, glory to Thee!

O Lord, between the condemned the lawless nailed Thee, and with a spear they pierced Thy side [f. 140vB] O Many-merciful One; and Thou didst accept burial, Who didst destroy the gates of hades, and didst rise on the third day! The women ran to see Thee, and announced the rising unto the Apostles. O Savior praised above all, Whom the Angels sing: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, glory to Thee!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Rejoice, holy mountain, impassable to man! Rejoice, animate and unburned Bush! Rejoice, only bridge of the world unto God, leading mortals unto eternal life! Rejoice, incorrupt Maiden, who without knowing man didst bear the Salvation of our souls!

Hypakoe, Tone 5:

Terrifying the mind with angelic appearance, yet enlightening their souls with the divine rising, the Myrrh-bearing women proclaimed the good tidings unto the Apostles: Announce among the nations the Resurrection of the Lord, Who works wonders with you, and grants us great mercy!

Hymns of Ascents (Anavathmoi), Tone 5:[^219]

Antiphon 1:

When I am in affliction, like David I sing unto Thee, O my Savior: Deliver my soul from a deceitful tongue!

Unto those in the wilderness life is blessed, being borne aloft by divine desire!

By the Holy Spirit all things are upheld, visible and invisible; for being self-sovereign, He is truly One of the Trinity!

Antiphon 2:

[f. 141rA] Let us be lifted up unto the mountains, O my soul; go thither, whence help shall come!

May Thy right hand, O Christ, stretched forth over me, preserve me from all deception!

Unto the Holy Spirit, speaking of God, let us say: Thou art God, Life, Desire, Light, Mind; Thou art Goodness, Thou reignest unto the ages!

Antiphon 3:

Concerning those who said unto me: We will go into the courts of the Lord; being filled with great joy, I send up prayers!

In the house of David dread things are accomplished; for there fire burns every defiled mind!

Unto the Holy Spirit belongs the life-originating dignity: from Whom every living creature is animated, as in the Father, together with the Word!

Prokeimenon, Tone 5:

Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; for Thou reignest unto the ages!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

Let every breath praise the Lord!

*Verse: Praise God in His saints; praise Him in the firmament of His power.*

Resurrection Gospel. We sing: Psalm 50 ("Have mercy on me, O God"). And the rest in order.

RESURRECTION CANON.

Ode 1. Tone 5. Heirmos:

The steed and rider into the Red Sea: Christ Who shatters warfare with a high arm shook them off; but He saved Israel, as they sang a song of victory!

[f. 141rB] The thorn-bearing Hebrew assembly, not preserving maternal love toward Thee, their Benefactor, O Christ, crowned Thee with thorns, Who didst loose the thorn-bearing sentence of the First-fashioned!

Having bowed down, O sinless Life-giver, Thou didst raise me up who had fallen into the pit; and without experiencing sin, O Christ, Thou didst endure my foul corruption, and didst perfume me with the myrrh of Thy divine essence! Theotokion:

The curse is dissolved, sorrow hath ceased; for the Blessed and Rejoicing One hath made joy to shine forth unto the faithful, bearing Christ the Blossom, a blessing unto all the ends of the earth!

Another Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection. Heirmos: Unto God the Savior:

Unto Him Who was willingly nailed upon the Cross in the flesh, and freed man who had fallen through the tree of the ancient sentence: unto Him alone let us sing, for He hath been glorified!

Unto Christ Who raised the dead from the tomb, and raised our nature with Himself, and adorned it with the Father's union: unto Him alone let us sing, for He hath been glorified! Theotokion:

O All-pure Mother of God, unceasingly pray unto God Who became incarnate of Thee and was not parted from the bosom of the Father, to deliver from every circumstance those whom He fashioned!

Another Canon to the Most-holy Theotokos.

*Heirmos: The steed and rider into the Red Sea...*

O All-pure One, pray unto Christ, the Light that dwelt within Thee and enlightened the world with the rays of His Godhead, to enlighten all who sing of Thee, O Virgin Mother!

[f. 141vA] Adorned with the beauty of virtues, O favored one, with the good-working, comely beams of the Spirit, all creation sings unto Him Who hath beautified all things.

The Bush on Sinai prefiguring Thee of old was not consumed, O Virgin, when united with the fire; for as a Virgin Thou didst give birth, and a Virgin Thou didst remain, beyond understanding, O Bride of God!

Ode 3. Heirmos:

Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and didst suspend its uncontainable weight: upon the unshakable rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, establish Thy Church, O only Good and Lover of mankind![^220]

Gall indeed they offered Thee, O Jesus Christ, Who didst pour forth honey from the rock and didst work wonders in the wilderness; and vinegar they gave Thee in return for the manna, the ungrateful children of Israel!

They who of old were covered with a light-bearing cloud placed Christ the Life in a tomb; but having risen by Thine own power, Thou didst grant unto all the faithful the mystical overshadowing of the Spirit's radiance from on high.

Thou wast revealed without corruption to be the Mother of God, Who didst shine forth from the incorrupt Father without maternal travails; therefore we confess Thee in orthodox manner to be the Mother of God, having given birth unto the incarnate Word.

[f. 141vB] Another [Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection].

Heirmos: By the power of Thy Cross, O Christ:

Thou didst rise from the tomb, O Christ, delivering from corruption the dead who sing of Thy three-day Resurrection, O Life-giver!

The Myrrh-bearing women hastened to anoint Thy body, O Christ; and not finding it, they returned, singing of Thy rising!

Pray unceasingly, O pure one, unto Him Who came forth from Thy womb, to deliver from the deceit of the devil those who hymn Thee as the Mother of God!

Another [Canon to the Theotokos].

*Heirmos: Thou Who upon nothing didst establish the noetic...*

The ladder whereby the Most High came down unto us to restore corrupted nature: having truly appeared unto all as the gateway unto the portals of Heaven, the All-good One was well-pleased to share with the world.

The mystery prepared of old, O Virgin, foreseen before the ages by God Who knows all things, in these latter days hath received its fulfillment in Thy womb, O all-immaculate one.

The curse, the ancient condemnation, is loosed through Thy mediation, O all-pure Virgin; for the Lord, having appeared from Thee, hath poured forth blessing unto all as the All-good One, the only adornment of mankind!

Ode 4. Heirmos:

Perceiving Thy divine condescension with prophetic insight, O Christ, Habakkuk cried aloud unto Thee in trembling: Thou art come for the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed ones!

Prefiguring in image Thy most-pure Cross, O Good One, which puts to death the sinful taste, Thou didst sweeten with wood the bitter waters of Marah!

The Cross in place of the tree of knowledge, gall in place of sweet food didst Thou receive, O my Savior; and in place of mortal corruption Thou didst pour forth Thy divine Blood!

[f. 142rA] Without union Thou didst conceive incorruptly in Thy womb, and without travail Thou didst give birth; and after childbirth Thou wast preserved a Virgin, having borne God in the flesh!

Another [Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection].

Heirmos: I heard the report of Thee:

At the place of the waters upon earth, the Cross was fixed on Golgotha; all the powers were shaken, and the veils in the heights were rent; and they cried: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

When the Savior descended unto those below as one dead, those who were dead from all ages arose with Him; and they cried: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Thou didst bear the Good One, unknown unto mothers; yet Thou art indeed a Mother, and remainedst a Virgin; whom singing, we cry aloud: Rejoice, O Mother of God!

Another [Canon to the Theotokos].

*Heirmos: Perceiving Thy divine condescension with prophetic insight, O...*

With pure heart and mind, and action and contemplation, grant me invincible and unconquerable grace, O pure one, that embracing the fruit of salvation from God, I may be saved through Thy prayers, O Virgin!

He Who created all things from the unseen was well-pleased as Benefactor to be created in the flesh from Thee, unto the salvation of those who sing of Thee in faith and love, O all-immaculate one!

The supramundane choirs sing of Thy childbirth, O all-immaculate one, rejoicing in the salvation of those who truly confess Thee in godly wisdom, O undefiled Virgin!

The rod of Jesse named Thee, from which blossomed forth unto us the fair flower, Christ God, unto the salvation of those who sing of Thee in faith and love, O all-immaculate one!

[f. 142rB] Ode 5. Heirmos:

Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment, unto Thee I rise early and unto Thee I cry: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as the only Merciful One!

The Lord of glory, in inglorious form, hangs willingly dishonored upon the Tree, ineffably devising divine glory for me!

Thou didst reclothe me in incorruption, tasting mortal corruption in the flesh without corruption, and shining forth from the tomb on the third day!

Having given birth without seed unto Christ, our Righteousness and Redemption, Thou didst make the nature of the forefather free from the curse, O Mother of God!

Another [Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection].

Heirmos: Rising early we cry unto Thee:

Thou didst stretch forth Thy hands upon the Tree, O our Savior, calling all unto Thyself as the Lover of mankind!

Having risen from the tomb on the third day, O Life-giver, Thou didst pour forth most beloved immortality unto all!

Unceasingly pray unto Him Whom Thou didst bear, O Mother of God, to save our souls, who sing of Thee in godly wisdom!

Another [Canon to the Theotokos].

*Heirmos: Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with...*

All the most-pure choirs, seeing that Thou wast to become the Mother of God, O most-pure Theotokos, found Thee alone to be pure and completely immaculate!

Blessed is the army of the Heavenly King; [f. 168vB] for even if the Passion-bearers were earthborn, yet they attained unto angelic rank: taking no care for their bodies, through their sufferings they were vouchsafed the honor of the bodiless! By their prayers, O Lord, save our souls!

Thy Passion-bearers, O Lord, being made like unto the angelic ranks, endured torments as though bodiless, having a steadfast hope of enjoying the promised blessings! By their prayers, O Christ, have mercy on us!

The holy Martyrs, having contested upon the earth, endured frost, were surrendered unto fire, and the water received them. Their voice cries: We went through fire and through water, and Thou broughtest us out into rest! By their prayers, O God, have mercy on us!

Sleeping together in torments, they cried aloud rejoicing: Slain by wounds, for the sake of the blows inflicted upon our bodies a radiant garment shall blossom forth for us in the heavenly Kingdom; for bonds, crowns of honor; for prison bonds, Paradise; and for condemnation with evildoers, life with the Angels! Therefore, by their prayers, O Lord, save our souls!

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Thou didst create me, O Lord, and lay Thy hand upon me; and Thou didst command and say: Unto the earth shalt thou go again! Guide me into the right way, forgiving my transgressions; remit and save me who pray, as Thou art the Lover of mankind!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

We bless Thee, O Virgin Mother of God, for from Thee hath shone forth Christ the Sun of Righteousness, Who hath great mercy!

[f. 169rA] *AT THE APOSTICHA, Stichera for the Departed, Tone 5.*

*Podoben: "Rejoice, O container of ascetics" (Ра́дꙋйся по́стникѡ):*

*Composition of Theophanes:*

Illumine the reposed with the light of Thy countenance, O Christ, as Thou art compassionate; settle them in a place of pasture, beside the waters of Thy pure and Divine rest, in the longed-for bosom of the Forefather Abraham, where Thy pure light shines and the fountains of grace pour forth, where the choirs of the universe dance, joyfully surrounding the righteous! Number Thy servants together with them, granting them great mercy!

*Verse: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord.*

Be Thou pleased, O Compassionate One, that those who have passed away from fleeting things unto Thee, the Benefactor of all and Lover of mankind, may sing with unceasing voices, glorifying Thy dominion: granting them to be enlightened with the beauty of Thy goodness, and to receive and purely enjoy sweet and lovely communion with Thee, where Angels dance around Thy throne and the assemblies of the saints joyfully surround it! Together with them, grant repose and great mercy unto Thy servants!

*Verse: Their souls shall dwell among good things.*

Where the choir of the Prophets, the ranks of the Apostles and the Martyrs, and all who from the beginning of the world have been justified by Thy saving Passion and [f. 169rB] Blood, wherewith Thou didst redeem captive man: there give rest unto those who have reposed in faith, as the Lover of mankind, forgiving their transgressions! For Thou alone didst live upon earth sinless, truly the Savior, and Thou alone art free among the dead. Therefore grant repose and great mercy unto Thy servants!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

From the law of sin which held us captive, Thou hast set us free, O Sovereign Lady, having conceived in Thy womb the Lawgiver and King, Christ, O only Virgin Mother, through Whom we are justified; for which cause also by grace entreat Him now to inscribe in the Book of Life the souls of those who hymn Thee as the Mother of God, that being saved by Thine intercession, O all-immaculate one, we may obtain the beloved redemption of Thy Son, worshipping Him Who bestows great mercy upon the world!

ON SATURDAY, The Beatitudes, Tone 5:

The Thief, believing Thee to be God upon the Cross, O Christ, confessed Thee purely from the heart: Remember me, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

Having slain and destroyed the author of evil, O Martyrs, ye ascended unto the heights, crowned with Divine victories, and ye stand before the King of all!

Enlightened with the light of the priesthood, the Hierarchs were glorified, and the multitudes of the Venerable obtained ever-existing [f. 169vA] life; therefore they are called blessed![^248]

Settle with the choirs of Thine elect in a place of refreshment, O Master, those whom Thou hast translated, O Word, overlooking their sins committed upon earth, O Christ!

Glory... Triadikon:

O Holy Trinity, be Thou entreated by the contests of the Hierarchs and Martyrs, and grant unto those who have died in faith salvation for their souls, and great mercy!

Now and ever... Theotokion:

Having given birth beyond understanding and word unto Him Whom naught can contain, O Thou full of God's grace: entreat Him to be merciful unto us all, O Sovereign Lady!

END OF TONE 5.

[^220]: Tone 5 Sunday Matins Canons, Odes 3–5 (physical leaves p141–p142): The Sunday Resurrection Canon, Cross-Resurrection Canon, and Canon to the Most-holy Theotokos, meditating upon Christ Who suspended the uncontainable weight of the earth upon nothing, the bitter gall offered in place of manna by ungrateful Israel, and the Virgin Mother prefigured in the unburned bush of Sinai.
[^221]: Tone 5 Sunday Matins Ode 6 and Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos (physical leaf p142): Celebrating Christ's descent into hades, shattering its eternal gates as Almighty, breaking the sting of death, and delivering Adam from the ancient curse; followed by the Ikos recounting the Myrrh-bearing women hearing the angelic proclamation and the Risen Lord appearing to send them unto Galilee.
[^222]: Tone 5 Sunday Matins Canons, Odes 7 and 8 (physical leaves p142–p143): Contemplating Christ Who assumed human flesh as bait upon a fishhook to draw down the spiritual serpent, the two natural wills and two essences of the Incarnate Word in His voluntary agony in Gethsemane, and the mocking of hades at His life-giving descent.
[^223]: Tone 5 Sunday Matins Ode 9 and Praises (Ainoi) (physical leaves p143–p144): The concluding ninth ode taking as its biblical model Isaiah's prophecy of Emmanuel, followed by the Resurrectional Praises Stichera in Tone 5, meditating upon the guards and the unbroken seals of the sepulchre, and the Anatolian (Eastern) Stichera proclaiming the overthrow of death.
[^224]: Tone 5 Praises Stichera (concluding) and Sunday Divine Liturgy Beatitudes (Makarismoi) (physical leaf p144): Concluding stichera depicting the soldiers' terror and the angel rolling away the stone, followed by the Sunday Liturgy Beatitudes praising the good thief who confessed Christ upon the cross and received paradise.
[^225]: Tone 5 Sunday Evening (Vespers for Monday) Penitential Stichera and Stichera to the Bodiless Hosts (physical leaves p144–p145): Opening the liturgical week with profound compunction, mourning the squandering of baptismal grace before the unquenchable fire and the sickle of death, followed by supplications unto the holy Archangels and the heavenly angelic hierarchies.
[^226]: Tone 5 Monday Matins Sessional Hymns and Penitential/Bodiless Canons, Odes 1–3 (physical leaves p145–p146): Sessional Hymns after the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Kathismata on the parable of the ten virgins and the impending judgment, followed by the Penitential Canon of Saint Joseph the Hymnographer and the Canon to the Bodiless Hosts.
[^227]: Tone 5 Monday Matins Canons (Odes 4–9), Aposticha, and Monday Divine Liturgy Beatitudes (physical leaves p146–p148): The completion of the Monday canons, celebrating the Archangels Michael and Gabriel as heralds and guardians, the Penitential Aposticha meditating upon the barren fig tree, and the Monday Liturgy Beatitudes.
