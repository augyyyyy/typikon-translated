# TONE 4: SATURDAY VESPERS, SUNDAY MATINS, AND WEEKDAY OFFICES

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 4)

[f. 103rA] Tone Four.

ON SATURDAY EVENING, AT GREAT VESPERS:[^153]

Resurrection Stichera, Tone 4.

Unceasingly bowing down before Thy life-creating Cross, O Christ, we glorify Thy three-day Resurrection; for thereby Thou hast renewed corrupted human nature, O Almighty One, and hast renewed for us the ascent into heaven, as the only good and mankind-loving God.

Thou didst loose the condemnation of the tree of disobedience, O Savior, by willingly being nailed to the wood of the Cross; and descending into hades, O Mighty One, Thou didst tear apart the bonds of death as God; therefore we bow down before Thy Resurrection from the dead, crying aloud with joy: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

Thou didst shatter the gates of hades, O Lord, and by Thy death didst destroy the kingdom of death; and Thou didst set free the human race from corruption, granting life, incorruption, and great mercy unto the world.

[f. 103rB] Other Stichera, Anatolian.

Composition of Anatolius.

Come, ye peoples, let us sing of the Savior's three-day rising, whereby we have been delivered from the unbreakable bonds of hades, and have received incorruption and life, crying aloud: O Thou Who wast crucified, buried, and hast risen: save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

Angels and men, O Savior, sing of Thy three-day rising, whereby the ends of the universe have been enlightened, and we all have been delivered from the bondage of the enemy, crying aloud: O Life-creator, Almighty Savior, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

Thou didst shatter the brazen gates and crush the iron bars, O Christ God; and Thou didst raise up fallen mankind; therefore in unison we cry aloud: O Lord Who didst rise from the dead, glory to Thee!

O Lord, Thy birth from the Father is timeless and co-eternal; [f. 103vA] and Thine incarnation from the Virgin is ineffable and inexplicable unto men; and Thy descent into hades was dreadful unto the devil and his angels; for having trampled down death, Thou didst rise on the third day, granting incorruption and great mercy unto mankind.

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:[^154]

David, the forefather of God, through Thee, O Prophet, musically proclaimed concerning Thee unto Him Who hath done great things for Thee: The Queen stood at Thy right hand; for God, Who willed to become man from Thee without a father, showed Thee as the Mother and mediatress of life, that He might renew His own image corrupted by passions; and having found the wandering sheep, caught by wolves, taking it upon His shoulders, He brought it unto the Father, and according to His own will united it with the heavenly powers; and Christ, Who possesses great and abundant mercy, saved the world through the Mother of God.

AT THE APOSTICHA, STICHERON

Resurrectional. Tone 4.[^155]

Ascending the Cross, O Lord, Thou didst wipe away our ancestral curse; and descending into hades, Thou didst set free the eternal prisoners, granting incorruption unto the human race. Therefore, singing we glorify Thy life-creating and saving Resurrection.

Other Stichera, Alphabetical.

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

Suspended upon the wood, O only Mighty One, Thou didst shake all creation; and laid in the tomb, Thou didst raise up those dwelling in the tombs, granting incorruption and life [f. 103vB] unto the human race. Therefore, singing we glorify Thy three-day rising.

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

The lawless people, O Christ, having betrayed Thee unto Pilate, condemned Thee to be crucified, appearing ungrateful toward their Benefactor; but of Thine own will Thou didst endure burial, and by Thine own sovereign power didst rise on the third day as God, granting unto us unending life and great mercy.

*Verse: Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

Reaching the tomb with tears, the women sought Thee; and not finding Thee, they wept and cried aloud with lamentation, saying: Woe unto us, our Savior, King of all! How wast Thou stolen away? What place contains Thy life-bearing body? An Angel answered them, saying: Weep not, but go and proclaim that the Lord is risen, granting us joy, as the only Compassionate One!

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:

Grant consolation unto Thy servants, O All-immaculate One, putting down the fierce risings against us, delivering us from every affliction; for Thee alone do we hold as a firm and sure hope, and we have acquired Thy protection; let us not be put to shame, O Lady, who call upon Thee; hasten Thou unto the supplication of those who faithfully cry unto Thee: Rejoice, O Lady, helper of all, joy and protection of our souls!

Troparion, Tone 4.

The joyful news of the Resurrection having been learned from the Angel by the disciples of the Lord, and having cast away the ancestral condemnation, [f. 104rA] they spoke exultantly unto the Apostles: Death is overthrown, and Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

Theotokion:

The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto Angels was revealed through Thee, O Mother of God, unto those on earth, God being incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake; whereby having raised up the first-fashioned, He saved our souls from death.

ON SUNDAY, AT MIDNIGHT OFFICE,

Triadicon Canon. Composition of Metrophanes. Tone 4.[^156]

Ode 1. Heirmos.

The deep of the Red Sea:

Let us glorify the Persons of the divine-originating Trinity, the one Nature equal in essence of the Three, co-eternal and enthroned together, praying and saying: Save those who faithfully glorify Thee!

Thou wast anointed by the Father with the oil of gladness through the Spirit, O God-crowned One, as Son; and becoming man, Thou didst teach the co-essential nature of the one Godhead.

The Seraphim, unable to endure the beauty of the inaccessible glory of Thy three-sunned Unity, cover themselves with their wings, and unceasingly glorify Thee with thrice-holy hymns.

Theotokion:

Ineffably Thou gavest birth unto the Creator of all, O Most-pure One, Who delivers mankind from the ancient curse and from mortal corruption; through Thee have we come to know the one co-essential God.

Ode 3. Heirmos.

Not by wisdom nor by power:

[f. 104rB] Having shown power unto Thy holy Apostles of old, in that Thou didst send the Comforter from the Father, O Christ, Thou didst reveal the one three-sunned Nature.

The Patriarch Abraham, when he beheld the appearance in the form of men, revealed the lordship of Thy goodness, O Trinity.

The one God believed in three Persons, uncircumscribed in heaven, unsearchable in time: deliver our souls from every affliction.

Theotokion:

Having been translated through Thy Son, as ones taught: glorify the co-essential and most-radiant Godhead, and ever bless Thee, O Virgin.

Sessional Hymn, Tone 4.

To: Speedily anticipate:

O Thou Who by Thine only beckoning and the pure counsel of Thy Father and the Spirit didst create all things, O Christ abundant in mercy, Thou didst accept crucifixion for the human race; therefore giving thanks, singing we cry aloud: Have mercy upon Thy flock, O only Lover of mankind!

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:

We sing of Thee, O Bride of God, Mother of Christ God, glorifying Thy birth-giving which cannot be comprehended, whereby we were delivered from the deception of the devil and from all perils, O Lady Mother of God; therefore in faith we cry aloud: Have mercy upon Thy flock, O only All-immaculate One!

Ode 4. Heirmos.

Sitting in glory upon the throne:

The transcendent Trinity, in the unity of the Godhead, [f. 104vA] and Lordship, with the Seraphim we glorify Thee as indivisible essence, as unmingled, as equal in dignity of glory, O God, Who art unsearchable.[^157]

Divided ineffably in the Persons of the Godhead, and united in the dominion of one Lordship, the unchangeable Father without beginning begat the uncircumscribed Word, and the divine Spirit equal in power proceeded from Him; therefore we piously proclaim the Trinity equal in essence as God.

Prefigured of old in types, the Word of God prefigured Thine incarnation from Thee, O Virgin; but afterward, having appeared unto men, He was revealed in truth as the three-hypostatic Unity.

Ode 5. Heirmos.

All things were amazed:

Understanding by faith the all-creative Godhead to be one inaccessible Essence, we sing of three life-originating and co-enthroned Hypostases: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, the co-eternal Unity.

O three-sunned Light of Thy substantial light, shine upon me, O unique Godhead, uncreated Nature, and Fountain of every light-giving ray, that I may behold Thine ineffable beauty.

As unto the one Father, Creator and Upholder of all, and all-wise Helmsman, the true Giver of life: falling down, [f. 104vB] preserve those who sing of Thee. Theotokion:

Willing to deify the man corrupted of old, whom He fashioned out of goodness, Thy Son, O Virgin, showing the image of the divine likeness, truly became man, and proclaimed the one three-sunned Godhead.

Ode 6. Heirmos.

Crying aloud, he prefigured:

The Father Who spoke was wholly revealed, and the Spirit was seen as Christ was baptized; therefore we glorify the one Trinitarian Godhead.

As Isaiah beheld Thee sung with thrice-holy voices, sitting upon a high throne, he knew the Hypostases of the one Trinitarian Godhead.

Show an exalted heart toward Thy servants, O exalted King co-essential, that the brightness of Thy glory may be seen.

Theotokion:

I have sinned, yet I have not departed from Thee, wretched as I am; have compassion upon me, O co-essential, one honorable Godhead, and deliver me from grievous condemnation, through the supplications of the maiden of God.

Sessional Hymn, Tone 4.

To: Lifted up on the Cross:

O incomprehensible God and inaccessible, Who art one by nature, good and tender-compassionate: O Father, Son, and most-holy Spirit, preserve and cleanse those who in faith cry aloud in Thy holy Church by night and by day; and deliver us not, O Master, unto the enemies who seek our destruction, [f. 105rA] that Thy servants may ever sing from the heart of Thy mercy, O Lady, calling and saying: O most-holy Virgin, hasten and deliver us from enemies visible and invisible, and from every threat, for Thou art our protection.[^158]

Ode 7. Heirmos.

In the furnace the Abrahamic children:

Imitating the heavenly natures and the ranks of the bodiless beings who dive into the depths, all we earthborn piously glorify the one Godhead in three equal-working Hypostases.

The sayings of the holy Prophets revealed Thee of old in types as the single Creator of all, the ineffable God and Lord in three divine Hypostases.

Theotokion:

The Word, invisible by nature and all-creative, appeared in flesh unto men, becoming man from the pure Mother of God, calling man unto communion with Thy Godhead, O Christ.

Ode 8. Heirmos.

Daniel stretched forth his hands:

O unique and thrice-radiant Light, beginningless Essence, incomprehensible Beauty: dwell within my heart, and show me to be a radiant and pure temple of Thy Godhead, as I cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him unto the ages!

From various passions, O indivisible Trinity, unmingled Unity, deliver me from the darkening of transgressions; and enlighten me with Thy deifying Spirit, that I may behold Thy glory and sing of Thee, [f. 105rB] the glory of the Lord.

Theotokion:

The Mind unbegotten is the Father, and the Word co-beginningless, and the Spirit co-enthroned: one Essence, Power, and Being, transcendent and ineffable, the great-working Trinity and Unity; preserve Thy flock through the prayers of the Mother of God, as the only Lover of mankind.

Ode 9. Heirmos.

Let every earthborn:

Unto Thee now I direct my heart and thought, and both day and night my whole soul and body unto my Creator and Deliverer, O three-radiant Ruler of all; and I cry unto Thee: Save me Thy servant from perils and great afflictions!

Exalt our mind and thought unto Thee on high, enlighten us with Thy most-pure radiances, O Father, Word, and Comforter, dwelling in inaccessible light, O Sun of glory holding the light: ever to glorify Thee, the one-originating God in three Persons.

One Light in three properties, divided in Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, by Whom all creation subsists; Whom the Son, descending, renewed, suffered, and rose on the third day.

Save those who believe in Thee as God, who proclaim the transcendent beginningless Essence as one God: the three Persons, equal in beginning and dominion, of Thy Lordship, and worthy of divine glory, through the prayers of the pure Mother of God.

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 4)

At Matins, after God is the Lord: Troparion:

God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us: and the rest...

[f. 105vA] Tone 4. Then the Troparion: The radiant proclamation of the Resurrection: Glory... Both now... Theotokion: The mystery hidden from the ages: Then the customary reading of the Psalter.

After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymns:

Resurrectional. Tone 4.[^159]

Gazing upon the entrance of the tomb, and unable to endure the angelic flame, the Myrrh-bearers marvelled with trembling, saying: Was He stolen away, He Who opened Paradise unto the thief? Or is He risen, Who proclaimed His rising before His passion? In truth Christ is risen, granting life and resurrection unto those in hades!

Rising as immortal from hades, O Savior, Thou didst raise up Thy world with Thyself by Thy Resurrection, O Christ our God; Thou didst shatter in Thy might the dominion of death; Thou didst show forth the resurrection unto all, O Merciful One; therefore we glorify Thee, O only Lover of mankind!

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:

We magnify Thee, O Mother of God, crying aloud: Thou art the mountain from which the stone was cut out ineffably, which shattered the gates of hades.

After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymns:

*Resurrectional, Tone 4.*

*Podoben: "Joseph was amazed" (По: [Оу҆диви́ся І҆ѡ́сифъ]):*

By Thy voluntary counsel Thou didst endure crucifixion, O Savior, and in a new tomb mortal men laid Thee, Who by Thy word didst establish the ends of the earth; therefore the enemy was bound, and death was grievously despoiled, and all those in hades cried out unto Thy life-bearing rising: Christ is risen, [f. 105vB] the Giver of life, abiding unto the ages!

Gabriel descended from the heights on high, and approaching the stone where the Stone of life lay, shining in white he cried aloud unto the weeping women: Cease your cries of lamentation, ye who ever have grace; for Him Whom ye seek weeping, take courage, for He is truly risen; therefore cry aloud unto the Apostles that the Lord is risen!

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:

Joseph was astonished, beholding that which was beyond nature, and meditating upon the rain upon the fleece in Thy seedless conception, O Mother of God: the Bush unburnt by fire, Aaron's rod that budded; and he bore witness, as Thy betrothed and guardian, crying unto the priests: A Virgin gives birth, and after giving birth remains still a Virgin!

Hypakoe, Tone 4.

Running swiftly unto Thy glorious rising, the Myrrh-bearers proclaimed unto the Apostles, O Christ: that Thou art risen as God, granting great mercy unto the world.

Hymns of Degrees [Anavathmoi], Tone 4.

Antiphon 1.[^160]

From my youth many passions war against me; but do Thou Thyself help me and save me, O my Savior.

Ye who hate Zion, be put to shame by the Lord; for ye shall be withered up by fire even as grass.

By the Holy Spirit every soul is quickened, and exalted in purity; it is illumined by the Trinitarian Unity in sacred mystery.

[f. 106rA] Antiphon 2.

I cried unto Thee, O Lord, fervently from the depth of my soul, and let Thy divine ears be attentive unto my hearing.

Whosoever hath acquired hope in the Lord is higher than all who cause sorrow.

Through the Holy Spirit the streams of grace gush forth, watering all creation unto life.

Antiphon 3.

Let my heart be lifted up unto Thee, O Word, and let none of the beauties of the world sweeten me unto pleasure.

As one hath love toward his mother, so are we bound to have warmer affection toward the Lord.

Through the Holy Spirit comes the wealth of the knowledge of God, contemplation, and wisdom; for from Him the Word reveals all the Father's commandments.

Prokeimenon, Tone 4: Arise, O Lord, help us, and deliver us for Thy name's sake. [Ps. 43:26] Verse: O God, we have heard with our ears. [Ps. 43:1] Let every breath praise the Lord. Resurrection Gospel, and the rest in order.

THE RESURRECTION CANON.

Ode 1. Tone 4. Heirmos.[^161]

Traversing of old the deep of the Red Sea with dry feet, Israel conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness by Moses' hands stretched out in the form of the Cross.

He was lifted up upon the most-precious wood of the Cross, correcting our fall, healing the ancient total ruin, [f. 106rB] O Master, as good and almighty.

In the tomb bodily, in hades with the soul as God, in Paradise with the thief, and on the throne wast Thou, O Christ, with the Father and the Spirit, filling all things, Thou Who art uncircumscribed.

Theotokion:

Without seed by the Father's will, from the divine Spirit, Thou didst conceive the Son of God and gavest birth unto Him in the flesh: Who is from the Father without a mother, and for our sake from Thee without a father.

Another Canon, of the Cross and Resurrection.

Tone 4. Heirmos: I will open my mouth:

Healing the shattered nature of mankind, O Lord, Thou didst renew it by Thy divine blood, and shattering him who was mighty in strength, who of old shattered Thy creation.

The rising of the dead came to pass through Thy being put to death; for the strength of death was taken away, being surpassed by eternal life, when God, Who rules over all, became incarnate.

Theotokion:

Remaining glorious above the heavenly hosts, Thy divine temple became animate; Thou didst bear in Thy womb, O Virgin, holy mountain, our Lady.

Another Canon, to the Most-holy Mother of God.

*Tone 4. Heirmos: The three mighty captains: He Who was born...*

The peoples were shaken, the nations were troubled, and the mighty kingdoms were bowed down, O Pure One, from fear of Thy birth-giving; for my King came, cast down the tyrant, and delivered the world from evil.

Descending unto men, Christ cleansed the dwelling where He Who dwells in the highest dwelt, and showed it to be unshakable, the only [f. 106vA] vessel of virginity after childbearing, having preserved her who gave birth unto the Creator.[^162]

Ode 3. Heirmos.

Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying aloud: Thou art my strength, O Lord, my refuge, and my confirmation!

The wood of life, the spiritual and true Vine, hangs upon the wood, pouring forth incorruption unto all.

As great, as dreadful, He cast down the insolence of hades; and as incorrupt God, He is now risen bodily from the tomb.

Theotokion:

Thou alone, O Mother of God, wast the mediatress of goods beyond nature for those on earth; therefore we cry unto Thee: Rejoice!

Another [Canon]. Heirmos: Thy singers, O Mother of God:

The serpents full of poison for me, O Savior, who bit bitterly, Thou, O Almighty Lord, didst shatter by the nails of Thy hands; for there is none holy among the saints above Thee, O Lord!

Willingly seen in the tomb as dead, O Lover of mankind, Giver of life, Thou didst break open the gates of hades for the souls from the ages; for there is none holy among the saints save Thee, O Lord!

Theotokion:

Thou wast shown forth as the unploughed field, having brought forth the living Ear of grain Who is the mediator of immortality for all who partake, resting holily in the Holy of Holies.

Another [Canon]. Heirmos: Let the heavens rejoice:

Earthly creation marvelled at the wonders, having been united through Thee unto the unendurable divine Fire, [f. 106vB] as bread baked well in clay, O Most-pure Virgin, having preserved human nature uninjured.

Who is she that is truly near unto God, as having surpassed the angelic ranks, alone in the beauty of virginity, as the Mother who bore the Almighty?

Ode 4. Heirmos.

Beholding Thee, the Sun of righteousness, lifted up upon the Cross, the Church stood in her station, worthily crying aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Ascending the Cross, Thou didst compel the death of Thy life-creating Body; worthily was it put to death, and we cry aloud unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Thou didst descend, healing my passions, suffering upon the Cross in Thy most-pure flesh, in which Thou didst willingly clothe Thyself; therefore we cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Theotokion:

Without knowing wedlock Thou gavest birth, O Virgin, and after childbearing wast shown forth remaining a Virgin; therefore with unceasing voices we cry aloud unto Thee with unwavering faith: Rejoice, O Lady!

Another [Canon]. Heirmos: The unsearchable:

Israel, the son according to the law, knew Thee not as God, O Christ Who wast without iniquity; but as a lawbreaker, transgressing the law, he nailed Thee unto the Cross, being unworthy of the giving of the law.

Having deified Thy soul, O Savior, Thou didst despoil the treasures of hades, raising up the souls from the ages; and Thy life-bearing body poured forth incorruption for all time.

Theotokion:

Truly we glorify Thee, O Virgin, as the only Mother of God, whom the Bush unburnt prefigured unto Moses the beholder of God, mingled with fire, O Most-pure One.

Another [Canon]. Same Heirmos.

He lived with men, the Invisible One became visible, being in the form of the incomprehensible Godhead; and taking form from Thee, O Virgin, of the Father's flesh, unto those who know Thee as the pure Mother of God.

The Virgin received the Immaterial One materially in the midst of matter, an infant wrapped in swaddling clothes; therefore the incarnate God is known in two natures, both earthly and above essence.

After childbearing He Who dwelt as God in the Virgin preserved Thee a Virgin; and being born without seed, He remained the Word and God, and preserved the Virgin in childbearing, as God and Creator.

Ode 5. Heirmos.[^163]

Thou, O Lord, art my light, Who camest into the world, the holy Light turning back from gloomy ignorance those who sing of Thee in faith.

Thou, O Lord, didst mercifully come down unto the earth; Thou didst lift up fallen human nature, being exalted upon the wood.

Thou didst take away for me the condemnation of transgressions, O Christ; Thou didst loose the pangs of death, O Compassionate One, by Thy divine Resurrection.

We offer Thee as an invincible weapon against enemies; we have acquired Thee as confirmation and the hope of our salvation, O Bride of God.

[f. 107rB] Another [Canon]. Heirmos: All things were amazed:

Senseless hades prepared to receive Thee entirely, for beholding Thee nailed to the Cross, pierced with the lance, breathless, the living God: it thought Thee to be a mere man; but experiencing the might of Thy Godhead, it came to know Thee.

The tomb and hades, O Lover of mankind, divided the ruined temple of Thy body, having gone thither unwillingly unto those who had decayed; so that the souls of Thy saints and their bodies, O all-mortal One, were quickened.

Lo, now the prophetic utterance is fulfilled; for Thou, O Virgin unwedded, didst receive in Thy womb the God Who is over all, and gavest birth unto the timeless Son, Who grants peace unto all who sing of Thee.

Another [Canon]. Heirmos: Unto those walking in darkness:

A house of glory, and a holy mountain of God, O Pure One, a bridal chamber, a temple of sanctification of the Son of God Who dwelt in Thee, and made for us the eternal wounds of delight.

Thou didst take flesh from virginal blood, O Christ, a thickened substance and composition, endowed with speech and intellect, animate, effective, having will, self-determining, and sovereign.

The virginal womb put to shame the mind of tyrants; for as an infant He tested the soul-destroying viper's den with His hand, and cast down the proud whisperer, subjecting him under the feet of the faithful.

Ode 6. Heirmos.

I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries unto Thee, having been cleansed from the blood of demons by the mercy [f. 107vA] flowing from Thy side.

Thou didst ascend the Cross, girded with power, and grappling with the tyrant as God, Thou didst cast that adversary down from on high; and Adam Thou didst raise up by Thine invincible power.

Rising radiant and beautiful from the tomb, O Christ, Thou didst scatter all Thine enemies by divine power, and as God didst fill all things with gladness.

Theotokion:

O wonder newest of all wonders! For a Virgin, having conceived without a man, contained without confinement in her womb Him Who upholds all things by His word.

Another [Canon]. Heirmos:

I came into the depth of the sea:

Hades opened its throat and swallowed me, and expanded its soul, being unavoidable; but Christ, descending, brought up my life, as the Lover of mankind.

Death perished by death; for He Who was put to death is risen, granting me incorruption; appearing unto the women, the Immortal One proclaimed joy.

Theotokion:

Thy pure womb was shown forth as the spacious dwelling-place of the incomprehensible Godhead, O Mother of God, which the other ranks are unable to behold without fear.

Another [Canon]. Same Heirmos:

The evil and venomous serpent slew me, because of my foremother Eve; but now, O Pure One, He Who fashioned me called me back from corruption through Thee.

The abyss of ineffable beauty, O Maiden, clearly showed Thee forth as chosen, in whose womb Christ the Savior formed the lightning of the Godhead...

My Savior and Deliverer from the tomb, as God, raised up the earthborn from their bonds, and shattered the gates of hades; and as Master He is risen on the third day!

IKOS

Let all we earthborn praise Christ, the Giver of life, Who is risen from the dead on the third day from the tomb, Who today shattered the gates of death by His power, put hades to death, crushed the sting of death, and set free Adam with Eve, gratefully crying aloud diligent praise; for He alone, as the mighty God and Master, is risen on the third day!

Ode 7. Heirmos.

In the fiery furnace the Persian children of Abraham, burned rather by the desire of piety than by the flame, cried aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!

Human nature was brought unto incorruption, washed by the divine blood of Christ, gratefully singing: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!

As life-bearing, as truly more beautiful than Paradise, and brighter than any royal bridal chamber, O Christ, Thy tomb was shown forth: the fountain of our resurrection.

Theotokion:

Rejoice, O holy divine dwelling of the Most High! For through Thee joy was given unto those who cry unto Thee, O Mother of God: Blessed art Thou among women, O all-immaculate Lady!

[f. 108rA] Another [Canon]. Heirmos:

The god-wise ones did not serve creation:

Lifted up upon the wood, Thou didst humble the haughty eye, and the exalted pride Thou didst cast down unto the earth; and Thou didst save man, O praised God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

By Thy power Thou didst exalt the horn of those who serve Thee, Who art risen from the dead; and Thou didst empty the rich storehouse of hades, O praised God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

Triadicon:

Shining with divine rays, we glorify the one Godhead, as in three lights, unmingled, undivided, the eternal flame enlightening all who cry: O praised God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

Another [Canon]. Heirmos:

The three youths in Babylon:

Virginal love draws me unto singing, the fire unto rest in the Mother and Virgin: Blessed art Thou, O Lord, Who art.

Thou art higher than all creatures, for Thou gavest birth unto the Creator and Lord; therefore I cry unto Thee, O blessed Mother of God: The Lord of hosts is with Thee!

Triadicon:

I honor one Nature indivisible in three Hypostases, and I sing of the three-hypostatic Nature: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

Ode 8. Heirmos.[^165]

Daniel stretched forth his hands and shut the mouths of lions in the pit; and the youths, lovers of piety, girded with virtue, quenched the power of the fire, [f. 108rB] crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him unto the ages!

Stretching forth Thy hands upon the Cross, Thou didst gather all nations from the ends of the earth, and didst show forth one Church, O Christ, unto those who sing of Thee, both earthly and heavenly, singing in unison: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him unto the ages!

Shining in white, the Angel appeared unto the women with the untouchable light of the Resurrection, crying: Why seek ye the living in the tomb as dead? In truth Christ is risen, unto Whom we cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him unto the ages!

Theotokion:

Thou alone in all generations, O Virgin Most-pure, wast shown forth as the Mother of God; Thou, O all-immaculate One, becamest the dwelling of the Godhead, unburnt by the fire of the inaccessible Light; therefore all of us bless Thee, O Mother, Bride of God!

Another [Canon]. Heirmos: The godly youths:

Beholding Thine unjust slaughter, creation darkened and lamented; for the earth was troubled, and the sun clothed itself in gloom as a dark garment; and we sing of Thee, O Christ, and exalt Thee unto all ages!

Thou hast given unto the true sufferers and Martyrs boldness to entreat Thee; grant therefore divine deliverance unto those who have reposed in faith, through their supplications, granting great things in the dwelling-place of the Saints, O Lord!

By Thine all-effective beckoning, Who orderest all things for good, possessing authority over the living and ruling over the dead as all-powerful: place Thy servants whom Thou hast translated beside the waters of repose!

Thou Who art good by nature, and rich in mercies and goodness, deliver from outer darkness those who call upon Thy Name; having justified them by faith, enlighten them by grace, as Thou art the Lover of mankind! Theotokion:

The Prophets proclaimed the types of Thy childbearing, O all-immaculate one, while others pre-eminently composed titles for Thee; for Thou didst bear Life unto those in hades, destroying the dominion of death!

At the Praises: Stichera to the Martyrs, Tone 4:

Who will not marvel, seeing [f. 135vA] O Martyrs, the good fight which ye have fought? How, being in the flesh, ye conquered the bodiless enemy, confessing Christ, and arming yourselves with the Cross? Therefore ye were worthily revealed as expellers of demons and vanquishers of barbarians, unceasingly praying that our souls may be saved!

Ye became partakers with the Angels, O holy Martyrs, having manfully preached Christ as heirs. For having forsaken all pleasant things in the world as non-existent, ye held fast unto the Cross as firm hope; therefore having driven away deception, ye grant unto the faithful gifts of healing, unceasingly praying that our souls may be saved!

How can we fail to marvel at your struggle, O holy Martyrs? For being clad in a mortal body, ye conquered bodiless foes. The threats of the tormentors terrified you not, nor did the infliction of tortures frighten you; therefore ye are worthily glorified by Christ!

Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints; for with sword and fire and contrite soul they poured forth their blood, having hope in Thee, to receive the reward of their labors; having endured, they received from Thee, O Savior, great mercy!

In Thy resting-place, O Lord, where all Thy Saints repose: give rest unto Thy reposed servants, as Thou art the only Lover of mankind!

O only pure and all-immaculate Virgin, who didst bear God without seed: pray that our souls may be saved!

[f. 135vB] *At the Aposticha, Stichera for the Departed, Tone 4.*

*Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ єсѝ зна́менїе):*

Truly dread is the mystery of death, how the soul is violently parted from the body, severed from the harmony and natural bond by divine will! Therefore we pray Thee: grant repose unto the translated, in the tabernacles of Thy righteous ones, O Life-giving Lover of mankind!

Death appears as sleep unto those who grieve; for Thou wast placed in the tomb, Who rulest over all, and didst shatter the dominion of death, bringing to naught its ancient violence! Therefore we pray Thee: place the translated in the light of Thy Saints, and in the splendor of the righteous!

Thou wast made righteousness and sanctification unto us, and deliverance for our souls; for Thou didst bring us justified and redeemed unto the Father, having taken upon Thyself our due condemnation. And now we pray Thee, grant repose unto the translated, in the joy and brightness of Thy Saints, O Benefactor and Lover of mankind!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion, Tone 4:

Following the words of the God-inspired Prophets, O All-immaculate One, we profess Thee as Mother of God; for Thou didst ineffably give birth unto God incarnate, Who delivered us from burning transgressions; Whom now entreat to illumine with His radiance Thy reposed servants, O All-pure One!
