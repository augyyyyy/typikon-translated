# TONE 8: SATURDAY VESPERS, SUNDAY MATINS, AND WEEKDAY OFFICES

## TONE 8

SATURDAY EVENING, AT LITTLE VESPERS

Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 8.

Creation of John of Damascus.

[f. 228rA] An evening song and rational service we offer unto Thee, O Christ: for Thou wast well pleased to have mercy upon us for the sake of Thy Resurrection!

O Lord, Lord, cast us not away from Thy face; but be well pleased to have mercy upon us for the sake of Thy Resurrection!

Rejoice, O holy Sion, mother of the Churches, dwelling-place of God: for thou didst first receive forgiveness of sins for the sake of the Resurrection!

Other Eastern Stichera.

Tone 8:

The Word born of God the Father before all ages: in the last times, incarnate of the unwedded Virgin by His own will, endured death upon the Cross: and saved man, deadened of old, by His Resurrection!

Thy Resurrection from the dead we glorify, O Christ: wherewith Thou didst set free the race of Adam from the tyranny of hades, and as God didst bestow upon the world eternal life and great mercy!

[f. 228rB] Glory to Thee, O Christ Savior, only-begotten Son of God, Who wast nailed upon the Cross, and didst rise from the tomb on the third day!

Thee do we glorify, O Lord, Who willingly didst endure crucifixion for our sake: and unto Thee we bow down, O almighty Savior, cast us not away from Thy face; but hear and save us for the sake of Thy Resurrection, O Lover of mankind!

Glory: Now and ever: Dogmatic Theotokion:

The King of heaven for His love of mankind appeared upon earth and lived among men: for taking flesh from the pure Virgin and coming forth from her with the assumption thereof, He is one Son, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis: therefore, truly proclaiming Him as perfect God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God! Entreat Him, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may be saved![^306]

[f. 228vA] At the Aposticha, Stichera

Resurrectional. Tone 8:

Thou didst ascend the Cross, O Jesus, Who camest down from heaven; Thou camest unto death, O immortal Life, the true Light unto them that were in darkness; the Resurrection and illumination of all them that had fallen: O our Savior, glory to Thee!

Other Stichera according to Alphabet:

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

We glorify Christ Who rose from the dead: for He assumed soul and body, and by His Passion separated them one from the other: His soul indeed descended into hades, which also He despoiled; but in the tomb His holy body saw no corruption, the Redeemer of our souls!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

With psalms and songs we glorify Thy Resurrection from the dead, O Christ, wherewith Thou didst free us from the tyranny of hades; and as God Thou didst bestow upon us eternal life and great mercy!

*Verse: Holiness becometh Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days [Ps. 92:5].*

O incomprehensible Master of all, Creator of heaven and earth: by the sufferings of the Cross Thou didst pour forth dispassion unto me; and having received burial and risen in glory, Thou didst raise up Adam with Thyself by Thine almighty hand! Glory to Thy third-day rising, wherewith Thou hast granted us eternal life and cleansing of sins, as Thou alone art compassionate!

[f. 228vB] Glory: Now and ever: Theotokion:

O unwedded Virgin, who ineffably didst conceive God in the flesh, Mother of God the Most High: receive the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, who grantest unto all cleansing of transgressions: now receiving our entreaties, pray that we all may be saved!

Troparion, Tone 8:

From on high didst Thou descend, O Compassionate One, and didst submit to three-day burial, that Thou mightest free us from our passions: O our Life and Resurrection, Lord, glory to Thee!

Glory: Now and ever: Theotokion:

O Thou Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin and didst endure crucifixion, O Good One: Who by death didst despoil death and show forth the Resurrection as God: despise not them whom Thou didst create with Thine own hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; accept the Birth-giver of God who bore Thee, who prayeth for us: and save a despairing people, O our Savior!

SUNDAY AT THE MIDNIGHT OFFICE

Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating

T R I N I T Y.

Creation of Metrophanes.

Ode 1. Tone 8. Heirmos:

He plunged the armed Pharaoh into the deep:

Troparia:

Unto the Triple-radiant dominion, the Creator and Provider of all things, God Who alone is good by nature, and possesseth the single glory of Godhead: we fall down before the single Beginning, singing the Thrice-holy hymn!

Remembering the divine utterances and the all-pure sayings that declared it: we glorify the single divine Nature, ever-existing, co-beginningless, in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, creative and almighty!

[f. 229rA] Abraham having become a sacred mystagogue, saw the Trinity of old in sacred manner, and receiving it as unto the Lord in three Persons, he rejoiced; and in three Hypostases he recognized the single dominion!

Theotokion:

Without experience of wedlock thou didst bear Christ, Who assumed our nature for our sake, O all-pure one, and remained unchanged in both: Whom do thou entreat without ceasing, to grant me forgiveness of sins and deliverance from temptations!

Ode 3. Heirmos:

Thou Who in the beginning established the heavens:

Troparia:

Isaiah, seeing Thee on high upon the throne, O inaccessible God and King of glory, and the Cherubim and Seraphim crying aloud with unceasing hymns, praised the single three-hypostatic God!

The marvelous Word, begotten of the Father as from Mind, and the Holy Spirit ineffably proceeding: comprehended by each thought and by scriptural teachings, we honor the one Triple-radiant God!

The unbegotten Father, having incorruptibly begotten the Son as the radiance of His own essence, Light from Light, setteth forth by procession the co-enthroned Light of the Spirit, all-creative and of equal honor!

Theotokion:

Thou wast shown forth as a pure temple, O Virgin Mother Mary: unto Him Who divinely, all-powerfully, and most wisely established the other creation, ordering and upholding it: make Him merciful through thy maternal prayers!

[f. 229rB] Sessional Hymn, Tone 8.

*Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Пове́лѣнное та́инственно):*

The power of the Triple-radiant and honorable Godhead let us, the faithful, praise in hymns: for by a nod alone He established all things: the higher angelic ranks and the lower sacred orders of the Church, that they may cry: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O most good God! Glory and song be unto Thy dominion!

Glory: Now and ever: Theotokion:

O thou who didst bear the Unchangeable God: my heart, ever changing through sin and slothfulness by the assaults of the deceitful one, do thou, as good, establish by thy maternal prayers, that I also may thankfully glorify thee: O Birth-giver of God Mary, have mercy upon thy flock which thou hast acquired, O all-immaculate one!

Ode 4. Heirmos:

The mysteries, O Christ Lord...

Troparia:

The Orient of the Godhead having appeared: it dispersed all the dark night of passions from them that were in darkness, and the Sun of Righteousness shone forth: simple indeed according to essence, but three-hypostatic in Persons: Whom we ever praise and glorify!

Praised by Seraphic mouths, with one mouth we glorify Thee, the single and Triune in glory, in essence and in hypostases: crying aloud unto Thee for all: grant diverse forgiveness of sins unto Thy servants, O Upholder of all existing things![^307]

[f. 229vA] Indivisible, all-compassionate, tender-hearted, loving mankind, honorable and divine Trinity: forget not me Thy servant unto the end, nor annul the covenant which Thou hast made with Thy servants, for Thine ineffable mercy's sake!

Theotokion:

He Who was born of thee, O all-pure one, One of the Trinity, the all-creative and divine Word, almighty God, renewed human nature: Whom do thou entreat without ceasing, to deliver me from all sorrow!

Ode 5. Heirmos:

My spirit seeketh Thee early in the morning:

Troparia:

I understand Thee, O three-hypostatic, co-ever-existing Three Persons glorified: yet one Lord, the divine Nature, simply dividing and uniting; and faithfully we cry aloud: O single Beginning, Holy Trinity, deliver Thy servants from sorrow!

I weep bitterly for the weakness of my mind: how do I suffer against my will? An unstable alteration in truth: therefore I cry unto the Life-originating Trinity: O Holy Trinity, establish me in the good station!

Weighed down by the slumber of my sin, and cast into the sleep of death: as Thou art loving mankind, most good, and all-merciful, O divine Holy Trinity, have compassion and raise me up!

Theotokion:

O Virgin Mother, Maiden, all-pure, all-immaculate, full of God's grace: by thy prayers make Thy Son and God, the Lord Who loveth mankind, merciful unto me; and speedily deliver Thy servant from the passions of sin!

[f. 229vB] *Ode 6. Heirmos: Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine...*

Troparia:

Emulating the ranks of heavenly minds, O single Beginning of all, supersubstantial Trinity: with Thrice-holy hymns we glorify Thee!

With our earthen lips, unto Thee Who didst create man according to Thine image, and didst most wisely bring all things from non-being, God in three Persons, I bow down and honor, sing and magnify Thee!

O God the Ruler of all, and only Indivisible One: beholding me in Thine ineffable mercy, O three-hypostatic Master: enlighten me and give me understanding, as Thou art merciful!

Theotokion:

Thou wast shown forth as a temple unto the Uncontainable God, O all-pure one: show me also forth as a temple of divine grace, O all-holy Sovereign Lady, by thy supplications, and preserve me unharmed!

Sessional Hymn, Tone 8.

*Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Повелѣ́нное та́инственно):*

As beginningless Root, and co-beginningless Son, and Divine Spirit, truly we confess: unconfusedly, unchangeably, and indivisibly: a simple Trinity in essence and equal in dignity, singing with the Angels: Holy art Thou, O Father, and Son, with the most holy and honorable Spirit: remember them whom Thou hast created according to Thine image, O Word!

Glory: Now and ever: Theotokion:

We ever thank thee, O Birth-giver of God, and we magnify thee, O pure one, and bow down before thee: singing of thy birthgiving, O full of grace, crying unceasingly: Save us, O most merciful Virgin, as thou art good [f. 230rA] before the trial, that we thy servants may not be put to shame!

Ode 7. Heirmos:

The descending of the Father, the fire:

Troparia:

By Thine ineffable wisdom and the abyss of Thy goodness, show me Thy servant pitied now as of old; and deliver me from affliction, O Trinity, single God, and from sins and passions!

The Father unbegotten Father, and the Word begotten of Him, and the Divine Spirit, proceeding incomprehensibly: O single Beginning and Triple-radiant: unto Thee I sing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

Theotokion:

Slaying all by giving them to drink of the venom of sin, the enemy came upon me; but fleeing unto thee in faith, who didst bear the Prince of life: by thy prayers make Thy servant alive, and deliver me from assaults and passions, O only pure one!

Ode 8. Heirmos:

Sevenfold the furnace was heated:

Troparia:

Light unwaning, triple-radiant and supremely brilliant, single Beginning, sovereign, most simple, incomprehensible God, and sovereign Lord: now illumine my dark and darkened heart, and show it forth radiant and light-bearing, faithfully to praise and glorify Thee unto all ages!

With most sacred wings the divine Seraphim reverently cover their faces and feet, unable to endure the incomprehensible glory of Thy divine, [f. 230rB] self-beginning, supremely radiant Trinity: yet we also dare to sing of Thee and glorify Thee unto all ages!

Beginningless Lordship, all-powerful, most good, perfect in origin, beneficent, creative, ever-existing, providing for and saving all, single in essence and in three Persons: I glorify Thee, O my God, in faith unto all ages!

Theotokion:

The unwaning Sun shone forth upon earth by thy virginal birthgiving, O all-pure Sovereign Lady, and delivered earthly beings from the dark gloom of idolatry and evil: therefore now illumine me even more with the rays of the Godhead, and preserve thy servant!

Ode 9. Heirmos:

The heaven was amazed at this:

Troparia:

O Nature ruling over all, all-creative, primordial, higher nature, life-originating, tender-heartedly loving mankind: O single Beginning, Trinity, we sinners now glorify Thee, asking forgiveness of sins, peace unto the world, and unity of mind unto the Churches!

O single Dominion and Triple-radiant, single Beginning and Triple-sun: accept Thine own singers in beauteous manner, and deliver from transgressions, temptations, and difficulties; and speedily grant peace, O Lover of mankind, and union unto the Churches!

Theotokion:

Taking up Thine abode in the virginal womb, O Christ my Savior...[^308]

[f. 230vA] ...wisely, in a God-man manner, unchangeably, unconfusedly in truth: and sanctifying forever, Thy mercy abideth with Thy servants; therefore by the prayers of her that bore Thee, grant peace unto Thy Church!

With the showers of your sacred teachings, quench the fire of heresies, and burn up the cruel tares of impiety, O all-wise hierarchs of piety: and enlighten the souls of the faithful!

Having put to death the flesh by abstinence, the ascetics live after death; the choir of holy Prophets and the company of women, having suffered, were glorified: by their prayers, O Christ, deliver us!

Of the Departed:

Give rest, O Christ, unto the souls of all them that have fallen asleep in the hope of life, overlooking their sins committed in life: for the sake of Thy great tender mercy, O Thou Who alone art compassionate, the God of our fathers, Who art blessed!

Theotokion:

As a head thou didst receive the Word incarnate in thy womb: but entreat Him, O all-pure one, to cleanse me from sins, who flee unto thee in soul and body, and with pure faith!

Another Heirmos: The Hebrew Children:

(Canon for the Departed Ode 7 Troparia):

All desire was stretched forth by the Martyrs unto the only Master, being united unto Him in love, and singing: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

[f. 259vB] Thou grantest unto them that have been translated in faith the splendor of the divine Kingdom, clothing them with gifts of incorruption, as they cry aloud: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

Fill with joy and gladness Thy servants whom Thou hast translated, O compassionate One: and account them worthy to exclaim and sing unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

Theotokion:

Thou didst loose the curse of Eve, taking up Thine abode in the all-immaculate Virgin: Who showed her forth as a fountain of blessing unto them that cry aloud: Blessed art thou, O all-pure one, and blessed is the Fruit of thy womb!

Ode 8. Heirmos:

The God-proclaiming Children in the furnace, trampling upon the flame with the fire, chanted: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

(Canon of All Saints Ode 8 Troparia):

Suffering, ye cast down the temples of idols, and fashioned yourselves as divine temples of the Trinity, O trophy-bearers of the Lord, companions of the Angels!

Thy priests, O Christ, clothed in righteousness, rejoice now together with the Venerables, clearly beholding Thy divine beauty!

By the prayers of Thy sacred Prophets, and of the all-blessed women, and of the righteous among men: grant Thy mercies, O Word, unto Thy world!

Of the Departed:

O righteous Judge, when Thou comest to judge: keep uncondemned those whom Thou hast received from us, overlooking their sins committed upon the earth, O Lord!

[f. 260rA] Theotokion:

Appearing unto me, O Virgin, quicken the dark clouds of my soul with thine enlightenment, O thou who didst bring forth the Sun of righteousness!

Another Heirmos: In the furnace the Children of Israel:

(Canon for the Departed Ode 8 Troparia):

Having passed through earthly passions, the true Martyrs received heavenly crowns, crying unceasingly: Sing praise unto the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

Descending into the nether pit, with Thy life-originating palm Thou didst raise them that dwelt in the graves: and Thou grantest rest unto Thy servants who have fallen asleep before us in faith, O compassionate One!

As the fountain of everlasting life, as the torrent of delight: account Thy servants who have piously departed unto Thee worthy to sing and glorify Thee unto all ages!

Theotokion:

O Mary, Virgin Theotokos, who in the flesh didst bear God the Savior unto men: save them that sing praise in faith unto thy Childbearing, and that supremely exalt the pure one unto all ages!

Ode 9. Heirmos:

Thee, the Mother of the Most High God, who knewest not wedlock, who beyond understanding didst bear the Word Who is truly God, and art higher than the all-pure Hosts, with unceasing glorifications we magnify!

(Canon of All Saints Ode 9 Troparia):

Standing as pillars before the most unrighteous tribunal, the Martyrs condemned every unrighteousness by the grace of Christ, and snatching men from the hold of the enemy, they received the crowns of righteousness!

Setting up teachers as rudders, piously steering this ship into the haven of God's commandments: all the sacred hierarchs and divine pastors: therefore as helmsmen we honor you!

[f. 260rB] The assembly of Prophets, the choir of Venerables, and the company of women who suffered brightly and shone in asceticism entreat Thee, O Lord: O Lover of mankind, we pray Thee: grant us Thy bounties!

That they may be accounted worthy of this honor and eternal glory, all the holy ones of Christ: account them that have departed from this life in faith worthy to receive the bright light, O merciful One, by the prayers of Thy saints!

Theotokion:

Bearing the fullness of good things in thine arms, O all-immaculate one: fulfill the supplications of us thy servants, and direct our paths unto God, strengthening us to live in virtues!

Another Heirmos: Every ear was amazed:

(Canon for the Departed Ode 9 Troparia):

Possessing unconquerable and invincible strength, O Martyrs of Christ: ye brought to naught the godless decree of the tormentors, and were accounted worthy of the heavenly Kingdom, being enlightened with the rays of the Trinity, O admirable ones!

Bitter hades was destroyed when Thou didst destroy it, O Lover of mankind: raising them that slept therein from the ages: and now also, as God, account them that have passed from this life worthy of Thine unwaning light!

All of Thee, O my Savior, art sweetness; all of Thee art desire and love truly insatiable; all of Thee art ineffable beauty: be pleased that they who have been translated unto Thee may take delight in Thy beauty, and make them partakers of Thy divine loveliness!

Theotokion:

Save...[^338]

[f. 260vA] Save me, O Mother of God, who didst bear my Savior, God and man: two natures, but not two persons: only-begotten of the Father, incorruptible, firstborn of all creation, Whom in two natures we magnify!

At the Praises, Stichera of the Martyrs. Tone 8:

What shall we call you, O holy ones? Cherubim? For Christ resteth upon you! Seraphim? For unceasingly ye glorify Him! Angels? For ye turned away from the body! Powers? For ye work miracles! Many are your names, and yet greater are your gifts! Pray ye that our souls may be saved!

Greatly did ye contend, O holy ones, manfully enduring torments from the lawless, confessing Christ before kings! And having departed from this life, ye work powers in the world, and heal the sick through your sufferings, O holy ones: pray that our souls may be saved!

Invincible Martyrs of Christ, having overcome deceit by the power of the Cross, ye received the grace of eternal life: ye feared not the threats of the tormentors, and when wounded by torments, ye made merry! And now your blood hath become healings for our souls: entreat Christ that our souls may be saved!

Clothed well in faith and by faith, and armed with the weapon of the Cross: ye appeared as valiant soldiers, manfully opposing the tormentors, and casting down the deceit of the devil; having become victors, ye were accounted worthy of crowns: [f. 260vB] pray unceasingly on our behalf, that our souls may be saved!

Of the Departed:

Behold Thy mortality, O Christ! For hadst Thou not been laid in a grave, Paradise would not have been opened: therefore, give rest unto them that have been translated, O Lord!

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:

O pure Virgin, door of the Word, Mother of our God: pray that we may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Departed. Tone 8.*

*Podoben: "O most marvelous wonder" (О преславное чудо):*

By a signature Thou didst write my liberation, O Master, staining Thy fingers with scarlet dye and reddening them with Thine own blood: and now we entreat Thee in faith: Number with Thy firstborn and with Thy righteous ones, and account them that have departed unto Thee, O merciful One, worthy to obtain joy!

*Verse: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord.*

Existing as man, Thou didst offer Thyself as a sacrifice unto the Father, raising man up from corruption: therefore settle them that have been translated in the land of the living, as Thou art the Lover of mankind, where the torrents of delight flow, and where the ever-flowing streams never run dry!

*Verse: Their souls shall dwell among good things.*

In the depths of Thine ineffable wisdom, Thou didst set a bound unto life, and didst foresee death: and translating Thy servants unto another life, whom Thou hast received in Thy goodness: settle them now upon the waters of rest...

[f. 261rA] ...in the splendors of the saints, where the voice of rejoicing and thanksgiving is heard!

Glory... Both now... Theotokion:

Having conceived the beginningless Word of God and God: with thy motherly boldness earnestly pray that He may settle thy servants where the never-dispersing assembly of them that rejoice and praise thee is found, where the splendors are assembled together, and the sweet voice of them that feast is heard, O divinely blessed one!

On Saturday,

The Beatitudes. Tone 8:

Remember us, O Christ, Savior of the world, even as Thou didst remember the thief upon the Tree: and account all worthy, O Thou Who alone art compassionate, of Thy heavenly Kingdom!

Firmly partaking in all illnesses: by your wounds, O holy passion-bearers, ye softened all the demonic darkness, through divine grace!

[f. 261rB] Venerables of Christ, and divine hierarchs, the assembly of the Prophets, and of all the righteous, have obtained the heavenly inheritance: whom let us worthily bless!

Of the Departed:

Settle, O God, in the lands of the righteous all them that have been translated in faith from this life: and show them to be heirs of Paradise, who God-fittingly hymn Thee!

Glory... Triadikon:

I offer unto Thee, O Trinity, the final hymnody: account them whom Thou hast translated in faith from the earth worthy of the mansions of the saints, and have mercy upon me, a prodigal!

Both now... Theotokion:

Having born the Fullness of all good things, fulfill our supplications, O Virgin: asking for us the forgiveness of transgressions, enlightenment, and great mercy!
