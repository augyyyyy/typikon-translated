# TONE 1: SATURDAY VESPERS, SUNDAY MATINS, AND WEEKDAY OFFICES

[f. 1rA] **Tone One**

**ON SATURDAY EVENING AT GREAT VESPERS**[^15]

*Resurrectional Stichera. Tone 1:*

Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins, for Thou alone art He Who hast revealed the Resurrection unto the world.

Surround Sion, O ye peoples, and encompass her, and give glory within her unto Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered us from our iniquities.

Come, O ye peoples, let us sing and worship Christ, glorifying His Resurrection from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered the world from the deceit of the enemy.

*Other Eastern Stichera, Tone 1:*

Rejoice, O heavens; sound the trumpet, ye foundations of the earth; cry aloud with joy, ye mountains! For behold, Emmanuel hath nailed our sins [f. 1rB] to the Cross, and, putting death to death, hath granted life, raising fallen Adam, the father of all, for He loves mankind.

Him Who was willingly crucified in the flesh for our sake, Who suffered, and was buried, and rose from the dead, let us praise, saying: Confirm Thy Church in Orthodoxy, O Christ, and make peace in our life, for Thou art good and lovest mankind.

Standing unworthy before Thy life-receiving tomb, we offer a hymn of praise unto Thine ineffable loving-kindness, O Christ our God; for Thou, being sinless, didst accept the Cross and death, that Thou mightest grant the Resurrection unto the world, as Thou lovest mankind.

Let us praise the Word, co-eternal with the Father [^16] [f. 1vA] Who ineffably came forth from a virginal womb, and willingly accepted the Cross and death for our sake, and rose in glory, saying: O Giver of life, O Lord, glory to Thee, the Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Let us sing of Mary the Virgin, the glory of the whole world, who blossomed forth from mankind and gave birth unto the Master, the heavenly gate, the hymn of the bodiless hosts and the adornment of the faithful. For she was revealed as heaven and the temple of the Godhead. She, having broken down the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace and opened the kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the anchor of faith, we have as our Defender the Lord Who was born of her. Be of good courage therefore, be of good courage, O people of God; for He shall conquer the enemies, for He is almighty.

*Rubrics:*

*Then: The Entrance. O Gladsome Light. The Prokeimenon of the day: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty. But outside of a vigil, if there is an augmented litany: Vouchsafe, O Lord, and the rest.*

*At the Aposticha, Resurrectional Sticheron, Tone 1:*

[f. 1vB] By Thy Passion, O Christ, we have been set free from passions, and by Thy Resurrection we have been delivered from corruption: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera according to the Alphabet. Tone 1:*

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

Let creation rejoice, let the heavens be glad; let the nations clap their hands with gladness! For Christ our Savior hath nailed our sins to the Cross, and, putting death to death, hath granted us life, raising the fallen Adam, the father of all, for He loves mankind.

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

Being King of heaven and earth, O Incomprehensible One, Thou wast willingly crucified for the love of mankind! Hades, meeting Thee below, was embittered, and the souls of the righteous, receiving Thee, rejoiced; and Adam, seeing Thee, the Creator, in the nethermost regions, arose! O wonder! How did He Who is the Life of all taste of death? But as He willed to enlighten the world, crying out and saying: O Lord Who art risen from the dead, glory to Thee!

[f. 2rA] *Verse: Holiness becomes Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.* [^17]

The Myrrh-bearing women, carrying myrrh, reached Thy tomb with haste and lamentation; and not finding Thy most pure Body, but learning from the Angel of the new and glorious miracle, they said unto the Apostles: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Lo, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled, for a Virgin hast thou given birth, and after giving birth, as before giving birth, thou didst remain. For God was He Who was born; therefore nature was miraculously made new. But, O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple; but as thou bearest the Merciful One in thine arms, have compassion upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*Then: Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart... Trisagion. After Our Father... Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 1:*

When the stone had been sealed by the Jews, and the soldiers were guarding [f. 2rB] Thine immaculate Body, Thou didst arise on the third day, O Savior, granting life unto the world. Therefore the Heavenly Powers cried out unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O Christ! Glory to Thy kingdom! Glory to Thy dispensation, O only Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*

As Gabriel proclaimed unto thee, O Virgin, *Rejoice!*, with that voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, as the righteous David said. Thou wast shown to be more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who dwelt in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who hath set us free through thy giving birth!

## ON MONDAY AT MATINS

*After the 1st Kathisma, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 1:*

In iniquities was I conceived, prodigal that I am; I dare not look up unto the height of heaven, but taking courage in Thy love for mankind, I cry: O God, cleanse me, a sinner, and save me!

If the righteous man scarcely be saved, where shall I, a sinner, appear, who have not borne the burden and heat of the day? But with the laborers of the eleventh hour, number me, O God, and have mercy on me.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Beholding the wonder of wonders in thee, O thou full of grace, creation rejoices; for thou didst conceive without seed and ineffably give birth unto Him Whom the ranks of Angels cannot behold: Him do thou implore, O Mother of God, that our souls may be saved.

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 1:*

Haste Thou to open Thy Fatherly embrace unto me: prodigally have I squandered my life, looking upon the inexhaustible wealth of Thy bounties, O Savior; despise not now my impoverished heart. For unto Thee, O Lord, in compunction do I cry: Father, I have sinned against heaven and before Thee!

Having distanced myself without measure from Thee, prodigally have I squandered my life, [f. 14rA] ever in bondage to irrational passions; but receive me through the prayers of Thine Angels, O compassionate Father, as the prodigal son, and save me, I pray Thee!

*For the Martyrs:*

With the praise of suffering and the honor of crowns were the glorious passion-bearers clothed by Thee, O Lord; for by the endurance of wounds they conquered the lawless, and by divine power they received victory from heaven. By their supplications, deliver us also from the invisible enemy, O Savior, and save us!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Protecting advocate and helper of all, unshakable hope and mediatress for the world: remember us, as thou art good, and grant remission of transgressions and salvation unto our souls, and guide us onto the paths of repentance. For unto thee do we flee in faith, and thee do we fervently entreat and lovingly implore: withhold not thy mercy from us![^24]

*Other Sessional Hymns, when the 3rd Kathisma is read, Tone 1:*

Thy judgment-seat is dreadful, and Thy judgment is righteous, but my deeds are vile; yet do Thou Thyself anticipate me, O merciful Savior, and deliver me from torment; rescue me, O Master, from the lot of the goats, and vouchsafe that I may stand at Thy right hand, O righteous Judge!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Unwedded and pure Virgin Mother of God, sole protectress and shelter of the faithful: deliver from perils and tribulations and all grievous distresses those who place their hope in thee, O Maiden, and save our souls by thy divine prayers!

### CANONS FOR MONDAY MATINS

## Penitential Canon, whose Acrostic is:
### "Wash away the defilement of my transgressions, O Word." The work of Joseph.[^25]
[f. 14rB] *Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

*Thy victorious right hand hath been glorified in strength in a manner befitting God; for, O Immortal One, as almighty it shattered the adversaries, forging a new path for Israel through the deep.*

Blot out the multitude of my sins according to the multitude of Thy mercies, I beseech Thee, O Christ, and grant me thoughts of repentance, that I may glorify the Mother of God, full of divine grace, who is beyond understanding.

Because of the tender mercy of Thy compassions, Thou didst appear on earth incarnate as man; therefore, O Word of God, receive me now who have sinned more than any man, as in repentance I fall down before Thy compassions.

*For the Martyrs:*

Imitating the death of Christ Who suffered willingly, O all-lauded and ever-memorable martyrs, ye endured manifold sufferings and death.

Bearing within you the fire of divine love, O sufferers, ye quenched it not; but ye burned up the fire of the flame of deception and extinguished all the fire of Gehenna.

*Theotokion:*

The ranks of the Bodiless Powers sing praise unto thee with us, O all-immaculate Virgin; for in awe thou didst give birth unto Him Who was truly incarnate without fiction, remaining a Virgin even as before childbirth, O Bride of God!

*Theotokion:*

Having adorned the orders of the supreme Powers as God, He Who is higher than the Cherubim dwelt in thy virginal womb, O Mother of God, and unchangeably became flesh.

Bow down, lament, O my soul, and cry aloud: I have sinned against the only One Who knows thy hidden secrets! And bring forth fruits of repentance, that the compassionate Lord may cleanse thee, His servant, and deliver thee from eternal [torment].

O good and compassionate Lord, save me: like the Publican I cry unto Thee with fear, dragged down deep by a multitude of sins, burdened with the weight of transgressions, and filled with boundless shame.

*For the Martyrs:*

Enlightened with the wisdom and understanding of the truth, the martyrs turned Hellenic wisdom into foolishness, manfully [f. 14vB] destroyed wickedness, and having suffered valiantly, worthily received the crowns of victory, rejoicing.

*For the Martyrs:*

Confessing the Trinity as a Monad in essence and three in Persons, O sufferers, ye destroyed the delusion of polytheism through divine faith; and ye were revealed as beacons, illuminating the hearts of all with the rays of grace.

*Theotokion:*

O pure Bride of God: thou didst give birth unto the Savior Who rests among the saints, the Son and Word co-unoriginate with the Father, Who through the Holy Spirit cleanses those who piously honor Him.

The Seraphim, drawing closely nigh unto the divine and deifying Light and abundantly filled with His primary outpourings, are illuminated in a primal manner; and they become secondary lights, being deified by grace.

Zealously desiring to praise the brightness of the Angels, let us fittingly entreat with purity of mind and pure lips the aid granted by God through them, that we may be renewed by their radiance.

Vouchsafed to behold the supersubstantial light as far as is possible, Gabriel brought the joyous greeting unto thee, O all-praised Maiden, proclaiming thereby the conception of the Word and foretelling His ineffable birth.

Ye were recognized upon earth as radiant morning stars, illuminating the faithful with the light of piety, O glorious warriors of Christ and all-wise hierarchs!

Ye were revealed as strangers upon earth and citizens of heaven, O God-bearing ascetics, having put to death the wisdom of the flesh through abstinence and humility!

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Show those whom Thou hast translated in faith to be partakers of Thine unwaning light and true delight, O Lover of mankind, and number them with the choirs of the saints!

*Theotokion:*

Those women who suffered valiantly and strove fervently were brought unto Christ through thee, O all-immaculate and all-hymned Mother of God, the Queen of glorious deeds!

*Another Canon [for the Departed]. Heirmos: The lowest abyss hath compassed us about, and...*

As Thou art merciful, grant unto the departed remission of sins and bestow eternal delight, where the brightness of Thy countenance shines and illuminates Thy passion-bearers!

With the blood that flowed from Thy side Thou didst redeem the world; therefore deliver those who have fallen asleep in faith from corruptible passions, O Christ, for Thou didst give Thyself as a ransom for all!

*Glory...*

Thou Who in the beginning didst fashion me with Thine all-pure hands [f. 32rB] and didst bestow a spirit, and when I had fallen grievously unto the earth didst renew me most beautifully: now do Thou Thyself give rest unto the souls of the departed!

*Both now... Theotokion:*

We hymn thee, O pure one clad with glory, through whom the unwaning Sun of Righteousness shone forth unto us who sit in darkness and the shadow of death: for thou wast the Mediatress of our salvation!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

*The fire did not touch nor harm Thy children in the furnace, O Savior; then the three sang as with one mouth, and blessed, saying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!*

Tried as gold in the fire by every torment, O martyrs, ye were manifestly revealed more radiant than any gold by divine grace, being preserved in the heavenly treasuries!

Being priests and divine ministers, all ye holy hierarchs offered unbloody sacrifices unto God, and shepherded the people where the Chief Shepherd is magnified!

Subduing the passions of the flesh and clad with dispassion as with strength, the venerable ones were revealed as companions of the Angels; by their prayers, O Christ, deliver us from assaults!

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Where the mystical food is, and where the light of Thy countenance shines, O Christ: settle those who have departed from among us in faith, O Benefactor, that they may piously glorify Thy goodness!

*Theotokion:*

Thee, O holy, most pure one, do all the holy women [f. 32vA] both women and the beauty of men, dancing in chorus with the Angels, joyfully praise God Who was born in the flesh of thee out of great loving-kindness!

*Another Canon [for the Departed]. Heirmos: Thee, O Mother of God, we faithful perceive...*

Grant unto those who have departed unto Thee to be illuminated with the rays of Thine all-pure glory, O Christ, that they may cry unto Thee without doubt: Blessed art Thou, O God praised by the fathers!

Thou didst truly become Adam, O Creator of Adam, and didst abolish the debt of Adam, therefore we pray Thee: grant rest unto the departed, crying unto Thee, O Christ, for Thou alone art greatly merciful!

*Glory...*

Thou Who alone knowest our natural weakness, O Christ God, good and merciful: place the departed in Thy Kingdom, where the unwaning light of Thy countenance shines, O Christ: praised by the fathers, O God highly exalted!

*Both now... Theotokion:*

The shadow of the Law and the ancient riddles passed away through thy birthgiving, O Mother of God, for thou didst cause the light of divine grace to shine forth unto us, whereby we were delivered from the ancient things, hymning God Who is highly exalted!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

*Him before Whom Angels and all the hosts tremble as their Creator and Lord: priests, hymn Him; children, glorify Him; people, bless and highly exalt Him unto all ages!*

All the martyrs passed through the herb-potions unharmed, and steadfastly endured the boiling caldrons, being gloriously refreshed by the fire of the divine Spirit! [f. 32vB] pre-eminently of the Spirit!

Thou didst give unto Thy prophets to foresee things far off, and didst sacredly make wise Thy hierarchs; by their prayers, O Christ God, enlighten the hearts of those who faithfully hymn Thee!

Having crucified yourselves unto the world by fastings, ye truly inherited transcendent life, even as from the ages all who have pleased God have done, ever and righteously!

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

Thou Who didst fashion man from the dust in a manner worthy of God: as Thou art good, account the faithful whom Thou hast translated from earth worthy of the delight of Paradise, overlooking all their transgressions!

*Theotokion:*

Knowing thee to be the beauty and undefiled among women, O pure Virgin, the women who suffered offered their prayers through thee unto God, meeting all the needs of thy servants!

*Another Canon [for the Departed]. Heirmos: The children in the furnace...*

Having justified them by faith, O Savior, by the prayers of the passion-bearers, show those whom Thou hast translated to be of the right hand, singing: Hymn the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

Having washed away every defilement of Thy departed servants with the dew of Thy love for mankind, grant them to praise Thee worthily, singing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, hymn and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

Thou Who hast dominion over death and life, and between all things: deign that those who have fallen asleep in faith may obtain Thy calling, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, hymn and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast the Mediatress of salvation unto us, O all-immaculate one...

## ON SATURDAY EVENING, AT LITTLE VESPERS, AT "LORD, I HAVE CRIED"

[f. 35rA] *Resurrectional Stichera, on 4, Tone 2:*[^44]

Unto the pre-eternal Word of God born of the Father, Who was incarnate of the Virgin Mary, come, let us bow down: for having endured crucifixion, He was committed unto burial as He Himself willed; and He rose from the dead, and saved me, wandering man!

Christ our Savior, being nailed upon the Cross, tore up the handwriting against us, and abolished the dominion of death: we bow down before His third-day resurrection!

With the Archangels let us hymn the resurrection of Christ: for He is the Redeemer and Savior of our souls, and in fearful glory and mighty power He comes again to judge the world which He created!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Beholding the wonders of the great mystery, I proclaim the Godhead; beholding the passions, I deny not the humanity. For Emmanuel opened the gates of nature as man, but as God He unloosed not the bars of virginity [f. 35rB] but He passed through in like manner as He was formed, even as He entered through hearing; and in like manner He was born as He was conceived: impassibly He entered, and ineffably He came forth, according to the Prophet who said: "This gate shall be shut, and no man shall pass through it, save only the Lord God of Israel, Who hath great mercy!"

### AT THE APOSTICHA

*Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 2:*

Thy resurrection, O Christ Savior, enlightened the whole universe and recalled Thine own creation: O All-powerful Lord, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera to the Theotokos, Tone 2.*

*Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Егда́ ѡ҆ дре́ва):*

*Verse: Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear.*

The joy of all who sorrow, protectress of the wronged, feeder of the hungry, consolation of travelers, haven of the tempest-tossed, visitor of the sick, shelter and defense of the afflicted, and staff of the blind: thou art all these, O Mother of the Most High God, most pure one; hasten, we pray thee, to save thy servants! [f. 35vA] shelter and defense of the afflicted, and staff of the blind: thou art all these, O Mother of the Most High God, most pure one; hasten, we pray thee, to save thy servants!

*Verse: The rich among the people shall entreat thy countenance.*

No other refuge unto God and the Good One have we acquired, save thee, O Virgin, Bride of God: cast us not away from thy protection, nor put us to shame who run with love unto thy patronage, O Mother of God; therefore hasten to our aid, and grant relief unto us, O Savior!

*Verse: I will remember thy name in every generation and generation.*

The fear of many-formed temptations and dire distresses now tempest-toss me, and the deep [f. 35vB] of despair drowns me; the winter of my transgressions grievously troubles me: anticipate and deliver me who am tempest-tossed, O only calm of the faithful, and lead me up unto the haven of the high divine calling!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou prayest for all, O Good One, who run with faith unto thy mighty protection: for we sinners have no other refuge unto God, in perils and sorrows an ever-present deliverance, laden with many sins, O Mother of the Most High God; therefore we fall down before thee: deliver thy servants from every distress![^45]

Then, "Now lettest Thou depart": Trisagion. Troparion: "When Thou didst descend unto death..." Theotokion: "All beyond comprehension..." And the dismissal. [f. 33rA] all-immaculate one, and abiding in brightness unto endless ages: therefore, O pure Virgin, do we ever bless and highly exalt all Thy works unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

*The life-receiving, ever-flowing fountain, the radiant, all-golden lampstand, the animate Church, the all-pure tabernacle more spacious than heaven and earth: as the Theotokos, let us, the faithful, magnify!*

Let us honor the sacred roots of the sacred martyrs, rejoicing: their sufferings and wounds, nailings and banishments, and blessed slaughter, whereby they were revealed as heirs of Christ!

As priests of God, the divine servants clothed themselves in righteousness, living ever in holiness; having finished their course in the midst of torments, they rejoice, magnifying Christ!

Having become the divine lips of the Lord, all the Prophets pre-announced His light unto all; with them now rejoice also the women who struggled valiantly and pleased God by fasting!

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

O Power that rulest over all: show those whom Thou hast translated from earth to be partakers of Thy Kingdom with Thy saints, and in Thine exceeding goodness overlook the deeds committed by them, O God of all!

O thou who didst bear the natural and timeless Light: loose all my temporal transgressions by thy prayers, and enlighten my mind that is ever darkened by slothfulness, that I may hymn thee and faithfully magnify thee!

[f. 33rB] *Another Canon [for the Departed]. Heirmos: The image of the pure...*

Spare Thine own creation, O God, as Thou art the Lover of mankind and merciful, and give rest unto all Thy servants where all the martyrs rejoice: those who have departed from temporal things in faith, O greatly merciful One!

Possessing an abyss of mercy that overflows the transgressions of Thy servants: receive those whom Thou hast chosen, O Lover of mankind, and give them rest in the bosom of Abraham, and show them in Thy light with Lazarus!

Having become the Redeemer and Savior of the human race by reason of the Crucifixion: as the Benefactor, account those who have now departed from among us worthy of divine delight, and of incorruptible life, and gladness, and brightness!

*Theotokion:*

O the wonder of thy wonders that are beyond understanding! For thou alone, O Virgin, hast given all under the sun to understand the newest wonder, O most pure one, of thine incomprehensible birthgiving; therefore we all magnify thee!

# AT THE PRAISES: STICHERA
*Martyrika, Tone 1:*

Passion-bearers of Christ, come, all ye people, let us honor them with spiritual songs and hymns: the luminaries of the world and preachers of the faith, the ever-flowing fountains from which healings pour forth unto the faithful! By their prayers, O Christ our God, grant peace unto Thy world, and great mercy unto our souls!

These warriors of the great King opposed the decrees of the torturers, manfully disregarding torments, and trampled down all deceit: worthily were [f. 33vA] they ask of the Savior peace, and great mercy unto our souls!

O all-lauded martyrs, neither tribulation, nor distress, nor famine, nor persecution, nor wounds, nor rage of wild beasts, nor sword, nor threatening fire were able to separate you from God; but rather by the love which ye had toward Him, as though contesting in bodies not your own, ye forgot nature and despised death! Therefore ye worthily received the reward of your labors, having become heirs of the Kingdom of Heaven: pray Christ for our souls!

All-lauded martyrs in the Lord: for ye contested with a good contest; ye opposed kings and conquered torturers; ye feared not fire nor sword, nor savage wild beasts that devoured your bodies! Sending up hymnody unto Christ with the Angels, ye received crowns from heaven: entreat that great mercy may be granted unto us!

*Hymn for the Departed (Поко́инъ):*

By Thy word, O my Savior, Thou dost reveal that Thou art the Resurrection of all; by a word, O Word, Thou didst raise Lazarus from the dead! Then the dead arose from the tombs, and the gates of Hades were shattered; then death appeared unto men as sleep! But Thou Who camest for the salvation of Thine own creation and not for condemnation: give rest unto those whom Thou hast chosen, for Thou art the Lover of mankind!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Rejoice on our behalf, O holy Virgin Theotokos, pure vessel of the whole universe: [f. 33vB] unquenchable lampstand, dwelling-place of the Uncontainable One, indestructible Church: Rejoice, from whom was born the Lamb of God, Who takes away the sins of the whole world!

### AT THE APOSTICHA

*Stichera for the Departed, Tone 1.*

*Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (Прехвальніи мꙋнцы):*

*Composition of Saint Theophanes.*

Unto Thee, O Savior, we pray: account the departed worthy of Thy sweet communion, and settle them in the mansions of the venerable ones, with Thy saints and in the heavenly habitations, in Thy tender mercy overlooking their iniquities and granting them rest!

*Verse: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord.*

Higher than visible things are Thy promises, O Savior, which eye hath not seen, and ear hath not heard, neither have entered into the heart of man; account the departed unto Thee worthy to obtain Thy sweet communion, and grant them rest!

*Verse: Their souls shall dwell among good things.*

Rejoicing in Thy Cross, and hoping in Thy Cross, Thy servants have departed unto Thee, O Lover of mankind: grant them now forgiveness of their transgressions through Thy Cross and the blood poured out for the life of the world, in Thy loving-kindness forgiving their sins and enlightening them with the light of Thy countenance!

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion:*

Entreat Christ, Whom thou didst bear, O Virgin Mother, to grant forgiveness of sins unto all who faithfully hymn thee!
