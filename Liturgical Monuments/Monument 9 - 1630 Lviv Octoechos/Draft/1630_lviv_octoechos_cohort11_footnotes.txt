[^148]: Saturday Matins, Stichera at the Aposticha: Dedicated to the Faithful Departed in Tone 3, sung to the model melody "Great is the power of Thy Cross, O Lord," with psalm verses from Ps. 64:5 (LXX) ("Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen...") and Ps. 24:13 (LXX) ("Their souls shall abide in good things").

[^149]: Saturday Beatitudes (Makarismoi): Commemorating the confession of the Good Thief (Luke 23:42), the contests of the martyrs, the prayers of all the saints, and supplications for the departed to dwell in the light of Christ's countenance.

[^153]: Tone 4, Saturday Evening Great Vespers: Opening with the canonical rubric and display title (# TONE 4), followed by the Resurrection Stichera on "Lord, I have cried" in Tone 4 and Anatolian Stichera celebrating Christ's victory over hades and death.

[^155]: Saturday Great Vespers, Aposticha Stichera: Resurrection Stichera in Tone 4 arranged alphabetically, accompanied by the Sabbath psalm verses from Ps. 92:1, 5 (LXX) ("The Lord is King...", "For He hath established the world...", "Holiness becometh Thy house...").

[^156]: Sunday Midnight Office, Triadicon Canon: Composed by Mitrofan (Metrophanes) of Smyrna in Tone 4, setting forth Orthodox Trinitarian theology on the co-essential, co-eternal, and co-enthroned Hypostases of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

[^157]: Sunday Midnight Office, Triadicon Canon, Odes 4–6 and Sessional Hymn: Meditating on the Trinitarian revelation in the baptism of Christ, the vision of the Prophet Isaiah (Isa. 6:1–3), and the uncreated divine nature.

[^158]: Sunday Midnight Office, Triadicon Canon, Odes 7–9: Concluding odes based on the biblical canticles of Daniel and the Three Holy Children, confessing the one Godhead in three Persons.

[^159]: Sunday Matins, Sessional Hymns (Kathismata): Sung after the First and Second Psalter readings, depicting the terror of the guards at the tomb, the amazement of the Myrrh-bearers, the appearance of the Archangel Gabriel, and the contemplation of the Mystery of the Incarnation by St. Joseph the Betrothed.

[^160]: Sunday Matins, Hymns of Degrees (Anavathmoi) in Tone 4: Antiphon 1 ("From my youth many passions war against me..."), Antiphon 2 ("I cried unto Thee, O Lord, fervently from the depth of my soul..."), and Antiphon 3, based on the Gradual Psalms (Psalms of Ascents, LXX 119–133) and extolling the vivifying action of the Holy Spirit.

[^161]: Sunday Matins, Resurrection Canons, Ode 1: Comprising the Canon of the Resurrection (Heirmos: "Traversing of old the deep of the Red Sea with dry feet..."), the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection (Heirmos: "I will open my mouth..."), and the Canon to the Most-holy Mother of God (Heirmos: "The three mighty captains...").

[^162]: Sunday Matins Canons, Odes 3 and 4: Extolling the life-giving Cross as the true Vine, the harrowing of hades, the typological fulfilment of the Burning Bush, and the two natures of the incarnate Christ.

[^163]: Sunday Matins Canons, Odes 5 and 6: Proclaiming Christ as the Light of the world, the quickening of the saints in hades, the Incarnation from the Virgin's pure blood with rational soul and human will, and the deliverance from the serpent.

[^164]: Sunday Matins, Kontakion and Ikos in Tone 4: "My Savior and Deliverer from the tomb...", celebrating Christ as the mighty God and Master Who shattered the gates of hades, crushed the sting of death, and set free Adam and Eve.

[^166]: Sunday Matins Canons, Ode 9: Meditating on the Cornerstone cut out without hands from the unhewn mountain (Dan. 2:34, 45) and the reconciliation of heaven and earth in the Resurrection.

[^167]: Sunday Matins, Stichera at the Praises (Ainoi): Resurrection and Anatolian Stichera in Tone 4 on "Let every breath praise the Lord" (Ps. 148, 150 LXX), celebrating the stone rolled away from the tomb, the astonishment of Mary Magdalene, and the rebuke of the unbelieving guards.

[^169]: Sunday Evening, Vespers for Monday: Penitential Stichera on "Lord, I have cried" in Tone 4 to the melody "Thou hast given a sign," invoking models of repentance (the Thief, the Prodigal, Manasseh, the Publican), followed by Stichera to the Bodiless Powers (the nine angelic ranks: Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones, Dominions, Principalities, Powers, Virtues, Archangels, and Angels).
