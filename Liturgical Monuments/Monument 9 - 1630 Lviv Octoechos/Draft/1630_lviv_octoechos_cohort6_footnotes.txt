[^66]: Folio 49v — Hymnographic Realia: Tuesday Matins Canon of Repentance and Canon to the Forerunner (Odes 8–9). Completing the Tuesday Matins hymnody begun in Cohort 5, Folio 49v presents Ode 8 with the Penitential Heirmos *"Въ купинѣ Мѡѵсею..."* (*"In the bush unto Moses..."*) and the second Canon to the Holy Forerunner (*"Пещь ѻгненна аногда въ Вавулѡнѣ..."*), meditating upon Saint John's baptism of Christ in the Jordan streams and entreating tears of repentance.

[^67]: Folio 50v — Typikon Rubrics: Tuesday Matins Aposticha and Beatitudes. Tuesday Matins concludes with penitential Aposticha (*"Содѣянныхъ моихъ зѡлъ помышляю..."*), the Martyrikon honoring the holy Martyrs as an unassailable wall of intercession, and the weekday Beatitudes (Blazhenna) with Troparia invoking the Thief's confession (*"Гласъ ти приноси разбойничь..."*).

[^68]: Folio 50v — Hymnographic Structure: Tuesday Vespers for Wednesday (Precious Cross & Theotokos). Tuesday evening initiates the liturgical commemoration of the Precious Cross with Stichera at *"Lord, I call"* set to the celebrated Automelon / Podoben *"When from the Tree"* (*"Егда ѿ древа..."*), depicting the cosmic convulsions at the Crucifixion (the darkening of luminaries, earthquake, rending of the temple veil) paired with the Compassion of the Mother of God (Stavrotheotokia).

[^69]: Folio 51v — Typikon Rubrics: Wednesday Matins Kathisma Sessional Hymns (Sedalna) of the Precious Cross. Following the first, second, and third poetic Kathismata of the Psalter, Wednesday Matins appoints Sessional Hymns honoring the Cross (*"Спасенїе содѣлалъ посредѣ земля, Христе Боже..."*), reflecting on Christ spreading His immaculate hands upon the Wood to gather the dispersed nations.

[^70]: Folio 51v — Hymnographic Realia: Wednesday Matins Canons of the Precious Cross and Theotokos. Wednesday Matins features the Canon to the Precious and Life-Giving Cross composed by Saint Joseph the Hymnographer (*"Твореніе Їѡсифово"*, Heirmos *"Въ глубинѣ потопи древле..."*) interwoven with the Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos (Heirmos *"Грядѣте людїе, поемъ пѣснь..."*).

[^71]: Folio 54v — Typikon Rubrics: Wednesday Matins Aposticha and Beatitudes. Wednesday Matins concludes with the Aposticha of the Cross (*"Креста твоего древо, Христе Боже, древо животное показа намъ..."*) and the weekday Beatitudes commemorating the Crucifixion and Adam's redemption.

[^72]: Folio 55r — Typikon Rubrics: Wednesday Vespers for Thursday (Holy Apostles & Saint Nicholas). Folio 55r bears a woodcut headpiece introducing Wednesday evening Vespers for Thursday, opening the weekly cycle dedicated to the Holy Apostles and Saint Nicholas of Myra with Stichera to the Podoben *"When from the Tree"* (*"Всю напоисте землю, якоже рѣки..."*).

[^73]: Folio 55v — Typikon Rubrics: Thursday Matins Kathisma Sessional Hymns to the Apostles and Saint Nicholas. Thursday Matins appoints Sessional Hymns after each Kathisma praising the fishermen who caught the nations in the net of the Spirit (*"Иже оумꙋдривый ловца паче рыторовъ..."*), followed by the Martyrikon and Theotokion.

[^74]: Folio 56r — Hymnographic Realia: Thursday Matins Canons to the Apostles and Saint Nicholas. Folio 56r introduces the dual Canons of Thursday Matins: the Canon to the Holy Apostles composed by Saint Theophanes the Branded (*"Твореніе Ѳеофаново"*, Heirmos *"Въ глубинѣ потопи древле..."*) and the Canon to Saint Nicholas composed by Saint Joseph the Hymnographer (*"Твореніе Їѡсифово"*, Heirmos *"Грядѣте людїе, поемъ..."*).

[^75]: Folio 58v — Typikon Rubrics: Thursday Matins Aposticha and Beatitudes. Concluding the Matins office of the Apostles and Saint Nicholas, the Aposticha celebrate the apostolic preachers whose names are magnified throughout the universe, followed by the Beatitudes commemorating their nets sweeping the earth.

[^76]: Folio 59r — Typikon Rubrics: Thursday Vespers for Friday (Precious Cross and Stavrotheotokia). Folio 59r opens Thursday evening Vespers for Friday with a woodcut headpiece and the solemn Stichera of the Precious Cross (*"Сыне, къ кресту пригвожденъ бывъ..."*) and the heart-rending laments of the Virgin at the foot of the Cross (*Stavrotheotokia*).
