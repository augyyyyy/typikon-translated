[^231]: Tone 5 Tuesday Matins Aposticha and Liturgy Beatitudes; Tuesday Evening (Vespers for Wednesday) Stichera of the Precious Cross (physical leaves p152–p153): Concluding Tuesday Matins with penitential compunction and praises of the Holy Martyrs, followed by the Tuesday Evening Vespers Stichera on "Lord, I have cried" to the melody "Rejoice, O container of ascetics," meditating upon Christ uplifted upon the Cross like a Lamb, quenching the flaming sword that barred access to Eden.

[^233]: Tone 5 Wednesday Matins Canon of the Precious Cross and Canon to the Most-holy Theotokos, Odes 1–4 (physical leaves p154–p155): Composed by Saint Joseph the Hymnographer, contemplating Christ as the fruitful Vine uplifted upon the Tree, distilling the wine of spiritual joy, and the Mother of God as the mystical cloud showering forth divine rain.

[^234]: Tone 5 Wednesday Matins Canons, Odes 5–8 (physical leaves p155–p156): Meditating upon the righteous Judge standing before an unjust judge to condemn ancient enmity in His flesh, the tearing of the veil and splitting of rocks at His expiration, and the Holy Martyrs who stood firm like adamants against fiery torments.

[^235]: Tone 5 Wednesday Matins Ode 9, Aposticha, and Wednesday Divine Liturgy Beatitudes (physical leaves p156–p157): Concluding the Matins canons with the Virgin beholding Her Son crowned with thorns, followed by the Cross Aposticha and Liturgy Beatitudes praising the Good Thief.

[^237]: Tone 5 Thursday Matins Sessional Hymns and Canons to the Apostles and Saint Nicholas, Odes 1–4 (physical leaves p158–p159): Sessional Hymns after the Kathismata celebrating the unlettered fishermen who put worldly philosophers to shame, followed by the Canon to the Apostles and the Canon to Saint Nicholas by Saint Joseph, meditating upon the spiritual arrows that broke the bows of Belial.

[^238]: Tone 5 Thursday Matins Canons to the Apostles and Saint Nicholas, Odes 5–8 (physical leaves p159–p160): Continuing the supplication unto the Apostles who seasoned corrupted souls with the salt of heavenly doctrine, and Saint Nicholas who silenced the blasphemous streams of Arius and delivered the unjustly condemned from execution.

[^239]: Tone 5 Thursday Matins Ode 9, Aposticha, Thursday Liturgy Beatitudes, and Thursday Evening (Vespers for Friday) Cross Stichera (physical leaves p160–p161): Completing Thursday Matins and Liturgy with the Apostles and Martyrs, followed by the opening of Thursday Evening Vespers of the Precious Cross on "Lord, I have cried" meditating upon all the trees of the forest rejoicing at the veneration of the Life-giving Wood.
