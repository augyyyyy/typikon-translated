# TONE 6: SATURDAY VESPERS, SUNDAY MATINS, AND WEEKDAY OFFICES

## TONE 6

ON SATURDAY AT LITTLE VESPERS:

Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 6:

[f. 170rA] Holding victory over hades, O Christ, Thou didst ascend upon the Cross, that Thou mightest resurrect with Thyself those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death, Thou Who art free among the dead, pouring forth life from Thine own light, O almighty Savior: have mercy on us!

Today Christ trampled down death, even as He said, and arose, and granted joy unto the world! That all of us crying aloud in hymns may say: O Fountain of Life, unapproachable Light, almighty Savior: have mercy on us!

From Thee, O Lord, Who art present throughout all creation, whither shall we sinners flee? In heaven Thou Thyself dwellest; in hades Thou didst trample down death; into the depths of the sea, there is Thy hand, O Master! Fleeing unto Thee, and falling down before Thee, we pray: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, have mercy on us!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Come, all ye nations, in a voice of rejoicing let us hymn the Most-holy Virgin and Mother of God, the furnace of human nature, the refuge of ineffable wonders! For through Her new things came to pass: the Unbeginning One hath a beginning; the Word becometh dense; God becometh man, that He may make man divine, not by confusion of natures, but by union of hypostases! For He cometh forth, One from two opposites, in each nature being perfect, indivisibly known, willing and acting according to each essence; for which cause we believe without doubt that for the sake of the saving dispensation, O Christ our God:

[f. 170rB] grant unto the world cleansing, peace, and great mercy!

At the Aposticha, Resurrectional Sticheron, Tone 6:

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, the Angels hymn in the heavens; and vouchsafe us on earth with pure heart to sing and glorify Thee!

*Other Stichera to the Most-holy Theotokos.*

*Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*

*Verse: Virgins shall be brought unto the King after her, her companions shall be brought unto Thee.*

As Thou gavest birth unto the incarnate Word, Who before the ages was co-everlasting with the Father, O undefiled Virgin: loose the bonds of my humble soul!

[f. 170vA] Unto Thee I cry, the help of the world: deliver me from the grip of passions and sinful woes, and pray, O all-immaculate one, save me from the snares of the alien, granting the great gift of Thy mercy![^249]

*Verse: Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear.*

Save me who am struck with dread in fornications, stripped of good deeds, and alienated from the grace of the Master, O Thou who alone gavest birth unto salvation for the world! Deliver me from perils and assaults who was willingly bound, and who through lack of understanding was cast into the pit of destruction, O Sovereign Lady, as Thou art merciful and profitable unto souls!

*Verse: I will remember thy name in every generation and generation.*

Be Thou my speedy defense, O Mother of God, and attend unto the prayer of Thy suppliant, O pure one! Stretch forth unto me the mighty hand of Thy help, O Mother of God; receive the groaning from my defiled heart, O Most-holy One, and bear it unto Thy Son and our God, O blessed Sovereign Lady, for the sake of Thy great mercy!

[f. 170vB] Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Pure one, who alone gavest birth unto God: deliver me from transgressions of soul, O Virgin, and save me, I pray, who am deadened! For Thou art the hope of the hopeless and the helper of the world; Thou hast the power, O Maiden, as Mother of the Lord! Despise not my supplication, nor forsake me, O all-ruling Sovereign Lady, but as Thou art gracious and loving, save me!

[Tailpiece: Medallion depicting the face of the Savior / Angel within a floral wreath].

[f. 171rA] ON SATURDAY AT GREAT VESPERS:

Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 6.

Composition of John of Damascus.

[f. 171rA] Holding victory over hades, O Christ, Thou didst ascend upon the Cross, that Thou mightest resurrect with Thyself those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death, Thou Who art free among the dead, pouring forth life from Thine own light, O almighty Savior: have mercy on us!

Today Christ trampled down death, even as He said, and arose, and granted joy unto the world! That all of us crying aloud in hymns may say: O Fountain of Life, unapproachable Light, almighty Savior: have mercy on us!

From Thee, O Lord, Who art present throughout all creation, whither shall we sinners flee? In heaven Thou Thyself dwellest; in hades Thou didst trample down death; into the depths of the sea, there is Thy hand, O Master! Fleeing unto Thee, and falling down before Thee, we pray: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, have mercy on us!

Other Stichera: Anatolian. Same Tone:

In Thy Cross, O Christ, do we boast, and Thy Resurrection do we hymn and glorify:

[f. 171rB] for Thou art our God, and beside Thee we know no other!

Glory to Thy power, O Lord, for Thou didst destroy the one that had the dominion of death! Thou didst renew us by Thy Cross, granting us life and incorruption!

Ever blessing the Lord, we hymn His Resurrection; for having endured crucifixion, by death He destroyed death!

Thy burial, O Master, shattered and rent asunder the bonds of hades! And Thy Resurrection from the dead enlightened the world: O Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Who shall not call Thee blessed, O Most-holy Virgin? Or who shall not hymn Thy most-pure birth-giving? For the Only-begotten Son, Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the Same came forth from Thee, O pure one, having ineffably become incarnate: being God by nature, and for our sake having become man by nature, not divided into two Persons, but...

[f. 171vA] ...natures, known without confusion. Entreat Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may be saved.

AT THE APOSTICHA, RESURRECTIONAL STICHERA. Tone 6.

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, the Angels hymn in the heavens; and vouchsafe us on earth with pure heart to sing and glorify Thee!

Other Stichera in alphabetical order.

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

Having shattered the brazen gates and smashed the bars of hades as God almighty, Thou didst resurrect the fallen human race! Therefore we also with one accord cry aloud: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

Desiring to right us from ancient corruption, O Christ, Thou art nailed to the Cross and laid in a tomb; Whom the Myrrh-bearing women seeking with tears, weeping said: "Woe is us, O Savior of all, how didst Thou accept to dwell in a tomb? And having dwelt therein willingly, how wast Thou stolen away? How wast Thou moved? What place hath concealed Thy life-bearing body? But, O Master, even as Thou didst promise us, appear, and still our weeping lamentation!" And as they wept, an Angel cried aloud unto them: "Cease from weeping, say unto the Apostles that the Lord is risen, granting the world cleansing and great mercy!"

[f. 171vB] *Verse: Holiness becometh Thine house, O Lord, unto length of days.*

Thou wast crucified even as Thou didst will, O Christ, and by Thy burial Thou didst take death captive; on the third day Thou didst rise as God with glory, granting unto the world unending life and great mercy!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

My Maker and Redeemer, O most pure one, Christ the Lord, having come forth from Thy womb, clothed in me, hath freed Adam from the first curse. Therefore unto Thee, O all-undefiled one, as truly God's Mother and Virgin, we cry unceasingly with the Angel's "Rejoice!": say, Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, protectress, shelter, and salvation of our souls![^250]

Troparion, Tone 6:

The Angelic Hosts were upon Thy tomb, and the guards became as dead men; and Mary stood at the grave, seeking Thy most pure body. Taking hades captive yet untouched by it, Thou didst meet the Virgin, granting life. O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

He Who called Thy Mother blessed came unto the Passion by voluntary counsel, shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the Angels: "Rejoice with Me, for the lost drachma is found!" O Thou Who hast wisely ordered all things, Lord, glory to Thee!

ON SUNDAY AT MIDNIGHT OFFICE.

Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity. Composition of Metrophanes.

[f. 172rA] Ode 1. Tone 6. Heirmos:

Traversing the deep as on dry land, Israel...

We hymn the three co-originate Persons of single nature, changeless form, God good and Lover of mankind, Who granteth unto us cleansing of transgressions.

Supersubstantial One, single and threefold-sunlit in delineations, O Lord, existing in one Godhead: [direct] us in the knowledge and likeness of Thy Divine radiance!

Truly Paul, the light of the Church of the Gentiles, clearly taught us to worship the one three-hypostatic God, from Whom, and through Whom, and unto Whom all things were made.

The noetic Sun arose and shone forth early, O Theotokos, and delivered our eyes from the gloom of sin by His rays; hymning Whom, we piously call Thee blessed.

Ode 3. Heirmos:

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord...

Adorned with threefold light, O God, having established the ranks of immaterial beings to hymn Thee with thrice-holy voices: together with them receive also us who praise Thy goodness!

Singing of the one changeless Trinity, conformable, singly-natured Godhead, we pray Thee, O Lover of mankind: send down upon us forgiveness of our many transgressions!

O Mind, unoriginate Father; O Word of God, consubstantial Image; O Divine Spirit, good and right: preserve in Thy dominion those who hymn Thee, for Thou art tender-hearted! Theotokion:

Having become man in substance, my God, having driven away corruption through Thee, O pure one, alone set free the forebears from the former condemnation.

[f. 172rB] Sessional Hymn, Tone 6.

*Podoben: "Fulfilling the dispensation for our sake" (Е҆́же ѡ҆ на́съ):*

O Master God, look down from heaven, behold our lowliness, O merciful One, and show compassion, O all-good Lover of mankind! For from nowhere else do we hope to receive pardon for the evil things we have committed; therefore be Thou with us, and none can be against us!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

O all-undefiled Theotokos, look down upon the wound of my affliction and heal it, O most pure one; and cure the burning of my conscience, dew-cooling it with Thy prayer, crying aloud unto Thy servants: "I am with you, and none can be against you!"

Ode 4. Heirmos:

Christ is my power, God and Lord...

Exalt our understanding, O Unity threefold-radiant, and swiftly raise up the souls and hearts of Thy chanters; take away our wickedness, and fashion us unto likeness of Thy brightness, and transform us by the prayers of Thy initiates!

O single, uncircumscribed, and immutable Trinity: [enlighten] Thy suppliants with Thy rays!

In wisdom didst Thou of old join the ranks of ministering Angels of Thy goodness, O three-hypostatic Unity: through them receive also my praise!

He Who is intangible by nature, God communicating with us, having assumed created human nature, was fashioned in Thy womb, O Theotokos, O Virgin!

Ode 5. Heirmos:

By Thy Divine light [f. 172vA] Understanding the Divine-originating nature, providential and saving of all that is, as one radiant light, we rise at dawn, entreating forgiveness of our sinful falls.

O unoriginate God, and co-eternal Son, and Holy Spirit: strengthen Thy chanters, O monarchical Trinity, and deliver them from surrounding perils and afflictions!

Directing me by Thy benefactions alone, and guiding me ever unto pleasing Thy three-hypostatic Godhead, O Sun of Glory, make me a partaker of Thy Divine Kingdom! Theotokion:

O changeless Word of God, Who upholdest and preservest all things by Thine almighty hand: preserve and protect those who glorify Thee, by the prayers of the Mother of God who bare Thee!

Ode 6. Heirmos:

Beholding the sea of life...

Grant wisdom and understanding, O three-hypostatic Godhead, unto those who hymn Thee this day; and vouchsafe us to be illumined together by the rays of beauty of Thy light-giving goodness, who are enlightened by indivisible faith!

O threefold-shining, almighty, unapproachable One: illumine the hearts of those who with faith praise Thy dominion, and raise them up unto Divine love!

God, the Sustainer and Lord of all, dwelt ever in Thee, O pure Virgin: teach Thy children to worship the one Godhead in three Persons!

Sessional Hymn, Tone 6:

With the Holy Spirit: [f. 172vB] look down upon us who repent in faith and glorify Thy dominion, O tender-hearted One; we of clay together with the fiery ones, for we know no other God beside Thee; and cry aloud unto those who sing to Thee: "I am with you, and none can be against you!"

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Look down upon us, O all-hymned Theotokos, raising up enlightenment for darkened hearts, and illuminate Thy flock, O most pure one! For whatsoever Thou desirest Thou art able to do, as Mother of Thy Creator; and cry aloud unto those who love Thee: "I am with you, and none can be against you!"

Ode 7. Heirmos:

The Angel made the furnace sprinkle dew...

Establish in me a steadfast mind, O Unity threefold-radiant in attributes, to keep and observe Thy Divine commandments, and ever to chant unto Thee with faith: "Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!"

Hymned ineffably in identity of essence, O singly-natured God, yet bearing the number of Trinity in Persons: preserve us all from diverse temptations and adversities!

We glorify Thee, the one God in essence, shining forth unconfusedly in personal properties as Trinity, co-unoriginate and co-reverenced, changelessly revealed in the heavens. Theotokion:

The supersubstantial God accepted our human mixture from Thy pure womb, O all-undefiled Mother, in His love for mankind, and taught all to cry aloud: "Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!"

[f. 173rA] Ode 8. Heirmos:

From the flame Thou didst pour forth dew for the venerable ones...

Swiftly grant me cleansing of my transgressions, delivering me from the multifaceted arrows [of the enemy], O consubstantial Trinity, three-hypostatic Unity, that I, Thy servant, may warmly glorify Thee!

O merciful God, Source of mercy unto those who worship Thee in truth: have mercy on all, O threefold-radiant Unity, all-good Trinity, as we praise Thy majesty!

With faith we glorify and supremely exalt the one God unto all ages: the Father co-eternal Light, Who bare the co-eternal Light the Word, together with the proceeding Spirit!

Unto mankind, O most pure one, Thou gavest birth to the almighty Word, Christ the Lord, Who healeth all the wounds of the forebears; supremely exalting Whom, O Virgin, we hymn Thee!

Ode 9. Heirmos:

It is impossible for men to see God...

The Cherubic ranks, O Master, unable to endure the splendor of Thy glory, covering themselves with their wings, unceasingly cry aloud unto Thee the Thrice-holy hymn, glorifying the single glory of the three-hypostatic Godhead!

O unsetting Sun of creation, grant goodness into the hearts of Thy servants, light in their souls, and deliver me from many transgressions who sing threefold-sunlit hymns unto Thee; [deliver us from dark villainy, and vouchsafe us Thy radiant dwellings, for Thou art all-good!] [f. 173rB] who hymn Thee, and deliver us from dark villainy, and vouchsafe us Thy radiant dwellings, for Thou art all-good! Theotokion:

Having in wisdom created man in the beginning, Thy Son, O Virgin, renewed him through Thee when corrupted, O all-undefiled one, and filled with the threefold-sunlit ray of Divine light all who in true faith glorify Thee as Theotokos!

AT MATINS, AFTER THE PSALMS.

"God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us", in Tone 6. And we chant the Resurrectional Troparion: "The Angelic Hosts", twice.

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion: "He Who called Thy Mother blessed". Then the usual Kathisma reading of the Psalter.

After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymns.

Resurrectional. Tone 6.

Beholding the tomb opened and hades lamenting, Mary cried aloud unto the Apostles in hiding: "Come forth, O workers of the vineyard, preach the Word of the Resurrection: the Lord is risen, granting the world great mercy!"

Life was laid in the tomb, and a seal was placed upon the stone; as a sleeping King the soldiers guarded Christ: and having smitten His enemies with blindness, the Lord is risen!

Glory... Now and ever...

Theotokion: Entreat Thy Son and our God, Christ [to deliver us from perils and afflictions, O pure one!][^251]

[f. 173vA] Resurrectional [Sessional Hymns]. Tone 6.

O Lord, standing at Thy tomb, Mary Magdalene wept, crying aloud; supposing Thee to be the gardener, she said: "Lord, where hast Thou laid the eternal Life? Where hast Thou hidden Him Who sitteth upon the Cherubic throne? For those who guarded Him became as dead men from fear! Either give me my Lord, or cry aloud with me: O Thou Who wast among the dead and hast resurrected the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!"

Jonah foretold Thy tomb, and Symeon revealed Thy Divine resurrection, O immortal Lord! For Thou didst descend as dead into the grave, Who didst destroy the gates of hades; and Thou didst rise without corruption as Master unto the salvation of the world, O Christ our God, Who hast enlightened those who sat in darkness!

Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:

Gideon depicted Thy conception, and David declared Thy birth-giving, O pure Theotokos Virgin! For as dew upon the fleece did the Word descend into Thy [womb]; without seed hast Thou budded forth, O holy earth, salvation unto the world, Christ our God, O full of grace!

Hypakoe, Tone 6:

By Thy life-giving death, having shattered the gates of hades, O Christ, as God Thou didst open ancient Paradise, and rising from the dead, Thou hast delivered our life from corruption!

[f. 173vB] [Unto heaven do I lift up mine eyes,] unto Thee, O Word: show compassion unto me, that I may live unto Thee!

Have mercy upon us who are set at naught, fashioning us into vessels well-pleasing unto Thee, O Word!

By the Holy Spirit is every saving cause: if He blow upon anyone according to worthiness, swiftly He lifteth him from earthly things, wingeth him, leadeth him, and causeth him to prosper on high!

Antiphon 2:

If the Lord had not been with us, not one of us could have withstood the combatant, overcoming the assaults of the enemy; for they who overcome are exalted from hence!

Let not my soul be caught in their teeth as a fledgling, O Word: woe is me, how shall I escape from the enemies, being a lover of sin?

By the Holy Spirit is deification unto all, good will, understanding, peace, and blessing; for He is equal in operation to the Father and to the Word!

Antiphon 3:

They who trust in the Lord are terrible to enemies and marvelous unto all, for they look on high!

Having Thee as Helper, O Savior, the lot of the righteous will not stretch forth its hands unto iniquities!

Through the Holy Spirit is dominion over all: Whom the hosts on high worship, together with every breath below!

Prokeimenon: [O Lord, stir up Thy strength, and] come to save us [LXX Ps. 79:3].

Let every breath... Matins Resurrectional Gospel, Psalm 50, and the rest in order.

[f. 174rA] SUNDAY RESURRECTIONAL CANON:

Ode 1. Tone 6. Heirmos:

Traversing the deep as on dry land, Israel, beholding Pharaoh the pursuer drowned, sang unto God a triumphant song!

With hands outstretched upon the Cross, O good Jesus, Thou didst fulfill the Father's good pleasure in all things; therefore we all sing unto Thee a triumphant song!

In dread as a handmaid commanded, death drew near unto the Master of life; through Whom unending life and the Resurrection are granted unto us!

Having received Thy Creator, as He Himself willed, ineffably incarnate beyond mind from Thy seedless womb, O pure one, Thou wast truly revealed as Sovereign Lady of creatures!

Another Canon: of the Cross and Resurrection.

Heirmos: Beneath the sea surge:

In awe unto Thee the gates of death open, and the eternal bars are rent asunder; by Thy precious descent the ancient dead were resurrected, thankfully chanting Thy Resurrection, O Christ!

Laid in the lowest pit, O Christ, having become dead, Thou didst put to death the slayer, and didst set free the prisoners in hades who glorify Thy precious Resurrection! Theotokion:

Rejoice, O light-bearing tabernacle, sanctification, Church, Table, and holy Mountain! Rejoice, O palace [f. 174rB] of glory, and Throne of our God! Rejoice, O unconquerable wall unto those who hymn Thee!

Another Canon to the Most-holy Theotokos.

Heirmos: Traversing the deep as on dry land:

Partaking of the forbidden food, our forebears brought in the curse; but Thou, O pure one, didst loose it, having given birth unto Christ the Author of blessing!

Having brought forth Christ, the lightning-flash of the Divine abyss, O pure one, disperse the gloom of my passions and the confusion of my transgressions by the light of Thy radiance!

Jacob foresaw with noetic eyes the Expectation of the nations secretly taking flesh in incorruption, God Who delivereth us through Thine intercession!

When the rulers from the tribe of Judah had failed, O most pure one, Thy Son and God came forth as Leader, and hath now truly become King unto the ends of the earth!

Ode 3. Heirmos:

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established us upon the rock of Thy confession!

Beholding God crucified in the flesh, creation dissolved in dread; yet by the almighty hand of Him Who was crucified, it was held firm!

By death is death destroyed, and wretched it lieth breathless; for unable to endure the Divine assault of Life, the strong one is slain, and the Resurrection is granted unto all! Theotokion:

The wonder of Thy Divine birth-giving, O pure one, transcendeth every order of nature...[^252] [f. 174vA] wonder: for beyond nature Thou didst conceive God in Thy womb, and having given birth, Thou remainest ever Virgin!

Another Heirmos:

Confessing Thee Who didst rise on the third day and appeared unto the Myrrh-bearing women, O Savior, saying unto them: "Rejoice!", we say: "There is none holy save Thee, O Lord, Lover of mankind!"

Glory to Thy resurrection, O our Savior, for as almighty Thou didst deliver us from secret hades and death; and singing, we say: "There is none holy save Thee, O Lord, Lover of mankind!" Theotokion:

Glorifying Thee divinely, the Word incarnate of the holy Maiden, Who didst save our forefather from deception, we cry: "There is none holy save Thee, O Lord, Lover of mankind!"

Another [Canon of the Theotokos]. Heirmos:

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord:

Having ineffably assumed my corruptible and mortal flesh from Thy womb, O all-pure Mother of God, the Good One divinized it and united it unto Himself!

Unable to gaze upon God taking flesh, O Virgin, the Angelic choirs were struck with dread; and as Mother of God they glorified Thee with unceasing hymns!

The Prophet Daniel saw Thee revealed as the noetic mountain, from which the Stone was cut without hands, mightily shattering the temples of the demons!

What human word and tongue, O Virgin, can worthily praise Thee? For in incorruption, without seed, God the Giver of life was pleased to take flesh [f. 174vB] of Thee, O most pure one!

Ode 4. Heirmos:

"Christ is my power, God and Lord!" the venerable Church divinely crieth aloud, keeping feast in the Lord from a pure understanding!

The Tree of true life hath blossomed, O Christ, for the Cross was set up; and watered by the Blood and Water from Thy corruptless side, life hath budded forth for us!

No longer doth the serpent falsely whisper deification unto me; for Christ, the Deifier of human nature, hath now opened unhindered unto me the path of life! Theotokion:

Truly ineffable and incomprehensible unto those on earth and in heaven are the mysteries of Thy divine birth-giving, O Theotokos, Ever-Virgin!

Another [Canon of Cross and Resurrection]. Heirmos:

He Who co-abideth divinely with the Father on high is bodily nailed to the Tree for our sake, destroying the curse as Lord, as almighty!

The all-pure flesh of the Redeemer lying in the grave was not separated [from the Godhead]; but as almighty, Christ shattered the bars of hades, and arose in glory on the third day! Theotokion:

Risen from the dead, our Savior cried aloud unto the Myrrh-bearers, "Rejoice!", and called unto them: "Go forth and announce the good tidings unto My Apostles!"

Another [Canon of the Theotokos]. Heirmos: Christ is my power:

We who are saved through Thee hymn Thee, O most pure and all-undefiled one, and chanting piously, cry aloud:

[f. 175rA] "Blessed art Thou, O Ever-Virgin, who didst give birth to God!"

Thou didst give birth unto the unsetting Light, O Virgin, shining in the flesh unto those who sit in the darkness of this life, O all-blessed one, and Thou hast poured forth joy unto those who hymn Thee, O Ever-Virgin!

Grace hath blossomed, the Law hath ceased through Thee, O all-hymned one! For Thou, O pure one, gavest birth to the Lord Who ever granteth us remission, O Virgin!

Tasting the poison showed me dead; but do Thou, O most pure one, Who didst bring forth the Tree of Life, revive me, and make me an inheritor of the delight of Paradise!

Ode 5. Heirmos:

With Thy Divine light, O Good One, illumine, I pray Thee, the souls of those who rise early unto Thee with love, to behold Thee, O Word of God, the true God, Who deliverest us from the gloom of sins!

The Cherubim give way before me now, and the flaming sword turns back from me, beholding Thee, O Word of God, the true God, Who didst open the way into Paradise for the Thief!

No longer do I fear the return unto the earth, O Christ; for from the earth Thou didst raise me up when forgotten, by reason of Thy manifold compassion, unto the height of incorruption by Thy Resurrection! Theotokion:

We have acquired Thee as an unconquerable protectress, O true Theotokos, and we praise Thee unashamed, O Lady!

Another [Canon of Cross and Resurrection]. Heirmos:

In compassion, O Good One, having taken our likeness, Thou wast willingly nailed to the Cross [f. 175rB] for my sake, who of old rejected Thy holy commandment for the sake of food; and having been put to death as man, Thou dost grant me life!

Beholding Thee on the throne with the Father, yet among the dead as dead in subterranean places, hades vomited forth those whom the wretched one held of old; and on the third day in power Thou didst resurrect fallen nature, and didst illumine the world, O Christ, for Thou art compassionate! Theotokion:

Without corruption of Thy womb didst Thou bring forth Thy Virgin birth, remaining as before, O Theotokos, unwedded Mother! Therefore pray fervently for Thy flock, that those who hymn Thee may be delivered from temptations!

Another [Canon of the Theotokos]. Heirmos: By Thy Divine light:

A divine radiant dwelling wast Thou prepared for the Master, O all-hymned one! For Thou alone wast revealed as the pure Mother of God, holding Him in Thine arms as an infant!

Bearing noetic beauty, Thy most comely soul was called Bride of God, sealed with virginity, O pure one, and enlightening the world with the light of purity!

Let the congregation of the wicked lament, who confess Thee not as the pure Mother of God! For Thou didst reveal unto us the door of Divine light, dispelling the gloom of our sins!

Ode 6. Heirmos:

Beholding the sea of life...[^253] [f. 175vA] swelled by the tempest of temptations: fleeing unto Thy calm haven, I cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O greatly merciful One!

When crucified with nails, O Master, Thou didst wipe out the curse that was against us; and pierced in the side with a spear, Thou didst tear up Adam's handwriting, setting the world free!

Adam was brought down, tripped by deceit into the abyss of hades; but as God and merciful by nature, Thou didst descend to seek him, and taking him upon Thy shoulders, Thou didst resurrect him with Thyself! Theotokion:

O most pure Sovereign Lady, who didst bear for mortals the Helmsman and Lord: still the unstable and fierce tumult of my passions, and grant calm unto my heart!

Another [Canon of Cross and Resurrection]. Heirmos:

How didst Thou endure wounds, insults, and mockery, O Word? For willingly as man Thou didst suffer passion in the flesh; yet being God, Thou didst remain impassible! Crucified upon the Tree between two thieves, Thou didst save our nature from corruption, for Thou art almighty!

Willingly wast Thou laid in the tomb for my sake as dead, O Savior; and Thou didst recall all who were held in darkness and the shadow of death! On the third day Thou didst shine forth, and meeting the Myrrh-bearing women, Thou didst cry aloud unto them: "Rejoice indeed, and tell the rest unto My Apostles!" Theotokion:

Thee, O Mother of God full of grace, pure Virgin, who didst receive in Thy womb and give birth in the flesh unto Him Who is before the ages unoriginate of the Father, do we entreat:

Another [Canon]. Heirmos: It is impossible for men to see God:

Hope strengthened the choirs of Martyrs, and winged them with burning desire for Thy love, prefiguring for them that truly unshakable rest which is to come; of which account the faithful departed worthy, O Good One!

Grant, O Christ, that those who have departed in faith may attain unto the radiant and divine brightness of Thy presence; and give them rest in the bosom of Abraham, as Thou alone bestowest mercy upon them and accountest them worthy of eternal blessedness!

O Thou Who art good and merciful in nature, the Lover of mercy and the abyss of tender compassion: where Thy light shineth, there establish those whom Thou hast translated, O Savior, from this place of affliction and the shadow of death!

Theotokion:

We understand Thee, O pure one, to be the holy Tabernacle, the Ark, and the Tablet of the Law and of grace; through Thee was forgiveness granted unto those justified by the blood of Him Who was incarnate from Thy womb, O all-undefiled one!

At the Praises, Stichera

Of the Martyrs. Tone 6.

O Lord, in the memory of Thy Saints, all creation keepeth feast; the heavens rejoice with the Angels, and the earth rejoiceth with men: by their prayers have mercy on us!...[^274]

[f. 196vA] O Lord, if we had not Thy Saints as intercessors and Thy goodness showing mercy upon us, how should we dare, O Savior, to hymn Thee, Whom the Angels glorify unceasingly? O Knower of hearts, spare our souls!

The memory of the Martyrs is a coming joy, O Lord; for having suffered for the sake of Christ, they received crowns from Him, and now with boldness they pray for our souls!

O chosen members of our holy God: Rejoice and make merry, all ye His servants, for He hath prepared crowns and His Kingdom for you; but we pray you, forget not us also!

For the Departed:

The tasting of the tree of old in Eden became sickness unto Adam, when the serpent spewed forth his venom; for on this account all-destroying death entered in, devouring man. But the Master came and cast down the serpent, and granted us the Resurrection. Therefore let us cry aloud unto Him: Spare those whom Thou hast received, O Savior, and give them rest with the Saints, as Thou art the Lover of mankind!

Glory: Now and ever: Theotokion:

Thou art our God, Who in wisdom didst make and finish all things; Thou didst send Prophets, O Christ, to foretell Thy coming, and Apostles to preach Thy mighty works. And some indeed prophesied Thy coming, while others enlightened the nations through Baptism; and the Martyrs obtained what they desired, and the choir of both entreateth Thee [f. 196vB] entreateth Thee, with Her that bare Thee, O Christ: Give rest, O God, unto the souls whom Thou hast received, and account us worthy of Thy Kingdom, O my Redeemer God Who didst endure crucifixion for my sake, the condemned one!

At the Aposticha, Stichera

For the Departed. Tone 6.

*Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*

Possessing incomprehensible compassion toward us, and unfailing fountains of divine goodness, O Most Merciful One: settle in the land of the living those who have crossed over unto Thee, O Master, in the beloved and desired habitations, the possession that ever abideth; for Thou didst shed Thy blood for all, O Savior, and by the life-bearing price didst ransom the world!

*Verse: Their souls shall dwell among good things.*

Thou didst submit to death by Thy life-creating will, and didst pour forth life and give ever-existing food unto the faithful; which grant now also unto the departed in the hope of the Resurrection, forgiving all their transgressions through Thy goodness, as Thou alone art sinless, the only God and Lover of mankind, that Thy name, O Christ, may be praised by all, and being saved, we may glorify Thy love for mankind!

*Verse: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself, O Lord.*

Ruling over the living by Thy God-originating power, and governing the dead, knowing Thee, O Christ, we pray: Give rest in that place unto Thy faithful servants who have departed unto Thee, the only Benefactor [f. 197rA] unto Thy faithful servants, with Thine elect, O Lover of mankind, in a place of refreshment, in the splendors of the Saints; for Thou art the Lover of mercy, and savest as God those whom Thou didst create according to Thine own image, O only Most Merciful One!

Glory: Now and ever: Theotokion:

Thou wast shown forth as a divinely-fitting habitation, O all-pure one; for Thou didst contain God and didst bear Christ, O Virgin Mother, in two essences, yet in one hypostasis. Entreat Him, O pure one, the only-begotten Firstborn, Who preserved Thee a blameless Virgin even after birth-giving, to give rest unto the souls of those who have fallen asleep in faith, in light and in incorruptible blessedness!
