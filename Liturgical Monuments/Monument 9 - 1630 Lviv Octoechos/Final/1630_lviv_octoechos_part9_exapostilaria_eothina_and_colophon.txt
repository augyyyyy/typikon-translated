# RESURRECTIONAL EXAPOSTILARIA, EVANGELICAL STICHERA (EOTHINA), AND COLOPHON

## EXAPOSTILARIA AND EVANGELICAL STICHERA

Resurrectional Exaposteilaria and Evangelical Stichera

Tone 1. Exaposteilarion 1:

Let us go up with the disciples into the mountain of Galilee, by faith to behold Christ saying: Authority over the highest and lowest hath been received; let us learn how He teacheth to baptize in the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, and to abide with the initiates of the mysteries, even as He promised, unto the end of the age. (Twice)

Theotokion:

Rejoice with the disciples, O Virgin Theotokos, for thou didst behold Christ risen from the tomb on the third day, even as He said; unto whom He appeared, teaching and saying as He revealed Himself: Baptize in the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; commanding us to believe in His Resurrection, and to glorify thee, O Maiden!

Evangelical Stichera 1. Tone 1:

When the disciples went up into the mountain for the earthly ascension, the Lord stood before them, and they worshipped Him; and having been taught of the authority given everywhere unto Him, they are sent forth into all under heaven to preach the Resurrection from the dead, and the translation into the heavens; which Christ God, the truthful One and the Savior of our souls, promised.

Tone 2. Exaposteilarion 2:

Beholding the stone rolled away, the Myrrh-bearing women rejoiced; for they saw a young man sitting in the tomb, and he said unto them: Behold, Christ is risen! Say unto the disciples with Peter: Make haste unto the mountain of Galilee; there shall He appear unto you, even as He foretold unto His friends!

Theotokion:

An angel indeed brought unto the Virgin the 'Rejoice!' before Thy conception, O Christ; and an angel also rolled away the stone from Thy tomb; in the place of sorrow, the one indeed brought signs of ineffable joy...[^341] [f. 1vA] of ineffable joy, while the other, in place of death, proclaimed and magnified Thee, the Giver of life, and declared the Resurrection unto the initiates of the mysteries.

Evangelical Stichera 2. Tone 2:

When the women with Mary came with myrrh and were wondering how it would be possible for them to attain their desire, the stone appeared taken away, and a divine youth, calming the turmoil of their souls, said: For Jesus the Lord is risen! Therefore preach unto the preachers, His disciples, to hasten unto Galilee, and to behold Him risen from the dead, as the Giver of life and the Lord!

Tone 3. Exaposteilarion 3:

That Christ is risen, let no one disbelieve! For He appeared unto Mary, and was then seen by two who were walking into the countryside; and He appeared again unto the eleven disciples as they reclined at table; and having sent them forth to baptize, He ascended into the heavens whence He had descended, confirming the preaching with multitudes of signs!

Theotokion:

Thou hast shone forth, O Sun, from the tomb today as a Bridegroom from his bridal chamber, and hast despoiled hades; by the maternal prayers of her who bore Thee, send down Light upon us: the Light that illumineth hearts and souls, a light for all souls to walk, setting their steps upon the paths of Thy commandments and into the way of peace!

Evangelical Stichera 3. Tone 3:[^342]

[f. 2rA] When Mary Magdalene announced the glad tidings of the Savior's Resurrection from the dead and His appearance, the disciples, being disbelieving, were reproached for the hardness of their hearts; but armed with signs and miracles, they were sent forth unto preaching; and Thou indeed, O Lord, didst ascend unto the Father, the primal Light; while they preached the Word everywhere, confirming it with miracles. Therefore, having been enlightened, we also glorify Thy Resurrection from the dead, O Lover of mankind!

Tone 4. Exaposteilarion 4:

Enlightened by the virtues, having seen the men standing in shining garments at the life-bearing Tomb, while the Myrrh-bearing women bowed their faces to the earth: let us learn of the Resurrection of the Ruler of heaven, and with Peter let us run unto life, unto the tomb; and marvelling at what hath been done, let us remain praising Christ!

Theotokion:

From thy womb, O Virgin, the Lord having become incarnate, willed to recall the perishing world from corruption, even as the Almighty One knoweth; and finding salvation in thee, O Theotokos, we all cry aloud unto thee as the angel: Rejoice, thou who alone art pure and most blessed among women, who didst bear salvation unto all the universe!

Evangelical Stichera 4. Tone 4:

Very early in the morning the women came unto Thy Tomb, O Christ, but the Body loved by them was not found; therefore, while they were perplexed, those standing by in shining garments said: Why seek ye the Living among the dead?...[^343] [f. 2vA] He is risen, even as He said before! Why do ye not remember His words? Believing whom, they preached what they had seen; but their glad tidings seemed to be idle tales: so dull of understanding were the disciples yet. But Peter ran, and saw, and glorified Thy all-powerful wonders!

Tone 5. Exaposteilarion 5:

Christ, the Life and the Way, having risen from the dead, journeyed with Cleopas and Luke, by whom He was also made known at Emmaus in the breaking of bread; whose souls and hearts were burning within them when He spoke unto them on the way, and expounded the Scriptures concerning what He had suffered: with them let us cry aloud: He is risen! Even as He appeared also unto Peter!

Theotokion:

The Creator of creation and God of all things, having taken earthly flesh from thy pure blood, O all-holy Theotokos: made whole nature which was corrupted new again, leaving thee after childbearing even as thou wast before birth; therefore in faith we all praise thee, crying aloud: Rejoice, O glory of the world!

Evangelical Stichera 5. Tone 5:

O how wise are Thy judgments, O Christ! How unto Peter indeed by the grave-clothes alone Thou gavest understanding of Thy Resurrection; and walking with Luke and Cleopas, Thou didst converse with them, and didst not reveal Thyself unto them quickly; therefore Thou wast reproached as being the only stranger in Jerusalem who knew not the counsel thereof unto the end; yet ordering all things unto the profit of creation, Thou didst unveil the prophetic words concerning Thyself; and in the blessing of the bread Thou wast made known unto them, whose hearts before that were kindled unto the knowledge of Thee; who also, when the disciples were gathered together, already clearly preached...[^344] [f. 3rA] Thy Resurrection, through which have mercy on us!

Tone 6. Exaposteilarion 6:

Having shown that Thou art man, O Savior, standing in the midst Thou didst teach: Risen from the dead, having partaken of food, He ordained the baptism of repentance; and straightway He ascended unto the Heavenly Father, and promised to send the Comforter unto the disciples, O all-divine God-man!

Theotokion:

When the all-pure one beheld her own Son risen from the dead, she was filled with joy, and sang praise unto His ineffable tender mercy: that He did not abandon human nature, but having willed to be born of thee, He delivered all from corruption by His passion on the Cross, and led them unto life according to His compassion.

Evangelical Stichera 6. Tone 6:

Thou art true peace, O Christ, unto the men of God! Giving Thy peace unto the disciples after the Resurrection, Thou didst show them to be terrified, thinking that they beheld a spirit; but Thou didst calm the turmoil of their soul, showing them Thy hands and Thy feet; yet while they were still disbelieving, by the partaking of food and the reminder of Thy teachings, Thou didst open their mind to understand the Scriptures; promising them also the Father's promise, and having blessed them, Thou didst ascend into heaven: therefore with them we worship Thee: O Lord, glory to Thee![^345]

[f. 3vA] Tone 7. Exaposteilarion 7:

When Mary said that they had taken away the Lord, Simon Peter and that other disciple of Christ whom He loved ran unto the tomb; and both ran together, and found the linen clothes lying alone within, and also the napkin that was about His head wrapped together apart from them; therefore they kept silence again until they beheld Christ.

Theotokion:

I praise Thy boundless mercy, O my Helmsman, that Thou didst empty Thyself to bear and save perishing human nature; for as Thou art, Thou didst will to be born of the pure Maiden of God, and to descend even unto hades, desiring to save us by the prayers of her who bore Thee, O all-bountiful Master!

Evangelical Stichera 7. Tone 7:

Behold, it was darkness and early; and why, O Mary, standest thou at the tomb, having great darkness in thy mind, in which thou askest where Jesus hath been laid? But behold the disciples running together, how by the linen clothes and the napkin they discovered the Resurrection, and called to mind the Scriptures concerning this; with whom, and through whom, having also believed, we sing praise unto Thee, Christ the Giver of life!

Tone 8. Exaposteilarion 8:

Beholding two angels within the tomb, Mary was amazed; and not knowing Christ, she asked Him as the gardener: Sir, where hast Thou laid the body of my Jesus?...[^346] [f. 4rA] and recognizing Him by His call, she heard that He was the Savior Himself: Touch Me not, for I ascend unto the Father; say unto My brethren!

Theotokion:

O Maiden, thou didst ineffably give birth unto One of the Trinity in two natures of essence, and in two operations, and in one hypostasis: Him therefore entreat thou unceasingly for them that worship thee in faith, that we may be delivered from every assault of the enemy; for we all flee unto thee, O Sovereign Lady and Theotokos!

Evangelical Stichera 8. Tone 8:

Mary's tears are not poured forth warmly in vain; for behold, she was accounted worthy of angelic teachings, and of the sight of Jesus Himself; yet she still mindeth earthly things, as a frail woman; therefore she is forbidden to touch Christ, but is sent forth as a herald unto His disciples, unto whom she declared the glad tidings, announcing His ascent unto the Father's inheritance: with her, account us also worthy of Thine appearance, O Master and Lord!

Evangelical 9. Exaposteilarion 9. Tone 5:

The doors being shut, O Master, when Thou didst enter, Thou didst fill the Apostles with the Spirit, unto whom Thou didst say to bind and to loose sins; and after eight days Thou didst show Thy side and hands unto Thomas, with whom we cry aloud: Thou art my Lord and my God!

Theotokion:

O pure Birth-giver of God and most holy Virgin, joy of the sorrowful, protection of the world, intercessor for sinners: comfort our sorrowing hearts by thy prayers, for after God we the faithful have no other hope save thee, O merciful and pure one, our expectation, lover of peace and salvation of the world, O Maiden![^347]

[f. 4vA] Evangelical Stichera 9. Tone 5:

As in the latter days, when it was late on the Sabbath, Thou didst stand before Thy friends, O Christ, and by wonder Thou dost confirm wonder: by the closed entry of the doors, Thy Resurrection from the dead! But Thou didst fill the disciples with joy, and didst impart the Holy Spirit unto them, and gavest them authority for the forgiveness of sins, and didst not leave Thomas to be submerged in the storm of disbelief: therefore grant unto us true understanding and the forgiveness of transgressions, O compassionate Lord!

Evangelical 10. Exaposteilarion 10. Tone 6:

The Sea of Tiberias with the children of Zebedee, and Nathanael with Peter, together with two others and Thomas of old, had them fishing; who, casting their net on the right side at Christ's command, drew forth a multitude of fishes; Whom Peter recognized, wading unto Him, unto whom He appeared the third time, and showed bread and fish upon the coals.

Theotokion:

Worshipping Thine honored icon, O all-pure one: show us to be children of the light, inhabitants of Paradise, and partakers of the Kingdom; for we are thy suppliants and thy people, O all-pure one! Let us not fall away from thy protection, O Theotokos, and account us all worthy of salvation, O all-immaculate one!

Evangelical Stichera 10. Tone 6:

After the descent into hades and the Resurrection from the dead, Thy disciples, sorrowing over Thy parting, O Christ, turned unto labor; and again there were boats and nets, but no catch at all; but Thou, O Savior, appearing as the Master of all, commandest them to cast the net on the right side; and straightway the word became deed, and the multitude of fish was great; and a strange supper...[^348] [f. 5rA] was ready on the earth; of which Thy disciples having partaken then, account us also now worthy spiritually to be filled, O Lord, Lover of mankind!

Evangelical 11. Exaposteilarion 11. Tone 2:

After the divine Resurrection, the Lord said thrice unto Peter: Lovest thou Me? Appointing him chief shepherd over His sheep; but when Peter saw him whom Jesus loved following after, he asked: Master, and what shall this man do? If I will that he tarry till I come again, He said, what is that to thee, friend Peter?

Theotokion:

Rejoice! we cry aloud unto thee, O all-pure Theotokos: for thou didst announce joy, and didst turn Eve's sorrow into joy, O Virgin; fill us also with thy joy, and by the light of Thy Son account us all worthy of the portion of the elect, O all-pure one, and we all cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O pure Maiden!

Evangelical Stichera 11. Tone 8:

Manifesting Thyself unto the disciples after the Resurrection, O Savior, Thou didst give the feeding of the sheep unto Simon in return for his love, seeking from him care for the flock; therefore Thou saidst: If thou lovest Me, Peter, feed My sheep! But he straightway showed his affection again, inquiring concerning that other disciple: by whose prayers, O Christ, preserve Thy flock from the wolves that destroy it!

[Scribal Colophon in brown ink:]

End of the Evangelical Stichera, on the 11th day of March in the year 1631.

### Notes

[^341]: Calligraphic Manuscript Supplement of the Eleven Resurrectional Exaposteilaria and Evangelical Stichera (physical leaf p261 Right Page / Manuscript Folio 1r): Inception of the contemporary seventeenth-century liturgical supplement appended to the printed 1630 Lviv Octoechos, supplying the full weekly cycle of the 11 Resurrectional Exaposteilaria (attributed to Byzantine Emperor Constantine VII Porphyrogenitus, 905–959) and the 11 Gospel Stichera (attributed to Emperor Leo VI the Wise, 866–912), designed to accompany the eleven Sunday Matins Resurrection Gospels; opening with Tone 1 Exaposteilarion 1 ('Let us go up with the disciples into the mountain of Galilee'), Theotokion ('Rejoice with the disciples, O Virgin Theotokos'), Tone 1 Evangelical Stichera 1 ('When the disciples went up into the mountain'), Tone 2 Exaposteilarion 2 ('Beholding the stone rolled away'), and Tone 2 Theotokion 2 incipit.
[^342]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Tone 2 through Tone 3 (physical leaf p262 Left Page): Concluding Tone 2 Theotokion 2, followed by Tone 2 Evangelical Stichera 2 ('When the women with Mary came with myrrh') on the angelic youth calming the women's turmoil; Tone 3 Exaposteilarion 3 ('That Christ is risen, let no one disbelieve!') on the appearances unto Mary, the two disciples at Emmaus, and the Eleven at table; Tone 3 Theotokion ('Thou hast shone forth, O Sun, from the tomb today as a Bridegroom'); and the heading for Tone 3 Evangelical Stichera 3.
[^343]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Tone 3 through Tone 4 (physical leaf p262 Right Page): Tone 3 Evangelical Stichera 3 ('When Mary Magdalene announced the glad tidings') on Christ reproaching the disciples for their hardness of heart before ascending unto the Father; Tone 4 Exaposteilarion 4 ('Enlightened by the virtues') on the radiant angels at the tomb and Peter's race unto the sepulchre; Tone 4 Theotokion ('The Lord, having taken flesh from thy womb'); and Tone 4 Evangelical Stichera 4 incipit ('Very early in the morning the women came unto Thy Tomb') recounting the women's perplexity.
[^344]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Tone 4 through Tone 5 (physical leaf p263 Left Page): Completion of Tone 4 Evangelical Stichera 4 with Peter glorifying Christ's all-powerful wonders; Tone 5 Exaposteilarion 5 ('Christ, the Life and the Way, having risen from the dead') on the road to Emmaus with Cleopas and Luke; Tone 5 Theotokion ('The Creator of creation and God of all things'); and Tone 5 Evangelical Stichera 5 opening ('O how wise are Thy judgments, O Christ!') on Peter recognizing the Resurrection from the grave-clothes and the burning hearts of the Emmaus travelers.
[^345]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Tone 5 through Tone 6 (physical leaf p263 Right Page): Concluding Tone 5 Evangelical Stichera 5 with the prayer for mercy; Tone 6 Exaposteilarion 6 ('Having shown that Thou art man, O Savior') on Christ eating before the Eleven and ordaining the baptism of repentance; Tone 6 Theotokion ('When the all-pure one beheld her own Son risen from the dead'); and Tone 6 Evangelical Stichera 6 ('Thou art true peace, O Christ, unto the men of God!') on Christ calming the terrified disciples, showing His hands and feet, and opening their understanding of the Scriptures before ascending.
[^346]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Tone 7 through Tone 8 (physical leaf p264 Left Page): Tone 7 Exaposteilarion 7 ('When Mary said that they had taken away the Lord') on Simon Peter and John running unto the sepulchre and discovering the folded napkin; Tone 7 Theotokion ('I praise Thy boundless mercy, O my Helmsman'); Tone 7 Evangelical Stichera 7 ('Behold, it was darkness and early') on Mary Magdalene's search and the disciples' belief; and Tone 8 Exaposteilarion 8 incipit ('Beholding two angels within the tomb, Mary was amazed').
[^347]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Tone 8 through Evangelical 9 (physical leaf p264 Right Page): Concluding Tone 8 Exaposteilarion 8 with the command 'Touch Me not, for I ascend unto the Father'; Tone 8 Theotokion ('O Maiden, thou didst ineffably give birth unto One of the Trinity in two natures'); Tone 8 Evangelical Stichera 8 ('Mary's tears are not poured forth warmly in vain') on Mary's apostolic commission as herald unto the disciples; Evangelical 9 Exaposteilarion 9 in Tone 5 ('The doors being shut, O Master') on the breathing of the Holy Spirit and Thomas's confession; and Evangelical 9 Theotokion ('O pure Birth-giver of God and most holy Virgin, joy of the sorrowful').
[^348]: Resurrectional Cycle Continued: Evangelical 9 through Evangelical 10 (physical leaf p265 Left Page): Evangelical Stichera 9 in Tone 5 ('As in the latter days, when it was late on the Sabbath') on Christ appearing through closed doors and confirming the forgiveness of sins; Evangelical 10 Exaposteilarion 10 in Tone 6 ('The Sea of Tiberias with the children of Zebedee') on the miraculous draught of fishes; Evangelical 10 Theotokion ('Worshipping Thine honored icon, O all-pure one'); and Evangelical 10 Evangelical Stichera 10 incipit in Tone 6 ('After the descent into hades and the Resurrection from the dead') on the disciples returning to their fishing boats.
[^349]: Resurrectional Cycle Concluded: Evangelical 10 through Evangelical 11 and 1631 Scribal Colophon (physical leaf p265 Right Page): Concluding Evangelical Stichera 10 on the mystical supper of bread and fish upon the coals; Evangelical 11 Exaposteilarion 11 in Tone 2 ('After the divine Resurrection, the Lord said thrice unto Peter: Lovest thou Me?') on Peter's pastoral commission and inquiry concerning John; Evangelical 11 Theotokion ('Rejoice! we cry aloud unto thee, O all-pure Theotokos'); Evangelical 11 Evangelical Stichera 11 in Tone 8 ('Manifesting Thyself unto the disciples after the Resurrection, O Savior'); and the scribal colophon marking the completion of the manuscript supplement on March 11, 1631 ('Конецъ Стихирамъ Еѵангелскимъ дня а̃і марца рwку ҂аха̃').
[^350]: 1964 Zaliztsi Novyi Colophon and Soviet State Museum Stamps (physical leaf p266 Left Page & Right Page): Autograph memorandum by cantor Mykola Makar dated December 6, 1964, documenting his clandestine concealment and preservation of the historic 1630 Lviv Octoechos for the Church of the Protection in Zaliztsi Novi during Soviet state-enforced religious closures; facing the cataloguing stamps of the State Museum of the Book and Printing of the Ukrainian SSR in Kyiv (Fond 2124, Inventory No. CD-97).
[^351]: Historic 17th-Century Blind-Tooled Calfskin Binding (physical leaf p267 Left Page & Right Page): Concluding bibliographic examination of the original 1630 Lviv Dormition Brotherhood binding, featuring bevelled wooden boards covered in dark brown calfskin, blind-stamped with renaissance roll-stamps, concentric framing fillets, and diagonal lattice panels, with three original brass clasps and catches preserved on leather thongs along the fore-edge, marking the physical and codicological completion of Monument 9: 1630 Lviv Octoechos.
