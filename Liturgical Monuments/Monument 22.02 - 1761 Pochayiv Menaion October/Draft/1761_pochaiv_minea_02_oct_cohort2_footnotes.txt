[^13]: Saint Ananias served as the first bishop of Damascus, where he tended his flock, converted many, and ultimately received the crown of martyrdom by stoning under the governor Lucian.

[^15]: Psalm 83:6 (LXX): "Blessed is the man whose help is in Thee; in his heart he has disposed to ascend."
[^16]: Saint Ananias is invoked as the apostolic hierarch, protector, and heavenly patron of Damascus, whose prayers preserve the Christian flock in orthodox piety.
[^17]: Liturgical petition for the reigning sovereign: in the 1761 Pochaiv Menaion, printed under the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth during the reign of King Augustus III Wettin (reigned 1734–1763), prayers were offered for the King of Poland (*король*).
[^18]: The Feast of the Protection (*Pokrov*) of the Most Holy Theotokos commemorates the miraculous appearance of the Mother of God in the Church of Blachernae in Constantinople in the tenth century to Saint Andrew the Fool-for-Christ and his disciple Epiphanius, where she was seen praying for the world and spreading her veil (*omophorion*) over the faithful. In the Ruthenian Greek Catholic Church, this service is celebrated with the highest liturgical solemnity.
[^19]: A reference to the historic defense of Constantinople and Christian realms through the intercession of the Virgin and the relic of her veil preserved at Blachernae.
