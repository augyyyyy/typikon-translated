# MONTH OF OCTOBER
*(Woodcut headpiece depicting the Protection of the Most Holy Theotokos)*[^5]

*Having 31 days.*

*October 1. The Protection of the Most Holy Theotokos:*

*On the 1st day: the Holy Apostle Ananias, one of the Seventy; and our venerable Father Romanus the Melodist.*
*On the same day we celebrate the Protection of the Most Holy Theotokos.*
*The Feast of the Most Holy Theotokos:*[^6] *The service is written following this service.*
*At Vespers, the customary kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Apostle:*
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Being called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*
Having received the divine radiance, when blinded by the better bidding,[^7] Saul, who had previously been held fast by darkness, came unto thee asking for divine purification. Then, O all-blessed one, like a wise hierarch, thou didst adopt him as a son through baptism, who thereafter was to adopt the whole inhabited world as sons. Therefore with him we bless thee, Christ's apostle, O God-wise Ananias; pray that our souls may be saved.

Having been illumined divinely and most gloriously, louder than thunder, O blessed one, thou didst trumpet the saving word of God, and didst awaken those sleeping in the graves to light; casting away the deadening that descends upon the dead and returning those held fast in Hades, which thou didst accomplish through Him Who put death to death, making them honorable vessels once again of Jesus, the Savior of our souls.

As a bearer of light, as a divine preacher, as a God-chosen witness of the passions of Christ and a fellow heir and partaker of the future ineffable glory, abiding with the Master and ever enjoying the out-flowing radiance from the unwaning Light, O God-proclaimer: [preserve] those who now celebrate thy bright

=== LEAF p5 ===
feast now; by thy prayers, O Ananias, deliver from gloomy calamities.

*Other Stichera of Saint Romanus, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Нб҃ныхъ чинѡ́въ):*
The fair-sounding psaltery of the Divine Spirit, the glorious pipe of the Church singing divine songs of wonders: he sets before us all his sweet-singing banquets, and thereby cheers the God-wise.

A most bright and insightful luminary, a sweet-sounding harp, a string of the Spirit's well-signifying words, he sings clearly, teaching the ends of the earth to glorify with unceasing hymns the one radiance of the Godhead.

As thou standest before the Master of all and hast acquired great boldness before Him, pray that we who celebrate thy memory, thy bright solemnity, may be delivered from tribulations and afflictions, O God-blessed Romanus.

*Glory to the Apostle, in the same Tone [Tone 1], By Byzantios:*[^8]
O chosen vessel, great receptacle of the Spirit, thou didst enlighten Paul the Apostle, O venerable one, having been taught by God through revelation, as an eyewitness of the Word and initiate of His wonders, and previously an apostle of the disciples, and a faithful steward of the New Covenant. Therefore, imitating Christ the Teacher, thou didst shed thy blood, having become a confessor through martyric suffering, O Ananias, Christ's sacred leader; and having finished the course and preserved the unstolen faith, dwelling with the celestial ministers in the highest, pray that our souls may be saved.

*Both now, Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion, Tone 1, Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (Прехва́льніи мч҃нцы):*
Standing by the Cross of thy Son and God, and beholding His long-suffering, the pure Mother said weeping: Woe is me, sweetest Child! Why dost Thou suffer these things unjustly, O Word of God, that Thou mayest save mankind?

*Aposticha of the Octoechos; and Sticheron of the Apostle, Tone 4, with the verses:*
*Verse:* Their proclamation has gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the universe. [LXX Ps. 18:5]
Thou hast appeared as a receptacle of passion-bearers, O Ananias the Apostle: pelted with stones as with flowers, thou didst shed thy blood for Him Who willingly suffered crucifixion in the flesh for our sake. Therefore Damascus also boasts in thy sacred solemnity today; and not only it, but the whole world under heaven, openly proclaiming the signs of thy wonders, cries aloud: Pray unto God to grant us remission of sins.

*Glory to the Venerable One, Tone 6:*
Thou hast appeared as the prime cause of good things and salvation, O Romanus our father; for having composed angelic psalmody, thou didst show forth thy life in God-befitting manner. Pray unto Christ God to deliver from afflictions and calamities those who sing unto thee.

*Both now, Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having risen on the third day" (Трїдне́венъ):*
Beholding Thee crucified, O Christ, she who bare Thee cried aloud: What is this strange mystery that I behold, O my Son? And how dost Thou die hanging in the flesh upon the Tree, O Giver of Life?

*Troparion of the Apostle, Tone 3:*
O holy Apostle Ananias, pray unto the merciful God to grant our souls remission of transgressions.

*Glory to the Venerable One, Tone 8:*
In thee, O father, was preserved with certainty that which is according to the image; for taking up the cross, thou didst follow Christ, and by thy deeds didst teach us to disregard the flesh, for it passes away, and to attend unto the soul, an immortal thing. Therefore thy spirit rejoices with the angels, O venerable Romanus.

*Both now, Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion:*
*At Matins: one Canon from the Octoechos, and that of the Apostle, to 4; whose acrostic is: "Let the glory of Ananias be glorified." The composition of the Monk John, Tone 8:*[^9]
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Having traversed the water as dry land, and escaped the evil of Egypt, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Redeemer and God!

*Troparia:*
Let us open our mouths in praises; let us articulate our speech in memory of the chief pastor Ananias, all ye faithful, and with him let us sing unto our God.

The most glorious Ananias leads in song the undefiled bride, the Church that is here, unto the most pure Christ; with her, let us sing unto our God.

Thou wast the first disciple, O Ananias, giving sight to the new disciple who of old was a persecutor, and thou singest: Let us sing unto our God!

*Theotokion:*
The angelic ranks and the generations of mortals unceasingly praise thee, unwedded Mother; for thou didst bear their Creator as an Infant in thine arms.

*Another Canon of the Venerable One, Tone 6. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Traversing the deep on foot as on dry land, Israel, seeing the pursuer Pharaoh drowned, cried aloud: Let us sing a song of victory unto God!

*Troparia:*
Send down upon us light-beaming rays from heaven through thy prayers, O wise and godly Romanus, that we may praise thy sacred memory in hymns.

From thy youth offering thyself wholly unto Christ, thou didst follow Him, illuminating thy mind with radiant beams from on high, O Romanus.

Adorned with the beauty of the All-Working and Holy Spirit, like a light-beaming star, Thou hast appeared to the ends of the world through the brightness of thy virtues, O wise Romanus.

*Theotokion:*
In the last times, the co-unoriginate Word came forth ineffably from thee; and He restored Adam, the first man who had fallen into corruption, O Theotokos.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
Thou art the confirmation of those who run unto Thee, O Lord; Thou art the light of the darkened, and my spirit sings of Thee.

*Troparia:*
Following the command of thy Teacher, thou didst become a hierarch and a passion-bearing offering, O apostle.

By thy God-pleasing prayers, make the Redeemer merciful unto those who sing of thee, O Apostle Ananias.

Lo, the Lord appeared unto Thee, checking the onslaught of the wild beast, having darkened his face with ineffable light;

*Theotokion:*
We Christians have acquired thee as our refuge and our wall, and we glorify thee unceasingly, O unwedded one.

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*
There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established us upon the rock of Thy confession.

*Troparia:*
Thy mind, O wise Romanus, became an abode of the Holy Trinity, being filled piously with true knowledge, pouring forth divine proclamations.

Thy honorable tongue gushed forth streams of eloquence, imparting divine things and wisely elucidating the ineffable Nativity of Christ unto us from the Virgin.

Thou dost richly inebriate our thoughts with wise teachings and beautiful psalmody, and dost fill them with the most divine sweetness, O God-proclaiming Romanus.

*Theotokion:*
Thou art higher than all the hosts, O Virgin, for thou didst conceive in thy womb their Creator, the Son of God, and having given birth, thou remainest ever a Virgin.

*Kontakion of the Venerable One, Tone 8:*
Adorned from thy youth with the divine virtues of the Spirit, O all-wise Romanus, thou wast the most honorable adornment of the Church of Christ...

thou wast; for having adorned her with exquisite singing, O blessed one, therefore we pray thee: grant unto those who desire thy divine gift, that we may cry unto thee: Rejoice, O all-blessed father, beauty of the Church!

*Sessional Hymn of the Apostle, Tone 8, Podoben: "Guiding unto wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости):*
Enlightened by divine knowledge, thou hast appeared as a priest and a martyr, and thou wast a divine apostle, O Ananias. And having immersed in water the former persecutor called Saul, who was blinded by the bidding from on high, O glorious one, by God-given naming thou didst show him forth as better, an enlightener of our souls. Therefore we cry unto thee: Pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who honor thy holy memory with love.

*Glory to the Venerable One, Tone 6, Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезна́чалное сло́во):*
Planted like a tree, O blessed father, by the outflows of the waters of fasting, by divine cooperation thou didst render unto the Planter and Creator the fruits of thy labors in due season. Therefore we sing of thy holy memory, honoring thee, O venerable Romanus.

*Both now, Theotokion:*
O fervent and invincible intercessor, certain and unashamed hope, wall, protection, and haven of those who run unto thee, O pure Ever-Virgin: with the angels, pray unto thy Son and God to give peace unto the world, and salvation, and great mercy.

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Beholding Thee hanging upon the Cross willingly between robbers, O Christ, Thy Mother, rent in her maternal bowels, said: O sinless Son, how art Thou unjustly nailed to the Cross like an evildoer, desiring to quicken mankind, as Thou art compassionate?

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation; I have considered Thy works, and glorified Thy Divinity.

*Troparia:*
Instructed by Thee, O Christ, the ever-memorable Ananias martyrially vanquishes death, having received the honors from Thee.

Thou wast a disciple of the persecuted Word, O ever-memorable Ananias, and a teacher of piety unto the persecutor.

The Lord showed thee unto Saul adorning thyself in God-honoring manner, and manifested thee, O Ananias, as the mediator of his healing.

*Theotokion:*

Pray unto God Whom thou didst bear, O all-pure Virgin, to deliver from every circumstance all who sing unto thee.

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*
Christ is my power, God and Lord, the venerable Church sings in God-befitting manner, crying out from a pure understanding, feasting in the Lord.

*Troparia:*
Thou wast a receptacle of the Holy Spirit and a fire-breathing mouth, proclaiming divine psalmody and delighting the souls of those who run unto thee, O Romanus.

Thou wast a distinct psaltery and a harp, drawing every mind unto thyself by thy narrations and thy beautiful psalmody.

The Good Spirit, of the all-luminous and Most Holy Spirit, O all-praised one, took up abode in thy light-bearing soul, and made thee a receptacle and wise God-proclaimer.

*Theotokion:*
The serpent poured poison into the hearing of our foremother, but thou, O pure one, didst shake it off, having conceived at the Archangel's voice and given birth to Christ.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Why hast Thou cast me away from Thy face, O unwaning Light, and an alien darkness has covered me, the wretched one? But convert me, and direct my paths toward the light of Thy commandments, I pray Thee.

*Troparia:*
A gushing fountain has shown thee to be a divine haven for those who were in Hebrew bonds, and the unapproachable Light made thee a ray for those in darkness, O blessed Ananias, dwelling mercifully in thee as in a praiseworthy disciple.

He Who knows secret things and knows all things before they come to be, revealed unto thee, while hiding, the persecutor who was formerly hidden, the sun of truth being shattered by him after no long time, O apostle.[^10]

The end of Thy dispensation has been attained, untraceable in its design; therefore, O Savior, death was made fearful and abolished. For this reason Ananias, as a victor and hierarch, is slaughtered rejoicing for Thy sake.

*Theotokion:*
Having maternal boldness before thy Son, O all-pure one, despise not kindred care for us, we pray thee; for we Christians present thee alone as a merciful propitiation unto the Master.

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*
By Thy divine light, O Good One, illuminate the souls of those who rise early unto Thee with love, I pray Thee, that they may know Thee, O Word of God, as the true God,

calling them out from the gloom of sin.

*Troparia:*
Having found Thee as a most God-befitting sanctuary, a most pure vessel, the grace of the Comforter, O God-wise Romanus, made Thee a temple adorned with light, and took up abode in Thee.

Having truly given birth to the incarnate Son of God, the Ever-Virgin, appearing unto thee by night, enlightened thy soul and filled thy mind with divine knowledge.[^11]

By the writings of thy sacred words, O ever-memorable one, thou hast truly appeared sweetest unto all, cheering all, enlightening the minds of all, and teaching all saving knowledge.

*Theotokion:*
O pure one who didst bear the Light, the co-eternal Word of the Father: by thy radiant prayers enlighten my mind, and drive away the darkness of my soul, O all-pure Sovereign Lady.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are my transgressions, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and give ear unto me, O God of my salvation!

*Troparia:*
Having learned to serve the living God, O most sacred one, Thou didst dismantle the manifold wickedness of the foolish, teaching them to worship the one three-hypostatic Godhead, O Ananias.

Having received the rudders of the Church into thy hands, thou didst come unto Christ the Pilot, O apostle; and by the Spirit, O Ananias, thou didst steer her like a divine ship.

Having created the straight path, O Good One, Thou didst command Thy glorious disciple to be sent unto him who walks uprightly, giving wings in prayer unto the vessel of Thy most upright election.

*Theotokion:*
That we may be delivered from grievous transgressions by thy supplications, O pure Parent of God, and that we may attain, O all-pure one, the divine radiance of the Son of God Who was ineffably incarnate of thee.

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*
Beholding the sea of life surging with the tempest of temptations, fleeing unto Thy calm haven, I cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O Greatly-Merciful One!

*Troparia:*
Being filled with illumination, and divinely led up to the height of virtues, thou didst disregard all earthly things, and wast an unshaken pillar and a luminary of piety.

Thou didst wither the desires of the flesh, and didst adorn thy soul with the brightest purity...
