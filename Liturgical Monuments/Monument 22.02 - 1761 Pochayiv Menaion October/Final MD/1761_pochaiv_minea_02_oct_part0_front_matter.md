# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 2: October (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-2-october-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

*(Flyleaf — Archival binding sheet) The Book of the*
*MENAION*
*For the Month of October.*

To the glory of the holy, consubstantial, life-creating, and undivided Trinity: Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit; under the reign of the great
*KING AUGUSTUS*
*THE THIRD,*
by the blessing of His Grace Lord
*SYLVESTER*
*LUBIENIECKI*
*RUDNICKI,*[^1]
Exarch of the Metropolia of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the volition of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk
*HYPATIUS BIELINSKI,*[^2]
Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; by the zeal and expense of the monks of the same Order, in the holy wonder-working Lavra of Pochaiv,
*PUBLISHED IN PRINT.*
In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers 7269,
and of the Nativity of Christ 1761.[^3]

*(Flyleaf — Library provenance stamp verso)*[^4]

### Notes

[^1]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylvester Lubieniecki Rudnytski, 1713–1777), Ukrainian Greek Catholic Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh (1750–1777), Exarch of the Kyiv Metropolia.
[^2]: Hypatius Bilynsky (Hipacy Bieliński, OSBM), Protoarchimandrite of the Basilian Order of Saint Josaphat (1759–1771) and Archimandrite/Superior of the Pochaiv Monastery.
[^3]: The year from the Creation of the World 7269 (҂зс҃ѯѳ) corresponds to 1761 AD (҂аѱѯа) in the Byzantine era (reckoned from 5509 BC).
[^4]: The verso of the title leaf bears an oval nineteenth-century library provenance stamp: *MICH. DYMET WE LWOWIE* (from the library/collection of Mykhailo Dymet in Lviv).
