# PART LVI: COLOPHON & IMPRINT OF THE STRIATYN TREBNYK [^4297]

By the good pleasure and goodness of God, glorified in Trinity as of one power: this book called the *Trebnik* [Euchologion] began to be printed by the personal expense and outlay of the noble Pan Teodor Yuriiovych Balaban;[^4298] and it was completed after his repose. And it was researched and corrected by the diligence of the greatly-sinful Teodor K.[^4299]

I beseech all who read or transcribe this book: correcting any errors that have occurred in a spirit of meekness, bless and curse not; so that you yourselves may be counted worthy of mercy from God and forgiveness from men.[^4300]

It began to be printed in the year from the creation of the world 7113, and from the saving Incarnation of the Son of God 1605, in the month of[^4301] *Imprint & Location:*[fol. 682v][^4302]

...July 22. And it was completed in the year 7114, and from the Inhumanation of Christ 1606, on the 14th of the month of September.

AT STRIATYN.[^4303]

*[Ornamental typographic woodcut printer's device: interlaced diamond floral knotwork vignette]*[^4304]

---

### Notes

[^4297]: Folio 682r: Opening of the formal colophon of the 1606 Striatyn Trebnyk. Theological opening doxology to the Holy Trinity.
[^4298]: Pan Teodor Yuriiovych Balaban (d. 1603/1606), starost of Terebovlia, brother of Bishop Hedeon Balaban of Lviv, who founded the Striatyn and Univ printing presses and funded the production of the 1606 *Trebnik*.
[^4299]: *Ѳеѡ́дѡра К*: Teodor/Feodor K., the learned editor and proofreader who revised and corrected the liturgical texts of the Striatyn press.
[^4300]: Traditional Ukrainian scribal/typographic apology and humility formula (*topos*), begging readers to correct typographical or editorial errors with meekness rather than curses.
[^4301]: Date of commencement of printing: Anno Mundi 7113 / Anno Domini 1605 (*҂з҃ р҃ г҃і / ҂а҃ х҃ є҃*). Text continues across the physical folio turn onto leaf p1380 (fol. 682v).
[^4302]: Folio 682v: Continuation and conclusion of the imprint colophon: printing began on July 22, 1605, and was completed on September 14, 1606 (AM 7114 / AD 1606, Feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross).
[^4303]: Location of publication: Striatyn (*В СТРѦ́ТИНѢ*), the estate of the Balaban family in the Rohatyn region (Western Ukraine), where the famous Striatyn press operated under Bishop Hedeon and Teodor Balaban before its equipment was acquired in 1618 by Archimandrite Yelysei Pletenetsky for the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra.
[^4304]: Concluding typographic woodcut printer's device: an elaborate diamond-shaped floral knotwork ornament marking the final impression of the 1606 edition.
