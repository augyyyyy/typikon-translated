# PART XXXI: THE ORDER OF THE GREAT SANCTIFICATION OF THE HOLY THEOPHANIES [^2203]

[Woodcut decorative tailpiece: winged cherub face within wreath flanked by palms and floral scrollwork][^2201] [Woodcut headpiece: The Baptism of Christ in the Jordan by Saint John the Baptist, with descending Holy Spirit and angels, within ornate floral scrollwork][^2202]

*When the priest says the prayer behind the ambo, all go out to the baptistery, the priest going before with tapers and censer, while we sing the following three troparia, Tone 8:*[^2204]

*Tone 8:*
The voice of the Lord cries upon the waters, saying: Come, receive all of you, the Spirit of wisdom, the Spirit of understanding, the Spirit of[^2205] the fear of God, of Christ Who has appeared.[^2206]

*Say it thrice. Then the other troparia, in the same Tone, twice each.*[^2207]

*Tone 8:*
Today the nature of waters is sanctified, and Jordan is parted, and turns back the currents of its waters, beholding the Master being baptized.[^2208]

*Tone 8:*
As a man Thou didst come to the river, O Christ King, and Thou hastest to receive a servant's baptism, O Good One, at the hands of the Forerunner, for the sake of our sins, O Lover of mankind.[^2209]

*Glory..., Now and ever..., Tone 8:*[^2210]

*Tone 8:*
At the voice of him that cries in the wilderness: "Prepare the way of the Lord," Thou didst come, O Lord, having taken the form of a servant, asking for baptism, though knowing not sin. The waters saw Thee and were afraid; trembling[^2211] the Forerunner became, and cried aloud, saying: "How shall the lamp illuminate the Light? How shall the servant lay his hand upon the Master? Sanctify me and the waters, O Savior, Who takest away the sins of the world."[^2212]

**Deacon:** Wisdom!

*Then the paremias. The reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah.*

*Heading:*[^2213]

Thus says the Lord: Rejoice, O thirsty desert; let the wilderness exult, and blossom as a lily. And it shall blossom and rejoice; the desert of Jordan shall exult, and the glory of Lebanon has been given unto it, and the honor of Carmel; and My people shall see the glory of the Lord, and the majesty of God.[^2214]

Be strong, ye slackened hands, and be comforted, ye feeble knees! Say unto them that are faint-hearted in mind: Be strong, and fear not; for behold, our God will render judgment; He will come and save us. Then shall the eyes of the blind be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall hear. Then shall the lame man leap as a hart, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be clear; for water has broken forth in the wilderness, and a ravine in a thirsty land. And the parched land shall become a pool, and in the thirsty land there shall be a spring of water; there shall be the habitation of birds, and the resting-place of sirens[^2215] reeds and pools. And there shall be a clean way, and it shall be called a holy way; and the unclean shall not pass by there, neither shall the dispersed walk upon it; but the redeemed shall walk upon it, and they shall not go astray. And there shall be no lion there, nor shall any evil beast go up thereon, neither shall it be found there; but the redeemed shall walk upon it, and those chosen of the Lord; and they shall return, and come unto Sion with gladness and joy, and everlasting gladness upon their head; praise and exultation and gladness shall overtake them, and sickness and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.[^2216]

*The reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah.*[^2217]

Thus says the Lord: Ye that thirst, go to the water; and as many as have no silver, go, buy, and eat and drink without silver and without price, wine and fatness. Why do ye value silver for that which is not bread, and your labor for that which satisfies not? Give ear unto Me, and ye shall eat good things, and your soul shall delight itself in good things. Incline with your ears, and follow after My ways; give ear unto Me, and your soul shall live in good things; and I will make with you an everlasting covenant: the sure holy things of David. Behold, I have given Him for a testimony among the nations, a ruler and commander to the nations.[^2218]

...behold, nations which know Thee not shall call upon Thee; and peoples who have not known Thee shall flee unto Thee, for the sake of the Lord Thy God, and the Holy One of Israel, for He has glorified Thee.[^2219] Seek ye the Lord, and when ye find Him, call upon Him; whensoever He draws near unto you, let the wicked man forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his counsels; and let him return unto the Lord, unto our God, and ye shall obtain mercy; and call aloud, for He shall abundantly pardon your sins. For My counsels are not as your counsels, neither are your ways as My ways, says the Lord.[^2220]

But as the heaven is distant from the earth, so is My way distant from your ways, and your thoughts from My thoughts.[^2221] For as rain or snow comes down from heaven, and returns not thither until it waters the earth, and brings forth, and buds, and gives seed to the sower and bread for food; so shall My word be, whatsoever goes forth from My mouth: it shall not return unto Me void, until it has accomplished all things that I have willed; and I will prosper My ways and My promises.[^2222] For ye shall go forth with gladness, and with joy shall ye be taught; for the mountains and the hills shall leap, awaiting you in joy, and all the trees of the field shall clap with their branches. And instead of the bramble shall come up the cypress, and instead of the fleabane shall come up the myrtle; and it shall be for the name of the Lord, and for an everlasting sign, and it shall not fail, says the Lord.[^2223]

*The reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah.*[^2224]

Thus says the Lord: Draw water with gladness from the wells of salvation.[^2225] And in that day thou shalt say: Sing unto the Lord, sing praises unto His name; proclaim His glorious deeds among the nations; make mention that His name is exalted. Sing praises unto the name of the Lord, for He has done high things; proclaim these things in all the earth. Rejoice and be glad, O thou that dwellest in Sion; for exalted in the midst of thee is the Holy One of Israel.[^2226]

*Prokeimenon, Tone 3:* The Lord is my light and my Savior; whom shall I fear?[^2227]

*Verse:* The Lord is the defender of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?[^2228]

*The reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Corinthians.*[^2229]

Brethren, I would not have you to be ignorant, how that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea; and were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea; and did all eat the same spiritual food; and did all drink the same spiritual drink; for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ.[^2230]

*Alleluia, Tone 4:* The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of glory has thundered.[^2231]

*Verse:* What ailed thee, O sea, that thou fleddest?[^2232]

*The Gospel according to Mark.*[^2233]

At that time, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized of John in Jordan. And straightway coming up out of the water, He saw the heavens rent open, and the Spirit like a dove descending upon Him. And there came a voice from heaven, saying: "Thou art My beloved Son, in Whom I am well pleased."[^2234]

*The litany of the deacon:*[^2235]

**Deacon:** In peace let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For the peace from above and for the salvation of our souls, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Deacon:** For the peace of the whole world, for the good estate of the holy churches of God, and for the union of all, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2236]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For this holy temple, and for them that with faith, reverence, and the fear of God enter therein, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For our Archbishop *[Name]*, for the honorable priesthood, the diaconate in Christ, and for all the clergy and the people, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2237]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For our pious and God-preserved rulers, and for all their court and army, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2238]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That He will aid them and subdue under their feet every enemy and adversary, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For this holy monastery, and for every city and countryside, and for them that in faith dwell therein, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2239]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For seasonable weather, for an abundance of the fruits of the earth, and for peaceful times, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For them that travel by sea and by land, the sick, the suffering, the captive, and for their salvation, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That this water may be sanctified by the power, operation, and descent of the Holy Spirit, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2240]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That there may be sent down upon it the grace of redemption, the blessing of Jordan, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be for the cleansing of souls and bodies unto all that ask and receive it, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be manifest as sanctification and salvation for our souls, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2241]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be for the driving away and cleansing of every operation and assault of visible and invisible enemies, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be manifest for the healing and cleansing of every infirmity of our souls and bodies, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That the power of the soul-destroying adversary may be drowned in this water, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2242]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That we who receive the sanctification of this water may be illuminated by the grace of Christ, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be unto all who anoint themselves therewith with faith, or drink thereof, for the cleansing of the defilement of flesh and spirit, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2243]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That the purification of the supersubstantial Trinity may come upon it by grace, let us pray unto the Lord.[^2244]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** For them that draw and drink thereof unto the sanctification of souls and bodies, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be a fountain of incorruption, and sanctification for homes, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That it may be blessed as the streams of Jordan, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That the Lord God will hear the voice of our supplication, and have mercy upon us, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** That we may be delivered from all affliction, wrath, and need, let us pray unto the Lord.

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** Help us, save us, have mercy on us, and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.[^2245]

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

**Deacon:** Commemorating our all-holy, pure, most blessed and glorious Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary, with all the saints, let us commend ourselves and one another, and our whole life unto Christ our God.[^2246]

**Choir:** To Thee, O Lord.

*While this is being said by the deacon, the priest prays, saying this prayer secretly to himself. But if there is no deacon, after the completion of the litany he says it:*[^2247]

O Lord Jesus Christ, only-begotten Son, Who art in the bosom of the Father, true God, fountain of life and immortality, Light of Light, Who didst come into the world to enlighten it: sanctify our thoughts by Thy Holy Spirit, and receive us who offer majesty and praise unto Thee for Thy eternal and marvelous wonders, and for[^2248] Thy saving dispensation in the last times, when Thou didst clothe Thyself in our weak and impoverished flesh, and didst condescend to the measures of servitude, O King of all; and moreover, by a servant's hand didst deign to be baptized in the Jordan, that, having sanctified the nature of the waters, O Sinless One, Thou mightest set us upon the path of regeneration by water and the Spirit, and restore us unto our primordial freedom.[^2249] Celebrating the remembrance of this divine mystery, we beseech Thee, O Master, Lover of mankind: sprinkle upon us, Thine unworthy servants, according to Thy divine promise, the pure waters of Thy mercy's gift; that the petition made by us sinners over this water may be acceptable to Thy goodness, and that through it Thou mayest grant Thy blessing unto us, and unto all Thy faithful people, to the glory of Thy holy and worshipful Name.[^2250] For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thine all-holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.[^2251]

*And after the prayer he does not exclaim aloud, but says to himself: Amen. And when the deacon has finished the litany, the priest begins this prayer aloud, the composition of Saint Sophronius, Patriarch of Jerusalem:*[^2252]

*Be it known that the present prayer, that is: "O Trinity transcendent in essence", is indeed read in some places; but in the Great Church and on the Holy Mountain it is not said.[^2253] But if thou wishest to read it, after the conclusion of the above-written prayer, say aloud:*

O Trinity transcendent in essence, all-good, all-divine, all-powerful, overseeing all, invisible, incomprehensible; Creator of noetic essences and rational natures;[^2254] supernatural Goodness; Light unapproachable, that enlightens every man that comes into the world: shine forth also in me, Thine unworthy servant; enlighten the eyes of my mind, that I may dare to hymn Thine immeasurable beneficence and power; and may the prayer offered by me for the people standing here be acceptable; let not my transgressions hinder Thy Holy Spirit from coming hither; but grant me without condemnation to cry out unto Thee, and to say now, O All-Good Trinity:[^2255]

We glorify Thee, O Master, Lover of mankind, Almighty, pre-eternal King! We glorify Thee, the Creator and Fashioner of all! We glorify Thee, Who art without father from a mother, and without mother from a Father! For in the foregoing feast we beheld Thee as an infant;[^2256]

but in the present feast we behold Thee mature, Who hast appeared as our perfect God from the Perfect One.[^2257] For behold, the time of the feast of the most pure Theophany of Thy Christ has arrived for us all; and the Angels feast together with men, and the assembly of the saints gathers with us.

Today the grace of the All-Holy Spirit in the likeness of a dove descended upon the waters.
Today the heavens drop dew, and rain down dew upon the earth.
Today the Sun that never sets has shone forth, and the world is illuminated with the light of the Lord.[^2258]
Today the moon enlightens the whole earth with its radiant beams.
Today the light-bearing stars adorn the universe with the brightness of their splendor.[^2259]

Today the clouds rain down the shower of righteousness from heaven upon mankind.
Today the Uncreated One of His own will is touched by the hands of His own creation.
Today the watery elements receive upon their shoulders the Master's footsteps.[^2260]
Today the Invisible One becomes visible, that He may make us see Him Himself.
Today the Prophet draws near with love unto the Creator, and stands in trembling, beholding the condescension of God.
Today the Unoriginate One bows His neck unto His own servant, that He may drown the serpent that is in the waters.
Today the prophetic hand touches the flame-bearing head, that He may clothe us with the laver of incorruption.
Today the waters of the Jordan are transformed into healing for men by the appearance of our God.[^2261]
Today all creation is watered with streams inspired by the fiery Spirit.[^2262]
Today the sins of mankind are washed away by the Holy Spirit in the waters of the Jordan.
Today we are delivered from the ancient weeping, and like ancient Israel we are saved.[^2263]
Today we strip off the old rags of sin, and put on new robes.
Today the salt waters of the sea are changed into sweetness by Thy manifestation, O Master.[^2264]
Today the darkness of the world is abolished by the manifestation of Christ our God.
Today deception is destroyed, and the way of salvation is prepared for us all by the Master's coming.
Today the holy and Orthodox faith of the Church has shone forth, as the celebration of those who keep spiritual festival.
Today things on high are joined together with things below, and things below feast together with things on high.
Today we have purchased the Kingdom of Heaven, for of the Lord's Kingdom there is no end.[^2265]
Today earth and sea have shared the joy of the world, and the world is filled with gladness.

The waters saw Thee, O God, the waters saw Thee and were afraid.[^2266] The Jordan turned back, seeing the fire of the Godhead descending bodily and entering into it. The Jordan turned back, seeing the Holy Spirit in the likeness of a dove descending, and hovering over Him.[^2267] The Jordan turned back, beholding the Invisible One become visible, the Creator become incarnate, the Master in the form of a servant. The Jordan turned back, and the mountains skipped, seeing God in the flesh; and the clouds gave voice, marveling at Him Who was coming, Light of Light, true God of true God, seeing today the Master's feast in the Jordan; and seeing Him Who drowned in the Jordan the very death of transgression, and the sting of delusion, and the bond of Hades, and granted unto the world the baptism of salvation.[^2268]

Therefore I also, a sinner and Thine unworthy servant, declaring the greatness of Thy wonders,[^2269] held fast by fear, in compunction cry out unto Thee:

*And again with a louder voice:*

Great art Thou, O Lord, and marvelous are Thy works, and there is no word that is sufficient to hymn Thy wonders![^2270]

*Say this up to here thrice.*

*(In the margin:)* *To make the sign of the Cross with three candles.*[^2271]

For by Thy will Thou hast brought all things out of non-existence into being; by Thy might Thou upholdest creation, and by Thy providence Thou orderest the world. Thou hast composed creation from four elements; with four seasons Thou hast crowned the circle of the year.[^2272] Before Thee all the noetic powers tremble; the sun sings of Thee; the moon glorifies Thee; Thee do the stars beseech; Thee does the light obey; before Thee do the abysses tremble; Thee do the fountains serve. Thou hast spread out the heavens like a curtain;[^2273] Thou hast established the earth upon the waters; Thou hast enclosed the sea with sand; Thou hast poured out the air for breathing. The Archangelic powers serve Thee, and the Angelic choirs worship Thee; the many-eyed Cherubim and six-winged Seraphim,[^2274] standing round about and flying, veil themselves in fear of Thine unapproachable glory. For Thou art God, uncircumscribable, without beginning, and ineffable;[^2275]

[Thou didst come] down upon the earth, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men;[^2276] for Thou couldst not endure, O Master, by reason of the compassions of Thy mercy, to see mankind tormented by the devil, but Thou didst come and save us. We confess grace, we proclaim mercy, we conceal not Thy benefactions. Thou hast set free the nature of our race; Thou hast sanctified the virginal womb by Thy nativity. All creation hymns Thee Who hast appeared. For Thou art our God, Who didst appear upon earth and live among men.[^2277] Thou hast sanctified the streams of the Jordan, having sent down from heaven Thy Holy Spirit, and Thou hast crushed the heads of the dragons nesting there.[^2278]

Do Thou Thyself, O Lover of mankind, O King, come now also through the visitation of Thy Holy Spirit, and sanctify this water.[^2279]

*Say this thrice.*

*(In the margin:)* *Blessing the water with the hand thrice.*[^2280]

And grant unto it the grace of redemption, the blessing of the Jordan; make it a fountain of incorruption, a gift of sanctification, a remission of sins, a healing of infirmities, a destruction of demons, unapproachable by adversarial powers, filled with angelic might;[^2281] that all who draw of it and partake of it may have it for the purification of soul and body, for the healing of afflictions, for the sanctification of homes, and for every suitable benefit. For Thou art our God, Who by water and the Spirit hast renewed our nature grown old through sin; Thou art our God, Who didst drown sins with water in the days of Noah; Thou art our God, Who by the sea didst deliver the Hebrew race from the bondage of Pharaoh through Moses; Thou art our God, Who didst cleft the rock in the wilderness, and the waters flowed, and torrents overflowed, and Thou didst give drink unto Thy thirsty people; Thou art our God, Who by water and fire in the days of Elijah didst deliver Israel from the delusion of Baal.[^2282] Do Thou now also, O Master, sanctify this water by Thy Holy Spirit.

*(In the margin:)* *To blow thrice.*[^2283]

*And he makes the sign of the Cross over the water, saying this thrice.*[^2284]

*Then he says this prayer:*

Grant unto all who touch it, and partake of it, and anoint themselves with it, and taste of it, sanctification, blessing, purification, health. And save, O Lord, Thy faithful servants, our rulers, (Name), and preserve them under Thy shelter in peace; and subdue under them every enemy and adversary; and grant unto them all petitions unto salvation, and life eternal;[^2285] that by all elements, and men, and Angels, visible and invisible, Thy most holy Name may be glorified, with the Father and with the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.[^2286]

**People:** Amen.

**Priest:** Peace be unto all.

**Deacon:**

Bow your heads unto the Lord.

*The priest, having bowed, says this prayer secretly:*

Incline Thine ear, O Lord, and hear us, Thou Who didst deign to be baptized in the Jordan and didst sanctify the waters; bless all of us who, by the bowing of our neck, show forth the semblance of servitude; and make us worthy to be filled with Thy sanctification through the partaking of this water; and may it be unto us, O Lord, for the health of soul and body.[^2287]

*Exclamation:*
For Thou art our sanctification, and unto Thee do we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thine all-holy and good and life-creating Spirit, now[^2288] and ever, and unto ages of ages. [**Choir:** Amen.]

*And the priest, having taken up the honorable Cross, immerses it upright into the water thrice, crosswise lowering it into the water and raising it, saying the Troparion, Tone 1:*[^2289]

*Tone 1:*
When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan, the worship of the Trinity was made manifest; for the voice of the Parent bore witness unto Thee, calling Thee His beloved Son; and the Spirit, in the form of a dove, confirmed the steadfastness of the word. O Christ God, Who hast appeared and enlightened the world, glory be to Thee.[^2290]

*Thrice. And there is the veneration of the Cross.*

*And the priest sprinkles the people with the holy water. And having entered into the church, having formed the choir, we sing: Glory... Both now... Tone 6:*[^2291]

*Tone 6:*
Let us hymn, O faithful, the greatness of God's dispensation concerning us; for having become man on account of our transgressions, He is cleansed with our cleansing in the Jordan, the only pure and immortal One, sanctifying me and the waters, and crushing the heads of the dragons nesting in the water. Let us draw water, therefore, with gladness, O brethren; for the grace of the Spirit is invisibly bestowed upon those who draw with faith, from Christ God and the Savior of our souls.[^2292]

*And we sing:* Blessed be the Name of the Lord...

*Then Psalm 33:* I will bless the Lord at all times..., *the whole unto the end.*[^2293]

*And the antidoron is given to the people; and we partake of the sanctified water before the antidoron. And the Dismissal.*[^2294]

*In the morning, on Theophany:*

*In the morning, after the Great Doxology at the conclusion of Matins:*

*The priest having vested in sacred vesture, carrying in his hands the honorable wood of the Cross, with censer and taper-bearers preceding, we go forth from the church unto the water-springs, singing the stichera, Tone 8: "The voice of the Lord is upon the waters...", and the rest of the stichera in order, as was written yesterday at the beginning of the sanctification.*[^2295]

*Then the readings: Prokeimenon, Epistle, and Gospel. The Deacon's litany.*

*If there is no deacon, the priest says the prayer secretly: "O Lord Jesus Christ, only-begotten Son..." when the paremias are read, until he is ready by the completion of the deacon's litany.*[^2296]

*Then the priest: "Great art Thou, O Lord...", and the rest of the service of the sanctification. And all having sprinkled themselves with the water, we sing the Troparion and Kontakion of the feast.*[^2297]

*And having returned, we enter into the church, singing: Glory... Both now... Troparion, Tone 2:*

*Tone 2:*
The manifestation of the Trinity took place in the Jordan; for that transcendent Godhead, the Father, proclaimed: "This One being baptized is My beloved Son"; and the Spirit came upon His like; Whom the peoples bless and supreme-exalt unto all ages.[^2298]

*Then the litany. And we venerate the honorable Wood. And there is the Dismissal.*[^2299]
