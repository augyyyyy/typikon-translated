# PART XLV: THE ORDER FOR A FURNACE [KILN] [^3774]

*The priest performs the sanctification of water; and after the sanctification he says the prayer:*[^3775]

> **Priest:** "Let us pray to the Lord."

> **Priest:** "O Eternal Lord, Who didst turn the sevenfold furnace and flame in Babylon into dew,[^3776] and didst preserve unharmed Thy holy youths Ananias, Azarias, and Misael cast into it by King Nebuchadnezzar: do Thou Thyself bless also this furnace, for the sake of Thy great mercy; and bless the works of the hands of Thy servants; prosper them by Thy beneficence; direct them according to the multitude of Thy compassions; through the prayers of our most pure Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary; by the power of the precious and life-creating Cross; by the intercessions of the most magnificent Archangels Michael and Gabriel,[^3777] and of all the heavenly bodiless Hosts; of the precious, glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John; of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles; of the holy Hieromartyr Cyprian;[^3778] and of our father among the saints, Nicholas, Archbishop of Myra in Lycia, the Wonderworker: for Thou art He that blesses and sanctifies all things, O our God, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."[^3779]

*Another prayer:*[^3780]

> **Priest:** "O Master Lord our God, Who dwellest on high and lookest upon all things,[^3781] Who didst bless the house of Laban at the entrance of Jacob,[^3782] and the house of Potiphar at the entrance of Joseph,[^3783] Who didst bless the house of Obed-edom at the entrance of the Ark,[^3784] and Who by the incarnation of Thy Christ didst become salvation unto the house of Zacchaeus:[^3785] do Thou Thyself bless this furnace, and preserve those working in it by Thy fear, and keep them unharmed from adversities; and send down upon them Thy blessing from the height of Thy habitation; and bless and multiply the works of their hands, that we may ever thankfully glorify Thy most holy name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."[^3786]

*Then he says the supplications:*[^3787]

> **Priest:** "Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee:"

> **Choir:** "Lord, have mercy."

> **Priest:** "Again we pray for mercy, life, peace, health, salvation, visitation, and remission of the sins of Thy servants *[Name]*; and that the work of the hands of those laboring in this furnace may be blessed and multiplied, we pray Thee, O Lord, give ear and have mercy."[^3788]

> **Choir:** "Lord, have mercy."

> **Priest:** "Give ear unto us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth, and of those far off upon the sea;[^3789] and be merciful, be merciful, O Master, unto our sins, and have mercy on us; for a merciful God and Lover of mankind art Thou, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever [and unto ages of ages]."[^3790]

*And he sprinkles the furnace and all that are present there; then he makes the dismissal.*[^3791]

### Notes

[^3774]: Headpiece and title marking the beginning of Part XLV: The Order for a Furnace or Kiln (Въслѣдова́нїе на пе́щїю; Greek Ἀκολουθία εἰς κάμινον). This liturgical office was appointed for the blessing and spiritual protection of metal-smelting furnaces, ceramic and tile kilns, glassworks, limekilns, or bakery ovens, which involved perilous fire operations in early modern Ruthenian towns and monastic estates.
[^3775]: The rubrical order specifies that the priest first perform the Lesser Sanctification of Water (ѻ҆свѧще́нїе воды̀), providing the holy water with which the furnace, the tools, and the artisans are subsequently asperged.
[^3776]: Dan. 3:19–27; Song of the Three Holy Children 1:26–27 (LXX): the angel of the Lord descended into the furnace with Azarias and his companions and "made the midst of the furnace as it had been a moist whistling wind, so that the fire touched them not at all, neither hurt nor troubled them." The prayer continues onto fol. 616r (leaf p1247).
[^3777]: Slavonic: предста́телствы превеличли́вшихъ тахїа́рхъ михаи́ла и҆ Гаврїи́ла. The Greek military and administrative title ταξίαρχος (taxiarches / commander of the celestial ranks) is preserved as a technical loanword in Ruthenian and South Slavic euchological manuscripts.
[^3778]: The invocation of Hieromartyr Cyprian of Antioch (commemorated October 2), renowned in Christian hagiography as a former practitioner of the occult arts who was converted and martyred, invokes his traditional patronage against demonic sabotage, sudden fires, witchcraft, and artisanal hazards.
[^3779]: Concluding Trinitarian doxology of the first blessing prayer over the furnace.
[^3780]: Rubric introducing the second blessing prayer over the furnace (мл҃тва, дроу́гаа), continuing onto fol. 616v (leaf p1248).
[^3781]: Ps. 112:5–6 LXX: "Who is like unto the Lord our God, Who dwells on high, and looks down upon the low things in heaven and on the earth?"
[^3782]: Gen. 30:27: Laban's acknowledgment to the Patriarch Jacob: "I have found favor in thine eyes: for God hath blessed me in thy coming."
[^3783]: Gen. 39:5: "The Lord blessed the Egyptian's house for Joseph's sake; and the blessing of the Lord was upon all that he had in the house, and in the field."
[^3784]: 2 Kings / 2 Sam. 6:11–12 LXX; 1 Chron. 13:14: "And the Ark of God remained with the family of Obed-edom in his house three months. And the Lord blessed the house of Obed-edom, and all that he had." The Church Slavonic witnesses the Hellenized form А҆веда́рїа (Greek Ἀβεδδαρά).
[^3785]: Luke 19:9: Christ's blessing upon the house of Zacchaeus the publican: "This day is salvation come to this house, forsomuch as he also is a son of Abraham."
[^3786]: Concluding doxology of the second prayer for the blessing of the furnace.
[^3787]: Rubric introducing the augmented litany of fervent supplication (мо́лбы / ἐκτενὴς ἱκεσία), incorporating specialized petitions for the artisans and their labor.
[^3788]: Specific economic and safety petition for the craftsmen: и҆ ѡ҆ е҆́же бл҃витисѧ и҆ ᲂу҆мно́житисѧ дѣ́лѡ рꙋ́къ трꙋжда́ющихсѧ въ пещѝ се́й ("and that the work of the hands of those laboring in this furnace may be blessed and multiplied"). The printed text contains a minor orthographic slip in line 4: зрдра́вїю (for здра́вїю).
[^3789]: Ps. 64:6 LXX: "Give ear unto us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth, and of those afar upon the sea."
[^3790]: Ecphonesis of the augmented litany, invoking God's mercy and Trinitarian glory.
[^3791]: Concluding rubrics: the priest asperges the newly consecrated furnace and the assembled laborers with sanctified water, followed by the formal liturgical dismissal.
