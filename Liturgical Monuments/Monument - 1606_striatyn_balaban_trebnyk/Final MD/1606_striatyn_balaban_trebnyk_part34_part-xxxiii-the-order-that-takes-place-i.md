*Supplicatory Canon for drought unto our Lord Jesus Christ.[^2464] Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

Come, ye peoples, let us sing a song unto Christ God, Who divided the sea and guided the people whom He brought forth from the bondage of Egypt, for He hath been glorified.[^2465]

*Troparion:*
Receive, O Savior, the supplication from our unworthy lips, and send down from heaven upon the earth a fruitful dew; for Thou art the Lover of mankind and a God of manifold mercy, overlooking our transgressions.[^2466]

*Troparion:*
Overlook now, O Lord, our transgressions, and turn away Thy wrath which Thou hast now justly brought upon us, O our Savior, and grant dew from heaven unto the earth and unto Thy people.

*Troparion:*
Come, O men, let us weep before God and our Creator, crying out with compunction:[^2467] Overlook our transgressions, O Lover of mankind, and send down rain from heaven upon the parched earth.

*Theotokion:*
Through thee have we known the Light, O Theotokos;[^2468] therefore do we pray thee: cease not interceding with thy Creator and Son to overlook our sins, and to send down dew upon His earth.

*Another Supplicatory Canon unto the Most Holy Theotokos. Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^2469]

I shall open my mouth, and it will be filled...[^2470]

*Troparion:*
Open, O Sovereign Lady, the heavens that are shut up because of our evil sins and because of our innumerable stumbling-blocks; pouring out the floodgates of heaven[^2471] for the nourishment of earthly dwellers, entreat the Meek One.

*Troparion:*
Having conceived without seed the heavenly Cloud[^2472] Who nourishes earthly dwellers with spiritual dew: pour out upon us a heavenly shower, appeasing the wrath of the Most Merciful God.

*Glory...*
O Master of all creation, thrice-unoriginate in properties, divided in Persons yet in one will:[^2473] not in wrath nor in anger punish us, Thou Who hast Thine innumerable love for mankind.

*Both now...*
O Thou Who didst shut up the heavens by the prayer of Thy prophet:[^2474] now open, O Master, the floodgates of heaven,[^2475] accepting the prayer of the Mother of God and Virgin who bore Thee without seed and without mingling, who entreats Thee for us in time of drought.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
Establish us in Thyself, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put to death sins; and plant Thy fear in our hearts, for Thou art the Lover of mankind.[^2476]

*Troparion:*
O Lord Who holdest all creation in Thy palm:[^2477] as Thou art compassionate, hear the supplications of Thy servants, and send down dew upon the face of all the earth.

*Troparion:*
Good art Thou and merciful, O Lord, as Thou hast an abyss of compassions; overlook not our supplication, but send us a peaceful cloud with good rain, O Merciful One.

*Troparion:*
The wealth which Thou gavest us we have foully squandered;[^2478] yet as Thou hast an abyss of mercy, O Christ—for Thou art a good Father—be moved with pity, granting fruitful rains unto the earth, overlooking our transgressions.

*Theotokion:*
As thou hast acceptable boldness toward the Lover of mankind and thy Son, O pure one: entreat Him to grant unto the earth well-tempered rain,[^2479] by thy prayers.

*Another Heirmos:* Thy hymnographers[^2480] *Troparion:*
Receiving our entreaties chanted unworthily from our lips: grant us cleansing from our innumerable offenses; for thee alone have we as helper before our God.

*Troparion:*
Turn back Thy wrath, O Lord, and give us not over to reproach;[^2481] but while chastening us,[^2482] show forth also Thy mercy upon us, through the prayers of Thine all-pure Mother who entreats Thee for us, O Christ.

*Glory...*
All-holy and Divine Trinity, glorified in three Persons: overcome the hardness of our hearts,[^2483] and hear the supplication of Thy sinful servants who pray with fervor.

*Both now...*
Thou art the cloud bearing Christ, and the door[^2484] heavenly, O all-pure Virgin: pour out upon thy people thine innumerable mercy; for thee alone have we as protection and mediatress and speedy help.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I sing to Thee: for having heard the report of Thee, O Lord, I was afraid;[^2485] for Thou didst come unto me, seeking me who was gone astray;[^2486] therefore do I glorify Thy great dispensation toward us, and our renewal.

*Troparion:*
Look down from on high upon us who have sinned against Thee, O Lord;[^2487] and do Thou Who art good grant Thy mercy unto all the earth; and send down, we pray, Thy most rich mercies unto Thy servants, O Master.

*Troparion:*
There hath come upon us who sin against Thee Thy wrath, O Merciful One; Thou hast shut up the heavens so as not to give rain unto the earth;[^2488] therefore do we Thy servants entreat Thee: punish us not in Thine anger, O Word, compassionate Master.

*Troparion:*
Altering the temperaments of the air by Thy command, O Lord: grant us abundant rain,[^2489] sending bountiful fruits unto the earth, that every living thing may be satisfied[^2490] through the good pleasure of Thy mercy.

*Theotokion:*
Thou hast appeared as Mother unto our God, O Virgin Theotokos; therefore we all entreat thee, O Sovereign Lady, as thou hast boldness: pray Him on behalf of us all to overlook our transgressions, and to send down rain upon the earth through thy prayers, O Lady.

*Another Heirmos. To the Archangels:*

He Who sits in glory upon the throne of the Godhead...[^2491]

*Troparion:*
Holy Archistrategoi Michael and Gabriel,[^2492] standing before the throne: fervently entreat Christ for the people, to deliver them from afflictions and tribulations, and to enrich the face of the earth for fruit-bearing.[^2493]

*Troparion:*
O Most Merciful God, not in wrath nor in anger punish us, but prevail over our manifold sins, according to Thy great mercy.

*Glory...*
As of old Thou didst receive the prodigal and the publican who repented:[^2494] receive us also, O Master, who pray unto Thee, enriching the face of the earth and giving rain in due season,[^2495] through the prayers of Thine all-pure Mother, O Christ.

*Both now* Cast down the assaults of enemies and assuage our afflictions, subduing them beneath the feet of our Christ-loving sovereign;[^2496] and through the prayers of the Most Holy Theotokos, loose the malice of our sins.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
O Lord, Giver of light and Creator of the ages:[^2497] guide us all in the light of Thy commandments, for beside Thee we know no other God.[^2498]

*Troparion:*
Through the prayers of Thy bodiless hosts, O Savior: graciously hear us who ever pray unto Thy dominion, and send down abundant and good water unto the thirsty earth,[^2499] for Thou alone art the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*
Be merciful unto us, O Lord, in the time[^2500] of our afflictions, and from the impending wrath save us; send rain unto Thy land, O Master, of Thy good pleasure, O Lover of mankind.[^2501]

*Troparion:*
The Twelve Apostles,[^2502] with all the choirs of martyrs, make prayer for us, to deliver [us] from every affliction and wrath, and not to deprive us Thy servants of Thy mercy.

*Theotokion:*
We earthly ones have thee as Mediatress of all unto God,[^2503] O Virgin Theotokos; therefore we pray thee, O holy one: save thy people from every affliction, and deliver [us] from the present wrath, by thy prayers, O Theotokos.

*Another Heirmos:*
All things were amazed at Thy divine [glory][^2504] *Troparion:*
Marvelous art Thou, O Christ God, in Thy holy powers,[^2505] chastening, and again by mercy protecting us; through the prayers of Thine all-pure Mother, being entreated by her, grant unto us remission of sins.

*Troparion:*
O Pure and undefiled Virgin, who without seed hast borne God, Whom an earthly father sowed not, nor did a mother bring forth in the heavens:[^2506] Him fervently entreat to grant unto us remission of sins, bringing to fullness in abundance the fruits of the earth.

*Glory...*
I glorify the Thrice-unoriginate Trinity,[^2507] uncreated in essential properties, yet divided in Persons, existing in one will and purpose, Whom the bodiless hosts glorify; and we earthly ones glorifying, ask mercy and the watering of the earth.

*Both now...*
Being compassionate, O Lady, having borne the most compassionate Lord: therefore consume my stumbling blocks, and cast down enemies, granting us victory, and subduing every foe and adversary beneath the feet of our faithful sovereign.[^2508]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy compassion: from corruption, O my God, raise me up.[^2509]

*Troparion:*
O multitude of all martyrs, as having great boldness toward God:[^2510] pray Him now to grant good rain unto the parched earth and to bestow fruits of abundance, and grace unto His people.

*Troparion:*
We pray Thee, O Master, through the prayers of Thy disciples: overlook, O Lord, and forgive our transgressions, and turn away Thy wrath from us, O Compassionate One;[^2511] and grant dew unto Thy land, through the prayers of Thy disciples.

*Troparion:*
O Master, our Creator, Thee as good God and Lover of mankind we entreat with all our soul: spare us, O Lord, spare [us], and grant unto the parched earth temperate rain,[^2512] bringing forth good fruits for all flesh.

*Theotokion:*
Praying unceasingly unto thy Son for us, we entreat thee, O all-pure Mother of God: overlook not thy unworthy servants; for thou hast boldness as Mother: entreat Him, O Virgin, that He may deliver us from the impending wrath.

*Another Heirmos:*
Prefiguring, he cried out...[^2513]

*Troparion:*
Graciously hear us who cry unto Thee faithfully, O Merciful Lord; pour forth the floodgates of heaven,[^2514] and remit our sinful stumbling blocks; grant victory over our invisible and visible enemies, O Most Merciful One.

*Troparion:*
Lifting up thine all-pure hands and eyes, O Lady: shelter us from enemies visible and invisible, subduing [their] malice beneath our feet, granting us rain from heaven from God.

*Glory...*
Held fast by hardness of sin,[^2515] we have not in our soul tearful lamentation: O all-pure Lady, ask for us rain from heaven from the Lord, for the fruitfulness of the earth.

*Both now...*
Overcome my darkening, and pour out upon my heart showers of tears, that through thee the fruit of repentance may grow in me, O all-pure Virgin.

*Kontakion, Tone 6:*
O Champion of Christians that cannot be put to shame, unchanging Mediatress unto the Creator:[^2516] overlook not the suppliant voice of sinners, but anticipate, as thou art good, to our help who faithfully cry unto thee; hasten to prayer and speed to supplication, ever protecting, O Theotokos, them that honor thee.

*Ikos:*
Stretch forth thy palms, wherewith thou didst embrace the Master of all as an Infant;[^2517] according to the multitude of mercy, forsake not us who ever hope in thee; by watchful protection and boundless compassion have mercy upon us, and spare our souls, being the fountain of compassion;[^2518] for thee have we gained as Protectress, O all-pure one, delivered from grievous afflictions; but as having a heart of compassions: hasten to prayer and speed to supplication, ever protecting, O Theotokos them that honor thee.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
The most wise youths bowed not down to the golden image, but into the flame went they of themselves; standing in the midst of the fire they sang; and an angel bedewed them, saying: Heard now is the prayer of your lips.[^2519]

*Troparion:*
We know Thee as Lord and Creator of every creature: therefore we pray, spare [us], O Compassionate One, and overlook our iniquities as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind; and from the present righteous wrath deliver Thy servants, and grant as Thou art good dew unto the thirsty earth.

*Troparion:*
As of old, O Master, Thou didst deliver Manasses repenting,[^2520] and from deadly afflictions didst rescue and save [him]: so also for us now, O Compassionate and Bountiful One, overlook all transgressions, and grant rain unto the earth, and mercy unto Thy servants, and preserve us from famine and affliction.

*Troparion:*
Have compassion upon us now as Thou art good and merciful; for we have sinned and committed iniquity exceedingly,[^2521] and that which is unrighteous before Thee have we all done; therefore we pray Thee with fear: punish not Thy people in Thine anger, but bestow rain unto the earth, O Compassionate One.

*Theotokion:*
Thee all we faithful have as an awe-inspiring refuge and unconquerable weapon, O Theotokos;[^2522] we Thy servants pray thee deliver us from every affliction, from famine and pestilence, and every distress; and send fruitful dew unto the earth, and unto thy servants, O Sovereign Lady.

*Another Heirmos:*
He Who saved in the fire the Abrahamic children...[^2523]

*Troparion:*
Thou art He Who saved in the sea and in perils, O Lover of mankind, and them that are held fast in sicknesses, a Helper in battles: therefore receive us also, and protect us by the prayer of Thy holy Mother.

*Troparion:*
Turn not away Thy face from us, O Merciful One, for we have provoked Thee to anger, not doing Thy commandment; therefore we pray Thee for the sake of the Theotokos: deliver Thy servants from every affliction and sorrow.

*Glory*[^2524] Let not the wrath of Thy rebuke consume us, O Christ, but show others instructed through us unworthy ones; water all the fruits with dew, gladdening our hearts.[^2525]

*Both now...*[^2526]

*Theotokion:*
Receive our contrite supplication, O Theotokos, seeing us sinking in passions; overcome our coarseness,[^2527] and be moved unto thy compassions, entreating the Savior and God for us.[^2528]

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Theotokion:*

*Troparion:*

*Glory...*[^2537]

*Troparion:*

*Both now...*[^2539]

*Theotokion:*
By thy compassions, O Mother of God, cover thy people who by passions are held fast; pray earnestly unto thy Son, for our transgressions cannot overcome thee; for thou loosest the bonds of sins, and healest infirmities, and deliverest from distresses them that faithfully cry aloud: "Praise the Lord and exalt Him above all unto the ages!"[^2540]

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*The Epistle: The Reading is from the Catholic Epistle of James:*[^2558]
Brethren: Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. Draw nigh to God, and He will draw nigh to you. Cleanse your hands, ye sinners, and purify your hearts, ye double-minded. Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep: let your laughter be turned into mourning, and your joy into sorrow. Humble yourselves before God, and He will exalt you. Speak not evil one of another, brethren; for he that speaks evil of a brother, or[^2559] judges his brother, speaks evil of the law, and judges the law; but if thou judge the law, thou art not a doer of the law, but a judge. One is the Lawgiver and Judge, Who is able to save and to destroy; but who art thou that judgest another?[^2560]

Hear now, ye that say: "Today and tomorrow we will go into this city, and spend a year there, and trade and make a profit"; whereas ye know not what shall happen tomorrow. For what is our life? For it is a vapor that appears for a little time, and then vanishes away; instead of your saying: "If the Lord wills,[^2561] and we shall live, and do this or that." But now ye boast in your proud boastings; all such boasting is evil. Therefore to him that knows to do good, and does it not, to him it is sin.[^2562]

Elijah was a man of like passions with us, and he prayed earnestly in prayer that it might not rain; and it rained not upon the earth for three years and six months. And he prayed again, and heaven gave rain, and the earth brought forth her fruit.[^2563]

Brethren, if any of you wander from the way of truth, and one turn him back, let him know that he who turns a sinner from the error of his way[^2564] shall save a soul from death, and shall cover a multitude of sins.[^2565]

*The Gospel according to Luke:*[^2568]
At that time, the multitudes marveled at the words of grace that proceeded out of the mouth of Jesus, and they said: "Is not this the son of Joseph?" And He said unto them: "Ye will surely say unto Me this proverb: 'Physician, heal Thyself! Whatsoever we have heard done in Capernaum, do also here in Thine own country.'" And He said: "Amen I say unto you: No prophet is accepted in his own country;[^2569]

...but of a truth I say unto you: Many widows were in Israel in the days of Elijah, when the heaven was shut up three years and six months, so that there was a great famine throughout all the land; and unto none of them was Elijah sent, save unto Sarepta of Sidon, unto a woman that was a widow. And many lepers were in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet; and none of them was cleansed, save Naaman the Syrian." And all in the synagogue, when they heard these things, were filled with wrath, and rose up, and thrust Him out of the city, and led Him unto the brow of the hill whereon their city was built, that[^2570] they might cast Him down headlong. But He, passing through the midst of them, went His way.[^2571]

The Lord said unto His disciples: "Have faith in God. *While we all bow our heads and knees, the priest says the prayers in order with all attentiveness and compunction of heart:*[^2603]

> **Deacon:** "Let us pray to the Lord."

> **Priest:** "Master, Lord our God, Who didst give ear unto Elijah the Prophet, Thy zealous servant, so as to send rain upon the earth in its season; Who likewise in the days of Elijah didst command that it should not rain, and again through his prayer didst bestow a fruitful shower:[^2604] do Thou Thyself, O Master of all, being entreated through Thine own tender compassion, send down bountiful rain upon Thine inheritance; and overlook our transgressions through Thy compassions; send Thy mercy upon those who have need and ask for mercy. Gladden the face of the earth for the sake of the poor among Thy people, for the sake of the infants and cattle, and of all who stand in need of mercy from Thee; for they trust in Thee, Who givest them food in due season.[^2605] For Thou art our God, a God of mercy and of salvation, O Lord our God; and unto Thee do we send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."[^2606]

*Second Prayer for the Same:*[^2607]

> **Deacon:** "Let us pray to the Lord."

> **Priest:** "O Lord Almighty, Who bringest up clouds from the ends of the earth, Who hast made lightnings for the rain, Who bringest forth the winds out of His treasuries; Who callest the waters of the sea[^2608] and pourest them out upon the face of all the earth: unto Thee do we pray, and Thee do we implore, and rich mercy do we ask of Thee.[^2609] Open the cataracts of heaven; command the clouds to rain down rain, and show compassion upon all Thy land, which Thou didst make with Thine all-pure hands, O Christ, the true Light. Have tender compassion upon the elders, and the youths, and the suckling infants at the breast; upon the male and female servants, and upon the beasts of the earth; that the fields may bring forth grass for the cattle, and green herb for the service of men, that the earth may yield her fruit.[^2610] We pray Thee, O King of hosts: receive the supplication of all Thy people; and rebuke us not in Thine indignation; slay not Thy people through famine and thirst. For the eyes of all look with hope unto Thee, and Thou givest them their food in due season; for in Thee do we put our trust, and beside Thee we know no other God; of Thee do we ask rich mercy.[^2611] For a good God and Lover of mankind Thou art, and unto Thee do we send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."[^2612]

*Third Prayer:*[^2613]

> **Deacon:** "Let us pray to the Lord."

> **Priest:** "Master, Lord our God, high and glorious Lover of mankind, Who renderest unto each man honor and glory according to his labors: make us partakers of Thine own Kingdom. Remember, O good Master, Thy servants who pray unto Thee, and receive our supplication, for Thy name is Lord, the Lover of mankind.[^2614] Even though we possess no good works, yet for the sake of Thy manifold tender compassion save us, O Lord, Lover of mankind; for Thou art our God, and we are Thy people, and the sheep of Thy pasture: the flock which in the beginning, when Thou didst become incarnate, Thou didst gather together, tearing us away from idolatry.[^2615] O Good Shepherd, Who didst lay down Thy life for the sheep, forsake us not; though we still wander until eventide, though we sin against Thee as newly bought servants in their first year unto a master of their own, abhor us not. Even though we are a little flock, cast us not away; but say unto us: "Fear not, My little flock, for it is your heavenly Father's good pleasure to give you the Kingdom."[^2616] O merciful Lord, good Lover of mankind, rich in mercy and compassions, Who hast promised to receive those who repent unto Thee, and Who awaitest the turning of sinners: remember not our manifold sins, but receive those who turn unto Thee, and blot out the handwriting of our stumbling blocks; appease the wrath whereby we have provoked Thee to anger, O Lover of mankind.[^2617] For Thou art the Lord and Master, and all things are Thine, and in Thee is the power either for us to live or to die. Appease Thy wrath, O Merciful One, of which we are worthy according to our deeds; turn aside the trial, for we are dust and ashes before Thee, O Lord.[^2618] Enter not into judgment with Thy servants, for we are Thy people; Thee do we seek, and before Thee do we fall down; unto Thee do we pray.[^2619] We have sinned before Thee, and have done evil; we have not kept nor observed as Thou didst command us; for being earthly, we have inclined unto earthly things, and have done wickedly before Thy face; turning away from Thy glory and love, we have rushed headlong into carnal lust, and we have enslaved ourselves to sin and to the cares of this life; and we have become runaways from our own Master, and, wretches that we are, destitute of good works by reason of our evil life. But we repent, and ask, and pray Thee, O merciful Lord, Lover of mankind, Who callest sinners to repentance, that Thou send Thy fear into our hearts.[^2620] We pray Thee, O Heavenly King, that at the dread judgment Thou wilt have mercy on us. Save and show compassion; look down and visit; have tender compassion and have mercy; for we are Thy servants, and the work of Thy hands.[^2621] If Thou shouldest mark our iniquities, O Lord, O Lord, who shall stand?[^2622] Or if Thou shouldest render unto each man according to his works, who then shall be saved? For with Thee is propitiation and mercy, and plenteous redemption.[^2623] Our soul is in Thy two hands, our breath is in Thy will; for as long as Thy gracious regard is upon us, we prosper; but if Thou lookest upon us with wrath, we perish like the morning dew.[^2624] For as dust stands not against the wind, so neither can we stand against Thy wrath; but as the creation, we ask mercy from Him Who created us.[^2625] Have mercy on us, O Lord, sinners that we are; have mercy on us according to Thy great mercy, and according to the multitude of Thy compassions blot out our iniquities; for all good comes from Thee upon us, whereas all that is unrighteous is from us toward Thee.[^2626] We have all turned aside together, and are become unprofitable; there is none among us that is diligent concerning heavenly things, but all look unto earthly things; and all of us are intent upon the cares of this life, having no care for the Kingdom of God, as though permanent upon the earth.[^2627] Not that Thou, O Lord, forsakest us, but rather that we seek Thee not, cleaving unto these visible things; therefore do we fear lest at some time Thou do unto us as Thou didst unto Jerusalem, when they had forsaken Thee and walked not in Thy ways; yet do not Thou unto us as unto them according to their deeds nor reward us according to our sins; but have patience with us yet, O long-suffering Lord; stay Thy wrathful flame that stretches forth against Thy servants.[^2628] Do Thou Thyself guide us into Thy truth, and teach us to do Thy will; for Thou art our God, and we are Thy people, Thy portion, Thine inheritance, and Thy creation.[^2629] We have not lifted up our hands unto a strange god, nor followed false prophets, nor do we hold heretical teaching; but Thee do we call upon, the true God; and unto Thee Who dwellest in heaven do we lift up our eyes[^2630] and unto Thee do we lift up our hands, and we pray Thee: forgive us, for Thou art good and the Lover of mankind; have mercy on us, Who callest sinners to repentance.[^2631] And at the dread judgment, separate us not from standing at Thy right hand, but make us worthy of the blessedness of the righteous.[^2632] As long as this world stands, bring not upon us tribulations and temptations, and deliver us not into the hands of aliens, lest Thy city be called a captive city, and Thy people be in a land not their own, lest the nations say: "Where is their God?"[^2633] Send not upon us tribulation, nor famine, nor civil strife, nor sudden death, nor from fire, nor deluge, lest they fall away from the faith, for they are not steadfast in the faith.[^2634] Chastise a little, but have mercy abundantly; wound a little, but heal mercifully; grieve a little, but gladden speedily; for our nature cannot endure to bear Thy prolonged wrath, even as stubble before the fire; but be Thou speedily moved to compassion, for Thine it is to show mercy and to save.[^2635] Therefore, O Master, extend Thy mercy upon Thy people: drive away enemies; establish peace; subdue barbarians; enrich the city with abundance; make our faithful sovereigns and princes formidable unto the nations; endow the boyars with wisdom; make the city mighty; make Thy Churches to grow; preserve Thine inheritance; save men, and women, and infants; deliver those that are in servitude, captivity, and exile; and have mercy on all those in prisons, in hunger, in thirst, and in nakedness; comfort them all, make them all glad; grant unto them joy both of soul and body; through the prayers of the all-pure Theotokos, and of the holy heavenly hosts; and of Thy Forerunner and Baptist John; and of the holy Apostles; and of the holy Prophets; and of the holy Martyrs; and of the holy venerable and God-bearing Fathers; and through the prayers of all the saints, be Thou moved to compassion upon us, that by Thy mercy being pastured in the unity of the faith, we may cheerfully and joyfully glorify Thee unto ages of ages. Amen."[^2636]
