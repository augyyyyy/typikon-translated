# PART XIV: THE ORDER PERFORMED WHEN SOMEONE WISHES TO CONFESS FROM THE BEGINNING [^544]

*First the spiritual father instructs him, saying thus:*[^545]

**Spiritual Father:** My child, who desirest to be renewed by honorable repentance,[^546] and to seek after thine original homeland, and to become a partaker of adoption as a son, from which thou hadst fallen away by transgression:[^547] hear, my child, how God awaits our repentance; through the Prophet He cries aloud unto us,[^548] **Spiritual Father:** saying: After all these things, turn ye unto Me, and I will heal you.[^549] Therefore whosoever dies without repentance, he is as one that is not a Christian; since he believes neither in Baptism nor in the Holy Trinity. And for such a one it is not fitting for the priest to vest himself, nor to bring an oblation to the church for him.[^550] Hear, my child, and understand well: God is one, Who made all creation, having from nowhere any beginning, but Himself being the beginning of all things; having within Himself the co-unoriginate Word, in His own image timelessly, endlessly, enthroned together with the Father and with the Holy Spirit: that is to say, Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit,[^551] **Spiritual Father:** three hypostases of like nature in one Godhead.[^552] And the Word of God, being God of God, for the sake of our salvation, having clothed His Godhead with flesh, was conceived without seed in the all-pure Virgin; He became doubly God and man together; Who willingly partook of our passions;[^553] having willingly received crucifixion and death; and He rose again the third day, and ascended into heaven, and sat at the right hand of the Father; and He shall come again to judge the living and the dead, and to render unto each according to his works.[^554] So hearing of that fearful hour to come, and of the terrible torments, we know the Scripture, and we are baptized into the Holy Trinity: in **Spiritual Father:** the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, washing away sins from ourselves. And those who have sinned after Baptism [wash them away] through repentance,[^555] confessing unto God, Who knows each by name and knows the thoughts of all; Who so commanded the church presbyters in His stead to bear your burdens, even as the Apostle said: For they watch over your souls.[^556]

*And after this he enters into the church with fear and humility, and with folded hands; and the priest says unto him:*[^557]

**Priest:** Bow down unto God, unto Whom thou hast fled for refuge.

*And he bows down thrice. And the priest speaks first, and he [repeats] afterward:*[^558]

**Penitent [repeating after Priest]:** O God, cleanse me a sinner, and have mercy on me. Lord, I have sinned, have mercy on me. Countless times have I sinned: have mercy on me, and forgive me.[^559]

*And after this the priest takes the Holy Gospel and the Cross, and places them upon the analogion before the sanctuary [altar];*[^560] *and he that desires to repent comes, and lays his hands and head upon the Gospel;*[^561] *and the priest says:*

**Priest:** Blessed is our God, [always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.][^562]

*Then the Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity... Our Father... For Thine is the kingdom... Lord, have mercy. (12 times) Come, let us worship... (Thrice)*

*Then Psalm 6: O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger... (see fol. 141)*[^563]

*Psalm 31 [LXX 31]:*

**Reader:** Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered. Blessed is the man unto whom the Lord imputes not sin, neither is there guile in his mouth.[^564] Because I kept silence, my bones grew old through my crying all the day long. For day and night Thy hand was heavy upon me: I was turned into distress when a thorn was fastened in me.[^565] Mine iniquity I acknowledged, and my sin I did not hide. I said: I will confess against myself mine iniquity unto the Lord; and Thou forgavest the ungodliness of my heart.[^566] For this shall every holy one pray unto Thee in a seasonable time; nevertheless, in a flood of many waters

**Reader:** they shall not draw nigh unto him. Thou art my refuge from the affliction that compasses me; O my joy, deliver me from them that have encircled me. I will instruct thee and guide thee in this way wherein thou shalt go; I will fix Mine eyes upon thee.[^567] Be ye not as the horse or as the mule, which have no understanding: whose jaws thou must muzzle with bit and bridle, lest they draw nigh unto thee. Many are the scourges of the sinner, but him that hopes on the Lord mercy shall encircle. Be glad in the Lord, and rejoice, ye righteous; and glory, all ye that are upright in heart.[^568]

*Psalm 50 [LXX 50]: Have mercy on me, O God... And I believe in one God*[^569] *Then these troparia: Tone 1:*

*Troparion:*

**Choir:** Hasten to open Fatherly embraces unto me: prodigally have I squandered my life; but looking upon the inexhaustible riches of Thy compassions, O Savior, despise not now my impoverished heart; for unto Thee, O Lord, in compunction I cry aloud: I have sinned, Father, against heaven and before Thee.[^570]

*Troparion:*

**Choir:** I have fallen among thieves, having been despoiled in mind by my thoughts; they have wounded my soul with sins, and I lie unhealed, O all-merciful One; but pour out Thy mercy through the prayers of the Forerunner, and heal the grievous wounds of my soul.[^571]

*Glory...*[^572]

*Troparion:*

**Choir:** Grant me tears, O God, as of old [unto the sinful woman...][^573]

**Choir:** [unto the] sinful woman; and vouchsafe me to wash Thy feet, which have delivered me from the deceit of hades; and to offer unto Thee fragrant myrrh: a pure life, acquired by me through repentance; that I also may hear Thy gracious voice: Thy faith has saved thee, go in peace.[^574]

*Both now...*[^575]

*Theotokion:*

**Choir:** I, O holy Virgin, Theotokos, flee under thy protection, knowing that through thee I shall find salvation; for thou art able, O all-pure one, to help me.[^576]

*This prayer the spiritual father says over him:*[^577]

**Spiritual Father:** O bountiful and merciful God, Who searchest the hearts and reins, and Who knowest the secrets of men;[^578]

**Spiritual Father:** there is no deed invisible before Thee, but all things are naked and revealed before Thine eyes;[^579] Who also knowest all things of mine: loathe me not, neither turn away Thy face from me;[^580] but overlook my transgressions at this hour, Who overlookest the transgressions of men that repent; and cleanse the defilements of my body and soul; and sanctify me wholly unto the end by this Thine invisible and spiritual right hand;[^581] lest, while promising release unto others, and dispensing it through Thine ineffable love for mankind, I myself, as an unapproved servant, should become guilty of sin.[^582] Yea, Master, Who alone art good and the Lover of mankind, let me not turn back[^583] **Spiritual Father:** humbled and put to shame. Send unto me power from Thy holy height: a word of wisdom, an understanding mind; grant me the grace of discernment of spirits;[^584] and strengthen me for the ministry lying before me, and of the most glorious heavenly mysteries; and form Christ within my members;[^585] and him that desires, Thy servant *[Name],*[^586] to be born of me, a wretched one, through confession and repentance, building him upon the foundation of the holy Apostles and Prophets:[^587] cast him not out, but plant him as a true plant in Thy holy, catholic, and apostolic Church;[^588] and preserve his faith unfeigned; cover him with Thy grace, and strengthen him,[^589] **Spiritual Father:** open his hearing, to hear well and meekly what I am about to speak unto him; direct his words unto truth; move his heart and soul to compunction; that in all truth, and faith, and love, he may expose his iniquity, as worthy of shame;[^590] forgive him all that he has evilly done; and strengthen him in purity and righteousness to serve Thee all the days of his life;[^591] that prospering, he may glorify Thine all-holy Name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.[^592]

*Then the penitent makes three prostrations to the ground, saying thus:*[^593]

**Penitent:** O God, be merciful unto me a sinner.[^594]

*Then having stretched forth his hands, he says thus:*[^595]

**Penitent:** I confess unto Thee, O Lord God of heaven and earth, all the secrets of my heart.[^596]

*The priest:*

**Priest:** Let us pray to the Lord.

*Prayer:*

**Priest:** O Lord, Master Who art eternal, Who hast made man according to Thine own image and likeness, and hast given him dominion of eternal life; and when he fell away through transgression, Thou didst not despise him, but through the Incarnation of Thy Christ didst fashion salvation for the world: do Thou Thyself, O Master, also deliver Thy servant *[Name]* from the bondage of the enemy,[^597] and guide him into the right path of Thy commandments; open his eyes to behold the light of Thy Gospel;[^598]

**Priest:** send unto his life an angel of light, delivering him from every assault of the enemy, from evil encounter, and from the noonday demon;[^599] and drive out from him every evil and unclean spirit hiding and nesting in his heart: a spirit of deceit, a spirit of malice, a spirit of idolatry, a spirit of falsehood and uncleanness, a spirit of fornication, and of all foul speaking wrought according to the instigation of the devil;[^600] and make him a reason-endowed sheep of the holy flock of Thy Christ, an honorable member of Thy Church, an honorable and sanctified vessel, a child of light, and an heir of Thy kingdom;[^601] that having lived according to Thy commandments,[^602]

**Priest:** and kept unharmed the seal of repentance, and preserved this robe of his soul undefiled, he may obtain the blessed dwelling with Thine elect in Thy kingdom.[^603] Through the grace and compassions and love for mankind of our Lord Jesus Christ, and Thine all-holy, and good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, [and unto ages of ages. Amen.][^604]

*Confession unto the Spiritual Father for Monks:*[^605]

**Monk:** Forgive me, holy father, whatsoever I have sinned from my youth even until now: in word, and in deed, and in thought;[^606] for indeed there is no sin and iniquity that I have not committed, and commit from my childhood; of which these are:[^607]

**Monk:** these: slanderings, condemnations;[^608] fornications, adulteries, incest, lusting after youths, bestiality, unnatural lusts, sodomy;[^609] uncleannesses, intemperance, defilements; hatred of mankind, remembering of wrongs, bitterness, anger, fury, destruction of souls;[^610] acts of disobedience, murmuring, contradiction, lying words;[^611] gluttony, drunkenness, vomiting, much speaking, idle talk;[^612] perjury, sacrilege, false humility, scandalizing one's neighbor, malice, vainglory, pride,[^613] **Monk:** arrogance, men-pleasing, hypocrisy; injuries, extortion, robbery, love of money;[^614] thefts, secret eating, fables, sorcery, witchcraft; evil speech, blasphemies in thoughts;[^615] reproaches, upbraidings, little faith; anathematizings of oneself and of others; parental, priestly, hierarchic, and hegumeneal curses;[^616] withholding forgiveness from the brethren, private possessions;[^617] and, to say it simply, every sin have I committed; always indeed following mine own will, and displaying virtues for the sake of human praise:[^618] these dishonorable[^619] **Monk:** passions have befallen me, and many others which I remember not.[^620] Therefore, more than all sinners have I become the most sinful, and more than all that have worked iniquity have I worked iniquity most lawlessly;[^621] and I desire to confess my sins, and to manifest and expose them; but I am ashamed and fearful lest I defile the ears of them that hear.[^622] Therefore indeed, out of all my evils, I have in part exposed certain iniquities, and set them forth here for manifestation; but those that are fiercer and more grievous than these, and unutterable, and most foul, have I concealed and kept silent; neither do I know whether I have remembered to speak all;[^623]

**Monk:** ...wretched [am I].[^624] And therefore I pray, first unto our Lord Jesus Christ, confessing that I may receive forgiveness;[^625] and thereafter also from you, spiritual fathers,[^626] to obtain absolution, who have received authority from God to loose and to bind the fallings and transgressions of men;[^627] that I may find the Lord our Jesus Christ merciful in the day of judgment.[^628] By reason of my vainglorious soul, I was a contentious rebel against my superiors,[^629] a gainsayer, and a disputer;[^630] and unto all my brethren a stumbling-block,[^631] disobedient, arrogant, reproachful, contradictory, and brawling;[^632]

**Monk:** by reason of abominable impudence and the lack of fear which I have, I have grieved and embittered men;[^633] and as I stood at prayer and singing, my mind wandered in crafty and worldly matters,[^634] and was occupied with defiled thoughts and recollections;[^635] and I nourished myself beyond measure,[^636] and spoke foolishly and rashly,[^637] and laughed unwisely;[^638] and I looked upon vain beauty, and was inflamed thereby;[^639] and mocked at things unbecoming;[^640] and grew negligent concerning my prayer, and fell into despondency;[^641] and displayed insolence, and spoke idle words, and committed all evil things.[^642] For all these things I ask of my Lord **Monk:** Jesus Christ to receive forgiveness, and from thee, my spiritual father.[^643]

*Absolution from the Spiritual Father:*[^644]

**Priest:** Forasmuch as Thou, O Master and Creator of all things, art the Savior of our souls, Who alone art by nature sinless and the Lover of mankind;[^645] Who desirest all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth;[^646] Who hast said: *After all these things, turn ye back unto Me;*[^647] Who through Nathan Thy prophet didst pardon David when he repented;[^648] and didst receive the prayer of Manasses made through repentance;[^649] Who didst grant unto Peter and unto the harlot the remission of sins by reason of their tears;[^650] and didst justify the publican who acknowledged his transgressions;[^651]

**Priest:** Who didst receive the prodigal son when he returned;[^652] Who camest not to call the righteous, but sinners unto repentance:[^653] do Thou Thyself now, O Master, look down with a merciful and gentle eye upon this Thy servant *[Name, saying it],*[^654] who falls down before Thy bountiful goodness, and confesses his sins;[^655] delivering him from every condemnation of sin and from the torment of Gehenna;[^656] Who hast commanded to forgive seventy times seven unto them that fall into transgressions;[^657] for even as is Thy greatness, so also is Thy mercy;[^658] Who didst entrust the keys of Thy kingdom

**Priest:** unto Thy supreme apostle Peter, and gavest him authority to bind and to loose in heaven and on earth;[^659] and having granted the same authority unto us, Thine unworthy servants: hear us also in this hour,[^660] and grant unto Thy servant the purification which he asks and prays to receive from Thee;[^661] for there is no man that shall live and not sin;[^662] and whatsoever he hath sinned as a man bearing flesh and dwelling in this world, and having been deceived by the devil:[^663] in word, in deed, in thought; in disobedience; in blasphemies proceeding from heretical instruction;[^664] from

**Priest:** evil and vain custom; from foolishness; from unreason; from contempt of Thy holy commandments;[^665] from a perturbed and contrary disposition; acting foully and performing impure works; every abominable deed and malice;[^666] and all impurities dwelling in his mind, and in his thoughts, and in his heart,[^667] from the age of childhood even unto his latest old age, committed foolishly by him: by the gaze of the eye, by hand, by foot, by tongue, and by lips; in the house, in the field, and in every place;[^668] voluntary and involuntary; by day and by night; and at every time:[^669]

**Priest:** in eating, and in drinking, and in conversations, and in answers; and in all his deeds, and in thoughts: from much subtlety and dark soaring of the mind, even unto his gross and grossest worldly sins;[^670] whether by word, or by oath, or by priestly or archiepiscopal interdict he fell at any time;[^671] or fell into his own anathema, or fell into this from others;[^672] or taking an oath transgressed it, voluntarily or involuntarily, through craftiness, and falsehood, and theft; or incited others unto the transgression of an oath, and unto

**Priest:** unseemly deeds, and joined himself unto the wicked;[^673] or judged judgment unrighteously for the sake of base gain, or otherwise, regarding persons, or through ignorance;[^674] or fell into soul-destroying deeds, or assisted therein for the sake of base gain, whether small or great;[^675] or made the final renunciation of the world and of all things that are in the world, many a time before Thine unhidden eyes, and holy men, and before many witnesses, by oath and by the interrogation of Thy holy Gospel, and again soon was found a transgressor;[^676] and

**Priest:** while the promise was yet in his mouth;[^677] or fell into any incestuous commingling with near kin;[^678] and likewise into unnatural passions of men, and into defilements of youth, and into lying with males, both committing these and suffering them;[^679] or fell into carnal union with beasts of every kind; and into other unnatural passions, through feeblemindedness and a will inclined unto evil;[^680] or set himself and strove toward the deposition of a hierarch or priest, and thereby transgressed these things;[^681] or strove unrighteously concerning the deposition of hierarchs or those who have renounced their vows, doing such things by himself and with others;[^682]

**Priest:** or with the whole heart and from the midst of the soul prayed, and promised, and bound himself by writing, fearing tribulations, and torments, and deadly necessity; or without necessity, by voluntary desire, did so many times and repeatedly:[^683] having promised to keep all Thy commandments from henceforth and unto ages, purely to please Thee in all deeds;[^684] whether in the monastic life, and the holy and Great Schema; or in ascetic withdrawal, and in deserts and mountains; or in the life of seclusion, and by fasts of many days, and by forsaking all pleasures;[^685]

**Priest:** [and their] abandonment;[^686] or was contrary unto all;[^687] or rather, while continually offering his supplication and prayer, continued [in evil]:[^688] priests and hierarchs, and patriarchs, and holy men, both living and departed, and those pleasing [Thee] in every manner and in bonds, slandering them guiltlessly, being high-minded through pride;[^689] and desiring to show himself greater than all, as one unhumble;[^690] or after the holy Anaphora,[^691] and after consecration,[^692] and after the holy oil, or after the dread and divine Communion, which he himself received, and others, did not preserve himself;[^693] but straightway fell into drunkenness and surfeiting, and into unclean

**Priest:** places vomited these things;[^694] and trampling upon them, or beholding another doing such things;[^695] or received Communion unworthily;[^696] and utterly disregarded all the spiritual commandments of his holy fathers, as one negligent, and despondent, and slothful unto every good work;[^697] and having defiled his strength, and mind, and desire;[^698] and showed himself to be a foolish elder, and defiled in mind; and in all things an ascetic of vainglory, abominable unto God;[^699] and worthy to burn eternally with the endless fire of Gehenna;[^700] and, to speak simply: every kind of fornication and adultery, and sins, and iniquities; and all impurities, and divers acts of self-abuse;[^701]

**Priest:** and impieties, and lack of faith, and all manner of heresies, which he hath committed voluntarily and involuntarily;[^702] or through rapacity, or through pettiness of mind, or through vanity; or being overcome by anger, or by love of pleasure, or by love of money; or as being ignorant, or as evilly ashamed:[^703] do Thou Thyself, O Lord, as a good God Who rememberest not evil, by Thy manifold and ineffable love for mankind, blot out and cleanse all these things from Thy servant;[^704] and make all other impiety and sin of his to be utterly non-existent;[^705] and whatsoever [hath been bound] by priestly word and injunction, and by the withholding of his spiritual fathers; and whatsoever things he hath sinned against them,[^706]

**Priest:** against hierarchs, priests, and against all clergy, and against holy and spiritual men: be Thou pleased that these be loosed, and that he be forgiven;[^707] and whatsoever things have been sinned by him in any way: have compassion, and grant healing unto his soul and body;[^708] [Thou Who art] slow to wrath, and swift unto love for mankind;[^709] for all things are revealed and naked before Thine eyes;[^710] for Thou didst not create man for perdition, but for the edification of both soul and body;[^711] and preserve him, that he may no longer follow after sins, but do Thy commandments, and be justified in Thy statutes.[^712] Yea, O Master

**Priest:** and Creator of all things, Who alone art without sin;[^713] rich in mercies, and good in compassions; Who desirest not the death of a sinner, but that he should turn and live:[^714] accept the repentance of Thy servant, and heal the contrition of his heart;[^715] and fill him with the grace of Thy Holy Spirit unto good works;[^716] that acceptably, and with a pure conscience, he may be accounted worthy of Thy divine, and sacred, and dread Mysteries, and of the eternal good things which Thou hast prepared for them that love Thee, O Lord.[^717] For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thine all-holy, and good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, [and unto ages of ages. Amen.][^718]

### Notes

[^544]: Fol. 123r. Heading and titular woodcut: *ЧИ́НЪ БЫВА́ЕМЫИ Е҆ГДА̀ КТО ХО́ЩЕТЪ ВЪ НАЧА́ЛѢ И҆СПОВѢ́ДАТИСѦ* (Greek: *Τάξις γινομένη ἐπὶ ἐξομολογουμένων*). The woodcut depicts the Crucifixion with the Mother of God and St. John the Apostle (*і҆ н ц і҆* / INRI). This service constitutes the foundational Ruthenian rite of initial or general life confession before the spiritual father.
[^545]: Fol. 123r. Preliminary pastoral exhortation of the spiritual father (*дꙋхо́вникъ* / Greek: *πνευματικός*): *прѣ́жде дꙋхо́вникъ поꙋчи́тъ є҆го̀ си́це гл҃ѧ* ("First the spiritual father instructs him, saying thus").
[^546]: Fol. 123r. The spiritual father addresses the penitent: *Ча́до моѐ хотѧ́щее ѡ҆бнови́тисѧ чтⷭны покая́нїемъ* ("My child, who desirest to be renewed by honorable repentance").
[^547]: Fol. 123r. Patristic theme of recovery of the heavenly fatherland and filial adoption (*възыска́ти пръваго ѻ҆тьчьства · и҆ сно́вства причасти́тисѧ* / Greek: *υἱοθεσία* / Romans 8:15, Galatians 4:5), forfeited through ancestral transgression (*престоупле́нїемъ*).
[^548]: Fol. 123r. Dominical patience awaiting the sinner's return: *ка́ко ти жде́тъ Бг҃ъ покая́нїя на́шего · прⷬко́мъ въпїе́тъ на́мъ* ("how God awaits our repentance; through the Prophet He cries aloud unto us"), citing the prophetic invitations to penance (Ezekiel 18:21–32, 33:11; Isaiah 1:16–19; Jeremiah 3:12–22), continuing onto Fol. 123v.
[^549]: Fol. 123v. Prophetic citation of divine call to repentance: *поси́хъ въсѣ́хъ ѡ҆брати́тесѧ къ мнѣ̀, и҆ а҆́зъ и҆сцѣлю̀ вы* ("After all these things, turn ye unto Me, and I will heal you" / cf. Jeremiah 3:7, 3:22; Hosea 14:4).
[^550]: Fol. 123v. Canonical warning regarding impenitence: dying without repentance deprives one of Christian burial and liturgical commemoration; the priest is forbidden to vest in sacerdotal robes (*ѡ҆блещи́сѧ*) or offer the liturgical oblation (*приноше́нїа* / Greek: *προσφορά*) for an unrepentant person.
[^551]: Fol. 123v. Exposition of orthodox Trinitarian catechesis given by the spiritual father to the penitent: the unoriginate oneness of God (*не и҆мы́й ни ѿкоу́доуже нача́ла* / Greek: *ἄναρχος*), the eternal Word (*сло́во събезнача́лное* / Greek: *συνάναρχος Λόγος*), and the consubstantial Trinity.
[^552]: Fol. 124r. Classical theological phrasing for the Holy Trinity: *трїе съста́вы съли́чны въ є҆диномъ бж҃твѣ̀* (Greek: *τρεῖς ὑποστάσεις συμφυεῖς ἐν μιᾷ θεότητι* — three hypostases of like nature in one Godhead).
[^553]: Fol. 124r. Christological confession of the Incarnation: the Word, being God of God, clothed His divinity with flesh (*пло́тїю ѡ҆бложи́въ своѐ Бж҃тво*), conceived without seed in the Virgin (*беⷭ сѣ́мени*), becoming truly God and man together (*соугꙋ́бо Бг҃ъ въскоу́пѣ и҆ чл҃къ* / Greek: *θεάνθρωπος*).
[^554]: Fol. 124r. Summary of the Paschal mystery and Eschatology: voluntary Passion and Crucifixion, Resurrection on the third day, Ascension, session at the right hand of the Father, and the Second Coming to judge the living and the dead according to their works (Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed; Romans 2:6; 2 Corinthians 5:10).
[^555]: Fol. 124v. Post-baptismal repentance (*по кр҃ще́нїи сътво́рше · покаѧ́нїемъ*): the Patristic understanding of confession as a "second baptism" of tears and repentance before the all-knowing God.
[^556]: Fol. 124v. Apostolic authority and pastoral burden of the priesthood: *цр҃ко́внымъ сщ҃е́нникѡмъ подъи́мати тѧ́готы ва́ша* (bearing burdens, cf. Galatians 6:2), citing Hebrews 13:17: *тї́и бо бдѧ́тъ ѡ҆ дꙋша́хъ ва́шихъ* ("for they watch over your souls").
[^557]: Fol. 124v. Ceremonial entrance of the penitent: *съгбѣ́ныма рꙋка́ма* ("with folded hands" or crossed upon the breast, expressing subjection and contrition).
[^558]: Fol. 124v. Priestly invitation to adore God (*поклони́сѧ Бг҃у*), the penitent making three bows (*г҃*), followed by dialogic responsive prayer (*по́пъ прѣ́жде, а҆ ѻ҆́нъ послѣди* — the priest reciting first, and the penitent repeating each petition after him).
[^559]: Fol. 125r. Responsive penitential versicles recited by the priest and repeated by the penitent: the Publican's prayer (*Боже, очисти мя грешнаго* / Luke 18:13), *Господи, согреших, помилуй мя*, and the supplication for pardon of countless sins (*без числа согреших*).
[^560]: Fol. 125r. Rubric for the disposition of the Holy Gospel and the Cross: the priest places them upon the analogion (*а҆нало́й* / Greek: *ἀναλόγιον*) before the sanctuary screen / iconostasis (*пред олтарем*).
[^561]: Fol. 125r. Bodily posture of the penitent during confession: laying hands and head directly upon the Book of the Gospels (*положи́тъ рꙋ́цѣ и҆ гла́воу на є҆ѵⷢлїи*), signifying direct accountability before Christ the Chief Shepherd.
[^562]: Fol. 125r. Opening doxology (*Благословен Бог наш...*) followed by the standard Trisagion Prayers through the Lord's Prayer, priestly ecphonesis, twelvefold *Kyrie eleison*, and threefold *Venite adoremus* (*Приидите поклонимся*).
[^563]: Fol. 125r. Incipit of the first Penitential Psalm, Psalm 6 (LXX 6: *Господи, да не яростию Твоею обличиши мене*), followed by the printer's rubrical folio cross-reference directing the reader to Fol. 141r (Page 297): *смотрѝ в листѣ̀ рм҃а*.
[^564]: Fol. 125v. Psalm 31 (LXX 31:1–2): *Блажени имже отпустишася беззакония...* ("Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered" / Romans 4:7–8).
