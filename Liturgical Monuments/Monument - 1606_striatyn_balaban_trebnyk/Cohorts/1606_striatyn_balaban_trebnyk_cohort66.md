*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Dost thou desire to be counted worthy of the angelic habit, and to be numbered among the choir of monastics?**

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, reverend father.**

*Priest:*  
**Priest:** **Truly a good and blessed work hast thou chosen, if thou also accomplish it; for good works are acquired with labor, and achieved through travail.**[^1816]

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Dost thou draw near unto the Lord of thine own free will?**

*Answer:*

*And again, the Catechesis:*[^1821]  
**Priest:** **Take heed, child, what manner of answers thou givest unto the Master Christ; for the Angels have come invisibly, writing down this thy confession, concerning which thou shalt be examined at the Second Coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.**[^1822]

=== LEAF p655 ===
**Priest (continued):** **I declare unto thee therefore the all-perfect life, wherein after the likeness of the Lord a manner of life is manifested, testifying what things thou oughtest to keep, and what things thou oughtest to flee. For renunciation is nothing else, according as it is said, than the promise of the Cross and of death. Know therefore that from this present day thou art to be crucified and to be dead unto the world, for the sake of the perfect renunciation. For thou dost renounce parents, brethren, wife, children, paternal kindred, companionship, customary friends, the turmoils that are in the world, cares, acquisitions, possessions, vainglorious and empty...**[^1823]

=== LEAF p656 ===
**Priest (continued):** **...pleasures also and glory; and thou must deny not only the things renounced, but also thine own soul, according to the voice of the Lord saying: Whosoever will come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow Me.**[^1824] **If therefore thou hast truly chosen to follow Him, and if thou desirest unfeignedly to be called His disciple, prepare thyself from this present time, not for ease, nor for freedom from care, nor for delicacies, nor for any other thing that is beautiful and sweet upon the earth; but for spiritual struggles, for the continence of the flesh, for the purification of the soul, for poverty...**[^1825]

=== LEAF p657 ===
**Priest (continued):** **...spiritual; for blessed weeping, for all sorrowful and painful things of the joy-producing life according to God. For thou shalt hunger and thirst, and endure nakedness, and receive insult, mockery, reproach, and persecution, and fall into many other temptations, whereby the life of those who live according to God is depicted. And when thou shalt suffer all these things: Rejoice, He said, for great is your reward in heaven.**[^1826] **With joy therefore rejoice, and with gladness be glad, that today the Lord God hath chosen thee and separated thee from the worldly life, and set thee as before His face, in the standing of the monk's...**[^1827]

=== LEAF p658 ===
**Priest (continued):** **...rank, in the warfare of the angel-like life, in the height of the heavenly imitative citizenship: to serve Him after the manner of angels, and to labor wholly for Him; to mind high things, to seek the things that are above; for our citizenship, according to the Apostle, is in heaven.**[^1828] **Oh, the new calling! Oh, the gift of the mystery! A second baptism dost thou receive today, brother, by the rich gifts of God Who loves mankind; and thou art cleansed from thy sins, and becomest a son of light. And Christ our God Himself rejoices with His holy angels over thy repentance, slaying for thee the fatted calf.**[^1829] **Walk therefore worthily of thy calling...**[^1830]

=== LEAF p659 ===
**Priest (continued):** **...turn aside from empty attachments. Detest the lust that drags thee down to low things; transfer all thy desire unto heavenly things. By no means turn back, lest thou become a pillar of salt, like Lot’s wife,**[^1831] **or like a dog returning to his own vomit;**[^1832] **and there be fulfilled in thee the word of the Lord, that no man having put his hand to the plow and turning back is fit for the Kingdom of Heaven.**[^1833] **For it is no small peril unto thee, having now promised to keep all the aforesaid things, afterward to be negligent concerning the vows, or to return to the former life, or to separate thyself from thy father...**[^1834]

=== LEAF p660 ===
**Priest (continued):** **...and from the brethren striving together with thee, or walking in vain to spend thy days in carelessness. For thou shalt find heavier condemnation than former sinners at the dread and unbribable judgment seat of Christ, in as much as thou hast now tasted of greater grace; and it had been better for thee, according to the word, not to vow, than having vowed not to pay.**[^1835] **Neither think again that the past time of thy sojourning here hath been sufficient in struggling against the invisible hostile powers; but know that rather from henceforth still greater struggles against the adversary shall take hold of thee, [yet] he shall in no wise prevail against thee...**[^1836]
