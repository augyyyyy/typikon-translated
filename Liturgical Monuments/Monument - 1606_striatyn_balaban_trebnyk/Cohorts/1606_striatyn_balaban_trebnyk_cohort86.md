As [Thou art] Lady Theotokos,[^2438] who beyond nature and word didst give birth unto the only-begotten God the Word,[^2439] the Creator and Master of every visible and intelligible creation, one Lord of the Trinity, both God and Man;[^2440] the divine receptacle of every holiness and receiver of grace, in Whom dwells the fullness of the Godhead bodily,[^2441] by the good pleasure of God the Father and the cooperation of the Holy Spirit;[^2442] who wast chosen out of all creation, and by Godlike dignity ineffably surpassest...

...and transcendest [all things]: the glory and food and unspeakable gladness of the angels, [the joy] of the Church, the crown of the apostles and prophets, the marvelous and all-lauded courage of the martyrs, who art the cause of their contest and victories, and the all-pure mediatress of their everlasting crowns of suffering and of their Godlike recompense; the honor and glory of the venerable ascetics; who art the unerring teacher of the way of stillness;[^2443] the door of revelations of spiritual mysteries; O fountain of light; the inexhaustible river of mercy; the unfathomable sea of miracles of all Godlike gifts:[^2444] thee do we entreat, and unto thee do we make supplication...
