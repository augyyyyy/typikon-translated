*Of the Holy Fathers:*

And if children of godparents unite carnally, let them not enter into marriage, nor be crowned; but if they will [if they nevertheless took each other]: let them be separated; let them repent for five years, [performing] thirty-six prostrations.[^4118]

Likewise also if the children of spiritual brothers defile themselves: let them also be treated in the same manner.[^4119]

If two spiritual brothers quarrel or strike one another: let them be separated for sixty days, [performing] twelve prostrations each day.[^4120] And if they forgive one another again and love each other: well and good; but if not, let them be separated from the church continually, since they have proved liars unto Christ and have grieved the Spirit of love.[^4121]

A spiritual brother is he who makes brotherhood upon the Holy Gospel.[^4122]

=== LEAF p1340 ===

*Injunctions:*

If any persons whatsoever quarrel: and one of them, taking the initiative, seeks forgiveness with a prostration from the other: he receives a twofold reward from God beyond the other, if his repentance is with love.[^4123]

And he who does not desire to seek forgiveness from his friend makes himself an enemy to his own soul; let such a one be separated from the church, and let the priest receive no prosphora whatsoever from him.[^4124]

And if both do not desire to seek forgiveness with love from the whole heart: then let both be separated from the church.[^4125]

Likewise also those who, concerning any great or small matter, are agitated and hate one another; or if without cause they quarrel, and keeping silence hate one another: let such persons amend themselves[^4126]
