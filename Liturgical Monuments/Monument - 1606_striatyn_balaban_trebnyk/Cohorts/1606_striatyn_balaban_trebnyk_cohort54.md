*After this takes place the Last Kiss:*[^1549]

*And we sing the following Stichera. Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Е҆гда̀ ѿ дрѣ́ва тѧ̀):*[^1550]

*Sticheron 1:* Come, brethren, let us give the last kiss unto the deceased, praising God; for behold, he has departed from his kindred and hastens unto the grave, no longer caring for vain things and the much-toiling flesh. Where are now kinsfolk and friends? Behold, we are parted! For him who has departed, let us pray unto the Lord that He may give him rest.[^1551]

*Sticheron 2:* What parting is this, O brethren! What weeping, what lamentation in the present...

=== LEAF p538 ===
...hour! Come therefore, kiss him who a little while ago was with us. For he is delivered unto the grave; he is covered with stones; he dwells in darkness; he is buried with the dead. But all we who are kinsfolk and friends are now parted! For him who has departed, let us pray unto the Lord that He may give him rest.[^1552]

*Sticheron 3:* Now is all the deceitful triumph of vain life dissolved; for the spirit has departed from its tabernacle; the clay has blackened; the vessel is shattered: speechless, senseless, dead, motionless! Escorting the departed unto the grave, let us pray unto the Lord to give him rest unto ages.[^1553]

=== LEAF p539 ===
*Sticheron 4:* What is our life? Truly a flower, and a vapor, and morning dew! Come therefore, let us look clearly into the graves: where is bodily beauty? Where is youth? Where are the eyes and the form of the flesh? All have withered like grass, all have vanished! Come, let us fall down before Christ with tears.[^1554]

*Sticheron 5:* Great is the weeping and lamentation, great is the groaning and distress, the parting of the soul! Hades and destruction is the life of temporal things; an unsubstantial shadow, a deceitful dream, unseasonably imagining, labor unto earthly life! Far off let us flee from the world and from every sin, that we may inherit the heavens.[^1555]

=== LEAF p540 ===
*Sticheron 6:* Beholding him lying dead before us, let us take the image of the final hour; for behold, he departs like smoke from the earth, he has withered like a flower, he has been mown down like grass, wrapped in grave-cloths, covered with earth. Leaving the departed unseen, let us pray unto Christ to give him rest unto ages.[^1556]

*Sticheron 7:* Come, ye that are of the race of Adam, let us behold him who is laid in the earth, who is according to our likeness: he has put off all comeliness, dissolved in the grave, wasted away by the stench of worms in darkness, covered with earth! Leaving the departed unseen, let us pray unto Christ to give him rest unto ages.[^1557]
