*Troparion:*
Behold, all my days have vanished, truly in vanity, as it is written, and my years with haste; and the bitter snares of death have overtaken my soul, which hold me fast.[^1385]

*Troparion:*
For fleeting temporal enjoyment, I am about to be tormented endlessly, O Sovereign Lady, and for a little carnal pleasure, to fall into the fire; but may thy mercy overtake me![^1386]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*
Surrounding me from every side they advance, holding me fast everywhere; and my soul shrinks back and is terrified, filled with great turmoil; comfort it, O Pure One, by thine appearing!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*
I have found no one who sorrows with me in my affliction,...[^1387]

=== LEAF p462 ===
...nor any who comforts me, O Sovereign Lady; for my friends and acquaintances have together forsaken me now; but, O my hope, in no wise do thou forsake me!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Out of the flame for the godly...*[^1388]

*Troparion:*
Sigh over me in my suffering, all ye ends of the earth, and weep together; for from on high the sentence hath come: having bound my hands and feet, to cast me out![^1389]

*Troparion:*
Having erred beyond all men, unto thee, O guardian of my wretched soul, do I cry out, sighing bitterly: behold the eternal affliction of my bonds, and cease not to pray for me!

=== LEAF p463 ===
*Troparion:*
Why did I come forth from the womb of my mother, being born unto passions?[^1390] How did I not perish immediately, being destined to be tormented in the eternal fire? Alas! Who will weep for me, or who will remember me?

*Troparion:*
Since I was delivered unto demons, and was brought down by force unto the bottom of hades, I know that all have forgotten me; but do thou remember me, O angel of the Lord!

*Theotokion:*
O Mother of my Christ, how hast thou forgotten me? How hast thou not inclined unto mercy Him Who poured forth blood from His ribs, that He might save those whom He created?[^1391] Alas! How hath the mercy of my Christ been shut up?

*Heirmos; then:*

*Troparion:*
As the philanthropic Mother of God the Lover of mankind, look with a gentle and merciful eye...

=== LEAF p464 ===
...when my soul departs from the body, that I may glorify thee, O holy Theotokos!

*Troparion:*
Vouchsafe me to flee the regiments of the bodiless barbarians, and to soar above the aerial chasms, and to ascend unto heaven, that I may glorify thee, O holy Theotokos!

*Troparion:*
O thou who didst bear the Lord Almighty, drive far away from me the bitter chief toll-gatherers of the ruler of this world, when I am about to pass away, that I may glorify thee, O holy Theotokos![^1392]

*Troparion:*
When the great, last trumpet sounds terribly and dreadfully, and the sinners arise unto the resurrection of judgment, remember me then, O holy Theotokos!

*Glory...*

=== LEAF p465 ===
*Troparion:*
Before all creation, both visible and invisible, when I stand before Christ trembling, naked, and shaken with fear: remember me then, O holy Theotokos!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*
O exalted chamber of Christ the Master, having sent down thy grace from on high, overtake me now in the day of distress, that I may glorify thee, O holy Theotokos!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: It is not possible for men to see God...*[^1393]

*Troparion:*
No longer shall I remember, for in hades there is no remembering the Lord; but sitting covered in grievous darkness, awaiting the resurrection of all earthly beings, that being condemned I may be cast into the fire.

*Troparion:*
No one any longer will remember me, neither the multitude of angels, nor the assembly of archangels,...

=== LEAF p466 ===
...nor the choir of prophets and apostles, nor the multitude of martyrs, and of all the righteous; for alone shall I draw out the punishment for the evil deeds which I did while living.[^1394]

*Troparion:*
Weep over me, lament for me now, O bottom of hades, and abyss, darkness, and Tartarus! And groan over me, all ye torments! For I alone, a prodigal from among Christians, have been delivered unto you to be tormented bitterly!

*Theotokion:*
O Mother of Christ, look into the abyss; behold the soul delivered unto torments to be tortured bitterly; and bending thy knees, shed tears, that He may be inclined by thy prayers, Who gave His blood for me, and may lead me up!

=== LEAF p467 ===
*Heirmos; then:*

*Troparion:*
Behold, the bitter sentence hath gone forth; behold, the barren fig tree, as unprofitable, is cut down; behold, the withered branch is cast into the fire; now the unprofitable servant is sent away into outer darkness, bound hand and foot piteously![^1395]

*Troparion:*
O how shall I gaze upon the Invisible One? How shall I endure that dreadful vision? How shall I dare to open mine eyes? How shall I venture to look upon my Master, Whom from my childhood I have not ceased constantly to provoke to wrath?

*Troparion:*
O holy Maiden, Mother of God, look mercifully upon my lowliness; receiving this my piteous and last supplication...

=== LEAF p468 ===
...offered unto thee with closed lips, and having received it, mercifully fulfill it!

*Troparion:*
Having defiled the holy temples, leaving behind the defiled temple of the body, I pray thee, the all-holy temple of God, O Maiden Virgin Mother: that my soul may escape the outer darkness and the fierce burning of Gehenna![^1396]

*Troparion:*
Grant the streams of my tears to be for the cleansing of my sins, O Good One, receiving the contrition of my heart as I trust in thee, the Hope of the good: that thou mayest deliver me without cost from dreadful torment, as the source of goodness itself, O Mother of God!

*Glory...*

=== LEAF p469 ===
*Troparion:*
Beholding the end of my life draw near, and contemplating how my soul has been the worker of unseemly thoughts and deeds, O all-pure one, I am fiercely wounded by the arrows of conscience; but being mercifully inclined, be thou my protectress!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*
A Son was given unto us for the sake of mercy, the Son of God and eternal King of angels, having come forth as man from thy pure blood; make Him merciful, O Maiden, unto my wretched soul fiercely torn away from my wretched body![^1397]
