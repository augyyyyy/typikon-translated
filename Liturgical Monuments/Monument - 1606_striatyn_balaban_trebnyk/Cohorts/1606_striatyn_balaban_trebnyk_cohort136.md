*Of the Holy Fathers:*[fol. 668r][^4190]

Midwives serving at births: we command them to approach Holy Communion by the seventh day, since they perform a truly necessary service.[^4191] But other women who are without work and without service, coming unto this at that hour of birth: let such not be received into church until the twelfth day, and others until the fifteenth day, and others until the twentieth day, according to the work they have served; for if they do not call any woman unto this for the sake of some service, let her not go.

And if a woman gives birth on Holy Pascha: then until the seventh day let her wash herself with water, and let her drink holy water [*agiasma*].[^4192]

And it is fitting for a wife and a husband to have two beds in their house, and to lie apart on Sundays, and on holy feasts of the Lord, and during the Great Week.[^4211] So that if at times someone is unable to abstain for God's sake, at least on these days it is fitting to abstain, and not always to wallow like a swine in the mire. For from such incontinent passions is born an evil and wicked seed; and from such passions comes fearlessness [of God], neither does the remembrance of death ever enter into the mind of a man.[^4212]
