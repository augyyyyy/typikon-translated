*Of the Holy Fathers:*[fol. 673r][^4225]

If a woman giving birth smothers her child: let her repent until death, eating dry food on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday; a thousand prostrations.[^4226]

If any woman drinks a potion that she may not conceive a child: seven years; two hundred prostrations.[^4227]

If a woman, having conceived, drinks any potion for corruption, so that she may cast out that which is conceived in her: eight years; three hundred and sixty-seven prostrations. But if it happens that she dies, let her not be buried.[^4228]

A woman who casts spells upon strangers and her own [family]: nine years; five hundred prostrations.[^4229]

A woman who is a poisoner: let her repent until the end of her life; ten thousand prostrations.[^4230]

*Injunctions:*[fol. 673v][^4231]

If a sorceress repents: nine years; five hundred prostrations.

A *mora* [nightmare-weaver]: five years; three hundred prostrations.[^4232]

A *mavlistra* [enchantress]: seven years; four hundred prostrations.[^4233]

If a woman speaks an evil word unto a priest: let her be cast out from the church for three years.[^4234]

If a woman takes up wood against a priest or a levite, and strikes him: let her repent for six years; three hundred prostrations.[^4235]

If a woman commands a priest concerning his Liturgy, or concerning the hours appointed for him, even if he is very young and that woman is wise; but if by authority she commands the priest: either let her cease, or let her be excommunicated; and if she repents, then let her repent for six months; a thousand prostrations.[^4236]

Likewise also a man, both a ruler and one who is in authority: in the same manner let him be forbidden.

Forasmuch, therefore, as from the beginning God created man and woman, that He might raise up the world and His own possession; He gives unto us also to behold a certain custom: so that from that which is seen each month by them, making manifest conception unto that which comes to be; lest, as some say, that a woman experiences childbirth from the man, but from the woman there is nothing. It is made manifest by the things that come to be, that blood is taken from the woman, which is called consanguinity; and flesh from the man, whereby we are called carnal.[^4238]

*Concerning Priests:*[fol. 676r][^4249]

He that greatly multiplies his silver: let him be barred from the Liturgy; forasmuch as he is mixed with falsehood and extortion; and better is he that trades and fornicates. But if he desists from such, let him again minister as priest; and if not, then let him be deposed from the priesthood.

A priest, or deacon, or hegumen who collects tolls: either let him cease from such, or let him be deposed.[^4250]

A priest who sings like a goat and plays: either let him cease from such things, or let him be deposed.[^4251]

If a priest falls into fornication, or masturbation, or with a beast: let such a one be barred from the Liturgy.[^4252] And if anyone be found in these [sins] while not yet[^4253]

=== LEAF p1368 ===

*Injunctions:*[fol. 676v][^4254]

...being a priest: let such a one not come unto the priesthood.

A priest who lies, or tells fables, or slanders: either let him desist from such things, or let him be deposed.[^4255]

A priest who seeks to perform any worldly services whatsoever, whether civic or princely, and to walk in them, and to attend the assembly of common people or of princes, except only if some necessity be upon him: let such a one either desist from such things, or let him be deposed.[^4256]

If a priest in his priesthood either becomes blind, or becomes lame, or a demonic disease comes upon him: let him desist from the Divine Liturgy, but let him perform the other prayers.[^4257]

*Concerning Priests:*[fol. 677r][^4258]

Likewise also if anyone has any of these blemishes: either blind, scarcely seeing; or lame; or having a withered hand; or leprous; or deaf; or the son of a thrice-married man; or the offspring of incest: of all such, let none dare to approach unto the priesthood. And if a bishop for the sake of reward ordains such a priest, let both be deposed, both that bishop and the priest.

A priest celebrating the sacred mysteries: let him guard himself from conversations with women, that he may preserve the treasure of the soul unstolen; and let him not boast in his virginity, lest he lose that which he has.[^4259]

If the wife of a priest commits adultery, and he knows it not: then let that priest serve.[^4260]

*Injunctions:*[fol. 677v][^4261]

But if someone tells him concerning her: let him cease serving, and let him investigate whether it is true concerning her. And if he sees with his own eyes, then let him put her away; and let him abide in patient endurance, serving again.

But if, having put her away, he falls again with another woman: let him desist from the Liturgy, and let him receive communion unhindered.[^4262]

But if he retains that [first adulterous] woman, he has the same prohibition; and at his death let him be ministered unto as other priests in sacred dignity.[^4263]

But if it happens that his wife dies before that, and if he touches not another, then let him minister as a priest without prohibition.[^4264]
