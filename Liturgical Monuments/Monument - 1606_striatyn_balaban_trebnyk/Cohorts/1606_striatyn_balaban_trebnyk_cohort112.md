*Prayer at the Construction of a Ship, or a Boat:*[^3182]

**Priest:** **"O Lord our God, God of our fathers, Who didst command Noah Thy servant to construct an ark[^3183] for the salvation of the world; Who out of many timbers didst deign it to become a single vessel of wood, and to be joined together: do Thou Thyself, O Master of all, Who didst deign to be piloted by inanimate timbers, and Who by Thy strong hand dost save the human race: do Thou Thyself also now, O Master, preserve this ship; grant unto it a good, peaceful Angel; and protect those who are to sail in it; grant unto them to return unto their own homes in good health; through the prayers...** (continues onto leaf p1112)

=== LEAF p1112 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...of our most-pure Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary; by the power of the precious and life-creating Cross; of the precious, heavenly, noetic, bodiless Powers; of the holy, glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John; of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles; of Saint [Name]; and of all Thy Saints.**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For Thou art He that pilots and sanctifies all things, O Christ our God; and unto Thee do we send up glory, thanksgiving, and worship, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thy most-holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."**

*Prayer over Salt:*[^3184]

**Priest:** **"O Lord our Savior, Who didst come unto Jericho in the days of the Prophet Elisha, and didst heal the harmful waters with salt:[^3185] do Thou Thyself bless this salt, and transform it into a sacrifice of joy;[^3186] for Thou art our God, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. [Amen]."**

*Prayer over a Woman when She Has Miscarried:*[^3187]

*The priest makes the beginning: Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. Our Father. For Thine is the kingdom. Troparion of the day. Then the prayer: Let us pray to the Lord.*[^3188]

**Priest:** **"O Master, Lord our God, Who wast born of the holy Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary, and didst recline as an Infant in a manger:[^3189] do Thou Thyself [look upon] this Thy servant [Name]...** (continues onto leaf p1114)

=== LEAF p1114 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...who today through sins hath fallen into voluntary [or involuntary] murder,[^3190] and hath cast forth that which was conceived within her: have mercy according to Thy great mercy, and forgive her her voluntary and involuntary transgressions; and preserve her from every craft of the devil; and cleanse her defilement; heal her sicknesses; grant health and vigor unto her body, together with her soul, O Lover of mankind; protect her with a radiant Angel from every assault of invisible demons; yea, O Lord, [heal her] of disease and plague, and cleanse her from bodily defilements, and the other disturbances of the womb that come upon her; bring her down by Thy humble compassion in this humble...** (continues onto leaf p1115)

=== LEAF p1115 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...body of hers; and raise her up from the bed whereon she is laid low:[^3191] for in sins were we born and in iniquities, and in defilement are we all before Thee, O Lord;[^3192] and with fear we cry out and say: look down from heaven, and behold the sickness of us who are condemned; and forgive this Thy servant [Name], who through sins hath fallen into murder, having whether by will or against her will cast forth that which was conceived within her; and have mercy and forgive all those who are present and who have touched her,[^3193] according to Thy great mercy, as a good God and the Lover of mankind; for Thou alone hast authority to remit sins and iniquities; through the prayers of Thy most-pure Mother,...** (continues onto leaf p1116)

**Priest (continued):** **"...and of all Saints.**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship, with the Father, and the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. [Amen]."**

*Prayer, Said before Prayer, and over Those Who Have Been Tempted in Sleep:*[^3194]

**Priest:** **"O Many-Hymned Lord, Incorruptible, Undefiled, Alone Sinless:[^3195] cleanse me Thy servant from every defilement of flesh and spirit;[^3196] and show me forth undefiled by the grace of Thy Christ; and sanctify me by the descent of Thy Holy Spirit; that, having awakened from the mist of the unclean imaginations of the devil, and from every temptation, I may be counted worthy at an opportune time to draw near and...** (continues onto leaf p1117)

=== LEAF p1117 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...partake of Thy dread and fearful Mysteries.[^3197]**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For Thou art He that blesses and sanctifies all things, O Christ our God; and unto Thee do we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thy most-holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, [and unto ages of ages. Amen]."**

*Prayer over the Emission of Lust:*[^3198]

**Priest:** **"[O Lord God] of hosts, Who healest every disease and every sickness among the people:[^3199] be merciful unto Thy servants, and grant not unto man communion with the demon;[^3200] rebuke the unclean spirits; crush Satan under the feet of Thy servants;[^3201] renew them by Thy Holy Spirit; unite them unto Thy holy Church; and having delivered them from every working...** (continues onto leaf p1118)

=== LEAF p1118 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...of the adversary, present them before Thy holy altar; and count them worthy of Thy heavenly, life-creating Mysteries.[^3202]**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For Thou art our God, the God of those that repent; and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, [and unto ages of ages. Amen]."**

*Second Prayer, unto the Same Purpose:*[^3203]

**Priest:** **"O Lord God of righteousness, my strength and help, and my refuge in the day of evils:[^3204] Thou savest in the time of affliction; save me, for the waters are come in even unto my soul; I am stuck fast in the mire of the deep, and there is no standing before the face of the enemy that doth violence;[^3205] for he hath persecuted my soul, and smitten my life down unto the earth;[^3206] fighting all night long he hath afflicted me;...** (continues onto leaf p1119)

=== LEAF p1119 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...fighting all night long he hath afflicted me;[^3207] deign, O Lord, to deliver me from the hand of the transgressor of the law and the wrongdoer, that I may not sin against Thee; for the fear of death is fallen upon me, and darkness hath covered me;[^3208] how long shall I cry, O Lord, and Thou wilt not hear? I will cry out unto Thee because of wrong, and Thou wilt not save;[^3209] look down from heaven, O Lord, and behold from the house of Thy sanctuary, and of Thy glory: where is the multitude of Thy compassions according to Thy mercy?[^3210] For it hath been withheld from us; O Lord, judge those who wrong me; be not like a sleeping man, and like a warrior that is unable to save.[^3211]**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For Thou art a merciful God and the Lover of mankind; and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages [of ages. Amen]."**
