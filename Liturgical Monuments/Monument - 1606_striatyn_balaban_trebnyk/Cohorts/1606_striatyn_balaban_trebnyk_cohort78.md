*When the priest says the prayer behind the ambo, all go out to the baptistery, the priest going before with tapers and censer, while we sing the following three troparia, Tone 8:*[^2204]

*Tone 8:*
The voice of the Lord cries upon the waters, saying: Come, receive all of you, the Spirit of wisdom, the Spirit of understanding, the Spirit of...[^2205]

...the fear of God, of Christ Who has appeared.[^2206]

*Say it thrice. Then the other troparia, in the same Tone, twice each.*[^2207]

*Tone 8:*
Today the nature of waters is sanctified, and Jordan is parted, and turns back the currents of its waters, beholding the Master being baptized.[^2208]

*Tone 8:*
As a man Thou didst come to the river, O Christ King, and Thou hastest to receive a servant's baptism, O Good One, at the hands of the Forerunner, for the sake of our sins, O Lover of mankind.[^2209]

*Glory..., Now and ever..., Tone 8:*[^2210]

*Tone 8:*
At the voice of him that cries in the wilderness: "Prepare the way of the Lord," Thou didst come, O Lord, having taken the form of a servant, asking for baptism, though knowing not sin. The waters saw Thee and were afraid; trembling...[^2211]

**Deacon:** Wisdom!

*Then the paremias. The reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah.*

Thus says the Lord: Rejoice, O thirsty desert; let the wilderness exult, and blossom as a lily. And it shall blossom and rejoice; the desert of Jordan shall exult, and the glory of Lebanon has been given unto it, and the honor of Carmel; and My people shall see the glory of the Lord, and the majesty of God.[^2214]

Be strong, ye slackened hands, and be comforted, ye feeble knees! Say unto them that are faint-hearted in mind: Be strong, and fear not; for behold, our God will render judgment; He will come and save us. Then shall the eyes of the blind be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall hear. Then shall the lame man leap as a hart, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be clear; for water has broken forth in the wilderness, and a ravine in a thirsty land. And the parched land shall become a pool, and in the thirsty land there shall be a spring of water; there shall be the habitation of birds, and the resting-place of sirens...[^2215]
