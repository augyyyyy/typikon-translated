As sitting upon Thy dread and unbribable throne, O Christ God, guilty of condemnation and subject to examination, and giving an account of the evils wrought by me:[^1138] even so today, before the day of my condemnation is come, I stand before Thee, and before Thy dread and holy angels, bowed down by my conscience, bringing forward my evil, polluted, and lawless deeds;[^1139] exposing and reproving them, I humble myself. Look therefore, O Lord, upon my humility, and remit all my transgressions; look, for my iniquities are multiplied more than the hairs of my head.[^1140]

=== LEAF p382 ===
What evil therefore have I not committed? What transgression have I not wrought? What evil have I not imagined in my soul? Nay, already in deeds also have I committed them:[^1141] fornication, adultery, sodomy, presumption, pride, reproach, debauchery,[^1142] insolence, disobedience, slander, falsehood, evil-speaking, belittling, perjury, calumny, swearing of oaths, blasphemy, idle talking, inflamed passion, mockery, drunkenness, gluttony, belly-madness, hatred, envy, love of money, avarice, usury, vainglory, covetousness of possessions, self-love, extortion, unrighteousness, filthy lucre,[^1143]

=== LEAF p383 ===
greed of gain, jealousy, defaming, lawlessness, disobedience, polluted thoughts, and unclean and enervated imaginings, blasphemous also and unspeakable; all my senses and every member have I defiled, corrupted, mired in filth, and made unprofitable;[^1144] I have become in every way a worker for the devil. I know, O Lord, that mine iniquities are gone over my head;[^1145] but measureless is the multitude of Thy compassions, and ineffable the mercy of Thine ungrudging goodness, and there is no sin that can overcome Thy love for mankind.[^1146] Therefore, O most marvelous King, unresentful Lord: make marvelous Thy mercies upon me also, a sinner;[^1147]

show forth the power of Thy goodness, and manifest the strength of the tender-hearted compassion of Thy mercy, and receive me, a sinner returning to Thee. Receive me, even as Thou didst receive the prodigal, the publican, the thief, Manasseh, and the harlot;[^1148] receive me who have transgressed beyond measure, in word, and in deed, and in thought, and by improper lust, and by unreasoning desire; and even as Thou didst receive those that came at the eleventh hour, who had wrought nothing worthy:[^1149] so receive me also, a sinner. For greatly have I sinned, and greatly have I committed iniquity, and become defiled;[^1150] greatly

have I provoked Thy Holy Spirit to wrath, and have bitterly grieved the compassionate bowels of Thy love for mankind, both by word, and deed, and thought, by night and by day, willingly and unwillingly, manifestly and secretly.[^1151] And I know that openly Thou wilt set all my sins before me, even such as they were wrought by me; and Thou wilt require an account of me, concerning those things wherein knowingly and in ignorance I have boundlessly transgressed.[^1152] But O Lord, Lord, do not by Thy righteous judgment, nor in Thine indignation rebuke me, neither in Thy wrath chasten me! Have mercy upon me, O Lord, for not only am I weak, but also I am Thine own creation.[^1153]

Thou hast set Thy hand heavily upon me; yet I have done evil before Thee, and against Thee only have I sinned. Enter not into judgment with Thy servant;[^1154] for if Thou shalt enter into judgment with Thy servant, and if Thou shalt mark iniquities, O Lord; O Lord, who shall stand?[^1155] For I am an abyss of transgressions, and I am not worthy, neither able to look up and behold the height of heaven, by reason of the multitude of my sins, whereof there is no number.[^1156] For every evil deed, and device, and subtlety of Satan, and craftiness; corruptions, and divers emissions, effeminacies, masturbations, defilements of youth,[^1157]

incests, scorns, remembrance of wrongs, evil counsel unto sin, perjuries, wanton assaults, titillations;[^1158] and other boundless passions have I committed, and they have not departed from me. By what evils was I not held fast? By what transgressions was I not corrupted? Every sin have I committed, every sin have I cast into my soul; unprofitable have I become unto God and unto men.[^1159] And who shall raise me up, that am fallen into so many wicked transgressions? O Lord my God, in Thee have I put my trust: if there be any hope of salvation for me; if Thy love for mankind shall prevail over the multitude of mine iniquities:[^1160]

Have mercy upon me, O God, have mercy upon me according to Thy great mercy;[^1162] and render not unto me according to my works, neither put me to shame according to my deeds; but turning again, save and deliver my soul from the evils growing together with her, and from grievous assaults.[^1163] Save me, O Lord, for Thy mercy's sake: that where sin hath abounded, Thy grace may much more abound.[^1164] And I will praise and
