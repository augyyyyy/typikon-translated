# PART XI: CHAPTERS OF PATRIARCH NICEPHORUS CONCERNING DIGAMISTS[^245]

CHAPTERS OF NICEPHORUS, PATRIARCH OF CONSTANTINOPLE, THE CONFESSOR, CONCERNING DIGAMISTS

[Fol. 72v. Initial D.]
A digamist is not crowned, but is forbidden to partake of the immaculate Mysteries for two years. And a trigamist, or one who enters into a third marriage, for three years.[^246]

*From the Answers of the Blessed Nicetas, Metropolitan of Heraclea, Concerning Which He Was Questioned by Bishop Constantine:*[^247]

[Fol. 72v. Initial O.]
Strict canonical rigor indeed is not accustomed to crown digamists; but the Great

=== LEAF p163 ===

Church's custom observes not such things, but places bridal crowns upon digamists also; and no one was ever condemned on this account. Nevertheless, for one year, or two, they are forbidden divine Communion. Moreover, they have ordained that the presbyter who has blessed the marriage should not sup with them, according to the Seventh Canon of the Council of Neocaesarea.[^248]
