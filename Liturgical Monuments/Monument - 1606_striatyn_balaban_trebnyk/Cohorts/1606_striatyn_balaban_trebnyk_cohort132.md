# PART LIII: DEGREES OF KINSHIP: AND CONCERNING LAWFUL MARRIAGE, AND THOSE WHO MARRY UNLAWFULLY, AND CONCERNING THE DISTINCTION OF KINSHIP WHICH IS OF FLESH AND BLOOD[^4011]

*Where it is befitting to contract marriage, and where it is not befitting; and where it is fitting to bring any person unto marriage, and whom not to bring; and whom the law forbids, and whom it does not forbid, and whom it discerns:*[^4012]

Three boundaries there are concerning forbidden marriages:[^4013] One which is from salvific

*Injunctions:*

...baptism; and the second, which is kinship according to the flesh; and the third, which is the union from marriage, that is, marital affinity [in-laws].[^4014] Moreover, marital in-laws also have many diverse rules according to conjunction, which it is fitting for true Christians very diligently to examine with investigation. And thus, when the man is fifteen years of age, and the woman twelve years of age, with the consent, desire, and blessing of parents, and of the priest.[^4015]

Be it known that what the law of holy baptism forbids:[^4016] it forbids only the baptizer from his baptized daughter [goddaughter], and forbids his son from her; and from the mother of the baptized daughter it forbids them; likewise also their other children born[^4017]

*Of the Holy Fathers:*

...it forbids, that is, those of the baptizer, and of that woman whose children he baptizes; but their other relatives, lesser and greater, near and distant, it does not forbid from marriage.[^4018]

Relatives according to the flesh let them be examined well and diligently, and let them be corrected; for kinship, having a generative and lawful origin, is divided into the orders of superior and inferior degrees, and that which is from the right and left sides [collateral], and that which is from distant lineages.[^4019] And the superior of the lineage are: father, and mother, grandfather, and grandmother. And the inferior degrees are: son, and daughter, grandson, and granddaughter. And that which is from the right and left sides are: brother and sister, maternal uncle (*uika*) and paternal uncle/aunt (*lelya*), male first cousin (*anepsios*) and female first cousin (*anepsia*)[^4020]

*Injunctions:*

...and indeed the paternal uncle (*stryika*) with the nephew (*anepsios*), a brother's son, are of the first degree;[^4021] and full brothers (*prisnii, yezhe samobratni*) are of the second degree; and first cousins (*pervii bratuchada*) are of the fourth degree; and second cousins (*vtorii bratuchada*) are of the sixth degree; and third cousins (*tretii bratuchada*) are of the eighth degree.[^4022] In all these degrees there is by no means any marital union. But the children and offspring of third cousins are unforbidden unto marriage; for lineage ascends unto the seventh degree, and the eighth is loosed [permitted].[^4023]

# PART LIV: CONCERNING CARRION [ABOMINATIONS] WHICH IT IS NOT BEFITTING TO EAT[^4032]

We command therefore, also concerning that which is strangled, and concerning carrion [abominations]: that no one among Christians should taste such things; nor having slaughtered [an animal], to drain the blood and bake it, which certain senseless persons do; for the Law of God commands that meat in the blood—the soul—shall not be eaten (cf. Gen. 9:4; Lev. 17:11–14); for the blood of every beast is its life [soul].[^4033]

Likewise also those who set nets for the sake of hunting, and beasts or creeping things are strangled therein, and they taking them eat [them], or [an animal] hunted and strangled by dogs: unto such persons the Law of God forbids to do these things; except only that which is slain, either with an arrow, or with a spear, or with a sword by human hands[^4034]
