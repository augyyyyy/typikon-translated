# PREFACE: TO THE READER

*Preface to this Book.*

*Hedeon Balaban, by the mercy of God humble Bishop of Halych, Lviv, and Kamianets, Exarch of the Ecumenical Throne of the Great Church of Constantinople: To the reverend priests and deacons, and to the other fellow ministers of the sacred clergy, and to the brethren beloved in the Lord, I pray for salvation.*

Seeing that many zealous lovers of the Divine law take care concerning the sacred books, by which the Church

=== LEAF p6 ===

of God is adorned, and the faith is defended, the praise of the Most High is multiplied, the faithful are confirmed, and heretics are confounded; and the people, raised up as if by a certain spur from slothfulness, are moved unto learning and are encouraged; and above all, by which God is entreated. Therefore, being desirous to print this book, I deemed it right to inform you in this preface—even as you were previously informed of this in the Liturgies[^3]—whence I arrived at this work, God prospering me: namely, having been moved by the writings of the blessed Meletios, Pope of Alexandria;[^4] but also, more than ten years ago, a synod

=== LEAF p7 ===

having taken place in Brest of Lithuania,[^5] under Mikhail Rahoza, Metropolitan of Kyiv,[^6] and the other bishops then assembled, before his apostasy with his partisans from the One Holy Eastern Catholic Apostolic Church: there was much inquiry and consideration by them concerning the great discrepancy of Church rites, and especially concerning the Euchologion,[^7] that is, the Molitvoslovets or Trebnyk: by which, being instructed as by a certain artistic guidance, those who perform sacred rites accomplish decently all things pertaining to the perfect calling of Christianity, and agree in their prayers unto God. And having considered these matters at the synod,

=== LEAF p8 ===

they entrusted to humble me, the Bishop of Lviv, to take all care and diligence concerning the correction of that book of the said Trebnyk. And shortly thereafter, Metropolitan Mikhail, having apostatized with certain bishops from the Holy Eastern Church and cast off the pastorship of the Ecumenical Patriarch, departed along the path of insubordination according to the devise of their own hearts. Yet I, following my own shepherd,[^8] did not apostatize; and keeping in remembrance the task entrusted unto me, I gathered many Trebnyks, both from these local regions and from the lands of Wallachia, Moldavia, and Serbia:[^9] in which, when I saw the great discrepancy, concerning this

=== LEAF p9 ===

I grieved exceedingly; but I found a remedy for my sorrow through an epistle sent to the most blessed Meletios, Pope of Alexandria—who had attained to the height of external wisdom and of our noble instruction, as an excellent steersman of the ship of Christ, who was then administering the place of the Throne of Constantinople[^10]—informing him concerning the discordance and lack of correction in our Slavonic books, whether on account of unskilled scribes or because of certain other causes; and at the same time beseeching him that, for the sake of certain and unconflicting knowledge of the Church services, he would send us a Greek Euchologion. And he did not disregard my petition, but sent me
