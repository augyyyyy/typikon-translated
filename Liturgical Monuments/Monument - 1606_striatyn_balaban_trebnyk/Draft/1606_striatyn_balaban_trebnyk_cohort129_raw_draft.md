# PART LI: AGAIN: DIVERSE INJUNCTIONS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES AND OF THE GOD-BEARING HOLY FATHERS[^3914]

I, Peter, and Paul, Apostles of Christ, enjoin upon the servants of Christ concerning human labors, that they shall work six days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday until midday,[^3915]

=== LEAF p1282 ===

...or until the third hour; or again, the entire day on Saturday to rest from heavy labors, and to attend unto the household, and to accomplish the memorials for the deceased, and hospitality unto strangers, and the love of hospitality;[^3916] for Saturday has the grace of creation and is called rest; while Sunday is the first day of God, possessing the grace of the Resurrection.[^3917] Therefore do we enjoin to honor these days: on Saturday to cease from every labor, both small and great, from the eighth hour, and to hasten unto church, all men with their wives and with their children; and to abide therein with the fear of God, praying even until the morning of Sunday; and when the holy[^3918]

=== LEAF p1283 ===

...Liturgy is dismissed, thus to depart unto their homes, and to rest until the morrow on Monday; and thus from the second hour to begin every manual work.[^3919]

*Concerning the Nativity of Christ:*

The feast day of the Nativity is likewise to be kept even as Sunday; for on this day our Lord Jesus Christ granted salvation unto the world.[^3920]

*Concerning the Theophany:*

The feast of the Holy Theophany it behooves every Christian to honor and keep likewise; for on this day the Creator granted renewal unto all by water and the Spirit.[^3921]

*Concerning the Annunciation:*

Render ye honor unto the feast of the Annunciation, as being most honorable among all[^3922]

=== LEAF p1284 ===

...the Master's feasts.[^3923]

[Honor ye also] the Saturday of righteous Lazarus, bearing the beginning and prefiguration of the Resurrection, and the most honorable feast of the miracles of Christ.[^3924]

And again: the Ascension of Christ, and the Descent of the Holy Spirit, when enlightenment and understanding came down into the world unto all; likewise also honor ye and keep all the holy feasts of Christ even as we have said: both the Transfiguration of the Savior, and the Exaltation of the Honorable Cross, being Master's feasts. All these feasts of Christ are thus to be kept and honored even as we have said concerning holy Sunday, the first divine day.[^3925]

*Feasts of the Mother of God:*

The feasts of our most holy Lady, the Mother of God[^3926]

=== LEAF p1285 ===

...it behooves to honor and glorify likewise, even as the feasts of Christ, which we have indicated; thus also these feasts of the Mother of God: the Conception,[^3927] and Her Nativity, the Entry into the Holy of Holies, and Her Dormition; and to accomplish and celebrate the memory of Her holy parents.[^3928]

The feast of the holy and all-praised Apostles Peter and Paul is likewise to be honored and celebrated, as we have said concerning them. Likewise also the feasts of the other twelve Apostles: whenever each one's day shall be, it behooves to celebrate that very day from morning until evening.[^3929]

The feasts of the honorable Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John: the Nativity, and the Beheading of his honorable[^3930]

=== LEAF p1286 ===

...head, it behooves to celebrate from the ninth hour and from morning the entire day.[^3931]

The memory of the Protomartyr Stephen the Archdeacon is to be celebrated from morning until evening.[^3932]

In like manner also the memories of the holy and venerable universal teachers, fathers, and hierarchs, pastors unto the rational sheep: these are to be celebrated, whensoever each one's [feast] shall be, from morning until evening.[^3933]

Of the holy Great Martyrs of Christ: George, Demetrius, Theodore, Panteleimon, Procopius, and the holy Unmercenaries; and of the holy Great Martyr women, as many as are called great: unto all these is to be celebrated each one's day, from morning until midday.[^3934]

=== LEAF p1287 ===

On all these days wherein we have commanded every Christian to celebrate:[^3935]

And again we say, that we do not command any Christian man to celebrate with timbrels, and pipes, and goat-like bellowing, and drunkenness; but to celebrate and honor with prayers, and hymns, and doxologies; and that every Christian is to call priests unto his house, and to perform blessings in his dwellings. For wheresoever the priest comes not and performs no blessing, thence God also departs; and wheresoever the priest censes not nor makes prayer, in despondency and laxity all things are defiled.[^3936]

These things do we enjoin upon the priests and church clerics: that they themselves[^3937]

=== LEAF p1288 ===

...walk in these commandments, and thereafter also teach the people always: not for the sake of any reward, or of gold, or for the sake of silver, but for the sake of the salvation of their own soul.[^3938]

Unto the people, both small and great, do we thus enjoin you: to give ear and submit unto the hierarchs and priests, and to honor and revere them, each of you according to his ability as he possesses, and to provide for them; for they pray on your behalf.[^3939]

Unto priests we enjoin: not with enmity and hatred to enter into the sanctuary, nor to dare to touch the holy things; nor to be a drunkard, nor to steal; neither to revile one another; nor to be contentious, nor foul-mouthed, nor insolent; as our Lord Jesus Christ, being beaten and[^3940]

=== LEAF p1289 ===

...mocked, suffered for our sake; but let them be gentle, quiet, humble, patient in all things, and walk in the fear of God, and be diligent concerning that ministry of theirs.[^3941]

We counsel them according to the tradition of the holy Apostles to be the husband of only one wife, that he may not approach unlawfully unto the holy altar; and that he be not a fornicator, nor an adulterer, or an abuser of himself with mankind, or an abuser of beasts; for let such a one not draw near, nor ever think upon the priesthood; neither let him be a murderer, nor an extortioner, nor a robber; but let him be pure from all things: the husband of only one wife, as we have said, and free from every sin[^3942]

=== LEAF p1290 ===

...and pure, and undefiled; and thus to approach unto this great and fearful ministry.[^3943]

*Concerning the Church Offering:*

The blessings brought unto the church, we command these to be not only for the priests, but also for all the church servers in the holy work of God; and it is meet to divide them into three parts: two parts unto the priest, and the third part unto the laborers who serve.[^3944]

*Concerning Holy Communion:*
