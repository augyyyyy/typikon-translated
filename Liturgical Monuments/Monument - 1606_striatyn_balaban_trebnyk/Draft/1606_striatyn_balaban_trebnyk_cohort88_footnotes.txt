[^2501]: Continuation of the supplication for rain from physical leaf p870: *Send rain unto Thy land, O Master...* Allusion to the prophetic prayers of Elijah for the cessation of drought (1 Kings / 3 Kingdoms 18:41–45) and the promise of seasonal rains (Joel 2:23).
[^2502]: *The Twelve Apostles* (Slavonic: `Ѡбоюна́десѧте [Ѡбона́десѧте] а҆пл҃ъ`): archaic dual-derived compound numeral denoting the sacred college of the Twelve Apostles interceding alongside the choirs of holy martyrs.
[^2503]: Slavonic: `Всѣ́хъ зе́мнїи ходата́ицоу къ Бг҃оу , и҆́мамы тѧ̀ дв҃о Бц҃е`. The nominative plural `зе́мнїи` ("we earthly ones / earthborn") functions as the subject of the clause, designating the collective mortal faithful invoking the Mother of God as universal mediatress before Christ.
[^2504]: Incipit of the second Heirmos of Ode 5: *All things were amazed at Thy divine [glory]...* (Slavonic: `Оу҆диви́шасѧ всѧ́ческаѧ ѡ Бж҃твѣнѣ́й [сла́вѣ твое́й]`), the classical Byzantine/Slavonic heirmos associated with Tone 4 and Tone 8 suppliant canons.
[^2505]: Allusion to Psalm 67:36 LXX: *Marvelous is God in His holy places / among His saints: the God of Israel, He will give power and strength unto His people.*
[^2506]: A classical Christological-theological antinomy articulating the dual generation of the Logos: eternally begotten of the Father without a mother in heaven, and temporally born in the flesh of the Virgin without an earthly father.
[^2507]: Triadological stanza invoking the *Thrice-unoriginate Trinity* (`Трибезначя́лноую тр҃цоу`), confessing the consubstantial and co-eternal Godhead, undivided in essence and single in divine will, yet existing in three distinct hypostases (*licy* / Persons).
[^2508]: Slavonic: `подъ нѡ́зѣ вѣ́рномоу цр҃ю на́шемоу` ("beneath the feet of our faithful sovereign"). Standard recensional prayer for the Christian sovereign / king, preserved faithfully in the 1606 Balaban imprint within the political realities of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.
[^2509]: Incipit of Ode 6 Heirmos: *Held fast in the abyss of sins...* (Slavonic: `Въ бе́зднѣ грѣхо́внѣй ѻ҆дръжи́мъ...`). The Jonah heirmologic archetype drawing upon Jonah 2:2–7, where the belly of the sea monster typifies the depths of human sin and deliverance from Hades.
[^2510]: Slavonic: `ꙗ҆́ко и҆моу́ще дръзнове́нїе мнѡ́го къ Бг҃у` (Greek *parrhesia*): the spiritual boldness and intimacy of intercession granted to the martyrs through their shedding of blood for Christ.
[^2511]: Scriptural allusion to Psalm 84:4–5 LXX: *Turn us, O God of our salvation, and turn away Thine anger from us. Wilt Thou be angry with us unto ages?*
[^2512]: *temperate rain* (Slavonic: `до́ждъ бл҃горастворе́нъ` / Greek *eukraton hyeton*): a well-tempered, fruitful rain that brings vegetation to maturity without destroying crops through inundation, hail, or violent squalls.
[^2513]: Incipit of the second Heirmos of Ode 6: *Prefiguring, he cried out...* (Slavonic: `възопи́лъ проѻбразоу́ѧ`), referencing the prophetic typological cry of Jonah prefiguring the three-day burial and resurrection of Christ.
[^2514]: Scriptural allusion to Genesis 7:11 and Malachi 3:10 (Slavonic: `хлѧ́би нб҃сныѧ` / Greek *katarraktas tou ouranou*), the opening of the celestial sluice gates or cataracts to pour water upon the parched earth.
[^2515]: Slavonic: `Жестото́ю грѣхо́вною ѻ҆дръжи́ми` ("held fast by the hardness/callousness of sin"). A patristic lament denoting the dry, stony heart incapable of weeping, for which the intercession of the Virgin is sought to obtain compunction and the grace of tears (*penthos*).
[^2516]: Kontakion, Tone 6: *O Champion of Christians that cannot be put to shame...* (Slavonic: `Застоу́пнице хрїтїа́нѡмъ непосты́днаѧ`), the universally renowned Byzantine Marian kontakion appointed widely throughout molebens and daily liturgical cycles.
[^2517]: Ikos: *Stretch forth thy palms, wherewith thou didst embrace the Master of all as an Infant...* (Slavonic: `Прострѝ дла́ни своѝ, и҆́маже въсѣ́хъ вл҃коу ꙗ҆́ко младе́нца ѻ҆бѧ́тъ`). Poetic theological juxtaposition of the physical maternal holding of the incarnate Christ with her spiritual intercession for mankind.
[^2518]: *fountain of compassion* (Slavonic: `млр҃дїа сꙋ́щи исто́чникъ`): traditional Byzantine Marian title praising the Mother of God as the channel through whom divine mercy flows to believers.
[^2519]: Ode 7 Heirmos: *The most wise youths bowed not down to the golden image...* (Slavonic: `Образоу злато́моу непоклони́шасѧ дѣ́ти премꙋ́дрїи`), drawn from the Song of the Three Holy Youths in Babylon (Daniel 3), where the Angel of the Lord turned the fiery furnace into a dewy breeze.
[^2520]: Allusion to the Prayer of Manasses, King of Judah (2 Chronicles 33:11–13 / LXX Odes 12), the classical liturgical archetype of earnest repentance from grievous sin resulting in divine mercy and deliverance from bondage.
[^2521]: Scriptural penitential formula: *for we have sinned and committed iniquity exceedingly...* (cf. Daniel 9:5; Baruch 2:12; Psalm 105:6 LXX), acknowledging corporate transgression as the moral cause of natural calamities.
[^2522]: Slavonic: `прибѣ́жище стра́шно , и҆ ѻ҆роу́жїе непобѣди́мо` ("an awe-inspiring refuge and unconquerable weapon"), expressing the invincible military-protective role ascribed to the Mother of God in Byzantine-Slavonic hymnography.
[^2523]: Incipit of the second Heirmos of Ode 7: *He Who saved in the fire the Abrahamic children...* (Slavonic: `Сп҃сы́й въ ѡ҆́гни а҆враа́мскыѧ дѣ́ти`), referencing the Three Youths of the lineage of Abraham delivered from the furnace by the pre-incarnate Word.
[^2524]: Rubric *Glory...* (`Сла́ва ·`) at the foot of physical leaf p880 marks the transition to the Doxasticon troparion concluded on the subsequent leaf (p881: *May the fury of Thy threatening not come to an end, O Christ...*).
