[^2117]: John 5:3–4 (pericope 14), concluding the Gospel reading for the Lesser Sanctification of Water begun on leaf p750. The pericope details the healing pool of Bethesda (*Виѳезда*) by the Sheep Gate in Jerusalem, where an angel of the Lord troubled the water at certain seasons, healing the first sufferer to step in.
[^2118]: Rubric introducing the Deacon's Great Litany (*И абие начинаетъ дїаконъства:* / *And straightway he begins the litany:*). In Ruthenian liturgical practice, *дїаконъство* denotes the deacon's litanic petitions (Greek: τὰ διακонικά).
[^2119]: The opening petitions of the Great Litany (peace synapte / *мирная ектения*). On this and the following leaves, the 1606 imprint provides a mix of full text and abbreviated incipits for the standard petitions, while printing the special petitions for the sanctification of water in unabridged detail.
[^2120]: Petition for this holy house and all who enter with faith (*О святѣмъ храмѣ семъ...*), spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p752.
[^2121]: Conclusion of the petition for this holy house and all who enter it (*...with faith, reverence, and the fear of God*), and the petitions for the bishop and the clergy. Note the red rubric *и҆́мⷬ҇къ рⷱ҇къ* ("*[Name]* is said").
[^2122]: Petitions for the civil authorities (*О благовѣрныхъ царехъ нашихъ...*) and the heavily abbreviated standard petitions: for this city and country (*О градѣ семъ...*), temperate seasons and abundance of fruits (*О благорастворенїи воздуховъ...*), and travelers, the sick, and captives (*О плавающи[хъ], пу[ть] шествующи[хъ]...*).
[^2123]: First special water-blessing petition: *That this water may be sanctified by the power, operation, and descent of the Holy Spirit* (*О еже освятитися водѣ сей силою и дѣйствомъ, и пришествїемъ Святаго Духа...*).
[^2124]: Second special water-blessing petition: *That there may be sent down upon it the grace of redemption and the blessing of Jordan* (*О еже послатися на ню благодати избавленїа, и благословенїе іорданово...*).
[^2125]: Third and fourth special water-blessing petitions: *That there may be granted unto it healing power by the descent of the Holy Spirit* (*О дароватися ей силу цѣлебну...*) and *That it may be for the sanctification of our souls and bodies, and for the turning away of every adverse power* (*О быти ей на освященїе душамъ и тѣломъ нашимъ...*).
[^2126]: Fifth and sixth special water-blessing petitions: *That it may be shown forth as a fountain of incorruption and a gift of salvation* (*О явитися ей источнику нетлѣнїа...*) and *For them that draw and take thereof* (*О чръплющихъ и взимающихъ от нея...*).
[^2127]: Seventh and eighth special water-blessing petitions: *That it may be for the remission of sins and the healing of diseases* (*О быти ей оставленїе грѣхомъ...*) and *That we may be enlightened with the illumination of understanding for the sake of the consubstantial Trinity* (*О просвѣтитися намъ просвѣщенїемъ разума...*).
[^2128]: Ninth special water-blessing petition: *That the purifying operation of the supersubstantial Trinity may be sent down upon this water* (*О низпослатися на воду сїю чистителному пресущественыя Троица дѣйству*). The physical leaf ends with the abbreviated noun *дѣ́йств[у]*, with the printer omitting *Господу помолимся* due to lack of space at the lower margin.
[^2129]: Tenth and eleventh special water-blessing petitions: *That the Lord our God may send illumination and understanding into our hearts* (*О еже Господу Богу нашему послати просвѣщенїе и разумъ въ сердца наша...*) and *For all who need help and protection from our God* (*О всѣхъ требующихъ яже от Бога нашего помощи и заступленїа...*).
[^2130]: Twelfth special water-blessing petition: *That the Lord our God may show us forth as sons and heirs of His Kingdom through the communion and sprinkling of this water* (*О яко да Господь Богъ нашъ явитъ насъ сыны и наслѣдники царствїа своего, причащенїа и окропленїа ради воды сея...*).
[^2131]: Concluding general petitions of the litany: for deliverance from all affliction, wrath, and need (*О избавитися намъ от всякоя скорби...*); the suffrages *Help us, save us, have mercy on us, and keep us...* (*Заступи, спаси...*); and the commemoration of the Most-holy Theotokos and all saints (*Пресвятую, чистую, и преблагословенную...*), spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p755.
[^2132]: Conclusion of the litany entrustment: *...commending ourselves and one another, and our whole life unto Christ our God* (*...и весь животъ нашъ Христу Богу [предадимъ]*), followed by the priest's exclamation (*възгласъ:*): *For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship...* (*Яко подобаетъ ти всяка слава...*).
[^2133]: Rubric introducing the great prayer of the Lesser Sanctification of Water: *Let us pray to the Lord. Then the priest [says] this prayer:* (*Господу помолимся. Таже іерей молитву сїю.*).
[^2134]: Opening Trinitarian invocation of the great prayer: *O God great and highest, Who art worshipped in the Holy Trinity...* (*Боже великый и вышнїй, иже въ троици святѣй покланяемый...*). The physical imprint reads *є҆стко*, an apparent typographical misprint for *естество* ("substance / nature").
[^2135]: Supplicatory address to the Master Who loves mankind (*Ты человѣколюбче Владыко...*), spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p756.
[^2136]: Petition for divine inspiration and spiritual understanding: *...and grant in our mind the spirit of wisdom and understanding...* (*...и даждь въ мысли нашей духъ премудрости и разума...*).
[^2137]: Scriptural allusion to Philippians 2:13: *For from Thee and from Thy life-giving gift it is both to will and to work according to Thy will and Thy good pleasure unto men* (*От тебе бо и твоего животворнаго дарованїа, и еже хотѣти, и еже дѣйствовати по воли твоей, и твоего благоволенїа человѣкомъ есть*).
[^2138]: Invocation of the blessing and healing grace upon the water, echoing Titus 3:5 (*Who hast given to our race the washing of regeneration...* / *иже банею пакы бытїа роду нашему даровавъ...*).
[^2139]: Allusion to Hebrews 10:22: *...and make us, who are sprinkled [in hearts] from an evil conscience...* (*...и сподоби насъ окропленыхъ сердци от [съвѣсти лукавны]*), spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p757.
[^2140]: Continuation of the baptismal and cleansing typology from Hebrews 10:22 (*...from an evil conscience, and having our body washed with pure water* / *...съвѣсти лукавны, и омывеныхъ тѣло водою чистою*), turning into a prayer for active good works and charity toward the brethren.
[^2141]: Petition that this spiritual service and water sanctification be not for carnal pleasure, but for the healing of soul and body (*не къ сласти плотстѣй, но къ исцѣленїю души и тѣлу творимая нами служба духовная*).
[^2142]: Invocation to the merciful Master (*Еи, Владыко многомилостиве, помилуй рабы Своя...*), spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p758.
[^2143]: Supplication for purification: *...bowing down unto our lowliness: Who hast given us to wear this pure garment from water and the Spirit, wash [us] with this water, and by the sprinkling of Thy blessing cleanse the defilements of our passions* (*...смиренїе наше прекланяяся... одежду от воды и духа, омый водою сею...*).
[^2144]: The extensive intercession of saints in the prayer of water blessing: invoking the Most-holy Theotokos, the heavenly bodiless powers, the Prophet and Forerunner John the Baptist, the holy Apostles, the holy Ancestors of God Joachim and Anna, Zacharias and Elizabeth, and the holy sovereign equals-to-the-apostles Constantine and Helen.
[^2145]: Commemoration of the great Hierarchs: Basil the Great, Gregory [the Theologian], spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p759.
[^2146]: Continuation of the Hierarchs (Gregory the Theologian, John Chrysostom, Nicholas the Wonderworker) and the holy Unmercenary Physicians (*безсребреници*): Cosmas and Damian, Cyrus and John, Panteleimon and Hermolaus, Florus and Laurus, Samson and Diomedes; Saint Simeon Stylite of the Mandra (*иже въ Оградѣ*); the holy Seven Maccabee Martyrs (commemorated on August 1); and Venerable Athanasius.
[^2147]: Intercession for the sovereign rulers (*рабы своя вѣрныя царя нашя...*), with rubric *[Name]* (*рⷱ҇къ* / *і҆́мⷬ҇къ*), asking for peace, victory over adversaries, and all petitions unto salvation; spanning across the leaf boundary onto leaf p760.
[^2148]: Continuation of the imperial petitions (*...and eternal life*), followed by intercessions for the hierarch (*our Archbishop [Name]* / *архїеппла нашего імя рекъ*) and the Orthodox episcopate rightly dividing the word of truth (2 Timothy 2:15).
[^2149]: Commemoration of the priestly and monastic orders, benefactors, those who remember the poor, the people present, and absent fathers and brethren.
[^2150]: Petitions for captives (*помяни, Господи, братїа наша плѣненыя, и избави ихъ всякаго злаго обстоянїа*) and the sick (*помяни, Господи, сущихъ въ болѣзни, и посѣти ихъ вся, и исцѣли по велицей милости твоей*).
[^2151]: Petition for travelers by land and sea (*помяни, Господи, въ [путь шествующихъ, и в мори...]*, spanning across the cohort boundary onto leaf p761.
