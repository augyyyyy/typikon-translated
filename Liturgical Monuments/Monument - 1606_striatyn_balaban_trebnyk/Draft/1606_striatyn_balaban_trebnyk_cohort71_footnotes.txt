[^1978]: Leaf p703 (fol. 346 / тм҃ѕ). First Tone 3 sticheron on the Cross, featuring the recurring liturgical refrain for monastic burial: "where all who rejoice have their dwelling with Thee, to glorify Thy Divinity" (cf. LXX Ps. 86:7: "As of them that rejoice is the dwelling of all in Thee").
[^1979]: Leaf p703. Second Tone 3 sticheron, appealing to Christ's unfathomable deep of compassion (*поучи́нѫ бл҃гости*) and His knowledge of human frail clay as our Creator (cf. LXX Ps. 102:14).
[^1980]: Leaf p704 (fol. 346v). Third Tone 3 sticheron for *Glory...*, meditating on Christ's bodily rest in the tomb and His divine resurrection of the dead.
[^1981]: Leaf p704. Tone 3 Theotokion for *Both now...*, hailing the Virgin as the "intelligible candlestick" (λυχνία νοερά) carrying the divine fire united to human bodily density (*де́бельство чл҃ч҃каго существа̀*).
[^1982]: Leaf p705 (fol. 347 / тм҃з). *Antiphon 4, Tone 4* (А҆нтифѡ́нъ четве́ртый · Гла́съ д҃). First antiphon of the Songs of Ascents of Tone 4, based on LXX Psalm 128:1–2 ("Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth").
[^1983]: Leaf p705. Second strophe of Tone 4 Anabathmoi, based on LXX Psalm 128:5–6 ("Let them be confounded and turned back all they that hate Zion; let them be as the grass upon the housetops").
[^1984]: Leaf p705. Celebrated pneumatological Doxasticon of Tone 4 (*Ст҃ы́мъ дх҃омъ всѧ́ка дш҃а живи́тсѧ*), sung at every Sunday Polyeleos Matins, proclaiming the Holy Spirit's quickening and sanctifying life in the Trinity.

[^1986]: Leaf p706 (fol. 347v). Concluding the sticheron on the dissolution of natural bodily bonds and imploring Christ to grant rest in the tabernacles of the righteous.

[^1993]: Leaf p708. *Antiphon 5, Tone 5* (А҆нтифѡ́нъ пѧ́тый · Гла́съ є҆). First antiphon of the Songs of Ascents of Tone 5, based on LXX Psalm 119:1–2 ("In my distress I cried unto the Lord... Deliver my soul, O Lord, from lying lips, and from a deceitful tongue").
[^1994]: Leaf p708. Second strophe of Tone 5 Anabathmoi, extolling the blessedness of the solitary desert life (*Пусты́ннымъ живо́тъ блаже́нъ є҆́стъ*), elevated by divine longing.
[^1995]: Leaf p708. Doxasticon to the Holy Spirit of Tone 5, professing His universal sovereignty and consubstantial union as One of the Holy Trinity.
