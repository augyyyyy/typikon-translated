*Heirmos; then:*[^1354]

*Troparion:*
O clouds, drop down dew as tears; O mountains and hills, weep piteously over me, who have departed far from God and drawn near unto the demons.

*Troparion:*
O unashamed, unerring refuge unto all that are in distress: O all-spotless Sovereign Lady, be thou a refuge unto me in the hour of trial.[^1355]

*Troparion:*
Having stretched forth thy most pure and all-venerable hands like the consecrated wings of a dove: beneath their shelter and shadow protect me, O Sovereign Lady.[^1356]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*
The aerial prince, the violent tormentor, the dread toll-gatherer of the pathways, and the ruthless inquisitor of words: vouchsafe that I may pass by him unhindered,[^1357]

=== LEAF p452 ===
as I depart from the earth.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*
Behold, O Sovereign Lady, fear hath met me, which also I did dread; behold, a great struggle hath encompassed me, wherein be thou my helper, O hope of my salvation.[^1358]

*Ode 5. Heirmos: By Thy divine light, O Good One...*

*Troparion:*
O sensible light, shine upon me, that I may behold Thee if but for a little while, O Christ; for never again shall I behold Thee: for a cloud of demons hath suddenly fallen upon me, and the darkness of my shameful deeds covers me.[^1359]

*Troparion:*
O all ye beloved of the Savior, why have ye shown yourselves unmerciful toward me in my wretchedness and all-afflicted state? For if ye had but uttered a single voice unto the common...

=== LEAF p453 ===
...Sovereign Lady, ye would have delivered me from darkness.[^1360]

*Troparion:*
Farewell, O great prince of God Michael, chief of the angels! For no longer shall I invoke thy holy name to aid me; for my lips, my tongue, and mine eyes are struck silent.[^1361]

*Theotokion:*
Farewell, thou also, O undefiled and pure Mother of my Christ! For no longer shall I behold thine all-spotless and pure holy countenance; for the light in me hath gone out, and night hath covered me.[^1362]

*Heirmos; then:*

*Troparion:*
Forget me not, O good lady, nor turn away thy face from me, thy servant; but hear me, for I am afflicted, and give heed unto my soul, and deliver it.[^1363]

*Troparion:*
O my kinsmen according to the flesh, and...

=== LEAF p454 ===
...brethren according to the spirit, familiar friends and acquaintances: weep, sigh, lament! For behold, I am now parted from you.[^1364]

*Troparion:*
There is none that delivers in any wise, and in truth no one that helps; do thou aid me, O Sovereign Lady, lest like a helpless man I be shut up in the hands of mine enemies.[^1365]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*
O how shall I endure that separation from thee, O Mother of God, which is truly mournful and grievous; and the bitter falling away from my Savior, and the estrangement from all the saints?[^1366]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*
May the multitude of my sins not prevail over thine abundant tender mercy, O Sovereign Lady;...

=== LEAF p455 ===
...but may thy mercy encompass me, and may it cover all mine iniquities.[^1367]

*Ode 6. Heirmos: The sea of life...*

*Troparion:*
Incline your ear unto me, O brethren, and attentively hear the brief utterance of a contrite voice; do not disobey, and ye shall surely receive a reward from God.[^1368]

*Troparion:*
Behold, now the soul is violently parted from the wretched body; bury not the body in the earth, for it is not worthy: but cast it out and throw it to the dogs![^1369]

*Troparion:*
For what profit is it unto me, while my soul is dragged into dreadful torments, that my body...

=== LEAF p456 ===
...should be sung over by you? Leave it unburied, so that the dogs may devour my heart.[^1370]

*Theotokion:*
Ye strangers passing by, as ye behold the bones dragged by the dogs, take pity and cry from the soul: "Help, O Sovereign Lady, the soul of this passible body!"[^1371]

*Heirmos; then:*

*Troparion:*
My lips are silent, and my tongue speaks not, but my heart utters its voice: for the fire of contrition, devouring it, burns within, and with unuttered cries calls upon thee, O Virgin.[^1372]

*Troparion:*
Look upon me from on high, O Mother of God, and mercifully give heed now to descend for my visitation, that having seen thee, I may depart from the body rejoicing.[^1373]

=== LEAF p457 ===
*Troparion:*
The bond is severed, the laws of natural cohesion and of all bodily composition are torn asunder; they bring upon me unbearable distress and anguish.[^1374]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*
Commit me, O Sovereign Lady, into the holy and venerable hands of the holy angels, so that sheltered by their wings, I may not behold the dishonorable, gloomy, and foul form of the demons.[^1375]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*
O all-venerable Bridal Chamber of God, vouchsafe me the heavenly noetic bridal chamber, having kindled my extinguished and unilluminated lamp with the holy oil of thy mercy.[^1376]

*Heirmos:*
Beholding the sea of life surging...

=== LEAF p458 ===
...with the tempest of temptations, and fleeing to Thy tranquil haven, I cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O Most Merciful One![^1377]

*Then the Little Litany, and the exclamation.*

*Kontakion, Tone 6:*
O my soul, my soul, arise! Why sleepest thou? The end is at hand, and thou shalt be confounded; arise therefore, that Christ God may spare thee, Who is everywhere present and fills all things.[^1378]

*Ikos:*
Seeing the medicine of Christ opened, and healing pouring forth therefrom unto Adam, the devil suffered and was wounded; lamenting as one receiving disaster, he cried aloud unto his companions: "What shall I do unto the Son of Mary? He slays me,...

=== LEAF p459 ===
...the Bethlehemite, Who is everywhere present and fills all things!"[^1379]

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The furnace moist with dew...*

*Troparion:*
Behold, having dragged it forth, they have fiercely torn my wretched soul from the body, and lead it away unto the dread Judge; remembering therefore my life together with you, O beloved, make memory of me.[^1380]

*Troparion:*
Have mercy on me, O most radiant angels of God the Almighty, and deliver me from all the wicked toll-houses; for I have no good deeds to set against the balance of evil actions.[^1381]

*Troparion:*
When ye ascend, O my holy angels, to stand before the judgment seat of Christ, bending your knees,...

=== LEAF p460 ===
...cry out unto Him with weeping: "Have mercy, O Creator of all, on the work of Thy most pure hands, and cast it not away from Thyself!"[^1382]

*Theotokion:*
Bowing down before the most pure Sovereign Lady, beseech the Mother of our God, that She may bend Her knees together with you and incline God unto mercy; for being His Mother and Nourisher, She shall be heard.[^1383]

*Heirmos; then:*

*Troparion:*
The night of death hath overtaken me unprepared, dark and moonless, sending me forth unready upon that long and dreadful journey; may thy mercy accompany me, O Sovereign Lady![^1384]
