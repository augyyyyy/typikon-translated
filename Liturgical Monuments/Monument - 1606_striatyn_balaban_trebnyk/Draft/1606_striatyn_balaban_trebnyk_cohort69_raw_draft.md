# PART XXIX: THE BURIAL: THE FUNERAL OFFICE FOR MONKS[^1919]

*When any of the monks departs unto the Lord: since it is not proper to wash his body, nor that he be seen naked at all; but a brother appointed for this, or the ecclesiarches, or the one serving the sick, wipes his relics*[^1920] *with warm water using a sponge.*  
*And upon the brow he first makes a cross with the sponge, cleansing the whole face; likewise upon the breast, and upon the hands, and upon the feet, and upon...*

=== LEAF p690 ===
*...the knees; and nothing more.*[^1921]  
*Then they take off his worn [ascetic] garment, and clothe him in clean robes, in no wise having seen his nakedness.*[^1922]  
*Then they place upon him the koukoulion, if he be of the Great Habit, enclosed with the helmet over the head, and letting it hang down even unto the beard, so that the face of his relics may not be seen.*[^1923]  
*Then they place upon him the analavos according to his rank, which are plaited bands; and they gird him with a belt, and shoe him in new sandals.*[^1924]  
*Then his mantiya is spread out wide; and having carried the relics from the reed mat, they lay them out...*[^1925]
