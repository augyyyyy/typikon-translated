*Ode 1. Tone 6. Heirmos:*
Traversing the deep on foot as on dry land, and beholding the persecutor Pharaoh engulfed, Israel cried: Let us sing a song of victory unto God, for He has been glorified.[^2312]

*Troparion:*
Let us make a common lamentation now, O brethren, every age and order, race and clan, and bitterly...

...sigh together, and make the Creator of all merciful.[^2313]

*Troparion:*
Thou Who out of tender compassion didst of old bring human nature forth from non-being, O Master, redeeming, Thou hast saved them who were imperiled from corruption; even now have mercy on us.[^2314]

*Troparion:*
Thou didst sacredly offer Thine own blood, O Word of God, and dost die as man, being God for our sake; yet we, having forsaken Thy fearful grace, have now been sorely wounded.[^2315]

*Troparion:*
Having cast aside Thy laws and ordinances, outraging Thee, O Christ, with shameless nature, we suffer righteous stripes, since the elements have departed from their natural order.[^2316]

*Theotokion:*
All things are full of terror and trembling and shame, seeing now the righteous wrath of the Master; but make Him merciful unto us, as Thy Son and God, O Lady.[^2317]

*Ode 3. Tone 6. Heirmos:*
There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established them upon the rock of Thy confession.[^2318]

*Troparion:*
Adam, having disobeyed the first commandment, was driven out from Paradise, and from life, and from God; while we, having departed from all things together, are now put to death.[^2319]

*Troparion:*
Wicked Cain appeared of old as an exemplar, having murdered his brother out of evil envy; emulating whom now...

...we all have been condemned to groan and tremble grievously, even as he.[^2320]

*Troparion:*
Fiercely does creation grow stubborn against us, and exceedingly rages, moving the natural impulses, proclaiming unto all the righteous wrath of the Master.[^2321]

*Troparion:*
The brightness of the sun is hidden, O Master Christ, and the flow of accustomed rains is withheld; drought and darkness hold sway; for Thou, the Sun of glory, hast turned away.[^2322]

*Theotokion:*
There is no strength in us, and we all have been stripped of all boldness because of shameful deeds; but do thou, as a pure Mother, appease the Master of all.[^2323]

*Ode 4. Tone 6. Heirmos:*

"Christ is my power, God and Lord," the venerable Church sings in a manner befitting God, crying out from a pure mind, keeping festival in the Lord.[^2324]

*Troparion:*
Today, O Master, is a day of wrath and affliction; and this day is neither day nor night, which now according to the prophets has overtaken all, and there is none who delivers.[^2325]

*Troparion:*
Of old the multiplying cry grievously opened heaven unto the Sodomites; but now, O Master, the multitude of sins righteously shuts it against us.[^2326]

*Troparion:*

*Ode 5. Tone 6. Heirmos:*
With Thy divine light, O Good One, lovingly illumine the souls of them that rise early unto Thee, O Lord, that they may see Thee, the only God, Who deliverest all from the gloom of sins.[^2330]

*Troparion:*
"We are hard-pressed on every side," David cried out, having seen the coming wrath of God; but what shall we do, passion-plagued ones, seeing this and repenting not?[^2331]

*Troparion:*
Even as Thou didst save Thy people of old from Pharaoh, O Good One, suddenly parting the deep, snatch us also from barbarian hands, and having calmed the sea, quiet the winds.[^2332]

*Troparion:*
Stay the distress of floods and the fierce rising of waves and contrary blast of winds; and let rains be stayed by Thy command, and let every slaughter cease, O Lover of mankind.[^2333]

*Troparion:*
A wave of mire and stench of pleasures and sins of every kind has covered us; therefore waves of divine wrath breathe forth, and now vex all.[^2334]

*Theotokion:*
