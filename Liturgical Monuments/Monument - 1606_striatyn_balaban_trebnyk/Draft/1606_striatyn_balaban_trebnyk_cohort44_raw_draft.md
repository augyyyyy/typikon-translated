# PART XVIII: THE ORDER OF HOW IT IS BEFITTING TO GIVE COMMUNION TO A SICK PERSON IN HASTE[^1318]

*The Priest says:* **"Blessed is our God..."**[^1319]

*Then: the Trisagion; All-Holy Trinity; after "Our Father": "For Thine is the kingdom..." Lord, have mercy (12 times). Come, let us worship (thrice).*

*Then:* **"I believe in one God..."** *all the way to the end.*[^1320]

*After this, say:*

**"Of Thy Mystical Supper, O Son of God, receive me today as a communicant; for I will not speak of the mystery to Thine enemies,"**[^1321]

=== LEAF p432 ===
**"nor will I give Thee a kiss as did Judas, but as the thief do I confess Thee: Remember me, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy kingdom."**[^1322]

*Glory...*

*The King of heaven out of love for mankind appeared on earth and lived among men; for taking flesh from a pure Virgin and coming forth from Her with that which He assumed, one is the Son, double in nature, but not in person. Therefore, proclaiming Him in truth as perfect God and perfect Man, we confess Christ our God; Whom do thou beseech, O unwedded Mother, that He have mercy on our souls.*[^1323]

*Both now...*

*We have known God incarnate of thee,*[^1324]

=== LEAF p433 ===
*O Virgin Theotokos; do thou beseech Him for the salvation of our souls.*

*Then this prayer:*[^1325]

**"May the Lord God most merciful have compassion on thee. May the Lord Jesus Christ grant thee every good petition. May the Lord Almighty deliver thee from every assault. May the Lord teach thee. May the Lord give thee understanding. May the Lord help thee. May the Lord save thee. May the Lord defend thee. May the Lord cleanse thee. May the Lord fill thee with spiritual joy. May the Lord be a protector to thy soul and body. May the Lord most good and merciful, the Lover of mankind, grant thee forgiveness of sins. May the Lord Jesus Christ in the Day of Judgment have mercy on..."**

=== LEAF p434 ===
**"...thee, and all the days of thy life. Unto Him be glory, now and ever, and unto ages."**

*A second prayer, from the person of the sick one:*[^1326]

**"Cleanse me, O Lord, from my sins, Who for the sake of sinners didst shed Thy blood. O Master, Thou knowest that I am not worthy to look upon heaven, nor upon earth; but Thou Thyself hast said: 'He that eats My flesh, and drinks My blood, dwells in Me, and I in Him.'[^1327] Therefore, making bold, I come unto Thee in prayer; for Thou art a merciful God and the Lover of mankind; and unto Thee do we send up glory, with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."**

*A third prayer, similar to the second:*[^1328]

=== LEAF p435 ===
**"And I know that I am not worthy that Thou shouldest enter under the roof of the temple of my soul, for it is all desolate and burnt up, and Thou hast not with me a worthy place where to bow Thy head.[^1329] But since Thou desirest to be with me, trusting in Thy compassions I come unto Thee. Command the doors of my unworthy lips to be opened, that I may be satisfied with Thee alone. Enter into me, and cleanse me from every defilement of flesh and spirit; be Thou unto me a helper and protector, and account me worthy to stand on Thy right hand; by the prayers and supplication of our all-pure Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary,"**[^1330]

=== LEAF p436 ===
**"and of all the saints who from the ages have been well-pleasing unto Thee; for Thou art blessed unto ages. Amen."**

*Then the prayer against any defilement, which is said before holy communion:*[^1331]

**"O Lord our God, the only Good One and Lover of mankind, Who alone art holy and restest in the saints; Who by a vision revealed to Thy supreme Apostle Peter that nothing of the things created by Thee for the food and enjoyment of men is to be accounted defiled or unclean;[^1332] and Who through Thy chosen vessel, the Apostle Paul, didst command that all things are pure unto the pure:[^1333] do Thou Thyself, O all-holy Master, through the invocation of Thy dreadful and all-pure..."**

=== LEAF p437 ===
**"...name, and by the sign of the precious and life-creating Cross, bless and cleanse Thy servant [Name], who has been defiled by every satanic work, from every hostile thing, from every venomous serpent, from every iniquity, from every stumbling block,[^1334] from every deceit, from every vanity, from every infirmity, from every plague, from every evildoing of the adversary devil, by day and by night. And now vouchsafe Thy servant [Name], unworthy though he be, according to Thy mercy to partake of Thine all-pure Mysteries; but first cleanse his soul and body..."**

=== LEAF p438 ===
**"...from every defilement. And forgive him, O Lord, every transgression voluntary and involuntary, known and unknown, which he has sinned in all the days of his life, by word, or by deed, or by thought, until the present hour. And grant him, O Lord, this awesome ministry of the heavenly orders, and the communion of Thy life-creating Mysteries,[^1335] not unto judgment, nor unto condemnation, but unto the remission of sins, unto the communion of the Holy Spirit, unto the life of everlasting joy, which Thou hast prepared for Thy true servants. And now, O all-powerful Master, preserve Thy servant [Name] from every malice and sin; keep him..."**

=== LEAF p439 ===
**"...undefiled and blameless from every adverse thing. And grant him, O Lord, to serve Thee in holiness and righteousness, until his last day and hour and breath. For Thou art He that blesses and sanctifies all things, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."**

*And thus he gives communion to the sick person.[^1336] And after holy communion he says this thanksgiving prayer of Saint Cyril of Alexandria, from the person of him who has received:*[^1337]

**"May Thy holy Body, O Lord Jesus Christ, be unto me for life and sanctification; and Thy precious Blood for the cleansing of sins; and at..."**

=== LEAF p440 ===
**"...Thy holy judgment set me on Thy right hand; and let this thanksgiving be unto me for joy and health, and for the healing of soul and body; for Thou art blessed unto ages. Amen."**

*A prayer of Saint John Chrysostom, to the same effect:*[^1338]

**"O Master God, King of the ages and Creator of all, Who hast bestowed upon us good things, through the communion of Thine all-pure and life-creating Mysteries: I pray Thee, O Good One and Lover of mankind, preserve me under the shelter of Thy wings, and grant me with a pure conscience, unto my last breath, worthily to partake of Thy holy things, unto the remission of sins, and unto life..."**
