# PART XXIV: THE ORDER FOR THE BURIAL OF AN INFANT[^1598]

**Priest:** **Blessed is our God.**

*Then the Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. After "Our Father...":*[^1599]

**Priest:** **For Thine is the Kingdom...**

*Lord, have mercy (12 times). "Come, let us worship..." (thrice). Then:*

*Psalm 19:*[^1600]

The Lord hear thee in the day of affliction; the Name of the God of Jacob defend thee. May He send thee help from the sanctuary, and protect thee out of Zion. May He remember all thy sacrifice, and may thy whole burnt offering...

=== LEAF p562 ===
...be made fat. May the Lord grant thee according to thine heart, and fulfill all thy counsel. We will rejoice in Thy salvation, and in the Name of the Lord our God will we be magnified. The Lord fulfill all thy petitions. Now have I known that the Lord has saved His Anointed: He will hear Him from His holy heaven; in mighty deeds is the salvation of His right hand. Some [glory] in chariots, and some in horses: but we will call upon the Name of the Lord our God. They were tripped up and fell, but we have risen and are set upright. O Lord, save the king, and hear us in the day whensoever we call upon Thee.[^1601]

*Psalm 141:*[^1602]

=== LEAF p563 ===
With my voice unto the Lord have I cried, with my voice unto the Lord have I made supplication. I will pour out before Him my prayer, and my affliction before Him will I declare. When my spirit was fainting within me, then Thou knewest my paths. In this way wherein I walked have they hidden a snare for me. I looked upon the right hand and beheld, and there was none that knew me. Flight perished from me, and there is none that seeks after my soul. I cried unto Thee, O Lord; I said: Thou art my hope, my portion art Thou in the land of the living. Attend unto my prayer, for I am brought very low. Deliver me from them that persecute me, for they are stronger than I.[^1603]

=== LEAF p564 ===
Bring my soul out of prison, that I may give thanks unto Thy Name. The righteous wait for me, until Thou shalt recompense me.[^1604]

*Psalm 27:*[^1605]

Unto Thee, O Lord, will I cry; O my God, be not silent from me: lest at any time Thou be silent from me, and I become like them that go down into the pit. Hear, O Lord, the voice of my supplication, when I pray unto Thee, when I lift up my hands toward Thy holy temple. Draw me not away with sinners, and destroy me not with the workers of iniquity: who speak peace with their neighbors, but evils are in their hearts. Give them, O Lord, according to their deeds...

=== LEAF p565 ===
...and according to the wickedness of their endeavors. According to the works of their hands give Thou unto them; render their recompense unto them. Because they regarded not the works of the Lord, nor the works of His hands: Thou shalt destroy them, and not build them up. Blessed is the Lord, because He has heard the voice of my supplication. The Lord is my helper and my protector; in Him my heart hoped, and I was helped: and my flesh flourished again, and willingly will I give thanks unto Him. The Lord is the strength of His people, and He is the saving protector of His Anointed. Save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance: and shepherd them, and lift them up unto the age.[^1606]

*We sing: "Glory... Both now..."*[^1607]

=== LEAF p566 ===
*Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. After "Our Father...":*

**Priest:** **For Thine is the Kingdom...**

*And the Troparion, Tone 2:*[^1608]

Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good, Thy servant, and whatsoever he has sinned in life, forgive: for none is without sin, save only Thou Who art able to grant rest unto the departed.

*Theotokion:*[^1609]

Holy Mother of the ineffable Light, honoring thee with angelic hymns, we sing unto thee and magnify thee.

*Then the Kontakion:* **With the saints give rest...**[^1610]

*The priest's exclamation:*

**Priest:** **For holy art Thou, O our God...**

*And we sing:* **Holy God...**

*Prokeimenon, Tone 6:*

**Reader:** **Blessed is the way wherein thou walkest [today], O soul.**

*Verse:* **Return, O my soul, unto thy rest.**

*Epistle: The reading from the Epistle of Paul to the Corinthians:*

=== LEAF p567 ===
**Reader:** Brethren, not all flesh is the same flesh: but there is one indeed of men, another flesh of beasts, another of fish, and another of birds. There are also celestial bodies, and bodies terrestrial: but the glory of the celestial is one, and the glory of the terrestrial is another. There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars: for star differs from star in glory. So also is the resurrection of the dead: it is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption. It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power. It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body.[^1611]

=== LEAF p568 ===
There is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body. And so it is written: The first man Adam was made a living soul; the last Adam was made a life-giving spirit.[^1612]

*Alleluia.*[^1613]

*Verse:* **Blessed is he whom Thou hast chosen and taken, O Lord.**

*Verse:* **His soul shall dwell among good things.**

*The Gospel according to John:*

**Priest:** The Lord said unto the Jews who came unto Him: I am the bread of life: he who comes to Me shall not hunger, and he who believes in Me shall never thirst. But I said unto you, that ye also have seen Me, and believe not. All that the Father gives Me shall come to Me...

=== LEAF p569 ===
...and him that comes to Me I will in no wise cast out. For I came down from heaven, not to do Mine own will, but the will of the Father Who sent Me. And this is the will of the Father Who sent Me, that of all which He has given Me I should lose nothing, but should raise it up at the last day.[^1614]

*Then the litany:*

**Deacon:** **Have mercy on us, O God...** *and the rest. And the prayer:* **O God of spirits...** *The exclamation:* **For Thou art the Resurrection and the Life...** *(see fol. 230)*[^1615]

*After this the priest says this prayer:*[^1616]

**Deacon:** Let us pray to the Lord.

**Priest:** O Lord Who guardest infants in the present life, and in the age to come hast prepared for them...

=== LEAF p570 ===
...the spacious bosom of Abraham, and according to their purity the angelic radiant places wherein the souls of the righteous dwell: do Thou Thyself, O Master Christ, receive in peace the soul of Thy servant, the infant, [Name]. For Thou Thyself hast said: "Of such is the Kingdom of Heaven." For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship, with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.[^1617]

*Then also the Dismissal.*[^1618]
