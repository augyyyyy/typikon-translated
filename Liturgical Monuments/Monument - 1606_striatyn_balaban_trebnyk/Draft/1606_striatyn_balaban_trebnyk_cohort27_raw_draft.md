# PART XIV: THE ORDER PERFORMED WHEN SOMEONE WISHES TO CONFESS FROM THE BEGINNING[^544]

*First the spiritual father instructs him, saying thus:*[^545]

**Spiritual Father:** My child, who desirest to be renewed by honorable repentance,[^546] and to seek after thine original homeland, and to become a partaker of adoption as a son, from which thou hadst fallen away by transgression:[^547] hear, my child, how God awaits our repentance; through the Prophet He cries aloud unto us,[^548]

=== LEAF p264 ===

**Spiritual Father:** saying: After all these things, turn ye unto Me, and I will heal you.[^549] Therefore whosoever dies without repentance, he is as one that is not a Christian; since he believes neither in Baptism nor in the Holy Trinity. And for such a one it is not fitting for the priest to vest himself, nor to bring an oblation to the church for him.[^550] Hear, my child, and understand well: God is one, Who made all creation, having from nowhere any beginning, but Himself being the beginning of all things; having within Himself the co-unoriginate Word, in His own image timelessly, endlessly, enthroned together with the Father and with the Holy Spirit: that is to say, Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit,[^551]

=== LEAF p265 ===

**Spiritual Father:** three hypostases of like nature in one Godhead.[^552] And the Word of God, being God of God, for the sake of our salvation, having clothed His Godhead with flesh, was conceived without seed in the all-pure Virgin; He became doubly God and man together; Who willingly partook of our passions;[^553] having willingly received crucifixion and death; and He rose again the third day, and ascended into heaven, and sat at the right hand of the Father; and He shall come again to judge the living and the dead, and to render unto each according to his works.[^554] So hearing of that fearful hour to come, and of the terrible torments, we know the Scripture, and we are baptized into the Holy Trinity: in

=== LEAF p266 ===

**Spiritual Father:** the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, washing away sins from ourselves. And those who have sinned after Baptism [wash them away] through repentance,[^555] confessing unto God, Who knows each by name and knows the thoughts of all; Who so commanded the church presbyters in His stead to bear your burdens, even as the Apostle said: For they watch over your souls.[^556]

*And after this he enters into the church with fear and humility, and with folded hands; and the priest says unto him:*[^557]

**Priest:** Bow down unto God, unto Whom thou hast fled for refuge.

*And he bows down thrice. And the priest speaks first, and he [repeats] afterward:*[^558]

=== LEAF p267 ===

**Penitent [repeating after Priest]:** O God, cleanse me a sinner, and have mercy on me. Lord, I have sinned, have mercy on me. Countless times have I sinned: have mercy on me, and forgive me.[^559]

*And after this the priest takes the Holy Gospel and the Cross, and places them upon the analogion before the sanctuary [altar];*[^560] *and he that desires to repent comes, and lays his hands and head upon the Gospel;*[^561] *and the priest says:*

**Priest:** Blessed is our God, [always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.][^562]
