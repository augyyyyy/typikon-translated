[^3697]: Slavonic: зна́менїе напра́сно смр҃ти (sudden sign / token of death). In epidemic nosology, this refers to the appearance of buboes, carbuncles, or petechiae ("tokens"), whose sudden eruption signaled inevitable and swift demise.
[^3698]: A vivid contemporary description of the high mortality and communal collapse during plague outbreaks: multiple deceased family members carried out together on a single bier. The sentence continues onto fol. 603v (leaf p1222).
[^3699]: Slavonic: и҆ ни́же довлѣ́ти ѡ҆ста́вльшемсѧ ѿ лиша́ющихсѧ проси́ти бли́жнихъ. The contagion renders neighbors unable to help one another, severing the basic social bonds of mutual assistance.
[^3700]: Slavonic: вы́шшихъ недꙋ́га, translating Greek ὑπερτέρους τῆς νόσου ("above the disease"). The sentence continues onto fol. 604r (leaf p1223).
