*Another prayer of the same, in drought:*[^2406]

**Deacon:** *Let us pray to the Lord.*

**Priest:**
O Master, Lord God, Who didst hear Elijah the Tishbite, zealous for Thee,[^2407] and didst command the rain sent in season upon the earth to be withheld, and then again through his supplication didst grant a fruit-bearing rain unto it:[^2408] do Thou Thyself, O Master of all, entreated by Thine own compassion, grant a willing rain unto Thine inheritance;[^2409] and our transgressions...

=== LEAF p847 ===
...overlook, and send Thy rains upon the places that need them and pray for them, gladdening the face of the earth, for the sake of the poor of Thy people, the infants, and the cattle, and all others; for they wait upon Thee to give them food in due season.[^2410] For Thou art our God, a God to show mercy and to save; and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

**Priest:**

Have mercy upon the old, and the young, and the infants sucking the breasts; and upon Thy manservants and maidservants, sons and daughters; and upon all the beasts of the earth:[^2414] Who bringest forth bread for us for food, and green herb for the cattle.[^2415] Receive the supplication of all Thy people, and the sighing of the poor; and rebuke us not in Thine anger, neither...
