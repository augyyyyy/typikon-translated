The prayer evokes the narrow way of the holy fathers and requests divine grace upon the spirit, soul, and body of the candidate who has received the beginning of the holy habit (*нача́токъ ст҃го ѡ́браза*).
[^1697]: Physical leaf p602 (fol. 292v). *ПОСЛѢДОВАНЇЕ МАЛАГО ѠБРАЗА* (Gk. *Ἀκολουθία τοῦ μικροῦ σχήματος*). The Order of the Little Schema (or Little Habit), historically also designated as the Order of the Mantiya (*мантия*), constituting the full formal profession of monastic vows in the Byzantine-Ruthenian tradition.
[^1698]: Physical leaf p602 (fol. 292v). The striking of the wood (*оударе́нїе дре́ва слу́жбы*) refers to the liturgical sounding of the wooden semantron (*клепало* / *било* / Gk. *σήμαντρον*) to summon the brotherhood to the Hours and the Divine Liturgy, during which the tonsure is celebrated.
[^1699]: Physical leaf p602 (fol. 292v). The candidate divests himself in the church narthex (*притворъ*) of his customary secular or novice garments, symbolizing the putting off of the old man (cf. Ephesians 4:22; Colossians 3:9) before entering the nave for tonsure.
[^1700]: Physical leaf p603 (fol. 293r). The rubric specifies that the candidate stands before the holy doors clothed only in a simple inner tunic (*срачица* / Gk. *χιτώνιον*), barefoot, unbelted, and bareheaded, expressing absolute voluntary poverty, penitence, and surrender before God.
[^1701]: Physical leaf p603 (fol. 293r). The tonsure is integrated into the Little Entrance of the Divine Liturgy. Following the entrance, after the kontakion of the day at "Glory...", this special penitential troparion is chanted at "Now and ever...".
[^1702]: Physical leaf p603 (fol. 293r). Monastic tonsure troparion, Tone 1: *Ѡбъꙗ́тїа ѿча ѿвръстѝ ми потщи́сѧ* (Gk. *Τὰς πατρικὰς ἀγκάλας διανοῖξαί μοι σπεῦσον*), directly adapted from the parable of the Prodigal Son (Luke 15:11–32).
[^1703]: Physical leaf p604 (fol. 293v). While the brethren chant the troparion *Ѡбъꙗ́тїа ѿча* thrice, the candidate prostrates himself cruciformly upon the floor before the Royal Doors where the hegumen stands.
[^1704]: Physical leaf p604 (fol. 293v). Lighted tapers/candles are distributed to the brotherhood standing near the central pillars (*стлъпѡ́къ*), symbolizing the light of good works and the illumination of the angelic life.
[^1705]: Physical leaf p604 (fol. 293v). The catechetical exhortation and preliminary examination of monastic vows begins with the words of Christ: Matthew 11:28–29.
[^1706]: Physical leaf p605 (fol. 294r). Matthew 11:29.
[^1707]: Physical leaf p605 (fol. 294r). The hegumen solemnly warns the candidate that Christ, the Mother of God, and the heavenly host are invisibly present, and that judgment will be rendered not merely on verbal promises, but on actual observance of ascetic struggle.
[^1708]: Physical leaf p606 (fol. 294v). The rubric provides that if the monastery hegumen is an ordained hieromonk (*іере́й*), he conducts the interrogation himself; if he is not ordained, an ordained priest conducts the examination.
[^1709]: Physical leaf p606 (fol. 294v). The candidate articulates his primary monastic motive: desire for the ascetic/fasting life (*житїа по́стническаго* / Gk. *ἀσκητικοῦ βίου*).
[^1710]: Physical leaf p607 (fol. 295r). Traditional monastic interrogation formula: *Въи́стинноу добро̀ дѣ́ло и бл҃же́нно избра́ єси* (Gk. *Ὄντως καλὸν ἔργον καὶ μακάριον ἐξελέξω, ἀλλ᾽ ἐὰν καὶ τελειώσῃς αὐτό...*).
[^1711]: Physical leaf p607 (fol. 295r). Interrogation confirming free will: the candidate must affirm that he is not entering monastic life under compulsion, debt, fear of civil punishment, or worldly distress.
[^1712]: Physical leaf p607 (fol. 295r). The vow of renunciation of the world (*Ѡрица́еши ли сѧ ми́ра*), grounded in 1 John 2:15–17.
[^1713]: Physical leaf p608 (fol. 295v). The three fundamental monastic vows: lifelong perseverance / stability of place (*stabilitas loci*), chastity/virginity (*дѣтство и цѣломудрїе*), and obedience (*послушанїе*).
[^1714]: Physical leaf p608 (fol. 295v). The solemn catechetical instruction (*Та́же ѻглаше́нїе* / Gk. *Κατήχησις*) delivered by the priest/hegumen immediately following the examination of vows.
[^1715]: Physical leaf p609 (fol. 296r). The vows are described as an irrevocable written record inscribed by the holy angels, to be examined before the judgment seat of Christ at His Second Coming.
[^1716]: Physical leaf p609 (fol. 296r). The monastic way is termed "the most perfect life" (*съвръше́нное житїѐ* / Gk. *τελειοτάτη πολιτεία*) because it strives for the direct imitation of Christ's earthly life.
[^1717]: Physical leaf p609 (fol. 296r). 2 Corinthians 7:1: "Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God."
[^1718]: Physical leaf p610 (fol. 296v). Practical ascetical instruction detailing vigilance against demonic thoughts (*помысли* / Gk. *λογισμοί*), steadfastness in fasting, patience in sickness, and perseverance in prayer and vigils, continuing on leaf p611.
