[^4364]: Rear exterior wooden board covered in contemporary blind-tooled calfskin (physical leaf p1401), marking the final physical leaf and rear cover of the 1606 Striatyn Euchologion (*Trebnyk*). The tooling features an outer foliate roll, an inner panel divided by a saltire cross with four circular medallions depicting the Tetramorph (Evangelist symbols), upper and lower medallion compartments, and two riveted leather clasp straps on the fore-edge.
