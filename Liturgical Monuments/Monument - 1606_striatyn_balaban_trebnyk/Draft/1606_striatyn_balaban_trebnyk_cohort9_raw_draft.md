Because I kept silence, my bones waxed old through my crying all the day long. For day and night Thy hand was heavy upon me; I turned into misery when the thorn was pierced into me. I acknowledged my sin, and mine iniquity have I not hidden. I said: I will confess against myself mine iniquity unto the Lord; and Thou forgavest the ungodliness of my heart. For this shall every one that is holy pray unto Thee in a time acceptable;

*Epistle to the Romans:*[^134]

**Reader:**
Brethren, as many of us as were baptized into Christ were baptized into His death. Therefore we were buried with Him through baptism into death; that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life. For if we have become united with Him in the likeness of His death, so also shall we be partakers of His resurrection: knowing this, that our old man was crucified with Him, that the body of sin might be done away, that henceforth we should not serve sin. For he that is dead is justified from sin. Now if we died with Christ, we believe that we shall also live

=== LEAF p88 ===

with Him: knowing that Christ, having been raised from the dead, dies no more; death has dominion over Him no more. For in that He died, He died unto sin once; but in that He lives, He lives unto God. Likewise reckon ye also yourselves to be dead indeed unto sin, but alive unto God in Christ Jesus our Lord.[^135]
