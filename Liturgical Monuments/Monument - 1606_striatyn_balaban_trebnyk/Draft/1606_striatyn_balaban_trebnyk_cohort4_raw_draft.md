# PART II: CANONS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES AND OF THE GOD-BEARING HOLY FATHERS CONCERNING HOLY BAPTISM

[Fol. 10r. Headpiece: Renaissance woodcut engraving with strapwork, foliate scrollwork, and mythological figures flanking a central cartouche.]

CANONS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES AND OF THE GOD-BEARING HOLY FATHERS CONCERNING HOLY BAPTISM[^61]

*Canon 47 of the Holy Apostles says:*[^62]
If any bishop or presbyter shall baptize anew one who has true baptism, or shall not baptize one who has been defiled by the ungodly, let him be deposed: as mocking the Cross and the death of the Lord, and not distinguishing priests from false priests. Likewise also Canon 50 of the Council of Carthage ordains.[^63]

*Canon 49 of the Holy Apostles:*[^64]
To baptize into the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, according to the Lord's com-

=== LEAF p38 ===

mand, but not into three unoriginate, nor into three sons, nor into three parakletes: for one is the Unoriginate Father, by reason of having no cause; and one is the Son, by reason of His ineffable generation; and one is the Paraklete, the Holy Spirit, by reason of His procession from the Unbegotten.[^65] And those bishops or presbyters who do not so baptize incur the sentence of final deposition.

*And Canon 50:*[^66]
To accomplish three baptisms of one mystery, that is, three immersions in one baptism; and at each immersion to commemorate one Name of the Holy Trinity; but not a single baptism into the death of the Lord,

=== LEAF p39 ===

[a single baptism into the death of the Lord] administered: that is, the one being baptized immersed once, and in that single immersion glorifying the death of the Lord, as the Eunomian heresy, full of folly, ordains (which also Canon 7 of the Second Council mentions). For the Lord said not: "Baptize into My death"; but: *Go, teach all nations, baptizing them in the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit* [Matthew 28:19]; and any bishop or presbyter who does not do thus [the canon] destroys by deprivation of the priesthood.[^67]

*Canon 7 of the Second Ecumenical Council:*[^68]
Moreover, Canon 7 of the Second Ecumenical Council also very accurately ordains how it is fitting to receive those turning from heresies:

=== LEAF p40 ===

[accurately ordains: for having divided] all into two classes, it legislates that some should be anointed with myron, while others should be baptized from the beginning: namely, the Arians, Macedonians, Sabbatians, and Novatians (who call themselves the pure and the left), and the Quartodecimans (that is, the Tetradites), and the Apollinarians.[^69] Since, therefore, they perform the divine baptism equally with us, it is not deemed proper to baptize them again; but upon their written renunciation and anathematization of their heresy, [the canon] commands to anoint them with the holy myron: the forehead, the eyes, and the nostrils, the mouth, and the ears; and signing them, to say: "The seal of the gift of the Holy Spirit."[^70]
