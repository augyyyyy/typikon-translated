*Glory...*

May Thy Body and Thy most precious Blood, O my Savior, be unto me as fire and as light, consuming the substance of sin, and burning up the thorns of passions, and enlightening all of me who worship Thy Divinity.[^1035]

*Both now...*

God was incarnate of thy pure blood; whence every generation hymns thee, O Sovereign Lady, and the bodiless multitudes glorify thee, as having through thee clearly beheld the Sovereign of all, substantialized in humanity.[^1036]

=== LEAF p353 ===
O Master, Lord Jesus Christ our God, King of them that reign, and Lord of them that rule;[^1041] Fountain of life and immortality; Maker of all creation, visible and invisible; co-eternal and co-unoriginate Son of the unoriginate Father; Who for Thine exceeding goodness in the last days didst clothe Thyself in flesh, and wast crucified, and buried for us ungrateful and perverse ones,[^1042] and by Thine own Blood didst renew our nature corrupted by sin: Thyself, O immortal King, Master, Lover of mankind, accept my sinful repentance; incline Thine ear unto me, and hear my words.[^1043]

=== LEAF p354 ===
For I have sinned, O Lord, I have sinned against heaven and before Thee,[^1044] and I am not worthy to gaze upon the height of Thy glory; for I have provoked Thy goodness, having transgressed Thy commandments, and not having given ear unto Thy precepts. But Thou, O Lord, being forgiving, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy, hast not given me over to perish with mine iniquities, awaiting my conversion by every means. For Thou, O Lover of mankind, hast said by Thy prophet: *As I live, says the Lord, I desire not the death of the sinner, but rather that he turn and live.*[^1045] For Thou willest not, O Master, to destroy the creation of Thy hands, nor dost Thou take pleasure

=== LEAF p355 ===
in the perdition of mankind, but Thou willest that all should be saved, and come unto the knowledge of the truth.[^1046] Therefore I also, though I am unworthy of heaven and of earth, and of this fleeting life, having wholly subjected myself unto sin, and enslaved myself unto pleasures, and defiled Thine image; yet being Thy creature and Thy creation, I, wretched one, despair not of my salvation. But trusting in Thine immeasurable compassion, I draw near. Receive therefore even me, O Lord, Lover of mankind, as the harlot, as the thief, as the publican, and as the prodigal;[^1047] and take away my heavy burden of iniquities, Thou Who takest away the sins of the world,[^1048] and healest

Yea, O Lord Jesus Christ my God, let not the communion of Thine all-pure and life-creating

Mysteries be unto me for judgment, nor may I become weak in soul and body from partaking thereof unworthily; but grant me unto my last breath to receive without condemnation the portion of Thy Holy Things, for communion of the Holy Spirit, for provision on the way unto eternal life,[^1051] and for an acceptable defense at Thy dread judgment-seat; that I also, with all Thine elect, may be a partaker of Thine incorruptible and eternal good things, which Thou hast prepared for them that love Thee, O Lord, in which Thou art glorified unto ages of ages. Amen.[^1052]
