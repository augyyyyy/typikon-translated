*Another prayer of the same, said in sudden and terrible death:*[^2377]

**Deacon:** *Let us pray to the Lord.*

**Priest:**
O Master, Lord our God, Who art the inexhaustible fountain of compassions, the unfathomable depth of love for mankind, the impassable abyss of longsuffering and goodness; Who also, according to generations, bringest down wounds and plagues that befall the human race, for the benefit of well-measured love for mankind;[^2378]

=== LEAF p836 ===
...so that our unrestrained and swift propensity toward sin, being curbed as with a bridle by Thy divine fear, may not move disorderly against the divine image, nor wickedly encroach upon the nobility of the soul:[^2379] stay, we pray Thee, this sharp and untimely weapon of death brought upon our nature; lest Thou make us diminished more than all nations,[^2380] nor let us become a reproach to our intelligible and sensible enemies;[^2381] and lest cities become desolate, and marketplaces be consumed, and sacred assemblies, and feasts, and compositions of divine songs be silenced for the most part...

=== LEAF p837 ===
...and good and great houses, according to the Scripture, are made desolate, and there is no one living in them;[^2382] fear and trembling are come upon us, and the thick and dark gloom of this terrible death has covered all. We have not time, O Master, to weep as is customary for kinsmen who have passed away, and the end overtakes us; we seek others who would weep, and together are we given over to the grave; nor do we who are seen living think that we yet live; by reason of this terrible plague, we scarcely believe even ourselves; each one of us thinks that he has in himself the mark of death and the plagues, and before death we die grievously of fear.[^2383]

=== LEAF p838 ===
This is the appointed reward of our malice; behold, the fitting conclusion of our wicked and unclean deeds; these are the severe corrections for defilement, injustice, and perjury; this three-headed serpent, having plunged bitter teeth into us, has violently brought upon us corruption and death.[^2384] For because of these things, it is said, the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.[^2385] Therefore we are able neither to lift up the eyes of our mind, nor to pray purely, nor to draw down mercy; weighed down by the burden of conscience, as by certain iron fetters, each of us is distressed.[^2386] But deliver us not over unto the end, for Thy Name's sake, neither...

=== LEAF p839 ===
...break Thy covenant; nor let us, who are subject to passions, be deprived of time for repentance and conversion, for the sake of which Thou hast in Thy love for mankind brought upon us this deadly plague. We know that having many times promised repentance, and many times proved false, we have been condemned to be cut down like the barren fig tree;[^2387] but yet again have patience, O mankind-loving Husbandman; Who willest not the death of a sinner, but rather that he turn and live;[^2388] Who hast commanded that a brother's transgressions be forgiven seventy times seven; Who art able from the hard and barren stone to raise up children unto Abraham;[^2389]

=== LEAF p840 ===
...for unto Thee alone all things are possible which are impossible with men, O Master Who lovest mankind;[^2390] that in us also Thine all-holy Name may be glorified, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
