# PART L: EPISTLE OF JOHN, MOST HOLY BISHOP OF KITROS, UNTO THE MOST SACRED BISHOP OF DYRRACHIUM, CABASILAS: ON THE DISPENSATION OF FOODS IN THE HANDED-DOWN WEEKS, AND WHICH THESE ARE[^3889]

We dispense therefore Wednesday and Friday of the New Week, reckoning this entire week as one single day, and rendering it nothing less than Sunday in radiance; therefore also on every day, at Matins, the reading of the Resurrection Gospels takes place.[^3890] We dispense also the Twelve Days, for the repulse[^3891]

=== LEAF p1272 ===

...of the Armenian fast;[^3892] for these men, fasting the twelve days before Theophany, celebrate thus three feasts combined on the fifth of the month of January: the Annunciation, I say, the Nativity of Christ, and the Baptism; and even more so, not openly in some manner, but in shadowy guise, holding forth the ruinous and defiled glory of their heresiarch, and the name blasphemous unto Him, asserting therefrom that the dispensation of the Word of God was in appearance and not in truth.[^3893]

We dispense also the entire week before Meatfare, deeming another crafty custom of those same Armenians overthrown thereby; for[^3894]

=== LEAF p1273 ===

...they fast throughout this [week] also on account of the loss of a dog, whose namesake was Artzivourion, badly going before and proclaiming in cities or villages the coming of their teacher Sergius; for Artzivourion in the Armenian tongue means "messenger"; which [dog], when Sergius wept for him having been devoured by wild beasts, he commanded all the Armenians to fast during all those days with total abstinence unto the end, and to lament fittingly and mourn;[^3895] even though, craftily concealing the abomination, they strive to accomplish it in remembrance of the Ninevite fast.[^3896]

Throughout the entire week before Cheesefare, the so-called[^3897]

=== LEAF p1274 ===

...Middle-Fasters have a great fast; and for the refutation of them all, we dispense the fasting days equally with the non-fasting days.

From Renewal [Week] even unto Pentecost, on Wednesdays and Fridays, we do not praise fasting until the ninth [hour]; but even if we reject the eating of cheese and meat out of reverence for the Apostolic Canon, yet we receive oil and fish;[^3898] for on these days, even though making remembrance of the Lord's Crucifixion, we sing troparia suitable to the Crucifixion, yet we also join the Resurrectional [hymns] unto them, as also on others we intermingle penitential [hymns].[^3899]

The same do we also at the feast of the Transfiguration, in the midst of the fasting days[^3900]

=== LEAF p1275 ===

...it being in the midst of the fasting days; and at all the universal festivals of the Mother of God, if they fall on a Wednesday or on a Friday; and also of the honorable Forerunner and Baptist, and of the foremost Apostles Peter and Paul, and of any other saint who is celebrated and radiant.[^3901]

If, however, Mid-Pentecost fall on Wednesday, we are accustomed indeed to sing the Resurrectional troparia, and not those of the Cross; yet we do not make a dispensation of meat or cheese; nor do we read the Resurrection Gospel at Matins, as [we do] on the Wednesday and Friday of the New Week, and for this reason neither do we eat meat thereon.[^3902]

=== LEAF p1276 ===

Nor yet on the Wednesday before the Ascension; for although we sing the Resurrection Canon thereon, yet with it we also combine the Forefeast hymns of the Ascension, and neither do we read the Resurrection Gospel at Matins.[^3903]

Whenever therefore on Wednesday or Friday honorable feasts occur, it is meet to observe both the joyful character thereof and the reverence of the fast in the single dispensation of oil and fish; for in this manner wilt thou find all our Fathers to have walked.[^3904]

We dispense also the entire week after Pentecost in honor of the Holy Spirit, even as [we dispense] the other in honor of our Savior[^3905]

=== LEAF p1277 ===

...Jesus Christ; for the Holy Spirit is equally honorable, even as unto the Father, so also unto the Son; for by Them, through the Father's good pleasure, the mystery of our edification was ordered, and the illumination of the knowledge of God shone forth unto us.[^3906]

If the Eve of any Master's feast happen to fall on Wednesday or on Friday, it is not fitting to dispense the fast in the evening for the sake of the morrow's feast.[^3907]

He who dispenses the fast of Wednesday and Friday on account of bodily weakness shall be judged according to what the sixty-ninth Apostolic Canon prescribes; but he who is constrained by weakness may comfort the body with oil and[^3908]

=== LEAF p1278 ===

...fish; but as for meat and cheese, neither in the Great Forty Days shall he be forgiven to touch them, even if he be drawing his last breath; nor on any Wednesday or Friday. For when we dispense these, lest we be thought to share the mindset of the heterodox, eating both meat and cheese as an armor of intention—and especially if we happen to dwell among such—it is not unto these appetites that we indulge the belly![^3909]

On fasting days, from the ninth hour, after the customary prayers, with bread only and water, and vegetables[^3910]

=== LEAF p1279 ===

...or figs, or dates, do we satisfy bodily necessity; unless some illness distress him. For since even on the day before the Holy Theophany, it is commanded to fast until after the evening psalmody and the divine hierarchical service joined thereto, when we are accustomed to come unto bodily food: let it be without partaking of oil and fish.[^3911] For if this day—I say, the Eve of the Holy Theophany—happen to fall on a Sunday, Theophilus of Alexandria commands to partake of figs only, or dates, so much as to preserve ourselves from appearing[^3912]

=== LEAF p1280 ===

...to fast on Sunday: how then, if it fall upon another day, shall we touch richer food?

If, however, the day of the Holy Theophany itself fall on a Wednesday or on a Friday: it is sufficient for us to dispense the fast prescribed on such days, according to the sixty-ninth Canon of the Holy Apostles, partaking of oil and fish; but to eat meat or cheese is the invention of those who manifest belly-madness, and not the command of Apostolic legislation. For the Kingdom of God is not food and drink, and the rest.[^3913]
