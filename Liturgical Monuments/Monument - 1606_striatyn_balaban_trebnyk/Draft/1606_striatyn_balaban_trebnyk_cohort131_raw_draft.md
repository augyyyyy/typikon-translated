*18. Concerning Relatives by Marriage:*[^3981]

That which pertains unto known relatives from marital kinship is this: If anyone takes a widow as wife, and that woman has a daughter by another husband: she is a daughter unto him also, even as unto that woman; and it is fitting for him to keep himself exceedingly from her, as from his very own daughter. In like manner also a father-in-law from the wife of his son; and a son from his stepmother; and a brother-in-law from the wife of his brother, and of a brother-in-law.[^3982]

Let a son look upon the face of his mother with great fear, and let him render honor unto her always. Let a father take heed unto himself concerning the gaze upon his daughter, and let him keep himself.[^3983] In like manner also...

*Concerning Forbidden Marriages:*

...let brethren take heed unto themselves from their sisters.

Concerning relatives by marriage of whom we spoke above: when they shall assemble at weddings so that marriage may be sought, let it be carefully investigated, lest marital kinship should happen to exist, that is to say, in-laws. It is fitting to search with certainty and with much examination, even as the Divine law commands.[^3984] For there are in-laws of the first, second, and third degree; it is fitting to take heed concerning these. It is fitting also to know concerning this: that unto each spouse, husband and wife, the parents indeed of the husband—the father, and the mother, and the brethren—and the parents of his wife and her brethren, are called the first in-laws.[^3985]

=== LEAF p1304 ===

And the first cousins of the husband and of his wife: these are called second in-laws. And the second cousins of the wife and of the husband are again third in-laws.[^3986] And so the first in-laws are not taken in marriage unto the first in-laws, nor unto the second. In like manner also a son or a brother unto a husband does not mix with the first cousins of his wife; but the second cousins of the husband are taken and mixed without hindrance with the second and third cousins of his wife.[^3987]

Since therefore we have spoken concerning the degrees of lineage, and of natural blood, and of kinship, we say again: that until eight degrees of kinsmen and kinship are concluded, let heed be taken that there be no taking in marriage among them;...[^3988]

*Concerning the Degrees of Kinship:*

...and concerning kinship by marriage, that is, concerning in-laws: and these until the seventh degree is concluded, that is, of husband and wife.[^3989] If that woman has a first cousin, she is with the husband of that woman of the fourth degree. And the son of my first cousin is unto me a nephew, from my first cousin; and it is not fitting to take the first cousin of my wife.[^3990] Even as also my brother [cannot take] the sister of my wife, [nor] can he take my first cousin, nor my nephew, nor the nephew of my son, nor the first cousin of my son, nor the second cousins who are according to the lineage of my wife.[^3991] For a wife with her brethren are of the second degree; and her husband with...[^3992]

...his own first cousin, are of the fourth degree; and the four degrees do not assemble together, nor can marital union exist within them, unless it reaches unto the measure and number of the seventh degree. Neither can the nephew of my wife take the daughter of my sister. For he is called the great maternal uncle who is the sister's brother, that is, brother and sister. And indeed the brother of my mother is unto me a great maternal uncle, and this is of the third degree by measure.[^3993] In like manner also the sister of my mother is unto me a great maternal aunt. And the first cousin of my mother or of my father is unto me a lesser maternal uncle and a lesser aunt.[^3994] And if there be my third cousins, or a son,...

If anyone baptizes a child, and being godfather shall take him again as son-in-law unto his daughter: let them be separated; and the priest who blessed them, let him be deposed; for he is a godson according to the Holy Spirit.[^3996]

If anyone takes a wife, and lies with her, and thereafter defiles himself with his mother-in-law: then let that mother-in-law go and be tonsured in a monastery, and let her serve God concerning her soul; and let that husband live with his wife; let them labor concerning that sin for fifteen years, having received penance from their spiritual father.[^3997]

If anyone shall be the adoptive son of anyone, that is,...

...an adopted son: such a one cannot defile himself with the wife of his named father, nor with his concubine, whom I call a paramour; therefore he is not to marry any woman whatsoever from the lineage of his named father.[^3998]

It is not fitting during the Holy Great Forty Days to perform dedications of martyrs, or baptisms, or crownings; except on Saturday and on Sunday to perform without hindrance the memory of saints, and for the departed, and baptisms. But if it should occur on account of mortal sickness, then on whatever day it may be, let the infant or anyone whatsoever be baptized.[^3999]

...how to take and marry that daughter of his. And if she leaves her husband, and goes and takes another husband, and bears a daughter with him: then I have no way to marry that daughter, nor my son whom I have by another wife; for they are brethren. And if any woman whatsoever be betrothed unto my father, and thereafter, even if he has not lain with her: I have no way to marry her, for she is a mother unto me. In like manner also if she be the betrothed of my brother, even if he has not lain with her: yet she is a sister-in-law unto me, and I dare not touch her.[^4001]

The holy councils, of the holy Apostles, and of the venerable Fathers, commanded that a third marriage shall not exist. But if any priest, knowing this, blesses...

=== LEAF p1310 ===

...one who marries thrice: let him be deposed from the priesthood.[^4002]

If anyone does not bear children with his first wife, nor with his second: then let him now desist from marriage; if his second wife dies unto him, let him thus remain in purity in the Lord. But if being yet thirty-five or forty years of age, he marries again a third time: let such a one be forbidden unto repentance for five years, and thereafter let him receive communion. But if being young, he is thirty years of age: then let him do penance for three years. But if he is forty-five or fifty years old, and does this: the Holy Church of God does not receive such a one, as defiled by deceit.[^4003]
