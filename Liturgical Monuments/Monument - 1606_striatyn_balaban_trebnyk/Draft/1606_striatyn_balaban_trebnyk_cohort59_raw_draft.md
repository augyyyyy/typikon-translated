*Ode 6 (continued).*

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Thy martyrs, O Savior, endured many torments, their souls having been wounded with Thy love, O Holy One,[^1641] desiring Thine everlasting glory and the sweet communion of Thyself.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst rend asunder the hostile womb of death, O Savior, and didst raise up all the prisoners therein, bestowing life; vouchsafe the same, O Benefactor,[^1642] unto him that has been translated.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver Thy servant from the tears and sighing in Hades, O Savior; for as Thou alone art compassionate, Thou hast taken away every tear from every face of them that faithfully bless Thee.[^1643]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

He Who by nature is the Creator is fashioned...

=== LEAF p582 ===
...in thy womb; as God, being emptied, O all-spotless one, the only Immortal One dies by death for the salvation of all.[^1644]

*Katavasia [Heirmos]:*[^1645]

**Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are my iniquities, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and give ear unto me, O God of my salvation.**

*The Little Litany:*[^1646]

*Again and again in peace [let us pray to the Lord].*

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*[^1647]

**With the saints give rest, O Lord, unto the soul of Thy servant, where there is neither sickness, nor sorrow, nor sighing, but life everlasting.**

*Ikos:*[^1648]

Thou alone art immortal from the beginning, Who didst make and fashion man...

=== LEAF p583 ===
...but we mortals were fashioned from the earth, and unto the same earth shall we go, even as Thou didst command, Who didst fashion me, and saidst unto me: "Earth thou art, and unto earth shalt thou return"; whither all we mortals depart, making over the tomb the weeping song: Alleluia.[^1649]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* **The Hebrew children in the furnace...**[^1650]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

All the desire of the martyrs was extended unto the only Master; united unto Him in love, and singing: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1651]

*Troparion:*

Fill with joy and gladness him whom Thou hast translated, Thy servant, O Compassionate One, having vouchsafed him to cry unto Thee, and to sing: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1652]

*Glory...*

=== LEAF p584 ===
*Troparion:*

Thou grantest unto them that have departed in faith the brightness of the divine Kingdom, bestowing the garment of incorruption on them that cry: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1653]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Dissolving the curse of Eve, Thou didst take up Thine abode in the all-spotless Virgin, pouring forth the fountain of blessing unto them that cry: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1654]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* **The harmony of musical instruments...**[^1655]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Having passed through earthly sufferings, the true martyrs received heavenly crowns, crying unto the Savior: "Praise the Lord, all His works, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!"[^1656]

*Troparion:*

Descending into the lowest pit, Thou didst raise up with life-originating hand them that dwell in the tombs; and...

=== LEAF p585 ===
...grantest rest, O Compassionate One, unto Thy servant who in faith has fallen asleep before.[^1657]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

As the ever-existing fountain of life, as the torrent of delight,[^1658] Thou waterest Thy servant who in piety has been translated unto Thee, who sings and exalts Thee, O Christ, unto the ages.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

O Virgin Mary, Mother of God, who didst bear in the flesh God, the Savior of men: save them that in faith hymn thy childbearing, and exalt thee unto the ages.[^1659]

*Ode 9. Heirmos:* **Every hearing was affrighted...**[^1660]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Having unsubdued and unconquerable strength, O martyrs of Christ, ye brought to naught the godless command of the tyrants, and manifestly...

=== LEAF p586 ===
...were vouchsafed the heavenly Kingdom, being illuminated by the rays of the Trinity, O richly blessed ones.[^1661]

*Troparion:*

Bitter Hades was destroyed when Thou didst overthrow it, O Lover of mankind, and didst raise up them that slept there from of old; but even now, as Thou art good, vouchsafe him that has passed over unto Thee, the Merciful One, unto Thy never-setting light.[^1662]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Wholly art Thou sweetness, O Savior, wholly desire and insatiable love in truth; wholly art Thou ineffable beauty.[^1663] Therefore, be well pleased that he who has departed unto Thee should delight in Thy comeliness, and vouchsafe him Thy divine goodness.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Save me, O Mother of God, who didst bear Christ...

=== LEAF p587 ===
...my Savior, dual in nature but not in hypostasis,[^1664] only-begotten indeed of the Father, and from thee the firstborn of all creation, with Whom we all magnify thee.

*Katavasia [Heirmos]:*[^1665]

**Every hearing was affrighted at the ineffable condescension of God: how the Most High of His own will came down even unto flesh, having become man from the virginal womb; therefore, we the faithful magnify the all-pure Mother of God.**

*Then the Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. After "Our Father...": Troparia, Tone 4:*[^1666]

**With the spirits of the righteous who have been perfected, give rest to the soul of Thy servant, O Savior, keeping it in the blessed life which is from Thee, O Lover of mankind.**

=== LEAF p588 ===
*Troparion, Tone 4:*

In Thy resting place, O Lord, where all Thy saints repose, give rest unto the soul of Thy servant.[^1667]

*Glory...*

*Troparion, Tone 4:*

Thou art God Who descended into Hades and loosed the bonds of the chained: do Thou give rest to the soul of Thy servant.[^1668]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion, Tone 4:*

O only pure and spotless Virgin, who didst bear God without seed: entreat Him that our souls may be saved.[^1669]

*Then the diaconal petitions:*[^1670]

**Deacon:** Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee: give ear and have mercy.

**Deacon:** Again we pray for the repose and blessed memory and the remission of sins of the departed servant of God, [Name]...

=== LEAF p589 ===
**Deacon:** ...that every transgression, voluntary and involuntary, may be pardoned him;[^1671]

**Deacon:** That the Lord God may establish his spirit where all the righteous repose;

**Deacon:** The mercy of God, and the Kingdom of heaven, and the remission of his sins having asked for him, let us commend ourselves and one another, and all our life unto Christ God.

**Choir:** Unto Thee, O Lord.

**Deacon:** Let us pray to the Lord.

*Then the priest says the prayer secretly:*[^1672]

**Priest:** **[O God] of spirits and of all flesh, Who hast trampled down death, and abolished the devil, and given life unto Thy world: do Thou Thyself, O Lord, give rest to the soul of Thy departed servant, [Name], in a place of light...**[^1673]

=== LEAF p590 ===
**Priest:** **...in a place of verdure, in a place of repose, whence sickness, sorrow, and sighing have fled away; and every transgression committed by him, in word, or deed, or thought, do Thou, as the good God and Lover of mankind, forgive. For there is no man that shall live and not sin against Thee; for Thou alone art without any sin, Thy righteousness is righteousness forever, and Thy word is truth.**[^1674]

*Exclamation:*[^1675]

**Priest:** **For Thou art the resurrection, and the life, and the repose of Thy departed servant ever to be remembered, [Name], O Christ our God; and unto Thee we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father...**[^1676]
