[^2002]: Physical leaf p711 (`Page_0711.jpg`, fol. 341r). The printed text contains the typographic error `пора́кѡщенныхъ` (*porakoshchennykh*) for `пора́бо́щенныхъ` (*poraboshchennykh*, "enslaved"), reflecting a graphic confusion between Cyrillic `б` and `к` in the Striatyn workshop. The Theotokion contrasts bondage under the law of sin (cf. Rom. 7:23, 8:2) with justification through grace. The phrase "Book of Life" (`въ кни́ѕѣ живо́тнѣй`) alludes to LXX Ps. 68:29 and Rev. 20:12, 21:27.

[^2003]: Physical leaf p712 (`Page_0712.jpg`, fol. 341v). The first strophe of the 6th Gradual Antiphon (Tone 6 Anabathmoi, first antiphon) paraphrases LXX Ps. 122:1 ("Unto Thee have I lifted up mine eyes, unto Thee that dwellest in heaven").

[^2004]: Physical leaf p712 (`Page_0712.jpg`, fol. 341v). Paraphrases LXX Ps. 122:2–3 ("for we are utterly filled with contempt"). The sticheron sentence continues across the leaf boundary onto leaf 713 (`Page_0713.jpg`).

[^2006]: Physical leaf p713 (`Page_0713.jpg`, fol. 342r, signature єє є҃). Conclusion of the first sticheron of Tone 6 from leaf 712. The phrase "land of the living" (`въ землѝ живы́хъ`) alludes to LXX Ps. 26:13 and Ps. 114:9; "beloved tabernacles" (`въ села̀ възлю́бленнаѧ`) cites LXX Ps. 83:2.

[^2007]: Physical leaf p713 (`Page_0713.jpg`, fol. 342r). The second sticheron of Tone 6 emphasizes Christ's voluntary Passion as the source of eternal nourishment (`пи́щꙋ вѣ́чнꙋю`) and forgiveness of sins for the departed priest.

[^2008]: Physical leaf p714 (`Page_0714.jpg`, fol. 342v). Tone 6 sticheron for *Glory...*, professing Christ's sovereign dominion over both the living and the dead (cf. Rom. 14:9).

[^2009]: Physical leaf p714 (`Page_0714.jpg`, fol. 342v). Theotokion of Tone 6 expressing Chalcedonian Christology: Christ born of the Virgin "in two essences and in one hypostasis" (`въ двою̀ соу́ществоу, въ є҆ди́нои же ѵ҆по́стаси` / Greek: ἐν δύο οὐσίαις, μιᾷ δὲ τῇ ὑποστάσει). Sentence continues onto leaf 715 (`Page_0715.jpg`).

[^2010]: Physical leaf p715 (`Page_0715.jpg`, fol. 343r). Conclusion of the Tone 6 Theotokion from leaf 714, affirming the perpetual virginity of the Mother of God (*post partum virgo*, `по рождествѣ̀ съхра́нша`).

[^2011]: Physical leaf p715 (`Page_0715.jpg`, fol. 343r). The 7th Gradual Antiphon (Tone 7 Anabathmoi, first antiphon) paraphrases LXX Ps. 125:1–6 ("When the Lord turned again the captivity of Zion... Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as streams in the south. They that sow in tears shall reap in joy"). The Trinitarian strophe enumerates the gifts of the Holy Spirit (cf. Isa. 11:2). The rubrical cue `С҃ . И҆ н҃ .` abbreviates `Сла́ва, И҆ ны́нѣ` (*Glory... Both now...*).

[^2012]: Physical leaf p716 (`Page_0716.jpg`, fol. 343v). Headpiece for Tone 7 Stichera of the Priest's Funeral. The model heading `Оу҆жѐ невозбра́нше` reflects the Ruthenian recension's witness of the Greek automelon Οὐκέτι κωλυόμεθα ("No longer are we forbidden").

[^2013]: Physical leaf p716 (`Page_0716.jpg`, fol. 343v). The second sticheron of Tone 7 invokes hesychastic imagery of uncreated divine light (`бж҃тве́наго свѣ́та твое́го лꙋча́ми`) and immaterial brightness (`въ невеще́ственѣй свѣ́тлости`). Line 16 ends with the rubrical cue `Сла́ва .` (*Glory...*), leading into leaf 717.

[^2014]: Physical leaf p717 (`Page_0717.jpg`, fol. 344r). Tone 7 sticheron for *Glory...* The rare term `неиздрї́мое` corresponds to `неиздря́емое` (inexhaustible / unemptiable, Greek: ἀδαπάνητον). The phrase `въ дѣ́тве́нѣмъ чръто́зѣ ꙗ҆́ко мл҃дъ` represents the soul entering the heavenly bridal chamber in pure childlike innocence (cf. Matt. 18:3).

[^2015]: Physical leaf p717 (`Page_0717.jpg`, fol. 344r). Theotokion of Tone 7 expounding the Pauline theology of justification by Christ over the ceremonial Law (cf. Rom. 3:20, 8:3–4, Gal. 2:16), invoking the Theotokos' maternal boldness (`мт҃рьнимъ дръзнове́нїемъ`) for the soul of the departed priest.

[^2016]: Physical leaf p718 (`Page_0718.jpg`, fol. 344v). The 8th Gradual Antiphon (Tone 8 Anabathmoi, first antiphon) paraphrases LXX Ps. 128:1–6 ("Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth... Let them be as grass upon the housetops, which withers before it is plucked up"). The Trinitarian strophe confesses the Holy Spirit as "Light of Light, God great" (`свѣ́тъ ѿ свѣ́та бг҃ъ вели́къ`).

[^2017]: Physical leaf p718 (`Page_0718.jpg`, fol. 344v). Headpiece for Tone 8 Stichera of the Priest's Funeral. Melodic model `Ѡ҆ пресла́вное чю́до` (Greek: Ὢ τοῦ παραδόξου θαύματος, "O most marvelous wonder"). The hymn uses imperial chancellery imagery: Christ signs the charter of emancipation of the Church (`свобожде́нїе`) by dipping His fingers into His own Blood upon the Cross as vermilion/cinnabar ink (`ша́ромъ чръвле́нымъ`, Greek: κινναβάρεως / πορφύρας). The sentence continues onto leaf 719 (`Page_0719.jpg`).

[^2018]: Physical leaf p719 (`Page_0719.jpg`, fol. 345r). Conclusion of the first sticheron of Tone 8 from leaf 718. The phrase "number with the firstborn" (`съ пръворо́дными причтѝ`) evokes Heb. 12:23 ("to the general assembly and church of the firstborn registered in heaven").

[^2019]: Physical leaf p719 (`Page_0719.jpg`, fol. 345r). The second sticheron of Tone 8 presents Christ as the High Priest Who is simultaneously the Offerer and the Offered Sacrifice (cf. Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom, Prayer of the Cherubic Hymn: "For Thou art the Offerer and the Offered, the Receiver and the Received"). The phrase "streams of delight" (`потѡ́ци пи́щїи`) echoes LXX Ps. 35:9 ("Thou shalt make them drink of the torrent of Thy delights").

[^2020]: Physical leaf p719 (`Page_0719.jpg`, fol. 345r). The third sticheron of Tone 8 meditates upon God's providence in determining human lifespan. The phrase "waters of rest" (`на вода́хъ поко́йныхъ`) quotes LXX Ps. 22:2 (ἐπὶ ὕδατος ἀναπαύσεως). Sentence continues onto leaf 720 (`Page_0720.jpg`).

[^2021]: Physical leaf p720 (`Page_0720.jpg`, fol. 345v). Conclusion of the third sticheron of Tone 8 from leaf 719. The phrase "voice of rejoicing and praise" (`гла́съ ра́дованїа и҆ хвале́нїа`) quotes LXX Ps. 41:5 (φωνὴ ἀγαλλιάσεως καὶ ἐξομολογήσεως).

[^2022]: Physical leaf p720 (`Page_0720.jpg`, fol. 345v). Tone 8 sticheron for *Glory...*, professing the full consubstantiality and co-enthronement of the incarnate Word with the Father and the Holy Spirit (`є҆диное́стве́но ѻ҆ц҃ꙋ, и҆ дх҃ови съпрⷭ҇то́лно`).

[^2023]: Physical leaf p720 (`Page_0720.jpg`, fol. 345v). Concluding Theotokion of the Tone 8 Stichera. The phrase "sweet voice of those who feast" (`сла́дкїй гла́съ пра́здноующихъ`) echoes LXX Ps. 41:5 (φωνὴ ἑορτάζοντος). This marks the completion of the 8-Tone cycle of Gradual Antiphons and Stichera in the Funeral Service for Priests, directly preceding the deacon's litany on leaf 721 (Cohort 73).
