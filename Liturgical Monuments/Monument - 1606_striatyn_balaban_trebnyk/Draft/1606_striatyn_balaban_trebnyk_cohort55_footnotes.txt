[^1564]: Concluding strophe of the Tone 6 Doxastikon (*но̀ прїидѣ́те всѝ любѧ́щїи мѧ̀...*), articulating the equality of master and servant before the divine tribunal; cf. Col. 3:25; Eph. 6:9.
