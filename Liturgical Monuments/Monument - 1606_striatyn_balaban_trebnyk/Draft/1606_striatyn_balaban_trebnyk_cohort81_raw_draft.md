And grant unto it the grace of redemption, the blessing of the Jordan; make it a fountain of incorruption, a gift of sanctification, a remission of sins, a healing of infirmities, a destruction of demons, unapproachable by adversarial powers, filled with angelic might;[^2281] that all who draw of it and partake of it may have it for the purification of soul and body, for the healing of afflictions, for the sanctification of homes, and for every suitable benefit. For Thou art our God, Who by water...
