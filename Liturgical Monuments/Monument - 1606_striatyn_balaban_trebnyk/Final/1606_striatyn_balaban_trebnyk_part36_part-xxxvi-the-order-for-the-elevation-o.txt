# PART XXXVI: THE ORDER FOR THE ELEVATION OF THE PRECIOUS CROSS [^2942]

[Woodcut headpiece at the head of the division: The Crucifixion of our Lord Jesus Christ with the Most Holy Theotokos and Holy Saint John the Theologian standing by, in an oval medallion surrounded by Renaissance foliage ornament and angelic faces][^2941]

*While the brethren sing the Doxology, the Priest vests himself in full priestly vestments.*[^2943] *And he comes unto the Holy Table with the censer, a taper-bearer going before him, and he censes there. And he takes the Precious Cross with the diskos upon his head, two taper-bearers preceding him.*[^2944] *And he carries it as far as the royal doors, that is, the great church doors; and there he awaits the completion (Rubric continued)* ...of the Doxology. And when they begin to sing: "Holy God," the Priest comes unto the midst of the church, near unto the royal doors; for there a four-legged analogion has been prepared, covered with an honorable cloth. And he says:[^2945]

> **Priest:** **"Wisdom! Upright!"**[^2946]

*And as we sing the Troparion:*[^2947]

> **Choir:** **"Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance, granting victories unto the Orthodox Christians over adversaries, and by Thy Cross preserving Thy commonwealth."**

*And the Priest places the diskos with the Precious Cross upon the analogion. And having censed the Cross cruciformly, he makes three bows, even if it be Saturday or Sunday.*[^2948]

*And taking the Precious Cross in his hands with fragrant basil, he stands before the analogion, facing eastward, and says in the hearing of all:*[^2949]

> **Priest:** **"Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy[^2950] according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee, hear and have mercy."**[^2951]

*And he begins the first hundred: "Lord, have mercy." And the Priest signs with the Cross thrice.*

*Then he inclines his head, so that it is a span's distance from the ground; and slowly he rises, until the hundred "Lord, have mercy" are completed.*

*And when it reaches ninety-seven "Lord, have mercy," then the Ekklesiarches raises his voice. And the Priest stands upright. And as we sing with a most elevated voice: "Lord, have mercy" thrice, so as to complete the hundred.*[^2952]

*And the Priest turns unto the south side, and signs with the Cross thrice, and says:*[^2953]

> **Priest:** **"Again we pray for our pious and God-preserved Tsar [Name], for his dominion, victory, endurance, peace health, and salvation, and remission of sins; and that the Lord our God will especially prosper and guide him in all things, and subdue under his feet every enemy and adversary, let us all say."**

*And we begin the second hundred: "Lord, have mercy." And the Priest performs the second elevation, as previously indicated; and again he turns unto the west, and says:*[^2954]

> **Priest:** **"Again we pray for our Archbishop [Name], and for the remission of sins for our spiritual fathers, and for all our brotherhood in Christ, let us all say."**

*And we begin the third hundred. And when that hundred also is completed, the Priest turns unto the north side, and says:*[^2955]

> **Priest:** **"Again we pray for every Christian soul that is afflicted and mistreated, requiring God's mercy and help; for their health, and salvation, and the remission of their sins, let us all say."**

*And the fourth elevation takes place according to the former manner, while we say the fourth hundred: "Lord, have mercy."*[^2956]

*Thereafter the Priest turns again unto the east, saying:*

> **Priest:** **"Again we pray for all who minister and have ministered in this holy monastery, for their health, and salvation, and the remission of their sins, let us all say."**

*And we begin the fifth hundred, while the Priest performs the fifth elevation. At each elevation the Priest signs with the Cross toward the people thrice, cruciformly. And upon the completion of the elevation"*[^2957] *(Completion of the elevation continued)* ...we sing the Kontakion, Tone 4:

> **Choir:** **"Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross of Thine own will, bestow Thy compassions upon the new commonwealth called by Thy name, O Christ God; gladden Orthodox Christians by Thy power, granting them victory over adversaries, having Thine alliance as a weapon of peace, an unconquerable trophy."**[^2958]

*While the brethren sing the Kontakion, the Priest places the Precious Cross with the diskos upon the analogion. And he bows together with the superior twice, and kisses the Precious Cross. And after the kissing, one prostration; then toward both choirs. And so in order all the brethren, two by two according to their rank,*[^2959] *singing:*

> **Choir:** **"Before Thy Cross we bow down, O Master, and Thy holy Resurrection we glorify."**

*And upon the completion, we sing the Stichera, Tone 2:* **"Come, ye faithful..."** *which look behind (see fol. 377)[^2960]. And after the veneration and kissing of the Cross, the Litany, and the rest in order. Then the Dismissal.*

(*Fol. 488r. Woodcut headpiece at the head of the division: A pelican in its piety feeding its young with its blood within an oval wreath, flanked by Renaissance strapwork, scrolling acanthus, and grotesque figures, inscribed: (*"СПЛА ЖИВОТ"*)*)[^2961]

### Notes

[^2942]: Major section title: `ЧИ́НЪ НА ВЪЗДВИЖЕ́НЇЄ Че́стнаго Крⷭ҇та •` (The Order for the Elevation of the Precious Cross), celebrated on September 14 (Universal Exaltation of the Precious and Life-Creating Cross).
[^2941]: Physical folio 485r (Leaf p999) opens with a monumental woodcut headpiece (100 × 50 mm) depicting the Crucifixion at Golgotha inside an oval wreath, flanked by angels and Renaissance foliage ornament, characteristic of the Striatyn printing house of Teodor and Hedeon Balaban.
[^2943]: Rubric: During the chanting of the Great Doxology at Matins, the officiating priest vests in full sacerdotal vestments (*sticharion, epitrachelion, zone, epimanikia, phelonion*), reflecting the high solemnity of the Elevation ritual.
[^2944]: Rubric: The Priest censes the Holy Table where the Cross has rested in place of the Gospel book, places the diskos bearing the Precious Cross upon his head, and processes solemnly to the Royal Doors, preceded by two candle-bearers. Spanning across onto leaf p1000 (`славосло́вїа...`).
[^2945]: Rubric: At the singing of the final "Holy God" (*Trisagion*) of the Great Doxology, the priest carries the Cross into the center of the church to the prepared four-legged analogion (*четвероножному налою*) covered with an honorable silk cloth (*паволокою*).
[^2946]: Elevation exclamation: `Премоу́дрость про́сти •` (Greek: *Σοφία, ὀρθοί*), proclaimed by the Priest as he raises the diskos with the Cross before the congregation.
[^2947]: Troparion of the Cross: `Сп҃сѝ Гдⷭ҇и лю́ди своѧ̑` (Greek: *Σῶσον, Κύριε, τὸν λαόν σου*), Tone 1.
[^2948]: Rubric: The priest places the diskos upon the analogion, censes it cruciformly on all four sides (`крⷭ҇та ѡ҆́бра́зно`), and makes three bows/prostrations, noted specifically as obligatory even on Saturday or Sunday when full prostrations are normally prohibited by ancient canons.
[^2949]: Rubric: The priest takes the Precious Cross decorated with fragrant basil leaves (`съ благоуха́нными васи́льками` / Greek: *μετὰ βασιλικοῦ*) into his hands, stands before the analogion facing eastward, and begins the first of the five ceremonial elevations.
[^2950]: Opening petition of the Elevation: `Поми́лꙋй на́съ Бж҃е повели́цѣй мл҃ти...` (Greek: *Ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς, ὁ Θεός, κατὰ τὸ μέγα ἔλεός σου*), the first petition facing east, during which the people chant "Lord, have mercy" one hundred times as the Cross is slowly lowered and elevated.
[^2951]: Elevation of the Precious Cross, First Elevation (facing East): concluding clause of the first petition (`твое́й , мо́лимтисѧ оу҆слы́ши и҆ поми́лꙋй`), followed by the first hundred of *Lord, have mercy* (*Господи помилуй*). The priest signs with the Cross thrice and slowly bows until his head is one span (*пядь*, approximately 18–20 cm) from the ground, then gradually straightens as the hundred petitions are chanted.
[^2952]: Rubric: at count 97 of the first centenary, the Ekklesiarches cues the choir and priest, who stands fully upright while the remaining three repetitions of *Lord, have mercy* are chanted with an elevated voice (*высоча́йшимъ гла́сомъ*) to complete the hundred.
[^2953]: Station to the South: the priest turns southward and signs with the Cross thrice before intoning the second petition for the sovereign and state (`Є҆щѐ мо́лимсѧ ѡ҆ бл҃гочти́вѣмъ , и҆ бг҃охрани́мѣмъ цр҃и на́шемъ...`).
[^2954]: Second Elevation (facing South) and Station to the West: following the second centenary, the priest turns westward to intone the third petition for the local archbishop, spiritual fathers, and monastic brotherhood.
[^2955]: Third Elevation (facing West) and Station to the North: following the third centenary, the priest turns northward to intone the fourth petition for all afflicted Christian souls (`Є҆щѐ мо́лимсѧ ѡ҆ всѧ́кой дꙋши хрⷭтїа́нстѣй...`).
[^2956]: Fourth Elevation (facing North): the choir chants the fourth centenary as the Cross is bowed and raised toward the north in the same manner as the previous stations.
[^2957]: Fifth Elevation (facing East): the priest returns to the eastward orientation for the final petition (for the monastery and serving clergy) and fifth centenary. At every station, the rubric requires the priest to bless the faithful thrice cruciformly with the basil-adorned Cross (`крⷭто́мъ къ наро́домъ , г҃ , крⷭта ѡ҆бра́зно`).
[^2958]: Kontakion of the Exaltation of the Cross: *Tone 4: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross of Thine own will..."* (`Възнесы́йсѧ на крⷭтъ во́лею` / Greek: *Ὁ ὑψωθεὶς ἐν τῷ Σταυρῷ ἑκουσίως*). The text in the Balaban Trebnyk reflects the received Byzantine/Ruthenian recension.
[^2959]: Ceremony of Veneration: the priest and superior (*престатель*, hegumen) make two bows, kiss the Precious Cross upon the analogion, make one metania, and bow to both monastic choirs. The brethren then venerate two by two while chanting the liturgical troparion *Кресту Твоему покланяемся Владыко* (Greek: *Τὸν Σταυρόν σου προσκυνοῦμεν, Δέσποτα*).
[^2960]: Folio cross-reference: the rubric notes *Ихже смотри на задѣ на листѣ то҃з* ("which look behind on folio 377"), referring back to physical leaf p783 in the codex for the full hymnographic text of the aposticha stichera *Tone 2: "Come, ye faithful, let us venerate the life-giving wood..."* (`Прїидѣ́те вѣ́рнїи , животворѧ́щему дре́вꙋ поклони́мсѧ`).
[^2961]: Woodcut emblem: fol. 488r opens with a Renaissance strapwork headpiece featuring a pelican in her piety in an oval cartouche, piercing her breast to feed her chicks with her own blood. In Christian iconology, this is a prominent Eucharistic and Christological symbol, with the motto *СПЛА ЖИВОТ* ("The Savior is Life").
