### Notes

[^1]: Hedeon Balaban (c. 1530–1607), Orthodox Bishop of Lviv, Halych, and Kamianets-Podilskyi from 1569 until his death, prominent defender of Orthodoxy who opposed the Union of Brest.
[^2]: The title of Exarch of the Ecumenical Throne was conferred upon Bishop Hedeon Balaban by Patriarch Jeremiah II Tranos of Constantinople during his patriarchal visitation of the Ruthenian lands in 1589.
