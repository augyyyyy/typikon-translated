*When the Priest makes the sign of the cross over the suffering of an infirmity with the holy spear:*[^2996]

**"When Thou wast crucified, O Christ, tyranny perished, the power of the enemy was trampled down; for neither an angel nor a man, but Thou Thyself, O Lord, didst save us."**

*Glory:*

**"Thou hast redeemed us from the curse of the law by Thy precious Blood; having been nailed to the Cross, and pierced with a spear, Thou didst pour forth immortality unto men: O our Savior, glory to Thee."**[^2997]

*Both now:*

**"With a spear Thy side was pierced, O my Christ; and her that was fashioned from man's rib, who became the cause of ruin unto all men, Thou didst deliver from the curse."**

*Glory, and both now, and the dismissal.*
