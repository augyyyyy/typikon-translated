*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

=== LEAF p826 ===
More honorable than the Cherubim, and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim, who without corruption didst bear God the Word: thee, truly the Mother of God, do we magnify.[^2354]

*Troparion:*
Receive the supplications of those who flee unto thee, as thou art good, and be thou an intercessor for us; stand forth, O Sovereign Lady, and loose our afflictions; for as a Mother thou art able to do all things, O Lady.[^2355]

*The Lady:*
O compassionate Son of Mine, with Thine hands Thou didst raise up these who were dead, and Thou didst feed them with Thine own flesh; Thou didst marvelously make Me a Mother unto these just ones; therefore receive My supplication, O My Word.[^2356]

*The Master:*
What debt, O Mother, dost Thou ask now?

=== LEAF p827 ===
What mercy dost Thou hasten to obtain from Me? These are unworthy of any love of mankind; they have proved false to the divine kinship.[^2357]

*The Lady:*
I know this, O Master and Son; but having repented, they now fall down, as Thou seest; they weep and lament, all lacerating themselves; My womb cannot endure to behold their wounds.[^2358]

*The Master:*

*The Lady:*
