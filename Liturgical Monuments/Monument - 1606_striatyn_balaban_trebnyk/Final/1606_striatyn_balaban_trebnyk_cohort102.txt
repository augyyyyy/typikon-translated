*Prayer over a youth who has apostatized, defiled himself, and is repenting:*[^2973]

**Priest:** **"O Lord God Almighty, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Who desirest that all men should be saved and come unto the knowledge of the truth;[^2974] Who for the sake of the lost sheep didst send Thine Only-begotten Son into the world as Savior and Deliverer, that He might seek out and save Thy likeness: unto Thee we fall down, unto Thee we pray: receive Thy servant [Name], who flees unto Thy compassions, who indeed received from youth the right and blameless faith in Thee through holy Baptism, yet has fallen away from it..."** (continues onto leaf p1012)

=== LEAF p1012 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...through childhood or some other circumstance; yet who now returns, and through repentance and acknowledgment flees again unto Thy goodness: pour out upon him the mercy of Thy love for mankind. Receive him as the prodigal returning unto Thee, his God and Father; and take away from him every deception and enemy remembrance; and unite him unto the flock of Thy rational sheep. Adorn him even now with the glory of the all-pure name of Thy Christ; make him a partaker with the faithful of Thy most pure priesthood;[^2975] unto the remission of the sins previously committed by him, unto..."** (continues onto leaf p1013)

=== LEAF p1013 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...the preservation of things to come, and unto the protection of soul and body. Grant him in every place and time to flee unto Thee, and to run unto Thy churches, and to do Thy well-pleasing commandments; that by good works he may be an heir of the Heavenly Kingdom. Whereunto vouchsafe us also with him, through the compassions and grace of Thy Christ, with Whom Thou art blessed, together with Thine All-Holy, Good, and Life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."**[^2976]

*The Priest:*

**Priest:** **"Peace be unto all."**

*The Deacon (or Priest):*

**Deacon:** **"Bow your heads unto the Lord."**

=== LEAF p1014 ===

**Priest:** **"O Thou Who didst bow the heavens and come down for the salvation of the human race; Who by Thy Cross didst free us from the ancestral curse and lead us up unto the blessed life; Who didst say that there is joy in heaven over one sinner that repents:[^2977] do Thou Thyself now also receive Thine own lost rational sheep, and number him unto Thy flock. Drive away from him the ravening wolves; vouchsafe him to tread upon serpents and scorpions, and upon all the power of the enemy; protected by Thy Cross, preserved by the glory of Thy name, which has been named upon us, Thy servants. For Thou art our God, a God to show mercy and to save..."** (continues onto leaf p1015)

=== LEAF p1015 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...and unto Thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."**[^2978]

*And taking the holy Myron, he signs him both on the forehead, and on the hands, and on the feet.*[^2979]

*And he says this prayer:*

*Let us pray to the Lord.*

**Priest:** **"O Lord God Almighty, Who through the sending of Thine All-Holy Spirit didst fill all the holy disciples with grace; Who, having turned back this Thy servant [Name] from the path of error, and guided him unto Thine own faith, and vouchsafed him the anointing of this Myron of the sweet fragrance of Thy Holy Spirit: preserve him in Thy holiness; grant him to walk according to Thy will; and..."** (continues onto leaf p1016)

=== LEAF p1016 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...vouchsafe him to be a partaker with us of Thy holy Mysteries; and having preserved the right faith in Thee, that he may be worthy of Thy Heavenly Kingdom; through the grace of Thy Christ, with Whom is due unto Thee all glory, honor, and worship, unto ages. Amen."**

*Prayer when thou wishest to catechize a pagan:*[^2980]

*Having bent his knees before the doors, the Priest also signs him thrice.*

*And the Priest says the prayer:*

*Let us pray to the Lord.*

**Priest:** **"Blessed art Thou, O Lord, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Who from all the nations hast chosen for Thyself a peculiar people, zealous of good works. Do Thou Thyself bless this Thy servant..."** (continues onto leaf p1017)

=== LEAF p1017 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...who has come unto Thy holy Church. Open his eyes to behold Thy wonders; open his ears to listen to Thy divine words; unite him with Thy catechumen people; that at a well-pleasing time he may be vouchsafed the font of regeneration, and the garment of incorruption; in Christ Jesus our Lord, to Whom is glory, with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."**

*And he signs him thrice.*[^2981]

*And again he says this prayer:*

**Priest:** **"Thou, O Master, didst send Thy Holy Word for the salvation of the world, that He might fill all things with Thy divine knowledge."** (continues onto leaf p1018)

=== LEAF p1018 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"Thou hast delivered the soul of Thy servant from the evil one: do Thou enlighten him, and guide him unto the holiness of Thy Christ; and suffer not any evil spirit to have place any longer in him. For Thou art the only Deliverer of our race; and unto Thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen."**

*Prayer over a Christian who was seduced into pagan error, and has returned again, and fled unto the Church of God:*[^2982]

*Let us pray to the Lord.*

**Priest:** **"O Lord, Lord, Creator and Master of all, fountain of goodness, Savior of all men, especially of those who believe;..."** (continues onto leaf p1019)

=== LEAF p1019 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...Who didst not despise man when he was led down by the devil's deceit into the abyss of perdition and error, but through the saving Incarnation of Thine Only-begotten Son and our God didst seek him out and save him, and bring him unto Thyself: do Thou Thyself, O Master, Lover of mankind, look now upon this Thy servant [Name], who is a sheep of the rational flock of Thy Christ; whom do Thou deliver from captivity to godless enemies,[^2983] with whom through ignorance he was enslaved, or under duress tormented, and lived according to their deceitful customs; yet now Thou hast vouchsafed him to be united unto the life of Thy people: cleanse [him]; and enlighten his heart and mind..."** (continues onto leaf p1020)

=== LEAF p1020 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...by the power and operation of Thine All-Holy Spirit; so that the sparks of saving baptism lying dormant in his soul, fanned by the breezes of Thy grace, may be noetically kindled into a spiritual flame;[^2984] and the seal imprinted within him may appear more radiant, depicting in his heart and thoughts, by the sign of the Cross of Thy Christ, hope in Thee and true knowledge; that he may know and worship Thee, the only true God and Father, and Thine Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, and Thine All-Holy Spirit; and unite him unto Thy holy, catholic, and..."** (continues onto leaf p1021 in Cohort 103)
