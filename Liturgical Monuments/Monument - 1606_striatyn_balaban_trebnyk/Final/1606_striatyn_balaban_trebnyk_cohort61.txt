# PART XXVII: THE ORDER OF THE LITTLE SCHEMA (THAT IS, THE MANTIYA)[^1697]

*At the striking of the wood of the service,[^1698] and when the brethren begin the Hours, the ekklesiarch brings in him who desires to be tonsured; and he makes three prostrations before the holy doors, and to the choirs one each, then unto the hegumen. And then having gone out into the narthex, he puts off his customary clothing.[^1699] And again he is brought within the church...*

=== LEAF p603 ===
*...by the ekklesiarch, and stands clad in a short garment, ungirded, barefoot, bareheaded, clad in a tunic only for decency's sake.[^1700]*

*And while the customary antiphons are being sung, after the Entrance the kontakion of the day is said at the "Glory...": and at the "Now and ever...", this troparion is sung, Tone 1:*[^1701]

*Tone 1:*  
Make haste to open unto me fatherly embraces; prodigally have I spent my life; but looking upon the inexhaustible wealth of Thy compassions, O Savior, despise not now an impoverished heart; for unto Thee, O Lord, in compunction I cry: I have sinned, Father, against heaven and before Thee![^1702]

*While this is being sung thrice, he who desires to be tonsured...*

=== LEAF p604 ===
*...prostrates himself before the holy doors, where the hegumen is standing.[^1703]*

*And after the aforesaid troparion, the hegumen comes with the brethren near the pillars, and stands there. And candles are given [to them].[^1704] And he who desires to be tonsured makes three metanias before the holy doors, then unto the hegumen one.*

*And when he has arisen, the hegumen catechizes him thus:*[^1705]

**Hegumen:** **Open the ears of thine heart, O brother, and give ear to the voice of the Lord saying: "Come unto Me, all ye that labor and are heavy laden with sins, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you, and learn...**

=== LEAF p605 ===
**Hegumen:** **...of Me, for I am meek and lowly in heart, and ye shall find rest unto your souls."[^1706] Now therefore, unto each question, with fear and joy give the fitting answer unto God. And know of a certainty that our Savior Himself, with His all-hymned Mother, and holy angels, and all His saints, is come hither, listening unto the words that proceed from thee; so that when He comes to judge the living and the dead, He will render unto thee not according to what thou art about to promise and profess, but according as thou shalt have kept and fulfilled [thy profession].[^1707] Therefore now, unto...**

=== LEAF p606 ===
**Hegumen:** **...the things wherein thou art about to be questioned by us, make answer.**

*After this he is questioned.*

*It is befitting to know that if the hegumen is a priest, he questions him himself; but if not, he is questioned by the priest.*[^1708]

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Why hast thou come, brother, falling down before this holy altar and before this holy company?**

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Desiring the ascetic life, honorable father.**[^1709]

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Dost thou desire to be counted worthy of the angelic schema, and to be ranked in the choir of monastics?**

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, God helping [me], honorable father.**

*The Priest [says]:*

=== LEAF p607 ===
**Priest:** **Truly a good and blessed work hast thou chosen, if thou also wilt accomplish it; for good works are acquired through labor, and perfected through travail.**[^1710]

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Dost thou approach the Lord of the free choice of thine own mind?**

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, God helping [me], honorable father.**

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Is it of some distress or constraint?**[^1711]

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Nay, honorable father.**

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Dost thou renounce the world and the things that are in the world, according to the commandment of the Lord?**[^1712]

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, honorable father.**

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Wilt thou abide in the monastery and in the ascetic life until thy last breath?**

*Answer:*

=== LEAF p608 ===
**Candidate:** **Yea, God helping [me], honorable father.**

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Wilt thou preserve thyself in virginity, chastity, and piety?**[^1713]

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, honorable father.**

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Wilt thou preserve obedience unto the hegumen and unto all the brotherhood in Christ?**

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, honorable father.**

*Question:*  
**Priest:** **Wilt thou endure every constraint and sorrow of the monastic life, for the sake of the Kingdom of heaven?**

*Answer:*  
**Candidate:** **Yea, God helping [me], honorable father.**

*Then the instruction:*[^1714]

**Priest:** **Take heed, brother, what manner of promises thou givest unto the Master Christ; for the angels are standing by invisibly, recording...**

=== LEAF p609 ===
**Priest:** **...this thy confession, concerning which thou shalt be examined at the Second Coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.[^1715] I declare unto thee therefore the most perfect life, wherein a conversation according to the likeness of the Lord is manifested, testifying what things it is befitting for thee to choose, and what things it is befitting to flee from.[^1716] For this purpose hast thou purposed, child, to approach and serve the Lord; if therefore thou desirest to be a monk, before all things cleanse thyself from all defilement of flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.[^1717] Acquire humble-mindedness, whereby thou shalt become an inheritor of eternal good things; put away...**

**Priest:** **...the arrogance of worldly custom; have obedience toward all; be without murmuring in the services commanded thee; in prayer patient; in vigils not sluggish; in temptation sorrow not; in fasts slacken not; and know that by prayer and fasting it is befitting for thee to entreat God; in infirmities faint not; discern also evil thoughts; for the enemy will not cease offering thee the memory of thy former worldly life, and hatred toward a virtuous way of life; it is befitting for thee therefore, having begun...**[^1718]
