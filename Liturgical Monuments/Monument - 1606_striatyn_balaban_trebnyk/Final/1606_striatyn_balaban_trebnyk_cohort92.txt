...shall be saved? For with Thee is propitiation and mercy, and plenteous redemption.[^2623] Our soul is in Thy two hands, our breath is in Thy will; for as long as Thy gracious regard is upon us, we prosper; but if Thou lookest upon us with wrath, we perish like the morning dew.[^2624] For as dust stands not against the wind, so neither can we stand against Thy wrath; but as the creation, we ask mercy from Him Who created us.[^2625] Have mercy on us, O Lord, sinners that we are; have mercy on us according to Thy great mercy, and according to the multitude of Thy compassions blot out our iniquities; for all good comes from...

=== LEAF p912 ===

...Thee upon us, whereas all that is unrighteous is from us toward Thee.[^2626] We have all turned aside together, and are become unprofitable; there is none among us that is diligent concerning heavenly things, but all look unto earthly things; and all of us are intent upon the cares of this life, having no care for the Kingdom of God, as though permanent upon the earth.[^2627] Not that Thou, O Lord, forsakest us, but rather that we seek Thee not, cleaving unto these visible things; therefore do we fear lest at some time Thou do unto us as Thou didst unto Jerusalem, when they had forsaken Thee and walked not in Thy ways; yet do not Thou unto us as unto them according to their deeds...

...nor reward us according to our sins; but have patience with us yet, O long-suffering Lord; stay Thy wrathful flame that stretches forth against Thy servants.[^2628] Do Thou Thyself guide us into Thy truth, and teach us to do Thy will; for Thou art our God, and we are Thy people, Thy portion, Thine inheritance, and Thy creation.[^2629] We have not lifted up our hands unto a strange god, nor followed false prophets, nor do we hold heretical teaching; but Thee do we call upon, the true God; and unto Thee Who dwellest in heaven do we lift up our eyes...[^2630]

=== LEAF p914 ===

...and unto Thee do we lift up our hands, and we pray Thee: forgive us, for Thou art good and the Lover of mankind; have mercy on us, Who callest sinners to repentance.[^2631] And at the dread judgment, separate us not from standing at Thy right hand, but make us worthy of the blessedness of the righteous.[^2632] As long as this world stands, bring not upon us tribulations and temptations, and deliver us not into the hands of aliens, lest Thy city be called a captive city, and Thy people be in a land not their own, lest the nations say: "Where is their God?"[^2633] Send not upon us tribulation, nor famine, nor civil strife, nor sudden...

...mighty; make Thy Churches to grow; preserve Thine inheritance; save men, and women, and infants; deliver those that are in servitude, captivity, and exile; and have mercy on all those in prisons, in hunger, in thirst, and in nakedness; comfort them all, make them all glad; grant unto them joy both of soul and body; through the prayers of the all-pure Theotokos, and of the holy heavenly hosts; and of Thy Forerunner and Baptist John; and of the holy Apostles; and of the holy Prophets; and of the holy Martyrs; and of the holy venerable and God-bearing Fathers; and through the prayers of all the saints, be Thou moved to compassion upon us, that by Thy mercy...
