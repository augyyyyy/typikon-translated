# PART XXVI: THE ORDER THAT TAKES PLACE AT THE CLOTHING OF THE RASSA [^1680]

*He who desires to receive the rassa comes, and makes a metania unto the hegumen. And he is questioned by him, whether indeed with all zeal and after many days' consideration he desires this.*

*And when he promises, he enters into the church, and makes three prostrations before the holy doors. And having gone out, he makes a metania unto the hegumen and unto the brethren.*

*And after the conclusion of the Hours, the priest makes the beginning, and we say the Trisagion.*

*All-Holy Trinity. After "Our Father...":*

> **Priest:** **For Thine is the Kingdom...**

*"Come, let us worship..." (thrice). Then Psalm 26:*[^1681]

*Psalm 26:*

The Lord is my illumination and my Savior; whom shall I fear? The Lord is the defender of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? When the wicked drew near against me to eat up my flesh, mine enemies that afflict me, they were weakened and fell. Though an army should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war should rise up against me, in this do I hope. One thing have I asked of the Lord, that will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life to behold the beauty of the Lord, and to visit His holy temple. For He hid me in His tabernacle in the day of my troubles; He sheltered me in the secret place of His tabernacle; He lifted me up upon a rock. And now, behold, He has lifted up my head above mine enemies. I went round about and offered in His tabernacle a sacrifice of praise and jubilation; I will sing and chant unto the Lord. Hear, O Lord, my voice wherewith I have cried; have mercy on me, and hear me. Unto Thee my heart said: I will seek the Lord. My face has sought Thee; Thy face, O Lord, will I seek. Turn not away Thy face from me, and turn not away in anger from Thy servant. Be Thou my helper, and cast me not off and forsake me not, O God my Savior. For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the Lord has taken me up. Set a law for me, O Lord, in Thy way, and guide me in the right path, because of mine enemies. Deliver me not over unto the souls of them that afflict me; for unrighteous witnesses have risen up against me, and iniquity has lied to itself. I believe that I shall see the good things of the Lord in the land of the living. Wait on the Lord; take courage unto thyself, and let thine heart be strengthened, and wait on the Lord.[^1682]

*The second Psalm, Psalm 50:*[^1683]

**Have mercy on me, O God...**

*Afterwards again the Trisagion. And after "Our Father...": Troparion, Tone 4:*[^1684]

**O God of our fathers, Who dealest ever with** us according to Thy gentleness:[^1685] take not away Thy mercy from us, but by their supplications direct our life in humility.[^1686]

*Glory... the same.*

*Both now... Theotokion, Tone 4:*[^1687]

**Unto the Mother of God let us sinners and humble ones now run with diligence, and fall down in repentance, crying from the depth of soul: O Lady, help us, having had compassion on us; make haste, for we are perishing from the multitude of our sins; turn not Thy servants away empty, for thee alone have we as a helper.**

*Then the hegumen says this prayer:*[^1688]

> **Deacon:** **Let us pray to the Lord.**

**Hegumen:** **We give thanks unto Thee, O Lord our God, Who according to Thy great mercy hast delivered Thy servant, [Name], from vain worldly life, and hast called him unto this honorable profession: vouchsafe him, therefore, to live worthily in this angelic life; and preserve him from the snares of the devil; and keep his soul and body pure unto death; and vouchsafe him to be a temple of Thy Holy Spirit; instructing him to remember Thee always, and Thy commandments; grant him humility, and love, and meekness: through the prayers of our all-pure Lady, the Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, and of all Thy saints. Amen.**[^1689]

*The second prayer:*[^1690]

> **Deacon:** **Let us pray to the Lord.**

**Hegumen:** **Into Thy saving yoke receive, O Master, Thy servant, [Name], and number him in the flock of Thine elect. Clothe him in the robe of sanctification; gird his loins with chastity; show him forth as a striver in all self-control, perfecting in him and in us the gift of Thy spiritual gifts: through the prayers of our all-pure Lady, the Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, and of all Thy saints. Amen.**[^1691]

*And the hegumen, having received the scissors from him, tonsures him cruciformly, saying:*[^1692]

**Hegumen:** **Our brother, [Name], is tonsured as to the hair of his head, in the Name of the Father, and Hegumen:** **...of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Let us say for him.**

*And the brethren [say]:*

**Brethren:** **Lord, have mercy (thrice).**

*Afterwards the brethren, having received [him], tonsure him, singing Psalm 50.*[^1693]

*And after this the hegumen vests him in the rassa and kamilavka; and while the Apostle is being read, he kisses the brethren; and [the priest] makes the final Dismissal.*[^1694]

*Dismissal Prayer:*[^1695]

> **Deacon:** **Let us pray to the Lord.**

**Hegumen:** **O Holy, Good Son of the Good Father, Who cuttest off pride, and bringest to naught the root of the adversary, and pourest out the glory of humble-mindedness upon the head of the saints, and crownest them with the unfading and incorruptible crown of life and immortality, and guidest into the narrow** way wherein the holy fathers entered, and received the place of resting itself: do Thou Thyself, O Master, Lord our God, grant the power that is from Thee, and the grace of Thy Divinity, into the spirit, and soul, and body of our brother, [Name], who has come unto Thee, having received the beginning of the holy habit; in Thy Name, which sanctifies all things, grant Thou Thyself to sanctify him by Thy name, all-powerful and good.[^1696]

*Exclamation:*

> **Priest:** **For holy art Thou, O our God, and unto Thee do we send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.**

> **Choir:** **Amen.**

### Notes

[^1680]: Physical leaf p593 (fol. 288r). *Чи́нъ быва́емый на ѡдѣꙗ́нїи ра́сы* (Gk. *Ἀκολουθία εἰς ῥασоφοροῦντα*). This order constitutes the novice's or candidate's tonsure and clothing into the inner rassa (cassock) and kamilavka, known as the ryasophore rank (*расофор*), prior to the Little Schema.
[^1681]: Physical leaf p594 (fol. 288v). LXX Psalm 26:1–4 (MT Ps 27). Monastic investiture psalm expressing reliance upon the Lord in spiritual warfare and the aspiration to dwell in His temple.
[^1682]: Physical leaf p596 (fol. 289v). LXX Psalm 26:9–14 (MT Ps 27).
[^1683]: Physical leaf p596 (fol. 289v). LXX Psalm 50 (MT Ps 51), the primary penitential psalm of the Byzantine rite.
[^1684]: Physical leaf p596 (fol. 289v). Troparion of the Holy Fathers, Tone 4 (*Боже отецъ нашихъ* / *Ὁ Θεὸς τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν*).
[^1685]: Physical leaf p597 (fol. 290r). Slavonic: *по твоему смотрению* (Gk. *κατὰ τὴν σὴν ἐπιείκειαν* / *κατὰ τὴν σὴν οἰκονομίαν*, "according to Thy gentleness/forbearance").
[^1686]: Physical leaf p597 (fol. 290r). Slavonic witness: *въ смире́нїи оу́строи живо́тъ на́шъ* ("in humility direct/order our life"), where standard modern recensions read *въ ми́рѣ упра́ви*.
[^1687]: Physical leaf p597 (fol. 290r). Theotokion, Tone 4 (*Къ Богородицѣ прилѣжно нынѣ притецемъ* / *Τῇ Θεοτόκῳ ἐκтеνῶς νῦν προσδράμωμεν*).
[^1688]: Physical leaf p597 (fol. 290r). The rubric designates the monastery superior (*игоуменъ*) to read the prayer over the candidate.
[^1689]: Physical leaf p598 (fol. 290v). First prayer of investiture (Gk. *Εὐχαριστοῦμέν σοι, Κύριε ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν...*). Note the designation of the monastic path as the "angelic life" (*въ агг҃льско семъ житїи* / *ἐν τῇ ἀγγελικῇ ταύτῃ πολιτείᾳ*).
[^1690]: Physical leaf p599 (fol. 291r). Second prayer of investiture (Gk. *Εἰς τὸν ζυγόν σου τὸν σωτήριον πρόσδεξαι, Δέσποτα...*).
[^1691]: Physical leaf p599 (fol. 291r). Slavonic: *дх҃ѡвны твоихъ да́рѡ съвръша́я да́ръ* (perfecting the gift of Thy spiritual gifts).
[^1692]: Physical leaf p599 (fol. 291r). The candidate personally presents the shears (*но́жицѣ*) to the hegumen, signifying voluntary surrender and obedience. The tonsure is made cruciformly upon four sides of the head.
[^1693]: Physical leaf p600 (fol. 291v). While the candidate is tonsured by the monastic brethren, the community chants Psalm 50.
[^1694]: Physical leaf p600 (fol. 291v). The candidate is invested in the rassa (*ряса* / Gk. *ῥάσον*) and kamilavka (*камилавка* / Gk. *καμηλαύκιον*). The rubric specifies that during the reading of the Epistle (*прочита́емꙋ а́пл҃у*), the newly clothed monk exchanges the kiss of peace with the brotherhood before the final dismissal.
[^1695]: Physical leaf p600 (fol. 291v). Dismissal Prayer of the Rassa (*Мл҃тва ѡпусти́лнаѧ* / Gk. *Εὐχὴ ἀπολυτική*), continuing on leaf p601 (*...narrow path wherein the holy fathers entered...*).
[^1696]: Physical leaf p601 (fol. 292r). Conclusion of the Dismissal Prayer of the Rassa (*Мл҃тва ѡпусти́лнаѧ*). The prayer evokes the narrow way of the holy fathers and requests divine grace upon the spirit, soul, and body of the candidate who has received the beginning of the holy habit (*нача́токъ ст҃го ѡ́браза*).
