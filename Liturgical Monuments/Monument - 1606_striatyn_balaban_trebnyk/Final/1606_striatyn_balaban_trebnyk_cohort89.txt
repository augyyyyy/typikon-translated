*Troparion:*
Elijah once by a word restrained rain from being upon the earth, and again by a word brought rain from heaven upon the earth; therefore we pray Thee, O Master: by his prayers send us rain from heaven upon Thy land.[^2530]

*Troparion:*
Angelic choirs, assemblies of apostles and prophets, of martyrs and hierarchs, and multitudes of the venerable: as the Creator of all and God, pray Him to save them that faithfully hymn Him.[^2531]

*Troparion:*
As Thou didst deliver Jonah from the beast and the sea, and Daniel from the pit and the lions, O Savior, by Thy mercy: likewise now give ear unto Thy servants, and deliver Thy people from every evil.[^2532]

*Theotokion:*
The Theotokos who bare Thee, O Savior, do we now summon unto supplication, that Thou mayest have compassion upon us, and save the people and the city that have sinned against Thee, and that exalt Thee above all unto the ages.[^2533]

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Glory...*[^2537]

*Troparion:*

*Theotokion:*

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
Him Who is God the Word of God, Who by ineffable wisdom came down to renew Adam, who had evilly fallen through corrupt food, and Who for our sake was ineffably incarnate of the Holy Virgin: with one accord[^2541] in songs, we faithful magnify Him.[^2542]

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*
