to issue it for the common need of the churches: yet lest there be any differing opinion concerning this also, we commanded that there be a synod of priests experienced in the sacred rites, to inspect and examine it. And I saw it to be necessary that what is found in our old Slavonic Trebnyks—namely, how to receive those coming from various heresies unto the Holy Catholic and Apostolic Church,[^18] the Consecration of the Great Myron,[^19] and the rest—lest the book be deficient in this also, we commanded to append this as well. And if there are any who take offense at this, that the authors of all the sequences are not known, let such know

=== LEAF p13 ===

that neither do we know [the authors] for the other sequences of the Church's holy mysteries—namely, Baptism, Holy Unction,[^20] and others; yet we know from the commandments of the Holy Apostles that these were instituted by them. Concerning which also the fiery-tongued Dionysius the Areopagite in the book *On the Ecclesiastical Hierarchy* clearly testifies (that these were handed down by the Apostles),[^21] and the rest by the Holy Fathers; which, having received from both, we firmly maintain. For if, as the Great Basil says, we begin to neglect unwritten customs as having little force, we shall unawares bring harm even unto the written things themselves.[^22] And the Divine Chrysostom said: Hence

=== LEAF p14 ===

it is manifest that the Apostles did not hand down all things by writing, but many things also unwritten; and both the one and the other are alike worthy of belief. Therefore we also deem the tradition of the Church worthy of belief: it is a tradition; seek nothing further.[^23] And when I was occupied with other necessary Church needs, and was taking care concerning other books—namely, Psalters and Didactic Gospels—to issue them from my printing house of Krylos,[^24] the aforementioned noble Teodor Yurievych Balaban zealously applied himself unto the work of this book: a man flourishing in many righteous deeds, and exceedingly [a fervent lover] of the pious dogma and the traditions of the fathers,

=== LEAF p15 ===

a fervent lover, who not in word only, but also in deed kept blamelessly the piety of the fathers. And especially where many of the multitude who go abroad—I mean to Italy[^25]—being beguiled by the vain philosophy of secular learning, fall into various heresies, and being stripped of piety fall away from the Church. But he, having lived there for a long time in his youth, harmed not piety, and turned aside not even a little from the traditions of the Eastern Church; but even as one in the midst of fire remained unburned, or as one in the midst of the sea's surges suffered no shipwreck. Therefore I praise him; on account of the righteousness of his virtue I praise him; whose

=== LEAF p16 ===

zeal and care in this was to issue into the light of day the books necessary for the Church: among which, after the Sluzhebnik, he took diligence to print this Trebnyk from his printing house of Striatyn, with the counsel and blessing of our humility, at his own cost and expense, having gathered for this work men skilled in that craft. Who, before this book had yet reached its completion, passed away, the faithful servant of his Lord, Teodor Yurievych, in the year after the Nativity of Christ one thousand six hundred and six, on the twenty-fourth day of the month of May.[^26]
And the composition of this book is proportioned to the span of man's age,[^27] from when he is born,

And because the canons are not found among all priests, for this reason, having selected the most necessary from the Apostolic and Holy Fathers' commandments, we have appended them at the end of this book.[^28] Which do you, receiving with gratitude and love, honor, performing what has been labored over and corrected sacredly without any gainsaying, in concord; [remembering] those who exerted themselves and [brought] all diligence
