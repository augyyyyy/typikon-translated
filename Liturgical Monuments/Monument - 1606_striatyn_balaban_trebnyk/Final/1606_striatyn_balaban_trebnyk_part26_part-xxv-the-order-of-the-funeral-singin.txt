# PART XXV: THE ORDER OF THE FUNERAL SINGING FOR ALL DEPARTED FATHERS AND OUR BRETHREN, AND OTHER DECEASED CHRISTIANS (THE PARASTAS) [^1619]

*The ranking priest, or the hegumen himself, with all the priests who are found there, go into the altar; and all the priests vest themselves in phelonia, and the deacons in sticharia. And having taken the censer with incense, the hegumen himself begins:*[^1620]

**Priest:** **Blessed is our God.**[^1621]

*And we say the Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. "Our Father...":*

**Priest:** **For Thine is the Kingdom...**

*Lord, have mercy (12 times). "Come, let us worship..." (thrice). Then Psalm 90: "He that dwells in the help of the Highest..." (see fol. 231).*[^1622]

*Glory... Both now... Alleluia (thrice) in Tone 8. And we sing the Troparion in the same tone:*[^1623]

*Troparion, Tone 8:*

O Thou Who by the depth of wisdom in love for mankind dost order all things, and dost dispense unto all that which is profitable, O only Creator: give rest, O Lord, unto the soul of Thy servant; for on Thee have they set their hope, on our Creator and Fashioner and God.

*Glory...*

For on Thee have they set their hope, on our Creator and Fashioner and God.

*Both now Theotokion, Tone 8:*[^1624]

Thee we have as a city and a haven, and an acceptable intercessor before God Who was born of thee, O unwedded Mother of God, the salvation of the faithful.

*Then the usual diaconal petitions, and the prayer:* **O God of spirits...** *together, all the priests.*[^1625]

*Then we sing the Blameless according to their order, until we arrive at the verse which is:*

**Unto the ages I will not forget Thy statutes, for in them Thou hast given me life.**

*We say it, singing it thrice. Then again the priest says the usual diaconal petitions, and the prayer, together all [the priests]. Then we complete the Blameless unto the end according to their order, and with their troparia, even as thou hast in the layman's burial, all in order.*[^1626]

*(See fol. 232).* *Then again the diaconal petitions and the prayer. Afterwards Psalm 50:* **Have mercy on me, O God...**[^1627]

*Canon for the Departed, Tone 8. Ode 1.*[^1628]

*Heirmos:* **Having traversed the water as upon dry land...**

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Through the death of Christ, and emulating His holy passions, all the venerable martyrs attained unto the divine and blessed life.

*Troparion:*

Overlooking the sins of youth and passing by transgressions, O Christ Savior, number Thy servant who has fallen asleep before with Thine elect.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Unto him that has obtained glory and joy, having acquired a blessed life, grant richly unto Thy servant whom Thou hast taken, O Plenteous in mercy.[^1629]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst conceive the Father's Word, hypostatically united unto the flesh, Who came forth from thee, O all-spotless Maiden, and by divine power abolished Hades.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* **Of the heavenly vault...**[^1630]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Cleansed from the ancient ancestral fall by baptism and rebirth, and sprinkled with the streams of their own blood, the blessed [martyrs] reign with Christ.

*Troparion:*

O Savior, Who of Thine own will wast laid dead in a tomb, and dost raise up them that dwell in the tombs: be well pleased that he whom Thou hast taken from us should dwell in the tabernacles of Thy saints.

*Glory Troparion:*[^1631]

Implored by Thy divine compassion, O Master, and by Thy consubstantial innate goodness: grant remission of sins unto Thy servant, and give him rest with Thyself.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Incarnate from thy womb and having become man, He that is pre-eminently the Lover of mankind saves man from the gates of death, O all-pure Mother of God, who alone art lauded.

*Katavasia [Heirmos]:*[^1632]

**Celestial vault's supreme Creator, O Lord, and Builder of the Church: do Thou confirm me in Thy love, Thou pinnacle of desires and confirmation of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.**

*Then the diaconal petitions, and the prayer:* **O God of spirits...** *After this, the Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^1633]

Give rest, O our Savior, with the righteous unto Thy servant, and settle him in Thy courts, even as it is written: overlooking, as Thou art good, his sins, both voluntary and involuntary, and all things known and unknown, O Lover of mankind.

*Glory...*

And all things known and unknown, O Lover of mankind.

*Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou Who didst shine forth from the Virgin unto the world, O Christ God, and through her didst reveal [us as] sons of light: have mercy on us.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* **Thou art my Lord, O Christ, Thou art also my strength...**[^1634]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

That they might behold Thy glory, O Master, and brightly obtain in heaven the rays of Thy light-giving [splendor], the divine martyrs endured every manner of torment singing unto Thee, O Christ: "Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!"[^1635]

*Troparion:*

Many mansions are with Thee, O Savior, apportioned unto all according to dignity, according to the measure of virtues: be well pleased, therefore, O Compassionate One, that he who has faithfully departed, piously crying unto Thee: "Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!", should fill these.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*[^1636]

Having appeared as a man like unto us, O Immortal One, Thou didst manifestly endure death for all, and showedst the path unto life. As the Lover of mankind, vouchsafe unto him that has departed, granting remission of sins as Master, and bestowing communion in [Thy] light.

*Both now Theotokion:*[^1637]

Thou art the boast of the faithful, O unwedded Bride; thou art the protectress, thou art also the refuge of Christians, a wall and a haven. Thou bearest supplications unto Thine own Son, O all-spotless one, and savest from perils those who with faith and love know thee as pure Mother of God.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* **Why hast Thou cast me away...**[^1638]

*Troparion:*

Having had compassion, vouchsafe him that has departed unto immortal life to partake of Thine ineffable glory, and of Thy beatitudes which are beyond word, in the tabernacles of the righteous, where the voice of them that feast is beautiful, O only Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Where the angelic ranks and the assemblies of the righteous rejoice, in the bosom of Abraham settle Thy servant, O Savior; and be well pleased that he stand with boldness before the dread and divine throne, O Compassionate One.[^1639]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst appear unto us as purification, righteousness, and redemption; and by Thy stripe Thou hast healed our weakness. Do Thou Thyself, therefore, O Compassionate One, as Thou art good, establish him that has departed in the delight of paradise.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*[^1640]

Mercifully, O Merciful One, Thou didst assume the measure of humanity, Who art adorned with the exceeding heights of divine glory: taking unto Thyself from virginal loins an animate flesh, whereby Thou didst destroy death.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* **Cleanse me, O Savior, for many** *Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Thy martyrs, O Savior, endured many torments, their souls having been wounded with Thy love, O Holy One,[^1641] desiring Thine everlasting glory and the sweet communion of Thyself.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst rend asunder the hostile womb of death, O Savior, and didst raise up all the prisoners therein, bestowing life; vouchsafe the same, O Benefactor,[^1642] unto him that has been translated.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver Thy servant from the tears and sighing in Hades, O Savior; for as Thou alone art compassionate, Thou hast taken away every tear from every face of them that faithfully bless Thee.[^1643]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

He Who by nature is the Creator is fashioned in thy womb; as God, being emptied, O all-spotless one, the only Immortal One dies by death for the salvation of all.[^1644]

*Katavasia [Heirmos]:*[^1645]

**Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are my iniquities, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and give ear unto me, O God of my salvation.**

*The Little Litany:*[^1646]

*Again and again in peace [let us pray to the Lord].*

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*[^1647]

**With the saints give rest, O Lord, unto the soul of Thy servant, where there is neither sickness, nor sorrow, nor sighing, but life everlasting.**

*Ikos:*[^1648]

Thou alone art immortal from the beginning, Who didst make and fashion man but we mortals were fashioned from the earth, and unto the same earth shall we go, even as Thou didst command, Who didst fashion me, and saidst unto me: "Earth thou art, and unto earth shalt thou return"; whither all we mortals depart, making over the tomb the weeping song: Alleluia.[^1649]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* **The Hebrew children in the furnace...**[^1650]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

All the desire of the martyrs was extended unto the only Master; united unto Him in love, and singing: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1651]

*Troparion:*

Fill with joy and gladness him whom Thou hast translated, Thy servant, O Compassionate One, having vouchsafed him to cry unto Thee, and to sing: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1652]

*Glory Troparion:*

Thou grantest unto them that have departed in faith the brightness of the divine Kingdom, bestowing the garment of incorruption on them that cry: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1653]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Dissolving the curse of Eve, Thou didst take up Thine abode in the all-spotless Virgin, pouring forth the fountain of blessing unto them that cry: "Blessed is the God of our fathers!"[^1654]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* **The harmony of musical instruments...**[^1655]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Having passed through earthly sufferings, the true martyrs received heavenly crowns, crying unto the Savior: "Praise the Lord, all His works, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!"[^1656]

*Troparion:*

Descending into the lowest pit, Thou didst raise up with life-originating hand them that dwell in the tombs; and grantest rest, O Compassionate One, unto Thy servant who in faith has fallen asleep before.[^1657]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

As the ever-existing fountain of life, as the torrent of delight,[^1658] Thou waterest Thy servant who in piety has been translated unto Thee, who sings and exalts Thee, O Christ, unto the ages.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

O Virgin Mary, Mother of God, who didst bear in the flesh God, the Savior of men: save them that in faith hymn thy childbearing, and exalt thee unto the ages.[^1659]

*Ode 9. Heirmos:* **Every hearing was affrighted...**[^1660]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Having unsubdued and unconquerable strength, O martyrs of Christ, ye brought to naught the godless command of the tyrants, and manifestly were vouchsafed the heavenly Kingdom, being illuminated by the rays of the Trinity, O richly blessed ones.[^1661]

*Troparion:*

Bitter Hades was destroyed when Thou didst overthrow it, O Lover of mankind, and didst raise up them that slept there from of old; but even now, as Thou art good, vouchsafe him that has passed over unto Thee, the Merciful One, unto Thy never-setting light.[^1662]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Wholly art Thou sweetness, O Savior, wholly desire and insatiable love in truth; wholly art Thou ineffable beauty.[^1663] Therefore, be well pleased that he who has departed unto Thee should delight in Thy comeliness, and vouchsafe him Thy divine goodness.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Save me, O Mother of God, who didst bear Christ my Savior, dual in nature but not in hypostasis,[^1664] only-begotten indeed of the Father, and from thee the firstborn of all creation, with Whom we all magnify thee.

*Katavasia [Heirmos]:*[^1665]

**Every hearing was affrighted at the ineffable condescension of God: how the Most High of His own will came down even unto flesh, having become man from the virginal womb; therefore, we the faithful magnify the all-pure Mother of God.**

*Then the Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. After "Our Father...": Troparia, Tone 4:*[^1666]

**With the spirits of the righteous who have been perfected, give rest to the soul of Thy servant, O Savior, keeping it in the blessed life which is from Thee, O Lover of mankind.**

*Troparion, Tone 4:*

In Thy resting place, O Lord, where all Thy saints repose, give rest unto the soul of Thy servant.[^1667]

*Glory...*

*Troparion, Tone 4:*

Thou art God Who descended into Hades and loosed the bonds of the chained: do Thou give rest to the soul of Thy servant.[^1668]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion, Tone 4:*

O only pure and spotless Virgin, who didst bear God without seed: entreat Him that our souls may be saved.[^1669]

*Then the diaconal petitions:*[^1670]

**Deacon:** Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee: give ear and have mercy.

**Deacon:** Again we pray for the repose and blessed memory and the remission of sins of the departed servant of God, [Name]...

**Deacon:** ...that every transgression, voluntary and involuntary, may be pardoned him;[^1671]

**Deacon:** That the Lord God may establish his spirit where all the righteous repose;

**Deacon:** The mercy of God, and the Kingdom of heaven, and the remission of his sins having asked for him, let us commend ourselves and one another, and all our life unto Christ God.

**Choir:** Unto Thee, O Lord.

**Deacon:** Let us pray to the Lord.

*Then the priest says the prayer secretly:*[^1672]

**Priest:** **[O God] of spirits and of all flesh, Who hast trampled down death, and abolished the devil, and given life unto Thy world: do Thou Thyself, O Lord, give rest to the soul of Thy departed servant, [Name], in a place of light...**[^1673]

**Priest:** **...in a place of verdure, in a place of repose, whence sickness, sorrow, and sighing have fled away; and every transgression committed by him, in word, or deed, or thought, do Thou, as the good God and Lover of mankind, forgive. For there is no man that shall live and not sin against Thee; for Thou alone art without any sin, Thy righteousness is righteousness forever, and Thy word is truth.**[^1674]

*Exclamation:*[^1675]

**Priest:** **For Thou art the resurrection, and the life, and the repose of Thy departed servant ever to be remembered, [Name], O Christ our God; and unto Thee we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father**[^1676] and Thine all-holy and good and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Then also the Dismissal:*[^1677]

**Priest:** **May Christ our true God, Who rose from the dead, through the prayers of His all-pure Mother, by the power of the precious and life-creating Cross, and of all the saints: give rest to the soul of His servant who has been translated from us, [Name], in the abodes of the righteous where the righteous repose; and number him in the bosom of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; and have mercy on us and save us, for He is good and loves mankind.**[^1678]

*Then all say:*

*How to sing the Memory:*[^1679]

**All:** **Memory eternal unto thee, O ever-to-be-remembered servant of God, our brother, [Name]!**

*We say thus thrice. Then also we depart.*

### Notes

[^1619]: Fol. 277r. Renaissance woodcut headpiece (foliate arabesque with acanthus scrollwork, blooming floral vase, and corner rosettes) opening Part XXV: The Order of the Funeral Singing for All Departed Fathers and Our Brethren, and Other Deceased Christians (*Чи́нъ надгро́бнаго пѣ́нія всѣ́мъ оусо́пшимъ отце́мъ и бра́тїямъ на́шимъ, и про́чіимъ христїа́номъ уме́ршимъ*), commonly designated in the Kyivan and Ruthenian tradition as the Great Parastas (*Параста́съ*).
[^1620]: Fol. 277r. Vesting rubric specifying that the presiding celebrant (or the monastery's hegumen himself) and all concelebrating priests enter the altar and vest in phelonia, and the deacons in sticharia, before beginning the solemn memorial office with the censing of the sanctuary and temple.
[^1621]: Fol. 277v. Initial liturgical blessing (*Благослове́нъ Богъ нашъ*) followed by the Trisagion prayers, the Lord's Prayer with Trinitarian doxology, the twelvefold *Kyrie eleison*, and the triple invitation to worship (*Прїиди́те поклони́мся*).
[^1622]: Fol. 277v. Cross-reference directing the chanting of Psalm 90 (LXX 90:1–16: *Живы́й въ по́мощи Вы́шняго* / *He that dwells in the help of the Highest*), which is printed in full on fol. 231r (physical leaf p479) in the burial rite for deceased laymen.
[^1623]: Fol. 277v. Concluding psalm doxology (*Glory... Both now...*), the threefold Alleluia in Tone 8, and the chanting of the funeral Troparion in Tone 8 (*И́же глубина́ми му́дрости* / *O Thou Who by the depth of wisdom*), with its thematic refrain repeated at *Glory...*.
[^1624]: Fol. 278r. Funeral Theotokion in Tone 8 (*Тебѐ гра́да и приста́нище и́мамы* / *Thee we have as a city and a haven*), appealing to the Mother of God as the secure refuge and acceptable intercessor before Christ her Son.
[^1625]: Fol. 278r. Rubric directing the deacon to proclaim the customary funeral petitions of the litany of fervent supplication (*Помилуй насъ, Боже*), followed by the prayer *O God of spirits and of all flesh* recited jointly by all concelebrating priests.
[^1626]: Fol. 278r. Rubric governing the chanting of the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118 LXX, the *Amomos* or "Blameless"), divided into stases. At the midpoint verse (*Unto the ages I will not forget Thy statutes, for in them Thou hast given me life* — LXX 118:93), the choir sings the verse thrice, followed by diaconal petitions, the prayer *O God of spirits*, and the completion of the Kathisma with the funeral troparia (*Evlogitaria*).
[^1627]: Fol. 278v. Cross-reference to fol. 232r (physical leaf p481), where the full text of the 17th Kathisma and the Funeral Troparia (*The choir of the saints have found the Fountain of life*) are printed in the order for the burial of laymen; followed by diaconal petitions, the prayer *O God of spirits*, and Psalm 50 (LXX 50:3–21: *Have mercy on me, O God*).
[^1628]: Fol. 278v. Opening of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, Ode 1, attributed to St. Theophanes the Branded. It begins with the Heirmos *Во́ду проше́дъ* (*Having traversed the water as upon dry land*), followed by the Martyrikon (*Through the death of Christ*), the first troparion for the departed (*Overlooking the sins of youth*), and the troparion at *Glory...*.
[^1629]: Fol. 279r. Conclusion of the troparion at *Glory...* in Ode 1, followed by the Theotokion (*Зачала̀ есѝ Сло́во О́тчее* / *Thou didst conceive the Father's Word*), confessing the hypostatic union of the divine Word and the harrowing of Hades.
[^1630]: Fol. 279r. Ode 3 of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, opening with the Heirmos *Небе́сному кру́гу* (*Of the heavenly vault*), followed by the Martyrikon celebrating the martyrs' baptism in blood, and the troparion petitioning rest for the departed in the courts of the saints through Christ's voluntary burial.
[^1631]: Fol. 279v. Troparion at *Glory...* in Ode 3, imploring divine mercy and remission of transgressions according to God's innate goodness, followed by the Theotokion (*Вопло́щся от чре́ва твоего̀* / *Incarnate from thy womb*), honoring the Virgin as the Mother of the Savior Who rescues mankind from the gates of death.
[^1632]: Fol. 279v. Katavasia of Ode 3, repeating the Heirmos *Celestial vault's supreme Creator* in full, followed by the diaconal petitions, the prayer *O God of spirits*, and the announcement of the funeral Sessional Hymn in Tone 5.
[^1633]: Fol. 280r. Funeral Sessional Hymn (Kathisma hymn) in Tone 5 (*Поко́й, Спа́се нашъ, съ пра́ведными раба̀ Твоего̀* / *Give rest, O our Savior, with the righteous unto Thy servant*), with its concluding refrain repeated at *Glory...*, followed by the Theotokion (*И́же от Дѣ́вы возсїя́вый ми́ру* / *Thou Who didst shine forth from the Virgin*).
[^1634]: Fol. 280r. Ode 4 of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, opening with the Heirmos *Ты ми Христе Господь, ты же и сила ми* (*Thou art my Lord, O Christ, Thou art also my strength*), and the opening lines of the Martyrikon troparion commemorating the steadfastness of the martyrs under torments.
[^1635]: Fol. 280v. Conclusion of the Martyrikon in Ode 4, followed by the troparion meditating on John 14:2 (*In My Father's house are many mansions*), petitioning that the departed soul be granted a dwelling place according to the measure of Christian virtue.
[^1636]: Fol. 280v. Troparion at *Glory...* in Ode 4, extolling the Incarnation of the Immortal One Who tasted death to reveal the pathway to life, and imploring remission of sins and participation in the divine light for the departed.
[^1637]: Fol. 281r. Theotokion of Ode 4 (*Ты вѣ́рнымъ похвала̀ есѝ, Безневѣ́стная* / *Thou art the boast of the faithful, O unwedded Bride*), depicting the Mother of God as an unassailable wall, haven, and advocate delivering believers from all perils.
[^1638]: Fol. 281r. Ode 5 of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, opening with the Heirmos *Вску́ю мя отри́нулъ есѝ* (*Why hast Thou cast me away from Thy face, O Light unwaning*), followed by the first troparion contemplating the ineffable heavenly banquet in the tabernacles of the righteous, and the opening lines of the second troparion.
[^1639]: Fol. 281v. Conclusion of the second troparion of Ode 5, invoking the parable of the rich man and Lazarus (Luke 16:22) to petition rest in Abraham's bosom and bold presentation before the dread throne of Christ; followed by the troparion at *Glory...* (*Ѡчище́нїе намъ яви́ся*), celebrating the healing of human fallenness through the Savior's wounds (cf. Isaiah 53:5).
[^1640]: Fol. 281v. Theotokion of Ode 5 (*Ми́лостивно, Ми́лостиве, человѣ́чества мѣ́ру восприя́лъ есѝ* / *Mercifully, O Merciful One, Thou didst assume the measure of humanity*), proclaiming the destruction of death through Christ's animate flesh taken from the Virgin's womb, followed by the incipit of the Heirmos for Ode 6 (*Ѡчи́сти мя, Спа́се* / *Cleanse me, O Savior*).
[^1641]: Fol. 285r. Martyrikon troparion of Ode 6 of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, extolling the holy martyrs whose souls were wounded with divine love (*любо́вію твое́ю ду́ша оуязвї́вше ст҃їи* / *amor Dei*), seeking eternal communion with Christ.
[^1642]: Fol. 285r. Troparion of Ode 6 contemplating Christ's harrowing of Hades, where by shattering the "hostile womb of death" (*чре́во вра́жїе сме́рти... растерза́лъ есѝ*), the divine Benefactor (*благодѣ́телю*) released all captive souls and granted life.
[^1643]: Fol. 285r. Troparion at *Glory...* in Ode 6, invoking Isaiah 25:8 / Revelation 21:4 (*God shall wipe away every tear from their faces*), beseeching Christ to deliver the departed servant from the tears and lamentation of Hades.
[^1644]: Fol. 285v. Conclusion of the Theotokion of Ode 6, expressing the Kenosis of the Incarnation (Philippians 2:7: *ἑαυτὸν ἐκένωσε* / *и҆стощава́етсѧ*), wherein the transcendent, immortal God voluntarily assumes flesh within the Virgin's womb and undergoes human death to redeem mankind.
[^1645]: Fol. 285v. Katavasia of Ode 6, repeating the Heirmos *Cleanse me, O Savior* (*Ѡ҆чи́сти мѧ̀, Спа́се* — Jonah 2:2–3) in full, chanted by the choir before the Little Litany.
[^1646]: Fol. 285v. Rubric for the Little Litany (*Дїа́конства ма́ла*), followed by the priest's secret prayer and exclamation.
[^1647]: Fol. 285v. Solemn funeral Kontakion in Tone 8 (*Съ святы́ми поко́й, Христѐ* / *With the saints give rest*), the quintessential hymn of Eastern Christian burial, petitioning eternal rest where pain and sorrow are banished.
[^1648]: Fol. 285v. Opening of the funeral Ikos in Tone 8 (*Са́мъ еди́нъ есѝ безсме́ртный* / *Thou alone art immortal*), attributed to St. Romanos the Melodist, juxtaposing God's uncreated eternity with mortal man's return to the dust of the earth.
[^1649]: Fol. 286r. Conclusion of the funeral Ikos, citing Genesis 3:19 (*Dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return*), acknowledging universal mortal dissolution transformed by faith into the funeral threnody of *Alleluia*.
[^1650]: Fol. 286r. Ode 7 of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, opening with the incipit of the Heirmos *Дѣ́ти евре́йскїя въ пещѝ* (*The Hebrew children in the furnace boldly trod the flame*), based on Daniel 3.
[^1651]: Fol. 286r. Martyrikon troparion of Ode 7, commemorating the martyrs who directed all their desire to Christ alone, enduring torments while chanting the ancestral refrain of the Three Youths.
[^1652]: Fol. 286r. Troparion of Ode 7 petitioning that the departed servant be filled with celestial joy and gladness, joining the choir of the saints in praising the God of the fathers.
[^1653]: Fol. 286v. Troparion of Ode 7 petitioning for the departed the brightness of the heavenly Kingdom and the garment of incorruptibility (*нетлѣ́нїя оде́жду дару́я*), echoing the baptismal robe of immortality (Galatians 3:27).
[^1654]: Fol. 286v. Theotokion of Ode 7, highlighting the reversal of the ancestral curse of Eve through the Incarnation of Christ from the spotless Virgin Mother.
[^1655]: Fol. 286v. Ode 8 of the Funeral Canon in Tone 8, opening with the Heirmos incipit *Мусикї́йскихъ орга́новъ согла́сїе* (*The harmony of musical instruments sounded...*), recalling the Three Youths in Babylon defying Nebuchadnezzar's idolatrous music.
[^1656]: Fol. 286v. Martyrikon troparion of Ode 8, praising the martyrs who exchanged earthly afflictions for incorruptible crowns, chanting the universal canticle of Daniel 3:57.
[^1657]: Fol. 287r. Conclusion of the second troparion of Ode 8, celebrating the resurrection of all who rested in the grave by Christ's life-giving hand, and petitioning repose for the departed believer.
