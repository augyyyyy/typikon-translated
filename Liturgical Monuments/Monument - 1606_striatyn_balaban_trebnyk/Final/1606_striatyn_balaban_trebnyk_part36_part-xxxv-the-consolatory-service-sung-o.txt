# PART XXXV: THE CONSOLATORY [SERVICE], SUNG OR PERFORMED FOR EVERY PETITION [^2816]

[Woodcut headpiece: Christ the Great High Priest in episcopal vestments within an oval medallion inscribed with IC XC, framed by Renaissance-Baroque foliate and floral scrollwork; below, the title in red cinnabar vyaz and semi-uncial script:]

*The Priest begins:* **"Blessed is our God..."**[^2817] *Then the Trisagion; "All-Holy Trinity"; after "Our Father," [the Priest says:] "For Thine is the kingdom." "Lord, have mercy" (12 times); "Come, let us worship" (thrice); then Psalm 142:[^2818] "O Lord, hear my prayer." "God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us; blessed is He that comes in the Name of the Lord." Troparion, Tone 4:*[^2819]

*Troparion:* Unto the Theotokos let us now run with diligence, sinners and humble as we are, and let us fall down in repentance, crying out from the depth of the soul: O Lady, help, having compassion upon us; make haste, for we perish from the multitude of our sins; turn not Thy servants empty away, for Thee alone do we have as a protectress.[^2820]

*Glory... Both now...:*[^2821]

Never will we, the unworthy, cease to speak of Thy powers, O Theotokos; for hadst Thou not stood interceding for us, who would have delivered us from so great perils, or who would have preserved us free until now? We will not depart from Thee, O Lady; for Thou savest Thy servants ever from every calamity, O only blessed one.[^2822]

*Then Psalm 50: "Have mercy on me, O God."*[^2823]

*After this, the Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos. Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^2824] **"Having traversed the water as if it were dry land, and escaped from the evil of Egypt..."**[^2825]

*Troparion:* Held fast by many temptations, unto Thee do I flee, seeking salvation: O Mother of the Word and Virgin, save me from hardships and distresses.[^2826]

*Troparion:* The assaults of passions trouble me, and many afflictions fill my soul: bring peace, O Maiden, by the tranquility of Thy Son and God, O all-immaculate one.[^2827]

*Glory...:* Having given birth to the Savior and God, I entreat Thee, O Virgin: deliver me from hardships; for unto Thee do I now flee, stretching forth my soul and thoughts.[^2828]

*Both now...:*

*Theotokion:* Being in sorrow and grief, vouchsafe me the divine visitation and providence from Thee, O only Mother of God, as Thou art good and the Parent of the Good One.[^2829]

*Katavasia:* Save Thy servants from calamities, O Theotokos; for all of us, after God, flee unto Thee as an unbreakable wall and protectress.[^2830]

*This Katavasia we sing after each ode.*[^2831]

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* **"O Supreme Artificer of the celestial vault, O Lord, and Builder of the Church..."**[^2832]

*Troparion:* A protectress and covering of my life have I set Thee, O Virgin Mother of God: pilot me unto Thy harbor, O Cause of good things and confirmation of the faithful, O only all-lauded one.[^2833]

*Troparion:* I pray Thee, O Virgin, disperse the confusion of my soul and the tempest of grief; for Thou, O Bride of God, hast given birth to Christ, the Author of peace, O only all-pure one.[^2834]

*Glory...:* Having given birth to the Benefactor, the Cause of good things, pour forth the wealth of benevolence unto all; for all things canst Thou do, as mighty in strength, having borne Christ, O divinely blessed one.[^2835]

*Both now...:* When examined by grievous ailments and painful afflictions, O Virgin, help Thou me; for I know Thee to be an inexhaustible treasury of healings, unstolen and unspent.[^2836]

*Katavasia:* Save Thy servants from calamities, O Theotokos...[^2837]

*Then the deacon's litanies, in which the priest, or deacon, commemorates the one for whom he sings the moleben:*[^2838]

**Deacon [or Priest]:** Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee: hear, and have mercy.[^2839]

> **People:** "Lord, have mercy *(thrice).*"[^2840]

*If thou singest for the Tsar, say:*[^2841]

> **Deacon:** "Again we pray for our pious and God-preserved Tsar, *[Name]*, for his dominion, victory, endurance, peace, health, and salvation; and that the Lord our God may especially prosper and assist him in all things, and subdue under his feet every enemy and adversary."[^2842]

> **People:** "Lord, have mercy *(thrice).*"[^2843]

*And if thou singest for a warrior, say this:*[^2844]

> **Deacon:** "Again we pray for the mercy of God, and help, visitation, and strengthening; for the vouchsafing, and remission of sins of the Christ-loving host, the servant of God, *[Name]*."[^2845]

> **People:** "Lord, have mercy *(thrice).*"[^2846]

*After this, the prayer of exclamation:*[^2847]

> **Priest:** "Hear us, O God our Savior, the Hope of all the ends of the earth, and of those far off upon the sea; O Merciful One, be merciful unto us concerning our sins, and have mercy on us; for a merciful and mankind-loving God art Thou, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."[^2848]

*And if thou singest a moleben for a bishop, or an hegumen, or for a monk, or for a layman, say:*[^2849]

**Deacon [or Priest]:** Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee: hear, and have mercy.[^2850]

> **Deacon:** "Again we pray to the Lord our God, to deliver His servant, *[Name]*, from every affliction, wrath, misfortune, and necessity, and from every sickness of soul and body; and to forgive him every transgression, voluntary and involuntary; let us all say:"[^2851]

> **People:** "Lord, have mercy *(fifty times).*"[^2852]

*And if thou singest for brethren who have departed for service, and for those sailing upon the sea, say thus:*[^2853]

> **Deacon:** "Again we pray to the Lord our God for our brethren who have departed for service, and for those sailing upon the sea: to deliver them from every affliction, misfortune, wrath, and necessity, and from every contrary thing; and to have mercy on them, and to forgive them every transgression, voluntary and involuntary; let us all say: O Lord, hear and have mercy."[^2854]

> **People:** "Lord, have mercy *(fifty times).*"[^2855]

*And if thou singest for the city wherein thou dwellest, or for the monastery wherein thou abidest, that it may be delivered from the calamities which have befallen it:*[^2856]

> **Deacon:** "Again we pray to the Lord our God, that this place, and this holy monastery, and every city and countryside, may be preserved from famine, pestilence, earthquake, flood, fire, and sword, the invasion of foreign tribes, and civil war, and every deadly wound; and that our good and mankind-loving God may be merciful, meek, gentle, and propitious concerning our sins; and turn away all wrath stirred up against us, and deliver us from His impending righteous rebuke, and have mercy on us."[^2857]

*And if thou singest concerning drought, add this also:*[^2858]

> **Deacon:** "And that He may rain down upon the earth a good rain by His goodness, we pray Thee: O Lord, hear, and have mercy."[^2859]

> **People:** "Lord, have mercy *(fifty times).*"[^2860]

*Exclamation:*[^2861]

> **Priest:** **"Hear us, O God our Savior, the Hope..."**[^2862]

*Then the Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*[^2863]

A fervent prayer together, and an impregnable wall, a fountain of mercy, and[^2864] *(Sessional Hymn continued:)* ...and a refuge unto the world: diligently unto Thee do we cry out, O Theotokos and Lady: make haste, and deliver us from calamities, Thou who alone dost swiftly intercede.[^2865]

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^2866] **"I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation..."**

*Troparion:* Having given birth to the Helmsman and Lord, still the tempest of my transgressions and the confusion of my passions, O Bride of God.[^2867]

*Troparion:* Grant Thine abyss of compassion unto me who call upon Thee, O Thou who hast given birth to the Tender-hearted One and Savior of all who hymn Thee.[^2868]

*Glory...:* Having obtained Thee as hope, and confirmation, and an immovable wall of salvation, O all-immaculate one, we are delivered from all grievous evils.[^2869]

*Both now...:*

*(Both now... continued)*

*Theotokion:* Lying in sins upon the bed of my sickness, help Thou me as mankind-loving, O Theotokos, Mother and Ever-Virgin.[^2870]

*Katavasia:* **"Save Thy servants from calamities, O Theotokos; for..."**[^2871]

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^2872] **"Enlighten us by Thy commandments, O Lord..."**

*Troparion:* Fill our life with gladness, O pure one, granting Thine incorruptible joy, having given birth to the Cause of gladness.[^2873]

*Troparion:* Deliver us from calamities, O pure Theotokos, having given birth to eternal redemption and the peace that surpasses every understanding.[^2874]

*Glory...:* Disperse the mist of my transgressions, O Bride of God, by the light of Thy radiance, O Thou who hast given birth to the divine and pre-eternal Light.[^2875]

*Both now...:*

*(Both now... continued)*

*Theotokion:* Heal the infirmity of my soul, O pure one, vouchsafing Thy visitation; and grant me health by Thine intercessions.[^2876]

*Katavasia:* **"Save Thy servants from calamities..."**[^2877]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^2878] **"My prayer will I pour out unto the Lord, and unto Him..."**

*Troparion:* As He saved from death and corruption my nature, which had been seized by corruption and death, by giving Himself over unto death, pray Thou Thy Lord and Savior, O Virgin, to deliver me from the malice of enemies.[^2879]

*Troparion:* A protectress of life do I know Thee to be, and a firm guardian, O Virgin, dispelling the tumult of temptations and driving away the assaults of demons; and I pray Thee ever: deliver me from passions, O all-immaculate one.[^2880]

*Glory...:*

*(Glory... continued)*

*Troparion:* As a wall and refuge have we obtained Thee, and perfect salvation of souls, and spaciousness in afflictions, O Maiden; and in Thy light do we ever rejoice: O Lady, save us now from passions and calamities.[^2881]

*Both now...:*

*Theotokion:* Upon a bed of illness do I now lie, and there is no healing for my flesh; but O Thou who hast given birth to God and the Savior of the world, and the Deliverer from ailments: I pray unto Thee, O good one, deliver me from the corruption of diseases.[^2882]

*Katavasia:* **"Save Thy servants from calamities, O Theotokos..."**[^2883]

*The Little Litany of the Deacon:*[^2884]

> **Deacon:** **"Again and again in peace let us pray to the Lord."**

*Exclamation:*[^2885]

> **Priest:** **"For Thou art the King of peace, and the Savior of souls..."**

*Kontakion, Tone 6:*[^2886]

*Kontakion:* O Protection of Christians unashamed, Mediatrix unto the Creator unwavering: despise not the prayerful voices of sinners; but make haste, as Thou art good, to the help of us who faithfully cry unto Thee: hasten unto intercession, and speed unto supplication, ever protecting, O Theotokos, those who honor Thee.[^2887]

*Another Kontakion:*[^2888]

We have no other help, we have no other hope save Thee, O all-pure Virgin: do Thou help us; in Thee do we hope, and in Thee do we boast; for we are Thy servants: let us not be put to shame.

*Then:*

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*[^2889] **"I will remember Thy name in every generation and generation."**

*Verse:*

*Verse:* **"Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear."**[^2890]

*Then the Deacon:*[^2891]

> **Deacon:** **"Let us pray to the Lord."**

> **Priest:** **"For holy art Thou, our God, and unto Thee do we send up glory..."**

> **Choir:** **"Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord."**

> **Deacon:** **"And that we may be vouchsafed to hear the Holy Gospel worthily, in peace let us pray to the Lord God."**[^2892]

> **Deacon:** **"Wisdom! Upright! Let us hear the Holy Gospel."**

> **Priest:** **"Peace be unto all."**

**Deacon [or Priest]:** **"The Reading from the Holy Evangelist Luke."**[^2893]

*In those days, Mary arose and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Judah; and she entered into the house of Zacharias, and greeted Elizabeth. And it came to pass, that when Elizabeth heard the greeting of Mary*[^2894] *the babe leaped in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit, and cried out with a loud voice, and said: Blessed art Thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of Thy womb. And whence is this to me, that the Mother of my Lord should come unto me? For, lo, as soon as the voice of Thy greeting sounded in mine ears, the babe leaped in my womb for joy. And blessed is she that believed, for there shall be a fulfillment of those things which were told her from the Lord.*[^2895]

*And Mary said:*

*My soul magnifies the Lord, and my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior. For He has looked upon the humility of His handmaiden; for, behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed.*[^2896]

*For He that is mighty has done to me great things, and holy is His Name. And Mary remained with her about three months, and returned to her own house.*[^2897]

*Then:*

*Glory... Tone 2:*[^2898]

> **Choir:** **"O Father, Word, and Spirit, Holy Trinity: cleanse the multitude of our sins."**

*Both now...*

> **Choir:** **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos, O Merciful One: cleanse the multitude of our sins."**

*Verse:* **"Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy..."**[^2899]

*Then the Sticheron, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ възло́жше оу҆пова́нїе на нб҃се́хъ):*[^2900]

Abandon me not unto human protection, O all-pure Lady, but receive the supplication of Thy servants; for afflictions encompass me, and I cannot endure *(Sticheron continued:)* ...the shooting of demons; a covering have I not obtained, nor where to flee, wretched as I am, being ever vanquished; consolation have I none save Thee, O Lady of the world, Hope and Protectress of the faithful: despise not my supplication, but accomplish it.[^2901]

*The Deacon says the prayer:*[^2902]

> **Deacon:** **"Save, O God, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance; and visit Thy world with mercy and compassions; exalt the horn of Christians, and send down upon us Thy rich mercies: through the intercessions of our all-pure Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary; by the power of the precious and life-giving Cross[^2903] of the honorable celestial bodiless Hosts; of the honorable and glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John; of the holy, glorious, and all-lauded Apostles; of the holy, victorious Martyrs; of our venerable and God-bearing Fathers; of the holy and righteous Ancestors of God, Joachim and Anna; and of all Thy Saints: we beseech Thee, O greatly-merciful Lord, hear us sinners praying unto Thee, and have mercy on us; let us all say:"**[^2904][^2905]

> **People:** *Lord, have mercy (fifteen times).*[^2906]

*The Priest makes the exclamation:*

> **Priest:** **"Through the mercy and compassions of Thine Only-begotten Son, with Whom Thou art blessed[^2907] art blessed, together with Thy most holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."**[^2908]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^2909] **"The Children having arrived from Judea in Babylon of old..."**

*Troparion:* As Thou wast pleased to dispense our salvation, O Savior, Thou didst take up Thine abode in the womb of the Virgin, Whom Thou didst show forth unto the world as protectress: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou.[^2910]

*Troparion:* Entreat now the Desirer of mercy Whom Thou hast borne, O Mother, to deliver from transgressions and spiritual defilement me who cry unto Thy Son: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou.[^2911]

*Glory...:* A treasury of salvation, and a fountain of incorruption, and a tower[^2912] *(Glory... continued)* ...of affirmation, and a door of repentance hast Thou shown forth unto those who cry out: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou.

*Both now...:*

*Theotokion:* Bodily ailments and spiritual sins, O Child of God, vouchsafe Thou to heal for those who draw near with love unto Thy protection, O Maiden, who hast borne Christ the Savior for us in the flesh.[^2913]

*Katavasia:*[^2914] **"Save Thy servants from calamities, O Theotokos; for we..."**

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^2915] **"The King of heaven, Whom the angelic hosts hymn..."**

*Troparion:* Despise not him that requires help from Thee, O Virgin, who hymns and supremely exalts Christ unto all ages.[^2916]

*Troparion:* The infirmity of my soul dost Thou heal, and bodily sicknesses, O Virgin; therefore[^2917] *(Troparion continued)* ...we hymn Thee and supremely exalt Thee unto ages.

*Glory...:*

*Troparion:* Riches of healings dost Thou pour forth unto those who faithfully hymn Thee, O Maiden, and who supremely exalt Christ unto all ages.[^2918]

*Both now...:*

*Theotokion:* Thou dost drive away the assaults of temptations and the incursions of passions, O Virgin, so that we may hymn Thee and supremely exalt Thee unto all ages.[^2919]

*Katavasia:*[^2920] **"Save Thy servants from calamities, O Theotokos..."**

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^2921] **"Confessing Thee in truth to be the Theotokos, we magnify Thee..."**

*Troparion:* Turn not away from the stream of my tears, O Maiden who hast given birth to Christ, Who has wiped away every tear from every face.[^2922]

*Troparion:* Fill my heart with joy, O Virgin, who didst receive the fullness of joy, consuming sinful sorrow[^2923] *(Troparion continued)* ...consuming sinful sorrow.

*Glory...:*

*Troparion:* Enlighten with the beams of Thy light, O Virgin, driving away the gloom of ignorance from those who piously confess Thee to be the Theotokos.[^2924]

*Both now...:*

*Theotokion:* Heal him that lies in a place of affliction and infirmity, O Virgin, transforming him from illness into health.[^2925]

*Katavasia:*[^2926] O most radiant cloud, Mother of God, conquer those who conquer us by Thy sovereign and all-powerful right hand; and assist those who are in afflictions. Intercede for the wronged, and absolve the sins of Thy suppliants; for whatsoever Thou dost will, Thou art able to do.

**"It is truly meet to bless Thee, the Theotokos..."**[^2927]

*Then the Supplicatory Stichera, Tone 2:*[^2928]

*Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Є҆гда̀ ѿ дре́ва):*[^2929]

Thou dost pray for all, O good one, who flee with faith unto Thy mighty protection; for we sinners have no other continuous deliverance unto God in calamities and afflictions, being weighed down by many sins, O Mother of God the Most High. Therefore we fall down before Thee: deliver Thy servants from every circumstance.[^2930]

*Verse:*[^2931] **"I will remember Thy name in every generation and generation."**

Joy of all that sorrow, and protectress of the wronged, nourisher of the hungry, comfort of strangers, haven of the tempest-tossed, visitation of the sick, of the infirm[^2932] *(Sticheron continued)* ...protection and protectress, staff of old age, O Mother of God the Most High, Thou art, O all-immaculate one: make haste, we pray, that Thy servants may be saved.

*Verse:*[^2933] **"Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear; and forget thine own people, and the house of thy father."**

Rejoice, O all-immaculate Virgin; rejoice, honorable scepter of Christ the King; rejoice, thou who didst bud forth the mystical cluster of grapes; rejoice, door of heaven, and unburnt bush; rejoice, light of the whole world; rejoice, joy of all; rejoice, salvation of all; rejoice, protectress of all Christians, and refuge, O Lady.[^2934]

*Glory... Both now..., Tone 8:*[^2935]

Rejoice, praise of the universe; rejoice, temple of the Lord; rejoice, overshadowing mountain *(Tone 8 Theotokion continued)* ...rejoice, refuge; rejoice, golden lampstand; rejoice, glory of the Orthodox, O pure one; rejoice, Mary, Mother of Christ God; rejoice, paradise; rejoice, divine table; rejoice, temple; rejoice, golden jar; rejoice, joy of all.

*Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. After "Our Father":*[^2936]

*Troparion:*[^2937] **"Unto the Theotokos let us now diligently flee..."**

*Glory... Both now...:* **"Never will we keep silence..."**

*Then the diaconal petitions:*[^2938]

> **Deacon:** **"Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee..."**

*And all the rest in order, for whomever thou dost pray (see at the fourth ode).*

*The Priest makes the exclamation:*[^2939]

> **Priest:** **"Hear us, O God our Savior..."**

*Or:*

> **Priest:** **"For Thou art a merciful God and lover of mankind..."**

*And the Dismissal.*

[Fol. 484v. Blank leaf without print (vacat), separating the sequence of the Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos from the Order for the Elevation of the Precious Cross.][^2940]

### Notes

[^2816]: Folio 478r (physical page 971) headpiece and title: *УТѢШИ́ТЕЛНЫЙ ПѢ́ТЫЙ ИЛЍ БЫВА́ЕМЫЙ ЗА ВСѦ́КО прошѐніе* (A Consolatory [Canon or Service], sung or performed for every petition). In Ruthenian and Byzantine liturgical practice, this marks the opening of the celebrated Supplicatory Canon (*Paraklesis* or *Moleben*) to the Most Holy Theotokos, chanted in times of illness, tribulation, public crisis, or personal necessity.
[^2817]: Rubric on fol. 478r (physical page 971): The initial blessing by the priest (*Благословенъ Богъ нашъ*), followed by the customary Trisagion Prayers, the Lord's Prayer with priestly doxology, twelve repetitions of *Lord, have mercy*, and the triple Invitation to Worship (*Come, let us worship*).
[^2818]: Psalm 142 (Septuagint LXX versification; Masoretic 143): *Господи услыши молитву мою* (O Lord, hear my prayer). This psalm constitutes the traditional opening penitential psalm of the Paraklesis / Moleben.
[^2819]: Folio 478r (physical page 971): Announcement of the Troparion in Tone 4, following the verses of *God is the Lord* (*Богъ Господь*, Ps. 117:27 [LXX]).
[^2820]: Troparion of the Theotokos, Tone 4 (*Къ Богородицѣ прилѣжно нынѣ притецѣмъ* / *Τῇ Θεοτόκῳ ἐκτενῶς νῦν προσδράμωμεν*), on fol. 478r-v (physical pages 971-972). The text witnesses the classic Byzantine penitential supplicatory troparion.
[^2821]: Liturgical florid flourish `Ꙃ` on fol. 478v (physical page 972), marking the cue for *Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.*
[^2822]: Theotokion Troparion, Tone 4 (*Не умолчимъ никогдаже, Богородице* / *Οὐ σιωπήσομέν ποτε Θεοτόκε*), on fol. 478v (physical page 972).
[^2823]: Rubric on fol. 479r (physical page 973): Directing the reading of Psalm 50 (Septuagint LXX; Masoretic 51): *Помилуй мя, Боже* (Have mercy on me, O God).
[^2824]: Rubric on fol. 479r (physical page 973): Indicating the beginning of the Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos (*кано́нъ молбѣ̀ пр҃тѣ́й Бц҃и*), Tone 8, Ode 1.
[^2825]: Heirmos of Ode 1, Tone 8, on fol. 479r (physical page 973): *Во́дꙋ прошѐдъ ꙗ҆́ко по сꙋ́хꙋ, и҆з̾ є҆гѵ́пе[тскаго зла бѣжав]* (*Ὑγρὰν διοδεύσας ὡсеὶ ξηράν*). Printed by incipit only, in strict conformity with Ruthenian liturgical typography.
[^2826]: Troparion 1 of Ode 1, Tone 8 (*Многими содержимь напастьми* / *Πολλοῖς συνεχόμενος πειρασμοῖς*), on fol. 479r (physical page 973).
[^2827]: Troparion 2 of Ode 1, Tone 8 (*Страстей мя смущаютъ прилози* / *Παθῶν με ταράττουσι προσβολαί*), on fol. 479r (physical page 973).
[^2828]: Troparion 3 of Ode 1, Tone 8 (*Спаса рождшую тя и Бога молю* / *Σωτῆρα τεκοῦσάν σε καὶ Θεόν*), on fol. 479r (physical page 973), preceded by the cue for *Glory*.
[^2829]: Troparion 4 (Theotokion) of Ode 1, Tone 8, on fol. 479v (physical page 974): *Въ скръби мя суща и въ тузѣ* (*Νοσοῦντα τὸ σῶμα καὶ τὴν ψυχήν*). The 1606 Striatyn print renders the opening phrase as *Въ скръби мя суща и въ тузѣ* (Being in sorrow and grief), a distinct Ruthenian recensional witness compared to standard post-Nikonian editions (*Недугующа тѣломъ и душею*).
[^2830]: Katavasia after Ode 1 on fol. 479v (physical page 974): *Спаси отъ бѣдъ рабы своя, Богородице* (*Διάσωσον ἀπὸ κινδύνων τοὺς δούλους σου, Θεοτόκε*).
[^2831]: Rubric on fol. 479v (physical page 974): Directing that this Katavasia (*Спаси отъ бѣдъ...*) be chanted after each ode of the canon (*Сїю̀ катава́сїю пое́мъ по ко́еждо пѣ́сни*).
[^2832]: Heirmos of Ode 3, Tone 8, on fol. 479v (physical page 974): *Небесному кругу верхотворче Господи* (*Οὐρανίας ἁψῖδος ὀροφοῦργε Κύριε*), witnessed here by incipit only (*Нбⷭ҇ному крꙋ́гꙋ връхо:*).
[^2833]: Troparion 1 of Ode 3, Tone 8 (*Заступленіе и покровъ жизни моея* / *Προστασίαν καὶ σκέπην ζωῆς ἐμῆς*), on fol. 479v (physical page 974).
