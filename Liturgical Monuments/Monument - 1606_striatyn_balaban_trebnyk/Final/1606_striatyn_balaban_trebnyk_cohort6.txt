*Second Exorcism.*[^90]

**Deacon [or Priest]:** Let us pray to the Lord.

**Priest:**
[Fol. 18r. Decorative woodcut initial B.]
O God, holy, fearful, and glorious, Who art incomprehensible and unsearchable in all Thy works and Thy power: Thou Who didst ordain for thee, O devil, the torment of eternal punishment: through us, His unworthy servants, He commands thee and all thy collaborative power to depart from him that has newly been signed in the Name of our Lord Jesus Christ, our true God. I adjure thee therefore, all-evil, unclean, defiled, abominable, and alien spirit,

=== LEAF p54 ===

by the power of Jesus Christ, Who has all power in heaven and on earth; Who said unto the deaf and dumb demon: "Come out of the man, and enter no more into him!" Depart, recognize thy vain power, which had no authority even over the swine! Remember Him Who commanded thee at thy request to enter into the herd of swine. Fear God, by Whose command the earth was established upon the waters; Who created heaven, and set the mountains by a plummet, and the valleys by a measure; and placed sand as a boundary for the sea, and a firm path in the mighty waters; Who touches the mountains, and they smoke;[^91]

I adjure thee by the saving Passion of our Lord Jesus Christ, and His precious Body and Blood, and His fearful coming: for He shall come and will not delay, judging all the earth and thee, and He will torment thy collaborative power in fiery Gehenna, where the worm[^92]

*Fourth Exorcism Prayer.*[^94]

**Deacon [or Priest]:** Let us pray to the Lord.

**Priest:**
[Fol. 20r. Decorative woodcut initial S.]
O Sovereign Lord, Who hast made man according to Thine own image and likeness, and given him the power of life everlasting; and then, when he had fallen through sin, didst not despise him, but didst provide salvation for the world through the incarnation of Thy Christ: do Thou now, having delivered this Thy creature from the bondage of the enemy, receive him into Thy heavenly kingdom. Open his spiritual eyes,

that the illumination of Thy Gospel may shine in him. Yoke with his life an angel of light, delivering him from every snare of the adversary, from encounter with the evil one, from the noonday demon, and from wicked apparitions.[^95]
