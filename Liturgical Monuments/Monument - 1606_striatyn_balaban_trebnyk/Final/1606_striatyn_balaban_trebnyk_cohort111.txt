*...in a garden, or in a vineyard, or in a field.*[^3167]

**Saint Tryphon:** **"When I was in the village of Campsada, and was feeding the geese and attending unto them, wrath came down from God the Almighty not only upon that place, but also into the surrounding borders:[^3168] namely, wrath upon every vineyard, field, and garden, suddenly weighing down leaves and fruits, withering and causing them to vanish. Seeing therefore, I, the least Tryphon, the fruits thus perishing, and on this account men being exhausted by the utter destruction of the fruits of the earth—of fields, vineyards,...** (continues onto leaf p1102)

=== LEAF p1102 ===

**Saint Tryphon (continued):** **"...gardens, and herbs, and of diverse trees, delivered unto such ruin: having taken pity, I prayed unto the Lord my God that all the beasts that harm and corrupt the vineyards, and fields, and gardens, and diverse trees and herbs might be brought to naught; especially for those who dwell in that village of the adjoining lake, who came to me for petition and supplication.[^3169] And when I had bowed my knees in prayer, and stretched out my hands unto God, God, Who hears those that hope in Him, Himself sent from His prepared dwelling His Angel to strike...** (continues onto leaf p1103)

=== LEAF p1103 ===

**Saint Tryphon (continued):** **"...every tribe and every kind of evildoing beasts, that harm the vineyard, the fields, and the garden of the servant of God [Name]. And knowing clearly the names of these beasts, I pronounce them here: caterpillar, worm, locust, beetle, mole-cricket, grub, mole, mice, midges of diverse kinds, corn-weevil, moths, lice, destructive flies, ants, gadflies, wasps, the venom of scorpion, serpent, multipede, and whatever other such corruptions;[^3170] likewise corn-mildew, rot, hail-blight, rime-frost, evil blasts, fierce pestilence, and if there be any other thing that works harm unto men, withering the fruit of the vine, and of other...** (continues onto leaf p1104)

=== LEAF p1104 ===

**Saint Tryphon (continued):** **"...species of herbs.[^3171] And not only so, but I myself also bound them with an oath, that they should no more come to dwell upon the places of those who invoke me, but I commanded them [to depart] unto impassable places.**

*And the exorcism is as follows:*[^3172]

**Saint Tryphon:** **"I adjure you by the holy many-eyed Cherubim, and the six-winged Seraphim, who fly round about the throne, and cry out: 'Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of Sabaoth,' unto the glory of God the Father, Amen.[^3173] I adjure you by the holy Angels, and by all the Powers, and by ten thousands of ten thousands, and thousands of thousands, who stand with much fear before the glory of the Lord:...** (continues onto leaf p1105)

=== LEAF p1105 ===

**Saint Tryphon (continued):** **"...that ye harm not the vineyard, nor the field, nor the garden, nor the trees and herbs of the servant of God [Name]; but depart unto the wild mountains, unto unfruitful trees, whereon God hath granted you your daily food.[^3174] I adjure you also by the precious Body and Blood of Christ, our true God and Savior, through Whom both salvation and redemption hath been given unto us, and for Whose name we desire to die: that ye harm not the field, nor the vineyard, nor the garden, nor any fruit-bearing or unfruitful tree; and that ye harm not even a leaf of herbs from the surrounding boundary and the place of the servant of God...** (continues onto leaf p1106)

=== LEAF p1106 ===

**Saint Tryphon (continued):** **"[Name]. But if ye disobey me, and transgress the oath wherewith I have adjured you, ye shall have vengeance not from me, the humble and least Tryphon, but from the God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob, Who is coming to judge the living and the dead.[^3175] Therefore, as I told you before: depart unto the wild mountains, unto unfruitful trees. But if ye do not listen to me, I will entreat God the Lover of mankind that He send His Angel who is over the beasts; and he will bind you with iron and lead, and will slay you, because ye have cast away the oath of me, the humble Tryphon. Yea, and sparrows sent by my prayer shall devour you,[^3176]...** (continues onto leaf p1107)

=== LEAF p1107 ===

**Saint Tryphon (continued):** **"...Furthermore, I adjure you by the great Name written upon the stone, which [the stone] was not able to endure, but was split like wax before the face of fire.[^3177] Go out from our places unto the places which I foretold unto you: impassable, and waterless, and unfruitful. Go out from the place and from the boundary of the servants of God who call upon me for their help, and protection, and salvation; that in these things also the most-holy name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit may be glorified, now and ever, [and unto ages of ages. Amen]."**

*Peace be to all.*

*Bow your heads unto the Lord.*

*Prayer of Bowing of Heads:*[^3178]

**Priest:** **"O Master, Lord our God, Who by Thy coming in the flesh didst bless Bethlehem, and Gethsemane, and at one time...** (continues onto leaf p1108)

=== LEAF p1108 ===

**Priest (continued):** **"...the house of Jacob, which also, having been blessed, was renamed Israel; and Who didst bless the house of David: do Thou Thyself bless this possession also, and make it to bear fruit in abundance;[^3179] through the prayers of our most-pure Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary; by the power of the precious and life-creating Cross; of the precious and glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John; of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles; of the holy victorious Martyrs; of the holy, glorious Great-Martyrs Tryphon, Eustathius, and Julian; of Saint [Name]; and of all Saints.**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For Thou art He that blesses and sanctifies all things, and unto Thee do we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thy life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. [Amen]."**

=== LEAF p1109 ===

*Prayer over a Threshing Floor:*[^3180]

**Priest:** **"O Lord our God, Fountain of blessings, Who hast commanded the earth to bring forth fruit for the sake of Thy tender mercy and goodness: bless and multiply this threshing floor also, and the fruitful produce of Thy servants; and fill their barns with every good fruit: of wheat, of wine, and of oil; and preserve them from every temptation, with all those that belong unto them; and sanctify them by the knowledge of Thee; that, well-pleasing unto Thee, they may be counted worthy of Thine eternal blessings.**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For blessed is Thy name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. [Amen]."**

=== LEAF p1110 ===

*Prayer over the Blessing of Nets:*[^3181]

**Priest:** **"O Lord our God, Who didst satisfy five thousand of the people from five loaves and two fishes, and didst arrange to gather an abundance from the fragments: do Thou Thyself, O all-powerful Master, bless also the nets lying before Thee; through the prayers of our all-blessed, glorious Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary; of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostle, and Chief of the Apostles, Peter; and preserve those who partake of these fish in peace, and in health of soul and body.**

*Exclamation:*

**Priest:** **"For Thou art the Giver of all good things, and unto Thee do we send up glory, with the Father, and the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. [Amen]."**
