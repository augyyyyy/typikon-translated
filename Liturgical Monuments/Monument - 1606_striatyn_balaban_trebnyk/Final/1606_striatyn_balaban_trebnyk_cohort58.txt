# PART XXV: THE ORDER OF THE FUNERAL SINGING FOR ALL DEPARTED FATHERS AND OUR BRETHREN, AND OTHER DECEASED CHRISTIANS (THE PARASTAS)[^1619]

*The ranking priest, or the hegumen himself, with all the priests who are found there, go into the altar; and all the priests vest themselves in phelonia, and the deacons in sticharia. And having taken the censer with incense, the hegumen himself begins:*[^1620]

=== LEAF p572 ===
**Priest:** **Blessed is our God.**[^1621]

*And we say the Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. "Our Father...":*

**Priest:** **For Thine is the Kingdom...**

*Lord, have mercy (12 times). "Come, let us worship..." (thrice). Then Psalm 90: "He that dwells in the help of the Highest..." (see fol. 231).*[^1622]

*Glory... Both now... Alleluia (thrice) in Tone 8. And we sing the Troparion in the same tone:*[^1623]

*Troparion, Tone 8:*

O Thou Who by the depth of wisdom in love for mankind dost order all things, and dost dispense unto all that which is profitable, O only Creator: give rest, O Lord, unto the soul of Thy servant; for on Thee have they set their hope, on our Creator and Fashioner and God.

*Glory...*

For on Thee have they set their hope, on our Creator and Fashioner and God.

*Both now...*

=== LEAF p573 ===
*Theotokion, Tone 8:*[^1624]

Thee we have as a city and a haven, and an acceptable intercessor before God Who was born of thee, O unwedded Mother of God, the salvation of the faithful.

*Then the usual diaconal petitions, and the prayer:* **O God of spirits...** *together, all the priests.*[^1625]

*Then we sing the Blameless according to their order, until we arrive at the verse which is:*

**Unto the ages I will not forget Thy statutes, for in them Thou hast given me life.**

*We say it, singing it thrice. Then again the priest says the usual diaconal petitions, and the prayer, together all [the priests]. Then we complete the Blameless unto the end according to their order, and with their troparia, even as thou hast in the layman's burial, all in order.*[^1626]

=== LEAF p574 ===
*(See fol. 232).* *Then again the diaconal petitions and the prayer. Afterwards Psalm 50:* **Have mercy on me, O God...**[^1627]

*Canon for the Departed, Tone 8. Ode 1.*[^1628]

*Heirmos:* **Having traversed the water as upon dry land...**

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Through the death of Christ, and emulating His holy passions, all the venerable martyrs attained unto the divine and blessed life.

*Troparion:*

Overlooking the sins of youth and passing by transgressions, O Christ Savior, number Thy servant who has fallen asleep before with Thine elect.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Unto him that has obtained glory and joy, having acquired a blessed life, grant richly unto Thy servant whom Thou...

=== LEAF p575 ===
...hast taken, O Plenteous in mercy.[^1629]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst conceive the Father's Word, hypostatically united unto the flesh, Who came forth from thee, O all-spotless Maiden, and by divine power abolished Hades.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* **Of the heavenly vault...**[^1630]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

Cleansed from the ancient ancestral fall by baptism and rebirth, and sprinkled with the streams of their own blood, the blessed [martyrs] reign with Christ.

*Troparion:*

O Savior, Who of Thine own will wast laid dead in a tomb, and dost raise up them that dwell in the tombs: be well pleased that he whom Thou hast taken from us should dwell in the tabernacles of Thy saints.

*Glory...*

=== LEAF p576 ===
*Troparion:*[^1631]

Implored by Thy divine compassion, O Master, and by Thy consubstantial innate goodness: grant remission of sins unto Thy servant, and give him rest with Thyself.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Incarnate from thy womb and having become man, He that is pre-eminently the Lover of mankind saves man from the gates of death, O all-pure Mother of God, who alone art lauded.

*Katavasia [Heirmos]:*[^1632]

**Celestial vault's supreme Creator, O Lord, and Builder of the Church: do Thou confirm me in Thy love, Thou pinnacle of desires and confirmation of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.**

*Then the diaconal petitions, and the prayer:* **O God of spirits...** *After this, the Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*

=== LEAF p577 ===
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^1633]

Give rest, O our Savior, with the righteous unto Thy servant, and settle him in Thy courts, even as it is written: overlooking, as Thou art good, his sins, both voluntary and involuntary, and all things known and unknown, O Lover of mankind.

*Glory...*

And all things known and unknown, O Lover of mankind.

*Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou Who didst shine forth from the Virgin unto the world, O Christ God, and through her didst reveal [us as] sons of light: have mercy on us.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* **Thou art my Lord, O Christ, Thou art also my strength...**[^1634]

*Troparion [Martyrikon]:*

That they might behold Thy glory, O Master, and brightly obtain in heaven the rays of Thy light-giving [splendor], the divine martyrs endured every manner of torment...

=== LEAF p578 ===
...singing unto Thee, O Christ: "Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!"[^1635]

*Troparion:*

Many mansions are with Thee, O Savior, apportioned unto all according to dignity, according to the measure of virtues: be well pleased, therefore, O Compassionate One, that he who has faithfully departed, piously crying unto Thee: "Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!", should fill these.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*[^1636]

Having appeared as a man like unto us, O Immortal One, Thou didst manifestly endure death for all, and showedst the path unto life. As the Lover of mankind, vouchsafe unto him that has departed, granting remission of sins as Master, and bestowing communion in [Thy] light.

*Both now...*

=== LEAF p579 ===
*Theotokion:*[^1637]

Thou art the boast of the faithful, O unwedded Bride; thou art the protectress, thou art also the refuge of Christians, a wall and a haven. Thou bearest supplications unto Thine own Son, O all-spotless one, and savest from perils those who with faith and love know thee as pure Mother of God.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* **Why hast Thou cast me away...**[^1638]

*Troparion:*

Having had compassion, vouchsafe him that has departed unto immortal life to partake of Thine ineffable glory, and of Thy beatitudes which are beyond word, in the tabernacles of the righteous, where the voice of them that feast is beautiful, O only Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Where the angelic ranks and the assemblies of the righteous rejoice, in the bosom of Abraham...

=== LEAF p580 ===
...settle Thy servant, O Savior; and be well pleased that he stand with boldness before the dread and divine throne, O Compassionate One.[^1639]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst appear unto us as purification, righteousness, and redemption; and by Thy stripe Thou hast healed our weakness. Do Thou Thyself, therefore, O Compassionate One, as Thou art good, establish him that has departed in the delight of paradise.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*[^1640]

Mercifully, O Merciful One, Thou didst assume the measure of humanity, Who art adorned with the exceeding heights of divine glory: taking unto Thyself from virginal loins an animate flesh, whereby Thou didst destroy death.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* **Cleanse me, O Savior, for many...**
