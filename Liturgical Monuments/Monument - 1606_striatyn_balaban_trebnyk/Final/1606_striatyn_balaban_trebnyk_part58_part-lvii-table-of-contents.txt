[Rear (lower) exterior binding cover of the monument: authentic contemporary early seventeenth-century binding (circa 1606) from the Balaban printing house in Striatyn. Dark brown calfskin over bevelled wooden boards with elaborate blind tooling. On the left fore-edge are preserved two wide leather clasp straps secured to the wooden board with four dark iron rivets, displaying surface craquelure from age. On the right edge is the spine joint showing the ridges of five raised cords and traces of leather reinforcement at the head of the spine. The blind-tooled decorative scheme comprises a wide outer ornamental border of roll-stamped foliate vines, leaves, and palmettes enclosed by double concentric linear fillet rules, and an inner rectangular field. The central panel is partitioned by a diagonal saltire cross of double linear fillets into four triangular compartments, each containing an embossed circular medallion bearing the zoomorphic symbols of the Four Evangelists (the Tetramorph): eagle, lion, ox, and angel. In the upper and lower horizontal compartments of the inner field are placed additional circular medallions with profile busts and stylized foliate palmettes. The leather surface displays authentic abrasions, rubbing, craquelure, and patina from centuries of liturgical use, exposing the wooden board at the corner edges.][^4364]
