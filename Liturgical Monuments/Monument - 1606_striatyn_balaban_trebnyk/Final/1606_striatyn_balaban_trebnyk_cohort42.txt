# PART XVII: PRAYERS OF THANKSGIVING AFTER HOLY COMMUNION[^1278]

*When thou hast obtained the most precious communion of the gifts of the life-creating Mysteries, sing immediately, give great thanks, and say these things fervently from the soul unto God:*[^1279]

*Prayer of Saint Basil the Great, after Holy Communion:*

I thank Thee, O Lord my God, that Thou hast not rejected me, a sinner, but hast vouchsafed me to be a partaker of Thy holy things. I thank Thee that Thou hast vouchsafed me, unworthy though I be, to partake of Thine all-pure and heavenly gifts. But, O Master[^1280]

=== LEAF p419 ===
Lover of mankind, Who for our sake didst die and rise again, and didst bestow upon us these Thy dread and life-creating Mysteries: grant that these may be unto me for the healing of both soul and body, for the driving away of every adversary, for the enlightenment of the eyes of my heart, for the peacemaking of my soul's powers, for faith unashamed, for love unfeigned, for the fullness of wisdom, for the keeping of Thy commandments, for the increase of Thy divine grace, and for the attainment of Thy kingdom;[^1281] that preserved by Thy holiness, I may ever remember Thy grace, and no longer live unto myself,[^1282]

=== LEAF p420 ===
but unto Thee, our Master and Benefactor; and thus departing this life in hope of eternal life, I may attain unto everlasting rest, where the voice of them that keep festival is unceasing, and infinite is the delight of them that behold the ineffable beauty of Thy countenance. For Thou art the true desire and ineffable gladness of them that love Thee, O Christ our God, and all creation doth glorify Thee unto ages. Amen.[^1283]
