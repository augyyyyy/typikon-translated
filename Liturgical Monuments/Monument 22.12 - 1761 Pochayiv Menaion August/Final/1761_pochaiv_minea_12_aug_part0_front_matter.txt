# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 12: August (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-12-august-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

*(Binding — Upper cover: dark leather with tooling, ornamental border of interlaced strapwork, a central gilded blossoming cross, and the numeral 8 in a dotted semicircle below) (Endpaper — Pastedown leaf with botanical pattern: green trefoil leaves and blue blossoms on a light olive ground with white undulating lines) (Flyleaf — Archival flyleaf with later cursive manuscript liturgical commemorations, cancelled with a diagonal blue pencil line)*[^1]

*At the dismissal:*
`<Bless:`
*Amen. Establish, O God, the most pious, most autocratic, great sovereign, our Emperor Nicholas Alexandrovich, and the Holy Orthodox faith of Orthodox Christians unto ages of ages.*

*Many Years:*
*The most pious, most autocratic, great sovereign, our Emperor Nicholas Alexandrovich of all Russia, His most pious consort the Lady Empress Alexandra Feodorovna, His most pious mother the Lady Empress Maria Feodorovna, His heir the orthodox sovereign Tsarevich and Grand Duke [Alexei] Nikolaevich, the Grand Duke Nicholas Nikolaevich, and the entire Ruling House, the Most Holy Governing Synod, and our lord the Most Reverend Eulogius, Archbishop of Volhynia and Zhytomyr, Sacred Archimandrite of the Pochaiv Lavra, and the Most Reverend Dionysius, Bishop of Kremenets, and all orthodox Christians: Lord, preserve them for many years.>` **THE BOOK OF THE MENAION**

**Month of August,**

*To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, under the realm of the great*

**KING AUGUSTUS THE THIRD,**[^2]

*By the blessing of His Grace Lord*[^3]

**SYLVESTER LUBIENIECKI-RUDNITSKY,**[^4]

*Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh, by the goodwill of the Most Reverend Father Hieromonk*

**HYPATIUS BILINSKY,**[^5]

*Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great, through the zeal and expense of the monks of the same Order, in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv, in print*

**WAS PUBLISHED.**

*In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers 7269,*
*And from the Nativity of Christ 1761.*

### Notes

[^1]: This archival flyleaf contains an unprinted late imperial Russian manuscript addition (c. 1913–1914) recording dismissal commemorations and a Polychronion (*Mnogoletiye*) for Emperor Nicholas II and Volhynian hierarchs (Archbishop Eulogius Georgiyevsky and Bishop Dionysius Valedinsky), entered after the Pochaiv Lavra was annexed into the Russian Orthodox Church (1831), subsequently struck through with a blue pencil line.
[^2]: Augustus III (1696–1763), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania (reigned 1734–1763).
[^3]: Slavonic: *куръ* (from Greek *κῦρ*, *κύριος*), an honorific ecclesiastical title applied to Eastern Catholic and Orthodox hierarchs in the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.
[^4]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Lubieniecki-Rudnicki, consecrated 1750, died 1777), Ruthenian Uniate Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh, Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus'.
[^5]: Hypatius (Ipatii) Bilinsky (1704–1792), Basilian Protoarchimandrite (in office 1759–1771), active promoter of liturgical printing at the Pochaiv Lavra.
