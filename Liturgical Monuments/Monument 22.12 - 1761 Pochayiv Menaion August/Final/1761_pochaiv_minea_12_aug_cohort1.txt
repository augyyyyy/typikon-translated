# MONTH OF AUGUST

*(Having 31 days)*

*August 1. The Procession of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross of the Lord, and the Holy Seven Maccabee Martyrs, with their Mother Solomonia and their Teacher Eleazar:*

*On the first day: The Procession of the Precious Wood of the Honorable and Life-Creating Cross; and the commemoration of the Holy Seven Maccabee Martyrs, and their mother Solomonia, and their teacher Eleazar.*

*At Vespers, the usual Kathisma.*

*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera on 6: of the Cross, 3.*

*Tone 4, Podoben: "Being called from on high" (Званный свыше):*

*Today the divine assembly of the faithful rejoices; for the Cross appears now unto the ends of the earth, illuminating the firmament with unapproachable light, enlightening the air, and adorning the face of the earth. The Church of Christ sings with divine songs, and serving, venerates the divine and most marvelous Cross that preserves her from on high. Strengthened by its power, let us draw near to the Master, crying out: Bring peace unto the world, and enlighten our souls.*

*Let creation rejoice and dance; for today the Cross hath shone from heaven unto the ends of the earth, enlightening the terrestrial bounds, and showing forth the scattered ones gathered together. Today men make festival with the angelic choirs; for the dividing wall of enmity hath been broken down by the Cross, and by divine grace He hath manifestly united all into one. Therefore, outshining the sun, it enlightens the whole creation by grace, and makes clear and saves those who faithfully venerate it.*

*Shining brighter than the solar rays, desiring to show unto the world the honorable scepter of Christ the King, and His consummation, the divine Cross: behold, it comes forth clearly unto the ends of the earth. This Cross hath led forth mankind from hades; having utterly despoiled hades, cast down the enemy, and brought the pride of demons to naught, it now manifests*

=== LEAF p6 ===
*the Resurrection of the Savior, and saves those who cry: Bring peace unto the world, and enlighten our souls.*

*And of the Saints, 3 Stichera.*

*Tone 1, Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (Всехвальніи мученицы):*

*The summit of the Law, raised upon seven pillars, the torment of tyranny shook not. For the noble youths and brethren, keepers of the martyric tradition, manfully endured the bestial rage of the persecutor, surrendering their bodies unto the executioners.*

*Truly having lifted up their mind above the visible, the pious and valiant youths, together with their divinely wise mother, severed their bodily limbs, sustained by great hopes; which now having obtained, they rest in the bosom of Abraham their forefather.*

*With courageous soul firmly arming themselves and as it were despising fury, the most sacred Eleazar and the all-wise youths with their divinely wise mother manfully contended against the adversary for the sake of piety and the keeping of ancestral law.*

*Glory... Tone 8. By John the Monk:*[^6]

*The holy Maccabees said unto the tyrant: To us, O Antiochus, there is one King, God, from Whom we have our being, and unto Whom we return; another world awaits us, higher and mightier than the visible. Our homeland is Jerusalem, steadfast and imperishable; and our festival is life with the angels. O Lord, by their prayers, have mercy and save us.*

*Both now... Tone 8. By John the Monk:*

*He Whom of old Moses prefigured in himself when he overthrew and conquered Amalek; and David the hymnodist, crying aloud unto Thy footstool, commanded us to bow down: today we sinners bow down with unworthy lips unto Thy precious Cross, O Christ God, and hymning Thee Who wast pleased to be nailed upon it, we pray: O Lord, with the thief make us worthy of Thy Kingdom.*

*At the Aposticha of the Octoechos.*

*Glory... Tone 8. By Cosmas the Monk:*[^7]

*The souls of the righteous are in the hand of the Lord, as of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the forefathers before the Law and ancestors of the Maccabees, who are now praised by us. For these courageous ones, being Abraham's grandsons, having emulated the faith of their forefather Abraham, strived even unto death for the sake of piety. For being piously reared and having lawfully suffered, they refuted the impiety of the proud Antiochus; and accounting the temporal life as nothing for the sake of the eternal, they offered all unto God: soul, courage, understanding, and tender body*


=== LEAF p7 ===
*tender body, and the rearing that yielded the reward of purity. O pious root, from which ye blossomed, O Maccabees! O holy mother, who didst bear the number equal to the week of seven! But we beseech you, O Maccabees, with your mother Solomonia and the wise priest Eleazar: when ye stand before Christ God, for Whose sake ye labored, to receive from Him the labors of your fruits, make fervent supplication for mankind; for He doth whatsoever He wills, and will fulfill the desires of you that fear Him.*

*Both now... Tone 8:*

*The voice of Thy prophet Moses is fulfilled, O God, saying: Ye shall see your Life hanging before your eyes.*[^8] *Today the Cross is exalted, and the world is freed from delusion. Today the Resurrection of Christ is renewed, and the ends of the earth rejoice, offering a song unto Thee in cymbals after the manner of David, and saying: Thou hast wrought salvation in the midst of the earth, O God, the Cross and the Resurrection; for the sake whereof Thou hast saved us, O Good One and Lover of mankind: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee.*

*Troparion, Tone 1:*

*By the sufferings which the saints endured for Thee, be Thou entreated, O Lord, and heal all our infirmities, O Lover of mankind, we pray Thee.*

*Glory... Both now... Tone 1:*

*Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance, granting victories unto our orthodox King*[^9] *over adversaries, and preserving Thy habitation by Thy Cross.*

*Be it known that before the sounding of the klepalo, the priest enters the vestry and places the precious Cross upon the Table of Oblation. And after the dismissal of Vespers, the priest and the deacon vest in sacred robes. And the deacon censes the precious Cross, and says:*

**Deacon:** *Master, bless.*

**Priest:** *Blessed is our God.*

*Then, the Trisagion. After Our Father:*

*Troparion of the Cross. Glory... Both now... Kontakion.*

*And the priest takes the Cross with the tray upon his head, and carries it into the sanctuary, two lamps preceding him. And he places the Cross upon the Holy Table, in the place of the Gospel, and the Gospel upon the High Place; and they kindle a candle before it for the entire night.*

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord," the Troparion of the Cross, twice; Glory... of the Martyrs; Both now... of the Cross. And the usual Kathismata, and the Sessional Hymns of the Octoechos, and a reading. One Canon of the Octoechos, on 4; and of the Cross, on 6, Tone 6; and of the Martyrs, on 4, Tone 8.*

=== LEAF p8 ===
*If this feast fall on a Saturday: and if it be a temple of Christ or of the Theotokos, we sing the Canon of the temple with the Heirmos on 4; and of the Cross on 6; and of the Saints on 4. Katavasia: "Moses inscribed the Cross."*

*Canon of the Cross, the acrostic whereof is: "I, the faithful one, worship the Cross unto salvation."*[^10]

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos:*

*Traversing the deep as on dry land, Israel, seeing Pharaoh the pursuer drowning with his feet, cried aloud: Let us sing unto God a song of victory!*

*Troparia:*

*I worship the Cross, the salvation of the faithful, and fervently embrace it, and clasping it I cry: O all-blessed Tree of Christ, enlighten my soul and mind, I pray.*

*The sign of the Cross hath appeared today victorious over passions and demons. Therefore all we faithful, having brightly enlightened our souls, now embrace it.*

*The precious Cross shines forth radiant beams, and enlightens those who faithfully worship it today, sanctifying our souls and bodies.*

*The life-giving Cross set forth and seen sends forth the light-resembling ray of grace: let us draw near and receive enlightenment, joy, and salvation, and remission, offering praise unto the Lord.*

*Theotokion:*

*O most pure Virgin, who in the flesh didst bear Christ Who suffered voluntarily upon the Cross for our sake: save by thy supplications those who honor thee with reverence.*

*Another Canon of the Martyrs, on 4.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

*Having passed through the water as on dry land, and having escaped the evil of Egypt, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Deliverer and God!*

*Troparia:*

*The choir of the sevenfold sufferers appeared of one mind in afflictions; for the confirmation of ancestral law, they refused not to die.*

*Having despised the glory upon earth, through desire for the divine Kingdom, the divine Maccabees endured every pain; therefore are they worthily honored today.*

*Keepers of the Law of Moses, truly valiant Maccabees, brethren one with another appeared, when they trampled underfoot the impiety of Antiochus.*

*Theotokion:*

*The ranks of angels and men, O unwedded Mother, praise thee without ceasing; for thou didst bear in thine arms as an infant the Creator of all.*

*Katavasia:*

*Moses, having inscribed the Cross straightway with his rod, divided the Red Sea for Israel who went on foot; and striking it across against the chariots of Pharaoh...*

=== LEAF p9 ===
*...struck and united it, drawing the invincible weapon across. Therefore let us sing unto Christ our God, for He hath been glorified.*

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

*There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and hast established us upon the rock of Thy confession.*

*Troparia:*

*The blessed Tree, the weapon of life, was of old given to be guarded because of the transgression of the first-fashioned Adam; but the Cross made a path unto this.*

*With eyes, lips, and souls, faces and hearts, let us all worship the all-holy Cross of Christ the Life-Giver, whereby the whole world is sanctified.*

*Today having prophetically become temples unto God by worshiping the Tree of the all-holy Cross, we, His heirs, all kiss it with fear.*

*We hymn Thee, O Cross, and praying with faith in Thy power: deliver us from the snares of enemies, and direct us unto the harbor of salvation of all who sing unto Thee.*

*Theotokion:*

*As a Virgin thou didst bear the Son, born before thee of the Father before the ages without a mother from God, crucified in the flesh, that He might save those who had sinned aforetime.*

*Another Canon. Heirmos:*

*O Lord, Creator of the celestial vault and Builder of the Church, do Thou establish me in Thy love, O summit of desires, confirmation of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.*

*Troparia:*

*Walking in the ancestral lawful ordinances, O blessed sufferers of Christ, by your blood ye acquired unfading life, O all-lauded ones.*

*Neither fire nor sword was ever able to overturn your courageous disposition in the faith, O blessed ones, wherein ye brought to naught the insolence of the tormentors.*

*Having endured tortures for the sake of Christ God, O all-lauded martyrs, ye received from on high the honors of victory, unceasingly praying that He save our souls.*

*Theotokion:*

*As a Virgin thou didst give birth, unwedded one, and a Virgin didst thou remain, O unwedded Mother, Theotokos Mary: entreat Christ our God that we may be saved.*

*Katavasia:*

*The rod is accepted as an image of the mystery, for by its budding it designated the priest;*[^11] *and for the Church that aforetime was barren, now hath blossomed forth the Tree of the Cross, unto power and establishment.*

*Kontakion of the Saints, Tone 2, Podoben: "Seeking the highest" (Въ вышнихъ ищя):*

*O sevenfold pillars of the Wisdom of God, and luminous beacons of the divine Light, all-wise Maccabees, most great martyrs before the era of martyrs: with them pray unto the God of all*

=== LEAF p10 ===
*...pray that they who honor you may be saved.*

*Ikos:*

*Fervently praise thy God, O Zion, for He hath strengthened the bars of thy gates, and hath blessed thy children;*[^12] *for as an invincible army, a host truly valiant and strong in wisdom, they piously stood against the wiles of the wicked, and together receive the crowns of victory of heavenly Zion, and stand before the divine throne, unceasingly praying for all: entreat that they who hymn you may be saved.*

*Sessional Hymn of the Saints, Tone 8, Podoben: "Guiding unto wisdom" (Премудрости):*

*Piously instructed, O wise youths, ye manfully put to shame the threats of the tormentors in martyric fashion; for having been champions of the Law and obedient to the fathers, O holy ones, ye zealously suffered with your divinely wise mother. Therefore, having purchased truly heavenly life by death, rejoice eternally, O courageous Maccabees: entreat Christ God to grant remission of offenses unto those who with love honor your holy memory.*

*Glory... Both now... of the Cross, Tone 6:*

*Thy Cross, O Lord, is sanctified, for in it healings come unto those who are infirm in sins; therefore we fall down before Thee: Have mercy on us.*

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

*Christ is my power, God and Lord, the venerable Church sings in godly manner, crying aloud from a pure mind, feasting in the Lord.*

*Troparia:*

*Let all the earth drop down joy; let the trees of the forest rejoice: today made radiant by the all-venerable Cross, shining unto the ends of the earth.*

*Showing the instrument of slaying to be life-bearing unto the world, an invincible victorious weapon, O most marvelous Cross: enlighten our hearts.*

*Thou art the divine victory; thou art the working of our salvation; thou art the triumph for the faithful and the divine sacrifice, O all-venerable Cross: sanctify those who sing unto thee.*

*For with all the earth let the passion-bearers, martyrs, and apostles rejoice, and the souls of the righteous now joyfully make glad; and the life-giving Tree visibly set forth in the midst saves all, and abounds with grace for the faithful.*

*Theotokion:*

*Truly thou hast appeared; truly thou didst bear the Son of the Most High, Who stretched out His hands upon the Cross, O Virgin Mother Mary, and called the world.*

*Another Canon. Heirmos:*

*From the overshadowing mountain...*
