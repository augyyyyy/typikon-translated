# APPENDIX: THEOTOKIA IN THE EIGHT TONES

*Resurrectional Theotokia in the Eight Tones:*

*Tone 1. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of all the world, who blossomed forth from men and bore the Master, the heavenly gate, the song of the bodiless hosts and the adornment of the faithful; for she was revealed as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the barrier of enmity, brought in peace and opened the kingdom.[^959] Having her, therefore, as the confirmation of our faith, we have as champion the Lord who was born of her. Be of good courage, therefore; be of good courage, O people of God; for He shall conquer the enemies, as He is almighty.[^960]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for thou, a Virgin, didst give birth, and after childbirth didst remain a Virgin as before childbirth, for God was He who was born; therefore He also reshaped nature.[^961] But O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants brought unto thee in thy temple; but as thou bearest in thine arms Him who is compassionate, have mercy upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 2.*
*Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
The shadow of the Law has passed away now that grace has come; for as the bush was not consumed when burning with fire,[^962] so didst thou, a Virgin, give birth and remain a Virgin. Instead of a pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness has shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls.[^963]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O new marvel among all ancient marvels! For who has known a mother who gave birth without a man, and carries in her arms Him who contains all creation? It is the will of God that was born. As thou hast borne Him as an infant in thine arms, O all-pure one, and hast maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to pray for those who honor thee, to have compassion and save our souls.

*Tone 3. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
How shall we not marvel at thy God-man childbirth, O all-pure one? For having known no trial of man, O all-spotless one, thou didst bear without a father a Son in the flesh, who was begotten of the Father before the ages without a mother, who underwent no mutation, nor confusion, nor division but preserved whole the properties of each nature.[^964] Therefore, O Mother, Virgin Sovereign Lady, pray to Him that our souls may be saved, who confess thee to be the Theotokos in Orthodox manner.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Without seed from the Holy Spirit, but by the will of the Father, thou didst conceive the Son of God, who was with the Father before the ages without a mother; and for our sake, having come from thee without a father, thou didst bear Him in the flesh and suckle Him as an infant with milk.[^965] Therefore cease not to pray that our souls may be delivered from tribulations.

*Tone 4. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
The prophet David, who was the ancestor of God through thee, in song proclaimed concerning thee unto Him who did great things for thee: The queen stood at Thy right hand.[^966] For God, who was pleased to become man from thee without a father, revealed thee as mother and mediatrix of life, that He might renew His image corrupted by passions,[^967] and having found the sheep that went astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulders,[^968] He might bring it to the Father, and according to His own will unite it with the heavenly hosts, and save the world, O Theotokos, for Christ has great and rich mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Look upon the supplications of thy servants, O all-spotless one, quelling the fierce assaults against us, turning away from us every affliction. For we have thee alone as a firm and sure foundation, and have acquired thy protection; let us not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady, who call upon thee. Hasten to the supplication of those who cry aloud in faith to thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, help of all, joy and protection, and salvation of our souls!

*Tone 5. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
In the Red Sea, an image of the unwedded Bride was inscribed of old.[^969] There Moses was the divider of the water; here Gabriel was the minister of the marvel. Then Israel traversed the deep dry-shod; now the Virgin has given birth to Christ without seed. The sea remained impassable after the passage of Israel; the blameless one remained incorrupt after the birth of Emmanuel.[^970] O Thou who art, and wast before, and hast appeared as man: O God, have mercy on us!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Thou art the temple and the door, the palace and the throne of the King, O all-honored Virgin, through whom my Redeemer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of righteousness,[^971] desiring to enlighten those whom He created according to His own image with His hand.[^972] Therefore, O all-hymned one, as thou hast acquired maternal boldness toward Him unceasingly pray that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 6. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
Who shall not call thee blessed, O most holy Virgin? Who shall not praise thy childbirth without spot? For the only-begotten Son, who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the same came forth from thee, pure one, having ineffably become incarnate: being God by nature, and having become man by nature for our sake; not divided into two persons, but recognized in two natures without confusion.[^973] Pray to Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may have mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
My Creator and Redeemer, Christ the Lord, having come forth from thy womb, O all-pure one, and clothed Himself in me, has freed Adam from the ancient curse.[^974] Therefore unto thee, O all-pure one, as the Mother of God and Virgin in truth, we cry aloud unceasingly with the angel: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady! Rejoice, protection and defense, and salvation of our souls![^975]

*Tone 7. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
Thou wast known as a Mother beyond nature, O Theotokos, yet didst remain a Virgin beyond word and understanding; and the marvel of thy childbirth no tongue can recount. For as the conception was all-glorious, O pure one, so the manner of birth is incomprehensible: for where God so wills, the order of nature is overcome.[^976] Therefore, knowing thee to be the Mother of God, we fervently beseech thee: pray that our souls may be saved.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Fleeing under thy protection, O Sovereign Lady, all we who are born of earth cry aloud to thee: O Theotokos, our hope, deliver us from our boundless transgressions, and save our souls!

*Tone 8. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*
Out of love for mankind, the King of heaven appeared upon earth and dwelt among men;[^977] for taking flesh from the pure Virgin, and having come forth from her with the assumed human nature, He is one Son, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis.[^978] Therefore, truly preaching Him as perfect God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God. Beseech Him, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may have mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O unwedded Virgin, who didst ineffably conceive God in the flesh, Mother of God the Most High: receive the supplications of thy servants, O all-spotless one, granting unto all cleansing of transgressions; now receiving our supplications, pray that we all may be saved.

*Theotokia in the Eight Tones:*
*Sung when there is "Glory" to the Saint in the Menaion, and "Both now" according to the Tone of the Saint.*

*Tone 1.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Joy of the heavenly hosts, and firm protection of men upon earth, O all-pure Virgin, save us who flee unto thee; for after God, we have placed our hope in thee, O Theotokos.

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Holier than all the holy hosts, more honorable than all creation, O Theotokos, Sovereign Lady of the world: save us who bore the Savior, from myriad transgressions and perils, as thou art good, through thy prayers.

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
O all-hymned Virgin, whom Moses of old mystically saw with prophetic eyes as a bush burning and unconsumed;[^979] for the fire of the Godhead did not consume thy womb, O pure one. Therefore we pray thee as the Mother of our God, to entreat peace for the world, and great mercy.

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
I have surpassed the harlot, the prodigal, and the robber, and have exceeded the tax collector and the Ninevites in transgressions;[^980] woe is me, what will become of me? How shall I, wretched one, escape torments? O pure one, I fall down before thee, have compassion on me by thy mercy, even as Thy Son saved them.

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, joy of the forebears, gladness of the apostles and martyrs, and protection of us thy servants, O Virgin!

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O Theotokos Virgin; rejoice, boast of all the universe; rejoice, all-pure, blessed Mother of God!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Truly beyond understanding are the marvelous greatnesses of thy childbirth, O Bride of God, which all the prophets proclaimed, all exceeding glorious; thine all-hymned conception and childbirth are incomprehensible and ineffable, whereby He saved the world, as He is merciful.

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Rejoice from us, O holy Theotokos Virgin, pure vessel of all the universe, unquenchable lamp, container of the Uncontainable, indestructible temple; rejoice, of whom was born the Lamb of God who takes away the sins of the whole world![^981]

*Tone 2.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, Mary Theotokos, indestructible temple, and most holy, even as the prophet cries: Holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness![^982]

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
We have placed our hope in thee, O Theotokos, let us not fall from our expectation; save us from perils, O helper of those in despair, and destroy the counsels of adversaries; for thou art our salvation, O blessed one.

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Impassable gate, mystically sealed,[^983] O blessed Virgin Theotokos, receive our supplications, and bring them unto thy Son and God, that through thee He may save our souls.

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, Mary Theotokos, indestructible temple, and most holy, even as the prophet cries: Holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness![^984]

*On Wednesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
As a fruitful olive tree,[^985] the Virgin brought Thee forth, the Fruit of life, to bear the fruit of great and rich mercy for the world.

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
All my hope I place in thee, O Mother of God; preserve me under thy protection.

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Save thy servants from perils, O Virgin Theotokos; for next to God we all flee unto thee, as unto an unbreakable wall and protection.

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Come, let us all glorify the Mother of the Light, calling upon her with unceasing hymns, for she gave birth to our salvation; and let us offer her: "Rejoice!", as the only one who gave birth to the Prince of all, who was God before the ages. Rejoice, thou who hast restored fallen Eve! Rejoice, O all-pure, unwedded Virgin!

*Tone 3.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
O Theotokos, mediatrix of all who pray to thee: in thee we take courage, and in thee we boast, and in thee is all our hope; pray to Him who was born of thee, on behalf of thy unprofitable servants thy servants.

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O most pure praise of the heavenly ranks, song of the apostles, and fulfillment of the prophets: O Theotokos, receive our prayers.

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Holy Mother of God among women, unwedded Mother: pray to Him whom thou didst bear, the King and God, that He may save us, as He loves mankind.[^986]

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Without seed thou didst conceive of the Holy Spirit, and glorifying [Thee] we hymn thee: Rejoice, O most holy Virgin!

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Snatch me, who sin with tears, O Maiden, by thy great prayer from the violent flame; and guide me, O pure one, by thy supplications, directing me unto the saving paths by thy maternal prayers.

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Without seed thou didst conceive of the Holy Spirit, and glorifying [Thee] we hymn thee: Rejoice, O most holy Virgin!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Holy Mother of God among women, unwedded Mother: pray to Him whom thou didst bear, the King and God, that He may save us, as He loves mankind.

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Without seed thou didst conceive of the Holy Spirit, and glorifying [Thee] we hymn thee: Rejoice, O most holy Virgin!

*Tone 4.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, cloud of the Light; rejoice, radiant lampstand; rejoice, urn wherein is the manna; rejoice, rod of Aaron; rejoice, unconsumed bush; rejoice, bridal chamber; rejoice, throne; rejoice, holy mountain; rejoice, refuge; rejoice, divine table; rejoice, mystical door; rejoice, joy of all![^987]

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O Theotokos, Queen of all, boast of the Orthodox: destroy the rebellions of heretics and put to shame the faces of those who do not bow down before nor honor thine honorable image, O all-pure one![^988]

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Deliver us from our afflictions, O Mother of Christ God, who didst bear the Creator of all that we all may cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, sole defense of our souls!

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Preserve thy servants from all perils, O blessed Theotokos, that we may glorify thee, the hope of our souls.

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Having thee as hope and protection, O Theotokos, we shall not fear the snares of enemies, for thou savest our souls.

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
We have acquired thee as a wall, O all-pure Theotokos, and a calm haven and confirmation; therefore pray [for me who am] storm-tossed in this life: guide me and save me!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Unquenchable lamp, righteous throne, O all-pure Sovereign Lady: pray that our souls may be saved!

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O only pure and all-pure Virgin, who didst bear God without seed: pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 5.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Dread and all-glorious and great is the mystery: the Uncontainable was contained within a womb, and the mother after childbirth remained again a Virgin; for she gave birth to God incarnate from her.[^989] Unto Him let us cry aloud, unto Him let us say a song, chanting with the angels: Holy art Thou, O Christ God, who for our sake didst become man: glory to Thee![^990]

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O full of grace, intercede through thy prayers, and entreat for our souls an abundance of compassions and cleansing of many transgressions, we pray thee.[^991]

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Assuage the afflictions of my much-sighing soul, O thou who didst wipe away every tear from the face of the earth;[^992] for thou drivest away the sicknesses of men and loosest the sorrows of sinners; for we all have acquired thee as hope and confirmation, O most holy Virgin Mother!

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O full of grace, intercede through thy prayers, and entreat for our souls an abundance of compassions and cleansing of many transgressions, we pray thee.

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
We call thee blessed, O Virgin Theotokos, and we, the faithful, duly glorify thee: unshakable city, impregnable wall, firm defender, and refuge of our souls.

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
We call thee blessed, O Virgin Theotokos, for from thee shone forth Christ, the Sun of righteousness, who has great mercy.[^993]

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Unto thee we pray as the Mother of God, O blessed one: pray for the salvation of our souls.

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O wretched soul of mine, what answer wilt thou give to the Judge in that hour when the thrones are set for judgment, and the Judge shall come from the heavens, having descended with myriads of angels?[^994] When He shall sit upon the judgment seat to hold reckoning with unprofitable servants like unto me, what wilt thou answer? And what wilt thou bring then? Truly nothing, having defiled thy mind and body. Therefore fall down before the Virgin, and cry unceasingly, that He may richly grant thee forgiveness of sins!

*Tone 6.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
In archangelic manner let us the faithful hymn the heavenly bridal chamber and truly sealed door:[^995] Rejoice, for whose sake Christ, the Savior of all, the Giver of life and God, budded forth for us! Cast down the tyrants, our godless enemies, O Sovereign Lady, by thine hand, O all-pure hope of Christians!

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Thou didst receive the archangelic word, and wast revealed as the cherubic throne,[^996] and in thine arms didst carry the hope of our souls, O Theotokos.

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
No one who flees unto thee departs from thee ashamed, O all-pure Virgin Theotokos; but one asks for grace and receives a gift according to what is profitable for one's petition.

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Of great gifts wast thou accounted worthy, O pure Virgin Mother of God: for thou didst bear in the flesh Christ the Giver of life, One of the Trinity, unto the salvation of our souls.[^997]

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
I lift up the eye of my heart unto thee, O Sovereign Lady:[^998] despise not my little sighing in the hour when Thy Son shall judge the world; be unto me protection and helper!

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Relief of the sorrowing, healing of the sick art thou, O all-hymned Theotokos: save the city and people, pacification of those who are beleaguered, calm of the storm-tossed, sole defender of the faithful!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Through the prayers of her who gave Thee birth, O Christ, and of Thy martyrs, apostles, prophets, hierarchs, venerable and righteous ones, and all the saints, give rest to Thy departed servants!

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
We have known God who became incarnate from thee, O Virgin Theotokos: pray to Him for the salvation of our souls.

*Tone 7.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
"Rejoice!" we cry to thee with the angel, O Bride of God,[^999] calling thee bridal chamber and door, and fiery throne, and uncut mountain, and unconsumed bush![^1000]

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Through the prayers of the Theotokos bring peace unto our life, as we cry aloud unto Thee: O merciful Lord, glory to Thee!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, cloud of the spiritual and ineffable Sun, O Sovereign Lady; rejoice, all-hymned lamp; rejoice, all-golden lampstand; through thee, O all-pure one, Eve was delivered from the curse.[^1001] But as thou hast boldness toward thy pliable Son and God, through thy maternal prayer cease not to pray, O all-pure one!

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
As Light, O Christ, Thou didst blossom forth from the Virgin and didst enlighten the human race: O Lord, glory to Thee![^1002]

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Let us hymn the only all-pure Virgin after childbirth as the Mother of God the Word, saying: Glory to Thee!

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Thou wast born ineffably from the Virgin, O Christ, and didst enlighten those who are in darkness,[^1003] who cry aloud: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
O thou who alone didst receive the Uncontainable and bear God the Word incarnate: pray that our souls may be saved!

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Pray, O Virgin, with the apostles and martyrs, that those who have departed may find great mercy at the judgment!

*Tone 8.*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Receiving the voice of the Archangel Gabriel, let us say: Rejoice, O Mother of God, who didst bear Christ the Giver of life unto the world![^1004]

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
The heavenly hosts praise thee, O full of grace, unwedded Mother, and we glorify thine unsearchable childbirth, O Theotokos: pray that our souls may be saved!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, boast of the universe; rejoice, temple of the Lord; rejoice, overshadowing mountain; rejoice, refuge of all; rejoice, golden lampstand; rejoice, honorable glory of the Orthodox; rejoice, Mary, Mother of Christ God; rejoice, paradise; rejoice, divine table; rejoice, tabernacle; rejoice, all-golden urn; rejoice, hope of all![^1005]

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Thy protection, O Virgin Theotokos, is a spiritual remedy; for fleeing under it, we are delivered from spiritual infirmities.

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
I flee unto thy protection, O holy Virgin Theotokos; I know that through thee I shall find salvation, for thou art able to help me, O pure one.

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Snatch me, O Sovereign Lady, from the hand of the man-slaying serpent who wishes by malice to swallow me up completely;[^1006] crush his jaws, I beseech thee, and destroy his wiles, that being delivered from his claws, I may magnify thy defense!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Pure Virgin, gate of the Word, Mother of our God: pray that we may be saved!

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
O Theotokos, receive the prayers of thy servants, and deliver us from every tribulation and sorrow!

*Dismissal Resurrectional Theotokia in the Eight Tones:*

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*
When Gabriel cried unto thee, O Virgin: "Rejoice!", with that voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy ark, as the righteous David said.[^1007] Thou wast revealed as more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator.[^1008] Glory to Him who dwelt within thee! Glory to Him who came forth from thee! Glory to Him who has freed us through thy childbirth!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
All thy mysteries are beyond comprehension, all exceeding glorious, O Theotokos: though thy purity was sealed and thy virginity preserved, thou wast known to be a true Mother, having given birth to the true God.[^1009] Pray to Him that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, we hymn, O Virgin Theotokos; for having accepted the Passion upon the Cross in the flesh assumed from thee, Thy Son and our God has delivered us from corruption, as He loves mankind.[^1010]

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown even to the angels was revealed through thee, O Theotokos, unto those on earth: God becoming incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised the first-formed man, He saved our souls from death.[^1011]

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord; rejoice, wall and protection of those who flee unto thee; rejoice, tempest-free haven and unwedded bride who didst bear in the flesh thy Creator and God: cease not to pray for those who hymn and worship thy childbirth![^1012]

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Calling Thy Mother blessed, Thou didst come to Thy Passion by voluntary counsel, shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: "Rejoice with Me, for the lost drachma is found!"[^1013] O our God, who didst wisely order all things: glory to Thee!

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
As the treasury of our resurrection, lead up from the pit and the depth of transgressions those who hope in thee, O all-hymned one for thou hast saved those guilty of sin, having given birth to our salvation: who before childbirth wast a Virgin, and in childbirth a Virgin, and after childbirth still remainest a Virgin![^1014]

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
O Good One, who for our sake wast born of a Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, who didst overthrow death by death and manifest the Resurrection as God:[^1015] despise not those whom Thou hast formed with Thine hand! Show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; receive the Theotokos who bore Thee as she prays for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people![^1016]

Marveling at the beauty of thy virginity and the exceeding brightness of thy purity, Gabriel cried aloud unto thee, O Theotokos: "What worthy praise shall I offer thee? What shall I call thee? I am at a loss and in dread! Therefore, as I was commanded, I cry unto thee: Rejoice, O full of grace!"[^1027]

*At the end of Matins:*
Whithersoever one is saved, thither one rightly flees; and what other such refuge is there, as thou art, O Theotokos, protecting our souls?

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*
Our refuge and strength, O Theotokos, mighty help of the world:[^1028] through thy prayers protect thy servants from every affliction, O only blessed one!

*At the end of Matins:*
Whithersoever one is saved...

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*
Having acquired the Cross of Thy Son as a rod of strength, O Theotokos,[^1029] we thereby cast down the insolence of the enemies, unceasingly magnifying thee in love.

*At the end of Matins:*

Our refuge and strength...

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*
The prophets proclaimed, the apostles taught, the martyrs confessed, and we have believed thee to be truly the Theotokos; therefore we also magnify thine ineffable childbirth.

*At the end of Matins:*
Whithersoever one is saved...

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
Having acquired the Cross of Thy Son as a rod of strength...

*At the end of Matins:*
Our refuge and strength, O Theotokos...

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, we hymn, O Virgin Theotokos; for having accepted the Passion upon the Cross in the flesh assumed from thee, Thy Son and our God has delivered us from corruption, as He loves mankind.[^1030]

*At the end of Matins:*
The prophets proclaimed...

*Tone 4.*
*On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*
Unto her who was nurtured in the temple in the Holy of Holies,[^1031] clothed with faith and wisdom, and boundless virginity, the Archistrategos Gabriel brought greeting from heaven, and the "Rejoice!": Rejoice, O blessed one; rejoice, O most glorified one, the Lord is with thee![^1032]

*At the end of Matins:*
As thou art higher than all creation, being at a loss to hymn thee worthily, O Theotokos: therefore we pray thee, have mercy on us!

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*
Let us sinners and humble ones now run earnestly unto the Theotokos, and fall down in repentance, crying from the depths of our soul:[^1033] O Theotokos, help us, having had compassion on us; hasten, for we perish from the multitude of our transgressions; turn not thy servants away empty, for thee alone do we have as hope![^1034]

*At the end of Matins:*
We magnify thee, O Theotokos, crying aloud: Thou art the bush wherein Moses unconsumingly beheld as flame the fire of the Godhead![^1035]

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*
O all-spotless Virgin, Mother of Christ God: a sword pierced thy most holy soul when thou didst behold Thy Son and God willingly crucified;[^1036] cease not, O blessed one, to pray to Him to grant us forgiveness of transgressions.

*At the end Tone 4*
*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* We magnify thee, crying aloud, O Theotokos: Thou art the mountain from which the stone was cut without hands[^1037], and shattered the gates of Hades.

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* The Word of the Father, Christ our God, Who was incarnate of thee, we have come to know, O Virgin Theotokos, alone pure, alone blessed; therefore, unceasingly hymning thee, we magnify thee.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* For thou art higher than all creations...

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O all-immaculate Virgin Mother...

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* We magnify thee, crying aloud, O Theotokos: Thou art the mountain...

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto angels, through thee, O Theotokos, has been revealed to those upon earth: God incarnate in unconfused union, Who willingly accepted the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-fashioned, He saved our souls from death.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* We magnify thee, crying aloud, O Theotokos: Thou art the bush...

*Tone 5. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* With the angels heavenly, with men earthly, with the voice of rejoicing we cry aloud to thee, O Theotokos: Rejoice, thou gate wider than the heavens; rejoice, sole salvation of the earthborn; rejoice, pure full of grace, who didst bear God incarnate.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Troparion:* O Christ God, Who didst shine forth from the Virgin upon the world, and through Her hast shown us to be sons of light, have mercy on us.

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Show forth thy speedy protection, and help, and mercy upon thy servants, and calm the waves of vain thoughts, O pure one, and raise up my fallen soul, O Theotokos; for I know, yea I know, O Virgin, that thou canst do whatsoever thou desirest.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* O most pure Mother of God, rampart of Christians, deliver thy people as is thy wont, who diligently call upon thee; withstand shameful and proud thoughts, that we may cry aloud to thee: Rejoice, Ever-virgin.

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*

*Tone 5 (continued)*
*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord" (continued):*
*Theotokion:* By the Cross of thy Son, O thou who art full of God's grace, all delusion of idols has been abolished, and the demonic might has been trampled down; therefore we, the faithful, as is our duty, ever hymn and bless thee, and confessing thee to be truly the Theotokos, we magnify thee.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Troparion:* O Christ God, Who didst shine forth from the Virgin upon the world...

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* The marvelous mystery of the Virgin has been shown forth to the world as saving; for of thee, O Joy of all, Thou wast born without seed, and in the flesh Thou didst show Thyself without corruption: Lord, glory to Thee.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* O most pure Mother of God...

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* By the Cross of thy Son...

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Troparion:* O Christ God, Who didst shine forth from the Virgin upon the world...

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord; rejoice, rampart and protection of those who flee unto thee; rejoice, tempest-free haven, and unwedded one, who didst bear in the flesh thy Creator and God: cease not to pray for those who hymn and worship thy nativity.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Troparion:* O Christ God, Who didst shine forth from the Virgin upon the world...

*Tone 6. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* The beginning of salvation was Gabriel's salutation unto the Virgin; for she heard the "Rejoice", and rejected not the greeting. She doubted not like Sarah in the tent[^1038], but spoke thus: Behold the handmaid of the Lord, be it unto me according to thy word.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Thou didst receive the angelic word, and didst appear as the cherubic throne, and in thine arms didst carry, O Theotokos, the Hope of our souls.

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O good Theotokos Virgin, hope of the world, we entreat thy sole and mighty protection: have compassion upon a people without a protector, and pray to the merciful God that our souls may be delivered from all threatening, O thou alone blessed.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* No one who flees unto thee goes away from thee put to shame, O most pure Theotokos Virgin; but asks for grace, and receives a gift *Tone 6 (continued)*
*At the conclusion of Matins (continued):*
*Theotokion:* ...a gift unto profitable petition.

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O Virgin Theotokos, pray to thy Son, Who was willingly nailed upon the Cross and delivered the world from delusion, Christ our God, to have mercy on our souls.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Thou, O Mother of God, without a husband didst in these latter times give birth unto the Son and Word of God, Who was born of the Father without a mother before the ages, incarnate of thy pure blood: pray to Him that remission of sins before the end may be granted unto us.

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O holy, pure Sovereign Lady, Mother of our God, who didst ineffably give birth to the Creator of all: ever pray with the holy apostles unto His goodness, to deliver us from passions and grant us remission of sins.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Thou, O pure Virgin Mother of God, hast been accounted worthy of great gifts: for in the flesh thou didst bear one of the Trinity, Christ the Giver of life, unto the salvation of our souls.

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O Virgin Theotokos, pray to thy Son...

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Who was born of the Father without a mother before the ages...

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Gideon foretells the conception[^1039], and David declares thy nativity, O Theotokos; for the Word came down like dew upon the fleece[^1040] into thy womb, and without seed thou didst sprout forth as a holy earth the salvation of the world, Christ our God, O full of grace.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Who was born of the Father without a mother before the ages...

*Tone 7. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O undefiled Virgin Theotokos, pray to thy Son with the heavenly hosts, that remission of transgressions before the end may be granted unto us who faithfully glorify thee.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Thou hast surpassed the heavenly hosts, for thou hast been shown forth as a divine temple, O blessed Theotokos, who didst bear Christ the Savior of our souls.

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Rejoice! we offer unto thee, O Theotokos *Tone 7 (continued)*
*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord" (continued):*
*Theotokion:* ...the Theotokos; for thou hast been shown forth as greater than the angels, having given birth to God.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* By the prayers of the Theotokos, make peaceful our life, who cry aloud unto Thee: O merciful Lord, glory to Thee.

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Unceasingly pray, O Virgin, unto Christ God, Who was crucified for us and destroyed the dominion of death, that He may save our souls.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Deliver us, O Theotokos, from the sins that hold us fast, for we the faithful have no other hope save thee, and God Who was born of thee.

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* The Fruit of thy womb, O most pure one, is the fulfillment of the prophets and the Law[^1041]; therefore, glorifying thee in knowledge as the Theotokos, we piously magnify thee.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* The Fruit of thy womb, O Bride of God, has been shown forth to men as the Mediator of salvation; therefore, glorifying thee in mind and tongue as the Theotokos, we the faithful magnify thee.

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Unto Christ God, Who was crucified for us...

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Deliver us, O Theotokos...

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* As the treasury of our resurrection, lead up from the pit and the depth of transgressions those who hope in thee, O all-hymned one; for thou didst save those guilty of sin, who didst bear our salvation: who before childbearing wast a virgin, and in childbearing a virgin, and after childbearing remainest still a virgin.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Rejoice, thou who in thy womb didst contain Him Whom the heavens cannot contain[^1042]; rejoice, O Virgin, proclamation of the prophets, through whom Emmanuel shone forth; rejoice, Mother of Christ God.

*Tone 8. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* Rejoice, thou who from the angel didst receive the joy of the world; rejoice, who didst bear thy Creator and Lord; rejoice, who wast accounted worthy to be the Mother of God.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* Rejoice, gate of the King of glory, which the Most High alone passed through[^1043], and kept alone sealed, unto the salvation of our souls.

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*

*Tone 8 (continued)*
*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord" (continued):*
*Theotokion:* As the unshakable affirmation of faith, and the precious gift of our souls, we the faithful magnify the Theotokos with hymns: Rejoice, thou who in thy womb didst contain the Rock of life[^1044]; rejoice, hope of the ends of the earth, protection of the afflicted; rejoice, unwedded Bride.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* O most pure Virgin, save us by thy prayers, moving maternal compassion toward thy Son and our God.

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*
*Stavrotheotokion:* Beholding upon the Cross the Lamb, and Shepherd, and Savior of the world, she who bore Thee wept and said: The world indeed rejoices, receiving deliverance, but my maternal heart burns, beholding Thy crucifixion, which Thou endurest for all, my Son and my God.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* The Fruit of thy womb, O most pure one, is the fulfillment of the prophets and the Law: therefore, glorifying thee in knowledge as the Theotokos, we piously magnify thee.

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O most pure Theotokos, noetic gate of life unto those who flee to thee faithfully and from afflictions: let us glorify thy most pure childbearing unto the salvation of our souls.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* O most pure Virgin, save us...

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Stavrotheotokion:* The Lamb, and Shepherd...

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* The Fruit of thy womb, O most pure one...

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*
*Theotokion:* O Good One Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, Who by death didst overthrow death, and as God didst manifest the Resurrection: despise not those whom Thou hast fashioned with Thine own hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; accept the Theotokos who bore Thee, who prays for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people.

*At the conclusion of Matins:*
*Theotokion:* O most pure Virgin, save us by thy prayers, moving maternal compassion toward thy Son and our God.

*The End of the Theotokia.*
