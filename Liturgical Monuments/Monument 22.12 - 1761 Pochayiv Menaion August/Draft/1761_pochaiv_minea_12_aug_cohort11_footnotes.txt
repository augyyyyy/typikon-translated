[^266]: *Podoben note*: The melodic model *Of Wisdom* (Slavonic: *Премудрости*, Greek: *Τὴν Σοφίαν καὶ Λόγον*) is the standard automelon for Tone 8 sessional hymns throughout the Menaion.
[^267]: *Gospel Pericope*: Slavonic *зач. 67* corresponds to John 21:15–25 (the dialogue between the risen Lord and Peter, standard matins Gospel reading for apostolic commemorations).
[^268]: *Acrostic note*: The Greek acrostic reads ᾌσμασί σε μέλπω, Ματθία, Χριστοῦ φίλε (Poem of Theophanes), preserved in Slavonic translation as: *Пѣсньми тя пою матѳіе христо́въ друже*.
[^269]: *Theological note*: The Ikos text exhorts the soul to shake off earthly sloth and ascend Mount Tabor with Peter, James, and John to witness the divine Transfiguration.
[^270]: *Podoben note*: The model *Thou hast appeared today* (Slavonic: *Яви́лся еси́ днесь*, Greek: *Ἐπεφάνης σήμερον*) is the Tone 4 kontakion model composed by Romanos the Melodist for Theophany.
[^271]: *Podoben note*: The model *In the spirit in the sanctuary* (Slavonic: *Духомъ во святилищи*, Greek: *Γυναιξὶν ἀκουτίσθητε* / *Ἐν πνεύματι τῷ ἱερῷ*) is the standard exapostilarion melody for apostles and martyrs.
[^272]: *Podoben note*: The model *O House of Ephratha* (Slavonic: *До́ме Евфра́ѳовъ*, Greek: *Οἶκος τοῦ Εὐφραθᾶ*) is the automelon for Tone 2 prosomoia.
[^273]: *Podoben note*: The model *As one valiant* (Slavonic: *Я́ко до́бля*, Greek: *Ὡς γενναῖον ἐν Μάρτυσιν*) is the standard Tone 4 prosomoion model.
[^274]: *Epistle Pericope*: Slavonic *зач. 2* corresponds to Acts 1:12–17, 21–26 (the election of Apostle Matthias to replace Judas).
[^275]: *Gospel Pericope*: Slavonic *зач. 40* corresponds to Luke 9:1–6 (the sending forth of the twelve disciples).
[^276]: *Podoben note*: The model *Thou hast given a sign* (Slavonic: *Да́лъ еси́ зна́меніе*, Greek: *Ἔδωκας σημείωσιν*) is the Tone 4 automelon.
