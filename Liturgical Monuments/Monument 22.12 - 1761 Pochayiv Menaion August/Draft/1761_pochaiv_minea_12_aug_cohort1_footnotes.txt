[^1]: This archival flyleaf contains an unprinted late imperial Russian manuscript addition (c. 1913–1914) recording dismissal commemorations and a Polychronion (*Mnogoletiye*) for Emperor Nicholas II and Volhynian hierarchs (Archbishop Eulogius Georgiyevsky and Bishop Dionysius Valedinsky), entered after the Pochaiv Lavra was annexed into the Russian Orthodox Church (1831), subsequently struck through with a blue pencil line.
[^2]: Augustus III (1696–1763), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania (reigned 1734–1763).
[^3]: Slavonic: *куръ* (from Greek *κῦρ*, *κύριος*), an honorific ecclesiastical title applied to Eastern Catholic and Orthodox hierarchs in the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.
[^4]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Lubieniecki-Rudnicki, consecrated 1750, died 1777), Ruthenian Uniate Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh, Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus'.
[^5]: Hypatius (Ipatii) Bilinsky (1704–1792), Basilian Protoarchimandrite (in office 1759–1771), active promoter of liturgical printing at the Pochaiv Lavra.
[^6]: John the Monk (Saint John of Damascus, c. 676–749), author of numerous festal stichera and liturgical idiomena in the Byzantine rite.
[^7]: Cosmas the Monk (Saint Cosmas the Melodist / of Maiuma, c. 675–752), foster-brother of Saint John of Damascus and prominent Byzantine hymnographer.
[^8]: Deut. 28:66 (LXX): "And thy life shall be hanging before thine eyes."
[^9]: The Troparion of the Cross here petitions for "our orthodox King" (*благовѣрному королю нашему*), referring to King Augustus III of Poland-Lithuania.
[^10]: The acrostic of the Canon of the Cross in Slavonic is: Кр[е]сту покланяюся вѣрный спасенію ("I, the faithful one, worship the Cross unto salvation"), formed by the initial letters of the odes and troparia.
[^11]: An allusion to the budding rod of Aaron (Num. 17:8), prefiguring both the priesthood and the wood of the Cross.
[^12]: Ps. 147:1–2 (LXX 147:12–13): "Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion. For He hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; He hath blessed thy children within thee."
