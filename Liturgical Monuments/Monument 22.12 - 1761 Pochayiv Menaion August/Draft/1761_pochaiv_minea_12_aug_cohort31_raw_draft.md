*Mark: Concerning this fasting rule for the Beheading of the honorable Head of the Forerunner, ordained by the ancient Holy Fathers on this day.*
*There are indeed some who prescribe that there be a quality of foods then, and not fasting; for concerning this we were able to find nothing written in the various Typika. But it is rather fitting on that day in every way to abstain by fasting, for the sake of the Prophet who suffered for the truth and preached the Lord in Hades. And let the dispensation be only for olive oil, and the rest from various orchard fruits, and by measure a single cup of wine unto the glory of God, on account of the labor of the vigil. For in every way it is fitting for us on that day to be with downcast lamentation, and not to indulge in gluttony, for the sake of the abstemious life of the saint, and his foul murderous bloodshed by Herod...*

=== LEAF p305 ===
*...for the head of the Forerunner was severed at a banquet through the dancing of a lustful dancer, and gluttony, and the slaughter of various animals and bloodshed. But unto us it is not fitting to taste even one of such animals from which blood is poured out, nor even fish (for they have blood, even if they live in the water, and are of the creaturely nature of animals), lest we be shown forth as partakers of Herod's gluttony. For how shall we wish to honor the honorable beheading of the Baptist? Shall we wish to eat meat, or of other various costly foods? Yet he lived in a waterless and grassless wilderness: he ate no bread, neither did he have a table! Shall we drink wine? Yet he never drank wine, nor any other worldly drink; neither did he live in houses, but lived dug in beneath a rock. A table, and banquet, and bed for him was the earth; only for the sake of natural necessity, once a day he tasted locusts and wild honey: his cup was his hands cupped together, and his drink was water flowing from the rock. Therefore, brethren, let us fervently honor this day with prayer and fasting in chastity, and let us flee from this evil disposition of drunkenness; for if anyone be held by unbelief, and keep not this ordained, most discerning command, such a one shall evilly suffer ruinous afflictions. But those that rightly maintain this contest, O Lord, bless Thou, and guide by the prayers of the holy Forerunner!*
