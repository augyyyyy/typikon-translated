# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 3: November (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-3-november-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

THE BOOK
MENAION
The Month of November,

To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit;[^1] under the reign of the Great King Augustus III,[^2]

By the blessing of His Pre-eminence, the Lord Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky,[^3] Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh;

By the goodwill of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk Hypatius Bilynsky,[^4] Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great;

By the diligence and expense of the monks of the same Order, in the Holy Wonderworking Pochaiv Lavra, issued in print.

In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers 7269,[^5]
And from the Nativity of Christ 1761.

*(Vacat — Blank verso facing opening of the Month of November) (Fol. 1r)*
*(Woodcut headpiece: The Lord Sabaoth in glory, blessing with both hands, holding the orb and cross, surrounded by clouds and heavenly powers)*

### Notes

[^1]: The customary Trinitarian invocation opening Ukrainian and Basilian Cyrillic imprints of the eighteenth century.
[^2]: Augustus III (1696–1763), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania, and Elector of Saxony (as Frederick Augustus II), reigned 1734–1763.
[^3]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Lubieniecki-Rudnicki, d. 1777), Greek Catholic (Uniate) Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh (1752–1777) and Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus'.
[^4]: Hypatius Bilynsky (Ipatii Bilynskyi, OSBM, 1704–1771), Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great (1751–1771), under whose leadership the Pochaiv press flourished.
[^5]: The Byzantine Creation Era date (Anno Mundi 7269), reckoned from September 1, 5509 BC, corresponds to 1760/1761 AD. The accompanying Christian Era date is explicitly given as 1761 AD.
