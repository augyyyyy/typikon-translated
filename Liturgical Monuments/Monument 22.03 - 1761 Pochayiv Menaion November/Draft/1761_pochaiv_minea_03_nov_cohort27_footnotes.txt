[^992]: An allusion to the famous historical encounter between St. Amphilochius and Emperor Theodosius I: when the Emperor hesitated to prohibit Arian assemblies, Amphilochius greeted the Emperor with profound respect but completely ignored his young son Arcadius, whom Theodosius had crowned Augustus. When the Emperor became indignant at the insult to his son, Amphilochius said unto him: "Thou seest, O Emperor, that thou canst not bear an insult to thy son; how then thinkest thou that the Heavenly Father can bear those who blaspheme His Only-Begotten Son?" Theodosius immediately acknowledged the lesson and enacted laws suppressing Arian conventicles (cf. Theodoret, *Hist. Eccl.* V, 16; Sozomen, *Hist. Eccl.* VII, 6).
