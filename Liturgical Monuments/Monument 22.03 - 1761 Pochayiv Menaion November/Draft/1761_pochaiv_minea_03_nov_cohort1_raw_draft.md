# MONTH OF NOVEMBER
Having 30 days.

*November 1: Holy Wonderworkers and Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian of Asia:*

*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," stichera of the saints, on 6.*[^6]
*Tone 6, Podoben: "Having laid aside all" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*

Having laid aside all earthly care, and placed their hope in the heavens, the saints laid up for themselves an unstolen treasure. Freely they received, freely they give healing unto those who are ailing. In evangelical manner, they possessed neither gold nor silver; and unto men and beasts alike they imparted benefactions, that having become obedient in all things unto Christ, they may pray with boldness on behalf of our souls.

Although they were disgusted by corruptible things upon the earth, yet like heavenly citizens in the flesh they appeared as angels, companions of one mind: a holy pair of one character and of one soul. Therefore, unto all who suffer they grant healings, freely giving benefactions unto those who are in need; whom let us worthily honor in this yearly feast, as with boldness they pray unto Christ on behalf of our souls.

Having welcomed the entire Trinity to dwell within themselves, the ever-memorable pair, the divinely wise Cosmas and Damian, pour forth streams from the life-bearing source, even the waters of healing. Their very relics heal passions by touch, and their names alone drive afflictions away from men; for all who run to them in Christ they are saving havens, and with boldness they pray on behalf of our souls.

Having rejected all tyrannical passion, and having trampled down every calculation of the wicked serpent

*Glory, in Tone 6:*
Always having Christ working within you, O holy unmercenaries, you work miracles in the world, curing the infirm. For your medicine is an inexhaustible fountain: when drawn from, it overflows all the more; and when poured out, it abounds; emptied daily, it increases; granting unto all and remaining unfailing; and those who draw from it are filled with remedies, while it remains unexhausted. What then shall we call you? Healing physicians of souls and bodies? Healers of incurable passions, freely healing all? Recipients of the gift from the Savior Christ, Who grants unto us great mercy!
