[^1046]: Sessional Hymn of St. Catherine: "Having loved thy Bridegroom, Christ..." (Жениха твоего Хрїста возлюбившы). The imagery of the wise virgin preparing her lamp reflects Matthew 25:1-13.
[^1047]: Ode 4 Heirmos: Habakkuk 3:2-3. The prophetic contemplation of the unsearchable divine counsel of the Incarnation.
[^1048]: Ode 4 Heirmos (Second Canon): Tone 8 heirmos based on Habakkuk 3:19 ("The Lord God is my strength").
[^1049]: Ode 5 Troparion of St. Catherine: "Running after the fragrance of His poured-out intelligible myrrh..." Allusion to Song of Songs 1:2-3 ("Thy name is as ointment poured forth, therefore do the virgins love Thee").
[^1050]: Ode 6 Kontakion of St. Catherine: Tone 2, Podoben: "Seeking the highest" (Вышнихъ ищя / Τὰ ἄνω ζητῶν).
[^1051]: Ode 6 Ikos of St. Catherine: Referring to Catherine's debate with the fifty philosophers and rhetoricians summoned by the emperor, all of whom she converted to faith in Christ.
[^1052]: Ode 7 Heirmos: Daniel 3:23-28 (The Three Holy Children in the furnace of Babylon refusing creature-worship).
[^1053]: Ode 7 Troparion 3 of St. Catherine: "The maiden following after thee was led to thee, beheaded with the sword..." Historical reference to Empress Faustina (wife of Maxentius / Maximinus), who was inspired by St. Catherine, converted, and suffered martyrdom by beheading.
[^1054]: Ode 8 Troparion 1 of St. Catherine: Re-emphasizing Empress Faustina's fortitude in the face of torture and martyrdom following Catherine's catechesis.
[^1055]: Ode 8 Troparion of St. Mercurius: The body of the martyr shown "whiter than snow" after his martyrdom, echoing Psalm 50:9 [LXX] / 51:7 ("Thou shalt wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow").
[^1056]: Ode 9 Heirmos of the Feast: "As she is the animate Ark of God..." (Яко одушевленному Божїю кївоту). St. John of Damascus and traditional Byzantine hymnography venerate the Virgin as the animate Ark of God.
[^1057]: Ode 9 Troparion 3 of the Feast: The liturgical catalogue of Old Testament typological figures of the Theotokos: the Golden Urn / Jar of Manna (стамну), the Ark of the Covenant (кївотъ), the Temple Veil (катапетасму), Aaron's Rod (жезлъ), the Indestructible Temple, and the Closed Gate (Ezekiel 44:2).
[^1058]: Ode 9 Troparion 4 of the Feast: Davidic typology from Psalm 44:10, 14-15 [LXX] / 45:9, 13-14 ("The queen stood at Thy right hand in gold of Ophir... all the glory of the king's daughter is from within").
[^1059]: Ode 9 Troparion 1 of St. Catherine: St. Catherine presented iconographically and hymnographically bearing her virginal bridal lamp in her right hand and her severed head in her left hand.
[^1060]: Ode 9 Theotokion: "Whom the Father poured forth from His heart before all ages..." Allusion to Psalm 44:2 [LXX] / 45:1 (Ἐξηρεύξατο ἡ καρδία μου λόγον ἀγαθόν / Отрыгну сердце мое слово благо).
