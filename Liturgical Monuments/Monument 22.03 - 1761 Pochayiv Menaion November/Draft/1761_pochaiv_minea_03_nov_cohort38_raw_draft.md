Ever let us, the faithful, praise him in the choir of the disciples of Christ, that he may pray with them, that He may be merciful unto us in the Day of Judgment.

*Both now, the same Tone:*[^1233]
Receive, O Bethlehem, the metropolis of God; for the unwaning Light comes to be born in thee! Ye angels, marvel in heaven; ye men, glorify on earth; ye Magi from Persia, bring most glorious gifts; ye shepherds, playing upon pipes, sing the thrice-holy hymn: Let every breath praise the Creator of all!
