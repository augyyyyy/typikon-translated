*Dismissal Resurrectional Theotokia of the Eight Tones.*[^227]

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*
When Gabriel said unto thee: "Rejoice!", O Virgin, with the voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy ark, as the righteous David said. Thou wast shown to be more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who dwelt in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who has freed us through thy childbirth!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
All beyond understanding, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Theotokos! Sealed in purity and preserved in virginity, thou wast known as a true Mother, having given birth unto the true God. Entreat Him that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, do we hymn, O Virgin Theotokos; for in the flesh assumed from thee, thy Son and our God, having accepted the Passion through the Cross, delivered us from corruption, as the Lover of mankind.

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto the angels, through thee, O Theotokos, was revealed unto those upon earth: God becoming incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-fashioned, He saved our souls from death.

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, wall and protection of those who flee unto thee! Rejoice, stormless haven and unwedded one, who gavest birth in the flesh to thy Creator and God: cease not praying for those who hymn and worship thy childbirth!

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Calling Thy Mother blessed, Thou camest to the Passion by Thy will, shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: "Rejoice with Me, for the lost drachma is found!" O our God, Who hast wisely ordered all things, glory to Thee!

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
As the treasury of our resurrection, O all-hymned one, lead up from the pit and the abyss of transgressions those who hope in thee...

=== LEAF p202 ===
...for thou hast saved those guilty of sin, having given birth to our Salvation: who before childbirth wast a Virgin, and in childbirth a Virgin, and after childbirth still remainest a Virgin.

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
O Good One, Who for our sake wast born of a Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, Who by death didst overthrow death, and manifested the resurrection as God: despise not those whom Thou hast fashioned with Thine own hand. Show Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One. Accept the Theotokos who gave Thee birth, who prays for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people.
