# MONTH OF APRIL
*April 1. Our Venerable Mother Mary of Egypt:*

*On the 1st day: our venerable mother Mary of Egypt.*

*At "Lord, I have cried," stichera of the Venerable One, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*

The defilement of defilements previously contracted hindered thee from beholding the venerable things; yet thy feeling and thy God-wise conscience concerning the deeds done wrought in thee a turning unto better things. For looking upon the icon of the blessed Maiden of God, having repented of all thy former transgressions, O all-praised one, with boldness thou didst venerate the precious Wood.

Having joyfully venerated the holy places, thou didst receive from thence a most saving viaticum of virtue. And exceedingly didst thou speed upon a good journey, having crossed the pathway of the Jordan; and in the dwelling-place of the Forerunner thou didst fervently take up thine abode, and by thy way of life didst darken the fierceness of the passions, thinning out the carnal strivings of the body by abstinence, O ever-memorable mother.

Having settled in the wilderness, thou didst piously blot out the images of thy passions, having inscribed in thy soul that most Godlike depiction: the forms of virtues. And so greatly didst thou shine forth, that even upon the waters thou didst walk lightly with thy footsteps, and didst rise above the earth in thy supplications unto God. And now with boldness, O glorious Mary, standing before Christ, pray for our souls.

*Glory... Tone 2. Idiomelon:*

The snares of the soul and the passions of the flesh thou didst cut down with the sword of abstinence, and the mental...

=== LEAF p4 === (Fol. 1v)
...sins of thought thou didst choke by the silence of fasting, and with the streams of thy tears thou didst water all the wilderness, and didst make to sprout forth for us the fruits of repentance. Therefore, O venerable one, we celebrate thy memory.

*Both now... Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion:*

Beholding Thee nailed unto the Wood of the Cross, O Jesus, she who knew not wedlock weeping said: O sweet Child, why hast Thou left me alone who bare Thee, O unapproachable Light of the Father Who is before all beginning? Make haste and be glorified, that they who glorify Thy divine passions may receive divine glory.

*Troparion, Tone 8:*

In thee, O mother, was that which is according to the image saved with certainty: for having taken up the Cross, thou didst follow Christ, and by thine actions didst teach us to despise the flesh, for it passes away, but to care for the soul, a thing immortal. Therefore, O venerable Mary, thy spirit also rejoices with the Angels.

*Canon, Tone 4.*  
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Having traversed the deep of the Red Sea on foot with dryshod steps of old, Israel by the hands of Moses stretched out crosswise conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Troparia:*

Cleanse the defilement of transgressions from my humble soul by Thy mercy, O Christ; drive away the mist and darkening of passions, by the prayers of Thy venerable one.

Having defiled the nobility of the soul by carnal passions, thou didst again cleanse it by abstinence; thou didst enlighten thy mind, having made thy soul radiant by the torrents of thy tears.

Thou didst flee from the Egypt of passions as from a sinful spring, and being delivered from the cruel defilement of Pharaoh, thou hast now inherited the land of dispassion, and dost choir with the Angels unceasingly.

*Theotokion:*

Beholding thine icon, O Sovereign Lady, pure Birthgiver of God, and the Word Who was born of thy most pure virginal womb, the glorious one fervently entreats thee to be a guarantor unto this.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thy Church makes merry in Thee, O Christ, crying aloud: Thou art my strength, O Lord, and my refuge and my firm support.

*Troparia:*

Festering and putrefied were the wounds of thy soul, but with the fountain of thy tears thou didst fervently wash them away.

The demonic hosts were vanquished by thee, and the wanton strivings of the passions thou didst wound with tears.

=== LEAF p5 === (Fol. 2r)
As a morning cloud and as a dropping dewdrop didst thou become, pouring forth the waters of saving repentance unto all.

*Theotokion:*

Having thee as protectress, O pure one, and as salvation and strength, the venerable one venerated the precious Wood of the Holy Cross.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "Guiding unto wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости):*

Having bridled all carnal wantonness by fasting labors, thou didst display the manly mindset of thy soul; for longing to behold the form of the Cross, O ever-memorable one, thou didst crucify thyself unto the world, whence also unto zeal for an blameless life thou didst fervently raise thyself up, O all-blessed and glorious Mary. Pray unto Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who honor thy holy memory with love.

*Theotokion:*

As a Virgin and alone among women, who without seed didst bear God in the flesh, all generations of mankind call thee blessed: for the fire of the Godhead took up His abode in thee, and as an infant thou dost nourish with milk the Creator and Lord. Therefore, the angelic and human race, we worthily glorify thy most holy Nativity,[^5] and with one voice cry aloud unto thee: Pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto us who venerate thy most holy childbirth with faith.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

The ewe-lamb, beholding the Lamb, Shepherd, and Deliverer upon the Cross, wept aloud, and bitterly lamenting cried out: The world indeed rejoices, receiving deliverance through Thee, but my womb burns, beholding Thy Crucifixion, which Thou endurest for the tender mercy of Thy lovingkindness, O longsuffering Lord. O Mother, abyss and inexhaustible spring, take pity and grant remission of transgressions unto those who hymn Thy divine passions with faith.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Beholding Thee lifted up upon the Cross, O Sun of righteousness, the Church stood in her station, worthily crying aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparia:*

Thou didst withdraw, fleeing from those in the world and from all sweet things; and alone thou didst purely unite thyself unto the One alone, by extreme abstinence and the endurance of thy deeds.

The movements and burnings of the flesh thou didst truly dry up by abstinence; whence thou didst adorn thy soul, O all-glorious Mary, with divine visions and actions.

By thy virtuous power, with tears and extreme fasting...

=== LEAF p6 === (Fol. 2v)
...and with prayer and heat, with frost and nakedness, thou didst become a precious receptacle of the Holy Spirit.

*Theotokion:*

Having fled unto thine icon, and having in paradise quired with Him Who was born of thee, O Virgin Mary, through thee she hath now found immortal life.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou, my Lord, hast come as a light into the world, a holy light turning those who hymn Thee with faith out of gloomy ignorance.

*Troparia:*

Thou didst follow after Christ rejoicing, bearing thy cross upon thy shoulder, O Mary, and thou didst wound the demons.

Thou hast shown us the medicine of repentance; thou hast shown also the path leading again unto unperishing life.

Be thou unto me an invincible and honorable protectress, and deliver me from passions and every disease by thy supplications unto the Lord.

*Theotokion:*

Looking upon thine icon, O pure Sovereign Lady, and ever praying unto thee, the venerable one put to shame the assaults of passions.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries aloud unto Thee, having been cleansed from the demonic blood by the blood that flowed for mercy's sake from Thy side.

*Troparia:*

With sweat thou didst wash away the defilement of sin, and gazing with thy mind toward uncorrupt glory, thou hast now found good fruit for thy labors, O glorious one.

Thy life was shown forth as an example unto all sinners, O Mary, that those who have sinned immeasurably in life may arise and cleanse their defilement with tears.

Show compassion upon my humble soul, O Lover of mankind, which I have defiled by unclean deeds of the desires of my flesh; but do Thou have mercy upon me, O God, by the prayers of the venerable one.

With all thy soul and heart thou didst love the living and incarnate God the Word born of the Virgin, who brought a voice unto thee, O venerable one.

*Kontakion, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йся):*

Having fled the mist of sin, and having illuminated thy heart with the light of repentance, O glorious one, thou didst come unto Christ, and didst bring His all-immaculate and holy Mother as a merciful intercessor. Whence also thou hast found remission of transgressions, and dost ever rejoice with the Angels.

*Ikos:*

The serpent that of old wrought evil in Eden against Eve through the deception of the tree, thou didst cast down into the pit by the wood of the Cross, O glorious Mary:...

...Mary. And having fled pleasures, thou didst desire purity; whence also thou wast accounted worthy to enter within the bridal chamber of thy Master with the virgins, worthily to delight with them. Fervently pray unto Him, therefore, that He may grant the remission of many sins, and vouchsafe us His life, to rejoice ever with the Angels.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

In the Persian furnace, the Abrahamic youths, burning with love of piety rather than with flame, cried aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!

*Troparia:*

Having trodden the sorrowful and narrow path, O virgin,[^6] and having made thy soul radiant with the beauty of virtues, thou didst attain unto heavenly, endless life, where Christ is the unending Light.

Having trampled upon all temporal things in the world, thou dost now choir with all the angelic hosts, singing: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!

The snares and weapons of the enemy were all brought to naught by thy strong fasting, and thy prayer, O venerable one, and thy tears. And now also the assault of passions is driven away, O venerable Mary.

*Theotokion:*

Having truly brought forth God without wedlock, Who is bodiless, and having remained truly a Virgin: by thy power, O all-pure one, thou hast driven away the armies of passions and demons.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Daniel, having stretched forth his hands, stopped the gaping mouths of lions in the pit; and the Children, lovers of piety, girded with virtue, quenched the power of fire, crying aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparia:*

Having illuminated all thy mind with the radiance of virtues, O glorious Mary, knowing God; for having thinned out the flesh by much fasting, with pious thought thou didst sing rejoicing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

Having protected thyself with the sign of the Cross, with thy dryshod feet thou didst cross the Jordan waters faithfully, O Mary; and having partaken of the Body and Blood of the eternal Christ, thou didst say: Now lettest Thou Thy handmaid depart![^7]

The divine priest Zosimas, the initiate of grace, when he saw thee, O glorious one, having crossed the rushing current of the Jordan with dryshod feet, being held with fear and trembling, rejoicing sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Theotokion:*

Through thee, O all-undefiled Sovereign Lady, she shook off all corruption and defilement, and was clothed in the garment of immortality; and through thee the venerable one cried aloud unto thy Son: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

A cornerstone uncut by hand was cut out from thee, O Virgin, from that unhewn mountain: even Christ, Who joined together the parted natures; therefore rejoicing, we magnify thee, O Theotokos.

*Troparia:*

Now art thou truly filled with incorruptible and divine food, and dost delight in the intelligible and never-setting light in the heavenly habitations, where the angelic ranks pray unto God for us.

Having loathed fleeting and corruptible glory, O Mary, thou hast inherited blessed glory and life; pray unto Christ ever for those who celebrate thy most holy memory.

Behold my sorrow, O venerable one, and the groaning of my heart; behold the narrowness of my life. Save me from my sin, and show compassion upon my soul, by thine intercessions unto the Lord.

*Theotokion:*

O pure Sovereign Lady Theotokos, salvation of sinners: accept this supplication, and deliver me from my sins as I flee unto thy Son, by the prayers of thy venerable one.

*Exapostilarion, Podoben: "O Unvarying Light" (Свѣ́те неизмѣ́нный):*

An example of repentance was given unto us, O Mary; for by thy fervent compunction thou didst reverse defeat, having acquired the Mother of God as protectress: with whom do thou pray for us.

*Theotokion:*

The Light that shone timelessly from the Father before the ages, in time hath now in the end appeared from thee, O Virgin, for the salvation of the world: unto Whom cease not to pray for thy people.

*April 2. Our Venerable Father and Wonderworker Titus:*

*On the same month, on the 2nd day: our venerable father and wonderworker Titus.*[^8]

*At "Lord, I have cried," stichera, Tone 8, Podoben: "O most marvelous wonder" (Ѡ пресла́внагω чꋋдесѐ):*

O God-wise Titus our father, bearing thy cross, thou didst follow Christ, and didst subdue all passions of the soul. Whence thou didst receive grace from on high, to heal the passions...

=== LEAF p9 === (Fol. 4r)
...of those who run unto thee, to calm infirmities, and to drive away spirits; therefore we celebrate and call blessed thy memory.

O all-blessed Titus our father, being sacredly instructed by monastic conduct, and enlightening thy mind by divine partakings, thou didst truly receive the most holy chrism of priesthood by the bidding of the Divine Spirit; and thou didst gallantly serve God and thy Creator upon earth, even as an Angel.

O venerable Titus, inspired by God, having illuminated thy soul with the light of Orthodoxy, thou didst diminish the dark mist of heresy. And having shone forth as a most radiant star, thou dost enlighten the ends of the world with thy radiant miracles always. Therefore, celebrating, we feast thy memory.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Unclean are my thoughts and deceitful are my lips, and all my deeds are defiled; and what shall I do? How shall I meet the Judge? O Sovereign Lady Virgin, pray unto thy Son and Creator and Lord, that He may receive my spirit in repentance, as He alone is compassionate.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

The undefiled heifer, seeing the young bull willingly nailed upon the Tree, weeping piteously cried aloud: Woe is me, O most beloved Child! What hath the ungrateful Hebrew gathering rendered unto Thee, desiring to make me childless of Thee, O dearly beloved?

*Canon, whose acrostic is: Let Titus the pillar of monks be praised.[^9] Tone 8.*  
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Having traversed the water as dry land, and having escaped the Egyptian evil, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Deliverer and God!

*Troparia:*

Since I have been grievously wounded by carnal passions and corrupted by pleasures: O Titus, who art a treasure of dispassion, save me by thy prayers.

As a divine chosen vessel of the Spirit, thou didst offer thyself unto God from thy mother's swaddling-bands, and didst become an initiate and servant of the unwedded Birthgiver of God.

Having shone forth as the sweetness of abstinence, thou didst cast away the bitterness of passions; whence, O father, sweeter than honey and the honeycomb dost thou delight our souls.

As a disciple of the new Paul do we praise thee, O Titus: for thou wast shown forth as a zealous emulator of his struggles, O God-blessed one, and with him hast received crowns.

*Theotokion:*

The timeless Word of the Father, without a husband...

=== LEAF p10 === (Fol. 4v)
...thou didst bear, O sole Mother of God, as thou art higher than all creation.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou art the firm support of those who run unto Thee, O Lord; Thou art the light of the darkened, and my spirit hymns Thee.

*Troparia:*

As an ointment of sanctification compounded from fasting spices, O venerable one, thou didst assemble thyself as an odor of sweet fragrance unto God.

Having abstinence as a weapon and prayer as a shield ever, O venerable one, as a reprover of the powers of darkness thou didst put them to shame.

Meditating in the laws of fasting, thou didst drown the passions like another host of Pharaoh's men by the torrent of thy tears.

*Theotokion:*

I ever beseech thee, O Sovereign Lady, that by thy prayers He Who was born of thee may be merciful unto me in the Day of Judgment.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "The Angelic Choir" (Ли́къ а́гг҃льскiй):*

Having fasted upon earth as one without a body, O Titus, thou didst slay the enemy, and together didst cast down the assembly of heresies by thy Orthodox teachings. Therefore today, celebrating thy most holy memory, we joyfully hymn Him Who hath magnified thee.

*Theotokion:*

O hope of Christians, most holy Virgin, Whom thou didst bear as God beyond mind and word: cease not to pray together with the heavenly powers to grant remission of all our sins and amendment of life unto those who ever glorify thee with faith and love.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

The undefiled ewe-lamb, beholding the Lamb and Shepherd hung dead upon the Tree, weeping said, crying aloud as a mother: How shall I endure Thy descent which is beyond word, O my Son, and Thy voluntary passions, O most good God?

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation; I have understood Thy works, and I have glorified Thy Godhead.

*Troparia:*

All the festering putrefaction of sin thou didst burn away, O blessed one, and with the arrow of abstinence thou didst wound the demonic hosts.

Having wounded thy soul with the desire for purity, thou didst fervently follow Christ, and didst enter into the chamber of glory, O all-blessed one.

Like a most fragrant garden of flowers and an animate paradise of virtues, thou didst blossom by abstinence, wherewith thou dost nourish all who honor thee.

Having acquired thee as a lawgiver of abstinence and a guileless image of meekness, as a true Moses and David, we call thee blessed, O father.
