[^69]: The Slavonic text reads: *Соприсносу́щное и҆ є҆ди́нородное сло́во бг҃а со ѻ҆тце́мъ и҆ дх҃омъ* ('Co-eternal and only-begotten Word of God with the Father and the Spirit'), where the printed leaf breaks *є҆ди́но-* across lines.
[^70]: The phrase *видѣ́нїемъ и҆ дѣ́янымъ любому́дрїемъ* translates the Greek ascetic hendiadys θεωρίᾳ καὶ πράξει ('by contemplation and practical ascetic action/philosophy').
[^71]: The Slavonic *я́кѡ гꙋ́ба и҆ налїя̀* renders the Greek ὡς σπόγγος καὶ ἀντλητήριον ('like a sponge and a vessel/ladle for drawing water'), signifying the saint's receptive absorption of the monastic virtues of Saint Chariton the Confessor.
[^72]: At the turn from fol. 48r to fol. 48v, the catchword printed at the foot of fol. 48r is *И҆ ла́дїю* ('And the bark/ship'), whereas the first troparion at the head of fol. 48v begins with *Наде́ждею я́же къ бг҃у* ('Strengthened by hope toward God'). This reflects an erratum in the Pochayiv pressroom's catchword setting.
[^73]: The Slavonic text *свяще́нникъ бы́въ пребл҃же́нне ст҃ителю* addresses Saint John of the Ancient Caves as *ст҃ителю* ('hierarch' or 'sacred celebrant'), reflecting his presbyteral/hierarchical dignity within the Lavra of Saint Chariton.
[^74]: The Slavonic text reads: *тя̀ мнѣ̀ сохранѝ*, where *мнѣ̀* appears as an ethical dative or an enclitic variant in the Pochayiv edition for *тя̀ невреди́ма сохранѝ* ('preserved thee unharmed') or *тя̀ соблюдѐ*.
