[^17]: Sts. Theodulus the Lector and Agathopus the Deacon of Thessalonica (martyred under Maximian/Diocletian, c. 303), drowned in the sea with stones tied around their necks. In many Greek and post-Nikonian editions, this day also commemorates Sts. Claudius, Diodorus, Victor, Victorinus, Papias, Serapion, and Nicephorus; the 1761 Pochaiv Menaion here gives sole precedence to Theodulus, Agathopus, and their companions.
[^18]: Slavonic acrostic: Твои́хъ воспѣва́ю мꙋ́чениковъ хрⷭ҇тѐ красотꙋ̀. І҆ѡ́сифъ.
[^19]: The 'Levitical vesture' (леѵі́тскаѧ ѡ҆де́жда) and 'ministry of the first martyr' (первомꙋ́ченика слꙋ́жба) refer to the diaconal rank of St. Agathopus, patterned after St. Stephen the Protomartyr and Archdeacon.
