[^227]: The Dismissal Theotokia (Apolytikia Theotokia) of the Eight Tones are sung throughout the liturgical year at daily Vespers, Matins, and Divine Liturgy.
[^228]: These Dismissal Theotokia (Bogorodichny Otpustytelny) are appointed to follow the troparia of the Saints throughout the liturgical year when the Menaion provides no dedicated Theotokion.
[^229]: The rubric appoints the general dismissal troparia to all saints and for the departed ('Apostles, martyrs, and prophets...' and 'Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good...') when Alleluia is sung at daily Matins (as during Great Lent and penitential seasons) in place of 'God is the Lord'.
[^230]: In the printed Slavonic text at the foot of folio 104r (p. 209), the rubric '*At the end of Matins:*' is printed without an accompanying hymn or incipit, immediately preceding the rubric for Monday at 'God is the Lord'. In standard liturgical practice, the sessional dismissal 'O all-pure Virgin, save us by thy prayers...' (printed on fol. 104v) is appointed here.
