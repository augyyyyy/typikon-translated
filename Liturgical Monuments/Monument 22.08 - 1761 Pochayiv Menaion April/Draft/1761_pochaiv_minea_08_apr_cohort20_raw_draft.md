*Resurrectional Theotokia for the Eight Tones.*[^218]

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of the whole world, who blossomed forth from mankind and gave birth to the Master, the gate of heaven, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful. For she appeared as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace and opened the kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the confirmation of our faith, we have as champion the Lord born of her. Be of good courage, therefore, be of good courage, O people of God; for He shall conquer the enemies, for He is all-powerful.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Lo, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for a Virgin thou hast given birth, and after giving birth, even as before birth, hast thou remained a Virgin, for God was He that was born. Therefore also He renewed nature. But, O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants, offered unto thee in thy temple; but as thou bearest the Tender-hearted One in thine arms, have compassion upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
The shadow of the Law has passed away, now that grace has come; for even as the bush burned with fire and was not consumed, so as a Virgin hast thou given birth, and a Virgin hast thou remained. Instead of the pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness has shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O wonder new among all ancient wonders! For who has known a mother to give birth without a husband, and to bear in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? It is the counsel of God, He that was born. Whom as an Infant having borne in thine arms, O all-pure one, and having maternal boldness toward Him, cease not praying for those who honor thee: that He have mercy and save our souls.

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
How shall we not marvel at thy God-man childbirth, O all-honored one? For not having received trial of man, O all-immaculate one, thou gavest birth in the flesh without a father unto the Son, Who was begotten of the Father without a mother before the ages; Who in no wise suffered change, or confusion, or division...

=== LEAF p192 ===
...division, but preserved intact the property of each nature. Therefore, O Sovereign Lady, Virgin Mother, entreat Him that the souls of those who confess thee as Theotokos in orthodox fashion may be saved.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Without seed from the Holy Spirit, but by the Father's will, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who exists before the ages from the Father without a mother; and for our sake, having come from thee without a father, thou didst give birth unto Him in the flesh, and nourished the Infant with milk. Therefore cease not praying, that our souls may be delivered from tribulations.

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
David the Prophet, forefather of God, because of thee in song proclaimed concerning thee unto Him that has done great things for thee: The queen stood at Thy right hand.[^219] For God, Who was well-pleased to become man of thee without a father, showed thee forth as mother, mediatress of life; that He might renew His own image, which was corrupted by passions; and having found the sheep that went astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He will bring it unto the Father, and according to His own will will unite it with the heavenly hosts, and will save the world, O Theotokos, Christ Who has great and rich mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Look upon the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, quelling the fierce uprisings against us, releasing us from every affliction. For thee alone do we have as a firm and sure confirmation, and we have acquired thy protection, that we who call upon thee may not be put to shame, O Theotokos. Speed unto the supplication of those who faithfully cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Theotokos, help of all, joy and protection, and salvation of our souls.

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
In the Red Sea an image of the unwedded Bride was prefigured of old: there Moses, the divider of the water; but here Gabriel, the minister of the wonder. Then did Israel traverse the deep dryshod; but now the Virgin has given birth unto Christ without seed. The sea after the passage of Israel remained impassable; the immaculate one after the childbirth of Emmanuel remained incorrupt. O Thou Who art, and art before all, Who didst appear as man, O God, have mercy on us.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Thou art a temple and a door, a palace and a royal throne, O all-honored Virgin; through whom my Deliverer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of righteousness, desiring to enlighten that which He fashioned according to His own image by His own hand. Therefore, O all-hymned one, as having maternal boldness toward Him, unceasingly...

=== LEAF p193 ===
...unceasingly pray that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Who shall not call thee blessed, O all-holy Virgin? Who shall not praise thy most pure childbirth? For the only-begotten Son, Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the same came forth from thee, pure one, having ineffably become incarnate. Being God by nature, and having become man by nature for our sake; not divided into two persons, but known in two natures without confusion. Entreat Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may have mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
My Creator and Deliverer, Christ the Lord, having come forth from thy womb, O all-pure one, and having clothed Himself in me, delivered Adam from the first curse. Therefore unto thee, O all-pure one, as truly both the Mother of God and a Virgin, we unceasingly cry out the angelic "Rejoice!": Rejoice, O Theotokos, protection, shelter, and salvation of our souls.

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
Thou wast known as a Mother beyond nature, O Theotokos, and didst remain a Virgin beyond speech and understanding; and the wonder of thy childbirth no tongue is able to tell. For the conception being most glorious, O pure one, the manner of the childbirth is incomprehensible; for where God so wills, the order of nature is overcome. Therefore, knowing thee as the Mother of God, we all fervently pray thee: pray that our souls may be saved.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Fleeing unto thy good protection, all we earthborn cry unto thee: O Theotokos, our hope, deliver us from our measureless transgressions, and save our souls.

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
The King of heaven, through love of mankind, appeared on earth and dwelt with men; for having taken flesh from a pure Virgin, and having come forth from her with the assumption [of humanity], one is the Son, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis. Therefore, truly proclaiming Him as perfect God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God. Entreat Him, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may have mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O unwedded Virgin, who ineffably didst conceive God in the flesh, Mother of God Most High: accept the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, granting unto all cleansing of transgressions. Now receiving our supplications, pray that we all may be saved.

*Theotokia of the Eight Tones, sung when there is Glory to the Saint in the Menaion, and Both now in the tone of the Saint.*[^220]

*Tone 1:*
*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
O rejoicing of the heavenly ranks, mighty protection of men upon earth, O all-pure Virgin, save us who flee unto thee; for next to God, O Theotokos, we have placed our hope in thee.

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Most holy of all holy powers, more honorable than all creation, O Theotokos, Sovereign Lady of the world: save us who didst give birth to the Savior, from our ten-thousandfold transgressions and tribulations, as thou art good, by thy prayers.

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
O all-hymned Virgin, Moses with prophetic eyes beheld the mystery that is in thee: the bush burning and unconsumed; for the fire of the Godhead, O pure one, burned not thy womb. Therefore we pray thee, as Mother of our God, ask peace for the world, and great mercy.

*On Tuesday at Matins, Theotokion:*
The harlot and the prodigal, and the thief have I surpassed, and the publican in transgressions have I exceeded, and the Ninevites. Woe is me, what shall become of me? How shall I, wretch that I am, escape torment? O pure one, I fall down before thee: have compassion upon me by thy mercy, even as thy Son has saved them.

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, joy of the forebears, gladness of the apostles and martyrs, and protection of us, thy servants, O Virgin.

*On Thursday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos; rejoice, boast of the whole universe; rejoice, all-pure, blessed Mother of God.

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Truly beyond understanding are thy marvelous great things, O Bride of God, of thy childbirth which all the prophets proclaimed, all most glorious! The conception and childbirth, O all-hymned one, are incomprehensible and ineffable; by Whom He has saved the world, as He is merciful.

*On Saturday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Rejoice from us, O holy Virgin Theotokos, pure vessel of the whole universe, unquenchable lamp, dwelling-place of the Incomprehensible, unconquerable temple! Rejoice, of whom was born the Lamb of God, Who takes away the sins of the whole world!
