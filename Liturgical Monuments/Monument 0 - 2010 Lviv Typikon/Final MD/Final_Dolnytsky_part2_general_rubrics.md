# PART II: GENERAL RUBRICS FOR VARIOUS SERVICES OF THE OCTOECHOS AND MENAION

There are 20 such rubrics, or paradigms of general divine services—that is, 7 outside a feast, and 13 within a feast, as presented below:

### Outside a Feast
1. Saint without Polyeleos on a Sunday
2. Saint without Polyeleos on weekdays (except Saturday)
3. Saint without Polyeleos on a Saturday
4. Saint with Polyeleos on a Sunday
5. Saint with Polyeleos on weekdays and Saturday
6. Saint with an All-Night Vigil on a Sunday
7. Saint with an All-Night Vigil on weekdays and Saturday

### Within a Feast

#### In a Forefeast
8. Forefeast on a Sunday with a Saint without Polyeleos
9. Forefeast on weekdays and Saturday with a Saint without Polyeleos

#### In a Feast
10. Feast of the Lord on a Sunday and weekdays
11. Feast of the Theotokos on a Sunday
12. Feast of the Theotokos on weekdays

#### In an Afterfeast
13. Afterfeast on a Sunday with a Saint without Polyeleos
14. Afterfeast on weekdays and Saturday with a Saint without Polyeleos
15. Afterfeast on a Sunday with a Saint with Polyeleos
16. Afterfeast on weekdays with a Saint with Polyeleos
17. Afterfeast on a Sunday with a Saint with an All-Night Vigil[^44]
18. Afterfeast on weekdays with a Saint with an All-Night Vigil

#### In the Apodosis of a Feast
19. Apodosis of a Feast on a Sunday
20. Apodosis of a Feast on weekdays

> **Preliminary Note on the Ranks of Saints without Polyeleos:**
> A Saint without Polyeleos may be sung either **on 4** or **on 6**. The designation of a saint "on 4" or "on 6" depends on the number of troparia appointed for each ode of his Canon at Matins.
> 
> There are three possible occurrences of a Saint without Polyeleos:
> 1. Only one saint on 4.
> 2. Two saints on 4.
> 3. One saint on 6.
> 
> When we say "one or two saints," we understand those having separate services; for several saints may be commemorated on the same day, but share only a single service. The ecclesiastical rubric never provides for more than two separate services to saints on one day[^45].
> 
> Now we shall present the order for each of the 20 aforementioned cases.

## 2.1 Saint without Polyeleos on a Sunday

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Kathisma 1 (*“Blessed is the man”*)—entire, according to the Typikon. (By our custom today, only selected verses are sung, as contained in the church hymnal).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: Sunday stichera of the *Octoechos* of the current tone—7, and from the *Menaion* (or *Anthologion*) to the saint according to the calendar—3; **Glory, Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the current tone from the *Octoechos* (or, as usual, from the *Heirmologion*).
   * *If there is a Doxastikon to the saint:* **Glory:** Doxastikon to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the current tone[^46].
   * *If two saints occur:* 4 stichera of the *Octoechos*, and from the *Menaion* to the first saint—3, and to the second—2; **Glory, Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone[^47]. (If there is a Doxastikon to the first saint: **Glory:** his Doxastikon, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone).
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* 6 stichera of the *Octoechos*, and from the *Menaion* to the saint—4; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the current tone.
3. **Prokimenon:** Great Sunday Prokimenon *“The Lord is King”*[^48].
4. **Aposticha:** Sunday stichera of the current tone.
   * *If there is a Doxastikon to the saint:* **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion of the Aposticha from the *Octoechos* in the tone of the Doxastikon[^49].
5. **Troparia:** First the Sunday troparion of the current tone; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** Theotokion from the Sunday ones in the tone of the saint's troparion (New Greek Constantinopolitan Typikon, p. 77).
   * *If two saints occur:* Sunday troparion and of the first saint; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the second[^50].
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of the Sunday service, of the first saint, and of the second, if there be one[^51].

> **Note on Finding Theotokia:**
> For convenience in finding the Theotokion used at Vespers and Matins in the tone of the saint's Doxastikon, at the end of every volume of the *Menaion* (as also in the *Anthologion*) are given all the Theotokia taken on *“Lord, I have cried”*, on the Aposticha, and after the troparia of the saint, in the order of the eight tones and days of the week—primarily Sunday ones, as well as daily ones.

### At Compline

1. **Canon:** Canon to the Theotokos of the *Octoechos* of the current tone, taken strictly as in the *Octoechos* (every ode with the Heirmos on 5, without Katavasias and without litanies). After the 6th Ode: "Lord, have mercy" (3); **Glory, Both now:** Sessional Hymn (or sometimes the Kontakion)[^52].
2. **Troparia:** After *“It is truly meet”*, no troparia are said, except the single Sunday Hypakoe of the current tone from the *Horologion*[^53].
3. **Dismissal:** From *“Glory to Thee, O Christ God”*, with the response *“Glory, Both now”*, "Lord, have mercy" (3), "Lord, bless"[^54].

### At the Midnight Office

* **Canon:** Canon of the *Octoechos* of the current tone, strictly as in the *Octoechos* (with the Heirmos on 5, without Katavasias and without litanies). After the 3rd and 6th Odes: "Lord, have mercy" (3) and the Sessional Hymn.

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion of the current tone twice; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion.
   * *If two saints occur:* Sunday troparion once, of the first saint once; **Glory:** of the second once, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the second[^55].
2. **Kathismata:** Two appointed current Kathismata, after each of which: Small Litany with the Sunday Sessional Hymns from the *Octoechos*[^56].
   * *Polyeleos / Kathisma 17:* Kathisma 17 (*“The Blameless”*) or Kathisma 19 (Polyeleos). Kathisma 17 is read on Sundays between the Apodosis of the Holy Cross (September 21) and the Forefeast of the Nativity (December 20); Kathisma 19 is sung on Sundays between the Apodosis of Theophany (January 14) and the 1st Sunday of Great Lent[^57]. After Kathisma 17 or 19: immediately the Resurrectional Evlogitaria (*“The Angelic Host”*), Small Litany, and the Hypakoe of the current tone.
3. **Graduals (Anavathmoi):** Gradual of the current tone from the *Octoechos* (or from the *Heirmologion*).
4. **Prokimenon:** Sunday Prokimenon of the current tone.
5. **Matins Gospel:** One of the eleven Sunday Resurrection Gospels.
6. **Post-Gospel Sticheron:** *“Jesus having risen from the tomb...”*
7. **Canons (4 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Three canons of the *Octoechos* of the current tone on 10, and one from the *Menaion* to the saint on 4.
     * *1st Canon (Resurrection):* with Heirmos on 4.
     * *2nd Canon (Cross-Resurrection):* without Heirmos on 3.
     * *3rd Canon (Theotokos):* without Heirmos on 3.
     * *4th Canon (*Menaion* of the Saint):* without Heirmos on 4.
     *(Only the Heirmoi of the first canon are sung; the others serve only as melodic models).*
   * *Katavasia:* The designated seasonal Katavasia (Heirmos of the first canon of the feast[^58]) after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the Saint (if appointed), and the Sessional Hymn of the saint.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *At the 8th Ode:* Before the Katavasia, the refrain *"We praise, we bless, we worship the Lord..."* is sung in the tone of the Katavasia.
   * *At the 9th Ode:* *“My soul magnifies the Lord”* with the refrain *“More honorable than the Cherubim”*.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* *“Holy is the Lord our God”* (3) in the tone of the Sunday Prokimenon, followed by the Sunday Exaposteilarion (Hymn of Light) corresponding to the Sunday Gospel; **Glory:** Exaposteilarion of the saint (if appointed), **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion of the Exaposteilarion[^59].
   * *If two saints occur:* Two canons from the *Octoechos* on 6 (Resurrection with Heirmos on 4, Theotokos on 2), and two from the *Menaion* on 8 (first saint on 4, second on 4). Katavasia after every ode. After the 3rd Ode: Kontakion and Ikos of both saints (if appointed), Sessional Hymn of the first saint without Theotokion; **Glory:** of the second saint, **Both now:** his Theotokion[^60]. After the 6th Ode: Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos. After the 9th Ode: Sunday Exaposteilarion, Exaposteilarion of the first saint; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion[^61].
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Resurrection Canon with Heirmos on 4, Cross-Resurrection on 2, Theotokos on 2, and of the saint on 6[^62].
8. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 Sunday stichera from the *Octoechos* of the current tone; **Glory:** appointed Gospel Sticheron (one of eleven); **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”* from the *Horologion*.
   * (The 8 stichera use 6 refrains from the Psalms of Praise, and for the 7th and 8th separate refrains: *“Arise, O Lord”* and *“I will confess Thee, O Lord”*).
   * *If the saint on 6 has Praises stichera:* 4 of the Resurrection and 4 of the saint with "**Glory:**", using his two proper refrains[^63].
9. **After the Great Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion:
   * Odd tones (1, 3, 5, 7): *“Today salvation has come to the world.”*
   * Even tones (2, 4, 6, 8): *“Having risen from the tomb.”*
10. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal, as at Vespers, with the commemoration of the Sunday service and of the saint (first and second, if there be).

### At the Hours

1. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion at all Hours; at the 3rd and 9th Hours: **Glory:** of the saint; at the 6th Hour: **Glory:** of the Temple[^64].
2. **Kontakia:** At the 1st and 9th Hours—Sunday Kontakion; at the 3rd Hour—of the saint; at the 6th Hour—of the Temple.
   * *If two saints occur:* Sunday troparion at all Hours; **Glory:** at 3rd and 6th to the first saint, at 6th to the Temple, at 9th to the second. Kontakia alternate: 1st—Sunday, 3rd—first saint, 6th—Temple, 9th—second saint[^65].
3. **Dismissal:** From *“Glory to Thee, O Christ God”*, with the commemoration of Sunday and the saint (first and second).

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons:** The first two Antiphons are Sunday (Resurrectional); the 3rd is the usual *“Come, let us worship”* with the Sunday refrain (in monasteries—the Beatitudes)[^66].
2. **Troparia & Kontakia:** Sunday Troparion; **Glory:** Sunday Kontakion, **Both now:** Festal Theotokion Kontakion in the tone of the Sunday Kontakion, or Kontakion of the Temple (if dedicated to the Theotokos); if not, then the Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the Sunday Kontakion[^67], or the general Theotokion *“O Protection of Christians”*[^68].
3. **Prokimenon & Alleluia:** Sunday, of the current tone.
4. **Readings:** Sequential Sunday Apostle and Gospel.
5. **Communion Hymn:** Sunday *“Praise the Lord from the heavens”*[^69].

> **Note on Votive / Supplicatory Services:**
> If a service for some particular need is also combined, it is preceded by the Sunday service. We replace the Kontakion of the supplication with the Theotokion of the supplication, if there is one; if not, with the Kontakion of the supplication, as of the Lord.

## 2.2 Saint without Polyeleos on Weekdays

### At Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Current Kathisma[^70]. (On Sunday evening, on account of the Vigil that sometimes occurs, the Kathisma is omitted; on the eves of feasts having an All-Night Vigil, the Kathisma is likewise omitted).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 6 stichera: of the *Octoechos* of the current tone—3[^71], and from the *Menaion* of the saint—3; **Glory, Both now:** Theotokion (or Stavrotheotokion) following the stichera of the saint.
   * *If there is a Doxastikon to the saint:* **Glory:** Doxastikon of the saint, **Both now:** Theotokion of the *Octoechos* in the tone of the Doxastikon and day of the week from *“Lord, I have cried”* (sung by both choirs together)[^72].
   * *If two saints occur:* Nothing from the *Octoechos*; 3 stichera to the first saint and 3 to the second; **Glory, Both now:** Theotokion (or Stavrotheotokion) after the second saint. (If the first saint has a Doxastikon, the Theotokion is selected as above).
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Nothing from the *Octoechos*; all 6 stichera to the saint; **Glory:** Doxastikon, **Both now:** Theotokion of the day and tone.
3. **Prokimenon:** Daily Prokimenon of the weekday from the *Horologion*.
4. **Aposticha:** Stichera of the *Octoechos* with the two weekday refrains from the *Horologion*.
   * *If there is a Doxastikon to the saint:* **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Theotokion of the *Octoechos* Aposticha in the tone of the Doxastikon and day of the week.
5. **Troparia:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Daily Dismissal Theotokion from the *Horologion* in the tone of the saint's troparion and day of the week.
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion to the first saint; **Glory:** to the second, **Both now:** Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the second saint and day of the week[^73].

### At Compline

1. **Canon:** Canon to the Theotokos of the *Octoechos* of the current tone (with Heirmos on 5, without Katavasias or litanies). After the 6th Ode: "Lord, have mercy" (3); **Glory, Both now:** Sessional Hymn.
2. **Troparia:** After *“It is truly meet”*: of the Temple (if of the Lord or Theotokos), then of the weekday, and the other four from Small Compline in the *Horologion*. If the Temple is of a saint: first of the day, then of the Temple. (On Wednesday and Friday, the Cross troparion of the Lord takes first place)[^74].

### At the Midnight Office

* Everything strictly as in the *Horologion* for weekdays.

### At Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion of the saint twice; **Glory, Both now:** Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion and day of the week[^75].
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion to the first saint twice; **Glory:** to the second once, **Both now:** Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the second saint[^76].
2. **Kathismata:** Two current Kathismata, after each of which: Small Litany and Sessional Hymn from the *Octoechos*[^77].
3. **Canons (3 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* First canon of the *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 6; second of the *Octoechos* without Heirmos on 4; and of the saint from the *Menaion* without Heirmos on 4.
   * *Katavasia:* Only after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9 (the Heirmos of the saint's canon)[^78].
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Sessional Hymn of the saint.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the saint (if appointed).
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Exaposteilarion from the *Octoechos*; **Glory:** Exaposteilarion of the saint (if appointed), **Both now:** Theotokion of the saint's Exaposteilarion. (If the saint has none: **Glory, Both now:** Theotokion of the *Octoechos* Exaposteilarion. On Wednesday/Friday, the Stavrotheotokion of the *Octoechos* is taken; on Thursday, the Theotokion of St. Nicholas from the *Octoechos*)[^79].
   * *If two saints occur:* First canon of the *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 6; canon of the first saint on 4; canon of the second saint on 4[^80]. Katavasia after Odes 3, 6, 8, 9 (Heirmos of the second saint)[^81]. After the 3rd Ode: Kontakion-Ikos of the second saint, Sessional Hymn of the first; **Glory:** Sessional Hymn of the second, **Both now:** his Theotokion[^82]. After the 6th Ode: Kontakion-Ikos of the first saint. After the 9th Ode: Exaposteilarion of the *Octoechos*, of the first saint; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** his Theotokion[^83].
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* First canon of the *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 4 (omitting 2 Martyria, except Thursday), second of the *Octoechos* on 4, and of the saint on 6[^84].
4. **Aposticha:** All from the *Octoechos*. If there is a Doxastikon to the saint: **Both now:** Theotokion of the Matins Aposticha in the tone of the Doxastikon and day of the week.
5. **Conclusion:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion and day of the week (different from the one at Vespers).
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the first; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the second[^85].

> **Notes on a Saint on 6:**
> 1. Sometimes a saint on 6 differs from a saint on 4 only by having 6 troparia in the Matins Canon and 6 stichera on *“Lord, I have cried”* (e.g. September 9).
> 2. Sometimes he has a proper sticheron with refrain on the Aposticha of Matins, plus a Doxastikon (e.g. November 17).
> 3. Sometimes he has 4 Praises stichera, followed by the Small Doxology and the *Octoechos* Aposticha (e.g. September 6, October 7)[^86].
> 4. Sometimes he has Praises stichera, Great Doxology, Dogmatikon on *“Lord, I have cried”*, proper Aposticha, and Sunday Theotokia (e.g. October 23, July 25). If a Feast of the Lord or Theotokos on 6, the canon is taken without the *Octoechos* (July 2, August 16, 31).

### At the Hours

1. **Troparia & Kontakia:** At the 1st Hour—of the weekday; at the 3rd and 9th Hours—of the saint; at the 6th Hour—of the Temple[^87].
   * *If two saints occur:* 1st Hour—of the weekday; 3rd Hour—first saint; 6th Hour—Temple; 9th Hour—second saint[^88].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Troparia & Kontakia (Daily Custom):** Troparion of the weekday; **Glory:** Kontakion of the day, **Both now:** Kontakion of the Temple (if of the Lord or Theotokos). If the Temple is of a saint: general Theotokion *“O Protection of Christians”*[^89] or festal Theotokion[^90]. (On Wednesday and Friday, replace the troparion of the day with the Kontakion of the Cross with *“Glory, Both now”*).
2. **Prokimenon, Alleluia & Communion Hymn:** Of the weekday from the *Sluzhebnik* or *Apostolos*.
3. **Readings:** Sequential daily Apostle and Gospel[^91].

> **Notes on Combining Services at Liturgy:**
> * *If celebrated only to the saint:* Theotokion or Kontakion of the Temple is taken as above.
> * *If celebrated for a specific need:* Theotokion of the need; if none, the troparion of the need is replaced by its Kontakion with *“Glory, Both now”*.
> * *If celebrated for both the day and the saint:* Troparion of the day, then of the saint; Kontakion of the day; **Glory:** Kontakion of the saint, **Both now:** Theotokion/Kontakion of the Temple.
> * *If celebrated for the day/saint and a need:* Service of the day/saint precedes the need; Kontakion of the day/saint; **Glory:** Kontakion for need, **Both now:** Theotokion for need.
> * *Votive / Need Liturgies:* Must always be celebrated singly, except for the daily Apostle/Gospel which may precede the readings for the dead[^92].
> * *Cathedral vs Parish Practice:* In cathedrals, the daily Apostle/Gospel is never omitted[^93]. Where multiple readings coincide, Slavic typikons combine them under the refrain of the first reading[^94][^95][^96][^97].

## 2.3 Saint without Polyeleos on a Saturday

> **Note on Saturday Precedence:**
> On the first five days of the week, the *Octoechos* precedes the *Menaion*. On Saturday, conversely, the service of the *Menaion* precedes the *Octoechos*, because Saturday is dedicated generally to all saints and the departed, whereas the *Menaion* provides the specific service of the day's saint.

### At Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Current Kathisma (18th Kathisma), omitted only before an All-Night Vigil.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 6 stichera: to the saint from the *Menaion*—3, and from the *Octoechos*—3[^98]; **Glory, Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the current departing tone[^99].
   * *If there is a Doxastikon to the saint:* After the Doxastikon, the 1st Theotokion of the tone is taken with **Both now:**.
   * *If two saints occur:* To the first saint—3, to the second—3; **Glory, Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone.
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* All 6 stichera to the saint; **Glory:** his Doxastikon, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone.
3. **Prokimenon:** Daily Saturday Prokimenon from the *Horologion*.
4. **Aposticha:** 3 Martyria stichera of the *Octoechos* with two weekday refrains. (Stichera for the dead are omitted on ordinary Saturdays, replacing them with Martyria from *“Lord, I have cried”*)[^100].
   * *If there is a Doxastikon to the saint:* **Both now:** Theotokion of the *Octoechos* Vespers Aposticha in the tone of the Doxastikon.
5. **Troparia:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion[^101].
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the first saint; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the second.
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal (in view of the Apodosis of the tone), with the commemoration of the day and the saint[^102].

### At Compline

1. **Canon:** Canon to the Theotokos from the *Octoechos* of the current tone.
2. **Troparia:** After *“It is truly meet”*: Troparia of the day (*“Apostles, Prophets, and Martyrs”*); **Glory:** Kontakion for the Departed (*“With the saints give rest”*), **Both now:** Kontakion of the day (*“To Thee, O Lord, the Planter of creation”*)[^103].

### At the Midnight Office

* Saturday Midnight Office from the *Horologion*.

### At Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion of the saint twice; **Glory, Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion[^104].
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the first saint twice; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the second.
2. **Kathismata:** Two current Kathismata, Small Litany, and Sessional Hymns (two Martyria and two for the departed).
3. **Canons (3 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Canon of the Temple (if of the Lord or Theotokos) with Heirmos on 6 (Heirmoi twice, troparia on 4)[^105]; of the saint from the *Menaion* on 4; and 1st canon of the *Octoechos* (Martyria) on 4[^106][^107].
   * *If the Temple is of a saint:* Canon of the *Menaion* saint with Heirmos on 6, Temple saint on 4, and *Octoechos* Martyria on 4[^108].
   * *Katavasia:* After Odes 3, 6, 8, 9 (Heirmos of the *Octoechos* canon).
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Sessional Hymn of the saint.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the saint.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Exaposteilarion of the saint; **Glory:** Exaposteilarion of the *Octoechos*, **Both now:** its Theotokion.
   * *If two saints occur:* Canon of the Temple (if Lord/Theotokos) on 6, first saint on 4, second on 4. If Temple is of a saint: first saint on 6, second on 4, *Octoechos* Martyria on 4. After 3rd Ode: Kontakion-Ikos of the second saint, Sessional Hymn of the first; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** his Theotokion. After 6th Ode: Kontakion-Ikos of the first saint. After 9th Ode: Exaposteilarion of the first; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** his Theotokion.
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Canon of Temple (if Lord/Theotokos) on 4, saint on 6, *Octoechos* Martyria on 4. If Temple of a saint: saint on 6, Temple on 4, *Octoechos* on 4[^109].
4. **Doxology:** Small Doxology (a saint on 6 never has the Great Doxology on Saturday).
5. **Aposticha:** Martyria stichera from the Praises[^110] with two weekday refrains[^111]; **Glory, Both now:** Theotokion from the Praises. (If there is a Doxastikon to the saint: Theotokion from the Praises of the *Octoechos* in the tone of the Doxastikon).
6. **Conclusion:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Daily Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion and day of the week[^112].
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the first; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the second.
7. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of the day and of the saint (first and second).

### At the Hours

1. **Troparia & Kontakia:** 1st Hour—Troparion and Kontakion of the day; 3rd and 9th Hours—of the saint; 6th Hour—of the Temple.
   * *If two saints occur:* 1st & 6th Hours as above; 3rd Hour—first saint; 9th Hour—second saint[^113].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Daily Custom:** Everything of the day; **Glory, Both now:** Kontakion of the day[^114].
2. **If Saint is combined with the Day:** Troparion to the saint and of the day; **Glory:** Kontakion to the saint, **Both now:** Kontakion of the day. Prokimenon, Alleluia, and Communion Hymn to the saint, then of the day. Apostle and Gospel first of the day, then of the saint[^115].

## 2.4 Saint with Polyeleos on a Sunday

> **Note on Polyeleos Ranks:**
> A Polyeleos Saint can be celebrated either on 6 or on 8[^116] (in standard Slavonic books, usually on 8)[^117].

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Kathisma 1 (*“Blessed is the man”*)—entire.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: Sunday *Octoechos* of the current tone—4, and to the saint—6; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the current tone.
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Great Sunday Prokimenon *“The Lord is King”*, followed by 3 Old Testament readings to the saint.
4. **Aposticha:** Sunday stichera of the *Octoechos*; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion of the Aposticha in the tone of the Doxastikon[^118].
5. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion[^119].
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of Sunday and of the saint.

### At Compline

* Canon of the *Octoechos* of the current tone. After the Trisagion: Kontakion of the saint alone[^120].

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion[^121].
2. **Kathismata & Polyeleos:** Two current Kathismata with Sunday Sessional Hymns from the *Octoechos*. Then the **Polyeleos** with the Magnification (Megalynarion) of the saint and the Evlogitaria (*“The Angelic Host”*), Small Litany, Hypakoe of the tone, 1st and 2nd Sessional Hymns of the saint without Theotokia; **Glory:** 3rd Sessional Hymn (Polyeleos hymn) of the saint, **Both now:** his Theotokion[^122].
3. **Graduals to Gospel:** Gradual of the current tone and everything up to the Canon—of the Sunday.
4. **Canons (3 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution (if Saint on 8):* Sunday Canon of the *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 4, Theotokos canon on 2[^123], and saint on 8[^124]. (Cross-Resurrection canon is omitted)[^126].
   * *Distribution (if Saint on 6):* Sunday Canon with Heirmos on 4, Theotokos canon on 4, and saint on 6[^125].
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion, Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the saint.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory:** Exaposteilarion of the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion of the Exaposteilarion[^127].
5. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 stichera: Sunday *Octoechos*—4, and to the saint with "**Glory:**"—4, using his two proper refrains[^128]; **Glory:** appointed Gospel Sticheron, **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”*.
6. **After the Great Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion (*“Today salvation...”* or *“Having risen from the tomb...”*).
7. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of Sunday and of the saint.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Sunday troparion; **Glory:** to the saint. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Sunday; 3rd and 9th—to the saint[^129].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Troparia & Kontakia:** Sunday Troparion and of the saint; Sunday Kontakion; **Glory:** Kontakion of the saint, **Both now:** Festal Theotokion in the tone of the Doxastikon (or *“O Protection of Christians”*).
2. **Readings & Prokimena:** Prokimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Hymn—first of the Sunday, then of the saint.

## 2.5 Saint with Polyeleos on Weekdays and Saturday

> **Note:** The *Octoechos* is completely omitted.

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Kathisma 1 (*“Blessed is the man”*)—only the 1st antiphon.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 6 stichera[^130] or 8[^131], all to the saint; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the current tone.
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Daily Prokimenon from the *Horologion*, followed by 3 Old Testament readings to the saint.
4. **Aposticha:** All stichera to the saint, with his refrains; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion from the Aposticha in the tone of the Doxastikon[^132].
5. **Troparia:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion[^133].
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of the saint alone (the weekday is never commemorated).

### At Compline

* Canon to the Theotokos from the *Octoechos* of the current tone[^134]. After *“It is truly meet”* and the Trisagion: Kontakion of the saint alone[^135].

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion to the saint twice; **Glory, Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion.
2. **Kathismata & Polyeleos:** Two current Kathismata with Sessional Hymns of the saint. Then Kathisma 19 (Polyeleos) with the Magnification and the post-Polyeleos Sessional Hymn of the saint.
3. **Graduals:** Gradual of Tone 4, 1st Antiphon (*“From my youth”*).
4. **Prokimenon & Gospel:** Prokimenon, Gospel, and post-Gospel Sticheron to the saint.
5. **Canons (2 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Canon to the Theotokos (*“Having traversed the water”*) with Heirmos on 6; one or two canons to the saint on 8[^136]. (On Saturday, if the Temple is of the Lord or Theotokos, the Temple canon is taken on 6 with Heirmos twice, and the saint on 8)[^137].
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Sessional Hymn of the saint.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the saint.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Exaposteilarion of the saint.
6. **Praises (Lauds):** 4 stichera to the saint; **Glory:** his Doxastikon, **Both now:** Theotokion following the Doxastikon.
7. **After the Great Doxology:** Troparion to the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion.
8. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal, as at Vespers.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Troparion and Kontakion of the saint.

### At the Divine Liturgy

* Everything to the saint. Epistle and Gospel: first of the weekday, then of the saint.

## 2.6 Saint with an All-Night Vigil on a Sunday

### At Small Vespers

1. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 4 Sunday stichera of the current tone; **Glory:** sticheron to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the tone[^138][^139][^140].
2. **Aposticha:** 1 Sunday sticheron and 3 to the saint with his refrains from Great Vespers[^141]; **Glory:** to the saint from Small Vespers, **Both now:** Theotokion from the Small Vespers Aposticha[^142].
3. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion.
4. **Dismissal:** Small Dismissal with the commemoration of Sunday and the saint.

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Kathisma 1 (*“Blessed is the man”*)—entire (or selected verses).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: 4 from the *Octoechos* and 6 to the saint; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the current tone.
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Daily Sunday Prokimenon, followed by 3 Old Testament readings to the saint.
4. **Litiya:** Stichera of the saint; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion from the Aposticha of Great Vespers in the tone of the Doxastikon[^143].
5. **Aposticha:** Sunday stichera; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the Doxastikon[^144].
6. **Blessing of Loaves:** Troparion *“Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos”* twice, and of the saint once[^145].

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion[^146].
2. **Kathismata & Polyeleos:** Two current Kathismata with Sunday Sessional Hymns. Polyeleos with Magnification and *“The Angelic Host”*, Hypakoe of the tone, and all three Sessional Hymns of the saint; **Glory:** 3rd (Polyeleos) Sessional Hymn, **Both now:** its Theotokion[^147].
3. **Graduals to Gospel:** Gradual, Prokimenon, Gospel, and Sticheron—all of the Sunday. (Anointing with blessed oil during the Canon).
4. **Canons (3 or 4 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Sunday canon with Heirmos on 4, Theotokos canon on 2, and saint on 8 (one or two canons)[^148].
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion, Ikos, and Sessional Hymn to the saint.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Theotokion of the Sunday Exaposteilarion[^149].
5. **Praises, Doxology, Hours & Liturgy:** Everything else proceeds exactly as for a Polyeleos Saint on a Sunday (Section 2.4).

## 2.7 Saint with an All-Night Vigil on Weekdays and Saturday

### At Small Vespers

1. **On *“Lord, I have cried”* & Aposticha:** All stichera to the saint with his proper refrains; Theotokia selected from the daily *Octoechos* in the tone of the Doxastikon[^150][^151].
2. **Troparia:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the saint's troparion.

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** *“Blessed is the man”* (1st antiphon).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 8 stichera to the saint; **Glory:** his Doxastikon, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) in the tone of the Doxastikon (on Friday evening: Dogmatikon of the departing tone)[^152].
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Daily Prokimenon and 3 Old Testament readings to the saint.
4. **Litiya:** Stichera of the saint; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the Doxastikon[^153].
5. **Aposticha:** All stichera to the saint with his two refrains; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** Sunday Theotokion in the tone of the Doxastikon[^154].
6. **Blessing of Loaves:** Troparion of the saint twice, and *“Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos”* once.

### At Great Matins, Hours, and Divine Liturgy

* Everything proceeds as for a Polyeleos Saint on a Weekday (Section 2.5), beginning Matins from *“Glory to the Holy, Consubstantial...”* with anointing during the Canon.

> **Note on Three Pre-eminent Feasts:**
> Three feasts—the **Nativity of St. John the Baptist** (June 24), the **Beheading of St. John the Baptist** (August 29), and the **Holy Pre-eminent Apostles Peter and Paul** (June 29)—surpass other Vigil saints in that their Matins Canons are taken alone without the Theotokos canon, and at Liturgy their Apostle/Gospel readings are taken alone[^155]. (The Moscow Typikon includes also St. John the Theologian and the Temple Feast)[^156].

## 2.8 Forefeast with a Saint without a Polyeleos on a Sunday

> **Note:** If two saints occur with a Forefeast on a Sunday, the service of the second saint is transferred to another day[^157].

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** *“Blessed is the man”* (all three antiphons).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: Sunday *Octoechos*—4, Forefeast—3, and saint—3; **Glory:** of the Forefeast, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone.
   * *If the saint has a Doxastikon:* **Glory:** Doxastikon of the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone[^158].
   * *If the saint is on 6:* Sunday—3, Forefeast—3, saint—4; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion of the tone[^159].
3. **Aposticha:** Sunday stichera; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast. (If the saint has a Doxastikon: **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast).
4. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
5. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of Sunday and the saint[^160].

### At Compline & Midnight Office

* **Compline:** After *“It is truly meet”*: Kontakion of the Forefeast alone[^161].
* **Midnight Office:** Sunday Trinitarian Canon as usual.

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
2. **Kathismata to Gospel:** All of the Sunday, as usual.
3. **Canons (4 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Sunday *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 4, *Octoechos* Theotokos on 2, Forefeast on 4, and saint on 4[^162]. (If saint on 6, he is taken on 4).
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the Forefeast and of the saint; **Glory:** Sessional Hymn of the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* *“Holy is the Lord our God”* (3), Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast[^163].
4. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 stichera: *Octoechos*—4, and to the saint (or Forefeast from Aposticha)—4; **Glory:** festal sticheron, **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”*[^164].
5. **After the Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion.
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with Sunday and festal commemorations.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Sunday troparion; **Glory:** at 1st and 3rd of the Forefeast, at 6th and 9th to the saint. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Forefeast; 3rd and 9th—Sunday[^165].

### At the Divine Liturgy

* Sunday service throughout, except troparia: Sunday Troparion and of the Forefeast; **Glory:** Sunday Kontakion, **Both now:** Kontakion of the Forefeast[^166].

## 2.9 Forefeast with a Saint without a Polyeleos on Weekdays and Saturday

> **Notes:**
> 1. From the Forefeast until the Apodosis, the *Octoechos* is omitted on weekdays and Saturday, replaced by the festal service in the first place combined with the saint.
> 2. Services have the daily form (Daily Vespers and Daily Matins).
> 3. Hymnography concludes with festal Theotokia (on Friday evening: Dogmatikon of the departing tone).

### At Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Current Kathisma.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 6 stichera: Forefeast—3, and saint—3; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast (or **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast).
   * *If two saints occur:* First saint—3, second—3; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast[^167].
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Forefeast—3, saint—3[^168].
   * *(On Friday evening: Dogmatikon of the departing tone).*
3. **Aposticha:** Stichera of the Forefeast; **Glory:** to the saint (if appointed), **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
4. **Troparia:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast.
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the first; **Glory:** of the second, **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
5. **Dismissal:** Middle Dismissal with the commemoration of the day and the saint[^169].

### At Compline & Midnight Office

* **Compline:** Canon to the Theotokos of the *Octoechos*. After *“It is truly meet”*: Kontakion of the Forefeast alone[^170].
* **Midnight Office:** Daily order from the *Horologion*; nothing to the saint.

### At Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion of the Forefeast twice; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the Forefeast once, first saint once; **Glory:** second saint once, **Both now:** of the Forefeast[^171].
2. **Kathismata:** Two current Kathismata with Sessional Hymns of the Forefeast.
3. **Canons (2 canons on 12):**
   * *Distribution:* Forefeast with Heirmos on 8, and saint on 4[^172].
   * *Katavasia:* After Odes 3, 6, 8, 9 (Heirmos of the saint)[^173].
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion, Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the Forefeast.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Exaposteilarion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast[^174].
   * *If two saints occur (3 canons on 14):* Forefeast with Heirmos on 6, first saint on 4[^175], second on 4. Katavasia after Odes 3, 6, 8, 9 (Heirmos of the second saint)[^176]. After 3rd Ode: Kontakia/Ikoses of both saints[^177], Sessional Hymn of the first; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast. After 6th Ode: Kontakion-Ikos of the Forefeast. After 9th Ode: Exaposteilarion of first saint; **Glory:** of second, **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Forefeast on 6, saint on 6[^178].
4. **Aposticha:** Stichera of the Forefeast; **Glory:** to the saint (if appointed), **Both now:** of the Forefeast.
5. **Conclusion:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Forefeast.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Troparion of the Forefeast; at 3rd and 9th Hours: **Glory:** to the saint. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Forefeast; 3rd and 9th—to the saint[^179].

### At the Divine Liturgy

* Troparion of the Forefeast; **Glory, Both now:** Kontakion of the Forefeast; everything else of the weekday[^180].

## 2.10 Feast of the Lord on a Sunday and Weekdays

> **Note:** The entire service is dedicated exclusively to the Feast, celebrated with an All-Night Vigil.

### At Small Vespers

* **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 4 festal stichera.
* **Aposticha:** Festal stichera with their proper refrains.
* **Troparia:** Troparion of the Feast once.

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Omitted on weekdays. On Saturday evening for Sunday: *“Blessed is the man”* (all three antiphons); on Sunday evening for Monday: 1st antiphon.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 8 stichera of the Feast; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Daily Prokimenon (if on Friday for Saturday, the Great Prokimenon is taken)[^181], followed by 3 festal Old Testament readings.
4. **Litiya & Aposticha:** All stichera of the Feast.
5. **Blessing of Loaves:** Troparion of the Feast three times.

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion of the Feast twice; **Glory, Both now:** once more.
2. **Kathismata & Polyeleos:** Two current Kathismata with festal Sessional Hymns. Polyeleos with Magnification and post-Polyeleos Sessional Hymn of the Feast.
3. **Graduals to Gospel:** Gradual of Tone 4 (1st antiphon); Prokimenon, Gospel, and Sticheron of the Feast.
4. **Canons (1 or 2 canons on 12):**
   * *Distribution:* Troparia on 12, Heirmoi twice.
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Festal Sessional Hymn.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Festal Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *At the 9th Ode:* *“More honorable”* is omitted; proper festal refrains are sung with the Heirmoi and troparia.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Festal Exaposteilarion.
5. **Praises (Lauds):** 4 festal stichera; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
6. **After the Great Doxology:** Troparion of the Feast.
7. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with proper festal commemoration.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Troparion and Kontakion of the Feast.

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons & Entrance:** Festal Antiphons and proper Festal Entrance Hymn[^182].
2. **Trisagion Substitution:** On the Exaltation of the Holy Cross and Sunday of the Cross: *“Before Thy Cross”*; on Nativity, Theophany, Lazarus Saturday, Great Saturday, Bright Week, and Pentecost: *“As many as have been baptized into Christ”*[^183].
3. **Hymn to the Theotokos (Zadostoinyk):** Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the Festal Canon with its proper refrain instead of *“It is truly meet”*.
4. **Readings & Prokimena:** All of the Feast.

## 2.11 Feast of the Theotokos on a Sunday

> **Note:** The festal service is combined with the Sunday *Octoechos*, which takes first place.

### At Small Vespers

1. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 4 Sunday stichera; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
2. **Aposticha:** 1 Sunday sticheron and 3 festal from Great Vespers[^184]; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast[^185].
3. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** *“Blessed is the man”* (all three antiphons).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: Sunday *Octoechos*—4, Feast—6; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast[^186].
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Sunday Prokimenon, followed by 3 festal readings.
4. **Litiya & Aposticha:** Litiya all of the Feast; Aposticha Sunday; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
5. **Blessing of Loaves:** Troparion of the Feast three times.

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
2. **Kathismata & Polyeleos:** Two current Kathismata with Sunday Sessional Hymns. Polyeleos with Magnification, *“The Angelic Host”*, Hypakoe of the tone, and all three festal Sessional Hymns[^187].
3. **Graduals to Gospel:** Gradual of the tone, *“Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ”*, and everything up to the Canon of the Feast[^188].
4. **Canons (3 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Sunday *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 4, *Octoechos* Theotokos on 2, and Feast on 8 (both canons without Heirmos)[^189].
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Sunday Kontakion-Ikos and festal Sessional Hymn (on Dormition: festal Hypakoe).
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Festal Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *At the 9th Ode:* *“More honorable than the Cherubim”*[^190].
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
5. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 stichera: 4 Sunday and 4 festal with two festal refrains; **Glory:** of the Feast, **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”*.
6. **After the Great Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion.
7. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with Sunday and festal commemorations; **Glory, Both now:** Gospel Sticheron[^191].

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Sunday troparion; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Sunday; 3rd and 9th—Feast.

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons & Entrance:** Sunday Antiphons. On *“Come, let us worship”*, if you wish, to *“Save us, O Son of God”* add the refrain: *“Through the prayers of the Theotokos, who sing to Thee, Alleluia”*[^192].
2. **Troparia & Kontakia:** Sunday Troparion and of the Feast; **Glory:** Sunday Kontakion, **Both now:** Kontakion of the Feast.
3. **Readings & Prokimena:** Trisagion; Prokimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Hymn—first of the Sunday, then of the Feast.
4. **Hymn to the Theotokos:** Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the Festal Canon with its refrain instead of *“It is truly meet”*[^193].

## 2.12 Feast of the Theotokos on Weekdays

* Everything proceeds according to the Typikon of a **Feast of the Lord** (Section 2.10), with these exceptions:
  * Antiphons are daily.
  * Entrance Hymn is *“Come, let us worship”*.
  * The **Trisagion** is always sung.
  * Hymn to the Theotokos is the Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the Festal Canon.

> **General Principles of the Afterfeast:**
> In all Afterfeasts:
> 1. Troparion and Kontakion of the Feast are sung daily.
> 2. The Feast is commemorated at every dismissal.
> 3. At Liturgy in an Afterfeast of the Lord: 1st and 2nd Antiphons of the Feast[^194], 3rd Antiphon daily with festal refrain; festal Entrance refrain[^195].
> 4. Prokimena and Communion Hymns on Sunday are of Sunday and the Feast; on weekdays, of the Feast alone; Epistle and Gospel of the day[^196].
> 5. *“It is truly meet”* is replaced by the Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the Festal Canon.

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
2. **Canons (4 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Sunday *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 4, Theotokos on 2, Feast on 4, and saint on 4. (If saint on 6, he is taken on 4).
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion-Ikos of the Feast and of the saint; **Glory:** Sessional Hymn of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *At the 9th Ode:* *“More honorable than the Cherubim”*.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* *“Holy is the Lord our God”*, Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
3. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 stichera: 4 Sunday and 4 of the Feast with its refrains (if saint has Praises, 4 to the saint instead)[^198]; **Glory:** Gospel Sticheron, **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”*.
4. **After the Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion.
5. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal, as at Vespers.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Sunday troparion; **Glory:** at 1st and 6th of the Feast, at 3rd and 9th of the saint. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Feast; 3rd and 9th—Sunday[^199].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons:** In Afterfeast of the Lord—1st and 2nd of the Feast, 3rd with festal refrain[^200]; in Afterfeast of Theotokos—Sunday Antiphons.
2. **Troparia & Kontakia:** Sunday Troparion and of the Feast; **Glory:** Sunday Kontakion, **Both now:** Kontakion of the Feast; Trisagion[^201].
3. **Readings & Prokimena:** All of the Sunday[^202].

## 2.14 Afterfeast with a Saint without a Polyeleos on Weekdays and Saturday

### At Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Current Kathisma.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 6 stichera: Feast—3, saint—3; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast (or **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast).
   * *If two saints occur:* First—3, second—3; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Stichera to him remain 3[^203].
   * *(On Friday evening: Dogmatikon of the departing tone)*[^204].
3. **Aposticha:** Stichera of the Feast; **Glory:** to the saint (if appointed), **Both now:** of the Feast.
4. **Troparia:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
5. **Dismissal:** Middle Dismissal with the commemoration of the Feast and the saint.

### At Compline & Midnight Office

* **Compline:** Canon to the Theotokos of the *Octoechos*. After *“It is truly meet”*: Kontakion of the Feast alone.
* **Midnight Office:** Daily order from the *Horologion*.

### At Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion of the Feast twice; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
   * *If two saints occur:* Troparion of the Feast once, first saint once; **Glory:** second saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
2. **Kathismata:** Two current Kathismata with festal Sessional Hymns.
3. **Canons (2 canons on 12):**
   * *Distribution:* Feast with Heirmos on 8, saint on 4.
   * *Katavasia:* After Odes 3, 6, 8, 9 (Heirmos of the saint).
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion, Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Kontakion and Ikos of the Feast.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Exaposteilarion of the saint twice; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast[^205].
   * *If two saints occur (3 canons on 14):* Feast on 6, first saint on 4, second on 4. Katavasia after Odes 3, 6, 8, 9 (Heirmos of second saint).
   * *If a saint on 6 occurs:* Feast on 6, saint on 6[^206].
4. **Aposticha:** Stichera of the Feast; **Glory:** to the saint (if appointed), **Both now:** of the Feast[^207].
5. **Conclusion:** Troparion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Troparion of the Feast; at 3rd and 9th Hours: **Glory:** to the saint. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Feast; 3rd and 9th—saint[^208].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons:** Afterfeast of the Lord—festal antiphons; Afterfeast of Theotokos—daily antiphons.
2. **Entrance:** Troparion of the Feast; **Glory, Both now:** Kontakion of the Feast; Trisagion.
3. **Readings & Prokimena:** Prokimenon, Alleluia, and Communion Hymn of the Feast; Epistle and Gospel of the weekday. Hymn to the Theotokos: Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the Festal Canon[^209].

## 2.15 Afterfeast with a Saint with a Polyeleos on a Sunday

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Kathisma 1 (*“Blessed is the man”*)—entire.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: Sunday *Octoechos*—3, Feast—3, saint—4; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the current tone[^210].
3. **Prokimenon & Readings:** Sunday Prokimenon *“The Lord is King”*, followed by 3 readings to the saint.
4. **Aposticha:** Sunday stichera; **Glory:** to the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
5. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with Sunday and saint commemorations[^211].

### At Compline & Midnight Office

* **Compline:** Canon to the Theotokos from the *Octoechos*. After *“It is truly meet”*: Kontakion of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** Kontakion of the Feast[^212].
* **Midnight Office:** Sunday Trinitarian Canon from the *Octoechos*.

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
2. **Kathismata & Polyeleos:** Two current Kathismata with Sunday Sessional Hymns. Polyeleos with Magnification and *“The Angelic Host”*, Hypakoe of the tone, and all three Sessional Hymns of the saint; **Glory:** 3rd Sessional Hymn, **Both now:** of the Feast.
3. **Canons (3 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Sunday with Heirmos on 4, Feast on 4, saint on 6.
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion-Ikos of the Feast and of the saint, Sessional Hymn of the saint; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos.
   * *At the 9th Ode:* *“More honorable than the Cherubim”*[^213].
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
4. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 stichera: 4 Sunday and 4 to the saint with his refrains; **Glory:** Gospel Sticheron, **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”*.
5. **After the Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion.
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with Sunday and saint commemorations.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Sunday troparion; **Glory:** at 1st and 6th of the Feast, at 3rd and 9th of the saint[^214]. Kontakia rotate: 1st and 9th—Sunday; 3rd—Feast; 6th—saint[^215].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons:** Afterfeast of the Lord—festal antiphons; Afterfeast of Theotokos—Sunday antiphons.
2. **Entrance:** Sunday Troparion, of the Feast, and of the saint; Sunday Kontakion; **Glory:** of the saint, **Both now:** of the Feast.
3. **Readings & Prokimena:** Prokimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, and Gospel—of the Sunday and to the saint[^216].

## 2.19 Apodosis of a Feast on a Sunday

> **Notes:**
> 1. The service of the daily saint falling on an Apodosis is transferred to the preceding[^228] or following day[^229], or sung at Compline.
> 2. On a Sunday, Vespers and Matins take the Great form on account of the Sunday service.

### At Great Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** *“Blessed is the man”* (all three antiphons).
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 10 stichera: Sunday *Octoechos*—4, Feast—6; **Glory:** of the Feast, **Both now:** 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the tone.
3. **Prokimenon:** Sunday Prokimenon *“The Lord is King”*.
4. **Aposticha:** Sunday stichera; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
5. **Troparia:** Sunday troparion; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with Sunday and feast commemorations[^230].

### At Compline & Midnight Office

* **Compline:** Canon to the Theotokos from the *Octoechos*. After *“It is truly meet”*: Kontakion of the Feast alone[^231].
* **Midnight Office:** Sunday Trinitarian Canon. (On Apodosis of Mid-Pentecost or Pascha: Troparion and Kontakion of the Feast after Trisagia).

### At Great Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Sunday troparion twice; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
2. **Kathismata to Gospel:** All of the Sunday, as usual.
3. **Canons (3 or 4 canons on 14):**
   * *Distribution:* Sunday *Octoechos* with Heirmos on 4, Theotokos on 2, and Feast on 8 (one or two canons).
   * *Katavasia:* Seasonal Katavasia after every ode.
   * *After the 3rd Ode:* Kontakion, Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the Feast.
   * *After the 6th Ode:* Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos[^232].
   * *At the 9th Ode:* *“More honorable than the Cherubim”* with festal refrains to the festal canon.
   * *After the 9th Ode:* Sunday Exaposteilarion; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast.
4. **Praises (Lauds):** 8 stichera: 4 Sunday and 4 of the Feast with "**Glory:**"[^233] and two festal refrains; **Glory:** Gospel Sticheron, **Both now:** *“Most Blessed Art Thou”*.
5. **After the Doxology:** Sunday Resurrection Troparion.
6. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with Sunday and festal commemorations.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Sunday troparion; **Glory:** of the Feast. Kontakia alternate: 1st and 6th—Sunday; 3rd and 9th—Feast[^234].

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons:** Apodosis of the Lord—festal antiphons; Apodosis of Theotokos—Sunday antiphons.
2. **Entrance:** Sunday Troparion and of the Feast; **Glory:** Sunday Kontakion, **Both now:** Kontakion of the Feast[^235].
3. **Readings & Prokimena:** Prokimenon, Alleluia, and Communion Hymn of Sunday and the Feast. Epistle and Gospel on Apodosis of the Lord: Sunday alone[^236]; on Apodosis of Theotokos: first of Sunday, then of the Feast[^237].

## 2.20 Apodosis of a Feast on Weekdays

### At Vespers

1. **Kathisma:** Current Kathisma.
2. **On *"Lord, I have cried"*:* 6 stichera, all of the Feast; **Glory, Both now:** of the Feast. (On Friday evening: Dogmatikon of departing tone)[^238][^239].
3. **Prokimenon:** Daily Prokimenon from the *Horologion* (no Entrance, no readings).
4. **Aposticha:** All stichera of the Feast from Great Vespers.
5. **Troparia:** Troparion of the Feast once.
6. **Dismissal:** Middle Dismissal with the commemoration of the Feast and the daily saint[^240].

### At Compline & Midnight Office

* **Compline:** Canon to the Theotokos from the *Octoechos*. After *“It is truly meet”*: Kontakion of the Feast once.
* **Midnight Office:** Daily order from the *Horologion*.

### At Matins

1. **On *"God is the Lord"*:* Troparion of the Feast twice; **Glory, Both now:** once more.
2. **Kathismata:** Two current Kathismata with festal Sessional Hymns, Psalm 50, and Canon.
3. **Canons to End of Matins:** Everything proceeds exactly as on the Feast itself. At the 9th Ode: *“More honorable”* is omitted; proper festal refrains are sung.
4. **Dismissal:** Great Dismissal with the commemoration of the Feast and the daily saint.

### At the Hours

* At all Hours: Troparion and Kontakion of the Feast alone, as on the Feast itself.

### At the Divine Liturgy

1. **Antiphons:** Apodosis of the Lord—festal antiphons; Apodosis of Theotokos—daily antiphons.
2. **Entrance:** Troparion; **Glory, Both now:** Kontakion of the Feast; Trisagion.
3. **Prokimenon, Alleluia & Communion Hymn:** All of the Feast.
4. **Readings:** On Apodosis of the Lord—Epistle and Gospel of the weekday alone; on Apodosis of Theotokos—first of the weekday, then of the Feast.
