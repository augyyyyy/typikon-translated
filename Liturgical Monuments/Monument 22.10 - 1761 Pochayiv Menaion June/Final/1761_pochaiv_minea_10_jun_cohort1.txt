# MONTH OF JUNE
*Having 30 Days.*

*On the 1st Day: The Holy Martyr Justin the Philosopher; and Another Martyr Justin, and Those with Them:*
*Alleluia, or Troparion.*[^5]
*At "Lord, I have cried," if we sing [the service], we sing 3 stichera of the Theotokos:*
*Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Егда̀ ѿ дре́ва):*
*And 3 of the Saint, Tone 2, Podoben the same (Подо́бенъ то́йже):*

When the ice of ignorance held fast all creation through the assaults of the enemy, and the multitude of idols was served, then, O glorious martyrs, with a fervent heart and with the warmth of divine faith ye brought this to an end, lovingly shedding your blood for Him Who openly poured out His blood upon the Cross.

When by divine beckoning ye separated yourselves for supernatural struggles, having cast off the corruptible body, then, being strengthened by the power of the Almighty, ye were not terrified of fire, nor of the cutting sword. Therefore, having bowed your necks unto God, O blessed ones, ye received your end with joy.

John the valiant sufferer, Valerian, and Chariton, and Charita with them, and divine Justin, Euelpistus, and glorious Hierax, having dyed their garment from their divine blood, and being clothed together, stand before the King of all in the heavens, before Christ the King with the Angels.

*Other stichera of the Theotokos, Tone and Podoben the same (Гла́съ и҆ подо́бенъ то́йже):*

Rejoice, star radiant as the sun; rejoice, all-immaculate cause of all good things! Rejoice, thou who didst contain the uncontainable God, who didst sprout forth the ear of immortality; rejoice, divine palanquin and all-radiant portal!

=== LEAF p4 === (Fol. 1v)
All-radiant portal! Rejoice, slaying of the ancestral curse, O Maiden, who art the mediatress of good things!

O Joy of all that sorrow, and protectress of the wronged, and nourisher of the poor, consolation of travelers, and staff of the blind, visitation of the sick, shelter and patroness of them that labor, and helper of orphans, Mother of God the Most High: thou art all-pure; hasten, we pray thee, that thy servants may be saved.

A protection and defense art thou unto us Christians, and a mighty refuge, O Mother of God the Most High. But now as always, through thy vigilant intercession, cease not to pray that thy servants may be saved from all tribulations; for next to God, all we Christians have thee as defense and refuge.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
No other mighty refuge, nor tower of strength, nor unassailable wall have we truly gained save thee, O all-pure one. Unto thee do we flee, and unto thee do we cry aloud: O Lady, help, lest we perish! Show forth thy grace upon us, and the glory of thy strength, and the majesty of thy tender mercy.

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Beholding Thee nailed unto the tree of the Cross, O Jesus, she that knew not wedlock weeping said: O sweet Child, why hast Thou left me alone who bare Thee, O inaccessible Light of the unoriginate Father? Hasten and be glorified, that they who glorify Thy divine passions may receive divine glory!

*At the Aposticha, from the Octoechos.*
*Troparion, Tone 4:*
Thy martyrs, O Lord, in their sufferings received incorruptible crowns from Thee, our God; for having Thy strength, they cast down the tyrants and shattered the powerless insolence of demons: through their supplications save our souls!

*Canon, Tone 2.*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*Come, O ye peoples, let us sing a song unto Christ God, Who divided the sea and guided the people whom He brought out of Egyptian bondage, for He hath been glorified.*

Adorned with the splendors of martyrdom, O martyrs, ye stand before the pure throne of the Master of all, granting the remission of our sins.

The pious champions of truth, having stepped forward with one mind before the tribunal of the tyrants, cast down the deception of the devil

=== LEAF p5 === (Fol. 2r)
by spiritual power.

By wounds, and stripes, and tribulations, the divinely called great-martyrs purchased the kingdom of Christ, wherein they now dwell crowned.

*Theotokion:*
Having seen the creation of Thy hands alienated, O Thou Who art rich in God's mercy, Thou didst take up Thy dwelling in the womb of a Virgin who knew not man, and hast shown her unto us as a protectress.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*Establish us in Thee, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put sin to death; and plant Thy fear in the hearts of us who sing praise unto Thee.*

Bearing the foundation of Christ in thy heart, O Justin, thou didst manfully oppose those who interrogated thee, who most unlawfully commanded thee to break the law.

A divinely bestowed choir, a God-chosen army of holy martyrs, a holy assembly gathered today, hath conquered the bodiless foes together with the body.

Having given over their bodies unto stripes, the magnanimous ones rejoiced, foreseeing eternal enjoyment and future glory and brightness.

*Theotokion:*
Dispel the clouds of my soul, O light cloud of the Sun of glory, and with light guide my mind, which is darkened by the blackness of malice.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5, Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́лное сло́во):*
Justin, and Chariton, Peon, Euelpistus, Charita, Hierax, and Valerian: radiant luminaries who illumine the ends of the earth with the rays of their struggles, let them be praised with a loud voice in faith, as they pray unto Christ that we may be saved.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Fervent protectress and invincible, certain and unashamed hope, wall, protection, and haven of them that flee unto thee, O pure Ever-Virgin: with the Angels pray thou unto thy Son and God to grant peace unto the world, and salvation, and great mercy.

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Beholding Thee hanging willingly upon the Cross between thieves, O Christ, Thy Mother, maternally torn in her inward parts, said: O sinless Son, how art Thou unjustly nailed unto the Cross like an evildoer, wishing as the Tender-hearted One to bring the human race to life?

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
*I have heard, O Lord, the report of Thy dispensation, and I have glorified Thee, O only Lover of mankind.*

At the suffering of Justin, and Chariton, and Charita, and Peon, the angelic ranks were amazed.

=== LEAF p6 === (Fol. 2v)
Their bodies being shattered by wounds mediated divine blessedness for their souls.

Like spiritual coals ye burned up deceit with divine fire, and were revealed, O martyrs, as luminaries of the world.

At the courage of Euelpistus and Hierax, unbending martyrs, the princes of darkness were struck with fear.

*Theotokion:*
O Virgin who in thy womb didst receive the fire of the Godhead, deliver me from fire and torment!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
*O Giver of light and Creator of the ages, O Lord, guide us in the light of Thy commandments; for beside Thee we know no other God.*

Filled with the Divine Spirit, O blessed martyr Justin, having suffered valiantly, thou didst drown the fierce spirits of malice in the streams of thy blood.

She who is named after grace was filled with divine grace; and forgetting woman's natural weakness, she trampled down the foe lawfully and received glory.[^6]

Having endowed thy soul with grace through sacred confessions, thou didst blunt the malice of the ancient enemy, O sufferer Chariton, precious ornament of divine passion-bearers.

*Theotokion:*
O pure one, who didst bear Christ the Savior, Who likened Himself unto earthly men through the surpassing multitude of the riches of mercy: pray unto Him as God to save thy servants.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
*Wallowing in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy tender mercy: bring me up from corruption, O God!*

Let us all glorify the most radiant memory of Justin, and Chariton, Peon, and Valerian, and Charita, and Hierax, and Euelpistus!

Without tempest, through divine governance, ye passed through the deep sea of torments, O martyrs, and reached the tranquil haven of the Kingdom of Christ.

Shining like the stars of heaven, by the precious confirmation of the Church, ye enlighten the whole universe with the radiance of your suffering.

*Theotokion:*
Deliver me from immeasurable hard-heartedness, me who am held fast by many sins, O Lady Mother of God, and who am liable unto Gehenna's fire!

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
With the wisdom of thy divine words, O Justin, the whole Church of God, having adorned herself, enlightens the world with the brightness of thy life; and having received a crown for the outpouring of thy blood, standing with the Angels before Christ: pray unceasingly for all

of us!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
*The wise Children did not serve the golden image, but entered into the flame themselves, and derided their gods; amid the flame they cried aloud, and an Angel bedewed them: The prayer of your lips hath already been heard.*

With the river of your blood ye dried up the depth of deceit, and poured forth streams of miracles, O martyrs, quenching the coals of fierce passions for them that ever cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

With the fire of divine love, O wise ones, ye valiantly burned up the matter of malice; and having shone forth like fire, ye piously enlightened the hearts of all who cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

Adorned with divine wisdom, wise Justin made foolish the pagan wisdom by grace, having confirmed [the faithful] to worship the Trinity and to cry aloud in an Orthodox manner: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*
O pure Virgin, who knewest not wedlock, pure and blessed one, restoration of the fallen, forgiveness of sinners: save me, the prodigal, save me as I cry aloud unto thy Son: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
*Praise ye Him as Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages: God Who descended into the fiery furnace unto the Hebrew Children, and turned the flame into dew.*

Having been patiently tried in torments as in a fiery furnace, O passion-bearers of the Lord, ye truly shone forth divinely brighter than gold, crying aloud: We exalt Christ above all unto all ages!

With wise Justin let Charita, and Chariton, and Peon, and great Hierax be praised, together with Valerian, who through suffering drove away deceit and were united unto the angelic ranks.

Being led unto eternal life, O wise ones, with valiant wisdom ye all bowed your necks unto the sword in unanimity of soul, O martyrs, having offered yourselves as sacrifices unto God.

*Theotokion:*
Being the wellspring of life, in that thou didst bear the Water of Life, O Virgin Mother of God, give drink unto my soul, which is corrupted by the flame of sin, that I may glorify thee unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
*God, God the Word, Who came by unutterable wisdom to renew Adam, who had fallen grievously into corruption through eating: from the Holy*

Virgin, Who was ineffably incarnate for our sake: Him do we, the faithful, magnify with songs in one mind.

Shining with divine glory, O valiant sufferers, ye broke the chill of deceit, and became partakers of the sacred passions of Him Who took flesh for our sake, and with Him ye rejoice eternally in the heavens.

At the wisdom of Justin, and the courage of Charita, and truly at the boldness of Chariton, at the valiant struggles of Peon and Hierax, and at the great endurance of Valerian amid fierce torments, even the Angels marveled.

As upon a divine chariot having mounted, O glorious ones, ye joyfully ascended toward it by your blood; and from the life-bearing hand, O wise ones, ye received victorious crowns, unceasingly praying for us.

*Theotokion:*
Having borne without man the Word beyond word, O pure Virgin who knewest not wedlock: deliver us from assaults, and from all tribulations, and from soul-corrupting harm, and from passions, and from the everlasting flame and condemnation!

*[Decorative Headpiece]*

*In the Same Month, on the 2nd Day: Our Father among the Saints, Nikephoros, Patriarch of Constantinople, the Confessor:*
*Stichera, Tone 4, Podoben: "As one valiant among martyrs" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́бля):*[^7]

As the setting-up of truth, the confirmation of the faith, and the proclaimer of dogmas do we know thee, the harmonizer of piety, dwelling of purity, and chosen receptacle, fragrance of the Spirit, and surpassing great treasure of teachings, and foundation of Christ's Church, O divinely inspired sacred luminary! We praise thee, the seal of teachers and divine image, the mystagogue of Christ, O blessed bishop, and the God-flowing river of understandings that wisely drowns the thoughts of the lawless and of blasphemers!

Successor of the Apostles, of equal character with the sufferers, equal in zeal with the ascetics: having multiplied the talent of wisdom, O exceedingly rich one, thou wast accounted worthy of the joy of thy Lord, adorned with the grace of divine radiance and mentally with the beam

=== LEAF p9 === (Fol. 4r)
of spiritual brightness, and now thou standest before the life-bearing right hand, ever illumined thence with rays, O glorious one!

*Other Stichera, Tone 8, Podoben: "O most marvelous wonder" (Ѡ пресла́внагѡ чꙋдесѐ):*

Into the inner divine sanctuary of the unwaning light, of the true Tabernacle which the Lord pitched,[^8] as a lawful high priest thou didst enter, O venerable father, not with the blood of another, but with thine own, imitating Christ; thou didst not submit to the conspiracy of torment, by which thou didst please God unto the end, O father!

Adorned with word and deed, and with that sacred vesture, and with the rectitude of teaching, O all-wise Nikephoros, fosterer of wisdom: through confession of these things thou didst accept exile, and being examined and tried, thou didst endure fervently with strength of mind all tribulations that befell thee, O blessed one!

An instrument of the Spirit wast thou revealed to be, addressed from above with divinely inspired voices, proclaiming the ineffable mystery of the Savior and His divine, true, and distinguished truths, loudly preaching unto us the Incarnation of the Word, which is beyond comprehension and higher than all understanding and thought, O most honorable one!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Save me, O all-pure Lady, who didst ineffably bear Christ the Savior; for thee alone have I gained as protectress, and unassailable wall, and shelter, and rejoicing, and the divine consolation of my soul. Deliver me, therefore, from the unsleeping worm and from the everlasting fire, O Mother of Christ God!

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Beholding Thee nailed unto the Cross, O Jesus, and accepting passions willingly, the Virgin and Thy Mother, O Master, cried aloud: Woe is me! O sweet Child, how dost Thou endure wounds unjustly, O Physician Who didst heal human infirmity and didst deliver all from corruption by Thy tender mercy?

*Troparion, Tone 4:*
A rule of faith and image of meekness, a teacher of abstinence hath the truth of things shown thee unto thy flock; therefore through humility hast thou acquired lofty things, and through poverty rich things: O father Nikephoros, pray unto Christ God that our souls may be saved![^9]

*Tone 4, Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*The Red Sea's deep abyss with unwetted feet did Israel of old traverse dryshod, and by Moses' arms outstretched in the form of a Cross*

vanquished the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

Action hast thou shown to be the stepping-stone unto contemplation; for having harmoniously attuned thy soul through active virtue, O exceedingly rich Nikephoros, thou didst soar aloft unto the supreme, preeminent contemplation.

Having put to death earthly thoughts through abstinence, and having directed thy soul with diligence, O divinely inspired Nikephoros, thou hast reached the havens of stormless enjoyment.

Having sustained the heresy of Mammon, the accursed lion Leo,[^11] dashed himself against thee, the firm rock, O God-receiver Nikephoros, and was swiftly shattered near Hades, the all-senseless tyrant.

*Theotokion:*
Heal the brokenness of mankind, O all-immaculate Virgin, who didst bear the Creator Who raised all of us who were cast down by the fall of our first parents, O Bride of God!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*Not in wisdom, nor in power, nor in riches do we boast, but in Thee, the hypostatic Wisdom of the Father, O Christ: for there is none holy beside Thee, O Lover of mankind.*

Having adorned thy life with thy virtues, and having visibly become the keeper of the luminary of the word of life, thou didst guide Christ's all-holy flock.

Thy God-speaking, fiery, divine tongue burned up thorny heresies with the spiritual fire, O divinely wise, glorious father!

Having searched out the depth of wisdom through the love of wisdom, with the cords of thy teachings thou hast clearly drawn in them that rejected the image of Christ, O right marvelous one!

*Theotokion:*
Confessing the Son, Who was born in the last days from thy womb, O all-pure one, to be co-beginningless with the eternal Father, we truly glorify thee as the Mother of God.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Of Wisdom" (Прему́дрости):*
The grace of thy wise words and the height of thy mystical teachings have become for us a divine ladder of ascent; for thou didst gain a spiritual trumpet, O father, and through it didst preach divine dogmas. Therefore hast thou also settled in a place of pasture, having driven away the beast with the sling of thy words, O blessed Nikephoros: pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto them that lovingly honor thy all-honorable memory!
