# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 10: June (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-10-june-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

*[Typographical decorative border]*

**BOOK OF THE MENAION**  
**MONTH OF JUNE**

To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; under the reign of the Great
KING AUGUSTUS III,[^1]
By the blessing of His Excellency, the Lord
SYLVESTER LUBIENIECKI-RUDNITSKY,[^2]
Exarch of the Metropolia of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the good pleasure of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk
HYPATIUS BILIANSKY,[^3]
Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; through the diligence and at the expense of the monks of the same Order in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv,
ISSUED IN PRINT.
In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers: 7269;
And from the Nativity of Christ: 1761.[^4]

*(Vacat — Fol. 1v: Unnumbered blank verso) [Woodcut Headpiece: The Holy Martyr Justin the Philosopher]*

### Notes

[^1]: Augustus III of Poland (Frederick Augustus II of Saxony), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania (1733–1763).
[^2]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Rudnycki-Lubieniecki), OSBM, Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh (1756–1777), and Exarch of the Metropolitan See of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus'.
[^3]: Hypatius (Ipatij) Biliansky (Bilynsky), OSBM, Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great from 1759 to 1771.
[^4]: The year of creation 7269 in the Byzantine era corresponds to 1761 A.D. (September year calculation: 7269 - 5508 = 1761).
