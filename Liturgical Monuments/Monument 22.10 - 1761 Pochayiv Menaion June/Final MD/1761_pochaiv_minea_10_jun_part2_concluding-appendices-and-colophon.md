# CONCLUDING APPENDICES AND COLOPHON

*Resurrectional Theotokia for the Eight Tones:*

*Tone 1. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^963][^962]
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of all the world, who budded forth from men and gave birth unto the Master, the gate of heaven, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful! For she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the barrier of enmity, ushered in peace and opened the Kingdom. Having her therefore as the confirmation of faith, we have the Lord Who was born of her as our Defender. Take courage therefore, take courage, O people of God, for He will conquer the enemies, as He is all-powerful!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^964]
Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and after childbearing thou didst remain as thou wast before giving birth; for God was the One born, therefore He also renewed the course of nature. But O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple: but as carrying the Compassionate One in thine arms, have mercy upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 2. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^965]
The shadow of the Law passed away when Grace arrived: for as the bush was not consumed when wrapped in flame, so as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and a Virgin thou didst remain. Instead of a pillar of fire, the righteous Sun shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^966]
O new wonder of all ancient wonders! For who has known a Mother who gave birth without a husband, and carried in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? The One born is the counsel of God! Whom as an infant having carried in thine arms, O all-pure one, and possessing maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to pray for those who honor thee, that He may have compassion and save our souls!

*Tone 3. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^967]
How shall we not marvel at thy God-man childbearing, O most honored one? For having received no temptation of a man, O all-immaculate one, thou didst give birth in the flesh without a father unto the Son, Who was born of the Father without a mother before the ages; Who suffered no change, nor mingling, nor division, but preserved entire the property of both natures. Therefore, O Mother, Virgin, Sovereign Lady, pray Him to save the souls of those who in orthodox manner confess thee as the Birthgiver of God!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^968]
Without seed from the Holy Spirit, but by the Father's will, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who exists before the ages from the Father without a mother; and Who for our sake became of thee without a father; thou didst give birth unto Him in the flesh, and didst nurse Him as an infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray that our souls may be delivered from dangers!

*Tone 4. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^969]
David the Prophet, the forefather of God, melodiously proclaimed beforehand concerning thee unto Him Who has done great things for thee:

"The Queen stood at Thy right hand."[^970] For God, Who was pleased to become man of thee without a father, revealed thee as the Mother and mediatress of life, that He might renew His own image corrupted by passions; and having found the sheep gone astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His own will, unite it with the heavenly powers, and save the world, O Birthgiver of God, Christ Who has great and rich mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^971]
Look down upon the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, quenching the fierce uprisings against us, releasing us from every affliction! For thee alone do we have as a firm and sure confirmation, and we have acquired thy patronage, that we who call upon thee may not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady! Hasten unto the entreaty of those who faithfully cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, help of all, joy and protection, and salvation of our souls!

*Tone 5. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^972]
In the Red Sea an image of the unwedded Bride was inscribed of old: there Moses, the divider of the water; and here Gabriel, the minister of the wonder! Then the deep was traversed dryshod by Israel; and now Christ is born seedlessly of the Virgin! The sea remained impassable after the passage of Israel; the Immaculate one remained undefiled after the birth of Emmanuel! O Thou Who art, and art before all, and appeared as man: O God, have mercy upon us!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^973]
Thou art the temple and the gate, the palace and throne of the King, O all-honored Virgin, through whom my Redeemer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of Righteousness, wishing to enlighten those whom He created according to His own image with His own hand. Therefore, O all-hymned one, as having acquired maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 6. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^974]
Who will not call thee blessed, O all-holy Virgin? Who will not hymn thy most pure childbearing? For the Only-begotten Son, Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the same came forth from thee, pure one, ineffably incarnate: being God by nature, and having become man by nature for our sake; not divided into two persons, but known in two natures without confusion. Him do thou entreat, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may be shown mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^975]
My Creator and Redeemer, O all-pure one, Christ the Lord, coming forth from thy womb and clothing Himself in me, delivered Adam from the first curse. Therefore, O all-pure one, as the Mother of God and Virgin, in truth we cry aloud unto thee unceasingly with the angel: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, intercession and protection, and salvation of our souls!

*Tone 7. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^976]
Thou wast known as a Mother beyond nature, O Birthgiver of God, and thou didst remain a Virgin beyond word and understanding; and the tongue cannot tell the wonder of thy childbearing: for the conception was most glorious, O pure one, and the manner of childbearing is incomprehensible: for where God so wills, the order of nature is overcome! Therefore, knowing thee to be the Mother of God, we all earnestly pray thee: Entreat Him that our souls may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^977]
Fleeing beneath thy protection, O Sovereign Lady, all we earthborn cry aloud unto thee: O Birthgiver of God, our hope, deliver us from immeasurable transgressions, and save our souls!

*Tone 8. Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:*[^978]
The King of Heaven out of love for mankind appeared upon the earth, and dwelt with men: for having taken flesh from the pure Virgin, and having come forth from her with the assumption [thereof], He is one Son, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis. Therefore, truly proclaiming Him perfect God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God: Whom do thou entreat, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may be shown mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^979]
O unwedded Virgin, who ineffably conceived God in the flesh, Mother of the most high God: accept the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, granting unto all cleansing of transgressions; receiving now our supplications, pray that we all may be saved!

*Resurrectional Dismissal Theotokia of the Eight Tones:*[^1042]

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*[^1043]
When Gabriel announced unto thee: Rejoice! O Virgin, with the voice the Master of all was incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, even as righteous David said; thou wast shown forth more spacious than the heavens, having carried thy Creator! Glory to Him Who dwelt in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who delivered us through thy childbearing!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*[^1044]
All beyond understanding, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Birthgiver of God! In purity sealed and virginity preserved, thou wast known as a true Mother, having given birth unto the true God: Him do thou entreat, that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^1045]
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, do we hymn, O Virgin Birthgiver of God: for having assumed the Passion upon the Cross in the flesh taken from thee, Thy Son and our God delivered us from corruption, as He loves mankind!

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*[^1046]
The mystery hidden from all ages and unknown to angels was shown forth through thee, O Birthgiver of God, unto those upon earth: God incarnate in unconfused union, Who voluntarily accepted the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised up the first-fashioned man, He saved our souls from death!

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*[^1047]
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord; rejoice, wall and protection of those who have recourse unto thee; rejoice, haven safe from tempests and unwedded one, who didst bear thy Creator and God in the flesh: cease not to pray for those who hymn and worship thy childbearing!

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*[^1048]
Thou Who didst call Thy Mother blessed, didst come unto the Passion by voluntary choice, shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: Rejoice with Me, for the lost drachma is found! O our God, Who didst wisely order all things: Glory to Thee!

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*[^1049]
As the treasury of our resurrection, O all-hymned one, lead up from the pit and depth of transgressions those who place their trust in thee! For thou didst save those guilty of sin, having given birth unto our salvation: thou who wast a Virgin before childbearing, and a Virgin in childbearing, and after childbearing still remainest a Virgin!

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*[^1050]
O Good One, Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, Who by death didst overthrow death, and as God didst reveal the Resurrection: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thy hand; manifest Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; accept the Birthgiver of God who bore Thee, who prays for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people!

*If Alleluia should occur in any Tone whatsoever, these are said:*[^1065]
Apostles, martyrs, and prophets, hierarchs, venerable ones, and the righteous, who have accomplished the good fight and kept the faith, having boldness toward the Savior: entreat Him as good on our behalf, we pray, that our souls may be saved!

Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good, the souls of Thy servants, and forgive whatever sins they committed in this life; for no one is without sin, except Thee Who art able to grant rest also unto the departed.

*O holy Mother of the ineffable Light...*

*Tone 3. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*[^1066]
At the beauty of thy virginity and the most radiant splendor of thy purity Gabriel was amazed, and cried aloud unto thee, O Birthgiver of God: What worthy praise shall I offer thee? What shall I call thee? I am in perplexity and I am astonished! Therefore, even as I was commanded, I cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O full of grace!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1067]
Whithersoever anyone finds salvation, thither rightfully does he flee; and what other refuge is such as thou, O Birthgiver of God, sheltering our souls?

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1068]
Our refuge and strength, O Birthgiver of God, mighty help of the world: by thy prayers shelter thy servants from every necessity, O only blessed one!

*At the end of Matins:*
*Whithersoever anyone finds salvation...*

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1069]
Having acquired the Cross of thy Son as a rod of power, O Birthgiver of God, thereby do we cast down the insolence of enemies, unceasingly magnifying thee with love!

*At the end of Matins:*
*Our refuge and strength...*

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*[^1070]
The prophets preached, the Apostles taught, the martyrs confessed, and we believed thee to be truly the Birthgiver of God: therefore also do we magnify thine ineffable childbearing!

*At the end of Matins:*
*Whithersoever anyone finds salvation On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Having acquired the Cross of thy Son as a rod of power...*

*At the end of Matins:*
*Our refuge and strength, O Birthgiver of God...*

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*[^1071]
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, do we hymn, O Virgin Birthgiver of God: for having assumed the Passion upon the Cross in the flesh taken from thee, Thy Son and our God delivered us from corruption, as He loves mankind!

*At the end of Matins:*
*The prophets preached...*

*Tone 4. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*[^1072]
Unto her who was reared in the temple in the Holy of Holies, clothed with faith and wisdom and measureless virginity, Gabriel the Supreme Commander brought greeting from heaven, and the "Rejoice!": Rejoice, O blessed one; rejoice, O most glorified one, the Lord is with thee!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1073]
As thou art higher than all creations, we are at a loss to hymn thee worthily, O Birthgiver of God; therefore we pray thee: Have mercy upon us!

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1074]
Let us sinners and humble ones now earnestly flee unto the Birthgiver of God, and fall down in repentance, crying aloud from the depth of soul: O Sovereign Lady, help, having shown compassion upon us! Hasten, we perish from the multitude of transgressions; turn not thy servants away empty, for thee alone do we have as hope!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1075]
Thee do we magnify, O Birthgiver of God, crying aloud: Thou art the bush wherein without burning Moses beheld the flame of the fire of the Godhead!

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1076]
O all-immaculate Virgin, Mother of Christ God, a sword pierced through thy most holy soul when thou didst behold Thy Son and God voluntarily crucified: Whom do thou, O blessed one, not cease to entreat, to grant us forgiveness of transgressions!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1077]
Thee do we magnify, O Birthgiver of God, crying aloud: Thou art the mountain from which the stone was ineffably cut off and crushed the gates of Hades!

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*[^1078]
The Father's Word, Christ our God, Who was incarnate of thee, have we known, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, only pure, only blessed one: therefore unceasingly hymning thee do we magnify thee!

*At the end of Matins:*
*As thou art higher than all creations...*

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*O all-immaculate Virgin, Mother...*

*At the end of Matins:*
*Thee do we magnify, O Birthgiver of God, crying aloud: Thou art the mountain...*

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*[^1079]
The mystery hidden from all ages and unknown to angels was shown forth through thee, O Birthgiver of God, unto those upon earth: God incarnate in unconfused union, Who voluntarily accepted the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised up the first-fashioned man, He saved our souls from death!

*At the end of Matins:*
*Thee do we magnify, O Birthgiver of God, crying aloud: Thou art the bush...*

*Tone 5. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*[^1080]
With the angels the heavenly things, with men the earthly things, with the voice of gladness, O Birthgiver of God, do we cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O gate more spacious than the heavens! Rejoice, sole salvation of the earthborn! Rejoice, pure one full of grace, who didst bear God incarnate!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1081]
O Christ God, Who didst shine forth unto the world from the Virgin, showing forth sons of light through her: Have mercy upon us!

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1082]
Show forth thy speedy protection and help and mercy upon thy servants, and calm the waves of vain thoughts, O pure one, and raise up my fallen soul, O Birthgiver of God: for I know, I know, O Virgin, that thou canst do whatsoever thou desirest!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1083]
O most holy Mother of God, rampart of Christians: deliver thy people as is thy wont, who earnestly cry unto thee; oppose shameful and proud thoughts, that we may cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, Ever-virgin!

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1084]
By the Cross of thy Son, O woman graced of God, all idolatrous delusion was abolished, and the demonic might was trampled down; therefore we the faithful, as is meet, ever hymn and bless thee, and confessing thee to be truly the Birthgiver of God, we magnify thee!

*At the end of Matins:*
*O Christ God, Who didst shine forth...*

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*[^1085]
A strange mystery of the Virgin was shown saving unto the world: for from her Thou wast born without seed, and in the flesh Thou wast revealed without corruption, O Joy of all: Lord, glory to Thee!

*At the end of Matins:*
*O most holy Mother of God...*

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*By the Cross of thy Son...*

*At the end of Matins:*
*O Christ God, Who didst shine forth...*

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*[^1086]
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord; rejoice, wall and protection of those who have recourse unto thee; rejoice, haven safe from tempests and unwedded one, who didst bear thy Creator and God in the flesh: cease not to pray for those who hymn and worship thy childbearing!

*At the end of Matins:*
*O Christ God, Who didst shine forth...*

*Tone 6. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*[^1087]
The beginning of salvation was Gabriel's utterance unto the Virgin: for she heard the "Rejoice!", and did not flee from the greeting; she doubted not, as Sarah in the tent, but thus she spoke: Behold the handmaid of the Lord, be it unto me according to thy word!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1088]
Thou didst receive the archangelic word, and wast shown forth as the cherubic throne, and in thine arms didst carry the hope of our souls, O Birthgiver of God!

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1089]
Good Virgin Birthgiver of God, hope of the world: we ask thy sole and sovereign protection; have compassion upon a people without defense; entreat the merciful God that our souls may be delivered from every threatening, O only blessed one!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1090]
No one who has recourse unto thee departs from thee put to shame, O all-pure Virgin Birthgiver of God: but asks for grace and receives a gift according to his profitable petition!

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1091]
O Virgin Birthgiver of God, entreat Thy Son, Who was voluntarily nailed upon the Cross and delivered the world from delusion, Christ our God, to have mercy upon our souls!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1092]
Him Who before the ages was born of the Father without a mother, the Son and Word of God, in the last years thou didst bear without a husband, incarnate from thy pure blood, O Birthgiver of God: Whom do thou entreat, that forgiveness of sins may be granted unto us before the end!

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*[^1093]
O holy Sovereign Lady, pure Mother of our God, who didst ineffably give birth unto the Creator of all: together with the sacred Apostles ever entreat His goodness to deliver us from passions and grant us forgiveness of sins!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1094]
Of great gifts wast thou vouchsafed, O pure Virgin Mother of God: for thou didst bear in the flesh One of the Trinity, Christ the Giver of life, unto the salvation of our souls!

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*O Virgin Birthgiver of God, entreat Thy Son...*

*At the end of Matins:*
*Him Who before the ages was born of the Father without a mother...*

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*[^1095]
Gideon foretells the conception, and David tells of thy childbearing, O Birthgiver of God: for as rain upon the fleece the Word descended into thy womb, and without seed thou didst sprout forth the salvation of the world, Christ our God, O holy earth full of grace!

*At the end of Matins:*
*Him Who before the ages was born of the Father without a mother...*

*Tone 7. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*[^1096]
O immaculate Virgin Birthgiver of God: entreat Thy Son with the celestial powers that forgiveness of transgressions may be granted before the end unto us who faithfully glorify thee!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1097]
Thou hast surpassed the heavenly powers, for thou wast shown forth as a divine temple, O blessed Birthgiver of God, as having given birth unto Christ, the Savior of our souls!

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1098]
"Rejoice!" do we offer unto thee, O Birthgiver of God: for thou wast shown forth higher than the Angels, having given birth unto God!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1099]
Pacify our life through the prayers of the Birthgiver of God, as we cry aloud unto Thee: O merciful Lord, glory to Thee!

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1100]
O Virgin Birthgiver of God, unceasingly entreat Christ God, Who was crucified for us and destroyed the dominion of death, that He may save our souls!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1101]
Deliver us, O Birthgiver of God, from the sins holding us fast: for we the faithful have no other hope save thee, and God Who was born of thee!

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*[^1102]
The Fruit of thy womb, O all-pure one, is the fulfillment of the prophets and the Law: therefore glorifying thee with knowledge as the Birthgiver of God, we piously magnify thee!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1103]
The Fruit of thy womb, O Bride of God, was shown unto men as mediating salvation: therefore glorifying thee with mind and tongue as the Birthgiver of God, we the faithful magnify thee!

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*O Virgin Birthgiver of God, unceasingly entreat Christ God, Who was crucified for us...*

*At the end of Matins:*
*Deliver us, O Birthgiver of God...*

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*[^1104]
As the treasury of our resurrection, O all-hymned one, lead up from the pit and depth of transgressions those who place their trust in thee! For thou didst save those guilty of sin, having given birth unto our salvation: thou who wast a Virgin before childbearing, and a Virgin in childbearing, and after childbearing still remainest a Virgin!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1105]
Rejoice, thou who in thy womb didst contain Him Who is uncontainable in the heavens; rejoice, O Virgin, the preaching of the prophets, through whom Emmanuel shone forth; rejoice, Mother of Christ God!

*Tone 8. On Sunday evening, and on Monday at "God is the Lord":*[^1106]
Rejoice, thou who through the Angel didst receive the joy of the world; rejoice, thou who didst give birth unto thy Creator and Lord; rejoice, thou who wast accounted worthy to be the Mother of God!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1107]
Rejoice, gate of the King of Glory, through which the Most High alone passed, and preserved it alone sealed, unto the salvation of our souls!

*On Monday evening, and on Tuesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1108]
The unshakable confirmation of faith and honored gift of our souls, the Birthgiver of God, do we the faithful magnify with hymns: Rejoice, thou who in thy womb didst contain the Rock of life; rejoice, hope of the ends of the earth, protection of the sorrowful; rejoice, unwedded Bride!

*At the end of Matins:*[^1109]
O all-pure Virgin, save us by thy prayers, moving the maternal heart toward Thy Son and our God!

*On Tuesday evening, and on Wednesday at "God is the Lord":*[^1110]
Beholding the Lamb, and Shepherd, and Savior of the world upon the Cross, she that bare Thee weeping said: The world indeed rejoices, receiving redemption, but my maternal heart burns, beholding Thy crucifixion which Thou endurest for all, O my Son and my God!

*At the end of Matins:*
*The Fruit of thy womb, O all-pure one...*

*On Wednesday evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*[^1111]
O all-pure Birthgiver of God, spiritual gate of life: deliver from perils those who faithfully flee unto thee, that we may glorify thy most holy childbearing unto the salvation of our souls!

*At the end of Matins:*
*O all-pure Virgin, save us...*

*On Thursday evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*
*Beholding the Lamb, and Shepherd...*

*At the end of Matins:*
*The Fruit of thy womb, O all-pure one...*

*On Friday evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*[^1112]
O Good One, Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, Who by death didst overthrow death, and as God didst reveal the Resurrection: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thy hand; manifest Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; accept the Birthgiver of God who bore Thee, who prays for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people!

*At the end of Matins:*
*O all-pure Virgin, save us by thy prayers, moving the maternal heart toward Thy Son and our God!*

*End of the Theotokia.*
