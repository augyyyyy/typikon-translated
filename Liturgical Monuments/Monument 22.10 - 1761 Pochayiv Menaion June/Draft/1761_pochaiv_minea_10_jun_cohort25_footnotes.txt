[^962]: Resurrectional Theotokia (Воскресны Богородичны) for the Eight Tones. This appendix provides the Dogmatika (Theotokia sung at "Lord, I Have Cried" after "Glory... both now...") and the Aposticha Theotokia for Saturday Vespers across all Eight Tones of the Octoechos, commonly appended to Menaia for daily or festal reference when a saint's service prescribes singing the Resurrectional Theotokion of the current Tone.
