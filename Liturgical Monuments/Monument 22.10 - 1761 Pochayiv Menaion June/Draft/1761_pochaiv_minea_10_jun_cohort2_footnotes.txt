[^16]: Saint Lucillian was an elderly pagan priest at Nicomedia who converted to Christ in extreme old age. Arrested under the governor Silvanus, he was cast into a fiery furnace together with four Christian youths—Claudius, Hypatius, Paul, and Dionysius. Miraculously preserved, they were transferred to Byzantium, where the four youths were beheaded and Lucillian was crucified. Saint Paula, a holy virgin who ministered to the martyrs in prison, was subsequently seized, scourged, and beheaded.
