*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Rising early from the night we sing of Thee, O Christ, Co-unoriginate with the Father, and Savior of our souls: grant peace unto the world, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparia:*
Rising early from the night unto prayer to God, O blessed one, spending the time, thou didst not prepare the grave for the deceased; therefore thou didst say: Go, tell the deceased to wait until morning, until I dig the place, and we will bury him, glorifying the Lover of mankind!

According to thy word, O venerable one, when he that was sent said: Brother, Mark says unto thee: live yet a little while, immediately the dead man opened his eyes, in the sight of all who looked on, and remained until morning: O marvelous wonder! As Thou Thyself, O God, hast dominion over life and death, so Thou grantest Thy servants also to hold dominion, O Lover of mankind!

Laboring night and day, thou didst prepare the place for burial, and didst say, O holy one: Tell the deceased: yield up thy spirit unto God! And he, having obeyed thee, closed his eyes and departed; and all, being astonished at the power of thy word, glorified God, the Lover of mankind!

*Glory...*
A strange wonder, that the dead should raise the dead! But unto those who heed God, all things work together for good;[^155] therefore all things come to pass unto our building up, that with warmer love we may flee unto the Master, and with the venerable ones glorify Him as the compassionate Lover of mankind!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
A strange wonder, how in a bodily womb thou didst receive the divine Fire, O Virgin, and didst remain unconsumed, even as of old the bush was unburned,[^156] which all took place that the mystery of divine dispensation might be fulfilled in thee, O pure one!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Thou didst save the Prophet from the sea monster, O Lover of mankind; and lead me up from the depth of transgressions, I pray Thee![^157]

*Troparia:*
Cleansing the defilement of self-indulgence, O blessed one, thou didst command deceased John to yield the higher place unto Theophilus: O wonder! The dead man, having obeyed, moved unto the lower place, and all were filled with fear; but thou didst thus bow down with humility, saying: Lead me up from the corruption of enmity, O God, I pray Thee![^158]

When Theophilus fell down at thy feet, asking forgiveness, thou didst say, O venerable one: The Lord Himself, deriding enmity, hath commanded the dead man out of love to yield seniority unto thee; but that thou mayest be delivered from the noetic sea monster who awaits thee, pray unto the Lover of mankind with hymnody!

Thy word was like thunder, O father, from the fear of which Theophilus was terrified, thinking that he had fallen and would die; but coming a little to himself, and entering his cell, he spent the rest of his years weeping, saying: Lead me up from corruption, O God!

*Glory...*
Thy chastening, O most blessed father, brought Theophilus unto such great humility that no one was able to raise him up from weeping, but tears became food and drink unto him; for day and night he cried aloud: Lead me up from my fall into sin, O God, I pray Thee!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having slipped into the corruption of self-indulgence, and possessing no compunction, grant me streams of tears, O Sovereign Lady, that having washed away the defilement of my soul, I may appear pure before the countenance of God, when His glory shall appear unto me!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
When the concord of martyrs summoned the people to serve the image, the youths of David, having suffered in the flame of Sion, destroyed the impious decree of the tyrant, and turned the flame into dew, singing the hymn: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers and our God, Who art highly exalted!

*Troparia:*
When the time for thee to appear before the countenance of God was drawing near, O father, thou didst call the blessed Theophilus, saying: Forgive me, for I have grieved thee; for behold, I am already departing, but do thou pray for me, that we both may be accounted worthy to sing with the saints: Blessed art Thou, O God!

Thy words, O holy one, added lamentation unto him that was weeping; for unable to endure being left without thee, he said: I know that the Lord will hear thee, if Thou wilt to take me with thee, to sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

Comforting him that was weeping, thou didst say, O venerable one, that he should not grieve, nor seek to see sensible light; for weeping for sins with noetic eyes, he shall see intelligible Light, and be accounted worthy of the beatitude of those who weep, for they that weep shall be comforted, singing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Glory...*
For the consolation of him who was sorrowing, O father, thou didst make known unto him this sign of his departure unto God: that three days before his end he should regain his sight, and thus pass unto the tearless places, to sing with the saints in unapproachable Light: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Being in the sorrow of my despair, I have no consolation; therefore unto thee, the Sovereign Lady of the world, do I flee, saying: O my Joy, deliver me from those that have surrounded me, for many dogs have encompassed me, and there is none able to deliver me save thee, O blessed Virgin!
