[^230]: Saint Eustratius the Faster and Venerable-Martyr of the Caves (d. March 28, 1097). Tonsured at the Kyiv Caves Monastery under St. Antony, he was renowned for strict fasting. In 1096, during a Polovtsian raid on Kyiv led by Bonyak, Eustratius was taken captive along with thirty monastic and lay companions and sold into slavery to a Jewish master in Chersonesus (Korsun). When their master attempted to force the Christian captives to renounce Christ, Eustratius exhorted them to endure starvation rather than apostatize; after all fifty captives died of hunger, the enraged master had Eustratius crucified upon a cross on the Paschal feast and pierced his side with a spear. See Kyiv Caves Paterikon, Discourse 29.
