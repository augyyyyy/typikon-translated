From all sides the people assembled to see the most glorious wonder, how the fiery flame devoured all other things, but harmed not in any wise the icons of thy painting; hearing which, we magnify God Who works wonders concerning Thee with doxologies!

Thou didst keep innocency and didst behold uprightness, spending thy life in great sanctity; therefore God glorified Thee on the right hand, and delivered Thee from every misfortune: deliver also us from this by thy prayers, O holy one, that we may ever magnify Thee in hymns!

*Glory:*
The city of Rostov rejoices, possessing the icon painted by the nod of God, and as with a costly treasure is filled against all passions; which kissing, it remembers how much God worked through thee, O blessed one; honoring this wonderworking also today, we marvel, and magnify in doxologies![^672]

*Both now:*
Having thee as a tower of strength, O Virgin Theotokos, driven by the face of our enemies, we thy servants flee unto thee, and falling down we pray: preserve us that are in misfortunes, for thou art unto us a tower of strength from the face of the enemy, and an impregnable wall!

*Exapostilarion:*[^673]
He that ever preserves thee, O venerable one, in afflictions, came in thy stead before thy face to paint the icon of the Mother of God, which having depicted radiantly in three hours, He gave it for adornment unto the most pure temple for the feast, and took thee unto the temple on high, to celebrate with the saints unto the ages of ages; where celebrating now with the voice of rejoicing, remember also us, O Alypius, most blessed father!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Being a temple created by the right hand of the Most High, raise up the temple of my soul that has fallen through sin, O Mother of God, renew it by thy visitation, and grant unto those who bring glory unto thee without slothfulness, ceaselessly to sing unto thee: Rejoice, O temple of the Lord, created as a refuge for Christians!

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 7, Podoben: "As noble among martyrs" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*[^674]
In Davidic manner today, ye people, let us sing in thy memory, O all-blessed father: thou wast planted in the house of the Lord, and didst flourish in the court of the Mother of God as a sweet-smelling tree, from which receiving fruits of healings, we glorify our God Who has glorified thee, and we pray diligently to obtain mercy through thy mediation, and healing for spiritual passions!

The palm tree, as a palm tree shall flourish: upon thee, O our Father Alypius, was this fulfilled; for at the streams of the living water of the teaching of the God-bearing fathers grew up the blossoms of thy virtuous corrections, looking upon which, as sons, we flee unto the shrine of thy relics, and embracing them with love we pray: grant, O holy one, through thy prayers to obtain peace in Christ, and the correction of life!

Who can worthily praise thy labors and struggles, O venerable one? For thou didst give no sleep unto thine eyes, nor slumber unto thine eyelids, until thou didst adorn thy soul with the colors of virtues, having depicted it perfectly after the first-beginning fathers; whose zeal of life inscribe also unto our memory, we pray diligently, that we also may not be deprived of heavenly dwellings with them!

*Glory, Tone 8:*
Having the image of God's glory ever in thy mind, thou didst depict in thy soul zeal ceaselessly to walk in the way of our first-beginning fathers; following whose life, in a mortal body thou wast likened in holiness unto the bodiless ones: standing now with them before the throne on high, pray, O venerable father, that unity of mind be given unto our monastery, as it honors thy holy memory with love!
