*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
Seeing with his prophetic eyes the mountain overshadowed by the grace of God, Habakkuk proclaimed that the Holy One of Israel should come forth from thee, for our salvation and renewal![^264]

Who shall recount thy boldness, O hierarch? For thou didst speak concerning the testimonies of the Lord before kings, not feeling shame before the diadem; therefore thou wast a second Elijah, rebuking lawless deeds![^267]

Correct me who act lawlessly, and humble the passions that rage within me, O Mother of God, that being set free from them through thee, I may find justification: for thou alone art the salvation and renewal of us all!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
O Christ Who didst enlighten with the radiance of Thy coming, and by Thy Cross didst illuminate the ends of the world: enlighten the hearts of those who orthodoxly hymn Thee with the light of Thy knowledge of God![^268]

*Troparia:*
The Russian Church hymns thee as a second Theodore the Studite, O blessed one, because of thy boldness and confession, and calls unto spiritual rejoicing those who lovingly praise thee, O father![^269]

Thou didst ever oppose those who walk uncanonically, in no wise consenting that a hierarch be elevated unto the throne without the blessing of the Oecumenical Patriarch of the city of Constantine, nor ungodly to give a wolf as a shepherd unto the sheep that piously glorify Christ![^270]

Thou art worthy of honor, O holy one, for thou didst not consent unto those who placed the head of Saint Clement upon him for the dignity of the hierarchate, but rather didst rebuke and condemn their synod together with the men-pleasers!

*Glory...*
Being a true son of the eastern oecumenical throne, thou didst not consent that the Russian sons fall away from their primacy, but didst faithfully confess that it was confirmed from the beginning by written decree that all things should be done with the approval of their visiting shepherd, the Patriarch of the city of Constantine!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who didst crown the human race with blessing: account us worthy of the blessing of the chosen ones, and grant unto those who orthodoxly praise thee to behold the glory of thy most sweet Son!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Seeking to restrain thee who couldest not be restrained, O blessed one, the hierarch instigated the prince to send thee into exile; and he restrained thy person, but could not restrain thy tongue, for thou didst rebuke him as having seized the throne unlawfully!

Dwelling, O most blessed father, in the wonder-filled monastery as in a heavenly bridal chamber, rejoicing thou didst render thanksgivings unto the Queen of all and her most sweet Son, singing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The human race that had righteously fallen away from the grace of God, thou didst raise up by thy most pure childbearing, O Mother of God; therefore joyfully every generation sings unto thee, the blessed one!

When the Oecumenical Patriarch of the city of Constantine heard that thou wast unburned in the furnace of temptations and in the flame of flattery of the lawless, he blessed thy courage, O father, and numbered thee with the ancient confessors, with whom thou now singest unto the Lord unto all ages![^276]
