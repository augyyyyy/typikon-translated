The souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and no torment shall touch them. O John of many virtues, who even after death by obedience unto thy brother didst show forth an image of love; and Theophilus, who didst wash away the defilement of vainglory with tears: as ye are undivided both here and there, pray together unto God, that we also may have true love toward Him and unfeigned love toward our neighbor!

O Damian, who through prayer and oil didst heal bodily passions, with Sabbas the Wonderworker, despise not us who are exhausted by spiritual passions; but from the healing gift of your prayers cure our wounds of heart, that we may offer acceptable prayers unto God!

O Nectarius the Obedient, with Sergius of one mind with thee: having yielded your souls under the yoke of the obedience of Christ, bow down our stubbornness through your prayer under the yoke of Jesus Who sent you forth, that we may diligently fulfill His commandments!

*Glory...*
The glory of the city of Smolensk, Mercurius the Shepherd,[^17] who well pastured the sheep of his Chief Shepherd, and in spirit was exalted on high to be inseparably of the choir of the Caves venerable ones: therefore after death he miraculously floated in a wooden coffin, and in the holy cave rests with his body, while in his soul he rejoices with the venerable ones unto eternal glory.

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having no ascent from good works unto the dwelling of the venerable, I flee unto thee, O Good Theotokos: for thou art the door of contemplation and the ladder beheld by the prophets; according unto thy goodness, therefore, grant an ascent in my heart, and open unto me the doors of thy mercy!

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
Beholding with noetic eyes the choir of ascetics gathered in mind, come, O people, let us honor them with praises, and bowing before their relics, offer gifts of love; for they are intercessors before God and pleaders for our souls.

*Ikos:*
Who can praise Thy saints one by one, O Good One? I will number them and they shall be multiplied above the sand; but do Thou Thyself, O Master Christ, Who countest the multitude of the stars and callest them all by name, receive the prayers of them all for us, and show Thy compassions upon Thy people: for we know that they have much boldness before Thy majesty, and intercede with Thee as unceasing pleaders for our souls!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
O Hebrew children in the furnace, Who didst preserve for her who bore Thee her virginity after childbearing: blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*
Help me to offer a childlike hymn, O Anyfey, divinely beautiful

among the fathers of the Caves, that through your instruction, with the people named after Christ, I may dispassionately sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

O Pimen the Long-Ailing, who not only didst pass through this life in illness with thanksgiving, but didst also grant relief in sicknesses unto others through prayer: do thou, who art now in endless rest, relieve me from sickness, that I may sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

Pure gold and silver tried sevenfold in the gifts, O righteous Eustathius: thou wast a righteous worker, and didst cross over into the working of noetic virtues; wherewith having adorned thy soul, thou hast presented an offering as gold, and singest: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

O glorious Nestor,[^18] who by thy writing didst declare unto us the times and years of the most precious Church, and the lives and virtues of the first fathers, and wast a diligent emulator of their deeds: together with them do we now beatify thee, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

O Eustratius, beauty of martyrs,[^19] thou didst appear as a second Elijah the charioteer: but he was taken up as unto heaven, while thou into the very heavenly gates didst enter on a fiery chariot, a voice summoning thee from the majesty of glory, where with a voice of joy thou singest: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

O glorious and blessed fathers, Helladius and Luke the Steward, renowned in gracious miracles: having been good laborers of the commandments of the Master, ye have now received recompense from the torrent of delight, and joyfully sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Glory...*
The Holy Spirit assists our spirit, O God-bearing fathers, for ye are the children of God and heirs of Christ; and Him Whom ye served, with Him are ye also glorified, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The Holy Spirit came upon Thee, and the power of the Most High overshadowed Thee, showing Thee to be an honorable Mother even after childbearing: delivered through whom from the servitude of the stranger, all generations bless Thee, for the blessed God of our fathers hath beatified Thee!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
Him Who was glorified on the holy mountain, and in the bush revealed unto Moses the mystery of the Ever-Virgin through fire: praise the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
Those who lived upon the holy Cave hill, you, the great luminaries, O God-bearing fathers, whence ye stretched out rays of grace and illuminated the whole Ruthenian land with miracles: therefore we praise the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!
