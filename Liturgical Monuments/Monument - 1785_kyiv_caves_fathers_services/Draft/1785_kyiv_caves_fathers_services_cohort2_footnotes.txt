[^41]: Typological parallel to the fall of the walls of Jericho before the Ark of the Covenant under Joshua (Joshua 6), prefiguring the collapse of Kyivan idols before the Cross and Saint Michael.
[^42]: The gold neck-chain (*hryvnia*, гривна) worn by nobility in Kyivan Rus', here employed as a metaphor for the holy Gospel adorning the neck of the baptized city of Kyiv.
[^43]: Saint Michael is likened to a second Moses leading the new Israel out of Egyptian idolatry, citing the prophetic verse of Psalm 71:16 (LXX 71:16): "There shall be an establishment of faith in the earth, upon the top of the mountains."
