*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

*Troparia:*
The disposition of thine understanding was like a bent bow, O father, for living in this vale of tears, thou didst ever have an ascent in thy heart toward God: and unto me who lead my life ever in sorrows, O Nestor, help, praying for me, that my heart may rejoice in the Lord!

Being seventeen years of age, mentally thou didst receive the stature of a perfect man;[^68] for thou didst shine forth, as with elder-like gray hairs, in good deeds: correct these things in me also, O blessed one, and my heart shall rejoice in the Lord!

*Glory...*
Having received purity and deep humility unto poverty, thou didst fly as with wings unto the mountain of monastic virtues: whence having perceived in the twinkling of an eye the passing fashion of this world, thou didst all the more wing thy heart into the love of God.

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who didst bring forth the Garden of purity, with the fruit thereof thou didst abundantly nourish those who believe thee to be the Mother of God: and grant unto me who bow before thee with faith, O Bride of God and Virgin, to taste even a little of this fruit, that my sorrowful heart may rejoice!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I heard of Thy glorious dispensation, O Christ God, that Thou wast born of the Virgin, to deliver from deceit those who cry: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparia:*
Having heard of the glorious and marvelous things upon the hill of the Caves, thou didst come thither, O venerable one, and having beheld the grace of God descending with fire and dew at the beginning of the foundation of the church,[^69] being yet outside with the elders, thou didst sing: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Making a greater and more perfect tabernacle in thy soul for the Holy Spirit, by the affectionate reception of the commandments from the God-bearing fathers thou didst adorn it, O holy Nestor: therefore as into a fair bridal chamber, the Father with the Son and the Spirit came and made an abode in thee.

*Glory...*
Having received the teaching of the God-bearing fathers like sweet milk, thou didst fulfill the word by deed, O blessed one, and wast filled with wisdom from on high: which we also reading from thy writings, cheer our souls with the knowledge of the grace of God that was upon these hills.

Being walled about with deep humility, O venerable one, thou didst cast down and trample underfoot the demon of vainglory that assaulted the brother; through thine intercession with God, O father, grant us also humility, and direct our steps therein!
