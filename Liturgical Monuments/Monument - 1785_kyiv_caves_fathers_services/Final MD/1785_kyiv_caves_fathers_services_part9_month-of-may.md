# MONTH OF MAY

*On the 3rd Day:*
*Service to our Venerable Father Theodosius, Hegumen of the Caves Monastery in Kyiv, and Initiator of the Monastic Coenobitic Life in the Holy Russian Land:*[^281]
*At Small Vespers, Stichera: Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly hosts" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*[^282]
True and blameless, venerable and guileless, keeping thyself back from every evil thing, thou wast known, O blessed one, truly adorned with virtues and shining with piety; therefore we call thee blessed, and celebrating today thy holy and honorable falling asleep, we rejoice!

*(Right page — Folio 77 recto, running header: TO THEODOSIUS, HEGUMEN OF THE CAVES:)*
Blameless and most honorable, a most lofty pillar and unconquerable by the wiles of the enemy, we all call thee blessed, and faithfully praise Theodosius the great, who wast a favorite of Christ and of His most pure Mother, and who diligently prayest unto Him to save our souls from misfortunes!

Thou didst clarify the monastic life, having shone forth like the sun in the Russian land: for having hedged about thy body with abstinence, thou didst mortify the stirrings of passion by prayers and vigils; therefore we pray thee, O all-blessed Theodosius: cease not helping us, and visiting thy servants!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
Come, ye assembly of the faithful, merrily this day; come, let us all sing with joy in the honorable temple unto the God-bearing fathers, and clap our hands on their most radiant memory, whom Christ our God hath glorified; therefore with a voice of joy and a pure conscience let us cry aloud, saying: Rejoice, earthly angel and heavenly man, all-blessed Anthony, dwelling-place of the venerable! Rejoice, golden-beaming eastern star, Father Theodosius, luminary and shepherd of monastics! Rejoice, our boast, joy and helpers of the universe! Dwelling with the angels in the light of the Holy Trinity that crowned you, diligently pray to deliver from enemies visible and invisible, and from every misfortune, us who celebrate with faith and love your all-festal memory!

*Both now... Of the Feast.*[^283]
*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 2, Podoben: "House of Ephratha" (До́ме Єѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^284]
Morning and radiant day hath thy feast appeared, enlightening our hearts who faithfully praise thine honorable ascetic struggles, O Father Theodosius!
*Verse: Honorable in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^285]
The day of salvation and feast of gladness hath appeared: let us gather together, ye faithful, having purified our souls, to hymn the all-blessed Theodosius!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord; he shall greatly delight in His commandments.*[^286]
Having found Anthony as a sun upon earth, shining with virtues, thou didst show thyself a most radiant star: do thou therefore lead those who remember thee unto good things, O venerable Theodosius!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O thou who didst receive the Joy of the world, Christ the Giver of life, and who didst assuage our foremother's grief, O Virgin!

*Troparion, Tone 8:*
Having been elevated unto virtue, from thy youth having loved the monastic life, having valiantly attained unto thy desire, thou didst settle in a cave; and adorning thy life with fasting and radiance *(Left page — Folio 77 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
...and radiance, in prayers thou didst abide as one bodiless; having shone forth in the Russian land like a radiant luminary, O Father Theodosius, pray unto Christ God that our souls may be saved![^287]

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast.*
*At Great Vespers:*
*We sing "Blessed is the man".*
*At "Lord, I have cried", Stichera: Tone 5, Podoben: "Venerable father" (Преподо́бне ѻ́ч҃е):*[^288]
O venerable father, God-bearer Theodosius, having striven greatly in temporal life with hymns, fasting, and vigil, thou wast an example unto thy disciples; and now indeed thou dancest with the bodiless hosts, unceasingly glorifying Christ, the Word of God from God, the Deliverer, Who bowed His head upon the Cross, and delivered the human race from deception: pray unto Him, entreat Him, O venerable one, to grant peace and great mercy unto the universe!

O venerable father, God-bearer Theodosius, truly wast thou accounted worthy of divine life, having found it by purity and fasting: for while living thou didst pass over unto true life, unto the King of all, unceasingly glorifying Christ with the bodiless hosts, Who ineffably received flesh from the Virgin, Who overthrew hades, and raised up the dead from the ages: pray unto Him, entreat Him, O venerable one, to grant peace and great mercy unto the universe!

O venerable father, God-bearer Theodosius, thou hast found that which was beloved unto thine honorable soul: for the grace of the All-Holy Spirit made His abode in thee, as a most pure light, by Whose visitation being radiantly illumined, thou didst unceasingly glorify Christ, the one God in two essences, Who suffered in the flesh upon the Cross, and remained impassible in His nature: pray unto Him, entreat Him, O venerable one, to grant peace and great mercy unto the universe!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
Come, let us all gather together at the divine memory of our Father Theodosius: for having received a calling from on high from youth, he was named by the Greeks "God-given gift";[^289] therefore unto Christ-named princes he was a teacher of the right faith, unto nobles a firm defense, unto orphans a merciful father, unto widows a warm protector, unto the sorrowing a consolation, unto the poor a treasure, unto the monastic choir a ladder ascending unto heavenly height, and simply unto those who have recourse unto him he was a fountain of ever-flowing water; he unceasingly prays that our souls may be saved!

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day.*
*And three readings.*

*(Right page — Folio 78 recto, running header: TO THEODOSIUS, HEGUMEN OF THE CAVES:)*
*The Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon:*
The righteous live unto the ages, and in the Lord is their reward, and the care of them is with the Most High. Therefore shall they receive the kingdom of majesty, and the crown of beauty from the hand of the Lord: for with His right hand shall He cover them, and with His arm shall He shield them. He shall take His zeal for full armor, and shall make the creation His weapon for vengeance on His enemies. He shall put on righteousness as a breastplate, and shall take unfeigned judgment for a helmet. He shall take holiness for an invincible shield; He shall sharpen His severe wrath for a weapon, and the world shall fight with Him against the senseless. Right-aiming shafts of lightnings shall go forth, and as from a well-drawn bow of the clouds they shall fly unto the mark. And from His stone-hurling wrath shall hailstones full of fury be cast. The water of the sea shall rage against them, and the rivers shall mercilessly overflow. A spirit of power shall stand up against them, and like a whirlwind shall scatter them; and iniquity shall make the whole earth desolate, and evildoing shall overthrow the thrones of the mighty. Give ear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye judges of the ends of the earth. Give ear, ye that rule multitudes, and that boast yourselves in crowds of nations: for power is given you from the Lord, and sovereignty from the Most High.[^290]

*The Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon:*
The souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and no torment shall touch them. In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to die, and their departure was accounted an affliction, and their going from us destruction; but they are in peace. For though they be punished in the sight of men, yet is their hope full of immortality. And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly benefited: for God proved them, and found them worthy for Himself. As gold in the furnace He tried them, and as a whole burnt-offering He accepted them. And in the time of their visitation they shall shine, and like sparks through stubble they shall run to and fro. They shall judge nations, and have dominion over peoples, and the Lord shall reign over them unto the ages. They that put their trust in Him shall understand truth, and the faithful in love shall abide with Him: for grace and mercy are in His venerable ones, and visitation in His chosen.[^291]

*The Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon:*
Though the righteous happen to die, he shall be in rest. For venerable age is not that which stands in length of time, nor is it measured by number of years. But wisdom is gray hairs unto men, and an unspotted life is ripe old age. Being well-pleasing unto God, he was beloved, and living among sinners he was translated. He was caught away, lest wickedness should alter his understanding *(Left page — Folio 78 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
...or deceit should beguile his soul. For the bewitching of naughtiness obscures good things, and the wandering of concupiscence undermines the simple mind. Being made perfect in a short space, he fulfilled long years: for his soul was pleasing unto the Lord: therefore He hastened to take him away from the midst of wickedness. And the peoples saw, and understood it not, neither laid they up such a thing in their mind, that grace and mercy are in His venerable ones, and visitation in His chosen.[^292]

*At the Litiya, Stichera: Tone 1:*
Come, ye feast-lovers this day, having assembled a choir in piety, let us celebrate the memory of the great father, the all-world luminary, honorable and glorious, praising him and saying: Rejoice, Theodosius, kinsman of the venerable and righteous! Rejoice, thou who didst assemble an honorable flock of the most sacred! Rejoice, faithful minister and builder of the monastery of the Mother of God, which excellent church thou didst erect for the glory of God: therefore now we pray thee: pray that we may be delivered from every assault and misfortune, and from barbarian invasion, and that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 2:*
O disciple of the Savior, God-wise Theodosius, God-bearing mouth, God-revealed mind, spiritual commander, good shepherd, boast of the venerable: Thou didst seek God, and Him Thou didst proclaim, being illuminated with rays of light; with God Thou didst converse, being unceasingly purified and illuminated; with streams of tears Thou didst make thy mind radiant; as a pillar of fire and a cloud of light Thou didst appear, O all-blessed one, boast of monastics, glory of fathers, and minister of the Trinity: and now preserve us all by thine intercession!

O venerable Father Theodosius, thou wast bountiful unto those melting in the heat of thirst; thou didst rule divinely with wonders of reward, as an excellent minister imitating thy Master: thou didst multiply the wine, and by thy command didst fill the empty granaries with flour, and didst make the failing vessel of honey to abound; knowing hidden things, such things thou didst clarify, showing forth martyr-like sufferings; thou didst grant multitudes of healings; thou didst gladden the poor by thy child-loving ways; and thou didst constrict thy belly with bread and water, in the flesh imitating the life of the bodiless: therefore we offer glory unto thee, praying: Forsake not us thy servants, preserving us by thy prayers!

Two most radiant luminaries have shone forth in the Russian land, Anthony and Theodosius: for the one by divine word brought down heavenly dew upon the earth, and brought down fire from heaven...

...brought down fire from heaven, and clearly prophesied things to come, having received prophetic grace; and the other asked rain from heaven, and rebuked the prince concerning brother-hatred and unrighteous flattery,[^293] and saw the Lord face-to-face, and in the air, like Elijah, was seen over the monastery in flaming light, lifting up his hands and making prayers unto God; and now rejoicing with the angels, they pray for us, that with them we may be accounted worthy of the true light, and that our souls may be saved!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
Preserving that which is according to the image unhurt, having ascetically set the mind as master over destructive passions, thou didst ascend, as far as was possible, unto that which is according to the likeness: for having manfully constrained nature, thou didst hasten to subject the worse unto the better, and to enslave the flesh unto the spirit; therefore thou wast shown forth as the head of monastics, a desert-dweller, a trainer of those that run well, a most certain rule of virtue; and now in the heavens, the mirrors being done away, O our Father Theodosius, thou purely beholdest the Holy Trinity, praying unceasingly for those who honor thee with faith and love!

*Both now... Of the Feast.*
*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 8, Podoben: "O most marvelous wonder" (Ѡ пресла́внагѡ чудесѐ):*[^294]
O God-wise Father Theodosius, having joined thy mind unto God from the womb, thou didst utterly disregard worldly things, O King all-ruler,[^295] offering worthy fruits unto Him from thy sweaty ascetic struggles, gathered by virtuous good things; therefore thou hast found the reception of good things, O wondrously worthy!
*Verse: Honorable in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^296]
Thou art the instructor of monastics, and a most radiant star of the Russian land, illuminating all this country with wonders: for God chose thee from youth, O father, as shepherd unto rational sheep; therefore the choir of the venerable received thee: with whom, we pray thee, pray that forgiveness of sins may be granted unto thy flock!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord; he shall greatly delight in His commandments.*[^297]
Rejoice, O all-hymned Virgin, who by a pillar of fire didst show the pasture where the divine flock was to be pastured: and that church Theodosius straightway erected, banquet-like and all-marvelous unto thee: wherein all we faithful celebrate thy falling asleep, O most pure Mother of God, standing around the reliquary of thy favorite![^298]

*Glory... Tone 8:*
We honor thee, O our Father Theodosius, as the instructor of multitudes of monastics: for by thy path we have truly learned to walk uprightly! Blessed art thou, who didst work for Christ, and didst expose the enemy's power, converser with angels, companion of the venerable and righteous: with whom pray unto the Lord, that our souls may be saved!

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

*Troparia:*

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
Heaven was terrified at this, and the ends of the earth were amazed, that God appeared unto men in the flesh, and thy womb became more spacious than the heavens; therefore the ranks of angels and men magnify thee, the Mother of God.[^369]
*Troparia:*
We marvel at thy holy life, O all-blessed Simon; we glorify thy struggles, and honor also thy memory, which now with the angels we magnify.
Having left the lower things, thou didst depart unto the higher, and thy body according to thy desire was brought from thy throne into the cave,[^370] showing how great love thou hadst toward this place, wherein we all magnify thee.
The cave rejoices, containing thy relics, O hierarch, and calls together the assemblies of monastics and feast-lovers, that all having gathered on this present day may magnify thy memory with praises and hymns.
*Glory...*
Thou hast departed in bodily senses, but thy memory abides with us inexhaustibly; for behold, we thy children, burning with love toward thee, ever remember thee and magnify thee.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Abiding with us unfailingly, O most holy Virgin, strengthen the people gathered in memory of thy faithful servant, drive away sloth, and enlighten our understanding, that we may all vigilantly magnify thee and thy Son!

*Exapostilarion:*
Let us praise the divinely-wise father who wrote most wise words; for his venerable writings are like morning dew, a sweet honeycomb, living bread, heavenly nectar, a fountain of living water, from which one is filled again with spiritual sweetness, and glorifies thy memory, O most blessed Simon!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
O Virgin, who didst bring forth the inscrutable Fountain of living waters higher than the heavens, give drink unto me who thirst for thy mercy, that being refreshed, I may glorify thee!

*At the Praises, Stichera: Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly hosts" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*[^371]
Thou wast a sacred initiate, O all-blessed one, while living in the flesh in this world; and having departed unto the better life, thou wast accounted worthy of the very vision of God, and beholdest Him as unapproachable Light: of which make us also worthy to behold, through thy fervent prayers unto God!
Those who give heed unto thy words, and attend unto thy instruction, O venerable Simon, learn profit in life, and are filled with correction, receive spiritual actions, and draw grace through thy teaching; wherewith enrich also us who honor thy memory with praises on this present day!

*(Right page — Folio 89 recto, running header: TO SIMON, BISHOP OF SUZDAL:)*
Today, ye people, let us honor with hymns the divine instrument of the Holy Spirit, Simon the sacred proclaimer, the first-enthroned hierarch of Suzdal, who rightly proclaimed spiritual corrections, bowing down before the reliquary of his relics; for as a hierarch and fervent intercessor he stands before the Master's throne, praying that we may be saved!
*Glory... Tone 6:*
The assemblies of angels and men marveled at the radiance of thy life and at thy hierarchic correction; for from on high thou wast shown forth as a sacred initiate in election, and wast an excellent mirror unto fasters; therefore as a worthy servant of God, O Simon, most blessed hierarch, we lovingly magnify thee in hymns!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Mother of God, thou art the true vine...
