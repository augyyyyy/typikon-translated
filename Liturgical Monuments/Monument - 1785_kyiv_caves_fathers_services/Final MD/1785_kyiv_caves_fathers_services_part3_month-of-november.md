# MONTH OF NOVEMBER

*On the 19th Day:*
*Service to our Venerable Father Barlaam, Abbot of the Caves:*
*At Small Vespers, Stichera: Tone 5:*
O venerable Father Barlaam, taking the yoke of Christ upon thy shoulder, valiantly didst thou bear the assault of the enemy, and having unswervingly traversed the narrow path, didst attain unto the prize of the high calling: but O most blessed one, boast of monastics, unceasingly pray Christ to grant great mercy unto our souls!

O Father Barlaam divinely wise, having loved Christ from thy youth, and by divine com- *(Left page — Folio 26 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
-mandments of the Holy Spirit having illumined thy heart, thou wast truly a beholder of God: therefore fulfill for the profit of salvation the petitions of those who flee unto thy church, and pray Christ God to grant great mercy unto us!

O Father Barlaam, taking thy cross upon thy shoulder, and having lovingly run the beautiful course of Christ thy King, thou hast stood before Him Who dwells in the holy heavenly city: whence look down now upon those who honor thy struggles, O holy one; pray Christ God to direct our steps also into the way of peace!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
Having adorned thy life with much abstinence, O venerable one, thou hast shown forth sweat leading into life through the acquisition of virtues: renouncing the world, and despising all the fair things that are in the world, by an austere life thou didst wear out thy flesh: therefore dwelling in heaven, pray that our souls may be saved!

*Both now... Theotokion.*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 2, Podoben: "O house of Ephratha" (Доме Евфрафов):*
Transitory life which is on earth didst thou despise, O blessed one, that thou mightest obtain endless life.

*Verse:* Blessed is the man that fears the Lord: in His commandments he will have great delight.[^73]
For the sake of the love of Christ, O venerable Barlaam, thou didst put to death the passionate wisdom of the flesh.

*Verse:* Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.[^74]
By thy watchful prayers, assist in tribulations those who lovingly sing of thy memory, O divinely blessed one!

*Glory... Tone 2:*
Having entered into the cave, O venerable one, thence for the sake of thy virtues thou didst pass over unto the heavenly city, and didst attain unto the rest of the righteous and the rejoicing of the saints: therefore with them fervently pray for all who honor thy memory with faith, that we may be delivered from every adverse circumstance, O Barlaam divinely wise!

*Both now... Theotokion.*

*At Great Vespers:*
*Blessed is the man... the 1st Antiphon.*
*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera: Tone 4, Podoben: "Called from on high" (Званный свыше):*
Beholding the ever-bubbling fountain of honey-flowing words coming forth from the mouth of the great father,[^75] O blessed Barlaam, as a hart unto the fountains of streams of living water,[^76] thou didst come thirsting with unspeakable love. Whence having drenched thy soul with compunction, thou didst despise all the fair things of this world, and in word didst follow the Word: before Whom now with rejoicing standing

*(Right page — Folio 27 recto, running header: TO BARLAAM, ABBOT OF THE CAVES:)*
standing, pray that He may save and enlighten our souls!

When with the eyes of the mind thou didst behold the great luminaries hidden beneath the bushel of the holy cave, O venerable one,[^77] then having illumined thy heart in youth with the light of virtues, and kindled with divine fire for dwelling together with them, thou didst leave the house of thy parents and thy friends, and becamest a partaker with the monastics. And now, numbered with the radiant choirs in heaven, enlighten us upon earth with the rays of grace, and grant gifts, praying Christ to save and enlighten our souls!

When the father worthy of beatitude showed thee the divine love of good things to come, and the ever-shining glory in the heavenly bridal chamber: immediately thou didst account earthly good things as dung:[^78] despising riches, glory, and the greatness of thy lineage, the beautiful chamber, and the bride betrothed unto thee,[^79] thou didst love Christ alone, and conforming thyself unto His poverty, didst traverse the narrow path in struggles and labors, and after thy falling asleep didst inherit the things desired: enjoying which now, pray the Good One to grant peace unto the world, and to save our souls!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
Living upon earth in bodily form, thou didst emulate the life of the bodiless hosts, O venerable Father Barlaam: by the renunciation of thy glory thou didst astonish men, by poverty and abstinence thou didst put the demons to shame, by purity thou didst make the angels rejoice, and by thy great struggles thou wast an exemplar unto monastics: therefore worthily do we beatify thee, adorned by the Sovereign Master on high: Whom ever pray, O blessed one, to grant peace unto our EMPRESS,[^80] and great mercy unto our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*The King of heaven...*

*Entrance, Prokeimenon of the day, and three Readings (see fol. 30 verso).[^81]*

*At the Litiya, Stichera: Tone 1:*
Today the holy cave joyfully rejoices, having thy holy relics, O blessed Barlaam, for they grant health unto those who with faith flee unto thy precious shrine: whence we also, being enlightened, say: make us worthy of thy gifts, O blessed one, and preserve thy flock by thy prayers, granting salvation unto our souls!

O venerable Father Barlaam, having from youth loved the beauty of the Master Christ, and having an unswerving desire for Him, thou didst leave thy fatherland and lineage, thou didst hate riches and glory, and unto Him alone *(Left page — Folio 27 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
didst follow Him alone, living in the cave as an equal of the angels: therefore now standing before Him in the heavens with the angels, pray with boldness for our souls!

*Tone 3:*
Strengthened by the almighty right hand, O venerable Father Barlaam, thou didst acquire abstinence in food, and by unceasing prayer and standing through the night wast accounted worthy to become a partaker with blessed Anthony;[^82] by streams of tears thou didst drown all the armies of demons: therefore also Christ, Whom thou didst desire and for Whose sake thou didst bear the burden of the day, glorified thee with the gift of wonders: Whom pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 3:*
Come, all ye assemblies of monastics, with psalms and hymns let us piously praise today the precious festival of the venerable one: who, even though he shone forth in the latter days, yet was accounted worthy of the honor of the ancient fathers, and prays Christ that our souls may be saved!

Thou wast a precious receptacle of the Holy Spirit, O venerable Barlaam: for imitating the bodiless hosts in the flesh, thou didst emulate their life: for this cause thou wast crowned by the almighty right hand, and even though thou didst pass away from the earth, yet living unto God thou standest before Him with the saints, praying for our souls!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
Having from youth hated the beauties of this world, O venerable one, thou didst love from thy heart Him Who is fairer than the sons of men,[^83] and having cleaved unto Him with all thy soul, as one bodiless upon earth, living in a mortal and corruptible body, thou didst obtain the honor of incorruption: and now purely beholding the Holy Trinity, fervently pray, O venerable one, to grant peace unto the world, and to save our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*O Virgin unwedded...*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 8, Podoben: "What shall we call you, O holy ones?" (Что васъ наречемъ святии):*
What shall we call thee, O venerable one? An angel in the flesh, for as an angel thou wast known upon earth? A luminary unto the venerable, for by their struggles thou didst astonish? Thou didst put demons to shame, having subdued the spirit over bodily passions; and now standing before Christ, pray that He may save and enlighten our souls!

*Verse:* Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.[^84]
How shall we address thee, O glorious one? An animate pillar, for thou didst remain unshaken in trials? A river of living water, pouring forth streams of spiritual grace

*(Right page — Folio 28 recto, running header: TO BARLAAM, ABBOT OF THE CAVES:)*
grace? An excellent guide of monastics, of one mind with the bodiless hosts, and a fervent intercessor for our souls!

*Verse:* The righteous man shall flourish like a palm tree, and like a cedar in Lebanon shall he be multiplied.[^85]
What shall we name thee, O holy one? An instructor of the undisciplined, and a true physician unto those in sickness, a graceful offspring of the cave, a confirmation of monastics, an ever-flowing fountain of wonders, and a good ascetic, a swift protector unto all who come unto thee with faith!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
O venerable father, having loved God from thy youth, thou didst prefer nothing above Him: having fled the turmoil of the world and of this life, thou didst attain unto the quiet haven of the holy cave, and having settled therein as in a fair bridal chamber, thou didst betroth thy soul unto God alone, having purified it beforehand with fasting and prayers: therefore thou wast a beautiful palace of the Holy Trinity, before Whom now joyfully standing, remember those who honor thine all-honorable memory!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*Be thou the true vine...*

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord":*
*Troparion, Tone 4:*
Leaving the fair things of the world and its glory, thou didst hasten unto the cave, where advancing from strength to strength, thou wast a leader of the flock of Christ, O all-glorious Barlaam: having tended which so well, shepherd us also, we pray thee, preserving us from all evils, that we may beatify thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion, in the same tone.*

*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn: Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-beginningless Word" (Собезначальное):*
Come, ye Ruthenian assemblies of monastics, and beholding in His saints the glory of God, open your lips in praise, blessing the Sovereign Master your Creator, Who glorifies Himself in His saints, and sing in the manner of psalms, saying: Exceedingly precious unto us are Thy relics, O Christ God, for exceedingly strengthened have their dominions become, concerning Thee, the Savior and Redeemer of our souls![^86]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
My heart is confirmed to sing, O all-immaculate Virgin, of thy most pure childbearing, whereby thou didst deliver us from the ancestral curse, and didst lift up fallen human nature unto the heights: therefore as is our duty we sing of thee and magnify thee!

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn: Tone 4, Podoben: "The angelic choir" (Ликъ ангельский):*
The spiritual wings of knowledge and action didst thou receive from the words of the great father, O Barlaam divinely wise, and by these raising thy mind unto the heights *(Left page — Folio 28 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
thou didst escape as a bird from the snare of the fowlers,[^87] and unto the dwelling where the choirs of the righteous abide, thou didst attain unto beatitude: glory unto Him Who gave thee this true desire, glory unto Him Who gave thee victory over passions: glory unto Him Who brought thee into the eternal dwelling!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Having received from the Holy Spirit into thy womb, O pure Virgin, and having brought forth the Word begotten of the Father before the ages: bring us who are likened unto unreasoning beasts into understanding, and grant amendment of life, that we may ever magnify thee!

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn: Tone 8, Podoben: "Having leaned upon Wisdom" (Премудрости возлег):*
Illumined with the dawn of the radiance of divine words, thou didst leave what is small in this life: and hastening unto the noetic Light, in the flesh thou didst receive the immaterial life with the bodiless hosts: whence being purified beforehand by the grace of the Spirit, thou wast an instructor of monastics, O Barlaam divinely wise; therefore with praises do we cry unto thee: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who lovingly honor thy holy memory!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
I have fallen into the mire of sin, and have no hope of rising; grievously have I sunk in despair: but as thou didst bring forth the Hope of salvation, look upon me thy servant, and deliver me from the sin that holds me fast, and from soul-corrupting passions, and from the perpetual malice of the crafty enemy, O Virgin unwedded; pray Christ God to grant me remission of sins: for I thy servant have thee as my hope!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*
Marvelous is God in His saints, the God of Israel.[^88]
*Verse:* In the congregations bless ye God, the Lord from the fountains of Israel.[^89]
*Gospel of a Venerable Father.*
*Sticheron after the Gospel: Tone 6:*

*Canon to the Venerable One:*
*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*
Delivered from bitter slavery, Israel traversed the impassable sea as dry land, and beholding the enemy drowned, sang a hymn unto God as Benefactor, Who worked wonders with a high arm, for He hath been glorified!

*Troparia:*
Grieved by the fierce slavery of this world, seeing many led captive, O blessed one, thou didst reflect that there is no deliverance from it, except one follow the steps of Christ the Redeemer: therefore thou wast an unflagging emulator of Him. By thy watchful prayer, O father, stir me up well from despondency unto the praising of thy struggles, that I may swiftly sing a hymn unto God, for He hath been glorified!

Drenched with honey-flowing words from the great father, thou didst no longer desire to behold wealth, but forgetting the things behind, as one winged thou didst press forward unto the things ahead,[^92] pondering one thing: to seek God with all Thy heart, and ever to sing unto Him a hymn, for He hath been glorified!

From the discourse of the venerable Anthony, thy heart being kindled with fervent love toward God as with a flame, O blessed one, rejoicing as one who had found a treasure,[^93] thou didst return unto thy house, yet the resolution of thy will thou didst leave at the feet of the father worthy of beatitude, after which hastening back unto him, thou didst ever sing unto God: for He hath been glorified!

*Glory...*
Heeding the divinely inspired discourse of great Anthony, thou didst tear asunder the entangling snares of love for the world, and hastening unto him, thou wast his true disciple and hearer, crying aloud with him unto God: for He hath been glorified!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Dispel by thine intercession, O pure Virgin, the afflictions and assaults of the enemy that ever harass me: and put me not to shame from mine expectation, O Mother of God: for thou together with thy Son art the only hope of the hopeless!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
The stone which the builders rejected, the same became the head of the corner: this is the stone upon which Christ confirmed the Church, which He redeemed from among the nations.[^94]

*Troparia:*
O Christ the unyielding Stone, Thou wast made the head of the corner, and upon Thee Thy venerable one, desiring to build a spiritual house, resolved beforehand to lay the walls of humility, poverty, and other virtues: therefore coming unto the great father, he prayed him, saying: Confirm me upon the rock of the commandments of the Lord, O venerable one!

*(Left page — Folio 29 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
In glory and riches, having come with many servants unto the holy cave, thou didst cast down before the feet of the elder all thy fair adornments and thyself also, together casting down the pride of the crafty one!

Perceiving, O holy one, that a spiritual treasure was hidden in the holy cave, thou didst sell unto Anthony all the riches brought by thee and thyself also, and as a wise merchant thou didst buy that field wherein thou didst become rich with this, whereby we also glorify thee after thy death!

*Glory...*
Judging the wealth, glory, and all the light of this age to be like a fleeting shadow, O venerable one: therefore counting all things as dung that Thou mightest gain Christ alone, Thou didst desire to walk toward Him along the path of monastics!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The path of my life being seized by unlawful passions, do thou, O pure one, with the right hand wherewith thou didst bear the Creator on high, clear and cleanse, that the path of virtues may be marked out for me also, and a right spirit renewed within mine inward parts.[^95]

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
Seeing thy desire to be unfeigned, the great one among the fathers placed upon thee the helmet of salvation, clothed thee in the breastplate of righteousness, and numbered thee with the monastic company; and thou, appointed a warrior in the heavenly kingdom, didst struggle well against visible and invisible enemies, overcoming whom thou didst receive the crown of heavenly honor, which pray that we also may obtain, O venerable Barlaam, who lovingly honor thy holy memory!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Against the enemies warring with me grant help from on high, O Virgin unwedded of God, and teach me how, fleeing their crafty wiles, to overcome them: for nothing shall vanquish them unless thou assist those who especially honor with love thy most immaculate childbearing!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
Of old Habakkuk heard Thy marvelous report, O Christ, and with fear cried aloud: God shall come from the south, and the Holy One from the mountain overshadowed with leafy thickets, to save His anointed ones: glory to Thy power, O Lord![^96]

*Troparia:*
When the three warring ranks of darkness heard that they were stripped of thy love when thou wast clothed in the garment of salvation, they grieved against thee, and raging they stirred up thy father against thee; but thou didst set thy heart upon the power of the Lord!

Thy senseless father ran against the holy flock with many servants unto the cave, and having driven all away, he dragged thee out who wast bound with warmest love unto God, hoping in Whom thou didst cry aloud:

*(Right page — Folio 30 recto, running header: TO BARLAAM, ABBOT OF THE CAVES:)*
glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Dragging thee outside, O blessed one, thy father stripped off the holy mantle and cast it into the ravine, likewise also taking the helmet of salvation which was upon thy head he cast it away; then he clothed thee in a bright and costly garment.[^97] But thou, despising all these things, didst unswervingly emulate the angelic life, crying aloud and shouting: glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Glory...*
Forcibly clad in a most radiant garment, thou didst trample it into the mire, together with it trampling the proud enemy, who craftily through various natures drew men to sing: glory to Thy power, O Lord![^98]

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Strip me, O Mother of God, who am covered with the shame of fierce iniquities, and my defiled garment, granting me repentance and compunction, do thou make clean by thy grace, that gratefully I may stand before thee, crying: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, garment of boldness for the naked!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Make Thine unsetting light to dawn, O Christ, in the hearts of those who faithfully sing of Thee, granting us peace which passes understanding: therefore from the night of ignorance toward the day running in Thy light, we glorify Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparia:*
When thou wast forcibly led home in radiant garments, O blessed one, the unsetting light of God illumined thy heart; therefore thou didst not look upon deceptive light, but beholding God alone at thy right hand, thou didst glorify Him as Lover of mankind!

When he who had prepared a feast called thee unto an immortal banquet, O venerable one, thou didst not taste of the delicacies of the table, but looking down with thine eyes, thou didst raise thy mind unto the heights, contemplating the table of the heavenly King, and the beatitude of him who shall eat bread in the kingdom of heaven!

Preparing in thy heart a fair palace for the Spirit, thou didst not sit in an honorable place in thy palace, but in a single corner, choosing rather to select a narrow place of life for the glorification of the Lord, Lover of mankind!

*Glory...*
Thou didst not incline thy heart unto the crafty words of thy betrothed, nor didst thou ascend her bed, taking heed unto the word spoken: The venerable ones shall rejoice in glory upon their beds,[^99] of whom thou art now a partaker and inheritor!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Raise me up, O Mother of God, who lie upon the bed of evils, and by thy guidance direct my life, that I may spend the rest of my time in repentance, and glorify thee, the Mother of Christ our God, Who is abundant in tender mercy!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
I am wholly held fast by boundless passions, and fall into the monster of evils; but lead me up from corruption, O God, *(Left page — Folio 30 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
as of old Thou didst lead up Jonah,[^100] and through faith grant me passionlessness, that with the voice of praise and in the spirit of salvation I may sacrifice unto Thee!

*Troparia:*
Wholly surrounded by distressing trials, thou didst cry aloud unto the Lover of mankind, O venerable one, to deliver thee from the snare of the fowlers, and thou didst remain fasting for three days, nourishing thy soul with prayer alone, and saying with the prophet: Lead me up from this corruption, O God, and I will live the rest of my years serving Thee in true deeds!

Concerning thee, O blessed one, when thou wast in such great trial, fervent prayer was made by the holy ones in the cave; for the righteous cried, and the Lord heard them,[^101] and delivered thee from sorrow, inclining the heart of thy father unto mercy, who had pity on thy patient endurance, and having kissed thee lovingly, released thee in peace unto the holy cave!

A mournful voice was then heard in the house of thy father: thy parents wept for thee their beloved son; thy wife bitterly lamented thee her betrothed; menservants and maidservants groaned with weeping for their master, and all accompanied thee with tears; but thou, having departed rejoicing as from Egypt into the promised land,[^102] O holy one, didst offer thanksgiving unto God, that He delivered thee from the fierce ruler of this world!

*Glory...*
Glory to Thee, God alone praised in the Holy Trinity, that Thou hast saved Thy servant from the manifold adversary: whom save also through the prayers of Thy pleasing one, who honor his memory!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Save, O Sovereign Lady, those who are destroyed by passions, and stretch forth thy hand unto the despairing, that being saved through thee, in a true spirit we may offer praise unto thee with thine Only-begotten Son and God!

*Kontakion, Tone 3:*
Thou didst not fear thy father's threats, thou didst not yield unto his flatteries, neither wast thou cast down by womanly deceit, O all-honorable Barlaam, but didst remain invincible against them, hoping in thy Lord, Whom pray for our souls!

*Ikos:*
Beholding thy voluntary suffering, who will not be astonished, O most blessed Barlaam! For thou didst expose the demonic wiles, and having manfully stood against passionate lusts, how much didst thou suffer from distress, that thou mightest present thy soul pure unto God: Whom also fervently pray for us, O holy one, that we may be delivered from carnal furies and invisible

*(Right page — Folio 31 recto, running header: TO BARLAAM, ABBOT OF THE CAVES:)*
invisible enemies through thy prayers unto God assisting our souls!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
The holy children who of old showed themselves for piety's sake, passed through the unbearable flame of the furnace as through a bridal chamber, and singing in concord, sang the hymn: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers![^103]`

*Troparia:*
Thou didst pass through the flame of temptation, O venerable one, and remaining unburned by it, thou didst rather burn the enemy who invisibly warred with thee, against whom the prayers of thine instructor assisted thee; for he unceasingly cried: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

Anthony was amazed at thy patient endurance, that warring against fierce Belial, thou didst not fear his manifold wiles, whereby even if thou wast tripped, yet by patience and humility thou didst conquer, and like the youths didst sing the hymn: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

When thou didst return with victory unto the fathers in the cave, all things were filled with joy when they saw thee crowned, and thine elder also rejoiced receiving thee in health; therefore they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Glory...*
Seeing, O venerable one, that upon the root of his teachings thou hadst grown up with virtues like a fair-flowering tree, and the shoot of thy youth had borne the fruits of aged grace, Anthony marveled, and thankfully cried aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Sprinkle with the dew of thy grace, O pure Virgin, the tree of my life rotting with sins, that at least it may put forth small branches, and bring thee fair, immortal fruit of humility and prayer, crying: Rejoice, acquisition of immortal fruit!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
The furnace moist with dew showed forth the image of a supernatural wonder of old, for the fire burned not the young children, showing forth the seedless childbearing of Christ: therefore singing let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
A wonder! That the gift began to operate while thou wast yet in thy youth, seeing which Anthony appointed thee leader over the brethren in his own place;[^104] and thou wast a good shepherd unto the fathers who sing and highly exalt the Lord unto the ages!

Having received the beginning of leadership, thou didst also receive anew a beginning for thy life, O holy one: for thou didst add labors unto labors, and ascetic deeds unto vigilance for God: so that all who desire to sing of the Lord unto the ages might have Thee as an exemplar!

*(Left page — Folio 31 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
A good instructor and father wast thou, O blessed one: for as thou thyself didst never alter thy rule, so also thou didst instruct and entreat the brethren to have care for salvation, and to attend unto the soul, an immortal thing, if they desire to highly exalt the Lord with the angels unto the ages!

Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.

Seeking the noetic Jerusalem, thou didst journey unto the sensible one, sweetly enduring all manner of labors on the way for Christ's sake:[^105] and having lovingly kissed His life-giving Tomb, thou didst return singing of the Lord unto the ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
All-hymned Mother, Sovereign Lady of the heavenly ranks, Queen of heaven and earth, tear me away from earthly things, and grant me to seek the things on high, and make me worthy in the heavenly Jerusalem to sing of thee, and to highly exalt the Lord unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
The ineffable mystery of the Virgin: for she was shown forth as heaven, the cherubic throne, and the light-bearing bridal chamber of Christ God the Almighty: her piously as Theotokos do we magnify!

*Troparia:*
Thou enjoyest ineffable joy, O father, now: for with the angelic ranks standing before the unwaning Light, direct us also by thy prayers to walk in the light of the commandments of the Lord!

From this life thou hast departed unto eternal life, O venerable one, and hast received the place of rest: preserve our life from harm, beset by the assaults of adversaries, and grant that through thy guidance we may attain unto the dwelling of paradise!

Returning from the city of Constantine,[^106] thou didst depart in soul unto the all-radiant city of the heavenly King, and thy relics thou didst leave in the monastery of the Holy Mountain, which thou didst command to translate unto the cave, where now they lie incorrupt, pouring forth an ever-flowing fountain of wonders!

*Glory...*
O only God Who art glorified in Thy saints, glorify all who faithfully bow down before Thy magnificent glory: for behold, we bring Thy pleasing one unto Thee in prayer, for whose sake save us all!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
We glorify thee, O Virgin, with unceasing lips, and beatify thee as is our duty: for thou alone art blessed among women, having brought forth the Father's Only-begotten Word: with Whom the faithful together magnify thee!

*Exapostilarion:*
Having wounded thy soul with sweet desire for the love of Christ, O venerable one, and desiring ever to be filled with it, thou didst renounce the corruptible world with its lusts, thou didst leave house and parents, thou didst disregard thy betrothed: therefore a hundredfold hast thou received, and dost inherit eternal life: of which that we also may not be deprived, grant help through thy prayers!

*Theotokion:*
Fair palace, Mary Bride of God, whose beauty the Most High desired, by the virginity of the All-Holy Spirit He dwelt in thine all-consecrated womb, and took flesh for our sake: make my soul a palace for the dwelling of the heavenly King, that I, thine unprofitable servant, may ever magnify thee!

*At the Praises, Stichera: Tone 3, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Далъ еси знамение):*[^107]
Having acquired a spotless life, patient endurance, meekness, and unfeigned love toward God, great abstinence, and divine compunction, O father, as an angel in the flesh didst thou live, O blessed Barlaam, an intercessor for our souls!

Preparing the beauty of thy soul as a house for the Holy Spirit, thou didst despise the beauty of this world, and having laid an immovable foundation upon the rock of the commandments of the Lord, thou didst complete it with the height of humility, and becamest a dwelling of the All-Holy Trinity; Whom pray for us, to give beauty unto our souls, and that we may become a spiritual receptacle of grace!

Having caused the noetic tree of thy life to grow by renunciation of the world, thou didst bring forth fruits of virtues unto God, O most blessed father; receiving from Whom the recompense of thy labors, thou now enjoyest the tree of life in paradise: of whose sweetness that we also may be accounted worthy, pray Jesus, the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
The instructor of monastics, the great one among the fathers,[^108] seeing thy warmth toward God, O father, filled with many virtues, entrusted unto thee the God-gathered flock, which having tended well upon the pastures of thine instruction, thou didst lead whole unto Christ God, the Chief of shepherds: Whom now entreat, to preserve His spiritual flock in peace, and to keep it from the wolves that destroy it!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Sovereign Lady, accept the prayers of thy servants, and deliver us from every distress and sorrow!

*Great Doxology, and Dismissal.*
*At the Liturgy, the service is common to a Venerable Father.*
