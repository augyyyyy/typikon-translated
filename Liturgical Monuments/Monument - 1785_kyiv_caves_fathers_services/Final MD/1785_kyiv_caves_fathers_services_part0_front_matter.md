*(Right page — Master title page framed within an ornate Baroque woodcut border:)*
To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit:
By command of the Most Pious, Most Autocratic, Great Sovereign, our Empress Catherine Alekseyevna of all Russia[^2]; during the reign of her heir, the Right-Believing Sovereign Tsesarevich and Grand Duke Paul Petrovich, and his consort, the Right-Believing Sovereign Grand Duchess Maria Feodorovna, and the Right-Believing Sovereigns and Grand Dukes Alexander Pavlovich and Constantine Pavlovich; and the Right-Believing Sovereigns and Grand Duchesses Alexandra Pavlovna and Elena Pavlovna;
And with the blessing of the Most Holy Governing Synod, this book:
SERVICES TO THE VENERABLE FATHERS OF THE CAVES, whose incorrupt relics rest in the Near and Far Caves,
Was printed in the Holy Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra, in the Stavropegion of the same Most Holy Synod, under Archimandrite Zosima[^3]:
In the year from the creation of the world 7293, and from the Nativity in the flesh of God the Word 1785, Indiction 3, in the month of August.

*Troparion, Tone 4:*
As a fiery pillar and radiant sun that shone forth upon the hill of the Caves, we bring before Thee, O Christ, the great Anthony unto prayer together with the whole synaxis of the God-bearing fathers; for their sake we pray: grant grace unto our monastery, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
Beholding with noetic eyes the choir of ascetics gathered in mind, come, O people, let us honor them with praises, and bowing before their relics, offer gifts of love; for they are intercessors before God and pleaders for our souls.

### Notes

[^1]: *Poluchnik* (получникъ): 19th-century Ukrainian/Ruthenian administrative term denoting a pensioner or recipient of an allowance, here identifying the codex's private owner, Yevstafiy Savych, who recorded his ownership as a parental blessing on February 17, 1836.
[^2]: Catherine II ('the Great'), Empress of Russia (reigned 1762–1796). The imperial family members enumerated are the Grand Duke Paul Petrovich (later Emperor Paul I), his wife Maria Feodorovna (Sophie Dorothea of Württemberg), and their children Alexander (later Emperor Alexander I), Constantine, Alexandra, and Elena.
[^3]: Archimandrite Zosima (Valkevich), Archimandrite of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra from 1762 to 1786. Under his tenure, the Lavra printing press issued numerous landmark liturgical editions.
[^4]: Engraver monogram (СФНМ) in Cyrillic, identifying the master engraver of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra workshop who executed the Dormition copperplate/woodcut.
