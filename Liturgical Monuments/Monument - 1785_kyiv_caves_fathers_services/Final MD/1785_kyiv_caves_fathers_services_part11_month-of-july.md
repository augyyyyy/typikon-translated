# MONTH OF JULY

*On the 10th Day:*
*Service to our Venerable Father Anthony of the Caves in Kyiv, who was the Founder of all Russian Monastics:*[^399]
*At Small Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried", we sing Stichera on 4, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly hosts" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*[^400]
Thou didst follow the footsteps of the great fathers of the Holy Mountain, O Father Anthony; and having struggled as a bodiless one in the flesh, thou wast an example of virtues unto many; wherefrom thou wast also accounted worthy of gifts of wonders from Christ, to heal *(Left page — Folio 94 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
...to heal diverse human diseases; and now pray that our souls may be saved! *(Twice)*
Having been the leader of monastics, O Father Anthony, thou didst offer thyself as a firstfruit unto God, mortified through abstinence; and by the high wounds of humility and measureless fasting thou didst put the enemy to shame, O venerable one: pray that our souls may be saved!
Thy light-bearing memory, O father, venerable Anthony, is shown forth full of joy and gladness; wherefrom thy sacred children, having come together, keeping festival spiritually, we glorify the Lord Who sincerely glorified thee!
*Glory... Tone 2:*
Today the Holy Mountain of Athos, having wonderfully received thee, O father, like another immigrant Abraham,[^401] rejoices; and thy homeland, O blessed one, having thee again as its fellow-citizen, exceedingly makes merry; and the cave wherein thou didst struggle is adorned concerning thee; the most radiant city of Kyiv, keeping festival at thy memory, sings songs of celebration unto the Lord, Who granted thee once unto it inextricably; and we thy children, instructed by thy teaching, now celebrating thy most glorious falling asleep, pray: pray that our souls may be saved!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Mother of God, thou art the true vine...
*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 2, Podoben: "House of Ephratha" (До́ме є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^402]
Gloriously hast thou departed from earthly unto heavenly mansions, O father, and wast accounted worthy of the honors of the venerable there: with them pray that our souls may be saved!
*Verse: Honorable in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^403]
By abstinence thou didst mortify the bodily senses, O father; therefore into the dark cave as into a most radiant bridal chamber thou didst enter, whither thou didst bring a multitude of disciples unto Christ!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord; in His commandments he shall greatly delight.*[^404]
Thy flock which honors thee, O venerable one, and celebrates thy falling asleep: deliver it from barbarian invasion, and pray that our souls may be saved!
*Glory... Both now... In the same Tone:*
A most pure church of the Mother of God thou didst erect, even as she herself willed, and in it thou didst assemble a multitude of disciples, together to hymn the Mother of God as well; whom pray to intercede, O Venerable Anthony, as thou didst promise, not to abandon thy sacred flock, delivering and saving it from the assaults of adversaries, that we may all unceasingly honor thee with hymns as a father who loves his children, asking through thee to receive great mercy from Christ!

*Troparion, Tone 4:*
Having gone forth from worldly turmoil, in renunciation of the world thou didst follow Christ according to the Gospel; and having lived an angel-like life, thou didst reach the calm haven of the Holy Mountain of Athos; wherefrom by the blessing of the fathers having come unto the hill of Kyiv, and there having finished thy life in love of labor, thou didst enlighten thy homeland; and having shown unto a multitude of monastics the path leading unto the heavenly kingdom, thou didst bring them unto Christ: Whom entreat, O Venerable Anthony, that He may save our souls![^405]

*At Great Vespers, we sing "Blessed is the man", the 1st antiphon.*
*At "Lord, I have cried", we sing Stichera on 8: Tone 4, Podoben: "Called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*[^406]
When divine desire came upon thee, O venerable one, then thou didst account worldly attachment as belonging to the foolish-minded; therefore thou wast an emigrant from thy homeland unto the Holy Mountain, and there in the midst of those fathers thou didst shine forth like a luminary through virtue, O Anthony: pray Christ, Whom thou didst serve from youth, to save and illuminate our souls!
When thou wast inflamed with the love of Christ, O venerable one, then thou didst hate the wisdom and glory of this world as passing away; and having made a cave for thyself, therein thou didst struggle as a bodiless one; therefore from on high thou wast accounted worthy of the honors of the bodiless: with them standing before Christ, pray that He may save and illuminate our souls!
When thou didst receive divine love in thy heart, O father, then into the dark cave as into a most radiant bridal chamber thou didst enter, and wast there enriched with understanding of ineffable things, to behold future things as present, and to speak clearly unto all: pray Christ, Whom thou didst serve from youth, O venerable one, to save and illuminate our souls!
*Other Stichera of the Venerable One, Tone 5, Podoben: "Venerable father" (Прпб́не ѻ́ч҃е):*[^407]
O venerable Father Anthony, having illuminated thy mind by the grace of the All-Holy Spirit, thereby as farsighted thou didst prophetically foretell the wrath of God about to come upon Orthodoxy;[^408] wherefrom thou didst also pledge salvation from warriors unto Shimon;[^409] and before the building of the church, thou didst foretell that he would be laid therein: therefore as an intercessor of all good things we ever hymn thee, asking through thee to receive great mercy from Christ!
O venerable Father Anthony, fulfilling the commandments of the true Shepherd and Teacher Christ, displaying thy love even after thy falling asleep, by thy prayers and apparition thou didst alter the deadly disease of a brother who lived slothfully concerning the repentance of possessions;[^410] and granting him time for repentance, thou didst guide others unto chastity: therefore *(Left page — Folio 95 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
...therefore pray Christ that our souls may be saved!
O venerable Father Anthony, with God in love for mankind thou didst direct the ship of thy mind, and didst cross the passionate deep of the sea of this world without turmoil; guided by the rudder of the All-Holy Spirit, thou didst reach the calm haven of purity, where is the dwelling of the saints and the rest of the venerable: with them pray, we beseech thee, that we also may obtain entrance there!
*Glory... Tone 6:*
When thy harsh life and labors for God's sake became known unto the fathers of the Holy Mountain, then by divine providence thou wast sent again unto thy homeland: "Go, my child," he said, "that there also through thee God may be made known unto those who know Him not!"[^411] Wherefrom having come, O venerable one, thou didst enlighten thy homeland, and wast a leader of monastics, having brought a multitude of them unto Christ: therefore also upon us who celebrate thy most radiant falling asleep, pray that we may be preserved unassailed by enemies, and that our souls may be saved!
*Both now... Theotokion:*

*At the Litiya, Stichera idiomethe, Tone 4:*
For the sake of thy life of the same name, by divine providence thou wast named Anthony,[^413] receiving thy appellation from deeds; for having gathered a multitude of disciples who follow thy teaching, thou didst bring them unto God: but as a companion of the venerable and with the venerable standing before the Trinity, pray for all who glorify thy most glorious falling asleep in hymns!
Fleeing the passionate darkness of this world, O Venerable Anthony, thou didst make thy dwelling in the cave, O most marvelous one, and didst there penetrate unto the depth of divine understanding; therefore thou wast also accounted worthy as farsighted to discern things afar off, and to speak clearly of things near at hand; and grieving over the sins of men, thou didst prophetically foretell the barbarian invasion about to come for human sins:[^414] but pray, O father, we beseech thee, that we may be delivered from such a threat, and that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 4:*
Now thy all-honorable and sacred body, O Venerable Anthony, lying in the cave wherein while yet in this life thou didst labor much, abundantly gushes forth healings unto those who come with faith: therefore we pray thee, through thy prayers ever grant also health of soul unto those who call thee blessed!
*Glory... Both now... Tone 8:*
Great boldness and faith toward God didst thou acquire, O Venerable Anthony; for emulating the prophet Gideon when testing victory by the fleece,[^415] even so thou, O father, didst desire to know by dew where the place was pleasing unto the Master and Queen; therefore thou didst say: "If I have found grace before Thee, let there be dew upon all the earth, but on the place where Thou willest a church to be, let there be drought!" And again: "Let there be dew on the place!" And both of these things, O father, thou didst obtain; therefore thou didst erect a most pure church of the Mother of God:[^416] with her pray Christ, we beseech thee, that our souls may be saved!
*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 4, Podoben: "O all-praised martyrs" (Всехва́льнїи мꙋ́ченицы):*[^417]
Thou didst follow the footsteps of great Anthony, O venerable one: for he dwelt in the desert for the sake of silence, and was accounted worthy to be a converser with angels; while thou didst shut thyself up in the underground places of the cave, and wast accounted worthy to behold the ineffable Light; and having emulated his life, thou didst receive his name and deeds: with him standing before the Holy Trinity, pray that our souls may be saved!
*Verse: Honorable in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^418]
Like a most radiant luminary thou didst shine forth in a dark place, and like a palm tree thou didst blossom venerably in the house of God, and didst offer thyself as a most holy fruit unto God, a living sacrifice without blood, O father; therefore having come together in love, we all worthily call thee blessed evermore!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord; in His commandments he shall greatly delight.*[^419]
The cave wherein thy sacred body lies, O Venerable Anthony, by the power of the All-Holy Spirit heals diverse infirmities of those who come with faith: demons are driven away from men, and the paralyzed depart having received health: while we, praising the Lord, say: Honorable in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints!

From generation to generation thy purity is glorified, for the sake of which having suffered much, thou didst receive the blessedness of the pure in heart; having passed over from this temporary life, thou pure beholdest the All-Holy Trinity; remember also us, O venerable one, who venerate thy memory!
The Lord called thee a dead man, for thou didst deaden fleshly lusts by marvelous endurance, and thou hast power to deaden passions in those who touch thy relics, as thou didst unto the much-suffering John, who having received peace in his bones, magnified God Who glorifies His saints![^537]
Rejoice, O mountain, that hast in the cave the incorrupt relics of the marvelous Moses; sing with praises *(Left page — Folio 112 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE)*
...sing with praises, ye assemblies of monastics: for behold, ye have an assistant and an intercessor in sorrows; bow down before the shrine of his relics, touching it with your lips, and ye shall find rest in your members!
*Glory:*
We glorify the All-Holy Trinity, for He gave unto us the luminary of the world, our Father Moses; celebrating whose yearly memory, we the faithful with one mind magnify the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit in one Godhead!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Ever do we glorify thee, O pure Virgin, for thou hast gathered thy faithful servants in this place, where many receive healing, bowing down before thine unhewn icon, and calling upon the prayer of our Caves Fathers![^538]
*Exapostilarion:*[^539]
O tri-hypostatic Light, true Creator and God, enlighten the darkness of our souls: for behold, we bring before Thee in prayer Thine pleased one, and with him we pray: calm the storm of our sins, and deliver us all from sorrow!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Brighter than the sun, O Mary Birthgiver of God: enlighten with grace all who bow down before thee and call upon thy name, and keep this monastery from every evil, and fill those who dwell in it with spiritual joy and gladness: for thou art the mediatrix of our joy!
*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "As one valiant" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*[^540]
Gathered together today with voices of rejoicing, O ye lovers of feasts, let us offer hymns of praise unto God, glorifying the all-blessed father, and falling down before the shrine of his relics, let us cry: Standing before the Master, forget not us who honor thy memory with love, but pray that we be delivered from all temptations and grievous encirclements!
Who can hymn thee worthily, O venerable father? For in this temporary life, though in a mortal body, yet thou didst live spiritually, being outside the world and the flesh, having an unceasing desire to be loosed from the flesh and to be with Christ;[^541] reigning now with Him, remember also us who glorify thee!
Rejoice, all-honorable choir of God-bearing Caves Fathers, for thou hast acquired a good luminary...

*(Right page — Folio 113 recto, running header: TO MOSES THE HUNGARIAN)*
...a good luminary, the crown of virgins and the beauty of martyrs, the pure dwelling of the Holy Spirit, Moses the spiritual God-seer; crowning whose memory with praises, we pray you: pray Christ God and the Master that we, your children, may be delivered from misfortunes, and not be deprived of the Heavenly Kingdom!
*Glory, Tone 6:*
As a true follower of Christ and a faithful hearer of His Gospel, we praise thee today, O venerable father: for looking unto eternal recompense, thou didst account all the fair things of this world as refuse, choosing rather to be a captive, that thou mightest keep thy mind free from sin; and having no care for the body, thou didst surrender thyself unto torments, that with streams of blood thou mightest drown the tormenting mental enemy; being thyself now in a place of rest and enjoying the joy of the saints, from on high enrich us also with thy gifts, who lovingly celebrate thy memory!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
*O Birthgiver of God, thou art the true vine...*
*Great Doxology, and Dismissal.*
