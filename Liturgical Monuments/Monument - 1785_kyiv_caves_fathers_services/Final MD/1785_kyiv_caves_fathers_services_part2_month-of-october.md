# MONTH OF OCTOBER

*On the 5th Day:*
*Commemoration of our Venerable Fathers: Damian the Presbyter, Matthew, and Jeremiah the Clairvoyant:*
*At Vespers, on "Lord, I Call," Stichera, to 6, Tone 4, Podoben: "As One Valiant" (Yako dobla):*
Being held fast by spiritual and bodily sicknesses, through the oil of thy mercy, as possessing sacred anointing from on high, O venerable Damian, speedily heal me who cry unto thee from the heart; and through fervent prayers unto God grant humility unto my heart, and cleanse the festering wounds of my soul, that with pure

*(Right page — Folio 13 recto, running header: TO DAMIAN, JEREMIAH, AND MATTHEW:)*
lips I may offer praise unto God Who is glorified in His saints.

Having illuminated thy soul with the grace of the Most Holy Spirit, O all-lauded Jeremiah,[^49] thou didst receive the gift from God to foretell future things as though present; and imitating the ancient prophet, thou didst unerringly predict all things, and didst foresee the secrets of the human heart: with thine illumination enlighten our hearts also, and ask great mercy from Christ God!

Thou didst follow Matthew, the preacher of the Word of God, O marvelous one;[^50] for as he boldly rebuked the disobedient Egyptian people, so also thou, O blessed one, didst put to shame the cunningly woven wiles of the subterranean wicked Ethiopians; and now we pray thee, O father: drive away the demonic legions that noetically darken our minds, and deliver our souls from afflictions!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
Come, O ye people, let us honor with praises the three most blessed fathers, three protectors: Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew; and let us offer doxology unto God Who sits on high, Who hath given His favorites unto the Ruthenian land for help and as pleaders for our souls.

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*At the Aposticha, Glory... Tone 6:*
Bright and radiant hath your memorial shone forth today, O our all-blessed fathers, for ye brightly shine with the rays of miracles upon those who come unto you and ask fervent intercession from you; therefore do we also fall down before your relics, eagerly kissing them, and we glorify God Who gave you such gifts, wherewith enrich us also, and ask great mercy from Christ!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*Troparion, Tone 4:*
Having enlightened your hearts with the light of the commandments of Christ, ye drove away the darkness of passions; therefore did ye become the dwelling of the Trinity, O thrice-numbered fathers: Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew; having received grace from Whom, ye heal the sick, foretell things to come, and as partakers with the angels, pray Christ God to grant us a portion with the saints!

*Canon, Tone 5, Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Unto God our Savior, Who guided the people in the sea with dry feet, and engulfed Pharaoh with all his host, unto Him alone let us sing: for He is glorified!

*Troparia:*
Let us offer praise unto God our Savior, glorifying His most blessed favorite Damian, *(Left page — Folio 13 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE OF THE CAVES:)*
the wise physician of the sick, and let us cry aloud unto Him Who gave such gifts: for He is glorified!

Thou didst imitate the angel-like life of thine instructor with unceasing zeal; instructed by whom, thou wast adorned with virtue, unceasingly remaining without sleep in doxology through all nights, and didst bless God: for He is glorified!

Standing now with the prophets before God the Savior, as possessing boldness, pray, O divinely inspired Jeremiah, that hearts may be illumined with grace, of those who beatify thee with faith and cry unto the Savior: for He is glorified!

*Glory...*
The all-seeing eye of God, beholding thy pure heart, O blessed Matthew, gave thee the gift to know the secrets of men, and to turn them unto profit for all, that guided by thee we may sing unto God: for He is glorified!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Being the noetic Sun, O pure Virgin, illuminate our minds darkened by passions and pleasures, that enlightened by thee, we may sing unto God: for He is glorified!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
By the power of Thy Cross, O Christ, establish my understanding, that I may sing and glorify Thy saving Ascension.

*Troparia:*
Strengthened by the power of God, thou wast meek and silent, O blessed one, ever with fervent prayers unto God, and didst occupy thyself with the diligent reading of the Holy Scriptures, and thereby wast established in the Lord.

The Word of God was thy food and drink, wherewith thou didst nourish thy soul, O venerable one, and thy body with bread only and water; and on this didst thou feed until the day of thy death, subjecting unto the spirit all the passions that arose.

Having fulfilled thy years in ripe old age, O Jeremiah, thou didst receive the gift to foresee the secrets of the heart, and didst rebuke in private the evil thoughts of men, and didst instruct all those who were influenced by the counsel of the devil.

*Glory...*
Having come unto the first fathers of the Caves, thou didst settle with them, and lived in monastic life conquering all carnal lusts; therefore didst thou receive the gift from God to see visibly the faces of the subterranean Ethiopians, O marvelous Matthew!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Terrify and drive away by the visitation of thy countenance, O Theotokos, the wicked Ethiopians who transform themselves into various forms and act within our members, that without confusion we may sing of thy saving power!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
Having received twofold grace from God, Thou healest twofold passions: spiritual ones through diligent prayers, and bodily ones through the anointing of holy oil; heal us who suffer from both sicknesses, and drive away the passions that oppress us, and pray Christ God that eternal life may be obtained by those who honor thy holy falling asleep.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
All-spotless Bride, spacious dwelling of the Word of God: cleanse me who am a foul dwelling of passions, and show me forth as a bright habitation of virtues; drive away the darkness of lawlessness, and make me worthy of the communion of the unwaning Light through thy prayers, that I may sing of thine ineffable mercy!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I heard the report of the power of the Cross, how paradise was opened thereby, and I cried aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparia:*
Thou wast shown forth as a sweet-smelling cluster of the vineyard of the Mother of God, watered with the dew of the teachings of the venerable fathers, and didst distill the wine of healings unto all who flee unto thee with faith, O Damian, minister of God!

Protected by the armor of faith and the flame of love, O blessed one, thou didst drive away the enemies who harm men with sicknesses; for thy diligent prayer, passing through the clouds, drove away all diseases, O marvelous Damian!

As a clairvoyant of the thoughts of men, thou didst foretell those who would turn their backs to the enemy and who desired to depart from this holy sheepfold; and though rebuking them with a sharp word, O Jeremiah, thou didst teach them to stand firmly in the battle and be unshaken against the enemies.

*Glory...*
With open eyes thou didst visibly behold once in the church a demon walking about in the likeness of a soldier and throwing onto the hems of the brethren's robes sticky flowers, that they might depart before the blessing, darkened by sleep:[^51] those, O father, didst thou instruct to stand unsluggishly and protect themselves with the weapon of the Cross!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Surrounded by demonic legions and wounded by the passions that flatter the heart, strengthen me, O Mother of God, and grant me grace to conquer their deceitful wiles, that I may flee under thy pure protection!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Keeping early vigil we cry unto Thee, O Lord: save us, for Thou art our God, and we know none other beside Thee.

*Troparia:*
Thy blessed instructor Theodosius commanded thee to pray over those who came in common sickness; and thou, being humble and well-obedient, *(Left page — Folio 14 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE OF THE CAVES:)*
considered himself unworthy of the gift of healing; but fulfilling the command of his father, through prayer and oil he granted health unto the sick.

As a good traveler, thou didst well traverse the path of this life, confirming thyself with monastic ascetic labors; and when thou didst attain unto thine end, having cleansed with sickness that which was lacking, purer than gold didst thou depart into painless rest.

By the foresight given unto thee by God, whatsoever thou didst see, O blessed one, thou didst tell truly unto every man, and thy word was fulfilled in truth; therefore, as the glory of prophets and companion of the venerable, we worthily beatify thee, O Jeremiah!

*Glory...*
Never didst thou leave the church before the conclusion of the singing, but wast always the last, having an insatiable desire to abide in the doxology of God; for this reason God glorified thee before angels and men, O God-bearing Matthew!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Knowing thee, O pure Virgin, to be the holy mountain of God and the house of the glory of the Most High, falling down in humility, I pray: lead me up unto the height of virtuous life, and make me a pure temple for the reception of divine gifts!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
The abyss encompassed me, the sea-monster was a tomb for me; but I cried unto Thee, the Lover of mankind, and Thy right hand saved me, O Lord!

*Troparia:*
Having walked the straight paths upon earth, at thy departure thou didst mingle prayers unto the Crown-Giver with tears, asking His goodness that He deprive thee not of the Kingdom of Heaven and of dwelling together with thy father, the venerable Theodosius.[^52]

Desiring not to be deprived of communion with the venerable in the unapproachable Light, with humility didst thou send up supplications unto Christ, and didst obtain that which thou didst ask: for the wise Creator informed thee with a marvelous assurance, that with good hope thou mightest depart to rest in the repose of the righteous.

In deep old age thy youth was spiritually renewed, O venerable Jeremiah; for having left the old bodily covering, thou didst enter with thy soul into a new dwelling, unto the ancient prophets for the vision of the glory of God.

*Glory...*
Sitting once beneath the church semantron (bilo),[^53] thou didst behold a great crowd of dark demons; and having questioned them, thou didst learn that they were going unto a certain brother; for this reason thou thyself didst lovingly instruct many of the brethren how to beware of them: teach us also, O blessed one, that they may not enter

into the doors of our hearts!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Deliver me, O Good One, from the demonic whispering of passions, that their deceitful darkness may not enter into the house of my soul; bar the noetic doors of the heart with fear, and grant me to be vigilant and attend unto my salvation!

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
As the ancient prophets, having received the grace of prophecy, unerringly foretold in the Spirit the things that were to come, so also ye in our life foretold the evil things that come upon us because of our sins: and now, that evils may not overtake us, ask from Christ God the correction of our deeds and great mercy!

*Ikos:*
Imitating those whose names ye bear, and having lived in an angelic manner upon the earth, ye were counted worthy of great gifts, O Jeremiah and Matthew; for foretelling things to come, ye led many unto correction, and astonished all men by the putting to shame [of the demons]; and now living after this temporal life in the endless life with the angelic choirs, ask from Christ God the correction of our deeds and great mercy!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
He Who saved the children that sang hymns in the furnace of fire: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*
An angel of God stood before thee, O holy Damian, in the likeness of the venerable Theodosius, and falling upon thy breast, lovingly kissed thee as one beloved of God and marvelous even unto the angels; and thou didst sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

He who appeared said: "Behold, child, concerning what thou didst pray unto the Lord, I am sent to announce unto thee that according unto thy petition it shall be unto thee, and thou shalt dwell in the Kingdom of God with the saints": hearing these words, thou didst joyfully sing, O Damian: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

What is sweeter, and what is greater, than to live together with the saints, where Christ lives in the unwaning Light? There also a place of rest was prepared for thee, O Damian; pray that we also who sing may obtain it: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

In the choir with the saints, in honor with the prophets, in standing with the angels, thou wast counted worthy, O Jeremiah, to be appointed by God: unto Whom pray that we also may stand before Him, purely singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Glory...*
Ever having love toward the brethren, O Matthew, whatsoever was revealed unto thee thou didst disclose for the benefit of all, and didst instruct them, coming unto their cells, to weep over their sins, *(Left page — Folio 15 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE OF THE CAVES:)*
and to sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Delivered through thee from all demonic wiles, O most pure one, unceasingly unto Him Who was born of thee do we cry: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
Him Who of the Father before the ages was begotten as Son and God, and in the last times was incarnate of the Virgin Mother, praise, O ye priests, and supremely exalt Him, O ye people, unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
Recognizing him who appeared unto thee, sent from the Father of lights, to be thy God-bearing instructor Theodosius, having summoned him, thou didst inquire concerning what he who stood there said, and what he promised thee, O Damian; and he was astonished at what was told, and supremely exalted the Lord unto the ages.

With joy didst thou question the promise from thine instructor and father, saying: "Shall it be, O father, as thou didst promise me upon my petition?" And he answered: "I know not, child, whereof thou questionest me; for an angel of God was he who promised thee, and he shall settle thee there where the saints sing unto the Lord unto the ages."

Being joyful and of good hope from such an assurance, thou didst depart unto the light-bearing places, escorted by light-bearing angels, with whom thou also singest unto the Lord unto the ages.

All thy knowledge was for instruction unto those being saved, O venerable Matthew, nor did the superior hide thine instruction: awakened wherewith, he quickly attained unto beatitude, where the saints supremely exalt the Lord unto the ages.

*Glory...*
After many revelations didst thou depart in deep old age, O Matthew, to stand with unveiled face before the throne of God: pray for us also, O father, that with thee we may be counted worthy to see the Lord and supremely exalt Him with praises unto the ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
He Who is one in essence with the Father, God the Word, took up His abode in thy pure womb, O Virgin, and was shown forth consubstantial with us in the flesh: Whom blessing, we supremely exalt Him unto all ages.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
Thee who above mind and word didst ineffably bring forth in time the timeless Mother of God, with one mind do we the faithful magnify.

*Troparia:*
What shall we call thee, O blessed place? A heavenly firmament, where the Trinity of fathers shine like stars with miracles, and enlighten those who celebrate their honorable memory and piously beatify them with hymns!

Having departed from the temporal light unto the eternal light, with your light-bearing rays illuminate our hearts; and ask through your prayers that walking in the light of the commandments of the Lord, with you we may behold the unwaning Light!

Having received the grace of healings from God, speedily heal us who are held fast by passions; for with faith we flee unto your relics, and praying, magnify you with hymns!

*Glory...*
Let us glorify God, Who hath glorified His saints, Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew, who having lived in their bodies like angels glorified Him; Whom we also pray, that He may not deprive us of His heavenly glory together with them, who today crown their memory with praises!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Remembering my end, when the time shall come for me to be loosed from the bonds of the flesh, I tremble, for the day of judgment approaches: from which who shall deliver me, if not thou, O pure Virgin? Therefore do I ever flee unto thee in doxology!

*Exapostilarion:*
O fathers who lived upon earth, ye are holy in heaven also, and stand with the angelic choirs before God: having received from Him recompense for your labors, forget us not, but guiding us, direct us into the right way, that walking therein we may find mercy on the day of judgment!

*Theotokion:*
Behold, thy temple is here, and truly marvelous;[^54] for being thyself the temple of the glory of God, by thy providence thou didst build it and didst adorn it with the angel-like life of the holy fathers for the sake of our way of life: transform our deformity into goodliness, that the King may desire the beauty of our souls, and pour out His great mercy upon us!

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Hast Given a Sign" (Dal yesi znameniye):*
Ye became a radiant receptacle of the Most Holy Spirit, O all-blessed fathers, and enlightened by Him, in a manner worthy of the vision of God ye were enriched with the gift of prophecy; ye acquired foreknowledge of things not yet come to pass, and true understanding: therefore we pray you: pray for us unto Jesus the Lover of mankind, and the Savior of our souls!

Like true prophets, by unerring indications ye foretold all things that were to come, and proclaimed them, O venerable fathers: Matthew, and Jeremiah with marvelous Damian, by deed *(Left page — Folio 16 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE OF THE CAVES:)*
for the Holy Spirit knows how to enlighten pure hearts, and to grant the grace of revelation: therefore do we glorify Him Who glorified you, Jesus the Lover of mankind, and the Savior of our souls.

Kindled with divine love and with desire for the blessings to come, with affection ye traversed the narrow path, counting its afflictions as nothing; therefore now rejoicing together in refreshment, unto those who honor you with love, grant a portion of your gifts, and pray for us, O Damian and Jeremiah with Matthew, unto Jesus the Lover of mankind, and the Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
Gathered together, O ye faithful, let us beatify the trinity of venerable fathers: the one as having received the gift of healings, the other as being filled with true prophecy, and the third as perceiving things invisible; honoring the day of whose festal triumph, we glorify our God, and pray that He make peace in the world, and save our souls!

*On the 14th Day:*
*Commemoration of our Venerable Father Nikola Sviatosha, Prince of Chernihiv:*
*At Vespers, "Blessed is the man," the first antiphon.*
*On "Lord, I Call," Stichera, to 6, Tone 8, Podoben: "O Marvelous Wonder" (O preslavnago chudese):*
O divinely wise, blessed Nikola, seeing that the fashion of this world passes away, and that all things are subject to corruption, thou didst quickly exchange the image of thy glory; thou didst forsake diadem and scepter, and looking toward the heavenly kingdom, didst choose to walk the narrow path; and now *(Left page — Folio 17 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
dwelling in the heavenly mansions, pray Christ God that we may obtain the blessedness of the eternal kingdom!

O Venerable Nikola, desiring to acquire a life corresponding unto thy name, thou didst conquer this world; thou didst forsake the throne of princely rule, counting wealth, glory, and earthly happiness as refuse; clothed in the poor monastic habit, thou didst follow Christ Who became poor for our sake: standing before Whom now, pray fervently for us, that peace and great mercy be granted unto our souls!

O Father Nikola, father most blessed, foreseeing with the eye of thine understanding that kings and princes are deprived of their glory and depart naked into the tomb, thou didst cast away the matching honor of the authority of this age, following the footsteps of Christ, for unto Him in humility didst thou labor: therefore thou didst obtain unfading blessedness, wherein remember us also who honor thy memory!

*Glory... Tone 2:*
Foreseeing, O Christ God, that Thy dominion abides unto the ages, Thy favorite Nikola cast away scepter and diadem, and distanced himself from manifold cares, running unto the tranquil life of those who practice monasticism: he was a true and faithful servant, having ministered unto Thee his Savior in holiness and righteousness.

*Both now... Dogmatikon: The shadow of the Law passed away... Then, the Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. And 3 Readings for the Venerable One: see folio 30 verso.[^56]*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the Heavenly Ranks" (Nebesnykh chinov):*
Kindled with divine desire, O venerable Father Nikola, thou didst despise worldly glory, and loved monastic life, becoming of one zeal with the venerable fathers: dwelling together with them now, pray for those who honor thy translation!

*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord (Ps. 111:1 [LXX 111:1]).*
Thou wast wholly enslaved unto Christ, directing all thy desire toward Him; for the sake of His love thou didst renounce this world, loving Him Who loved the human race: therefore with hymns we beatify thee, and with love bow down before the shrine of thy relics.

*Verse: Blessed are all they that fear the Lord (Ps. 127:1 [LXX 127:1]).*
Deprive not of thine intercession, O father, all those who reign now from thy race, and the people who praise thee; but stand before all as a fellow-helper, and assist in afflictions and tribulations, granting profitable things through thy prayers!

*Glory... Tone 5:*
O venerable father, thou didst not give rest unto thy body until thou didst cleanse the passions of thy heart; fulfilling the commandments of Christ, thou didst leave house, kinsmen, and friends; running unto the tranquil haven of the Mother of God, thou didst subject thy greatness and glory under the feet of all; shining forth in fasting, thou didst mortify the body, and being ever guided by the Spirit of God, thou wast shown forth as a son and heir of the unwaning Light: where living now, pray that we also may obtain mercy on the day of judgment!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who art full of grace, intercede through thy prayers, and ask for our souls a multitude of compassions, and cleansing of our many transgressions, we pray thee!

*At Matins, on "God is the Lord," Troparion, Tone 8:*
Having left the homeland and glory of thy princely rule, a prince of humility, thou didst fervently follow Christ, O our venerable father Nikola;[^57] therefore thou didst receive from Him the eternal kingdom and glory in heaven, where keeping festival, remember us who faithfully honor thy memory!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion: All beyond understanding...*

*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "The Soldiers Standing Guard" (Grob tvoy):*
The Ruthenian people are filled with gladness, and joyfully sing of thy memory, O blessed one, having thee as a swift helper in afflictions and deliverance in all diseases: therefore, as one who received from God the grace to drive away sicknesses by a word, cease not even now to assist all who honor thy venerable falling asleep!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Knowing thee to be a fountain of healings, O Virgin, we thy sinful servants flee unto the warmth of thy heart; and possessing natural care for us, forget not those who are tossed about by tribulations, but stretching out thy hands, pray unto thy Son, that we may receive swift relief from them!

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "Thou Hast Appeared" (Yavilsia yesi):*
Having hated that which passes away in the light upon earth, O blessed one, and loved from the heart that which is ever-existing in heaven, these things after thy many labors didst thou inherit: where living, pray unto Christ for all of us, O venerable one, who in hymns honor thy memory!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
By thy holy childbearing, O Virgin, thou didst renew the nature of the earthborn decayed by passions, and didst raise it up from death unto incorruptible life: therefore as is due, O pure Mother, we ever beatify thee, as blessed throughout all generations of the faithful.

Let thy holy forefather rejoice today, the autocratic Vladimir sitting on the height of the throne, for from his root thou didst sprout as a good branch, O most blessed Nikola, bearing upon thyself fitting fruit, as an image unto all who desire to be saved and in humility to follow Christ, even as thou didst do, O venerable father; and now as they rejoice in thy glory, the fruit of thy monastic labors abides unto the ages!

Deprive me not of the Kingdom on high, O Lady who rulest over heavenly and earthly things; for seeking this, I keep aloof, being overcome by passions that ever wage war against me: behold, I flee unto thee with unhesitating hope!

[^49]: Saint Jeremiah the Clairvoyant (commemorated October 5, c. 1070), who was tonsured under Saint Anthony and received the gift of discerning the secret thoughts and spiritual struggles of the brethren.
[^50]: Saint Matthew the Clairvoyant (commemorated October 5, late 11th century), who saw demonic spirits in the form of dark "Ethiopians" (a traditional monastic idiom for demons in early Christian and Kyivan literature) attempting to seduce the monastics.
[^51]: The vision of Saint Matthew recorded in the *Kyiv Caves Paterikon* (Discourse 20): while standing during the service, he saw a demon in the guise of a soldier walking through the church and throwing sticky flowers (*lyepki*) onto the monks' robes; those to whom the flower stuck became drowsy and left the service before the dismissal, whereas those who resisted were untouched.
[^52]: Saint Theodosius of the Caves (commemorated May 3 and August 14), Abbot and co-founder of the Kyiv Caves Monastery, spiritual father of Saint Damian, whose appearance the angel assumed when visiting Damian on his sickbed.
[^53]: The church semantron (*bilo* / *klepalo*, би́ло): a resonant wooden board suspended outside the monastery church, struck with a wooden mallet to summon the brethren to services. Saint Matthew was resting beneath it after Matins when he saw a legion of demons marching toward a monk's cell.
[^54]: The Great Lavra Church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos, miraculously founded in 1073 and consecrated in 1089, regarded as the earthly dwelling of the Mother of God, who sent Greek master-builders from Constantinople with her icon and relics.
[^55]: Prince Sviatoslav Davydovych (monastic name Nikola Sviatosha, c. 1080–1143), Prince of Lutsk and Chernihiv, son of Davyd Sviatoslavych and grandson of Grand Prince Sviatoslav II of Kyiv. In 1106 he renounced his princely rank and entered the Kyiv-Pechersk Monastery, becoming the first East Slavic prince to embrace monasticism. He served as gatekeeper, kitchen laborer, and orchardist, and funded the construction of the Holy Trinity Gate Church (Troitska Nadbramna tserkva) above the monastery gates.
[^56]: The three Old Testament readings for a Venerable Father (Wisdom 3:1–9; Wisdom 5:15–6:3; Wisdom 4:7–15) are indexed to folio 30 verso of the 1785 print (physical leaf p44), where common festal readings are printed in full.
[^57]: Slavonic typographical error in the 1785 Lavra print: *па́пене* (papene) appears on Folio 18 recto as an epigraphic misprint for *прпⷠне* (prepodobne, "O venerable").
[^58]: Saint Joasaph of India: The protagonist of the Christianized tale of Barlaam and Josaphat (*Повѣсть о Варлаамѣ и Іоасафѣ*), traditionally attributed to Saint John Damascene, who renounced his royal inheritance to pursue asceticism in the desert, serving as the quintessential patristic exemplar for Christian royalty renouncing secular rule.
[^59]: Prince Nikola Sviatosha served for three continuous years in the monastery kitchen (*пова́рня*) chopping wood and preparing meals for the brethren; when his brothers Princes Iziaslav and Vladimir urged him to cease such menial labor, he requested permission to remain for an additional year.
[^60]: Saint Nikola Sviatosha served for three continuous years as the monastery gatekeeper, sitting by the outer gates of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra, strictly regulating entry and greeting all visitors with deep prostrations, regardless of their social rank.
[^61]: Following his three years as gatekeeper, Prince Nikola was assigned to the monastery refectory (*трапеза*), where he washed dishes, carried water, and set out food for the monks.
[^62]: At the end of his cycle of menial obediences, the superior and brethren directed Saint Nikola to work in the monastery garden and orchard (*вертоград*), where he tended trees and produced fruits for the community.
[^63]: The Syrian or Greek physician Peter, who had served as court physician to Prince Nikola before his tonsure, was sent by Nikola's brother Prince Iziaslav to admonish him to stop degrading himself through menial labor and harsh fasting; Saint Nikola instead converted Peter to monastic life.
[^64]: Saint Nikola Sviatosha had excavated a burial niche for himself in the Near Caves of Saint Anthony; when the former physician Peter fell mortally ill and wept that he had not prepared a tomb, Saint Nikola ceded his own prepared burial place to Peter.
[^65]: Codicological and hagiographical witness from the *Kyiv-Pechersk Paterikon*: Following the tonsure and death of the monk Peter, Saint Nikola Sviatosha lived an additional thirty years in the monastery without leaving its walls, falling asleep in the Lord around 1143.
[^66]: The contact relics of Saint Nikola Sviatosha: his wooden headrest, brass pectoral cross, and hairshirt (*власяница*). According to the Paterikon, during an illness of Prince Iziaslav, Saint Nikola sent him his hairshirt, which immediately restored the prince to health; Iziaslav later wore it into battle as an invulnerable armor.
[^67]: Saint Nestor the Chronicler (c. 1056–1114), monk of the Kyiv-Pechersk Monastery, disciple of Saint Theodosius, and author of the fundamental Ruthenian chronicle *The Tale of Bygone Years* (*Повѣсть временныхъ лѣтъ* / *Primary Chronicle*), as well as the *Life of Saint Theodosius* and the *Passion of Saints Boris and Gleb*.
[^68]: Saint Nestor entered the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra at the age of seventeen during the abbacy of Saint Theodosius, under whose spiritual direction he was trained in monastic ascetical discipline, later receiving the diaconate from Abbot Stephen.
[^69]: Reference to the miraculous foundation of the Great Lavra Church of the Dormition (1073): according to the *Paterikon*, dew fell upon the entire region except the foundation site, and fire fell from heaven to consume the dew and trace the foundation boundaries, an event witnessed by Saint Nestor as a young novice.
[^70]: Saint Nestor was ordained to the holy diaconate by Abbot Stephen (successor of Saint Theodosius), as recorded in his *Life* in the *Kyiv-Pechersk Paterikon*.
[^71]: In August 1091, under Abbot John, Saint Nestor was appointed to excavate and uncover the incorrupt relics of Saint Theodosius from the Far Caves, subsequently participating in their solemn translation into the Great Dormition Church on August 14.
[^72]: Saint Barlaam, first Abbot of the Kyiv-Pechersk Monastery (c. 1020–1065), son of the Kyivan boyar Ivan Yanovych; tonsured by Saint Anthony despite the fierce opposition of Grand Prince Iziaslav and his father; later appointed by Iziaslav as superior of the Monastery of Saint Demetrius in Kyiv. While returning from a pilgrimage to Constantinople and Mount Athos, he fell asleep in the Lord at the Holy Mountain Monastery in Volodymyr-Volynskyi; his relics were later translated to the Near Caves of Saint Anthony.
