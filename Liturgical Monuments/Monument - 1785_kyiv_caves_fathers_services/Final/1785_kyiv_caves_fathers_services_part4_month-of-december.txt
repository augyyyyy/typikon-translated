# MONTH OF DECEMBER

*On the 2nd Day:*
*Service to our Venerable Father Athanasius the Recluse of the Caves:*
*At Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera: Tone 4, Podoben: "As one valiant" (Яко добля):*
Desiring to mortify thy flesh, thou didst enter into the narrow reclusion as into a grave, O father, and didst bury it unyieldingly through ascetic deeds; but before the general resurrection thou didst show forth a certain rising of the dead, having risen from the dead after two days: and living now in immortality,

*(Right page — Folio 33 recto, running header: TO ATHANASIUS THE RECLUSE OF THE CAVES:)*
living, pray that we also may escape eternal death, and obtain great mercy from Christ God!

"He that believes in Me, as the Scripture hath said, though he were dead, yet shall he live forever,"[^110] was fulfilled on thee in deed, O venerable Athanasius: for having died unto the world and the flesh, yet cooperating with God by good deeds, He raised thee up unto a better life, and thou wast an astonishing wonder unto all, and rather unto profitable amendment: which grant unto us also through thy prayers, who pray, celebrating thy memory!

Declaring the paths of eternal life, Thou didst not suffer Thy venerable one to see corruption,[^111] raising him up after two days, as Thou didst four-day-dead Lazarus, having broken the bonds of death by the calling of Thy divine voice: by this call us up also, O Christ the King, and raise us up from dead deeds, we pray Thee, O Giver of life and Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Tone 3:*
After Thy rising, O Christ the Savior, the bars of death were shattered, and faithful people are no longer held under its dominion, as Athanasius, marvelous among the venerable, showed us by his terrifying rising, and by three deeds gave profitable amendment unto monastics; confirm this also in our hearts, O Savior, we pray Thee, who honor the yearly memory of Thy venerable one!

*Both now... Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion.*
*At the Aposticha, from the Octoechos:*
*Glory... Tone 2:*
With showers of tears, O venerable one, thou didst drown the death that held sway in Judea, and resembling the Giver of life Who abode three days in the tomb, having risen from the dead after two days, thou didst choose the narrowest path unto eternal life; having attained unto which in the land of immortality, look down upon us thence without delay, drive away the passions that slay us, and make us partakers of thine immortality, who on this present day celebrate thy memory, O father Athanasius most blessed!

*Both now... Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion.*

*Troparion, Tone 3:*
Desiring to obtain the beatitude of those who weep,[^112] day and night thou didst unceasingly weep, having in mind the hour of judgment: therefore after thy falling asleep thou didst find consolation in heaven, O venerable Athanasius. Grant that we also may ever weep here for our sins, so that escaping endless weeping, we may receive eternal consolation there!

*Canon, Tone 1:*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Let us all sing a song of victory unto God, Who hath wrought marvelous wonders with a high arm, and saved Israel, for He hath been glorified!

*(Left page — Folio 33 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
Desiring now with clayey lips to sing of thy dread falling asleep, O father, despise me not, but be bountiful unto me, who cry out to pray now on the day of thy solemnity, on which thy memory hath been glorified!

Perceiving human life to be cut down in this world as by the shadow of death, O venerable one, that having crushed it within thyself thou mightest hasten unto the firm Rock: and having drowned the assaults of death by virtues, thou singest unto God, for He hath been glorified!

*Glory...*
Having died first unto the world by the renunciation of its many-passioned deeds, O holy one, thou didst direct the steps of thy journeyings unto unshakable life; therefore being now a son of life, look upon us who sing unto God in thy memory: for He hath been glorified!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
My soul slain by voluptuous passions, do thou make haste to bring to life, O pure Virgin: for thou art the Mother of Life, and dost grant resurrection unto the dead!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
The stone which the builders rejected, the same became the head of the corner: this is the stone upon which Christ confirmed the Church, which He redeemed from among the nations.

*Troparia:*
Bound with the cord of a life pleasing unto God, O most blessed one, death as a captive had no power over thee: for even if it seized thee, yet it was commanded to return thee again for the profit of many, through Him Who by His death redeemed all from death!

Dead and bound with the edge of the mantle thou didst lie upon the bed, O father, and the Providence on high sought to work wonders through thee: for after two days He called back thy soul, and alive thou wast found, unto the astonishment of all who looked upon thee, who had seen thy falling asleep!

*Glory...*
Thy sickness, O venerable one, was not unto death, but unto the glory of God; for having died, thou didst receive death as a sleep for the relief of thy sickness, and after two days as from sleep awaking, thou didst wash thy face with streams of tears!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having cut off the sickness of death by thy holy childbearing, and given life unto all the world, I alone am left dead by defiled deeds: O Mother of God, lay thy hand upon me, and I shall find a better life!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
Looking unto immortality, that thou mightest escape eternal death, thou didst choose a narrow life, O most blessed Athanasius, in thy mortified body: therefore marveling at the marvelous dispensation shown upon thee, we glorify the only Lover of mankind, Who hath given life and incorruption unto all!

*(Right page — Folio 34 recto, running header: TO ATHANASIUS THE RECLUSE OF THE CAVES:)*
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Those who were subject to the slavery of death hast thou freed by thy childbearing, O Virgin Bride of God; therefore the generations of Christians praise thee with hymns, for thou hast given us life and incorruption!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
The sun was lifted up, and the moon stood still in its order: Thou wast lifted up upon the Tree, and didst establish Thy Church upon it.[^113]

*Troparia:*
Lying lifeless, thou didst rise, and having received breath, thou didst come back unto existence, O father: seeing which those who stood by glorified the power of God with fear!

Seeing how terrible is the hour of death and judgment, O blessed one, thou didst weep bitterly, and pour forth silence and groaning, and thy tears became for many a depiction of the dread judgment!

*Glory...*
Ever remembering thy departure, O holy one, thou didst weep in this painful life: and thine existing lamentations moved thee, having sensibly seen what manner of fear and trembling those who depart possess: which that we also may escape, pray, O venerable father!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
I fear not the dread judgment, nor do the bitter torments of Gehenna terrify me, for from many passions my heart is hardened: by the substance of thy tender mercy, O pure Virgin Mother, melt it, that it may become sensible through the remembrance of dread torments!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Grant us Thy peace, O Son of God, for other than Thee we know no God, we call upon Thy name, for Thou art the God of the living and of the dead!

*Troparia:*
Seeing thy weeping and disconsolate lamentation, those who stood by, terrified, asked that thou mightest speak a word unto them for their profit: and thou didst speak three profitable things unto them: to keep obedience, repentance, and prayer,[^114] as hearing the beginnings of virtues; in which that we also may be confirmed, pray, O father Athanasius!

Being thyself obedient unto the commandments of Christ, thou didst tell the brethren that obedience is above all virtues, O venerable one: for even after death it dies not, but preserves life with God, Who is appointed Judge of the living and of the dead!

*Glory...*
Repentance and prayer are two fiery wings, and by these thou didst declare an unhindered passage through the aerial toll-houses,[^115] O blessed one; thou didst choose the path of silence, whereby thou didst hasten unto the highest things, leaving instruction unto those who asked: grant amendment unto us also, who celebrate the day of thy rejoicing!

*(Left page — Folio 34 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O swift intercessor for the Christian race, Virgin Mary, having received the prayers of thy singers into thy most pure hands, bring them unto thy Son, and ask remission of sins for us, and grant tranquility unto those who are in passions!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Thou didst cast me into the depths of the heart of the sea, and didst save me, O Savior, from the slavery of death, and didst loose the bonds of mine iniquities!

*Troparia:*
Having utterly renounced life and seeing the sensible light, thou didst enter into a dark reclusion, exchanging the sensible for the vision of noetic light:[^116] which that we also may obtain through thy prayers, O father, having renounced our iniquities!

That thou mightest bring to life the flesh mortified by ascetic labors, O holy one, thou didst ever drench it with streams of thy tears, remaining twelve years in the cave buried alive, brought to life by the hope of immortal life!

*Glory...*
The tree of life, thy flesh, O venerable Athanasius, brought forth fruits of repentance for all, of which grant that through thy love I also may taste, that I may be strengthened to loose the bonds of mine iniquities!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Thou art the ever-flowering Tree, O most pure Virgin, covering with thy shadow those scorched by the heat of iniquities; cover us also with it, who are burned by passions, and let us sing: Rejoice, tree with fair and leafy shade, beneath which many are sheltered!

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
As one named for immortality,[^117] after thy death thou didst rise alive; and having shown how exceedingly bitter is death, thou didst weep bitterly; now having found unending joy, pray for us, that we may escape eternal weeping!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
The fire touched not, neither did it distress Thy children in the furnace, O Savior; then the three as with one mouth sang and blessed, saying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*
Having spent thy life in weeping and groaning, and having made white the robe of the angelic habit with streams of thy tears, thou didst depart unto the tearless regions, rejoicing and singing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

The angelic ranks arrived on the day of thy falling asleep, and having peacefully received thy spirit, they conducted it unto the paradise mansions, where living, remember us,

*(Right page — Folio 35 recto, running header: TO ATHANASIUS THE RECLUSE OF THE CAVES:)*
O venerable father, who sing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Glory...*
Twice wast thou a dead man in this life, O holy one: and first indeed all saw thee returning again unto bodily form, and now loosed a second time, thou rejoicest with the angelic ranks; where forget not us also who sing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Make haste to bring me to life, who am dead by evil deeds, O Sovereign Lady: for in thee have I placed my hope without doubting, as having brought forth the immortal King and Giver of life unto all the world!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
O heaven of heavens, and ye waters that are above the heavens, bless, sing of the Lord, and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
The heavens have joyfully received thy spirit into their bosom, and we rejoice bowing down before thy relics, O venerable one; from them grant grace unto those in need, and make us worthy to sing of the Lord unto the ages!

The angels celebrate having thee as a fellow citizen, and the Caves mountain rejoices, containing the shrine of thy relics as a most radiant pearl; by this enlighten us who are held fast by the darkness of passions, and grant that we may sing of the Lord unto the ages!

Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.

Thou wast well-pleasing in this life unto thy Master, O father, and didst receive the gift of healing sicknesses: visit us who are held fast by passions, as through prayers thou didst heal the sick Babylas,[^118] and make us worthy to sing of the Lord unto the ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
I am in sorrow and grief, reflecting upon my defiled life, and have no hope: unto thee alone, the Joy of all the world, do I flee, O pure Virgin, and pray that joy may shine forth upon my soul unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
He hath showed strength with His arm, for He hath put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted the humble, the God of Israel; wherein the Dayspring from on high hath visited us, and hath guided us into the way of peace.[^119]

*Troparia:*
Show love unto us who pray unto thee, O father, and by thy prayer grant healing unto those who suffer with sicknesses of soul: especially direct into the way of peace those who are warred upon by the assaults of the enemy!

The Great Lavra sings of thy labors and ascetic deeds, O venerable one, and the cave ever glorifies thee, preserving thy wonderworking relics: bowing down before which we also pray thee: Direct our steps into the way of peace!

*(Left page — Folio 35 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
Arise swiftly, and come, heal my soul, O father Athanasius most blessed: for behold, out of great sickness of soul I cry unto thee: having no relief, I rather pray thee: guide me into the peaceful path of salvation!

*Glory...*
Glory to Thee, All-Holy Trinity, O Father unoriginate, with the co-eternal Son, and the Spirit of Thine Holy Spirit: Whom grant unto my heart, through the intercession of Thy pleasing one, that I may find the peaceful path!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
If anyone glorifies not thy pure conception, and sings not of thine ineffable childbearing, O Mary Bride of God, let him fall away from eternal glory, and let him not be a partaker of the Light and a son of the ever-existing peace!

*Exapostilarion:*
With hymns let the people praise Athanasius, marvelous among the venerable, for alive he died, and after two days he rose, and again fell asleep; he lives no longer held by death, but began life anew as a partaker, as a son sharing in life, and an heir of the resurrection of the righteous!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Who will tell of thy marvelous mysteries, O pure one? Or who will declare the coming to pass of ineffable things in thee? For in time thou didst bring forth the Son begotten of the Father before the ages, the Firstborn of life and resurrection; entreat Him therefore, that we also may become heirs of resurrection and of life!

*At the Praises, Stichera: Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Егда от древа):*[^120]
Come, ye people, let us sing of daytime Athanasius, who is sung among the angels in heaven, the beauty of ascetics, and the radiance of the venerable; whose life emulating, he unflaggingly traversed the narrow sweat, having wounded every passion by abstinence; he had uncontainable desire toward God, before Whom he now stands with the angels, and prays for our souls!

Come, brethren and assemblies of feast-keepers, every company of God-bearing fathers, let us bring crowns of hymn-sung flowers, having journeyed unto the tomb of the most blessed father, and let us fall down, saying: O venerable and all-glorious one, fellow citizen with the angels, forget not thine intercession unto God, the Good of all, that we may obtain eternal life, and mercy on the day of judgment!

Come, ye choirs of monastics, unto the shrine of the many-miraculous relics of venerable Athanasius, and let us honor the divinely eloquent lips that instruct us unto obedience, and unto perpetual repentance and prayer, and by these virtues ascending unto God as upon fiery wings, may we find grace and great mercy!

*(Right page — Folio 36 recto, running header: TO ATHANASIUS THE RECLUSE OF THE CAVES:)*
*Glory... Tone 8:*
O most blessed father,[^121] holy among the saints Athanasius, thine instruction became an amendment for many, and a raising up from falling through the remembrance of the judgment to come, which expecting thyself also after thy resurrection, thou didst pour forth inconsolable tears from thine eyes: standing now with joy before Christ, pray for those who sing of thy precious falling asleep, that they may receive remission of sins, and great mercy on the day of judgment!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Sovereign Lady, accept the entreaties of thy servants, and deliver us from every distress and sorrow!

*(Left page — Folio 36 verso, full-page woodcut engraving within an ornamental border: The Venerable Nikon the Dry of the Caves standing bound in chains in the midst of a Polovtsian encampment, with an inscribed cartouche: "The holy one was brought invisibly into the Caves church of the Most Holy Mother of God"):*

*On the 11th Day:*
*Service to our Venerable Father Nikon the Dry:*[^122]
*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera: Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Далъ еси знамение):*
Pillar of patient endurance, O Nikon glorious and God-bearing, firmly set in the love of God, wherein thou didst remain unmoved through many trials: therefore we glorify thee, O father, on this present day, celebrating thy memory, as one glorified in the heavens by Jesus, the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls!

Tried with fire and afflicted in every way,

*(Right page — Folio 37 recto, running header: TO THE VENERABLE NIKON THE DRY:)*
thou didst cry aloud thanksgiving, O blessed one, unto the Creator and God: therefore delivered from the hand of the impious, thou didst stand in the holy church,[^123] and now standing before Him in the church of those who keep festival, pray Jesus, the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls, that we may be delivered from tribulations!

The fire of thy love toward God extinguished the sensible fire, and the streams of thy blood drowned the hosts of the noetic enemy, and thou didst conquer those who tortured thee, O venerable one: therefore also Jesus, the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls, granted thee the honor of the heavenly ranks!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
The multitudes of monastics are amazed at thy patient endurance, how being afflicted by the infidels, tried by fire, and cut with knives, enduring heat and scorching, hunger and thirst, and having the hope of thy salvation in God, thou didst easily traverse the sweat of the body, and didst attain unto ultimate beatitude: blessed art thou having thus labored for Christ, and thou hast received the reward of thy labor, a dwelling with the angels, and communion in the divine Light: therefore we pray thee, O most blessed Nikon, as having boldness before Christ God, ask peace for the world, and great mercy for our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion.*

*At the Aposticha, from the Octoechos.*
*Glory... Tone 6:*
Come, assemblies named for Christ and loving Christ, and gatherings of monastic ranks: come, let us piously bring philosophic songs of praise as a gift unto the holy cave, and let us bow down before the relics of glorious Nikon, so that by his holy prayers we may receive the crown of unfading glory from Christ God, the Savior of our souls!

*Troparion, Tone 3:*
Having loved the good God, Thou didst receive the monastic life, and having struggled well therein, Thou wast taken captive by the Polovtsians;[^124] being much tortured by them, O stout-hearted Nikon, by the grace of Christ Thou wast delivered from them invisibly: and now abiding in the unapproachable Light, pray that we may be saved who honor thy memory!

*Canon to the Saint, Tone 4:*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
I will sing unto Thee, O Lord my God, for Thou hast delivered Thy people from the bondage of Egypt, and covered the chariots and power of Pharaoh![^125]

*Troparion:*
Enable me, O Lord my God, to sing of the all-honorable memory of Thy venerable one, *(Left page — Folio 37 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
and to honor glorious Nikon in hymnody, send the all-holy Paraclete, that girded with vigilance I may offer a hymn unto Thee, Who givest strength unto all!

Who will worthily praise thee, O most blessed father, who from youth didst love thy Lord, and followed Him with voluntary poverty even unto death? Therefore living now with the angels, pray that strength unto patient endurance may be sent also unto us!

*Glory...*
Having crossed without stumbling through the darkness and gloom of the lustful world by burning love for Christ, and having been numbered with the brightness of the angelic habit, thou wast as if outside the flesh: therefore thou singest unto Him Who hath given thee strength!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Drive away, I pray thee, O pure Virgin, the darkness that has blinded my noetic eyes with passions: for thou art the light-receiving candle unto those who are in darkness, kindled by immaterial fire, and thou dost guide unto the understanding of the divine draught: Rejoice, lamp of unwaning Light!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
As the barren church from among the nations brought forth, and the assembly multiple in children languished,[^126] let us cry aloud unto our marvelous God: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Troparia:*
As she who was barren rejoices when she conceives in her womb, so the holy Caves mountain rejoices, having received thee as a light-bearer, and with monastic ranks sings: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

Emulating the first fathers, having joyfully laid the cross upon thy shoulder, O glorious Nikon, thou didst bear it magnanimously even unto death for the sake of the love of thy Lord, unto Whom thou now singest: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Glory...*
Armed with the shield of firm faith, thou didst go into captivity, O venerable one, and through magnanimity thou didst bind the enemy who sought to lead thee captive there into destruction, trampling the adversary with thy foot, and unto God Who strengthens thee thou didst sing: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Unable to overcome the enemy who rises up against me through the strength of passions, I flee unto thee, O Mother of God, help me: for thou art a strong pillar, a weapon in battle, and an unconquerable general!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
Desiring to cultivate the field of thy soul, thou didst lay upon thyself the cross like a plow, and with this as with a plow thou didst cultivate the soil barren of passions, sowing with tears and prostrations of ascetic labors,[^127] from which now thou reapest joyful

*(Right page — Folio 38 recto, running header: TO NIKON THE DRY:)*
sheaves,[^128] standing before Christ God: Whom pray, O glorious Nikon, as being an unceasing intercessor, for those who lovingly bless thy holy memory!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Rejoice, untilled field, that hast brought forth the incorrupt grain of wheat, from which the saving Bread was made for the world: with this nourish the souls of those who lovingly honor thine immaculate conception, O pure Virgin!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
For the sake of the love of Thine image, O Merciful One, Thou didst stand upon Thy Cross, and the nations melted away: for Thou, O Lover of mankind, art my strength and praise!

*Troparia:*
Thy love toward God was strong, O father, for Whose sake desiring to suffer from the infidels, thou in no wise didst desire to be ransomed;[^129] for being beloved of the Lord Who loved thee, thou didst set thy hope upon Him: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

Having lovingly received the chastening of the Lord, sweetly didst thou pass through the trial of sicknesses, O blessed one, saying with David: Blessed is the man whom Thou chastenest, O Lord;[^130] therefore also thou criest: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Glory...*
Looking unto the things to come, thou didst count present pains as nothing, O beloved Nikon; though fire and frost and iron touched thee, yet all these flowed away: but thou didst remain like a strong adamant, saying: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Not looking unto the things to come, my soul has cleaved unto the earth, and I am become like senseless beasts:[^131] but do thou, O pure Virgin, open my noetic eyes, and make marvelous thy mercy upon me, that rejoicing I may cry: Holy art thou and marvelous in righteousness!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Thou, O Lord my Light, art come into the world, the holy Light that turns from gloomy ignorance those who sing of Thee in faith!

*Troparia:*
Being afflicted in every way by the infidels, O father, thou didst not care for this, but didst cry aloud thanksgiving unto God, saying: Save me, O God, for the waters are entered into my soul:[^132] for Thou alone art He Who brings out those who are bound with courage, who sing of Thee in faith![^133]

Thy patient endurance, O most blessed one, confounded the Polovtsian who had taken thee captive; therefore for three years he continued torturing thee every day, afflicting thee with hunger, thirst, cold, and heat: but thou, O holy one, didst have hope in God, that He will deliver those who sing of Him in faith!

*Glory...*
The noetic enemy, seeing the streams of thy blood, the cutting of the sinews of thy shins,[^134] and other torments, caused the Polovtsian to hope; but neither hearing that deliverance was about to come unto thee, he placed heavy fetters *(Left page — Folio 38 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE:)*
to be placed upon thee: but thou, O father, didst cry aloud in the words of the Psalm: O God, attend unto my help![^135] For Thou alone art He Who savest those who sing of Thee in faith!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
With the arrows of thy help, O Virgin Bride of God, drown the invisible enemies who drag my soul into the abyss of Hades with the chains of passions, that being free from them I may cry unto thee: Rejoice, sea that hast drowned the noetic Pharaoh!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Prefiguring the three-day burial, the Prophet Jonah prayed in the sea monster: Deliver me from corruption, O Jesus, King of powers!

*Troparia:*
The iron of sorrows pierced thy soul, O glorious Nikon, until through suffering the martyr gave the deliverance of release sent unto thee swiftly from God, unto Whom thou didst cry aloud: In Thee, O Lord, have I hoped; in Thy righteousness deliver me, O Jesus, King of powers![^136]

*Glory...*
As once with Habakkuk,[^137] the right hand of the Most High seized thee invisibly; and those who guarded thee were amazed, hearing a voice from on high, and being unable to overtake God, for He alone is King of powers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O strong Deliverer, deliver me from the pit of the adversaries, for behold, my head is swallowed into the abyss, and I shall not come forth from the violent bars of Hades, unless thou grant me help, who art the Mother of the King of powers!

*Kontakion, Tone 6:*
Emulating the First Martyr,[^138] thou didst pray for those who tortured thee, and having delivered him from death, thou didst lead him unto Christ, O venerable martyr, glorious Nikon: therefore with the first sufferer beholding Christ at the right hand of the Father, pray for us, that we may not be cast outside the bridal chamber of Christ!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
The Abrahamite children of old in Babylon trampled the flame of the furnace, crying aloud in hymns: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers![^139]

*Troparia:*
While the time of the dread celebration of the mysteries was taking place in the church, seized by divine power from captivity, thou didst stand in the midst of the brethren, O glorious Nikon, and thy fetters and the blood dripping from thy shins struck all with awe, who lifted up their hands for thy deliverance, saying: Blessed art Thou, O God!

He who formerly held thee in captivity, O father, seeing thee afterward free in the midst of the brethren by the right hand of the Most High, came to compunction, and being baptized, made himself captive unto the obedience of Christ, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Glory...*
Blessed are the captives who have thee as a wise priest: for through thee, freed from sickness, they were delivered from the hands of the impious, and that which is said in the Scripture was fulfilled:

*(Right page — Folio 39 recto, running header: TO NIKON THE DRY:)*
The prayer of a righteous man avails much, working with power.[^140]

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Taken captive by noetic enemies, and bound with the chains of passions by fierce Belial, unto thee do I cry, O unwedded Mother: tear me away from them, that being free I may cry: Rejoice, thou who didst bring forth the Deliverer of captives!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
All-powerful Deliverer of all, coming down in the midst of the flame Thou didst moisten those who were pious, and didst teach them to sing: All ye works, bless and sing of the Lord!

*Troparia:*
Fearing the Deliverer of all and thine own Savior, in meekness of feet, O father, thou didst fear the torturer who sought to crucify thee: but rendering good for evil, thou didst heal him lying in sickness, so that he also, turning, might bless the Lord, and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

In the earthen vessel of thy body,[^141] how great a treasure of wonderworking do we honor, O blessed one, and we glorify the heavenly Father Who hath given thee such things for our profit; with this heal us also, we pray thee, that through thee we may sing of Him Who works marvelous things, and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.

Thou didst stand against the world and the flesh, and having bound both, thou didst subject them unto the spirit: therefore having ascended unto the world on high, thou rejoicest, and with the angels singest, and highly exaltest the Lord unto all ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Stand by to help us who swiftly call upon thee, O most pure Virgin, and strengthen us against the world and the flesh that lust against the spirit: for behold, we bring glorious Nikon unto thee in prayer, for whose sake grant us to highly exalt thee in the light of the saints unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
Eve indeed through the disease of disobedience introduced the curse, but thou, O Virgin Theotokos, through the budding of the childbearing of thy womb, didst cause blessing to blossom forth upon the world: therefore we all magnify thee!

[^122]: St. Nikon the Dry (*Никонъ Сухїй*, d. ca. 1101) was a monk of the Kyiv Caves Monastery. In 1096, during the raid on the Lavra led by the Polovtsian Khan Boniak, Nikon was taken captive along with St. Eustratius of the Caves and other monks. Nikon remained a captive in the Polovtsian steppe for over three years, enduring cruel tortures, starvation, and exposure to burning heat and freezing cold. Because his body became completely emaciated and dried from these tortures and fasts, he received the cognomen "the Dry."
[^123]: According to the *Kyiv Caves Paterikon* (Discourse 24), after three years of captivity, as Nikon predicted his impending release, his Polovtsian master cut the tendons of his legs to prevent escape. A few days later, Nikon was suddenly and invisibly transported through the air to the Dormition Cathedral of the Caves Lavra while the brethren were chanting the Eucharistic Canon during the Divine Liturgy.
[^124]: Referring to the Polovtsians (Cumans), a nomadic Turkic people of the Pontic-Caspian steppe whose recurring incursions against Kyivan Rus' in the 11th–13th centuries included catastrophic raids on the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra.

Mark dug graves for the brethren of the Kyiv Caves Lavra, and was granted power over the dead; Theophilus was his disciple who wept for many years in deep repentance.

[^147]: Another miracle recorded in Discourse 33: a brother died before Mark could finish digging his grave, so Mark commanded the dead brother to wait until morning; the dead man opened his eyes and remained alive until the grave was ready the next day, at which point he peacefully fell asleep again.

When Theophilus complained that the senior place belonged to him, Mark commanded the dead John to yield the place, and the deceased brother rose up and moved to the lower place. Terrified by this miracle, Theophilus spent the rest of his life in unbroken weeping and repentance.
[^159]: Discourse 33 of the *Kyiv Caves Paterikon* recounts that Theophilus wept so copiously throughout his remaining years that he filled whole vessels with his tears. An angel subsequently appeared to him, showing him a flask containing his tears and announcing that God had accepted his repentance.
