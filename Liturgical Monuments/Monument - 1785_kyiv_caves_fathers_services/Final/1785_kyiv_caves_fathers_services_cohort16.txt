# APPENDIX: SUNDAY THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES

*Sunday Resurrection Theotokia of the Eight Tones, at Vespers at "Lord, I have cried," and at the Aposticha.*[^791]

*Tone 1. At "Lord, I have cried":*[^792]
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of all the world, who blossomed forth from mankind and bore the Master, the celestial gate, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful; for she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead: she destroyed the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace, and opened the Kingdom! Having her therefore as the confirmation of our faith, we have the Lord Who was born of her as our Defender: take courage therefore, take courage, O people of God; for He will conquer the enemies, as the Almighty!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^793]
Lo, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for a Virgin thou hast given birth, and after giving birth thou hast remained even as thou wast before birthgiving; for He Who was born was God, therefore also He made nature anew: but O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple; but as carrying the Tender-hearted One in thy hands, have compassion upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*[^794]
The shadow of the law passed away when grace came; for even as the bush burned not when it was on fire, so a Virgin thou hast given birth, and a Virgin thou hast remained: instead of a pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness has shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ the salvation of our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^795]
O wonder new, exceeding all ancient wonders! For who has known a Mother who has given birth without a man, and carries in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? It is the counsel of God that has been born, Whom as an Infant having carried in thy hands, O all-pure one, and having maternal boldness toward Him: cease not to pray for those who honor thee, to show mercy and save our souls!

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^796]
How can we not marvel at thy God-man birthgiving, O all-honored one? For having received no trial of man, O all-immaculate one, thou didst bear in the flesh a Son without a Father, born of the Father without a Mother before all ages, Who underwent no change, nor mingling, nor division, but preserved intact the property of both natures. Therefore, O Mother and Virgin Sovereign Lady, pray Him that the souls of those who orthodoxly confess thee as Theotokos may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^797]
Without seed from the Holy Spirit, but by the will of the Father, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who exists before the ages from the Father without a Mother, but for our sake came to be from thee without a Father, Whom thou didst bear in the flesh, and didst nourish as an Infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray, that our souls may be delivered from tribulations!

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*[^798]
David the Prophet, the forefather of God, melodiously proclaimed concerning thee for thy sake unto Him Who worked great things in thee: At Thy right hand stood the Queen! For God Who was well pleased without a father to become man from thee showed thee forth as the Mother and mediatress of life, that He might renew His image corrupted by passions, and having found the sheep that went astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His will unite it with the celestial powers, and save the world, O Theotokos: Christ Who has great and rich mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^799]
Look down upon the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, assuaging the fierce assaults against us, and delivering us from every sorrow; for thee alone do we possess as a firm and sure confirmation, and we have acquired thy protection: let us not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady, who call upon thee; hasten unto the supplication of those who faithfully cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, help of all, joy, protection, and salvation of our souls!
