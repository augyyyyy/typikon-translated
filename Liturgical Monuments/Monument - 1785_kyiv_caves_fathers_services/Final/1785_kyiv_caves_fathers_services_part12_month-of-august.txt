# MONTH OF AUGUST

*On the 7th day:*
*Service to our Venerable Father Pimen the Much-Ailing of the Kyiv Caves.*[^542]
*At Vespers, Blessed is the man: the 1st antiphon. At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чино́въ):*[^543]
He Who foreknew thee from thy mother's womb for blessedness, by His providence granted an example of salvation, permitting disease like unto Job's upon thee, that tried thereby as gold in the furnace, thy body might be weakened through much affliction;[^544] and now...

*(Right page — Folio 114 recto, running header: TO PIMEN THE MUCH-AILING)*
...and now being in painless rest, remember us who suffer with passions of the soul, O Pimen, our all-blessed father!
Hating the world, O venerable one, and a life dragging down through fleshly pleasures, thou didst ask of the Lord for thyself that it be exchanged for a disease of many years, in which giving thanks, thou didst cry out thanksgiving unto God, saying after the manner of the Psalmist: My soul hath cleaved after Thee, so let Thy right hand uphold me, O Christ, guiding unto the right way and life eternal![^545]
Unto thee, O Venerable Father Pimen, according to the multitude of painful temptations which thou didst most lovingly offer in body, consolations now gladden thy soul in unapproachable light: for standing with the angelic ranks before the Master's throne, pray there also for us, that from on high we may obtain peace and deliverance from passions!
*Glory, Tone 6:*
Come, ye choirs of monastics, let us praise Pimen the Venerable, who was like unto great Anthony: for even as he received the monastic habit from an angel, so also this one was accounted worthy to be clothed in the monastic state by an angelic hand;[^547] with whom now wearing robes of incorruption, he rejoices with the righteous, keeps festival with the venerable, and with them prays Christ God for our souls!
*Both now... Of the Feast.*[^546]
*Entrance, Prokeimenon of the day, and three readings (see fol. 82 on the reverse).*
*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 8, Idiomela [Samoglasny]:*
The candle given unto thee by the angels prefigured the brightness of thy soul, O venerable father; therefore thy life was undefiled, and through trial it shone before men; honoring which, we magnify and glorify the Heavenly Father with the Son and the Holy Spirit!
*Verse: Honorable in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^548]
Beholding the desire of thy heart and thy love toward heavenly things, the supreme King sent His angels unto thee, O father, to clothe thee in the monastic habit, and to regale thee with heavenly singing, that sweetened by endurance of this diseased life, thou mightest crown its bitterness, and by diseases acquire painless rest!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord: he shall delight exceedingly in His commandments.*[^549]
Renunciation of the world, voluntary poverty, undefiled virginity, measureless endurance *(Left page — Folio 114 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE)*
...measureless endurance, and perfect love toward God, are truly great virtues, wherewith as upon an animate chariot thou didst ascend unto heavenly things, and didst stand before the Creator of all and God: pray Him also for us, O venerable one, that we may finish our course without offense, and obtain mercy in the day of judgment!
*Glory, Tone 8:*
Multitudes of monastics, we run unto the shrine of thy relics, O father; we bow down before thine incorrupt relics; we draw healing for our diseases; we receive strength against the enemies who war against us, and find victory over passions; we receive remission of sins, and are accounted worthy of the Lord's mercy through thy prayers!
*Both now... Of the Feast.*
*Troparion, Tone 5:*
Emulating the God-bearing Anthony in humble-mindedness, and being likened unto Job in endurance, thou didst desire for thyself not healing from disease, but rather its lengthening, O patient-spirited Pimen, lest having become healthy and being torn away from this place by thy parents, thou shouldst be deprived of the fellowship of the venerable and their order; therefore beyond all hope thou wast tonsured by the angels themselves; with them pray Christ God that our souls may be saved!
*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast.*
*At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the Feast. Glory, to the Venerable One. Both now, of the Feast.*
*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-unoriginate Word" (Собезнача́льное Сло́во):*[^550]
Great endurance didst thou possess, O father: for lying upon a bed of sickness for many years, beset with corruption from head to foot, thou didst give thanks unto God, choosing in this life to rot entirely, that in the next thou mightest be without corruption, and enduring the smell of stench, that thou mightest be shown forth the sweet fragrance of Christ; therefore by Thy prayers make us also patient!
*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
Afflictions like arrows have compassed me about, and I lie upon the bed of my deeds; but heal my infirmities, and raise me up unto virtues, making me whole through thy prayers!
*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*[^551]
Speedily did God give ear unto thee as thou didst pray, O blessed one, and fulfilled the desire of thy heart, sending His angels to clothe thee in the angelic habit; therefore be thou also swift unto our help, and fulfill the desire of our heart, praying the angelic Master to clothe us from on high with His mercy!

*(Right page — Folio 115 recto, running header: TO PIMEN THE MUCH-AILING)*
*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
Speedily give ear unto the supplications of thy servants, O Sovereign Lady, and anticipate to overshadow us with the shelter of thy wings, lest we be enslaved unto sin unto the end; but that we may fervently serve thy Son, our Lord, Who grants us great mercy!
*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "Of Wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости):*[^552]
Thy many diseases, O blessed Pimen, became the cause of health for many: being sick thyself, thou didst heal the sick; ministering thyself in infirmity, thou didst raise up the infirm, by a word driving away every weakness; therefore running now unto thine incorrupt relics, we pray thee: pour forth health unto bodies, and grant healing unto souls, praying Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who honor thy holy memory with love!
*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast.*
*The Gradual Hymns, the 1st antiphon of the 4th Tone.*
*Prokeimenon, Tone 4: The venerable ones shall boast in glory, and shall rejoice upon their beds.*[^553] *Verse: Sing unto the Lord a new song. Let every breath praise the Lord. Common Gospel for a Venerable Monastic.*
*After Psalm 50, Sticheron: Come, ye choirs of monastics... (Seek at "Lord, I have cried").*

*Canon to the Venerable One, Tone 2.*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
In the deep of old, the overwhelming power laid low the whole Pharaohite army; and the Word incarnate hath abolished all-evil sin: supremely glorified is the Lord, for He is gloriously glorified![^554]
*Troparia:*
Held fast by the deep of perplexity, I pray Thee, O Jesus, Deep of Wisdom: elevate my mind, and stir my tongue, to praise the great endurance of Thy pleased one, Pimen!
Having drowned passions in the deep of dispassion, O venerable one, thou didst cross the sea of this world with dry feet, so that not even thy mind touched the sinful mire; from which preserve me also through thy prayers!
*Glory:*
In the deep of thy tears thou didst drown the mental Pharaoh, and now at the heavenly haven thou singest the hymn of victory, where pray also for me, O venerable one!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Unto me who am drowning in the deep of evils, O Sovereign Lady, stretch forth the hand of help *(Left page — Folio 115 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE)*
...stretch forth the hand of help, as Thy Son did unto Peter, and guide me unto the haven of salvation!
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
The wilderness hath blossomed as a lily, O Lord, the barren church of the nations, by Thy coming, wherein my heart hath been established![^555]
*Troparia:*
The Caves Monastery hath blossomed with venerable fathers, even as an orchard with fragrant lilies; in the midst of whom thou also, O venerable one, didst blossom fair by thy stance in life, and after thy falling asleep by incorruption!
Thou didst blossom by thy God-pleasing life as a full flower, O venerable one; and now thy holy relics in the cave emit sweet fragrance as a lily of the valley, and pour forth grace unto those who run with faith!
*Glory:*
A lily among thorns, O Pimen, blossoming in purity through the endurance of diseases, which being transplanted into the gardens of the enemy, now wafts fragrance upon us through his prayers!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O sweet-scented Flower, all-pure Virgin: drive away the stench of my sin, and show me forth a sweet fragrance of Christ through thy prayers!
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*[^556]
Radiant was thy prayer unto God, O holy one, when the Caves fathers were praying for thy healing, while thou didst ask the Lord for the increase of diseases for thyself, and the supplication of thee alone prevailed over the prayers of all;[^557] we know therefore that even now thy holy prayers can do much!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
A Mother's prayer can do much toward winning the Master's favor; pray Him therefore for us, O Virgin Mother, and make known our sorrow unto Him, that giving ear unto thee, He may be merciful unto us, and having turned sorrow into joy, grant life eternal!
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I hymn Thee, O Lord, for I heard Thy report and was afraid: for Thou comest unto me, seeking me who am gone astray; therefore I glorify Thy great condescension toward me, O Plenteous in mercy![^558]
*Troparia:*
Grant us by thy prayers, O venerable father, to sing and glorify God worthily, that we may ever call thee blessed!
The fathers who were near heard angelic singings at midnight, when they who entered clothed thee with salvation; account us also worthy to enjoy angelic songs through thine intercession, O blessed one!

*(Right page — Folio 116 recto, running header: TO PIMEN THE MUCH-AILING)*
...account us also worthy to enjoy angelic songs through thine intercession, O blessed one!
*Glory:*
Fervently I sing unto thee, O father; I bless thy singing, I magnify thy guilelessness and meekness, I praise thine endurance, I marvel at thine incorruption, and I bow down before thy healing relics!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Save thy hymnographers from eternal weeping, delivering them, O all-hymned Mother: with thy veil wipe away every tear from mine eyes, and account me worthy of eternal joy!
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Having become a mediator between God and men, O Christ God: for through Thee, O Master, we have access from the night of ignorance unto the Father, the Author of Light![^559]
*Troparia:*
Through thine intercession, O holy one, preserve from every assault of the enemy the monastery in which thou didst live in a God-pleasing manner, and be a helper unto monastics, for in thy prayers do we hope!
Be an intercessor for me unto the righteous Judge, O blessed one: for I have been negligent in this, even as that wretched monk whom thou didst show unto the brethren at thy falling asleep, who had the great schema, but no works at all; therefore his incorrupt body is reserved for his exposure and condemnation at the judgment![^560]
*Glory:*
Intercede for salvation for me who am despairing, O blessed one; guide a sinner unto repentance, O venerable one; grant amendment unto the perverse, O father, that I may not be put to shame, trusting in thy prayers!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Mediatrix before the Creator, all-pure Virgin: cleanse me who am all defiled with sins through thy prayers, for placing all my hope in thee, I shall not be put to shame forever!
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Wallowing in the abyss of sin, I call upon the fathomless abyss of Thy compassion: Lead me up from corruption, O God![^561]
*Troparia:*
The whole world lies in the abyss of evils; therefore Thou didst renounce it, that in God Thou mightest please God: account me also worthy to please Him, O pleased one of God!
Lying in the abyss of heavy diseases, neglected and forsaken many times by those who served thee, thou hadst God alone visiting thee; forsake not us, O father, through the visitation of thy prayers, as we are ever sick *(Left page — Folio 116 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE)*
*Glory, Tone 2:*
That I may not be condemned unto the abyss of hades by reason of the multitude of my sins, stand thou before the righteous Judge, O father, and entreat Him to deliver me from His righteous judgment!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
As thou hast borne the abyss of mercy, O Mother of compassion, snatch me away from the abyss of mine immeasurable iniquities, who pray unto thee!

*Kontakion, Tone 2:*[^562]
Come, having assembled together, let us praise Pimen the much-enduring, who thankfully endured long-continued disease; for he who was once loathed in his disease on account of the stench is now filled with sweet fragrance for those who approach with faith unto the reliquary of his relics, and drives away the foul stench of passions from those who cry: Come unto us, O Pimen, and the enemy shall in no wise prevail against us!

*Ikos:*
Ye that are held fast by diverse diseases and passions, having assembled unto Pimen the much-ailing, marvel at his patience; for having remained many times hungry and thirsty, he sorrowed not against those that served him, but with joy endured all things, giving thanks unto God; having spent twenty years in his suffering, he was tried as gold in the furnace,[^563] and was found worthy unto God, and received from Him the gift to heal infirmities and to drive away passions from those that run unto him with faith; therefore let us also run unto him and call upon him, crying: Come unto us, O Pimen, and the enemy shall in no wise prevail against us!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
The contrary commandment of the lawless tormentor raised high the flame; but Christ spread spiritual dew over the pious youths, He Who is blessed and supremely glorified.[^564]

*Troparia:*
Thou didst hate the God-opposing world, O father, knowing Him Who says: Whosoever will be a friend of the world is made the enemy of God;[^565] grant therefore unto us also to flee from the world and all its lusts!

The contrary law of mine evil custom draws me by force unto evil deeds, and not what I will, that do I, but what I will not, that I perform;[^566] do thou therefore, O venerable one, free me from that evil by thy prayers!

*Glory:*
The contrary commandment of reigning sin in my members...

*(Right page — Folio 117 recto, running header: TO PIMEN THE MUCH-AILING)*
...in my members forces me often to anger God;[^567] unto thee do I flee, O father, and deliver me from grievous sins!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Virgin who hast borne God, grant us to fulfill the commandments of thy Son, and that in nothing we may be found contrary unto Him through thy help!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
The fiery furnace in Babylon once divided its action by divine command, burning the Chaldeans, but bedewing the faithful as they sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord![^568]

*Troparia:*
That thou mightest extinguish the furnace of passions, O father, thou didst ask an increase of diseases from God, wherein bodily and spiritual purity was refreshment unto thee, O father; grant that we also may preserve it through thy prayers, that with thee we may sing unto all-pure God: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

Though the furnace of many years' diseases scorched thy body, yet could it in no wise harm the modesty of thy soul; for in all these things that befell thee thou didst not sin, but abiding in the midst of temptations unconsumed as in the midst of the flame, thou didst sing with the youths: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord.*
As in a furnace in my flesh, I have kindled the fire of lusts, and have increased the flame of uncleanness; O God, do Thou cool the flame, extinguish the fire of evil thought, and bedew the furnace of my passions through the prayers of Thy servant Pimen, that with him I may sing unto Thee, God glorified in Trinity: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The fiery furnace once not burning the youths prefigured thy childbirth, O all-pure one: for having conceived the Divine Fire, thou didst remain unburnt; and I therefore pray thee, O Sovereign Lady: preserve me unburnt from the fire of the adversary, that rejoicing I may sing unto thee: Sing, all ye works of the Lord, unto the Lord, and supremely exalt, glorifying His all-holy name unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
The Son of the Unoriginate Begetter, God and Lord, incarnate of the Virgin, hath appeared unto us to enlighten the darkened, to gather the dispersed; therefore do we magnify the all-hymned Theotokos.[^569]

*Troparia:*
O Unoriginate Begetter, we bring unto Thee in prayer Thy servant Pimen, who from the beginning of his monastic life loved Thee with his whole soul *(Left page — Folio 117 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONE)*
...loved Thee with his whole soul, and with all his strength served Thee: look upon his struggles, and for his sake give ear unto his supplication for us, and grant us a good end!

O Co-unoriginate Son and Holy Spirit, we present unto Thee in prayer Thy good and faithful servant Pimen: look upon his many-yeared and grievous diseases, and for his sake give ear unto his supplication for us, and be merciful unto us!

*Glory:*
O Holy Spirit, co-ever-existing with the Father and the Son, we offer unto Thee the fervent prayers of blessed Pimen; give ear unto him, and for his sake have mercy upon us!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Mother of God, with blessed Pimen and with all the venerable Caves Fathers who pleased God, entreat the All-Holy and Undivided Trinity to have mercy upon Her servants who glorify thee in hymns!

*Exapostilarion:*[^570]
The bridal chamber of the Savior is opened unto Thee, O Venerable Pimen; for thou hast the wedding garment of the angelic habit placed upon Thee by angels, wherewith having entered into the marriage feast of the Lamb, forget not us who honor Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
O undefiled bridal chamber, all-pure Virgin, cover us who have not the wedding garment with thy precious robe, and lead us into the heavenly bridal chamber!

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 8, Podoben: "O most marvelous wonder" (Ѡ҆ пресла́внагѡ чꙋдесѐ):*[^571]
O most marvelous wonder! O miraculous operation of the Holy Spirit upon thee, which operated in thy frail flesh, O venerable one! For while ailing thyself, thou didst heal others, and by thy patience didst lead them unto amendment; therefore unto those who praise thee, O father, show thy mercy, and entreat of Christ God healing in our infirmities, who with love honor thy holy falling asleep!

O God-wise Pimen, from childhood thou didst surrender thyself unto diseases through prayer, and by bitter sufferings didst wear away thy body, that thou mightest preserve the purity of the flesh and present a spotless soul unto God, before Whom standing in the painless life...

*(Right page — Folio 118 recto, running header: TO PIMEN THE MUCH-AILING)*
...before Whom standing in the painless life, as one powerful to assist by thy prayer, pray ever for us who sing thy holy falling asleep with faith!

O Venerable Pimen, let everyone who suffers in this much-troubled life learn to emulate thy patience, and to send up thanksgivings unto God, from Whom we receive chastening as trial for the correction of spiritual diseases; but be an intercessor for all who run unto the reliquary of thy relics, and ask from Christ God deliverance for spiritual and bodily infirmities!

*Glory, Tone 2:*
Truly mystical myrrh for the healing of the sick does the shrine of thy holy relics pour forth, and it breathes sweet fragrance upon those who approach with faith, granting them rest in necessities; grant therefore unto us also, O all-blessed father, deliverance from spiritual and bodily diseases from the ever-flowing fountain of thy love, as in songful beauties we praise thy precious falling asleep!

*Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
Deliver thy servants from misfortunes...

*Month of August, on the 11th day.*
*Service to our Venerable Fathers Theodore and Basil of the Caves.*[^573]
*At Vespers, Blessed is the man: the 1st antiphon.*
*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera on 6.*
*To Venerable Theodore, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*[^574]
Thou didst gather all corruptible treasure, and following the utterance of Christ, O our Father Theodore, thou didst most blessedly distribute it unto the needy, and by their hands didst convey it into an unstealable place;[^575] whence enriched with the light of grace...

*(Right page — Folio 119 recto, running header: TO THEODORE AND BASIL OF THE CAVES)*
...whence enriched with the light of grace, thou didst shine forth in the world as the sun; and now standing with boldness before the Sovereign Lady, enlighten those who sing thy memory with the rays of thy radiant gifts!

Desiring to receive spiritual riches, thou didst neglect sensible wealth; having surrendered it into the hands of the poor, thou didst choose in poverty to follow Christ Who became poor for our sake;[^576] and taking up His cross upon thy shoulder, thou didst walk the narrow path, and after a blessed end didst inherit spacious places, where dwelling with the angels, pray for those who praise thee, O all-blessed Theodore!

Having left all the fair things of this world, thou didst gladly accept the monastic habit, and having struggled well therein, thou didst settle in a dark cave, where by much abstinence and labors thou didst subdue thy flesh unto the spirit, and having wounded the adversary who was warring against thee, thou didst drive him away with the precious weapon; and now living in the unwaning light, remember also us who celebrate thy precious falling asleep!

*Other Stichera, to Venerable Basil, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*[^577]
Fulfilling the law that is in the Scripture, O Venerable Basil, thou didst acquire love toward God, and didst love thy neighbor as thyself;[^578] and him thou didst establish, O blessed one, not to be shaken by the invisible enemy, against whom Jesus the Lover of mankind, the Savior of our souls, granted thee strength.

Having in thy mouth a word seasoned with the salt of grace, O all-blessed one,[^579] thou didst restore in virtue a heart rotted through money-loving; thou didst discern the wiles of the serpent that was hiding, and didst drive him far away in the name of Christ, before Whom the opposing powers tremble!

Bound by the bond of firm love, O all-blessed fathers, ye were found even in this life as a strong fortress, which the deceitful angels that serve Belial were not able to undermine; and being put to flight, wounded they fell; but ye sang in praise: Jesus the Lover of mankind, the Savior of our souls, is our power and strength!

*Glory, Tone 6:*
O all-blessed fathers, good warriors of Christ! Taking up the shield of unhesitating faith by the utterance of unceasing prayer, and being girded with hope in God, ye conquered the demonic regiments *(Left page — Folio 119 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE ONES)*
...regiments, and having cleansed your robes in the blood of your martyrdoms, ye entered into the heavenly bridal chamber;[^580] whence forget not us, but show compassion upon us, asking for us from Christ God peace and great mercy!

*Both now... Of the Feast.*

*Entrance, Prokeimenon of the day, and three readings (see fol. 81 on the reverse).*[^581]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Е҆гда̀ ѿ дре́ва):*[^582]
When the grace of the All-Holy Spirit kindled thy heart with the fire of warm love toward God, O venerable one, then despising all the fair things of this world and following the blessed footsteps, thou didst choose to walk the narrow path unto this life, bearing the cross of Christ upon thy shoulder, and through long-suffering didst enter into spacious places; whence forget not us who call thy precious memory blessed, O Basil!

*Verse: The venerable ones shall boast in glory, and shall rejoice upon their beds.*[^583]
Fleeing the rumor and turmoil that captivates the human mind in temptation, having cast away all things, O all-blessed one, thou didst depart unto a quiet place, settling in a dark cave, and there didst make it as a certain most beautiful paradise; and having lived therein in many labors, thou didst overcome invisible enemies and wast shown forth a companion of angels; and now standing with them before the Master Christ, pray also for us, O Holy Theodore, that we may be delivered from every rumor and misfortune!

*Verse: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^584]
Beholding a ladder established from the holy Caves Mount unto heaven, most sacred fathers, whereon the Mother of God placed Her dwelling, and angelic choirs with the saints ascended thereby unto heaven,[^585] ye followed their steps as though bodiless, and having ascended through vigilance and deed, ye stand now before the Holy Trinity; where remember also us who bow down before your relics, O all-blessed ones!

*Glory, Tone 4:*
Having shone forth in the world through many struggles, O holy martyrs, marvelous Theodore with valiant-minded Basil! Ye were clothed with an all-embroidered robe, and live now in the ever-existing light; whence looking down upon earthly things...

*(Right page — Folio 120 recto, running header: TO THEODORE AND BASIL OF THE CAVES)*
...looking down upon earthly things, enlighten with the rays of your radiant gifts us who are in distress in the darkness of this life, and ask great mercy from Christ God!

*Both now... Of the Feast.*

*Troparion, Tone 4:*[^586]
Bound by the bond of love, O venerable ones, ye trampled down every bond of the wiles of the enemy, and valiantly endured innocent suffering and death from the money-loving prince;[^587] therefore we pray you who lived together and received the crowns of martyrdom: pray unto the Lord for us, that having lived well in abundant love, faith, and hope, we may ever call you blessed, O Theodore and Basil, triumphant in good!

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast.*

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the Feast.*
*Glory: To the Venerable Ones.*

Come, ye people, let us honor in hymns the victorious pair of all-blessed fathers: for having been greatly tried, they appeared as pure gold in the world, and now standing before the throne of the Master, they assist those who are in distress in many misfortunes, that they may receive deliverance from all temptations!

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
O swift helper of all the world and quiet harbor of the storm-tossed! Bring us who are tossed by the waves of voluntary passions into the sorrowless stillness of thy goodness, and deliver from misfortunes those who sing unto thee: Rejoice, the joy and salvation of all!

Having labored in many sweats, and having received innocent martyrdom, O God-wise fathers, ye passed over into the place of rest, where rejoicing with the angels, pray the all-bountiful Master to grant protection from enemies also unto us who suffer; for we are your children, and the spiritual sheep of your teachings!

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*

O immovable pillar, Titoe the Recluse, being vigilant in seclusion, thou didst not require sensible brightness, since intelligible light illuminated thee: by it pray, O blessed one, that those who honor thee may be enlightened![^727]

*Glory:*
Glory to Thee, O Most Holy Trinity, for Thou hast glorified an earthly place, like unto heaven in its citizens; in whose memory, with the singing offered for us, we sinners glorify Thee, O Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, with slavish veneration, and supremely exalt Thy One Godhead!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Glory to thee, O pure boast of the Orthodox; for the human race glorifies thee together with the angels, and falling down *(Left page — Folio 140 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE FATHERS OF THE CAVES)*
...and falling down cries aloud: For thy glory, O Theotokos, has stretched forth unto all for salvation!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
*Enlighten us by Thy commandments, O Lord, and by Thy high arm grant Thy peace unto us, O Lover of mankind!*[^728]

*Troparia:*
Having enlightened the whole Russian land by the grace given unto them, O all-blessed fathers, the darkness of evil belief has now perished utterly; extend those rays upon us for the sake of their entreaties, we pray Thee, O only Lover of mankind![^729]

O marvelous Sisoes, and Gregory who hadst the name from watchfulness, both having withered your passions by fasting, subdue the ferocious lust of our flesh also; for grace has been given unto you to assist us in our passions![^730]

O blessed Paul, lover of temperance, with the two canonarchs, extend supplications for us unto the Master, that by His grace we may restrain ourselves from belly-satiation, and become heirs with you in temperance![^731]

The Word of God that instructs men taught thee not worldly book-learning, O holy Nestor, but the wisdom from on high, wherein thou didst behold angels during thy prayers, and foresawest thine own repose: make us also partakers thereof, we pray thee who honor thy memory![^732]

O most valiant Titus, when thou wast once a soldier, the adversaries struck thy head with a weapon, and the wound became the cause of thy coming unto the Caves Mount, where thou didst weep for thy sins; having received assurance of forgiveness thereof, thou didst depart rejoicing unto the heavenly dwellings on high, O venerable one![^733]

I dare to honor obedient Pamva, and I marvel; for in obedience he was captured by the faithless, and for the sake of the faith endured many bonds, yet being snatched away and carried by angels, he was found in his cell; and upon him was fulfilled that obedience delivers from death![^734]

*Glory:*
I have fallen into the hands of spiritual enemies, and am bound by them with heavy fetters, from which a brother cannot deliver me: do Thou, O Most Holy Trinity, as merciful, have mercy on Thy creation, and by Thy goodness free me from mortal bonds!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
I am bound with many cords of passions, and have no hope of release: but have mercy on me, as the Mother of the Most High God, and loose me from them; for after God, Thou alone art able to loose sinful bonds!

*(Right page — Folio 141 recto, running header: IN THE FAR CAVE)*
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
*I will pour out my prayer unto the Lord, and unto Him will I proclaim my sorrows, for my soul is filled with evils, and my life has drawn near unto hades, and like Jonah I pray: Lead me up from corruption, O God!*[^735]

*Troparia:*
We offer our prayers unto you, O God-bearing fathers, for ye are intercessors and pleaders for us unto God: therefore by your mediation deliver from sinful bonds those who honor your venerable assembly!

Having sleepless spiritual vision toward God, ye were harmless as infants, but ye were an eye against demons, so that they trembled even at your name: we pray you, entreat ye, that the assaults of the enemy against us also may turn back with shame!

O Zechariah, eponymous with the remembrance of the Lord, having acquired a life according to thy name; for thou didst so valiantly arm thyself against enemies with fasting, that in thy whole life thou wast content feeding on herbs; therefore demons feared thy name: delivering us from their wickedness, hasten unto our help, we humbly pray thee![^736]

O blessed Theodore, who didst consign the glory of thy noble birth unto the dust, exchanging thy princely coronet for the monastic habit; wherein having well pleased thy Master, and now living with the angels in heaven: pray for us, that we may become partakers of unfading glory![^737]

O Venerable Sophronius, who didst shut thyself up in a dark place, and didst unceasingly sing unto God in the Psalter; and now in the spacious dwelling on high enjoying angelic melodies: pray that we also may be accounted worthy to hear them![^738]

Having ministered unto the Most High in holiness and righteousness, O marvelous Pancratius, thou wast enriched with gifts of wonderworking, which thou didst bountifully grant unto those in need: grant these therefore also unto us in illnesses, who fervently come unto thee![^739]

*Glory:*
For the glory of Thy name, O Super-essential Trinity, Thou hast glorified Thy righteous ones and this place: by Thy goodness therefore grant unto us also, O Good One, to behold the glory of the saints in Thy glory, and with them to glorify Thee, the One God in three Hypostases!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Whosoever glorifies thee not, O Most Holy Virgin, let him not behold the glory of eternal life; but grant unto us to behold it, for we know thee to be glorified by all *(Left page — Folio 141 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE FATHERS OF THE CAVES)*
...by all the heavenly powers, and we offer this unto thee: Rejoice, certain boast of the faithful!

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*[^740]
Chosen from all generations, holy venerable favorites of God of the Caves, having blossomed forth with virtues upon these hills, the earth has not concealed you, but heaven and the paradise habitation have opened unto you! Therefore we offer songs of praise in your commemoration unto God Who has glorified you: do ye, as having boldness, protect by your prayers from all misfortunes those who honor your assembly, as our protectors and intercessors before God!

*Ikos:*
Who is able to praise Thy saints one by one, O Good One? For they are multiplied more than the sand; but do Thou Thyself, O Master Christ, Who numberest the multitude of the stars, and callest them all by their names, receive all their supplications for us, and manifest Thy compassions upon Thy people; for we know that they have great boldness before Thy majesty, and intercede unto Thee as our constant prayerful advocates for our souls!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
*The youths coming from Judea in Babylon once by faith in the Trinity trampled down the flame of the furnace, singing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!*[^741]

*Troparia:*
Fertile and curdled art thou, O Caves Mount; for in thy citizens the Most High was pleased to place His habitation; therefore we also sing with them: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou![^742]

Filled with myrrh and like unto the hill of Lebanon art thou, O Caves Mount, dripping with the fragrant myrrh of the God-bearing fathers, for the healing of corrupting diseases; receiving whose sweet fragrance, we sing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

O Ammon and Mardarius, a striving pair, who were lovers of poverty, and have now acquired imperishable riches: pray that we also may acquire the same in the heights, where they sing: O God, blessed art Thou![^743]

We hymn Rufus, the mirror of fasters and laborers, with blessed Benjamin, who gave all his substance unto the poor that he might acquire the one pearl, Christ, whose emulators may we be, singing: O God, blessed art Thou![^744]

O Theophilus, adornment of hierarchs, who didst occupy the throne of Novgorod, and didst behold the Lord Himself: entreat His goodness, that we also...

*(Right page — Folio 142 recto, running header: IN THE FAR CAVE)*
...that we also may behold Him with thee, and sing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou![^745]

O Martyrius, luminary among deacons, and example among laborers, lover of purity, who hadst the gift to drive away evil spirits from men and to heal infirmities: drive away from us the spirit of malice and every deceit, that we may purely sing: O God, blessed art Thou![^746]

*Glory:*
Glorify with glory those who glorify Thy saints, O all-good Trinity, which Thou hast prepared from the ages for Thy servants, and make them worthy to sing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Mother and Queen full of joy, make those who bring joyful hymns unto thee worthy of eternal joys, and open the gates of paradise unto the whole Christian race that ever sings unto thee: Blessed art thou, O Theotokos!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
*The King of heaven, Whom the angelic hosts hymn: praise and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!*[^747]

*Troparia:*
Habitation of the heavenly Queen art thou, O Caves Mount of a hundred caves, which the multitude of glorious venerable fathers surround; all having sharpened weapons—prayers against the ruler of the world—they sing a song unto God: We sing and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

East and west, south and north, behold in thine enclosure, O Caves Mount, a spring bubbling with the myrrh of marvels of the God-bearing fathers, pouring forth abundantly unto all, who sing and supremely exalt the Lord unto all ages!

What praise shall we offer unto you, O God-bearing fathers? How shall we declare your labors and struggles with an earthly tongue? We know not: but do thou, O holy Euthymius, teach us silence, that with thee we may sing the Lord unto all ages![^748]

O Cassian and blessed Pior, radiance of fasters and laborers, both shining by your life as heavenly luminaries: illuminate us with the brightness of grace given unto you, that we may be accounted worthy with you to sing the Lord unto all ages![^749]

Remembering the beatitude of those that mourn, O Venerable Paphnutius, thou didst ever weep, and hast now inherited joyful places: pray that we also may take up our abode there in the shelter free from tears, that with thee we may supremely exalt the Lord unto all ages![^750]

With marvelous Joseph, we all honor your holy memory, O saints, who by fasting and labors well pleased God *(Left page — Folio 142 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE FATHERS OF THE CAVES)*
...who well pleased God, and we pray that through you we may find protection from all misfortunes, and be accounted worthy to supremely exalt the Lord unto the ages!

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord.*
Traversing the enclosure of the holy cave in mind, I behold upon its firmament a multitude of venerable fathers as stars, whom it is impossible to number: rendering praise unto God for all of them, we pray that He may grant us with them to supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Driven by the dragon that rules the world, I have fled unto thine enclosure, O pure one: protect me therefore beneath the shelter of thy mercy, and deliver me from that beast, that being saved, I may supremely exalt thee, the Mother of God, unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
*Truly do we confess thee as the Theotokos, saved through thee, O pure Virgin, with the bodiless choirs magnifying thee!*[^751]

*Troparia:*
Truly formerly this holy mount was a valley of weeping and a den of thieves, but now it is the house of God, and those dwelling therein are blessed, since it was sanctified by you, O God-bearing fathers; for this cause we all magnify you![^752]

The voice of rejoicing and the assembly of feast-lovers gather together to praise your congregation, O Pechersk fathers: receiving their praises, save them from all sorrows and griefs, that we may ever magnify you!

Having taken up the yoke of the Cross with a joyful soul, and having tilled this mount with the plow of labors, ye planted the spiritual gardens of your sweat, O God-bearing fathers; receiving the fruits whereof, we gladden our souls, and magnify you all!

Walking in the one path of the commandments of the Lord, ye sowed diverse seeds: one purity, another obedience, another fasting, another poverty and tears; yet all alike reap sheaves of joy; grant also of them unto us, that we may ever magnify you all!

The Lord shepherds you now in a place of green pasture, O all-blessed fathers, and what good thing does He withhold from you? For the torrent of sweetness waters you; wherefrom with but one drop sweeten, we pray, the bitterness of our life, that joyfully we may sing unto our God!

Having crossed over from the place of weeping unto that free from tears, we pray you, O all-blessed fathers: deliver from every affliction us who faithfully honor your memory, that we all may magnify you!

*Glory:*

*(Right page — Folio 143 recto, running header: IN THE FAR CAVE)*
*Glory:*
Glory art Thou, O Trinity, unto the choir of venerable fathers, and unfailing rejoicing; for in Thee having conquered the world and the flesh with the invisible enemy, they received crowns, and rejoice with the angels, ever magnifying Thee!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
We glorify thee, O all-glorious city of God, for the Most High Himself founded thee, and gave thee as a refuge unto all that flee from the face of the enemy: be thou also a harbor unto us, that we may ever magnify thee!

*Exapostilarion:*[^753]
Royal priesthood, chosen generation, holy hierarchs, priests, monastics, virgins, wonderworkers, and the whole fasting assembly of the venerable Pechersk fathers: assemble today in prayer, that for your sake the King of glory may save our EMPRESS and all people who piously honor your congregation!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
We glorify thee, O pure Virgin, as holier than the holy, and we magnify the Word born of thee: do thou receive from us also this doxology, and grant us to glorify thee where all the saints glorify thee as the Mother of God!

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*[^754]
Carnal wisdom ye took captive through subjection of the mind in obedience unto Christ, O all-blessed fathers, and having sailed across the sea of passions, ye reached the summit of desire; therefore Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls granted unto you the honors of the calling from on high!

Ye quenched the furnace of worldly attachments and wilful tumults by streams of tears and ultimate poverty, O venerable fathers; and as in a radiant bridal chamber ye abode in the dark caves, angelically glorifying Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls!

Having cleansed your mind from the depths of tempestuous passions, sorrows, and worldly cares, O God-bearing fathers, by hope of the future life ye despised all corruptible things; and now being in incorrupt life, pray for us unto Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls!

*Glory, Tone 6:*[^755]
Desiring the beatitude of the blameless, ye ran in the path of the commandments of the Lord, and seeking the testimonies of the will of God *(Left page — Folio 143 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE VENERABLE FATHERS OF THE CAVES)*
...and seeking the testimonies of the will of God, ye took up your abode in mountains, dens, and dark caves; ye crucified the world with its passions, and having conquered the demonic hosts, now inherit life and unending glory. Pray for us, we entreat you, O venerable fathers, that we may obtain communion with you in the Jerusalem on high, and salvation for our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Theotokos, thou art the true vine that hast blossomed forth for us the Fruit of life: we pray thee, O Sovereign Lady, intercede with the holy apostles and with all the saints, that our souls may be saved!
