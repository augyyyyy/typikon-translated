# GENERAL MENAION (COMMON SERVICES TO THE SAINTS)

*Common Service to One Venerable Monk, on the Day Whosoever Wills May Sing It.*[^758]
*At Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 8, Podoben: "O martyrs of the Lord" (Мч҃нцы Гдⷭни):*[^759]
A holy and venerable luminary, divine and light-bearing, is never hidden beneath the bushel of worldly grossness; but the Lover of mankind places him upon the summit of high marvels: by his prayers, O Christ, grant great mercy unto Thy people![^760]

Thou didst put the labor of thy hands unto the plow, O all-honored one, ever working divine deeds, and didst not turn back to look behind thee: but wast made fit for the Kingdom of Christ, God incarnate, unto the salvation of our souls![^761]

Borne in the bodily skiff, with gentle breezes of the gentle Spirit, thou didst lightly traverse the deep sea of life: and having sold thy possessions for the pearl of great price, and found it, thou didst retain it within thyself, having become blessed through His divine gifts![^762]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
My thoughts are impure, my lips deceitful, and all my deeds defiled: and what shall I do? How shall I meet the Judge? O Sovereign Lady Virgin, pray thy Son, Creator, and Lord, that He may receive my spirit in repentance, as the only tender-hearted One!

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Beholding the Bullock voluntarily nailed upon the Tree, the heifer undefiled, lamenting sorrowfully *(Left page — Folio 144 verso, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
...lamenting sorrowfully: Woe is me, my dearest Child! What has the ungrateful Jewish assembly rendered unto Thee, wishing to make me childless of Thee, O all-beloved!

*If thou celebrate with the Polyeleos, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera to 6.*
*Glory, Tone 6:*[^763]
Having preserved that which is according to the image unharmed, and having ascetically appointed the mind as sovereign over the corrupting passions, thou didst ascend as far as possible unto that which is according to the likeness; for having courageously compelled thy nature, thou didst hasten to subdue the worse unto the better, and to enslave the flesh unto the spirit; therefore thou wast shown forth as an instructor of monastics, an inhabitant of the wilderness, a teacher of those that run well, a most certain rule of virtue; and now in the heavens, the mirrors being dissolved, O our Father [Name], thou dost purely behold the Holy Trinity: pray unceasingly for those who honor thee with faith and love!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*Who will not bless thee, O Most Holy Virgin: seek at the end of this book.*[^764]

*The Entrance.*
*The Prokeimenon of the day, and the readings for a venerable monk: seek in the beginning of this book, Folio 4 on the reverse.*[^765]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the ranks of heaven" (Нбⷭныхъ чинѡ́въ):*[^766]
Thy feast has arrived, O God-bearer, brighter than the sun, illuminating those who come unto thee with faith, pouring forth the sweet fragrance of immortality, and welling forth healings unto our souls, O Venerable [Name], pleader for our souls!

*Verse: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*[^767]
Having restrained sensible bodily passions with the bridle of temperance, O wonderworker [Name], and showed forth on earth the zeal of the bodiless ones, thou didst subject all carnal desires unto the spirit; therefore now dwelling in the heavenly habitations, pray for our souls!

*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord, he will delight exceedingly in His commandments.*[^768]
O blessed [Name], having laid the foundation of virtue, thou didst put off the old man with his lusts, and didst truly put on Christ; therefore also, O venerable one, thou didst expose many enemy arrays, having become an instructor of monastics: pray that our souls may be saved!

*(Right page — Folio 145 recto, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
*Glory, Tone 8:*[^769]
We honor thee, O our Father [Name], as the instructor of multitudes of monastics; for by thy path we have learned truly to walk uprightly: blessed art thou who didst labor for Christ, and didst expose the power of the enemy, companion of angels, fellow-partaker with the venerable and righteous; with whom pray unto the Lord that He have mercy upon our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
*O unwedded Virgin, who God... seek at the end of this book.*

*If thou celebrate not, say this Theotokion:*
O pure Virgin, gate of the Word, Mother of our God, pray that we may be saved!

*Stavrotheotokion:*
I cannot bear, O Child, seeing Thee asleep upon the Tree, Who grantest vigilance unto all: that Thou mayest grant divine and saving vigilance unto those who of old fell asleep with the sleep of perdition through the fruit of transgression, the Virgin said weeping, whom we magnify!

*Troparion in the Typikon; but if there be no Typikon, say this:*[^770]
*Troparion, Tone 8:*[^771]
In thee, O father, was preserved with certainty that which is according to the image; for taking up the Cross, thou didst follow Christ, and by thy deeds didst teach us to overlook the flesh, for it passes away, and to care rather for the soul, a thing immortal; therefore also with the angels rejoices thy spirit, O Venerable [Name]!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion: According to the Tone of the Troparion.*

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord," the same Troparion.*

*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*[^772]
Thou didst follow the calling of thy Lord, having cast away the world and all the fair things in the world, O all-blessed [Name]: thou didst endure wilderness affliction fervently, and the hostile legions of demons courageously; therefore we also ever faithfully praise thy memory in hymns! *(Twice)*

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
By thy divine birthgiving, O pure one, thou didst renew the mortal nature of the earthborn corrupt with passions, and didst raise all from death unto life incorruptible *(Left page — Folio 145 verso, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
...unto life incorruptible; therefore, as is due, we all bless thee, O all-glorious Virgin, even as thou didst foretell!

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-beginningless Word" (Собезнача́льное Сло́во):*[^773]
Let us honor the faster of the Lord in hymns, as having truly put to death all attacks of passions by temperance and firm patience, and greatly put to shame the adversary foe and all his pride; and now praying unto the Lord that He have mercy on our souls! *(Twice)*

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
The dreadful wonder of thy conceiving and the ineffable manner of thy birthgiving was made known in thee, O pure Ever-Virgin: it confounds my mind and amazes my thought; thy glory, O Theotokos, has stretched forth unto all, for the salvation of our souls!

*At the Polyeleos, Megalynarion:*[^774]
We bless thee, O Venerable Father [Name], and we honor thy holy memory, instructor of monastics and companion of angels!

*Selected Psalm: With patience I waited for the Lord.*

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior" (Гро́бъ тво́й сп҃се):*[^775]
Thou didst bury all the rising of passions by mortification of the flesh, O sacred father, and after death didst receive endless life; therefore today the Church of Christ celebrates thy marvelous memory, O adornment of the venerable! *(Twice)*

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Direct us into the path of repentance, who ever turn aside unto evil byways, and provoke the all-good Lord to anger, O unwedded, blessed Mary, refuge of desperate men, habitation of God!

*Gradual Hymn, the 1st Antiphon of the 3rd Tone.*

*Prokeimenon: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*
*Verse: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He has rendered unto me?*
*Let every breath praise the Lord.*
*Gospel according to Matthew, Section 43 [Matthew 11:27–30].*

*After Psalm 50, Sticheron, Tone 6:*[^776]
O Venerable Father, into all the earth has gone forth the proclamation of thy corrections: therefore in the heavens thou hast found the recompense of thy labors; thou didst destroy the demonic legions, and reached the angelic ranks, whose life thou didst blamelessly emulate: having boldness before Christ God, ask peace for our souls!

*(Right page — Folio 146 recto, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
*If thou celebrate with the Polyeleos: Canon of the Theotokos to 4; and of the Saint to 8.*
*Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*Traversing the water as dry land, and fleeing from the malice of Egypt, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Deliverer and God!*[^777]

*Troparia:*
Adorned with good manners, thou didst cleave unto Christ from youth, having put carnal passions to death by temperance, and hast departed unto life, O venerable one!

Having been a fulfiller of the words and laws of God, thou wast filled, O all-wise father, with divine gifts and wonders, and hast abundantly cast forth rays unto all!

Having prevailed through the strength of Christ, thou didst cast down the dominion and power of the enemy, and received the prize of victory, O venerable one, with wonderworking radiances!

*Glory:*
A good conscience thou didst possess, and a heart's eye looking unto God; therefore in reward of Thy prayer, O all-wise one, He united thee with the venerable![^778]

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Assuage the passions of my flesh, and the improper movements of my mind, I pray thee, O all-pure Virgin, and correct me who have gone astray in mind!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*Thou art the confirmation of those that flee unto Thee, O Lord; Thou art the light of the darkened, and my spirit hymns Thee!*[^779]

*Troparia:*
Wholly cleaving unto the Almighty, O venerable and all-wise father, thou didst escape all demonic malice!

Adorned with the height of humility, thou didst cast down the boastful one unto the earth by thine excellent deeds!

United with flesh, O all-wise father, thou didst humble the haughty neck of the wicked one by the humility of thy words!

*Glory:*
Having the power of God assisting Thee, O all-wise one, as marvelous thou dost pour forth marvels and drive away infirmities!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Incarnate in thy womb, O all-immaculate one, the Creator took up His abode, for a blessing unto those who faithfully sing of thee!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*[^780]
Thou didst subdue the passionate carnal mind, and having enslaved the worse unto the better, O Father [Name], all-glorious one, through fasting thou wast shown forth as a vessel of the operation of life, and didst shine forth in the world as a solar ray, with radiances *(Left page — Folio 146 verso, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
...with radiances of thy virtues; therefore do we hymn thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Thou art an invincible wall unto us Christians, O Theotokos Virgin; for fleeing unto thee, we abide unharmed, and when again we sin, we have thee as an intercessor; therefore giving thanks, we cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, thou that art full of grace, the Lord is with thee!

*Stavrotheotokion:*
The all-immaculate ewe-lamb and Mother of Christ God: a sword passed through thy most holy soul when thou didst behold thy Son and God voluntarily crucified; Whom, O all-blessed one, cease not to pray, that He may grant us forgiveness of transgressions!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^781]
*I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation; I have understood Thy works, and I have glorified Thy Godhead!*

*Troparia:*
Having made thy soul a temple unto the Holy Spirit, O all-glorious one, thou wast an heir of the Kingdom on high, with the celestial powers!

Thou ever healest the painful passions of those who hasten unto thee; for thou didst receive grace from God, O venerable one, to work signs and marvels!

*Glory:*
As a most comely sprout in the house of God thou didst blossom forth, O father, adorned with virtues, and abundant with the fruits of marvels!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O spiritual field, as from a furrow thou hast brought forth an ear of corn, Who nourishes all creation, the God of the universe, O Ever-Virgin!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^782]
*Rising early, we cry aloud unto Thee, O Lord, save us; for Thou art our God, besides Thee we know no other!*

*Troparia:*
With a cleansed mind, O all-glorious one, thou beholdest the ineffable beauty of Christ, the God of the all-honored!

As Elijah upon a chariot, O father, by thy virtues thou didst ascend unto heaven, made light by the Spirit!

*Glory:*
Having braced thy body by temperance and purity, thou didst reach the breadth of the dwellings on high, O father!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Heal the darkening of my ailing mind, O thou that didst bear Christ the Physician, O all-immaculate Sovereign Lady!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^783]
*I will pour out my prayer unto the Lord, and unto Him will I proclaim my sorrows, for my soul is filled with evils, and my life has drawn near unto hades, and like Jonah I pray: Lead me up from corruption, O God!*

*Troparia:*
The spiritual serpent was slain by thy sacred prayers, O God-blessed one, and those who asked signs through thee are loosed from his malice; for yea, thou wast known as a most radiant and faithful favorite of God!

*(Right page — Folio 147 recto, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
Having renewed thy soul with the diligent plow, O sacred father, thou didst most wisely cast into heaven the fruitful seeds of virtues, and didst reap the rich ears of manifold designations!

*Glory:*
Having prevailed through the strength of the Spirit, O father, thou didst cast down the dominion and power of the enemy, and didst receive the prize of victory, O venerable one, with wonderworking radiances!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The Lord is with thee, O all-pure one, as He was well pleased to be: O Maiden, through thine intercession He has delivered all of us from the dominion of the deceiver; therefore now, as is due, generations of generations bless thee!

*Kontakion in the Typikon; but if there be no Typikon, say this:*[^784]
*Kontakion, Tone 2, Podoben: "Seeking the highest" (Вы́шнихъ и҆щѧ̀):*[^785]
Having divinely armed thyself with spiritual purity, and holding unceasing prayers firmly in thy hands as a spear, thou didst pierce the demonic arrays, O our Father [Name]: pray unceasingly for all of us!

*Ikos:*
Having loved the divine commandments of Christ, O venerable one, and hated the enjoyment of this world, thou didst come fervently unto Him, and wast a luminary, enlightening the ends of the earth with spiritual radiance; therefore, falling down, I pray thee: Enlighten my spiritual eyes, to hymn thy struggles, fasting, and vigilance, and painful tears, and mortification of the body, for the sake of the blessed future life which thou now enjoyest: pray unceasingly for all of us!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^786]
*The youths coming from Judea in Babylon once by faith in the Trinity trampled down the flame of the furnace, singing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!*

*Troparia:*
Having traversed ascetic struggles with a firm mind, O father, thou didst humble the most haughty mind by divine humility, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

As a most comely sprout, O father, thou didst blossom forth in the house of God *(Left page — Folio 147 verso, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
...adorned with virtues, and didst abound with the fruits of marvels!

*Glory:*
Having enlightened thy heart, thou wast the leader of the sacred assembly, O father, admonishing and teaching, and bringing all unto the will of God, singing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having born the pre-beginningless Word as a new Child, thou hast renewed us who are grown old through sin, O Virgin, and confirmed us to sing: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^787]
*The God-proclaiming youths in the furnace, trampling down the flame with the fire, sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!*

*Troparia:*
O heir of divine dwellings, thou didst live angelically, O father; therefore with the angels rejoices thy spirit!

Having walked without turning back, O all-wise and most marvelous father, in the divine paths leading unto heaven, thou didst completely turn away from those leading unto evil!

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord.*
By the grace that took up its abode in thy soul, O father, the unclean demonic spirits that pursue men are greatly driven away!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Thou art an inexhaustible water, O Virgin, drinking wherefrom we are all filled with grace, sanctified in soul and body!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^788]
*Every hearing was struck with awe at the ineffable condescension of God: that the Most High voluntarily descended even unto the flesh, from the virginal womb becoming man; therefore the faithful magnify the all-pure Theotokos!*

*Troparia:*
Thy precious shrine pours forth healing abundantly by the Spirit of God, and heals the long-lasting passions of those who come unto thee, O father, and drives away the wicked, ferocious spirits, that we may praise thy radiant struggles!

As a great sun thou hast shone forth upon us by the greatness of thy struggles, and hast illuminated all the ends of the earth, O God-wise one: having died, thou hast passed from the light unto the light that has shone forth; therefore we cry aloud unto thee: Enlighten our thoughts, O Venerable Father [Name]!

Thy patient body, O blessed one, wounded by heavy chains, heals incurable passions by its touch, since God and the Savior greatly glorified thee, O all-wise and most marvelous father...

*(Right page — Folio 148 recto, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
...for by thy good deeds thou didst glorify Him, O venerable one!

*Glory:*
Thou didst blossom forth in ascetic valleys, O [Name], our all-praised father, as a fragrant rose, and as a lily thou didst abound, O sacred initiate, in virtues and wonders; therefore, O venerable one, drive away from us the stench of stinking passions!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Enlighten, O pure Virgin, my heart that is ever blinded by transgressions and many worldly tumults; leave me not to be a rejoicing unto enemies, that I may glorify thee and sing of thee with love, O all-hymned one!

*Exapostilarion, Podoben: "Give ear, ye women" (Жены̀ оуслы́шите):*[^789]
Thou didst blossom forth as a palm tree according to David, O father, and wast shown to be a habitation of the All-Holy Spirit, and He showed thee forth glorious unto the universe: pray unceasingly for us who honor thy most precious memory with faith, O Venerable [Name]!

*Theotokion:*
We bless thee in unceasing hymns, O Virgin, for thou didst bear One of the Trinity, O Theotokos, and dost bear upon thy divine arm the pre-wealthy Word, without change and without confusion!

*If thou celebrate his Polyeleos.*
*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 6:*[^790]
O all-blessed God-bearer [Name], thou didst subject all carnal wisdom unto the spirit, having been confirmed by ascetic sufferings, shining as gold tested in the furnace; thou didst show thyself and wast a receptacle of the All-Holy Spirit; having gathered multitudes of monastics, and by thy teachings, as by a certain ladder leading up unto heaven, thou didst lead them up unto the height of virtues: remember us who ever honor thy sacred memory, and pray that our souls may be saved! *(Twice)*

Today has shone forth thy most radiant and all-festal memory, O all-glorious [Name], summoning a numerous assembly of fasters and choirs of the venerable; and angels with men truly unite for the praise of Christ our God, Who is worshipped in the Trinity; therefore, hastening unto the sacred shrine of thy relics, we receive bountiful gifts of healing, and we glorify Christ, the Savior of our souls, Who has crowned thee!

O all-blessed God-bearer, Father [Name], thou didst bedew the earth with tears *(Left page — Folio 148 verso, running header: COMMON SERVICE TO A VENERABLE MONK)*
...thou didst bedew the earth with tears, and gavest no sleep unto thine eyes, nor slumber unto thine eyelids, manifesting heartfelt desire unto Christ Whom thou didst love; therefore thou wast an example unto monastics, and didst fulfill every kind of virtue: therefore do we bless thee, magnifying Him Who has glorified thee!

*Glory, Tone 2:*
O Venerable Father, having diligently trained thyself in virtues from childhood, thou wast an instrument of the Holy Spirit; and receiving from Him the operation of marvels, thou didst admonish men to despise sweet pleasures; and now being most purely illuminated by divine light, enlighten also our thoughts, O our Father [Name]!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
All my hope do I place upon thee, O Mother of God: preserve me beneath thy shelter!

*The Great Doxology, and the Dismissal.*

*At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes from the Canon, Odes 3 and 6.*
*Prokeimenon, Tone 7:*
*Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*
*Verse: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He has rendered unto me?*
*Epistle to the Galatians, Section 213 [Galatians 5:22–6:2].*
*Alleluia, Tone 6:*
*Blessed is the man that fears the Lord, he will delight exceedingly in His commandments.*
*Verse: His seed shall be mighty upon earth.*
*Gospel according to Matthew, Section 43 [Matthew 11:27–30], or according to Luke, Section 24 [Luke 6:17–23].*
*Communion Verse: The righteous man shall be in everlasting remembrance.*

### Notes

[^758]: Canonical opening of the Common Service to One Venerable Monk (*Слꙋ́жба ѡ҆́бща прпⷣбномꙋ є҆ди́номꙋ*), appointed to be sung on the commemoration day of any holy monk without a dedicated feast in the Menaion.
[^759]: Opening Stichera at "Lord, I have cried," Tone 8, Podoben: "O martyrs of the Lord" (*Мч҃нцы Гдⷭни*).
[^760]: Evangelical allusion to Matthew 5:14–16 ("neither do men light a candle and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick").
[^761]: Allusion to Luke 9:62 ("No man, having put his hand to the plow, and looking back, is fit for the kingdom of God").
[^762]: Allusion to the Parable of the Pearl of Great Price (Matthew 13:45–46).
[^763]: The classical Great Vespers Doxasticon for a Venerable Monk, Tone 6: *Є҆́же по ѻ҆́бразꙋ соблю́дъ невреди́мѡ...*, synthesizing the Patristic ascetical theology of Genesis 1:26 (the preservation of the image and attainment of the likeness).
[^764]: Rubrical cross-reference to the Dogmatikon Theotokion of Tone 8 at the back of the Anthologion (*Кто̀ тебѐ не оу҆блажи́тъ...*).
[^765]: Cross-reference to the Old Testament Paremias for Monastics at Folio 4 verso (physical leaf p10).
[^766]: Aposticha Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the ranks of heaven" (*Нбⷭныхъ чинѡ́въ*). The placeholder `[и҆́мⷬ҇къ]` indicates where the name of the commemorated saint is inserted.
[^767]: Psalm 115:6 [LXX].
[^768]: Psalm 111:1 [LXX].
[^769]: Aposticha Doxasticon for a Venerable Monk, Tone 8: *Мона́хѡвъ мно́жества, наста́вника тѧ̀ почита́емъ...*.
[^770]: Rubric distinguishing between a saint with a prescribed Troparion in the Typikon and the General Troparion for a Venerable Monk (*Въ тебѣ̀ ѻ҆́ч҃е и҆звѣ́стнѡ спасе́ся...*).
[^771]: General Troparion for a Venerable Monk, Tone 8: *Въ тебѣ̀ ѻ҆́ч҃е и҆звѣ́стнѡ спасе́ся є҆́же по ѻ҆́бразꙋ...*, based on Matthew 16:24–26.
[^772]: Sessional Hymn after the 1st Kathisma, Tone 3, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (*Ско́рѡ предварѝ*).
[^773]: Sessional Hymn after the 2nd Kathisma, Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-beginningless Word" (*Собезнача́льное Сло́во*).
[^774]: Polyeleos Megalynarion (Pripivalo) for a Venerable Monk: *Оу҆блажа́емъ тѧ̀ прпⷣбне ѻ҆́ч҃е [и҆́мⷬ҇къ]...*.
[^775]: Sessional Hymn after the Polyeleos, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior" (*Гро́бъ тво́й сп҃се*).
[^776]: Matins Sticheron after Psalm 50, Tone 6: *Прпⷣбне ѻ҆́ч҃е, во всю̀ зе́млю и҆зы́де вѣща́нїе...*, citing Psalm 18:5 [LXX].
[^777]: Common Matins Canon to a Venerable Monk, Tone 8, Ode 1 Heirmos: *Во́дꙋ проше́дъ ꙗ҆́кѡ сꙋ́шꙋ...*.
[^778]: Troparion to the Holy Trinity / Doxasticon of Ode 1, transcribing the printed text *вна́чѣ моли́твѣ твое́й* ("in reward of thy prayer").
[^779]: Heirmos of Ode 3, Tone 8: *Ты̀ є҆сѝ оу҆твержде́нїе...*.
[^780]: Sessional Hymn after Ode 3, Tone 3, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (*Ско́рѡ предварѝ*).
[^781]: Heirmos of Ode 4, Tone 8: Biblical Canticle of Habakkuk (Habakkuk 3:2; LXX Hab. 3:2: *Κύριε, εἰσακήκοα τὴν ἀκοήν σου καὶ ἐφοβήθην, κατενόησα τὰ ἔργα σου καὶ ἐξέστην*).
[^782]: Heirmos of Ode 5, Tone 8: Biblical Canticle of Isaiah (Isaiah 26:9, 13; LXX Isa. 26:9: *Ἐκ νυκτὸς ὀρθρίζει τὸ πνεῦμά μου πρὸς σέ, ὁ Θεός*).
[^783]: Heirmos of Ode 6, Tone 8: Biblical Canticle of Jonah (Jonah 2:3–8; LXX Jon. 2:3: *Ἐβόησα ἐν θλίψει μου πρὸς Κύριον τὸν Θεόν μου*).
[^784]: Rubric directing the substitution of the Common Kontakion when no specific proper feast is assigned in the Typikon.
[^785]: Melodic model (*podoben*): Tone 2, *Seeking the highest* (Вы́шнихъ и҆щѧ̀ / *Tὰ ἄνω ζητῶν*), the classic kontakion automelon of the Sunday of the Forefathers and general ascetics.
[^786]: Heirmos of Ode 7, Tone 8: Song of the Three Holy Youths in Babylon (Daniel 3:26–56; LXX Dan. 3:28).
[^787]: Heirmos of Ode 8, Tone 8: Song of the Three Holy Youths in Babylon (Daniel 3:57–88; LXX Dan. 3:57).

[^789]: Melodic model (*podoben*): Exapostilarion, *Give ear, ye women* (Жены̀ оуслы́шите / *Γυναῖκες ἀκουτίσθητε*). Biblical allusion to the palm tree of David (LXX Ps. 91:13: *δίκαιος ὡς φοῖνιξ ἀνθήσει*).
[^790]: Stichera at the Praises (*Ainoi*), Tone 6 automela/prosomoia for a venerable father.
