The righteous live unto the ages, and in the Lord is their reward, and the care of them is with the Most High. He shall take holiness for an invincible shield; He shall sharpen His severe wrath for a weapon, and the world shall fight with Him against the senseless. The souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and no torment shall touch them. As gold in the furnace He tried them, and as a whole burnt-offering He accepted them. And in the time of their visitation they shall shine, and like sparks through stubble they shall run to and fro.
