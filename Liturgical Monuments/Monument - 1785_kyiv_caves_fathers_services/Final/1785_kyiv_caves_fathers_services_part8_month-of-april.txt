# MONTH OF APRIL

*On the 8th Day:*
*Service to our Father among the Saints Niphont, Bishop of Novgorod:*[^257]
*At Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera: Tone 4, Podoben: "As one valiant" (Яко добля):*[^258]
Let us, O ye people, honor this day with hymns the adornment of hierarchs and zealot of piety, lovingly weaving wreaths of praise, and bowing down before the reliquary of the relics of the well-blessed father, with suppliant voices let us cry aloud: O holy hierarch of Christ, Niphont, as having boldness toward God, forget not *(Left page — Folio 72 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HIERARCH:)*
...forget not, diligently praying to intercede for thy flock evermore!

The ink of thy honey-flowing lips, O most blessed hierarch Niphont, being the reed of the Holy Spirit, cast down the pride of those who hated thee;[^259] and now put to shame the enemies that rise up in pride against thy flock, and confirming us in orthodox dogmas, save us by thine intercession, that with a pure conscience we may lovingly offer praise unto God concerning thee!

With the rays of the light of God's grace, O blessed father, thou didst shine forth like the sun in the world, and with the beams of thy virtues didst enlighten many lands; therefore by direction from on high, O venerable one, thou wast chosen by all with one accord as shepherd unto the rational flock, for which also after thy departure diligently pray, that it may be preserved unassailed by enemies!

*Glory... Tone 2:*
Come, ye orthodox assemblies of Novgorod, having lovingly formed a festival with us this day on the annual memory of the well-blessed father, let us give glory unto God our Deliverer, that He gave us a shepherd and teacher, and an intercessor for our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion.*
*At the Aposticha, from the Triodion.*
*Glory... Tone 3:*
Having fashioned thy heart as a temple for the Holy Spirit, thou didst erect houses of prayer for the increase of the glory of God,[^260] which having beautifully adorned, thou didst gather thy rational sheep as into a heavenly fold, and offering unto them the pasture of thy sweet and divinely-inspired teaching, didst guide and instruct them in the way of piety and salvation; whereunto also guide us by thy prayers, O venerable father, that we may ever glorify the only God and Savior of our souls!

*Both now... Theotokion, or Stavrotheotokion.*
*Troparion, Tone 3:*
Being by character a true defender of the traditions of the orthodox eastern church, thou wast accounted worthy to see the great one among the fathers, Theodosius, after death as living, announcing unto thee the inheritance of the heavenly kingdom, whereof now inseparably enjoying the ineffable good things with him, pray also for us thy children, O all-praised Niphont![^261]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion.*

*(Right page — Folio 73 recto, running header: TO NIPHONT, BISHOP OF NOVGOROD:)*
*Canon to the Hierarch.*
*Tone 1.*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Thy victorious right hand hath been glorified in power in a manner befitting God: for it, O Immortal One, as all-powerful, shattered the adversaries, fashioning a new path through the deep for the Israelites![^262]

*Troparia:*
May Thy right hand, O my Christ, guide me on this day to hymn the memory of Thy favored one, the hierarch; and having tripped up the enemies that hindered me, may it renew the path grown old with passions in my belly!

Having sent the grace of Thy Comforter, O Christ, unto Thy servant, Thou didst call him to run after Thee: who straightway renounced the world, and loved Thy law, which renews the path unto Thy love!

Guided by Thy right arm, O Lord, Thy venerable one came unto the untroubled haven in the most honorable monastery of the Caves of Thy Mother, and became a monk, adorned with monastic virtues!

*Glory...*
Thy virtue extended far, O father, seeing which many magnified thee, and glorified the Heavenly Father, Who gave thee such grace, whereof may we also not be deprived, through thy righteous deeds, O father!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Be thou my directress, O Virgin Bride of God, guiding me unto the saving path, and by thy tender-heartedness renewing my soul that is grown old with passions, O Mary!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
O Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human nature, and mercifully didst take form therein: gird me with power from on high, that I may cry unto Thee: Holy is the living temple of Thine ineffable glory, O Lover of mankind![^263]

*Troparia:*
Seeing the one right path, O faithful servant of Thine, to take up Thy cross in this life and follow Thee, his Master, he straightway ran swiftly unto it: therefore now he is filled with Thine ineffable glory, O Lover of mankind!

Unceasingly sending up thy prayers unto the only God from a pure heart, thou didst destroy the snares of the weak enemy, slaying him with the spear of faith; therefore, O hierarch, thou didst receive the crown of glory from Christ the Lover of mankind!

Having flown on high by vigilance and fasting, thou didst not cast down thy mind below unto fleeting things, and by such deeds thou didst acquire the vision of the glory of God, which also for us *(Left page — Folio 73 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HIERARCH:)*
...which also for us pray that we may obtain, O favored one of Jesus the Lover of mankind!

*Glory...*
So did thy life shine forth before men, that seeing thy virtues, O father, they chose thee for the archiepiscopal throne of Novgorod, unto which thou wast a shepherd, bringing thy sheep unto the Chief Shepherd, Jesus the Lover of mankind!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Being the Mother of the Chief Shepherd, O pure one, guide me, the straying sheep, unto the right path, and direct my life, that being directed by thee I may be accounted worthy to be filled with the glory of Jesus the Lover of mankind!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:*
Struggling with great zeal for Orthodoxy, thou didst multiply the glory of God in thy flock; now therefore being glorified where is the unceasing voice of those that keep festival, pray for those who honor thy memory, O Niphont, our most sacred father!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who didst bear the glory of the Lord, delivering us from the inglorious standing that holds us fast, account us worthy to be partakers of the glory of the saints, and glorify those who honor thy childbearing, O pure one!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
Seeing with his prophetic eyes the mountain overshadowed by the grace of God, Habakkuk proclaimed that the Holy One of Israel should come forth from thee, for our salvation and renewal![^264]

*Troparia:*
Thou didst humble those who walked in the mountain of high-mindedness, O blessed one, not suffering them to wage internecine warfare, but didst bless them with peace, bringing them unto salvation and the renewal of love![^265]

Following the apostolic words, in season and out of season thou didst stand fast, O father, enjoining thy sheep with all thy power not to depart from the commandments of the Lord and from the upright traditions of His Church, for they are unto our salvation and renewal![^266]

Who shall recount thy boldness, O hierarch? For thou didst speak concerning the testimonies of the Lord before kings, not feeling shame before the diadem; therefore thou wast a second Elijah, rebuking lawless deeds![^267]

*Glory...*
Thou didst boldly rebuke the princes who gave their body not unto Christ but unto fornication by an unlawful marriage, in apostolic manner commanding thy church and all the clergy to reject those who walk thus disorderly!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Correct me who act lawlessly, and humble the passions that rage within me, O Mother of God, that being set free from them through thee, I may find justification: for thou alone art the salvation and renewal of us all!

*(Right page — Folio 74 recto, running header: TO NIPHONT, BISHOP OF NOVGOROD:)*
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
O Christ Who didst enlighten with the radiance of Thy coming, and by Thy Cross didst illuminate the ends of the world: enlighten the hearts of those who orthodoxly hymn Thee with the light of Thy knowledge of God![^268]

*Troparia:*
The Russian Church hymns thee as a second Theodore the Studite, O blessed one, because of thy boldness and confession, and calls unto spiritual rejoicing those who lovingly praise thee, O father![^269]

Thou didst ever oppose those who walk uncanonically, in no wise consenting that a hierarch be elevated unto the throne without the blessing of the Oecumenical Patriarch of the city of Constantine, nor ungodly to give a wolf as a shepherd unto the sheep that piously glorify Christ![^270]

Thou art worthy of honor, O holy one, for thou didst not consent unto those who placed the head of Saint Clement upon him for the dignity of the hierarchate, but rather didst rebuke and condemn their synod together with the men-pleasers!

*Glory...*
Being a true son of the eastern oecumenical throne, thou didst not consent that the Russian sons fall away from their primacy, but didst faithfully confess that it was confirmed from the beginning by written decree that all things should be done with the approval of their visiting shepherd, the Patriarch of the city of Constantine!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who didst crown the human race with blessing: account us worthy of the blessing of the chosen ones, and grant unto those who orthodoxly praise thee to behold the glory of thy most sweet Son!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
The nethermost abyss hath compassed us, there is none to deliver; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter: save Thy people, O our God, for Thou art the strength of the weak and their restoration![^271]

*Troparia:*
The hierarch who hated righteousness compassed thee about with malice, for thou didst not take counsel with him, seeing that he had unrighteously seized the throne; but thou, O father, didst not fear him, having hope in God, Who is the strength of the weak and their restoration!

Seeking to restrain thee who couldest not be restrained, O blessed one, the hierarch instigated the prince to send thee into exile; and he restrained thy person, but could not restrain thy tongue, for thou didst rebuke him as having seized the throne unlawfully!

Thy words were a sword unto the lawless, wherewith thou didst cut down their madness; therefore they hated thee, and from the throne *(Left page — Folio 74 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HIERARCH:)*
...and from the throne they alienated thee; but thou didst not faint, O father, hoping in the blessedness of those who are persecuted for righteousness' sake![^272]

*Glory...*
Thy rebuke, O hierarch Niphont, was a wound unto the ungodly, for it devoured them as transgressors of the law; and their hearts were torn asunder, gnashing their teeth like beasts, wishing to tear thee to pieces!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Noetic beasts have compassed us about, gnashing their teeth, and rejoice over our destruction; but do thou, O Virgin mighty in strength, break their jaws, and restore us to stand against them: for thou art the strength of the weak and their restoration!

*Kontakion, Tone 5:*
Emulating the zeal of the ancient fathers in the firm preservation of true dogmas, O stout-hearted Niphont, thou didst not desire to be a concelebrant with Clement who willfully seized the throne of the metropolitanate; and being overcome by him who was chosen for this by all, thou didst put aside celebration with him, and having ended thy life, thou didst find the unending kingdom of Christ![^273]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
We faithful contemplate thee, O Mother of God, as a spiritual furnace: for even as the Exalted One saved the three youths, the All-Powerful One renewed the world in thy womb, the praised and supremely glorified God of our fathers![^274]

*Troparia:*
Dwelling, O most blessed father, in the wonder-filled monastery as in a heavenly bridal chamber, rejoicing thou didst render thanksgivings unto the Queen of all and her most sweet Son, singing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

Having stood boldly for Orthodoxy, O venerable one, thou wast deprived of thy throne by the lawless; but a throne was prepared for thee in the heavenly fold, as a true witness of the truth!

The Chief Shepherd, seeing thy sheep troubled by thy repose, again grants thee as a shepherd unto them: he that did evil, by the righteous judgment of God himself received his own evil, and he that removed thee from the throne himself fell from the throne!

*Glory...*
The Christ-loving Prince George, having righteously driven Izyaslav from the throne of the principality, sent thee forth with great honor unto thy throne: whereon seeing thee, O venerable one, thy sheep sang with joy: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The human race that had righteously fallen away from the grace of God, thou didst raise up by thy most pure childbearing, O Mother of God; therefore joyfully every generation sings unto thee, the blessed one!

*(Right page — Folio 75 recto, running header: TO NIPHONT, BISHOP OF NOVGOROD:)*
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
In the furnace the children of Israel, as in a crucible, shone brighter than gold with the beauty of piety, saying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him unto all ages![^275]

*Troparia:*
When the Oecumenical Patriarch of the city of Constantine heard that thou wast unburned in the furnace of temptations and in the flame of flattery of the lawless, he blessed thy courage, O father, and numbered thee with the ancient confessors, with whom thou now singest unto the Lord unto all ages![^276]

Seeing thy boldness written in the book of the living, the Patriarch of the city of Constantine sent also his written epistle unto thee, wherein he blessed thy zeal for Orthodoxy, which the Russian sons also remembering, exalt the Lord unto all ages!

Kindled with twofold desire, O hierarch, thou didst hasten unto the city of Kyiv, desiring to bow down before the Mother of God in the monastery of the Caves and before the venerable fathers, and likewise to receive a blessing from the coming hierarch: therefore thou wast accounted worthy to see a marvelous vision, for Saint Theodosius said unto thee: In common shalt thou be with us inseparably, glorifying and singing unto the Lord unto all ages![^277]

*Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord!*
As a fledgling loves the nest made upon the hill of the Caves for the pure Dove, thou didst lovingly desire to dwell therein without departing; and though such desire urged thee, yet the providence of God was preparing thy departure, O father, from temporal things unto eternal!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
I am a fledgling born in thy fold, O my Lady, but I have fallen down, ensnared by demonic assaults; yet I pray thee, lead me back again unto it, that where my beginning was, there also I may receive a good end, and with the holy fathers exalt thy power unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
The unburned bush showed forth an image of thy pure childbearing: and now we pray thee to quench the furnace of misfortunes raging against us, that we may unceasingly magnify thee, O Mother of God![^278]

*Troparia:*
By the image of a marvelous vision the Master of all made known unto thee, O holy one, that after thy labors thou shouldest depart unto peaceful places, where the angelic hosts unceasingly glorify the throne of the Trinity, which we also with them magnify!

Sinking into a light sleep, O father, thou didst seem to stand in the church of the Caves, stretching forth supplication unto the Most Pure Mother of God to see the face of the blessed Theodosius; and it came to pass even as thou didst desire!

According to thy desire, someone taken from the brethren led thee to the vision, O venerable one, and brought thee unto the blessed *(Left page — Folio 75 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HIERARCH:)*
...unto the blessed Theodosius; whom seeing with joy, thou didst fall at his feet, and wast accounted worthy of a blessing; held by desire whereof, we magnify the most holy labor!

*Glory...*
O sweet voice! O good embrace of our father! For he said: In common shalt thou be with us; and showed thee a scroll, wherein was written: Behold, I and the children whom God hath given me![^279] May we also hear this voice, we pray unto the Mother of God, and ever magnify her with thee, O father!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O most holy Virgin, spiritual scroll, for in thee the pre-eternal Word was written by the finger of God! Write us also in the scroll with thy servants, for we ever pray unto thee concerning this, and magnify thee with hymns!

*Exapostilarion:*
Having lived in angelic manner upon earth, thou wast shown forth as a fellow-dweller of the angels; for having departed from things below, thou didst go unto things on high, where standing before God the Master of all, pray for those who honor thy memory, O blessed Niphont!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who art higher than the heavenly ranks: depart not from us who are below, but be ever with us, delivering us from all assaults: for seeing thy mighty help on our behalf, no one shall rise up against us!

*At the Praises, Stichera: Tone 1:*
On the auspicious day of the memory of the God-bearing father, come, ye people named after Christ, let us leap for joy, offering songs of praise, and being regaled with spiritual sweetness, let us lovingly cry aloud: O Niphont, our most sacred father, pray unto Christ God for the salvation of our souls!

We bring thee song-gifts with love this day, O venerable father, remembering the upright deeds of thy virtues, and we pray unto Christ God, that having escaped harmful thoughts, we may correct our life with good deeds, and not be deprived of heavenly honors through thy prayers!

Who can worthily praise Thy saints, O Christ God? For being wounded with Thy love, they disregarded all earthly things, and being in the flesh, they conquered the bodiless enemy, and now rejoice with the bodiless ones in the heavens; whereof account us also worthy to be choristers, through the prayers of Thy hierarch!

*(Right page — Folio 76 recto, running header: TO NIPHONT, BISHOP OF NOVGOROD:)*
*Glory... Tone 6:*
The adornment of hierarchs and glory of the fathers, the fountain of wonders and swift helper of the faithful, let us feast-loving people hymn this day with praises, assembling in the most honorable temple, saying: Rejoice, O good ascetic! Rejoice, O vigilant shepherd of rational sheep! Rejoice, thou who with the rod of faith didst drive away the noetic wolf from thy flock, and didst bring it whole unto thy Chief Shepherd: unto Whom ever pray for us also, that we may be preserved unhurt from all enemies, and become heirs of eternal life!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O Mother of God, thou art the true Vine...

*Great Doxology, and Dismissal.*
*At the Liturgy, the common service for a Hierarch.*[^280]

### Notes

[^257]: Saint Niphont, Bishop of Novgorod (d. April 8, 1156). Tonsured at the Kyiv Caves Monastery, he was consecrated Bishop of Novgorod in 1130. A staunch defender of canonical order, he refused to sanction the uncanonical marriage of Prince Svyatoslav Olgovich and opposed the uncanonical elevation of Clement Smoliatich as Metropolitan of Kyiv without the blessing of the Patriarch of Constantinople. Returning to the Kyiv Caves Monastery shortly before his death, he fell asleep in the Lord and was buried in the Near Caves of Saint Antony. See Kyiv Caves Paterikon, Discourse 14.
[^258]: Melodic model: Tone 4 Podoben: *As one valiant* (Яко добля).
[^259]: 2 Cor. 3:3 ("...written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God; not in tables of stone, but in fleshy tables of the heart").
[^260]: The Church of the Transfiguration of the Savior on Nereditsa and the Church of Saint Clement in Novgorod, along with the monumental Mirozhsky Monastery Cathedral frescoes in Pskov and the Dormition frescoes, were among the monumental shrines founded, built, and decorated under Bishop Niphont's episcopate.
[^261]: The miraculous posthumous apparition of Saint Theodosius of the Caves to Bishop Niphont: shortly before his falling asleep, Bishop Niphont fell into a light sleep in the Caves Monastery and beheld Saint Theodosius holding an open scroll inscribed with the words of Isaiah: *Behold, I and the children whom God hath given me* (Isa. 8:18; Heb. 2:13), welcoming Niphont to eternal rest with the Caves brethren. See Kyiv Caves Paterikon, Discourse 14.
[^262]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *Thy victorious right hand* (Твоя победительная десница). Exod. 15:6.
[^263]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *O Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human nature* (Едине ведый человеческаго существа немощь). 1 Sam. (1 Kgs.) 2:2.
[^264]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *Seeing with his prophetic eyes the mountain overshadowed by the grace of God* (Гору тя благодатию Божиею приосененную). Hab. 3:3.
[^265]: In 1135 and 1147, Bishop Niphont personally intervened on the battlefield between the warring princes of Kyiv, Chernihiv, and Novgorod, courageously preventing bloodshed and reconciling the brothers in peace. See Novgorod First Chronicle and Kyiv Caves Paterikon, Discourse 14.
[^266]: 2 Tim. 4:2 ("Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort with all longsuffering and doctrine").
[^267]: In 1136, Prince Svyatoslav Olgovich entered into an uncanonical marriage in Novgorod; Bishop Niphont refused to bless the union or allow his clergy to crown them, defying the prince's threats. See Novgorod First Chronicle.
[^268]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *O Christ, Who didst enlighten with the radiance of Thy coming* (Просветивый сиянием пришествия твоего).
[^269]: Saint Theodore the Studite (759–826), abbot of the Studios Monastery in Constantinople and champion of canonical order, who was exiled for resisting the adulterous marriage of Emperor Constantine VI (the "Moechian controversy"). Niphont is likened to him for withstanding both princes and uncanonical prelates.
[^270]: Clement Smoliatich, consecrated Metropolitan of Kyiv in 1147 by a synod of Rus' bishops without the sanction or blessing of Patriarch Nicholas IV Mouzalon of Constantinople, using the relic of the head of Saint Clement of Rome in place of patriarchal imposition of hands. Bishop Niphont of Novgorod firmly refused communion with Clement, citing the sacred canons that forbade setting up a metropolitan without the Oecumenical Patriarch.
[^271]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *The nethermost abyss hath compassed us* (Обыде нас последняя бездна). Jonah 2:5; Ps. 43:23 (44:22).
[^272]: In 1149, Grand Prince Izyaslav II Mstislavich of Kyiv, enraged by Niphont's refusal to recognize Metropolitan Clement, detained Niphont and confined him to the Kyiv Caves Monastery; when Prince Yuri Dolgorukiy took Kyiv in the autumn of 1149, he liberated Niphont and restored him to his see in Novgorod with great honor.
[^273]: Kontakion to Saint Niphont, Bishop of Novgorod, Tone 5.
[^274]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *We faithful contemplate thee, O Mother of God, as a spiritual furnace* (Тебе умную Богородице пещь). Song of the Three Holy Children / Dan. 3:49–50.
[^275]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *In the furnace the children of Israel, as in a crucible, shone brighter than gold* (В пещи отроцы Израилевы). Song of the Three Holy Children / Dan. 3:57.
[^276]: Patriarch Luke Chrysoberges of Constantinople (served 1156–1169) sent an official patriarchal charter to Bishop Niphont, praising his steadfast orthodoxy and canonical discipline, comparing him to the ancient holy fathers and confessors. See Kyiv Caves Paterikon, Discourse 14.
[^277]: In early 1156, Bishop Niphont traveled to Kyiv to welcome the canonically consecrated Metropolitan Constantine I from Constantinople, but fell ill at the Kyiv Caves Monastery before Constantine's arrival and peacefully fell asleep in the Lord on April 8, 1156.
[^278]: Melodic model: Tone 1 Heirmos: *The unburned bush showed forth an image of thy pure childbearing* (Образ чистаго рождества твоего). Exod. 3:2.
[^279]: Isa. 8:18; Heb. 2:13 ("Behold, I and the children whom God hath given me").
[^280]: Common service for a Hierarch in the Menaion / Anthologion (e.g., at the Liturgy: Prokeimenon in Tone 1: *My mouth shall speak wisdom*; Epistle to the Hebrews, sec. 318 [Heb. 7:26–8:2]; Gospel according to John, sec. 36 [John 10:9–16]).
