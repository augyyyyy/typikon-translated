# MONTH OF MARCH

*On the 28th Day:*
*Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr Eustratius of the Caves:*[^230]
*At Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera: Tone 4, Podoben: "Of the heavenly hosts" (Небесных чинов):*[^231]
O thou who didst first crucify thyself willingly unto the world by the cutting off of all passions, thou now at last, having become a partaker of the eternal suffering, sufferest the passion of ultimate mortification, being nailed unto a cross by the Judeans; as by a ladder...

*(Right page — Folio 67 recto, running header: TO EUSTRATIUS OF THE CAVES:)*
...as by a ladder thou ascendest unto heaven; whence, O blessed Eustratius, look down, raise up our minds unto the heights, and pray unto Christ God to grant us the cutting off of all passions!

A second chariot-driver Elijah, by zeal for Christ thou wast shown forth, O marvelous one: for even as he rebuked the madness of the reigning king, so didst thou also censure the God-killing madness of the foolish Judean; and from him thou didst receive to be crucified upon a cross in the body, but thy soul ran unto the heavenly realms in a chariot of fire, where thou rejoicest, illumined by unwaning light: whereof may we also not be deprived, through thy prayers!

Portraying the passion of thy Master, thou wast crucified upon a cross by a Judean, by whom also thou wast pierced in the ribs with a spear; ascending upon a chariot of fire, thou didst signify thy fervent love toward God. Standing before Him now in rejoicing, pray for those who hymn thee, O blessed father Eustratius!

*Glory... Tone 2:*
Ever looking unto the heavenly glory, O venerable one, thou didst first crucify thy body by abstinence; and afterwards, having thy hands and feet nailed by the Judeans, thou didst find the ladder of desire, whereby having ascended unto the hosts on high, a magnificent voice calling thee: This is a good citizen of the heavenly Sion! Rejoicing now in it with the saints, remember us who honor thy holy memory, that we also may become heirs thereof with thee!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
The shadow hath passed...

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. And Readings: Three for a Venerable Monk. (Seek folio 30, on the reverse).*[^232]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera: Tone 1, Podoben: "All-praised martyrs" (Всехвальнии):*[^233]
O all-praised venerable-martyr Eustratius, thou didst commit thy life unto the care of virtues; for with hymnody the faithful praise Christ in faith, being instructed by thy teachings: standing before Him, entreat the Divine Spirit that we may be confirmed in peace!

*Verse: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*
O divinely-inspired martyr Eustratius, thy life indeed was shown like unto the angels unto earthly men; and unto the heavenly ones thy confession was offered as a sweet-smelling sacrifice and good incense. And now pray, that peace and great mercy may be granted unto our souls!

*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord (Left page — Folio 67 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HOLY VENERABLE-MARTYR:)*
...the man that fears the Lord, he shall greatly delight in His commandments.

O exceedingly rich venerable-martyr Eustratius, having become a conqueror of passions, thou didst conquer the tormentor. Dancing in choir with the crown-bearers, O blessed one, remember all who celebrate thy memory! And now pray unto Christ, to grant peace and great mercy unto our souls!

*Glory... Tone 4:*
The Judeans did not understand the words of the prophets, going astray from the womb, speaking a lie against our Savior, and with the reproach of a curse condemned the Master's death. But thou, O blessed one, rebuking their God-killing, didst show forth the blessing given unto all the world through the Crucifixion. Therefore the lawless ones nailed thee to a cross, and pierced thy ribs with a spear, not knowing that for the sake of thy cross thou receivest a reward from God, while the ungodly shall quickly perish. They inherited a curse through the tree, but thou enjoyest the Tree of Life: whereof that we also may be partakers with thee, we glorify the Savior and Lord in thy memory, O father Eustratius!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Receive the supplications of thy servants...

*Troparion, Tone 7:*
Thou wast shown forth as the praise of ascetics and martyrs, O venerable Eustratius: for having first mortified the flesh by abstinence, thou didst follow Christ, bearing thy cross. Afterwards thou thyself wast also lifted up upon a cross, and pierced in the ribs with a spear, and didst commit thy soul into the hands of God. Carried up into the heavens in a chariot of fire, whence thou wast crowned by Christ God and the Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion.*

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord," the same Troparion.*

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymn: Tone 4, Podoben: "The angelic choir" (Лик ангельский):*[^234]
Emulating the passion of thy Master, thou didst endure the wood of the cross, O all-blessed one, and didst put to shame the blaspheming Judean with thy divinely-inspired lips: therefore the ungodly one pierces thy ribs with a spear, but thou rejoicing ascendest unto thy Lord; pray unto Him unceasingly for us, who lovingly honor thy sufferings, O valiantly-wise Eustratius!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
I offer angelic hymnody unto thee, O unwedded Virgin: Rejoice, thou who art full of grace and blessed among women, who didst receive in thy womb...

*(Right page — Folio 68 recto, running header: TO EUSTRATIUS OF THE CAVES:)*
...who didst receive in thy womb the Word of God seedlessly, Who delivered mankind from irrational deeds!

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn: Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-beginningless Word" (Собезначальное):*[^235]
Let us, O ye people, honor with hymns the citizen of the Jerusalem on high, the true witness, and good soldier of Christ, the valiantly-wise Eustratius, fellow-dweller of the venerable and fellow-chorister of the martyrs, singing his memory: for standing before Christ God, he unceasingly prays for our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
O Queen of the Jerusalem on high, only immaculate Mother among women, crown of virgins, and holiest of the holy, unsearchable intercessor before the Holy Trinity: have mercy upon us who have strayed far away, O Mother of God, and deliver from misfortunes those who hymn thee!

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn: Tone 3, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Скоро предвари):*[^236]
Kindled with the flame of burning love toward the crucified Christ, O blessed one, thou didst not fear the Hebrew God-killer breathing with fury, but didst valiantly rebuke him, and didst preach the confirmation of the legal Passover from the Scriptures when grace had come: therefore thou wast nailed unto a cross by the Judeans, and having received the crown of confession, thou didst enter into the heavenly courts. Where forget not us also, O father Eustratius!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
When we are burned by the flame of passions from destructive assaults, make haste, O most holy Virgin, to send down the water of grace to quench the fire burning in our members, that leading this life in tranquility, we may radiantly enjoy the Resurrection of Christ for thy sake!

*Anabathmoi, the First Antiphon of the 4th Tone.*
*Prokeimenon: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.*
*Verse: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He hath rendered unto me?*
*Let every breath praise the Lord.*
*Gospel according to Mark, pericope 37.*[^237]
*After Psalm 50, Sticheron: Tone 6:*
This day the hill of the Caves rejoices, holding thy relics as a priceless treasure, and is enlightened by the rays of thy sufferings, bringing flowers of spiritual songs unto thee and saying: O favored one of Christ, our father Eustratius, standing before the Master of all, unceasingly pray unto the Lord for us, to send down great mercy upon our souls!

*(Left page — Folio 68 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HOLY VENERABLE-MARTYR:)*
*Canon to the Venerable-Martyr.*
*Tone 1.*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Thy victorious right hand hath been glorified in power in a manner befitting God: for it, O Immortal One, as all-powerful, shattered the adversaries, fashioning a new path through the deep for the Israelites![^238]

*Troparia:*
Desiring to be a partaker of the light and day of the Resurrection, O venerable one, on the day of the Master's Passion thou didst ascend upon a cross, and wast conformed unto Christ's Passion, having conquered the world; and being now a partaker of ineffable glory, thou rejoicest, singing a song of victory!

Nothing in this turbulent world didst thou love, O venerable one, like the Cross of Christ, and having it as thy boast, with the Apostle thou didst cry aloud: God forbid that I should boast, save in the Cross of the Lord, whereon thou rejoicest, singing a song of victory![^239]

Being nailed unto a cross by the Judeans, O most blessed father, thou didst nail down all bodily passions, so that with the Apostle thou couldest say: The world hath been crucified unto me, and I unto the world; therefore worthily now thou rejoicest with the Risen One, singing a song of victory!

*Glory...*
Having departed from the lustful Egypt of this world, by the cross thou didst untroubled cross its stormy sea, O holy one; and having written over it with thy nailed palms, thou didst drown the noetic Pharaoh with his aerial armies, and now rejoicest, singing a song of victory!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having departed from the lustful Egypt of this world, I return unto former things, and pleasing the belly, feed in beastly manner; grant unto me, O Virgin Bride of God, to turn away at least a little from these things, that being set free I may offer praise unto thee, singing a song of victory!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
O Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human nature, and mercifully didst take form therein: gird me with power from on high, that I may cry unto Thee: Holy is the living temple of Thine ineffable glory, O Lover of mankind![^240]

*Troparia:*
Thou didst follow Him Who said: Come unto Me, all ye that labor; and by His Cross thou didst disperse the noetic enemies, who being utterly conquered returned with shame; pray for us also, that we may stand against their ranks: for in this we are confirmed in Christ!

Having laid the cross upon thy shoulder, O holy one, by undoubting faith and love thou didst bind thyself unto the One, that thou mightest gain Christ; and having found Him according to thy desire by the narrow path, thou didst traverse it...

*(Right page — Folio 69 recto, running header: TO EUSTRATIUS OF THE CAVES:)*
...thou didst traverse it without stumbling, having the Cross as thy strength and confirmation!

Clothed in the monastic habit, thou didst strip thyself of the world, O blessed one, and having traversed the path lightly into the promised land, into the monastery of the Caves, which pours forth the teachings of the God-bearing fathers like honey and milk, wherewith being fed thou wast made strong: by their teachings grant that we also may be confirmed!

*Glory...*
Having subjected the sensible enemy unto the spirit by the word of prayer and fasting, and by humility and obedience having conquered the noetic Amalek, thou wast pleasing unto thy Master, O father, and from Him didst receive the Cross as an unconquerable weapon, whereon we also are confirmed![^241]

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Ever shaken by the enemy's assaults, having no confirmation, I, the passionate one, flee unto thee, O Mother of God: for thou art an impregnable wall unto the faithful, wherein we are confirmed!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*
Come, ye people, let us hymn the memory of the victorious sufferer, passing the day of his rejoicing with songs and hymnody: for from this vale of tears he hath passed unto unending joy, and standing before the Master's throne, cries aloud unto us: Be ye imitators of me, even as I am of Christ, Who grants His rich mercy unto all!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Having a passionate life in the world, burdened with waves of deadly sins, O thou who didst bear the Helmsman, Christ: direct my life unto the calm haven, I pray thee, and grant me thy rich mercy!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
Seeing with his prophetic eyes the mountain overshadowed by the grace of God, Habakkuk proclaimed that the Holy One of Israel should come forth from thee, for our salvation and renewal![^242]

*Troparia:*
Knowing, O venerable one, that because of intemperance the first man settled in hades, thou didst give thyself unto abstinence and severe fasting, and from this didst acquire the name of faster: for thou wast fed by the Word of God rather than with food, singing unto Christ: For Thou art all-powerful!

By permission from on high having been taken captive by the Polovtsians, O holy one, thou wast in apostolic manner: Sorrow it is that brings every thought into captivity unto the obedience of Christ; therefore in sorrows, O blessed one, thou didst not faint, but rather marveling at the judgments of God, didst glorify Christ: For Thou art all-powerful![^243]

*(Left page — Folio 69 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HOLY VENERABLE-MARTYR:)*
Ever bearing upon thy body the image of the betrayed Jesus, O blessed father, thou wast sold into the Greek land, into the city of Korsun,[^244] unto a certain God-killing Judean, who sought to turn thee away from the love of God, for the sake of which thou wast ready even unto death, singing: Christ is risen, for He is all-powerful!

*Glory...*
Shut up in prison with other Christians, and tormented with hunger by the Hebrew, thou didst warmly and sweetly say: Who shall separate me from the love of Christ? Neither hunger, nor death, nor distress: for all these things are small compared to the glory that is to be revealed in us, who sing: Christ is risen, for He is all-powerful![^245]

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having been taken captive by noetic enemies, and being already near the prison of hades, turning unto thee I cry aloud: O Mother of God, when thou shalt turn back the captivity of my soul, then shall my tongue be filled with rejoicing, and shall sing: Thy Son is risen, for He is all-powerful!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
O Christ Who didst enlighten with the radiance of Thy coming, and by Thy Cross didst illuminate the ends of the world: enlighten the hearts of those who orthodoxly hymn Thee with the light of Thy knowledge of God![^246]

*Troparia:*
Thou didst rise early with love toward Christ, O valiant Eustratius, opposing the Hebrew who compelled thee to depart from thy Christ, Who is the Sun of Righteousness, shining forth ever-existing life unto all!

By thy teaching, O father, all the captives who were in prison spat upon the madness of the God-killing Hebrew, and chose rather to die of hunger and thirst than to deny Christ, Who grants life unto all!

A company of martyrs fifty in number, starved by hunger and thirst by the Judean, thou didst send before thee unto thy Christ, O blessed one, thyself afterwards desiring to suffer the same for the love of Jesus, Who grants life unto all![^247]

*Glory...*
Seeing, O wretched one, that when all the prisoners had died in the dungeon his gold was lost, and his hope of drawing them unto his impiety had vanished, he resolved to crucify thee upon a cross, unto which thou didst run with haste, O venerable one, crying aloud: O my Christ, lead up my soul from the bodily prison!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O most blessed Virgin, have compassion in thy goodness upon my soul that is perishing with hunger, and satisfy it with the angelic bread, and give it to drink from the torrent of invisible sweetness, that thence I may find peace for my soul!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
The nethermost abyss hath compassed us, there is none to deliver; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter: save Thy people, O our God, for Thou art the strength of the weak and their restoration![^248]

*(Right page — Folio 70 recto, running header: TO EUSTRATIUS OF THE CAVES:)*
*Troparia:*
Like that of a voracious beast was the belly of the lawless Judean, who sought to swallow thee up and bring thee into the number of the darkened Jewish synagogue; but thou, O father, didst boldly stand against his malice, and didst conquer him, being strengthened by the help of the Most High!

On the very day whereon the Judeans raged and hung the Giver of Life upon a cross, then also the wretched Hebrew crucified thee: that with Him with Whom thou didst suffer, with Him thou mayest also be glorified, O blessed one, in unwaning light, having received a reward from thy Savior!

Remembering the Master's Passion with mocking, the defiled God-killers, even as their fathers did Christ, so these, mocking at the wood of the cross, nailed thee thereto, O father; but unto them the wood of the cross became a curse, while unto thee it became the saving power of God!

*Glory...*
Rebuking the feasts of the defiled Judeans, while upon the cross, O holy one, thou didst not fear their curse: for it was abolished by Christ God nailed in the flesh, Who enlightened the world by His three-day Resurrection!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O pure Mother, who by thy blessed childbearing didst bury our forefather's curse: account me worthy to be given the blessing of God's chosen ones, O blessed and glorified one!

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*
Being a zealot of the Master's Passion and an ardent follower of fasters, having instructed the captives by thy teaching, thou didst bring them unto Christ, O Eustratius; and deliver us who are held captive by passions through thy prayers, and show us to be imitators of thy honorable life, who cry aloud: Rejoice, thou who didst sweetly drink the cup of Christ's sufferings![^249]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
We faithful contemplate thee, O Mother of God, as a spiritual furnace: for even as the Exalted One saved the three youths, the All-Powerful One renewed the world in thy womb, the praised and supremely glorified God of our fathers![^250]

*Troparia:*
Being nailed upon a cross, O father, thou didst rebuke the foolish Judean command by the prophetic Scriptures, and didst not desire to taste of the legal passover, desiring to be a partaker of the Passover which was sacrificed for the whole world: Christ God, Who is blessed!

Thou didst declare the abolishing of the curse that came from the tree from the prophets, O blessed one, and didst preach the entrance of life with Life hanging before their eyes upon the wood of the cross, and didst put to shame the Judeans who knew not how to sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*(Left page — Folio 70 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HOLY VENERABLE-MARTYR:)*
Unto the Judeans who crucified thee thou didst prophesy, O venerable father: Swiftly shall vengeance come upon you for my blood and that of the other Christians whom ye have slain innocently, and your feasts shall be turned into mourning: for the Lord hates your sabbaths, wherein ye were accustomed to sing: O Christ God, blessed art Thou![^251]

*Glory...*
Thy words were sharp arrows unto the lawless ones, wherewith being pierced they could not endure, but pierced thy ribs with a spear; and thou didst depart unto the springs of life for all the world from the pierced side, to sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Unto me who am pierced with the sinful spear of fierce Belial, swiftly apply the plaster of thy mercy, O Virgin Mother, Bride of God, that being healed I may ever sing to thee, O Lady: Blessed art thou!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
In the furnace the children of Israel, as in a crucible, shone brighter than gold with the beauty of piety, saying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and exalt Him unto all ages![^252]

*Troparia:*
Honor, O ye people, the good sufferer, who having first mentally crucified all his members by himself, afterwards being sensibly crucified by the Judeans, cried aloud unto Christ: Behold, I bear Thy marks upon my body, and I sing to Thee unto all ages![^253]

Unto thee, O father, caught up unto heaven in a chariot of fire and with horses, the choirs of martyrs and righteous went forth to meet thee, and praised the wounds of thy hands and feet and thy pierced side, receiving thee into the dwelling of the saints, to praise the Lord unto all ages!

A voice was brought unto thee, O holy one, from the magnificent glory: This good heavenly citizen, of whom the whole world was not worthy, ascends from the earthly world unto the transcendent dwellings, to sing unto the Lord unto all ages!

*Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord!*
Thy relics, a priceless treasure, O all-blessed one, the lawless Hebrew wishing to hide, cast into the sea; but by God's providence they were found here in the cave, where venerating them, we glorify the Lord Who glorified thee, O blessed one, with incorruption and wonders unto all ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who didst bring forth the priceless treasure, the most precious Jesus, from thy maternal womb: redeem my soul from the captivity of the lustful world, that I may sing to thee here...

*(Right page — Folio 71 recto, running header: TO EUSTRATIUS OF THE CAVES:)*
...that I may sing to thee here, and in the unwaning light unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
The unburned bush showed forth an image of thy pure childbearing: and now we pray thee to quench the furnace of misfortunes raging against us, that we may unceasingly magnify thee, O Mother of God![^254]

*Troparia:*
Shine, shine, O monastery of the Caves of the Mother of God! For in thee flourished the marvelous Eustratius, a blossoming tree adorned with fragrant flowers of suffering: that we also may become partakers of his beauty, we venerate his relics and magnify him with hymns!

God, Who reveals secrets, revealed the defiled murderers unto the pious emperor, showing that the Judeans who pretended to be citizens secretly slay Christians, and their iniquity came down upon their own head: for they received a death equal to their deed according to thy prophecy, O most blessed father![^255]

Thy blood innocently poured out by the Judeans, like Abel's, O father, cried aloud unto God, Who by righteous judgment brought vengeance for it; and thy tormentor being hanged upon a tree, received his portion with Judas in gehenna: therefore we magnify the portion of the Lord, Whom we venerate on this day![^256]

*Glory...*
Thy blood, O holy one, and thy unfailing prophecy afterwards brought many unto the faith: for seeing its fulfillment, truly repenting they believed in Christ, and being baptized lived well, and were accounted worthy of the blessed life, whereof that we also may not be deprived, we bow down before the reliquary of thy relics, and magnify thee!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
O immaculate Mother, who didst embrace the ever-existing Life in thy womb: direct us from temporal life unto eternal by thy mediation, and grant unto all to behold the unwaning day of the kingdom of thy Son!

*Exapostilarion:*
Foreseeing the ever-existing life, O blessed one, thou didst disregard temporal life, enduring all things inflicted by the Judeans with valor, and not wishing to taste of the legal passover, thou wast nailed unto a cross, and ascending from earth in a chariot of fire didst testify: Christ is the Passover of incorruption, the salvation of the world!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Being the mediatress of the ever-existing Light, O Virgin Bride of God: stand by us who dwell in darkness, and direct our life, that without stumbling we may reach *(Left page — Folio 71 verso, running header: SERVICE TO THE HOLY VENERABLE-MARTYR:)*
...where Christ is, the Passover of incorruption, the salvation of the world!

*At the Praises, Stichera: Tone 6:*
Having reddened thy sacred robe with martyr's blood, O venerable one, thou didst worthily enter into the Holy of Holies, O glorious one, where is ineffable light, where is divine glory, where is the voice of those that keep festival: from which, for thy martyr's labors, thou didst receive victories, and an unfading crown, and eternal glory of good things, and the life of paradise, O all-wise Eustratius; with boldness pray for our souls! *(Twice)*

The storm of torments did not shake thy soul, nor did long confinement in a dark prison, which hid thee away, O glorious sufferer: but like light thou didst shine forth, giving light unto those in distress in darkness, whom thou didst show forth as sons of the day by the laver of baptism, an exalted and saving birth, and by divine conversation, as a God-pleasing sufferer, O all-wise Eustratius!

Who shall worthily recount the victories of thy virtues? What lips shall utter thy torments and patience? For in both thou wast well-pleasing unto Christ, O Eustratius; and now cease not praying, O favored one, to save our souls, as having great boldness, O venerable-martyr!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
The Cherub, seeing thee coming on a chariot of fire unto the heavens, did not bar thee from tasting of the Tree of Life with his flaming sword, but with joy received thee, O martyr of Christ, Eustratius! Therefore now enjoying the food of paradise, forget not us who honor thy memory, but stretch forth thy palms, wherewith thou wast nailed unto the tree, and pray unto the Creator and Master of all for all, that we also may become partakers with thee, O most blessed father!

*Both now... Theotokion.*
*Great Doxology, and Dismissal.*

### Notes

[^230]: Saint Eustratius the Faster and Venerable-Martyr of the Caves (d. March 28, 1097). Tonsured at the Kyiv Caves Monastery under St. Antony, he was renowned for strict fasting. In 1096, during a Polovtsian raid on Kyiv led by Bonyak, Eustratius was taken captive along with thirty monastic and lay companions and sold into slavery to a Jewish master in Chersonesus (Korsun). When their master attempted to force the Christian captives to renounce Christ, Eustratius exhorted them to endure starvation rather than apostatize; after all fifty captives died of hunger, the enraged master had Eustratius crucified upon a cross on the Paschal feast and pierced his side with a spear. See Kyiv Caves Paterikon, Discourse 29.
