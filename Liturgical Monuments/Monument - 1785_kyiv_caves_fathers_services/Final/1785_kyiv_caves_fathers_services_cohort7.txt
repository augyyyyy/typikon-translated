Having received the calling from on high by the exchange of the angelic habit, thou wast crucified unto the world, crucifying together with it all its lustful passions, treading upon them by a harsh manner of life, O venerable one, having the only God Who loves mankind as thy helper!

That thou mightest not inherit a portion with the goats in the world to come, O father, mortifying thy flesh in this life, thou didst clothe thyself in a single garment of haircloth and in a goatskin,[^222] wherein thou didst traverse the narrow way of monastic life, calling upon God, the only Lover of mankind, for help!

Thou didst settle in the mount, and shut thyself up in a narrow cave; and reaching forth unto things on high by the ladder of virtues, O wise one, thou didst set an ascent in thy heart, ever to hymn God the Lover of mankind!

*Glory...*
The narrow way of humility and poverty didst thou traverse, O wise one, dashing the crafty enemy like an infant against the rock of the love of God...

...the love of God, seeking which, having cleansed thy soul by fasting, by all-night vigilance thou didst humble the body, that it might stand without slothfulness before God the Lover of mankind!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Overcome by slothfulness, I cannot dash the passions born within me like infants against the rock of the commandments; thee do I call upon for help, O Mother of God: help me as Mother of God the Lover of mankind, and they shall fall under my feet, conquered by thy power!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
Mortifying thy flesh by abstinence, thou didst disregard corruptible food, satisfying thyself every other day with a single prosphora; thou didst abide in abstinence, and didst not rest upon thy ribs; thus struggling against the invisible enemy, thou didst cry aloud: O Christ God, grant help unto those who are in warfare!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Darkened by the sleep of slothfulness, I stretch forth my soul unto thee, O pure one; by thy wakeful prayer and unsearchable goodness raise me up unto hymnody, and grant help unto me who am in warfare!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
For the sake of the love of Thine image, O Bountiful One, Thou didst stand upon Thy Cross, and the nations melted away: for Thou, O Lover of mankind, art my strength and praise![^223]

*Troparia:*
For the sake of the love of God in many labors and fastings, Thou didst abide, O venerable one, until the seventh year not going forth from the cell, ever remembering thy fearful departure, but unto Christ Who strengthened Thee Thou didst cry aloud: Thou, O God, art my strength and praise!

Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity: a brother helped by a brother is like a strong city, but he that contends alone against the enemy falls; which came to pass in thee also, O father, that thou shouldest not rely upon thyself, but upon the living God: for He alone is strength and praise unto all!

*Glory...*
Wishing to darken thy mind, O holy one, the devil came with a darkness of bright demons, and he shone forth like the sun, but covered thee with the darkness of deceit; from which being delivered, thou didst cry aloud unto God: Thou, O God, art my strength and praise!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having a mind darkened by passions, I am held fast by many sins; but unto thee do I pray, who beyond mind didst bear the Light of the whole world: take up the weapon of thy power, O Mother of God, and stand by me for help, that I may see the light of true knowledge of God!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Make the light of Thy commandments to shine upon me, O Lord, for my spirit rises early unto Thee, and sings to Thee: for Thou art our God, unto Thee I flee, O King of peace![^224]

*Troparia:*
Shining forth in the form of light, the sons of eternal darkness made a great cry, calling thee their own, O father Isaac, and compassed thee about like many dogs with their assaults; escaping from which, thou didst flee unto God the King of peace!

He who once ordained the sound of music to be before the gold-beaten idol for the deception of those who knew not God, the same commanded to sound with pipes and timbrels for thy deception; from which, though thou wast deceived, thou wast delivered by the help of God the King of peace!

Seeing thee exhausted by ascetic labors, the devil commanded to wear thee out with dancing; fulfilling whose command, the evil spirits leaped with thee for a long space of time, and having mocked thee, left thee scarcely alive; but afterwards amending thyself, thou didst mock him, and trampled him underfoot unto the end, by the help of God the King of peace!

*Glory...*
The venerable fathers carried thee out before the cave, terrified by the enemy's assault, speechless, breathing only a little; and knowing that this had happened from demonic working, they diligently aided thee with prayer; and the Lord the King of peace gave ear unto their prayer, and had mercy on thee!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having a heart wounded by the darkness of demonic assaults, unto thee do I pray, O Lady: apply the good plaster of mercy unto it, and save my darkened soul from falling: for thee alone do I have as a swift visitation, O Queen of light!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Engulfed in the tempest of sin, as in the belly of the sea-monster, with the Prophet I cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O Lord, and save me![^225]

*Troparia:*
Assaulted by the tempest of demonic temptations, O blessed one, thou wast paralyzed in mind and body, and knowing nothing concerning thyself, thou didst lie upon a bed for two years, neither eating bread nor drinking water; but being entreated by the prayer of the venerable Theodosius, the Lord led up thy life from corruption, and saved thee!

A strange wonder it is for human nature to be without food; yet this was fulfilled in thee, O blessed one: for where God wills, the order of nature is overcome, for in His hand is the breath of all creation, and He does whatever He wills!

Thy Cross, O Christ, is a mighty weapon against enemies, wherewith if we fence not ourselves, we shall be tempted...

...we shall be tempted like Isaac; fencing ourselves therefore with this, we pray Thee: as Thou didst not suffer Isaac to be tempted unto the end, so also suffer not us to be tempted above our strength, and save us!

*Glory...*
Glory to Thee, O Unoriginate Trinity, Cause of goodness, Who by Thy compassions liftest up all from falling: have compassion upon us Thy servants, and raise up those who have fallen into corruption, as Thou didst save Thy favored one Isaac, granting him greater strength against demons!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Glory to thee, O Mother of God, refuge of the faithful: for through thee the fallen rise up, the slain are quickened, they receive strength against the devil, and are accounted worthy of eternal life in the glory of thy Son and God!

*Kontakion, Tone 5:*
Having cast down the deception of the enemy, and shone forth radiantly in fasting, come, ye faithful, let us praise Isaac the victorious, who shone forth as gold in temptations, and enlightens the hearts of those who faithfully flee unto him, praying unceasingly unto the Lord for our souls![^226]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
Deliver us not up unto the end for Thy name's sake, and destroy not Thy covenant, and take not away Thy mercy from us, O Lord God of our fathers, praised above all unto ages![^227]

*Troparia:*
Having passed through many temptations, O venerable one, thou didst abound the more unto the glory of God, and standing firmly against the enemy, in psalmody thou didst sing: When I was fallen in distress, Thou hast set my feet in a spacious place, O Christ God, praised above all unto the ages!

Thou didst bind thyself tightly with blessed bonds, firmly binding also the enemy by steadfast obedience in the kitchen; and thou didst mock him who had mocked thee, driving him away by prayer, and anticipating all by obedience and ascetic labors!

Restoring the fallen temple of thy soul by temptations, thou didst run before all others unto the temple of God's Mother, and with thy feet frozen unto the stone from winter, thou didst stand until the end of the rule, the fire of thy heart vehemently kindling thee unto the love of God!

*Glory...*
Having formerly found grace after the demonic deception, again thou didst attain it, O father, which the bird caught by thee signified; seeing which, they knew the grace of God that was with thee, and all marveled and glorified Christ Who glorified thee, and honored thee as Christ's favored one!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Darkened by holy folly through demonic wiles, and doing things unseemly, I transgress the commandments of my Lord; but do thou, O pure Mother, grant me understanding, that I may discern the right way, and escaping the hand of Belial who hunts me, I may praise thee unto the ages!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
Him Who was nailed in the flesh upon the Cross, and showed forth a weapon of salvation unto us: ye youths, exalt Christ our God unto all ages![^228]

*Troparia:*
Having ascended the cross of patience, thou wast crucified unto the world, O most blessed Isaac: for thou didst nail thy members by the cutting off of passions, and with the spear of mockery, those who knew not thy holy folly for God's sake pierced thee; but thou didst joyfully endure all things, praising Christ unto the ages!

Stepping with bare feet upon the flame of a kindled furnace, Thou didst remain standing motionless until it was extinguished; and this signified the quenching of demonic assaults, which Thou didst swiftly cool by the power of the Cross of Christ, and in this Thou boastest unto the ages!

Many times the demons assaulted thee, O father, calling thee their own, because thou hadst bowed down before their prince; but by the power of Christ thou didst drive them away like flies, and with shame they fled from thee, unable to endure thy rebuke; and thy latter victory over them was greater than the first!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Tempted by the assaults of fierce passions, unto thy protection do I flee, O all-immaculate Virgin: for the sake of thy venerable Isaac, cover me, and save me from these assaults, that I may exalt thee with Christ unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
All of us magnify Thy love for mankind, O Christ our Savior, the glory of Thy servants and crown of the faithful, Who hast magnified the memory of her that bare Thee![^229]

*Troparia:*
All of us magnify God the Lover of mankind, Who so strengthened His servant, that he turned all demonic terrors into flight, and remained henceforth unconquered by any weapon, like an adamant, firm, whole, and unbreakable!

Thy adversary grew weak, O venerable one, though many times by night he brought many demons unto his aid, with mattocks and spades, and they cried aloud: Let us dig up this cave, and bury him here, for already he overcomes us, calling upon the unconquerable name of the Most High and her that bare Him for help!

Having driven away the nocturnal darkness, O blessed one, thou didst drive away those who came by night with the light-bearing sign of the Cross, wherewith...

Having endured many labors in life, struggling with enemies, thou didst reach a blessed end, and wast translated unto things on high into transcendent rest; but thy memory abides among us, and the Church confesses thy labors: remembering which we pray thee, visit us, and grant a blessing unto those who honor thy translation on this day, O our father Isaac!

*At the Praises, Stichera: Tone 3:*
Thou didst do good according to the word of the Lord, taking the furrows of thy heart without pain with the plow of thy narrow life, O venerable one; having tilled the field of thy soul, thou didst water it with a stream of tears, and sowing labors like pure wheat, thou didst grow ears of monastic virtues, and didst reap sheaves of unceasing prayers, which thou didst bring into the heavenly granary; and now receiving praise with the good servants, remember us, O all-blessed father, who lovingly honor thy memory!

Having walked by the narrow path in this temporal age with sorrows, tears, and unceasing vigilance, with groanings and afflictions, O worthy of glory, thou didst turn away from the broad, ruinous way, and didst reach the heavenly expanse, where having rest...
