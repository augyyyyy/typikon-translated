# APPENDIX: SUNDAY THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES

*Sunday Resurrection Theotokia of the Eight Tones, at Vespers at "Lord, I have cried," and at the Aposticha.*[^791]

*Tone 1. At "Lord, I have cried":*[^792]
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of all the world, who blossomed forth from mankind and bore the Master, the celestial gate, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful; for she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead: she destroyed the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace, and opened the Kingdom! Having her therefore as the confirmation of our faith, we have the Lord Who was born of her as our Defender: take courage therefore, take courage, O people of God; for He will conquer the enemies, as the Almighty!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^793]
Lo, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for a Virgin thou hast given birth, and after giving birth thou hast remained even as thou wast before birthgiving; for He Who was born was God, therefore also He made nature anew: but O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple; but as carrying the Tender-hearted One in thy hands, have compassion upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*[^794]
The shadow of the law passed away when grace came; for even as the bush burned not when it was on fire, so a Virgin thou hast given birth, and a Virgin thou hast remained: instead of a pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness has shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ the salvation of our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^795]
O wonder new, exceeding all ancient wonders! For who has known a Mother who has given birth without a man, and carries in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? It is the counsel of God that has been born, Whom as an Infant having carried in thy hands, O all-pure one, and having maternal boldness toward Him: cease not to pray for those who honor thee, to show mercy and save our souls!

*(Left page — Folio 149 verso, running header: SUNDAY THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES)*
*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^796]
How can we not marvel at thy God-man birthgiving, O all-honored one? For having received no trial of man, O all-immaculate one, thou didst bear in the flesh a Son without a Father, born of the Father without a Mother before all ages, Who underwent no change, nor mingling, nor division, but preserved intact the property of both natures. Therefore, O Mother and Virgin Sovereign Lady, pray Him that the souls of those who orthodoxly confess thee as Theotokos may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^797]
Without seed from the Holy Spirit, but by the will of the Father, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who exists before the ages from the Father without a Mother, but for our sake came to be from thee without a Father, Whom thou didst bear in the flesh, and didst nourish as an Infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray, that our souls may be delivered from tribulations!

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*[^798]
David the Prophet, the forefather of God, melodiously proclaimed concerning thee for thy sake unto Him Who worked great things in thee: At Thy right hand stood the Queen! For God Who was well pleased without a father to become man from thee showed thee forth as the Mother and mediatress of life, that He might renew His image corrupted by passions, and having found the sheep that went astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His will unite it with the celestial powers, and save the world, O Theotokos: Christ Who has great and rich mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^799]
Look down upon the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, assuaging the fierce assaults against us, and delivering us from every sorrow; for thee alone do we possess as a firm and sure confirmation, and we have acquired thy protection: let us not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady, who call upon thee; hasten unto the supplication of those who faithfully cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, help of all, joy, protection, and salvation of our souls!

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*[^800]
In the Red Sea the image of the unwedded Bride was prefigured of old: there Moses was the parter of the water, and here Gabriel was the minister of the marvel. Then Israel traversed the deep dry-shod; and now the Virgin has given birth unto Christ without seed. The sea remained impassable after the passage of Israel; the immaculate one remained incorrupt after the birthgiving of Emmanuel...

*(Right page — Folio 150 recto, running header: SUNDAY THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES)*
...incorrupt. He Who is and Who was before, manifested as man, O God, have mercy on us!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^801]
Thou art a temple and a gate, a palace and a throne of the King, O all-honored Virgin, through whom my Deliverer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those asleep in darkness, being the Sun of righteousness, wishing to enlighten what He created according to His image by His own hand. Therefore, O all-hymned one, as having acquired maternal boldness toward Him, unceasingly pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*[^802]
Who will not bless thee, O Most Holy Virgin? Who will not sing of thy most pure birthgiving? For the only-begotten Son Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the same came forth from thee, pure one, having ineffably become incarnate: being God by nature, and for our sake having become man by nature, not divided into two Persons, but known in two natures without confusion: pray Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that He have mercy upon our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^803]
My Maker and Deliverer, Christ the Lord, O all-pure one, coming forth from thy womb and clothing Himself in me, has delivered Adam from the primal curse. Therefore unto thee, O all-pure one, as truly the Mother of God and Virgin, do we cry aloud unceasingly: Rejoice angelically! Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, protection, shelter, and salvation of our souls!

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*[^804]
Thou wast known as Mother above nature, O Theotokos, yet didst remain a Virgin above word and understanding, and no tongue can declare the marvel of thy birthgiving; for the conception being all-glorious, O pure one, the manner of birthgiving is incomprehensible: for where God wills, the order of nature is overcome. Therefore, knowing thee to be the Mother of God, we all fervently pray thee: Pray that our souls may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^805]
Beneath thy shelter, O Sovereign Lady, all the earthborn fleeing, do we cry aloud unto thee: O Theotokos, our hope, deliver us from boundless transgressions, and save our souls!

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*[^806]
The King of heaven, through love for mankind, appeared upon earth and lived with men; for from the pure Virgin *(Left page — Folio 150 verso, running header: SUNDAY THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES)*
...taking pure flesh from the pure Virgin and coming forth from her with the assumption, is the One Son, twofold in nature, but not in Hypostasis. Therefore, truly proclaiming Him perfect God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God: Whom pray, O unwedded Mother, that He have mercy upon our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^807]
O unwedded Virgin, who didst ineffably conceive God in the flesh, O Mother of God the Most High: receive the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, granting unto all purification of transgressions: now receiving our supplications, pray that we all may be saved!

*Sunday Dismissal Theotokia after the Troparia of the Saints, of the Eight Tones.*[^808]

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*[^809]
When Gabriel announced unto thee, O Virgin: Rejoice! with the voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, as righteous David said: Thou hast appeared more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator: glory to Him Who took up His abode in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who has delivered us through thy birthgiving!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*[^810]
All thy mysteries are beyond comprehension, all thy mysteries are all-glorious, O Theotokos; for purity being sealed and virginity preserved, thou wast known as a true Mother, having born the true God: pray Him that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^811]
We hymn thee who didst mediate the salvation of our race, O Theotokos Virgin; for taking the Passion upon the Cross in the flesh assumed from thee, thy Son and our God has delivered us from corruption, as the Lover of mankind!

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*[^812]
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto angels, was manifested through thee, O Theotokos, unto those upon earth: God becoming incarnate in unconfused union, and voluntarily accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised the first-fashioned man, He saved our souls from death!

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*[^813]
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, rampart and protection of those who hasten unto thee! Rejoice, haven unshaken by storms and unwedded Bride, who didst bear in the flesh thy Maker and God: cease not to pray for those who hymn and worship thy birthgiving!

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*[^814]
Calling Thy Mother blessed, Thou didst come unto the Passion by voluntary will, having shone forth *(Left page — Folio 151 verso, running header: SUNDAY THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES)*
...having shone forth upon the Cross, wishing to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: Rejoice with Me, for the lost coin has been found! O our God Who hast wisely ordered all things, glory to Thee!

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*[^815]
As the treasury of our resurrection, lead up from the pit and the deep of transgressions those who hope in thee, O all-hymned one; for thou didst save those guilty of sin, having born our Salvation, who before birthgiving wast a Virgin, and in birthgiving a Virgin, and after birthgiving again abidest a Virgin!

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*[^816]
He Who for our sake was born of the Virgin, and endured crucifixion, O Good One, Who by death didst overthrow death, and manifested resurrection as God: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thy hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; receive the Theotokos who bore Thee, who intercedes for us; and save, O our Savior, a despairing people!

*The End, and unto God be the glory.*[^817]

### Notes

[^791]: Liturgical appendix containing the eight classical Sunday Dogmatika (*Dogmatica Theotokia*) sung at Great Vespers at *Lord, I have cried*, along with the corresponding Sunday Aposticha Theotokia.
[^792]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 1 (*Всемї́рную сла́вꙋ* / *Τὴν παγκόσμιον δόξαν*), attributed to Saint John of Damascus.
[^793]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 1 (*Сѐ и҆спо́лнися И҆са́їино прорече́нїе* / *Ἰδοὺ πεπλήρωται ἡ τοῦ Ἡσαΐου πρόρρησις*); Scriptural reference to the prophecy of the Virgin birth in Isaiah 7:14 (LXX Isa. 7:14: *ἰδοὺ ἡ παρθένος ἐν γαστρὶ ἕξει καὶ τέξεται υἱόν*).
[^794]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 2 (*Пре́йде сѣ́нь зако́ннаѧ* / *Παρῆλθεν ἡ σκιὰ τοῦ νόμου*); typological contrast between the Old Testament burning bush (Exodus 3:2) and pillar of fire (Exodus 13:21) and the Virgin Birth of Christ.
[^795]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 2 (*Ѡ҆ чꙋдесѐ но́вагѡ* / *Ὢ θαύματος καινοῦ*).
[^796]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 3 (*Ка́кѡ не диви́мсѧ* / *Πῶς μὴ θαυμάσωμεν*); dogmatic definition of the Chalcedonian christological union (*without change, confusion, or division*).
[^797]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 3 (*Без̾ сѣ́мене ѿ Бж҃е́ственнагѡ Дх҃а* / *Ἀσπόρως ἐκ θείου Πνεύματος*).
[^798]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 4 (*И҆́же тебѐ ра́ди Бг҃ооте́цъ прро́къ Дв҃дъ* / *Ὁ διὰ σὲ θεοπάτωρ προφήτης Δαυῒδ*); citation of LXX Psalm 44:10 (*παρέστη ἡ βασίλισσα ἐκ δεξιῶν σου*), with the Parable of the Lost Sheep (Luke 15:4–6).
[^799]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 4 (*При́зри на моле́нїа твои́хъ ра̑бъ* / *Ἔπιδε ἐπὶ τὰς δεήσεις τῶν οἰκετῶν σου*).
[^800]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 5 (*Въ чермнѣ́мъ мо́ри* / *Ἐν τῇ Ἐρυθρᾷ θαλάσσῃ*); typological fulfillment of the Red Sea crossing (Exodus 14:21–29) and the virginity of the Theotokos (*post partum*).
[^801]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 5 (*Хра́мъ и҆ две́рь є҆сѝ* / *Ναὸς καὶ πύλη ὑπάρχεις*); Scriptural allusion to the Sun of righteousness (Malachi 4:2; LXX Mal. 3:20).
[^802]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 6 (*Кто̀ тебѐ не оу҆блажи́тъ* / *Τίς μὴ μακαρίσει σε*); classical exposition of the hypostatic union in two natures without confusion (*ἀσυγχύτως*).
[^803]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 6 (*Творе́цъ и҆ и҆зба́витель мо́й* / *Ὁ ποιητὴς καὶ λυτρωτής μου*).
[^804]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 7 (*Мт҃и оу҆́бѡ позна́ласѧ є҆сѝ* / *Μήτηρ μὲν ἐγνώσθης*); patristic aphorism: *ὅπου γὰρ βούλεται Θεός, νικᾶται φύσεως τάξις* ("where God wills, the order of nature is overcome").
[^805]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 7 (*По́д̾ кро́въ тво́й Влⷣчце* / *Ὑπὸ τὴν σὴν εὐσπλαγχνίαν*).
[^806]: Sunday Dogmatikon, Tone 8 (*Цр҃ь нбⷭный* / *Ὁ βασιλεὺς τῶν οὐρανῶν*); Scriptural allusion to the Incarnation (Baruch 3:37; LXX Bar. 3:38: *μετὰ τοῦτο ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς ὤφθη καὶ ἐν τοῖς ἀνθρώποις συνανεστράφη*).
[^807]: Sunday Aposticha Theotokion, Tone 8 (*Безневѣ́стнаѧ Дв҃о, ꙗ҆́же Бг҃а неизрече́ннѡ заче́нши пло́тїю* / *Ἀνύμφευτε Παρθένε, ἡ τὸν Θεὸν ἀφράστως συλλαβοῦσα σαρκί*).
[^808]: Collection of the Sunday Dismissal Theotokia (*Theotokia Apolytikia*) chanted throughout the liturgical year following the dismissal troparia of the saint or feast in each of the Eight Tones.
[^809]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 1 (*Гаврїи́лꙋ вѣща́вшꙋ тебѣ̀ Дв҃о* / *Τοῦ Γαβριὴλ φθεγξαμένου σοι Παρθένε*); Scriptural reference to the Annunciation (Luke 1:28) and King David's Ark of the Covenant (LXX Ps. 131:8: *ἀνάστηθι, Κύριε, εἰς τὴν ἀνάπαυσίν σου, σὺ καὶ ἡ κιβωτὸς τοῦ ἁγιάσματός σου*).
[^810]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 2 (*Всѧ̑ па́че смы́сла* / *Πάντα ὑπὲρ ἔννοιαν*).
[^811]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 3 (*Тѧ̀ хода́тайствовавшꙋю спасе́нїе ро́да на́шегѡ* / *Σὲ τὴν μεσιτεύσασαν τὴν σωτηρίαν*).
[^812]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 4 (*Є҆́же ѿ вѣ́ка оу҆тае́ное* / *Τὸ ἀπ᾽ αἰῶνος ἀπόκρυφον*); patristic synthesis of Colossians 1:26 and Ephesians 3:9.
[^813]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 5 (*Ра́дꙋйсѧ, две́ре Гдⷭнѧ непроходи́маѧ* / *Χαῖρε πύλη Κυρίου ἡ ἀδιόδευτος*); Scriptural allusion to the closed eastern gate in Ezekiel 44:1–2.
[^814]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 6 (*Бл҃гослове́ннꙋю нареки́й твою̀ Мт҃рь* / *Ὁ τὴν εὐλογημένην καλέσας σου Μητέρα*); Scriptural reference to the Parable of the Lost Coin (Luke 15:8–10).
[^815]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 7 (*Ꙗ҆́кѡ на́шегѡ воскресе́нїѧ сокро́вище* / *Ὡς τῆς ἡμῶν ἀναστάσεως θησαύρισμα*); affirmation of the perpetual virginity (*semper virgo / ἀειπάρθενος*) of the Theotokos before, during, and after childbirth.
[^816]: Sunday Dismissal Theotokion, Tone 8 (*И҆́же на́съ ра́ди рожде́йсѧ ѿ Дв҃ы* / *Ὁ δι᾽ ἡμᾶς γεννηθεὶς ἐκ Παρθένου*).
[^817]: Traditional concluding colophon formula of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra printing house (*Коне́цъ, и҆ Бг҃ꙋ сла́ва* / *Finis, et Deo gloria*), marking the formal completion of the 1785 print run.
