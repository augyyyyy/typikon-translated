# PART V: TYPIKON CONCERNING TEMPLES

## Typikon Concerning Temples

The Temple Typika (*Ustavy Khramoviyi*) in use among us are of a twofold kind: some are borrowed from the local Western Churches, which do not enter into the original fabric of our Divine Services, but have been attached to it; and others are innate to our Divine Services. The typika borrowed from the Western Churches relate to the Holy Mysteries of the Eucharist, which on the day of the Patronal Feast are exposed upon the Holy Table, and with Them a Procession takes place around the church; and concerning these it behooves us first to set forth the Typikon, and afterwards concerning the rest.

### Chapter I
#### Concerning the Exposition of the Holy Mysteries Upon the Holy Table

The Priest, on one of the days preceding the Feast, consecrates at the Liturgy two Lambs, and of the one he communicates, but the other, having placed it into the Pyx (*Kivot*), he preserves until the day of the Feast. All these things he performs according to the Typikon written for Great Thursday, here on page 435. When the feast-day itself arrives, after the conclusion of the Proskomide, immediately before the beginning of the Liturgy, the Deacons (or the Priest himself) having censed all things

according to custom, the Exposition of the Holy Mysteries takes place unalterably as was indicated on the Feast of the Eucharist, here on page 512.
After the Prayer behind the Ambo, the Holy Mysteries are hidden in the Tabernacle, and before the beginning of Vespers are exposed again; for it is not fitting to leave the Holy Mysteries without adorers, and without a sufficient number of candles, of which according to the Synod of Lviv there are to be at least six of wax.[^667]

### § 2. Concerning the Procession with the Holy Mysteries
#### At Vespers

Before the beginning of Vespers, the Priest vested in all the priestly vestments, and the Deacon in all the deacon's vestments, having come before the steps of the Holy Table, open the Holy Doors; and the first Deacon, having laid aside the Gospel Book to the right, takes the Holy Mysteries from the Tabernacle, and sets Them upon the Iliton; and the Priest having censed Them thrice, in all things as at the beginning of the Liturgy, he sets Them atop the Tabernacle upon the folded Iliton, and having placed the Gospel Book again in the middle of the Holy Table, exclaims: **"Bless, Master"** (*Благословѝ, Влады́ко*); and the Priest begins: **"Blessed is our God"** (*Благослове́нъ Бо́гъ на́шъ*), standing with the Deacons by the Holy Table; and Vespers is sung with the Entrance, the Priest stepping away from the Holy Table only at the Entrance, and the Deacons also at the Ektenia, and after the Prokeimenon for the Sermon, if this is ever to take place, the Holy Mysteries being veiled by the Tabernacle.
Before the Dismissal of Vespers, all having bended their knees, the Deacon, having taken the Sacred Gospel Book from the middle of the Holy Table, and having set it on the right side thereof, takes down

the Holy Mysteries from atop the Tabernacle, and sets Them upon the folded Iliton; and the Priest having censed Them thrice with a bow of the head, and having returned the Censer, and having taken upon himself the humeral veil, approaches the Holy Mysteries, and having bowed profoundly, takes Them with both hands, with the Deacon assisting, and having turned by his right toward the people, and having intoned the beginning of some devout hymn, goes forth by the Royal Doors into the church; and having entered beneath the Canopy, advances unto the doors of the church, the Deacons preceding him with Censers, and unceasingly censing the Holy Mysteries from the sides; then the other Priests in Phelonia, and others only in Epitrakhelia, with lighted candles in hand, in two wings; likewise also the Church Brotherhood, then four Maidens carrying icons, and Banner-bearers, and the Cross-bearer. With bells chiming, the Procession issuing from the church circles around the same, beginning from its northern side, and returning from the southern. When the Holy Mysteries have reached the middle of the northern side, the Procession halts, and the chanting being interrupted, and all having bended their knees, the Priest blesses the entire people present with the Holy Mysteries four times, that is, first straight ahead of himself, then to his right, then to his back, then to his left side, saying at each turning in a gentle voice: **"O God, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance"** (*Спасѝ, Бо́же, лю́ди твоя̀, и҆ благословѝ достоя́нїе твоѐ*). In like manner it takes place also on the eastern side, and on the southern, and on the western.[^668] And after the blessing at the western side of the church, [the Priest] enters into

the church: first the Priest carrying the Holy Mysteries, with the other Sacred Ministers, then the rest of the Procession and the people. Then the Priest having set the Holy Mysteries upon the Holy Table, upon the folded Iliton, and having laid aside the humeral veil, and having bended his knees, and having censed the Holy Mysteries thrice, all having bended their knees, sings with the other Sacred Ministers the Verses of the Penitential Litiya: **"Holy God"** (*Святы́й Бо́же*), all the people repeating each Verse. After the conclusion of the Litiya, the Priest having censed the Holy Mysteries thrice, and having taken upon himself the humeral veil, approaches the Holy Table, and having bowed profoundly, takes the Holy Mysteries, and having turned by his right toward the people, sings with devotion the Verse of the Ambrosian Hymn (*Te Deum*): **"Save Thy people, O Lord"** (*Спасѝ лю́ди твоя̀, Го́споди*), twice, the people repeating the same; then a third time: **"Save Thy people, O Lord, and bless Thine inheritance"** (*Спасѝ лю́ди твоя̀, Го́споди, и҆ благословѝ достоя́нїе твоѐ*); after: **"and bless"**, he blesses the people crosswise and slowly, completing a semicircle from the right to the left side; and, while the Choir sings the rest of the Ambrosian Hymn, having set the Holy Mysteries upon the Holy Table, and having descended before the steps, and having laid aside the humeral veil, and having censed Them thrice, he kneels himself and all the rest, until the Deacon encloses the Holy Mysteries into the Tabernacle; then takes place the Great Dismissal according to custom.
On one of the days after the Feast, the Priest, celebrating the Liturgy, consumes after his communion also the Lamb preserved in the Pyx, taking It from the small vessel in which It is kept. These things he does if he has an Ostensorium; for if the Holy Mysteries are exposed only with the Pyx, none of these things takes place.

### Chapter II
## Temple Typika
### Entering into the Fabric of Our Divine Services

Such Typika are either general or proper. The general apply to many cases, the proper only to certain ones. We set forth first the general Typika, and then the proper ones.

### § 1. General Typika[^669]

1. On a Patronal Feast, even if it be of a Saint of the lowest rank, an All-Night Vigil must be celebrated. Thus do we perform, and thus has the *Tserkovne Oko* in Chapter 4 concerning Temples.[^670]
2. The Feast of the Patron Saint of a Temple, even if of the lowest rank, takes precedence even over the Feast of a Saint with a Vigil, first in that if it occurs on a Sunday, it excludes at Matins before the Canon the Prokeimenon, Gospel, and Resurrectional Sticheron, unalterably as does a Feast of the Theotokos, and likewise transfers the Gospel Sticheron from the Praises to the end of Matins. Besides this, if it occurs

on a weekday, it excludes at the Liturgy the Epistle and Gospel of the day, which are then read on the day preceding the Feast. Finally, the Patronal Feast of a Saint has its own Apodosis, which takes place on the very day of the Feast at Vespers toward the following day, and this with an Entrance, like a Feast of the Lord. There will be therefore at this Apodosis at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Го́споди воззва́хъ*), 6 Stichera: 3 to the Temple [Saint], and 3 to the Saint of the day; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) to the Temple; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion from the Resurrectional ones (Dogmatikon) according to the Tone of the Doxastikon (*Naslávnyk*). If the Temple is dedicated to the Lord, or to the Theotokos, then both **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Temple. On Friday evening toward Saturday, we always sing the Dogmatikon of the departing Tone. — After the Prokeimenon: **"Let us all say"** (*Рцѣ́мъ всѝ*). At the Aposticha, [Stichera] of the Temple with its refrains; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion from the Resurrectional ones according to the Tone of the Doxastikon. If the Temple is dedicated to the Lord or to the Theotokos, then **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Temple. At the conclusion, Troparion of the Temple; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*), Troparion of the Saint of the day; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion from the Resurrectional ones according to the Tone of the Doxastikon. If the Temple is dedicated to the Lord, or to the Theotokos, then Troparion of the Saint of the day; **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), of the Temple. Great Dismissal with commemoration of the Temple, and of the Saint of the day. With this Vespers the Service of the Patron Saint of the Temple is concluded; however, the Patron Saint of the Temple has no Apodosis if on the morrow there occurs a Sunday, or a Saint with Polyeleos.
3. If the Temple [Feast] of a Saint occurs inside a Feast, that is, in the Forefeast and Afterfeast, and at the Apodosis of a Feast, hold to the Typikon of a Saint with a Vigil occurring in an Afterfeast, whether on a Sunday or on weekdays, here on pages 116 and 117;[^671] only on Sunday at Matins, we sing the Prokeimenon, Gospel, and Sticheron of the Temple; at

the Praises, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) to the Temple, but the Gospel Sticheron after the Dismissal of Matins.[^672]
4. On the very day of the Feast after the Prayer behind the Ambo, commonly in our lands there takes place the Blessing of Waters outside the church, whither the Procession goes, the Cross-bearer preceding, and the Censer-bearer, and the Deacon with the Gospel Book, and the Priest with the Hand Cross. And the church servers ought to carry thither also the Incense Boat (*Korablets*) with incense, and the Aspergillum (*Kropylo*) with the Aspergil-bucket (*Kropilnitsya*), and a vial (*stklyanitsya*), and a small towel (*obrusets*) for wiping the Cross upon its immersion into the water, and the Priest's hands. After the Dismissal of the Liturgy there takes place the Polychronion (*Mnoholitstviye*) to the Ecumenical Pontiff, and to the Emperor, and to the Metropolitan, and to the local Hierarch, and to all the Sacred Ministers present, and to the people of the Parish.
5. On the morrow of the Feast, according to the local custom, the Liturgy is sung for the Departed of the Parish, with a Parastas, at which it is not fitting to take anything of the Feast, but all things only for the Repose.
6. The Feast of the Patron Saint of a Temple is commonly transferred among us to the following Sunday; but in Cathedral Churches it is celebrated on its own day.

### § 2. Proper Temple Typika[^673]

#### Outside the Triodion

##### September 1: Temple of the Venerable Simeon the Stylite

###### On Weekdays

####### At Little Vespers

1. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Го́споди воззва́хъ*): 4 Stichera of the Temple, and **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) to him, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
2. At the Aposticha: Stichera of the Indiction, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
3. At the conclusion: Troparion of the Indiction, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Synaxis of the Theotokos.[^674]

####### At Great Vespers

1. From the beginning, that is, from **"Come, let us worship"** (*Прїидѣ́те поклони́мсѧ*), even to the Litiya, all things as also outside the Temple [Feast], here on page 142.
2. At the Litiya: Stichera of the Temple, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion according to the Tone of the Doxastikon (*Naslávnyk*), from the Aposticha of Resurrectional Great Vespers.

3. At the Aposticha: Stichera of the Temple, and **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) to him, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
4. At the Blessing of Loaves: Troparion of the Temple twice, and **"O Theotokos and Virgin"** (*Богоро́дице Дѣ́во*) once.

####### At Great Matins

1. From the beginning, that is, from **"Glory to the holy"** (*Сла́ва ст҃ѣ́й*), even to the Polyeleos, all things as also outside the Temple [Feast], here on page 124; only after the 2nd Psalter Section (*Stikhoslóviye*) the Doxastikon (*Naslávnyk*) of Callistus is omitted, and there is sung **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion of the Indiction.
2. From the Polyeleos even to the Canon all things only to the Temple. Prokimenon: **"Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones"** (*Честна̀ предъ Го́сподемъ сме́рть преподо́бныхъ є҆гѡ̀*). Gospel from Luke, Pericope 24: **"Jesus stood on a level place"** (*Ста̀ І҆исоу́съ на мѣ́стѣ ра́внѣ*).
3. Two Canons for 14: of the Indiction with the Heirmos for 6, and of the Temple for 8. Katavasia ordinary.
After the 3rd Ode: Kontakion of the Indiction, and Sessional Hymn of the Temple, **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), Sessional Hymn of the Indiction. — After the 6th [Ode]: Kontakion and Ikos of the Temple. — After the 9th [Ode]: Exaposteilarion of the Temple, **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), of the Indiction.
4. At the Praises, 8 Stichera: of the Indiction 4, and of the Temple 4; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
5. After the Doxology, Troparion of the Indiction, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion of the year (*lyeta*).

####### At the Hours, and at the Liturgy

All things as also outside the Temple [Feast], here on page 125.

##### September 1: Temple of the Venerable Simeon the Stylite on Sunday

###### At Little Vespers

1. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Го́споди воззва́хъ*), Resurrectional Stichera of the Octoechos.
2. At the Aposticha: one Resurrectional Sticheron, then Stichera of the Temple; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
3. At the conclusion: Resurrectional Troparion, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.[^675]

###### At Great Vespers

1. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Го́споди воззва́хъ*), 10 Stichera: Resurrectional 3, and to the Indiction 3, and of the Temple 4; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the Tone.
2. At the Litiya, Stichera of the Temple, written at the Praises; only **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.[^676]
3. At the Aposticha, Resurrectional Stichera, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
4. At the Blessing of Loaves, **"O Theotokos and Virgin"** (*Богоро́дице Дѣ́во*) twice, and of the Temple once.

###### At Great Matins

1. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Бо́гъ Госпо́дь*): Resurrectional Troparion twice, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
2. After the 1st and 2nd Psalter Sections (*Stikhoslóviye*), Resurrectional Sessional Hymns of the Octoechos.

3. After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymns of the Temple, once each, then **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), of the Indiction.[^677]
4. Prokimenon, Gospel, and Sticheron of the Temple.
5. Three Canons: Resurrectional with Heirmos for 4, and of the Indiction for 4, and of the Temple for 6. — Katavasia ordinary.
After the 3rd Ode: Kontakion and Ikos of the Temple, and Kontakion of the Indiction, and Sessional Hymn of the Temple, **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), of the Indiction. After the 6th [Ode]: Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos,[^678] after the 9th [Ode]: Resurrectional Exaposteilarion, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Indiction.
6. At the Praises, 8 Stichera: Resurrectional 3, and of the Indiction 2, and of the Temple 3, with two refrains to him;[^679] **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*): **"All-blessed art Thou"** (*Преблагослове́нна є҆сѝ*).
7. After the Doxology, Resurrectional Troparion, one of the two ordinary ones.
8. After the Dismissal: **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), Gospel Sticheron.

###### At the Hours and at the Liturgy

All things as also outside the Temple [Feast], here on page 127.

#### January 1: Temple of Saint Basil the Great on the Sunday before the Theophany

"If it occur on the 1st day of January that the Temple is of Saint Basil of Caesarea, or of any Saint on the Sunday before the Theophany, we sing the Service of the Temple as for the Temple of Simeon the Stylite on Sunday,"[^680] substituting for the Service of the Indiction the Service of the Circumcision.

### In the Triodion Cycle

#### The Lenten Triodion

##### 1. Temple of a Saint on the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee, Prodigal Son, Meatfare, and Cheesefare, and on Monday of the 1st Week of the Fast

"We sing the Service of the Temple on all these days, as for the Meeting of the Lord,"[^681] here on pages 245 and 246. However:
1. Troparia and Stichera are concluded by the ordinary Theotokia, as in the case of a Saint with a Vigil.
2. At Great Vespers: at the Litiya we sing the Stichera of the Triodion from the Praises, then of the Temple; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), one Sticheron from the Praises of the Triodion; only on Meatfare and Cheesefare Sundays, since the Theotokion of the Triodion is proper, therewith we conclude the Doxastikon (*Naslávnyk*). Aposticha Resurrectional,

**"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Triodion. — At the Blessing of Loaves, Troparion **"O Theotokos and Virgin"** (*Богоро́дице Дѣ́во*) twice, and of the Temple once.
3. At Great Matins: three Canons, for 14: Resurrectional for 4, and of the Triodion for 4, and of the Temple for 6. — After the 3rd Ode, Kontakion and Ikos of the Triodion, then Sessional Hymn of the Temple, **"Glory... Both now..."** (*Сла́ва, и҆ ны́нѣ*), of the Triodion; after the 6th [Ode], Kontakion and Ikos of the Temple; after the 9th [Ode], Resurrectional Exaposteilarion, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Triodion. — Katavasia ordinary, only on Meatfare Sunday Katavasia of the Triodion. — At the Praises, 8 Stichera: Resurrectional 4, and of the Temple 4, with two refrains to him; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*): **"All-blessed art Thou"** (*Преблагослове́нна є҆сѝ*); however, on Meatfare and Cheesefare Sundays, after the four Stichera of the Temple with their two refrains, we sing the Idiomelon Sticheron of the Triodion with the refrain **"Arise, O Lord"** (*Воскреснѝ Го́споди*), **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*), the same, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*): **"All-blessed art Thou"** (*Преблагослове́нна є҆сѝ*).[^682]

##### 2. Temple of a Saint on Meatfare Saturday

We sing the Service of the Temple on Saturday, and for the Departed on the preceding Thursday.[^683]

##### 3. Temple of a Saint on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday of Cheesefare

All things according to the general Typikon of a Temple Saint, here on page 516; Only:
1. At the Aposticha of Little Vespers, and at the Praises, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) will be the Idiomelon of the day in the Triodion.

2. On Wednesday and Friday at the end of Vespers, and Matins, and each Hour we make 3 great prostrations; and at the 6th Hour there will be the Troparion of the Prophecy (*Paroimia*), and the Prophecy; but the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom.[^684]

##### 4. Temple of a Saint on Cheesefare Saturday

All things according to the Typikon of the Meeting of the Lord, here on page 247; only:
1. To the Troparia and Stichera we supply ordinary Theotokia.
2. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Го́споди воззва́хъ*), and at the Aposticha of Little and Great Vespers, and at the Praises, the Doxastikon (*Naslávnyk*) will be to the Temple, and **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Theotokion from the ordinary ones according to the Tone of the Doxastikon; at the Litiya, however, the Doxastikon will be to the Fathers, and **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), ordinary Theotokion (from the Aposticha) according to the Tone of the Doxastikon.
3. At the Blessing of Loaves, Troparion of the Temple once, and to the Fathers once, and **"O Theotokos and Virgin"** (*Богоро́дице Дѣ́во*) once.[^685]

##### 5. Temple of a Saint on Monday of the 1st Week of the Fast

The entire Service of the Temple is sung on Cheesefare Sunday according to the Typikon here on page 249.[^686]

##### 6. Temple of a Saint on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday of the 1st Week of the Fast

The Service of the Temple is sung on the Saturday of the 1st Week of the Fast together with the Service of Theodore, according to the Typikon of that same Saturday, as in the following number 7.

###### On Friday Evening

###### At Little Vespers

All things according to the general Typikon of a Saint with a Vigil on Sunday.

###### At Great Vespers

1. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Го́споди воззва́хъ*), 10 Stichera: Resurrectional 3, and of the Triodion 3, and of the Temple 4 with the Doxastikon; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Triodion, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), 1st Theotokion (Dogmatikon) of the Tone.
2. At the Litiya, Stichera of the Temple, and of the Triodion, which are at the Praises; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Triodion.
3. Aposticha Resurrectional; only **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*) of the Temple, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*) of the Triodion.

3. At the Praises, 9 Stichera: Resurrectional 3, and of the Triodion 2, and of the Temple 3, with the Doxastikon and with two refrains to him, and Idiomelon Sticheron of the Triodion with the refrain **"Arise, O Lord"** (*Воскреснѝ Го́споди*), **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*), the same, **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*): **"All-blessed art Thou"** (*Преблагослове́нна є҆сѝ*).[^688]

##### 9. Temple of a Saint on One of the Fasting Days of the 2nd and Subsequent Weeks of the Fast, that is, until Lazarus Saturday

"We sing on those days the Service of the Temple, as also for other great Saints occurring in the Great Fast; and where there is no Triodion Ode (*Tripísn*) at Matins, we sing the Canon of the Theotokos with the Heirmos for 6, and to the Temple for 8. Katavasia of the Theotokos; and where we sing the Triodion Ode, we sing the Canon to the Temple for 6, and in the Triodion according to its order" (for 8), "and at the end the Heirmos in the Triodion."[^689]

## Directory of Services of the Entire Year

### Notes

**1.** When any Service is transferred from a weekday to a Sunday, then the Service of the Saint occurring on that Sunday is transferred to the weekday of the Service transferred to Sunday.

**2.** In the Menologion, we give the names only of the Saints having a Service; the others we omit, since we do not need to commemorate them, but only the former.

**3.** The celebrant does not omit Services written in cinnabar letters; and he does not combine Services for the Dead with Festal Services having a cinnabar sign in the margin, but takes only the Festal Service with the intention for the dead.[^700]

**4.** Of the Services of the entire year, some are Immovable, that is, of the Menaion, and others are Movable, that is, of both Triodia and the Octoechos. We provide first the Directory of Immovable Services, then of the Movable.[^701]

**5.** Since the Services of the Menaion are of various classes, on this account, that the reader may understand what class of Service he is to undertake, we attach in the margin beside each Service one of the various signs defining the class of the Service, as follows:

- **`⊕`** Sign of a Feast of the Lord with an Afterfeast.
- **`⊕`** Sign of a Feast of the Mother of God with an Afterfeast.
- **`⊕`** Sign of a Feast with a Vigil without an Afterfeast.
- **`+`** Sign of a Polyeleos Feast.
- **`(:·`** Sign of a Feast for 6 with Great Doxology.
- **`(:`** Sign of a Feast for 6 with Small Doxology.
- **`(·`** Sign of a Feast for 4 with Epistle and Gospel at Liturgy.
- **`(`** Sign of a Feast for 4 with Troparion.
- **`(`** Sign of a Feast for 4 without Troparion.[^702]

# Typik of Fr. Isidore Dolnytsky (1899) — Cohort 29

## Directory of Movable Services

### Of the Lenten Triodion

#### Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee
In the six days from Monday until the Saturday of the Prodigal Son, the Triodion has nothing, but only the Octoechos and the Menaion. — Fast-free week.

#### Sunday of the Prodigal Son
**1.** In the five days from Monday until Meatfare Friday, the Triodion has nothing, but only the Octoechos and the Menaion.  
**2.** Meatfare Saturday: For the Dead (All Souls), without the Menaion.

#### Sunday of Meatfare
##### Of the Dread Judgment
**1.** The Service of Cheesefare Week is Penitential, as a Forefeast of the Great Forty Days.  
**2.** On Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday, the Triodion has little, and the Octoechos and the Menaion hold the rest.  
**3.** On Wednesday and Friday, the Triodion has the fuller Fasting Service.  
**4.** **(:** Cheesefare Saturday: To the Holy Ascetics.

#### Sunday of Cheesefare
##### Adam’s Expulsion from Paradise
**1.** In the five days from Monday until Friday of the 1st Week of the Fast, the Service is Fasting. — With Monday begins the Great Forty Days.  
**2.** 1st Saturday of the Fast: Great Martyr Theodore the Recruit (Tyro).

#### 1st Sunday of the Fast
##### Orthodoxy: Against the Iconoclasts
**1.** In the five days from Monday until Friday of the 2nd Week of the Fast, the Service is Fasting.  
**2.** From Monday and downward, we sing at Compline the Canons of the Saints of the Menaion that will occur from Lazarus Saturday until Thomas Sunday, both inclusive.  
**3.** 2nd Saturday of the Fast: Service to the Martyrs, Hierarchs, and all Saints, likewise for the Deceased, according to the Typikon of Alleluia Saturdays, with the Service of the ordinary Saint of the Menaion. Thus it takes place also on the 3rd and 4th Saturdays of the Fast.

#### 2nd Sunday of the Fast
##### Nameless, Penitential
**1.** In the five days from Monday until Friday of the 3rd Week of the Fast, the Service is Fasting.  
**2.** 3rd Saturday of the Fast: Service of an Alleluia Saturday, as that of the 2nd.

#### 3rd Sunday of the Fast
##### Veneration of the Precious Cross
**1.** In the five days from Monday until Friday of the 4th Week of the Fast, the Service is of the Cross-Veneration.  
**2.** 4th Saturday of the Fast: Service as on the 2nd and 3rd of the Fast.

#### 4th Sunday of the Fast
##### Nameless, Penitential
At discretion, also the Service to Saint John of the Ladder (Climacus).  
**1.** On Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday of the 5th Week of the Fast, the Service is Fasting.

**2.** On Thursday of the 5th Week of the Fast: Penitential Service of the Great Canon, with prostrations.  
**3.** 5th Saturday of the Fast: Of the Akathist.

#### 5th Sunday of the Fast
##### Nameless, Penitential
At discretion, also the Service of Venerable Mary of Egypt.  
In the five days from Monday until Friday of the 6th Week of the Fast, the Service is Fasting.

### Of the Flowery Triodion (Pentecostarion)

#### 6th Saturday of the Fast: Of Lazarus
The Canons of the Saints that occur in the ordinary course of the Menaion from this Saturday until Thomas Sunday have been pre-sung at Compline from Monday of the 2nd Week of the Fast downward.

#### ⊕ 6th Sunday of the Fast: Palm Sunday (Flowery)
Feast of one day, with a Vigil.  
**1.** On Great Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday: Passion-Penitential Service, as a Forefeast of Christ's Sufferings.[^741]  
**2.** On Great Thursday: The Mystical Supper, and the beginning of Christ's Passion.  
**3.** On Great Friday: The Passion of Christ.  
**4.** On Great Saturday: The Laying of Christ in the Tomb.

### Explanation of the Tablet

**1.** The Tablet contains 35 Columns of the Sundays of the entire year according to the number of the 35 Boundary Keys, and the 35 Paschal days corresponding to them.

**2.** At the head of the Tablet, that is, in its 1st horizontal row, stand all 35 Boundary Keys; and beneath each key extends a row, or Column, of the Sundays of the entire year, with an indication of the day of the month within whose boundaries these Sundays occur, and their numbers or titles. And the months are written outside the column, on the left, separated from one another by horizontal, red, step-like lines; the day of the month is written inside the Column, while the number or title of the Sunday is written outside the Column, on the right of the Tablet.

**3.** In the 7th row stand all 35 days on which the Sunday of the Publican may occur, with which the Triodion begins.

**4.** In the 8th row stand all 35 Paschal days written in cinnabar (red), corresponding to the 35 Boundary Keys.

**5.** In the 5 rows contained between the Publican and the Boundary Keys, stand all 5 Sundays that can occur between Theophany and the Publican, namely, the Sunday after Enlightenment (Theophany), and the 4 last ordinary Sundays (29th, 30th, 31st, 32nd). However, since not all these Sundays always take place, as we explained on page 159 (for this depends on the earliness or lateness of Pascha), on this account in these 5 rows certain spaces are found blank, which signifies that then the Sundays corresponding to them on the right of the tablet have no place. For example, under the Boundary Key 1 (A), all spaces are blank; it signifies, therefore, that of

these 5 Sundays not a single one is then taken, but immediately after Theophany the Triodion begins.

**6.** Of the Triodion Sundays we have set down only three, that is, the central one, which is Pascha, and the two extreme ones, namely, that of the Publican, and that of All Saints; but the other intervening ones we have omitted, since it is easy to keep them in memory even without the Tablet, for they usually have special titles, and proper Services corresponding to the titles.

**7.** On the Sundays preceding that of the Publican we have set down neither the Tone nor the Sunday Gospel, for these depend on the preceding Pascha, and on this account one must seek them at the end of the Tablet in the boundaries of January and February under the Boundary Key of the preceding Pascha.

**8.** Beside certain Sundays, red Latin letters are placed. This signifies that then, together with the Gospel of that ordinary Sunday, another Gospel of a certain Sunday of the 10 special ones of the Menaion is also to occur, which we have set down at the end of the Tablet with corresponding Latin letters.[^743]

**9.** After this, if one desires to know which Sundays in a given year ought to be taken between Theophany and the Publican, let one seek first of all the Boundary Key of the coming Pascha, according to the Directory of Tablet I or II, and under it in Tablet III one will find those Sundays; only, if it is a leap year, adhere to the Column immediately following. And if one desires to know which Sunday, Tone, and Sunday Gospel is to be taken on any of the Sundays after the Descent of the Holy Spirit, then know—

## Rubric Concerning the Holy Doors and the Curtain of the Iconostasis

The Holy Doors, according to the rubric of the Greek *Euchologion*, are opened only: *at the beginning of Great Vespers, when the Priest alone incenses; at all the Entrances, namely of Vespers and of the Liturgy, and of the Holy Gospel. Likewise it is opened also from "With the fear [of God]..." until the completion of the Divine Liturgy.* [Greek: εἰς τὴν ἀρχὴν τοῦ μεγάλου Ἑσπερινοῦ, ὅτε θυμιᾷ μόνος ὁ Ἱερεὺς, εἰς τὰς εἰσόδους πάσας, ἤγουν τῶν Ἑσπερινῶν καὶ τῆς Λειτουργίας, καὶ τοῦ ἁγίου Εὐαγγελίου. Ὡσαύτως ἀνοίγεται καὶ ἀπὸ τοῦ Μετὰ φόβου... μέχρι τῆς συμπληρώσεως τῆς θείας Λειτουργίας.]

The Curtain, however, according to the current custom of the Athonite Monasteries, is opened at the Liturgy upon the completion of the Proskomide, and after the Great Entrance it is closed, and at *Especially* (*Izryadno*) it is opened, and after *And may the mercies* (*I da budut*) it is closed, and at *With fear* (*So strakhom*) it is opened, and remains open until the end of the Liturgy.[^744] According to the former custom of the Holy Mountain of Athos, when a single Priest celebrates, that is, without a Deacon, the curtain, having been closed after the Great Entrance, is not opened until the completion of the Communion. Furthermore, it remains open at Vespers and at Matins with the First Hour, and at the Moleben. But when the Hours are chanted without the Liturgy, it is opened for the Reading of the Epistle, and is closed after the Dismissal of the Typika.[^745]
