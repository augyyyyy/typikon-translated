*Preface: Epistle of Bishop Hedeon Balaban.*

Unto the Orthodox followers of the sacred rites of the holy Eastern Catholic and Apostolic Church,[^7] and fellow ministers of our Humility, brethren in Christ: Hedeon Balaban, humble Bishop of Lviv, Halych, and Kamianets of Podolia, Exarch of the Throne of Constantinople: Grace from on High.

Truly, according to Gregory the Theologian,[^8] what food is to the body, that the word is to the soul.

*Instruction unto Priests, by our Father among the Saints, Basil the Great, Archbishop of Caesarea in Cappadocia:*[^15]

Take heed unto thyself, O priest, and unto those whom thou desirest to instruct; and watch over the ministry which thou hast received, that thou mayest fulfill it. For thou hast not been entrusted with an earthly ministry, but a heavenly one; not a human one, but an angelic one. Strive to present thyself unto the Lord as a workman who has no need to be ashamed, rightly dividing His word of truth.[^16]

*Instruction unto Priests.*

Neither stand thou ever in the assembly having enmity against anyone, lest thou drive away the Comforter.[^17] On the day of the assembly, be not quarrelsome, nor contradict at all; but pray in private and read until the hour of the service; and so with fear stand before the holy altar. Look not around, nor shorten the prayers in haste. Have no respect of persons, but behold the King Who lies before thee, lest hypocritically thou give the Holy Body where it is not fitting. *Concerning the Holy Lamb Immolated on Great and Holy Thursday:*[^22]

It is fitting for the priest to do thus, and to preserve the Holy Body of Christ: On Great and Holy Thursday, in the evening at the Liturgy, he prepares two Lambs, and celebrates the Liturgy with them;

**[Right Page — Fol. 8r]**

*Instruction unto Priests.*

And after the exclamation: "Let us attend: The Holy Things for the holy," he celebrates with one of them at that time; but the other he moistens with the Blood of Christ upon the holy chalice, and he preserves it well with great fear and trembling.[^23] And let the prosphora not be unsuitable, or unbaked, or unsalted. And watch, O priest, with fear, and dry it so that it become not moldy, nor crumble. And on Tuesday of Bright Week, in the morning, having vested in all the priestly vestments, having washed his hands, and taken the censer, having censed the Holy Lamb upon the holy table, and having unfolded the antimension, and placed a small stone or plinth upon it, then he places a clay vessel with fire upon the stone;
