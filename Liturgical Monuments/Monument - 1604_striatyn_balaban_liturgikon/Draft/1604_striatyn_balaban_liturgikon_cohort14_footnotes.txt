[^423]: In the printed codex, Fol. 128r is numbered page 241 (`см҃а`), but Fol. 128v is numbered page 244 (`см҃д`), skipping page numbers 242 and 243 in the printed foliation. Signature collation confirms that no physical leaf was omitted from the gathering (`ѳ г` on Fol. 128r, followed immediately by `ѳ д` on Fol. 129r). However, the printed text on Fol. 128v opens abruptly with the final clause of the Prayer of the Second Antiphon (*"бж҃твенною твое́ю си́лою..."* / *"...by Thy divine power, and forsake us not who hope in Thee"*), omitting the diaconal petitions of the second antiphon and the opening portion of the prayer (*"Господи Боже наш, спаси люди Твоя..."* / *"O Lord our God, save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance..."*), which the celebrant was expected to supply from the earlier text of the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom.
[^424]: The Prayer of the Third Antiphon (*"Иже общыя сия и согласныя даровавый нам молитвы..."* / *"Ὁ τὰς κοινὰς ταύτας καὶ συμφώνους ἡμῖν χαρισάμενος προσευχάς..."*), identical to that in the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom and common to both Byzantine liturgies.
[^425]: Cross-reference to the rubrics for the Little Entrance and the singing of the Apolytikia and Kontakia in the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom earlier in the codex (fols. 57r–61v).
[^426]: *Мл҃тва тре́тїаго пѣ́нїа* (Prayer of the Thrice-Holy Singing / Trisagion): In Byzantine and Early Modern Slavic euchological usage, *тре́тїе пѣ́нїе* refers to the singing of the Trisagion hymn (*"Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us"*).
[^427]: The Trisagion Prayer of Saint Basil the Great (*"Боже святый, иже во святых почиваяй..."* / *"Ὁ Θεὸς ὁ ἅγιος, ὁ ἐν ἁγίοις ἀναπαυόμενος..."*), preserving the classical ancient Cappadocian euchological text.
[^428]: Physical Lacuna in the Exemplar: A physical folio (Fol. 132, bearing printed pages 250 [`сн҃`] and 251 [`сна҃`]) is missing between physical leaf p133 (p. 249) and leaf p134 (p. 252). The missing leaf contained: (1) the conclusion of the Trisagion Prayer (*"Прости́ на́мъ всѧ́кое согрѣше́нїе... въ прпⷣо́бїи и҆ пра́вдѣ слꙋжи́ти тебѣ̀..."*); (2) the Trisagion exclamation and the diaconal elevation/synthronon dialogue; (3) the Prokimenon, Epistle reading (*Apostolos*), Alleluia, and diaconal censing; and (4) the opening petitions of the Prayer Before the Gospel (*"Возсїѧ́й въ сердца́хъ на́шихъ, чл҃колю́бче влⷣко, твоегѡ̀ бг҃оразꙋ́мїѧ нетлѣ́нный свѣ́тъ..."* / *"Illumine our hearts, O Master Who lovest mankind, with the pure light of Thy divine knowledge..."*). The text on p. 252 resumes mid-clause with *"разꙋмѣ́ти є҆ѵ́лскаѧ твоѧ̑ проповѣ́данїа..."* (*"...that we may understand Thy Gospel preachings..."*). This missing folio is corroborated by the 19th-century librarian's corrected pencil foliation (`133` correcting `134`).
[^429]: The rubric *"И҆ про́чее всѐ порѧ́дꙋ"* directs the celebrants to perform the reading of the Holy Gospel according to the standard liturgical order set forth in the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom.
[^430]: *ѡ҆ вели́комъ кра́ли на́шемъ* ("for our Great King [Name]"): Rubrical petition for the reigning sovereign of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth (King Sigismund III Vasa, reigned 1587–1632).
[^431]: Rubrical distinction between monastic and parish settings for the Augmentative Litany (*Є҆щѐ мо́лимсѧ...*). Note the gathering signature `п` (quaternion 16 in the standard Striatyn alphabetical sequence) on Fol. 134r.
[^432]: *Мл҃тва ѡ҆ ѡ҆глаше́нныхъ преⷣ сты́мъ възноше́нїемъ* ("The Prayer for the Catechumens before the Holy Elevation"): The Slavonic *възноше́нїе* directly translates the Greek *ἀναφορά* (Anaphora / Elevation / Oblation).
[^433]: The Prayer for the Catechumens in the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great (*"Господи Боже наш, иже на высоких живый..."* / *"Κύριε ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν, ὁ ἐν ὑψηλοῖς κατοικῶν..."*), distinguished from the Chrysostom prayer by its invocation of the "light yoke" (cf. Matt. 11:30) and the catechumens becoming "members of Thy holy Church."
[^434]: *и҆лито́нъ* (eileton): The liturgical cloth corporal antimension-wrapper unfolded upon the holy table at the dismissal of the catechumens.
[^435]: Rubric governing the Litany and Prayer for Those Preparing for Illumination (*Оглашенніи, иже къ просвѣщенію...* / *τῶν πρὸς τὸ φώτισμα εὐτрепиζομένων*), prescribed from Wednesday of the Fourth Week of the Great Fast (Mid-Lent / *Средокрестная*) through Great and Holy Wednesday. In classical Byzantine euchologia, this office appears in both the Presanctified Liturgy and the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great when celebrated on Great Thursday and Great Saturday.
[^436]: The Prayer for Those Preparing for Illumination in Saint Basil's Liturgy (*"Яви, Владыко, лице Твое..."* / *"Ἐπίφανον, Δέσпота, τὸ πρόσωπόν σου..."*), emphasizing baptismal regeneration as members of the body of Christ.
[^437]: *Здѐ пое́ли пре́же средопо́стї* ("Here sing as before Mid-Lent"): Rubric indicating that outside the liturgical period from Mid-Lent to Holy Wednesday, the celebrants omit the petitions for the illuminated and use the customary catechumen dismissal formulas.
[^438]: *по вънегда̀ разви́тоу бы́вшоу и҆лито́нꙋ* ("after the eileton has been unfolded"): The rubric confirms that the antimension/eileton remains unfolded upon the altar for the Prayers of the Faithful and the subsequent Great Entrance.
[^439]: The First Prayer of the Faithful in the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great (*"Ты, Господи, показал еси нам великую сию спасения тайну..."* / *"Σύ, Κύριε, κατέδειξας ἡμῖν τὸ μέγα τοῦτο τῆς σωτηρίας μυστήριον..."*). Note the Cappadocian emphasis on priestly unworthiness and the invocation of the Holy Spirit to make the celebrants fit (*"ты̀ ᲂу҆довлѝ на́съ"* / *"ἱκάνωσον ἡμᾶς"*) to offer the sacrifice of praise.
[^440]: *"ѡ҆ на́шихъ съгрѣше́нїихъ, и҆ ѡ҆ людскыхъ невѣ́жествїихъ"* ("for our own sins and for the ignorances of the people"): Exact biblical phrasing from Hebrews 9:7 (*ὑπὲρ ἑαυτοῦ καὶ τῶν τοῦ λαοῦ ἀγνοημάτων*).
[^441]: *а҆́щеже са́мъ і҆ере́и* ("but if the priest is alone"): Important rubrical witness for solitary celebration (priest celebrating without a deacon), directing the priest to recite the diaconal petition and exclamation himself.
[^442]: The Second Prayer of the Faithful in the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great (*"Боже, посетивый в милости и щедротах смирение наше..."* / *"Ὁ Θεός, ὁ ἐπισκεψάμενος ἐν ἐλέει καὶ οἰкτιρμοῖς τὴν ταпείνωσιν ἡμῶν..."*). The prayer concludes on the subsequent leaf (Cohort 15, fol. 139v / p141).
