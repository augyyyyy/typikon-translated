[^940]: Slavonic: `В ли́стѣ р҃а` (At leaf 101, or folio/page 101/104). Refers to the petitional suffrage in the Ektenia where the typesetter omitted the standard liturgical phrase `и стра́хомъ бж҃їимъ`.
[^941]: Slavonic: `бл҃гоговѣ́нїемъ въходѧ́щихъ кънъ` ("of those entering therein with reverence", where `кънъ` represents `къ нь` / `въ нь`, "into it").
[^942]: Slavonic: `ты чьтѝ` (read thou / imperative singular directed to the officiating priest or reader).
[^943]: Slavonic: `бл҃гоговѣ́нїемъ и стра́хомъ бж҃їимъ въходѧ́щихъ кънъ` ("of those who with reverence and the fear of God enter therein"). Restoring the canonical tripartite formula `съ вѣ́рою, бл҃гоговѣ́нїемъ и҆ стра́хомъ бж҃їимъ въходѧ́щихъ` of the Great Litany and Litany of Fervent Supplication.
[^944]: Slavonic: `к ли́стѣ с҃к҃д` (at leaf 224). Refers to the special petitional suffrage for rain during drought (`А҆́щеже бꙋ́детъ бездо́жїе`).
[^945]: Slavonic: `И҆ е́же ѡ҆дъжити дъжъ бл҃гъ` ("And that [He] may send down rain, good...").
[^946]: Slavonic: `тыже чьтѝ` (read thou likewise).
[^947]: Slavonic: `И҆ о́еже ѡ҆дъждити земли дъжъ бл҃гъ` ("And that He may send down rain upon the earth, good..."). The corrector restores the missing direct object `землѝ` ("upon the earth").
[^948]: Slavonic: `Прѡ́чаꙗ оу҆до́бнѣ разсмотрели́въ чита́тель испра́витъ` (Traditional colophonic and errata formula: "The rest, the attentive/discerning reader will easily correct", invoking the charitable indulgence and editorial vigilance of the clergy and reader).
