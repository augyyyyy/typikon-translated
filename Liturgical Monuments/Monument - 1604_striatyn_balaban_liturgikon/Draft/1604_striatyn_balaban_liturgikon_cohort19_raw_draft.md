# PART III: THE DIVINE LITURGY OF THE PRESANCTIFIED GIFTS[^522]

## On the Slaughter of the Holy Lamb with Which the Holy Liturgy of the Presanctified Is Celebrated

*It is fitting to know that the Holy Liturgy of the Presanctified is sung only during the Holy Great Forty Days (Great Fast). Therefore, if the priest wishes to celebrate it on Wednesday, or on Friday, or on another*

=== LEAF p182 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 182v (p. 352b)]**

*On the Slaughter of the Holy Lamb*

*any day: first, on Cheese-fare Sunday, when the priest prepares himself for the Divine Liturgy according to custom and performs the slaughter of the Holy Lamb, he immediately also slaughters the other holy Lambs with which they are to celebrate the Holy Liturgy of the Presanctified; performing and saying over all of them according to the pattern, exactly as over the first, as is written in the holy Prothesis.*[^523]

**[Right Page — Fol. 183r (p. 353)]**

*The Holy Liturgy of the Presanctified*

*And they are placed on the holy diskos, and covered. And thus they are carried across at the Great Entrance, and are consecrated. When the deacon exclaims:*
**Deacon:** "Let us attend."
*The priest, taking up the holy Lambs, says:*
**Priest:** "Holy Things unto the holy."
*And thus taking the holy labis—that is, the spoon—in his right hand, and the holy Lamb in his left hand, and raising it over the holy chalice, and turning the holy slaughter upward,*[^524]

**[Left Page — Fol. 183v (p. 354)]**

*The Holy Liturgy of the Presanctified*
