[^36]: The Entrance Prayer (*Господи, ниспосли руку Твою*), recited by the celebrant before entering the holy sanctuary, imploring divine strengthening for the celebration of the unbloody sacrifice.

[^39]: The blessing of the deacon's vestments (the sticharion and orarion) by the celebrant prior to vesting. Active present indicative is strictly maintained for ceremonial actions (*comes*, *holds*, *bows*, *says*, *blesses*, *departs*).
[^40]: Both celebrant and deacon vest in the sticharion while reciting the Scriptural vesting verses.

[^53]: Psalm 25:9–12 (LXX) [Masoretic Ps 26:9–12], completing the lavabo psalm.
[^54]: *Блюдо анафорное* (*anaphora dish*): the wide commemorative dish or tray used in the Ruthenian tradition to hold the prosphora loaves and anaphora particles during the Proskomedia.
[^55]: The classical Troparion of Great and Holy Friday (*Искупилъ ны еси отъ клятвы законныя*), establishing the sacrificial and redemptive typology of the Proskomedia.
[^56]: *Въ ображеніа просфорнаго*: literally, over the representation/impression/seal stamped on the upper crust of the prosphora (bearing the cross and the IC XC NIKA inscription).
[^57]: The inaugural dedication of the First Prosphora (the Lamb), in remembrance of the Lord's redemptive incarnation and passion (Luke 22:19; 1 Corinthians 11:24).
[^58]: Isaiah 53:7–8 (LXX), the Suffering Servant prophecy recited during the cutting of the four sides of the seal.
[^59]: *Святый хлѣбъ* (the Holy Bread / the Lamb), excised from the center of the first prosphora.
[^60]: Isaiah 53:8 (LXX), completing the extraction of the Lamb from the loaf.
