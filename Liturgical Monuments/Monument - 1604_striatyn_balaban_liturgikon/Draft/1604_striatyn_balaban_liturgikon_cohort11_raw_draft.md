**Deacon:** "Break, Master, the Holy Bread."[^310]

**Priest:** "Broken and divided is the Lamb of God, the Son of the Father, broken and not divided, Who is ever eaten and never consumed, but sanctifies those who partake thereof."[^311]
*Concerning the breaking of the Holy Lamb:*
*The priest ought to know (Behold):*[^312]

*that when breaking the Holy Lamb, the parts are placed with the sign of the cross downward upon the holy diskos, and the cut side upward, even as formerly when it was sacrificed.[^313] Place IC, indeed, on the upper side of the holy diskos; and XC below; and on the side of the holy chalice, NI; and KA on the left side.[^314] And having taken the IC portion, fill the holy cup.[^315] And break the XC portion*
