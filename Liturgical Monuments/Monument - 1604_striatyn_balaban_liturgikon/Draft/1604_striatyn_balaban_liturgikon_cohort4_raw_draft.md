*A Brief Explanation Concerning the Holy Proskomedia:*

*Which is the right side of the Holy Bread, whereon they are accustomed to place the particle of the most holy Theotokos, and the other holy nine particles; and why nine are placed, and not more, nor fewer.*[^134][^133]

=== LEAF p40 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 30v (p. 79)]**

*A Brief Explanation.*

**The Author:** "Truly the mystery of the fearful and holy Sacrifice is ineffable and incomprehensible even unto the supreme celestial Powers themselves. For that which they—the incorruptible and most pure Lord's Body, sitting upon the heavenly and most fitting thrones unto Him—serve with fear and trembling, veiled with their wings: this very same [Sacrifice] is visibly accomplished each day upon earth by us earthly ones.[^135]

**[Right Page — Fol. 31r (p. 80)]**

*Concerning the Proskomedia.*

Yet since He Who performs and is performed,[^136] Jesus—(not for the sake of [any alteration],[^137] but possessing the love toward mankind which ever abides)—was well-pleased that it be so, and having partaken of our whole compound nature, without sin:[^138] this figure of His ineffable divine condescension, having become perfect man, and having suffered in the flesh and not in the Godhead, and as a sheep..."[^139]
