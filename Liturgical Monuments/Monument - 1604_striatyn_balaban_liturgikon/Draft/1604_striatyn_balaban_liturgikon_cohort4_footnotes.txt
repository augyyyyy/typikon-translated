[^133]: A prominent theological and polemical treatise appended to the Proskomedia rubrics in the 1604 Striatyn Liturgikon: *И҆столкова́нїе въкра́тцѣ ѡ҆ ст҃ѣ́й просфоми́дїи...* ("A Brief Explanation Concerning the Holy Proskomedia"). The text articulates the historical Ruthenian defense of the arrangement of the Holy Lamb and particles against contemporary Western and Latinizing deviations.
[^134]: The treatise addresses two central liturgical questions: (1) which side of the Lamb is designated the liturgical "right hand" (*де́сная страна̀*), where the particle of the Theotokos is placed in accordance with Psalm 44:10; and (2) why exactly nine particles (*де́вять часте́й*) are extracted for the angelic and saintly orders, corresponding to the nine celestial choirs of Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite.
