*Concerning the Proskomedia of the Deacons: Why It Is Forbidden.*[^101]
*It is fitting to know that it is forbidden for deacons to perform the Proskomedia, by the holy books and the First Council of the holy and God-bearing Fathers, even as is said in Canon 18:*[^102]
*"In no wise is it for deacons to offer the oblation" — that is, not to perform the Proskomedia with prosphoras,*

=== LEAF p30 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 20v (p. 59)]**

*Proskomedia.*

*"...nor to give unto the presbyters the Divine Communion, nor to touch [the Gifts] before them, but after the presbyters to receive such Thanksgiving themselves, either from the bishop or from a presbyter; neither to sit among the presbyters, lest sitting above them they think themselves to be more honorable, which is disorderly. If anyone will not desist from this, by this canon let him be deposed."*[^103]
