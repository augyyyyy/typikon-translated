# PART III: SUNDAY OF THE HOLY MYRRH-BEARING WOMEN AND THIRD WEEK AFTER PASCHA

## SUNDAY OF THE MYRRH-BEARING WOMEN

*The Third Sunday after Pascha: of the Holy Myrrh-bearing Women, and of Righteous Joseph.*[^501]

*On Saturday evening, at Small Vespers, Resurrectional stichera, 3. Tone 2:*

*Unto Him Who before the ages was born of the Father... (see fol. 283)*[^502]  
*Christ our Savior, having nailed to the Cross the handwriting against us... (see fol. 283)*  
*With the Archangels let us hymn Christ's Resurrection... (see fol. 283)*  

*And 1 Anatolian [Eastern] sticheron, the same Tone:*

*Thee Who wast crucified and buried... (see fol. 283)*  

*Glory...*

*Why do ye mingle myrrh with tears, O disciples?... (see fol. 284)*  
*Seek all these stichera at Great Vespers.*

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Dogmatikon, Tone 2:*

O the exceedingly great mystery! Beholding the marvels, I proclaim the Godhead: for Emmanuel opened indeed the gates of nature as Lover of mankind, yet broke not the locks of virginity as God; but in such wise came He forth from the womb as He entered through the hearing: thus was He incarnate, even as He was conceived: impassibly He entered, ineffably He came forth, according to the Prophet who says: "This gate shall be shut, no man shall pass through it, save only the Lord God of Israel, having great mercy." (Ezek. 44:2)[^503]

*O Gladsome Light.*  
*Prokeimenon: The Lord is King: (Ps. 92:1a [93:1a])*  
*At the Aposticha, Resurrectional sticheron, Tone 2:*

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, hath enlightened the whole inhabited earth, and Thou hast called back Thine own creation: almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Е҆гда̀ ѿ дре́ва):*
*Verse: I will remember thy name in every generation and generation. (Ps. 44:18a [45:17a])*

Joy of all who sorrow, and protectress of the wronged, and feeder of the poor, consolation of travelers, and staff of the blind, visitation of the sick, shelter and defender of them that labor, and helper of orphans, Mother of the most high God art thou, O Most Pure One: make haste, we pray thee, that thy servants may be saved!

*Verse: Give ear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear. (Ps. 44:11a [45:10a])*

All manner of iniquity disorderly, every sin incontinently have I, wretched one, committed, and I am worthy of all condemnation: grant me grounds for repentance, O Virgin, that I appear not there condemned; for thee do I record as my intercessor, thee do I summon as defender, that thou put me not to shame, O Bride of God!

*Verse: The rich among the people shall entreat thy face. (Ps. 44:13b [45:12b])*

Other refuge unto the Creator and Master have we not, O Pure One, save thee, O Bride of God: cast us not away from thy fervent intercession, neither put to shame those who with love run beneath thy shelter, O Mother of our God: make haste, and grant thy help, and deliver us from the present wrath!

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 2:*

*The myrrh-bearing women being early... (see fol. 284)*[^504]  
*Seek it at Great Vespers.*  
*After the Trisagion, Troparion: "The noble Joseph..." Litany, and the Dismissal.*

*At Great Vespers:*

*"Christ is risen...", as customary. Then the Introductory Psalm: "Bless the Lord, O my soul..." (Ps. 103 [104]). And the 1st Kathisma of the Psalter.*  
*At "Lord, I have cried", we set 10 verses, and chant 6 Resurrectional stichera.*  
*Tone 2:*  
*Verse: Bring my soul out of prison, that I may confess Thy name. (Ps. 141:8a [142:7a])*

Come, let us worship God the Word, begotten of the Father before the ages, Who was incarnate of the Virgin Mary; for having endured the Cross, He delivered Himself to burial, as He Himself willed; and having risen from the dead, He saved me, an erring man!

*Verse: The righteous shall wait for me, until Thou shalt recompense me. (Ps. 141:8c [142:7b])*

Christ our Savior, having nailed to the Cross the handwriting that was against us, blotted it out, and abolished the dominion of death: we worship His three-day Resurrection!

*Verse: Out of the depths have I cried unto Thee, O Lord; Lord, hear my voice. (Ps. 129:1-2a [130:1-2a])*

With the Archangels let us hymn Christ's Resurrection: for He is the Redeemer and Savior of our souls, and in fearful glory and mighty power He comes again to judge the world which He created!

*And 4 Anatolian [Eastern] stichera. The same Tone:*  
*Verse: Let Thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplication. (Ps. 129:2b [130:2b])*

The Angel proclaimed Thee, the crucified and buried Master, and said unto the women: Come, see the place where the Lord lay; for He is risen, as He said, as almighty; therefore we worship Thee, the only Immortal One: O Giver of life, Christ, have mercy upon us!

*Verse: If Thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? For with Thee there is forgiveness. (Ps. 129:3-4a [130:3-4a])*

By Thy Cross Thou didst abolish the curse of the tree; by Thy burial Thou didst slay the dominion of death; and by Thy Rising Thou didst enlighten mankind; therefore we cry unto Thee: O Benefactor, Christ our God, glory to Thee!

*Verse: For Thy name's sake have I waited for Thee, O Lord; my soul hath waited for Thy word, my soul hath hoped in the Lord. (Ps. 129:5-6a [130:5-6a])* Unto Thee, O Lord, the gates of death were opened in fear, and the gatekeepers of hades seeing Thee were terrified: for Thou didst shatter the brazen gates, and crush the iron bars, and lead us out from darkness and the shadow of death, and tear our bonds asunder!

*Verse: From the morning watch until night, from the morning watch let Israel hope in the Lord. (Ps. 129:6b [130:6b])*

Chanting a saving hymn, let us send it up from our mouths: Come all, let us fall down in the house of the Lord, saying: O Thou Who wast crucified upon the Tree, and didst arise from the dead, and art in the bosom of the Father: cleanse our sins!

*Then 3 Idiomelon Stichera of the Myrrh-bearing Women.*  
*Tone 2. By Anatolius:*[^505]  
*Verse: For with the Lord there is mercy, and with Him is plenteous redemption; and He shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities. (Ps. 129:7-8 [130:7-8])*

The myrrh-bearing women, deep in the morning, having taken sweet spices, reached the tomb of the Lord; not expecting to find what they found, they pondered in awe at the rolling away of the stone, and said one to another: Where are the seals of the tomb? Where is Pilate's custody, and the secure guard? And the radiant Angel became a herald unto the unknowing women, and said unto them: Why seek ye with weeping the Living One, Who hath made alive mankind? Christ our God is risen from the dead as almighty, granting unto us all incorruption and life, illumination and great mercy!

*Verse: Praise the Lord, all ye nations; praise Him, all ye people. (Ps. 116:1 [117:1])*

Why do ye mingle myrrh with tears, O women disciples? The stone is rolled away; the tomb is emptied: behold corruption trampled down by life, the seals clearly bearing witness, the fierce watchmen of the disobedient asleep; mortal nature is saved by the Flesh of God; hades weeps: run with joy and say unto the Apostles: Christ, having slain death, the Firstborn from the dead, goes before you into Galilee!

*Verse: For His mercy is confirmed upon us, and the truth of the Lord endures for ever. (Ps. 116:2 [117:2])*

The myrrh-bearing women being early, and having reached Thy tomb with haste, sought Thee, O Christ, that they might anoint Thy most pure Body; and instructed by the words of the Angel, they proclaimed joyful tokens unto the Apostles: that the Author of our salvation is risen, having made death captive, and granting unto the world eternal life and great mercy!

*Glory... Tone 6. By Cosmas the Monk:*[^506]

The myrrh-bearing women reached Thy tomb, and having seen the sepulchral seals, yet not finding Thy most pure Body, weeping, they came with haste, saying: Who hath stolen away our hope? Who hath taken away the naked corpse, anointed with myrrh, the only consolation of His Mother? O, how hath He Who made the dead alive been put to death? How hath He Who made hades captive been buried? But arise, O Savior, by Thine own sovereign authority, as Thou didst say, on the third day, saving our souls!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
*Tone 2:*

The shadow of the Law hath passed away, grace having come: for as the bush burned not being engulfed in flames, so a Virgin didst thou give birth, and a Virgin didst thou remain. Instead of a pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness hath shone forth: instead of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls!

*Entrance.*  
*O Gladsome Light.*  
*Prokeimenon: The Lord is King: (Ps. 92:1a [93:1a])*  
*At the Litiya, idiomelon stichera, Tone 1:*

O myrrh-bearing women, why came ye unto the tomb? Why seek ye the Living among the dead? The Lord is risen: take courage! cries the Angel.

With fear the women came unto the tomb, hastening to anoint Thy Body with sweet spices; and not finding it, they were perplexed one with another, not knowing of the Resurrection; but an Angel stood before them and said: Christ is risen, granting unto us great mercy!

Magdalene and the other Mary came unto the tomb seeking the Lord, and they saw an Angel sitting upon the stone as lightning, and saying unto them: Why seek ye the Living with the dead? He is risen as He said; ye shall find Him in Galilee; unto Whom let us cry aloud: O Thou Who didst arise from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Tone 6:*

Joseph begged for the Body of Jesus, and laid It in his own new tomb; for it was fitting for Him to go forth from the tomb as from a bridal chamber: O Thou Who didst crush the dominion of death, and didst open the gates of paradise unto men, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion, the same Tone:*

My Maker and Redeemer, O Most Pure One, Christ the Lord, having come forth from thy womb, clothed in me, hath set free Adam from the first curse; therefore unto thee, O All-pure One, as Mother of God and Virgin in truth, we cry aloud unceasingly: Rejoice, as the Angel hailed thee! Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady: protection, shelter, and salvation of our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Resurrectional sticheron, Tone 2:*

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, hath enlightened the whole inhabited earth, and Thou hast called back Thine own creation: almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Then the Stichera of Pascha with their verses. (see fol. 15)*[^507]

*Glory... Tone 5:*

Thee Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment, when Joseph took down from the Tree with Nicodemus, and beheld dead, naked, and unburied, he took up a heartfelt lamentation, and weeping said: Woe is me, O sweetest Jesus! Whom a little while ago the sun, beholding suspended upon the Cross, veiled itself in darkness, and the earth quaked with fear, and the veil of the temple was rent in twain; but lo, now I behold Thee, Who for my sake didst willingly submit to death! How shall I bury Thee, O my God? Or how shall I wrap Thee in a winding sheet [plashchanytsia]?[^508] With what hands shall I touch Thine incorruptible Body? Or what songs shall I sing unto Thy departure, O Compassionate One? I magnify Thy Passions, I hymn also Thy Burial together with the Resurrection, crying: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. "The Day of Resurrection..." and "Christ is risen...", once. Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." At the blessing of the loaves, Troparion: "Rejoice, O Virgin Mother of God...", thrice.*

*And the customary reading from the Acts of the Apostles.*

*If it happen that there is no Vigil: at "Lord, I have cried", 6 Resurrectional stichera are chanted; and 6 of the Myrrh-bearing Women with the Litiya stichera. Entrance. Prokeimenon. At the Aposticha, the Stichera of Pascha. Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 5: "Thee Who clothest Thyself with light..." At Midnight Office, the Triadic Canon is chanted according to custom.*

*Be it known: That from the Sunday of Saint Thomas, there is no breaking of bread after the blessing of the loaves, because of the shortness of the night.*[^509]

## ON THE THIRD SUNDAY AFTER PASCHA, OF THE HOLY MYRRH-BEARING WOMEN, AT MATINS

*At "God is the Lord", Troparion, Tone 2:*

When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal, then didst Thou slay hades with the brilliance of the Godhead; and when Thou didst also raise the dead from the nethermost parts, all the Heavenly Hosts cried aloud: O Giver of life, Christ our God, glory to Thee! *(Twice)*

*Glory...*

The noble Joseph, taking down Thy most pure Body from the Tree, wrapped It in a clean shroud [plashchanytsia] and with sweet spices covered It and laid It in a new tomb; but on the third day Thou didst arise, O Lord, granting unto the world great mercy.

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.*

Standing by the tomb, the Angel cried unto the myrrh-bearing women: Myrrh is fitting for the dead, but Christ hath shown Himself a stranger to corruption; rather cry aloud: The Lord is risen, granting unto the world great mercy!

*After the 1st stichology, Sessional Hymns, Tone 2:*

Not forbidding the sepulchral stone to be sealed, Thou didst grant unto all the rock of faith, having arisen: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up. (Ps. 9:33 [10:12])*

Not emptying the most pure bosom in the highest, Thou didst accept Burial and Resurrection on behalf of all: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

All thy mysteries are beyond understanding, all most glorious, O Mother of God: with purity sealed and virginity preserved, thou wast known to be a true Mother, having given birth unto the true God: entreat Him that our souls may be saved!

*After the 2nd stichology, Sessional Hymns, Tone 2:*

The myrrh-bearing women coming early in the morning and seeing the tomb empty said unto the Apostles: The Mighty One hath overthrown corruption, and snatched those in hades from bonds; proclaim with boldness that Christ God is risen, granting unto the world great mercy!

*Verse: I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart. (Ps. 9:2a [9:1a])*

Bearing the myrrh of Thy burial, the women secretly came early unto the tomb, fearing Jewish boldness and foreseeing the guard of the soldiers; yet frail nature overcame courage, for their prayerful intent was pleasing unto God; therefore fittingly they cried: Arise, O Lord, help us, and deliver us for the sake of the glory of Thy name!

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Most glorified art thou, O Virgin Mother of God, we hymn thee: for by the Cross of thy Son hades was overthrown and death was slain; we who were put to death have arisen and have been deemed worthy of life; we have received paradise, the ancient delight: therefore giving thanks we glorify Christ our God, as the Mighty One and the only Most-merciful!

*And the reading in the Commentary Gospel according to its order.*

*After the Blameless [Amomos], the Troparia: "The angelic assembly..." And immediately:*

*Hypakoe, Tone 2:*

After the Passion going unto the tomb that they might anoint Thy Body, O Christ God, the women saw Angels in the tomb and were terrified, for they heard a voice from them: that the Lord is risen, granting unto the world great mercy!

*And the reading. And immediately the Songs of Degrees [Anabathmoi], Tone 2, twice each.*

*Antiphon 1:*

Unto heaven do I direct the eyes of my heart, unto Thee, O Savior: save me by Thine illumination!

Have mercy upon us who sin against Thee much at every hour, O my Christ, and grant us a manner of repenting unto Thee before the end!

*Glory...*

Unto the Holy Spirit it belongs to reign, to sanctify, and to move creation: for He is God, consubstantial with the Father and the Word.

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*Antiphon 2:*

If the Lord had not been among us, who could have been kept safe from the enemy, who is at once an adversary and a manslayer?

Deliver not Thy servant unto their teeth, O Savior: for like lions my enemies move against me.

*Glory...*

Unto the Holy Spirit belong origin of life and honor: for as God He upholds all created things by His power, in the Father and through the Son.

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*Antiphon 3:*

They that trust in the Lord are like unto the holy mountain, which can in no wise be moved by the assaults of the enemy.

Let not them that live divinely stretch forth their hands unto iniquity: for Christ doth not leave His rod upon His heritage.

*Glory...*

Through the Holy Spirit all wisdom wells forth: hence grace unto the Apostles, and the martyrs are crowned in suffering, and the prophets foresee.

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*Prokeimenon, Tone 2:*

Arise, O Lord my God, in the precept which Thou hast commanded, and the congregation of the peoples shall surround Thee. (Ps. 7:7b-8a [7:6b-7a])

*Verse: O Lord my God, in Thee have I hoped, save me. (Ps. 7:2a [7:1a])*

*Let every breath... Matins Resurrection Gospel 3, from Mark, pericope 71 (Mark 16:9-20): "Now when Jesus was risen early the first day of the week..."*[^510]

*Then: "Having seen the Resurrection of Christ, let us worship...", thrice. Psalm 50, and the rest.*

*Canon of Pascha with the heirmos and with its Theotokia, to 6; then of the present Sunday, the Canon of the Triodion, to 8, Tone 2: The composition of the Lord Andrew of Crete.*[^511] *Katavasia: "The Day of Resurrection..."*

*Canon of Pascha.*

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

*The Day of Resurrection... Let us purify our senses... Let the heavens indeed worthily rejoice... (see fol. 5)*[^512]

*Another Canon, Theotokion, similar; whose acrostic:*

*In the first troparia: THEOPHANES, the same Tone:*[^513]

*Troparion:*
Thou hast broken the bounds of mortality, having given birth unto Christ, the eternal Life, Who hath shone forth from the tomb today, O all-blameless Virgin, and hath enlightened the world!

*Troparion:*
Beholding Thy Son and God risen, rejoice with the Apostles, O Pure One full of the grace of God; and thou wast the first to hear "Rejoice!", having received it as the cause of joy unto all, O all-blameless Mother of God!

*Another Canon of the Myrrh-bearing Women, to which we set 8 verses.*

*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

Taking up the song of Moses, cry aloud, O my soul: A helper and a protector hath He become unto me for salvation; He is my God and I will glorify Him! (Exod. 15:2)

*Troparion:*
Thou wast crucified in the flesh, Thou Who art impassible in the Father's essence; Thou wast pierced in the ribs, welling forth blood and water unto the world: Thou art our God, and we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:*
I venerate Thy Cross, and I glorify Thy Burial, O Good One; and I sing and worship Thy Resurrection, and cry aloud: Thou art our God, and we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:*
Though Thou didst taste of gall, O Sweetness of the Church, yet Thou didst pour forth incorruption unto us from Thy ribs: Thou art our God, and we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:*
Thou wast numbered with the dead, O Savior, Who didst raise the dead; Thou didst taste of mortality, yet in no wise didst Thou know corruption: Thou art our God, and we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:*
Let Zion make merry, and let heaven rejoice; Christ is risen, Who raised the dead, who hymn: Thou art our God, and we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:*
Wrapping Thy Body in winding sheets [plashchanytsi], Joseph laid Thee in a new tomb, O Christ our Salvation; and as God Thou didst raise the dead!

*Troparion:*
Coming early in the morning, the women saw Christ, and cried aloud unto the divine disciples: Truly Christ is risen; come, sing with us unto Him!

*Glory...*

All-holy Trinity, one Godhead, Unoriginate Unity, Father, Son, and Divine Spirit, save the world: Thou art our God, and we glorify Thee!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Having dissolved the ancient curse, O Pure One, thou didst cause blessing to sprout forth for us in thy womb, having given birth unto a Child: for He is God, though He bears flesh!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

*Come, let us drink a new drink... Now all things are filled with light... Yesterday I was buried with Thee, O Christ... (see fol. 6)*[^514]

*Theotokion:*
Unto incorruptible life do I come today, by the goodness of Him Who was born of thee, O Pure One, and Who hath shed light upon all the ends of the earth.

*Theotokion:*
Beholding God, Whom thou didst bear in the flesh, risen from the dead as He said, O Pure One, dance, and magnify Him as God, O Most Pure One!

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*

My barren mind show forth fruitful, O God, Cultivator of good things, Planter of blessings, by Thy tender compassion!

*Troparion:*
Thou didst stretch forth Thy palms upon the Cross, O Jesus, gathering all nations from delusion, and calling them unto the knowledge of Thyself, O Savior!

*Troparion:*
The Jewish people cry unto Pilate: Release unto us the malefactor robber; take away, take away, crucify the Sinless One!

*Troparion:*
When Thou wast crucified, O Christ, the light was darkened, the earth quaked, and many dead arose from the graves through fear of Thy dominion!

*Troparion:*
Standing by the Cross, Thine Ewe-lamb, O Jesus, cried with weeping: Whither goest Thou, O Savior? Whither art Thou going, O Lamb, Who wast slain for all?

*Troparion:*
I worship Thy Cross, I hymn also Thy Burial, I venerate the Passions, and the nails of Thy hands, and the spear, O Jesus, and Thy Resurrection!

*Troparion:*
Thou didst arise, O Jesus; the enemy was made captive; Adam was delivered, and Eve with him from bonds and corruption, by Thy Resurrection!

*Troparion:*
When Thou didst arise, O Christ, the bars and gates of hades were shattered, and the bonds of death were dissolved immediately through fear of Thy dominion!

*Troparion:*
Come, God-receiver Joseph, stand with us, crying aloud: Risen is Jesus the Redeemer, Who raised up Adam through His tender compassion!

*Troparion:*
Let the Twelve of the disciples rejoice with us, together with the Myrrh-bearing women, and Joseph, and with the other disciples and women disciples of Christ!

*Glory...*

With the Father I worship and hymn the Son and the upright Spirit, the one Nature, dividing Them by Persons and uniting Them by Essence.

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Blessed art thou, O Pure One, being from the root of Jesse, from whom thou didst bring forth according to the flesh for our sake Christ, the Rod and Flower!

*Kontakion, Tone 8: "Though Thou didst descend into the tomb..." (see fol. 7)[^515] And the Ikos.*

*And Sessional Hymn, Tone 2, Podoben: "Seeking the highest" (Вы́шнихъ и҆щѧ̀):*

The women fervently bring myrrh unto the tomb, O Savior, delighting their souls with the brilliance of the Angel, and they proclaimed Thee the God of all, and cried unto the disciples: Truly from the tomb is risen the Life of all!

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same:*

The choir of Thy disciples rejoices in concord with the Myrrh-bearing women: for they celebrate a common feast with them, unto the glory and honor of Thy Resurrection; and through them we cry aloud unto Thee, O Lord, Lover of mankind: Grant unto Thy people great mercy!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

*At the divine watch... The male child that opened the womb... As a yearling Lamb... David the ancestor of God... (see fol. 8)*[^516]

*Theotokion:*
He Who created Adam, thy forefather, O Pure One, is fashioned from thee, and hath destroyed the dwelling of death by His death today, and hath illumined all by the divine splendors of the Resurrection!

*Theotokion:*
Beholding Christ, Whom thou didst bear, risen most beautifully from the dead today for the salvation of all, O Pure One, good and blameless among women and beautiful: rejoicing with the Apostles, glorify Him!

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*

Foreseeing Thy birth from the Virgin, the Prophet proclaimed, crying: I heard Thy report, and was afraid; for from Teman and from the holy overshadowed mountain art Thou come, O Christ! (Hab. 3:2-3)[^517]

*Troparion:*
By the Cross Thou didst take captive the belly of hades, and didst raise the dead with Thyself, and abolish the tyranny of death; therefore, we who are born of Adam, worshipping, hymn Thy Burial and Thy Rising, O Christ!

*Troparion:*
Thou Who wast well pleased, O our Savior, through the tender compassion of mercy, to be nailed upon the Cross, and to deliver us from the ancestral curse: loose the bonds of my many transgressions; for Thou canst do all things whatsoever Thou willest!

*Troparion:*
Thou Who didst nail my ancient curse upon the Cross, and pour forth blessing upon me, O Savior, by the blood from Thy ribs: loose the bonds of my many transgressions; for Thou canst do all things whatsoever Thou willest!

*Troparion:*
Hades, O Savior, meeting Thee in the nether regions, was embittered, considering how he who once had power to swallow up is now compelled against his will to surrender, and melts away in the lower regions, and is despoiled and stripped of the dead!

*Troparion:*
Though the stone was sealed with a guard by the lawless enemies, yet the Lord arose, as He foretold, loosing the bonds of my many transgressions; for He can do all things whatsoever He will!

*Troparion:*
Thou Who didst arise from the tomb, and lead hades captive, and make alive the dead, and pour forth incorruption upon me by Thy Rising: loose the bonds of my many transgressions; for Thou canst do all things whatsoever Thou willest!

*Troparion:*
Truly be ashamed, ye lawless ones, for Christ is risen, and hath raised the dead with Himself, crying: Be of good cheer, I have overcome the world! Believe Him therefore, or keep silence, ye deceivers who deny His Resurrection!

*Troparion:*
Having proclaimed unto the Myrrh-bearing women: "Rejoice!", when Thou didst rise from the tomb, O Good One, and: "Proclaim My Rising unto the Apostles": loose the bonds of my many transgressions; for Thou canst do all things whatsoever Thou willest!

*Troparion:*
Let us honor with the Myrrh-bearing women the noble Joseph, the zealot of piety, counsellor and disciple, and with the Apostles, calling aloud with them, and faithfully hymning radiant the Savior's Resurrection!

*Glory...*

Who can declare the indivisible glory of the Super-essential Godhead? For being one by nature, the unoriginate Trinity is hymned, connatural, sung as a Monad in Trinity with simple Hypostases!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Having given birth without a husband unto Him Who dwelt in thy womb, O Mother and Virgin, Pure One: unceasingly pray as Mother of God to loose the bonds of my many transgressions; for thou art able to help in all things whatsoever thou willest!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

*Let us rise early in the deep dawn... Seeing Thy boundless tender compassion... Let us approach, bearing lamps... (see fol. 9)*[^518]

*Theotokion:*
The assembly of the pious is illumined by the divine and life-bearing rays of the Resurrection of thy Son, O most pure Mother of God, and is filled with joy.

*Theotokion:*
Thou didst not open the gates of virginity in the Incarnation, nor didst Thou break the seals of the tomb, O King of creation: therefore seeing Thee risen, Thy Mother rejoiced!

*Another [Canon], Heirmos:*

Dispel the gloom of my soul, O my Savior; illumine me with the light of Thy commandments, as the only King of peace!

*Troparion:*
My first garment, which the cutter of sin wove for me, alas for me! Thou didst put off, O my Savior, when Thou didst clothe Thyself in me.

*Troparion:*
My sin sewed fig leaves for me, alas for me! who kept not Thy most pure commandment, O my Savior, by the serpent's counsel.

*Troparion:*

On this day, the third Sunday after Pascha, we celebrate the feast of the holy Myrrh-bearing women. And we also commemorate Joseph of Arimathea, who was a secret disciple, being one of the Seventy; and with them also the nocturnal disciple Nicodemus, who from among the rulers came unto Christ. For these women indeed are the unlying and first witnesses of the Resurrection, and Joseph and Nicodemus of the Burial, which things are the truest and most foundational dogmas among us. For even as Nicodemus was cast out of the synagogue, having refused to consent unto the Jews; so Joseph, after burying the Lord's Body, was cast into a pit by the Jews, and by divine power was snatched away thence, and departed unto Arimathea, his own homeland. And Christ, having risen, appeared unto him while held fast in bonds, and confirmed unto him still more the mystery of the Resurrection. Though suffering many things from the Jews, he endured not to give over the mystical things unto silence, but with boldness taught all that had come to pass. And it is said that this Nicodemus later made clear by a composition the things concerning Christ's Passion and Resurrection, being the first of all, as belonging to the council, knowing accurately the counsels and words of the Jews, and simply all things.[^520] And for this cause, as we have said, as unlying witnesses of the Burial, they were ranked together with the women who had seen the Resurrection. And after the first assurance of Thomas, it was pre-ordained for them, because after eight days, I say, the good tidings came. For these women first saw the Resurrection, and announced these good tidings unto the disciples. For it was fitting that the sex which had first fallen under sin and inherited the curse should also be the first to see the Resurrection and hear the joy, having first heard: "In sorrows shalt thou bring forth children." (Gen. 3:16) They were called Myrrh-bearers: because, since the preparation of the Passover was at hand on Friday, and that Saturday was a great day, Joseph and Nicodemus made great haste to bury the Lord's Body; and according to Jewish custom they anointed It with myrrh, yet not as was fitting: for having applied aloe and myrrh hastily, and wrapped It in a shroud [plashchanytsia], they committed It to the tomb. Therefore these women, having fervent love toward Christ as disciples, having bought costly myrrh, came by night: both for fear of the Jews, and because it was the custom to weep early and anoint It, and to supply what had been lacking then due to haste. When they came, they saw various apparitions: two shining Angels inside the tomb, and another sitting upon the stone; and after these they saw Christ and worshipped Him. And Magdalene, supposing Him to be the gardener, questioned Him concerning Himself. Many indeed were the Myrrh-bearing women, but the Evangelists made mention of the prominent ones only, leaving aside the others. And they were these: First of all, Mary Magdalene, from whom Christ cast out seven demons; who also, after Christ's Ascension, went unto Rome, as with a new preaching delivered Pilate and the high priest unto death, having made known unto Tiberius Caesar the things concerning Christ the Savior.[^521] And afterward she died in Ephesus and was buried by John the Theologian; and by the most wise Leo she was translated unto the city of Constantine.[^522] The second was Salome. The third of the Myrrh-bearers is Joanna, the wife of Chuza, who was the steward and manager of the house of King Herod. The fourth and fifth were Mary and Martha, the sisters of Lazarus. The sixth was Mary the wife of Cleopas. The seventh was Susanna. And there were many others, as the divine Luke records, who, he saith, ministered unto Christ and His disciples from their possessions. (Luke 8:3) As these women proclaimed the Resurrection, and expounded many dogmas among us for the assurance and true testimony of the Resurrection of Christ: after Thomas, the Church of Christ received to celebrate them also, as having first seen Christ risen from the dead, and having proclaimed unto all the saving message, and having led their life according to Christ exceedingly, and as was fitting for women taught by Christ. By the prayers of the Holy Myrrh-bearing Women, O God, have mercy upon us.

All beyond understanding, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Theotokos; sealed in purity and preserved in virginity, thou wast acknowledged to be an unfeigned Mother, having given birth to the true God: Him do thou entreat that our souls may be saved!

*After the 2nd Kathisma Psalmody, Sessional Hymn.*

*Tone 2, Podoben: "Unto the Myrrh-bearing women" (Мѵроно́сицамъ жена́мъ):*

The choir of Thy disciples rejoices in unison with the Myrrh-bearing women; for with them we celebrate a common feast, unto the glory and honor of Thy Resurrection, and through them we cry aloud unto Thee: O Lord, Lover of mankind, grant great mercy unto Thy people!

*Glory... both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ..." Psalm 50.*

*The Canon of the Myrrh-bearers, with the heirmos, to 6; and of the temple saint, to 4; and of the Menaion saint, to 4.*[^557]

*The Kontakion and Exapostilarion of the Myrrh-bearers.*

*At the Praises, we set 4 verses, and we sing the following stichera:*

*Tone 2:*

Every breath and every creature glorifies Thee, O Lord, for by the Cross Thou hast abolished death, that Thou mightest show unto the people Thy Resurrection from the dead, as the only Lover of mankind.

Let the Jews say how the soldiers who guarded the King lost Him; for why did the stone not safeguard the Rock of life? Either let them surrender Him Who was buried, or let them worship Him Who is risen, saying with us: Glory to the abundance of Thy tender mercies, O our Savior, glory to Thee!

Rejoice, O ye peoples, and make glad: an Angel seated upon the stone of the tomb, he brought good tidings unto us, saying: Christ is risen from the dead, the Savior of the world, and hath filled all things with fragrance! Rejoice, O ye peoples, and make glad!

An Angel indeed brought "Rejoice!" unto her who was full of grace before Thy conception, O Lord; and an Angel rolled away the stone of Thy glorious tomb at Thy Resurrection: the former instead of sorrow announcing signs of gladness, the latter instead of death proclaiming unto us the Master and Life-giver; therefore we cry aloud unto Thee: O Benefactor of all, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 1:*[^558]

Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came unto the tomb, seeking the Lord, and they saw an Angel like lightning sitting upon the stone, and saying unto them: Why seek ye the Living One with the dead? He is risen as He said; ye shall find Him in Galilee; unto Whom let us cry aloud: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*At the Aposticha, stichera of the Feast.*

*Tone 2, Automela (Samopodobny):*

When from the Tree Joseph of Arimathea took Thee down dead, Who art the Life of all, he wrapped Thee, O Christ, with myrrh and a winding-sheet, and with love was moved in heart and lips to kiss Thy most pure Body; yet restrained by fear, rejoicing he cried aloud unto Thee: Glory to Thy condescension, O Lover of mankind!

*Verse: Lord, Thou hast been favorable unto Thy land; Thou hast turned away the captivity of Jacob. (Ps. 84:2 LXX)*

When in a new tomb Thou wast laid for the sake of all, O Redeemer of all, hades, laughed to scorn, seeing Thee was dismayed; the bars were broken, the gates were shattered, the tombs were opened, the dead arose; then Adam, gratefully rejoicing, cried aloud unto Thee: Glory to Thy condescension, O Lover of mankind!

*Verse: Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other. (Ps. 84:11 LXX)*

When the heavenly powers beheld Thee, O Christ, slandered by the lawless as a deceiver, they were struck with awe at Thine ineffable long-suffering, and at the stone of the tomb sealed by the very hands that pierced Thy corruption-free Side; yet rejoicing in our salvation they cried aloud unto Thee: Glory to Thy condescension, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory... both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 6:*

Joseph asked for the Body of Jesus, and laid It in his own new tomb; for it was fitting for Him to come forth from the tomb as from a bridal chamber, Who crushed the dominion of death and opened the gates of paradise unto men: Lord, glory to Thee!

*Then: "It is good to give thanks..." and the Troparion. The First Hour, and the Dismissal.*

*On Saturday of the Myrrh-bearers, the Four-Ode Canon.*[^559]

*Tone 2. Ode 6. Heirmos:*

"Lead me up from the depths!" Jonah cried aloud unto Thee, being an image of the Burial and the Resurrection which Thou hast bestowed upon us, O Almighty Savior!

*Troparia:*

The Myrrh-bearing women, having reached Thy tomb, were shedding tears; but an Angel announced joy unto them, crying aloud: Christ is risen, proclaim it unto the world!

When Thou wast lifted up upon the Cross, the sun hid its radiance; but Israel did not recognize Thee as God and Creator, Who didst willingly lay down Thy life for all!

*Glory...*

Let all creation rejoice, for Christ hath arisen from the tomb, taking hades captive and shattering all its power; and let them celebrate a common feast of gladness!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Though thou didst give birth to an Infant, yet thou didst remain a Virgin, even as thou wast before giving birth, O pure Virgin Theotokos: for He Who was born renewed nature as God!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

"O Thou Who extinguished of old the all-blazing furnace, and bedewed the youths with spiritual moisture: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!"

*Troparia:*

The senseless people of the Jewish assembly, in return for the benefits which Thou hadst bestowed upon them, nailed Thee, O Christ, unto the Cross!

O Wellspring of mercy, Thou wast laid down for all, that we, having all been released from the debt of death, may hymn Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory...*

By Thy three-day death and life-bearing Burial, Thou hast granted eternal life unto those in the tombs, who faithfully hymn Thee, O Savior!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Unto thee, the only mediatress of joy, it is fitting to receive from the Angel the "Rejoice!", which we faithful cry aloud unto thee, O Mary Ever-Virgin!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

"The unwearied fire, which was nurtured in boundless matter, stood in fear before the pious youths—whose unblemished bodies were fashioned in harmony with their souls—and withdrew from them; and as the ever-living flame was cleft, an everlasting song was sung: Hymn the Lord, all ye works, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!"

*Troparia:*

The godless counsel is destroyed, and the seals became vain for the Jews: He Who was buried hath arisen, having laughed all to scorn; for God willingly accepts suffering, but by His own authority He orders all things as He wills: as the Lord do we unceasingly hymn Him and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

"Go," said he whose appearance was shining like lightning unto the women: "Christ is risen! Proclaim henceforth His Resurrection unto the whole world, and weep not, for it is no time for tears: for death reigns no longer, hades rules no more, the guilt of condemnation shall no longer prevail!"

*Glory...*

Our life was hidden in Thee, O Christ the Word, Who didst suffer in the flesh and art risen from the dead, O Word, and Who livest for ever: in Whom we Christians hope to have part at Thy dread second coming, we faithful worthily hymning Thy Divine Sufferings and Thy Resurrection from the dead!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Through thee our ancestral mother received release from the ancient condemnation: no longer does she give birth in sorrow, for thou hast given birth to Christ, O Mother of God; therefore we worthily call thee blessed, singing praise unto Him Who was born of thee without seed, and exalting Him above all unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

"Thou hast magnified her that gave Thee birth, O Christ, the Theotokos, from whom, O our Creator, Thou didst clothe Thyself in a body of like passions with us, which released us from our transgressions: calling her blessed, all generations, we magnify Thee!"

*Troparia:*

"Hear the voice of thy Bridegroom, O Church!" cries the divine David; for unto thee from His Side was granted water with Divine Blood for the cleansing of sins: for He hath delivered thee from the deceit of idols, as the Lover of mankind!

Thou hast shone forth life unto the multitude of the Gentiles by Thy three-day Arising, O Christ: unto those that asked not, nor sought Thee, Thou wast revealed in divine glory; but the Jewish people who did not believe in Thee Thou hast condemned!

*Glory...*

The curse that sprouted for me through the deceit of the tree in Eden Thou hast dissolved, O Word, being obedient even unto the Cross to the Father's counsels of Thine own sovereign will: therefore I hymn and magnify unceasingly Thine ineffable self-emptying!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

Thou hast drawn near unto Thyself a holy nation that was afar off, having taken flesh of the Virgin, O Savior, and those who were not Thy people Thou hast named Thy divine people: therefore we all now worship Thee, O Almighty One!
