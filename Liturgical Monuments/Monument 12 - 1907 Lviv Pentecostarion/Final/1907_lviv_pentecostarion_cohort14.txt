*It is fitting to know also this: that we have received in Palestine, on this holy day of Great Friday, not to celebrate the Presanctified nor again the complete Liturgy; neither do we set a table, nor do we eat on this day of the Crucifixion.*[^262]

*For thus have we received from the Holy Precepts of the Holy Apostles: not to eat on Great Friday; for the word of the Lord which the Lord spoke unto the Pharisees: "When the Bridegroom shall be taken away from them, then shall they fast in those days." Here the most blessed Apostles received this, and this shalt thou find in the Apostolic Constitutions, if thou study them attentively. Moreover, the canonical epistle of the most holy Dionysius, Archbishop of Alexandria, clearly sets this forth.*
