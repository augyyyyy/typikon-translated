The choir of the Saints now shines with divine gifts: all the Patriarchs before the law, the Prophets, the Apostles, and the assemblies of the Martyrs, and of the Ascetics, and of the Teachers, and of the Righteous, together with the Hieromartyrs. *(Twice)*
