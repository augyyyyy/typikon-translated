# PART V: SUNDAY OF THE SAMARITAN WOMAN AND FIFTH WEEK AFTER PASCHA

## FIFTH SUNDAY AFTER PASCHA, OF THE SAMARITAN WOMAN

*On Saturday evening, at Small Vespers:*

*At "Lord, I have cried," 4 Resurrectional stichera.*

*Tone 4:*

*To Thy life-creating Cross... (see fol. 385)*[^737]  
*Of the tree of disobedience... (see fol. 385)*  
*The gates of hades hast Thou shattered, O Lord... (see fol. 385)*  
*Come, ye peoples, let us hymn... (see fol. 385)*  

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Tone 6:*

*At Jacob's well... (see fol. 386)*[^738]  

*Seek all these ahead at Great Vespers.*

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Dogmatic Theotokion. Tone 4:*
Without seed thou didst conceive, and ineffably gavest birth unto Him Who put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted the humble, and raised up the horn of His anointed who glorify Christ's Cross and Burial, and glorious Resurrection: therefore, O Theotokos, mediatress of so many good things, with unceasing hymns we call thee blessed, as thou ever entreatest that our souls may be saved.[^739]

*At the Aposticha, Resurrectional sticheron:*
*O Lord, having ascended the Cross... (see fol. 386)*[^740]  
*Seek this at Great Vespers.*

*Then the following prosomoia stichera of the Most Holy Theotokos.*
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Being called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*

*Verse:* I will remember thy name in every generation and generation.[^741]
The Son Who is from the Unoriginate Father without beginning of years, for the sake of condescension and the salvation of men, God became man, that He might grant paradise unto the first-created now; then also all nature shall be delivered from the delusion of the serpent, and He shall save the fallen image, as Well-Changer; on both sides He hath made a Mother, a pure Bride-Chamber, as undefiled: whom, as anchor and haven, all of us call blessed.

*Verse:* Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear.[^742]
Thou didst hold in thy womb the incarnate Creator of all, O Divinely Blessed One, Who re-formed man who of old had fallen through the transgression of the serpent; for thou gavest birth unto God ineffably for us, and by thy Childbearing didst set free all our corrupted and aged nature: therefore we hymn and glorify thy grace, O Unwedded Virgin; cease not to pray that our souls may be saved.

*Verse:* The rich among the people shall entreat thy countenance.[^743]
That thou mayest reveal unto all the multitude of thy mercy, and unto us the abyss of goodness that never passes away, blot out all the sins of thy servants: for, O All-Blameless One, as Mother of God, thou hast authority over creation, and thou guidest all things as thou wilt by thy power: for the grace of the Holy Spirit having manifestly dwelt in thee, works together with thee in all things ever, O Most Blessed One.

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Tone 8:*
When Thou wast manifested in the flesh, O Christ God, in Thine ineffable dispensation, the Samaritan woman, hearing Thy word, O Lover of mankind, left her water-pot by the well, and ran, saying unto them that were in the city: Come, see the Knower of hearts! Is not this indeed the expected Christ, Who hath great mercy?[^744]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same:*
When the feast was halfway through, as Thou wast teaching, O Savior, the Jews said: Whence knows this Man letters, having never learned? not understanding that Thou art the Wisdom that fashioned the world: glory to Thee![^745]

*Dismissal Troparion. Tone 4:*
Having learned from the Angel the radiant proclamation of the Resurrection, and cast off the ancestral condemnation, the women disciples of the Lord boasting said unto the Apostles: Death is overthrown! Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Troparion of the Feast:*[^746]
When the feast is halfway through, give my thirsty soul to drink of the waters of piety, for unto all, O Savior, Thou didst cry aloud: Let him that thirsts come unto Me and drink! O Wellspring of our life, Christ God, glory to Thee!

*And the dismissal.*

*On the Sunday of the Samaritan Woman, at Great Vespers.*

## AT GREAT VESPERS

*We set 10 verses. And we sing 3 Resurrectional stichera, and 1 Anatolian; and 3 prosomoia of Mid-Pentecost; and 3 Idiomela of the Samaritan Woman.*

*Resurrectional stichera, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron 1:*
Unto Thy life-creating Cross, unceasingly bowing down in worship, O Christ God, we glorify Thy third-day Resurrection; for thereby hast Thou renewed the corrupted human nature, O Almighty One, and hast renewed for us the ascent unto heaven, as the only good One and Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron 2:*
The condemnation of the tree of disobedience hast Thou dissolved, O Savior, by being willingly nailed unto the tree of the Cross; and having descended into hades, O Mighty One, Thou didst tear asunder the bonds of death as God; therefore we bow down in worship unto Thy Resurrection from the dead, crying out with joy: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 3:*
The gates of hades hast Thou shattered, O Lord, and by Thy death hast Thou destroyed the kingdom of death; and the human race hast Thou freed from corruption, granting life and incorruption unto the world, and great mercy.

*Another sticheron, Anatolian:*

*Sticheron 4:*
Come, ye peoples, let us hymn the Savior's third-day Arising, whereby we have been delivered from the unbreakable bonds of hades, and have all received incorruption and life, crying aloud: O Thou Who wast crucified, and buried, and didst arise, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*And stichera of Mid-Pentecost, 3, the same Tone. Podoben: "O Lord, having ascended the Cross" (Гд҃и возше́дъ на кр҃тъ):*

*The midpoint of the days has arrived...*  
*Heard and rejoiced...*  
*Draw near, ye peoples, unto the Divine... (see fol. 372)*[^747]  

*And of the Samaritan Woman, idiomela, 3.*  
*Tone 1:*

*Sticheron 8:*
Unto the fountain didst Thou come, O Fountain of wonders, at the sixth hour, to capture the fruit of Eve; for Eve at that hour went forth from paradise by the deceit of the serpent;[^748] the Samaritan woman drew near to draw water, whom having seen, the Savior said: Give Me water to drink, and I will satisfy thee with flowing water; and having run into the city, the chaste woman immediately proclaimed unto the multitudes: Come, see Christ the Lord, the Savior of our souls![^749]

*Tone 2:*

*Sticheron 9:*
When the Lord came unto the well, the Samaritan woman entreated the Compassionate One: Give me the water of faith, and I shall receive the waters of the font, rejoicing and redemption; O Life-Giver, Lord, glory to Thee![^750]

*Tone 3:*

*Sticheron 10:*
The Son and Word of the Father, co-unoriginate and co-eternal, came unto the fountain, being the Fountain of healings; and a woman came from Samaria to draw water, whom having seen, the Savior said: Give Me water to drink, and go, call thy husband. But she, speaking as unto a man and not unto God, striving to conceal herself, said: I have no husband. And the Teacher said unto her: Truly hast thou said: I have no husband; for thou hast had five, and now he whom thou hast is not thy husband. But she, being amazed at His word, ran unto the city and cried out unto the multitudes, saying: Come, see Christ, Who grants unto the world great mercy![^751]

*Glory, Tone 6:*

*Doxasticon:*
At Jacob's well, Jesus, finding the Samaritan woman, asks water of her, He Who covers the earth with clouds.[^752] O wonder! He Who is borne upon the Cherubim conversed with a woman who was a harlot; asking for water, He Who suspended the earth upon the waters; seeking water, He Who pours forth springs and lakes of waters; desiring truly to draw her who was captured by the adversary foe, and to give her living water to drink, who was sorely burned in unseemliness, as the only compassionate One and Lover of mankind!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Dogmatic Theotokion, Tone 4:*
David the Prophet, who through thee became the forefather of God, cried out concerning thee in psalmody unto Him Who worked great things for thee: The Queen stood at Thy right hand;[^753] for God, Who was pleased without a father to become man from thee, showed thee forth as the Mother and mediatress of life, that He might renew His image corrupted by passions, and having found the sheep that went astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His own will, unite it with the heavenly hosts, and save the world, O Theotokos, Christ Who hath great and rich mercy.

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day:*  
*At the Litiya, sticheron of the temple.*  
*Glory, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 3:*

*Litiya Sticheron:*
Let heaven and earth rejoice radiantly today, for Christ hath appeared, being incarnate as man, that He might deliver Adam from the curse of the entire race, and is admired for His marvels, having come into Samaria; and He presented Himself unto the woman, asking for water, He Who arrays the clouds with waters; therefore all we faithful worship Him Who for our sake willingly became poor, by His compassionate counsel![^754]

*At the Aposticha, Resurrectional sticheron, Tone 4:*
O Lord, having ascended the Cross, Thou didst abolish our ancestral curse; and having descended into hades, Thou didst free those bound from the ages, granting incorruption unto the human race; therefore with hymns we glorify Thy life-creating and saving Arising!

*Then the Stichera of Pascha, with their refrains; seek on the Sunday of Pascha at Matins: (see fol. 205).*[^755]

*Glory, Tone 8:*
When Thou didst appear in the flesh, O Christ God, by Thine ineffable dispensation, the Samaritan woman, having heard the word of Thee, the Lover of mankind, left her water jar at the well and ran, saying unto those in the city: Come, see the Knower of hearts; is this not the expected Christ, Who hath great mercy?[^756]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.*  
*The day of Resurrection:*  
*And all the rest, as was prescribed in the other Sundays.*

*Dismissal Troparion, Tone 4:*  
The radiant proclamation of the Resurrection having learned from the Angel, the Lord's disciples cast off the ancestral condemnation, and boasting, said unto the Apostles: Death is overthrown, Christ God is risen, granting unto the world great mercy!

*Glory, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Of the feast, Tone 8:*  
At Mid-Pentecost, satisfy my thirsty soul with the waters of piety, for Thou, O Savior, didst cry aloud unto all: Whosoever thirsts, let him come unto Me and drink; O Fountain of our life, Christ God, glory to Thee![^757]

*If there be no vigil, at the Midnight Office the Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, in the Octoechos, of the 4th Tone.*  
*Then "It is truly meet," and the rest.*

## ON THE FIFTH SUNDAY AFTER PASCHA, OF THE SAMARITAN WOMAN, AT MATINS

*At "God is the Lord," Resurrectional Troparion:*  
*Tone 4:*  
*The radiant proclamation of the Resurrection: Twice. Glory, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.*  
*Of the feast: At Mid-Pentecost:*  
*Then the customary chanting of the Psalter.*

*After the 1st chanting of the Psalter, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*  
Beholding the entrance of the tomb, and unable to endure the flame of the Angel, the Myrrh-bearing women marveled with trembling, saying: Was He stolen away Who opened paradise unto the thief? Or is He arisen Who proclaimed His Arising even before the Passion? Truly Christ is risen, granting life and resurrection unto those in hades![^758]

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*[^759]

*Tone 4, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*  
Thou didst arise from the tomb as immortal, O Savior; Thou didst raise up Thy world together with Thyself by Thy power, O Christ our God; Thou didst crush the dominion of death in strength; Thou didst show forth the Resurrection unto all, O Merciful One; therefore we also glorify Thee, O only Lover of mankind![^760]

*Glory, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto Angels was revealed through thee, O Theotokos, unto those upon earth: God incarnate in inconfused union, Who willingly accepted the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-fashioned man, He saved our souls from death.[^761]

*After the 2nd chanting of the Psalter, Sessional Hymn.*  
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Joseph was amazed" (Оу҆диви́сѧ І҆ѡ́сифъ):*  
Having descended from the heights on high, Gabriel came unto the stone where the Rock of life lay, and clothed in white, he cried unto the weeping women: Cease your lamentation and crying, ye who even now have compassion; for take courage, He Whom ye seek weeping is truly risen; therefore cry aloud unto the Apostles that the Lord is risen![^762]

*Verse: I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart, I will tell of all Thy marvelous works.*[^763]

*Sessional Hymn:*  
By Thy voluntary counsel Thou didst endure the Cross, O Savior, and mortal men laid Thee in a new tomb, Who didst establish the ends of the earth by Thy word; therefore the alien foe was bound; death was sorely despoiled, and all those in hades cried out unto Thy life-bearing Arising: Christ is risen, the Life-Giver Who abides unto the ages!

*Glory, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion, automelon:*  
Joseph was amazed, beholding that which was above nature, and he considered the rain upon the fleece in thy seedless conception, O Theotokos, the bush unburned by fire, Aaron's rod that budded; and thy betrothed and guardian, bearing witness, cried unto the priests: A Virgin gives birth, and after childbirth remains again a Virgin![^764]

*Then the reading in the Commentary on the Gospel of John: Homilies 31, 32, 33, and 34.*[^765]  
*After "Blessed are the blameless,"*[^766] *we say the Resurrectional troparia: "Blessed art Thou, O Lord," in Tone 5: "The Angelic Council," and the rest.*

*And immediately the Hypakoe, Tone 4:*  
Having run beforehand, the Myrrh-bearing women proclaimed Thy glorious Arising unto the Apostles, O Christ, that Thou art risen as God, granting unto the world great mercy.[^767]

*And immediately the ANTIPHONS OF ASCENT, Tone 4.*  
*Antiphon 1:*  
From my youth many passions war against me; but do Thou Thyself help me and save me, O my Savior!  
Ye that hate Zion, be put to shame by the Lord; for ye shall be withered up even as grass by fire.[^768]  
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
By the Holy Spirit every soul is quickened, and through purity is exalted; it is made radiant by the Triune Unity in sacred mystery.  
*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*Antiphon 2:*  
I cried unto Thee, O Lord, fervently from the depth of my soul, and let Thy Divine ears be attentive unto me for hearing.[^769]  
Whosoever hath acquired hope in the Lord is higher than all who grieve.[^770]  
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
By the Holy Spirit stream the floods of grace, watering all creation unto revitalization.  
*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*Antiphon 3:*  
Let my heart be lifted up unto Thee, O Word, and let none of the world's allurements beguile me unto faintness.  
Even as one hath love for his mother, so are we bound with warmer affection unto the Lord.  
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
By the Holy Spirit is the wealth of divine knowledge, contemplation, and wisdom; for in Him the Word reveals all the Father's decrees.  
*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*  
Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for Thy name's sake.[^771]  
*Verse: O God, with our ears have we heard.*[^772]  
*Then: Let every breath praise the Lord.*  
*Verse: Praise God in His saints.*  
*The Gospel according to John, 7th Matins Resurrection Gospel: "On the first day of the week..."*[^773]  
*Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ: Thrice. Psalm 50. And the rest.*

*Then the CANONS: Of Pascha, with the heirmoi and Theotokia, to 6; of Mid-Pentecost, to 4; and of the Samaritan Woman, to 4.*  
*Katavasia: The day of Resurrection:*  
*Canon of Pascha, Tone 1. Ode 1. Heirmos: The day of Resurrection:*  
*Second Canon of Mid-Pentecost.*  
*The work of Andrew of Crete.*[^774]  
*Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos:*  
Thou hast congealed the sea:

*Troparion:*
Clap your hands, ye nations; weep, ye Jews; for Christ the Life-Giver hath burst asunder the bonds of hades, and raised the dead, and healed infirmities by His word; this is our God, Who hath given life unto those who believe in His name![^775]

*Troparion:*
A miracle didst Thou show, water transformed into wine, O Master Who didst turn the rivers in Egypt into blood; and Thou didst raise the dead, having accomplished this second sign: glory, O Savior, to Thine ineffable counsel; glory to Thy self-emptying, whereby Thou hast renewed us![^776]

*Troparion:*
Being the ever-flowing stream of true life, O Lord, Thou art our Resurrection; willingly didst Thou labor, O my Savior, and voluntarily didst Thou thirst, submitting unto the laws of nature; and having gone in the flesh unto Sychar, Thou didst ask water to drink of the Samaritan woman.[^777]

*Theotokion:*
Thou alone didst contain thy Creator in thy womb, O Mother of God, and didst ineffably bear Him in the flesh, and didst remain a Virgin, thy virginity being in no wise defiled; therefore, O Pure One, as thy Son and God, unceasingly pray unto Him ever for thy flock!

*Another Canon, of the Samaritan Woman. The work of Joseph.*[^778]  
*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*  
Thou Who didst smite Egypt, and didst drown Pharaoh the tormentor in the sea, didst save the people from servitude, who sang a song of victory after the manner of Moses: For He is glorified![^779]

*Troparion:*
He Who was buried is risen, and hath raised up the human race with Himself; let all creation rejoice, and let the spiritual clouds today manifestly drop down righteousness![^780]

*Troparion:*
Having willingly accepted the Cross in the flesh, Thou didst arise on the third day from the dead, having shattered the strongholds of hades, O Life-Originating Lord, and having led forth the bound souls!

*Troparion:*
Beholding the Angel flashing in appearance, the Myrrh-bearing women fled from the tomb with fear; but having learned of Christ's Resurrection, they strive to proclaim it unto the disciples.[^781]

*Troparion:*
O Lord Who coverest the highest heavens with waters, being Thyself the Water of life, Thou didst give Thy precious waters unto the Samaritan woman who asked, who understood Thy compassion.[^782]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Trinity Hymn:*  
O Trinity, O Father, Son, and Spirit, save those who honor and glorify Thee with faith, as Creator of all, and grant us cleansing of sins, as Thou art supremely good!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
Rejoice, O Fiery Throne; rejoice, O all-golden Lampstand; rejoice, O Cloud of light; rejoice, O Palace of the Word, and living Table, who didst worthily bear Christ, the Bread of life![^783]

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*  
My heart was established:[^784]

*Troparion:*
Judge not according to appearance, ye Jews! the Master cried teaching, when He came into the temple, as it is written, at Mid-Pentecost of the lawful feast.[^785]

*Troparion:*
Judge not according to appearance, ye Jews! for Christ is come, Whom the Prophets foretold, coming forth from Zion, and calling the world.[^786]

*Troparion:*
Even if ye believe not His words, ye Jews, believe the works of the Master; why are ye deceived, rejecting the Holy One, concerning Whom Moses wrote in the law?[^787]

*Theotokion:*
Being One of the Trinity, Thou wast seen as flesh, without changing Thy essence, O Lord, nor burning the incorrupt womb of her that bore Thee, being wholly God and fire!

*Another, Heirmos:*  
My heart was established in the Lord, Who gives prayer unto him that prays; for the bow of the mighty hath grown weak, and the feeble have girded themselves with strength.[^788]

*Troparion:*
Willingly wast Thou lifted up upon the Tree, O Word; and beholding it, the stones were rent, and all creation bowed down, and the dead arose from the tombs as if from sleep![^789]

*Troparion:*
Beholding Thee having come into hades with Thy soul, O Word, all the souls of the righteous were loosed from their eternal bonds, singing of Thy power which is beyond understanding![^790]

*Troparion:*
Why are ye amazed? Why seek ye the Master in the tomb with myrrh, O women? He is risen, and hath raised up the world together with Himself! the flashing Angel cried unto the Myrrh-bearing women.[^791]

*Troparion:*
Being Life, O Lord, and the Fountain of immortality, Thou didst sit by the fountain, O Compassionate One, and with Thy all-wise waters didst fill the Samaritan woman who asked and sang unto Thee.[^792]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Trinity Hymn:*  
One God over all in the Trinity is hymned, the Father, and the Son, and the Divine Spirit, Whom the heavenly ranks glorify with fear, clearly crying out: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O Lord![^793]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
Having ineffably conceived in thy womb the God of all, O Virgin Mother, thou didst give birth beyond thought and word, remaining a Virgin even as before childbirth, O Bride of God!

*Kontakion of Mid-Pentecost, and the Ikos: (see fol. 371).*[^794]  
*Sessional Hymn of the Samaritan Woman, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йсѧ):*  
Let heaven rejoice, let earthly things be glad, for Christ hath appeared from the Virgin as man, and hath delivered all humanity from corruption by His death; and shining forth with miracles unto the Samaritan woman who asked for water, He grants a fountain of healings, as the only Immortal One![^795]

*Glory, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 4, the same Podoben:*  
O Bestower of wisdom and Master, Thou didst present Thyself at the lawful feast, and sitting in the temple, Thou didst teach, saying thus unto all: Come, ye that thirst, drink the water which I now give, whereby all men shall enjoy life from God and delight![^796]

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*  
The Prophet Habakkuk:[^797]

*Troparion:*
If the Messiah must come, and Christ is the Messiah, O lawless ones, why believe ye Him not? Behold, He is come, and the things that He does bear witness: He made water into wine; He strengthened the paralytic by His word![^798]

*Troparion:*
Not understanding the Scriptures, ye are all deceived, ye lawless Hebrews; for truly Christ is come and hath enlightened all, and among you hath shown forth many signs and wonders, yet ye deny yourselves true Life![^799]

*Troparion:*
One work have I shown you, and ye all already marvel, cried Christ unto the Jews: Ye circumcise a man even on the Sabbath, He said: why then do ye slander Me further, Who raised up the paralytic by a word?[^800]

*Troparion:*
Many works have I done, and for which work do ye stone Me with stones? cried Christ rebuking the Jews: Is it because I made an entire man whole by a word? Judge not according to appearance, O men![^801]

*Another Heirmos:*  
I heard Thy report, O God, and was afraid; I considered Thy works and was terrified, O Lord; for the earth is full of Thy praise.[^802]

*Troparion:*
Let the heavens be glad, let all creation keep festival: the Lord is risen, and hath appeared unto all His all-wise Apostles!

*Troparion:*
Thy strength was swallowed up, O death, for Christ having died, the dead went forth from the tombs by His Arising, as if from bridal chambers![^803]

*Troparion:*
Why weep ye, O women? Why seek ye the Immortal One with myrrh? He is risen, even as He said! the Angel spoke unto the Myrrh-bearing women.[^804]

*Troparion:*
Unto the Samaritan woman who asked, O Lord, Thou didst give water, the knowledge of Thy dominion; therefore unto the ages she shall not thirst, singing praise unto Thee![^805]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Trinity Hymn:*  
O transcendent Trinity, Father, Word, and Divine Spirit, of one power, co-unoriginate: save us all who hymn Thee with faith!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
The lawgiver of old beheld thee as the unburned bush, and Daniel contemplated the holy mountain, O only Virgin Mother, Sovereign Lady![^806]

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*  
O Lord our God, peace:[^807]

*Troparion:*
With wonders hast Thou adorned Thine Apostles, marvelous hast Thou magnified Thy disciples, having glorified them in all the world, O our Savior, and having given unto them Thy Kingdom![^808]

*Troparion:*
The disciples enlightened all the ends of the earth by wonders and teachings, and in various manners proclaimed the word of Thy Kingdom, O Christ Savior!

*Troparion:*
Praise we send up unto Thy Kingdom, and hymnody we offer unto Thee, Who didst appear upon earth for our sake, and didst enlighten the world, and didst recall Adam!

*Theotokion:*
Thy womb became a Holy Table, having the Heavenly Bread; of which whosoever eats dies not, even as the Nourisher of all hath said, O Mother of God![^809]

*Another, Heirmos:*  
Make the light of Thy commandments shine upon me, O Lord, for unto Thee my spirit rises early and sings unto Thee; for Thou art our God, and unto Thee do I flee, O King of peace![^810]

*Troparion:*
Having reached Thy holy tomb very early in the morning, the Myrrh-bearing women beheld a young man flashing with light, and were terrified, having learned of Thy Divine Arising, O Christ![^811]

*Troparion:*
Death is put to death, hades is despoiled, those in bonds are set free by Christ's Resurrection; let us rejoice and clap our hands, celebrating radiantly![^812]

*Troparion:*
Leap for joy, ye Apostles, and dance, ye Angels; let all earthborn mortals rejoice: the Lord is risen, corruption is overthrown, and sorrow hath ceased, and Adam leaps for joy!

*Troparion:*
Being the Fountain of life, O Lord, Thou didst grant the water of remission and understanding unto the woman who asked of old, the Samaritan woman; therefore we sing of Thine ineffable compassions![^813]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Trinity Hymn:*  
The three-hypostatic Monad, the consubstantial Trinity, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit, indivisible in essence, we venerate as one God, Creator and Lord and Master of all!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
Thee do we name the Gate impassable, and the Field untilled, and the Ark bearing the Manna and the Jar, and the Lampstand, and the Censer of immaterial coal, O Pure One![^814]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*  
As the waters of the sea:[^815]

*Troparion:*
Upholding all the ends of the earth, Thou didst ascend, O Jesus, and didst teach the peoples in the temple the word of truth, at Mid-Pentecost, even as John cries aloud![^816]

*Troparion:*
The Father's work hast Thou accomplished, by works hast Thou confirmed Thy words, performing healings and signs, O Savior: making whole the paralytic, cleansing the lepers, and raising the dead![^817]

*Troparion:*
The unoriginate Son became originate, having assumed our nature, He became man, and in the midst of the feast He taught, saying: Come ye unto the ever-flowing Fountain, draw life![^818]

*Theotokion:*
A Virgin after childbirth we hymn thee, a Virgin and Mother we glorify thee, thee alone, O Pure Maiden, Bride of God; for from thee truly God was incarnate, having renewed us!

*Another. Heirmos:*

*Coming to draw corruptible water, O woman,*  
*Thou drawest living water, whereby thou washest away the defilements of the soul.*[^827]

On this same day, the Fifth Sunday after Pascha, we celebrate the feast of the Samaritan Woman; because unto her Christ plainly confessed Himself to be the Messiah, which is Christ: [or the Anointed.][^828] For with the Hebrews the measure is oil; therefore, I think, it was appointed also for the week of Mid-Pentecost. And even as in the Sunday prior to this He worked a miracle at the pool,[^829] so in this present one, Christ conversed with the woman over Jacob’s well, which well Jacob himself dug and gave to Joseph his son;[^830] for this was an excellent place, where also near the existing Mount Somor, the Samaritans inhabited many cities. And Christ comes into Sychar, where Jacob had dwelt of old with his sons and with Dinah his daughter. Shechem, son of Hamor the Horrite, lusted after her, and having violated her, was with her; and from that cause her brothers, being provoked to great jealousy, suddenly entered into the city and killed them all together, both that Shechem and Hamor his father.[^831] Jacob therefore dwelt there, and dug the present well. Yet the Samaritans did not dwell near that mountain previously, but the Israelites, having provoked that anger, in the reign of Pekah, during the first and second captivities, when the Assyrians came, were subjected to tribute by them; and afterward, in the reign of Hoshea, they attached themselves to the Ethiopians; which when the Assyrian king learned, he deported them to Babylon. And he commanded various nations to dwell in that place; but God let loose lions upon those foreigners. And the Assyrian king, having learned this in turn, sent unto them priests from among the Jews, (for they were still captives there,) that they might also receive the laws of God. And from the idols indeed they quickly broke away. But only...[^832]

...the five books of Moses did they accept, rejecting the Prophets and the rest of the Scripture.[^833] These were also called Samaritans, as being from Mount Somor; and they were hated by the Hebrews returning from captivity, because they Judaized only in part and would not eat with them, abominating them unyieldingly; therefore also they often called Christ a Samaritan, as one who despised and broke certain things of the Law even as they did.[^834]

The Lord therefore comes into Sychar, and being weary from the journey, He sat down about the sixth hour of the day. And a certain woman from the city came to draw water, the disciples having gone away to buy food; Jesus therefore asks water. But she proposes not to touch Him, having recognized Him both from His speech and from His apparel. But He leads her upward, bringing spiritual water into the midst, which He shows to be inexhaustible and cleansing; for the Spirit is ever likened unto water and fire. But the woman, persisting importunately, that He had no such water, so as not to carry a vessel for drawing, and that the well belonged to Jacob, she replies; furthermore she also raises word concerning the forefather Jacob, that he himself dug the well, and he and his sheep drank of it, setting forth the rich abundance of the springs, and that the well was otherwise well-suited.[^835]

Christ therefore does not make Himself greater than Jacob, lest He terrify the woman, but speaks concerning the water, setting forth the abundance thereof prefiguratively: for he who drinks of that water shall by no means thirst.[^836] And the woman asks for the water. But He tells her to summon her husband, as she was in need of understanding more solid words. But she denies that she has a husband. And He Who knows all things says: Thou hast well said, for five husbands hast thou had, as the Law commands, and he whom thou now hast as the sixth, because thou cohabitest with him unlawfully, is not thy husband.[^837]

[Some indeed have thought the five husbands to be the five books of Moses, which the Samaritans accepted, and the sixth to be those words of Christ, which were not yet spoken, for grace was not yet poured out. Others say they were the five laws given by God: that in paradise, and that after the expulsion; that in the time of Noah; that in the time of Abraham; that in the time of Moses; while the sixth is the Gospel, which she did not yet possess. There are also those who say they are the five senses.][^838]

The woman answers Him, calling Him a Prophet. Then she also questions Him concerning the mountain, where it is proper to worship: in Somor or in Jerusalem? For the Samaritans did not deem that God is completely everywhere, but that God abides only there where they also worshiped, on Mount Gerizim, because the blessing was given there from God; or because there Israel first built an altar unto God; even as the Jews said in turn: In Jerusalem it is proper to worship the one God; therefore also those from all quarters gathered there for the feast.[^839]

And Christ answers: Salvation for the world, He says, is of the Jews; yet He said: God is immaterial, and those deemed worthy to worship Him are no longer to worship in sacrifices, but in spirit and in truth; or they shall know God thus: not as solitary, but in the Holy Spirit and the Son, for He is the Truth.[^840]

The woman says again: We hear from the Scriptures that Messiah comes, Who is called Christ. And Jesus says: I am He; having understood the noble disposition of the woman.[^841]

And the Samaritans knew also concerning the Messiah from the books of Moses, and especially from this: A Prophet shall the Lord God raise up unto you; and from many other passages.[^842]

And as the conversation was ending, the disciples also come, and they marvel exceedingly at His condescension, how He was speaking with a woman. Nevertheless they entreat Him to eat, both because He had toiled and because of the heat of the season. But He speaks unto them of eternal food, that is the salvation of mankind, and that they ought to reap the labors of the Prophets.[^843]

And when the woman reached the city and related what had befallen her, all were stirred up and came unto Christ, having believed; for the woman would not have put herself to shame unless she had understood something great. And having entreatingly besought Him to abide with them, they persuade Him to stay for two days. And having tarried, He wrought very many miracles, which indeed by reason of their multitude were not written in the Gospel.[^844]

And this is that Samaritan woman, who was afterward named Photina by Christ, who also was crowned with the wreath of martyrdom under Nero Caesar, together with her seven sons, after much dragging about, scraping, cutting off of breasts, crushing of hands, inserting of sharp reeds under fingernails, pouring of molten lead, and countless other torments, and at the end was tied to two trees and torn asunder.[^845]

Be it known also that the well-curb of that well, that is, its mouth, Emperor Justinian honorably brought thence, and placed it in the Great Church of the Word of God, Hagia Sophia; and the stone also whereon Christ sat as He conversed with the Samaritan woman; and both abide unto this day before the narthex from the east, on the left side to those entering the temple, healing every disease, and especially those who happen to suffer from burning fevers and are tossed with the ague, and they cleanse and drive away enchantments that are wrought.[^846]

By the intercessions of Thy martyr Photina, O Christ God, have mercy on us. Amen.

He Who is borne upon the Cherubim conversed with a harlot woman, asking water; He Who suspended the earth upon the waters, seeking water; He Who pours out fountains and lakes of waters, desiring truly to draw her who was ensnared by the adversary enemy, and to give her to drink of living water, who was grievously burned in hidden sins, as the only Compassionate One and Lover of mankind.[^1095]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion, Tone 4:*  
David, the Ancestor of God and Prophet, through song proclaimed beforehand concerning thee unto Him Who hath done great things for thee: At Thy right hand stood the Queen. For God, Who was pleased without father to become man from thee, showed thee forth as Mother, the mediatress of life: that He might renew His own image, corrupted by passions, and having found the sheep gone astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and by His own will unite it with the heavenly powers, and that Christ, Who hath great and rich mercy, might save the world, O Theotokos![^1096]

*At the Aposticha, Resurrection stichera of the Octoechos.*  
*Tone 4:*  
Bowing down unceasingly before Thy life-creating Cross, O Christ God, we glorify Thy third-day Resurrection: for thereby hast Thou renewed human nature which had fallen into corruption, O Almighty One, and renewed for us the ascent unto heaven, as the only Good One and Lover of mankind.[^1097]

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is clothed with majesty.*[^1098]  
The condemnation of the tree of disobedience hast Thou loosed, O Savior, being nailed of Thine own will unto the Tree of the Cross, and having descended into hades, O Mighty One, Thou didst tear asunder the bonds of death as God: therefore do we worship Thy Resurrection from the dead, crying aloud with joy: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee![^1099]

*Verse: For He hath established the world, which shall not be moved.*[^1100]  
The gates of hades hast Thou crushed, O Lord, and by Thy death Thou hast destroyed the kingdom of death, and delivered the human race from corruption, granting life and incorruption unto the world, and great mercy.[^1101]

*Verse: Holiness befits Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days.*[^1102]  
O Lord, having ascended the Cross, Thou hast abolished our ancestral curse, and having descended into hades, Thou hast freed the prisoners from the ages, granting incorruption unto the human race: therefore with hymns we glorify Thy life-creating and saving Rising.[^1103]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 8:*  
When Thou didst appear in the flesh, O Christ God, for Thine ineffable dispensation, the Samaritan woman, hearing the word of Thee the Lover of mankind, left her bucket at the well, and ran, saying unto those in the city: Come, see the Knower of hearts; can this be the expected Christ, Who hath great mercy?[^1104]

## ON SATURDAY OF THE SAMARITAN WOMAN, AT MATINS

*Resurrection Troparion of the Tone.*[^1105]

*After the First Stichology, Resurrection Sessional Hymn.*  
*Tone 4:*  
Gazing upon the entrance of the tomb, and not enduring the fiery brilliance of the Angel, the Myrrh-bearing women marveled with trembling, saying: Was He stolen away Who opened Paradise unto the thief? Or is He risen Who even before the Passion proclaimed the Rising? Truly Christ is risen, granting life and resurrection unto those in hades!  
*Twice.*[^1106]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*  
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto Angels, through thee, O Theotokos, hath been revealed unto those on earth: God incarnate in unconfused union, and of His own will accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-formed man, He hath saved our souls from death.[^1107]

*After the Second Stichology, Sessional Hymn.*  
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йсѧ на крⷭ҇тъ):*  
Let heaven rejoice, let earthly things be glad, for Christ hath appeared from the Virgin as man, and hath delivered all mankind from corruption by His death; shining forth with wonders unto the Samaritan woman asking water, He grants the fountain of healings, as the only Immortal One.[^1108]  
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*[^1109]

*Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ... Psalm 50.*[^1110]

*Canon of the Samaritan Woman, to 6; and of the holy patron of the monastery, to 4; and of the Menaion to 4.*[^1111]

*Kontakion and Exapostilarion of the Samaritan Woman.*[^1112]

*At the Praises, we set 4 verses, and we sing the appointed Resurrection stichera.*[^1113]

*Tone 4:*  
Thou Who didst endure the Cross and death, and didst rise from the dead, O Almighty Lord, we glorify Thy Resurrection.[^1114]

By Thy Cross, O Christ, Thou hast set us free from the ancient curse; and by Thy death Thou hast abolished the devil who tormented our nature; and by Thy Rising Thou hast filled all things with joy: therefore do we cry unto Thee: O Lord Who didst rise from the dead, glory to Thee![^1115]

By Thy Cross, O Christ Savior, guide us unto Thy truth, and deliver us from the snares of the enemy: O Thou Who didst rise from the dead, raise us up who have fallen into sin, stretching forth Thy hand, O Lord Lover of mankind, by the prayers of Thy saints.[^1116]

Without parting from the Father’s bosom, O only-begotten Word of God, Thou didst come down to earth for love of mankind, becoming man without undergoing change; and Thou didst endure the Cross and death in the flesh, being impassible in Thy Godhead: and having risen from the dead, Thou hast granted immortality unto the human race, as the only Almighty One.[^1117]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 6:*  
Jesus our Savior, the Fountain of the principle of life, coming unto the fountain of the Patriarch Jacob, asked water to drink from the Samaritan woman: and when she objected that Jews have no dealings with them, the wise Creator led her by sweet words rather unto asking for ever-flowing water; which having received, she proclaimed unto all, saying: Come, see the Knower of secrets and God, Who came in the flesh to save man![^1118]

*At the Aposticha, stichera.*  
*Tone 4, Podoben: "As one valiant among martyrs" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*  
Beholding the ever-flowing Fountain sitting at the well, the woman enduring the heat of exceedingly many passions asked to receive the healing water of life poured forth from Him as a torrent, which also she received freely from the Word, and no longer hastens unto an earthly and corruptible well.[^1119]

*Verse: Bend Thy bow, and proceed prosperously, and reign, for the sake of truth, and meekness, and righteousness.*[^1120]  
While the woman pursued Jewish customs and brought forward her own non-communion, Christ the wise Creator, accustomed to communion of water, by sweet utterances led her to ask for this divine living-flowing water, which having drunk, she came into the city, crying aloud as she announced it.[^1121]

*Verse: Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity.*[^1122]  
Thou hast no drawing-vessel, and the well is very deep; whence then dost Thou give me incorruptible water? The Samaritan woman cried unto Thee, O Christ, supposing Thee to be a man and not God, and marveling at Thy words; whom having given to drink with sweetest sayings, Thou didst cause to confess Thee as the God of all creation.[^1123]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 6:*  
Thus saith the Lord unto the Samaritan woman: If thou hadst known the gift of God, and Who He is that saith unto thee: Give Me water to drink, thou wouldst have asked of Him, and He would have given thee to drink, that thou mightest never thirst unto ages, saith the Lord.[^1124]

*On Saturday of the Samaritan Woman, the Four-Ode Canon, whose acrostic is: ᾌδω μέλος τέταρτον, that is: I sing the fourth song.*[^1125]

*Tone 4, Ode 6. Heirmos:*  
I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries unto Thee, having been cleansed from the blood of demons by the blood that flowed through mercy from Thy side.[^1126]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter A]:*  
Having stretched forth Thine immaculate palms upon the Cross, Thou didst crucify the spiritual Amalek by Thy divine power, and didst deliver the world from bondage, O Christ our God.[^1127]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter D]:*  
Having shattered hades by Thy dread descent, O Christ, and having snatched by divine power the prisoners whom it held, Thou didst rise as God from the tomb on the third day.[^1128]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
*Troparion [Acrostic letter O]:*  
When the women came unto the tomb to anoint Thee, the priceless Myrrh, an Angel cried unto them: Weep not; He Whom ye seek, the Lord is risen![^1129]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion [Acrostic letter M]:*  
No longer weep, O Virgin Mother of God, for thy Son is risen as God from the tomb on the third day: but rejoice, and entreat Him that our souls may be saved.[^1130]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*  
Thou Who didst save Thy children of Abraham in the fire and didst slay the Chaldeans whom justice righteously snared, O all-hymned Lord, the God of our fathers, blessed art Thou![^1131]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter E]:*  
At Golgotha for our sake, being suspended upon the Cross between two thieves, Thou didst abolish on it the powers of darkness, O Almighty Lord, the God of our fathers, Who art blessed![^1132]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter L]:*  
Being pierced in the side with a spear, O Giver of life, Thou didst pour forth unto us the ever-flowing fountain of Thy divine blood, O Christ, unto them that cry: O all-hymned Lord, God of our fathers, blessed art Thou![^1133]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
*Troparion [Acrostic letter O]:*  
The Angel, radiant in apparel, cried unto the mourning women: Why do ye seek with sweet fragrances as dead Christ Who hath authority over life? He is risen, even as He said before the Passion![^1134]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion [Acrostic letter S]:*  
He Who upholds all creation and the ends of the earth took flesh from thee, O Pure One, and was pleased to die for our sake: Whom beholding risen in glory from the tomb, rejoice thou![^1135]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*  
Daniel, stretching forth his hands, shut the open mouths of the lions in the pit; and the young men, lovers of piety, girt with virtue, quenched the power of the fire, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord![^1136]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter T]:*  
By Thy wound, O Master, Thou hast healed all mankind, O Savior, and having endured death, Thou hast freed it from corruption by Thy death, whereby Thou hast also immortalized us who cry aloud: Hymn the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him above all for ever![^1137]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter E]:*  
Rejoice, all ye peoples; let all earthly things and heavenly things now be glad: for the Lord, the Giver of life, is risen from the tomb, raising up our nature together with Himself, as it cries: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, hymn and exalt Him above all for ever![^1138]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
*Troparion [Acrostic letter T]:*  
Having bound the tormentor by Thy descent, O Christ, being laid in the tomb Thou didst raise up all the dead, who thankfully hymn Thee, the Benefactor of all and Almighty: and who cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, hymn and exalt Him above all for ever![^1139]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion [Acrostic letter A]:*  
Desiring to loose the misfortune of Adam fallen in Eden, O Almighty Lord, Thou didst become incarnate of thee, O All-immaculate One, and wast crucified and buried, and didst rise, saving us who hymn thee with love, O Mary Bride of God.[^1140]

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*  
Eve indeed, through the disease of disobedience, brought in the curse: but thou, O Virgin Theotokos, through the blossoming of thy pregnancy, didst blossom forth blessing unto the world: therefore do we all magnify thee.[^1141]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter R]:*  
By Thine elevation upon the Cross, O Long-suffering One, the veil was rent, and the sun hid its rays, and the rocks splitting asunder showed forth the unjust slaying which Thou didst endure of Thine own will, O Savior, for the human race.[^1142]

*Troparion [Acrostic letter T]:*  
Being pierced with a spear upon the Tree, O Jesus Lover of mankind, Thou didst heal Adam who was wounded by transgression in Paradise, O Long-suffering One: and being laid among the dead, as Immortal, Thou didst lead forth all that hymn Thee.[^1143]

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*  
*Troparion [Acrostic letter O]:*  
Holding all things in Thy palm, and ordering the universe beneath heaven by Thy nod, how wast Thou numbered among the dead, being Immortal, desiring to deliver those wounded by the serpent’s bite? Thee do we hymn as the Lover of mankind.[^1144]

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion [Acrostic letter N]:*  
Beholding Thy Son dead and naked hanging upon the Tree, thou didst cry aloud: but straightway seeing Him risen on the third day, thou didst rejoice, O All-immaculate One; Whom as Mother entreat for those that honor thee with faith.[^1145]
