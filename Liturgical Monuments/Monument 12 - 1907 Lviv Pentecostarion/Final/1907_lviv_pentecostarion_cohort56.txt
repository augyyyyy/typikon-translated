*Synaxarion for the Sunday of Holy Pentecost.*[^2353]  
*Verses:*  
With violent breath Christ imparts in tongues of fire  
The Divine Spirit unto the Apostles.  
The Spirit was poured out in a great day upon the fishermen.  

On this day, on the seventh Sunday after Pascha, we celebrate Holy Pentecost, and this also we received from the Hebrew books.[^2354] For even as they celebrate the Pentecost among them, honoring the number seven, and because, fifty days having passed after the Passover, they received the Law: so also we, celebrating fifty days after Pascha, receive the All-Holy Spirit, Who gives the Law, and guides unto all truth, and commands what is pleasing unto God. It should be known that among the Hebrews there were three feasts: the Passover, Pentecost, and Scenopegia, that is, the Feast of Tabernacles.[^2355] The Passover indeed they celebrated in remembrance of the crossing of the Red Sea: for Passover is interpreted as "passing over." And that feast pointed to our passing over and return from dark sin back unto Paradise. Pentecost they celebrated in remembrance of their affliction in the wilderness, and how through many sorrows they were led into the promised land: for then also they enjoyed the fruit of wheat and wine. And this feast pointed to our entering into the Church from unbelief: for then also we partake of the Master's Body and Blood. Some indeed say that Pentecost is celebrated among the Hebrews for this cause; but others say it was in honor of the fifty days during which Moses fasted and received the God-written Law, together remembering the slaughter of the calf and the other things that took place when Moses went up into the mountain and came down. Others considered Pentecost to have been devised among the Hebrews for the sake of the honor of the number seven, as was said: for that number, multiplied by itself, makes fifty, lacking one day. The honor of the number seven among the Hebrews is not only in days, but also extends unto years, from which years they also observe what among them is called the Jubilee, which means remission. For when the years are septupled, this takes place: when they leave the land unsown, and give rest unto the beasts, and command purchased slaves to go free.[^2356] The third feast, Scenopegia, that is, the Feast of Tabernacles, celebrated after the gathering of the fruits, which is five months after the feast of Passover: this was celebrated in memory of the day on which Moses first pitched the tabernacle seen by him in a cloud on Mount Sinai and prepared by Bezaleel, the chief craftsman; for making booths, they celebrated this feast, dwelling in the fields and thanking God as they gathered the fruits of their labors. Unto this feast David seems also to have inscribed the Psalms "Concerning the Winepresses." And this was an image of our Resurrection from the dead, when, our bodily tabernacles having been dissolved and again reconstructed, we shall receive the fruit of our labors, keeping festival in the eternal tabernacles.[^2357] It should also be known that on this day, as Pentecost was being celebrated, the Holy Spirit came upon the Disciples. The Holy Fathers themselves resolved to divide the feasts, on account of the majesty of the All-Holy and Life-Creating Spirit, forasmuch as He is One of the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity. This also we shall tell tomorrow: how the Holy Spirit came.[^2358]

For seasonable weather, abundance of the fruits of the earth, and peaceful times, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For travelers by water and land, the sick, the suffering, those in captivity, and for their salvation, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For the people standing here and awaiting the grace of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.[^2413]  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For those who have bowed their hearts and their knees before the Lord, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For the sanctification of the souls and bodies of all of us who have righteously with faith and piety gathered together into this most honorable temple, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may be accounted worthy of the imperishable Kingdom by the grace of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may be a temple and dwelling-place of the Holy and Life-Creating Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may preserve our faith and unashamed love by the grace of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may find grace and mercy of the All-Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For the revelation, so that we may be partakers of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may receive the remission of sins and the knowledge of spiritual grace, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may not be alien from the Holy Spirit at the dread and righteous judgment-seat of Christ, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For the granting of rich mercy and compassions and the grace of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That the Holy Spirit may loose us from every bond of the adversary, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For the salvation from on high of us who have bowed our hearts' and bodies' knees in faith on this fiftieth day, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That the Lord our God will hear us sinners, and send down upon us the Holy Spirit, and sanctify us, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may ever worship in soul and body, and hymn the Holy Consubstantial Trinity in Unity, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That the Lord God will be well pleased toward us who have bowed our knees, and send forth upon us His Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That our kneeling and our prayers before Him may be acceptable, as the incense of a spiritual fragrance, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
That we may be communicants of the radiance of the Divine, True, and Life-Creating Trinity, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
For our deliverance from all affliction, wrath, and necessity, let us pray to the Lord.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
Help us, save us, have mercy on us, and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.  
*Choir:* Lord, have mercy.  
Commemorating our most holy, most pure, most blessed and glorious Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary, with all the saints, let us commend ourselves and one another, and all our life unto Christ our God.[^2414]  
*Choir:* To Thee, O Lord.
