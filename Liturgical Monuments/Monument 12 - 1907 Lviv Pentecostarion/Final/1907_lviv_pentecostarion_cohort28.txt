## ON THURSDAY OF THOMAS WEEK, AT MATINS

*At "God is the Lord", the Troparion of the feast, thrice.*
*After the 1st stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1. Podoben: "When the stone had been sealed" (Ка́мени запеча́танꙋ):*

Thou wast nailed to the Cross, O Life of all, and wast numbered among the dead, O immortal Lord; Thou didst rise on the third day, O Savior, raising Adam up with Thyself from corruption; therefore the heavenly hosts cried aloud unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy divine Passion, O Christ! Glory to Thy Resurrection! Glory to Thy condescension, O Thou Who alone lovest mankind!

*Verse: Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world. (Ps. 18:5a [19:4a])*

O most wise fishers of the inhabited earth, having received mercy from God, pray even now for us who cry aloud: O Lord, save the Emperor,[^480] and Thy city, and from surrounding afflictions deliver our souls!

*Verse: God is marvelous in His saints, the God of Israel. (Ps. 67:36a [68:35a])*
*Tone 1, Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й Сп҃се): For the Martyrs:*[^481]

All we entreat Christ's martyrs, for they make intercession for our salvation, and let us all draw near unto them in faith; they pour forth the grace of healings, they drive away demonic hosts, as guardians of the faith.

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*

The prophets clearly proclaimed thee beforehand, O Maiden, to be the Mother of God, and the divine Apostles proclaimed it in the world, and we have believed; therefore all we, honoring thee with pious mind, hymn thee, and truly ever name thee the Mother of God.

*After the 2nd stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1. Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й Сп҃се):*

Beholding My ribs, do not disbelieve Me, O impetuous one,[^482] cried the Word unto Thomas who disbelieved: touch Me with thy finger, and examine with thy hand; and having recognized the marks of My wounds, proclaim unto the world My life-bearing Resurrection from the tomb!

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. The same.*
*Having seen the Resurrection of Christ... Psalm 50.*
*Canon of the feast, with the heirmoi to 8, and of the Menaion to 4.*
*After Ode 3, Sessional Hymn of the Menaion. Kontakion and Exapostilarion of the feast.*

*At the Praises, stichera to 4. Tone 1. Resurrectional:*

Glorifying Thy divinely fitting condescension, we hymn Thee, O Christ: Thou wast born of the Virgin, yet wast not separated from the Father; Thou didst suffer as man, and of Thine own will didst endure the Cross; Thou didst rise from the tomb as from a bridal chamber, that Thou mightest save the world: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Twice.*

Peter and Paul let us praise in unison, Luke, Matthew, Mark, John, Andrew, Thomas, Bartholomew, Simon the Cananaean, James, Philip: and the entire assembly of the disciples let us worthily extol!

*For the Martyrs:*

Rejoice in the Lord, O martyrs, for ye fought the good fight: ye resisted emperors, and vanquished tyrants; ye feared neither fire nor sword, nor wild beasts devouring your bodies; sending up song unto Christ with the Angels, from Whom ye received crowns from heaven, pray that peace be granted unto the world, and unto our souls great mercy!

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 8:*

O most marvelous wonder! John reclined upon the breast of the Word, and Thomas was counted worthy to touch the ribs; but the one drew forth dreadly from thence the profound mystery of theology, while the other was counted worthy to initiate us into mystery, presenting clear proof of His Rising, crying aloud: My Lord and my God, glory to Thee![^483]

*At the Aposticha, stichera.*
*Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме Еѵ҆фра́ѳовъ):*[^484]

Let the sorrow of passions and the tempest of thoughts be driven far away, and so let the springtime of faith blossom forth!

*Verse: Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world. (Ps. 18:5a [19:4a])*

Thou appearedst, O Savior, unto Thine holy Apostles when the doors were shut, renewing through them the Holy Spirit for us this day.

*Verse: The heavens declare the glory of God, and the firmament proclaims the work of His hands. (Ps. 18:2a [19:1a])*

Now beholding Thee, O King of all, not with eyes of flesh, but having believed in God with heartfelt love, with songs we magnify Thee.

*Glory... Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 6:*

After eight days from Thy Resurrection, O Jesus the King, Only-begotten Word of the Father, Thou appearedst unto Thy disciples when the doors were shut, granting Thy peace, and unto the disbelieving disciple Thou didst show the tokens: Come, and touch My hands and feet, and My incorruptible ribs; and he, having believed, cried aloud unto Thee: My Lord and my God, glory to Thee!

*First Hour, and the Dismissal.*
