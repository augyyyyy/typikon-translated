Thou didst raise Lazarus by Thy divine word, O Christ; and raise me also, I pray Thee, who am dead through many transgressions.

*Another. Heirmos: Of the celestial vault:*

*Troparion:*
Standing before the tomb, O wonder-working Lord, in Bethany Thou didst shed tears over Lazarus according to the law of nature, confirming Thy flesh, O Jesus my God, which Thou didst take upon Thee.

*Troparion:*
Thou didst straightway assuage the weeping of Mary, O Savior, and didst show Thy sovereign power unto Martha; for Thou art the Resurrection, Thou art also Life, even as Thou saidst: for Thou art the Truth, and Lord of all.

*Glory...*
*Troparion:*
Bound with swaddling bands, Thy beloved Lazarus, O Lord, Thou didst snatch from dark Hades by Thy voice all-powerful, shattering the bars and realms of death.

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.*
*Theotokion:*
Thou didst dwell bodily in the Virgin, O Lord, and appearedst unto men as was fitting for Thee to be seen; and her hast Thou revealed as the true Mother of God and helper of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.

*Katavasia:*
Supreme Creator of the celestial vault, O Lord, and Builder of the Church: do Thou establish me in Thy love, O Summit of desire, Confirmation of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.
