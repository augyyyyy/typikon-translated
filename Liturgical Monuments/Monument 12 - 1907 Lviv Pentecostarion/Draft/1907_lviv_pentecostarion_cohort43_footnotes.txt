[^1106]: Sessional Hymn following the First Stichology, Tone 4 (Resurrectional), chanted twice. Physical Leaf p430 (printed fol. 424v). The holy Myrrh-bearing women trembling at the tomb entrance before the dazzling brilliance of the Angel, marveling whether the Lord Who opened Paradise unto the thief had risen from the dead.

[^1107]: Sessional Hymn following the First Stichology, Tone 4: Theotokion (*Бг҃оро́диченъ*). Physical Leaf p430 (printed fol. 424v). Confessing the mystery hidden from eternity and unknown even to angelic orders: God Incarnate in unconfused union, willingly submitting to the Cross to redeem Adam and save mankind from death.

[^1108]: Sessional Hymn following the Second Stichology, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йсѧ на крⷭ҇тъ). Physical Leaf p430 (printed fol. 424v). Cosmic rejoicing at Christ's nativity from the Virgin and His destruction of corruption through His death, shining with miracles unto the Samaritan woman and bestowing the fountain of healing.

[^1109]: Sessional Hymn following the Second Stichology: Doxology rubric indicating the repetition of the Sessional Hymn after "Glory... both now...". Physical Leaf p430 (printed fol. 424v).
