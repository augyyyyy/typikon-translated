## ON HOLY AND GREAT SATURDAY EVENING

*At the tenth hour of the day, the klepalo is struck for Vespers, and the Priest vests in his vestments; and when he hath given the blessing, we begin the Initial Psalm; thereafter, the Great Litany.[^346]*

*Then, "Lord, I have cried," in Tone 1. [In monasteries,] all the priests and deacons, when the chanter reaches the verse: "They shall fall into their own net," make a prostration, go forth, vest themselves also in their vestments, and perform the Proskomedia.[^347]*

*And we set 8 stichoi. And we sing 3 Resurrection Stichera of the Oktoechos, and 1 from the Aposticha.*

*Resurrection Stichera, Tone 1:*

*Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins, for Thou alone art He Who hath manifested resurrection unto the world.*

*Encompass Zion, ye people, and surround her, and give glory within her unto Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered us from our iniquities.*

*Come, ye people, let us sing and worship Christ, glorifying His resurrection from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered the world from the deceit of the enemy.*

*By Thy Passion, O Christ, we have been set free from passions, and by Thy Resurrection we have been delivered from corruption: O Lord, glory to Thee!*

*Other Idiomelon Stichera of Great Saturday, three, repeating one:*

*Tone 8:*

*Today hades groaning cries aloud: "It had been better for me if I had not received Him Who was born of Mary; for coming upon me, He hath dissolved my dominion, and hath broken the bronze gates; the souls which I held before, being God, He hath raised up. Glory, O Lord, to Thy Cross, and to Thy Resurrection!"*

=== LEAF p182 ===

*Today hades groaning cries aloud: "My power hath been dissolved; I received a Dead Man as one of the dead, yet Him I cannot hold at all, but with Him I lose those over whom I reigned; I had dead ones from the ages, but behold, He raises up all! Glory, O Lord, to Thy Cross, and to Thy Resurrection!"*

*Today hades groaning cries aloud: "My dominion hath been swallowed up; the Shepherd was crucified, and hath raised Adam; those over whom I reigned, I have lost; and whom I swallowed in my might, I have disgorged all. The Crucified One hath emptied the tombs; the power of death hath grown weak. Glory, O Lord, to Thy Cross, and to Thy Resurrection!"*

*Glory, Tone 6:*

*This present day Moses mystically prefigured, saying: "And God blessed the seventh day." For this is the blessed Sabbath; this is the day of rest, whereon the Only-begotten Son of God rested from all His works, keeping the Sabbath in the Flesh through the dispensation unto death; and returning again by the Resurrection unto that which He was, He hath granted us eternal life, as He alone is good and the Lover of mankind.[^348]*

*Both now, Theotokion (Dogmatikon), Tone 1:*

*Let us hymn Mary the Virgin, the universal Glory who sprouted forth from mankind and gave birth unto the Master, the Heavenly Gate, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful; for she was shown forth as Heaven and the Temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace and opened the Kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the confirmation of our faith, we have as our champion the Lord Who was born of her. Take courage, therefore, take courage, O people of God, for He will defeat the enemies, as He is almighty!*

*The Fourth Reading: from the Prophecy of Jonah (Chapter 1 [Jonah 1:1 - 2:10]):[^352]*

The word of the Lord came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying: "Arise, and go to Nineveh, that great city, and preach in it, for the cry of its wickedness is come up before Me." And Jonah arose to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the Lord; and the prophet went down to Joppa, and found a ship going to Tarshish; and he paid his fare, and went into it, to sail with them unto Tarshish from the presence of the Lord. But the Lord raised up a great wind upon the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was in danger of being broken. And the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his god, and cast forth the vessels that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten it of them. But Jonah was gone down into the hold of the ship, and lay there fast asleep, and was snoring. And the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him: "Why snorest thou? Arise, and call upon thy God, if so be that God will save us, that we perish not!" And they said every one to his fellow: "Come, let us cast lots, that we may know for whose sake this evil is upon us!" And they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah. And they said unto him: "Tell us, for whose sake is this evil upon us? What is thine occupation? and whence comest thou? Whither goest thou, and of what country, and of what people art thou?" And he said unto them: "I am a servant of the Lord, and I worship the Lord God of heaven, Who hath made the sea and the dry land." And the men were exceedingly afraid, and said unto him: "Why hast thou done this?" For the men knew that he fled from the presence of the Lord, because he had told them. And they said unto him: "What shall we do unto thee, that the sea may be calm unto us?" For the sea surged and lifted up its waves yet more. And Jonah said unto them: "Take me up, and cast me into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you." Nevertheless the men strove hard to bring the ship back to the land; but they could not, for the sea surged and was tempestuous against them. And they cried unto the Lord, and said: "By no means, O Lord, let us not perish for the sake of this man's life, and lay not upon us innocent blood; for Thou, O Lord, hast done as it pleased Thee!" And they took up Jonah, and cast him into the sea; and the sea ceased from its raging. And the men feared the Lord with a great fear, and offered a sacrifice unto the Lord, and made vows. And the Lord commanded a great whale to swallow up Jonah; and Jonah was in the belly of the whale three days and three nights. And Jonah the prophet prayed unto the Lord his God out of the whale's belly, and said:

"I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the Lord my God, and He heard me; out of the belly of hades my cry: Thou heardest my voice! For Thou didst cast me into the depths of the heart of the sea, and the floods compassed me about; all Thy billows and Thy waves passed over me. And I said: I am cast out of Thy sight; yet shall I look again toward Thy holy temple? The waters poured around me, even to my soul; the lowest deep compassed me about, my head was plunged into the clefts of the mountains. I went down into the earth whose bars are eternal barriers; yet let my life come up from corruption unto Thee, O Lord my God! When my soul was failing from me, I remembered the Lord; and let my prayer come unto Thee, into Thy holy temple. They that observe vanity and lies have forsaken their own mercy. But I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise and thanksgiving; as much as I have vowed, I will pay unto Thee for my salvation unto the Lord!"

*(Chapter 3)*

"Arise, and go unto Nineveh, that great city, and preach in it according to the former preaching which I spoke unto thee." And Jonah arose, and went unto Nineveh, according to the word of the Lord. Now Nineveh was an exceeding great city unto God, of about three days' journey. And Jonah began to enter into the city, about a day's journey, and he preached and said: "Yet three days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown!" And the men of Nineveh believed God, and proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them even to the least of them. And the word reached the king of Nineveh, and he arose from his throne, and laid aside his robe from him, and clothed himself with sackcloth, and sat in ashes. And it was proclaimed and published in Nineveh by the decree of the king and his nobles, saying: "Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste any thing: let them not feed, nor drink water! But let man and beast be clothed with sackcloth, and cry mightily unto God; and let them turn every one from his evil way, and from the injustice that is in their hands, saying: Who knows if God will turn and repent, and turn away from the fierceness of His anger, that we perish not?" And God saw their works, that they turned from their evil ways; and God repented of the evil which He had said that He would do unto them; and He did it not.

*(Chapter 4)*

And Jonah was grieved with great grief, and was confounded. And he prayed unto the Lord, and said: "O Lord, were not these my words when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish, for I knew that Thou art a gracious God, and merciful, longsuffering, and of great kindness, and repentest Thee of the evils of men. And now, O Master, Lord, take, I pray Thee, my life from me; for it is better for me to die than to live." And the Lord said unto Jonah: "Art thou greatly grieved?" And Jonah went out of the city, and sat over against the city, and made him a booth there, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city. And the Lord God commanded a gourd, and it grew up over the head of Jonah, to be a shadow over his head, to shade him from his griefs. And Jonah was exceeding glad of the gourd. But the Lord God commanded a worm early on the morrow, and it smote the gourd, that it withered. And it came to pass, when the sun did rise, that God commanded a vehement scorching wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, and he fainted, and renounced his soul, and said: "It is better for me to die than to live." And the Lord God said unto Jonah: "Art thou greatly grieved for the gourd?" And Jonah said: "I am greatly grieved, even unto death." And the Lord said: "Thou hast had pity on the gourd, for which thou hast not labored, neither madest it grow; which sprang up in a night, and perished in a night. And should not I spare Nineveh, that great city, wherein dwell more than sixscore thousand persons that cannot discern between their right hand and their left hand; and also much cattle?"

Let my soul rejoice in the Lord: for He hath clothed me with the robe of salvation, and with the garment of gladness hath He covered me; as a bridegroom hath He put a crown upon me, and as a bride hath He adorned me with beauty. For as the earth that causes her bud to spring forth, and as a garden causes the things that are sown in it to spring forth: so the Lord God will cause righteousness and praise to spring forth before all the nations. For Zion's sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem's sake I will not rest, until my righteousness go forth as light, and my salvation as a burning lamp. Thou shalt also be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a royal diadem in the hand of thy God.
