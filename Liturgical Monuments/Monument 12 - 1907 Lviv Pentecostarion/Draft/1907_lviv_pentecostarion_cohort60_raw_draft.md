Enkindled by the fire of the Lord's love, they despised fire, and ignited like divine coals in Christ, the honorable Martyrs burned up the stealthy insolence of deceit; they muzzled the mouths of wild beasts by honorable invocation, and being beheaded, they cut down all the battle arrays of the enemy, and patiently pouring out streams of blood, they watered the Church that is enlightened by faith.
