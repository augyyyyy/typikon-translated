[^2900]: Rubrical decree citing the Provincial Council of Lviv (1720): The Church Slavonic text explicitly references *Собо́ръ Львóвскїй бы́вшїй въ лѣ́то ҂аѱч҃а* (The Council of Lviv held in the year 1720), which designates the landmark Provincial Synod of Zamość (1720) celebrated under Metropolitan Leo Kiška and confirmed for the Metropolitan province of Kyiv, Halych, and All Rus' centered in Lviv.
