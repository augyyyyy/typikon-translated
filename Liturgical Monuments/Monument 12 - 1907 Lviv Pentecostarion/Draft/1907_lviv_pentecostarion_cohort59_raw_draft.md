## ON TUESDAY AFTER PENTECOST, AT MATINS[^2626]

*After the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й):*[^2627]  
I number together, I sing together, I glorify together, and I honor together with the Father and the Son the all-holy Spirit, uniting [Them] in Godhead, yet dividing [Them] in personal properties; for having now clothed Christ's Apostles, He made them most wise to preach in all the world.  
*Glory, both now: the same.*  
*After the 2nd Stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*[^2628]  
The grace of the Spirit, having dreadfully descended from heaven in the form of fire, enlightened Christ's Disciples and showed them forth as luminaries, proclaiming the one power and one Lordship of the Holy Trinity, Which we glorify by faith.  
*Glory, both now: the same.*  
*Psalm 50. Canon of the feast, Tone 4, with the heirmos to 8, and of the Menaion to 4.*[^2629]  
*Kontakion and Exapostilarion of the feast.*  
*At the Aposticha, stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме Є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^2630]  
Having enlightened their mind with the God-originating radiance, Thy Disciples, O Word, essentially received the Holy Spirit.  
*Verse: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me (Ps. 50:12 LXX).*  
Tongues with fire, seen from on high, came down upon the Apostles, being divided, enlightening, not consuming.[^2631]  
*Verse: Cast me not away from Thy face, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me (Ps. 50:13 LXX).*  
Having come to us from on high, O Comforter, as formerly unto the Apostles, sanctify and save those who proclaim Thee as God.  
*Glory, both now, Tone 1:*  
The Holy Spirit provides all things: He pours forth prophecies, He perfects priests, He taught wisdom to the unlearned, He revealed fishermen as theologians, He gathers the whole assembly of the Church. O Comforter, consubstantial and co-enthroned with the Father and the Son, glory to Thee![^2632]

Another Comforter, Christ our Comforter, sent down unto us by grace, even the Spirit Who spake in the Prophets and Who spoke through fiery tongues of the God-proclaiming and all-wise Apostles.[^2636]

*Troparion:*  
He that sanctifies the angelic choirs and pilots their ranks as God, He hath now come, having descended from heaven, and having made the all-wise Apostles most wise with divine lightnings, He sent them forth to the inhabited earth.  
*Troparion:*  
The Holy Spirit manifestly distributes the wealth of the Godhead, abiding and untakeable, unto the Apostles in fiery tongues, and He made them stronger than fire against deceit, burning up the mire of idols.  
*Glory:*  
O Spirit of truth and understanding, Spirit of wisdom, of might, and of counsel, Who proceeds from the Father and, for the sake of the Son, is richly given unto creation, save us who by faith worship Thy dominion.[^2638]  
*Both now, Theotokion:*  
O Virgin blessed of God, all my hope do I earnestly set upon thee: save me, O Mother of Life itself, and vouchsafe me, O Pure One, to be satisfied with eternal sustenance, that in faith and love I may magnify thee in hymns.

## ON TUESDAY AFTER PENTECOST, AT VESPERS[^2639]

*At "Lord, I have cried," we set 6 verses, and sing prosomoia stichera: Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Нбⷭ҇ныхъ чинѡ́въ):*[^2640]  
As innate, as proper to rational beings, [was it given] to the dumb with fire-formed tongues to proclaim unto the nations the glory of God, Who adorned the heavens with fire and illuminated the sensible world, together with the Son and the Spirit.  
Unto Christ's Disciples gathered in Zion, as was the promise, the Holy Spirit, having come upon them in fiery form, showed them forth as fire-inspired, speaking with mystical utterances, eloquently declaring the doctrines of the Trinity.  
Unforged swords, the sacred Disciples, the Holy Spirit having come perfected; and through them they purified the earth for God the Creator, burning up the unsanctified, so that the weapons of the evil one might vanish and our souls be saved.[^2641]  
*And 3 from the Menaion.*  
*Glory, both now, Tone 2:*  
We have seen the true Light, we have received the heavenly Spirit, we have found the true faith, worshipping the undivided Trinity; for She hath saved us.[^2642]  
*At the Aposticha, prosomoia stichera, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*[^2643]  
Today the operation of Thy most Holy Spirit came upon Thine Apostles, O Lord, and having shown them forth as all-wise through knowledge of God, He filled them with Thy blessed teaching; therefore we glorify Thy saving dispensation, O Jesus almighty, Savior of our souls.  
*Verse: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me (Ps. 50:12 LXX).*  
Today Thy Spirit all-powerful, O Master, is sent from the Father, being consubstantial with Thee, distributed in fiery tongues, Who disposed Thine Apostles to speak Thy marvelous works; therefore we glorify Thy saving dispensation, O Jesus almighty, Savior of our souls.  
*Verse: Cast me not away from Thy face, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me (Ps. 50:13 LXX).*  
Today Thou didst pour out, O Savior, the gifts of Thy Comforter Spirit, granting unto human nature to prophesy, even as Thou saidst, O Word, and Thou didst declare that we should worship the Trinity indivisibly; therefore we glorify Thy saving dispensation, O Jesus almighty, Savior of our souls.[^2644]

*Glory, both now, Tone 6:*  
O Lord, the descent of the Holy Spirit, which filled Thine Apostles, disposed them to speak in other tongues; therefore this most glorious event seemed drunkenness to the unbelieving, but to the faithful an intercession of salvation. Vouchsafe us also His illumination, we pray Thee, O Lover of mankind.[^2645]  
*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." Troparion of the feast, and the Dismissal.*  

## ON WEDNESDAY AFTER PENTECOST, AT MATINS[^2646]

*At "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast, thrice.*  
*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5, Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́льное Сло́во):*[^2647]  
The spiritual grace having come, from poor fishermen Christ enlightened Thine Apostles as all-wise and princes, by whose teaching we also ever celebrate the bright feast of Pentecost, praising Thee together with the Father and the Divine Spirit.  
*Glory, both now: the same.*  
*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and Word of God" (Премꙋ́дрости):*[^2648]  
The all-holy Spirit having now descended upon the Apostles in the appearance of fire, filled the assemblies of the nations with awe; for when they spake with fiery tongues, O Lover of mankind, each heard his own language; therefore the marvel was recognized by the unbelieving as drunkenness, but by the faithful truly as salvation. Therefore we glorify Thy dominion, O Christ God, asking that forgiveness of transgressions be richly sent down upon Thy servants.[^2649]  
*Glory, both now: the same.*  
*Canon of the feast, and of the Menaion.*  
*Kontakion and Exapostilarion of the feast.*  
*At the Aposticha, stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме Є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^2650]  
O Comforter Who camest unto us from on high, even as formerly unto the Apostles, sanctify and save those who proclaim Thee as God.

*Verse: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me (Ps. 50:12 LXX).*  
Suddenly from heaven the power of the divine Comforter showed all the Apostles to be all-wise and theologians.  
*Verse: Cast me not away from Thy face, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me (Ps. 50:13 LXX).*  
Having enlightened their mind with the God-originating radiance, Thy Disciples, O Word, essentially received the Holy Spirit.

O Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of truth, Who art everywhere present and fillest all things, Treasury of good things and Giver of life: come and abide in us, and cleanse us from every impurity, and save our souls, O Good One.[^2651]

Thou Who art the confirmation of them that trust in Thee, confirm, O Lord, the Church which Thou didst purchase with Thy precious Blood.[^2652]  
*Troparion:*  
He Who confirmed the heavenly powers, the Comforter Spirit, today showed the Apostles not to be dumb.[^2653]  
*Troparion:*  
The Spirit consubstantial, equal in power, co-enthroned, and of like form with the Father and the Word, worshipping piously we magnify.  
*Glory:*  
The undefiled, purest Spirit, the most pure Comforter, we speak of theologically, as ruling over all creation.  
*Both now, Theotokion:*  
A holy temple, and ark, and lampstand, O all-pure Lady, hast thou been, of the never-setting Light, having shone forth Christ unto those in darkness.  
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*  
Joined unto the unbearable Fire, the youths standing for piety, unharmed by the flame, sang the divine hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him supreme unto all ages.[^2654]  
*Troparion:*  
We worship the one indivisible divine Essence in three Persons, singing of the single Sovereignty and Kingdom with godly wisdom: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.  
*Troparion:*  
The life-originating and divine Spirit, and perfective, recognized in the Trinity, we praise orthodoxly with thrice-holy glorifications: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.  
*Glory:*  
Spirit intellectual, Spirit divine, imparting qualities, all-richly distributing operations of gifts unto those singing in faith: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.

The beauty of Jacob, even thee most comely in godly beauty, O all-pure Mother of God, He Who is fairer than all, God the Word, chose, unto Whom we sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.[^2655]  
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*  
In the shadow and the scriptures of the Law we see a type, O ye faithful: every male that opens the womb is holy unto God; therefore the firstborn Word of the unoriginate Father, the Son Who is the firstborn of a Mother that knew not man, we magnify.[^2656]  
*Troparion:*  
The most honorable and grace-moved Spirit we speak of theologically, Who of old in the Prophets pre-announced the present illumination and the all-rich outpouring of good gifts, distributed unto the Disciples and witnesses of the Word.[^2657]  
*Troparion:*  
The divine-originating, unharmed Spirit we glorify, Who pours forth and works divine gifts for all, the Spirit, the other Comforter, Who proceeds from the unoriginate Father and is understood together with the co-unoriginate Son.  
*Glory:*  
The beneficent and rich Spirit we praise in song, Who sanctifies all and illuminates them with intelligible light, the almighty, ever-hymned Dominion, honored together with the Father and glorified together with the Son.  
*Both now, Theotokion:*  
The shadow of the Law hath passed away, and illumination hath shone forth upon them that sit in darkness, for He Who was incarnate of thee, O Virgin, by reason of His abundant tender mercy, was born of the unoriginate Father before the ages, consubstantial with Him that begat Him.  

## ON WEDNESDAY AFTER PENTECOST, AT VESPERS[^2658]

*At "Lord, I have cried," we set 6 verses: 3 of the feast, and 3 of the Menaion. Stichera of the feast, Tone 4, Podoben: "Being called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*[^2659]  
This day of joy and gladness, from on high appeared unto the sacred Disciples the Holy Spirit today, from the Father in fiery form, and He enlightened all; He revealed fishermen as wise narrators, as the God-proclaiming Luke wrote, filling all things with a violent breath; therefore marveling, in faith we praise the abundant mercy and uttermost condescension of Christ our God.[^2660]

=== LEAF p587 ===
We celebrate Pentecost, the glory of the Holy Spirit, and the true joy, the fulfillment of divine hope, and of the mystery revealed unto us before the ages; therefore we also cry unto Thee: Creator of all, take not Thy Spirit away from us, O Word, but send Him from heaven, O Savior, and by the prayers of Thy holy and all-glorious, honorable Apostles, grant peace unto our souls, we pray Thee.[^2661]  
The heavens, being clothed, declare today the marvelous deeds of the Savior, and they traversed all the world, clearly to proclaim the divine power of the Comforter, and they enlightened all creation by their teachings, that we may hymn the undivided Trinity, one in three unconfused Persons; therefore by faith we celebrate the coming of the Holy Spirit, Which appeared from on high and illuminates the ends of the earth.  
*Glory, both now, Tone 7:*  
Having Christ God as our Advocate with the Father, unto the other Comforter Who hath come unto us upon earth today, even the Holy Spirit, let us bow down in faith.[^2662]  
*Then: "O Gladsome Light."*  
*At the Aposticha, stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (Прехва́льнїи мч҃нцы):*[^2663]  
The Holy Spirit, Who proceeds from the Father and is glorified in the Son, by Whom all things are borne and upheld, and are sustained in life, and live, and abide, and are saved, we speak of theologically: O incomprehensible Comforter, grant peace unto Thy world![^2664]  
*Verse: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me (Ps. 50:12 LXX).*  
The Holy Spirit, the Wellspring of every goodness, came essentially to fill the ends of the earth with heavenly power through the Apostles and with divine goodness; unto Him let us cry: O ineffable Comforter, grant peace unto Thy world!  
*Verse: Cast me not away from Thy face, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me (Ps. 50:13 LXX).*  
The Holy Spirit is God, recognized as innate and co-enthroned with the Father and the Word, Light most perfect having shone forth from Light, from the unoriginate and perfect Father, and coming forth through the Son; unto Him let us cry: O Giver of life, Comforter, grant peace unto Thy world!  
*Glory, both now, Tone 8:*  
When Thou sentest Thy Spirit, O Lord, upon the sitting Apostles, then the children of the Hebrews, beholding it, were struck with awe; for they heard them speaking in other foreign tongues, even as the Spirit gave them utterance; for being unlearned, they were made wise, and having caught the nations into the faith, they declaimed divine things. Therefore we also cry unto Thee: O Thou Who didst appear on earth and didst save us from delusion, O Lord, glory to Thee![^2665]  
*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." and the Dismissal.*

## ON THURSDAY AFTER PENTECOST, AT MATINS[^2666]

*At "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast, thrice.*  
*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й):*[^2667]  
Now are fulfilled the prophetic foretellings; for He Who concealed these things with shadowy beams as a manifestation of things to come, as God the Comforter, hath now been poured out richly upon the Apostles, and through them upon those who faithfully worship the uncreated Trinity.  
*Glory, both now: the same.*  
*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Повелѣ́нное та́йнѡ):*[^2668]  
The lovers of the Savior were filled with joy and received boldness, who formerly were fearful; for the Holy Spirit today came down from on high upon the house of the Disciples, and one spake one thing and another another unto the people; for tongues were visibly parted as fire, and consumed them not, but rather bedewed them.  
*Glory, both now: the same.*  
*Canon of the feast, Tone 7, with the heirmos to 8, and of the Saint in the Menaion to 4. After Ode 6, Exapostilarion of the feast, twice.*[^2669]  
*At the Aposticha, stichera, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having risen on the third day" (Тридне́внѡ):*[^2670]  
The all-holy Spirit, consubstantial with the Father and the Word, having come upon the Disciples, made their earthen tongues to be of fire, enlightening them with divine grace.  
*Verse: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me (Ps. 50:12 LXX).*  
Renew within us, O God, the all-holy Spirit Whom of old Thou sentest unto Thy Disciples, O Merciful One, and Whom Thou didst strengthen to fulfill Thy saving purposes in the world.  
*Verse: Cast me not away from Thy face, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me (Ps. 50:13 LXX).*  
Thou Who spakest of old in the Prophets, O Comforter, hast now made the divine Apostles to utter the glory of God; through whom grant unto us cleansing and great mercy.

*Glory, both now, Tone 2:*  
In Thy courts, O Lord, we the faithful, bowing the knees of our souls and bodies, hymn Thee, the unoriginate Father, and the co-unoriginate Son, and the co-ever-existing and most Holy Spirit, Who illuminates and sanctifies our souls.[^2671]  
*Then the 1st Hour, and the Dismissal.*

*Ode 4, Tone 4. Heirmos:*

Incomprehensible is the all-holy Spirit: He surpasses every mind and the swiftness of thought, as being more ancient than all ages, as ranked together with the Father and the Only-Begotten Son.[^2673]

The all-holy Spirit overshadowed thee, O all-holy Maiden, having made thee a fair and divinely-befitting dwelling-place for the Incarnation of the Son, exalted above all.

*Troparion:*  
Disperse the mist of sorrow, O All-Good One, by Thy consolation, from them that piously praise Thee and faithfully cry: Bless the Lord, ye works of the Lord.
