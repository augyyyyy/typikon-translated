## IT BEHOOVES TO KNOW ALSO THIS[^3002]

*That the Pillars of the Sunday Matins Gospels begin from the 1st Sunday, that is, of All Saints, and are reckoned according to the Tones of the Oktoechos, beginning from the 2nd Sunday; and they extend together with the Tones even unto Palm Sunday, taking away nothing, taking up [extra] or leaving behind [omitting] nothing: as can be seen in the Oktoechos, before the Gospel Stichera, and in the Horologion under each letter of the Visual Paschalion, arranged in 6 pillars.[^3003] Which can never all be completed, yet they are laid down for this reason, that so far as needed, they may serve unto Palm Sunday.[^3004]*

*And that which in the Liturgikon is set down concerning the taking up, or sometimes leaving behind of the Tones, understand this concerning the Liturgical Epistles and Gospels: for they alone up to the Sunday of the Publican, following [in their order], are sometimes superfluous, when it is necessary to take up, or to leave behind, but not the Tones, nor the Matins Gospels.[^3005]*
