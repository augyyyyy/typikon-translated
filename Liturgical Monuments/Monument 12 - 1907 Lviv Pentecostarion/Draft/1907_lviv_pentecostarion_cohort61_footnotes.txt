[^2741]: The celebrated Troparion of All Saints in Tone 4 *И҆́же во все́мъ мі́рѣ Мч҃нкъ твои́хъ* ("Thy Church, adorned with the blood of Thy Martyrs throughout the whole world as with purple and fine linen") represents the primary dismissal hymn of the feast.
[^2742]: The Tone 4 Theotokion *Є҆́же ѿ вѣ́ка оу҆тае́ное* ("The mystery hidden from all ages") articulates the Incarnation as an unconfused union (*въ несли́тномъ соедине́нїи*) preserving both divine and human natures.

[^2751]: Antiphon 3 invokes the tripartite theology of the Trinity (*Бг҃осло́вїе є҆ди́ница трист҃а́ѧ*): the unoriginate Father, the timelessly begotten Son, and the co-enthroned Holy Spirit.
