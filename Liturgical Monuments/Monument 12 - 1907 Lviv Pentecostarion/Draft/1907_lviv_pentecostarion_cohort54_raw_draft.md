O Thou Who in the beginning didst lay the foundations of the earth, and by Thy word didst establish the heavens: blessed art Thou unto the ages, O Lord God of our fathers.[^2226]

*Troparion:*  
Performing the memory of those who have fallen asleep in piety from the ages, we cry aloud: Blessed art Thou unto the ages, O Lord God of our fathers.[^2227]
