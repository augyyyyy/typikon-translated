*Synaxarion:*[^336]

*Verse:* In vain dost thou guard the tomb, O watch:  
For the sepulchre cannot contain the Fountain of Life.

On Holy and Great Saturday, we celebrate the godly-bodily Burial of our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ, and His Descent into hades, whereby our race, having been recalled from corruption, passed over unto eternal life.

For holy Forty Days surpasses all days, and this Holy and Great Week is greater than it, and this Great and Holy Saturday is greater than Great Week. It is called Great Week, not because these days or hours are larger, but because great and supernatural wonders and preeminent deeds of our Savior were wrought therein, and especially on this day.

For even as at the first creation of the world, God, having wrought every work, and lastly on the sixth day having created man, the most efficacious work, on the seventh day rested from all His works, and sanctified it, naming it Sabbath, which is interpreted as rest: so also in the cultivation of the spiritual world, having preeminently wrought all things, and on the sixth day having recreated corrupted man and renewed him by the life-bearing Cross and death, on this present seventh day again He rested with a perfect rest from works, having fallen asleep with a life-natural and saving sleep.

The Word of God, therefore, descends with the Flesh into the tomb, and descends also into hades with His incorruptible and divine Soul, which was separated by death from the Body, which also He delivered into the hands of the Father, unto Whom also He offered His Blood, which became our redemption, without being asked. For the Soul of the Lord was not held fast in hades as the souls of other saints; for how could it be? Having brought nothing of the ancestral curse, as they did. Nor did our enemy the devil take His Blood, by which we were bought, even though he held us; for how could he, when the robber devil was not only unable to take it from God, but could not even take God Himself?[^337]

Nevertheless, bodily and with the Divinity our Lord Jesus Christ dwelt in the tomb, being supremely united to the flesh; and He was with the thief in Paradise, and was in hades, as was said, with His deified Soul; and supernaturally He was with the Father, seated together with the Spirit, as the uncircumscribed God, and He was everywhere, the Divinity suffering nothing in the tomb, even as neither upon the Cross.

And thus the Lord's Body suffered that which is the dissolution of the soul from the body; but corruption—that is, the destruction of the flesh and limbs, and complete annihilation—He suffered in no wise.

And Joseph, therefore, having taken down the holy Body of the Lord, buris It in a new tomb, and hard by the Jews in a garden he laid a very great stone over the entrance of the tomb.

And the Jews after Friday, having approached, say unto Pilate: "Sir, we remember that that deceiver said while He was yet alive: After three days I will rise; it seems good to us, therefore, that thy authority command the soldiers to secure the tomb." Pilate, perplexed, answered: "If he is a deceiver, why are ye anxious concerning what he said? When he was alive, as confessedly he died. And when did he say: I will rise? Perhaps they gathered this from the sign of Jonah? In any case, if the tomb be secured, he will not be stolen." Oh, how senseless they are! Whatsoever

=== LEAF p175 ===

they wrought against themselves, not understanding what they were doing! But Pilate having commanded, they themselves with the military rank and careful sealing secured the tomb, so that since the guard and seals were not foreign, the Resurrection of the Lord might not be slandered. But hades from henceforth is curtailed and at a loss, having felt the firmer power, and after a little while it spews forth, by an unjust swallowing, Christ the firmer and cornerstone; and those whom from the ages it held in its belly, it made food for Him.

By Thine ineffable condescension, O Christ our God, have mercy on us. Amen.
