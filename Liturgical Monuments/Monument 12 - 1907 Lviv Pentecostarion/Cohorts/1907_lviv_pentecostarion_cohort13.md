## THE ORDER OF THE HOURS OF HOLY AND GREAT FRIDAY[^221]

*A composition of Cyril, Archbishop of Alexandria.*

*(At the second hour of the day the klepalo is struck, and the priest vests, and having blessed, we begin:)*

*The First Hour.*

*Heavenly King. Trisagion. All-Holy Trinity. Our Father. Exclamation:*  
**Priest:** For Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and for ever and unto ages of ages.  
**Choir:** Amen.  
*Lord, have mercy (12 times). Glory, both now. Come, let us worship (thrice). And we say these psalms which are here set forth:*

*Psalm 5:*[^222]  
Give ear to my words, O Lord, consider my cry. Attend unto the voice of my supplication, my King and my God, for unto Thee will I pray, O Lord. In the morning Thou shalt hear my voice; in the morning I will stand before Thee, and Thou shalt look upon me. For Thou art not a God that desirest iniquity; the worker of evil shall not dwell with Thee. Neither shall transgressors abide before Thine eyes; Thou hast hated all them that work iniquity. Thou shalt destroy all them that speak a lie; the man of blood and deceit the Lord abhorrs. But as for me, in the multitude of Thy mercy I will enter into Thy house; I will worship toward Thy holy temple in Thy fear. Lead me, O Lord, in Thy righteousness; because of mine enemies, make straight my way before Thee. For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is vain; their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have dealt deceitfully. Judge them, O God; let them fall from their counsels; according to the multitude of their impieties cast them out, for they have embittered Thee, O Lord. And let all them that hope in Thee be glad; they shall rejoice for ever, and Thou shalt dwell in them; and they that love Thy name shall boast in Thee. For Thou shalt bless the righteous, O Lord; Thou hast crowned us as with a shield of good pleasure.

*Psalm 2:*[^223]  
Why have the heathen raged, and the peoples meditated on vain things? The kings of the earth stood up, and the rulers gathered together, against the Lord and against His Anointed: "Let us break their bonds asunder, and cast away their yoke from us!" He that dwells in the heavens shall laugh them to scorn, and the Lord shall deride them. Then shall He speak unto them in His wrath, and in His fury shall He trouble them. But as for Me, I was established King by Him upon Zion His holy mountain, proclaiming the commandment of the Lord. The Lord said unto Me: "Thou art My Son, this day have I begotten Thee. Ask of Me, and I will give Thee the nations for Thine inheritance, and the ends of the earth for Thy possession. Thou shalt shepherd them with a rod of iron; as a potter's vessels shalt Thou shatter them." And now, O ye kings, understand; receive instruction, all ye that judge the earth. Serve the Lord with fear, and rejoice in Him with trembling. Accept instruction, lest the Lord be angry, and ye perish from the righteous way, when His wrath is kindled in a moment. Blessed are all they that put their trust in Him.

*Psalm 21:*[^224]  
O God, my God, attend unto me; why hast Thou forsaken me? Far from my salvation are the words of my transgressions. O my God, I will cry by day, and Thou wilt not hear; and by night, and it shall not be accounted folly unto me. But Thou dwellest in the holy place, O Thou Praise of Israel. In Thee our fathers hoped; they hoped, and Thou didst deliver them. Unto Thee they cried, and they were saved; in Thee they hoped, and were not put to shame. But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and an outcast of the people. All they that saw me laughed me to scorn; they spoke with their lips, they shook the head: "He hoped in the Lord, let Him deliver him; let Him save him, since He desires him." For Thou art He that drew me out of the womb, my hope from my mother's breasts. Upon Thee was I cast from the womb; from my mother's belly, Thou art my God. Depart not from me, for tribulation is nigh, for there is none to help. Many bullocks have compassed me; fat bulls have beset me round. They opened their mouth against me, as a ravening and roaring lion. I am poured out like water, and all my bones are scattered; my heart is become like wax melting in the midst of my belly. My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue hath cleaved to my jaws, and into the dust of death hast Thou brought me down. For many dogs have compassed me; the assembly of the wicked hath enclosed me. They pierced my hands and my feet. They numbered all my bones, and they looked and stared upon me. They parted my garments among them, and upon my vesture they cast lots. But Thou, O Lord, remove not Thy help far from me; attend unto my defense. Deliver my soul from the sword, and mine only-begotten from the paw of the dog. Save me from the lion's mouth, and my lowliness from the horns of the unicorns. I will declare Thy name unto my brethren; in the midst of the Church will I hymn Thee. Ye that fear the Lord, praise Him; all ye seed of Jacob, glorify Him. Let all the seed of Israel fear Him. For He hath not despised nor abhorred the supplication of the poor, neither hath He turned away His face from me; and when I cried unto Him, He heard me. From Thee is my praise; in the great Church will I confess Thee; I will pay my vows before them that fear Him. The poor shall eat and be satisfied, and they that seek the Lord shall praise Him; their hearts shall live unto ages of ages. All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn unto the Lord, and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before Him. For the kingdom is the Lord's, and He rules over the nations. All the fat ones of the earth have eaten and worshipped; all they that go down into the earth shall fall down before Him; and my soul lives unto Him. And my seed shall serve Him; the generation that comes shall be declared unto the Lord. And they shall declare His righteousness unto a people that shall be born, whom the Lord hath made.

*Glory, both now. Alleluia (thrice). Lord, have mercy (thrice).*  
*Glory. Troparion, Tone 1:*[^225]

*Both now. Theotokion:*[^226]  
What shall we call thee, O thou full of grace? Heaven, for thou hast made the Sun of righteousness to shine forth; Paradise, for thou hast blossomed the Flower of incorruption; Virgin, for thou hast remained undefiled; Pure Mother, for thou hast held in thy holy arms a Son, the God of all: Him do thou entreat, that our souls may be saved.

But God commends His love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us. Much more then, being now justified by His Blood, we shall be saved from wrath through Him. For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of His Son, much more, being reconciled, we shall be saved by His life.
