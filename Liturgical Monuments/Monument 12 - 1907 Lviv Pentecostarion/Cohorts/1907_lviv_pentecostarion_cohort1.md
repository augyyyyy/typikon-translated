## ON FRIDAY OF PALM WEEK, AT VESPERS

*At the Lucernarium [Lord, I have cried], the customary chanting of the Psalter.*

*At Lord, I have cried, we set 10 verses. And we chant the Idiomelon of the day twice, and the Martyrikon; and 5 Idiomela of Righteous Lazarus, repeating them to make 7.*

*Tone 8. Idiomelon of the Day:*
Having completed the forty days that bring profit to our souls, we entreat Thee, O Lover of mankind: enable us also to see the Holy Week of Thy Passion, that we may glorify therein Thy marvelous deeds and Thine ineffable dispensation for our sake, as with one mind we chant: Lord, glory to Thee! *(Twice)*

*Martyrikon:*
O martyrs of the Lord, pray unto our God, and entreat for our souls a multitude of compassions and the cleansing of our many offenses, we beseech you.

*Then we chant 5 Idiomela of Saint Lazarus. The composition of the pious emperor, lord Leo the Wise.*[^17] *Tone 6:*
Lord, desiring to see the tomb of Lazarus, though of Thine own will Thou wast about to dwell in a tomb, Thou didst ask: Where have ye laid him? And having learned that which was not unknown to Thee, Thou didst call aloud unto him whom Thou lovedst: Lazarus, come forth! And the lifeless one gave ear unto the breath of Him Who gives breath unto him, Thee, the Savior of our souls. *(Twice)*

*Sticheron 2. Tone 6:*
Lord, Thou camest unto the tomb of the four-days-dead Lazarus, and having shed tears over the tomb, Thou didst raise the dead man four days in the grave, O Bread of Life. Therefore was death bound by Thy voice, and the graveclothes were loosed by hands. Then was the multitude of Thy disciples filled with joy, and one agreement of service sounded from all: Blessed art Thou, O Savior, have mercy upon us! *(Twice)*

*Sticheron 3. Tone 6:*
Lord, Thy voice destroyed the realm of Hades, and the word of Thy power raised from the tomb him that was dead four days; and Lazarus became a saving prefiguration of regeneration. All things are possible unto Thee, O Master, King of all: grant unto Thy servants cleansing and great mercy. *(Once)*

*Sticheron 4. Tone 6:*
Lord, desiring to assure Thy disciples of Thy Resurrection from the dead, Thou camest unto the tomb of Lazarus; and when Thou didst call him, Hades was despoiled and released the four-days-dead man, who cried unto Thee: Blessed Lord, glory to Thee! *(Once)*

*Sticheron 5. Tone 6:*
Lord, taking Thy disciples with Thee, Thou didst come unto Bethany that Thou mightest raise Lazarus; and having wept over him according to the law of human nature, yet as God Thou didst raise him up four days in the grave, and he cried unto Thee, O Savior: Blessed Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Tone 8:*
Standing before the tomb of Lazarus, O our Savior, and calling unto the dead man, Thou didst awaken him as from sleep. He shook off corruption at the nod of Thine incorruption, and at Thy word he came forth bound with graveclothes. Thou art able to do all things, all things serve Thee, O Lover of mankind, all things obey Thee, O our Savior, glory to Thee!

*Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Tone 8:*
Having completed the forty days that bring profit to our souls, let us cry aloud: Rejoice, city of Bethany, home of Lazarus! Rejoice, Martha and Mary, his sisters! For tomorrow Christ comes to quicken your dead brother by His voice. And bitter and insatiable Hades, hearing His voice, shall tremble in fear and groan aloud, releasing Lazarus bound in graveclothes. And the assembly of the Hebrews, marveling at His miracle, shall meet Him with palms and branches; and the children shall appear praising Him, while the fathers envy Him: Blessed is He that comes in the name of the Lord, the King of Israel!

*Entrance with the censer.*
*O Joyful Light:*
*Prokeimenon, Psalm 123, Tone 6:*
Our help is in the name of the Lord, Who made heaven and earth.
*Verse:* If it had not been that the Lord was with us, let Israel now say.

*The Reading from Genesis (LXX Gen. 49:33–50:26):*[^18]
Jacob ceased commanding his sons; and Jacob drew up his feet into the bed, and expired, and was gathered unto his people. And Joseph fell upon his father's face, and wept bitterly over him, and kissed him. And Joseph commanded his servants the undertakers to embalm his father; and the undertakers embalmed Israel. And forty days were fulfilled for him, for so are the days of embalming numbered; and Egypt mourned for him seventy days. And when the days of mourning were past, Joseph spoke unto the princes of Pharaoh, saying: If I have found grace before you, speak concerning me in the ears of Pharaoh, saying: My father bound me by an oath before he died, saying: In the tomb which I dug for myself in the land of Canaan, there shalt thou bury me. Now therefore I will go up and bury my father, and I will return.
