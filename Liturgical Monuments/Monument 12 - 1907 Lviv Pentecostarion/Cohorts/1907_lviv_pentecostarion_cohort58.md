On this day of Pentecost, indeed, He came upon the Holy Apostles substantially, in the form of fiery tongues, sitting upon each one of them in the upper room, wherein they were abiding. And for the sake of honor toward the All-Holy Spirit, and separately, also on this Pentecost the divine Fathers, who understood all things well, ordained to celebrate Him.[^2579] For the Savior, before the Passion, promising the coming of the Comforter, said: "It is expedient for you that I go away; for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come" (John 16:7). And again: "When He is come, He will teach you, and guide you into all truth" (John 16:13). And again: "I will pray the Father, and He shall give you another Comforter, the Spirit of truth, Who proceeds from the Father" (John 14:16, 15:26). And after the Passion also, when He was ascending into heaven, He said: "Tarry ye in Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high" (Luke 24:49). He sends Him, therefore, unto them who were abiding in His Name; and when the day of Pentecost was fully come, at the third hour of the day in the upper room, suddenly there was a thunder from heaven, such that even a multitude of the inhabited earth hastened together; and in the form of fiery tongues the Holy Spirit appeared, unto each one of them, not only to the Twelve, but also to the Seventy.[^2580] And they spoke with strange tongues, that is, each one of the Apostles spoke with all the languages of the nations. Not indeed that while the Apostle spoke in his own language, the foreigner heard, but each language of a nation the Apostle heard and spoke; whence also unto the gathered multitudes they seemed to be drunk. For each not knowing how the Apostle particularly spoke in all languages, supposed that he was drunk. And others marveled, saying: "What then will this be?" For these were gathered from all the earth for the sake of the feast: Parthians, Medes, and Elamites, who not long before had been taken captive by Antiochus.[^2581] Ten days, then, having passed after the Ascension, the Holy Spirit comes, and not immediately when Christ ascended, that He might make the Disciples who were awaiting Him all the more fervent. And some say that on each of the days, each one of the angelic ranks, coming, bowed down before that deified flesh. Ten days then having been completed, on the tenth day the Comforter came, reconciliation having been made by the Son.[^2582] And fifty days after Pascha, in remembrance of the Old Law; for Israel, from the time it passed through the Red Sea, fifty days having elapsed, received the Ten Commandments. Behold also the signs: there a mountain, here an upper room; there fire, here fiery tongues; and in place of thunders and darkness, here a violent wind.[^2583] In the form of tongues, therefore, the Holy Spirit came down, showing that He has affinity with the living Word; or also because the Apostles were to teach and bring the nations, having it by tongue. Of fire, moreover, because God is a consuming fire (Deut. 4:24, Heb. 12:29), and for the sake of purification. Divided, again, for the sake of gifts. And as of old He divided into many the one language of them that were building [the tower],[^2584]

and divided it: so now, He divided into many the one language of them that knew it, that He might gather from those nations scattered throughout the ends of the inhabited earth.[^2585] And at the feast it takes place, that many being gathered together, the matter might be proclaimed everywhere, and that those who were present at Pascha and had heard of the wonders concerning Christ, might have cause to wonder.[^2586] And at Pentecost, because it was fitting that at the very time when the Law was given of old, after it the grace of the Spirit should also be poured out; even as Christ did at the legal Passover, having accomplished His own Pascha, the true Pascha. Or because the Spirit pours out a certain voice as an image by the tongues, so as to ordain the Apostles through their heads to be active teachers of the whole world; for ordination takes place upon the crown of the head.[^2587] And the sound and the fire, because these also were at Sinai: that the same Spirit may be shown to be the Lawgiver and the Disposer of all, both then and now. And the multitude was confounded by the noise of the wind, because they thought that the end had come, which Christ had foretold unto the Jews concerning their destruction. And like fiery ones, it says, that no one should think anything bodily concerning the Holy Spirit. By drunkenness, then, the Apostles are accused; but Peter, standing up in their midst, then refuted this thing that was not truly held, having adduced the prophecy of Joel to his speech, and converted about three thousand from among them (Acts 2:14-41).[^2588] And the Holy Spirit is called the Comforter, as being able to comfort and give rest unto us: for in place of Christ we have received Him, having Him also by this: for He also intercedes for us with ineffable groanings unto God (Rom. 8:26), standing before us as the Lover of mankind, even as Christ also does: for the Son is also a Comforter. For this reason the Holy Spirit is called "another Comforter." For the Apostle says: "We have an Advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ" (1 John 2:1). And "another" is said for consubstantiality; for to say "another and another" [*allos kai allos*] is said of those that are of the same essence and divine; but to say "another thing and another thing" [*allo kai allo*] we know of different natures.[^2589] This Holy Spirit is one with the Father and the Son in all things; therefore He also works all things together with Them: and that very resurrection which is to come. Simply, He has all things that the Father and the Son have, except unbegottenness and begottenness: proceeding from the Father and the Son.[^2590] The Holy Spirit then having been poured out upon all flesh, the world was filled with all kinds of gifts, and through them all the nations were guided unto the knowledge of God, and every sickness and every plague was driven away. The power from on high which Thou didst promise to send down unto Thy Disciples, O Christ, having fulfilled it, Thou sendest Thy Holy Spirit, having shown unto us that Thou art the Truth, and they that trust in Thee, O Good One, truly are not put to shame.[^2614]

*And 3 from the Menaion.*[^2616]  
*Glory, both now, Tone 1:*  
We celebrate Pentecost, and the coming of the Spirit, and the pledge of the promise, and the fulfillment of hope; and a mystery how great, as it is both great and venerable! Therefore we cry unto Thee: Creator of all, O Lord, glory to Thee.[^2617]  
*At the Aposticha, stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме Є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^2618]  
In deed unto the Disciples, as Thou didst promise, O Word, Thou hast sent Thy divine and Holy Spirit, and hast enlightened them.[^2619]  
*Verse: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me (Ps. 50:12 LXX).*[^2620]  
Suddenly from heaven the power of the Divine Comforter showed all the Apostles to be all-wise and God-speaking.[^2621]  
*Verse: Cast me not away from Thy face, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me (Ps. 50:13 LXX).*[^2622]  
The race of the foreigners, hearing in their own languages, were perplexed at the divine Apostles magnifying the Trinity.[^2623]  
*Glory, both now, Tone 1:*  
With the tongues of foreigners Thou hast renewed Thy Disciples, O Christ, that through them they might proclaim Thee, the immortal Word and God, Who grantest great mercy unto our souls.[^2624]  
*On Tuesday.*[^2625]
