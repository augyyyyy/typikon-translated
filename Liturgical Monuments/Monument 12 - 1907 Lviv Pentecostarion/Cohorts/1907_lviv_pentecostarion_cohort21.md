## The Holy and Great Sunday of Pascha

*At Matins:*[^384]

*At the hour of Matins, the paraecclesiarch, having taken a blessing from the superior, goes forth and strikes the great bell, and beats the klepalo sufficiently. And entering into the temple, he lights all the tapers and the oil lamps; and he prepares two vessels with burning coals, and puts therein much fragrant incense, and sets one vessel in the midst of the church, and the other in the holy sanctuary, so that the whole church may be filled with fragrance. Then the superior, entering into the holy sanctuary with the priests and deacons, vests in all his most radiant vestments. And he distributes tapers unto the brethren; and taking the precious Cross, while the deacon takes the censer, a priest the Holy Gospel, and another priest the Icon of the Resurrection of Christ, they stand with their faces toward the west. And they close the church doors that face toward the west. And the superior goes forth with the priests into the narthex through the northern door, while deacons go before them with two tapers, and both choirs, chanting the Sticheron:*

*Tone 6:*

Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, the angels sing in the heavens; enable us also on earth to glorify Thee with a pure heart.

*Then they peal all the bells and the heavy bell, and strike the klepalo sufficiently. And entering into the narthex, they stand with the Gospel and with the Icon, with their faces toward the west, as was indicated aforetime.
