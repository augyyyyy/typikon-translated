# PART VIII: TYPIKON APPENDIX — PASCHALTIDE MENAION COMBINATIONS AND THE ELEVEN MATINS RESURRECTION GOSPEL PILLARS

## TYPIKON APPENDIX: PASCHALTIDE MENAION COMBINATIONS AND GOSPEL PILLARS[^2954]

## TYPIKON OF THE FEAST OF THE ANNUNCIATION: WHEN IT COINCIDES WITH LAZARUS SATURDAY[^2955]

Be it known that when the Paschal Key is K [20],[^2956] then the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos falls on Lazarus Saturday.

On Friday evening, we do not sing a Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera: the Idiomelon of the day twice: "Completing the soul-profiting forty days:" the lesser one; and of the feast 3: "The pre-eternal Counsel:" and of Saint Lazarus 5. Glory: "Completing the soul-profiting forty days:" the great one; Both now: of the feast: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent:" Entrance. Prokeimenon, and readings of the day. And of the feast, 5 readings; and the rest of the Presanctified. At the refectory, dispensation for wine and oil.

At the 7th hour of the night [1:00 AM], the great bell tolls sufficiently, and all the bells. And having gathered into the temple, we sing Great Compline. After the 1st Trisagion, Troparion of the feast. After the 2nd, Kontakion of the feast; and after the "Glory to God in the highest," procession into the narthex. And we sing the Idiomela Stichera of the feast 3, and of the Triodion Idiomela 3, written at the Praises: "Beholding Thy bodily presence, O Christ:" Glory: "Let the heavens be glad:" Both now: "Gabriel announcs good tidings." At the Aposticha, Idiomela Stichera of the feast, with their verses. Glory: "Thou didst stand before the tomb." Both now: "Today is the joy:" After the "Our Father," Troparion of the feast, thrice; and the blessing of the loaves; and reading of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast, twice. Glory: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection:" Both now: of the feast. After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn of the Triodion, Tone 1: "Thou didst have compassion on the tears of Martha and Mary," twice; Glory... Both now: of the feast; and reading concerning Lazarus. After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn of the Triodion: "The Fountain of wisdom," twice; Glory... Both now: of the feast; and reading concerning righteous Lazarus, or of the feast. Then Polyeleos, and Sessional Hymn of the feast; and reading of the feast. Anabathmoi, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon, and Gospel of the feast. "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ," and Psalm 50. Sticheron of the feast. Canon of the feast with the heirmoi on 6; and of Saint Lazarus, both canons on 8, likewise also the Four-Ode Canon. Katavasia: of the 2nd Canon of Lazarus, the heirmos: "Having traversed the water," both choirs together. After Ode 3, Kontakion and Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of Saint Lazarus. Glory... Both now: of the feast; and reading of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the feast, and Ikos, and reading. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the refrain of the feast, as was prescribed for the feast itself. Unto the Canon of the Triodion we say the refrain: "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee." Katavasia: "Thee who art truly the Theotokos:" Then the refrain of the feast, and heirmos: "As unto an animated ark of God:" We sing "Holy is the Lord our God" in Tone 1. Exapostilarion of the feast, twice; Glory... Both now: of Lazarus. At the Praises, Stichera of the feast 3, and of Lazarus 3. Then the verses: "Arise, O Lord my God;" 2. "I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart;" Glory: of Saint Lazarus: "Great and marvelous;" Both now: of the feast: "The mystery which was hidden from eternity." Great Doxology. Troparion of the feast; Glory... Both now: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection;" litanies, and dismissal, and catechesis; and the First Hour in the narthex: Troparion of the feast; Glory: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection;" Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the "Our Father," Kontakion of Lazarus. Thus we say the troparia also at the other Hours, alternating the Kontakia.

At the 20th hour of the day [2:00 PM], we make the procession outside the monastery; and having returned, we sing the Hours. At the Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the feast, Ode 3 on 4; and of Lazarus, Ode 6 on 4. After the Entrance, Troparion of the feast, and of Lazarus; Glory: Kontakion of Lazarus; Both now:

of the feast. Instead of the Trisagion, we sing: "As many of you as have been baptized into Christ."[^2957] Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, and Gospel, first of the feast, then of Lazarus. Instead of "It is truly meet," we sing the Heirmos of the feast. Communion Hymn, first of the feast, then of Saint Lazarus.

## CONCERNING THE SAME FEAST, IF IT FALL ON THE SUNDAY OF PALMS[^2958]

On Saturday at Small Vespers, Stichera of Palms on 4; Glory... Both now: of the feast: "In the sixth month." At the Aposticha: Idiomela of the feast from Great Vespers with their verses; Glory... Both now: of Palms: "I hymn Thy terrible dispensation:" Troparion of the feast; Glory... Both now: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection:" Little Litany, and dismissal.

At Great Vespers, "Blessed is the man:" the entire Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the feast 6, repeating them; and of Palms on 4; Glory: of Palms: "Today the grace of the Holy Spirit:" Both now: of the feast: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day; and 5 readings of the feast, and 3 of Palms. At the Litiya, Stichera of the feast 6, and of Palms 3; Glory: of Palms: "Six days before the Passover:" Both now: of the feast: "Gabriel announcs good tidings." At the Aposticha, Idiomela Stichera of Palms, Tone 8, with their verses; Glory: of Palms: "Today the grace:" Both now: of the feast: "The mystery which was hidden from eternity." At the blessing of the loaves, Troparion of the feast twice, and of Palms once; and reading of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast twice; Glory... Both now: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection." After the 1st Kathisma: Sessional Hymn of Palms; Glory... Both now: of the feast, and reading. After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn of Palms; Glory... Both now: of the feast, and reading. Then Polyeleos of the feast, and Sessional Hymn, and reading of the feast. Anabathmoi, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon of Palms. "Let every breath." Gospel of Palms, Psalm 50. But "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ" we do not say. And when the brethren kiss the holy Gospel, the superior distributes the palms with candles unto the brethren, according to custom. Instead of "Through the prayers:" we sing Glory: "Today Christ enters the city:" Both now: the same. Then, "Have mercy on me, O God:" We sing the Sticheron, Tone 6: "Today the grace of the Holy Spirit." Canon of the feast with the heirmos on 8, and of Palms with the heirmos on 8. Katavasia: of both canons, each choir its own Heirmos. After Ode 3, Kontakion of Palms, and Ikos, and Sessional Hymn; Glory... Both now: of the feast, and reading of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the feast and Ikos. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the refrain of the feast, as was prescribed before. And of Palms we sing according to custom the refrain: "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee." At the Katavasia, first we sing the refrain of the feast and Heirmos of the feast: "As unto an animated ark of God:" then of Palms. "Holy is the Lord our God" in Tone 1. Exapostilarion of the feast twice. At the Praises, Stichera of the feast 3, and of Palms 3; Glory: of the feast: "Let the heavens be glad;" Both now: of Palms: "Six days before the Passover." Great Doxology. Troparion: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection;" Glory... Both now: of the feast; litanies, and dismissal, and catechesis; and the First Hour in the narthex, and dismissal.

At the 20th hour of the day [2:00 PM], we go forth with a Litiya outside the monastery, as was indicated in the order; and having returned, we sing the Hours. At the Hours, Troparion of the feast; Glory: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection;" Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the "Our Father," we alternate the Kontakia. At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes of the feast, Ode 3 on 4; and of Palms, Ode 6 on 4. After the Entrance, we say the verse: "Blessed is He that comes in the name of the Lord; God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us." Troparion of the feast, and of Palms: "Giving assurance of the general resurrection;" Glory: Kontakion of Palms: "Borne upon a throne in heaven;" Both now: of the feast: "To thee, the Champion Leader." Then Prokeimenon of Palms and of the feast; Epistle of the feast and of Palms; Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the feast and of Palms.

At the Liturgy, instead of "It is truly meet," the Heirmos of Palms: "God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us." But if it be a temple of the Annunciation of the Theotokos: we sing instead of "It is truly meet," the Heirmos: "As unto an animated ark of God."

## IF THE ANNUNCIATION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS FALL ON GREAT MONDAY[^2959]

On Palm Sunday at Small Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the feast on 4; Glory... Both now: of the feast. At the Aposticha, Stichera of the feast from Great Vespers; Glory... Both now: of the feast, and Troparion of the feast. At Great Vespers, we sing "Blessed is the man:" the 1st Antiphon. At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the feast on 6, and of Palms on 4; Glory: of Palms, Tone 6: "He that sits upon the cherubic throne:" Both now: of the feast: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day; 3 readings of the feast. At the Litiya, Stichera of the feast; Glory... Both now: of the feast: "Gabriel announcs good tidings." At the Aposticha, Idiomela Stichera of the day with their verses; Glory: of Palms: "With branches and palms:" Both now: of the feast: "The mystery which was hidden from eternity." At the blessing of the loaves, Troparion of the feast thrice, and reading of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast thrice. After the 1st Kathisma and after the 2nd, Sessional Hymns of the day, and reading of the day. After the 3rd Kathisma, Little Litany, and both Sessional Hymns of the feast, and reading of the feast. Polyeleos, and Sessional Hymn, and reading of the feast. Anabathmoi, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon: "Proclaim from day to day the salvation of our God." "Let every breath." Gospel of the feast, Psalm 50, Sticheron of the feast (the Gospel of the day is omitted, if the Annunciation fall on whatever day of this week). Canon of the feast with the heirmos on 6, and the Three-Ode Canon on 8. Katavasia: the Heirmos of the Triodion, both choirs together.

And where there is no Three-Ode Canon, we sing the Canon of the feast with the Heirmos on 12. Katavasia of the feast. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the day and Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the feast, and reading of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion and Ikos of the feast. Prologue and Synaxarion. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the refrain of the feast: "O earth, announce great gladness;" then the Heirmos of the feast: "As unto an animated ark of God;" unto the troparia of the Three-Ode Canon, the refrain: "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee."

Katavasia: we sing the Heirmos of the Triodion; after that, the refrain of the feast, and the Heirmos: "As unto an animated ark of God." Exapostilarion of the feast; Glory: of the day; Both now: of the feast. At the Praises, Stichera of the feast on 6; Glory: of the feast: "The mystery which was hidden from eternity;" Both now: of the day: "The dried-up fig tree:" Then the superior or reader says: "Glory to Thee Who hast shown us the light." Litany: "Let us complete our morning prayer:" At the Aposticha, Idiomela Stichera of the day, Tone 1: "The Lord comes:" Verse: "We were filled:" "Having attained, ye faithful:" Verse: "Make us glad for the days wherein Thou hast humbled us:" "Lord, coming unto the Passion:" Verse: "And look upon Thy servants:" "Lord, unto the mystery:" Verse: "And let the brightness:" "Lord, to mind most perfect things:" Glory: Tone 8: "A second Eve:" Both now: of the feast: "Let the heavens be glad:" Then, "It is good to give praise," once. After the Trisagion, Troparion of the feast. Litany, and 3 great prostrations. After this, "Come, let us worship," thrice. And the First Hour: Troparion of the feast; Both now: Theotokion: "What shall we call thee:" After the Trisagion: Kontakion of the feast, and at the end 3 great prostrations; then: "O Christ, the true Light," and dismissal.

At the 3rd hour of the day [9:00 AM], we make the Litiya outside the monastery. Having returned, we sing the 3rd Hour and the 6th with Kathismata and with reading; and we read the Tetra-Gospel.[^2960] And we say at them the Troparion of the feast, alternating the Kontakia of the day and of the feast; and at the end of each Hour, 3 great prostrations. Likewise we sing also the 9th Hour, and the Beatitudes simply without singing, and "Remember us, O Lord," and the rest; after the "Our Father," Kontakion of the day; Glory... Both now: of the feast; "Lord, have mercy," 40; Glory... Both now: "More honorable than the Cherubim;" and 3 great prostrations. And immediately: "Come, let us worship," thrice. And we sing Vespers without prostrations and without a Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," Idiomela Stichera of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day: "The dried-up fig tree;" Both now: of the feast: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance with the Gospel. "O Joyful Light." Prokeimenon, and readings of the day, and two of the feast. Then the Little Litany, and the Trisagion. Prokeimenon, Epistle, and Alleluia of the feast. Gospel of the feast, and of the day; and the rest of the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom. And the Communion Hymn of the feast.[^2961]

*And where because of great necessity there is no Liturgy:*[^2962]
At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera on 10: and we sing of the day 3 in Tone 1, repeating the first two, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day, Tone 8: "A second Eve;" Both now: of the feast. Entrance with the Gospel. "O Joyful Light." Prokeimenon, and reading of the day; and of the feast, two. Then Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, and Gospel of the feast, and of the day. "Vouchsafe, O Lord." Litany: "Let us complete our evening prayer." At the Aposticha of the day, Tone 5: "Lord, to mind most perfect things," twice, with the daily verses; and the other verse, Tone 8: "The dried-up fig tree;" Glory, Tone 5: "Lord, coming unto the Passion;" Both now: of the feast: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart." Troparion of the feast. Litanies, and dismissal. Thus we sing also on the other days of Great Week. At the refectory, dispensation for oil and wine only. At Small Compline there are no prostrations, save only after "Glory to God in the highest," 3 bows; at the Trisagion, 3 bows. At the end, 3 great prostrations and 12 small bows. At the final Trisagion, 3 bows. And we sing also the Three-Ode Canon of Kyr Andrew.

## CONCERNING THE SAME FEAST, IF IT FALL ON GREAT TUESDAY[^2963]

On Monday evening we do not say a Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," Idiomela Stichera of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day: "A second Eve;" Both now: of the feast: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance with the Gospel. "O Joyful Light." Prokeimenon, and readings of the day, and of the feast 3. "Let my prayer be set forth," and 3 prostrations. Gospel of the day; and the rest of the Presanctified. At the refectory we drink wine.

At the 1st hour of the night [7:00 PM], the great bell tolls, and all the heavy bells; and having gathered into the temple, we sing Great Compline: "God is with us," we sing to both choirs with chanting. The priest at the beginning censes according to custom. And after "Glory to God in the highest," procession into the narthex; and we sing at the Litiya the Idiomela Stichera of the feast; Glory... Both now: of the feast: "Gabriel announcs good tidings." At the Aposticha, Idiomela Stichera of the feast 3, with their verses; Glory... Both now: "Today is the joy." At the blessing of the loaves, Troparion of the feast thrice, and readings of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast thrice. After their Kathismata, Sessional Hymns of the Triodion, and reading of the day. After the 2nd Kathisma, Little Litany, and Sessional Hymn of the feast, and reading. Polyeleos and Sessional Hymn of the feast. Anabathmoi, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon: "Let every breath." Gospel of the feast, Psalm 50, Sticheron of the feast. Canon of the feast with the Heirmos on 12. Katavasia of the feast: the Heirmos, both choirs together. And where there is a Two-Ode Canon of the Triodion, we sing the Canon of the feast with the Heirmoi on 6; the Two-Ode Canon on 8. Follow the Heirmos of the Triodion, both choirs together. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the day and Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the feast, and reading of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the feast and Ikos, and we read the Prologue. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the refrain and Heirmos of the feast, as was indicated on Great Monday. Exapostilarion of the feast; Glory: of the day; Both now: of the feast.

At the Praises, Stichera Prosomoia of the feast on 6; Glory: of the day: "Through spiritual slothfulness;" Both now: of the feast: "The mystery which was hidden from eternity." At the Aposticha, Idiomela of the day with their verses, as was prescribed on Monday: "We were filled in the morning;" Glory: of the feast: "Let the heavens be glad;" Both now: of the day, Tone 6: "When Thou shalt come in glory:" Troparion of the feast. Litany, and 3 great prostrations; then "Come, let us worship," thrice. The First Hour; at the end thereof 3 great prostrations; and the prayer: "O Christ, the true Light;" and dismissal.

At the 3rd hour of the day [9:00 AM], we make the Litiya outside the monastery; and having returned, we sing the Hours with Kathismata, and the Tetra-Gospel is read. We say at the Hours the Troparion of the feast, and reading of the feast. We say the Kontakia alternating between the day and the feast; and at the end of each Hour, 3 great prostrations. And the 9th Hour without a Kathisma. The Beatitudes quickly without singing; and "Remember us, O Lord," and the rest as was prescribed before. After the "Our Father," Kontakion of the feast; "Lord, have mercy," 40; Glory... Both now: "More honorable than the Cherubim;" and 3 great prostrations, and immediately we say: "Come, let us worship," thrice. And we sing Vespers without a Kathisma and without prostrations: At "Lord, I have cried," Idiomela Stichera of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day; Both now: of the feast. Entrance with the Gospel. Prokeimenon, and readings of the day two, and of the feast two. Little Litany, and the exclamation of the Trisagion. Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia of the feast; Gospel of the feast, and of the day; and the rest of the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom. Communion Hymn of the feast.

At the refectory, we grant dispensation for olive oil and wine only. And we sing Small Compline without prostrations; and we sing also the Three-Ode Canon thereon by Kyr Andrew of Crete.

## CONCERNING THE SAME FEAST, IF IT FALL ON GREAT WEDNESDAY[^2964]

On Tuesday evening we sing Vespers without a Kathisma and without prostrations. At "Lord, I have cried," Idiomela Stichera of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day, Tone 7: "Behold, the talent unto thee:" Both now: of the feast. Entrance with the Gospel. Prokeimenon, and readings of the day, and of the feast 3. "Let my prayer be set forth," and 3 prostrations. Gospel of the day; and the rest of the Liturgy of the Presanctified. At the refectory, cooked food without oil; and we drink wine.

At the 1st hour of the night [7:00 PM], the great bell tolls, and all the heavy bells; and we sing Great Compline: "God is with us," to both choirs. The priest censes at the beginning according to custom. And after "Glory to God in the highest," procession into the narthex; and we sing at the Litiya the Stichera; Glory... Both now: of the feast. At the Aposticha, Stichera, and Glory... Both now: of the feast. At the blessing of the loaves, Troparion of the feast thrice, and reading of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast thrice. After both Kathismata, Sessional Hymns of the Triodion, and reading of the day. After the 2nd Kathisma, Litany, and both Sessional Hymns of the feast, and reading of the feast. Polyeleos, and Sessional Hymn, and reading of the feast. Anabathmoi, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon: "Let every breath." Gospel of the feast, Psalm 50, Sticheron of the feast. Canon of the feast with the Heirmos on 12. Katavasia of the feast: the Heirmos, both choirs together.

And where there is a Three-Ode Canon: we sing the Canon of the feast with the Heirmos on 6, and the Three-Ode Canon on 8. Katavasia: the Heirmos of the Triodion. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the day, and Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the feast, and reading of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the feast and Ikos. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the refrains of the feast as was prescribed before; Exapostilarion of the feast; Glory: of the day; Both now: of the feast. At the Praises, Stichera of the feast on 6; Glory: of the day, Tone 6: "Today Christ comes:" Both now: of the feast, Tone 2: "The mystery which was hidden from eternity."

At the Aposticha of the day, with their verses, as was prescribed on Great Monday; Glory: of the feast; Both now: of the day; and the rest according to what was shown before. At the 3rd hour of the day [9:00 AM], we make the procession outside the monastery; and having returned, we sing the Hours with Kathismata, and the Tetra-Gospel is read; and the rest of the Hours and of the Liturgy, as was set forth on Monday and Tuesday of this week. At the end of the Hours there takes place the mutual forgiveness of the monastery, according to custom. And we sing Small Compline without prostrations, and the Three-Ode Canon of Kyr Andrew of Crete.

## CONCERNING THE SAME FEAST, IF IT FALL ON GREAT THURSDAY[^2965]

On Great Wednesday we sing Vespers without a Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried," Idiomela Stichera of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory... Both now: of the feast; and the rest as was indicated on Tuesday.

At the 1st hour of the night [7:00 PM], the great bell tolls, and all the heavy bells. And having gathered into the temple, we sing Great Compline: the priest censes according to custom. And after "Glory to God in the highest," procession into the narthex; and we sing at the Litiya the Idiomela Stichera; Glory... Both now: of the feast. At the Aposticha, Stichera; Glory... Both now: of the feast. At the blessing of the loaves, Troparion of the feast thrice, and reading of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the feast twice; Glory... Both now: "When the glorious disciples." Then Polyeleos, and Sessional Hymn, and reading of the feast. Anabathmoi, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon: "Let every breath." Gospel of the feast, Psalm 50, Sticheron of the feast.

Canon of the feast with the Heirmos on 8, the Heirmos twice; and of the day with the Heirmos on 8. Katavasia: the Heirmos of the day, both choirs together. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the day and Ikos, and Sessional Hymn of the day; Glory... Both now: of the feast, and reading of the feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the feast and Ikos, and Prologue, and Synaxarion. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the refrains of the feast as was prescribed before; at the end, the Heirmos: "The Master's hospitality:" Then the refrain of the feast and Heirmos. Exapostilarion of the feast; Glory: of the day; Both now: of the feast. At the Praises, Stichera of the feast on 6; Glory: of the feast; Both now: of the day. After this, "Glory to God in the highest," and litanies. Then at the Aposticha, Stichera of the day with their verses; Glory: of the day; Both now: of the feast. After "It is good to give praise," and after "Our Father," Troparion of the day; Glory... Both now: of the feast. Litany: "Have mercy on us, O God;" and dismissal. Then: "Come, let us worship," thrice; and the First Hour: whereat the Troparion, and the Paremia of the day, and the catechesis of the feast, and dismissal.

At the 5th hour of the day [11:00 AM], there takes place the procession outside the monastery.[^2966] And having returned, we sing the Hours in the narthex: the 3rd, 6th, and 9th. At them we say the Troparion of the feast; Glory: of the day; Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion of the Hours. After the "Our Father," we alternate the Kontakia: "Remember us, O Lord," and the rest. The other choir: after the "Our Father," Kontakion of the feast; "Lord, have mercy," 40; and the prayer: "All-Holy Trinity;" and dismissal. "I will bless the Lord at all times:" we do not say.

In the evening, the bell tolls for the great bell and for all the heavy bells at the 8th hour of the day [2:00 PM], and we sing Vespers. At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera Idiomela of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day; Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. Of the feast. Entrance with the Gospel. "O Joyful Light;" and the reading of the day; and of the feast, two readings. Little Litany; then the Trisagion. Prokeimenon of the day, and of the feast. Epistle of the day, and of the feast; and the rest of the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great.

Instead of "It is truly meet," the Heirmos: "The Master's hospitality." In temples of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos, the Heirmos: "As unto an animated ark of God." Instead of the Cherubic Hymn, we sing the Troparion, Tone 6: "Of Thy mystical supper," thrice; the same we sing also instead of the Communion Hymn, once.[^2967]

And when the brethren receive Communion, we sing, instead of "The Body of Christ:" the same: "Of Thy mystical supper," until the brethren have communicated; and instead of "Let our mouths be filled:" once, with the Alleluias.

At the refectory we do not grant dispensation for fish, but only for olive oil and wine.[^2968]

## CONCERNING THE SAME FEAST, IF IT FALL ON GREAT FRIDAY

On Thursday evening, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the day 5, and of the feast 5; Glory: of the day: "O brood of vipers;" Both now and for ever and unto ages of ages. Amen. ## IT BEHOOVES TO KNOW ALSO THIS[^3002]

*That the Pillars of the Sunday Matins Gospels begin from the 1st Sunday, that is, of All Saints, and are reckoned according to the Tones of the Oktoechos, beginning from the 2nd Sunday; and they extend together with the Tones even unto Palm Sunday, taking away nothing, taking up [extra] or leaving behind [omitting] nothing: as can be seen in the Oktoechos, before the Gospel Stichera, and in the Horologion under each letter of the Visual Paschalion, arranged in 6 pillars.[^3003] Which can never all be completed, yet they are laid down for this reason, that so far as needed, they may serve unto Palm Sunday.[^3004]*

*And that which in the Liturgikon is set down concerning the taking up, or sometimes leaving behind of the Tones, understand this concerning the Liturgical Epistles and Gospels: for they alone up to the Sunday of the Publican, following [in their order], are sometimes superfluous, when it is necessary to take up, or to leave behind, but not the Tones, nor the Matins Gospels.[^3005]*

This final canonical rubric of the 1907 Lviv Pentecostarion regulates the correlation between the eleven Matins Resurrection Gospels (*столпѝ Є҆ѵⷢ҇лїй воскре́сныхъ ꙋ҆́тренныхъ*), the eight Tones of the Oktoechos, and the liturgical Epistle and Gospel pericopes read at the Divine Liturgy.
[^3003]: The 11 Sunday Matins Gospels rotate cyclically starting on All Saints Sunday (1st Sunday after Pentecost) with Gospel 1 (Matthew 28:16-20) in Tone 8. Beginning with the 2nd Sunday after Pentecost (Tone 1, Gospel 2), the 8 Tones and 11 Gospels advance in uninterrupted progression throughout the entire liturgical year through Great Lent up to Palm Sunday (*Недѣ́лѧ Цвѣтоно́сїѧ*), regardless of calendar shifts or the date of Pascha. The six pillars (*столпы̀*) represent the six complete 11-week cycles tabulated in the Visual Paschalion (*Зрѧ́чаѧ Пасха́лїѧ*) of the Horologion (*Часосло́въ*).
[^3004]: Because the moveable date of Pascha yields between 32 and 37 weeks between Pentecost and Great Lent, a complete ecclesiastical year typically utilizes between 40 and 45 Sundays from All Saints to Palm Sunday. Thus, while 6 full cycles of 11 Gospels would total 66 Sundays—more than any single liturgical year contains—all six pillars are codified in the tables so that even in the longest possible liturgical year, the sequence never runs short before Palm Sunday.
[^3005]: A critical liturgical distinction between the Divine Liturgy and Matins: The table of "omissions and additions" (*ѡ҆ прїѧ́тїи и҆ ѡ҆ставле́нїи*) in the Liturgikon (*Слꙋже́бникъ / Лїтꙋргі́онъ*) applies strictly to the Liturgy Epistles and Gospels, which terminate their regular post-Pentecost series at the 32nd Sunday after Pentecost (Zacchaeus) or the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee (*Недѣ́лѧ ѡ҆ Мытарѣ̀ и҆ Фарисе́и*). When the date of Pascha requires adjustments (the "Lucan jump" and supplementary pericopes), only the Liturgy readings are skipped or repeated; the Sunday Oktoechos Tones and Matins Resurrection Gospels proceed without any omission or repetition straight through to Palm Sunday.
