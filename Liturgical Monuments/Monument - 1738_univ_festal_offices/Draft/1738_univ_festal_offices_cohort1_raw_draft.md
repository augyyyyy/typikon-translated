To the Very Reverend Fathers Protopresbyters, Reverend Presbyters, and to the entire Clergy of the Church abiding within our Eparchies: greeting in the Lord.  

Since the Holy Synod of Zamość, in the General Table of Feasts of the whole year, has designated for us also these present ones, that is to say,[^4] of the MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST, of the COMPASSION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS, and of the BLESSED HIEROMARTYR JOSAPHAT, and commanded that these be celebrated everywhere: Therefore, unto the Very Reverend Fathers Protopresbyters and Reverend Presbyters, We pastorally command that they announce all these Feasts in their churches unto the people belonging to their flock, and command them to keep them holy in every way, and themselves strictly follow these Feasts in the manner prescribed without alteration.  

Athanasius, Archbishop-Metropolitan of all Rus', with his own hand.[^5]

# PART I: OFFICE OF THE FEAST OF THE MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST

*The Office of the Feast of the Most Holy Mystery of the Eucharist.*[^6]  
*Celebrated on the Thursday after the Sunday of All Saints.*[^7]  
*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried", Stichera, Tone 5, Podoben: "On the Third Day" (Трїдне́венъ):*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
Revealing the great mystery of Thy becoming man unto the initiates as Thou didst recline at supper, O Lover of mankind, Thou didst say: "Eat the living Bread; this is My Body, and the Blood of incorruptible life."  

*Sticheron 2:*  
The Upper Room was shown forth as a heavenly tabernacle, wherein Christ accomplished the Passover and the Supper as an unbloody service for mankind; and the Table of the Mysteries accomplished there, a noetic altar.  

*Sticheron 3:*  
Having blessed the Bread—being Himself the Heavenly Bread—having given thanks unto the Father Who begat Him, He gave also the Cup to be drunk unto the Disciples, crying aloud: "Take, eat, this is My Body; and drink the Blood of incorruptible life."  

*Glory... Both now... Tone 4:*  
Come, ye faithful lovers of the feasts, let us clap our hands today at the saving solemnity for the world of the Living Bread and the Lamb of God Who takes away the sins and transgressions of the world; not in the old leaven of malice, but in the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth; having cleansed ourselves, therefore, let us worthily partake of Him, that we may obtain His eternal Kingdom.  

*Entrance, and the Prokeimenon of the day, then the Lections.*  

*A Reading from Exodus (Exodus 19:10–19 LXX):*[^8]  
The Lord said unto Moses: "Go unto this people, and testify unto them, and sanctify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments. And let them be ready against the third day; for on the third day the Lord will come down upon Mount Sinai before all the people. And thou shalt set bounds unto the people round about, saying: 'Take heed to yourselves, that ye go not up into the mount, or touch the border of it; whosoever touches the mount shall surely die. There shall not a hand touch it, for he shall surely be stoned, or shot through with an arrow; whether it be beast or man, it shall not live.' When the voice of the trumpet and the cloud depart from the mount, they shall go up upon the mountain." And Moses went down from the mount unto the people, and sanctified them; and they washed their garments. And he said unto the people: "Be ready against the third day; come not near a woman." And it came to pass on the third day in the morning, that there were voices and lightnings, and a thick cloud upon Mount Sinai; and the voice of the trumpet sounded exceedingly loud, so that all the people that were in the camp trembled. And Moses brought forth the people out of the camp to meet God; and they stood beneath the mount. And Mount Sinai was altogether on a smoke, because the Lord descended upon it in fire; and the smoke thereof ascended as the smoke of a furnace, and the whole people were greatly amazed. And the voices of the trumpet sounded forth, waxing exceedingly loud. Moses spoke, and God answered him with a voice.

*A Reading from the Third Book of Kingdoms (3 Kingdoms 19:3–8 LXX):*[^10]  
The word of the Lord came unto Elijah, saying: "Go hence eastward, and hide thyself by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan. And it shall be, that thou shalt drink water of the brook; and I have commanded the ravens to feed thee there." And Elijah went and did according to the word of the Lord, and sat by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan. And the ravens brought him bread in the morning, and flesh in the evening; and he drank water of the brook. And it came to pass after a while, that the brook dried up, because there had been no rain upon the earth. And the word of the Lord came unto Elijah, saying: "Arise, and go to Zarephath of Sidon, and dwell there; behold, I have commanded a widow woman there to sustain thee." And he arose and went to Zarephath to the gate of the city; and behold, the widow woman was there gathering sticks; and Elijah called to her, and said unto her: "Fetch me, I pray thee, a little water in a vessel, that I may drink." And she went to fetch it; and Elijah cried after her, and said: "Bring me, I pray thee, a morsel of bread in thine hand." And the woman said: "As the Lord my God lives, if I have a cake, but only a handful of meal in a barrel, and a little oil in a cruse; and behold, I am gathering two sticks, that I may go in and dress it for me and my children, that we may eat it, and die." And Elijah said unto her: "Fear not; go and do according to thy word; but make me thereof a little cake first, and bring it unto me, and after make for thee and for thy children. For thus says the Lord God of Israel: 'The barrel of meal shall not fail, neither shall the cruse of oil diminish, until the day that the Lord sends rain upon the face of the earth.'" And the woman went and did according to the word of Elijah, and gave unto him, and she and he and her children did eat. And from that day the barrel of meal wasted not, neither did the cruse of oil fail, according to the word of the Lord, which He spoke by the hand of Elijah.

*The Reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Corinthians (pericope 144 / 1 Cor. 11:23–32):*[^16]  
Brethren: I have received of the Lord that which also I delivered unto you, that the Lord Jesus the same night in which He was betrayed took bread: and when He had given thanks, He broke it, and said: "Take, eat; this is My Body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of Me." After the same manner also He took the cup, when He had supped, saying: "This cup is the new testament in My Blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of Me." For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do show the Lord's death till He come. Therefore whosoever shall eat this bread, or drink this cup of the Lord, unworthily, shall be guilty of the Body and Blood of the Lord. But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of that bread, and drink of that cup. For he that eats and drinks unworthily, eats and drinks judgment to himself, not discerning the Lord's Body. For this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep. For if we would judge ourselves, we should not be judged. But when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord, that we should not be condemned with the world.

*ON THE SAME THURSDAY EVENING [AT VESPERS]*  

*On "Lord, I have cried", we set 4 Stichera, on 6, repeating 2.*  

*Stichera, Tone 6, Automela:*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
The pre-eternal High Priest after the order of Melchizedek, our Lord Jesus Christ, offered bread and wine at His Mystical Supper, offering Himself unto God the Father for the sins of the human race; Whom today, as the true God, we festively worship.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
The Lord, being compassionate and merciful, has given food unto them that fear Him, saying unto His Disciples: "Take, eat, this is My Body; drink of it, all of you, this is My Blood of the New Testament, which is shed for you for the remission of sins."  

*Sticheron 3:*  
The Lord has made a remembrance of His marvelous works, for the sake of His mercy and compassion; in these Mysteries of His Flesh and Blood, He has given His Flesh for food and His Blood for drink. O great power! The works of the Lord are sought out in all His will; holy and terrible is His Name, which is above every name, that at the Name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth.  

**[Right Page — Fol. 7r]**

*Sticheron 4:*  
"Keep the Passover with My Disciples"; and they did as was commanded, and prepared the Passover; and as they were eating, Jesus took Bread, and having blessed and broken it, He gave it unto the Disciples, saying: "Take, eat, this is My Body; drink of it, this is My Blood, for the remission of sins."  

*Sticheron 5:*  
When His father David brought the Ark from the house of Obed-edom into his own city, playing upon trumpets, timbrels, and cymbals, all the people accompanied it with joy; but the King danced, saying: "This Ark was a figure of the Ark of the Holy Things, of the Body and Blood of our Christ, Who today is worshipped by all"; Whom we also, worshipping as God and the Creator of all, implore that He may save our souls.  

*Sticheron 6:*  
The Lord fed His Disciples with heavenly food; before His Passion He gave them the Bread of Angels, surpassing marvelous beyond the manna: "Your fathers," said the Lord, "ate manna in the wilderness, and are dead; this is the Bread which comes down from heaven, that if any man eat thereof, he shall not die, but shall live forever"; of Which make us also now worthy to partake, for the sake of the prayers of Thy Mother, the Theotokos.  

*Glory... Both now... Tone 3:*  
O marvelous wisdom! O ineffable mercy of God toward us! Beautifully God was pleased not only to become man for our sake and conceal His Godhead in the cloud of the body, but He even hid Himself in the bread, and gave Himself unto men for food: glory to Thy divine compassion!

*ON MONDAY AT MATINS, AT THE APOSTICHA*[^19]  

*Stichera, Tone 4, Podoben: "As One Valiant" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
O Lover of mankind, Christ our Savior, Who for our salvation didst come down from heaven and was incarnate of the Virgin Mary, willing of Thine own accord to go unto death: Thou didst institute this divine Mystery, giving Thyself for food unto the Disciples and unto all the faithful; unto whom grant grace and life eternal.  

*Verse:* And He rained down manna upon them to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
"I am the Bread of life," said the Lord unto His Disciples, having come down from heaven: "I am the Bread of life; he that comes to Me shall never hunger, and he that believes on Me shall never thirst; for if any man eat of this Bread, he shall live forever; and the Bread that I will give is My Flesh, which gives eternal life unto them that partake."  

*Verse:* Man did eat the bread of angels; He sent them food to the full.  

*Sticheron 3:*  
Beholding the mystery of the divine Flesh, the supernatural Bread having come down and become incarnate of the Virgin Mother, let us bow down with tenderness; for this is the innocent Lamb Who takes away the sins of the world and saves our souls.  

*Glory... Both now... Tone 2:*  
All creation was amazed at the dread divine condescension; for God, Who is uncontainable by heaven and earth, is wholly contained within a small particle of bread, with His Godhead, Flesh, and divine Spirit; Whom thus confessing, we worship the divine Body of Christ, that we may obtain a blessing from Him.  

*ON MONDAY EVENING: AT "LORD, I HAVE CRIED"*  

*Stichera (repeating 3), Tone 5, Automela:*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
Come, all ye heavenly Angels and all ye powers of God, let us praise together with us God Who has rejoiced; Who for the sake of mercy toward the human race:  

**[Right Page — Fol. 8r (p. 4)]**

by His unsearchable divine wisdom has shut up Himself within the Mysteries of bread, and left Himself for food unto men; Whom today as God let us festively hymn, saying: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God, glory to Thy tender compassion!  

*Sticheron 2:*  
Come, all ye ends of the earth, draw near touching the Body of God, which was broken for us and for our sins, and given over unto death, and after the Resurrection was taken up into the heavens, seated in glory at the right hand of God the Father, and in these Mysteries on earth was likewise left unto the faithful; falling down before which upon the face of the earth, all we earthborn mortals praise with tenderness.  

*Sticheron 3:*  
Come, ye assemblies of feast-lovers, come and let us form a choir, come let us crown the Church of Christ, having festively adorned her today; in which Christ the supernatural Bread is seen, and is given unto men for food; Whom all we faithful worship with fear and gladness, and with the choirs of heavenly powers let us praise, saying: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O Lamb of God, Who takest away the sins of the world!  

*Glory... Both now... Tone the same (Tone 5):*  
Come, all ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord together with us, Who is worshipped here: the Sun, Moon, and Stars, Fire, Sea, Air, Clouds, and all creation; for God has exceedingly and unto the end loved the world and the human race: for whose sake He emptied Himself, taking the form of a servant; and in these Mysteries He emptied His Godhead and concealed His humanity, where as bread He is seen, but as God He is known: Whom worshipping with fear and trembling, we confess: That He is Christ, the Son of the living God, and the Savior of our souls.

*Sticheron 1:*  
O house of David, sing the psalm: for behold, the Ark of the Most High, in which is contained Christ the God of Israel, Who contains all things!  

*Verse:* The Lord has sent redemption unto His people.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
He Who gave manna in the wilderness for food unto His people, the same today is seen veiled under the Bread, and is given unto the faithful for food, saving our souls.
