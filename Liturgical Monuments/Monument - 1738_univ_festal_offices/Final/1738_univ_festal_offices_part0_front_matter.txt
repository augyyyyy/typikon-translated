# 1738 Univ Monastery Festal Offices / Vozslidovaniia Prazdnykom (Вослідованія праздником, Унів: Друкарня монастиря, 1738) <a id="1738-univ-monastery-festal-offices-vozslidovaniia-prazdnykom"></a>

*(Binding — Front cover: brown paper over boards with cloth clasp remnants) (Pastedown — Inside front cover with archival registry)*[^1]
MKDU  
KN-46906  
SD-707 *FRONT MATTER: TITLE PAGE:*

**ORDER OF THE OFFICES FOR THE FEASTS**  [^2]
**OF THE MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST,**  
**OF THE COMPASSION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS,**  
**AND OF THE BLESSED HIEROMARTYR**  
**JOSAPHAT,**  
**DELIVERED BY THE HOLY SYNOD OF ZAMOŚĆ.**  

Printed at the Monastery of Univ  
of the Order of Saint Basil the Great.  

In the year from the Incarnation of God the Word, 1738.

ATHANASIUS ON SHEPTYTSI SHEPTYTSKY,[^3]  
BY THE MERCY OF GOD AND OF THE HOLY  
APOSTOLIC THRONE  
ARCHBISHOP-METROPOLITAN OF KYIV  
AND OF ALL RUS',  
BISHOP OF LVIV, HALYCH, AND KAMIANETS-PODILSKYI, ARCHIMANDRITE OF THE KYIV CAVES AND OF UNIV.  

To the Very Reverend Fathers Protopresbyters, Reverend Presbyters, and to the entire Clergy of the Church abiding within our Eparchies: greeting in the Lord.  

Since the Holy Synod of Zamość, in the General Table of Feasts of the whole year, has designated for us also these present ones, that is to say,[^4] of the MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST, of the COMPASSION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS, and of the BLESSED HIEROMARTYR JOSAPHAT, and commanded that these be celebrated everywhere: Therefore, unto the Very Reverend Fathers Protopresbyters and Reverend Presbyters, We pastorally command that they announce all these Feasts in their churches unto the people belonging to their flock, and command them to keep them holy in every way, and themselves strictly follow these Feasts in the manner prescribed without alteration.  

Athanasius, Archbishop-Metropolitan of all Rus', with his own hand.[^5]

### Notes

[^1]: Inscription in twentieth-century archival pencil on the front pastedown: *мкду КН-46906 СД-707*, representing the catalog shelfmark of the Museum of the Book and Printing of Ukraine (Kyiv).
[^2]: The title page bears the imprint of the Basilian Printing House of Univ Holy Dormition Monastery (*Типомъ издашася въ Манастырѣ Уневскомъ Чину Святаго Василіа Великаго*), dated 1738 (*Въ лѣто ѿ Воплощеніа Бга Слова ҂аѱли*).
[^3]: Athanasius Sheptytsky (*Аѳанасій на Шептичахъ Шептицкій*, 1686–1746), Metropolitan of Kyiv, Galicia, and all Rus' (1729–1746), Bishop of Lviv, Halych, and Kamianets-Podilskyi, and Archimandrite of Univ and Kyiv Caves Monasteries.
[^4]: Church Slavonic: *настоящїа тїесть* ('these present ones, that is to say', calquing Polish *to jest* / Latin *id est*), introducing the three feasts promulgated by Title III (*De Cultu Sanctorum et Festis*) of the 1720 Synod of Zamość: the Most Holy Mystery of the Eucharist, the Compassion of the Most Holy Theotokos, and the Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat.
[^5]: Church Slavonic: *р. в.*, an abbreviation for the Polish-Ruthenian chancery formula *ręką własną* / *рукою власною* ('with his own hand'), denoting the personal sign-manual of Archbishop-Metropolitan Athanasius Sheptytsky.
