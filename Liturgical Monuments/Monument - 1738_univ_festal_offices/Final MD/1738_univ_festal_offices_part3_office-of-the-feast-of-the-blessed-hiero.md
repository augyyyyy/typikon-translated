# OFFICE OF THE FEAST OF THE BLESSED HIEROMARTYR JOSAPHAT

*Celebrated on the 16th Day of the Month of September.*[^25]  

*If it occur on a Sunday, on Saturday at Vespers we recite the Kathisma "Blessed is the man", and we sing at "Lord, I have cried": Resurrection Stichera of the Tone, 4; and of the Saint, 6. But if on another day, we sing the Stichera of the Feast of the Exaltation, and of the Saint:*  

*Stichera unto the Saint, Tone 1, Podoben: "O All-Praised Martyrs" (Prekhvalnii muchenitsy):*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
Stained with martyr's blood upon thy sacred vestment, thou didst enter into the Holy of Holies, O crowned head; where are the voices of them that feast, from Whom for thy labors thou dost receive the victories: an unfading crown of eternal beauty, and paradise-dwelling, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; and now pray that there be granted unto the Churches union and peace, and unto our souls great mercy.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
First thou didst offer unto God the unbloody sacrifice, as a divine Hierarch; as a fruitful, acceptable sacrifice, by thine own blood thou didst bring thyself unto Christ as a true Hieromartyr, O divinely-speaking, all-honorable one; and now pray that there be granted unto the Churches union and peace, and unto our souls great mercy.  

*Sticheron 3:*  
Having cast down the schismatic eating by thy hierarchal word, and having strengthened by faith the hearts of the unconfirmed, thou didst pass unto life eternal, having suffered for union: a witness of the Lord thou wast, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; and now pray that there be granted unto the Churches union and peace, and unto our souls great mercy.  

*Sticheron 4:*  
Having dyed thy sacred vesture with the blood of thy sacred flesh, O holy father Hierarch; a double crown from Christ thou hast received, all-rich one, and with the Angels thou dancest in the heavens, standing before the Holy Trinity; Whom do thou now pray that there be granted unto the Churches union and peace, and unto our souls great mercy.  

*Sticheron 5:*  
O Hieromartyr Josaphat, what an honorable life hast thou desired! And unto union thou hast guided the people by thine honorable words, and by grace thou hast passed unto eternal things, having washed thyself with suffering blood having washed thyself with suffering blood, O blessed one; and now pray that there be granted unto the Churches union and peace, and unto our souls great mercy.

*Sticheron 6:*  
A goodly guiding cloud, entering noetically into the unutterable light, as a Hieromartyr and Shepherd, thou wast taught the ineffable mysteries of Christ; as a sun enlightened by God, as a Shepherd mystically instructed, therefore also promised double crowns of glory, O favorite of God; and now pray that there be granted unto the Churches union and peace, and unto our souls great mercy.  

*Glory... Tone 6:*  
A lawful priest, even unto thy departure wast thou, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: for thou didst celebrate the divine ineffable mysteries, and didst pour out thy blood for Christ God, offering an acceptable sacrifice; therefore having boldness unto Him, pray fervently for them that with faith keep thy most honorable memory and honor it, that thy flock and the whole world may be delivered from assaults, from all foreign nations, and from all tribulations and distresses.  

*Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, the same Tone. (If it be on Saturday: the Dogmatic Theotokion of the Tone; but if on another day: Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation).*  

*After the Entrance, the Prokeimenon of the day, then the Lections.*  

*A Reading from the Proverbs of Solomon (Proverbs 10:7, 3:13–16, 8:6, 34, 4, 12, 14, 17, 5–9 LXX):*  
The memory of the righteous is celebrated with praises, and the blessing of the Lord is upon his head. Blessed is the man that finds wisdom, and mortal man who understands prudence. For the merchandise thereof is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold. She is more precious than costly stones; and all honorable things are not worthy of her. Length of days and years of life are in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and glory. Out of her mouth proceeds righteousness, and law and mercy she carries upon her tongue. Give ear therefore unto me, O children, for I speak honorable things; and blessed is the man that keeps my ways. For my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and will shall be prepared from the Lord. For this cause I entreat you, and put forth my voice unto the sons of men: for I, wisdom, have established counsel and understanding, and knowledge I have called upon. Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom; mine is understanding, and strength is mine. I love them that love me; and those that seek me shall find grace. Understand therefore, O simple ones, subtlety, and ye uninstructed, apply your hearts. Give ear unto me again, for I speak honorable things; and I will open from my lips right things. For my throat shall meditate truth, and lying lips are an abomination before me. All the words of my mouth are with righteousness; there is nothing crooked or perverse in them. They are all plain to him that understands, and right to them that find knowledge. For I teach you truth, that your hope may be in the Lord, and ye may be filled with the Spirit.  

*A Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Wisdom 4:7–15 LXX):*  
When the righteous man is praised, the peoples will praise him; for his memory is immortality, because he is known both by the Lord and by men, and his soul was well-pleasing unto God. Love wisdom therefore, O men, and live; desire her, and ye shall be instructed. For the beginning of her is love, and the keeping of laws. Honor wisdom, that ye may reign forever; I will tell you, and will not hide from you the mysteries of God. For He is the guide of wisdom, and the directer of the wise; and in every understanding and work an artificer, and with all understanding wisdom will teach: for in her is an understanding and holy spirit, the brightness of the everlasting light, and the unspotted mirror of the power of God. She makes men friends of God and prophets; she is more beautiful than the sun, and above all order of stars: being compared with the light, she is found before it. She delivers them that please her from afflictions, and directs them into straight paths. She gives them to have holy understanding, and preserves them from them that lie in wait, and gives them a sore conflict. That they may understand all, that godliness is stronger than all, and malice shall never overcome wisdom, neither shall judgment passing by let the wicked go unrebuked. For they said within themselves, reasoning not rightly: "Let us oppress the righteous man, and let us not spare his holiness, neither let us respect the ancient gray hairs of the aged. Let our strength be the law. Let us lie in wait for the righteous, because he is displeasing unto us, and is contrary to our doings; he upbraids us with our offending the law, and objects to our infamy the transgressions of our education. He professes to have the knowledge of God, and he calls himself the servant of the Lord. He was made to reprove our thoughts, and he is grievous unto us: we see that his life is not like other men's, his ways are of another fashion. We are esteemed of him as counterfeits; he abstains from our ways as from filthiness; he pronounces the end of the just to be blessed. Let us see if his words be true, and let us prove what shall happen in the end of him. With despitefulness and torture let us examine him, that we may know his meekness, and prove his patience. Let us condemn him with a shameful death; for by his own saying he shall be respected." Such things did they imagine, and were deceived; for their own malice blinded them. As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not, and considered not that Thou art the only God, having power of life and death, and saving in time of affliction, and delivering from all evil: compassionate and merciful, and giving grace unto His holy ones, and with His arm resisting the proud.

*A Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Wisdom 4:16–5:7 LXX):*  
Though the righteous be prevented with death, yet shall he be in rest. Thus the righteous that dies shall condemn the ungodly which are living. For they shall see the end of the wise, and shall not understand what God in His counsel has decreed of him: that the Lord shall cast down the ungodly speechless, and shall shake them from the foundation; and they shall be utterly laid waste in sorrow, and their memorial shall perish. For they shall come with fear unto the reckoning of their sins, and their own iniquities shall convince them to their face. Then shall the righteous man stand in great boldness before the face of such as have afflicted him, and made no account of his labors. When they see it, they shall be troubled with terrible fear, and shall be amazed at the strangeness of his salvation. And they repenting and groaning for anguish of spirit shall say within themselves: "This was he whom we had sometimes in derision, and a proverb of reproach, fools that we were! We accounted his life madness, and his end to be without honor: how is he numbered among the children of God, and his lot is among the saints! Therefore have we erred from the way of truth, and the light of righteousness has not shined unto us, and the sun of understanding rose not upon us. We wearied ourselves in the way of wickedness and destruction; yea, we have gone through deserts, where there lay no way, but as for the way of the Lord, we have not known it."  

*AT THE LITIYA, Stichera of the Feast of the Exaltation, Tone 1:*  
Now truly the prophetic word of David has received its fulfillment: for behold, manifestly we worship the footstool of Thine all-pure feet, and trusting under the shadow of Thy wings, O all-compassionate Son, we cry aloud unto Thee: let the light of Thy countenance be marked upon us; exalt the horn of the Orthodox people, for the sake of the Exaltation of Thy Precious Cross, O Christ abundant in mercy.

*Stichera unto the Saint, Tone 1:*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
With divine sacred hymns, come, all ye people, let us spiritually keep festival in honor of the Hieromartyr Josaphat, who cut down the thorns of schism, and planted for souls saving teachings, bearing fruit unto the blossom of holiness through virtues.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
Having celebrated the divine Gospel of God, having sealed the divine teachings with thy blood shed by schismatic sheep, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, thou didst escape their snares, and as a victor didst enter into heaven, receiving the crown of victory.  

*Sticheron 3:*  
A step of ascents the Church acquired through thy sacred struggles, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, which thou also keepest unharmed and unassailed by mighty wolves, who proclaim thy valiant deeds and magnify thee, O blessed one.  

*Glory... Tone 5:*  
Radiant is thy solemnity; the Church makes an assembly for thee, rejoicing; O Hieromartyr Josaphat, mystically honoring thee: do thou therefore as a Shepherd cease not to pray for us all.  

*Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, the same Tone: "Come, ye peoples, seeing the marvelous wonder, let us worship the power of the Cross..."*  

*If it occur on a Sunday, we sing: the Resurrection Aposticha; Glory... unto the Saint; Both now... unto the Feast. But if on another day, we sing all unto the Saint.*  

*At the Aposticha, Stichera unto the Saint, Tone 4, Podoben: "As One Valiant" (Yako doblia):*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
As a many-splendored sun, spiritually thou hast shone forth upon the firmament of the Church, enlightening souls darkened by the malice of the enemy, through the radiance of words and God-wise teaching, and by a life equal to the Angels; and having guided the wandering into the way of truth, and drawn them unto the union of the Orthodox Catholic Church, therefore today with a loud voice we celebrate thy radiant and holy memory.  

*Verse:* Thy priests shall be clothed with righteousness, and Thy venerable ones shall rejoice.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
Equal in honor unto the Apostles wast thou, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, having received a mind of equal zeal, not fearing the cruelty of wicked tormentors, but manfully preaching union with boldness, Thou didst endure manifold wounds and tortures, and didst receive great reward for thy long-suffering from the one Umpire of the contest, Christ our God; Whom do Thou fervently entreat, on behalf of all that with faith honor thy holy memory.

*Verse:* Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saint and His venerable one.  

*Sticheron 3:*  
Offering an honorable liturgy, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, and an unblemished sacrifice unto good Christ God, a whole burnt offering complete thou didst bring of thyself unto Him: having suffered manfully for the sake of His love, and shed thy blood, as a true Shepherd and heir of Christ, crying out unceasingly: "Father, keep them in Thy name, that they may be one"; therefore also now, all-blessed Josaphat, pray that there be granted unto the Churches union, and that all of us who abide in union may be saved.  

*Glory... Tone 4:*  
Thy good works have gone forth like the sun in heaven and upon earth, O favorite of Christ, all-blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat. For orthodoxly thou didst preach unto us the true and unblemished faith of Christ; for whose sake also as a good Shepherd thou didst lay down thy life, as a true heir of the Chief Shepherd, Christ our God; Whom do thou entreat, O all-blessed Hierarch, in thy commemoration, O God-bearer, that all who with love celebrate the all-honorable memory of thy suffering may be saved.  

*Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, the same Tone: "That which Moses prefigured in himself of old..."*  

*If on a Sunday, Resurrection Troparion; Glory... unto the Saint; Both now... unto the Feast. But if on another day, Troparion of the Saint, Tone 4:*  

*Troparion of the Saint, Tone 4:*  
A radiant lamp hast thou been shown forth, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; for like a good Shepherd, thou didst lay down thy life for union; slain by apostate enemies, thou hast entered into the Holy of Holies, dwelling together with the Bodiless Hosts; therefore we entreat thee, O much-suffering one: pray unto the Chief Shepherd, Christ, to number us also with the flock of sheep at His right hand, and to save our souls.  

*Glory... Both now... "Save, O Lord, Thy people" At Matins, after the 1st Kathisma: Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "Of Wisdom" (Premudrosti):*
Having forsaken all earthly things, and lived in the world like an Angel, in preparation of spirit thou wast a partaker with the Angels; for having put to death the present bodily passions, by holy labors thou wast shown forth a favorite of God, O all-blessed one; therefore thou healest the passions of the sick, and castest out evil spirits by the grace of Christ, O God-bearing Father, our Hieromartyr Josaphat: pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto them that with love celebrate thy holy memory.  

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, the same Tone: "The wood alone brought forth death..."*  

*After the 2nd Kathisma: Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Joseph Was Amazed" (Udivisia Iosif):*  
Having made thy heart a beautiful altar, thou didst offer unbloody priestly sacrifices unto God; and having suffered strongly from wicked enemies, and endured manifold tortures, an acceptable sacrifice wast thou offered unto Christ our God Who sacrificed Himself for our sake, O all-blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat; Whom do thou fervently entreat always, that all who with love praise thee may be saved.  

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, Tone and Podoben the same: "Moses, stretching forth his hands unto the height..."*  

*If it occur on a Sunday, all the Sessional Hymns are of the Resurrection. Then: "Praise ye the Name of the Lord..."*  

*We sing the Magnification:*  
We magnify thee, O Hieromartyr of Christ, Josaphat, and we honor thy precious sufferings, which thou didst endure for union from the schismatic assembly.  

*Verses:*  
*1. Hear this, all ye nations, give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world.*  
*2. My mouth shall speak of wisdom, and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding.*  
*3. The righteous man shall flourish like the palm tree, and shall be multiplied like the cedar in Lebanon.*  
*4. Blessed are they that dwell in Thy house; they will praise Thee unto ages of ages.*  
*Glory... Both now... Alleluia, thrice.*  

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*  
Having cast down the haughtiness of schismatic enemies, as a good Shepherd and mighty warrior, O blessed one, thou didst receive the crown of victory from the right hand of the Most High, the Chief Shepherd, Christ our God; therefore we call thee blessed today as a good Shepherd and victorious Sufferer, with faith keeping thine unceasing memory, O Hieromartyr of Christ, Josaphat, much-suffering one.  

*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, Tone the same, Podoben: "Sent forth secretly" (Poslannoe taino): "In Paradise of old the tree stripped me bare..."*  

*If it be not Sunday, the Gradual Hymns, Tone 4, 1st Antiphon. Prokeimenon, Tone 4: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saint and His venerable one. Verse: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He has rendered unto me? The Gospel from John, Pericope 35 from the half: "I am the Door...". After Psalm 50: Glory... Through the prayers of the Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, cleanse the multitude of our transgressions; Both now... Through the prayers of the Ever-Virgin Theotokos...*  
*Then the Sticheron unto the Saint, Tone 6: "A Lawful Priest", (see fol. 13v)[^26] at "Lord, I have cried".*  
*If it occur on a Sunday: the Resurrection Antiphons, Prokeimenon, Gospel, and the rest are sung; we sing the Resurrection Canon with the Heirmos, and of the Theotokos, then of the Feast and of the Saint, Katavasia of the Feast. But if on another day, we sing the Canon of the Feast of the Exaltation, and of the Saint.*  

*Canon to the Hieromartyr Josaphat*  
*Tone 2. Ode 1. Heirmos:* Come, ye peoples, let us sing a song unto Christ...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
As a God-radiant star the spiritual Sun showed thee forth upon the height of His Church; for by thy sufferings and wonders thou hast illuminated the universe.  

*Troparion 2:*  
From early youth thou wast dedicated unto God, O Josaphat; by God-given virtues thou didst enlighten thy soul, and with honorable sufferings for the sake of union didst adorn it.  

*Troparion 3:*  
Thou didst shine forth radiantly in the Church of Christ with concordant teaching, O newly-manifest Hieromartyr; therefore also the Benefactor of all has hierarchally crowned thee with a crown of victories.  

*Theotokion:*  
O Most Holy Theotokos, who beyond nature didst bear the pre-eternal and transcendent Word Who became incarnate: we praise thee.  
*Katavasia: "Moses inscribed the Cross..."*  

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* Establish me upon the rock of faith...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Offering an unblemished, awesome, and unbloody sacrifice unto the Lord, thyself also unto slaughter by blood, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, unstained thou didst bring.  

*Troparion 2:*  
Conformed unto Christ, for the sake of union thou didst surrender thy life, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, and the walls of schism thou didst tear down.  

*Troparion 3:*  
The array of wisdom of the schismatics thou didst destroy, O blessed Josaphat, having in thy heart the subsistent Wisdom of God, wherewith by strength thou didst strike down their feeble arrogance.  

*Theotokion:*  
Grant us help by thy prayers, O All-Pure, driving away the assaults of bitter afflictions.  
*Katavasia: "The rod is taken as an image of the mystery..."*  

*Kontakion of the Feast of the Exaltation, Tone 4: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross..."*  
*Sessional Hymn unto the Saint, Tone 4:*  
Thou didst burn up the error of the schismatics, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; well didst thou multiply the faith through union, and enlighten the world; whereby thou wast shown forth a sign-bearer and sufferer, a pillar of the Church, and a true Shepherd: pray unceasingly unto Christ, that all of us may be saved.  
*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, Tone 4: "Thou hast made manifest the form of the Cross in extremities..."*  

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* I have heard the report of Thy coming...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Guiding the flock of Christ, O Hieromartyr, unto the light of union, thou didst drive away the darkness of schism; and having suffered for union, receiving the martyr's crown, crying: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!  

*Troparion 2:*  
The refuge of the thrice-radiant Godhead, the never-setting light, shone within thee, making thee a pure receptacle of divine gifts, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; therefore singing: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!  

*Troparion 3:*  
Walking along the paths of virtues, O Josaphat, thou hast reached the highest mansion, O blessed one, adorned with the anointing of the priesthood, and shining with martyrdom.  

*Theotokion:*  
Enlighten my darkened soul, O All-Pure, with thy light, as having borne the consubstantial Light.  
*Katavasia: "I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation" Ode 5. Heirmos:* O Giver of light and Creator of the ages...
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
"God is my light and the giver of understanding," cried Josaphat before the schismatics; "their threats I will not fear"; therefore having been struck on the head with a mace, now in the everlasting light he rejoices in the Lord.  

*Troparion 2:*  
Riches and radiant glory, length of days and life incorruptible, Christ the true Wisdom granted unto thee, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, for thou didst preeminently love Him.  

*Troparion 3:*  
Thou didst escape from the nets of them that hunt thee unto the heavenly nests; and dwelling together with the choirs of martyrs, remember us who abide in union, who joyfully celebrate thy memory, O blessed Josaphat.  

*Theotokion:*  
Calm the unsteady storm of my passions, O Theotokos, who didst bear God the Sustainer and Lord.  
*Katavasia: "O thrice-blessed Tree..."*  

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* Held fast in the depth of sin...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Unto the depths of the river Dvina the foolish schismatic people cast thy holy relics; yet there a pillar from heaven appeared, and unharmed, the people of the Union having found thee, took thee with joy, thanking God Who shows forth signs and wonders.  

*Troparion 2:*  
A divine harp of the Spirit, preaching through thee unto us the divine mysteries of union, the Shepherd of Polotsk and benefactor, the theological tongue: come, all ye who are united, let us honor him festively.  

*Troparion 3:*  
As a guide of union and a fountain of healings we recognize thee, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, confirmation of the Church, and citizen of heaven.  

*Theotokion:*  
A temple of God, and Ark, and animate bridal chamber, and heavenly gate, O Theotokos, we faithful magnify thee.  
*Katavasia: "The monster in the watery belly..."*  

*Kontakion unto the Saint, Tone 4:*  
Illuminated from thy youth with a fiery dawn from the crucified Christ, thou wast made like an Angel in the monastic life, and piously didst live in the hierarchal dignity; clearly having preached union, by thy martyr's blood thou didst extinguish the hearts of apostates inflamed with schism, and didst receive a crown from Christ; therefore remember us who cry unto thee: Rejoice, unshakable pillar of union!  

*Ikos:*  
As an Angel didst Thou appear upon earth, clad in flesh, proclaiming the ineffable mysteries of God; Thou didst confirm us to sing unto Thee, O speaker of divine words, these things: Rejoice, through whom the darkness of apostates was confounded! Rejoice, mirror of the light of union! Rejoice, Angel of the transcendent Godhead! Rejoice, true reprover of their agreements! Rejoice, for well hast Thou accomplished the glory of God! Rejoice, who hast shown forth the radiant sun! Rejoice, dispenser of the cup of divine drink! Rejoice, for Thou hast received the martyrs' crown! Rejoice, for in the heavens before the throne of the Holy Trinity Thou dancest with the Angels! Rejoice, for Thou rejoicest with the Martyrs! Rejoice, through whom the truth is glorified! Rejoice, unshakable pillar of union!  

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* He Who the furnace of old...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Thou didst quench with the drops of thy blood, O all-glorious one, the furnace of error of the schismatics, and upon the water of life thou didst take up thine abode, crying aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!  

*Troparion 2:*  
The sword and arrows of the ill-reputed thou didst crush, and the foolish pride of the schismatics truly didst thou scatter, like a very great stone, O Hieromartyr, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!  

*Troparion 3:*  
By thy words and dogmas the united faith was sealed, and the bitterness of schism ceased, O glorious Josaphat; the overthrow of union was brought to naught, and the strength of the foolish was broken.  

*Theotokion:*  
Thou hast budded forth a Rod from the root of Jesse, O all-hymned Virgin, bearing fruit that blossoms for the holy ones who in faith cry aloud unto thy Son: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!  
*Katavasia: "The senseless decree of the wicked tyrant..."*  

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* The fiery furnace of old...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
With praises is thy memory celebrated, O speaker of divine words; for from divine inspiration thou wast taught knowledge of future things, victory in suffering, and thy departure unto the highest; and Angels escorted thy soul unto heaven, joyfully singing with the heavenly beings: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him unto ages!  

*Troparion 2:*  
Now hast thou stood with the theologians before the throne of the Almighty, as like unto them, of one mind, O all-blessed Josaphat: primate of Polotsk, unconquered champion of the united Church; therefore with righteous testimony and peace thou hast sealed it unto ages.  

*Troparion 3:*  
Now is the assembly of the schismatics overcome and distressed by thy deeds and words of peace, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; and like smoke their rotten folly vanished away, through thy thunder-voiced and theological tongue.

*Theotokion:*  
Supernaturally thou didst bear the Creator and Savior, God the Word, O Virgin, Whom we exalt unto all ages.  
*Katavasia: "Bless the Lord, ye children equal in number to the Trinity..."*  

*Ode 9. Heirmos:* God the Word, Who is from God...  
*Refrain:* Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Wholly filled with the higher wisdom, O all-glorious one, thou didst shine the light of union upon the world; thou didst conceive divine fear, and didst bring forth words of the Spirit, therefore thou wast the most radiant dwelling of the Holy Trinity; therefore we call thee blessed.  

*Troparion 2:*  
Armed with love and hope, and hedged round with faith, thou didst tear down the crafts and deceits of the enemy; and having become a victor, now thou dwellest in the highest with the divine choirs of Angels and Venerable Ones, around the throne of the King of all in heaven.  

*Troparion 3:*  
As thou hast boldness unto God, O Hieromartyr, remember fervently, O Josaphat, them that joyfully celebrate this thy divine festival, and with faith and praise honor thy precious and sacred suffering; and deliver us from tribulations and distresses.  

*Theotokion:*  
By Thine arm, O Christ, crush the insolent who oppose Thy Church, and establish our King,[^27] that we may unceasingly magnify Thee.  
*Katavasia: "A mystery art thou, O Theotokos, Paradise..."*  

*EXAPOSTILARION*  
Unmercifully the enemy severed thy head, O blessed Hierarch, whom Christ God crowned in the highest with a crown of victory for eternity, O much-suffering one, great lamp, adornment of Hierarchs, glory of Martyrs, and praise of all the Venerable Ones.  
*Glory... Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation: "The Cross is the preserver of the whole universe..."*  

*AT THE PRAISES*  
*Stichera, Tone 6:*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
Wholly from youth dedicated unto good Christ, having become separate from the flesh and from worldly love: chosen for the monastic life, O most sacred father Josaphat, and a receptacle of the Holy Spirit, thou didst preserve purity of soul and body, having lived on earth like an Angel among monks, in obedience and poverty, in love toward God and neighbor, in prayers and all-night vigils, being an example and mirror for all monks and a mirror of every virtue, thou didst fix thy mind upon heavenly things, to behold the ineffable beauty of Christ the King and God, Whom having loved with all thy heart, and strengthened by His power, thou didst endure many afflictions and torments: Whom do thou with boldness entreat for our souls.

*Sticheron 2:*  
Sitting upon the hierarchal throne, O all-blessed one, thou didst adorn thyself with great virtues, and by teaching and deeds didst reveal us unto repentance and salvation. Therefore, armed with purity and fervent prayers, with fasting and all-night vigil, and like good Christ gently drawing near unto passion and death, prostrated cruciformly upon the earth in prayer,[^28] thou didst prepare thyself for suffering, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; therefore the wicked enemies, coming upon thee like unmerciful beasts, unjustly tore thee in pieces, tenderhearted one, like a guileless lamb, who suffered valiantly and with boldness dost pray for our souls.

*Sticheron 3:*  
Thy fierce and wicked enemies, as Solomon of the Jews said in the Psalms,[^29] they that hate thee hated thee, and lifted up their head against thee, O blessed Josaphat; they dealt craftily of their own will, and having taken wicked counsel, said: "Come, and let us destroy him from among the nations, and his name shall be remembered no more"; as they took counsel with one mind, the mighty fell upon thee, and severed thy precious head, striking thee unmercifully and dragging thee into a ditch; they poured out thy blood like water throughout the whole city; likewise having tied stones to thy neck and to thy holy feet, from the hill they hurled and cast thee into the river's torrent; by reason of thy courage and long-suffering, for whose sake thou didst receive, O much-suffering Josaphat, an unfading crown.

*Glory... Tone 6:*  
Having loved Christ God from infancy, O all-blessed Josaphat, and for the sake of His love forsaken the world and all the beautiful things that are in the world: in every obedience, purity, and monastic poverty thou didst follow Him all the days of thy life, and didst truly please Christ God by deeds that cleanse us; and straightway as a good Shepherd thou didst prepare thyself manfully for suffering, and for the union of the Church of God thou didst endure many afflictions patiently from wicked men; reviled and beaten, and wounded with many stripes every day, struck upon the head, thou wast vouchsafed a crown from Christ God: entreat Him, O all-blessed one, for us who with love celebrate thine ever-honored memory, that we may be delivered from enemies visible and invisible, from assaults and attacks of the evil one, and from future judgment, and that our souls may be saved.

*Both now... Of the Feast of the Exaltation, the same Tone: "Today the Cross of the Lord goes forth..."*  
*Great Doxology, and Dismissal.*

*AT THE DIVINE LITURGY*  
*Prokeimenon, Tone 7:*  
Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saint and His venerable one.  
*Verse:* What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He has rendered unto me?  

*A Reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Hebrews (Hebrews 4:14–5:10, Pericope 334):*[^30]  
Brethren: Having a great Priest Who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession. For we have not an High Priest Who cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities, but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin. Let us therefore come with boldness unto the Throne of His grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need. For every High Priest taken from among men is ordained for men in things pertaining to God, that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins: who can have compassion on the ignorant, and on them that are out of the way; for that he himself also is compassed with infirmity. And by reason hereof he ought, as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins. And no man takes this honor unto himself, but he that is called of God, as was Aaron. So also Christ glorified not Himself to be made an High Priest, but He that said unto Him: "Thou art My Son, today have I begotten Thee." As He says also in another place: "Thou art a Priest forever after the order of Melchizedek." Who in the days of His flesh, having offered up prayers and supplications with strong crying and tears unto Him that was able to save Him from death, and was heard in that He feared; though He were a Son, yet learned He obedience by the things which He suffered; and being made perfect, He became the author of eternal salvation unto all them that obey Him: called of God an High Priest after the order of Melchizedek.

*Alleluia, Tone 4:*  
Thy priests shall be clothed with righteousness, and Thy venerable ones shall rejoice with joy.  
*Verse:* The righteous man shall flourish like the palm tree, and shall be multiplied like the cedar in Lebanon.  

*A Reading from the Holy Gospel according to John (John 10:9–16, Pericope 36):*  
The Lord said unto the Jews who came unto Him: "I am the door: by Me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture. The thief comes not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly. I am the good Shepherd: the good Shepherd lays down His life for the sheep. But he that is an hireling, and not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming, and leaves the sheep, and flees; and the wolf catches them, and scatters the sheep. And the hireling flees, because he is an hireling, and cares not for the sheep. I am the good Shepherd, and know Mine own, and am known of Mine. As the Father knows Me, even so know I the Father: and I lay down My life for the sheep. And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear My voice; and there shall be one flock, and one Shepherd."

*Communion Hymn:*  
The righteous man shall be in everlasting remembrance. Alleluia, thrice.

### Notes

[^25]: The feast of Saint Josaphat Kuntsevych, Archbishop of Polotsk (beatified in 1643 by Pope Urban VIII), was prescribed by the Synod of Zamość (1720) for September 16, within the Afterfeast of the Universal Exaltation of the Precious Cross.
[^26]: Fol. 13v refers to the Sticheron at "Lord, I have cried" of the Feast of Saint Josaphat, physical leaf p16.
[^27]: The petition for the King (*Короля нашего*) reflects the contemporary political sovereign of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth in 1738, King Augustus III (r. 1734–1763).
[^28]: The Church Slavonic reads *крⷭтоѡбра́зно возлегъ на землѝ мл҃твꙋ творѧ̀* ("lying cruciformly upon the earth making prayer"), referring to Saint Josaphat's well-documented ascetic practice of praying prostrated with arms outstretched in the form of the Cross.
[^29]: The hymn text attributes this citation to "Solomon of the Jews in the Psalms" (*ꙗ҆́коже Соло́мѡнъ І҆ꙋдѣ́ѡвъ речѐ въ Ѱалмѣ́хъ*); however, the quotation is taken directly from Psalm 82:3–5 LXX (Psalm 83:2–4 Masoretic): "For lo, Thine enemies have made a noise, and they that hate Thee have lifted up the head. They have taken crafty counsel against Thy people... They have said: Come, and let us destroy them from among the nations, and let the name of Israel be remembered no more."
[^30]: The printed Apostol rubric designates this reading as Pericope 334 (*Зача́ло тл҃д*), covering Hebrews 4:14–5:10. In standard Slavic liturgical Apostol pericope numbering, this reading corresponds to Pericope 314 (*Зача́ло т҃ді*), prescribed for the Common of a Hieromartyr.
[^31]: The rear flyleaf and pasteboard binding mark the physical completion of this copy of the 1738 Univ Monastery edition (*Вослѣдованія праздникомъ*, Univ: Basilian Monastery Press, 1738), preserved in the National Museum of the Book and Printing of Ukraine (Kyiv), Inventory No. SD-707.
