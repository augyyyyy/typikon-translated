# OFFICE OF THE FEAST OF THE COMPASSION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS [^23]

*Canon to the Theotokos, Tone 8.*  
*Ode 1. Heirmos:* Moses inscribed the Cross...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
When the Pure Virgin beheld suspended upon the Cross Her Son and Lord, tearing Herself asunder She cried aloud bitterly with the other women, groaning and saying: "I behold Thee now, my beloved and dearest Child, hanging upon the Cross, and I am pierced bitterly in heart," said the All-Pure: "yet grant a word, O good One, unto Thy handmaid."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 2:*  
"Willingly, O my Son and Creator, Thou sufferest a bitter death upon the Tree," the Virgin said, standing by the Cross with the beloved Disciple.  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
"Now am I bereaved of my solace, joy, and gladness, of my Son and Lord; woe is me with sickness in heart!" the Pure One weeping said.  
*Katavasia. Heirmos: "I shall open my mouth..."*  

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* The rod is taken as an image of the mystery...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
"For fear of the Jews Peter hid himself, and all the faithful fled, forsaking Christ," the Virgin lamenting said.  

*Troparion 2:*  
"At Thy strange and awesome birth, O my Son, I was magnified beyond all mothers; but woe is me, now seeing Thee upon the Tree, my heart is consumed with grief."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
"I look to receive from the Tree into my arms my very Child, in which I held Him as an Infant," said the Pure One, "yet alas for me, no one gives Him unto me."  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
"Behold, my sweet Light, my hope and good Life, my God is extinguished upon the Cross; my heart is consumed with grief," the Virgin groaning said.  
*Katavasia: "Thy hymn-singers..."*  

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*  
The flock which Thy Son and God acquired, O All-Pure One, sprinkling it with His Blood: deliver from afflictions by thy pure prayers; drive away visible and invisible enemies like wild beasts, and fill their faces with shame, even as David the singer of the Lord conquered of old.  

*Glory... Both now... The same.*  

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
"O never-setting Sun, eternal God, and Creator of all creations, O Lord: how dost Thou endure passion upon the Cross?" the Pure One weeping said.  

*Troparion 2:*  
Weeping the unwedded Mother said unto the noble one: "Make haste, O Joseph, to approach Pilate, and ask to take down from the Tree the Teacher, my Son."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
Seeing Pilate weeping bitterly, Joseph was moved, and weeping drew near unto Pilate: "Give me," he cried with tears, "the Body of my God."  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
"Beholding Thee wounded and without glory, naked upon the Tree, O my Child, my heart is consumed with grief as I lament," the Virgin Mother declared.  
*Katavasia: "He that sits in glory..."*  

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* The rejected Tree...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Tearing his clothes and lamenting even more, together with Nicodemus Joseph took Him down, and having kissed the all-pure Body, he wept and groaned, and praised Him as God.  

*Troparion 2:*  
"Receive Him with tears, O unwedded Mother; lay Him upon thy knees, kissing Him with tears, and lamenting and crying out."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
"One hope and life, O Master, my Son and God, the light in mine eyes had I, thy handmaid; but now am I bereaved of Thee, my sweet and beloved Child."  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
"Pains and sorrows and sighings have overtaken me, woe is me!" the Pure One weeping bitterly said, seeing Thee, my beloved Child, naked and wounded and anointed with bruises, dead.  
*Katavasia: "All creation was amazed..."*  

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* The monster in the watery belly...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
"Dead I behold Thee, O Lover of mankind, Who didst make alive the dead; tearing myself bitterly, and wounded in heart, I would choose to die with Thee," the All-Pure said: "for I endure not to see Thee dead without breath."  

*Troparion 2:*  
"I am amazed beholding Thee, O all-good God and most compassionate Lord, without glory, without breath, and without form; and I weep holding Thee, beyond all hope, woe is me! to see Thee, my Son and God."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
"Wilt Thou not speak a word unto Thy handmaid, O Word of God? Wilt Thou have compassion, O Master, upon Her that bore Thee?" the Pure One said, seeing and weeping, kissing the Body of Her Lord.  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
"I reflect, O Master, that no more shall I hear Thy sweet voice, nor behold the beauty of Thy countenance as formerly Thy handmaid; for Thou hast set from mine eyes, O my Son."  
*Katavasia: "This divine and all-honorable" Kontakion, Tone 8:*
Beholding Thee nailed upon the Cross, O Jesus, and accepting passions of Thine own will, O good One, the Virgin and Thy Mother cried aloud, woe is me: "O sweet Child, how dost Thou endure unjust wounds, O Physician and Healer of human infirmity, and Redeemer of all from corruption by Thy tender mercy?"  

*Ikos:*  
Beholding Her own Lamb drawn unto slaughter, Mary followed with outspread tears together with the other women, crying aloud thus: "Whither goest Thou, Child? For what cause dost Thou complete this swift course? Is there yet another marriage in Cana of Galilee, and dost Thou now hasten thither, that Thou mayest make wine for them from water? Shall I go with Thee, Child, or shall I rather wait for Thee? Give Me a word, O WORD, go not past Me in silence, Who hast preserved Me pure: for Thou art my Son and my God."  

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* The senseless decree of the wicked tyrant...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
"Where, O my Son and God, is that ancient annunciation which Gabriel spoke unto me, calling Thee King and Son of the Most High God? But now I behold Thee, my sweet Light, naked, wounded, dead."  

*Troparion 2:*  
"Deliverer from pains, now take me with Thyself, O my Son and God, that I also, O Master, may go down into Hades with Thee: leave me not alone, for I no longer endure to live, not seeing Thee, my sweet Light."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
With the other Myrrh-bearing Women, the blameless one bitterly weeping, beholding Christ of old, said: "Woe is me, what do I see! Whither goest Thou now, O my Son, and leavest me alone?"  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
Fainting and lamenting, the Blameless One said unto the Myrrh-bearers: "Lament with me and weep with me, for behold, my sweet Light, your Teacher, is delivered unto the Tomb."  
*Katavasia: "The godly youths worshipped not creation..."*  

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* Bless the Lord, ye children equal in number to the Trinity...  
*Refrain:* Most Holy Theotokos, save us.  

*Troparion 1:*  
Seeing thee lamenting, Joseph was wholly torn with grief, and cried out bitterly: "How shall I, thy servant, now bury Thee, O my God? With what winding-sheets shall I wrap Thy Body?"  

*Troparion 2:*  
Beyond mind and understanding was the strange sight of Thee, the Lord Who upholdest all creation; therefore Joseph carries Thee as dead upon his arms together with Nicodemus, carries and buries Thee.  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
"A strange and most marvelous mystery I behold," the Virgin cried unto Her Son and Lord:  

"how art Thou laid in a narrow tomb, Who by Thy command dost raise the dead in the tombs?"  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
"Neither from Thy tomb shall I depart, O my Child, nor shall I, thy handmaid, cease shedding tears, until I also go down into Hades: for I cannot endure separation from Thee, O my Son."  
*Katavasia: "The pious children in the furnace..."*  

*Ode 9. Before the Heirmos, we sing the Refrain:*  
*Magnify, O my soul, the Virgin Mary standing by the Cross of the Lord.*  
*Heirmos:* A mystery art thou, O Theotokos, Paradise...  
*Other Refrain:*  
*The Angels, seeing the affliction of the All-Pure, were amazed: how the Virgin suffers together with Her Son.*  

*Troparion 1:*  
"Joy shall never touch me henceforth," the blameless one lamenting said: "my light and my joy has set in the tomb, but I will not leave Him alone; here will I die and be buried together with Him."  

*Troparion 2:*  
"Heal now the wound of my soul, O my Child," the All-Pure cried with tears: "arise and soothe my pain and sorrow, for Thou canst do whatsoever Thou wilt, even though of Thine own will Thou wast buried."  

*Glory...*  
*Troparion 3:*  
"O how has the abyss of mercies been hidden from thee?" the Lord said in secret unto His Mother: "for willing to save My creation, I was well pleased to die; but I will also rise, and will magnify thee, as God of heaven and earth."  

*Both now...*  
*Troparion 4:*  
"I praise together Thy compassion, O Lover of mankind, and I worship the riches of Thy mercy, O Master: willing to save Thine own creation, Thou hast accepted death," said the All-Pure: "yet by Thy Resurrection, O Savior, have mercy upon us all."  
*Katavasia: "Let every earthborn mortal..."*  

*EXAPOSTILARION*  
Standing by the Cross of Thy Son and God, O Virgin All-Pure, glory of the Angels, not enduring to behold the reviling of the enemies, thou didst cry aloud, weeping with motherly love: "How dost Thou endure, O Lover of mankind, the contempt of all? I glorify Thy long-suffering."  
*Glory... Both now... The same.*  

*AT THE PRAISES*  
*Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "Thine Angels shall go forth, O Savior" (А҆́гг҃ли твоѝ прїи́дꙋтъ):*  

*Sticheron 1:*  
By many passions, make alive my slain soul, O Theotokos: who without passion didst bear God the Word, Who without passion endured passions and put death to death; and do thou by His deadness slay them that live in the darkness of sins, and direct my deadened thoughts toward divine things.  

*Sticheron 2:*  
When Thou wast willing to be lifted up upon the Cross, O Christ, then seeing the Disciple standing by whom Thou lovedst, and Thy Mother, Thou didst answer unto them: "The Virgin is thy mother; and this youth is thy son; O Mother, pure Virgin, he is Jesus whom I loved, a virgin and My disciple."  

*Sticheron 3:*  
Thenceforth the Virgin-Disciple led the Virgin into his own home; for he was named a son by the all-compassionate Son. He showed forth a son's care for a Mother, worthy filial conduct and warm kinship always; by whose prayers, O Christ God, save our souls.  

*Glory... Both now... Tone 6:*  
When She beheld the Lamb nailed upon the Cross, Who was born of thee, O Ewe-lamb, She cried aloud: "Woe is me! O sweetest Child; without pangs formerly I bore Thee, but now Simeon's word is fulfilled: to behold Thee, my Sustainer, crucified, O Christ my God, abundant in mercy."  

*We sing the Great Doxology. After the Trisagion: Troparion of the Feast: "With the Virgin Disciple", (see fol. 28)[^24] at Vespers.*  
*After the Litany: Dismissal.*  

*AT THE DIVINE LITURGY*  
*The usual Antiphons: 1. "It is good to give thanks unto the Lord..."; 2. "The Lord is King...", and the rest. The Beatitudes from the Canon, Odes 3 and 6. After the Little Entrance, Troparion of the Feast, Tone 1: "With the Virgin Disciple"; Glory... Both now... Kontakion: "Beholding Thee upon the Cross...". Then after the Trisagion, Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*  
She wept bitterly in the night, and Her tears are upon Her cheeks.  
*Verse:* And there is none to comfort Her among all that love Her.  

*A Reading from the Book of Judith (Judith 13:18–20 LXX):*  
The Most High Lord God bless thee, Who through thee has brought our enemies to nothing. And Ozias, the ruler of the people of Israel, said unto her: "Blessed art thou, O daughter, by the Most High Lord God beyond all women upon the earth. Blessed be the Lord God, Who created the heaven and the earth, Who directed thee to the cutting off of the head of the chief of our enemies; Who today has so magnified thy name, that thy praise shall not depart from the mouths of men, that they may remember the power of the Lord forever, who hast not spared thy life by reason of the sorrow and affliction of thy nation, but hast helped our fall before the face of the Lord our God."  

*Alleluia, Tone 6:*  
My strength has failed in me, for the Lord has given into my hand the strength of the enemy.  
*Verse:* And they that comfort me and take away my soul are departed far from me.  

*A Reading from the Holy Gospel according to John (John 19:25–27, Pericope 61):*  
At that time: There stood by the Cross of Jesus His Mother, and His Mother's sister, Mary the wife of Cleopas, and Mary Magdalene. When Jesus therefore saw His Mother, and the Disciple standing by whom He loved, He said unto His Mother: "Woman, behold thy son!" Then said He to the Disciple: "Behold thy mother!" And from that hour that Disciple took Her unto his own home.  

*Communion Hymn:*  
If there be any sorrow like unto my sorrow, therefore my heart is troubled within me. Alleluia, thrice.  

*At the Trapeza, by reason of the labor of the vigil, there is great consolation for the Brethren: We eat fish with olive oil and drink wine to the glory of God.*
