§. 35.

Order of Vespers on the Descent of the Holy Spirit

This Vespers on Sunday of the Descent of the Holy Spirit is celebrated immediately after the Divine Liturgy, and that together with the kneeling prayers for the invocation of the Holy Spirit. The order of this Vespers is as follows: After “Blessed be the name of the Lord” the Priest, having entered into the holy altar, beginneth: “Blessed is our God,” etc., and saith “Heavenly King,” the Trisagion, and the rest of the ordinary beginning, and the Psalm: “Bless the Lord, O my soul,” etc. After “Alleluia” (thrice), “Glory to Thee, O God,” the Priest chanteth the litany of peace, unto which after the petition: “For the people here present” he appendeth also the special litany “For the invocation of the help of the Holy Spirit” found in the *Sluzhebnik*, which he reciteth with kneeling. After the exclamation: “For unto Thee is due,” the choir singeth: “Lord, I have cried” and the stichera. (There is no kathisma.) “Glory,” and “Now and.” Entrance with the censer. Great Prokimenon: “Who is a God as great as our God?” etc. After the prokimenon the Priest ascendeth onto the ambo and proclaimeth: “Again and again, having bowed our knees, let us pray unto the Lord,” and having bowed his knees, reciteth the 1st prayer: “O undefiled, unblemished,” etc. After this, having arisen, he saith the small litany: “Help us, save us,” etc., and then the litany: “Let us all say.” — After the litany he proclaimeth: “Again and again, having bowed our knees,” etc., and reciteth the 2nd prayer:

§. 36.

Order of Vespers on the Paramony of the Nativity of Jesus Christ, if it should occur on a Saturday or on a Sunday

On the Paramony of the Nativity of Jesus Christ, the Royal Hours conclude in order in the morning together with Vespers and the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great. But if the Paramony should fall on a Saturday or on a Sunday, the celebration is divided, and that thus: the Royal Hours are celebrated beforehand on Friday; Vespers is celebrated on the Paramony itself (that is, on Saturday or on Sunday) in the evening at the 3rd hour with the evening paremias, with the troparia, and with the Epistle and Gospel of the Paramony; while the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil is celebrated on the very day of the Nativity of Christ. — Concerning this Great Vespers we note that it hath the ordinary beginning. Stichera at “Lord, I have cried” of the Nativity on 8. Entrance with the censer and with the Gospel. After “Gladsome Light” the Priest proclaimeth: “Let us attend! Peace be to all! Wisdom, let us attend!” and the prokimenon of the day is sung, after which there are 8 readings with troparia. These troparia are inserted at the 3rd and after the 6th paremia. At the conclusion of the 8th reading, the small litany, and immediately: “Let us attend! Peace be to all! Wisdom, let us attend!” and the prokimenon of the Epistle is taken: “The Lord said unto Me” [Ps. 2:7]. — The Epistle is read, pericope 303 [Gal. 3:15–22]. Alleluia and the Gospel from Matthew, pericope 53. Then “Let us all say,” “Vouchsafe, O Lord,” and “Let us complete our evening prayer,” and immediately the great dismissal. — (There are no stichera at the aposticha.)
Note. In like manner the celebration of the Paramony of Theophany is divided, if it should occur on a Saturday or Sunday. There, in addition, it is also necessary to note that this Vespers in this case is not only similar to this one, but in such a case it is also combined with the blessing of water. — That is to say,

ARTICLE II.

§. 37.

On Complines

Ordinary Complines are divided: (α) into small, and (β) into great. And of the extraordinary complines the most notable are: (α) Small Compline without a canon; (β) Complines in the Great Fast; (γ) Compline with Litiya.
Small Compline among us is celebrated publicly in church only on Holy Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday, and on the Descent of the Holy Spirit; but privately at home throughout the entire year, even in the Great Fast on Saturday and on Sunday (that is, for Sunday and for Monday). Great Compline, however, is celebrated publicly: (α) on Tuesday and on Thursday (that is, for Wednesday and for Friday) of Cheesefare Week; (β) on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday (with the exception of Wednesday of the 5th week), Thursday, and Friday (with the exception of Friday of the 5th week) of all weeks of the Great Fast; (γ) on Monday and Tuesday of Passion Week; (δ) on the Paramony of the Nativity of Christ and of Theophany; (ε) on the Paramony of the Annunciation, if that feast should occur on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday of the first 6 weeks of the Great Fast, or if it should occur on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday of Passion Week.
