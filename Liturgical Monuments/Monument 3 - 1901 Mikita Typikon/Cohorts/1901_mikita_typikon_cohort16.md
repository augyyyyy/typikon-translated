VI.) If It Be Sunday and One Great Saint and One Small Saint.

(α) At Vespers and at Matins the small Saint is entirely omitted, and all things are celebrated of Sunday and to the great Saint;

§. 79.

XI.) If It Be Sunday and the Patronal Feast of a Male or Female Saint [Temple Feast].

In this case on Saturday evening and on Sunday at Matins all things are celebrated just as when on Sunday there occurs a Saint having a Litiya. Only here at Matins there are the prokimenon, Gospel, and sticheron of the temple.
On the evening of the same day there is the Apodosis for the honor of the temple, concerning which we note the following:
(α) At Vespers (that is, for Monday): On “Lord, I have cried” stichera of the temple 3, to the Saint of the day falling on Monday 3. “Glory”: of the temple; “Now and”: Sunday Theotokion of the same tone. Prokimenon of the day: “Vouchsafe, O Lord.” Litany: “Let us complete.” Stichera on the aposticha of the temple. “Glory”: of the temple or of the Saint; “Now and”: Sunday Theotokion. After the Trisagion, troparion of the temple. “Glory”: of the Saint (if there be one); “Now and”: Sunday Theotokion. Litany: “Have mercy on us, O God.” Small dismissal. And thus the service of the temple is concluded at the Apodosis without fail;

(β) At Matins there is no Apodosis as at Vespers. The entire office is to the Saint and of the day in order from the Octoechos.
Note: If on the morrow of a patronal Saint there occur a Sunday, a feast of Christ or of the Theotokos, or the feast of a great Saint having a Vigil or a Polyeleos, then in the evening there is no Apodosis of the temple.
