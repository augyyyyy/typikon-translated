### ARTICLE V.

§. 61.

Concerning the Canonical Hours and the Typika [*Obidnytsia*].

The ordinary Hours are: (α) simple and (β) royal. The simple are celebrated daily, that is, on all feasts and simple days; while the royal are on the Eve of the Nativity of Jesus Christ and of Theophany, likewise also on Great Friday.

Of extraordinary Hours we cite: (α) the Hours in the Holy Great Fast; (β) the Hours in Bright Week.

The Typika [*Obidnytsia*] is celebrated either separately, when there is no Holy Liturgy; when, however, there is a Holy Liturgy, it is then combined with the Ninth Hour, and then only certain prayers are taken from it for completion.

They are celebrated in the same manner as the ordinary Hours. — Here it is only needful to note that at all the Hours, instead of the usual psalms, other psalms are prescribed.
