[^232]: Where the temple is of Christ or of the Mother of God, there in every case unconditionally at “Now and forever” the kontakion of the temple is sung, except those cases when there is an afterfeast of a Feast of the Lord or of the Mother of God, for then we cover it with the kontakion of the occurring feast.
[^233]: Where, however, the temple is of a Saint, there on feasts of Saints at “Now and forever” “O Protection of Christians” is taken; but on Sundays and on weekdays it is concluded with the above-mentioned kontakia, except the above-mentioned afterfeasts.
[^234]: Festal antiphons are sung only on the very first day of the feast; during the afterfeasts, however, the typical antiphons are sung, but the Prokimenon, Heirmos, Communion Hymn, and dismissal of the feast are always taken during afterfeasts even until the Apodosis.
[^235]: If the patronal feast of a male or female Saint should occur on a Sunday, at the Liturgy we sing the entire service unchanged as for a Great Saint that hath occurred on a Sunday. (See Case III.)
[^236]: If the patronal feast of a male or female Saint should occur within a feast on a Sunday, at the Liturgy the service of the Sunday and of the temple is taken, and that of the afterfeast is omitted.
[^237]: For if a Polyeleos or Great Saint should occur on a weekday, then the service of the Oktoechos is entirely omitted, and at the Liturgy of that day the entire service is unto the Polyeleos Saint. The weekday service of the Oktoechos is likewise omitted during the forefeasts and afterfeasts (until the Apodosis) of Feasts of the Lord and of the Mother of God; likewise from Lazarus Saturday until the Sunday of All Saints, and then also at weekday Liturgies the weekday service unto the Angels, the Forerunner, the Cross, etc., is omitted.
[^238]: The daily antiphons are taken at weekday Liturgies only when a Minor or Middle Saint occurs on a weekday; but if any of these hath [stichera] at the Beatitudes, then the typical antiphons are taken.
[^239]: On Thursday: the troparion unto the Apostles and unto St. Nicholas; the kontakion unto the Apostles; “Glory”: the kontakion unto Nicholas; “Now and forever”: as above.
[^240]: On Thursday we omit the troparion and kontakion unto St. Nicholas; but wherever there is a temple of St. Nicholas, we omit the troparion and kontakion unto the holy Apostles, and take them unto St. Nicholas and of intention.
[^241]: On Saturday the Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn are first of the Saint, then of the day. If there is an intention: the Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Saint, of the day, and so of the petition.
