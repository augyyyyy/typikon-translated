[^145]: The Ninth Hour is usually celebrated before Vespers, and then begins from: “Blessed is our God,” “O Heavenly King.”
[^146]: When it is proper to say the verse: “O God, be bountiful unto us” and when “Through the prayers of the holy Fathers”—let us note this: At the Midnight Office after the prayer: “Thou Who at all times”: “O God, be bountiful unto us.” And at the First Hour likewise: “O God, be bountiful unto us.” At the Third and Sixth Hours: “Through the prayers of the holy Fathers”; likewise also at the Ninth Hour, if the Hours are sung together. But if the Ninth Hour is not sung together with the Third and Sixth, but before Vespers, then: “O God, be bountiful unto us.” At Great Compline after the 1st “Lord, have mercy” (40 times): “Through the prayers of the holy Fathers”; after the 2nd “Lord, have mercy” (40 times): “Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us.” After the 3rd “Lord, have mercy” (40 times): “O God, be bountiful unto us.”
