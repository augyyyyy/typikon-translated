(ε) At the Hours: At the 1st Hour: Troparion and kontakion of the day; at the 3rd Hour: Troparion and kontakion of the 1st Saint; at the 6th Hour: Troparion and kontakion of the temple; at the 9th Hour: Troparion and kontakion of the 2nd Saint. — At the Typika [*Obidnytsia*] all things are in order.

§. 97.

XXIX.) If It Be a Weekday and Two Saints; Namely: One Middle or Great Saint and One Small.

In these cases the small Saint is transferred to Compline, where his stichera and his canon are taken. The rest of Divine Worship is celebrated just as though it were only a weekday and a middle Saint (at 6) or a great Saint (at 8).

§. 98.

XXX.) If It Be a Weekday and Many Saints.

The Saints either: (α) Together have one office, and then the entire service concludes just as though there were only one Saint; or: (β) Each one of them hath his own particular office, and then only the 1st Saint is taken, while all the others are omitted and are commemorated only in the Dismissals.

§. 99.

XXXI.) If It Be a Weekday and a Saint Having a Litiya.

In this case the Octoechos is omitted and the entire office is taken from the Menaion or from the Anthologion.
