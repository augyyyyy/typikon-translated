[^119]: On Saturday the canon of the Saint takes precedence, and after it that of the temple of the Saint, and then in the Octoechos the martyr canon. (The canon for the departed is sung on Friday at Compline.) At the Praises on Saturday we do not sing the stichera in the Octoechos, but we sing them at the Aposticha: 3 martyr stichera with the usual refrains. The Saturday aposticha stichera are omitted, and they are sung only on All Souls Saturdays with their refrains for the departed. (This exchange of stichera at the Aposticha takes place also on Friday evening at Vespers.) *Typikon*, p. 53.
