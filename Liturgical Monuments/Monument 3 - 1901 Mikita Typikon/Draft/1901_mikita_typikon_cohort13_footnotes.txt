[^124]: If it be a feast of the Lord, at “Glory” the sticheron of the feast (“All things today are filled with joy,” etc.); at “Now and,” the same; and unto the refrain “Have mercy on me, O God,” etc., the sticheron of the feast. — If, however, it be a feast of the Mother of God, at “Glory”: “Through the prayers of the Theotokos,” etc.; at “Now and,” the same; and unto the refrain “Have mercy on me, O God,” the sticheron of the feast. — If, however, it be the feast of the holy Apostles Peter and Paul, at “Glory”: “Through the prayers of the holy Apostles Peter and Paul, O Merciful One, cleanse,” etc.; at “Now and”: “Through the prayers of the Theotokos”; and unto the refrain “Have mercy on me, O God,” the sticheron to the holy Apostles.
