During the singing the people kiss the Cross, the Gospel, and the icon of the risen Christ. Likewise at that time the myrovania takes place and the blessed bread, or so-called antidoron, is distributed. After that the great dismissal takes place, that is to say: “Wisdom,” “Bless,” etc., “Christ is risen from the dead, trampling down death by death, and unto those in the tombs bestowing life, our true God,” etc., as in the Molitvoslov, p. 478. After the dismissal the Priest, having turned unto the people with the Cross, saith thrice: “Christ is risen!” and the people answer: “Truly He is risen!” Blessing. And thus he celebrates Vespers throughout the entire Bright Week. In Bright Week every blessing takes place with the Cross.

(β) Order of Vespers in Bright Week, if on a certain day a polyeleos Saint should occur. — The Priest, having put on all the vestments and censed the church, before the Holy Table begins from: “Blessed is our God.” After this: “Christ is risen” (*Христосъ воскресе*) thrice with the verses. At the end the Priest sings: “Christ is risen” (*Христосъ воскресе*) once, and the choir twice. And immediately the litany of peace. (There is no kathisma in Bright Week.) At “Lord, I have cried,” 6 stichera, namely 3 of the Resurrection, and 3 unto the Saint. “Glory,” of the Saint; “Now and,” Theotokion-dogmaticon, that is, the first of the tone. Entrance with the censer. Prokimenon of the day. Readings of the Saint. At the aposticha, one sticheron of the Resurrection, and the rest the stichera of Pascha. (Veneration of the Cross.) “Glory,” of the Saint; “Now and,” “The Day of Resurrection,” etc., “Christ is risen” (*Христосъ воскресе*) thrice. And immediately “Glory,” Troparion unto the Saint; “Now and,” Theotokion according to the tone of the Saint. Paschal dismissal with the Cross. Blessing with the Cross throughout the entire week.

Note. Concerning Vespers from Thomas Sunday until Ascension, it is necessary to note the following regarding these: (α) Sunday Vespers, sung on Saturday evening, is celebrated thus: After “Blessed is our God” (*Благословенъ Богъ нашъ*) the Priest sings “Christ is risen” (*Христосъ воскресе*) once, and the cantor twice. (The verses are not sung.) And immediately the Trisagion.[^98] “Our Father” (*Отче нашъ*) with the Exclamation. “Lord, have mercy” (*Господи помилуй*) (12 times). “Come, let us worship” (*Прїиди́те поклони́мся*) (thrice). The Introductory Psalm: “Bless the Lord, O my soul.” Litany of peace. Kathisma. At “Lord, I have cried,” 10 stichera.[^99] Great entrance.
