[^148]: If, however, it is celebrated without Matins, then the Priest in the epitrachelion before the royal doors saith: “Blessed is our God.” — “O Heavenly King,” etc.
[^149]: It is proper to know that on all Mondays of the Holy Forty Days we do not say a kathisma at the 1st Hour. Likewise also on Friday the 1st and 9th Hours are without a kathisma.
[^150]: On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday of the Week of the Veneration of the Cross, instead of “Order my steps,” there is sung “To Thy Cross” with bows. This takes place in such wise that the Priest vests in the full priestly vestment, opens the royal doors, and having come forth unto the Tetrapod, begins “To Thy Cross”; after the singing he shuts the royal doors.
[^151]: It is needful to note that on Great Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday, at the 1st and 9th Hours we do not say a kathisma. On these 3 days at the Hours there are readings from all four Evangelists—and that at the 1st Hour the Gospel is not read, but at the 3rd, 6th, and 9th Hours it is read.—See *Typikon*, p. 231. (Among us, however, the Gospel of the 9th Hour is taken at the Presanctified Liturgy.)
[^152]: There are Typika in which it is required to say this prayer not at the Typika [*Obidnytsia*], but in its own place, that is, at the 9th Hour.
[^153]: Instead of the 3rd Kathisma, from the 1st Sunday after the Leave-taking of the Feast of the Exaltation of the Precious Cross until December 20, and from January 14 until “Cheesefare Sunday,” the Polyeleos is taken: “Praise ye the name of the Lord.”
[^154]: Among us the deacon comes forth before the royal doors and there proclaims: “Holy is the Lord our God.”
[^155]: The Pochaiv Typikon [*Ustav*] commands to sing at 6, but all the ancient ones at 4.
