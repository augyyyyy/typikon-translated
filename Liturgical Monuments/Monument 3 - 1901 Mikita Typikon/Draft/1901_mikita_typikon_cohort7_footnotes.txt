[^48]: If the stichology at Vespers at “Lord, I have cried” is taken on 6 stichera, the verses are taken beginning from the verse “If Thou shouldst mark iniquities”; if on 8 stichera, then from the verse “Out of the depths have I cried”; if on 10 stichera, then from the verse “Bring my soul out of prison”; — in a word, the counting begins from the last verse “For His mercy is confirmed” and proceeds backwards. — At the beginning, 2 verses are sung from the Psalm “Lord, I have cried,” while the other verses are read until that verse which is already combined with a sticheron. — In like manner it is counted also at the Praises [Lauds], only there sometimes between “Glory” and between the taken verses given [prescribed] verses are intercalated. Thus on Sunday, when it is taken on 8, at the beginning 2 verses are sung from Psalm 148, and the rest are read until the verse “This glory shall be to all His saints”; — thereafter follows the stichology on 8, namely: 6 verses are taken from the Psalm, and 2 given verses are printed in the Octoechos.
