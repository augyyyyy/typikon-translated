[^314]: At the laying of the stone into the trench, some are accustomed to say this: “This church is founded unto the glory of the great God,” etc., and likewise they are accustomed to pour oil upon the stone with the words: “Blessed and marked be this place unto a house of prayer,” etc.
[^315]: There is a distinction between the Blessing and the Consecration of a new church. The Blessing of a new church is performed by a Priest, whereas the Consecration is performed by the Bishop himself. — For this latter rite see the Trebnik, p. 533.
[^316]: But if there be the Holy Liturgy, then after the internal sprinkling of the church the Priest, having returned from the church doors unto the Altar-Table, immediately proclaims: “Again and again, with compunction having bowed our knees, let us pray to the Lord.”
[^317]: Priestly vestments are blessed either all together or all individually one by one. For these latter see the Trebnik.
