[^28]: Saints, regarding their liturgical celebration, as we shall see later, are divided into 3 parts: into Great, Middle, and Lesser Saints. Great Saints have Great Vespers on 8 with an entrance, and Matins with a Polyeleos and Doxology. — Middle Saints have Vespers on 6 without an entrance, and Matins without a Polyeleos, but a canon on 6; a Great Doxology is present for some, but not for others. — Lesser Saints have Vespers on 3, and a canon at Matins on 4. — Great Saints belong to middle feasts; Middle Saints to lesser feasts; and the days of Lesser Saints are called non-festal days.
[^29]: Great Saturday, as the Eve of the Resurrection of Christ, is a fast. — Fasting is observed besides this on the Eve of the Nativity and on the Eve of Theophany, but if these Eves fall on a Saturday or a Sunday, the fast is transferred to the preceding Friday, since the forefeast of these feasts consists—as was said—of several days.
