### CHAPTER II.

§. 148.

Concerning Liturgies on Various Occasions.

It was already stated that the Greek Church in a single Horologion combines the Oktoechos with the Menaion, that is, the celebration of an immovable feast with the celebration of a movable feast, because she celebrates every day a twofold feast—and from this combination there have arisen various occurrences. So it is likewise concerning the holy Liturgy. But here the variety of these occurrences is further increased also by the fact that here with the daily Liturgy there can be combined also a Liturgy of various intentions, as for example: for every petition, or for the remission of sins, or for the sick, etc. But besides this there are also certain extraordinary occurrences in holy Liturgies. And in accordance herewith we shall speak here: (α) concerning regular occurrences, and (β) concerning the most notable extraordinary occurrences.

#### ARTICLE I.

Concerning Regular Occurrences.

§. 149.

I. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and a Minor Saint?

At the Liturgy the typical antiphons are sung, that is, “Bless the Lord, O my soul” (*Благослови душе моя Господа*) (Psalm 102) and “Praise the Lord, O my soul” (*Хвалѝ, дꙋшѐ моя̀, Го́спода...*) (Psalm 145). The first is concluded with “Glory... now and forever,” and the second after “Glory

=== LEAF p232 ===
[Book Page 208]

and now and forever” with the hymn “Only-begotten Son,” etc. At the Beatitudes, the Sunday stichera of the Oktoechos on 8. After the entrance, the Sunday troparion; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the Sunday kontakion; “Now and forever”: the kontakion according to the tone from the feasts of the Mother of God,[^231] or of the temple, if the temple is of Christ or of the Mother of God,[^232] or “O Protection of Christians,” if the temple is of a Saint.[^233] The Prokimenon of the tone. The Epistle and Gospel of the Sunday. The Communion Hymn likewise of the Sunday: “Praise the Lord.” From this it is evident that a Minor Saint hath no proper celebration at the Liturgy.

§. 150.

II. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and a Saint on 6.

Among these Saints there are such as have a proper celebration, and then the Liturgy is celebrated thus: The typical antiphons; stichera at the Beatitudes of the tone of the Oktoechos on 6, and of the Saint Ode 3 on 4. After the entrance, the troparia are taken in such wise that first the Sunday troparion is sung, thereafter the troparion of the Saint, the Sunday kontakion; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: the Theotokion as stated above. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday in course, and—if the Saint likewise hath them—then they are taken also for him.

§. 151.

III. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and a Polyeleos Saint.

“Bless the Lord, O my soul,” etc. The Beatitudes of the tone in course on 6, and unto the Saint Ode 3 on 4. After the entrance, the Sunday troparion and the troparion of the Saint; the Sunday kontakion; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: the kontakion according to the tone from the feasts of the Mother of God, or “O Protection of Christians.” The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the Saint.

=== LEAF p233 ===
[Book Page 209]

§. 152.

IV. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and a Forefeast of a Feast of the Lord or of the Mother of God?

The typical antiphons are taken; the Beatitudes of the Oktoechos on 6, and of the Forefeast Ode 3 on 4. After the entrance, the Sunday Troparion and the Troparion of the Forefeast; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the Sunday Kontakion; “Now and forever”: the Kontakion of the Forefeast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn are taken of the Sunday alone.

§. 153.

V. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and a Feast of the Mother of God?

“Bless the Lord, O my soul,” and the Sunday Beatitudes on 6, and of the feast Ode 3 on 4. After the entrance, the Sunday troparion and the troparion of the feast; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the Sunday kontakion; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the Feast. At the Megalynarion [“Especially”], instead of “It is truly right,” the refrain and Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the Feast.

§. 154.

VI. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and a Feast of the Lord?

Festal antiphons are taken.[^234] After each verse of the 1st festal Antiphon there is appended the refrain: “Through the prayers of the Mother of God, O Savior, save us”; after each verse of the 2nd Antiphon there is appended the refrain: “Save us, O Son of God, Who art risen from the dead” (or “Who wast born of the Virgin,” or “Who wast transfigured on the mount,” corresponding to the feast), “who chant unto Thee: Alleluia.” After the 2nd Antiphon: “Glory... now and forever,” “Only-begotten Son.” After each verse of the 3rd Antiphon the troparion of the feast is sung. Likewise instead of “Come, let us worship,” the great feasts have an Introit [Entrance Hymn], that is, entrance verses of the feast taken from the prophetic Psalms pertaining unto the

=== LEAF p234 ===
[Book Page 210]

feast. After the entrance, the troparion of the Feast; “Glory... now and forever”: the kontakion of the Feast. At the Megalynarion [“Especially”], instead of “It is truly right,” the refrain of the feast is sung with the Heirmos of the 9th Ode. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Feast; of the Sunday nothing is taken.[^235]

Notes: 1) If it is Sunday, a Minor Saint, and a service of intention:
After the entrance: the troparion of the Sunday and the troparion of intention, the kontakion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the Sunday kontakion; “Now and forever”: as above (that is, if the temple is of Christ or of the Mother of God, the kontakion of the temple; if of a Saint: “O Protection of Christians” (*Заступнице*) or from the Theotokion kontakia). The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn first of the Sunday, and of intention. (Unto the Minor Saint nothing is taken.)
2) If it is Sunday, a Great Saint, and a service of intention:
After the entrance: the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the Saint, and the troparion of intention; the kontakion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion unto the Saint; “Now and forever”: as above.
3) If it is Sunday, a Feast of the Lord, and a service of intention:
After the entrance: the troparion of the feast and of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of intention; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. (Of the Sunday nothing is taken.) The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the feast and of intention.

§. 155.

VII. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and within a Feast there is a Minor Saint?

Unto the Minor Saint nothing is taken, but only unto the Sunday and the Afterfeast. There are sung: the typical antiphons; stichera at the Beatitudes of the tone on 6, and of the feast the ode in course from the canon on 4. After the entrance, if it is an afterfeast of a Feast of the Lord, after “Wisdom! Stand aright!” (*Премудрость прости*): “Come, let us worship” (*Прїиди́те поклони́мся*) and “Save us, O Son of God, Who wast born of the Virgin,” or “Who wast transfigured on the mount,” etc. After the entrance, the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the feast; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the Sunday kontakion; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Prokimenon of the tone and of the feast. The Epistle and Gospel of the Sunday only. Instead of “It is truly right,” the refrain of the feast with the Heirmos of the 9th Ode. The Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the Feast.

=== LEAF p235 ===
[Book Page 211]

§. 156.

VIII. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and within a Feast there is a Middle Saint having a Celebration?

The typical antiphons. The Beatitudes of the tone on 4, of the feast the ode in course from the canon on 4, and of the Saint Ode 3 on 4. After the entrance, if it is an afterfeast of a Feast of the Lord, there is sung “Come, let us worship,” and the final refrain of the feast. After the entrance: the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the Saint, and the troparion of the feast; likewise the Sunday kontakion; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Prokimenon of the tone and of the Saint. The Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the Saint. Instead of “It is truly right,” the refrain of the feast and the Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the canon are sung.

§. 157.

IX. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if it is Sunday and within a Feast there is a Great Saint?

The typical antiphons; stichera at the Beatitudes of the Oktoechos on 4, of the feast the ode in course on 4, and of the Saint Ode 3 on 4. At the entrance, if it is an afterfeast of a Feast of the Lord, there is sung: “Come, let us worship” (*Прїиди́те поклони́мся*) and the final refrain of the feast. After the entrance: the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the feast, and the troparion of the Saint; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Prokimenon of the tone and of the Saint. The Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the Saint.[^236] Instead of “It is truly right,” the refrain of the feast.

§. 158.

X. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if the Apodosis of a Feast Falls on a Sunday?

The typical antiphons. The Beatitudes of the tone on 6, and of the feast Ode 9 on 4, that is, unto 4 verses of the Beatitudes the 4 troparia of the 9th Ode of the Matins canon prescribed for the feast are appended. At the entrance, if it is the Apodosis of a Feast of the Lord, there is sung: “Come, let us worship” (*Прїиди́те поклони́мся*) and the final refrain of the feast. After the entrance: the Sunday troparion and the troparion of the feast;

=== LEAF p236 ===
[Book Page 212]

“Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the Sunday kontakion; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Epistle and Gospel of the Sunday in course only. Instead of “It is truly right,” the Heirmos of the 9th Ode of the feast is sung. The Prokimenon and Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the Feast. The dismissal of the Sunday.

§. 159.

XI. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if the Patronal Feast of a Saint Falls with a Forefeast or with the Apodosis of a Feast on a Sunday?

The typical antiphons; stichera at the Beatitudes: the Sunday ones on 4, of the feast Ode 3 on 4, of the temple Ode 6 on 4. After the entrance, the troparion of the Sunday, of the feast, and of the temple of the Saint; the Sunday kontakion; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the temple; “Now and forever”: of the feast. The Prokimenon of the tone and of the temple. The Epistle in course and of the temple. The Gospel in course and of the temple. The Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of the temple.

Notes: 1) If it is Sunday, within a feast a Minor Saint, and a service of intention:
After the entrance: the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the feast, and the troparion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of intention; “Now and forever”: of the feast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday and of intention.
2) If it is Sunday, within a feast a Great Saint, and a service of intention:
After the entrance: the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the feast, the troparion of the Saint, and the troparion of intention; the kontakion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: of the feast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the Sunday, unto the Saint, and of intention.
3) If it is Sunday, the Apodosis of a feast, and a service of intention:
After the entrance: the Sunday troparion, the troparion of the feast, and the troparion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of intention; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast.

§. 160.

XII. How the Holy Liturgy is Celebrated if it is a Weekday and a Minor Saint?

The weekday service according to the Oktoechos is taken on Monday unto the Angels, on Tuesday unto the Forerunner, on Wednesday and Friday unto the Cross, on Thursday unto the Apostles and Saint Nicholas, and on Saturday unto All Saints. But the service unto those Saints at the Liturgy is taken only

then, when upon a weekday there falls a Minor or Middle Saint.[^237]
On such days the daily antiphons are taken (1st Antiphon: “It is good to give praise” (*Бла́го є̂́сть*); 2nd Antiphon: “The Lord reigns” (*Госпо́дь воцари́ся*); 3rd Antiphon: “Come, let us rejoice in the Lord”).[^238]
After the entrance (if the service is taken for the day alone): The troparion of the day; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the day; “Now and forever”: the kontakion from the feasts of the Mother of God according to the tone, or the kontakion of the temple, if the temple is of Christ or of the Mother of God; but if the temple is of a Saint, at “Now and forever”: “O Protection of Christians.”[^239] The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day. (Unto the Minor Saint nothing is taken.)
Note. With a service of intention it is taken thus: The troparion of the day and the troparion of intention; the kontakion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the day; “Now and forever”: “O Protection of Christians” (*Заступнице*) (or the kontakion of the temple, where the temple is of Christ or of the Mother of God).[^240] The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day [and] of intention.

§. 161.

XIII. How the Liturgy is Celebrated on a Weekday if there is a Saint on 6.

The daily antiphons, unless the Saint hath [stichera] at the Beatitudes, otherwise the typical antiphons. After the entrance: the troparion of the day and the troparion of the Saint (if he hath an Epistle and Gospel), the kontakion of the day; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: the kontakion as above. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day and of the Saint, if he hath them.[^241]

[Book Page 214]

Note. If there is a service of a particular intention, there is sung: the troparion of the day, the troparion of the Saint, and the troparion of intention; the kontakion of the day, the kontakion of the Saint; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of intention; “Now and forever”: the Theotokion, as above. On Wednesday or Friday it is covered with the daily kontakion of the Cross.

§. 162.

XIV. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if a Great Saint Falls on a Weekday.

When on a weekday (other than Sunday) a Great Saint falls, then the entire daily service—the troparion with the kontakion, the Prokimenon with the Communion Hymn—is omitted at the Liturgy. The Epistle and Gospel in course (that is, the daily series [*stolp*]) are read one day beforehand, and the entire service is taken unto the Saint.[^242] The typical antiphons. After the entrance: the troparion unto the Saint; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion unto the Saint; “Now and forever”: “O Protection of Christians,” or the kontakion of the temple, where the temple is of Christ or of the Mother of God. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn unto the Saint.[^243]
Note. If the priest hath a particular intention, then: the troparion of the Saint and the troparion of intention; the kontakion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: as above. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn unto the Saint and of intention.

§. 163.

XV. What We Need to Note Concerning Weekday Liturgies Celebrated during the Holy Pentecostarion (That Is, from Thomas Sunday until the Sunday of All Saints).[^244]

Since those Sundays are festal, whose entire service is repeated also on weekdays, therefore the troparia, kontakia, prokimena, and communion hymns of those Sunday services are likewise repeated on weekdays at the liturgical services. The Epistles and Gospels are read daily in course, corresponding to the festal Sundays. Concerning these Liturgies we note the following:

=== LEAF p239 ===
[Book Page 215]

1) At a weekday Liturgy during the Holy Pentecostarion with the service of a Minor Saint:
(α) Without an intention: The typical antiphons and the Beatitudes of the feast, the ode in course. After the entrance: the Sunday troparion (festal, of the resurrection),[^245] “Glory... now and forever”: the festal kontakion (of the Sunday).[^246] The Prokimenon and Communion Hymn of the festal Sunday: “Praise the Lord” (*Хвали́те*):[^247] In place of “It is truly right” (*Достойно есть*): “The Angel cried aloud,” and at Mid-Pentecost: the Heirmos of the 9th Ode until the Apodosis.
(β) With an intention: The Sunday troparion and the troparion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of intention; “Now and forever”: the festal kontakion. The Prokimenon, Epistle, and Gospel in course and of intention. The Communion Hymn: “Praise the Lord” (*Хвали́те*) and of intention. In place of “It is truly right” (*Достойно есть*): “The Angel cried aloud,” and at Mid-Pentecost: the Heirmos of Mid-Pentecost.

2) At a weekday Liturgy during the Holy Pentecostarion with the service of a Polyeleos Saint:
(α) Without an intention: The troparion of the Sunday and the troparion of the Saint; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion unto the Saint; “Now and forever”: the festal kontakion (of the Sunday). The Prokimenon, Epistle, and Gospel in course and unto the Saint. The Communion Hymn: “Praise the Lord” (*Хвали́те*) and unto the Saint.
(β) With an intention: The festal troparion, the troparion unto the Saint and of intention; the kontakion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion unto the Saint; “Now and forever”: the festal kontakion (of the Sunday). The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn unto the Saint and of intention.

§. 164.

XVI. How the Liturgy is Celebrated on Weekdays if a Forefeast Occurs with a Minor Saint?

Unto the Minor Saint at the Liturgy nothing is taken. There are taken: the typical antiphons. After the entrance: the troparion of the forefeast; “Glory... now and forever”: the kontakion of the forefeast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day (in course).
If there is also a service of some intention, then it is taken thus: The troparion of the forefeast and the troparion of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of intention; “Now and forever”: of the forefeast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day and of intention.

=== LEAF p240 ===
[Book Page 216]

§. 165.

XVII. How the Liturgy is Celebrated if on Weekdays a Feast of the Mother of God or of the Lord Should Occur?

Feasts of the Mother of God do not have proper antiphons, and therefore on those feasts the typical antiphons are taken. The Beatitudes from the canon: Odes 3 and 6 on 8.
Feasts of the Lord, however, have their proper antiphons with the final refrains of the feast. After the entrance: the troparion of the feast; “Glory... now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Prokimenon, Epistle, Gospel, Magnification, and Communion Hymn of the feast.

§. 166.

XVIII. How the Liturgy is Celebrated on Weekdays if within a Feast a Minor Saint Should Occur?

Unto the Minor Saint nothing is taken. The typical antiphons; the Beatitudes of the feast on 4, of the Saint on 4; at the entrance, if it is an afterfeast of a Feast of the Lord, we sing “Come, let us worship” (*Прїиди́те поклони́мся*) and the final refrain of the feast. After the entrance: the troparion of the feast; “Glory... now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Epistle and Gospel of the day (in course), but the Prokimenon and Communion Hymn of the feast. Instead of “It is truly right,” the Heirmos of the 9th Ode with the refrain of the feast is sung.
If the service is also one of various intentions, then after the entrance it is taken thus: The troparion of the feast, the troparion of the service of intention; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the service of intention; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Epistle and Gospel of the day and of the service of intention.

§. 167.

XIX. How the Liturgy is Celebrated on Weekdays if within a Feast there is a Middle Saint?

The antiphons are taken as was stated above. After the entrance: the troparion of the feast and the troparion of the Saint; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): the kontakion of the Saint; “Now and forever”: the kontakion of the feast. The Prokimenon of the feast and of the Saint. The Epistle and Gospel of the day (in course) and of the Saint. Instead of “It is truly right,” the Heirmos of the feast. The Communion Hymn of the feast and of the Saint.
