In this case the small Saint is transferred to Compline, where his canon is sung with the canon of the Theotokos. After Ode 9 his stichera are sung. — In this manner this office is celebrated just as though there were only one middle Saint within the feast.[^167]

§. 110.

In this case the small Saint is likewise transferred to Compline. — The office is celebrated just as though there were only one Saint with a Polyeleos (great) within the feast.

Note: It may happen within a feast on a weekday that there are also many Saints: Then those Saints either have one joint office, and in this case it is celebrated as was said above; or each individual Saint hath his own proper office, and then the office of only the 1st Saint is celebrated, while the others are omitted and are commemorated only in the Dismissals.

In this case the entire office of the Saint is celebrated one day in advance (except the Entry, when the office of the Saint is celebrated together with it), and all things are celebrated just as on the very feast except: the entrance, the readings at Vespers, the Polyeleos, and the Matins Gospel. However:

(α) At Vespers: On “Lord, I have cried” (*Господи воззвахъ*) stichera of the feast at 6, sung on the very feast. “Glory,” and “Now and”: of the feast. (On Friday, however: “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): of the feast; “Now and”: Dismissal Dogmatikon of the outgoing tone, except for the Apodosis of the Feast of the Nativity of Christ and Theophany.) There is no entrance. After the prokimenon: “Vouchsafe, O Lord.” Ektenia: “Let us complete.” Stichera on the aposticha of the feast. “Glory,” and “Now and”: of the feast. After “Now lettest Thou dismiss”: Troparion of the feast once. Ektenia. Small Dismissal;

(β) At Compline: Kontakion of the feast;

(γ) At the Midnight Office: The customary daily troparia. The prayer is also said: “Remember, O Lord, in the hope” (*Помянѝ, Го́споди, въ наде́ждѣ...*);

(δ) At Matins: At “God is the Lord” (*Богъ Господь*) the troparion of the feast twice. “Glory,” and “Now and”: the same. After the Kathismata, Sessional Hymns of the feast. (There is no Polyeleos.) Psalm 50. Festal canons; the heirmoi of both canons are sung, if there are two. Katavasia of the day after each ode. After Ode 3 of the canon: Sessional Hymn of the feast. At Ode 9, however, instead of “More honorable,” the refrain is sung with the heirmos of the feast, and at the convergence the katavasia and heirmos with the refrain. After Ode 9: Exaposteilarion of the feast. Stichera on the Praises at 4. “Glory,” and “Now and”: of the feast. Great Doxology. Trisagion. Troparion of the feast. Great Dismissal;

(ε) At the Hours: Troparion and kontakion of the feast. — At the Typika [*Obidnytsia*] all things are in order.

§. 114.

XLVI.) If the Apodosis of a Feast and Two Saints Fall on a Weekday.

In this case one Saint is transferred to Compline, and the other Saint is transferred one day in advance.
