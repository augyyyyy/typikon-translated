[^168]: On the Praises there are stichera and they are called “for the Departed” [*Pokoyni*], but they are not sung except on the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Saturday of the Fast, or when “Alleluia” is sung.
[^169]: Some say also this, that on Saturday a Great Dismissal ought to be taken for the sake of the apodosis of the tone, and that after the troparion at “God is the Lord,” at the end of Matins the great Resurrection Theotokion is taken.
[^170]: If a feast or a Polyeleos Saint occur on Sunday, then only two kathismata are taken, and that: the 2nd and the 3rd; instead of the 17th, the Polyeleos is taken.
[^171]: If a festal celebration occur on Sunday, then we sing: “Blessed is the man” [Ps. 1], the 1st Antiphon.
[^172]: Whenever, however, a festal celebration occur on these days in which 3 kathismata are said at Matins: then we say 2 kathismata at Matins, and the 3rd daily kathisma at Vespers.
[^173]: On Meatfare and Cheesefare Sunday we chant the stichology of two kathismata at Matins, and the 3rd daily kathisma at Vespers.
