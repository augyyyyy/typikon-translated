[^166]: If a Feast of the Lord fall on a Saturday: Then on Friday evening the prokimenon of the day is omitted and the great prokimenon is sung with its verses; on Saturday evening, that is, on the very feast, we sing the prokimenon: “The Lord is King.” If, however, a Feast of the Lord fall on a Sunday, we sing on Saturday evening the prokimenon: “The Lord is King,” and on Sunday evening we sing the great prokimenon with its verses.
[^167]: Some say further that in this case, although the small Saint is omitted, nevertheless at the conclusion of Vespers, as well as at the beginning and conclusion of Matins, the troparion of the small Saint is also taken. That is, thus: troparion of the feast and troparion of the 1st Saint, “Glory”: troparion of the small Saint; “Now and”: of the feast. — At the Hours, however, the troparion and kontakion of the middle Saint are taken at the 3rd Hour, and of the small Saint at the 9th Hour.
