### ARTICLE V.

§. 61.

Concerning the Canonical Hours and the Typika [*Obidnytsia*].

The ordinary Hours are: (α) simple and (β) royal. The simple are celebrated daily, that is, on all feasts and simple days; while the royal are on the Eve of the Nativity of Jesus Christ and of Theophany, likewise also on Great Friday.

Of extraordinary Hours we cite: (α) the Hours in the Holy Great Fast; (β) the Hours in Bright Week.

The Typika [*Obidnytsia*] is celebrated either separately, when there is no Holy Liturgy; when, however, there is a Holy Liturgy, it is then combined with the Ninth Hour, and then only certain prayers are taken from it for completion.

The Epistle (*Apostol*). The Gospel. The Creed (*Viruyu*). “Blessed be the name of the Lord.” “I will bless the Lord,” etc., and the remaining parts are joined unto the Ninth Hour for completion.

§. 63.

Order of the Royal Hours.

They are celebrated in the same manner as the ordinary Hours. — Here it is only needful to note that at all the Hours, instead of the usual psalms, other psalms are prescribed. Besides this, after the troparia 3 stichera are sung with “Glory,” and “Now and.” After this the Priest from the sanctuary saith: “Let us attend, peace be to all, wisdom, let us attend.” Whereunto the chanter sings the Prokimenon. After this there is one reading from the Old and one from the New Testament. After the readings the Priest opens the royal doors, proclaiming: “Wisdom, let us attend,” etc., and at the royal doors reads the Gospel. After the Gospel he shuts the royal doors. The chanter reads: “Order my steps” (*Стопы̀ моя̀ напра́ви...*) at the 1st, “Blessed is the Lord God” (*Господь Богъ благословенъ*) at the 3rd, “Let Thy compassions quickly go before us” at the 6th, and “Deliver us not up” (*Не предаждь насъ*) at the 9th Hour. All the rest are concluded in order, as at the simple Hours, that is, when “Lord, have mercy” (*Господи помилуй*) is said 40 times, the Priest comes forth before the royal doors in the epitrachelion, and there saith all things as at other times. After the prayer of the Ninth Hour there are also taken from the Typika [*Obidnytsia*] certain parts for completion, and that: (α) On the Eve of the Nativity and of Theophany: “Bless the Lord, O my soul.” “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): “Praise the Lord, O my soul.” “Now and”: “Only-begotten Son.” After this: “In Thy kingdom.” “Glory,” and “Now and”: “Remember us.” “The heavenly choir.” The Creed (*Viruyu*). “Pardon, remit.” “Our Father.” The kontakion of the feast. “Lord, have mercy” (*Господи помилуй*) (40 times), and “Through the prayers of our holy Fathers,” etc., when the Priest comes forth before the royal doors, and there reads the prayer: “All-Holy Trinity.” After this: “Blessed be the name of the Lord” (*Буди имя Господне*) (thrice). “I will bless the Lord at all times.” “It is meet and right,” and the dismissal. (If, however, there is a Liturgy, then after the prayer: “All-Holy Trinity,” there is immediately the dismissal. “Blessed be the name of the Lord” (*Буди имя Господне*) [thrice] and “I will bless the Lord at all times” (*Благословлю Господа*) are taken at the end of the Liturgy.)
