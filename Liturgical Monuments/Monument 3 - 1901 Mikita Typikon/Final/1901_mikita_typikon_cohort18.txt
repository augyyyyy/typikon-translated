XLIV.) If It Be a Weekday and Within a Feast There Occur a Temple of a Saint (Male or Female).

Concerning this case it is only necessary to note that here all things are celebrated as when within a feast there is a Saint having a Litiya.

The Apodosis of the temple takes place on the feast itself at Vespers, and that in such manner that the office of the afterfeast is omitted, and on “Lord, I have cried” (*Господи воззвахъ*) stichera of the temple 3 and of the Saint of the day 3 are sung; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): of the temple; “Now and”: Resurrection Theotokion of the same tone. After “Now lettest Thou dismiss”: Troparion of the temple; “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): of the Saint; “Now and”: Resurrection Theotokion. All the rest as usual, and thus the office of the temple is surrendered.

§. 113.

XLV.) If the Apodosis of a Feast and One Saint Fall on a Weekday.

=== LEAF p180 ===
[Book Page 156]

In this case the entire office of the Saint is celebrated a day beforehand (excepting the Entry into the Temple, when the office of the Saint is also celebrated together), and all things are celebrated as on the feast itself, except: The entrance, the Readings at Vespers, the Polyeleos, and the morning Gospel. However:

(α) At Vespers: On “Lord, I have cried” (*Господи воззвахъ*) stichera of the feast at 6, sung on the feast itself; “Glory,” and “Now and”: of the feast. (On Friday, however: “Glory” (*Сла́ва*): of the feast; “Now and”: Theotokion-Dogmatikon of the incoming tone, except at the Apodosis of the Feast of the Nativity of Christ and Theophany.) There is no entrance. After the prokimenon, “Vouchsafe, O Lord.” Ektenia: “Let us complete.” Stichera on the aposticha of the feast; “Glory,” and “Now and”: of the feast. After “Now lettest Thou dismiss”: Troparion of the feast once. Ektenia. Small Dismissal;

(β) At Compline: Kontakion of the feast;

(γ) At the Midnight Office: The usual daily troparia; likewise is said also the prayer: “Remember, O Lord, in hope”;

(δ) At Matins: At “God is the Lord” (*Богъ Господь*) the troparion of the feast twice; “Glory,” and “Now and”: the same. After the kathismata, Sessional Hymns of the feast. (There is no Polyeleos.) Psalm 50 [Ps. 50]. The festal Canons; the heirmoi of both canons are sung, if there be two. Katavasia of the day after each ode. After Ode 3 of the canon: Sessional Hymn of the feast. At Ode 9, however, instead of “More honorable than the Cherubim,” the refrain is sung with the heirmos of the feast, and at the Katavasia the heirmos with the refrain. After Ode 9: Exaposteilarion of the feast. On the Praises stichera at 4; “Glory,” and “Now and”: of the feast. Great Doxology. Trisagion. Troparion of the feast. Great Dismissal;

(ε) At the Hours: Troparion and kontakion of the feast. — At the Typika [*Obidnytsia*] all things are in order.

§. 114.

XLVI.) If the Apodosis of a Feast and Two Saints Fall on a Weekday.

In this case one Saint is transferred to Compline, and the other Saint is transferred one day beforehand.
