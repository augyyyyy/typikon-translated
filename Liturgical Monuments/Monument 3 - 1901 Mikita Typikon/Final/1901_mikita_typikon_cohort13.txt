Order of Matins Services in Passion Week.

(α) Order of Matins on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday of Passion Week. These Matins services are small with the Gospel, but without the Great Doxology. They are celebrated thus: The beginning is the usual one. After the Six Psalms the litany of peace and “Alleluia” (thrice) in the 8th Tone. Troparion: “Behold, the Bridegroom” (*Се женихъ*) (thrice) aloud. Then the usual kathismata and sessional hymns from the Triodion. Upon the conclusion of these the Priest, having put on the phelonion, opens the royal doors and saith: “That we may be accounted worthy,” etc., “Wisdom, let us attend, let us hear the holy Gospel,” etc., as at other times, and reads the holy Gospel. After the Gospel the chanter reads Psalm 50 [Ps. 50], and the Priest chants aloud the litany: “Save, O God, Thy people,” etc. After this litany, the Triodion canon from the Triodion (and on Tuesday, the Two-Ode Canon [*Dvoopesnets*]). After the 8th ode “More honorable than the Cherubim” (*Честнѣйшую*) is not sung, but the Heirmos. (From Lazarus Saturday until New Sunday “More honorable than the Cherubim” (*Честнѣйшую*) is not sung, but instead of that the Heirmos of the 9th ode is taken.) After the 9th ode the Priest comes forth before the royal doors for a bow. After the 9th ode the Exaposteilarion: “Thy bridal chamber” (*Черто́гъ тво́й*) (thrice). The rest in order. After the aposticha: “It is good to give praise,” and the rest in the usual manner with prostrations. And the First Hour begins. Dismissal: “The Lord, coming unto His voluntary passion.”
