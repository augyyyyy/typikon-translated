O God, take heed unto helping me; O Lord, hasten to help me.  
Let them be put to shame and confounded that seek after my soul; let them be turned backward and brought to shame that wish me evils.  
Let them be turned back immediately in shame that say unto me: Aha, aha!  
Let all that seek Thee rejoice and be glad in Thee, O God; and let them that love Thy salvation say continually: Let God be magnified.

*...of each [day] in order. After the canon: "It is truly meet" and "More honorable than the Cherubim", and immediately we begin Psalm 4 [LXX Ps. 4]:*  
When I called, the God of my righteousness heard me; in affliction Thou didst enlarge me. Have compassion upon me, and hear my prayer.  
O ye sons of men, how long will ye be heavy of heart? Why love ye vanity, and seek after falsehood?  
And know ye that the Lord hath made His holy one marvelous; the Lord will hear me when I cry unto Him.  
Be ye angry, and sin not; that which ye say in your hearts, repent of upon your beds.  
Sacrifice a sacrifice of righteousness, and hope in the Lord.  
Many say: Who will show us good things? The light of Thy countenance, O Lord, hath been signed upon us. Thou hast given gladness into my heart; from the fruit of their corn, wine, and oil have they been multiplied.  
In peace together will I sleep and take my rest; for Thou, O Lord, alone hast made me to dwell in hope.

Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak; heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled.  
And my soul is troubled exceedingly; and Thou, O Lord, how long?  
Turn Thee, O Lord, deliver my soul; save me for Thy mercy's sake.  
For in death there is no one that remembers Thee; and in Hades who shall give thanks unto Thee?  
I have labored in my groaning; every night will I wash my bed, with my tears will I water my couch.  
Mine eye is troubled through wrath; I have grown old among all mine enemies.  
Depart from me, all ye that work iniquity, for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping.  
The Lord hath heard my supplication; the Lord hath received my prayer.  
Let all mine enemies be put to shame and greatly troubled; let them be turned back and put to shame exceedingly quickly.

=== LEAF p104 ===

How long, O Lord, wilt Thou forget me unto the end? How long wilt Thou turn away Thy face from me?  
How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrow in my heart day and night? How long shall mine enemy be exalted over me?  
Look down and hear me, O Lord my God; lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep in death;  
lest mine enemy say: I have prevailed against him; those that trouble me will rejoice if I am moved.  
But I have hoped in Thy mercy; my heart shall rejoice in Thy salvation. I will sing unto the Lord Who hath dealt bountifully with me, and I will chant unto the name of the Lord Most High.

*A Psalm of David, 24 [LXX Ps. 24]:*  
Unto Thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul. O my God, in Thee have I trusted; let me not be put to shame forever, neither let mine enemies laugh at me.  
For all they that wait on Thee shall not be put to shame; let them be put to shame that do iniquity in vain.[^153]

=== LEAF p105 ===

Make Thy ways known unto me, O Lord, and teach me Thy paths.  
Guide me into Thy truth, and teach me, for Thou art God my Savior; on Thee have I waited all the day.  
Remember Thy compassions, O Lord, and Thy mercies, for they are from of old.  
Remember not the sins of my youth, nor mine ignorances; according to Thy mercy remember Thou me, for Thy goodness' sake, O Lord.  
Good and upright is the Lord; therefore will He set a law for them that sin in the way.  
He will guide the meek in judgment; He will teach the meek His ways.  
All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth, unto such as seek His covenant and His testimonies.  
For Thy name's sake, O Lord, pardon also my sin, for it is great.  
What man is he that fears the Lord? He will set a law for him in the way which He hath chosen.  
His soul shall dwell in prosperity, and his seed shall inherit the earth.  
The Lord is the strength of them that fear Him, and His covenant shall be made manifest unto them.[^154]

=== LEAF p108 ===

I became a reproach among all mine enemies, and exceedingly to my neighbors, and a fear to mine acquaintances; they that saw me without fled from me.  
I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind; I became like a broken vessel.  
For I have heard the slandering of many that dwell round about; when they gathered together against me, they took counsel to take away my life.  
But I have hoped in Thee, O Lord; I said: Thou art my God; my lots are in Thy hands.  
Deliver me out of the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.  
Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant; save me in Thy mercy.  
O Lord, let me not be put to shame, for I have called upon Thee; let the ungodly be put to shame, and let them go down into Hades.  
Let the deceitful lips be put to silence, which speak iniquity against the righteous, with pride and contempt.  
How great is the multitude of Thy goodness, O Lord, which Thou hast hidden for them that fear Thee, which Thou hast wrought for them that hope...[^157]
