# PART II: TROPARIA AND KONTAKIA THROUGHOUT THE YEAR

*Troparia and Kontakia throughout the Year (Тропари и кондаки презъ лѣто):*[^183]  
*On Monday, of the Angels, Troparion, Tone 4:*  
*Troparion:*  
O commanders of the heavenly hosts, we the unworthy do ever beseech you, that by your prayers you may encompass us with the shelter of the wings of your immaterial glory, preserving us who fall down and cry earnestly: Deliver us from tribulations, as commanders of the higher powers.  
*Theotokion, Tone 4:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Since thou art better than all creations, we who are at a loss to hymn thee worthily, O Theotokos, freely by thy mercies have mercy on us.  
*Kontakion, Tone 2:*  
*Kontakion:*  
Commanders of God, ministers of divine glory, guides of men and captains of the bodiless powers: entreat what is profitable for us and great mercy, as commanders of the bodiless hosts.  
*On Tuesday, of the Forerunner, Troparion, Tone 2:*  
*Troparion:*  
The memory of the righteous is with praises, but the testimony of the Lord is sufficient for thee, O Forerunner; for thou hast truly appeared as more honorable than the prophets, since thou wast accounted worthy to baptize in the streams Him Whom thou didst proclaim...

...Therefore, having truly suffered for the truth, rejoicing thou didst proclaim the good tidings even to them in hades of God Who appeared in the flesh, Who takes away the sins of the world, and grants us great mercy.[^184]  
*Theotokion, Tone 2:*  
*Theotokion:*  
We became partakers of the divine nature through thee, O Theotokos and Ever-Virgin; for thou didst bear for us God incarnate. Therefore, as is our duty, we all piously magnify thee.  
*Kontakion, Tone 3, Podoben: "Today the Virgin" (Дѣва днесь):*  
*Kontakion:*  
Today she that was barren bears Christ's Forerunner, and he is the fulfillment of all prophecy; for having laid his hand in the Jordan upon Him Whom the prophets proclaimed, he was shown forth as the Prophet, Preacher, and Forerunner of God the Word.  
*On Wednesday, of the Cross, Troparion:*  
*Troparion:*  
Save, O Lord, Thy people...  
*Theotokion:*  
Having acquired thy protection, O all-pure one, and being delivered from tribulations by thy prayers, preserved everywhere by the Cross of thy Son, as is our duty, we all piously magnify thee.  
*Kontakion, Tone 4:*  
*Kontakion:*  
Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross...

...written among the troparia in Matins.[^185]  
*On Thursday, of the Apostles, Troparion, Tone 3:*  
*Troparion:*  
Holy Apostles, entreat the merciful God, that He may grant remission of transgressions unto our souls.  
*And to Saint Nicholas, Troparion, Tone 4:*  
*Troparion:*  
A rule of faith and an image of meekness, a teacher of abstinence, did the truth of things reveal thee unto thy flock. Therefore, thou hast achieved heights through humility, and riches through poverty; O father hierarch Nicholas, entreat Christ God that our souls may be saved.  
*Theotokion, Tone 4:*  
*Theotokion:*  
The Word of the Father, Christ our God, Who was incarnate of thee, O Theotokos Virgin: Him do thou entreat, that our souls may be saved.  
*Kontakion, Tone 2:*  
*Kontakion:*  
The steadfast and divinely eloquent preachers, the summit of Thy disciples, O Lord, Thou hast received into the enjoyment of Thy good things and rest; for their labors and death didst Thou accept above every whole-burnt offering, O Thou Who alone knowest what is in the heart.  
*On Friday, as on Wednesday again, write: Save, O Lord, Thy people... and Kontakion, Tone 4: Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross of Thine own will...*  
*On Saturday, Troparion, Tone 2:*  
*Troparion:*  
Apostles, martyrs, prophets,...

...and hierarchs, venerable and righteous, who have accomplished the good fight and kept the faith, having boldness unto the Savior, Him do ye entreat for us, we pray, that our souls may be saved.  
*For the departed, Troparion:*  
*Troparion:*  
Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good, Thy servants, as many as [in this life have sinned]...  
*Theotokion:*  
Holy Mother of the ineffable Light, honoring thee with angelic hymns, we magnify thee.  
*Kontakion of the Departed:*  
*Kontakion:*  
With the saints give rest, O Christ...  

## RESURRECTIONAL TROPARIA AND HYPAKOAI

*Resurrectional Troparia and Hypakoai of the Eight Tones (Тропари воскресни и ипакои осмиимь гласовомъ):*[^186]  
*Troparion, Tone 1:*  
*Troparion:*  
When the stone had been sealed by the Jews, and the soldiers were guarding Thine all-pure body, Thou didst rise on the third day, O Savior, granting life unto the world. Therefore, the powers of heaven cried out unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy resurrection, O Christ; glory to Thy kingdom; glory to Thy dispensation, O Thou Who alone art the Lover of mankind.  
*Theotokion, Tone 1:*  
*Theotokion:*  
When Gabriel said unto thee, Rejoice, O Virgin, with that voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy...

...ark, as the righteous David said; thou wast shown to be more spacious than the heavens, having borne Thy Creator. Glory to Him Who made His abode in thee; glory to Him Who came forth from thee; glory to Him Who hath set us free through thy birthgiving.[^187]  
*Hypakoe, Tone 1:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
The repentance of the thief plundered paradise, and the lamentation of the myrrh-bearing women proclaimed joy, that Thou art risen, O Christ God, granting great mercy unto the world.  
*Troparion, Tone 2:*  
*Troparion:*  
When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life immortal, then didst Thou slay hades with the brilliance of the Godhead; and when Thou didst also raise the dead out of the nethermost depths, all the powers of heaven cried out: O Giver of life, Christ God, glory to Thee.  
*Theotokion, Tone 2:*  
*Theotokion:*  
All beyond thought, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Theotokos; sealed in purity and preserved in virginity, thou wast shown forth to be a true Mother, having given birth unto the true God: Him do thou entreat, that our souls may be saved.  
*Hypakoe, Tone 2:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
After the Passion, having gone unto the tomb to anoint Thy body,...

...all-pure, the women, O Christ God, beheld angels in the tomb and were dismayed; for they heard from them a voice, that the Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.[^188]  
*Troparion, Tone 3:*  
*Troparion:*  
Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; for the Lord hath wrought might with His arm: He hath trampled down death by death, He is become the firstborn of the dead, from the belly of hades hath He delivered us, and granted great mercy unto the world.  
*Theotokion, Tone 3:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, we hymn, O Theotokos Virgin; for by the flesh which He took upon Himself from thee, Thy Son and our God, having accepted the suffering upon the Cross, delivered us from corruption, as the Lover of mankind.  
*Hypakoe, Tone 3:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
Astonishing by his sight, bedewing with his words, the radiant angel said unto the myrrh-bearing women: Why seek ye the Living in the tomb? He is risen, having emptied the graves! Understand the Transformer of corruption to be unchangeable; say unto God: How fearful are Thy works, for Thou hast saved the human race!

*Troparion, Tone 4:*  
*Troparion:*  
Having learned from the angel the joyful proclamation of the resurrection, and cast off the ancestral condemnation, the women disciples of the Lord boasting said unto the Apostles: Death is overthrown, Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto all the world.[^189]  
*Theotokion, Tone 4:*  
*Theotokion:*  
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto angels, through thee, O Theotokos, hath been revealed unto them that are upon the earth: God being incarnate in unconfused union, and of His own will accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-formed, He saved our souls from death.  
*Hypakoe, Tone 4:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
Having outrun [the others unto the tomb] at Thine all-glorious uprising, the myrrh-bearing women proclaimed unto the Apostles, O Christ: That Thou art risen as God, granting great mercy unto the world.  
*Troparion, Tone 5:*  
*Troparion:*  
The Word Who is co-unoriginate with the Father and the Spirit, Who for our salvation was born of the Virgin, let us hymn, O faithful, and worship; for He was well-pleased to ascend the Cross in the flesh, and to endure death,...

...and to resurrect the dead in His glorious resurrection.[^190]  
*Theotokion, Tone 5:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord; rejoice, wall and shelter of them that flee unto thee; rejoice, storm-free haven and unwedded bride, who didst bear in the flesh thy Creator and God: pray and despise not them that hymn and worship thy birthgiving.  
*Hypakoe, Tone 5:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
Troubling their mind with the angelic apparition, and enlightening their souls with the divine vision, the myrrh-bearing women proclaimed the good tidings unto the Apostles: Announce ye among the nations the resurrection, as the Lord works together with wonders, and grants us great mercy.  
*Troparion, Tone 6:*  
*Troparion:*  
The angelic powers were upon Thy tomb, and they that guarded it became as dead; and Mary stood in the tomb, seeking Thine all-pure body. Thou didst despoil hades, not being tried by it; Thou didst meet the Virgin, granting life. O Thou Who didst rise from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!  
*Theotokion, Tone 6:*  
*Theotokion:*  
He Who called...

...Thee His blessed Mother, came unto the Passion by voluntary counsel, shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: Rejoice with Me, for the lost coin hath been found. O God, Who hast ordered all things in wisdom, glory to Thee![^191]  
*Hypakoe, Tone 6:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
By Thy voluntary and life-creating death, O Christ, having broken down the gates of hades as God, Thou hast opened unto us the ancient paradise; and having risen from the dead, Thou hast delivered our life from corruption.  
*Troparion, Tone 7:*  
*Troparion:*  
Thou didst destroy death by Thy Cross, and open paradise unto the thief; Thou didst transform the weeping of the myrrh-bearing women, and command Thine Apostles to preach: that Thou art risen, O Christ God, granting great mercy unto the world.  
*Theotokion, Tone 7:*  
*Theotokion:*  
As the treasury of our resurrection, O all-pure one who art our refuge, lead us up from the pit and depth of sins; for thou didst save them that are guilty of sin, having given birth unto our Salvation: who before childbirth wast a Virgin, and in childbirth a Virgin, and after childbirth art again a Virgin...

...*Hypakoe, Tone 7:*[^192]  
*Hypakoe:*  
O Thou Who didst take upon Thyself our likeness, and endure crucifixion in the flesh, save me by Thy resurrection, O Christ our God, as the Lover of mankind.  
*Troparion, Tone 8:*  
*Troparion:*  
From the heights didst Thou descend, O Compassionate One, and accept three days' burial, that Thou mightest set us free from the passions; O our Life and Resurrection, Lord, glory to Thee!  
*Theotokion, Tone 8:*  
*Theotokion:*  
O Thou Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, O Good One, Who by death didst despoil death, and manifest the resurrection as God: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thine own hand; manifest Thy love toward mankind, O Merciful One; receive the Theotokos who bore Thee, interceding for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people.  
*Hypakoe, Tone 8:*  
*Hypakoe:*  
Standing before the life-giving tomb, the myrrh-bearing women sought the immortal Master among the dead; and having received joy from the angel, they proclaimed unto the Apostles: that the Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.  
*These troparia are sung after the Great Doxology on Sunday. And this say:*

*Dismissal Troparion after the Great Doxology (Tones 1, 3, 5, 7):*[^193]  
*Troparion:*  
[Today salvation is come unto the] world; let us hymn Him Who is risen from the tomb and the Author of our life; for having destroyed death, He hath given us the victory, and peace, and great mercy.  
*And say this when the Tone is 2, 4, 6, 8:*  
*Dismissal Troparion (Tones 2, 4, 6, 8):*  
*Troparion:*  
Having risen from the tomb, and broken the bonds of hades, Thou didst destroy the condemnation of death, O Lord, delivering all from the snares of the enemy; and having shown Thyself unto Thine Apostles, and sent them forth to preach, through them Thou hast granted Thy peace unto the universe, O Thou Who alone art Plenteous in mercy.  
*Then the litany and dismissal.*

## THEOTOKIA AND STAVROTHEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES

*Theotokia according to the Tones: Troparia to the Cross and the Theotokos (Богородичны противо гласу: тропари Кресту и Богородице), Tone 1:*[^194]  
*Theotokion, Tone 1:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Receive the prayers of thy servants, O Theotokos, and deliver us from every tribulation, for thou didst bear Christ God, the Deliverer of our souls.  
*To the Cross, Tone 1:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Having acquired thy protection, O all-pure one, and being delivered from tribulations by thy prayers, preserved everywhere by the Cross of thy Son, as is our duty, we all piously magnify...

...thee.[^195]  
*Tone 2:*  
*Theotokion, Tone 2:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Holy Mother of the ineffable Light, honoring thee with angelic hymns, we magnify thee.  
*To the Cross, Tone 2:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Preserved by the precious Cross of thy Son, O all-pure Lady Theotokos, we easily overcome all the assaults of the adversary enemy; therefore, as is our duty, we ever bless thee as the Mother of God, the only hope of our souls.  
*Tone 3:*  
*Theotokion, Tone 3:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Whithersoever one is being saved, thither he rightly flees; and what other refuge is such as thine, O Theotokos, sheltering our souls?  
*To the Cross, Tone 3:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Having acquired the Cross of thy Son, O Theotokos, as a rod of strength, thereby we cast down the haughtiness of the enemy, ever magnifying thee.  
*Tone 4:*  
*Theotokion, Tone 4:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Thou art an invincible wall unto Christians, O Theotokos Virgin; for fleeing unto thee, we remain unharmed, and having sinned again, we have thee as an intercessor. Therefore, giving thanks, we cry: Rejoice, full of grace, the Lord is with thee!  
*To the Cross, Tone 4:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Beholding Thy Son hanging upon the Tree, torn in her maternal womb, she that bare Thee [cried]: Woe is me! How hath the ageless Light set,...

...Who didst shine forth unto all the world from thee, O Virgin, Christ God, Who by her sake didst show forth the unwaning light, have mercy on us![^196]  
*To the Cross, Tone 5:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
By the Cross of thy Son, O woman full of the grace of God, all the deception of idols hath been abolished, and the power of demons trampled down. Therefore, we the faithful, as is our duty, ever hymn and praise thee, confessing and magnifying thee as the true Theotokos.  
*Tone 6:*  
*Theotokion, Tone 6:*  
*Theotokion:*  
The most glorious Mother of Christ, and holier than the holy angels, let us hymn with heart and lips, for she bore for us God incarnate, praying unceasingly for our souls.  
*To the Cross, Tone 6:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
She that bare Thee, O Christ, Ever-Virgin, when she beheld Thee lifted up upon the Cross, looking upon Thee in their midst, was wounded in soul by a sword of grief, and weeping maternally with tears: Him do thou entreat, O Christ; have mercy on us, O Lord!  
*Tone 7:*  
*Theotokion, Tone 7:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Rejoice, thou that in thy womb didst contain Him Who cannot be contained in the heavens; rejoice, proclamation of the prophets, through whom Emmanuel hath shone forth upon us;...

...rejoice, Mother of Christ God![^197]  
*To the Cross, Tone 7:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Rejoice, O pure Theotokos, Ever-Virgin, who upon the Cross didst behold thy Son hanging, weeping as a Mother, magnifying His fearless descent.  
*Tone 8:*  
*Theotokion, Tone 8:*  
*Theotokion:*  
Thee do we have as a wall and a haven, and an acceptable intercessor before God Who was born of thee, O unwedded Theotokos, salvation of the faithful.  
*To the Cross, Tone 8:*  
*Stavrotheotokion:*  
Beholding upon the Cross the Lamb, and Shepherd, and Savior of the world, she that bare Thee said weeping: The world indeed rejoices, receiving redemption through Thee, but my womb burns, beholding Thy crucifixion, which Thou endurest for all, my Son and my God!

### Notes

[^183]: Folio 67r concludes Great Compline with the rubric for monastic retirement to the cells in silence ('и ѡ́ходѧ въ своа кѣлеа со всѣцѣ мл҃чанніемь'). The folio marks a major structural division of the Horologion, introduced by a monumental cinnabar title in South Slavic / Early Cyrillic vyaz calligraphy: 'ТРОПАРИ И КОНДАЦИ ПРѢЗЪ ЛѢТО' (Troparia and Kontakia throughout the Year), using the distinctive Middle Ukrainian preposition 'презъ' (Ukrainian през, through/throughout). It inaugurates the weekly liturgical cycle (седмичный круг): Monday dedicated to the Bodiless Powers ('Нб҃ныі воинствъ а́рхістратиѕи...', with Theotokion and Kontakion) and Tuesday to the Holy Forerunner ('Па́мѧ праведнаго с похвалами...').
[^184]: Folio 67v completes the Tuesday Troparion of St. John the Forerunner ('...тѣмже поистиннѣ пострадавъ · радоуѧсѧ бл҃говѣстилъ є́си...'). It provides the Tuesday Theotokion ('Бж҃ію быхомъ є́стествоу причастници...') and the Tone 3 Kontakion to the Forerunner chanted to the famous podoben incipit of Romanos the Melodist ('д҃ва дн҃ь' / 'Дѣва днесь': 'Неплоды дн҃ь хв҃а прч҃тю ража́еть...'). Lines 14–19 introduce Wednesday, dedicated to the Precious Cross, giving the incipit of the Troparion of the Cross ('сп҃си ги люди своа' / LXX Ps. 27:9), the full Stavrotheotokion ('И жетвое ꙁастоупленіе стяжавше прч҃таа...'), and the incipit of the Tone 4 Kontakion ('въꙁнесы на кр҃т' / 'Вознесыйся на Крест волею').
[^185]: Folio 68r directs the reader for the full text of Wednesday's Kontakion to Matins ('пише въ тро́ре · въ оу́трници'). It then details the services for Thursday, dedicated to the Holy Apostles and St. Nicholas of Myra: Troparion of the Apostles (Tone 3, 'Ап҃ли ст҃іи молите мл҃тиваго б҃а...'), Troparion of St. Nicholas (Tone 4, 'Правило вѣрѣ и ѡ́браꙁь крости...'), Theotokion ('Слово ѡч҃ее...'), and the Romanos Kontakion of the Apostles (Tone 2, 'Твръдыꙗ и бг҃овѣщанныꙗ проповѣдателя...'). Friday rubrics repeat Wednesday's hymns of the Cross ('Въ пѧ · и́ ꙗ́къ въ сре па пи...'). The folio concludes with the opening of Saturday's All Saints Troparion ('Ап҃ли мч҃нци · пророци...').
[^186]: Folio 68v completes the Saturday All Saints Troparion ('...и ст҃ліє · прѣподобніи и праведніи...') and provides the liturgical memorials for the departed: the Troparion 'Помяни гн҃ яко благъ рабы своя...', the Theotokion 'Мати ст҃аа прѣчистаго свѣта...', and the incipit of the Kontakion of the Departed 'Съ ст҃ыми покои х҃е'. Lines 9–10 introduce the next monumental subsection under the cinnabar title: 'ТРОПАРИ ВОСКРЕСНИ И ИПАКОИ' (Resurrectional Troparia and Hypakoai of the Eight Tones). For Tone 1, it provides the Sunday Resurrectional Troparion ('Камени ꙁнаменаноу ѡ́ іоудеи...') and begins the Resurrectional Theotokion ('Гавриилоу провѣщавшю тебѣ дв҃ко радо...').
[^187]: Folio 69r concludes the Tone 1 Resurrectional Theotokion ('...мь кивотѣ · іакоже рече праведныи дв҃дь...') and gives the Tone 1 Hypakoe ('Раꙁбоиниче покааніе раи прокраде...'). It then witnesses Tone 2: the Resurrectional Troparion ('Егда съниде къ съ́мрти животе бесъ́мрътныи...'), the Dogmatic Theotokion ('Въся паче оума въся прѣславна...'), and the opening incipit of the Tone 2 Hypakoe ('По страсти шедьше на гробъ, помаꙁати тѣло твое...').
[^188]: Folio 69v concludes the Tone 2 Hypakoe ('...прѣчистое жены х҃е б҃е · видѣша а́гг҃лы въ гробѣ и оужасошѧ...'). It then provides the complete Sunday resurrectional cycle for Tone 3: the Troparion ('Да веселятся нб҃наа и радоуются ꙁемнаа...'), the Theotokion ('Тебе ходатаицю сп҃нію роду нашему, пое́мь бц҃е дв҃о...'), and the vivid angelic Hypakoe ('Оудивляꙗ видѣніемь ѡрошаа гланіи блистаяся а́гг҃ль...').
[^189]: Folio 70r contains the resurrectional cycle for Tone 4: the Troparion ('Свѣтлѫꙗ въскрсеніа проповѣдь...'), the Dogmatic Theotokion ('Еже ѡ вѣка оу́таеное...'), and the Hypakoe ('Иже твое́го прѣславнаго въстаніа...'). Lines 15–19 begin Tone 5 with the Resurrectional Troparion ('Съ беꙁнача́лное слово ѡц҃у и дх҃ови...'), concluding with Christ's voluntary crucifixion and endurance of death.
[^190]: Folio 70v completes the Tone 5 Resurrectional Troparion ('и въскр҃снти оу́мръшаꙗ въ славно́е въскрсеніе свое́'). It provides the Tone 5 Resurrectional Theotokion ('Ра́доуисѧ двери гн҃ꙗ непроходимаꙗ...') and the Hypakoe ('А́гг҃льскыимь ꙁрако́мь оу́мь съмѫщающе...'). Lines 14–19 introduce Tone 6 with its Resurrectional Troparion ('А́гг҃льскыꙗ силы на гробѣ тво́емь...'), concluding with the catchword incipit in cinnabar for the Tone 6 Theotokion ('Иже бл҃ве'). Red marginal letterings identify the structural genres ('б҃' for Theotokion, 'ѵ' for Hypakoe).
[^191]: Folio 71r concludes the Tone 6 Resurrectional Theotokion ('...ннаꙗ нарекыисѧ себѣ мт҃рь · пріиде на съдѫрть вѡльндыь съвѣтѡмь...'). The text displays distinctive archaic Middle Ukrainian orthography ('съдѫрть' / 'сѫдръть' for 'страсть'; 'вѡльндыь' for 'вольнымь'). Lines 6–10 give the Tone 6 Hypakoe ('Вл҃ною животворною ти съ́мрътїю х҃е...'). Lines 10–15 witness Tone 7: the Resurrectional Troparion ('Раꙁдроуши кр҃стѡмь свои́мь съ́мрътъ...') and the Dogmatic Theotokion ('Ꙗко нашемоу въскр҃нію сокровище...').
[^192]: Folio 71v opens with the Tone 7 Hypakoe ('И́же нашь ꙁракъ въспріе́млыи...'). Lines 3–7 contain the Tone 8 Resurrectional Troparion ('Съ вышинѣ съниде мл҃рде...'), followed by the Dogmatic Theotokion ('И́же на́ ради роже́исѧ ѡ дв҃ы...') and the Hypakoe ('Мѵ́роносица живодавномоу прѣстоаще гро́боу...'). The leaf concludes with the cinnabar rubric directing the singing of the two Sunday dismissal troparia after the Great Doxology ('сіа, тро́рѣ · по славословіи велицѣ , въ нп҃елѧ · сеже гл҃и').
[^193]: Folio 72r contains the two solemn Sunday troparia chanted following the Great Doxology: the troparion for odd tones (1, 3, 5, 7), 'Днесь спасение миру бысть...' (the opening words of which are trimmed at the top margin), and the troparion for even tones (2, 4, 6, 8), 'Въскр҃сь ѡ́ гроба, и́ оу́ꙁы растрѫꙁа...'. The bottom half of the leaf inaugurates a rare medieval calendar table recording the so-called 'Egyptian' or non-working days for September through January ('мц҃ь септе · а҃ до · ѕ҃ до · и҃ ве...'), noting whether the prohibition applies until noon ('до' / 'до полоудна'), in the evening ('ве'), or for the entire day ('весь'). The leaf bears the printed quire signature 'к҃ꙁ' (quire 27).
[^194]: Folio 72v completes the calendar table of Egyptian and fasting days for February through August. In lines 2–4, an extraordinary cinnabar monastic statute is embedded: 'вı же ѡтъ лаꙁарѧ въ в҃ · въ вт҃рѫ · срѣдоу · четвертокъ · пѧтокъ · всю · по клѧтвою ст҃ыхъ ѡц҃ь · ктѡ не празнуе' (Likewise from Lazarus Saturday: on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, all week, under the curse of the holy fathers, whosoever does not keep it as a feast). The second half of the folio opens a new hymnographic division under the monumental vyaz headpiece: 'БГ҃ОРОДИЧНЫ ПРОТИВО ГЛ҃ОУ ТРОРЕН КР҃Тꙋ И БЦ҃И' (Theotokia according to the Eight Tones: Troparia to the Cross and Theotokos), providing Tone 1.
[^195]: Folio 73r continues the dismissal Theotokia and Stavrotheotokia of the Eight Tones: Tone 2 ('Мт҃и ст҃аа прч҃тго свѣта...' and 'Чт҃ныи кр҃тъ сн҃а твое́го...'), Tone 3 ('Кождо и́де же сп҃саетсѧ...' and 'Жезлъ силы стяжавше кр҃то...'), and Tone 4 ('Стѣна непобѣдимаꙗ хрстіано́мъ є́си...'). Lines 17–19 begin the Tone 4 Stavrotheotokion in cinnabar ink ('На древѣ висѧще воꙁвѣи на сн҃а твоего...').
[^196]: Folio 73v completes the Tone 4 Stavrotheotokion ('...двы въсіꙗвыи въсемоу мироу х҃е б҃е...'). It provides Tone 5 Stavrotheotokion ('Кр҃тѡмь сы́на твоего · бг҃обл҃годатнаа...'), Tone 6 Theotokion ('Прѣславноу́ю х҃воу мт҃рь...') and Stavrotheotokion ('Тебе рожьшіа х҃е пр҃но дв҃аа...'), and opens Tone 7 with the Theotokion ('Радоуисѧ тебе въмѣстилаго на нб҃сехь въ оу́тробѣ...').
[^197]: Folio 74r concludes the Tone 7 Theotokion and gives its Stavrotheotokion ('Ра́доуисѧ бц҃е чт҃аа пр҃но дв҃о · на кр҃тѣ ꙁрящи...'). It then gives Tone 8: the Theotokion ('Тѧ стѣноу и пристанище и́мамы...') and Stavrotheotokion ('А́гнца и пастыря и сп҃са мироу...'). Lines 14–18 mark a monumental transition in the Fiol Horologion: the opening of the Menologion (Part III) under the decorative cinnabar vyaz title: 'МЦ҃Ь СЕПТЕВРИИ ИМЯ дній · л҃', marking the New Year / Indiction and the memory of St. Symeon the Stylite (September 1). The folio bears quire signature 'к҃ѳ' (quire 29).
