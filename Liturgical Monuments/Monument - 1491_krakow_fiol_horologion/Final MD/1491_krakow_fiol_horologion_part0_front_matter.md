### Notes

[^1]: Physical leaf p1 serves as the protective paper flyleaf preceding the incunabulum folios in the surviving copy from Kyiv St. Michael’s Golden-Domed Monastery (now preserved in the National Library of Ukraine, Kyiv, Incunabula Collection).
[^2]: Cyrillic archival shelfmark 'ГА-173' (GA-173) inscribed in the upper right margin.
[^3]: The entry records the formal accession of this Schweipolt Fiol 1491 Krakow incunabulum into the monastic library of St. Michael’s Golden-Domed Monastery in Kyiv on April 23, 1862.
[^1205]: Major structural landmark heading marking the beginning of the Rule of Holy Communion (Правило ко святому причащению).
[^1354]: Concluding landmark for the landmark publication edition of the Krakow Horologion (*Chasoslov*), the earliest printed Cyrillic liturgical monument, printed at Krakow in the printing shop of Schweipolt Fiol in 1491.
[^1359]: The surviving copy of the 1491 Krakow Horologion preserved in the National Library of Ukraine in Kyiv (formerly the Library of the Kyiv Golden-Domed St. Michael's Monastery, shelfmark GA-173 / Inv. I 143) concludes with physical leaf 582. Leaves 581 and 582 constitute the original rear flyleaf sheet (laid paper) bearing historical provenance, archival certification, and multiple layers of early modern reader marginalia and scribal pen trials.
