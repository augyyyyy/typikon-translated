[^402]: May 21: Feast of the Holy Equals-to-the-Apostles Constantine and Helena. The codex records the full texts of the famous Troparion in Tone 8 (*Креста Твоего образъ на небеси видѣвъ...*) and Kontakion in Tone 3 (*Константинъ днесь съ матерію Еленою Крестъ являютъ...*) sung to the model *Дѣва днесь*. At the Divine Liturgy, the Prokeimenon is "Sing praises to our God, sing praises" (LXX Ps. 46:7) with verse "O clap your hands, all ye nations" (LXX Ps. 46:2), and the Epistle is Galatians 1:11–19 (lection 200). The rubrics preserve an important liturgical contingency: if May 21 falls on a Friday, the Epistle is read instead from the Acts of the Apostles (Acts 15:5–34, Thursday of the 5th week after Pascha).
