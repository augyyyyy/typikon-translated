[^360]: On leaf p311, the Great Blessing of Waters (Величественное освященіе водъ) is performed on the feast of Theophany at the water springs or cisterns following Matins and the dismissal. The three Old Testament lections (пареміи) read over the water are from the Prophet Isaiah (Isa. 35:1–10; Isa. 55:1–13; Isa. 12:3–6).
[^361]: Epistle reading at the Great Blessing of Waters on Theophany: 1 Cor. 10:1–4. The incipit *бра̃е, не велю* corresponds to *братіе, не хощу васъ не вѣдѣти* ("Brethren, I would not that ye should be ignorant"). The Great Prayer of Sanctification begins here with the incipit *Славимъ Тя, Владыко* ("We glorify Thee, O Master, Lover of mankind"), a variant opening of the great consecratory prayer attributed to St. Sophronius of Jerusalem.
[^362]: The festal antiphons of the Divine Liturgy of Theophany: Antiphon 1 is drawn from Psalm 113 [LXX 113:1–3] with the refrain *Through the prayers of the Mother of God, O Savior, save us*; Antiphon 2 is drawn from Psalm 114 [LXX 114:1–3] with the festal refrain *Save us, O Son of God, Who wast baptized in the Jordan, who sing unto Thee: Alleluia*.
[^363]: The Entrance hymn of the Liturgy of Theophany combines LXX Ps. 117:26 and Ps. 117:27 (*Blessed is He that comes in the name of the Lord; God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us*). In place of the Trisagion (*Святый Боже*), the baptismal hymn from Galatians 3:27 is sung: *As many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ. Alleluia*. The Kinonikon (Communion verse) is Titus 2:11.
[^364]: January 7: Synaxis of the Holy Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John (Соборъ святаго славнаго пророка, предтечи и крестителя Іоанна). The Great Prokeimenon at Vespers is Psalm 113:11 (*Our God is in heaven and on earth: He hath done whatsoever He pleased*). The Kontakion sung after Ode 6 is the Tone 6 Kontakion of the Synaxis (*Плотскаго Ти, Христе, пришествія убоявся Іорданъ...*).
[^365]: Liturgy of the Synaxis of the Forerunner: the Epistle lection from Acts 19:1–8 is appointed for the 6th Week of Pascha (*в па̃. ѕ҃. н҃е* = въ память 6-я недѣли, i.e. Acts 19:1–8); the Gospel is John 1:29–34; the Kinonikon is Psalm 111:6 (*In everlasting memory shall the righteous be*). Rubrics follow regarding the concurrence of the feast with a Sunday.
[^366]: Rubrics for Saturday and Sunday after Theophany: Saturday Epistle Eph. 6:10–17, Gospel Mt. 4:1–11; Sunday after Theophany Prokeimenon Psalm 32:22, Gospel Mt. 4:12–17. Followed by a liturgical rubrical directive (*сказъ*) regulating the calculation of variable Lucan/Matthean lections based on the day of the month. January 8 commemorates Ven. Domnica, Ven. George the Chozebite (in the text written as *Єфеѡрьꙗ/Геѡргіа*), and St. Emilian the Confessor.
[^367]: January 9: Commemoration of the holy martyr Polyeuctus and holy father Marcian (written as *маркелїна*). January 10: Commemoration of our father among the saints Gregory of Nyssa, St. Dometian of Melitene, and Ven. Marcian of Constantinople. The Sessional Hymn after Ode 3 is set to the model *Ликъ ангельскій* ("The angelic choir"). The Prokeimenon at Liturgy is Psalm 149:5 (*Восхвалятся преподобніи въ славѣ*).
[^368]: January 11: Commemoration of our venerable father Theodosius the Cenobiarch (Преподобнаго отца нашего Ѳеодосія Великаго, общаго житія начальника). At Vespers, three lections common to venerable monastics are appointed (Wisdom of Solomon 5:15–6:3; 3:1–9; 4:7–15). The Matins Gospel is Luke 6:17–23. The Katavasia is *Глубины откры подошву* (Heirmos of the Theophany Canon).
[^369]: January 12 commemorates the Holy Martyr Tatiana (Святыя мученицы Татіаны); January 13 commemorates Martyrs Hermylus and Stratonicus (Ермила и Стратоника); January 14 is the Apodosis (Apodosis / отданіе) of the Holy Theophany, combined with the commemoration of the Holy Fathers slain in Sinai and Raithu (преподобныхъ отцевъ въ Синаи и Раиѳѣ избіенныхъ) and St. Sava of Serbia.
