[^420]: On fol. 371, Matins and Divine Liturgy rubrics for the feast of the Holy Preeminent Apostles Peter and Paul (June 29) are set forth, including the Matins Gospel, the automelon kontakion in Tone 2 (*Твердыя и боговещанныя проповѣдателя*), the distribution of candles at Ode 9, the post-dismissal anointing with holy oil, and the Liturgy Epistle from 2 Corinthians (lection 193).
[^421]: On fol. 372, the Divine Liturgy for Sts. Peter and Paul concludes with Matthew 16:13–19, followed by the complete Vespers and Matins appointments for the Synaxis of the Twelve Holy Apostles (June 30), featuring their kontakion in Tone 2 (*Петра вѣрнїи камень вѣрѣ похвалимъ*).
[^422]: On fol. 373, the Divine Liturgy for the Twelve Apostles concludes, followed by the decorative headpiece opening the month of July (31 days). Commemorations include Sts. Cosmas and Damian (July 1) and the feast of the Deposition of the Precious Robe of the Most Holy Theotokos at Blachernae (July 2) with its troparion in Tone 8 (*Богородице приснодѣво*).
[^423]: On fol. 374, the feast of the Deposition of the Precious Robe concludes with its kontakion in Tone 4 (*Ѡдѣнїе всѣмъ вѣрнымъ*) and Liturgy lections, followed by the commemorations of St. Hyacinth (July 3) and St. Andrew, Archbishop of Crete (July 4), with his kontakion in Tone 2 (*Вострубивъ ясно*).
[^424]: On fol. 375, the kontakion of St. Andrew of Crete concludes, followed by the commemorations of Venerable Martha and St. Athanasius the Athonite, founder of the Great Lavra of Mount Athos (July 5), with All-Night Vigil rubrics and his troparion in Tone 3 (*Иже въ плоти житїю твоему*).
[^425]: On fol. 376, Matins and Liturgy for St. Athanasius of Athos conclude with his kontakion in Tone 4 (*Яко невещественныя существомъ зря*), followed by Venerable Sisoes the Great (July 6) with his kontakion (*Постникъ аггелъ на земли*), and Sts. Thomas and Acacius (July 7).
[^426]: On fol. 377, the commemoration of St. Acacius of the Ladder concludes, followed by the feast of the Holy Great-Martyr Procopius (July 8) with Vespers, Matins, his kontakion in Tone 2 (*Божественною ревностїю Христовою распалаемь*), and Liturgy rubrics.
[^427]: On fol. 378, the Liturgy for St. Procopius concludes, followed by Hieromartyr Pancratius of Taormina (July 9), the 45 Martyrs of Nicopolis (July 10), and the feast of the Holy Great-Martyr Euphemia the All-Praised (July 11), commemorating her miracle at the Council of Chalcedon, with her troparion in Tone 4 (*Жениха своего возлюбивши*) and kontakion in Tone 8 (*Подвигы странїя*).
[^428]: On fol. 379, the kontakion of St. Euphemia concludes with explicit reference to the 630 God-bearing Fathers of Chalcedon, followed by the Liturgy, the Martyrs Proclus and Hilary (July 12), and the Synaxis of the Holy Archangel Gabriel and St. Stephen the Sabbaite (July 13).
[^429]: On fol. 380, the Synaxis of Archangel Gabriel concludes with his kontakion in Tone 4 (*Бесплътнымъ слугамъ ты еси предстатель*), followed by the Holy Apostle Aquila (July 14) with his kontakion in Tone 4 (*Яко велїе солнце церкви*), and the feast of the Holy Martyrs Cyricus and Julitta alongside the Falling Asleep of the Holy Equal-to-the-Apostles, the Great Prince Volodymyr [Vladimir], named Basil, who baptized the Rus' Land (July 15).
