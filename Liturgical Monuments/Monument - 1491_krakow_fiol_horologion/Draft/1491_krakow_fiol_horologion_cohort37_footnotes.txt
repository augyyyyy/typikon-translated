[^410]: On fol. 361, the Menaion commemorates the Holy Martyr Aquilina and St. Triphyllius of Leucosia on June 13, and opens the office for the Prophet Elisha and Patriarch Methodius on June 14.
[^411]: On fol. 362, the rubrics prescribe dual kontakia for St. Methodius (Tone 2, honoring his defense of the holy icons) and the Prophet Elisha (Tone 2, Podoben: "Seeking the highest..."), concluding with the commemoration of the Prophet Amos on June 15.
[^412]: On fol. 363, commemorations include St. Tychon of Amathus (June 16), the Persian Martyrs Manuel, Sabel, and Ismael (June 17), the Martyr Leontius (June 18), and the Apostle Jude, brother of the Lord (June 19), whose troparion refers to the common troparion for St. Symeon the Kinsman of the Lord on April 27.
[^413]: On fol. 364, the liturgical appointments conclude for the Apostle Jude, followed by St. Methodius of Patara (June 20), St. Julian of Tarsus (June 21), St. Eusebius of Samosata (June 22), St. Agrippina (June 23), and the vigil opening for the Nativity of St. John the Baptist (June 24).
[^414]: On fol. 365, the rubrics set forth the order of Small and Great Vespers for the feast of the Nativity of the Honorable Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John (June 24), including the troparion *Прро́че и҆ прѣдте́че* (Tone 4), Old Testament readings, Litiya, and the blessing of loaves.
[^415]: On fol. 366, Matins for the Nativity of the Forerunner continues with the Polyeleos, Megalynarion, the Gospel lection from Luke, and the two canons (one in Tone 6 and one in Tone 4 by St. John Damascene), followed by the Kontakion in Tone 2 (*Непло́ды днесь*).
[^416]: On fol. 367, the Liturgy for the Nativity of the Forerunner is concluded, followed by the commemoration of the Venerable Martyr Febronia on June 25 with her kontakion in Tone 4 (*И҆зъ мла́да все́ ѡ҆ста́вльши*).
[^417]: On fol. 368, the afterfeast of the Forerunner is concluded alongside St. Febronia, followed by the commemoration of St. David of Thessalonica (June 26) with his tree-ascetic kontakion in Tone 1 (*Са́дъ присноцвѣтꙋ́щь*), and St. Samson the Hospitable (June 27) with his kontakion in Tone 8 (*Ꙗ҆́кѡ врача́ прѣизрѧ́дна*).
[^418]: On fol. 369, St. Samson's kontakion concludes, followed by the feast of the Translation of the Relics of the Holy Unmercenaries Cyrus and John on June 28 with their troparion in Tone 1 and kontakion in Tone 2 (*Велї́кое враче́ство*).
[^419]: On fol. 370, the Divine Liturgy for Sts. Cyrus and John concludes, followed by the opening of the All-Night Vigil for the Holy Preeminent Apostles Peter and Paul on June 29, including Small and Great Vespers, the common troparion in Tone 4 (*И҆́же а҆по́столѡвъ прѣкопрѣсто́лници*), Litiya, Aposticha, and the patristic reading of the interpretation (*толкованїе*).
