[^430]: On fol. 381, the service of the Holy Martyrs Cyricus and Julitta concludes with the completion of their kontakion in Tone 3 and Liturgy lections (including 1 Cor. 13:11). July 16 commemorates the Hieromartyr Athenogenes alongside the solemn Synaxis of the Holy Fathers of the Six Ecumenical Councils, meticulously detailing the traditional patristic counts: Chalcedon (630), Nicaea I (318), Constantinople I (150), Ephesus (200), Constantinople II (167), and Constantinople III (170).
[^431]: On fol. 382, the feast of the Holy Fathers of the Ecumenical Councils continues with the troparion in Tone 8 (*Препрославленъ еси Христе Боже нашъ*), the famous dogmatic kontakion in Tone 8 (*Апостольское проповѣданїе и отеческая преданїя*), and complete liturgical rubrics for Matins and Divine Liturgy, prescribing the Epistle from Titus 3:8–15 (the standard lection for the Holy Fathers).
[^432]: On fol. 383, the Liturgy for the Holy Fathers concludes with Matthew 5:14–19, noting that the commemoration of St. Athenogenes is transferred at the ecclesiarch's discretion. Commemorations for July 17 follow: the Holy Great-Martyr Marina (Margaret) and the translation of the relics of St. Lazarus of Mount Galesios, featuring Marina's ancient kontakion in Tone 3 (*Марина днесь вражїю главу скрушаетъ*).
[^433]: On fol. 384, the commemorations for the second half of July proceed with the Martyr Emilian (July 18, Alleluia service), St. Macrina (sister of St. Basil the Great) and St. Dios the Wonderworker (July 19) with his kontakion in Tone 2 (*Мужествомъ души божественнѣ вооружився*), and the opening of the Great Feast of the Holy and Glorious Prophet Elijah (July 20), featuring his classical troparion in Tone 4 (*Во плоти ангелъ, пророковъ степень*).
[^434]: On fol. 385, the feast of the Prophet Elijah concludes with his troparion's ending, his kontakion in Tone 2 (*Пророче и прозорливче великихъ дѣлъ Бога нашего*), and complete Divine Liturgy rubrics (specifying James 5:10–20 and Luke 4:22–30). Commemorations for July 21 follow: Venerable John and Symeon the Fool-for-Christ and the Prophet Ezekiel, with the opening of the saints' kontakion in Tone 2 (*Христовою любовїю распалаема*).
[^435]: On fol. 386, the commemoration of Venerable John and Symeon concludes, followed by Holy Myrrh-bearer Mary Magdalene, Equal-to-the-Apostles, and the Hieromartyr Phocas (July 22), with her troparion in Tone 1 (*Христу, насъ ради отъ Дѣвы рождшемуся*) and the opening of her kontakion in Tone 3 to the podoben *Today the Virgin* (*Предстоящи, преславная, у креста Спасова*).
[^436]: On fol. 387, Mary Magdalene's kontakion concludes, followed by Liturgy rubrics, the Martyrs Trophimus and Theophilus (July 23, Alleluia), and the opening of the feast of the Holy Martyr Christina alongside the Holy Passion-bearing Princes Boris and Gleb (baptized Roman and David) on July 24, prescribing an All-Night Vigil with Old Testament lections and solemn Aposticha stichera.
[^437]: On fol. 388, Matins for St. Christina and the Holy Passion-bearers Boris and Gleb proceeds with the Polyeleos, the Matins Gospel from Luke, St. Christina's kontakion in Tone 4 (*Свѣтовидная голубица позналася еси*), and the opening of the kontakion of Sts. Boris and Gleb in Tone 3 to the podoben *Today the Virgin* (*Возсїя днесь преславная память ваю*).
[^438]: On fol. 389, the service of Sts. Boris and Gleb concludes with their Praises, an allusion to their status as the first-fruits of Rus' baptism (*Первоновокрѣщени*), and the Divine Liturgy lections (Romans 8:28–39 and John 15:17–16:2). Commemorations for July 25 follow: the Falling Asleep of St. Anna, mother of the Mother of God, alongside Holy Olympias the Deaconess and Venerable Eupraxia, with St. Anna's troparion in Tone 4 (*Жизнь рождшую во утробѣ носила еси*).
[^439]: On fol. 390, the feast of the Falling Asleep of St. Anna concludes with her troparion's ending, her kontakion in Tone 2 (*Прародителей Христовыхъ празднуемъ память*), and Divine Liturgy lections. Commemorations follow for the Hieromartyr Hermolaus and his companions (July 26, Alleluia), and the opening of the Great Feast of the Holy Great-Martyr and Unmercenary Healer Panteleimon (July 27) with his troparion in Tone 3 (*Страстотерпче Христовъ Пантелеимоне*).
