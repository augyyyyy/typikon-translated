[^320]: Leaf p271 marks the conclusion of the Sixth Hour and the opening of the Ninth Hour of the Royal Hours for the Eve of the Nativity of Christ. The Sixth Hour features the Troparion of Tone 5 (*Come, ye Christ-bearing people* / *Прїидите христоноснїи людїе*), Prokeimenon from LXX Ps. 109:3 (*From the womb before the morning star*), Prophecy from Isaiah 7:10–16; 8:1–4, 9–10, the Epistle from Hebrews 1:10–2:3 (appointed for the 2nd Sunday of the Great Fast), and the Gospel from Matthew 2:1–12 (*When Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea*). The hour concludes with the Troparion of the Forefeast (*She was enrolled once* / *Списася иногда*), the Sessional hymn (*Quickly go before* / *Скоро предвари*), the Kontakion (*Today the Virgin* / *Дѣва днесь*), and a period of silent secret prayer (*молящеся въ себе*) before opening the Ninth Hour.

[^321]: Leaf p272 contains the Royal Hours Ninth Hour. In this early Ruthenian recension, the three appointed psalms are Psalm 109 (*The Lord said unto my Lord*), Psalm 84 (*Lord, Thou hast been favourable unto Thy land*), and Psalm 85 (*Bow down Thine ear, O Lord*). The service appoints the Troparia of Tone 1 (*When Joseph went up to Bethlehem*), Tone 6 (*Today He is born of the Virgin*), and Tone 8 (*O lawgivers of Israel* / *Законоположници*), followed by the Prokeimenon from LXX Ps. 86:5, 1 (*A man shall say: Mother Zion*), the 7th Paremia from Isaiah 9:6–7 (*A child is born unto us*), the Epistle to the Hebrews cross-referenced to the Synaxis of the Theotokos on December 26 (Heb. 2:11–18), and the Gospel from Matthew 2:13–23 (*When the wise men were departed*).

[^322]: Leaf p273 concludes the Ninth Hour with Dan. 3:34 (*Deliver us not up utterly for Thy holy Name's sake*) and sets forth the Typika (*Изобразительны*) with Psalms 102 and 145, the Beatitudes, and the Kontakion of the Forefeast. It then provides the rubrics for Great Vespers joined to the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great on the Eve of the Nativity, rung at the 9th or 10th hour. Uniquely, the priest completes the Proskomedia during the chanting of the stichologia of *Lord, I have cried* (Tone 2) while the deacon censes the church.

[^323]: Leaf p274 details the Vespers and Liturgy of St. Basil for Christmas Eve: 8 stichera on *Lord, I have cried* (including *Come, let us rejoice in the Lord*, *When the Lord Jesus was born*, *Thy kingdom*, *What shall we offer Thee*, and the Doxastikon *When Augustus reigned upon earth* by the Nun Cassia), Little Entrance with the Gospel, Old Testament Paremias interspersed with sung festal troparia, Trisagion (*Holy God*), Epistle to the Hebrews 1:1–12 (appointed for the 1st Saturday of the Great Fast), Alleluia with verses from LXX Ps. 109:1–3, Gospel from Luke 2:1–20, and the Liturgy of St. Basil with Communion verse from LXX Ps. 148:1 (*Praise the Lord from the heavens*). The rubric introduces the rubrical transfer when Christmas Eve falls on a Saturday or Sunday.

[^324]: Leaf p275 details the liturgical dispensation when the Eve of the Nativity of Christ falls on a Saturday or Sunday: the fast is mitigated, the Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom is celebrated in the morning, and the brethren consume bread and wine in church after the dismissal, while the Liturgy of St. Basil the Great is deferred to the Feast Day itself (December 25). Vespers is sung in the late afternoon (at the 10th hour) with Kathisma 1 (*Blessed is the man*), the Old Testament paremias, the Little Litany without *Holy God*, the Apostle from Galatians 3:23–29 (*Before faith came*), and the Gospel from Luke 2:1–20.

[^325]: Leaf p276 concludes the Eve meal dispensation (permitting oil and two cups of wine in the refectory) and outlines the precedence rules when the Nativity of Christ falls on Sunday: all Octoechos Resurrection elements (Sunday canons, Resurrection Gospel, *Having seen the Resurrection of Christ*, and *Holy is the Lord our God*) are completely superseded by the Feast, with only 3 festal kathismata sung. In the first hour of the night, the great bell is sounded for the All-Night Vigil beginning with Great Compline (*Павечерница великая*), followed by the Litiya procession singing the festal idiomela of Tone 1, Tone 5 (*The Persian magi*), and Tone 6 (*The angels dance*).

[^326]: Leaf p277 contains the Aposticha of Great Compline / Litiya, the Blessing of the Loaves (Artoklasia) with the Festal Troparion sung 3 times (Tone 4, *Thy Nativity, O Christ our God* / *Рожество Твое, Христе Боже наш*), and a patristic reading from St. John Chrysostom's Homilies on Matthew. It then sets forth the order of Festal Matins: *God is the Lord*, Kathismata 20 and 21, Polyeleos, Festal Sessional hymns, Gradual Hymns of Tone 4, Gospel from Matthew 1:18–25, Psalm 50, and the two festive canons of Tone 1 by St. Cosmas of Maiuma (*Christ is born, give ye glory*) and St. John of Damascus (*Of old, the Master that worked wonders saved a people*).

[^327]: Leaf p278 details the Matins canons and post-Ode hymnography: katavasia heirmoi sung by both choirs together, the Hypakoe of Tone 8 (*The firstfruits of the Gentiles* / *Начатокъ языкъ*), St. Gregory the Theologian's Oration 38 on the Theophany or Nativity (*Christ is born, glorify Him*), the Kontakion of Tone 3 by St. Romanos the Melodist (*The Virgin today gives birth to the Transcendent in essence* / *Дѣва днесь Пресущественнаго раждаетъ*), St. Basil the Great's Homily on the Nativity, distribution of lit candles unto the brethren at Ode 9 with omission of the Magnificat (*More honorable than the Cherubim*), the Exapostilarion (*He hath visited us from on high* / *Посѣтилъ ны есть съ высоты*), and 4 idiomela at the Praises.

[^328]: Leaf p279 marks the conclusion of Matins with the Praises (Tone 4, *Rejoice, ye righteous* / *Веселитеся праведнїи*), Glory in Tone 6 (*When the time of Thy coming upon earth was fulfilled*), Both now in Tone 2 (*Today Christ is born of the Virgin in Bethlehem*), the Great Doxology, anointing of the brethren with holy oil by the hegumen, and the opening of the Christmas Day Divine Liturgy (December 25). Celebrated early on account of the vigil, the Liturgy features the Festal Antiphons: Antiphon 1 (LXX Ps. 110 with refrain *By the prayers of the Theotokos, O Savior, save us*) and Antiphon 2 (LXX Ps. 111 with refrain *Save us, O Son of God, who wast born of the Virgin, who sing unto Thee: Alleluia*).

[^329]: Leaf p280 concludes the Festal Antiphons of the Christmas Day Liturgy: Antiphon 2 verses from LXX Ps. 111:1–4 with the hymn *Only-begotten Son*, and Antiphon 3 from LXX Ps. 109:1–3 with the Festal Troparion (*Thy Nativity, O Christ our God*). It concludes with the Little Entrance verse from LXX Ps. 109:3–4 (*From the womb before the morning star have I begotten Thee. The Lord hath sworn and will not repent: Thou art a Priest unto ages after the order of Melchizedek*), followed by the Festal Troparion.
