[^130]: Folio 40v witnesses the transitional rubrics from the Ninth Hour to Vespers (Вечерня / Hesperinos), specifying the concluding prayers in the narthex (притворъ / pronaos), the ascent of the brethren into the temple after the priest's blessing, and introduces the Prooemiac or Introductory Psalm of Vespers, Psalm 103 (LXX Ps. 103:1–6; 'Благослови, душе моя, Господа...'). In this 15th-century Ruthenian monastic witness, the psalm is interspersed with ancient Studite refrains (припѣвы): 'Благословенъ еси, Господи' ('Blessed art Thou, O Lord') after the opening verse, and 'Дивна дѣла Твоя, Господи' ('Marvelous are Thy works, O Lord') following verse 4.
[^131]: Folio 41r continues Psalm 103 (LXX Ps. 103:7–17), celebrating God's providential ordering of the waters, the sustenance of the beasts of the field and the birds of heaven, and the gifts of wine, oil, and bread that gladden and strengthen the heart of man. It preserves the ancient choral refrain following verse 12: 'Слава Ти, Господи, сотворившему вся' ('Glory to Thee, O Lord, Who hast made all things!').
[^132]: Folio 41v continues Psalm 103 (LXX Ps. 103:18–28), praising the nocturnal cycle, the young lions seeking prey from God, the daytime labor of mankind, and the leviathan sporting in the vast sea. At the bottom of the leaf (line 19), Schweipolt Fiol's typesetters produced an anomalous corrupted line in cinnabar ('слава ти г҃и , анепепааипаньѣамене...'), caused by repetitive typesetting sorts and scrambled syllables anticipatory of the refrain completed on fol. 42r ('Слава Ти, Господи, наипаче сотворившему вся').
[^133]: Folio 42r completes Psalm 103 (LXX Ps. 103:29–35), resolving the typesetting corruption of the preceding leaf ('нааипаньѣ сътворившомоу вса...'), followed by three Alleluias with metanias, the Great Litany, and 15th-century Typikon rubrics regulating whether the Psalter Stichologia (the appointed evening Kathisma) is chanted or omitted, before advancing to the Lucernarium ('Господи, воззвахъ'). The signature at the bottom right marks the opening of quire 22 (к҃в).
[^134]: Folio 42v contains Psalm 140 (LXX Ps. 140:1–8), the foundational psalm of the Lucernarium (LXX Ps. 140; 'Господи, воззвахъ къ Тебѣ, услыши мя...'; Greek Κύριε, ἐκέκραξα πρὸς σέ, εἰσάκουσόν μου), designated in the left margin by the red numeral 'р҃м'. It preserves the ancient vocal refrain 'Услыши мя, Господи' ('Hear me, O Lord') following verse 1, and the solemn evening prayer of incense ('Да исправится молитва моя, яко кадило предъ Тобою...').
[^135]: Folio 43r concludes Psalm 140 (LXX Ps. 140:8–10) and introduces Psalm 141 (LXX Ps. 141:1–6; 'Гласомъ моимъ къ Господу воззвахъ...'; Greek Φωνῇ μου πρὸς Κύριον ἐκέκραξα), rubricated as the 'Разумъ Давыдовъ, внегда бы въ пещерѣ моляшеся' (Maskil of David, when he was in the cave praying; LXX Ps. 141), accompanied by the penitential penitential refrain 'Воззвахъ к Тебѣ, спаси мя' ('I cried unto Thee, save me!').
[^136]: Folio 43v concludes Psalm 141 (LXX Ps. 141:7; 'Изведи изъ темницы душу мою...'), and introduces Psalm 129 (LXX Ps. 129:1–8; 'Изъ глубины воззвахъ к Тебѣ, Господи...'; Greek Ἐκ βαθέων ἐκέκραξά σοι, Κύριε), appointed for the insertion of the evening Stichera. It witnesses the Christological refrain 'Христе Спасе мой, спаси мя' ('O Christ my Savior, save me!'), followed by the opening of Psalm 116 (LXX Ps. 116:1–2; 'Хвалите Господа, вси языцы...').
[^137]: Folio 44r completes Psalm 116 (LXX Ps. 116:2) and outlines the rubrical distribution of the six Stichera between the Octoechos and Menaion. It witnesses the ancient evening hymn of thanksgiving, 'Phos Hilaron' (Свѣте тихій / Joyous Light), bearing an explicit authorial attribution to the Hieromartyr Athenogenes ('Твореніе святаго священномученика Анфиногена'), collating with the testimony of St. Basil the Great (De Spiritu Sancto 29:73), followed by the Sunday evening Prokeimenon (LXX Ps. 133:1–2; 'Се нынѣ благословите Господа...').
[^138]: Folio 44v records the complete weekday cycle of evening Prokeimena (Monday through Saturday) with their appointed psalm verses (LXX Ps. 4:4, 22:6, 53:3, 120:2, 58:10, 92:1). It details the Typikon ceremonial for the appointed brother's reverences and the recitation of 'Сподоби, Господи' (Vouchsafe, O Lord), as well as the ancient monastic Lenten order of chanting 'Alleluia' in place of the prokeimenon when no festal troparion is appointed.
[^139]: Folio 45r details the antiphonal performance of the Lenten Alleluia at Vespers, prescribing the specific daily psalm verses chanted by alternating choirs (Monday: LXX Ps. 6:2; Tuesday: LXX Ps. 98:5; Wednesday: LXX Ps. 18:5; Thursday: LXX Ps. 98:5), concluding with the Doxology and three metanias before the holy doors. It preserves the opening petitions of the ancient evening hymn of supplication, 'Сподоби, Господи' (Vouchsafe, O Lord; Greek Καταξίωσον, Κύριε), based on Daniel 3:26 and Psalm 118:12.
