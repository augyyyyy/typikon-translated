[^370]: On leaf p321, the service of the venerable fathers slain in Sinai and Raithu (преподобныхъ отецъ въ Синаи и Раиѳѣ избіенныхъ) is sung in conjunction with St. Sava of Serbia. The Kontakion after Ode 3 is Tone 2 (*Ѡ мира сего мятежа обѣжасте...*). The Prokeimenon at Liturgy is Psalm 67:36 (*Marvelous is God in His saints*). Following this on January 14 begins the Apodosis (Apodosis / отданіе) of Holy Theophany, celebrating exclusively the festal hymns of Theophany at Vespers.
[^371]: On leaf p322, the full order for the Apodosis of Theophany (January 14) is set forth for Vespers, Matins, and Divine Liturgy, sung entirely from the feast without the Kathisma or lections at Vespers, and with both festal canons at Matins and Liturgy. The Communion verse (Kinonikon) is Titus 2:11 (*The grace of God that brings salvation hath appeared unto all men*).
[^372]: January 15: Commemoration of our venerable fathers Paul of Thebes (Павелъ Ѳивейскій) and John the Hut-Dweller (Іоаннъ Кущникъ / Calabytes). Beginning on this day, the regular winter Psalter allocation resumes following the conclusion of Theophany post-feasts: three Kathismata are chanted at Matins, and Kathisma 18 (*Къ Господу внегда скорбѣти*, Psalms 119–133) is chanted at daily Vespers. The Kontakion of St. Paul of Thebes after Ode 3 is sung to the model *Дѣва днесь* ("Today the Virgin").
[^373]: On leaf p324, a significant structural rubric decrees that from January 15 onward, the Mid-Hours (междучасія / Mesoria) are sung in the church, and the Supplicatory Canon (Paraklesis) to the Mother of God is added at Compline. January 16 commemorates the Veneration of the Precious Chains of the Holy Apostle Peter (Поклоненіе честныхъ веригъ святаго и всехвальнаго апостола Петра).
[^374]: At the Liturgy for the Chains of the Apostle Peter (January 16), the Epistle is from Acts 12:1–11 (St. Peter miraculously released from chains by an angel), the Prokeimenon is Psalm 18:5 (*Their sound is gone out into all the earth*), and the Gospel is the 11th Matins Resurrection Gospel (John 21:15–25, Christ's charge to Peter to shepherd His flock). January 17 opens the commemoration of our venerable father Antony the Great (Антоній Великій).
[^375]: January 17: Commemoration of our venerable and God-bearing father Antony the Great (Антоній Великій), celebrated with an All-Night Vigil (*всенощное*). At Great Vespers, Kathisma 1 (*Blessed is the man*) is chanted; the Stichera on *Lord, I have cried* are sung to the model *Called from on high* (Званному свыше); the three Old Testament lections are common to venerable fathers (Wisdom 5:15–6:3; 3:1–9; 4:7–15); and the blessing of loaves (Artoklasia) is celebrated.
[^376]: Matins and Liturgy of St. Antony the Great: the Polyeleos (*Многомилостивый*) is chanted, followed by the Gospel from Luke 6:17–23 and the Supplicatory Canon to the Mother of God (*Многими одержимь напастьми*). After the dismissal of Matins, the ancient rubric directs the priest to anoint the brethren with holy oil taken from the vigil lamp of the saint (*и даетъ святое масло братіи от кандила святаго*). The Liturgy Epistle is Galatians 5:22–6:2.
[^377]: January 18: Commemoration of our fathers among the saints, Archbishops of Alexandria, Athanasius the Great and Cyril (Аѳанасій и Кирилъ Александрійскіи). The Tone 4 Kontakion is *Священноначальницы велицыи благочестія...* ("Great hierarchs of piety..."). The Epistle is Hebrews 13:17–21 (appointed for Dec. 6 / St. Nicholas); Gospel is Matthew 5:14–19. January 19 commemorates our venerable father Macarius of Egypt (Макарій Египтянинъ).
[^378]: January 19: Commemoration of our venerable father Macarius of Egypt (Макарій Египтянинъ). The Kontakion after Ode 6 is Tone 4 (*Блаженную жизнь скончавъ въ житіи...*). January 20 opens the commemoration of our venerable and God-bearing father Euthymius the Great (Евѳимій Великій), where an All-Night Vigil is observed at the discretion of the superior (*аще хощеть настоятель / предстоятель*). The Stichera on *Lord, I have cried* are set to the model *O all-praised martyrs* (Прехвальніи мученицы).
[^379]: January 20: Order of Vespers and Matins for St. Euthymius the Great. At Vespers, the Tone 4 Troparion is *Веселися, пустыня нераждающая...* ("Be glad, O barren desert that bearest not...", based on Isaiah 54:1 / Galatians 4:27). At Matins, 3 Kathismata and the Polyeleos are sung, accompanied by the Canon of the Mother of God (*Holding fast amidst many temptations*) and the canon of the saint to 8. The Tone 8 Kontakion after Ode 6 is *Въ честнѣмъ ти рождествѣ радость тварь обрѣте...* ("In thy precious nativity the creation found joy...").
