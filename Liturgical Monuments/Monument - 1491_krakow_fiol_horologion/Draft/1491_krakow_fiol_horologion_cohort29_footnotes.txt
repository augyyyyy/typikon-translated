[^330]: Leaf p281 details the conclusion of the Christmas Day Divine Liturgy (December 25) and Great Vespers of the feast celebrated in the late afternoon. The Liturgy concludes with the festal Kontakion of Romanos the Melodist (*The Virgin today* / *Дѣва днесь*), the baptismal hymn *As many as have been baptized into Christ* (Gal. 3:27) replacing the Trisagion, Prokeimenon from LXX Ps. 65:4, 1, the Epistle from Galatians 4:4–7 (*When the fulness of time was come*), Alleluia from LXX Ps. 88:6 and Ps. 18:3, the Gospel from Matthew 2:1–12 (*When Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea*), and the Koinonikon from LXX Ps. 110:9 (*The Lord hath sent redemption unto His people*). Following the monastic refectory consolation, Great Vespers is sung without the opening stichologia, featuring 6 festal stichera on *Lord, I have cried* (Tone 2, *Come, let us rejoice in the Lord*), the festal Doxastikon (*Glory to God in the highest*), the Entrance, and the Great Prokeimenon of Tone 7 from LXX Ps. 76:14–15, 11–12 (*Who is a great God as our God?*).

[^331]: Leaf p282 continues the evening Aposticha of Christmas Day with idiomela in Tone 8 (*A glorious wonder* / *Преславное чюдо*) and verses from LXX Ps. 109:1, 3, concluding with Tone 1 (*When the Magi had come together in Bethlehem*). It then introduces December 26: the Synaxis of the Most Holy Theotokos (*Съборъ святыя Богородица*), the commemoration of St. Joseph the Betrothed, and Hieromartyr Euthymius, Bishop of Sardis (d. 824). The Matins structure prescribes the festal Troparion (Tone 4, 3 times), two Kathismata, Sessional hymns of the feast, and the two festive canons of St. Cosmas and St. John (first to 6 with heirmoi doubled, second with heirmos to 6; Katavasia *Christ is born*). Uniquely, the Horologion notes the total suppression of the Biblical Odes and Magnificat through the entire post-festive period up to the Apodosis of Theophany. Following Ode 6, the codex appoints the Idiomelon Kontakion in Tone 6 (*He who was born before the morning star*).

[^332]: Leaf p283 records the full text of the Kontakion for the Synaxis of the Theotokos (Tone 6: *He who was born of the Father without mother before the morning star* / *Преже дньница от Отца без матере родися*). It details Matins Praises (Tone 4, 4 stichera, Glory in Tone 6 *Today the Invisible*, Both now in Tone 2 *Today Christ*), Great Doxology, and the Divine Liturgy for December 26: Beatitudes from festal Odes 3 and 6, Kontakion, Prokeimenon from the Magnificat (Lk. 1:46, 48), the Epistle to the Hebrews 2:11–18 (*Both He that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one*), Alleluia from LXX Ps. 131:8 (*Arise, O Lord, into Thy rest*), and Gospel from Matthew 2:13–23 (the Flight into Egypt). It then outlines the Saturday after the Nativity: Liturgy Prokeimenon from LXX Ps. 44:18, 11 (*I will remember Thy name*), Epistle from 1 Tim. 6:11–16, Alleluia from LXX Ps. 131:1 (*Remember, O Lord, David*).

[^333]: Leaf p284 concludes the Liturgy of the Saturday after Nativity with the Gospel from Matthew 9:27–35 (Healing of the two blind men) and Communion verse from LXX Ps. 115:4 (*The cup of salvation*). It then introduces the rubrics for the Sunday after the Nativity of Christ (*Недѣля по рожествѣ Христовѣ*), commemorating the Holy Ancestors: Righteous Joseph the Betrothed, David the King, and James the Brother of the Lord (*брата Господня*). [^334]: Leaf p285 completes the Troparion of the Holy Ancestors (*Proclaim the marvelous wonders*) and details Sunday Matins: Resurrection Troparion twice, Glory of the saints, Both now of the feast; Kathisma sessional hymns; Psalm 118 with troparia; Hypakoe; Polyeleos; festal sessional hymn; Graduals; Resurrection Gospel; Psalm 50; and Canons (Resurrection to 4, Theotokos to 2, Righteous Ancestors to 4). After Ode 3, it sets forth the full text of the Kontakion of the Holy Ancestors in Tone 1: *The prophetic choir celebrates divinely the marvel wrought unto thee, O Virgin* (*Ликъ пророчьскый празднуетъ божественнѣ*), praising the Incarnation, angels, shepherds, magi, and Joseph crying aloud the wonders of David. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the feast; after Ode 9, two Exapostilaria; Praises with 4 Resurrection stichera and festal stichera in Tone 4.

[^335]: Leaf p286 concludes Sunday Matins with the Doxastikon from Joel 2:30 / Acts 2:19 (*Blood and fire*), the Great Doxology, and the Divine Liturgy of the Sunday after Nativity: Beatitudes to 6 and saints' Ode 6 to 4, Prokeimenon from LXX Ps. 67:36, 27 (*God is marvelous in His saints*), Epistle from Galatians 1:11–19 (appointed for the 20th Sunday after Pentecost), Alleluia from LXX Ps. 131:1, Gospel from Matthew 2:13–23, and Communion verse from LXX Ps. 148:1. The leaf then records crucial Typikon precedence rules: if the Nativity of Christ falls on Sunday, the commemoration of the Holy Ancestors (Joseph, David, and James) cannot be observed on the following Sunday (January 1) because of the double celebration of the Circumcision of Christ and St. Basil the Great, nor on Saturday (December 31) because of the Apodosis of the Nativity; therefore, their commemoration is transferred to December 26. Furthermore, it regulates the distribution of Gospels when two Sundays occur between Christmas and Theophany (Matthew 2:13–23 on the first; Mark 1:1–8 on the Sunday before Theophany).
