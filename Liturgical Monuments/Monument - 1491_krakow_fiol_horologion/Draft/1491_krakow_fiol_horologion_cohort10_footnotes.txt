[^140]: Folio 45v witnesses the conclusion of the evening hymn of supplication, 'Сподоби, Господи' (Vouchsafe, O Lord; Greek Καταξίωσον, Κύριε), with the Father Paul Doxology Standard ('unto ages of ages'), followed by the Litany of Supplication ('Исполнимъ вечернія молитвы наша', Greek Πληρώσωμεν τὴν ἑσпеρινὴν δέησιν ἡμῶν τῷ Κυρίῳ) and the Prayer at the Bowing of Heads with its priestly exclamation ('Буди держава царствія Твоего...'). It then introduces the Aposticha (стихиры на стиховнѣ / aposticha) with the weekday psalm verses from Psalm 122 (LXX Ps. 122:1–4; 'Къ Тебѣ возведохъ очи мои, живущему на небеси...').

[^141]: Folio 46r witnesses the conclusion of the evening Stichera and the Typikon ceremonial of the canonarch (канонархъ / кононархъ), who performs reverences in the center towards the holy icons and to both monastic choirs before returning to his stall. It contains the Canticle of Simeon the God-Receiver ('Нынѣ отпущаеши', Nunc Dimittis; Luke 2:29–32), the Trisagion prayers, the dismissal litany, and the ancient Lenten evening troparia opening with the Angelic Salutation, 'Богородице Дѣво, радуйся' (Ave Maria; Luke 1:28, 42), accompanied by solemn metanias (дл҃е а҃ = 1 prostration).

[^142]: Folio 46v continues the penitential evening troparia for fasting seasons: the Troparion to the Forerunner ('Крестителю Христовъ, тебѣ ся молимъ...') with a prostration, the Troparion to the Apostles, Prophets, and Martyrs ('Молите за ны, святіи апостоли...') with a prostration, and the Sub Tuum Praesidium ('Подъ твое благоутробіе прибѣгаемъ, Богородице Дѣво...'), rubricated to be recited standing without prostration ('без мѣнія'). It records forty Kyrie eleisons, the blessing of the priest, and the ancient monastic intercessory prayer 'Небесный Царю' (O Heavenly King, strengthen our faithful sovereigns...).

[^143]: Folio 47r concludes the prayer 'Небесный Царю' and details the monastic execution of the Prayer of St. Ephrem the Syrian ('Господи и Владыко живота моего...'), prescribing 3 great prostrations, 12 intermediate metanias with the three-part silent prayer of repentance ('Боже, очисти мя грѣшнаго...'), and a final 16th great prostration with hands uplifted on high. The rubrics stipulate that this penitential order is observed during the Apostles' Fast, Dormition Fast, Nativity Fast, and throughout the year on Wednesdays, Fridays, and days with Alleluia, and regulates the transition of the brethren to the refectory for supper (трапеза).

[^144]: Folio 47v witnesses the ancient monastic Table Ritual (Чинъ трапезы), opening with the dining antiphon from Psalm 21 (LXX Ps. 21:27; 'Ядятъ нищіи и насытятся...'). Following the meal, it prescribes the ritual Elevation of the Panagia (Чинъ о возношеніи Панагіи / прѣтѧжа / прѣчиста), the bread dedicated to the Mother of God, accompanied by the Marian troparion 'Бысть чрево Твое святая трапеза...' (Thy womb became a holy table...), 'Честнѣйшую Херувимъ', and the thanksgiving verses from Psalm 91:5 and Psalm 4:7–8.

[^145]: Folio 48r concludes the post-prandial thanksgiving from Psalm 4:8–9, the superior's blessing ('Съ нами Богъ...'), and opens the Order of Small Compline (Чинъ о малой павечерници / Apodeipnon / Compline) with a prominent display title in red cinnabar ligatured script (вязь). The rubric defines the liturgical seasons and feasts when Small Compline is chanted: during All-Night Vigils, feasts of ranked Saints, post-feasts of the Mother of God, the Winter Paschaltide span from December 24 to January 14, and throughout Meatfare and Cheesefare weeks. The signature at the bottom right marks the opening of quire 23 (к҃г).

[^146]: Folio 48v continues Small Compline, prescribing Psalm 50, Psalm 69, and Psalm 142, the Lesser Doxology ('Слава въ вышнихъ Богу'), the Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos (Канонъ молебный Пресвятѣй Богородицѣ), and the appointed troparia according to the day (the Sunday Hypakoe, Festal Troparion, or the daily cycle: 'Боже отецъ нашихъ', 'Иже во всемъ мирѣ мученикъ Твоихъ', 'Со святыми упокой, Христе', 'Молитвами, Господи, всѣхъ святыхъ'). It concludes with the foundational nocturnal prayers of Paul of the Monastery of the Benefactress ('Нескверная, неблазная...') and Antiochus the Monk ('И даждь намъ, Владыко, на сонъ грядущимъ...').

[^147]: Folio 49r introduces the Order of Ordinary Compline (Чинъ обычныя павечерница) with a red cinnabar ligatured display heading (вязь), chanted throughout the year outside of Great Lent. It details the opening Trisagion, Psalm 90 ('Живый въ помощи Вышняго'), the Messianic hymn of the Prophet Isaiah ('С нами Богъ, разумѣйте, языцы, и покаряйтеся, яко с нами Богъ!'; Isaiah 8:9–9:6), the Cherubic hymn ('Безплотное естество Херувимское...'), the Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed ('Вѣрую во единаго Бога...'), and the daily troparia.

[^148]: Folio 49v continues Ordinary Compline, providing the ancient nocturnal prayer of St. Basil the Great ('Господи, Господи, избави насъ отъ всякія стрѣлы летящія во дни...'), the three evening Psalms (Ps. 50, Ps. 69, Ps. 142), the Supplicatory Canon to the Mother of God, and the weekly cycle of evening troparia: Monday for the Archangels, Tuesday for the Forerunner, Wednesday and Friday for the Holy Cross, Thursday for the Apostles and St. Nicholas, followed by the patronal saint of the temple, forty Kyrie eleisons, and the General Prayer of the Hours ('Иже на всякое время...').

[^149]: Folio 50r concludes Ordinary Compline with the 3 great prostrations and 12 metanias of the Prayer of St. Ephrem, the nocturnal prayers of Paul of the Evergetis and Antiochus, the priest's dismissal, and the mutual forgiveness ceremonial between the superior and the brethren ('Благословите, отцы святіи, и простите мя грѣшнаго...'), followed by the evening litany and retirement to cells. At the bottom of the leaf, a magnificent red cinnabar display heading in ligatured script (вязь) inaugurates Great Compline for Great Lent ('Начало павечерници великои въ святую великую четыредесятницю'). The signature at the bottom right marks the opening of quire 24 (к҃д).
