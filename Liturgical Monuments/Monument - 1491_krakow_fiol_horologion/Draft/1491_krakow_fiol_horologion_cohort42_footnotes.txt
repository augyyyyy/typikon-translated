[^486]: August 20 commemorates the Holy Prophet Samuel, the 15th and last Judge of Israel (11th c. BC), who anointed Saul and David as kings of Israel. The Kontakion (*Яко драгий и честный даръ...*, Tone 3) celebrates his childhood dedication at Shiloh and his prophetic ministry.
[^487]: August 21–22 commemorations: August 21: Holy Apostle Thaddaeus of the Seventy and Holy Martyr Bassa of Edessa with her children; August 22: Holy Martyr Agathonicus of Nicomedia and his companions (d. under Maximian).

[^489]: August 23 marks the Apodosis (Apodosis / *Отданіе*) of the Feast of the Dormition, coinciding with the feast of the Holy Martyr Lupus of Novae (d. c. 306, faithful servant of St. Demetrius of Thessalonica). Vespers and Matins repeat the full festive hymnography of August 15.
[^490]: Physical leaf p415 marks August 24: the Holy Hieromartyr Eutyches (1st–2nd c.), disciple of St. John the Theologian and fellow-laborer of the Holy Apostle Paul. His Kontakion (*Апостоломъ сопричастникъ...*, Tone 3) extols him as a star dispelling the dark night of idolatry.
[^491]: August 25 commemorates the Translation of the Relics of the Holy Apostle Bartholomew from Anastasiopolis to the island of Lipari (c. 508), joined with the commemoration of the Holy Apostle Titus.
[^492]: Physical leaf p416 details the combined office for the Holy Apostles Bartholomew and Titus. The Kontakion to Titus (*Павловъ явился еси способникъ...*, Tone 2) commends his collaboration with the Apostle Paul in Crete. Liturgy pericopes: Epistle Titus 1:1–4; Gospel Matthew 5:14–19 (lection 23).
[^493]: August 26 commemorates the Holy Martyrs Adrian and Natalia of Nicomedia (c. 306 under Emperor Galerius Maximian), celebrated with two Idiomela (Tone 1 and Tone 6), Troparion (*Мученицы Твои, Господи...*), and Kontakion (*Мучениковъ возсія божественная память...*, Tone 4).
[^494]: An early modern East Slavic epigraphic gloss in brown cursive script (*скоропись*) in the lower margin of folio 214r: *"Сего днь празноуеть праздникъ срѣтенію иконѣ пр[ечистые] вл[а]д[ы]ч[и]ца наша б[огороди]ца, иже гл[агол]ють володимерьская"* ("On this day is celebrated the feast of the Meeting of the Icon of our Most Pure Lady Theotokos, which is called Vladimirskaya"). This refers to the feast established in commemoration of the miraculous deliverance of Moscow from the invasion of Khan Tamerlane in 1395, officially instituted across Rus' liturgical calendars on August 26.
[^495]: Physical leaf p417 marks August 27: Our Venerable Father Poemen the Great (c. 340–450), renowned desert father of the Egyptian Skete whose sayings form a major foundation of the *Apophthegmata Patrum*. Kontakion: Tone 4, Podoben: "Явился еси".
[^496]: August 28 commemorates Our Venerable Father Moses the Black (the Ethiopian / *Муринъ*, c. 330–405), former brigand turned desert father and martyr in Scetis. The Kontakion (*Мурины в лице заушивъ...*, Tone 4, Podoben: "Явился еси") uses *Мурины* allegorically for the dark demonic forces vanquished through spiritual warfare.
[^497]: August 29 commemorates the Beheading of the Honorable Head of the Holy, Glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John (*Усѣкновеніе честныя главы святаго славнаго Пророка, Предтечи и Крестителя Іоанна*), celebrated with an All-Night Vigil and strict day of fasting.
[^498]: Physical leaf p418 details the vigil order for the Beheading of St. John the Baptist, prescribing three Old Testament lections (Isaiah 40:1–3, 5, 9; 41:17–18; 45:8; 48:20–21; 54:1; Malachi 3:1–3, 5–7, 12, 18; 4:1–6; Wisdom 4:7, 11–14; 5:15–6:3) and the solemn festive Kontakion (*Предтечево славное усѣкновеніе...*, Tone 5).
[^499]: Physical leaf p419 concludes the services for the Beheading of the Forerunner with Liturgy pericopes: Epistle Acts 13:25–32 (lection 33); Gospel Mark 6:14–30 (lection 24).
[^500]: August 30 commemorates the Holy Patriarchs of Constantinople: Alexander (d. 337, champion of the Nicene Council), John the Cappadocian (d. 520), and Paul the New (d. 784, advocate of icon veneration).
[^501]: Physical leaf p420 preserves the additional Kontakion of the Forerunner (*Иродъ, Предтече, беззаконновавъ...*, Tone 4), chanted on August 30 during the commemoration with the Holy Patriarchs.
[^502]: August 31: The Deposition of the Precious Sash (Cincture / Belt / *Положеніе честнаго пояса*) of the Most Holy Theotokos in the Chalkoprateia church in Constantinople (*въ Халкопратіи*), established during the reign of Emperor Theodosius II and Empress Pulcheria (c. 5th c.) and later renewed under Emperor Leo VI.
