[^380]: On leaf p331, concluding the commemoration of St. Euthymius the Great (January 20) with the Ikos, Praises, and Liturgy readings. January 21 commemorates St. Maximus the Confessor (*Маѯимъ Исповѣдникъ*, misprinted as *иконвикъ* in Fiol's text), St. Neophytus (*Неофитъ*), and St. Eugene (*Евгеній*). The Tone 8 sticheron incipit rubric contains an aberrant typesetting form (*покыдно жество*, reflecting *О преславное чюдо* / *Подобенъ*).
[^381]: January 21: Full texts of both Kontakia are printed in the codex: first after Ode 3 to the Martyr Neophytus (*Богоявленію звѣзду вѣрѣ...*), and second after Ode 6 to St. Maximus the Confessor (*Свѣтъ трисіянный вселивыйся въ душу твою...*). At the Divine Liturgy, the Epistle is Hebrews 11:33–12:2 (appointed for All Saints) and Gospel is Matthew 7:24–8:4 (Saturday lection).
[^382]: January 22: Commemoration of the Holy Apostle Timothy (*Тимоѳей*) of the Seventy and the Venerable Martyr Anastasius the Persian (*Анастасій Персянинъ*). The joint Kontakion after Ode 6 is Tone 4 (*Божественнаго ученика и соузника Павлова, Тимоѳея...*). At the Divine Liturgy, the Epistle is 2 Timothy 1:3–9.
[^383]: Between leaf p333 (folio 172v) and leaf p334 (folio 173r), the Fiol codex transitions directly from the Liturgy of St. Timothy and St. Anastasius (January 22) to the Matins of St. Gregory the Theologian (January 25, *Григорій Богословъ*). The intermediate commemorations for January 23 (Hieromartyr Clement of Ancyra) and January 24 (Venerable Xenia of Rome), as well as the opening Vespers of St. Gregory, are omitted in this printing. The Kontakion after Ode 6 is the famous Tone 3 *Богословнымъ языкомъ своимъ...* sung to the model *Дѣва днесь*.
[^384]: January 26 commemorates Venerable Xenophon (*Ксенофонтъ*) and his family, along with the Translation of the Relics of St. Theodore the Studite (*Ѳеодоръ Студитъ*). The Kontakion is Tone 4 to the model *Явися днесь*. January 27 begins the major feast of the Translation of the Relics of St. John Chrysostom (*Пренесеніе мощей Іоанна Златоуста*). The rubrics explicitly cite the texts previously appointed for his main feast on November 13 (*по якоже 13*).
[^385]: January 27: Matins and Liturgy for the Translation of the Relics of St. John Chrysostom. The Kontakion after Ode 3 is Tone 1 (*Возвеселися тайно честная Церковь...*), while the Kontakion after Ode 6 is his primary feast Kontakion from November 13 (*Оть небесъ принялъ еси...*). The rubric specifies anointing the brethren with holy oil from the vigil lamp of the saint (*и дается святое масло, брано от кандила святаго*). January 28 opens the commemoration of our venerable father Ephrem the Syrian (*Ефремъ Сиринъ*).
[^386]: January 28: Order of Matins and Liturgy for St. Ephrem the Syrian. The Kontakion after Ode 6 is Tone 2 (*Часъ присно провидя испытанія...*). The Epistle is Galatians 5:22–6:2 and Gospel Matthew 11:27–30. January 29 commemorates the Translation of the Relics of the Holy Hieromartyr Ignatius the God-Bearer of Antioch (*Пренесеніе мощей Игнатія Богоносца*).
[^387]: January 30: Commemoration of the Three Holy Hierarchs (*Святіи три святители: Василій Великій, Григорій Богословъ, Іоаннъ Златоустъ*), together with the Hieromartyr Hippolytus, Pope of Rome (*Ипполитъ Римскій*). The rubric bears exceptional hymnographic witness by explicitly attributing the common service of the Three Hierarchs to its historical author, the 11th-century Byzantine hymnographer John Mauropous, Metropolitan of Euchaita (*въслѣдованіе... сътворено въсесвященымь митрополитомь Евхаїтскымь гдл Іѡанномъ*), while directing that St. Hippolytus's office be relegated to Compline.
[^388]: January 30: Matins order for the Three Holy Hierarchs. The famous Kontakion by John Mauropous is Tone 2 (*Священныя и боговещанныя проповѣдатели...*) sung to the model *Твердыя и боговѣщанныя*. During the distribution of holy oil after the dismissal, the choir chants stichera taken from the previous services of each hierarch earlier in the month: St. Basil (January 1), St. Gregory the Theologian (January 25), and St. John Chrysostom (January 27).
[^389]: January 31 concludes the month of January with the Holy Unmercenaries Cyrus and John (*Кѵръ и Іоаннъ*). The Kontakion after Ode 6 is Tone 3 (*Отъ божественныя благодати дарованіе чудесъ пріимше...*) sung to the model *Дѣва днесь*. At the base of leaf p340, a prominent decorative headpiece marks the transition into the Month of February (*Мѣсяць Февруарій*), specifying 28 days and opening with the commemoration of the Holy Martyr Tryphon (*Трифонъ*) and the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord in the Temple (*Срѣтеніе Господне*).
