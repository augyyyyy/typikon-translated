[^390]: February 1: Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord in the Temple (*Срѣтеніе Господне*) and the Holy Martyr Tryphon (*Трифонъ*). The codex records the full texts of the ancient Forefeast Troparion (*Небесный ликъ небесныхъ ангелъ...*, Tone 1) and the Kontakion of St. Tryphon (*Мучительскаго прещенія многобожіе разрушилъ еси...*, Tone 8) sung to the model *Яко начатки* (here printed as *яко паткы*, preserving the archaic South Slavic/Old Church Slavonic translation of the Greek Ὡς ἀπαρχὰς τῆς φύсеως). Fiol's typesetter exhibits minor mechanical corruptions: *глапоють* for *глаголють* / *въспѣвають*, and *дїнкъ* for *мч҃нкъ*.
[^391]: February 2: The Great Feast of the Meeting of Our Lord Jesus Christ in the Temple (*Оусърѣтеніе Господа нашего Ісуса Христа*). Order of Little Vespers with the full Troparion (*Радуйся, обрадованная Богородице Дѣво...*, Tone 1) and Great Vespers with Old Testament lections from Exodus (Ex. 13:1–16), Isaiah (Is. 6:1–12), and Ezekiel (Ez. 43:27–44:4).
[^392]: February 2: Litiya and Matins for the Meeting of the Lord. The rubrics bear Studite liturgical witness, appointing the Heirmos twice and troparia on 4, followed by the Heirmos sung jointly by both choirs (*и паки ірмосъ оба лика вкупѣ*). The Matins Gospel is cross-referenced directly to the midday Liturgy reading from Luke (*отъ полу, емуже начало: Бѣ человѣкъ въ Іерусалимѣ*, Lk. 2:25–32).
[^393]: February 2: Concluding Matins and Divine Liturgy for the Meeting of the Lord. The Kontakion (*Иже утробу Дѣвичу освятивый...*, Tone 1) contains the archaic 15th-century Ruthenian/Slavic reading *но укрѣпи князя [люди / царя], егоже возлюбилъ еси* ("strengthen the prince whom Thou hast loved"). At Ode 9, the rubric directs that *Честнѣйшую* is omitted and candles are distributed to the brethren (*даются свѣщи братіи*), followed after the dismissal by a procession outside the monastery (*творимь исхоженіе изъ монастыря*). Divine Liturgy appointments include Hebrews 7:7–17 and Luke 2:22–40.
[^394]: February 3–5: Commemorations of the Holy and Righteous Symeon the God-Receiver (*Симеонъ Богопріимецъ*, Feb 3), Venerable Isidore of Pelusium (*Исидоръ Пелусіотъ*, Feb 4), and the Holy Martyr Agatha (*Агаѳія*, Feb 5). The full text of St. Isidore's Kontakion (*Яко веліе солнце Церкви...*, Tone 4) is printed in full. The text breaks off at line 19 of leaf p345 with the opening words of St. Agatha's Kontakion (*Язвила разумѣвъ...*).
[^395]: Major Codicological Lacuna: A massive quire loss occurs between leaf p345 (folio 175v) and leaf p346 (folio 176r). The surviving unique copy of Fiol's Horologion abruptly jumps from February 5 (St. Agatha) to May 7 (Apparition of the Sign of the Precious Cross over Jerusalem in 351 AD), omitting the remainder of February (Feb 6–28), the entire months of March and April, and the opening of May (May 1–6). Early ink foliation marks this leaf as folio `176` (with a later archivist's pencil query `241?`), reflecting continuous foliation of this defective physical volume. The leaf contains the full Troparion (*Креста Твоего образъ нынѣ паче солнца провозсія...*, Tone 8) and Kontakion (*Показавый Креста божественный образъ на небеси...*, Tone 4) of the Cross, transitioning directly into May 8 (St. John the Theologian and St. Arsenius the Great).
[^396]: May 8: Commemoration of the Holy Apostle and Evangelist John the Theologian (*Іоаннъ Богословъ*) and Venerable Arsenius the Great (*Арсеній Великій*). Prescribes Little Vespers, Great Vespers with lections, and a solemn Litiya with five idiomela (*Рѣка богословія*, *Прозяблъ чистоты*, etc.). At the conclusion of the Blessing of the Loaves, an appointed Patristic/Scriptural reading is taken specifically from the Apocalypse (*и чтеніе Апокалипціи*).
[^397]: May 8: Matins and Divine Liturgy for St. John the Theologian and St. Arsenius the Great. Includes the full Kontakion to St. Arsenius (*Отъ Рима возсіялъ еси, якоже солнце веліе...*, Tone 3). Epistle is 1 John 1:1–7 and Gospel John 19:25–27, 21:24–25. Physical leaf p348 exhibits a triangular loss at the top center of the parchment/paper leaf.
[^398]: May 9: Commemoration of the Holy Prophet Isaiah (*Исаія*), the Holy Martyr Christopher (*Христофоръ*), and the Translation of the Relics of St. Nicholas the Wonderworker (*Пренесеніе мощей святаго великаго отца нашего Николы* from Myra to Bari in 1087). Contains the full Kontakion to the Prophet Isaiah (*Пророчества даръ воспріемъ, пророче мучениче...*, Tone 2) sung to the model *Въ молитвахъ* (here noted as *яко паткы*).
[^399]: May 10–11: Commemoration of the Holy Apostle Simon the Zealot (*Симонъ Зилотъ*, May 10) with full Kontakion (*Павлу явися собесѣдникъ, апостоле...*, Tone 2, model *Къ вышнимъ*). May 11 opens the Dedication of the Imperial City of Constantinople (*Обновленіе Царяграда*) and the Hieromartyr Mocius the Presbyter (*Мокій презвитеръ*) with full Kontakion Tone 2 (*Вооруженъ щитомъ вѣрнымъ...*). The leaf concludes with the quire signature `ѕ҃ 4` (quaternion 6, leaf 4).
