# 1491 Krakow Horologion of Schweipolt Fiol (Часослов Швайпольта Фіоля, Краків, 1491) <a id="1491-krakow-horologion-of-schweipolt-fiol"></a>

*Front Flyleaf: Archival and Provenance Inscriptions*[^1]

*Shelfmark (upper right margin):* GA-173[^2]  
*Archival Registry (top center):* 2. D. No. 404  

*Bibliographic inscription in nineteenth-century pencil:*  
Schweipolt Fiol  
Horologion [Chasoslovets]  
Krakow, 1491.  

*Monastery provenance inscription in nineteenth-century cursive ink:*  
From among the books of the Library of the Kyiv Golden-Domed St. Michael’s Monastery, April 23, 1862.[^3]  

*Round purple library ink stamp:*  
Library of the Kyiv Golden-Domed St. Michael’s Monastery, 189...  

*Lower margin inventory notations:*  
Inv. I 143.  
J. N 6573

### Notes

[^1]: Physical leaf p1 serves as the protective paper flyleaf preceding the incunabulum folios in the surviving copy from Kyiv St. Michael’s Golden-Domed Monastery (now preserved in the National Library of Ukraine, Kyiv, Incunabula Collection).
[^2]: Cyrillic archival shelfmark 'ГА-173' (GA-173) inscribed in the upper right margin.
[^3]: The entry records the formal accession of this Schweipolt Fiol 1491 Krakow incunabulum into the monastic library of St. Michael’s Golden-Domed Monastery in Kyiv on April 23, 1862.
