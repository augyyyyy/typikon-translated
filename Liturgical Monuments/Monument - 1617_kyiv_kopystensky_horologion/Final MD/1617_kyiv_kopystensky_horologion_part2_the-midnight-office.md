# THE MIDNIGHT OFFICE

## The Beginning of the Midnight Office[^127]

*After the sounding of the semantron (klepalo), when the brethren have gathered in the church narthex (pritvor) — if it be "God is the Lord": but if it be "Alleluia", then in the church proper — the Priest begins:*

> **Priest:** "Blessed is our God always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."

*And we say:*

> **Choir:** "Amen."

*And we begin to chant, quietly and with attentiveness:*

Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.[^128] *Thrice.*

*O Heavenly King:*[^129] *Trisagion.* *Three prostrations, if it be "Alleluia."*[^130] *Glory... Both now...*

*Our Father:*

*And the exclamation from the Priest:*

> **Priest:** "For Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."

*And we [say]:*

> **Choir:** "Amen."

*And:* Lord, have mercy. *12 times.* *Glory... Both now...*

O come, let us worship God our King.

O come, let us worship Christ, our King and our God.

O come, let us worship and fall down before Christ Himself, our King and our God.

*And 3 prostrations. Then immediately:*

*To the end: A Psalm of David, when Nathan the Prophet came unto him,*[^131] *after he had gone in unto Bathsheba, the wife of Uriah. 50.*[^132]

*Psalm 50*

Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy; and according to the multitude of Thy compassions blot out my transgression. Wash me thoroughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. For I know mine iniquity, and my sin is ever before me. Against Thee only have I sinned, and done this evil before Thee; that Thou mightest be justified in Thy words, and conquer when Thou art judged. For behold, I was conceived in iniquities, and in sins did my mother bear me. For behold, Thou hast loved truth; (Ps. 50:1–8a)[^133] the unknown and secret things of Thy wisdom hast Thou revealed unto me. Thou shalt sprinkle me with hyssop, and I shall be cleansed; Thou shalt wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow. Thou shalt make me hear joy and gladness; the bones which have been humbled shall rejoice. Turn away Thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities. Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me. Cast me not away from Thy presence, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me. Restore unto me the joy of Thy salvation, and establish me with a governing Spirit. I will teach transgressors Thy ways, and the ungodly shall turn back unto Thee. Deliver me from (Ps. 50:8b–16a)[^134] bloodguiltiness, O God, the God of my salvation; and my tongue shall rejoice in Thy righteousness. O Lord, Thou shalt open my lips, and my mouth shall declare Thy praise. For hadst Thou desired sacrifice, I would have given it; in whole-burnt-offerings Thou delightest not. A sacrifice unto God is a broken spirit; a broken and a humbled heart God will not despise. Do good, O Lord, in Thy good pleasure unto Zion, and let the walls of Jerusalem be built. Then shalt Thou be pleased with a sacrifice of righteousness, oblation and whole-burnt-offerings; then shall they offer bullocks upon Thine altar. (Ps. 50:16b–21)[^135]

*After this:*

*Kathisma 17.*

*First Stasis.*[^136]

*Psalm 118: First Stasis*

Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord. Blessed are they that search His testimonies; they shall seek Him with their whole heart. For they that work iniquity have not walked in His ways. Thou hast commanded Thy commandments to be kept diligently. O that my ways were directed to keep Thy statutes! Then shall I not be ashamed, when I look upon all Thy commandments. I will confess Thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned the judgments of Thy righteousness. (Ps. 118:1–7)[^137] I will keep Thy statutes; O forsake me not utterly. Wherewithal shall a young man direct his way? By keeping Thy words. With my whole heart have I sought Thee; cast me not away from Thy commandments. In my heart have I hidden Thy sayings, that I might not sin against Thee. Blessed art Thou, O Lord; teach me Thy statutes. With my lips have I declared all the judgments of Thy mouth. In the way of Thy testimonies have I taken delight, as in all manner of riches. In Thy commandments will I meditate, and (Ps. 118:8–15)[^138] understand Thy paths. I will meditate on Thy statutes; I will not forget Thy words. Render unto Thy servant: give me life, and I will keep Thy words. Open Thou mine eyes, and I will understand the wonders of Thy law. I am a sojourner on the earth; hide not Thy commandments from me. My soul has longed to desire Thy judgments at all times. Thou hast rebuked the proud; accursed are they that turn aside from Thy commandments. Remove from me reproach and contempt, for I have sought Thy testimonies. (Ps. 118:15b–22)[^139]
