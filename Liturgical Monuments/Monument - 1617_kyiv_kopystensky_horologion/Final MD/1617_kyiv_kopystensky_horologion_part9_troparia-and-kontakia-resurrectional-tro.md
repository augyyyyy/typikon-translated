# TROPARIA AND KONTAKIA: RESURRECTIONAL TROPARIA, THEOTOKIA, AND HYPAKOAI OF THE EIGHT TONES [^506]

*Tone 1. Troparion:*

While the stone was sealed by the Jews and soldiers were guarding Thy most pure body, Thou didst arise on the third day, O Savior, granting life unto the world. Therefore the heavenly powers cried aloud unto Thee, (cont. on fol. 172v) *Resurrectional Troparia*

O Giver of life: Glory to Thy resurrection, O Christ! Glory to Thy kingdom! Glory to Thy dispensation, O Thou Who alone art the Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*

When Gabriel announced unto thee: "Rejoice, O Virgin!" together with the voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy ark, as the righteous David said; thou wast shown to be more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who dwelt in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who has freed us through thy childbearing!

*These Troparia Are Chanted on Sunday After the Blameless (Kathisma 17), Tone 5. Refrain:*[^507]

*Blessed art Thou, O Lord: teach me Thy statutes.*

*Troparia:* (cont. on fol. 173r) *Resurrectional Evlogetaria. [Fol. 173r]*

The assembly of the Angels was amazed, beholding Thee numbered among the dead; yet Thou, O Savior, destroying the power of death, didst raise up Adam with Thyself, and free all from Hades.

*Blessed art Thou, O Lord: teach me Thy statutes.*

Why do ye mingle the myrrh with tears of pity, O disciples? the radiant Angel within the tomb spoke unto the myrrh-bearing women: Behold the tomb and understand; for the Savior has risen from the tomb.

*Blessed art Thou, O Lord: teach me Thy statutes.*

Very early in the morning the myrrh-bearing women ran unto Thy tomb, O Savior, lamenting; but the Angel stood before them and said: The time for lamentation has ceased, weep not; but announce the Resurrection unto the Apostles.

*Blessed art Thou, O Lord: teach me Thy statutes.* (cont. on fol. 173v) *Resurrectional Evlogetaria*

The myrrh-bearing women, having come with myrrh unto Thy tomb, O Savior, lamenting, heard the Angel speaking unto them: Why do ye reckon the Living One with the dead? For as God, He has risen from the tomb!

*Glory:*

Let us worship the Father, and also His Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Holy Trinity in one essence, crying aloud with the Seraphim: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Both now:*

Having borne the Giver of life, O Virgin, thou didst deliver Adam from sin, and didst grant joy unto Eve in place of sorrow; and He Who became incarnate from thee, God and Man, directs him that had fallen away back unto life.

Alleluia, Alleluia, [Alleluia,] glory to Thee, O God! *(thrice).*

*Hypakoe:* (cont. on fol. 174r) *Resurrectional Hymns. [Fol. 174r]*

*Tone 1. Hypakoe:*

The repentance of the thief opened Paradise, and the weeping of the myrrh-bearing women proclaimed joy: that the Lord has risen, granting great mercy unto the world.

*This Troparion Is Said Before Psalm 50 on All Sundays, Unless the Typikon Commands Otherwise:*[^508]

Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ, let us worship the holy Lord Jesus, the only sinless One. We worship Thy Cross, O Christ, and Thy holy Resurrection do we hymn and glorify; for Thou art our God, for beside Thee we know no other, and we call upon Thy name. Come, all ye faithful, (cont. on fol. 174v) *Resurrectional Hymns*

let us worship the holy Resurrection of Christ; for behold, through the Cross joy has come into all the world. Ever blessing the Lord, we hymn His Resurrection; for having endured crucifixion, He has destroyed death by death.

*After Psalm 50. Glory:*

Through the prayers of the holy Apostles, O Merciful One, cleanse the multitude of our offenses.

*Both now:*

Through the prayers of the most pure Theotokos, O Merciful One, cleanse the multitude of our offenses.

*Then:*

Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy; and according to the multitude of Thy compassions, blot out my transgressions, O God my Savior.

*After this, Resurrectional Sticheron, Tone 6:*

Jesus, having risen from the tomb as He foretold, has granted unto us (cont. on fol. 175r) *Resurrectional Hymns. [Fol. 175r]*

eternal life, peace, and great mercy.

*Theotokion, Chanted on Sunday Before the Great Doxology, and Whenever the Typikon Commands:*[^509]

*Glory: Evangelical Sticheron, or another according to the Typikon. Both now: Tone 2:*

Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos; for through Him Who became incarnate from thee, Hades has been led captive, Adam recalled, the curse abolished, Eve set free, death put to death, and we have been made alive. Therefore, hymning, we cry aloud: Blessed is Christ our God Who has been well pleased thus!

*These Two Resurrectional Troparia Are Said After the Great Doxology at the Trisagion on All Sundays. When in the Octoechos it is (cont. on fol. 175v)*[^510] *Resurrectional Dismissal Troparia*

*Tone 1, 3, 5, or 7 in the Octoechos, we chant:*

Today salvation is come unto the world: let us hymn Him Who has risen from the tomb, the Author of our life, Christ our God; for having destroyed death, He gave us the victory and great mercy.

*And when it is Tone 2, 4, 6, or 8, we say:*

Having risen from the tomb and burst the bonds asunder, Thou didst destroy the condemnation of death, O Lord, delivering all from the snares of the enemy; and showing Thyself unto Thine Apostles, Thou didst send them forth to preach, and through them hast granted Thy peace unto the inhabited world, O Thou Who alone art greatly merciful.

*Resurrectional Hymns. [Fol. 176r]*

*Tone 2. Troparion:*[^511]

When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal, then didst Thou slay Hades with the radiance of Thy Divinity; and when Thou didst also raise the dead from the nethermost depths, all the heavenly hosts cried aloud unto Thee: O Giver of life, Christ our God, glory to Thee!

*Theotokion:*

All beyond comprehension, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Theotokos! Sealed in purity and guarded in virginity, thou wast acknowledged to be a true Mother, having borne the true God: make supplication unto Him that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 2. Hypakoe:*

Going after Thy Passion unto the tomb to anoint Thy body, the women, O Christ God, beheld Angels in the tomb and were terrified; for they heard a voice (cont. on fol. 176v) *Resurrectional Hymns*

from them, that the Lord has risen, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Tone 3. Troparion:*[^512]

Let the heavens rejoice, let the earth be glad; for the Lord has shown strength with His arm, trampling death by death; He became the firstborn of the dead; He has delivered us from the belly of Hades, and grants great mercy unto the world.

*Theotokion:*

We hymn thee, who didst intercede for the salvation of our race, O Virgin Theotokos; for Thy Son and our God, having accepted suffering through the Cross in the flesh which He took from thee, has delivered us from corruption, as He is the Lover of mankind.

*Tone 3. Hypakoe:*

Astonishing by his appearance, refreshing with his words, (cont. on fol. 177r) *Resurrectional Hymns. [Fol. 177r]*

the radiant Angel spoke unto the myrrh-bearing women: Why do ye seek the Living One in the tomb? He has emptied the graves by His rising! Know Him as the Transformer of corruption, the Changeless One; say unto God: How terrible are Thy works! For Thou hast saved the human race.

*Tone 4. Troparion:*[^513]

Having learned the radiant proclamation of the Resurrection from the Angel, and cast off the ancestral condemnation, the women disciples of the Lord exultantly said unto the Apostles: Death is overthrown! Christ God has risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Theotokion:*

The mystery hidden from all ages and unknown to Angels was revealed through thee, O Theotokos, unto them that are upon the earth: God becoming incarnate in unconfused union, (cont. in cohort 21) *Resurrectional Hymns*

and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby He raised up the first-fashioned and saved our souls from death.

*Tone 4. Hypakoe:*

Preceding Thy most glorious Resurrection, the myrrh-bearing women ran and proclaimed the good tidings unto the Apostles: that Christ is risen as God, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Tone 5. Troparion:*[^514]

Let us, the faithful, hymn and worship the Word, co-unoriginate with the Father and the Spirit, Who was born of the Virgin for our salvation; for He was well pleased to ascend the Cross in the flesh, and to endure death, and to raise the dead by His glorious Resurrection.

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, (cont. on fol. 178r) *Resurrectional Hymns. [Fol. 178r]*

wall and protection of them that flee unto thee! Rejoice, unstormy haven and unwedded Bride, who in the flesh didst bear Thy Creator and God! Despise not Thy supplication for them that hymn and worship Thy childbearing.

*Tone 5. Hypakoe:*

Perplexed in mind by the angelic appearance, but enlightened in soul by the divine Resurrection, the myrrh-bearing women proclaimed the good tidings unto the Apostles: Declare among the nations the Resurrection, the Lord working together with you by miracles, and granting great mercy unto us.

*Tone 6. Troparion:*[^515]

The angelic powers were upon Thy tomb, and they that guarded it became as dead men; and Mary stood (cont. on fol. 178v) *Resurrectional Hymns*

at the tomb, seeking Thy most pure body. Thou didst despoil Hades, being not tempted by it; Thou didst meet the Virgin, granting life. O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Theotokion:*

He that called thy blessed one His Mother came unto His Passion by His own will, shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the Angels: Rejoice with Me, for the lost coin is found! O Thou Who hast ordered all things in wisdom, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Tone 6. Hypakoe:*

By Thy voluntary and life-creating death, O Christ God, having shattered the gates of Hades as God, Thou hast opened ancient Paradise unto us; and having risen from the dead, Thou hast delivered our life from corruption.

*Resurrectional Hymns. [Fol. 179r]*

*Tone 7. Troparion:*[^516]

Thou didst destroy death by Thy Cross; Thou didst open Paradise unto the thief; Thou didst transform the weeping of the myrrh-bearing women, and didst command the Apostles to preach: that Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.

*Theotokion:*

As the treasury of our resurrection, O all-hymned one, lead up from the pit and the depth of transgressions them that hope in thee; for thou hast saved them that were guilty of sin, having given birth to our Salvation: thou who before childbearing wast a Virgin, and in childbearing a Virgin, and after childbearing again a Virgin.

*Tone 7. Hypakoe:*

O Thou Who didst take our form, and didst endure crucifixion in the flesh, save me by Thy Resurrection, O Christ God, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Resurrectional Hymns*

*Tone 8. Troparion:*[^517]

From on high Thou didst descend, O Compassionate One, and didst accept three days' burial, that Thou mightest free us from the passions. O our Life and Resurrection, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Theotokion:*

He Who for our sake was born of the Virgin: (written in the Sixth Hour, see fol. 121).[^518]

*Tone 8. Hypakoe:*

The myrrh-bearing women, standing before the life-giving tomb, sought the Immortal Master among the dead; and having received the joy of the good tidings from the Angel, they declared unto the Apostles: Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

## DAILY TROPARIA, THEOTOKIA, AND KONTAKIA OF THE WEEK[^519]

*Daily Hymns. [Fol. 180r]*[^520]

*On Monday: To the Angels. Tone 4. Troparion:*[^521]

Supreme Commanders of the heavenly hosts, we, the unworthy, beseech you now that by your prayers ye may compass us about beneath the shelter of the wings of your immaterial glory, guarding us who fall down and cry out earnestly: (cont. on fol. 180v) *Daily Hymns*

and cry aloud: Deliver us from dangers, as ye are the commanders of the hosts on high.

*Theotokion:*

As thou art superior to all rulers, O Theotokos, we who are at a loss to hymn thee worthily beseech thee freely: have mercy on us!

*Tone 2. Kontakion:*

Supreme Commanders of God, ministers of divine glory, guides of angels and instructors of men: ask for that which is profitable unto us, and great mercy, as ye are supreme commanders of the bodiless hosts.

*On Tuesday: To the Forerunner. Tone 2. Troparion:*[^522]

The memory of the righteous is celebrated with hymns of praise, but the testimony of the Lord suffices for thee, O Forerunner; for thou wast shown forth in truth as the most honorable of the prophets, (cont. on fol. 181r) *Daily Hymns. [Fol. 181r]*

in that thou wast vouchsafed to baptize in the streams Him Whom thou didst preach. Therefore, having suffered for the truth with joy, thou didst proclaim the good tidings even unto them that were in Hades: of God Who appeared in the flesh, Who takes away the sins of the world and grants great mercy unto us.

*Theotokion:*

Having become partakers of the divine nature through thee, O Theotokos and Ever-Virgin, for thou didst bear God incarnate unto us, we all therefore worthily magnify thee with piety.

*Tone 3. Kontakion:*

Today the barren woman gives birth to the Forerunner of Christ, and he is the fulfillment of all prophecy; for having laid his hand in the Jordan upon Him Whom the prophets foretold, he was shown forth (cont. on fol. 181v) *Daily Hymns*

as the prophet, preacher, and forerunner of God the Word.

*On Wednesday: To the Cross. Tone 1. Troparion:*[^523]

Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance...

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Having acquired thy protection, O Most Pure One, we are delivered from dangers by thy prayers; for being preserved everywhere and at all times by the Cross of thy Son, we all worthily magnify thee with piety.

*Tone 4. Kontakion:*

O Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross of Thine own will: (written with the Troparion, at Matins, see fol. 45).[^524]

*On Thursday: To the Apostles. Tone 3. Troparion:*[^525]

Holy Apostles, entreat the merciful God, that (cont. on fol. 182r) *Daily Hymns. [Fol. 182r]*

He grant remission of sins unto our souls.

*Theotokion:*

Knowing Christ our God, the Word of the Father, Who was incarnate of thee, O Virgin Theotokos, pray unto Him to save our souls.

*To Saint Nicholas. Tone 4. Troparion:*[^526]

A rule of faith and image of meekness, a teacher of temperance did the reality of things show thee forth unto thy flock; therefore thou didst acquire by humility the lofty, and by poverty riches: O our father, Hierarch Nicholas, entreat Christ God that our souls may be saved.

*To the Apostles. Tone 2. Kontakion:*

The steadfast and divinely-speaking preachers, the summit of Thy disciples, O Lord, Thou didst accept (cont. on fol. 182v) *Daily Hymns*

into the enjoyment of Thy good things and rest; for their labors and death didst Thou accept beyond every whole burnt offering, O Thou Who alone knowest the secrets of the heart.

*To Saint Nicholas. Tone 3. Kontakion:*

In Myra, O holy Nicholas, thou wast shown forth as a hierarch; for having fulfilled the Gospel of Christ, O venerable one, thou didst lay down thy life for thy people and didst save the innocent from death. Therefore thou wast sanctified as a great initiate of the mystery of the grace of God.

*On Friday: The Troparion and Kontakion are the same as on Wednesday: (written on fol. 181).*[^527]

*On Saturday: To All Saints. Tone 2. Troparion:*[^528]

Apostles, prophets, and martyrs, hierarchs, venerable and righteous: (written on fol. 107).

*Daily Hymns. [Fol. 183r]*

*For the Departed. Tone 2. Troparion:*

Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good, Thy servants: (see fol. 23).

*Theotokion:*

O Holy Mother of the most pure Light, honoring thee with angelic hymns, we praise and magnify thee.

*For the Departed. Tone 8. Troparion:*

O Thou Who by the depth of Thy wisdom: (see fol. 23).

*Theotokion:*

We have thee as a wall and a haven, and an acceptable intercessor before God Who was born of thee, O unwedded Theotokos, salvation of the faithful.

*To All Saints. Tone 8. Kontakion:*

As the first-fruits of nature: (see fol. 107).

*For the Departed. Kontakion:*

With the Saints give rest, O Christ, to the souls of Thy servants: (written in the Midnight Office, see fol. 24).

## THEOTOKIA AND STAVROTHEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES[^529]

*Theotokia and Stavrotheotokia. [Fol. 183v]*

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*[^530]

Receive the prayers of thy servants, O Theotokos, and deliver us from every distress, as thou didst bear Christ the Savior, the Redeemer of our souls.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Having acquired thy protection: (written on Wednesday, see fol. 181).

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*

O Holy Mother: (written on Saturday, see fol. 183).

*Stavrotheotokion:*

We magnify thee, O Theotokos, crying aloud: Rejoice, O rod from which without seed thou didst sprout forth God, (cont. on fol. 184r) *Stavrotheotokia. [Fol. 184r]*

Who by the Tree destroyed death.

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^531]

Wheresoever each one is saved, thither he rightly flees; and what other haven is there like unto thine, O Theotokos, which covers our souls?

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Having acquired the rod of strength, the Cross of thy Son, O Theotokos, thereby we cast down the insolence of enemies, that we may unceasingly magnify thee.

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*

An unconquerable rampart unto us: (written in the Inter-Hour of the Third Hour, see fol. 85).

*Stavrotheotokion:*

O Virgin all-immaculate, Mother of Christ God: a sword pierced thy most pure soul when (cont. on fol. 184v) *Theotokia and Stavrotheotokia*

thou didst behold thy Son and God crucified of His own will; entreat Him unceasingly, O most blessed one, that He may grant remission of our transgressions.

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*[^532]

O Christ God, Who didst shine forth from the Virgin unto the whole world, and on her account didst show us forth as sons of the light: have mercy on us.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

By the Cross of thy Son, O woman full of the grace of God, the deceit of idols was abolished, and the power of demons was trampled down. Therefore we, the faithful, as is due, ever hymn and bless thee, and confessing thee to be in truth the Theotokos, we magnify thee.

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*[^533]

According to thy word, O true Mother: behold, all generations, blessing thee, humbly magnify thee.

*Stavrotheotokia. [Fol. 185r]*

*Stavrotheotokion:*

O Virgin Theotokos, entreat thy Son, Who was voluntarily nailed unto the Cross and freed the world from deceit, Christ our God, that He may have mercy on our souls.

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*[^534]

Though the King knew thee as Mother, yet the Creator showed thee forth as Virgin; for thou didst bear in the flesh Christ our God, Who saves our souls.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

O pure Ever-Virgin Theotokos, beholding thy Son hanging upon the Cross, weeping as a Mother, thou didst magnify His strange descent.

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*

Rejoice, thou who from the Angel didst receive the joy (cont. on fol. 185v) *Theotokia and Stavrotheotokia*

didst receive the joy for the world; rejoice, thou who didst bear the Creator of creation; rejoice, thou who wast vouchsafed to become the Mother of Christ God.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

The Lamb, and Shepherd, and Savior of the world, upon the Cross: (written in the Ninth Hour, see fol. 113).

*End of the Theotokia and Stavrotheotokia, and of the Whole Horologion.*[^535]

## FEAST OF THE DORMITION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS[^536]

*Month of August. [Fol. 186r]*

*On the 15th Day of the Month of August: The Dormition of our Most Holy Lady Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary. Tone 1. Troparion:*

In thy childbearing thou didst preserve virginity, and in thy dormition thou didst not forsake the world, O Theotokos; thou didst pass over unto life, being the Mother of Life, and by thine intercessions deliverest our souls from death.

*Tone 2. Kontakion:*

Neither the tomb nor death could hold the Theotokos, who is sleepless in supplications and an unfailing hope in intercessions; for as the Mother of Life, He Who dwelt in her ever-virginal womb translated her unto life.
