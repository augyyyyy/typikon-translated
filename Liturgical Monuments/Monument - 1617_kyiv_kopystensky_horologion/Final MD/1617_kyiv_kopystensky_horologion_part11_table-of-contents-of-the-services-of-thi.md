### Notes

[^544]: The printed Table of Contents (Оглавленіе) provides the exact foliation of the 1616–1617 Horologion as issued by the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra Typography. Cyrillic letter numerals correspond to internal printed leaf numbers (Fol. 1–191).
[^545]: The Table of Contents concludes without assigning a starting folio number to the Rus' Saints, reflecting their positioning as an unindexed supplementary hymnographic section added at the conclusion of the volume (Fol. 186v–190r).
