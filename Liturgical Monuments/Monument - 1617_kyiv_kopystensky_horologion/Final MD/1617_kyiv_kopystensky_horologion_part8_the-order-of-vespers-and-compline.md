# THE ORDER OF VESPERS AND COMPLINE

*The Beginning of Vespers with a Priest:*[^368]
*[When the priest has blessed,]* and we say: Amen.

*And we begin: if it is Alleluia, Trisagion with prostrations. And after Our Father: Lord, have mercy (12). Glory... Both now...*

O come, let us worship... (3). Then the Psalm of David, concerning the worldly creation, 103:[^369] (cont. on fol. 119v) *The Order of Vespers*

Bless the Lord, O my soul! O Lord my God, Thou hast been magnified exceedingly; Thou hast clothed Thyself with confession and majesty, Who coverest Thyself with light as with a garment, Who stretchest out the heaven like a curtain; Who coverest His chambers with waters, Who makest the clouds His chariot, Who walkest upon the wings of the wind; Who makest His Angels spirits, and His ministers a flame of fire. Who founded the earth upon its firm foundation; it shall not be shaken unto ages of ages. The abyss like a garment is its vesture; upon the mountains the waters shall stand. At Thy rebuke they shall flee; at the voice of Thy thunder they shall be afraid. The mountains rise, and the plains descend (cont. on fol. 120r) *The Order of Vespers. [Fol. 120r]*

unto the place which Thou hast established for them. Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass over, neither turn again to cover the earth. Who sendest forth springs in the valleys; between the mountains the waters shall run. They give drink to all the wild beasts of the field; the wild asses await them for their thirst. By them shall the birds of the heaven lodge; from the midst of the rocks they shall give voice. Who waterest the mountains from His chambers; the earth shall be satisfied with the fruit of Thy works. Who causest grass to spring up for the cattle, and green herb for the service of men, that Thou mayest bring forth bread out of the earth; and wine makes glad the heart of man, to make his face cheerful with oil, and bread strengthens man's heart. The trees of the plain shall be satisfied, (cont. on fol. 120v) *The Order of Vespers*

even the cedars of Lebanon which Thou hast planted. There the sparrows make their nests; the house of the heron takes the lead among them. The high mountains are for the harts; the rock is a refuge for the hares. He made the moon for seasons; the sun knows its going down. Thou appointedst the darkness, and it was night, wherein all the wild beasts of the forest will go abroad: young lions roaring to snatch prey, and to seek their food from God. The sun arose, and they were gathered together, and they shall lie down in their dens. Man goes forth unto his work, and unto his labor until evening. How magnified are Thy works, O Lord! In wisdom hast Thou made them all; the earth is filled with Thy creation. Yonder is the sea, great and wide; there are creeping things (cont. on fol. 121r) *The Order of Vespers. [Fol. 121r]*

whereof there is no number, living creatures small with great. There the ships go; there is that Leviathan, whom Thou hast formed to make sport of him. All things look unto Thee, to give them food in due season. When Thou givest it them, they will gather it; when Thou openest Thy hand, all things shall be filled with goodness. But when Thou turnest away Thy face, they will be troubled; Thou wilt take away their spirit, and they will fail, and will return unto their dust. Thou wilt send forth Thy Spirit, and they shall be created, and Thou wilt renew the face of the earth. Let the glory of the Lord endure unto the ages; the Lord shall rejoice in His works. He looks upon the earth, and makes it tremble; He touches the mountains, and they smoke. I will sing unto the Lord in my life, (cont. on fol. 121v) *The Order of Vespers*

I will chant unto my God as long as I have my being. May my speech be sweet unto Him, and I will be glad in the Lord. Let sinners disappear from the earth, and the unrighteous, so that they be no more. Bless the Lord, O my soul!

*Then this, twice:*
The sun knows its going down; Thou appointedst the darkness, and it was night. How magnified are Thy works, O Lord! In wisdom hast Thou made them all.

*Then:* Glory... Both now... Alleluia (3). *If alone, instead of the litany, there is said: Lord, have mercy (12)[^370]. Glory... Both now... But if in church: then the Great Litany, by the priest or by the deacon: In peace let us pray to the Lord. Then the appointed Kathisma. And after the Kathisma, the Little Litany. Then:* (cont. on fol. 122r) *The Order of Vespers. [Fol. 122r]*

*Then we chant the verses: Psalm 140:*[^371]
Lord, I have cried unto Thee, hear me; attend unto the voice of my supplication, when I cry unto Thee.
Let my prayer be set forth as incense before Thee; the lifting up of my hands as an evening sacrifice.
Set a watch, O Lord, before my mouth, and a door of enclosure about my lips.
Incline not my heart unto words of malice, to make excuses in sins, with men that work iniquity; and I will not join with their chosen ones.
The righteous man shall chasten me with mercy, and reprove me; but let not the oil of the sinner anoint my head. (cont. on fol. 122v) *The Order of Vespers*

For yet my prayer also shall be in their pleasures. Their judges have been swallowed up close by the rock; they shall hear my words, for they are sweet. As a clod of earth is broken upon the ground, their bones have been scattered beside hades. For mine eyes are unto Thee, O Lord, O Lord; in Thee have I hoped, take not away my soul. Keep me from the snare which they have laid for me, and from the stumbling blocks of the workers of iniquity. The sinners shall fall into their own net; I am alone until I pass by.

*Of understanding, for David, when he was in the cave: a Prayer, 141:*[^372]
With my voice cried I unto the Lord; with my voice unto the Lord did I make supplication.
I will pour out my supplication before Him; (cont. on fol. 123r) *The Order of Vespers. [Fol. 123r]*

and mine affliction will I declare before Him.
When my spirit failed from me, then Thou knewest my paths.
In this way wherein I walked have they hidden a snare for me.
I looked on my right hand and beheld, and there was none that knew me.
Flight failed me, and there is none that cares for my soul.
I cried unto Thee, O Lord; I said: Thou art my hope, my portion art Thou in the land of the living.
Attend unto my supplication, for I am brought very low.
Deliver me from them that persecute me, for they are stronger than I.
Bring my soul out of prison, that I may confess Thy name.
The righteous shall await me, until Thou shalt recompense me.

*An Ode of Ascents, 129:*[^373] (cont. on fol. 123v) *The Order of Vespers*

Out of the depths have I cried unto Thee, O Lord; Lord, hear my voice.
Let Thine ears be attentive unto the voice of my supplication.
If Thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? For with Thee there is forgiveness.
For Thy name's sake have I waited for Thee, O Lord; my soul has waited for Thy word, my soul has hoped in the Lord.
From the morning watch until night, from the morning watch, let Israel hope in the Lord.
For with the Lord there is mercy, and with Him is plenteous redemption; and He shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.

*Alleluia, 116:*[^374]
O praise the Lord, all ye nations; praise Him, all ye peoples. (cont. on fol. 124r) *The Order of Vespers. [Fol. 124r]*

For His mercy is confirmed upon us, and the truth of the Lord endures unto eternity.

*Then the Stichera of the current day. Glory... Both now... Theotokion, according to the Tone.*

*On Saturday evening, Prokeimenon:*
The Lord is King, He is clothed with majesty. *Verse 1:* The Lord is clothed with strength and has girded Himself. *Verse 2:* For He has established the world, which shall not be shaken. *Verse 3:* Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days. *And again:* The Lord is King.[^384]

*And we say these verses:*
*On Monday evening:*
O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger.[^387]

*On Tuesday and Thursday evening:*
Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship at His footstool, for He is holy.[^388]

*On Wednesday evening:*
Their sound has gone forth into all the earth, and (cont. on fol. 126v) *The Order of Vespers*

*And so the Singer says:* And unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Let us complete our evening prayer unto the Lord.*

Unto Thee have I lifted up mine eyes, unto Thee that dwellest in heaven. *And the Canonarch makes a metania in the midst, toward the icons; likewise to both choirs, on both sides, one to each.*

*Then the Prayer of Saint Symeon the God-Receiver:*[^393]

## THE BEGINNING OF GREAT COMPLINE[^408]
*AS IT IS SUNG IN GREAT LENT*

*When the priest has blessed, we say:* O Heavenly King... *Trisagion. And after Our Father: Lord, have mercy (12). Glory... Both now...*

O come, let us worship... (3).

*And if it is the First Week of the Great Fast, we say Psalm 4:* (cont. on fol. 132v) *Great Compline*

O God, attend unto my help: (see fol. 66)[^409]

*And after this, the Great Canon of Saint Andrew of Crete.*[^410] *And after the Canon, we say Psalm 4:* When I called... *and all the rest in order.*

*On the other days of the holy Fast, after* O come, let us worship..., *we say this Psalm:*

*To the end, in hymns, a Psalm of David, 4:*[^411]
When I called, the God of my righteousness heard me; in affliction Thou hast enlarged me. Have mercy on me, and hear my prayer. O ye sons of men, how long will ye be heavy-hearted? Why love ye vanity, and (cont. on fol. 133r) *Great Compline. [Fol. 133r]*

seek a lie? But know ye that the Lord has made marvelous His holy one; the Lord will hear me when I cry unto Him. Be ye angry, and sin not; what ye say in your hearts, be pricked with compunction upon your beds. Sacrifice the sacrifice of righteousness, and hope in the Lord. Many say: Who will show us good things? The light of Thy countenance has been signed upon us, O Lord; Thou hast put gladness into my heart: by the fruit of their corn, wine, and oil they have been increased. In peace together I will lie down and sleep, for Thou, O Lord, alone hast made me dwell in hope.

*To the end, in hymns, upon the eighth, a Psalm of David, 6:*[cont. on fol. 133v][^412] *Great Compline*

O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger, neither chasten me in Thy wrath. Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak; heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled, and my soul is troubled exceedingly; and Thou, O Lord, how long? Turn Thee, O Lord, deliver my soul; save me for Thy mercy's sake. For in death there is no one that remembers Thee, and in hades who shall confess Thee? I am weary with my groaning; every night will I wash my bed, I will water my couch with my tears. Mine eye is troubled because of wrath; I am grown old among all mine enemies. Depart from me, all ye that work (cont. on fol. 134r) *Great Compline. [Fol. 134r]*

iniquity; for the Lord has heard the voice of my weeping, the Lord has heard my supplication, the Lord has received my prayer. Let all mine enemies be put to shame and be troubled exceedingly; let them turn back and be put to shame right speedily.

*To the end, a Psalm of David, 12:*[^413]
How long, O Lord, wilt Thou forget me unto the end? How long wilt Thou turn away Thy face from me? How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrows in my heart day and night? How long shall mine enemy be exalted over me? Look upon me, and hear me, O Lord my God; lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death; lest (cont. on fol. 134v) *Great Compline*

mine enemy say: I have prevailed against him. They that afflict me will rejoice if I be moved. But I have hoped in Thy mercy; my heart shall rejoice in Thy salvation. I will sing unto the Lord Who has dealt bountifully with me, and I will chant unto the name of the Lord Most High.

*Then:* Glory... Both now... *Alleluia (3), and 3 metanias. Lord, have mercy (3). Glory... Both now... Psalm 24:* Unto Thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul: *written, (see fol. 78)*[^414]

*To the end, a Psalm of David, of ecstasy, 30:*[^415]
In Thee, O Lord, have I hoped, let me never be ashamed; in Thy righteousness deliver me, and rescue me. Incline Thine ear unto me, make haste to deliver me; (cont. on fol. 135r) *Great Compline. [Fol. 135r]*

be Thou unto me a God protector, and a house of refuge to save me. For Thou art my strength and my refuge; and for Thy name's sake Thou wilt guide me and nourish me. Thou wilt bring me out of this snare which they have hidden for me, for Thou art my protector, O Lord. Into Thy hands I commit my spirit; Thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of truth. Thou hast hated them that regard vain vanities, but I have hoped in the Lord. I will be glad and rejoice in Thy mercy, for Thou hast looked upon my low estate; Thou hast saved my soul from afflictions, and hast not shut me up into the hands of the enemy; Thou hast set my feet in a spacious place. Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am in (cont. on fol. 135v) *Great Compline*

affliction; mine eye is troubled because of wrath, my soul and my belly. For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing; my strength has grown weak with poverty, and my bones are troubled. I became a reproach among all mine enemies, and exceedingly so to my neighbors, and a fear to my acquaintances; they that saw me without fled from me. I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind; I am become like a broken vessel. For I have heard the slander of many that dwell round about; when they assembled together against me, they took counsel to take away my life. But I have hoped in Thee, O Lord; I said: Thou art my God; in Thy hands are my lots. (cont. on fol. 136r) *Great Compline. [Fol. 136r]*

Deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me. Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant, and save me in Thy mercy. O Lord, let me not be ashamed, for I have called upon Thee; let the ungodly be ashamed, and let them be brought down into hades. Let the deceitful lips be put to silence, which speak iniquity against the righteous with pride and contempt. How great is the multitude of Thy goodness, O Lord, which Thou hast hidden for them that fear Thee! which Thou hast wrought for them that hope in Thee before the sons of men! Thou wilt hide them in the secret of Thy countenance from the disturbance of man; Thou wilt shelter them in a tabernacle from the contradiction of tongues. Blessed be the Lord, for He has made marvelous His mercy in (cont. on fol. 136v) *Great Compline*

a strong city. But I said in mine ecstasy: I am cast away from before the face of Thine eyes; therefore Thou didst hear the voice of my prayer when I cried unto Thee. Love the Lord, all ye His saints, for the Lord requires truth, and repays plentifully them that act with pride. Be of good courage, and let your heart be strengthened, all ye that hope in the Lord.
