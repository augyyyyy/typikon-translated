# THE BOOK OF HOURS (HOROLOGION) [^140]

## The Book of Hours, with God the Holy One

Containing the night and day service, according to the Typikon of the Great Lavra in Jerusalem, of our Father among the saints Sabbas; such an order is maintained also in the other holy monasteries of Jerusalem.[^141]

## The Order upon Rising Swiftly from Sleep[^142]

*Without slumbering, without laziness, and having collected thyself upon rising from sleep, step away from the bed, and say this, thrice:*

Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee for all things.[^143]

*After this, stand for a little while in silence, until all the senses are quieted; and then make three prostrations unto the earth, saying:*

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.

*Then say this prayer of Saint Macarius the Great:*[^144]

O God, cleanse me, a sinner, for I have never done any good before Thee; but deliver me from the evil one, from the evil one, and let Thy will be in me, that uncondemned I may open mine unworthy mouth and praise Thy holy name: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.[^145]

*Then begin thus:*

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, for the sake of the prayers of Thy most pure Mother and of all Thy saints, have mercy on us. Amen.[^146]

O Heavenly King, the Comforter, the Spirit of truth, Who art everywhere present and fillest all things, Treasury of blessings and Giver of life: (cont. on fol. 2v)[^147] come and abide in us, and cleanse us from every stain, and save our souls, O Good One.[^148]

Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us. *Thrice.*

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

All-Holy Trinity, have mercy on us; Lord, cleanse our sins; Master, pardon our iniquities; Holy One, visit and heal our infirmities, for Thy name's sake. Lord, have mercy. *Thrice.*

Glory... Both now...

Our Father, Who art in the heavens, hallowed be Thy name.

Thy kingdom come.

Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. (cont. on fol. 3r)[^149] Give us this day our daily bread.

And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.

And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.[^150]

For Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, unto the ages. Amen.[^151]

*And immediately these three Troparia to the Trinity. Tone 1:*

*Troparion, Tone 1:*
Having risen from sleep, we fall down before Thee, O Good One, and we cry aloud unto Thee the angelic hymn, O Mighty One:
Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God; through the protection of Thine incorporeal hosts, have mercy on us.[^152]

*Glory... Tone 2:*

*Troparion, Tone 2:*
From the couch and sleep hast Thou raised me up, O Lord, and (cont. on fol. 3v)[^153] enlighten my mind and heart, and open my lips to sing of Thee, O Holy Trinity:
Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God; through the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy on us.[^154]

*Both now... Tone 3:*

*Troparion, Tone 3:*
Suddenly the Judge shall come, and each man's deeds shall be laid bare; but with fear let us cry at midnight:
Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God; through the prayers of the Birthgiver of God, have mercy on us.[^155]

*Lord, have mercy. 12 times. And this prayer:*[^156]

*Prayer of Saint Basil the Great:*
Having risen from sleep, I give thanks to Thee, O Holy Trinity, that for the sake of Thy great goodness and longsuffering Thou hast not been angry with me, a sinner and slothful, neither hast Thou destroyed me with mine iniquities; but hast shown Thy customary love toward mankind, and (cont. on fol. 4r)[^157] hast raised me up as I lay in despair, that I might offer morning praise and glorify Thy dominion. And now enlighten the eyes of my mind, open my mouth to meditate on Thy words, to understand Thy commandments, to do Thy will, to sing unto Thee with confession of heart, and to hymn Thy most holy name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.[^158]

*Second Prayer:*[^159]

Glory to Thee, O King, God Almighty, Who by Thy divine and loving providence hast vouchsafed me, a sinner (cont. on fol. 4v)[^160] and unworthy, to rise from sleep and to attain entrance into Thy holy house. Receive, O Lord, the voice of my supplication also, as that of Thy holy and noetic Powers; and be well pleased that with a clean heart and a humble spirit I may offer praise unto Thee from my defiled lips, so that I also may become a partaker with the wise virgins, with the radiant lamp of my soul, and glorify Thee, God the Word Who art glorified in the Father and the Spirit. Amen.[^161]

## ANOTHER MIDNIGHT OFFICE, SUNG ON SATURDAYS

*When the Beginning has been made, as was previously written for the Daily Midnight Office: after the Fiftieth Psalm [Ps. 50], we chant the Ninth Kathisma.*[^205]

*Psalm 64:*[^206]
Praise is meet for Thee, O God, in Zion, and unto Thee shall prayer be rendered in Jerusalem.
Hear my prayer: unto Thee shall all flesh come.
Words of the iniquitous (cont. on fol. 29v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...have overpowered us; and as for our ungodly deeds, Thou wilt purge them away. Blessed is he whom Thou hast chosen and taken to Thyself; he shall dwell in Thy courts. We shall be filled with the good things of Thy house; holy is Thy temple, marvelous in righteousness. Hear us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are afar off upon the sea; Who preparest the mountains by Thy strength, being girded with power; Who troublest the depth of the sea. Who can withstand the roar of its waves? The nations shall be troubled, and they that dwell in the uttermost bounds shall be afraid of Thy signs. Thou shalt beautify the outgoings of the morning and the evening. Thou hast visited the earth and watered it; Thou hast greatly enriched it. The river of God is filled with (cont. on fol. 30r) *Saturday. [Fol. 30r]*

...waters. Thou hast prepared their food, for so is its preparation. Water Thou her furrows; multiply her fruits; in her drops she shall rejoice springing up. Thou wilt bless the crown of the year of Thy goodness, and Thy plains shall be filled with fatness. The pastures of the wilderness shall become fat, and the hills shall gird themselves with joy. The rams of the sheep are clothed, and the valleys shall abound with wheat; they shall cry aloud, yea, they shall sing.

*Psalm 65:*[^207]
Shout with joy unto the Lord, all the earth; chant ye unto His name, give glory unto His praise.
Say unto God: How awesome are Thy works; in the multitude of Thy power Thine enemies shall fawn upon Thee.
All the earth shall worship (cont. on fol. 30v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...Thee, and sing unto Thee; yea, let it sing unto Thy name, O Most High.
Come and see the works of God, how awesome He is in His counsels beyond the sons of men.
He turns the sea into dry land; they shall pass through the river on foot; there shall we rejoice in Him, Who rules by His power unto the ages.
His eyes look upon the nations; let not them that provoke Him be exalted in themselves.
Bless our God, ye nations, and cause the voice of His praise to be heard;
Who has appointed my soul unto life, and has not given my feet over unto slipping.
For Thou, O God, hast proved us; Thou hast tried us with fire, even as silver is tried. Thou hast brought us into the snare; Thou hast laid (cont. on fol. 31r) *Saturday. [Fol. 31r]*

...afflictions upon our back; Thou hast set men over our heads.
We went through fire and through water, and Thou hast brought us out into a place of refreshment.
I will go into Thy house with whole burnt-offerings; I will pay Thee my vows, which my lips have uttered, and my mouth has spoken in mine affliction.
I will offer unto Thee whole burnt-offerings full of marrow, with incense and rams; I will offer unto Thee bullocks with goats.
Come, give ear, and I will declare unto you, all ye that fear God, what things He has done for my soul.
I cried unto Him with my mouth, and I exalted Him with my tongue.
If I have regarded iniquity in my heart, let not the Lord hear me.
Therefore God has heard me; He has attended to the voice of my (cont. on fol. 31v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...supplication.
Blessed be God, Who has not turned away my prayer, nor His mercy from me.

*Psalm 66:*[^208]
God be gracious unto us, and bless us; cause His face to shine upon us, and have mercy on us;
That we may know Thy way upon the earth, Thy salvation among all the nations.
Let the peoples praise Thee, O God; let all the peoples praise Thee.
Let the nations be glad and rejoice, for Thou shalt judge the peoples with uprightness, and shalt guide the nations upon earth.
Let the peoples praise Thee, O God; let all the peoples praise Thee.
The earth has yielded her fruit.
God, our God, bless us; (cont. on fol. 32r) *Saturday. [Fol. 32r]*

...God bless us, and let all the ends of the earth fear Him.
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*

*Psalm 67:*[^209]
Let God arise, and let His enemies be scattered; and let them that hate Him flee from before His face.
As smoke vanishes, so let them vanish; as wax melts before the face of fire, so let the sinners perish from before the face of God.
And let the righteous be glad; let them rejoice before God, let them delight with gladness.
Sing unto God, sing praises unto His name; make way for Him Who rides upon the heavens; the Lord is His name, and rejoice ye before Him.
They shall be troubled before His face, the Father of orphans, and Judge of widows: (cont. on fol. 32v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...God in His holy place.
God settles the solitary in a house, leading forth the bound with strength, likewise them that provoke Him, who dwell in graves.
O God, when Thou wentest forth before Thy people, when Thou didst traverse in the wilderness, the earth shook;
Yea, the heavens dropped at the face of the God of Sinai, at the face of the God of Israel.
A rain of free will didst Thou set apart, O God, for Thine inheritance; and it was weakened, but Thou didst perfect it.
Thy creatures dwell therein; Thou hast prepared it in Thy goodness for the poor, O God.
The Lord gives the word unto them that preach good tidings with great power.
The King of the powers of the beloved, for the beauty of the house to divide the spoils.
Though ye sleep among the lots, (cont. on fol. 33r) *Saturday. [Fol. 33r]*

...[yet shall ye be as] the wings of a dove covered with silver, and her pinions with the glistening of gold.
When the Heavenly One scatters kings upon her, they shall become white as snow in Salmon.
The mountain of God is a fertile mountain, a curded mountain, a fertile mountain.
Why do ye look askance, ye curded mountains?
This is the mountain wherein God has been pleased to dwell; yea, the Lord will dwell therein unto the end.
The chariot of God is ten thousand times ten thousand, thousands of rejoicing ones; the Lord is among them in Sinai, in the holy place.
Thou hast ascended on high, Thou hast led captivity captive; Thou hast received gifts among men, yea, even for the disobedient, that Thou mightest dwell [there].
Blessed is the Lord God, blessed is the Lord day by day; the God of our salvation will prosper us; our God is the God (cont. on fol. 33v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...of salvation; and of the Lord, of the Lord are the issues of death.
But God shall crush the heads of His enemies, the hairy crown of them that go on in their trespasses.
The Lord said: I will turn back from Bashan, I will bring back from the depths of the sea;
That thy foot may be dipped in blood, the tongue of thy dogs from the enemies, from him.
Thy goings have been seen, O God, the goings of my God, my King, Who is in the sanctuary.
The princes went before, next to them that sing, in the midst of maidens playing on timbrels.
In the churches bless ye God, the Lord from the fountains of Israel.
There is Benjamin the younger in ecstasy, the princes of Judah their leaders, the princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali.
Command, O God, Thy power; strengthen, O God, this (cont. on fol. 34r) *Saturday. [Fol. 34r]*

...which Thou hast wrought in us.
From Thy temple at Jerusalem, kings shall bring gifts unto Thee.
Rebuke the wild beasts of the reed, the assembly of bulls among the heifers of the peoples, to exclude them that are tried with silver.
Scatter Thou the nations that desire wars.
Ambassadors shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall stretch out her hands beforehand unto God.
Ye kingdoms of the earth, sing unto God; chant ye unto the Lord;
Unto Him that rides upon the heaven of heavens toward the east; lo, He will utter His voice, a voice of power.
Ascribe ye glory unto God; upon Israel is His magnificence, and His power is in the clouds.
Marvelous is God in His saints, the God of Israel; He will give power and strength unto His people.
Blessed be God.
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.*

*Saturday Midnight Office*

*Psalm 68:*[^210]
Save me, O God, for the waters have entered in even unto my soul.
I am stuck fast in the mire of the deep, and there is no standing.
I am come into the depths of the sea, and a tempest has overwhelmed me.
I am wearied with crying, my throat is grown hoarse; mine eyes have failed, while I hope in my God.
They that hate me without a cause are multiplied more than the hairs of my head.
Mine enemies that persecute me unjustly are grown strong; what things I took not away, then did I restore.
O God, Thou knowest my foolishness, and my trespasses are not hidden from Thee.
Let not them that wait on Thee, O Lord, Lord of hosts, be ashamed for my sake; (cont. on fol. 35r) *Saturday. [Fol. 35r]*

...neither let them that seek Thee, O God of Israel, be put to confusion on my account.
For Thy sake have I borne reproach; shame has covered my face.
I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto the sons of my mother.
For the zeal of Thy house has eaten me up, and the reproaches of them that reproached Thee have fallen upon me.
And I covered my soul with fasting, and it was made a reproach unto me.
And I made sackcloth my garment, and I became a proverb unto them.
They that sit in the gate talked against me, and they that drank wine sang of me.
But as for me, with my prayer [I come] unto Thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time.
O God, in the multitude of Thy mercy hear me, in the truth of Thy salvation.
Save me from the mire, that I sink not; deliver (cont. on fol. 35v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...me from them that hate me, and from the deep waters.
Let not the waterflood drown me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me.
Hear me, O Lord, for Thy mercy is good; look upon me according to the multitude of Thy compassions.
And turn not away Thy face from Thy servant, for I am in trouble; hear me speedily.
Attend unto my soul, and redeem it; deliver me because of mine enemies.
For Thou knowest my reproach, and my shame, and my confusion; all they that afflict me are before Thee.
Reproach has broken my soul and my misery; and I looked for one that would grieve with me, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none.
And they gave me gall for my food, (cont. on fol. 36r) *Saturday. [Fol. 36r]*

...and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.
Let their table become a snare before them, and a recompense, and a stumbling-block.
Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and bow down their back continually.
Pour out Thine indignation upon them, and let the wrath of Thine anger overtake them.
Let their habitation be desolate, and in their tents let there be none to dwell.
For they have persecuted him whom Thou hast smitten, and they have added to the grief of my wounds.
Add iniquity unto their iniquity, and let them not enter into Thy righteousness.
Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.
Poor and in pain am I; the salvation of Thy face, O God, has upheld me.
I will praise the name of my God (cont. on fol. 36v) *Saturday Midnight Office*

...with a song, I will magnify Him with thanksgiving.
And this shall please God better than a young bullock that has horns and hoofs.
Let the poor see and be glad; seek ye God, and your soul shall live.
For the Lord has heard the poor, and His bound He has not despised.
Let the heavens and earth praise Him, the sea and everything that creeps therein.
For God will save Zion, and the cities of Judah shall be built; and they shall dwell there, and inherit it;
And the seed of Thy servants shall possess it, and they that love Thy name shall dwell therein.

*Psalm 69:*[^211]
O God, attend unto my help; O Lord, make haste to help me.
Let them be ashamed and (cont. on fol. 37r) *Saturday. [Fol. 37r]*

...confounded that seek after my soul.
Let them be turned backward and brought to confusion that wish me evil.
Let them be turned back immediately in shame that say unto me: Well done! Well done!
Let all those that seek Thee, O God, rejoice and be glad in Thee; and let such as love Thy salvation say continually: The Lord be magnified!
But as for me, I am poor and needy; O God, help me.
Thou art my helper and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.*
