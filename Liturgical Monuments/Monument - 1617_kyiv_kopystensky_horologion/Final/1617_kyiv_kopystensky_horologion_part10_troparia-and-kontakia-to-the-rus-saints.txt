# TROPARIA AND KONTAKIA TO THE RUS' SAINTS [^537]

*Troparia and Kontakia to the Rus' Saints. [Fol. 186v]*

*On the 10th Day of the Month of July: Commemoration of our Venerable Father Anthony of the Caves. Tone 4. Troparion:*[^538]

Departing from the turmoil of the world, thou didst reach the quiet haven of the Holy Mountain of Athos; and renouncing the world, according to the Gospel thou didst follow Christ, having lived an angel-like life. And thence, by the blessing of the Fathers, coming unto this mountain, having laboriously in the Caves completed (cont. on fol. 187r) *Troparia and Kontakia to the Rus' Saints. [Fol. 187r]*

thy life, thou didst show forth the path unto the Heavenly Kingdom for a multitude of monastics, and didst bring them unto Christ; entreat Him, O venerable father, that He may save our souls.

*Tone 8. Kontakion:*

Committing thyself unto God from thy youth, O venerable father, loving Him above all things, and following Him with love from thy whole soul, counting corruptible earthly things as nothing, thou didst fashion for thyself a cave in the earth; wherein having striven well against the wiles of the invisible foe, thou didst shine forth like the radiant sun unto the ends of the earth. Whence rejoicing thou didst pass over unto the heavenly mansions, and now standing with the Angels before the eternal (cont. in cohort 22) *Kontakion continuation from previous cohort:*
remember us who honor thy memory, that we may cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Antony, our father!

*May 3:*
*Commemoration of our venerable father Theodosius of the Caves:*[^539]

*Tone 8. Troparion:*
Having ascended unto virtue from youth, thou didst love the monastic life, and having valiantly attained thy desire, thou didst settle in a cave; and having adorned thy life with fasting and radiance, abiding in prayers as though bodiless, thou didst shine forth like a radiant beacon in the land of Rus', O father Theodosius: entreat Christ God that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 3. Kontakion:*
*(Catchword: The star) Continuation of Kontakion:*
Today let us honor the star of Rus', which shone forth from the east and came unto the west; for he enriched all this land with wonders and goodness, and all of us by the diligent labor and grace of the monastic rule, the blessed Theodosius.

*July 11:*
*Commemoration of the venerable Olga, in Holy Baptism Helen, Princess of Rus':*[^540]

*Tone 1. Troparion:*
Having feathered thy mind with the wings of divine understanding, thou didst soar above visible creation, seeking after God and the Creator of all things; and having found Him, thou didst receive rebirth in baptism, delighting in the Tree of Life, *Continuation of Troparion:*
abiding incorrupt unto ages, O Helen ever-renowned.

*Tone 4. Kontakion:*
Today let us hymn the Benefactor of all, God, Who glorified in Rus' Helen the divinely wise; by her prayers, O Christ, grant remission of sins unto our souls.

*July 15:*
*Commemoration of the right-believing Prince Vladimir:*[^541]

*Tone 4. Troparion:*
Having likened thyself unto a merchant seeking goodly pearls, O gloriously ruling Vladimir, sitting upon the height of the throne of the Mother of Cities, God-saved Kyiv,[^542]
*(Catchword: Kyiv) July 24:*
*Commemoration of the Holy Passion-Bearers Roman and David (Boris and Gleb):*[^543]

*Tone 3. Kontakion:*
Today your glorious memory has shone forth, O noble passion-bearers of Christ, Roman and David, summoning us unto the praise of Christ our God; therefore, fleeing unto the shrine of your relics, we receive the gift of healing through your prayers, O holy ones; for ye are divine physicians.

*(Tailpiece: Medallion woodcut ornament)*

### Notes

[^537]: Fol. 186v introduces the monumental Kyiv-Pechersk appendix of Troparia and Kontakia to the Rus' Saints (*ТРѠПАРИ, И КОНДАКИ РѠССКИМЪ СТЫ́МЪ*), headed by a xylographic headpiece portraying the Lavra eagle crest.
[^538]: Fol. 186v. Feast of Venerable Anthony of the Caves (commemorated July 10), founder of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra and father of Rus' monasticism.
[^539]: St. Theodosius of the Caves (d. 1074), founder of cenobitic monasticism in Kyivan Rus' and co-founder of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra, commemorated on May 3 (his repose) and August 14 (the translation of his relics).
[^540]: Holy Equal-to-the-Apostles Olga (in baptism Helen), Grand Princess of Kyiv and grandmother of St. Vladimir, who received Holy Baptism at Constantinople (c. 957) and was celebrated on July 11.
[^541]: Holy Equal-to-the-Apostles Great Prince Vladimir (baptized Basil), Baptizer of Kyivan Rus' (988), commemorated on July 15.
[^542]: Physical codex lacuna: Folio 189 is missing from the Trinity-Sergius Lavra exemplar (RGB f. 801/09 № 1241), as noted both on the codex flyleaf (p4) and by the librarian's pencil inscription ('Листа нѣтъ!') beneath the catchword on Fol. 188v. The missing folio contained the remainder of the Troparion of St. Vladimir ('...testing and sending unto the Imperial City...'), his Kontakion ('In an old age, O great Vladimir...'), and the feast and Troparion of the Holy Passion-Bearers Boris and Gleb on July 24 ('Righteous passion-bearers and true fulfillers of the Gospel...'). The text resumes on Fol. 190r with the Kontakion of Saints Boris and Gleb.
[^543]: Holy Passion-Bearers Prince Boris and Prince Gleb (baptized Roman and David), sons of St. Vladimir and the first canonized saints of Kyivan Rus', martyred in 1015 and commemorated together on July 24.
