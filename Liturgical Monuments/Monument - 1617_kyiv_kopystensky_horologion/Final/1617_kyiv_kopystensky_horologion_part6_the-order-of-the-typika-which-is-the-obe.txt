*A TALE FROM THE CHRONICLER*
*Concerning the Elevation of the All-Holy [Panagia]: How, and When It Was, and for What Reason.*[^346]

After the awesome Resurrection of our Savior Jesus Christ, and the Descent of the Holy Spirit, and even unto the dispersal of the holy Apostles unto preaching, all the Apostles were together in one accord. And whenever, after prayer, they sat down at the meal: they would leave an empty place, upon which they placed a cushion, and upon the cushion a portion of bread, from that which they ate, in honor of Christ. And after the meal, arising, having prayed and given thanks, they would take up the portion of bread which they had set apart in honor of the Lord. And elevating this, they would say: Glory to Thee, (cont. on fol. 108r) *Concerning the Same. [Fol. 108r]*

O our God, glory to Thee; Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. And instead of saying 'Great is the name', they would say: 'Christ is risen', even unto the Ascension. Thereafter: 'Great is the name of the Holy Trinity; O Lord Jesus Christ, help us.' And these things they performed thus all the year round. And each of them did this also wherever they happened to be, even unto the Dormition of the Birthgiver of God. And when, for the universal Dormition of our all-holy Lady the Birthgiver of God, they were gathered upon the clouds, and had accomplished what was meet; and when, after her burial, they were eating on the third day, and after the meal were elevating according to custom the portion of bread lying in the name of Christ, and proclaiming the Great Name: (*O most glorious wonder!*)[^347] (cont. on fol. 108v) *Tale concerning the Same*

that alive upon a cloud, with light-bearing Angels attending her, she appears in the air, saying unto them: 'Rejoice, for I am with you all the days; this gift have I received from my Son.' And the disciples, marveling at the miracle, cried aloud instead of 'O Lord Jesus Christ': 'All-holy Birthgiver of God, help us!' After this, having come to the tomb, and not finding her most pure body, they were assured of a truth that alive with her body, having on the third day arisen from the dead as also did her Son, she had passed over unto the heavenly realms.[^348] (cont. on fol. 109r) *The Beginning of the Hours.
