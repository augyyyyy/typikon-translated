[^296]: Psalm 54 (LXX 54 / MT 55), titled 'To the end, in songs of instruction, of Asaph' (Въ коне́цъ в̾ пѣ́снехъ, ра́зоу Аса́фоу). In standard Septuagint and liturgical Psalters, this psalm is ascribed to David (συνέσεως τῷ Δαυιδ / ра́зꙋма Дави́дꙋ); the 1617 Kyiv Horologion typesetter introduces an anomalous typographical substitution attributing it to Asaph.
