[^220]: These Supplicatory Triadica (*Троичны припѣлы* / *Τριαδικά*) are attributed in the rubrics to Saint Gregory of Sinai (c. 1260–1346), the great hesychastic father who revitalized contemplative prayer and monastic hymnography in the Byzantine Empire, Mount Athos, and Paroria.
[^221]: The Sunday Hypakoe (*ѵ҆пакои* / *ὑπακοή*) is the brief liturgical troparion of the Resurrection according to the tone of the week chanted in the Sunday Octoechos cycle.
[^222]: The penitential troparia (*Помилуй насъ, Господи, помилуй насъ...*) and the Theotokion (*Милосердія двери отверзи намъ...*) are cross-referenced to Fol. 25 (physical leaf p53), where they were printed in the Compline order.
[^223]: The monumental nocturnal prayer to the Holy Trinity (*Всемогущая и животворящая Святая Троице...*) is attributed to Mark the Monk (Saint Mark the Ascetic, 5th century).
