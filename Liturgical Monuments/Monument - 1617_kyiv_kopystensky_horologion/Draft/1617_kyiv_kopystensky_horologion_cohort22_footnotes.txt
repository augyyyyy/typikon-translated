The missing folio contained the remainder of the Troparion of St. Vladimir ('...testing and sending unto the Imperial City...'), his Kontakion ('In an old age, O great Vladimir...'), and the feast and Troparion of the Holy Passion-Bearers Boris and Gleb on July 24 ('Righteous passion-bearers and true fulfillers of the Gospel...').
