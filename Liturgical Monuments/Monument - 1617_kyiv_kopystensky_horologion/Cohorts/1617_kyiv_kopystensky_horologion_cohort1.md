# PREFACE

**Eliseus Pletenetsky,[^4] Archimandrite of the Caves of Kyiv,[^5] unto the Orthodox Russian Race,[^6] the most beloved sons of the Eastern Church: soul-saving health!**

Even as, therefore, according unto the grace given unto me, over those entrusted unto me within the monastery of the Holy Caves Lavra, both inwardly and outwardly, the Lord working together with me, I labor, and in zeal for the piety of the Eastern Church do abide: even so, with soul-profiting works, to gladden the hearts of her orthodox sons, and in orthodoxy to confirm them, and to adorn them, did it seem good [to provide] ecclesi-

=== LEAF p5 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 1v]**

...astical books. And at the beginning I deemed it meet to send forth a small book as a forerunner,[^7] that it might prepare the way for greater ones; for neither does the day dawn without its forerunner, the morning star. And nothing did I judge more fitting than this prayer book, named the *Horologion*, wherein for ordinary and fasting days God-inspired prayers are found. And for this reason also, that from the Holy Caves Monastery—which has blossomed forth with the beauty of prayer and fasting unto humility, to such a degree that wonderworking saints were made manifest therein, and unto this day many abide in the incorruption of their bodies—by those very virtues whereby she has blossomed into so great a flower, with these it was fitting to greet you,

**[Right Page — Fol. 2r]**

that ye likewise by these two virtues might blossom unto the glory of God. Truly great is the power of this book, that is, of prayer; for this is the morning star that enlightens souls and disposes the faithful to converse with God. And thereby it is possible to escape visible and invisible enemies, and to be delivered from present afflictions. For King Hezekiah, it is said, and the Prophet Isaiah prayed and cried aloud even unto heaven, and the Lord sent His angel, who smote all the mighty men of valor and the warriors and the leaders of the hosts in the camp of the King of Assyria.[^8] And again, King Manasseh prayed diligently in Babylon, and the Lord heard his prayer and his cry, and restored him unto Jerusalem unto his kingdom;[^9]

=== LEAF p6 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 2v]**

...his kingdom. And it is delivered down in remembrance by Africanus,[^10] that when Manasseh made prayer, his iron bonds were rent asunder, and he obtained for himself all health. And let no one say: "Seeing that already not once, nor from a single printing press has this book gone forth, why then this one?" Good merchandise is ever in the marketplace; necessary sustenance is continually in the shambles; and an instrument wherewith we frequently make use, the craftsman ever constructs.[^11] Ought it not all the more so to be with prayer, the spiritual instrument? Which is a continual work, and greater labor lies therein, even as Abba Agathon said: "Every way of life that a man undertakes has a guard therein, acquiring rest; but as for prayer, until...

**[Right Page — Fol. 3r]**

...his last breath it requires labor."[^12] In addition unto these things, I was also entreated by the orthodox, that we might fulfill the need which is in the school in the Orthodox City of Kyiv,[^13] and among the rest. Even so was it pleasing unto us, when by God's help we purposed to publish books unto the world, that it was not fitting to begin otherwise than from prayer, which surpasses all things. Therefore, as a single blessing from our venerable Caves Fathers, receive ye lovingly this book of greeting, hoping, if the Lord will, shortly to be deemed worthy of embracing others also; and pray to God One in Trinity, that He may prosper what we have devised through the typographical craft to please the Orthodox Churches...

...present, and committing the undertaking that is to be unto the judgment and correction of the Eastern Church. Fare ye well in the Lord!

## FOREWORD TO THE HOROLOGION[^14]

*All flesh shall come to worship before Me in Jerusalem, says the Lord.* (Isa. 66:23)[^15]

Lo, orthodox Christian, and every devout reader: from the renowned Kyivan sites in Rus', that is, from the Caves Lavra of Kyiv, by the grace of God, and by the diligence and care of the Most Venerable among the Fathers,

=== LEAF p8 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 4v]**

Kyr Eliseus Pletenetsky,[^16] Archimandrite of the Caves of Kyiv, there comes forth printed the prayer book *Horologion*, which is interpreted in Slavonic as the *Chasoslov*; containing within itself the day and night prayers, arranged according to the order of the Church of Jerusalem and of the Lavra of Saint Sabas the Sanctified, archimandrite thereof.[^17] For, lo, it is meet to follow the dogma, typikon, and order of the Eastern Church. And this we do as in acceptable comeliness. For when we pray, we turn our eyes unto the east, where our fatherland of Paradise is, concerning which it is written: *And God planted Paradise eastward in Eden;* (Gen. 2:8)[^18] unto which God, disposing us ever to yearn, commanded

**[Right Page — Fol. 5r]**

Moses to turn the Tabernacle toward the east; and the tribe of Judah pitched their tents toward the east; (Num. 2:3)[^19] for from thence also do we behold the sun enlightening the universe. Therefore, it is lawful for us to receive from the east piety with all ecclesiastical good order, and to embrace it. And all the more so, since the righteous throne of Jesus Christ our Savior upholds the Church of Jerusalem; from thence, as formerly from Mount Sinai, the world received the covenant of the new grace; from thence, when God was incarnate and dwelt with men, (Bar. 3:38)[^20] the human race attained unto its salvation; from thence the universe manifestly learned the mysteries of the Tri-hypostatic Godhead; and, to speak simply: from thence, as from an ever-flowing fount...


=== LEAF p9 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 5v]**

...fount, according unto the God-speaking Prophet: *For out of Zion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem;* (Isa. 2:3)[^21] and into all the world the rivers of theology are poured forth, nor do they fail in flowing from thence. From that Church Christ commanded the word of grace to begin, as Luke says: *and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in His name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.* (Lk. 24:47)[^22] In that Church the Apostles, having been filled with the Holy Spirit, went forth to confirm the universe; and unto her they turned again, and assembled together; and diverse discords and the upspringing of heresies, by divine counsel and judgment, as with a sword, they cut off from the fullness of the Church; sending forth from Jerusalem dogmas and injunctions unto the Churches...

**[Right Page — Fol. 6r]**

...of Christ they delivered, and transmitted them to be kept. And that Church of Jerusalem alone remained pure from heresy, for the Arians (as Hermias Sozomen testifies) did not enter into her.[^23] That Church is the Mother of Churches, according unto the Prophet: *A man will say, "Mother Zion," and a man was born in her, and the Highest Himself has established her;* (Ps. 86:5)[^24] and according unto Isaiah, who said: *And afterward thou shalt be called the righteous city, Mother of faithful cities, Zion;* (Isa. 1:26)[^25] and Damascene: *Rejoice, O holy Zion, Mother of Churches, dwelling-place of God; for thou first didst receive remission of sins on account of the Resurrection;*[^26] and the Second Ecumenical Council, in its synodical letter, calls the Church of Jerusalem the Mother of all Churches, speaking thus:[^27]

=== LEAF p10 ===

**[Left Page — Fol. 6v]**

"Thus: And of the Mother of all Churches, which is in Jerusalem, we make known unto you that the most honorable and most God-beloved Cyril is bishop."[^28] From that Church of Jerusalem, the Mother of Churches, the foremost among the Apostles, Andrew, after the apportioning among the nations, having enlightened the Thracian lands with the light of the Gospel, and in the Thracian city of Byzantium, which is now Constantinople, having laid firm foundations of faith; and having been the first Patriarch there; traversing the ends of the universe, he reached the hills of Kyiv,[^29] and by the erection of the sign of the life-creating Cross, having put to shame the aerial adversaries,[^30] prophetically [proclaimed] that the readers of the true faith of Christ should dwell there, and that unfeigned worshipers of God in Trinity should abide...

**[Right Page — Fol. 7r]**

...abide; and foreseeing this from afar by many ages, he prophesied it; which also in our Russian race, in the times of the Sovereign Vladimir—who was named a second Constantine Equal-to-the-Apostles—by the grace of Christ was radiantly fulfilled.[^31] When, filling the place of the fallen through ecclesiastical deprivation, and [coming into] light (the darkened stars), as a most radiant dawn with his peoples, he was abundantly deemed worthy of holy Baptism; and was numbered among those forechosen by God, in these latter times, with all his host, being illumined with the light of piety from the east and from Jerusalem; receiving all ecclesiastical traditions, order, and administration, as a patristic heritage from the Church of Constantinople...
