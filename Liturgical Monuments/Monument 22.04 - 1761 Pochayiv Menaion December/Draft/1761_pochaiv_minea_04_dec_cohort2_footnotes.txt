[^16]: Saint Barbara the Great-Martyr of Heliopolis (beheaded c. 306 under Emperor Maximian) and Saint John of Damascus (John Damascene, c. 676–749), Monk of the Lavra of Saint Sabbas the Sanctified, Father and Doctor of the Church, celebrated together on December 4.
[^17]: The Matins canon pairs the Canon of Saint Barbara (composed by Saint John of Damascus, Tone 2, Heirmos: *In the deep of old*) with the Canon of Saint John Damascene (composed by Saint Theophanes the Branded, same Tone and Heirmos).
