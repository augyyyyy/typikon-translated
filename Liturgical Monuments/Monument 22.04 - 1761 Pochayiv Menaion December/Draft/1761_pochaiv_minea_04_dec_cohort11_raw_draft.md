*It is needful to know: That from this 11th day the Sunday of the Forefathers can begin, and it extends in this same December unto the 17th day. And it happens thus: If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Sunday:*[^89]

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 11th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers, which is before the Nativity of Christ, is on the 18th day of December.*

*If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Monday:*

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 17th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers, which is before the Nativity of Christ, is then on the 24th day of December.*

*If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Tuesday:*

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 16th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers, which is before the Nativity of Christ, is on the 23rd day of December.*

*If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Wednesday:*

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 15th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers is on the 22nd day of December.*

*If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Thursday:*

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 14th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers is on the 21st day of December.*

*If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Friday:*

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 13th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers is on the 20th day of December.*

*If the Nativity of Christ fall on a Saturday:*

*Then the Sunday of the Forefathers is on the 12th day of December; and the Sunday of the Holy Fathers, which is before the Nativity of Christ, is on the 19th day of December.*[^90]

*Here observe the Sunday of the Holy Forefathers, on whichever date it happens to occur, whether before the 28th Sunday [after Pentecost] or after: at the Liturgy read the Gospel of the 28th Sunday, Luke, Pericope 76: "A certain man made a great supper." Without change, this one alone. And on the 28th Sunday read the sequential [Gospel], whichever of another Sunday happens to fall.*
