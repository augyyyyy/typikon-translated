[^24]: Slavonic: *ꙋ҆ꙗзвлѧ́лъ є҆сѝ ꙋ҆́глїе* (literally *thou didst wound/quench the coals*), referring to extinguishing the scorching coals of demonic temptations in the wilderness.
[^25]: Slavonic: *И҆з̾ ꙋ҆лтро́бы ѡ҆ст҃и́лсѧ є҆сѝ*, where *ꙋ҆лтро́бы* is an epigraphic misprint for *ꙋ҆тро́бы* (*from the womb*).
[^26]: Slavonic: *Неꙋдержи́мыми на на́съ сме́рть стремле́ньми и҆тїа̀*, where *и҆тїа̀* is an archaic/epigraphic aorist form from *и҆тѝ* (*came* / *advanced*); the text expresses: *Death came with unrestrainable assaults upon us*.
[^27]: The printed text has *чꙋлодѣ́йствꙋѧй*, an epigraphic misprint for *чꙋдодѣ́йствꙋѧй* (*wonderworking* / *working wonders*).
[^28]: The Slavonic text has *ѡ҆шла́нїемъ слезе́й*, an epigraphic/orthographic variant for *ѡ҆лиѧ́нїемъ* / *ѡ҆мыва́нїемъ* (*by the washing / pouring forth of tears*).
[^29]: The printed text has *Ѡ҆ложи́лъ є҆сѝ*, an epigraphic misprint for *Ѿложи́лъ є҆сѝ* (*thou didst put off* or *renounce*).
[^30]: The printed Slavonic has *ꙋ҆ѻбло́вленнїи*, an epigraphic dittography/misprint for *ꙋ҆ловле́ннїи* (*caught* or *enamored*).
