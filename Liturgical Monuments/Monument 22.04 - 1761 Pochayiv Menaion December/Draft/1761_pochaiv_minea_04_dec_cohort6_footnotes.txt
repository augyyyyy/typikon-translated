[^40]: Pericope 36 of the Gospel according to John comprises John 10:9–16, the classical liturgical Gospel lection for holy hierarchs.
[^41]: Slavonic: *Творе́нїе дїофа́ново*, an epigraphic error for *Ѳеофа́ново* (Theophanes the Branded, c. 778–845). The Greek acrostic reads: Σοὶ Νικόλαε θεῖον ἐξᾴδω μέλος. Ποίημα Θεοφάνους ("Unto thee, O Nicholas, do I sing a divine song. Poem of Theophanes").
[^42]: The Greek text of this second canon of Saint Nicholas forms an alphabetical acrostic (ἀλφαβητικός) from Alpha to Omega, with non-acrostic Troika (Trinity hymns) and Theotokia appended to each ode.
[^43]: Slavonic: *стла́мнꙋ* (epigraphic variant for *ста́мнꙋ*, urn/pot), translating the Greek στάμνον, the golden urn holding the manna (Heb. 9:4), a standard Old Testament type of the Mother of God.
[^44]: Slavonic: *побѣ́ды на врагѝ тезоимени́тъ лю́демъ вѣ́рнымъ* (namesake of victory over enemies unto the faithful people), reflecting the Greek hymnographic play on the etymology of Saint Nicholas' name: Νικόλαος, from νίκη (victory) and λαός (people).
