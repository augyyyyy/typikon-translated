# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 4: December (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-4-december-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

[Title Page with typographical ornament border printed in cinnabar and black ink]

THE BOOK OF THE MENAION
Month of December

To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; under the reign of the Great King Augustus the Third;[^1] by the blessing of His Grace, Lord Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytskyi,[^2] Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the volition of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk Hypatius Bilynskyi,[^3] Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; through the zeal and at the expense of the monks of the same Order; in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv, printed with types: Published. In the year from the Creation of the world according to the Greek chronographs 7269,[^4] and of the Nativity of Christ 1761.

*(Vacat — Blank page)* [Fol. 1r]

### Notes

[^1]: King Augustus III of Poland (Frederick Augustus II of Saxony, reigned 1734–1763).
[^2]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytskyi (Sylwester Rudnycki-Lubieniecki, c. 1708–1777), Basilian Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh (1750–1777), Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus'.
[^3]: Hypatius Bilynskyi (Ipatij Bilyns'kyj, 1704–1771), Protoarchimandrite of the Basilian Order of Saint Basil the Great (OSBM, 1751–1771).
[^4]: The Byzantine Anno Mundi year 7269 corresponds to 1760/1761 AD (September 1 era).
