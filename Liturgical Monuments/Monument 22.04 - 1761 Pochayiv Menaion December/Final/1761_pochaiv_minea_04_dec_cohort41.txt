# CONCLUDING APPENDICES AND COLOPHON
*Service of the Holy Great Martyr Barbara.*[^730]
*At Small Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera on 4, Tone 4. Podoben: "As one noble" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*[^731]
Having adorned thyself with blood-dyed vesture, O glorious Barbara, holy great martyr, thou wast united unto the Bridegroom, Christ our God, Who is comely in beauty, O virgin maiden, Who preserves thy virginity undefiled in heavenly habitations, in the bridal chamber not made with hands, O martyr. *(Twice)*

Imperfect in body, yet perfect in mind, having conquered the great dragon, thou didst crush his head, O glorious one, by spiritual power, and didst show his might to be powerless. Therefore an unconquerable crown was given unto thee, O Barbara of great renown, champion of the Trinity.

Having whitened thyself with drops of blood, thou didst shine forth manifestly like the sun, O all-praised one, and didst drive away the darkness of paternal godlessness, and didst wash away from thyself its mire by the grace of Christ, O great martyr Barbara: save those who honor thy valiant exploits and who festively celebrate thy radiant memory.

*Glory, Tone 2:*
Having been richly enlightened by spiritual light, thou didst despise corruptible wealth, having loved that which is heavenly alone; and having manifestly put on the strength of martyrdom, thou didst eagerly bend thy neck unto the sword: therefore thou didst also put to shame the enemy—thy father who beheaded thee—being crowned with the wreath of victory, O most glorious great martyr Barbara; pray that we all may be saved.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Upon thee have we set our hope, O Theotokos: O pure one, let us not fall away; save us from perils, O helper of the perplexed, and scatter the counsels of our adversaries: for thou art our salvation, O blessed one.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Unto the heavenly ranks" (Не́бнымъ чинѡ́мъ):*[^732]
Having despised an impious father and mother, O Barbara, thou didst love God and the heavenly Jerusalem, in which thou now dwellest: entreat good Christ that we also may abide together with thee.

*Verse:* Marvelous is God in His saints, the God of Israel.[^733]
Neither affection for parents, nor pleasures, nor bodily beauty, nor wealth, nor...

...torments tear thee away from the love of the Creator: Whom make merciful unto us, O great martyr.

*Verse:* Unto the saints that are in His earth.[^734]
Confessing one God in Trinity with boldness, O Barbara, and beheaded, thou didst receive from on high from Christ the crown of victory, with Whom thou now reignest: entreat peace for all.

*Glory, Tone 2:*
Like precious myrrh didst thou bring thy blood unto the Bridegroom Christ, O invincible martyr Barbara; and from Him thou didst receive an incorruptible crown as thy reward. Therefore, even after thy suffering, healings richly flow from thy precious relics unto those who approach with faith and honor thee; which pour forth unstintingly also unto us, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*Troparion, Tone 4:*
O all-blessed ewe-lamb Barbara, having been divinely illumined with the thrice-sunlit light of the Holy Trinity, and established in the font unto the defeat of paternal deception, thou didst confess the faith of Christ.
