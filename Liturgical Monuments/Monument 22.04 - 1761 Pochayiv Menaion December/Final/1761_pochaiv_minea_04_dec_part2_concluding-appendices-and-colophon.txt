# CONCLUDING APPENDICES AND COLOPHON

*Service of the Holy Great Martyr Barbara.*[^730]
*At Small Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera on 4, Tone 4. Podoben: "As one noble" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*[^731]
Having adorned thyself with blood-dyed vesture, O glorious Barbara, holy great martyr, thou wast united unto the Bridegroom, Christ our God, Who is comely in beauty, O virgin maiden, Who preserves thy virginity undefiled in heavenly habitations, in the bridal chamber not made with hands, O martyr. *(Twice)*

Imperfect in body, yet perfect in mind, having conquered the great dragon, thou didst crush his head, O glorious one, by spiritual power, and didst show his might to be powerless. Therefore an unconquerable crown was given unto thee, O Barbara of great renown, champion of the Trinity.

Having whitened thyself with drops of blood, thou didst shine forth manifestly like the sun, O all-praised one, and didst drive away the darkness of paternal godlessness, and didst wash away from thyself its mire by the grace of Christ, O great martyr Barbara: save those who honor thy valiant exploits and who festively celebrate thy radiant memory.

*Glory, Tone 2:*
Having been richly enlightened by spiritual light, thou didst despise corruptible wealth, having loved that which is heavenly alone; and having manifestly put on the strength of martyrdom, thou didst eagerly bend thy neck unto the sword: therefore thou didst also put to shame the enemy—thy father who beheaded thee—being crowned with the wreath of victory, O most glorious great martyr Barbara; pray that we all may be saved.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Upon thee have we set our hope, O Theotokos: O pure one, let us not fall away; save us from perils, O helper of the perplexed, and scatter the counsels of our adversaries: for thou art our salvation, O blessed one.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Unto the heavenly ranks" (Не́бнымъ чинѡ́мъ):*[^732]
Having despised an impious father and mother, O Barbara, thou didst love God and the heavenly Jerusalem, in which thou now dwellest: entreat good Christ that we also may abide together with thee.

*Verse:* Marvelous is God in His saints, the God of Israel.[^733]
Neither affection for parents, nor pleasures, nor bodily beauty, nor wealth, nor torments tear thee away from the love of the Creator: Whom make merciful unto us, O great martyr.

*Verse:* Unto the saints that are in His earth.[^734]
Confessing one God in Trinity with boldness, O Barbara, and beheaded, thou didst receive from on high from Christ the crown of victory, with Whom thou now reignest: entreat peace for all.

*Glory, Tone 2:*
Like precious myrrh didst thou bring thy blood unto the Bridegroom Christ, O invincible martyr Barbara; and from Him thou didst receive an incorruptible crown as thy reward. Therefore, even after thy suffering, healings richly flow from thy precious relics unto those who approach with faith and honor thee; which pour forth unstintingly also unto us, and pray that our souls may be saved.

*And now:*
All my hope I place upon thee, O Mother of God: preserve me under thy shelter.

*Troparion, Tone 4:*
O all-blessed ewe-lamb Barbara, having been divinely illumined with the thrice-sunlit light of the Holy Trinity, and established in the font unto the defeat of paternal deception, thou didst confess the faith of Christ. Therefore, O honorable Barbara, grace was granted thee from on high to heal all sicknesses and diseases: by her prayers, O Christ God, save our souls.[^735]

*At Great Vespers:*
*We sing "Blessed is the man," Antiphon 1. And Stichera to the Saint on 8, Tone 2. Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Є҆гда̀ ѿ дре́ва):*[^736]
When the all-blessed Juliana beheld the glorious martyr Barbara suffering with wounds at the dread tribunal, and her whole body cut to pieces with diverse tortures, she cried aloud with fervent tears: O Word of God, O Lover of mankind, account me now worthy to be a partaker with her!

One indeed in disposition toward piety was Barbara and Juliana, and by the power of the Holy Spirit they strove against the enemy, and having powerfully conquered him, were accounted worthy of glory from Christ God. Therefore, having destroyed the harm of destructive passions, they bestow healings upon all the faithful.

When sweet death was pronounced upon thee, O most honorable martyr Barbara, rejoicing and hastening thou didst finish thy course; and sacrificed by the all-lawless hands of an impious parent thou didst offer thyself as a fruit unto God; therefore, truly rejoicing with the wise virgins, thou beholdest the radiance of Christ thy Bridegroom.

*Glory, Tone 6:*
Having forsaken country, kindred, and possessions, O Barbara, and hated an impious father, thou didst love God, unto Whom thou wast betrothed, and wast named a great merchant: pray Him that our souls may be saved.[^737]

*And now, Theotokion:*
*Who would not call thee blessed...*[^738]

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. And three readings:*

*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 43):*[^739]
Thus says the Lord: All the nations were gathered together, and princes shall be gathered out of them. Who will declare these things among them? Or who will declare unto you things from the beginning? Let them bring forth their witnesses, and let them be justified; and let them hear, and let them speak the truth. Be ye My witnesses, and I too am a witness, says the Lord God, and the servant whom I have chosen: that ye may know, and believe Me, and understand that I am He. Before Me there was no other God, and after Me there shall be none. I am God, and beside Me there is no savior. I have declared, and have saved, I have brought reproach, and there was no strange god among you. Ye are My witnesses, and I am the Lord God; even from the beginning I am He, and there is none that can deliver out of My hands: I will work, and who shall turn it back? Thus says the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel Who delivers you.

*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 62):*[^740]
Thus says the Lord: Upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, have I set watchmen all the day and all the night, who shall not hold their peace unto the end, making mention of the Lord; for there is none like unto you, if thou shalt establish and make Jerusalem a praise upon the earth. The Lord hath sworn by His glory and by the strength of His arm: I will no more give thy corn and thy provisions unto thine enemies, nor shall the sons of strangers drink thy wine, for which thou hast labored. But they that gather it shall eat it, and praise the Lord; and they that harvest it shall drink it in My holy courts. Go through, go through My gates; prepare the way, and make a path for My people; cast away the stones from the way, and lift up a standard for the nations. For behold, the Lord hath made it to be heard unto the ends of the earth. Say ye to the daughter of Sion: Behold, thy Savior comes, having His reward with Him, and His work before His face. And He shall call them a holy people, redeemed by the Lord; and thou shalt be called a sought-out city, and not forsaken.

*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Chapter 5):*[^741]
The righteous live unto the ages, and their reward is with the Lord, and the care of them is with the Most High. Therefore shall they receive a kingdom of glory, and a crown of beauty from the hand of the Lord: for with His right hand shall He cover them, and with His arm shall He shield them. He shall take His zeal for complete armor, and shall make the creature His weapon for vengeance upon the enemies. He shall put on righteousness as a breastplate, and shall take unfeigned judgment instead of a helmet. He shall take holiness for an invincible shield: His severe wrath shall He sharpen for a sword, and the world shall fight with Him against the foolish. Right-aiming shafts of lightnings shall go forth, and from the well-rounded bow of the clouds shall they fly unto the mark. And from the stony-throwing fury shall hailstones full of wrath be cast; the water of the sea shall rage against them, and the rivers shall inundate them sharply. A spirit of power shall stand up against them, and like a whirlwind shall sift them: thus lawlessness shall make the whole earth a wilderness, and wrongdoing shall overthrow the thrones of the mighty. Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that are judges of the ends of the earth. Give ear, ye that rule multitudes, and that boast yourselves in the throngs of the nations: For power is given unto you from the Lord, and sovereignty from the Most High.

*At the Litiya, Stichera, Tone 2:*
Radiantly honoring the incorruptible bride who was adorned by the Holy Spirit, we keep thy radiant memory, O martyr passion-bearer; and through devout veneration of thy holy shrine of relics, continually drawing healing for our passions, O all-praised Barbara, we glorify thee in faith.

Sustained by the power of thy Creator, O all-praised one, thou didst depict His sufferings in the flesh, and didst endure all patient suffering of afflictions; and now thou dwellest on high, wearing painless glory and an unfading crown, and thou beholdest Him Whom the angelic ranks behold rejoicing, O God-inspired Barbara.

Behold, thou didst offer the severance of thy members unto thy Bridegroom, O most honorable one; therefore He accounted thee worthy of the heavenly bridal chamber, and enlightens thee with the ever-existing light of divine glory; before Whom thou standest rejoicing and entreating Him for us who celebrate thy memory in faith, O Barbara.

*Glory, Tone 2:*

The crafty enemy was put to shame, conquered by a woman, even as he had wielded power toward sin through the foremother; for the Father's Word, incarnate of the Virgin without change and without confusion, as He alone knows, loosed the curse of Eve and of Adam, even Christ Who worthily crowned the martyr Barbara, and for her sake grants unto the world cleansing and great mercy.[^742]

*And now, Theotokion:*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 5:*
Rejoicing, thou didst stand before the tormentor, O glorious martyr, clearly proclaiming the faith: many scourgings and torments didst thou endure, O passion-bearing Barbara; having put the tormentor to shame, thou wast betrothed unto Christ: pray Him on behalf of us who in faith celebrate thy memory.

*Verse:* Planted in the house of the Lord, in the courts of our God.[^743]
Beholding thee suffering valiantly for the sake of the true faith, the angels rejoiced with joy, glorifying the God of all Who strengthened thee; and men, worshipping Him, praise Him with hymns, Who gave thee, O virgin Barbara, victory over the enemy: pray Christ God that we also may conquer him, and that our souls may be saved.

*Verse:* With patience I waited for the Lord, and He gave ear unto me, and heard my prayer.[^744]
Fearing neither wounds nor afflictions, thou wast not terrified by the tormentor's fury, and thou didst disregard all things, O martyr Barbara; but rather didst choose the love of Christ, and having been betrothed unto Him, thou standest at His right hand, arrayed and adorned as a queen in gilded raiment, fervently praying for those who with faith and love celebrate thy memory.

*Glory, Tone 6:*
Walking by the path of suffering, thou didst escape ancestral counsel, O God-fearing Barbara; and as a wise virgin bearing a lamp, thou didst enter into the courts of thy Lord; and as a valiant martyr, thou didst receive grace to heal evil bodily passions. But deliver also from spiritual sicknesses us who praise thee, through thy prayers unto God.[^745]

*And now, Theotokion:*
No one that hastens unto thee turns away put to shame from thee, O most pure Virgin Theotokos; but he asks for grace, and receives a gift according to his profitable petition.[^746]

*At the blessing of the loaves: Troparion of the Saint, twice (look at Small Vespers); and "O Theotokos Virgin," once.*

*At Matins, after the first Kathisma: Sessional hymn, Tone 5. Podoben (Подо́бенъ): Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-unoriginate Word" (Собезначя́лное):*
Assembling in faith at the memory of the saints, we celebrate the memory of the passion-bearer; for by prayer she slew the author of evil, the enemy, who of old through deceit seduced Eve in incorruption, and she prays unto the Lord to have mercy on our souls.

*Glory, and now, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O holy and God-trodden mountain! Rejoice, O animate and unburnt bush! Rejoice, O only bridge unto God for the world, leading mortals across unto eternal life! Rejoice, O incorrupt maiden, who without knowing man didst bear the Salvation of our souls!

*After the second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up" (Вознесы́йсѧ):*
Having loved Christ Thy Bridegroom, and having brightly prepared Thy lamp, thou didst shine forth with virtues, O all-praised one; therefore thou didst enter with Him into the wedding, having received from Him the crown of suffering. Snatch us from perils, O Barbara, who celebrate Thy memory.

*Glory, and now, Theotokion:*
The Father's Word, Christ our God, Who was incarnate of thee, have we known, O Virgin Theotokos, alone pure and blessed; therefore we ever glorify thee, magnifying thee.

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou didst quickly anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*
Having forsaken paternal wealth, and having loved Christ divinely, thou didst find the wealth of heavenly glory, O martyr; and, wholly shielded by the prayer of grace and by the weapon of the Cross, thou didst trample down the tormentor. Therefore the angels, marveling at thy contest, said: "The enemy has fallen, conquered by a woman." Thou hast appeared as a crown-bearer, O martyr, since Christ has crowned thee, Who grants great mercy unto the world.

*Glory, and now, Theotokion:*
Having received the Word from the angel into thy womb, and having given birth to the incarnate God Emmanuel: O Theotokos, pray for our souls.

*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
Of old, the overwhelmingly equipped power cast Pharaoh's whole host into the deep; and the incarnate Word destroyed all-wicked sin, the most glorious Lord, for gloriously has He been glorified.

*Troparion:*
O super-divine, venerable Trinity, Who above mind bestowest a gift upon Thy servants, breathe a radiant ray into my darkened mind, that I may praise Thy ever-memorable martyr Barbara. *(Twice)*

*Troparion:*
Truly the glory of Thy martyrs has surpassed every bound of praises, O Lover of mankind; but having accepted the praise from faith according to our strength, send down richly in return, O Master, a recompense.

*Troparion:*
From a thorny root sprang forth a most beautiful rose, perfuming the Church of Christ, and having reddened herself with the purple of suffering by blood, O glorious Barbara, whom we worthily hymn.

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
Having traversed the water as dry land, and escaped Egyptian evil, the Israelite cried: "Let us sing unto our Deliverer and God!"

*Troparion:*
Having forsaken the mire-filled water of impiety, thou didst hasten unto the purifying water, O Barbara; do thou also purify our hearts, purely to sing of thy sufferings.

*Troparion:*
Having purified thyself with the water of baptism, O great-martyr, thou didst cleave unto the pure Bridegroom Christ; unto Whom do thou also purely unite us through thy prayers.

*Glory:*
Emulating Him Who drowned the crafty dragon in the water, by thy suffering blood thou didst submerge the enemy's power, O passion-bearer; pray the Savior that we also may be delivered from him.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Grant me, O all-pure one, the purifying water of tears, and having cleansed me from the mire of lawlessness, present me pure, O Bearer of God, unto Thy Son and God.

*Ode 3, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
The desert has blossomed as a lily, O Lord, the barren church of the nations, by Thy coming, wherein my heart has been established.

*Troparion:*
Wounded by Thy love as by a bridegroom's, O Master, with irrepressible longing the passion-bearer Barbara hated all her paternal godlessness.

*Troparion:*
Neither beauty of pleasure, nor flower of comeliness, nor wealth, nor youthful pleasures delighted thee, O glorious Barbara, who wast betrothed unto Christ, O good virgin.

*Troparion:*
In the face of perfect contests, nothing proved a hindrance to womanly weakness, nor youth of age, O Christ; for she was strengthened by Thy invisible power.

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 3. Heirmos:*
Celestial vault's supreme Creator, O Lord, and Builder of the Church, do Thou establish me in Thy love, O summit of desires, confirmation of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*
Beholding the ornament of the celestial vault, O wise virgin Barbara, thou didst recognize the supreme Creator, and having loved Him, thou didst choose to serve Him: do thou also establish us in His love through thy prayers.

*Troparion:*
Depicting the all-radiant essence of the celestial Creator in Trinity, thou didst command three windows to be fashioned in thy father's bathhouse, teaching us to worship the Triune Godhead; vouchsafe that we also may worship Him eternally.[^751]

*Glory:*
Longing for the celestial bridal chamber, thou didst preserve the beauty of thy virginity for the heavenly Bridegroom, having despised the temporary: strengthen me also, O martyr, to serve Him in purity.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Having given birth to the celestial Creator, O all-hymned Theotokos, thou hast appeared more spacious than the heavens: having turned me away from the broad and ruinous path, guide me unto the heavenly path, I pray thee.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5. Podoben: "The beauty of virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства):*
In thy suffering thou didst astonish all, in that thou didst endure the scourgings, bonds, torments, and prisons of the tormentors, O most praised Barbara; therefore Christ gave thee a crown from heaven. Unto Whom having longed, thou didst zealously hasten, and He bestowed grace upon thee, O martyr, to pour forth healings unto men.

*Glory, and now, Theotokion:*
Thou wast the divine tabernacle of the Word, O only all-pure Mother and Virgin, who by purity didst surpass the angels: me, who beyond all have become dust, defiled by fleshly trespasses, do thou purify with the divine waters of thy prayers, granting great mercy, O pure one.

*Ode 4, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
Thou didst come from the Virgin, neither mediator nor angel, but Thyself, O Lord, incarnate, and didst save me, whole man; therefore I cry unto Thee: "Glory to Thy power, O Lord!"

*Troparion:*
Born of the Virgin, Thou didst grant unto maidens by deed of martyrdom to cast down the deceiver; therefore the fair-victorious Barbara mocked his pride.

*Troparion:*
The foremother, deceived of old by the wiles of the deceiver, was cast out from the delight of paradise; but Barbara, having mocked him, now abides in the heavenly bridal chamber.

*Troparion:*
By Thy Cross Thou didst destroy the power of death; therefore, not sparing her body, O Christ, the maiden Barbara most valiantly endured wounds with a steadfast mind.

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation; I understood Thy works, and I glorified Thy Godhead.

*Troparion:*
Having heard the heavens declaring the glory of God, O Barbara, Thou didst desire to glorify God upon earth: vouchsafe that we also may share in His glory.

*Troparion:*
When thy father heard thee confessing the supreme Father, he pursued thee unto slaughter; yet he could not tear truth away from the true God.

*Glory:*
Hearing the paternal threats and enduring torments from him, O Barbara, thou didst remain unshaken: direct me also to be unshaken in virtues.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Give ear, O all-pure Lady, unto my supplication, and deliver me from evil things, and vouchsafe that I may hear the voice of the blessed calling into the Kingdom.

*Ode 5, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
Thou becamest a Mediator between God and men, O Christ God; for through Thee, O Master, we have access from the night of ignorance unto Thy Father, the Source of Light.

*Troparion:*
Having commanded the bathhouse to be illumined with three windows, thou didst mystically portray baptism, O Barbara, the soul's radiant, shining cleansing by the light of the Trinity.

*Troparion:*
Kindled with zeal for the holy God, the all-praised Barbara spat upon the defiled faces of the falsely named gods, as the maiden mocked the external ruler of the world.

*Troparion:*
Fleeing the fury of thy cruel father, O Barbara, the mountain straightway cleft asunder and received thee, even as of old it did the ever-memorable protomartyr Thecla, when Christ worked wonders.[^752]

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Illumine us with Thy commandments, O Lord, and with Thy high arm grant Thy peace unto us, O only Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*
Having illumined thy lamp with faith, O martyr, thou didst surrender thy body to be burned with torches, desiring to enter brightly into the heavenly bridal chamber.

*Troparion:*
People darkened by unbelief were illumined with faith, beholding thee, O martyr, valiantly enduring burnings, beatings, and lacerations.

*Glory:*
Illumine me, O Barbara, who am darkened by the mist of sin and make me to be shone upon by the never-waning light, O radiant virgin, I pray thee.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Thou didst illumine darkened nature, O Theotokos, having given birth to the true Light; therefore, having illumined me with light, deliver me from dark enemies.

*Ode 6, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
Held fast in the depth of sins, O Savior, and tempest-tossed in the abyss of life, yet even as Thou didst Jonah from the beast, lead me also up from passions, and save me.

*Troparion:*
As Thou didst promise, O Savior, to grant wisdom in godly confidence unto those standing for Thy sake before the tribunal of the tormentors, with this wisdom the martyr Barbara was filled.

*Troparion:*
Thou didst refute the deceit of the godless by God-wise word, O glorious Barbara, and by the endurance of deeds thou didst strangle the vain thoughts of the tormentors.

*Troparion:*
To be scraped unsparingly with wounds, and her ribs to be severely rubbed with haircloth for Christ's sake, the blameless passion-bearer surrendered her body.

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 6. Heirmos:*
I will pour out my prayer unto the Lord, and unto Him will I declare my afflictions; for my soul is filled with evils, and my life has drawn near unto Hades, and I pray like Jonah: "Lead me up from corruption, O my God!"

*Troparion:*
Christ heard thy martyr's prayer, O Barbara: He visited thee shut up in prison, granted wholeness unto thy wounds, and strengthened thee for the contest; do thou also heal my sinful wounds, O martyr, I pray thee.

*Troparion:*
By thy prayer and steadfast martyr's endurance, and by marvelous healing, thou didst draw Juliana away from wickedness to suffer with thee for Christ; draw me also unto heavenly thoughts, O passion-bearer.

*Glory:*
Everyone who brings prayer with faith unto thee, O martyr, and hastens unto thy holy relics, quickly receives healing of soulful and bodily passions; grant this richly unto us also, we pray.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Receive our supplication, O all-pure one, and by thy all-powerful prayer pray the Lover of mankind, God Whom thou didst bear, that we may be delivered from enemies visible and invisible, from sins, and from every torment.

*Kontakion, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up" (Вознесы́йсѧ):*
Having followed God piously hymned in Trinity, O passion-bearer, thou didst trample down the idol shrines; and having contested in the midst of the arena O Barbara, thou wast not terrified by the threats of the tormentors, O manly-minded one, ever singing with a loud voice: "I venerate the Trinity, the one Godhead!"

*Ikos:*
Assembling, let us worthily honor Barbara, who was betrothed unto Christ through martyrdom; that, having been delivered through her prayers from soul-corrupting pestilence, destruction, earthquake, and ruin,[^753] we may pass our life in peace, being vouchsafed with all the saints who from the ages have well-pleased God to walk in the light, and to sing with them: "Thou hast made marvelous Thy mercies, O Savior, unto all men who confess in faith: We venerate the Trinity, the one Godhead!"

*Ode 7, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
The God-opposing decree of the lawless tormentor raised a high flame; but Christ spread spiritual dew over the pious children, He Who is blessed and supremely glorified.

*Troparion:*
The tormentor who furiously raged to destroy the earth and consume the sea, lies at the end like a plaything beneath the feet of the maiden Barbara; for Christ trampled him down and bound him like a petty bird.

*Troparion:*
With unbearable wounds, while thy whole body was scraped and reddened with drops of blood, thou didst endure being burned with torches upon thy ribs, O all-praised martyr, thanking Christ, O glorious Barbara.

*Troparion:*
O inhuman and unmerciful cruelty of the tormentors, and surpassing godlessness! For as in a butchery they cruelly cut off the martyr's breasts with swords,[^754] while she fixed her mind upon Christ the Contender.

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 7. Heirmos:*
The Hebrew children boldly trampled upon the flame in the furnace, and changing the fire into dew, they cried: "Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages!"

*Troparion:*
The angel who preserved the Hebrew children unburnt from the fire, also protected thee from shame, O Barbara, as thou wast led naked, clothing thee with a raiment from on high, who sangest: "Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages!"

*Troparion:*
Counting torments as children's play, thou didst not spare thy body from being scraped and acutely rubbed for Christ's sake, O great-martyr, singing: "Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages!"

*Glory:*
The Hebrew children I also emulate, O Barbara: I praise Christ Who gave thee strength. Do thou strengthen me unto the contest of virtues, and vouchsafe that I may be clothed in the raiment of incorruption unto the ages.

*And now, Theotokion:*
At thy childbearing, who remained a virgin even after childbirth, I marvel, O Theotokos. Make marvelous therefore upon me also the mercy of thy Son, that, having found mercy through thee, I may sing: "Blessed is God Who blessed thee as His Mother unto the ages!"

*Ode 8, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
Of old, the fiery furnace in Babylon divided its operation by the divine command, burning the Chaldeans, but bedewing the faithful as they sang: "Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!"

*Troparion:*
Christ appeared unto thee in unapproachable light, O Barbara, when thou wast shut up in prison, commanding thee to be of good cheer, healing thy wounds, and granting joy; therefore thou wast winged with the desire for thy Bridegroom.

*Troparion:*
Clearly was Thy prophecy shown fulfilled, O Christ; for a father betrays his child unto death,[^755] and the wretched parent himself became the perpetrator of the slaughter of Thy martyr; therefore he was consumed by fire from heaven.

*Troparion:*
A radiant angel clothed thee, venerable Barbara, with light-bearing raiment when thou wast stripped naked for Christ's sake, as a bride going through passions; for thou didst strip off garments, O martyr, having received divine transformation.

*Second Canon of the Saint, Tone 8. Ode 8. Heirmos:*
Having become conquerors over the enemy and the flame by grace, the children, striving zealously for the commandment, sang: "Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!"

*Troparion:*
The tormentors, striving to conquer thy invincible faith, unsparingly cut off thy breasts; but thou, steadfastly enduring, didst nourish the bystanders with the milk of godly teaching, as they blessed Christ unto the ages.

*Troparion:*
By thy courage, O martyr Barbara, thou didst conquer all the subtle wiles of the tormentors: pray then the Master Christ, we pray thee, to grant victory over enemies unto all the faithful who sing: "Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!"

*Glory:*
Thou hast appeared as conqueress of thy cruel father, O martyr; for he was cast down by heavenly fire into hellish fire, while thou didst ascend into heaven: intercede that this may be granted unto me also, delivering me from the fire unto the ages.

*And now, Theotokion:*
Conquer, O all-pure one, the enemies visible and invisible who strive to cast us down, and deliver me from all passions, that I may celebrate and sing: "Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!"

*Ode 9, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
The Son of the unoriginate Father, God and Lord, having become incarnate of the Virgin, has appeared unto us, to illumine the darkened and gather the dispersed; therefore the all-hymned Theotokos do we magnify.

*Troparion:*
When thou wast about to receive a glorious end of thy martyrdom by the sword, O Barbara, being vouchsafed martyr's crowns together with Juliana, thou didst hear a divine voice fulfilling your petitions.

*Troparion:*

*Resurrectional Theotokia for the Eight Tones.*

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of the whole world, who sprang forth from mankind and bore the Master, the heavenly door, the song of the bodiless and the adornment of the faithful! For she appeared as heaven and temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the barrier of enmity, brought in peace and opened the Kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the confirmation of faith, we have the Lord born of her as our Defender. Take courage, therefore, take courage, O people of God, for He will conquer the enemies, as He is all-powerful.[^765]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Behold, Isaiah's prophecy is fulfilled: for thou, O Virgin, didst give birth, and after childbirth didst remain even as before childbirth, for God was He Who was born; therefore nature also was renewed.[^766] But O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple; but as thou bearest the Merciful One in thy hands, have compassion on thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved.[^767]

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
The shadow of the law passed away when grace arrived; for even as the bush burned yet was not consumed, so as a virgin thou didst give birth, and a virgin thou didst remain. In place of the pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness shone forth; in place of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls.[^768]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O new wonder, surpassing all ancient wonders! For who ever knew a mother who gave birth without a man, and who carried in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? It is the divine will of Him Who was born. Whom as an Infant having borne in thy hands, O all-pure one, and possessing maternal boldness toward Him, cease not praying for those who honor thee, to have mercy and to save our souls.[^769]

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
How can we not marvel at thy God-man childbirth, O all-pure one? For without receiving trial of man, O all-blameless one, thou didst bear without a father a Son in the flesh, Who before the ages was begotten of the Father without a mother, having undergone no change, nor confusion, nor division[^770] but preserved whole the properties of each nature. Therefore, O Lady Virgin Mother, pray Him to save the souls of those who in orthodox fashion confess thee to be the Mother of God.[^771]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Without seed, of the divine Spirit, and by the Father's will, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who before the ages was of the Father without a mother; and for our sake, having come from thee without a father, thou didst bear Him in the flesh and didst nourish the Infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray, that our souls may be delivered from perils.[^772]

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
David the ancestor of God, the prophet, through song proclaimed concerning thee unto Him Who hath done great things for thee: The Queen stood at Thy right hand.[^773] For God, Who was pleased without a father to become incarnate from thee, showed thee forth as Mother and Mediatress of life, that He might renew His own image, which was corrupted by passions; and having found the sheep that went astray in the mountains, taking it upon His shoulders, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His own will might unite it with the heavenly powers, and save the world, O Theotokos, Christ Who hath great and rich mercy.

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Look down upon the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, quenching the fierce uprisings against us, releasing us from every affliction. For thee alone have we as a firm and sure confirmation, and we have acquired thy protection, that we who call upon thee may not be put to shame, O Theotokos. Hasten to the supplication of those who cry faithfully unto thee: Rejoice, O Theotokos, help of all, joy and protection, and the salvation of our souls![^774]

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
In the Red Sea, an image of the bride that knew not wedlock was inscribed of old. There Moses was the divider of the water; and here Gabriel was the minister of the wonder. Then Israel traversed the deep without being wetted; and now the Virgin gave birth unto Christ without seed. The sea after the passage of Israel remained impassable; the immaculate one after the childbirth of Emmanuel remained incorrupt. O God Who art and Who wast before, Who didst appear as man: have mercy on us![^775]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Thou art temple and gate, palace and throne of the King, O all-honored Virgin, through whom my Deliverer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of righteousness, desiring to enlighten that which He fashioned according to His own image with His own hand. Therefore, O all-hymned one, as thou hast acquired maternal boldness toward Him, cease not[^776] cease not to pray that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Who will not bless thee, O all-pure Virgin? Or who will not sing of thy childbirth free of defilement? For the only-begotten Son, Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the Same came forth from thee, pure one, having been ineffably incarnate; being God by nature, and having become man by nature for our sake; not divided into two persons, but known in two natures without confusion. Him do thou beseech, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may find mercy.[^777]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
My Maker and Deliverer, O all-pure one, Christ the Lord, having come forth from thy womb, clothed in me, delivered Adam from the first curse. Therefore unto thee, O all-pure one, as the Mother of God and Virgin in truth, we cry unceasingly with the angelic salutation: Rejoice, O Lady; rejoice, O Theotokos: protection, shelter, and the salvation of our souls![^778]

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
A Mother indeed wast thou known to be beyond nature, O Theotokos, and thou didst remain a virgin beyond word and understanding; and the wonder of thy childbirth no tongue is able to tell. For exceeding glorious was thy conception, O pure one; incomprehensible is the manner of thy childbirth; for where God wills, the order of nature is overcome. Therefore, knowing thee as the Mother of God, all of us pray thee earnestly: pray that our souls may be saved.[^779]

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Beneath thy shelter, O Theotokos, all we earthborn flee, crying unto thee: O our hope, deliver us from immeasurable transgressions, and save our souls.[^780]

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
The King of heaven, for His love of mankind, appeared on earth and lived with men; for having taken flesh from the pure Virgin, and having come forth from her with that which He assumed, one Son is He, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis. *At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O unwedded Virgin, who didst ineffably conceive God in the flesh, Mother of God Most High: accept the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, granting unto all the cleansing of transgressions. Now receiving our entreaties, pray that we all may be saved.[^782]
