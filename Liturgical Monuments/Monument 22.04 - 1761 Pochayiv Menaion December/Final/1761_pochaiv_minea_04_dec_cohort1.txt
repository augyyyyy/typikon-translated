THE BOOK OF THE MENAION
Month of December

To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; under the reign of the Great King Augustus the Third;[^1] by the blessing of His Grace, Lord Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytskyi,[^2] Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the volition of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk Hypatius Bilynskyi,[^3] Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; through the zeal and at the expense of the monks of the same Order; in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv, printed with types: Published. In the year from the Creation of the world according to the Greek chronographs 7269,[^4] and of the Nativity of Christ 1761.

# MONTH OF DECEMBER
*Having 31 days.*

*December 1: Holy Prophet Nahum:*

*Alleluia, or Troparion: Stichera, Tone 3.*[^5]
*Tone 3, Podoben: "Great is the power of Thy Cross, O Lord" (Ве́лїя крⷭ҇та́ твоегѡ̀):*

The grace of the Holy Spirit dwelt in thee, O prophet of God, and by His ray made thee a most radiant lamp; and through thee He proclaims things to come unto Nineveh the great.

A light-bearing cloud wast thou revealed to be, O Nahum, prophet of God, pouring forth the shower of the knowledge of God; and thou didst give to drink the potion of the divine judgment[^6] unto the city of Nineveh, which had forthwith fallen sick with impiety.

Having become a partaker of incorruptible blessing, O Nahum, prophet of God, and of ineffable glory, unutterable joy, and divine sweetness: pray unto the Master of all on our behalf, O God-inspired one.

*Other Stichera to the Theotokos, same Tone and Podoben.*
*(If Alleluia is sung, the Stichera to the Theotokos are sung first, then those of the Saint.)*

Wounded am I by the weapon of the serpent, and scarcely alive, O Maiden; lying upon the bed of despair, heal me swiftly by the medicine of thy maternal prayer, and make me a servant of Thy Son and God.

Knowing my bodily weakness and the affliction of my soul, and the malice of my heart: vouchsafe me divine visitation, O all-immaculate Virgin; and save me, I pray thee, by thy fervent prayer.

I have surpassed all men in sins, O gracious Lady; yet having cleansed the multitude of these, O pure Virgin Mother, vouchsafe me on account of thy maternal supplication to obtain forgiveness at the future judgment of Thy Son and God.

*At the Aposticha, the Octoechos.*

*Troparion, Tone 2:*

Celebrating the memory of Thy prophet Nahum, O Lord, therefore we pray Thee: Save our souls.

*At Matins, the Canon: A composition of Theophanes.[^7] Tone 5.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

The horse and his rider into the Red Sea, He Who crushes warfare with a high arm, Christ shook out; but Israel He saved as they sang a song of victory.

Having been anointed with the Holy Spirit of God, O God-receiver, and enlightened by His grace: honoring therefore the divine foresight of thy mind, we call thee blessed, O most excellent one.

The grace of the Spirit having resounded from on high upon thy soul, O prophet, worthily inspired the prophetic operation, and fashioned thy God-inspired tongue, O worthy of all wonder.

Thou wast revealed as an instrument struck by the plectrum of the Spirit, chanting of God the Jealous One, and proclaiming unto all the impartiality of the divine judgment, O much-lauded one.

*Theotokion:*

O most blessed and all-pure Virgin, save from tribulations those who with faith and love sing of thee as the honorable Mother of God, loosening untamed savagery.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and didst suspend what gravitates without restraint: establish Thy Church upon the immovable rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, Who alone art good and the Lover of mankind.

By participation in the Life-Creating and Divine Spirit, thou wast most God-like in soul; therefore by divine beckoning thou wast prepared to foretell distant things openly unto all, O God-inspired one.

Thou didst foresee the judgment of vengeance upon the enemies, O blessed and most God-seeing one, when the impious despised the forbearance and mercy of God...

*Theotokion:*

He Who of old fashioned all things out of nothingness passes forth from thy holy womb, having become man, O pure Virgin, through His exceedingly great love for mankind, the Lover of mankind, that He might deliver mankind.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5, Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́лное сло́во):*

Having acquired a mind unmingled with earthly figures, O glorious one, thou didst become a most pure receptacle of the Divine Spirit, O Nahum, receiving His radiance brightly and shining forth unto all; therefore we pray thee: Intercede for the peace of the world.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Beseech God and Lord Who was incarnate from thee for the sake of us who have perished through transgressions, O pure one, fervently to show mercy upon us, and to turn away His wrath and anger from those who with faith honor and unceasingly praise His power and dominion.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Beholding Thee, O Christ, hanging voluntarily upon the Cross in the midst of thieves, Thy Mother, maternal sorrow tearing at her heart, cried: O sinless Son, how art Thou unjustly nailed to the Cross like an evildoer, desiring to quicken the human race as the Compassionate One?

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Perceiving Thy divine emptying clairvoyantly, O Christ, Habakkuk cried out to Thee with trembling: For the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed art Thou come.[^8]

Illumined by the dawn of the Spirit, thou didst foretell unto the Ninevites the threat of the decree, revealing the inescapable power of God, O God-manifested one.

Thou dost proclaim the righteous judgment of the Creator, dire total ruin unto the hostile people, as thou didst utter in prophecies, O Nahum, prophet of God, all-blessed one.

Adorned with humility of mind, the soul of Thy prophet was supernaturally lifted up unto the vision of Thee, O Good One, and unto the brightness of the divine contemplation.

*Theotokion:*

O Mother and Sovereign Lady of creation, having given birth to the King of all, only all-immaculate one: show me free from the tyranny of passions through thy prayers.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou Who deckest Thyself with light as with a garment, unto Thee I rise early and unto Thee I cry: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as the only Compassionate One.

To acquire a blameless and pure life through the operation and illumination...

She who was revealed as the temple of divine grace, O ye faithful, and became a most pure receptacle beyond understanding: let us hymn her divine manifestation.

Having received the unsetting beam of the Divine Spirit and acquired a divine mind, he instructs the pious unto hymnody.

*Theotokion:*

The spiritual powers and all generations of men glorify thee as the Mother of God, O blessed, all-immaculate one.

*Kontakion, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou hast appeared today" (Яви́лся є҆сѝ):*

Enlightened by the Spirit, thy pure heart became a receptacle of most radiant prophecy; for thou seest things far off as though they were present. Therefore we honor thee, O blessed and glorious prophet Nahum.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The supremely exalted Lord of the fathers quenched the flame and bedewed the children, as with one accord they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God.

Having the most God-beholding prophet as the leader of our choirs, we celebrate honorably in divine manner, singing: Blessed art Thou, O God.

Knowing thee as a Spirit-moved instrument and a God-inspired pipe, we call upon thee as our defender, singing in hymns: Blessed art Thou, O God.

The Lord crowned thee, O marvelous prophet, and gave thee grace to foretell things to come; unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God.

*Theotokion:*

The prophet beheld thee, O all-pure Virgin, as the impassable gate through which He alone passed; unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God.

Thee we bring unto God as an intercessor for peace, O blessed one; pray fervently, O prophet, as having boldness, to give unto the faithful beautifully abiding eternal deliverance.

*Theotokion:*

*December 2: Holy Prophet Habakkuk:*

*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Prophet, Tone 4.*[^9]
*Tone 4, Podoben: "As one valiant among martyrs" (Я́кѡ до́бля):*

The marvelous Habakkuk, having received the beam of the Spirit, became wholly divine; and beholding the impiety of judges and unrighteous judgment, he is indignant, showing forth the uprightness of Christ and Master, in God-loving manner and with warm intent, as one inflamed with comfort.

Standing upon his divine watch, the venerable Habakkuk...

[Fol. 3v]
...heard the ineffable mystery of Thy coming unto us, O Christ; and his preaching prophesies most clearly, foreseeing also the most wise Apostles as horses stirring up the sea of multi-tribal nations.

Rejoicing in the Lord, God Thy Savior, O glorious God-proclaimer, making glad; and receiving the beam of the radiance which is there, and spiritually enlightened by divine light: from afflictions and tribulations deliver by Thy prayers those who with faith celebrate Thy most festive memory.

*Other Stichera to the Theotokos, same Tone and Podoben.*
*(If Alleluia is sung, these Stichera are sung first.)*

With showers of the Holy Spirit, O all-pure one, bedew my mind, who didst bear Christ, the boundless drop that washed away the iniquities of earthborn men by His mercies; dry up the fountain of my passions, and vouchsafe me the torrent of delight by thy prayer free from grief.

Cast down unto the earth, I am deadened by sins, by passions of soul and body; therefore am I covered by the darkness of despair; but raise me up now, guiding me unto the incorruptible life, unto the homeland on high, where is the pure voice of those who feast, and where is the light of Thy countenance, O Christ.

Thou who didst contain in thy womb the uncontainable God, Who became man in His love for mankind, and took our mixture from thee, and manifestly deified it: despise me not, O all-pure one, who am now in sorrow, but have compassion swiftly, and free me from every enmity and harm of the evil one.

*Glory... Both now... same Tone and Podoben:*

Myriad times, O all-pure one, did I promise to make repentance for my sins, but my unseemly evil habit does not leave me. Therefore do I cry unto thee, and fervently pray: Deliver me, O gracious Lady, from such torment, guiding me unto better things and those near unto salvation.

*Stavrotheotokion, same Podoben:*

When the Ewe-lamb who bare Thee beheld Thee upon the Wood, the Lamb and Shepherd, she wept and cried out to Thee in maternal sorrow: O Son greatly desired, how art Thou suspended upon the wood of the Cross, O Long-Suffering One? How are Thy hands and Thy feet nailed by the lawless, O Word, and Thou hast poured out Thy blood, O Master?

*Troparion, Tone 2:*

Celebrating the memory of Thy prophet Habakkuk, O Lord, therefore we pray Thee: Save our souls.

*Canon of the Prophet.*
*Whose acrostic is: I sing of thee, O blessed Prophet Habakkuk.[^10] A composition of Theophanes. Tone 4.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

I will open my mouth unto Thee, O Lord my God, for Thou didst lead Thy people out of Egyptian bondage, and didst cover the chariots of Pharaoh and his power.

Make me wise, O Lord my God, to hymn Thine inspired prophet Habakkuk, and by Thy grace, O Good One, enlighten my heart.

How long, O Lord, the prophet cries, shall I cry unto Thee and Thou wilt not hear?[^11] Why hast Thou shown me the impiety of judges?

Unto those who piously celebrate thy honorable memory, O prophet, deliver them from tribulations and cruel circumstances by thy supplications.

*Theotokion:*

The Holy God appeared when the time drew nigh, having become man from thee, O Theotokos, that He might save man.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

The bow of the mighty has become weak, and the powerless have girded themselves with strength; therefore my heart is established in the Lord.

Uplifted by gazing upon God, and enlightened by beams of light from Him, thou didst foresee the prefiguration of things to come, O God-manifested one.

Every virtue didst thou embrace, and every malice from thy mind didst thou hate; and justly didst thou turn away with loathing from the lawless, O all-blessed one.

By the utterance of the Holy Spirit, Thou as one pure wast most clearly entrusted to prophesy the fulfillment of things to come, O worthy of all wonder.

*Theotokion:*

A mountain overshadowed with virtues wast thou revealed to be, O all-honorable one, from which the Master came in the form of a servant, to deliver mankind from bondage.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йся):*

Thou didst stand upon thy divine watch, O blessed one, and didst perceive the divine coming with prophetic eyes, O God-inspired one. Therefore didst thou cry aloud, O Habakkuk, with fear: O Lord, I have heard of Thy fearful coming, and I hymn Thee, Who didst desire to bear earthly flesh from the Virgin.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

After God, unto thy divine protection do I, the humble one, flee, O Theotokos, and falling down I pray...

*Stavrotheotokion:*

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Proclaiming Thine appearance on earth, O Christ God, and Thy coming, the prophet cried out with gladness: Glory to Thy power, O Lord.

Light-like through virtue wast Thou revealed to be, O God-inspired one, and Thou didst herald the unwaning light, unto Whom we now cry with faith: Glory to Thy power, O Lord.

The all-radiant grace of the Spirit, having abundantly taken up dwelling in thee, O all-wise one, perfected thee as a prophet, proclaiming the Savior unto all.

Having received the report of the Almighty, thou didst reverently fear, O marvelous one; and understanding His works, thou wast filled with awe.

*Theotokion:*

The ark of testimony portrays thee, O Virgin, who didst truly receive the Bread of Life, O pure one, the Word co-eternal with the Father.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou Who hast made the light to dawn and enlightened the morning, and shown forth the day: Glory to Thee, glory to Thee, O Jesus, Son of God.

Now the venerable Church sees the Sun lifted up upon the Wood, even as thou didst foretell, O most sacred Habakkuk.

Having winged thy mind, thou didst stand upon thy watch, O most rich one, and didst behold the coming of the Lord.

Loudly didst thou cry, O wise one: I will rejoice and be glad in God my Savior, O all-blessed Habakkuk.

*Theotokion:*

Weighed down by the bonds of my many transgressions, unto thee do I flee: Save me, O Sovereign Lady Theotokos, praise of the faithful.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Prophet Jonah cried aloud, prefiguring the three-day burial, praying in the sea-monster: Deliver me from corruption, O Jesus, King of hosts.

God came, having become incarnate, even as thou didst foretell, enlightened brightly by Him, O most blessed Habakkuk, and He illuminated the world with light.

Illumine, O God, the souls of those who hymn Thee, by the prayers of Habakkuk...
