[Binding: dark embossed cloth cover; in the center a relief four-pointed cross with foliate vignettes, enclosed within a double linear and ornamental border. Leaf without printed text.]

*Divine Services of the Nativity, Circumcision of the Lord, and Theophany.*

[Frontispiece: full-page woodcut engraving depicting the Nativity of Christ. The Divine Infant Jesus lies swaddled in a manger within radiant beams of light; the Most Pure Virgin Mary kneels in prayer, righteous Joseph bows in reverence; shepherds with a lamb offer adoration; from above shines the Star of Bethlehem surrounded by an angelic host. Leaf without printed text.]

PUBLICATION OF BOOKS OF THE ORDER OF ST. BASIL THE GREAT
Book XXIV.
___

DIVINE SERVICES

OF THE NATIVITY, CIRCUMCISION OF OUR LORD JESUS CHRIST,[^1]

AND

THEOPHANY

[Vignette: Christ Emmanuel / An angel of the Lord within a circular medallion framed by foliate scrollwork]

Zhovkva
Press of the Monks of the Order of St. Basil the Great
1905.[^2]

No. 79

It is permitted to be printed.[^3]

Lviv, the 21st day of June 1905.

+ Andrey
Metropolitan
