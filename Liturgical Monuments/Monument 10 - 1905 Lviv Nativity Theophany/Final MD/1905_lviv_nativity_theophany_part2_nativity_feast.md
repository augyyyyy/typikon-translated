# PART II: THE FEAST OF THE NATIVITY OF OUR LORD JESUS CHRIST (GREAT COMPLINE, MATINS, AND DIVINE LITURGY)

## Great Compline on the Nativity of Christ[^105]

**Priest:** Blessed is our God always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.
**Choir:** Amen. Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. O Heavenly King... Trisagion, and after the Our Father: Come, let us worship... and we begin:
*Psalm 4:*
When I called upon Thee, the God of my righteousness heard me; in tribulation Thou hast enlarged me.
Have mercy on me, and hear my prayer.
O ye sons of men, how long will ye be slow of heart? Why love ye vanity, and seek after falsehood?
Know ye also that the Lord has made marvelous His holy one; the Lord will hear me when I call unto Him.
Be ye angry, and sin not; what ye say in your hearts, repent thereof upon your beds.
Offer the sacrifice of righteousness, and hope in the Lord; many say: Who will show us good things?
The light of Thy countenance is signed upon us, O Lord; Thou hast put gladness into my heart.
By the fruit of their wheat, wine, and oil have they been multiplied.
In peace, together, will I sleep and take my rest.
For Thou, Lord, only hast made me to dwell in hope.

*Psalm 6:*[^106]
O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger, nor chasten me in Thy wrath.
Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak; heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled.
And my soul is troubled exceedingly; and Thou, O Lord, how long?
Turn Thee, O Lord, deliver my soul; save me for Thy mercy's sake.
For in death there is no one who remembers Thee; and in Hades who will give thanks unto Thee?
I have laboured in my groaning; every night will I wash my bed, I will water my couch with my tears.
Mine eye is troubled through anger; I have grown old among all mine enemies.
Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity, for the Lord has heard the voice of my weeping.
The Lord has heard my supplication; the Lord has accepted my prayer.
Let all mine enemies be ashamed and troubled exceedingly; let them turn back and be ashamed very quickly.

*Psalm 12:*[^107]
How long, O Lord, wilt Thou forget me unto the end? How long wilt Thou turn away Thy face from me?
How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrow in my heart day and night?
How long shall mine enemy be exalted over me? Look upon me and hear me, O Lord my God.
Enlighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death; lest mine enemy say: I have prevailed against him.

Those that afflict me will rejoice if I am moved; but I have hoped in Thy mercy.
My heart shall rejoice in Thy salvation; I will sing unto the Lord Who has dealt bountifully with me, and I will chant unto the name of the Lord Most High.
*Glory... Both now... Alleluia (thrice). Lord, have mercy (thrice).*
*Glory... Both now... Psalm 24:*[^108]
Unto Thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul; O my God, in Thee have I hoped, let me not be put to shame forever.
Neither let mine enemies laugh me to scorn; for none of them that wait on Thee shall be put to shame.
Let them be ashamed that do iniquity in vain.
Show me Thy ways, O Lord, and teach me Thy paths.
Lead me in Thy truth, and teach me, for Thou art God my Savior, and on Thee have I waited all the day.
Remember Thy compassions, O Lord, and Thy mercies, for they are from everlasting.
The sins of my youth and of mine ignorance remember not.
According to Thy mercy remember Thou me, for the sake of Thy goodness.
Good and upright is the Lord; therefore will He set a law for them that sin in the way.
The meek will He guide in judgment; the meek will He teach His ways.
All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth, unto them that seek His covenant and His testimonies.
For Thy name's sake, O Lord, pardon also my sin, for it is great.

Who is the man that fears the Lord? He will set a law for him in the way which He has chosen.
His soul shall dwell among good things, and his seed shall inherit the earth.
The Lord is the strength of them that fear Him, and His covenant will He show them.
Mine eyes are ever toward the Lord, for He will pluck my feet out of the snare.
Look upon me and have mercy on me, for I am an only child and poor.
The afflictions of my heart are multiplied; bring me out of my distresses.
Behold my lowliness and my toil, and forgive all my sins.
Behold mine enemies, for they are multiplied, and have hated me with an unjust hatred.
Keep my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I have hoped in Thee.
The innocent and the upright have cleaved unto me, for I have waited on Thee, O Lord.
Deliver Israel, O God, out of all his afflictions.
*Psalm 30:*[^109]
In Thee, O Lord, have I hoped, let me not be put to shame forever; in Thy righteousness deliver me and rescue me.
Bow down Thine ear unto me, make haste to deliver me.
Be Thou unto me a God of defense, and a house of refuge to save me.
For Thou art my strength and my refuge, and for Thy name's sake wilt Thou guide me and nourish me.

Thou wilt bring me out of this snare which they have hidden for me, for Thou art my defender, O Lord.
Into Thy hands will I commit my spirit; Thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of truth.
Thou hast hated them that regard vain vanities in vain.
But I have hoped in the Lord; I will be glad and rejoice in Thy mercy.
For Thou hast looked upon my lowliness; Thou hast saved my soul out of distresses.
And Thou hast not shut me up into the hands of the enemy; Thou hast set my feet in a spacious place.
Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am in trouble; mine eye is troubled with anger, yea, my soul and my belly.
For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing;
My strength has failed in poverty, and my bones are troubled.
I was made a reproach among all mine enemies, and especially among my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintances;
They that saw me without fled from me; I was forgotten as a dead man out of mind.
I became like a vessel that is lost; for I heard the slander of many that dwell round about.
When they were assembled together against me, they took counsel to take away my life.
But I have hoped in Thee, O Lord; I said: Thou art my God; in Thy hands is my lot.
Deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.

Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant; save me in Thy mercy; O Lord, let me not be put to shame, for I have called upon Thee.
Let the ungodly be ashamed, and let them go down into Hades; let the deceitful lips be put to silence,
Which speak iniquity against the righteous, with pride and contempt.
How great is the multitude of Thy goodness, O Lord, which Thou hast hidden for them that fear Thee!
Which Thou hast wrought for them that hope in Thee, before the sons of men!
Thou shalt hide them in the secret of Thy countenance, from the turmoil of man.
Thou shalt shelter them in a tabernacle from the contradiction of tongues.
Blessed is the Lord, for He has made marvelous His mercy in a fortified city.
As for me, I said in mine ecstasy: I am cast away from the countenance of Thine eyes.
Therefore hast Thou heard the voice of my prayer, when I cried unto Thee.
Love the Lord, all ye His saints; for the Lord searches out truth, and repays exceedingly them that act with pride.
Be of good courage, and let your heart be strengthened, all ye that hope in the Lord.

*Psalm 101:*[^122]
Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come unto Thee.
Turn not away Thy face from me; in the day when I am in trouble, incline Thine ear unto me.
In the day when I call upon Thee, quickly hear me.
For my days have vanished like smoke, and my bones are dried up like firewood.
I am stricken like grass, and my heart is withered, for I forgot to eat my bread.
By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bone has cleaved to my flesh.
I am become like a pelican of the wilderness; I am become like an owl in a ruined house.
I have watched, and am become like a solitary bird upon the roof.
All the day long mine enemies reproached me, and they that praised me swore against me.
For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping.

Because of the face of Thine anger and Thy wrath; for having lifted me up, Thou hast cast me down.
My days have declined like a shadow, and I am withered like grass.
But Thou, O Lord, abidest forever, and Thy memorial unto generation and generation.
Thou shalt arise and have compassion upon Sion, for it is time to have compassion upon her, for the time is come.
For Thy servants took pleasure in her stones, and shall have pity on her dust.
And the nations shall fear the name of the Lord, and all the kings of the earth Thy glory.
For the Lord shall build up Sion, and He shall appear in His glory.
He has regarded the prayer of the humble, and has not despised their supplication.
Let this be written for another generation, and the people that is being created shall praise the Lord.
For He has looked down from His holy height; the Lord from heaven has looked upon the earth,
To hear the groaning of the fettered, to release the children of the slain,
To declare the name of the Lord in Sion, and His praise in Jerusalem,
When the peoples are gathered together, and the kings, to serve the Lord.
He answered Him in the way of His strength: Declare unto me the shortness of my days.
Take me not away in the midst of my days; Thy years are unto generation of generations.
In the beginning, Thou, Lord, didst lay the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the works of Thy hands.

They shall perish, but Thou abidest; and all of them shall wax old like a garment.
And as a vesture shalt Thou fold them, and they shall be changed; but Thou art the same, and Thy years shall not fail.
The children of Thy servants shall dwell, and their seed shall be guided aright forever.
*Prayer of Manasses, King of Judah:*[^123]
O Lord Almighty, God of our fathers, of Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, and of their righteous seed; Who hast made heaven and earth with all the array thereof; Who hast bound the sea by the word of Thy commandment; Who hast shut up the abyss, and sealed it by Thy fearful and glorious name; Whom all things fear, and tremble before the face of Thy power; for the majesty of Thy glory cannot be borne, and the anger of Thy threatening toward sinners is intolerable; yet the mercy of Thy promise is immeasurable and unsearchable; for Thou art the Lord Most High, compassionate, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy, and repenting of the evils of men. Thou, O Lord, according to the multitude of Thy goodness, hast promised repentance and forgiveness unto them that have sinned against Thee, and in the multitude of Thy compassions hast appointed repentance unto sinners for salvation. Thou therefore, O Lord, God of hosts, hast not appointed repentance unto the righteous, unto Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, who have not sinned against Thee; but Thou hast appointed repentance unto me, a sinner; for I have sinned above the number of the sand of the sea. Multiplied are mine iniquities, O Lord, multiplied— —are mine iniquities, and I am not worthy to gaze and see the height of heaven for the multitude of mine unrighteousnesses. I am bowed down with many iron bonds, so that I cannot lift up my head, and there is no release for me; because I have provoked Thy wrath, and done evil before Thee, not doing Thy will, nor keeping Thy commandments. And now I bow the knees of my heart, entreating goodness from Thee. I have sinned, O Lord, I have sinned, and I acknowledge mine iniquities; but I ask, praying Thee: Remit unto me, O Lord, remit unto me, and destroy me not with mine iniquities, neither keep evils for me in hostility forever, nor condemn me into the nethermost parts of the earth; for Thou art God, the God of them that repent; and in me Thou wilt show all Thy goodness, for Thou wilt save me, unworthy though I be, according to Thy great mercy; and I will praise Thee continually all the days of my life; for all the power of the heavens praises Thee, and Thine is the glory unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Tone 1. Idiomela:*
Let heaven and earth prophetically rejoice today; let Angels and men spiritually make festival; for God has appeared in the flesh to those who sit in darkness and shadow. Born of the Virgin, the cave and the manger received Him; shepherds proclaim the wonder; Magi from the East bring gifts to Bethlehem; and we with unworthy lips, like Angels, offer praise to Him: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace; for the Expectation of the nations is come, and having arrived, He has saved us from the bondage of the enemy.
Heaven and earth are joined today, Christ being born! Today God is come upon earth, and man is gone up into [the] heavens; today He Who is invisible by nature is seen in the flesh for the sake of man. Therefore we also, glorifying, cry unto Him: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace; for Thy coming has granted [peace], O our Savior: glory to Thee!
Glory to God in the highest, I hear today in Bethlehem from the bodiless ones, on earth peace unto Him Who was well-pleased that it should be; now the Virgin is more spacious than the heavens; for light has shone forth upon them that were darkened, and has exalted the humble, who sing like the Angels: Glory to God in the highest!
Jesus, seeing him who was in the image and likeness corrupted through transgression, bowed the heavens and came down, and took up His abode in a virginal womb without change, that therein He might renew corrupted Adam, who cries: Glory to Thy manifestation, O my Deliverer and God!

Caught by love for the King of all, the Children reproached the impious, wicked-tongued raving of the tyrant that raged without measure; to them the raging fire yielded as they said to the Master: Blessed art Thou unto the ages!
Fiercely it consumed the servants, but preserved the youths with reverence, the all-devouring furnace heightened by a sevenfold burning; whom the flame crowned, the Lord bounteously granting dew for their piety's sake.
O Christ, Helper of men, possessing an ineffable incarnation, Thou hast put to shame the adversary's boast; bearing the riches of deification, Thou hast now taken form, therefore, through hope from on high, we have come into the nether darkness.
The sin which was wickedly and unrestrainedly exalted, raging impiously through the perversion of the world, which drew us down of old, Thou hast almighty overthrown;

and today from the snares Thou savest us, incarnate of Thine own will, O Benefactor.
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^182]
The dew-bearing furnace portrayed the type of a supernatural marvel; for it burned not the youths whom it had received, just as the fire of the Godhead burned not the Virgin's womb into which it entered; therefore, singing, let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!
The daughter of Babylon draws unto herself the captive children of David from Zion; but she sends the Magi youth bearing gifts, entreating the God-receiving daughter of David; therefore, singing, let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!
Instruments turned aside mournful songs, for the sons of Zion sang not in a strange land; but Christ, having shone forth in Bethlehem, looses all the deceit of Babylon and musical harmonies; therefore, singing, let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!
Babylon received the spoils of the kingdom of Zion and its captive wealth; but Christ draws unto Zion the treasures thereof, and its kings, the stargazers, guiding them by a star; therefore, singing, let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!
*Another Canon. Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^183]
The youths burned in the Old Testament prefigure without burning the womb of the Maiden who supernaturally gives birth sealed; grace, accomplishing both as a single miracle, rouses the people to song.
Having escaped the destruction of seeking deification through deceit, all creation unceasingly praises the Word Who emptied Himself, and in youthlike manner with trembling, in fear offers an inglorious praise, being corruptible, even though wisely enduring.
Thou comest turning the strayed unto the flower-bearing pasture from the desert hills, to quell the violent power of the manslayer over the uprising of the nations, human nature; having appeared as man and God by providence.

*And the Heirmos:*[^185]

*Magnify, O my soul, God adored by the Magi.*

*Magnify, O my soul, the pure Virgin and only Mother of God, who bore Christ the King.*
