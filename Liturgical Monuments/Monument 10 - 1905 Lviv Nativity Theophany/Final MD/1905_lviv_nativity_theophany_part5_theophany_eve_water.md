# PART V: EVE OF HOLY THEOPHANY AND THE GREAT SANCTIFICATION OF WATER (ROYAL HOURS, VESPERAL LITURGY, AND SANCTIFICATION OF WATER)

## Order of the Royal Hours on the Eve of Holy Theophany

*If the Eve fall on a Saturday or on a Sunday, we sing these Hours in advance on Friday.*[^722]

*The First Hour:*

**Priest:** Blessed is the kingdom...
**Choir:** Amen. O Heavenly King... Trisagion. Our Father... Lord, have mercy (12). Glory... Both now... Come, let us worship (3).

*Psalm 5:*
Give ear unto my words... (see p. 2)[^723]

*Psalm 22:*
The Lord shepherds me, and nothing shall be lacking unto me; in a place of green pasture, there He has made me to dwell.
Beside the water of rest He has nurtured me; He has converted my soul.
He has guided me along the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.
For even if I walk in the midst of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for Thou art with me.
Thy rod and Thy staff, they have comforted me.

Thou hast prepared a table before me, against those who afflict me.
Thou hast anointed my head with oil, and Thy cup inebriates me as the most excellent.
And Thy mercy shall follow me all the days of my life;
and that I may dwell in the house of the Lord, unto length of days.[^724]

*Psalm 26:*
The Lord is my illumination and my Savior, whom shall I fear?
The Lord is the defender of my life, of whom shall I be afraid?
When evildoers drew near against me, to eat up my flesh;
those who afflict me and my enemies, they grew weak and fell.
Though an host should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear.
Though war should rise up against me, in this do I put my trust.
One thing have I asked of the Lord, this will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life,
to behold the beauty of the Lord, and to visit His holy temple.
For He hid me in His tabernacle; in the day of my evils He sheltered me in the secret place of His tabernacle.
He set me high upon a rock; and now, behold, He has lifted up my head above mine enemies.[^725]

I went about, and offered in His tabernacle a sacrifice of praise and jubilation; I will sing, and chant psalms unto the Lord.
Give ear, O Lord, unto my voice wherewith I have cried; have mercy on me, and hear me.
My heart said unto Thee: I will seek the Lord. My face has sought Thee; Thy face, O Lord, will I seek.
Turn not away Thy face from me, and turn not aside in anger from Thy servant.
Be Thou my helper; cast me not away, neither forsake me, O God my Savior.
For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the Lord has taken me up.
Set a law for me, O Lord, in Thy way, and guide me in the right path, because of mine enemies.
Deliver me not over to the souls of those that afflict me; for unjust witnesses have risen up against me, and iniquity has lied to itself.
I believe that I shall see the good things of the Lord in the land of the living.
Wait on the Lord, be a man of good courage, and let thy heart be strengthened, and wait on the Lord.[^726]

Glory... Both now... Alleluia (3). Lord, have mercy (3).

*Glory: Troparion, Tone 4:*
The river Jordan once turned back by the mantle of Elisha when Elijah was taken up, and the waters were divided hither and thither, and the wet path became dry ground for him, as a true figure of baptism, by which we pass through the fleeting course of life: Christ has appeared in the Jordan to sanctify the waters.[^727]

*Both now: Theotokion:*
What shall we call thee, O full of grace? Heaven? For thou hast caused the Sun of Righteousness to shine forth. Paradise? For thou hast blossomed the flower of incorruption. Virgin? For thou didst remain incorrupt. Pure Mother? For thou hast held in thy holy arms the Son, Who is God of all. Him do thou beseech, that our souls may be saved.[^728]

*Then we sing these Troparia twice.*
*Tone 8:*
Today the nature of the waters is sanctified, and the Jordan is divided and turns back the stream of its waters, beholding the Master being baptized.[^729]

*And the second choir sings the same Troparion without a Refrain. And again the first choir sings the Refrain:*
Therefore will I remember Thee from the land of Jordan, and from Hermonim.[^730]
As a man Thou didst come to the river, O Christ the King, and Thou dost hasten to receive a servant's baptism, O Good One, at the hands of the Forerunner, for our sins, O Lover of mankind.[^731]

*The second choir sings the second Refrain:*
The waters saw Thee, O God, the waters saw Thee and were afraid.[^732]

*And again the same Troparion.*
As a man Thou didst come to the river, O Christ the King, and Thou dost hasten to receive a servant's baptism, O Good One, at the hands of the Forerunner, for our sins, O Lover of mankind.

*Glory:*
To the voice of him who cries in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord! Thou didst come, O Lord, having taken the form of a servant, asking for baptism, though Thou knewest no sin. The waters saw Thee and were afraid; the Forerunner became trembling, and cried aloud, saying: How shall the lamp enlighten the Light? How shall a servant lay his hand upon the Master? Sanctify me and the waters, O Savior, Who takest away the sin of the world![^733]

*Both now: Again the same Troparion.*
To the voice of him who cries in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord! Thou didst come, O Lord, having taken the form of a servant, asking for baptism, though Thou knewest no sin. The waters saw Thee and were afraid; the Forerunner became trembling, and cried aloud, saying: How shall the lamp enlighten the Light? How shall a servant lay his hand upon the Master? Sanctify me and the waters, O Savior, Who takest away the sin of the world!

*Then the Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*
The Lord thundered from heaven, and the Most High gave His voice.[^734]
*Verse:* I will love Thee, O Lord, my strength; the Lord is my support.[^735]

*The Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 35):*
Thus says the Lord: Let the thirsty desert rejoice; let the wilderness rejoice, and blossom like a lily; and it shall blossom forth, and everything shall be glad. And the desert places of Jordan shall rejoice, and the glory of Lebanon has been given unto it, and the honor of Carmel; and My people shall see the glory of the Lord, and the majesty of God. Be strengthened, ye relaxed hands, and ye feeble knees be comforted; and say to them that are of a fearful heart: Be strong, fear not! Behold, our God renders judgment; He will come and save us. Then shall the eyes of the blind be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall hear; then shall the lame leap like a deer, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be clear; for water has broken forth in the desert, and a torrent in a thirsty land. And the parched ground shall become pools, and in the thirsty land there shall be a spring of water; there shall be the rejoicing of birds, the dwelling of sirens, and reeds, and pools. There shall be a clean way, and it shall be called a holy way, and no unclean one shall pass thereby, neither shall there be an unclean way there; but the dispersed shall walk upon it, and they shall not go astray. And no lion shall be there, nor shall any evil beast go up upon it, neither shall it be found there; but the redeemed and gathered of the Lord shall walk upon it. And they shall return, and shall come unto Zion with gladness and joy, and everlasting joy shall be upon their head; praise and rejoicing and gladness shall overtake them; sickness, sorrow, and sighing have fled away.[^736]

*The Reading from the Acts of the Holy Apostles (Pericope 33):*
In those days, as John was finishing his course, he said: Whom do you suppose that I am? I am not He. But behold, there comes One after me, the strap of Whose sandals I am not worthy to loose. Brethren, sons of the race of Abraham, and those among you who fear God: unto you the word of this salvation has been sent. For those who dwell in Jerusalem, and their rulers, because they did not know Him, nor the voices of the Prophets which are read every Sabbath, have fulfilled them by condemning Him. And having found no cause of death in Him, they asked Pilate that He should be put to death. And when they had fulfilled all that was written concerning Him, they took Him down from the tree and laid Him in a tomb. But God raised Him from the dead; and He was seen for many days by those who came up with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are now His witnesses unto the people. And we preach unto you the promise which was made unto the fathers, how that God has fulfilled the same unto us their children, having raised up Jesus.[^737]

*Wisdom! Stand upright! Let us hear the Holy Gospel.*
*The Reading is from the Holy Gospel according to Matthew.*
At that time, John the Baptist came preaching in the wilderness of Judea, and say- -ing: Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand. For this is He Who was spoken of by the prophet Isaiah, saying: The voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord, make His paths straight. Now John himself had his garment of camel's hair, and a leather belt about his loins; and his food was locusts and wild honey. Then went out unto him Jerusalem, and all Judea, and all the region round about Jordan; and they were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins. But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said unto them: Generation of vipers, who has warned you to flee from the wrath to come? Bring forth therefore fruits worthy of repentance, and begin not to say within yourselves: We have Abraham for our father. For I say unto you that God is able from these stones to raise up children unto Abraham. For now the axe is laid unto the root of the trees; every tree therefore that brings not forth good fruit is cut down and cast into the fire. I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance; but He that comes after me is mightier than I, Whose sandals I am not worthy to bear: He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire. His winnowing fan is in His hand, and He will thoroughly purge His threshing floor, and will gather His wheat into the granary; but the chaff He will burn with unquenchable fire.[^738]

*After this we say:*
Order my steps according to Thy word, and let no iniquity have dominion over me; deliver me from the false accusation of men, and so will I keep Thy commandments. Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant, and teach me Thy statutes.[^739]
Let my mouth be filled with Thy praise, O Lord, that I may sing of Thy glory, and of Thy majesty all the day long.[^740]

**Priest:** For Thine is the kingdom...

*Psalm 28:*
Bring unto the Lord, O ye sons of God, bring unto the Lord young rams.
Bring unto the Lord glory and honor, bring unto the Lord glory unto His name; worship the Lord in His holy court.
The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of glory has thundered, the Lord is upon many waters.

The voice of the Lord is in power; the voice of the Lord is in majesty.
The voice of the Lord Who breaks the cedars; and the Lord will break the cedars of Lebanon.
And He will crush them small like a calf of Lebanon; and His beloved like a young unicorn.
The voice of the Lord Who divides the flame of fire.
The voice of the Lord Who shakes the wilderness, and the Lord will shake the wilderness of Kadesh.
The voice of the Lord Who perfects the hinds, and will uncover the thickets; and in His temple everyone speaks of glory.
The Lord inhabits the flood; and the Lord shall sit as King forever.
The Lord will give strength unto His people; the Lord will bless His people with peace.[^743]

*Psalm 41:*
As the hart pants after the fountains of waters, so pants my soul after Thee, O God.
My soul has thirsted for the strong and living God; when shall I come and appear before the face of God?
My tears have been my bread day and night, whilst it was said unto me daily: Where is thy God?
These things I remembered, and poured out my soul within me; for I shall go over into the place of the marvelous tabernacle, even unto the house of God,
with the voice of rejoicing and thanksgiving, the sound of one keeping feast.
Why art thou sorrowful, O my soul? And why dost thou disquiet me?[^744]

Hope in God, for I will give thanks unto Him, the salvation of my countenance, and my God.
My soul within me is troubled; therefore I will remember Thee from the land of Jordan, and Hermonim, from the little mountain.
Deep calls unto deep at the voice of Thy cataracts;
all Thy billows and Thy waves have passed over me.
By day the Lord will command His mercy, and by night His song shall be with me,
a prayer from me unto the God of my life. I will say unto God: Thou art my helper.
Why hast Thou forgotten me? And why go I mourning whilst the enemy afflicts me?
Whilst my bones were being broken, mine enemies reproached me,
whilst they said unto me daily: Where is thy God?
Why art thou sorrowful, O my soul? And why dost thou disquiet me?
Hope in God, for I will give thanks unto Him, the salvation of my countenance, and my God.[^745]

(Twice)[^749]

And they said unto him: Nay, we have not even heard whether there be any Holy Spirit. And he said unto them: Unto what then were ye baptized? And they said: Unto John's baptism. Then said Paul: John indeed baptized with the baptism of repentance, saying unto the people that they should believe on Him Who should come after him, that is, on Christ Jesus. When they heard this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. And when Paul had laid his hands upon them, the Holy Spirit came upon them, and they spoke with tongues, and prophesied. And all the men were about twelve. And he entered into the synagogue, and spoke boldly for three months, disputing and persuading concerning the kingdom of God.[^757]

*Psalm 73:*
O God, why hast Thou cast us off unto the end? Why has Thine anger smoked against the sheep of Thy pasture?
Remember Thy congregation, which Thou hast purchased from the beginning.
Thou hast redeemed the rod of Thine inheritance, this mount Zion, wherein Thou hast dwelt.
Lift up Thy hands against their pride unto the end, whatsoever things the enemy has done wickedly in Thy holy place?[^762]

And they that hate Thee have boasted in the midst of Thy feast;
they set up their standards for signs, and knew it not, as in the exit above;
as in a forest of trees, with axes they cut down its doors together, with axe and hatchet they broke it down.
They set on fire Thy sanctuary, they defiled the dwelling place of Thy name to the earth.
The kindred of them said together in their heart: Come, and let us abolish all the feasts of God from the earth.
Our signs we have not seen, there is no more any prophet, and he will know us no more.
How long, O God, shall the enemy reproach? Shall the adversary provoke Thy name unto the end?
Why turnest Thou away Thy hand, and Thy right hand out of the midst of Thy bosom unto the end?
Yet God is our King before the ages; He has wrought salvation in the midst of the earth.
Thou didst establish the sea by Thy strength; Thou didst crush the heads of the dragons in the water.
Thou didst break in pieces the head of the dragon; Thou gavest him for food to the Ethiopian people.
Thou didst cleave the fountains and the torrents; Thou driedst up the rivers of Etham.
Thine is the day, and Thine is the night; Thou hast prepared the light and the sun.
Thou hast set all the borders of the earth; summer and spring hast Thou fashioned.
Remember this: the enemy has reproached the Lord, and a foolish people has provoked Thy name.[^763]

Deliver not unto the wild beasts the soul that confesses Thee; forget not the souls of Thy poor unto the end.
Have respect unto Thy covenant; for the dark places of the earth are filled with the habitations of iniquity.
Let not the humble return ashamed; the poor and needy shall praise Thy name.
Arise, O God, judge Thine own cause; remember Thine insults that are from the foolish man all the day long.
Forget not the voice of Thy suppliants; the pride of them that hate Thee ascends continually.[^764]

*Psalm 76:*
With my voice have I cried unto the Lord; with my voice unto God, and He gave ear unto me.
In the day of my trouble I sought God; with my hands in the night before Him, and was not deceived.
My soul refused to be comforted; I remembered God, and was glad; I complained, and my spirit grew faint.
Mine eyes prevented the watches; I was troubled, and spoke not.
I thought upon the days of old, and called to mind the eternal years.
And I meditated in the night with mine own heart; I searched, and my spirit made diligent inquiry.
Will the Lord cast off forever? And will He be favorable no more?
Or will He cut off His mercy unto the end, from generation to generation?
Or will God forget to be gracious? Or will He in His anger shut up His tender mercies?[^765]

And I said: Now have I begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High.
I remembered the works of the Lord; for I will remember Thy wonders from the beginning.
And I will meditate on all Thy works, and in Thy undertakings will I exercise myself.
O God, in the holy place is Thy way; who is a great God like our God? Thou art the God Who workest wonders.
Thou hast made known Thy power among the peoples; Thou hast with Thine arm redeemed Thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph.
The waters saw Thee, O God, the waters saw Thee, and were afraid; the depths were troubled.
Great was the sound of the waters; the clouds gave forth a voice.
For Thine arrows go abroad; the voice of Thy thunder was in the wheel.
Thy lightnings enlightened the world; the earth was moved and trembled.
Thy way is in the sea, and Thy paths in many waters, and Thy footsteps will not be known.
Thou leddest Thy people like sheep by the hand of Moses and Aaron.[^766]

*The Reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Romans (Pericope 91):*
Brethren, as many of us as were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into His death. Therefore we were buried with Him through baptism into death, that just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life. For if we have been united together in the likeness of His death, certainly we shall also be in the likeness of His resurrection, knowing this, that our old man was crucified with Him, that the body of sin might be done away with, that we should no longer be slaves to sin. For he who has died has been freed from sin. Now if we died with Christ, we believe that we shall also live with Him, knowing that Christ, having been raised from the dead, dies no more; death no longer has dominion over Him. For the death that He died, He died to sin once for all; but the life that He lives, He lives to God. Likewise you also, reckon yourselves to be dead indeed to sin, but alive to God in Christ Jesus our Lord.[^779]

*All of creation rejoices in thee... (see p. 204)[^855]*

Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the heights.[^856]

## Great Sanctification of Water on Holy Theophany

*After the Prayer behind the Ambon, we go forth to the sanctification of water, singing: "The voice of the Lord upon the waters..."*[^857]
*If, however, Vespers is not joined with the Divine Liturgy, then after the Little Litany we do not sing the Trisagion, but immediately the Prokeimenon; then the Epistle and the Gospel, the Litany: "Let us all say", "Vouchsafe, O Lord", "Let us complete our evening [prayer]"; and after the Exclamation we go forth to the sanctification of water, singing:*[^858]
*Tone 8:*
The voice of the Lord upon the waters cries aloud, saying: Come, receive all of you the Spirit of wisdom, the Spirit of understanding, the Spirit of the fear of God, of Christ Who has appeared. *(Thrice)*[^859]
Today the nature of the waters is sanctified, and the Jordan is divided, and turns back the streams of its waters, beholding the Master baptized. *(Twice)*[^860]
As a man Thou didst come to the river, O Christ King, and dost hasten to receive the baptism of a servant— —O Good One, at the hands of the Forerunner, for our sins, O Lover of mankind. *(Twice)*[^861]
*Glory; Both now: In the same Tone.*
Unto the voice of him that cries in the wilderness: "Prepare ye the way of the Lord", Thou didst come, O Lord, having taken the form of a servant, asking for baptism, Who knewest not sin. The waters saw Thee and were afraid; the Forerunner was seized with trembling and cried aloud, saying: How shall the candlestick enlighten the Light? How shall a servant lay his hand upon the Master? Sanctify me and the waters, O Savior, Who takest away the sin of the world.[^862]
*The Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 35):*
Thus says the Lord... (see p. 211)[^863]
*The Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 55):*[^864]
Thus says the Lord: Ho, everyone that thirsts, come ye to the waters, and he that has no money; come ye, buy, and eat; yea, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price. Why do ye spend money for that which is not bread, and your labor for that which satisfies not? Give ear unto Me, and eat ye that which is good, and let your soul delight itself in fatness. Incline your ears, and follow My paths; hear Me, and your soul shall live in good things; and I will make an everlasting covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David. Behold, I have given Him for a witness to the people, a leader and commander to the people. Behold, nations that knew Thee not shall call upon Thee, and peoples that knew Thee not shall run unto Thee because of the Lord Thy God, and for the Holy One of Israel; for He has glorified Thee. Seek ye God, and when ye find Him— —call upon Him; and whensoever He draws near unto you, let the ungodly forsake his ways, and the unrighteous man his counsels, and return unto the Lord your God, and ye shall find mercy; for He will abundantly pardon your sins. For My counsels are not as your counsels, neither are your ways My ways, says the Lord. But as heaven is distant from earth, so is My way distant from your ways, and your thoughts from My mind. For as rain or snow comes down from heaven, and returns not thither, until it waters the earth, and makes it bring forth and bud, and gives seed to the sower, and bread for food: so shall My word be, whatsoever goes forth out of My mouth, and it shall not return unto Me empty, until it accomplishes all that I have willed; and I will prosper My ways, and My commandments. For ye shall go out with joy, and be taught with gladness; for the mountains and hills shall skip, expecting you with joy, and all the trees of the field shall clap their branches. And instead of the brier shall come up the cypress, and instead of the nettle shall come up the myrtle; and the Lord shall be for a name, and for an everlasting sign, and shall not fail.[^865]
*The Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 12):*
Thus says the Lord... (see p. 225)[^866]
*Prokeimenon, Tone 3:*
The Lord is my light and my Savior; whom shall I fear?
*Verse:* The Lord is the defender of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?[^867]

*The Reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Corinthians (Pericope 143):*[^868]
Brethren: I would not have you to be ignorant, that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea; and were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea; and did all eat the same spiritual food; and did all drink the same spiritual drink; for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ.
*Alleluia, Tone 4:*
The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of glory has thundered, the Lord is upon many waters.[^869]
*The Gospel according to Mark (Pericope 2):*[^870]
At that time, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized of John in the Jordan. And straightway coming up out of the water, He saw the heavens opened, and the Spirit like a dove descending upon Him. And there came a voice from heaven, saying: Thou art My beloved Son, in Whom I am well pleased.
*After this, the Litany:*[^871]
**Deacon:** In peace let us pray to the Lord.
*The Choir says after each petition:*
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.
**Deacon:** For the peace from above and for the salvation of our souls, let us pray to the Lord.
For the peace of the whole world, for the good estate of the holy churches of God, and for the union of all, let us pray to the Lord.
For this holy temple, and for them that with faith, reverence, and the fear of God enter therein, let us pray to the Lord.

**Deacon:** For our most reverend Archbishop and Metropolitan, Lord *(Name)*, and for our God-loving Bishop, Lord *(Name)*, for the venerable presbytery, the diaconate in Christ, for all the clergy and the people, let us pray to the Lord.[^872]
For our most religious Emperor *(Name)*, for all his court and army, let us pray to the Lord.[^873]
That He will aid him and subdue under his feet every foe and adversary, let us pray to the Lord.
For this city *(or village, or this monastery)*, and for every city, village, and countryside, and for them that dwell therein in faith, let us pray to the Lord.
For seasonable weather, for an abundance of the fruits of the earth, and for peaceful times, let us pray to the Lord.
For them that sail, travel, are sick, weary, imprisoned, and for their salvation, let us pray to the Lord.
That this water may be sanctified by the power, operation, and descent of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.[^874]
That there may come down upon this water the cleansing operation of the supersubstantial Trinity, let us pray to the Lord.[^875]
That it may be for the cleansing of the souls and bodies of all who need and receive it in faith, and who partake thereof, let us pray to the Lord.

**Deacon:** That there may be granted unto it the grace of redemption, the blessing of the Jordan, by the power, operation, and descent of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.[^876]
That Satan may quickly be crushed under our feet, and every evil counsel stirred up against us may be brought to nought, let us pray to the Lord.
That we may be enlightened with the enlightenment of understanding and piety by the descent of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.
That we may receive the gift of sanctification, for the cleansing of sins, for the healing of soul and body, and for every extraordinary benefit, let us pray to the Lord.
That it may be shown to be the sanctification and salvation of our souls, let us pray to the Lord.
That it may serve for the turning away and cleansing of every operation and snare of visible and invisible enemies, let us pray to the Lord.
That it may be shown to be a medicine and cleansing of every disease of our souls and bodies, let us pray to the Lord.
That the power of the soul-destroying adversary may be sunk in this water, let us pray to the Lord.
That we who receive the sanctification of this water may be enlightened through the grace of Christ, let us pray to the Lord.
That it may be unto all who anoint themselves therewith in faith, or partake thereof, for the cleansing of the defilement of flesh and spirit, let us pray to the Lord.
That there may come upon it cleansing by the grace of the supernatural Trinity, let us pray to the Lord.[^877]

**Deacon:** For them that draw and drink thereof unto the sanctification of souls and bodies, let us pray to the Lord.
That it may be a fountain of incorruption and for the sanctification of homes, let us pray to the Lord.
That it may be blessed as the streams of the Jordan, let us pray to the Lord.
That we may be accounted worthy to be filled with sanctification through partaking of this water, by the invisible manifestation of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to the Lord.
That we may be accounted worthy of the Heavenly Kingdom through partaking of this water, let us pray to the Lord.
That the Lord God may hear the voice of our supplication, and have mercy on us, let us pray to the Lord.
That we may be delivered from all affliction, wrath, and need, let us pray to the Lord.
Help us, save us, have mercy on us, and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.
Remembering our most holy, most pure, most blessed and glorious Lady, the Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, with all the saints, let us commend ourselves and one another, and our whole life unto Christ God.[^878]
**Choir:** To Thee, O Lord.

*While these petitions are being said by the Deacon, the Priest prays, saying this prayer secretly to himself. If, however, there is no Deacon, he says it after the conclusion of the diaconal petitions:*[^879]
**Priest:** O Lord Jesus Christ, the Only-begotten Son, Who art in the bosom of the Father, true God, Fountain of life and immortality, Light of Light, Who didst come into the world that Thou mightest enlighten it: illumine our mind with Thy Holy Spirit, and receive us who bring majesty and thanksgiving unto Thee—[^880] —for Thy marvelous mighty deeds from the beginning of time; and for Thy saving dispensation in the latter ages, wherein, having clothed Thyself in our weak and poor nature, and descended to the measure of a servant, O King of all, Thou didst moreover endure to be baptized by a servant's hand in the Jordan, that having sanctified the nature of the waters, O Sinless One, Thou mightest make a way for our regeneration by water and the Spirit, and restore us unto our original freedom. Celebrating the commemoration of this divine mystery, we beseech Thee, O Master, Lover of mankind: sprinkle also upon us, Thine unworthy servants, according to Thy divine promise, clean water, the gift of Thy tender mercy, that our supplication of us sinners over this water may be acceptable through Thy goodness, and that Thy blessing through it may be granted unto us and unto all Thy faithful people, unto the glory of Thy holy and worshipful Name. For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thy most holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.[^881]

*And after the prayer he does not exclaim aloud, but says to himself: Amen. And when: "Remembering our most holy, most pure, most blessed..." is said, the Priest begins again this prayer aloud:*
**Priest:** Great art Thou, O Lord, and marvelous are Thy works, and no word is sufficient to hymn Thy wonders. *(Thrice)*[^882]

*And he signs the water thrice with three burning candles.*

For by Thy will Thou hast brought all things out of non-existence into being, and by Thy power Thou upholdest creation, and by Thy providence Thou orderest the world. Having compounded creation out of four elements, Thou hast crowned the circle of the year with four seasons. Before Thee tremble all the intelligent powers; the sun hymns Thee; the moon glorifies Thee; the stars attend Thee; the light obeys Thee; the abysses tremble before Thee; the springs of water serve Thee. Thou hast stretched out the heaven like a skin; Thou hast established the earth upon the waters; Thou hast walled about the sea with sand; Thou hast poured forth the air for breathing. The archangelic powers minister unto Thee; the angelic choirs worship Thee; the many-eyed Cherubim and the six-winged Seraphim, standing round about and flying, veil themselves in fear of Thine unapproachable glory.[^883]

For Thou, being God uncircumscribed, unoriginate, and ineffable, didst come upon earth, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men; for Thou couldst not endure, O Master, because of the tender compassions of Thy mercy, to behold mankind tormented by the devil; but Thou didst come and save us. We confess Thy grace, we proclaim Thy mercy, we conceal not Thy benefaction. Thou hast set free the generations of our nature; Thou didst sanctify the virginal womb by Thy birth; all creation hymns Thee Who hast appeared. For Thou, our God, hast appeared on earth, and dwelt among men. Thou didst sanctify the streams of the Jordan, having sent down from heaven Thy Holy Spirit, and Thou didst crush the heads of the dragons that nested there.[^884]

Do Thou Thyself, therefore, O King Lover of mankind, come now also through the visitation of Thy Holy Spirit, and sanctify this water. *(Thrice)*[^885]

*And saying this thrice, the Priest blesses the water with his hand, dipping his fingers in the form of a cross at each verse; then he says this prayer:*
And grant unto it the grace of redemption, the blessing of the Jordan; make it a fountain of incorruption, a gift of sanctification, a loosing of sins, a healing of diseases, a destruction of demons, unapproachable by adverse powers, filled with angelic might; that all who draw and partake thereof may have it for the cleansing of souls and bodies, for the healing of passions, for the sanctification of homes, and for every extraordinary benefit. For Thou art our God, Who by water and the Spirit didst renew our nature grown old through sin; Thou art our God, Who with water didst drown sin in the days of Noah; Thou art our God, Who through the sea, by Moses, didst deliver the Hebrew race from the bondage of Pharaoh; Thou art our God, Who didst cleave the rock in the wilderness, and the waters gushed forth, and the streams overflowed, and didst satisfy Thy thirsty people; Thou art our God, Who by water and fire, through Elijah, didst turn Israel back from the delusion of Baal.[^886]

Do Thou Thyself now also, O Master, sanctify this water by Thy Holy Spirit.

*And the Priest signs it with the cross, breathing upon the water crosswise thrice; then he says:*
Grant also unto all who touch it, and partake thereof, and anoint themselves therewith, sanctification—[^887] —health, cleansing, and blessing. And save, O Lord, Thy servants, the Universal Pontiff [Name], Pope of Rome, and our most devout Emperor [Name], *(The Choir sings Many Years unto them)*, and preserve them under Thy protection in peace; subdue under their feet every enemy and adversary, and grant them all their petitions which are unto salvation, and life eternal. Remember, O Lord, our Archbishop [Name], (or our Bishop [Name]), the whole presbytery, the diaconate in Christ, and every sacred order; and the people here present, and our brethren who for a worthy cause are absent; and have mercy on them and on us, according to Thy great mercy; that also by all elements, and angels, and men, visible and invisible, Thy most holy Name may be glorified, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.[^888]

**Choir:** Amen.
**Priest:** Peace be unto all.
**Choir:** And to thy spirit.
**Deacon:** Bow your heads unto the Lord.
**Choir:** To Thee, O Lord.
*The Priest, having bowed down, prays secretly:*
**Priest:** Incline Thine ear, O Lord, and hear us, Who didst deign to be baptized in the Jordan and didst sanctify the waters; and bless all of us who, by the bowing of our neck, manifest our servant-like semblance; and account us worthy to be filled with Thy sanctification through partaking of this water, and let it be unto us, O Lord, for the health of soul and body.[^889]

*Exclamation:*
**Priest:** For Thou art our sanctification, and unto Thee do we send up glory, and thanksgiving, and worship, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thy most holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.
**Choir:** Amen.

*And immediately, blessing the water crosswise with the precious Cross, he immerses it straight down, dipping it into the water and raising it up, holding it with both hands, singing this Troparion:*[^890]
*Tone 1:*
When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan, the worship of the Trinity was made manifest; for the voice of the Parent bore witness unto Thee, calling Thee Beloved Son, and the Spirit in the form of a dove confirmed the certainty of the word. O Christ God Who hast appeared, and hast enlightened the world, glory to Thee.[^891]

*And a second time he likewise signs the water with the Cross, and a third time likewise, singing the same Troparion, the Priest himself. Then the choirs also sing the same Troparion. And when the server has poured sanctified water into the aspergillum vessel, the Priest, having turned his face to the west, holding the precious Cross in his left hand, and the aspergillum in his right, sprinkles all the people with the sanctified water; and after this the Choir sings:*[^892]
*Glory; Both now: Tone 6.*
Let us hymn, O ye faithful, the greatness of God's beneficence toward us; for having become man because of our transgression, He is cleansed in the Jordan with our cleansing, He Who alone is pure and incorrupt, sanctifying me and the waters, and crushing the heads of the dragons in the water. Let us draw water therefore with gladness, brethren; for the grace of the Spirit— —is invisibly granted unto them that draw in faith from Christ God and the Savior of our souls.[^893]

*Then:*
Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and unto ages. *(Thrice)*

*Psalm 33:*
I will bless the Lord at all times... (see p. 90)[^894]
*And the Dismissal.*

*Some, after the prayer which the Priest says secretly: "O Lord Jesus Christ...", appoint the prayer: "O Trinity supersubstantial..."; yet it is found only in certain editions. We set it forth here:*[^895]
**Priest:** O Trinity supersubstantial, all-good, and transcendent in Godhead, almighty, overlooking all things, invisible, incomprehensible, Creator of intelligent substances and rational natures, supernatural goodness, Light unapproachable, that enlightens every man coming into the world: shine forth also in me, Thine unworthy servant; enlighten the eyes of my mind, that I may dare to hymn Thine immeasurable beneficence and power; and let the supplication made by me for the people here present be acceptable; and let not my transgressions prevent Thy Holy Spirit from coming hither; but forgive me, that uncondemned I may cry aloud unto Thee, and say now, O All-good: We glorify Thee, O Master, Lover of mankind, Almighty, Eternal King; we glorify Thee, Creator and Maker of all; we glorify Thee, without father from a mother, without mother from a Father—[^896] —for in the preceding feast we beheld Thee as an Infant, but in the present feast we behold Thee mature: our God Who hast appeared perfect from the Perfect. For today the time of festival has arrived for us all, and angels feast together with men, and the choir of saints gathers with us; for today the grace of the Holy Spirit, in the appearance of a dove, came down upon the waters; today the unsetting Sun has shone forth, and the world is illuminated with the light of the Lord; today the moon with its bright rays illumines the whole earth; today the light-bearing stars with the brilliance of their radiance adorn the inhabited earth; today the clouds from heaven drop down the dew of righteousness upon mankind; today the Uncreated is willingly touched by the hands of His own creation; today the watery elements take up upon their shoulders the footsteps of the Master; today the Invisible becomes visible, that He may make us beholders of Himself; today the Prophet and Forerunner draws near in love unto the Creator, and stands by with trembling, beholding God's descent unto us; today the Unoriginate bows His neck unto His own servant, that He may drown the dragon that is in the waters; today the prophetic hand touches the flame-bearing head, that with the laver of incorruption He may clothe us; today the streams of the Jordan are ordered for healing unto men by the manifestation of our God; today by mystical streams all creation is watered; today human sins are washed away by the waters of the Jordan; today Paradise is opened unto men, and the Sun of righteousness shines upon us; today the bitter water which—[^897][^898] —was for the people under Moses is transformed into sweetness by the coming of the Lord; today we are delivered from the ancient lamentation, and as the new Israel we have been saved; today we are set free from darkness and illuminated with the light of the knowledge of God; today we put off the old garments of sin, and put on new robes; today the salty waters of the sea are turned into sweetness by Thy manifestation, O Master; today the gloom of the world is abolished by the manifestation of Christ our God; today all nature is brightly illuminated from on high; today delusion is brought to nought, and the path of salvation is wrought for us all by the Master's coming; today the holy and orthodox faith of the Church has shone forth, the spiritual triumph of them that keep the feast; today the things on high feast together with the things below, and the things below are joined with the things on high; today the sacred and loud-voiced festival of the orthodox rejoices; today the Master hastens unto baptism, that He may raise mankind unto the heights; today the Unbowed bows Himself down unto His servant, that He may free us from bondage; today we have purchased the Heavenly Kingdom, for of the Lord's kingdom there is no end; today the earth and the sea of this world have divided their joy, and the world is filled with gladness.[^899]

The waters saw Thee, O God, the waters saw Thee and were afraid; Jordan turned back, beholding the fire of the Godhead descending bodily and entering into it; Jordan turned back, seeing the Holy Spirit in the form of a dove descending and hovering round about it; Jordan turned back, seeing the Invisible become visible, the Creator incarnate—[^900] —the Master in the form of a servant; Jordan turned back, and the mountains skipped, seeing God in the flesh; and the clouds gave voice, marveling at Him Who has come, Light of Light, true God of true God, beholding the Master's festival today in the Jordan; and Him Who drowned in the Jordan transgression itself, death, the sting of delusion, and the bonds of hades, and granted the baptism of salvation unto the world. Therefore I also, a sinner and Thine unworthy servant, declaring the majesty of Thy wonders, held fast by fear, in compunction cry unto Thee.[^901]
