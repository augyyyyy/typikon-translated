# PART VI: THE FEAST OF HOLY THEOPHANY AND SYNAXIS OF ST. JOHN THE FORERUNNER (GREAT COMPLINE, MATINS, LITURGY, AND VESPERS OF THE FORERUNNER)

## Great Compline on Holy Theophany[^902]

**Priest:** Blessed is our God...
**Choir:** Amen.
*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. O Heavenly King... Trisagion. And the rest as on the Eve of the Nativity:*
*We cry aloud with singing: "God is with us"; and in place of the usual Troparia: "Illumine my eyes...", we say the Troparion of Theophany:*
*When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan... (see p. 260)[^903]*
*And in place of: "Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us...", we say the Kontakion of Theophany:*
*Tone 4:*
Thou hast appeared today unto the inhabited earth, and Thy light, O Lord, is signed upon us who with understanding sing unto Thee: Thou hast come and hast appeared, O Light unapproachable.[^904]

*After "Glory to God in the highest", there is the Litya.*

*Litya.*
*Idiomelon Stichera, Tone 4:*
He Who clothes Himself with light as with a garment vouchsafed for our sake to become like unto us, and—[^905] —is clothed today with the streams of the Jordan, not needing these for purification, but ordering for us our regeneration through Himself. O the wonder! Without fire He refashions, and without breaking He restores, and saves them that are enlightened in Him: Christ God and the Savior of our souls.[^906]

Beholding Thee in the Spirit and with fire cleansing the sin of the world, the Baptizer, as he saw Thee coming unto him, filled with awe and trembling, cried aloud, saying: I dare not hold Thy most pure head; do Thou sanctify me, O Master, by Thy divine Epiphany, O only Lover of mankind.[^907]

Come, let us imitate the wise virgins; come, let us meet the Master Who has appeared, for He comes as a Bridegroom unto John. Jordan, seeing Thee, was afraid and halted; John cried aloud: I dare not touch the head of the Immortal One; the Spirit descended in the form of a dove to sanctify the waters; and a voice from heaven [cried]: "This is My Son, Who has come into the world to save the human race." O Lord, glory to Thee.[^908]

Christ is baptized, and comes up from the water, for He raises the world with Himself; and He beholds the heavens opening, which Adam shut against himself and them that were with him; and the Spirit bears witness unto His Godhead, for He anticipates Him that is equal, and a voice [resounds] from heaven; for from thence is He witnessed to, the Savior of our souls.[^909]

The baptizer's hand trembled when it touched Thy most pure head; the river Jordan turned back, not daring to minister unto Thee; for if it had respect unto Joshua the son of Nun, how—[^910] —should it not have been afraid before its own Creator? But Thou hast fulfilled the whole dispensation, O our Savior, that Thou mightest save the world by Thine Epiphany, O only Lover of mankind.[^911]

*Glory: Tone 8:*
O Lord, desiring to fulfill what Thou hadst ordained from eternity, Thou didst accept from all creation ministers of Thy mystery: from among the angels, Gabriel; from among men, the Virgin; from the heavens, the star; and from the waters, the Jordan, in which Thou didst wipe away the transgression of the world. O our Savior, glory to Thee.[^912]

*Now and ever: Tone the same:*
Today creation is illuminated; today all things rejoice, things heavenly together with things earthly; angels and men mingle together. For where the King's presence is, there also His retinue arrives. Let us run, therefore, unto the Jordan, and let us all see John, how he baptizes the uncreated and sinless Head. Therefore, chanting in accompaniment the apostolic voice, let us cry aloud in unison: The saving grace of God has appeared unto all men, illuminating and granting great mercy unto the faithful.[^913]

*At the Aposticha, Idiomelon Stichera, Tone 2:*
Seeing Thee coming unto him at the river Jordan, John said: O Christ God, why hast Thou come unto a servant, O Lord Who hast no defilement? In whose name shall I baptize Thee? In the Father's? But Him Thou bearest in Thyself. In the Son's? But Thou Thyself art the incarnate One. In the Holy Spirit's? And This Thou knowest how to give unto the faithful by the breath of Thy mouth. O God Who hast appeared, have mercy on us.[^914]

*Verse: The sea saw it and fled; Jordan was driven back.*

The waters saw Thee, O God, the waters saw Thee and were afraid; for the Cherubim cannot gaze upon Thy glory, nor the Seraphim look upon it; but standing by in fear, some indeed bear [Thee], while others glorify Thy power; with whom, O Merciful One, we proclaim Thy praise, saying: O God Who hast appeared, have mercy on us.[^915]

*Verse: What ailed thee, O sea, that thou didst flee? And thou, Jordan, that thou wast driven back?*

Today the Creator of heaven and earth comes in the flesh unto the Jordan, the Sinless One seeking baptism, that He may cleanse the world from the enemy's deception; and the Master of all is baptized by a servant, and grants purification through water unto the human race. Unto Him let us cry aloud: O our God Who hast appeared, glory to Thee.[^916]

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 6:*
The bright Lamp, who is from a barren woman, seeing the Sun, Who is from the Virgin, asking baptism in the Jordan, cried aloud unto Him in awe and joy: Sanctify Thou me, O Master, by Thy divine Epiphany.[^917]

*Then: Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart... (see p. 89);[^918] Trisagion. And after Our Father: Troparion of Theophany, Tone 1:*
*When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan... (see p. 260);[^919]*
*And the blessing of loaves. Then: Blessed be the name of the Lord... (three times). And Psalm 33: I will bless the Lord at all times... (see p. 90).[^920]*

## Matins on Holy Theophany[^921]

*After the Six Psalms, at "God is the Lord", the Troparion of Theophany, three times; and the usual Kathisma readings from the Psalter. After the first Kathisma reading, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3. Podoben: Beholding the beauty of thy virginity...*
When Thou wast manifested in the Jordan, O Savior, and wast baptized by the Forerunner, O Christ, Thou wast witnessed to as the beloved Son; therefore Thou wast also revealed as co-unoriginate with the Father; and the Holy Spirit descended upon Thee, enlightened by Whom we cry aloud: Glory to God Who exists in Trinity.[^922]

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 4. Podoben: Speedily anticipate...*
Thou hast sanctified the streams of the Jordan, and crushed the dominion of sin, O Christ our God; Thou didst bow Thyself down beneath the hand of the Forerunner, and didst save the human race from deception. Therefore we beseech Thee: Save our souls.[^923]

*After the second Kathisma reading, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5. Podoben: Let us, the faithful, praise the Word co-unoriginate...*
Wrapped in the streams of the Jordan, Thou Who art most gloriously clothed with light as with a garment, in them Thou hast renewed Adam's nature, crushed by evil disobedience, O Word of God. Therefore we praise Thee, and all of us glorify Thy holy Epiphany.[^924]

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 4. Podoben: Joseph was amazed...*
O river Jordan, why wast thou amazed, beholding the Invisible One naked? I saw and trembled, it said; and how should I not have been afraid of Him and retreated? His angels were terrified as they beheld Him; heaven was dismayed, and earth trembled—[^925] —the sea was troubled, and all things visible and invisible. Christ has appeared at the Jordan to sanctify the waters.[^926]

*Polyeleos.*
*Praise the name of the Lord... (see p. 99).[^927]*

*Again, the Megalynarion:*
We magnify Thee, O Christ Giver of life, Who for our sake wast baptized in the flesh by John in the waters of the Jordan.[^928]

*Verse: God be bountiful unto us, and bless us, cause His face to shine upon us, and have mercy on us.*
*And the Lord thundered from heaven, and the Most High gave forth His voice.*
*The floods have lifted up, O Lord, the floods have lifted up their voices.*
*Therefore will I remember Thee from the land of Jordan, and of Hermon.*
*The sea saw it and fled; Jordan was driven back.*
*The waters saw Thee, O God, the waters saw Thee and were afraid.*
*Thy ways are in the sea, and Thy paths in many waters.*
*How magnified are Thy works, O Lord! In wisdom hast Thou made them all.*
*Glory... Now and ever... Alleluia (three times).*
My Jesus is baptized by a servant in the river Jordan, illuminating all creation.[^929]

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: Joseph was amazed...*
Come, O faithful, let us behold where Christ was baptized; let us now follow unto the river Jordan, unto the voice of one crying in the wilderness; and let us see there the Creator of Adam, having a servant's hand laid upon Him in His ineffable tender compassion, and let us cry aloud unto Him with a loud voice: Thou hast come and hast appeared in the Jordan to sanctify the waters.[^930]

*Glory... Now and ever... the same.*

*Gradual Hymns, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4:*
*From my youth... (see p. 101).[^931]*

**Priest:** Let us attend!
*And the Prokimenon, Tone 4:*
*The sea saw it and fled; Jordan was driven back.*
*Verse: What ailed thee, O sea, that thou didst flee, and thou, Jordan, that thou wast driven back?*

*The Gospel according to Mark, Pericope 2:*
*At that time, Jesus came... (see p. 226).[^932]*

*After Psalm 50, Glory... Tone 2:*
Today let all creation rejoice, for Christ has appeared in the Jordan.[^933]
*Now and ever... the same.*

*Then, the Idiomelon Sticheron, Tone 6:*
God the Word appeared in the flesh unto the human race; He stood to be baptized in the Jordan, and the Forerunner said unto Him: How shall I stretch forth my hand and touch the head of Him Who upholds all things—[^934] —though Thou art an Infant from Mary, yet I know Thee to be the pre-eternal God; Thou walkest upon earth Who art hymned by the Seraphim, and I, a servant, have not learned to baptize the Master. Incomprehensible Lord, glory to Thee.[^935]

*Canon.*
*There are two Canons: the Heirmoi of both canons twice, the Troparia to 12. And afterwards, the first choir chants the Heirmos of the first Canon, and the second choir chants the Heirmos of the second Canon.*

*Tone 2. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
The Lord, mighty in battles, has laid bare the bottom of the deep, and leads His own across dry land, having covered the adversaries therein: for He has been glorified.[^936]
By the streams of the Jordan the Lord, the King of ages, renews fallen Adam, and crushes the heads of the dragons nesting therein: for He has been glorified.[^937]
Clothed in material flesh with the immaterial fire of the Godhead, the Lord Who was incarnate of the Virgin is enveloped in the Jordan's water: for He has been glorified.[^938]
Washing away the defilement of men, the Lord, Who enlightens those who are in darkness, is cleansed in the Jordan for their sakes, having willed to be made like unto them, while remaining what He was: for He has been glorified.[^939]

*Second Canon, Tone 2. Heirmos:*
Israel traverses the surging tempest of the sea, suddenly revealed as dry land; while the Red Sea completely submerged the Egyptian captains, a watery grave, by the mighty strength of the Master's right hand.[^940]

When the light-bearing dawn appeared unto men, coming now from the wilderness unto the streams of the Jordan, O King, Sun [of righteousness], Thou didst bow Thy neck to snatch away the dark prince from his flock, and to cleanse creation from all defilement.[^941]

Co-buried with Thee in the streams, O unoriginate Word, Thou bringest him who was corrupted by deception made new again, receiving this mighty voice ineffably from the Father: This is My beloved Child, and equal unto Me in nature.[^942]

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
The Lord Who gives strength unto our kings, and exalts the horn of His anointed, is born of the Virgin, and comes unto baptism; unto Him, O faithful, let us cry aloud: There is none holy as our God.[^943]

Rejoice today, O Church of Christ, who aforetime wast barren and grievously childless; for through water and the Spirit sons have been born unto thee, crying aloud in faith: There is none holy as our God.[^944]

With a loud voice the Forerunner cries aloud in the wilderness: Prepare the ways of Christ, and make straight the paths for our God, crying aloud in faith: There is none holy as our God.[^945]

*Second Canon. Heirmos:*
As many as have been released from the ancient snares of the lions whose teeth are broken, let us rejoice and open wide our mouth, weaving words of sweet singing from words [of praise], whereby He takes delight in the gifts granted unto us.[^946]

He who aforetime planted mortification in creation, having taken on the nature of the malevolent beast, is darkened by [Christ's] bodily coming; having rushed against the Master when the dawn appeared, that he might crush his own hostile head.[^947]

The tyrant's belly draws unto itself the nature fashioned by God, buried within its bounds; but the renewal of earthborn men is born anew, the Master mightily accomplishing the work: for He has come, desiring to cleanse it.[^948]

*Hypakoe, Tone 5:*
When Thou didst enlighten all things by Thine Epiphany, then the salty sea of unbelief fled, and Jordan flowing downward turned back, raising us up unto heaven; but by the height of Thy divine commandments preserve us, O Christ God, through the prayers of the Theotokos, and have mercy on us.[^949]

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I have heard, O Lord, Thy voice which Thou didst speak, the voice of one crying in the wilderness, when Thou didst thunder over many waters, bearing witness unto Thy Son; being filled with the Spirit Who descended, he cried aloud: Thou art Christ, the wisdom and the power of God.[^950]

Who has seen the sun, which is most radiant by nature, being cleansed? the preacher cries aloud. Shall I wash Thee with waters, the Radiance of glory, the Image of the ever-existing Father? And shall I, who am grass, touch the fire of Thy Godhead? For Thou art Christ, the wisdom and the power of God.[^951]

Moses manifested the divine reverence which he possessed when he drew near unto Thee; for when he perceived that Thou didst speak out of the—[^952] —bush, he immediately turned away his face; how then shall I behold Thee clearly, or how shall I place my hand upon Thee? For Thou art Christ, the wisdom and the power of God.[^953]

Accomplishing what is of the soul wisely, and honored with reason, I am put to shame by things inanimate; for if I baptize Thee, the mountain smoking with fire will accuse me, as will the sea divided in two, and this Jordan which turned back: for Thou art Christ, the wisdom and the power of God.[^954]

*Second Canon. Heirmos:*
Cleansed by the fire of mystical contemplation, the prophet, singing the renewal of mankind, utters a voice inspired by the Spirit, declaring the incarnation of the ineffable Word, by Whom the dominions of the mighty have been shattered.[^955]

Sent forth from the Father, O all-radiant Word, Thou comest to drive away the dark and evil surge of night, and to uproot the sins of men; and by Thy baptism, O Good One, to draw forth radiant sons from the streams of the Jordan.[^956]

Foreseeing the very Word foretold, the preacher cries aloud clearly unto creation: This One, Who is after me, second according to the flesh, is made like unto us, shining forth with divine power to take away our hostile sin.[^957]

Bearing in Himself life-giving pasture, God the Word captures the nests of dragons, rushing upon them with many snares and casting them down; He trips up him who wounded the race of all the world, and having destroyed him, delivers creation.[^958]

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Jesus, the Prince of life, comes to loose the condemnation of Adam the first-formed; and though as God He needs no cleansing, He cleanses fallen [man] in the Jordan, wherein having slain the enmity, He grants the peace that passes all understanding.[^959]

When countless people came together to be baptized by John, He Himself stood in their midst; and he proclaimed aloud unto those who were present: Who has shown you, O disobedient ones, to avoid the wrath to come? Bring forth fruits worthy for Christ; for He Who stands present now grants peace.[^960]

The Husbandman and Creator, standing in their midst as one of all, searches the hearts; and taking into His hand the winnowing fan, He all-wisely separates the threshing floor of the whole world, burning up the unfruitful, and granting eternal life unto the fruitful.[^961]

*Second Canon. Heirmos:*
Washed clean by the purification of the Spirit from the dark and defiled poison of the enemy, we have attained unto a new, unerring path, leading unto unapproachable joy, accessible only unto those with whom God has been reconciled.[^962]

Seeing him whom He formed from the dust bound with inescapable cords in the gloom of transgressions, the Creator lifts him up and places him upon both shoulders; for now in the abundant flowing waters He washes away the ancient shame of Adam's wicked disposition.[^963]

With piety let us eagerly run unto the most pure fountains of the saving stream, beholding the Word from an undefiled [Virgin]—[^964] —drawing vessels of divine thirst, sweetly healing the disease of the world.[^965]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
The voice of the Word, the lamp of the Light, the morning star of the Sun, the Forerunner, cries aloud in the wilderness unto all peoples: Repent, and purify yourselves beforehand; for behold, Christ is present, delivering the world from corruption.[^966]

Born incorruptibly from God and the Father, Christ is incarnate without defilement from the Virgin; the Forerunner teaches that it is hard to loose the strap—the union of the Word with us—delivering the earthborn from deceit.[^967]

With final fire Christ baptizes those who oppose and do not esteem Him as God; but by the Spirit He renews through water of grace those who acknowledge His Godhead, delivering [them] from transgressions.[^968]

*Second Canon. Heirmos:*
With a most blessed voice the Father made known the Beloved, Whom He brought forth from the womb; yea, He says: This consubstantial Son, being radiant with light, has shone forth from the human race; [He is] My living Word, and by providence a man.[^969]

From the belly of the deep monster, the prophet strangely tumbled within for three evenings; straightway he came forth, manifesting unto all in the last times the salvation of regeneration from the manslaying dragon.[^970]

The disciple beholds the heavens opened, [as spoken] in the all-radiant Scriptures, [the Spirit] sent forth from the Father; and the Spirit abiding upon the most pure Word, having come upon [Him] as—[^971] —a dove in an ineffable manner, is revealed unto the peoples who have drawn near unto the Master.[^972]

*Kontakion, Tone 4:*
Thou hast appeared today... (see p. 264)[^973]

*Ikos:*
Unto Galilee of the Gentiles, unto the country of Zebulun, and unto the land of Naphtali, as the prophet said: a great light, Christ, has shone forth unto those who were darkened; a radiant dawn has appeared, flashing forth from Bethlehem; yea rather, the Lord from Mary, the Sun of righteousness, shines rays upon the whole universe; therefore, all you who are naked from Adam, come, let us clothe ourselves in Him, that we may be warmed; for He covers the naked and enlightens those who are in darkness: Thou hast come and appeared, the unapproachable Light.[^974]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
When the godly youths were committed unto the fiery furnace, a rustling dewy wind preserved them unharmed, as did the descent of the angel of God; therefore, bedewed in the midst of the flame, they sang in thanksgiving: Blessed art Thou, O exceedingly praised Lord and God of our fathers.[^975]

Even as in heaven, the angelic hosts stood in the Jordan with trembling and wonder, beholding so great a condescension of God: how He Who holds the firmament of the waters above stood clothed in flesh in the waters, the God of our fathers.[^976]

Of old the cloud and the sea prefigured the wonder of divine baptism, wherein the ancient people of the Law were baptized when departing; and the sea—[^977] —was an image of the water, and the cloud of the Spirit; being perfected by them, we cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto ages.[^978]

All we the faithful, in Whom we have received perfection, unceasingly blessing Him with the angels, let us glorify the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit; for behold, the Trinity is consubstantial in hypostases, yet one God; unto Whom we sing: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto ages.[^979]

*Second Canon. Heirmos:*
He Who with a stream burned the heads of the dragons, and extinguished the high flame of the furnace holding the godly youths, has stilled the uncontrollable gloom born of sin, and washes away all things by the dew of the Spirit.[^980]

Portraying Thee in image, Thou dost render terrible the Assyrian flame turned into dew; therefore, the water now, even as the flame, O Christ, burns the hidden harmful malice that clothes [the enemy], calling [us] back from the slippery path.[^981]

When the Jordan was divided of old, the people of Israel passed through on dry ground, depicting Thee, the most mighty One Who upholds creation without delay, now in the waters, [leading] unto an incorruptible and salutary path.[^982]

We know of the all-destroying deluge of old, whereby Thou didst mercifully bring all things into corruption, O Thou Who workest most great and marvelous deeds; but now, O Christ, Thou hast drowned sin through Thy tender compassion and for human salvation![^983]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
The Babylonian furnace showed forth a most glorious mystery, pouring forth dew, because the Jordan was to receive with its streams—[^984] —the immaterial fire, and embrace the Creator being baptized in the flesh; Whom the peoples bless and highly exalt unto all ages.[^985]

Lay aside all fear, the Deliverer said unto the Forerunner, and submit unto Me as good, approach Me, for by nature I have become this; obey My command, and baptize Me Who have descended; Whom the peoples bless and highly exalt unto all ages.[^986]

When the Baptist heard the Master's words, he stretched forth his palm with trembling; yet touching with his hand the head of his Creator, he cried aloud unto Him Who was being baptized: Sanctify me, for Thou art my God, Whom the peoples bless and highly exalt unto all ages.[^987]

A manifestation of the Trinity took place in the Jordan; for the supreme divine Nature Itself, the Father, proclaimed: This One Who is baptized is My beloved Son; and the Spirit came upon His peer; Whom the peoples bless and highly exalt unto all ages.[^988]

*Second Canon. Heirmos:*
Creation is recognized as set free, and those previously darkened are [now] sons of light; the ruler of darkness alone groans; now let the inheritance of the Gentiles, formerly wretched, fervently bless the Cause.[^989]

The three godlike youths, bedewed in the fire and illuminated with three all-radiant sanctities, clearly showed forth the transcendent Nature in human communion, mercifully burning up all destructive deceit by dew.[^990]

Let all earthly nature be whitened, now being led up unto heaven from the fall; for having been washed in the flowing streams by the Word by Whom all things are preserved, it has escaped former transgressions, washed most radiantly.[^991]

*At Ode 9 we do not sing "More honorable", but we sing the Refrains of Theophany:*
Magnify, O my soul, the most pure Virgin Theotokos, more honorable than the heavenly hosts.[^992]

*And the Heirmos:*
Every tongue is at a loss to praise [Thee] worthily, and even an intellect beyond this world is dazed in singing [praise] unto Thee, O Theotokos; yet, being good, accept our faith, for Thou knowest our divine love; for Thou art the protectress of Christians: Thee do we magnify.[^993]

*Then the second choir sings the same refrain and the heirmos; the other six refrains are chanted to each troparion once:*
Magnify, O my soul, Him Who seeks baptism from the Forerunner.[^994]
Magnify, O my soul, Him Who has come to be baptized in the Jordan.[^995]

O David, come in the Spirit unto the enlightened, singing: Draw near now unto God with faith, saying: Be enlightened; this poor Adam cried in his fall, for the Lord gave ear unto him, and having come unto the streams of the Jordan, renewed him who was corrupt.[^996]

Magnify, O my soul, Him Who was borne witness to by the Father's voice.[^997]
Magnify, O my soul, One of the Trinity, Who bowed His neck and received baptism.[^998]

Isaiah says: Wash yourselves, make yourselves clean, put away your evil doings before the Lord; you who thirst, go unto living water; for Christ sprinkles with water, renewing those who draw near unto Him with faith, and baptizes with the Spirit unto unaging life.[^999]

*Refrain:* O prophet, come unto Me, stretch forth thy hand and baptize Me quickly.[^1000]
*Refrain:* O prophet, permit it now and baptize Me who desire it; for I have come to fulfill all righteousness.[^1001]

We the faithful are preserved by grace and by the seal; for as of old the Hebrews escaped the destroyer through the blood-marked lintel, so also this divine bath of regeneration shall be an exodus for us; and hence we shall behold the unwaning light of the Trinity.[^1002]

*Then the first choir sings the Refrain of the Second Canon:*
Today the Master bows His neck beneath the hand of the Forerunner.[^1003]

*Heirmos:*
O the marvels beyond comprehension of thy childbearing, all-pure Bride, blessed Mother! Having obtained all-perfect salvation through her, we worthily praise [Thee] as our Benefactor, bringing as a gift a song of thanksgiving.[^1004]

*And the second choir likewise sings the refrain and the heirmos; and the other five refrains, each once:*
*Refrain:* Today John baptizes the Master in the streams of the Jordan.[^1005]
*Refrain:* Today the Master buries human sin with the waters.[^1006]

We have come to know what was revealed unto Moses by the bush; come, let us behold what was accomplished by strange decrees: for as the Virgin, carrying fire, was preserved unharmed, [having] brought forth the light-bearing—[^1007] —Benefactor, manifested in the streams of the Jordan.[^1008]

*Refrain:* Today the Master is borne witness to from on high as the beloved Son.[^1009]
*Refrain:* Today the Master receives baptism by the hand of the Forerunner.[^1010]

Thou anointest human nature, perfecting it, O King without beginning, by communion of the Spirit, having washed it from gloom with pure streams; and having put to shame the exalted power, [Thou grantest us] now to dwell in unending life.[^1011]

*Then both choirs, having come together, sing the first refrain of Theophany, and the heirmos:*
Every tongue is at a loss...[^1012]

*Then the refrain of the second canon, and the heirmos:*
O the marvels beyond comprehension...[^1013]

*Exapostilarion. Podoben: Thou hast visited us:*
The Savior, grace and truth, has appeared in the streams of the Jordan, and has enlightened those sleeping in darkness and shadow; for He has come and appeared, the unapproachable Light. *(Thrice)*[^1014]

*At the Praises, Stichera on 4, Tone 1:*
Light from Light, Christ our Savior, God manifested, has shone forth upon the world; let us, O peoples, worship Him.[^1015]

How shall we servants worthily honor Thee, O Christ our Master? For in the waters Thou hast renewed us all.[^1016]

Thou, our Savior, being baptized in the Jordan, hast sanctified the waters; when the hand of the servant was placed upon—[^1017] —Thee, and Thou didst heal the passions of the world: great is the mystery of Thine Epiphany, O Lord, Lover of mankind, glory to Thee.[^1018]

The true Light has appeared, and grants enlightenment unto all; Christ, Who is higher than all purity, is baptized with us; He imparts sanctification unto the water, and it becomes a cleansing for souls; what is seen is earthly, and what is understood is higher than the heavens; salvation is through the bath, and through the water is the Spirit; by immersion our ascent unto God is accomplished: marvelous are Thy works, O Lord, glory to Thee.[^1019]

*Glory: Tone 6:*
Thou hast clothed Thyself with the waters of the Jordan, O Savior, Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment; and Thou hast bowed Thy head unto the Forerunner, Thou Who didst measure the heaven with a span; that Thou mightest turn the world away from deceit and save it, as Lover of mankind.[^1020]

*Now and ever: Tone 2:*
Today Christ comes unto the Jordan to be baptized; today John touches the head of the Master; the heavenly powers were struck with awe, beholding the most glorious mystery; the sea saw and fled, Jordan seeing it was turned back; and we who are enlightened cry aloud: Glory to God Who has appeared, and was seen on earth, and has enlightened the world.[^1021]

*Great Doxology: "Glory to God in the highest" (see p. 119)[^1022]; After the Trisagion, the Troparion of Theophany: "When Thou wast baptized in the Jordan..." (see p. 260)[^1023]; Litanies; and the Dismissal.*

## Vespers on the Synaxis of the Holy and Glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John[^1056]

*On "Lord, I Have Cried", Stichera of Theophany, 3, Tone 2:*
Our Enlightener... (see p. 235)[^1057]

*And of the Forerunner, Tone 1. Podoben: "O all-praised martyrs":*
Beholding Thee coming unto him, O Christ, and asking for baptism, the Forerunner cried aloud with trembling: What dost Thou command me, to perform things beyond my power, O all-powerful Lord? How with my hand—[^1058] —shall I touch Thee, Who holdest all things in Thy hand? Do Thou rather baptize me, Thy servant.[^1059]

I have appeared as whole man now, and have become accessible unto thee, though inaccessible by nature; willingly I have become poor, being rich, that I may enrich what has grown poor with incorruption and deliverance; approach and baptize [Me], Who am not subject to corruption, and Who take away the world from corruption.[^1060]

I am hemmed in on every side, and how to flee I know not, said the Forerunner unto the Creator: being the torrent of delight, O Bountiful One, how shall the river streams receive Thee Who hast entered, and Who pourest out salvation unto those who honor Thy holy manifestation, O Word?[^1061]

*Glory... Tone 6:*
O lamp in the flesh, Forerunner of the Savior, offspring of the barren one, friend of Him Who is from the Virgin, Whom thou didst worship with leapings while in the womb, and didst baptize in the waters of the Jordan: pray unto Him, we beseech thee, O Prophet, that we may escape future tempests.[^1062]

*Now and ever... of Theophany, the same Tone:*
God the Word appeared in the flesh... (see p. 270)[^1063]

*Entrance. Prokimenon, Tone 6:*
Our God is in heaven and on earth; all things whatsoever He willed, He has done.[^1064]
*Verse 1:* When Israel went forth out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a barbarous people, Judea became His sanctuary, and Israel His dominion.[^1065]
*Verse 2:* The sea saw and fled; Jordan turned back.[^1066]

*Verse 3:* What ailed thee, O sea, that thou didst flee? and thou, O Jordan, that thou didst turn back?[^1067]
*And again:*
Our God is in heaven and on earth...[^1068]
*Then the Litany.*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign":*
When John the Forerunner beheld Thee coming unto him, O Master, he was struck with dread, and as a well-disposed servant he cried aloud with fear: What is this condescension, O Savior? What is this poverty wherewith Thou hast clothed Thyself? By the wealth of Thy goodness Thou hast exalted man who had humbled himself, as Thou art compassionate, since Thou hast clothed Thyself in him.[^1069]
*Verse:* The sea saw and fled; Jordan turned back.[^1070]
Thou didst answer the Forerunner, O Savior of all: Come unto Me Who am accomplishing a saving mystery; serve with fear and be not terrified; for renewing Adam who was crushed by sin, I am baptized as man, being undefiled by nature, in the waters of the Jordan, into which thou seest Me come.[^1071]
*Verse:* What ailed thee, O sea, that thou didst flee? and thou, O Jordan, that thou didst turn back?[^1072]
Who among the earthborn has seen the sun being cleansed? John said in return; and [who has seen] Him Who clothes the heaven with clouds stripped entirely, and the Maker of fountains and rivers stepping into the waters? I marvel at Thine ineffable dispensation, O Master: burden not Thy servant with fearful commands.[^1073]

*Glory... Tone 4:*
As a lover of the Spirit and the grace-announcing swallow, as [proclaiming] the kingly dispensation unto the human race of Him Who shone forth brightly from the pure Virgin unto the renewal of men, thou hast enlightened [them], O Forerunner, utterly taking away the habit of those who mourn; and directing the hearts of those being baptized in repentance to receive eternal life, O blessed one accepted of God.[^1074]

*Now and ever... the same Tone:*
Come, let us imitate the wise virgins... (p. 364 [sic; cf. p. 264])[^1075]

*Troparion, Tone 2:*
The memory of the righteous is celebrated with hymns of praise, but the testimony of the Lord is sufficient for thee, O Forerunner; for thou wast shown forth in truth as the most honorable of the prophets, since thou wast accounted worthy to baptize in the streams Him Whom thou didst proclaim; therefore, having suffered for the truth with rejoicing, thou didst announce good tidings even unto those in Hades of God Who appeared in the flesh, Who takes away the sin of the world, and grants us great mercy.[^1076]

*Glory... Now and ever... of Theophany:*
When Thou wast baptized in the Jordan... (see p. 285)[^1077]
