# PART I: EVE OF THE NATIVITY OF CHRIST (ROYAL HOURS AND VESPERAL DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. BASIL)

## Order of the Royal Hours on the Eve of the Nativity of Christ

*It should be known concerning these Hours that if the Nativity of Christ happen to fall on a Sunday or Monday, we chant the Hours beforehand on Friday. But if the Nativity happen to fall on a Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday, we chant the Hours on the very day of the Eve.*[^4]

*First Hour.*

*The Priest, having taken his stand before the Royal Doors, begins:*

**Priest:** Blessed is our God, always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.

**Choir:** Amen.

Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.

O Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of Truth, Who art everywhere present and fillest all things, Treasury of good things and Giver of life, come and dwell within us, and cleanse us from every stain, and save our souls, O Good One.

Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us. *(Thrice)*

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

All-Holy Trinity, have mercy on us; Lord, cleanse our sins; Master, pardon our iniquities; Holy One, visit and heal our infirmities for Thy name's sake.

Glory: Now and ever: Lord, have mercy. *(Thrice)*

Our Father, Who art in the heavens, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.

**Priest:** For Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.

**Choir:** Amen.

Lord, have mercy. *(Twelve times)* Glory: Now and ever:

† Come, let us bow down before our King and God.

† Come, let us bow down before Christ, our King and God.

† Come, let us bow down and fall before the Lord Himself, Jesus Christ, our King and God.

*Psalm 5.*[^5]

Give ear unto my words, O Lord; understand my cry.

Attend to the voice of my supplication, my King and my God.

For unto Thee will I pray, O Lord; in the morning Thou wilt hear my voice.

In the morning I will stand before Thee and look up; for Thou art not a God Who desires iniquity.

Neither shall the wicked dwell with Thee, nor shall the transgressors abide before Thine eyes.

Thou hast hated all that work iniquity; Thou wilt destroy all that speak falsehood.

The Lord abhors the man of blood and deceit; but as for me, in the multitude of Thy mercy,

I will enter into Thy house; I will worship toward Thy holy temple in Thy fear.

O Lord, lead me in Thy righteousness; because of mine enemies make straight my way before Thee.

For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is vain.

Their throat is an open sepulchre, with their tongues they have dealt deceitfully; judge them, O God.

Let them fall from their counsels; cast them out according to the multitude of their impieties, for they have provoked Thee, O Lord.

And let all that put their hope in Thee be glad; they shall rejoice unto the ages, and Thou shalt dwell in them.

And all that love Thy name shall boast in Thee; for Thou wilt bless the righteous.

O Lord, Thou hast crowned us as with a shield of good will.

*Psalm 44.*[^6]

My heart hath poured forth a good word; I speak of my works unto the King.

My tongue is the pen of a scribe writing swiftly.

Thou art beautiful in comeliness beyond the sons of men, grace is poured forth upon Thy lips; therefore God hath blessed Thee unto the ages.

Gird Thy sword upon Thy thigh, O Mighty One.

In Thy beauty and in Thy comeliness, bend Thy bow, proceed prosperously, and reign.

Because of truth, meekness, and righteousness; and Thy right hand shall guide Thee marvelously.

Thy weapons are sharpened, O Mighty One, the peoples shall fall under Thee, in the heart of the enemies of the King.

Thy throne, O God, is unto the age of the age; the sceptre of Thy kingdom is a sceptre of uprightness.

Thou hast loved righteousness and hated iniquity; therefore God, Thy God, hath anointed Thee with the oil of gladness beyond Thy fellows.

Myrrh, stacte, and cassia are from Thy garments, from the ivory palaces, whereby the daughters of kings have made Thee glad in Thine honor.

At Thy right hand stood the Queen, arrayed in vesture woven with gold, adorned in varied colors.

Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear; and forget thine own people and thy father's house.

And the King shall greatly desire thy beauty; for He is the Lord Thy God, and thou shalt worship Him.

And the daughter of Tyre shall come with gifts; the rich among the people shall entreat thy face.

All the glory of the daughter of the King is from within, arrayed in tassels of gold and adorned in varied colors.

The virgins in her train shall be brought unto the King; her companions shall be brought unto Thee.

They shall be brought with gladness and rejoicing; they shall be led into the temple of the King.

Instead of thy fathers, sons have been born unto thee; thou shalt make them princes over all the earth.

I will remember Thy name in every generation and generation.

Therefore shall the peoples give praise unto Thee unto the ages, and unto the age of the age.

*Psalm 45.*[^7]

God is our refuge and strength, a helper in afflictions that have come upon us exceedingly.

Therefore will we not fear when the earth is shaken, and the mountains are carried into the heart of the sea.

Their waters roared and were troubled; the mountains were shaken by His might.

The rush of the river gladdens the city of God; the Most High hath sanctified His dwelling place.

God is in the midst of her, she shall not be shaken; God shall help her at the break of dawn.

The nations were troubled, kingdoms wavered; the Most High gave forth His voice, the earth was shaken.

The Lord of hosts is with us; our protector is the God of Jacob.

Come and behold the works of God, what wonders He hath wrought upon the earth, taking away wars unto the ends of the earth.

He will break the bow and snap the spear, and the shields will He burn with fire.

Be still, and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted upon the earth.

The Lord of hosts is with us, our protector is the God of Jacob.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen. Alleluia, alleluia, alleluia; glory to Thee, O God. *(Thrice)* Lord, have mercy. *(Thrice)*

*Glory: Troparion of the Forefeast, Tone 4:*

Mary was once registered with the elder Joseph in Bethlehem as being of the seed of David, bearing in her womb the seedless conception; and the time of the birth arrived, and there was no place in the inn; but as a fair palace, the cave was shown forth to the Queen. Christ is born, to raise up the image that fell of old.

*Both now: Theotokion:*

What shall we name thee, O full of grace? Heaven, for thou hast shone forth the Sun of Righteousness; Paradise, for thou hast blossomed forth the Flower of incorruption; Virgin, for thou didst remain incorrupt; pure Mother, for thou hast held in thy holy arms a Son, Who is the God of all: Him do thou entreat, that our souls may be saved.

*Then we chant these Troparia:*

*Tone 8:*

Bethlehem, make ready; let the manger be adorned; cave, receive; truth is come, the shadow hath passed away, and God hath appeared from the Virgin unto men, fashioned as we are, and deifying the flesh; therefore Adam is renewed with Eve, crying out: Good will hath appeared on earth, to save our race.

*Tone 6:*

*Verse:* God shall come from Theman, and the Holy One from the mountain overshadowed and densely wooded.[^8]

Now the prophetic utterance draws near to its fulfillment, which secretly said: And thou Bethlehem, land of Judah, shalt by no means be called the least among the rulers, preparing the cave; for out of thee unto Me shall come forth the Ruler of the nations in the flesh, from the Virgin Maiden, Christ God, Who shall shepherd His people, the new Israel: let us all give Him majesty.

*Verse:* Lord, I have heard Thy report, and was afraid; Lord, I considered Thy works, and was amazed.

*Now the prophetic utterance...*

*Then Glory: Tone 8:*

These things saith Joseph unto the Virgin: Mary, what is this deed that I see in thee? I am at a loss and wonder, and in mind I am dismayed; therefore depart from me in secret speedily. Mary, what is this deed that I see in thee? In place of honor, shame; in place of gladness, sorrow; instead of being praised, thou hast brought me reproach. I can no longer bear the reproaches of men; for from the priests of the temple of the Lord I received thee as blameless, and what is this that is seen?

*Both now:*

*These things saith Joseph unto the Virgin...*

*Then the Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*

The Lord said unto Me: Thou art My Son, this day have I begotten Thee.[^9]

*Verse:* Ask of Me, and I will give Thee the nations for Thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for Thy possession.

*Then the Reading.*

*Reading from the Prophecy of Micah, Chapter 5:*[^10]

Thus saith the Lord: And thou Bethlehem, house of Ephratha, art thou too least among the thousands of Judah? For out of thee shall come forth unto Me a ruler, to be prince in Israel; and His goings forth have been from the beginning, from the days of eternity. Therefore shall He give them up, until the time that she that is in travail hath brought forth; and the rest of His brethren shall return unto the children of Israel. And He shall stand, and see, and shepherd His flock in the strength of the Lord; and they shall abide in the glory of the name of the Lord their God: for now shall He be magnified unto the ends of the earth.

*Reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Hebrews, Pericope 303:*[^11]

God, having of old spoken unto the fathers in the prophets in many portions and in many manners, hath in these last days spoken unto us in His Son, Whom He hath appointed heir of all things, through Whom also He made the worlds; Who being the brightness of His glory, and the express image of His person, and upholding all things by the word of His power, when He had by Himself made purification of our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high; having become so much better than the angels, as He hath inherited a more excellent name than they. For unto which of the angels said He at any time: Thou art My Son, this day have I begotten Thee? And again: I will be to Him a Father, and He shall be to Me a Son? And again, when He brings the Firstborn into the world, He says: And let all the angels of God worship Him. And of the angels He says: Who makes His angels spirits, and His ministers a flame of fire. But unto the Son He says: Thy throne, O God, is unto the age of the age; a sceptre of uprightness is the sceptre of Thy kingdom.

Thou hast loved righteousness and hated iniquity; therefore God, Thy God, hath anointed Thee with the oil of gladness beyond Thy fellows. And: Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the works of Thy hands. They shall perish, but Thou remainest; and they all shall grow old as doth a garment; and as a vesture shalt Thou roll them up, and they shall be changed; but Thou art the same, and Thy years shall not fail.

*Reading from the Holy Gospel according to Matthew, Pericope 2:*[^12]

The birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When His mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Spirit. Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not willing to expose her to public shame, was minded to put her away secretly. But while he thought on these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying: Joseph, son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife; for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Spirit. And she shall bring forth a Son, and thou shalt call His name Jesus; for He shall save His people from their sins. Now all this took place, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken of the Lord by the prophet, saying: Behold, a virgin shall be with child, and shall bring forth a Son, and they shall call His name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us. Then Joseph, being raised from sleep, did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife; and knew her not until she had brought forth her firstborn Son; and he called His name Jesus.

*Then we say the Troparion:*

Order my steps according to Thy word, and let no iniquity have dominion over me. Deliver me from the false accusation of men, and I will keep Thy commandments. Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant, and teach me Thine ordinances. Let my mouth be filled with Thy praise, O Lord, that I may hymn Thy glory, and Thy majesty all the day long.

*Then the Trisagion; and after the Our Father:*

*Kontakion, Tone 3:*

The Virgin this day comes to give birth ineffably in a cave unto the pre-eternal Word: rejoice, O inhabited earth, when thou hearest; glorify with the angels and the shepherds Him Who wills to appear, a young Child, the pre-eternal God.

*Then Lord, have mercy (40 times), and the prayer:*

Thou Who at all times and at every hour, in heaven and on earth, art worshipped and glorified, O good God, long-suffering, plenteous in mercy, most compassionate; Who lovest the righteous and hast mercy on sinners; Who callest all men to salvation through the promise of good things to come: do Thou Thyself, O Lord, receive also our supplications at this hour, and direct our life unto Thy commandments. Sanctify our souls, purify our bodies, set aright our thoughts, cleanse our minds, make us sober, and deliver us from every tribulation, evil, and sickness (and passions of the soul); encompass us with Thy holy angels, that guarded and guided by their host, we may attain unto the unity of the faith and unto the knowledge of Thine unapproachable glory; for blessed art Thou unto ages of ages. Amen.

Lord, have mercy. *(Thrice)*

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

More honorable than the Cherubim, and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim, who without corruption didst bear God the Word, very Mother of God, thee do we magnify.

In the name of the Lord, bless, father.

*The Priest exclaims:*

**Priest:** God be bountiful unto us, and bless us, and show the light of His countenance upon us, and have mercy on us.

*Then this prayer:*

O Christ, the true Light, Who enlightenest and sanctifiest every man that comes into the world: let the light of Thy countenance be marked upon us, that therein we may behold the unapproachable light; and direct our steps unto the fulfilling of Thy commandments, by the prayers of Thy most pure Mother and of all Thy saints. Amen.

*And the Dismissal.*[^13]

*Third Hour.*

† Come, let us worship: *(Thrice)*

*Psalm 66.*[^14]

God be bountiful unto us, and bless us, show the light of His countenance upon us, and have mercy on us.

That we may know Thy way upon earth, Thy salvation among all nations.

Let the peoples give praise unto Thee, O God; let all the peoples give praise unto Thee.

Let the nations rejoice and be glad, for Thou judgest the peoples with equity, and guidest the nations upon earth.

Let the peoples give praise unto Thee, O God, let all the peoples give praise unto Thee: the earth hath yielded her increase.

God, our God, bless us; God bless us; and let all the ends of the earth fear Him.

*Psalm 86.*[^15]

His foundations are in the holy mountains: the Lord loves the gates of Sion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

Glorious things have been spoken of thee, O city of God.

I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon unto them that know me.

And behold, aliens, and Tyre, and the people of the Ethiopians, these were born there.

Mother Sion shall say: A man, and a man was born in her; and the Most High Himself hath established her.

The Lord shall reckon in the registers of the peoples, and of the princes: of these that were born in her.

As of all them that rejoice, so is the dwelling in thee.

*Psalm 50.*[^16]

Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy.

And according to the multitude of Thy compassions, blot out my transgression.

Wash me thoroughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

For I know mine iniquity, and my sin is ever before me.

Against Thee only have I sinned, and done evil before Thee, that Thou mightest be justified in Thy words, and conquer when Thou art judged.

For behold, I was conceived in iniquities, and in sins did my mother bear me.

For behold, Thou hast loved truth; the hidden and secret things of Thy wisdom hast Thou revealed unto me.

Thou shalt sprinkle me with hyssop, and I shall be cleansed; Thou shalt wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow.

Thou shalt make me hear joy and gladness; the bones that have been humbled shall rejoice.

Turn away Thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities.

Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.

Cast me not away from Thy presence, and take not Thy Holy Spirit from me.

Restore unto me the joy of Thy salvation, and uphold me with a guiding Spirit.

I will teach the lawless Thy ways, and the ungodly shall turn back unto Thee.

Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, Thou God of my salvation; and my tongue shall rejoice in Thy righteousness.

O Lord, Thou shalt open my lips, and my mouth shall show forth Thy praise.

For if Thou hadst desired sacrifice, I would have given it; Thou wilt not delight in whole-burnt-offerings.

A sacrifice unto God is a broken spirit; a broken and a contrite heart God will not despise.

Do good, O Lord, in Thy good pleasure unto Sion, and let the walls of Jerusalem be built.

Then shalt Thou be pleased with a sacrifice of righteousness, oblation and whole-burnt-offerings; then shall they offer bullocks upon Thine altar.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen. Alleluia, alleluia, alleluia; glory to Thee, O God. *(Thrice)* Lord, have mercy. *(Thrice)*

*Glory: Troparion, Tone 6:*

Mary was once registered with the elder Joseph in Bethlehem as being of the seed of David, bearing in her womb the seedless conception; and the time of the birth arrived, and there was no place in the inn; but as a fair palace, the cave was shown forth to the Queen. Christ is born, to raise up the image that fell of old.

*Both now: Theotokion:*

O Mother of God, thou art the true vine that didst bud forth for us the Fruit of life: thee do we entreat, pray thou, O Sovereign Lady, with the holy Apostles, that our souls may be saved.

*Then we chant these Troparia:*

*Tone 6:*

This is our God, no other shall be compared unto Him, Who was born of the Virgin and lived among men; in a humble manger the Only-Begotten Son is seen lying as a mortal man, and swaddled in swaddling clothes is the Lord of glory, and a star proclaims Him unto the Magi for His worship; and we sing: O Holy Trinity, save our souls.

*Tone 8:*

*Verse:* God shall come from Theman, and the Holy One from the mountain overshadowed and densely wooded.[^17]

Before Thy birth, O Lord, beholding Thy mystery with trembling, the spiritual hosts were astonished; for Thou Who didst adorn the heavens with stars wast well pleased to be born as an infant, and Thou Who holdest the ends of all the earth in Thy palm liest in a manger of brute beasts; for by such a dispensation Thy tender mercy hath been made known, O Christ: great is Thy mercy, glory to Thee.

*Verse:* Lord, I have heard Thy report, and was afraid; Lord, I considered Thy works, and was amazed.

*And again we chant the same Troparion.*

*Glory: Tone 2.*

Tell us, Joseph, how is it that the Virgin whom thou didst receive from the Holy of Holies thou bringest with child to Bethlehem? I, saith he, have searched the prophets, and having received word from an angel, I am persuaded that Mary shall ineffably give birth to God; unto Whom the Magi from the East shall come to worship, serving Him with precious gifts: O Thou Who wast incarnate for our sake, Lord, glory to Thee.

*Both now: again the same Sticheron.*

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*

Unto us a Child is born, a Son, and unto us He is given.[^18]

*Verse:* Whose government was upon His shoulder.

*Reading from the Prophecy of Jeremiah, Chapter 3:*[^19]

This is our God, no other shall be compared unto Him. He hath found out every way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob His servant, and unto Israel His beloved. Afterward upon earth...

He appeared, and lived among men. This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endures unto the ages: all they that hold fast to it are appointed unto life, but they that forsake it shall die. Turn thee, O Jacob, and lay hold upon it: walk toward the brightness over against the light thereof. Give not thine honor unto another, nor the things that are profitable for thee unto a foreign nation. Blessed are we, O Israel, for the things that are pleasing unto God are made known unto us.

*Reading from the Epistle of the Holy Apostle Paul to the Galatians, Pericope 208:*[^20]

Brethren, before faith came, we were kept under the law, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed. Therefore the law was our schoolmaster unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith. But after that faith is come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster. For ye are all the children of God through faith in Christ Jesus. For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ. There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus. And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise.

*Reading from the Holy Gospel according to Luke, Pericope 5:*[^21]

In those days, there went out a decree from Caesar Augustus, that all the world should be registered. This first registration was made when Cyrenius was governing Syria. And all went to be registered, every one into his own city. And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, unto the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and lineage of David; to be registered...

...with Mary his espoused wife, being great with child. And so it was, that while they were there, the days were accomplished that she should be delivered. And she brought forth her firstborn Son, and wrapped Him in swaddling clothes, and laid Him in a manger, because there was no room for them in the inn. And there were shepherds in the same country abiding in the field, keeping watch by night over their flock. And lo, the angel of the Lord stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone round about them, and they were sore afraid. And the angel said unto them: Fear not; for behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people: for unto you is born this day a Savior, Who is Christ the Lord, in the city of David. And this shall be a sign unto you: Ye shall find the Babe wrapped in swaddling clothes, lying in a manger. And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host praising God, and saying: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men. And it came to pass, as the angels were gone away from them into heaven, the shepherd men said one to another: Let us go even unto Bethlehem, and see this thing which has come to pass, which the Lord has made known unto us. And they came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the Babe lying in a manger. And when they had seen it, they made known abroad the saying which was told them concerning this Child. And all they that heard it wondered at those things which were told them by the shepherds. But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering them in her heart. And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, as it was told unto them.

*Then we say:*

The Lord God is blessed; blessed is the Lord day by day; the God of our salvation shall make our way prosperous; our God is the God of salvation.[^22]

*Then the Trisagion, and after the Our Father:*

*Kontakion, Tone 3:*

Today the Virgin comes to the cave to give birth ineffably to the Pre-eternal Word: dance, O inhabited earth, when thou hearest; glorify with the angels and the shepherds Him Who wills to appear as a young Child, the Pre-eternal God.

*Prayer: Thou Who at all times and at every hour...*

*Then the Prayer:*

O Master, God, Father Almighty, O Lord, the Only-Begotten Son, Jesus Christ, and Holy Spirit: one Godhead, one Power, have mercy on me, a sinner; and by the judgments which Thou knowest, save me, Thine unworthy servant: for Thou art blessed unto ages of ages. Amen.

*And the Dismissal.*[^23]

*Sixth Hour.*

† Come, let us worship: *(Thrice)*

*Psalm 71.*[^24]

Give the king Thy judgment, O God, and Thy righteousness unto the king's son:
To judge Thy people with righteousness, and Thy poor with judgment.

Let the mountains receive peace for the people, and the hills righteousness.
He shall judge the poor of the people, and shall save the children of the needy, and shall humble the false accuser.
And He shall continue as long as the sun, and before the moon, from generation to generation.
He shall come down like rain upon a fleece, and like drops falling upon the earth.
In His days shall righteousness flourish, and abundance of peace, until the moon be removed.
And He shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the rivers unto the ends of the earth.
The Ethiopians shall fall down before Him, and His enemies shall lick the dust.
The kings of Tharsis and the islands shall bring presents; the kings of Arabia and Saba shall offer gifts.
And all the kings of the earth shall worship Him; all nations shall serve Him.
For He has delivered the needy from the mighty, and the poor that had no helper.
He shall spare the poor and the needy, and shall save the souls of the poor.
He shall redeem their souls from usury and injustice, and precious shall His name be in their sight.
And He shall live, and unto Him shall be given of the gold of Arabia; and they shall pray for Him continually, all the day long shall they bless Him.
There shall be a support upon the earth on the tops of the mountains; His fruit shall be exalted above Lebanon, and they of the city shall blossom like the grass of the earth.

His name shall be blessed unto the ages; before the sun His name abides.
And in Him shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed; all nations shall call Him blessed.
Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, Who alone works marvels.
And blessed be the name of His glory unto the age, and unto the age of the age; and all the earth shall be filled with His glory: so be it, so be it.

*Psalm 131.*[^25]

Remember David, O Lord, and all his meekness.
How he swore unto the Lord, and made a vow unto the God of Jacob:
I will not enter into the tabernacle of my house, I will not go up upon the bed of my couch;
I will not give sleep to mine eyes, nor slumber to mine eyelids,
Nor rest to my temples, until I find out a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob.
Lo, we heard of it in Ephratha, we found it in the fields of the wood.
We will enter into His tabernacles, we will worship at the place where His feet have stood.
Arise, O Lord, into Thy rest, Thou and the ark of Thy holiness.
Thy priests shall be clothed with righteousness, and Thy righteous ones shall rejoice with joy.
For Thy servant David's sake, turn not away the face of Thine anointed.
The Lord has sworn in truth unto David, and He will not annul it: Of the fruit of thy loins will I set upon thy throne.

If thy sons keep My covenant and these My testimonies which I shall teach them,
Their sons also shall sit upon thy throne unto the age.
For the Lord has chosen Sion, He has desired it for a habitation for Himself.
This is My rest unto the age of the age; here will I dwell, for I have chosen it.
Blessing I will bless her provision; I will satisfy her poor with bread.
Her priests will I clothe with salvation, and her righteous ones shall rejoice with joy.
There will I make the horn of David to bud; I have prepared a lamp for Mine anointed.
His enemies will I clothe with shame, but upon him shall My sanctification flourish.

*Psalm 90.*[^26]

He that dwells in the help of the Most High shall abide in the shelter of the God of heaven.
He shall say unto the Lord: Thou art my protector and my refuge; my God, and in Him will I trust.
For He shall deliver thee from the snare of the hunters, and from every troubling word.
With His pinions shall He overshadow thee, and under His wings shalt thou hope.
With a shield shall His truth encompass thee; thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night,
Nor for the arrow that flies by day, nor for the thing that walks in darkness, nor for mishap and the noonday demon.
A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand, but unto thee it shall not come nigh.

Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold, and see the reward of sinners.
For Thou, O Lord, art my hope; Thou hast made the Most High thy refuge.
No evils shall come unto thee, and no scourge shall draw nigh unto thy dwelling.
For He shall give His angels charge concerning thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
In their hands shall they bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.
Thou shalt tread upon the asp and basilisk, and the lion and dragon shalt thou trample under foot.
Because he has set his hope on Me, I will deliver him; I will shelter him, because he has known My name.

*The Choir says after each petition:*
**Choir:** Grant this, O Lord.
**Deacon:** An angel of peace, a faithful guide, a guardian of our souls and bodies, let us ask of the Lord.
Pardon and remission of our sins and offenses, let us ask of the Lord.
Things good and profitable for our souls, and peace for the world, let us ask of the Lord.
That we may complete the remaining time of our life in peace and repentance, let us ask of the Lord.
A Christian ending to our life, painless, unashamed, peaceful, and a good defense before the dread judgment seat of Christ, let us ask.
Commemorating our all-holy, immaculate, most blessed and glorious Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary, with all the Saints, let us commend ourselves and one another, and our whole life unto Christ our God.
**Choir:** To Thee, O Lord.
**Priest:** Through the bounties and love toward mankind of Thine Only-begotten Son, with Whom Thou art blessed, together with Thine all-holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.
**Choir:** Amen.

**Priest:** Wisdom!
**Choir:** Bless.
**Priest:** Blessed and most glorified is He Who is, Christ our God.
**Choir:** Establish, O God, the Catholic faith unto ages of ages.[^102]
**Priest:** Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.
**Choir:** More honorable than the Cherubim, and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim, who without corruption gavest birth to God the Word, the very Theotokos, thee do we magnify.
**Priest:** Glory to Thee, O Christ God, our hope, glory to Thee.
**Choir:** Glory... Both now... Lord, have mercy (twice). Lord, bless.
*The Priest says the Dismissal:*[^103]
**Priest:** May He Who was born in a cave in Bethlehem of Judea, and lay in a manger for the sake of our salvation, Christ our true God, through the prayers of His most pure Mother, of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles, of our venerable and God-bearing fathers, and of all the Saints, have mercy on us and save us, as He is good and loves mankind.
**Choir:** Amen.
*Thereafter both choirs, gathering together in the midst of the church, chant together the Troparion of the Feast:* Thy Nativity... *Glory... Both now... The Kontakion of the Feast:* Today the Virgin...[^104]
