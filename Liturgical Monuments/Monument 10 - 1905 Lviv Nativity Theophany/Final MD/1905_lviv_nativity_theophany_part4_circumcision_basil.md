# PART IV: THE CIRCUMCISION OF OUR LORD JESUS CHRIST AND ST. BASIL THE GREAT (VESPERS, MATINS, AND DIVINE LITURGY)

## Vespers on the Circumcision of Our Lord Jesus Christ and St. Basil the Great[^541]

*And the commemoration of our father among the saints, Basil the Great, Archbishop of Caesarea in Cappadocia.*

*At Great Vespers we sing: "Blessed is the man", the first antiphony.*[^542] *At "Lord, I have cried", Stichera on 8: of the Circumcision, 4.*
*Tone 8:*
The Savior, descending unto the human race, accepted swaddling in swaddling clothes; He disdained not bodily circumcision: eight days old according to His Mother, beginningless according to His Father; unto Him let us the faithful cry aloud: Thou art our God, have mercy on us. (Twice)[^543]
The all-good God was not ashamed to be circumcised with the circumcision of the flesh; but He gave Himself as an image and pattern unto all for salvation; for the Creator of the law fulfills the things of the law, and the prophecies of the prophets concerning Him; O Thou Who holdest all things in Thy hand, and wast wrapped in swaddling clothes, Lord, glory to Thee. (Twice)[^544] *Other Stichera of Saint Basil, on 4, Tone 4:*[^545]
Named according to royalty, when thou, O father, by philosophy and sacred art didst shepherd the church's sacred order, the holy people of Christ, then with the crown of the kingdom did the King of kings and Lord of all adorn thee, O Basil, even the co-essential Son, co-eternal and co-beginningless with Him Who begot Him; Whom entreat, to save and enlighten our souls.
Adorned with the vestments of hierarchical dignity, rejoicing thou didst proclaim the Gospel of the Kingdom, O Basil, pouring forth the doctrines of Orthodoxy for the Church; enlightened by which now, we theologize and glorify one Godhead in the Father Almighty, and in the Only-begotten Word of God, and in the Divine Spirit, in three Persons indivisibly; Whom entreat, to save and enlighten our souls.[^546]
Dwelling together with the heavenly choirs and taking up thine abode with them, O father Basil, whose manner of life thou didst also emulate by the pure radiance of thy life, while yet living in the flesh with those on earth as one bodiless; entreat Christ our God to save and enlighten our souls, and deliver from perils and the darkness of ignorance those who delight in thy divinely-inspired teaching.[^547]
*Glory: Tone 8:*
Having become a lover of wisdom, O venerable one, and having preferred meditation upon death as a companion before all things that lead unto God, thou didst lead thy life becomingly; for having girded thyself against bodily passions with the strongholds of self-restraint, and[^548] by the law of divine meditation thou didst preserve the dignity of the soul unenslaved; by the wealth of virtue thou didst subject all carnal wisdom unto the Spirit; therefore having hated the flesh and the world and the ruler of this world, standing before Christ, ask great mercy for our souls.[^549]
*Both now: The same tone:*
*The Savior, descending unto the human race...*
*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day.*[^550]
*The Reading from Genesis (Chapter 17):*[^551]
The Lord appeared to Abram, and said unto him: I am thy God; be well-pleasing before Me, and be blameless. And I will establish My covenant between Me and thee, and will multiply thee exceedingly. And thou shalt be a father of many nations. Neither shall thy name any more be called Abram, but thy name shall be Abraham; for a father of many nations have I made thee. And I will increase thee exceedingly, exceedingly, and I will make thee into nations, and kings shall come out of thee. And I will establish My covenant between Me and thee, and between thy seed after thee throughout their generations for an everlasting covenant, to be a God unto them. And Abraham fell upon his face, and bowed down unto the Lord. And God said unto Abraham: Thou therefore shalt keep My covenant, thou, and thy seed after thee in their generations. And this is the covenant which thou shalt keep between Me and you, and between thy seed after thee in their generations: Ye shall circumcise your foreskin, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between Me and you, and between thy seed after thee in their generations. And an eight-day-old child shall be circumcised among you, every male throughout your generations. And the male whose foreskin is not circumcised on the eighth day, that soul shall be destroyed from his kindred, for he has broken My covenant.[^552]
*The Reading from Proverbs (Chapter 8):*[^553]
The Lord created Me the beginning of His ways for His works. Before the age He established Me in the beginning, before He made the earth, and before He formed the deeps; before the springs of waters came forth: before the mountains were settled, and before all the hills He begets Me. The Lord made lands and uninhabited places, and the inhabited heights beneath heaven. When He prepared the heaven, I was present with Him; and when He marked out His throne upon the winds; when He made firm the clouds above, and made secure the fountains beneath heaven; when He set for the sea its bound, and the waters shall not pass His word; and when He made firm the foundations of the earth: I was with Him, arranging all things; I was that in which He delighted; and daily I was rejoicing before His face at all times.
*The Reading from Proverbs (Chapter 10):*[^554]
The mouth of the righteous drops wisdom, but the tongue of the unrighteous shall perish. The lips of righteous men drop grace, but the mouth of the impious is perverse. False balances are an abomination before the Lord, but a just weight is acceptable unto Him. Wherever reproach enters, there also is dishonor; but the mouth of the humble meditates on wisdom. The integrity of the upright shall guide them, and the stumbling of the rebellious shall overthrow them.[^555] Riches profit not in the day of wrath, but righteousness shall deliver from death. When the righteous man dies, he leaves regret; but the destruction of the ungodly is sudden and a subject of mockery. The righteousness of the blameless directs ways, but unrighteousness falls into impiety. The righteousness of upright men shall deliver them, but transgressors are caught in their own counsel-lessness. When a righteous man dies, his hope does not perish, but the boast of the ungodly perishes. The righteous man escapes from the hunt, and in his stead the ungodly is delivered up. In the mouth of the ungodly is a snare to citizens, but the perception of the righteous is prosperous. By the good things of the righteous a city prospers, and at the destruction of the ungodly there is rejoicing. By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but by the mouths of the ungodly it is overturned. He that lacks understanding sneers at citizens, but a wise man keeps silence.

*At the Litya:*[^556]
*Sticheron of the temple; then of Saint Basil, Tone 3:*
Having made Christ, the fountain of life, to dwell within thy soul by thy venerable life, O divinely revealed Basil, thou didst pour forth rivers of pious doctrines for the universe, watered by which the faithful people of the Church, offering the fruit of lips confessing Him, bring thanksgiving unto Him Who has glorified thy memory unto ages of ages.

The myrrh of grace poured out upon thee has anointed thee to minister the Gospel of the heavenly Kingdom, O divinely revealed Basil; having become the sweet savor of Christ, thou hast filled the universe with the fragrance of His knowledge; therefore favorably receiving the voices of thy servants, ask great mercy for us who honor thee.[^557]

Clothed in the vesture of hierarchical dignity, O champion of the Trinity, Basil, thou didst stand before the tribunal of the judgment-seat, enduring danger for the faith; and having shown a martyr's resistance, thou didst put to shame the wrath of the prefect raging with the power of impiety, and who threatened thee with merciless tearing asunder; having eagerly discerned this beforehand, O father, and having become a martyr in intent, thou didst receive the crown of victory from Christ, Who possesses great mercy.[^558]

*Glory: Tone 4:*
Grace was poured out upon thy lips, venerable father, and thou becamest a shepherd of Christ's Church, teaching the rational sheep to believe in the consubstantial Trinity in one Godhead.[^559]

*Both now: Tone 8:*
*The Savior, descending unto the human race...* (see p. 171)[^560]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Saint, Idiomela.*
*Tone 1:*
O divine and sacred bee of Christ's Church, all-blessed Basil! For arming thyself with the sting of divine desire, thou didst wound the blasphemies of God-hated heresies, and didst treasure up the sweetness of piety for the souls of the faithful; and now passing through the incorruptible pastures of the divine paradise, remember us also, standing before the consubstantial Trinity.[^561]
*Verse: My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart understanding.*[^562]
Thou didst gather the virtues of all the saints, our father Basil: the meekness of Moses, and the zeal of Elijah, the confession of Peter, the theology of John; like Paul thou didst not cease crying aloud: Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to stumble, and I burn not? Therefore, dwelling together with them, pray that our souls may be saved.[^563]
*Tone 2:*
*Verse: The mouth of the righteous meditates on wisdom, and his tongue shall speak judgment.*[^564]
Having learned the nature of existing things, and having surveyed the instability of all things, thou didst find the One Who is immovable, the Creator of all Who is transcendent in essence; and having united thyself all the more unto Him, thou didst reject the desire of non-existing things; pray that we also may obtain divine desire, O divinely revealed Basil.[^565]
*Glory: Tone 6:*
Having received from heaven the grace of miracles and refuted the idol-deception by thy teachings, thou art the glory and confirmation of hierarchs, O all-blessed Basil, and the model of teaching for all venerable monastics; having boldness toward God, pray Him that our souls may be saved.[^566]
*Both now: Tone 8:*
The all-good God was not ashamed to be circumcised with the circumcision of the flesh, but gave Him- -self as an image and pattern unto all for salvation, for the Creator of the law fulfills the things of the law, and the prophecies of the prophets concerning Him; O Thou Who holdest all things in Thy hand, and wast wrapped in swaddling clothes, Lord, glory to Thee.[^567]

*Troparion of the Saint, Tone 1:*
Thy proclamation went forth into all the earth, for it received thy word, whereby thou didst teach in a manner becoming God; thou didst explain the nature of existing things, thou didst adorn human customs, royal priesthood, venerable father: pray Christ God that our souls may be saved.[^568]

*Glory: Both now: Of the Circumcision, the same tone:*
Enthroned on high upon a fiery-formed throne with the beginningless Father and Thy divine Spirit, Thou wast well-pleased to be born on earth of a maiden, Thy Mother who knew not man, O Jesus; therefore Thou wast circumcised as an eight-day-old man; glory to Thy all-good counsel; glory to Thy dispensation; glory to Thy condescension, O Thou Who alone lovest mankind.[^569]

*And the Dismissal.*

## Matins on the Circumcision of Our Lord Jesus Christ and St. Basil the Great[^570]

*At "God is the Lord", Troparion of the Circumcision: "Enthroned on high upon a fiery-formed throne...", twice. Glory: To Basil: "Thy proclamation went forth into all the earth...". Both now: "Enthroned on high upon a fiery-formed throne...". — After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5. Podoben: "Co-beginningless Word...":*[^571]
As royal adornment of the Church let us all praise Basil, the inexhaust- -ible treasury of doctrines; for by these he instructed us to revere the Holy Trinity, united indeed in essence, yet divided in hypostases.[^572]

*Glory: Tone 1. Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior...":*[^573]
O all-wise Basil, standing before the Trinity, pray that on the day of judgment grace, mercy, and purification of transgressions may be obtained by us who sing unto thee and honor thy memory, that with mouth and heart we may glorify the only Lover of mankind.[^574]

*Both now:*
The Creator of all and Master of the world, Who is on high with the Father and the Spirit, is circumcised on earth as an eight-day-old infant; truly divine and marvelous are Thy works: for Thou, O Master, wast circumcised for our sake, being the fulfillment of the law.[^575]

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8. Podoben: "The Wisdom...":*[^576]
Having drawn forth the secret wealth of ineffable wisdom in divine contemplation, thou didst pour forth for all the waters of Orthodoxy, divinely gladdening the hearts of the faithful, yet worthily drowning the commands of the unfaithful; whence by both sweatings of piety thou didst show thyself an invincible champion of the Trinity, hierarch Basil: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who celebrate thy holy memory with love.[^577]

*Glory: Tone 3. Podoben: "Of the divine faith...":*[^578]
Thou didst water the whole universe as with waters by thy teachings, by the dogmas of piety gathered together; for the fountain of life truly rested in thee, O Basil; pray Him, as one having acquired boldness, O divinely inspired one, to grant us great mercy.[^579]

*Both now: Tone 4:*
He Who by nature is invisible is now seen in the flesh; the uncreated Word, now being born of the pure Virgin, takes created form; therefore also according to the law for the sake of us men, He is circumcised as an eight-day-old infant; unto Him therefore let us bow down as unto our Savior.[^580]

*Polyeleos.*
*Praise the name of the Lord...* (see p. 99)[^581]

*Megalynarion of the Circumcision:*
We magnify Thee, O Christ Giver of life, and we honor the lawful Circumcision of Thy most pure flesh.[^582]

*To Basil:*
We magnify thee, O hierarch of Christ, Basil, and we honor thy holy memory: for thou prayest for us unto Christ our God.[^583]

*Verse of the Circumcision: Shout with joy unto the Lord, all the earth, sing ye unto His name, give glory to His praise.*[^584]
*Verse of Basil: Hear this, all ye nations, give ear, all ye inhabitants of the universe.*[^585]
*Of the Circumcision: The Lord sent redemption unto His people: holy and awesome is His name.*[^586]

*Of Basil: The mouth of the righteous meditates on wisdom, and his tongue shall speak righteousness.*[^587]
*Of the Circumcision: Blessed be the name of the Lord from henceforth and unto the age.*[^588]
*Of Basil: Thy priests shall clothe themselves with righteousness, and Thy venerable ones shall rejoice with joy.*[^589]
*Of the Circumcision: Blessed be the name of His glory unto the age and unto ages of ages.*[^590]
*Of Basil: Blessed are they that dwell in Thy house, they will praise Thee unto ages of ages.*[^591]
*Glory: Both now: Alleluia, thrice.*

*Thereafter, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8. Podoben: "The Wisdom...":*[^592]
By the power of divine words thou didst overthrow dark heresies, thou didst drown all the ragings of Eunomius; for unto men thou didst proclaim the Spirit to be God, and by the lifting up of hands thou didst slay enemies; thou didst drive away all the worship of Sabellius, and didst abolish all the vain reasonings of Nestorius, O hierarch Basil: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who celebrate thy holy memory with love.[^593]

*Glory:*[^594]
The grace of thy divine words and the mystical height of dogmas became for us a ladder of divine ascent:

for having acquired the spiritual trumpet, O father, thereby didst thou proclaim the divine doctrines; therefore on the place of pasture thou didst rejoice, receiving the rewards of thy struggles, O venerable hierarch Basil: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who celebrate thy holy memory with love.[^595]

*Both now:*
The Master and Creator of all, having received flesh from thy most pure womb, showed thee to be the protectress unto men, O all-immaculate one; therefore we all flee unto thee, asking purification of transgressions, O Sovereign Lady, and to be delivered from eternal torment and from every affliction of the cruel ruler of the world; therefore we cry aloud unto thee: pray Thy Son and God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who bow down with faith before thy most holy giving-of-birth.[^596]

*Thereafter the Gradual, the 1st Antiphon of the 4th Tone:*
*From my youth...* (see p. 101)[^597]

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4: My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart understanding.*[^598]
*Verse: Hear this, all ye nations, give ear, all ye inhabitants of the universe.*[^599]

*The Gospel according to John, Pericope 36:*[^600]
The Lord said unto the Jews who came unto Him: I am the door; by Me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in, and shall find pasture. The thief comes not, but to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly. I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd lays down His life for the sheep. But he that is a hireling, and not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming, and leaves the sheep, and flees; and the wolf catches them, and scatters the sheep. The hireling flees, because he is a hireling, and cares not for the sheep. I am the good shepherd, and know Mine, and Mine know Me. As the Father knows Me, even so know I the Father; and I lay down My life for the sheep. And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold; them also I must bring, and they shall hear My voice; and there shall be one flock, and one shepherd.[^601]

*After Psalm 50, Idiomelon Sticheron.*[^602]
*Tone 6:*
Grace was poured out upon thy lips, venerable father, and thou becamest a shepherd of Christ's Church, teaching the rational sheep to believe in the consubstantial Trinity in one Godhead.[^603]

*Canon.*[^604]
*The Heirmos twice; and Troparia on 4.*
*Tone 2. Ode 1. — Heirmos:*[^605]
Come, ye peoples, let us sing a song unto Christ God, Who divided the sea and guided the people whom He brought out of Egyptian bondage, for He has been glorified.[^606]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
The eightfold number of days, bearing the image of the future, is enlightened and sanctified, O Christ, by Thy voluntary poverty; for on this day Thou wast circumcised in the flesh according to the law.[^607]

Christ accepts circumcision on the eighth day of His nativity; and today He folds up the shadow, shining forth the light of the new grace.[^608]

*Another Canon of the Hierarch Basil, on 8.*[^609]
*Tone and Heirmos the same.*
It were fitting for thy voice to come, O Basil, unto those who desire to begin thy praises; but O father, receiving our supplication, bestow grace bountifully upon us.[^610]

Thou didst walk the harsh path of the virtues, and didst attain unto the smooth and unopposed heavenly ascent, and becamest a pattern unto all, O Basil.[^611]

The passion-arousing wantonness tormenting the flesh thou didst discipline by desire for divine wisdom; therefore thou dwellest in the incorruptible kingdoms, O father Basil.[^612]

The passions of the soul, fittingly, O Basil, together with those of the body, thou didst cut down with the spiritual sword; and thou didst offer thyself as a sacrifice unto the Master.[^613]

Having become an initiate of ineffable mysteries, O Basil, thou didst mystically instruct Christ's royal order, O sacred father; clearly thou didst shine forth Trinitarian light upon us.[^614]

*Theotokion:*
Who can worthily tell of thy conception beyond word? For thou, O all-pure one, didst bear in the flesh God, Who has appeared unto us as Savior and Lord.[^615]

*Katavasia: the First Choir sings the Heirmos:*
The Lord mighty in battle uncovered the bottom of the deep, and led His own across dry land; in it He covered their adversaries, for He has been glorified.[^616]

*The Second Choir:* *Heirmos:*
Israel traversed the surging storm of the sea, which immediately appeared as dry land; but the dark deep, a watery tomb, covered together the Egyptian captains, by the mighty strength of the Master's right hand.[^617]

*Ode 3. — Heirmos:*
Establish us in Thee, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put sin to death; and plant Thy fear in the hearts of us who sing unto Thee.[^618]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
The transcendent Word, having become incarnate, was circumcised for the cessation of the law; and He gave unto us the firstfruits of divine grace and incorruptible life.[^619]

As Fulfiller of the law, not as an adversary of God, Christ having become incarnate was shown forth; and He deigned voluntarily to be circumcised on the eighth day.[^620]

*Of Basil:*
Thy memory, O father, concurred with Christ's nativity and shone forth with it; Whose ineffable mystery thou didst make manifest through thy teachings.[^621]

Established by the fear of the Lord—for this is the beginning of wisdom—thou wast winged with a higher love for supreme wisdom, O Basil.[^622]

Being filled with all learning, not only of the earthly and transient, but far more of the superior, thou didst appear as a light unto the world, O Basil.[^623]

By action thou didst enter in, O all-wise Basil, and didst show action to be the ascent unto most divine contemplation; and thou wast clearly taught the knowledge of existing things.[^624]

*Theotokion:*
Seedlessly thou didst conceive God in thy womb, and ineffably dost bear Him incarnate, upon Whom the pure heavenly hosts dare not gaze, O Ever-Virgin.[^625]

*Katavasia: the First Choir:*
The Lord, Who gives strength unto our kings and exalts the horn of His anointed ones, is born of a Virgin, and comes unto baptism; unto Him let us, the faithful, cry aloud: None is holy as our God.[^626]

*The Second Choir:*
As many of us as have been delivered from the ancient snares of lions' jaws, their teeth crushed: let us rejoice and open wide our mouth, weaving a hymn out of sweet-singing words, delighting in the gifts bestowed upon us.[^627]

*Kontakion of the Circumcision: Tone 3. Podoben: "The Virgin today...":*
The Lord of all endures circumcision, and as good circumcises the transgressions of mortals; He grants salvation today unto the world, and on high rejoices also the Creator's hierarch and light-bearing, divine initiate of Christ, Basil.[^628]

*Then Sessional Hymn: Tone 8. Podoben: "The Wisdom...":*
Having learned the words of wisdom, and laid aside the letter of the law, thou didst proclaim unto all the words of grace, and didst smooth the tablets of souls with thy words, wherein thou didst inscribe divine teachings; therefore, having passed through the gloom by the cloud of the body's covering, thou didst shine forth by the Holy Spirit, O Basil; entreat Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who honor thy holy memory with love. *(Twice)*[^629]

*Glory; Both now: Of the Circumcision: Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast appeared today...":*
As being an abyss of love for mankind, Thou didst clothe Thyself, O Master, in the form of a servant, and wast circumcised in the flesh, granting unto all people Thy great mercy.[^630]

*Ode 4. — Heirmos:*
I have heard, O Lord, the report of Thy dispensation, and I glorified Thee, O only Lover of mankind.[^631]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
Circumcision ceased from the time Christ was circumcised voluntarily, saving the multitude of the nations by grace.[^632]

It prefigures the unending life of the eighth age to come, wherein the Master was circumcised in the flesh.[^633]

*Of Basil:*
Even as Christ's bride the Church is adorned by His nativity, so also is she adorned by thy memory, O all-blessed one.[^634]

Thou wast shown forth invincible, contending for God, O Basil, submitting in all things unto His commandment.[^635]

Thou wast given by God unto the Church as a defense and a firm wall, O all-blessed Basil.[^636]

Thou didst appear as a cleaving axe unto God's adversaries, and a fire devouring delusion, O father Basil.[^637]

*Theotokion:*
We pray thee, O pure one, who didst seedlessly conceive God, ever to intercede for thy servants.[^638]

*Katavasia:*
I have heard, O Lord, Thy voice which Thou didst speak, the voice of one crying in the wilderness, when Thou didst thunder over many waters, bearing witness unto Thy Son; being wholly filled with the descending Spirit, he cried out: Thou art Christ, the wisdom and power of God.[^639]

*The Second Choir:*
Purified by the fire of mystical vision, singing of the renewal of mankind, the prophet proclaims with a voice inspired by the Spirit, disclosing the incarnation of the ineffable Word, by Whom the dominions of the mighty were shattered.[^640]

*Ode 5. — Heirmos:*
O Giver of light and Creator of the ages, O Lord, guide us in the light of Thy commandments; for we know no other God besides Thee.[^641]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
Having ineffably come forth from the Virgin Mother, O Lord, Thou didst not disesteem to clothe Thyself in the human form; as an infant according to the law, Thou didst fulfill the law.[^642]

The mother that bore Israel was likened unto night, loving the legal shadow; out of which Christ, having shone forth as the Light of the world, appeared.[^643]

*Of Basil:*
The Church truly celebrates a twofold feast today: that of the circumcision of the Master Who appeared on earth as an infant, and the memory of His wise and all-blessed servant.[^644]

The unapproachable Light, having rested in thee, O venerable one, Christ showed thee forth unto the world as a most radiant lamp; therefore we sing of thy memory, O Basil.[^645]

Expounding from divine treasures the creation obscurely written by Moses at Sinai, thou didst delineate it unto all, O all-blessed Basil.[^646]

Neither Moses the lawgiver nor Joseph before the law was such a measurer of wheat for food as thou; for thou thyself wast shown forth as a distributor of the divine Flesh, O Basil.[^647]

*Theotokion:*
Unto thee who didst bear Christ, the Maker of all, we cry: Rejoice, O pure one; rejoice, thou who didst make the Light to shine upon us; rejoice, thou who didst contain the uncontainable God.[^648]

*Katavasia:*
Jesus, the Prince of Life, comes to loose the condemnation of Adam the first-formed; needing no purification as God, He cleanses the fallen in the Jordan, wherein, having slain the enmity, He grants peace passing all understanding.[^649]

*The Second Choir:*
Washed clean by the purification of the Spirit from the dark and defiled poison of the enemy, we have attained unto a new, unerring path, leading unto inaccessible joy, accessible only unto those with whom God has been reconciled.[^650]

*Ode 6. — Heirmos:*
A great abyss of sins surrounds me, and imitating the prophet I cry unto Thee: Bring me up from corruption, O Lord.[^651]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
The law has reached its end, since Christ, having become an infant, as Fulfiller of the law received circumcision, and loosed the curse of the law.[^652]

Sabbath-keeping and circumcision, the pride of the Hebrews, ceased at the nod of Christ Who appeared, and the spring of grace has dawned.[^653]

*Of Basil:*
Thou didst abundantly nourish the souls of the poor that were wasting with hunger, and didst fill the hearts of the starving with all divine gladness, O Basil.[^654]

Thou didst richly nourish the hungry with heavenly food, for thy word is the bread of angels, O Basil, whereby thou wast an excellent giver of wheat.[^655]

Like a bee, O Basil, thou didst painstakingly gather the flower of every virtue, and didst become skilled therein, wherein thou art counted blessed.[^656]

Thou didst hasten to separate thyself from the world and to live with God; therefore, having forsaken present and corruptible things, O all-blessed Basil, as wise thou didst acquire abiding things.[^657]

*Theotokion:*
Save thy servants from dangers, O Theotokos, for after God we all flee unto thee, as unto an unbreakable wall and intercession.[^658]

*Katavasia:*
The voice of the Word, the lamp of the Light, the morning star of the Sun, the Forerunner, cries in the wilderness unto all peoples: Repent and be purified beforehand; for behold, Christ is at hand, delivering the world from corruption.[^659]

*The Second Choir:*
With a most blessed voice the Father made manifest the Beloved Whom He brought forth from the womb: Yea, He says, this radiant One, being My consubstantial Son, has sprung forth from the human race: My living Word, and mortal by providence.[^660]

*Kontakion, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast appeared today...":*
Thou wast shown forth as an unshakable foundation of the Church, dispensing unto all mortals an inviolable dominion, sealing it by thy decrees, O heavenly-manifest Basil, venerable one.[^661]

*Ikos:*
A cup of chaste sobriety, the mouth of wisdom, and the pinnacle of teachings, Basil the Great mentally shines forth upon all; come therefore, let us stand in the house of God, fervently gazing, delighting in this saint's wonders, enlightened as by a ray of his life, refreshed by a pure spirit, imitating his faith, warmth, and humility, whereby he was shown to be a dwelling of the divine nature; unto whom crying aloud we sing: O heavenly-manifest Basil, venerable one.[^662]

*Ode 7. — Heirmos:*
When the golden image was worshiped on the plain of Dura, Thy three youths disregarded the godless com- mandment; but cast into the midst of the fire, bedewed they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^663]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
Radiant and most bright is Christ's nativity, which today prefigured the mystery of the renewal to come; for according to the legal order the Savior is circumcised, not as God, but as man, and as the fulfillment of the law.[^664]

Fulfilling the law, the Creator of the law is voluntarily circumcised today in the flesh, working the circumcision of the winter of sin, and granting us to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^665]

*Of Basil:*
The Son is the image of the Father, and the Spirit is of the Son; and thou, O Basil, art an unblemished mirror of the Spirit, and a dwelling of the whole Trinity; blessed is thy memory, and those who glorify it are glorified in turn.[^666]

Plunging thy mind into the unsearchable depths of God, and having gathered the pearl of great price of understanding, thou didst fill the world with wisdom, and didst teach us to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^667]

O single breathing of mind of the sacred pair! Like a single soul in two bodies indivisibly, Basil in Gregory, united by divine desire, and Gregory in Basil: pray now unto Christ for your flock.[^668]

A father of orphans, and protector of widows, and wealth of the poor, consolation of the sick, and pilot of those in wealth, a staff of old age wast thou shown forth, and instruction of youth, and rule of virtue for monastics, O Basil.[^669]

Having cleansed thy mind from every miry defilement, O Basil, thou didst perceive spiritual things with thy longing spirit; therefore, hymning the Trinity, with boldness thou didst cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^670]

*Theotokion:*
Moses foresaw thee on Mount Sinai, O pure one, as the burning bush that was not consumed, having unburnt received the unbearable radiance of the ineffable Essence, which united with the grossness of flesh in the one of His holy hypostases.[^671]

*Katavasia:*
The sounding of the dew-laden wind and the descent of the angel of God preserved unharmed the godly youths who were cast into the fiery furnace; therefore, bedewed in the midst of the flame, they thankfully sang: O most-praised Lord and God of our fathers, blessed art Thou.[^672]

*The Second Choir:*
He who quenched the lofty flame of the furnace which bore the godly youths, and washed away all the intractable murky gloom of sin with the dew of the Spirit, has consumed the heads of the dragons in the stream.[^673]

*Ode 8. — Heirmos:*
Bless the Lord, ye works, as Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages: God the Word, Who by ineffable wisdom composed all things, and brought them from non-existence into being.[^674]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
On the eighth day the Master is circumcised as an infant, and receives the name Jesus, for He is Savior and Lord of the world.[^675]

Unto Christ's nativity the eighth day is worthily yoked with the all-glorious memory of the hierarch, honoring which in faith we hymn the Lord and supremely exalt Him unto all ages.[^676]

*Of Basil:*
Bless the Lord, ye works of the Lord, Who by ineffable providence bestowed Basil upon the world as a light of piety and a trumpet of theology.[^677]

Bless the Lord, ye works of the Lord, Who by rich working dwelt in wise Basil, and through him piously theologized.[^678]

Having delighted in thy divine words, O Basil, continually feasting in thy memory we cry: Bless the Lord, ye works of the Lord.[^679]

Thou didst send forth rays of words unto the world, O Basil, guiding all by their light to venerate the one Trinitarian nature: Bless the Lord, ye works of the Lord.[^680]

*Theotokion:*
Bless the Lord, ye works of the Lord: God the Word, Who came forth from the Father before the ages and is timeless, and in these last days shone forth from the Virgin.[^681]

*Katavasia:*
The Babylonian furnace, pouring forth dew, prefigured a most glorious mystery: how the Jordan was to receive in its streams the immaterial fire, and encompass the Creator being baptized in the flesh; Whom the peoples bless and supremely exalt unto all ages.[^682]

*Another [Katavasia]:*
Creation is acknowledged as truly free, and sons of light those who were formerly darkened; the ruler of darkness alone groans; now let the formerly wretched inheritance of the nations fervently bless the Cause.[^683]

*At Ode 9, we do not sing More Honorable, but we sing the Refrains of the Circumcision:*
Magnify, O my soul, the Lord Who according to the law was circumcised in the flesh.

*Ode 9. — Heirmos:*
Thee Who ineffably didst incarnate God the Luminary, Who shone forth before the sun and came unto us in the flesh from a virginal womb, O blessed and all-pure Theotokos, we magnify.[^684]

*Then the second choir sings the same Refrain and Heirmos, and the other three refrains we chant unto the Troparia:*
Magnify, O my soul, Him Who on the eighth day received circumcision. (Twice)
Today the Master is circumcised in the flesh as an infant, fulfilling the law.
Today the Master was circumcised in the flesh, and was named Jesus.[^685]

*Troparia:*
Having surpassed the bounds of all human nature, Christ is supernaturally born of the Virgin; and as the written law commands, He is circumcised in the flesh and is shown forth as the fulfiller of the law.[^686]

Come, let us celebrate in holiness the glorious names of the Master Christ, for today He is divinely named Jesus; and together with these, we magnify the memory [of the hierarch].[^687]

*Then the refrains of Basil unto his troparia:*
Magnify, O my soul, Basil the Great among hierarchs. (Twice)
Magnify, O my soul, the most radiant luminary of the universe. (Twice)
Magnify, O my soul, Basil the Great from Caesarea. (Twice)

*And this instead of Glory:*
Magnify, O my soul, him who adorned the Church of Christ.

*Instead of Both now:*
Magnify, O my soul, the Virgin all-pure Theotokos, more honorable than the heavenly hosts.[^688]

*Troparia of Basil:*
As a faithful sheep of Christ, O Basil, thou didst follow the life-bearing footsteps of the Chief Shepherd; for thou didst surrender thy soul unto the tormentor, O father, valiantly imperiling thyself for the Church, O all-blessed one.[^689]

Having seen the all-sacred assembly of the Church of Christ adorned by thy office as hierarch, O wise one, the all-destroying tormentor, struck as by thunder, fell down; for he could not endure the radiance of the Spirit that was within thee, O Basil.[^690]

Thou wast vouchsafed the seat of the Apostles, the company of the passion-bearers of Christ, the tent of the patriarchs, the sweetness of the righteous, and the joy of the prophets, O Basil; for thou wast a servant of the Theotokos and an initiate of the Trinity.[^691]

*Theotokion:*
The only Lord, Who covers His upper chambers with waters, Who restrains the sea and dries up the depths, having received pure flesh from thee, hastens from Bethlehem to the Jordan to be baptized in the flesh.[^692]

*Then both choirs, having come together, sing the first Refrain of the Circumcision, and the Heirmos:*
Every tongue is at a loss to praise thee worthily, and even a mind beyond the world is bewildered to hymn thee, O Theotokos; yet, being good, accept our faith, for thou knowest our divine love, for thou art the protectress of Christians: thee we magnify.[^693]

*Then the Refrain:*
Magnify, O my soul, the Virgin all-pure Theotokos, more honorable than the heavenly hosts.

*And the Heirmos:*
O the wonders of thy childbearing beyond understanding! O all-pure bride, blessed Mother, through whom having obtained all-perfect salvation, we worthily praise Him as Benefactor, bringing as a gift a hymn of thanksgiving.[^694]

*Exapostilarion. — Podoben: By the Spirit in the sanctuary:*
Through the desire of love of wisdom, O father, thou didst circumcise the veil of thy soul, and wast shown forth unto the world like a sun by wonders; and thou didst illumine the thoughts of the faithful, O divinely wise initiate of the Trinity, O wise Basil, and servant of the Theotokos. (Twice)[^695]

*Glory; Both now: Of the Circumcision:*
He Who fulfills the law is circumcised in the flesh as an eight-day-old infant, the Creator of the ages; and He is wrapped in swaddling clothes as a man, and is nourished with milk, He Who holds all things by His measureless power as God, and raises them up by His nod.[^696]

*At the Praises, Stichera on 6.*
*Tone 5. Podoben: Rejoice...*
He Who was ineffably begotten as the Word of the Father without severing or corruption, and Who as God from God abides in immutable Godhead, endures circumcision in the flesh; therefore, having come under the law though being above the law, He delivers all from the curse of the law, and grants blessing from on high; therefore, praising His most-good condescension, we sing and thankfully glorify Him, entreating Him to grant our souls great mercy. (Twice)[^697]

Having become a son of God by grace through the rebirth of divine baptism, thou didst confess Him Who by nature and truth is the co-eternal Son of God and Word, consubstantial with the Father and co-unoriginate, O venerable one; and the unstopped mouths of heretics thou didst close with the brightness of thy words; therefore also thou didst take up dwelling in the heavenly kingdoms, reigning together with Christ Who alone reigns by nature, Who richly dispenses great mercy unto the world.[^698]

Within the heavenly temple, as a consecrated hierarch thou hast found room, vested as with a sacred vestment in action and contemplation, the principles of wisdom; and now ministering as priest at the altar on high, O venerable one, and standing before God and celebrating the immaterial service, mercifully remember, O all-blessed Basil, those who celebrate thy sacred and all-honorable memory, praying unto Christ, Who grants great mercy unto the world.[^699]

Wholly consecrated unto God, and in every way dedicated from childhood, illumined with the rays of transcendent wisdom, thou didst make clear the understanding of existing things, lucidly expounding and wisely narrating, bringing good order to existing things, an action of most divine knowledge; therefore we proclaim thee a divinely speaking and divine teacher, and a light-bearing luminary of the Church, praying unto Christ, Who grants great mercy unto the world.[^700]

Established by the fear of God, thou didst understand the beginning of wisdom, O Basil; by wisdom and by uprightness of life thou didst transcend fear, O father; and having been commingled with God in love, thou becamest most intimate with Him; therefore thou wast instructed in divine ordinances and judgments; thou didst learn the ways of men, and didst adorn the minds of the faithful, illuminating the assembly by spiritual statutes; pray therefore unto Christ to grant our souls great mercy.[^701]

*Glory, Tone 6:*
Grace was poured forth upon thy lips, O venerable father; and thou becamest a shepherd of Christ's Church, teaching the rational sheep to believe in the consubstantial Trinity, in one Godhead.[^702]

*Both now, Tone 8:*
The Savior, descending unto the human race, accepted swaddling clothes, and did not abhor circumcision in the flesh, being eight days old according to His mother, unoriginate according to the Father; unto Him let us, the faithful, cry aloud: Thou art our God, have mercy on us.[^703]

*After the Great Doxology: Troparion of Basil:* Into all the earth... *Glory; Both now: Of the Circumcision:* On a fiery throne... (see p. 179)[^704]. *And the Dismissal.*

## Divine Liturgy on the Circumcision of Our Lord Jesus Christ and St. Basil the Great

*At the entrance: Troparion of the Circumcision:* On a fiery throne... (see p. 179)[^705]. *And of Basil:* Into all the earth... (p. 179)[^706]. *Glory: Kontakion of Basil:* Thou hast appeared... (p. 192)[^707]. *Both now: Of the Circumcision:* The Lord of all... (p. 187)[^708].

*Prokeimenon, Tone 6:*
**Reader:** O Lord, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance.
**Choir:** O Lord, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance.
**Reader:** *Verse:* Unto Thee, O Lord, will I cry; O my God, be not silent unto me.
**Choir:** O Lord, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance.[^709]

*Prokeimenon of Basil, Tone 1:*
**Reader:** My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart understanding.
**Choir:** My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart understanding.[^710]

*Epistle to the Colossians, Pericope 254 [Colossians 2:8–12]:*
**Reader:** Brethren, beware lest anyone spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, according to the tradition of men, according to the rudiments of the world, and not according to Christ. For in Him dwells all the fullness of the Godhead bodily; and you are complete in Him, Who is the head of all principality and power; in Whom also you were circumcised with the circumcision made without hands, in putting off the body of the sins of the flesh by the circumcision of Christ; buried with Him in baptism, wherein also you were raised with Him through faith in the working of God, Who raised Him from the dead.[^711]

*And of Basil:*
*Epistle to the Hebrews, Pericope 318 [Hebrews 7:26–8:2]:*
**Reader:** Brethren, such a High Priest was fitting for us: holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and become higher than the heavens;

Who has no need daily, as the high priests, to offer up sacrifice first for His own sins, and then for those of the people; for this He did once for all, having offered up Himself. For the law appoints men high priests having infirmity; but the word of the oath, which was after the law, appoints the Son, perfected unto ages of ages. Now of the things which are spoken this is the sum: We have such a High Priest, Who sat down on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens, a minister of the sanctuary and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.[^712]

*Alleluia, Tone 8:*
**Choir:** Alleluia, alleluia, alleluia.
**Reader:** *Verse:* Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, Thou that leadest Joseph like a sheep.
**Choir:** Alleluia, alleluia, alleluia.
**Reader:** *Verse:* The mouth of the righteous shall meditate on wisdom, and his tongue shall speak of judgment.
**Choir:** Alleluia, alleluia, alleluia.[^713]

*Gospel according to Luke, Pericope 6 [Luke 2:20–21, 40–52]:*
**Deacon:** Let us attend.
**Priest:** Peace be unto all.
**Choir:** And to thy spirit.
**Deacon:** The reading is from the Holy Gospel according to Luke.
**Choir:** Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.
**Deacon:** At that time, the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, as it was told unto them. And when eight days were accomplished for the circumcising of the Child, His name was called Jesus, which was so named of the angel before He was conceived in the womb. And the Child grew, and waxed strong in spirit, filled with wisdom; and the grace of God was upon Him. Now His parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the Passover. And when He was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem after the custom of the feast. And when they had fulfilled the days, as they returned, the Boy Jesus tarried behind in Jerusalem; and Joseph and His mother knew it not.

But supposing Him to have been in the company, they went a day's journey; and they sought Him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance. And when they found Him not, they turned back to Jerusalem, seeking Him. And it came to pass, that after three days they found Him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers, both hearing them, and asking them questions. And all that heard Him were astonished at His understanding and answers. And when they saw Him, they were amazed; and His mother said unto Him: Son, why hast Thou thus dealt with us? Behold, Thy father and I have sought Thee sorrowing. And He said unto them: How is it that ye sought Me? Wist ye not that I must be about My Father's business? And they understood not the saying which He spoke unto them. And He went down with them, and came to Nazareth, and was subject unto them; but His mother kept all these sayings in her heart. And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.[^714]

*And of Basil:*
*Gospel according to Luke, Pericope 24 [Luke 6:17–23]:*
**Deacon:** Let us attend.
**Priest:** Peace be unto all.
**Choir:** And to thy spirit.
**Deacon:** The reading is from the Holy Gospel according to Luke.
**Choir:** Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.
**Deacon:** At that time, Jesus stood on a level place, and a crowd of His disciples, and a great multitude of people from all Judea and Jerusalem, and from the seacoast of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear Him and to be healed of their diseases, and those who were troubled with unclean spirits; and they were healed. And the whole multitude sought to touch Him, for power went out from Him and healed them all. And He lifted up His eyes on His disciples, and said: Blessed are the poor in spirit, for yours is the kingdom of God. Blessed are ye that hunger now, for ye shall be filled. Blessed are ye that weep now, for ye shall laugh. Blessed are ye when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you from their company, and shall reproach you, and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of Man's sake. Rejoice ye in that day, and leap for joy; for behold, your reward is great in heaven.[^715]

*Instead of "It is truly meet", we sing:*
**Choir:** In thee all creation rejoices, O full of grace: the angelic assembly and the human race; O sanctified temple and rational paradise, boast of virgins, from whom God was incarnate and became a child, Who is our God before the ages! For He made thy womb a throne, and made thy body more spacious than the heavens. In thee all creation rejoices, O full of grace: glory to thee![^716]

*Or the Refrain:*
Magnify, O my soul, the Lord Who according to the law was circumcised in the flesh.

*The Second Refrain of Saint Basil:*
Magnify, O my soul, Basil the Great from Caesarea.

*Heirmos:*
Thee Who ineffably didst incarnate God the Luminary, Who shone forth before the sun and came unto us in the flesh from a virginal womb, O blessed and all-pure Theotokos, we magnify.[^717]

*Communion Hymn:*
**Choir:** Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest.[^718]

*Another:*
**Choir:** The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance; he shall not be afraid of evil tidings. Alleluia.[^719]

*If the Circumcision of the Lord and of Saint Basil the Great fall on a Sunday:*
*At Great Vespers: At "Lord, I have cried", we chant 3 Resurrection stichera, and 3 of the Circumcision, and 4 of Basil. Glory: Of Basil:* O lover of wisdom... *Both now: Theotokion of the Tone of the week. Entrance. Prokeimenon. And 3 readings. At the Litiya: Sticheron of the temple, and of Saint Basil. Glory: Of Basil. Both now: Of the Circumcision. At the Aposticha, stichera of the Octoechos. Glory: Of Basil. Both now: Of the Circumcision. At the blessing of loaves, Troparion of Basil twice, and "Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos" once.*

*At Matins: At "God is the Lord": Resurrection troparion twice; Glory: Of Basil; Both now: Of the Circumcision. After the kathismata, Resurrection sessional hymns with their Theotokia. After the Polyeleos, Troparia:* The angelic host... *and the Hypakoe of the Tone, and all the Sessional Hymns of Basil, each once. Glory: Sessional Hymn after the Polyeleos; Both now: Of the Circumcision. Gradual hymns, and the Prokeimenon of the Tone:* Let every breath... *Resurrectional Gospel;* Having beheld the resurrection of Christ... *and Psalm 50. Resurrectional sticheron. Resurrectional canon with the Heirmos on 4, and of the Circumcision on 4, and of Basil on 6. Katavasia:* He uncovered the bottom of the deep... *and* He travels through the surging deep... *each choir its own Heirmos. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the Circumcision, and Sessional Hymn of Basil twice; Glory; Both now: Of the Circumcision. After Ode 6, Kontakion of Basil and the Ikos, and we read the Prologue. At Ode 9, we sing "More honorable..."; the Refrains of the Circumcision and of Basil we do not sing. Resurrectional Exapostilarion. Glory: Of Basil; Both now: Of the Circumcision. At the Praises, 4 Resurrection stichera, and 4 Prosomoia of Basil with his customary refrains. Glory: Gospel sticheron; Both now:* Most blessed art thou... *Great Doxology: Resurrection troparion. Litanies and Dismissal.*[^720]

*At the Divine Liturgy: After the Entrance, Troparion of the Resurrection, and of the Circumcision, and of Basil. Glory: Kontakion of Basil; Both now: Of the Circumcision. Prokeimenon of the Sunday before the Theophany (Enlightenment), Tone 6:* Save, O Lord, Thy people... *Verse:* Unto Thee, O Lord, will I cry... *And of Basil, Tone 1:* My mouth shall speak of wisdom... *Epistle of the Sunday before the Theophany: To Timothy, Pericope 298. Another Epistle: To the Colossians, Pericope 254. Alleluia, Tone 8, of the Sunday before the Theophany:* God be gracious unto us... *Verse:* O Shepherd of Israel... *Gospel of the Sunday before the Theophany: Of Mark, Pericope 1; and of the Circumcision, Pericope 6. Communion Hymn:* Praise the Lord... *Another:* In everlasting remembrance... *Liturgy of Basil the Great. Instead of "It is truly meet":* In thee all creation rejoices...[^721]
