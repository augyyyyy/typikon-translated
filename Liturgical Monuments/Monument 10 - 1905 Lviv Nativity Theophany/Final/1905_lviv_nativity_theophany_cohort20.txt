*Katavasia: the First Choir sings the Heirmos:*
The Lord mighty in battle uncovered the bottom of the deep, and led His own across dry land; in it He covered their adversaries, for He has been glorified.[^616]

*The Second Choir:* *Heirmos:*
Israel traversed the surging storm of the sea, which immediately appeared as dry land; but the dark deep, a watery tomb, covered together the Egyptian captains, by the mighty strength of the Master's right hand.[^617]

*Ode 3. — Heirmos:*
Establish us in Thee, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put sin to death; and plant Thy fear in the hearts of us who sing unto Thee.[^618]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
The transcendent Word, having become incarnate, was circumcised for the cessation of the law; and He gave unto us the firstfruits of divine grace and incorruptible life.[^619]

As Fulfiller of the law, not as an adversary of God, Christ having become incarnate was shown forth; and He deigned voluntarily to be circumcised on the eighth day.[^620]

*Of Basil:*
Thy memory, O father, concurred with Christ's nativity and shone forth with it; Whose ineffable mystery thou didst make manifest through thy teachings.[^621]

Established by the fear of the Lord—for this is the beginning of wisdom—thou wast winged with a higher love for supreme wisdom, O Basil.[^622]

Being filled with all learning, not only of the earthly and transient, but far more of the superior, thou didst appear as a light unto the world, O Basil.[^623]

=== LEAF p192 ===
By action thou didst enter in, O all-wise Basil, and didst show action to be the ascent unto most divine contemplation; and thou wast clearly taught the knowledge of existing things.[^624]

*Theotokion:*
Seedlessly thou didst conceive God in thy womb, and ineffably dost bear Him incarnate, upon Whom the pure heavenly hosts dare not gaze, O Ever-Virgin.[^625]

*Katavasia: the First Choir:*
The Lord, Who gives strength unto our kings and exalts the horn of His anointed ones, is born of a Virgin, and comes unto baptism; unto Him let us, the faithful, cry aloud: None is holy as our God.[^626]

*The Second Choir:*
As many of us as have been delivered from the ancient snares of lions' jaws, their teeth crushed: let us rejoice and open wide our mouth, weaving a hymn out of sweet-singing words, delighting in the gifts bestowed upon us.[^627]

*Kontakion of the Circumcision: Tone 3. Podoben: "The Virgin today...":*
The Lord of all endures circumcision, and as good circumcises the transgressions of mortals; He grants salvation today unto the world, and on high rejoices also the Creator's hierarch and light-bearing, divine initiate of Christ, Basil.[^628]

*Glory; Both now: Of the Circumcision: Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast appeared today...":*
As being an abyss of love for mankind, Thou didst clothe Thyself, O Master, in the form of a servant, and wast circumcised in the flesh, granting unto all people Thy great mercy.[^630]

*Ode 4. — Heirmos:*
I have heard, O Lord, the report of Thy dispensation, and I glorified Thee, O only Lover of mankind.[^631]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
Circumcision ceased from the time Christ was circumcised voluntarily, saving the multitude of the nations by grace.[^632]

It prefigures the unending life of the eighth age to come, wherein the Master was circumcised in the flesh.[^633]

*Of Basil:*
Even as Christ's bride the Church is adorned by His nativity, so also is she adorned by thy memory, O all-blessed one.[^634]

Thou wast shown forth invincible, contending for God, O Basil, submitting in all things unto His commandment.[^635]

Thou wast given by God unto the Church as a defense and a firm wall, O all-blessed Basil.[^636]

Thou didst appear as a cleaving axe unto God's adversaries, and a fire devouring delusion, O father Basil.[^637]

*Theotokion:*
We pray thee, O pure one, who didst seedlessly conceive God, ever to intercede for thy servants.[^638]

*Katavasia:*
I have heard, O Lord, Thy voice which Thou didst speak, the voice of one crying in the wilderness, when Thou didst thunder over many waters, bearing witness unto Thy Son; being wholly filled with the descending Spirit, he cried out: Thou art Christ, the wisdom and power of God.[^639]

*The Second Choir:*
Purified by the fire of mystical vision, singing of the renewal of mankind, the prophet proclaims with a voice inspired by the Spirit, disclosing the incarnation of the ineffable Word, by Whom the dominions of the mighty were shattered.[^640]

*Ode 5. — Heirmos:*
O Giver of light and Creator of the ages, O Lord, guide us in the light of Thy commandments; for we know no other God besides Thee.[^641]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
Having ineffably come forth from the Virgin Mother, O Lord, Thou didst not disesteem to clothe Thyself in the human form; as an infant according to the law, Thou didst fulfill the law.[^642]

The mother that bore Israel was likened unto night, loving the legal shadow; out of which Christ, having shone forth as the Light of the world, appeared.[^643]

*Of Basil:*
The Church truly celebrates a twofold feast today: that of the circumcision of the Master Who appeared on earth as an infant, and the memory of His wise and all-blessed servant.[^644]

=== LEAF p195 ===
The unapproachable Light, having rested in thee, O venerable one, Christ showed thee forth unto the world as a most radiant lamp; therefore we sing of thy memory, O Basil.[^645]

Expounding from divine treasures the creation obscurely written by Moses at Sinai, thou didst delineate it unto all, O all-blessed Basil.[^646]

Neither Moses the lawgiver nor Joseph before the law was such a measurer of wheat for food as thou; for thou thyself wast shown forth as a distributor of the divine Flesh, O Basil.[^647]

*Theotokion:*
Unto thee who didst bear Christ, the Maker of all, we cry: Rejoice, O pure one; rejoice, thou who didst make the Light to shine upon us; rejoice, thou who didst contain the uncontainable God.[^648]

*Katavasia:*
Jesus, the Prince of Life, comes to loose the condemnation of Adam the first-formed; needing no purification as God, He cleanses the fallen in the Jordan, wherein, having slain the enmity, He grants peace passing all understanding.[^649]

*The Second Choir:*
Washed clean by the purification of the Spirit from the dark and defiled poison of the enemy, we have attained unto a new, unerring path, leading unto inaccessible joy, accessible only unto those with whom God has been reconciled.[^650]

*Ode 6. — Heirmos:*
A great abyss of sins surrounds me, and imitating the prophet I cry unto Thee: Bring me up from corruption, O Lord.[^651]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
The law has reached its end, since Christ, having become an infant, as Fulfiller of the law received circumcision, and loosed the curse of the law.[^652]

Sabbath-keeping and circumcision, the pride of the Hebrews, ceased at the nod of Christ Who appeared, and the spring of grace has dawned.[^653]

*Of Basil:*
Thou didst abundantly nourish the souls of the poor that were wasting with hunger, and didst fill the hearts of the starving with all divine gladness, O Basil.[^654]

Thou didst richly nourish the hungry with heavenly food, for thy word is the bread of angels, O Basil, whereby thou wast an excellent giver of wheat.[^655]

Like a bee, O Basil, thou didst painstakingly gather the flower of every virtue, and didst become skilled therein, wherein thou art counted blessed.[^656]

Thou didst hasten to separate thyself from the world and to live with God; therefore, having forsaken present and corruptible things, O all-blessed Basil, as wise thou didst acquire abiding things.[^657]

*Theotokion:*
Save thy servants from dangers, O Theotokos, for after God we all flee unto thee, as unto an unbreakable wall and intercession.[^658]

*Katavasia:*
The voice of the Word, the lamp of the Light, the morning star of the Sun, the Forerunner, cries in the wilderness unto all peoples: Repent and be purified beforehand; for behold, Christ is at hand, delivering the world from corruption.[^659]

*The Second Choir:*
With a most blessed voice the Father made manifest the Beloved Whom He brought forth from the womb: Yea, He says, this radiant One, being My consubstantial Son, has sprung forth from the human race: My living Word, and mortal by providence.[^660]

*Kontakion, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast appeared today...":*
Thou wast shown forth as an unshakable foundation of the Church, dispensing unto all mortals an inviolable dominion, sealing it by thy decrees, O heavenly-manifest Basil, venerable one.[^661]

*Ikos:*
A cup of chaste sobriety, the mouth of wisdom, and the pinnacle of teachings, Basil the Great mentally shines forth upon all; come therefore, let us stand in the house of God, fervently gazing, delighting in this saint's wonders, enlightened as by a ray of his life, refreshed by a pure spirit, imitating his faith, warmth, and humility, whereby he was shown to be a dwelling of the divine nature; unto whom crying aloud we sing: O heavenly-manifest Basil, venerable one.[^662]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
Radiant and most bright is Christ's nativity, which today prefigured the mystery of the renewal to come; for according to the legal order the Savior is circumcised, not as God, but as man, and as the fulfillment of the law.[^664]

Fulfilling the law, the Creator of the law is voluntarily circumcised today in the flesh, working the circumcision of the winter of sin, and granting us to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^665]

*Of Basil:*
The Son is the image of the Father, and the Spirit is of the Son; and thou, O Basil, art an unblemished mirror of the Spirit, and a dwelling of the whole Trinity; blessed is thy memory, and those who glorify it are glorified in turn.[^666]

Plunging thy mind into the unsearchable depths of God, and having gathered the pearl of great price of understanding, thou didst fill the world with wisdom, and didst teach us to cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^667]

O single breathing of mind of the sacred pair! Like a single soul in two bodies indivisibly, Basil in Gregory, united by divine desire, and Gregory in Basil: pray now unto Christ for your flock.[^668]

A father of orphans, and protector of widows, and wealth of the poor, consolation of the sick, and pilot of those in wealth, a staff of old age wast thou shown forth, and instruction of youth, and rule of virtue for monastics, O Basil.[^669]

Having cleansed thy mind from every miry defilement, O Basil, thou didst perceive spiritual things with thy longing spirit; therefore, hymning the Trinity, with boldness thou didst cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.[^670]

*Theotokion:*
Moses foresaw thee on Mount Sinai, O pure one, as the burning bush that was not consumed, having unburnt received the unbearable radiance of the ineffable Essence, which united with the grossness of flesh in the one of His holy hypostases.[^671]

*Katavasia:*
The sounding of the dew-laden wind and the descent of the angel of God preserved unharmed the godly youths who were cast into the fiery furnace; therefore, bedewed in the midst of the flame, they thankfully sang: O most-praised Lord and God of our fathers, blessed art Thou.[^672]

*The Second Choir:*
He who quenched the lofty flame of the furnace which bore the godly youths, and washed away all the intractable murky gloom of sin with the dew of the Spirit, has consumed the heads of the dragons in the stream.[^673]

*Troparia of the Circumcision:*
On the eighth day the Master is circumcised as an infant, and receives the name Jesus, for He is Savior and Lord of the world.[^675]

*Katavasia:*
