# PART III: POST-FEAST OF THE NATIVITY (SYNAXIS OF THE MOST HOLY MOTHER OF GOD & ST. JOSEPH AND ST. STEPHEN THE FIRST-MARTYR)

## Synaxis of the Most Holy Mother of God and Holy Joseph the Betrothed

*At Vespers, if it falls on a weekday, except Monday:*[^216]
*The usual beginning; Psalm 103 (see p. 44);[^217] the Great Litany: "In peace let us pray to the Lord" (see p. 47);[^218] after this, Psalm 140: "Lord, I have cried" (see p. 48)[^219] with 8 Stichera of the Feast, Tone 2, namely: "Come, let us rejoice in the Lord," twice (see p. 50);[^220] "The Lord Jesus being born," twice; "Thy Kingdom, O Christ God," twice (see p. 51);[^221] "What shall we offer Thee," twice (see p. 52).[^222]*
*Glory... Both now... Tone 6:*[^223]
Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace! Today Bethlehem receives Him Who sits ever with the Father; today the angels divinely glorify the newborn Babe: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will among men!
*After this, "O Gladsome Light," with the Entrance (see p. 52).[^224]*
*After the Entrance, the Great Prokeimenon of the Feast, Tone 7:*[^225]
Who is a great God as our God? Thou art the God Who doest wonders!

*Verse:* Thou hast made Thy power known among the peoples; and with Thine arm hast Thou redeemed Thy people.[^226]
*Verse:* And I said: Now have I begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High.[^227]
*Verse:* I remembered the works of the Lord; for I will remember Thy wonders from the beginning.[^228]
*Litany:* Let us all say (see p. 62).[^229] — Vouchsafe, O Lord (p. 63).[^230] — *Litany:* Let us complete [our evening] prayer (p. 63).[^231] — *Then, at the Aposticha:*[^232]
*Tone 8:*
A most glorious mystery is accomplished today: nature is renewed, and God becomes man; what He was, He remained, and what He was not, He assumed, undergoing neither confusion nor division.[^233]
*Verse:* Out of the womb before the morning star have I begotten Thee; the Lord has sworn, and will not repent.[^234]
O Lord, coming to Bethlehem, Thou didst dwell in a cave; having heaven as Thy throne, Thou wast laid in a manger; whom the angelic hosts surround, unto the shepherds Thou didst condescend, that Thou mightest save our race as Thou art compassionate: glory to Thee![^235]
*Verse:* The Lord said unto my Lord: Sit Thou at My right hand.[^236]
How shall I declare the great mystery? The Incorporeal takes flesh; the Word becomes physical; the Invisible is seen, and the Intangible is touched; and the Unoriginate begins; the Son of God becomes the Son of Man: Jesus Christ, yesterday and today, the same also unto the ages.[^237]
*Glory... Both now... The same Tone:*[^238]
Unto Bethlehem the shepherds ran together, proclaiming the true Shepherd Who sits upon the che- -rubim, and who reclines in a manger, having assumed the form of an infant for our sake: O Lord, glory to Thee![^239]
*After this, "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart" (p. 89).[^240] The Trisagion; and after "Our Father," the Priest:*
**Priest:** For Thine is the kingdom...
*And the Troparion to Joseph, Tone 4:*[^241]
Today the Church celebrates with gladness thy memory, O chaste Joseph, beloved of Christ God, Betrothed of His Mother; for the Father's pre-eternal Word, Who was ineffably incarnate in the virginal womb, worthily found thee to be for Himself on earth a nourisher and guardian: pray unto Him, together with His most pure Mother and thine all-immaculate Betrothed, that He may save our souls.[^242]
*Glory... Both now:*
Thy Nativity, O Christ... (see p. 121).[^243]
*And the Dismissal.*[^244]
*If the Synaxis falls on Sunday, that is, the Sunday after the Nativity, on which we celebrate the memory of the Holy Righteous Ancestors of God: after the Great Litany we take "Blessed is the man" (see p. 61);[^245] and at "Lord, I have cried," 10 Stichera: 3 Resurrectional of the occurring tone, and 4 of the Feast: "Come, let us rejoice in the Lord" (see p. 50);[^246] "The Lord Jesus being born" (p. 51);[^247] — "Thy Kingdom" (p. 51);[^248] "What shall we offer Thee, O Christ" (p. 52);[^249] and 3 of the Saints:*[^250]
*Tone 4. Podoben: Of the heavenly hosts:*[^251]
Let us all praise the Ancestor of God, David the King, for from him came forth the rod, the Virgin, and from her blossomed forth Christ, and He recalled Adam with Eve from corruption, for He is compassionate.[^252]

Joseph the Betrothed in his old age clearly saw the prophetic proclamations visibly fulfilled, a truly strange betrothal, having received revelation by angels who cry: Glory to God, Who has sent down peace upon the earth![^253]
Let us all praise the Brother of the Lord, as being a hierarch, and again having shone forth valiantly by martyrdom; by whose prayers, O Jesus our God, Who wast swaddled in swaddling clothes in the cave, save all who sing unto Thee.[^254]
*Glory... Tone 6:*[^255]
We celebrate the memory of the pious David and James, the prophet-king and the apostle, the first bishop; for having been delivered from deception by their teachings, we glorify Christ Who shone forth and was incarnate of the Virgin, that He might save our souls.
*Both now... Theotokion of the Tone.*[^256]
*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the Day:*[^257]
The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.
*Verse:* The Lord is robed in power, and has girt Himself.
*Verse:* For He has established the world, which shall not be moved.
*Verse:* Holiness becomes Thy house, O Lord, unto length of days.
*Then the Litany: "Let us all say" (p. 62).[^258] "Vouchsafe, O Lord" (p. 63),[^259] and the Resurrectional Stichera at the Aposticha.*[^260]
*Glory... Tone 6:*[^261]
Thy Nativity has become the memory of the hallowed, the dominion of kings, and gladness; and boasting in it, we say: Our Father Who art in the heavens, hallowed be Thy name, O Lover of mankind!

*Both now... The same Tone:*[^262]
Today the invisible nature is united to men from the Virgin; today the immeasurable essence in Bethlehem is wrapped in swaddling clothes; today God by a star leads the Magi unto worship, prefiguring His three-day burial as in gold, myrrh, and frankincense, unto Whom we sing: O Christ God incarnate of the Virgin, save our souls!
*At the conclusion:*[^263] *Resurrectional Troparion.* *Glory:* "Thy Nativity has become the memory of the hallowed..."[^264] *Both now:* "Thy Nativity, O Christ our God..." *And the Great Dismissal.*[^265]
*If the Synaxis falls on Monday, we take also together the Service of the Saints of the Sunday after the Nativity.[^266] We do not sing "Blessed is the man"; at "Lord, I have cried," 6 Stichera: namely, 3 of the Nativity: "Come, let us rejoice in the Lord" (see p. 50);[^267] "The Lord Jesus being born"; "Thy Kingdom, O Christ God" (see p. 51);[^268] and 3 to the Ancestors of God: "Let us all praise the Ancestor of God" (p. 127);[^269] "Joseph the Betrothed clearly saw"; "Let us all praise the Brother of the Lord" (p. 128).[^270] Glory: "We celebrate the memory of David" (p. 128).[^271] Both now: "Glory to God in the highest" (see p. 103).[^272]*
*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the Nativity:*[^273]
Who is a great God as our God? Thou art the God Who doest wonders!
*Then the Litany: "Let us all say," and the rest as on page 62.[^274] — The Aposticha of the Nativity (p. 88).[^275] Glory... Both now: "Thy Nativity has become the memory of the hallowed..." At the conclusion:*[^276]
*Troparion:*[^277]
Proclaim the wonders unto David the Ancestor of God, O Joseph: thou hast seen the Virgin giving birth; with the shepherds thou hast given glory; with the Magi thou hast worshiped, having received word from an angel: pray unto Christ God to save our souls.
*Glory... Both now: of the Nativity; and the Great Dismissal.*[^278]

*Matins*[^279]
*If it falls on a weekday, except Monday:*[^280]
**Priest:** Blessed is our God...
**Choir:** Amen. Heavenly King; Trisagion; Our Father; Lord, have mercy (12 times); Glory... Both now: Come, let us worship (thrice).[^281]
*Psalm 19:*[^282]
The Lord give ear to Thee in the day of affliction; the name of the God of Jacob defend Thee.
May He send thee help from the sanctuary, and uphold thee out of Sion.
May He remember every sacrifice of thine, and may thy whole-burnt offering be fat.
May the Lord give Thee according to Thy heart, and fulfill all Thy counsel.
We will rejoice in Thy salvation, and in the name of the Lord our God we will be magnified.
May the Lord fulfill all Thy petitions; now have I known that the Lord has saved His Anointed.
He will give ear to him from His holy heaven; the salvation of His right hand is in mighty deeds.
These trust in chariots, and these in horses; but we will call upon the name of the Lord our God.
They were bound and fell; but we have arisen and stood upright.
O Lord, save the king, and give ear to us in the day whensoever we call upon Thee.
*Psalm 20:*[^283]
O Lord, in Thy strength the king shall be glad, and in Thy salvation he shall rejoice exceedingly.
Thou hast given him the desire of his heart, and hast not denied him the requests of his lips.

For Thou hast preceded him with blessings of goodness; Thou hast set upon his head a crown of precious stone.[^284]
He asked life of Thee, and Thou gavest him length of days unto age of age.
Great is his glory in Thy salvation; glory and majesty shalt Thou lay upon him.
For Thou wilt give him blessing unto age of age; Thou wilt make him glad with joy with Thy countenance.
For the king hopes in the Lord, and through the mercy of the Most High he shall not be moved.
May Thy hand be found on all Thine enemies; may Thy right hand find all those who hate Thee.
For Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven in the time of Thy presence; the Lord shall confound them in His wrath, and fire shall devour them.
Their fruit shalt Thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the sons of men.
For they intended evils against Thee; they devised counsels which they could by no means establish.
For Thou shalt make them turn their back; among Thy remnants Thou shalt prepare their face.
Be Thou exalted, O Lord, in Thy strength; we will sing and praise Thy mighty acts.
*Glory... Both now: Trisagion; and after "Our Father," the Priest:*[^285]
**Priest:** For Thine is the kingdom...
*And the Troparion:*[^286]
O Lord, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance, granting victories to our right-believing Emperor over adversaries, and preserving Thy people by Thy Cross.

*Glory:*[^287]
Thou Who of Thine own will wast lifted up upon the Cross, O Christ God, grant Thy compassions unto Thy new commonwealth named after Thee; make glad our right-believing Emperor by Thy power, granting him victories over adversaries, having Thine alliance, an armor of peace, an invincible trophy.
*Both now:*[^288]
O awesome and unashamed Mediatress, despise not our prayers, O good, all-hymned Theotokos: establish the commonwealth of the Orthodox, and save those whom thou hast commanded to rule, and grant them victory from heaven, for thou didst bear God, O only blessed one.
**Priest:** Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee: give ear and have mercy.[^289]
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy (thrice).
**Priest:** Again we pray for our right-believing and God-preserved Emperor *(Name)*, for all his court and his Christ-loving armed forces.[^290]
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy (thrice).
**Priest:** For a merciful and man-loving God art Thou, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.[^291]
**Choir:** Amen. In the name of the Lord, bless, father.
**Priest:** Glory to the holy, consubstantial, life-creating, and undivided Trinity, always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.[^292]
**Choir:** Amen.

*After this, "Glory to God in the highest," and the Six Psalms (see pp. 92–98);[^293] Great Litany: "In peace let us pray to the Lord" (see p. 47);[^294] and "God is the Lord" (see p. 98).[^295] Troparion: "Thy Nativity, O Christ," twice (p. 121);[^296] Glory: "Today the Church celebrates" (p. 127);[^297] Both now: "Thy Nativity, O Christ..."*
*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1. Podoben: The stone having been sealed:*[^298]
When Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea, creation was enlightened, having recognized the Creator; the angelic choirs were amazed, beholding the Master on earth having assumed the form of a servant: Glory to God Who was incarnate; glory to Him Who was born on earth; glory to Him Who was well pleased to save our race![^299]
*Glory... Both now... The same once more.*
*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: Joseph was amazed:*[^300]
Why art thou amazed, O Mary? What strikes awe at that which is in thee? Because I gave birth to the timeless Son in time, she said; having not learned the conception of childbirth, I am unwedded, and how shall I bear a Son? Who has seen a seedless birth? For where God wills, the order of nature is overcome, as it is written: Christ is born, to raise up the image that was fallen aforetime.[^301]
*Glory... Both now... The same once more.*
*Psalm 50:*[^302]
Have mercy on me, O God... (see p. 12).[^303]
*Canons of the Nativity, the Heirmoi of both canons twice; Troparia to make 12. Katavasia: the Heirmoi of both canons. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the Feast: "Today the Virgin" (see p. 109);[^304] Ikos: "Bethlehem made Eden open" (p. 109);[^305] and the Hypakoe: "The star led the Magi" (p. 105).[^306]*

*After Ode 6, Kontakion, Tone 6:*[^307]
He Who was born before the morning star from the Father without a mother, was incarnate on earth today without a father from thee; therefore the star brings good tidings to the Magi, and angels with shepherds sing of thy ineffable childbearing, O Full of Grace![^308]
*Ikos:*[^309]
Thou who didst bud forth the uncultivated Cluster of Grapes, O mystical vineyard, bore Him in thine arms as on branches, and said: Thou art my Fruit; Thou art my Life; since I knew that I also remain what I was: Thou art my God; for beholding the seal of my virginity unbroken, I proclaim Thee, the immutable Word, having become flesh; I know no seed, I know Thee as the Looser of corruption; for I am pure, since Thou didst come forth from me, for as Thou didst find my womb, so hast Thou left it, beholding it intact; therefore all creation rejoices together, crying unto me: Rejoice, O Full of Grace![^310]
*At Ode 9 we do not sing "More honorable," but we sing the Refrains as on the Nativity itself.*[^311]
*Exapostilarion:*[^312]
Our Savior, the Dayspring of daysprings, has visited us from on high; and we who were in darkness and shadow have found the truth: for from the Virgin the Lord is born (thrice).[^313]
*At the Praises, Stichera on 4, Tone 4. Idiomela:*[^314]
Rejoice, ye righteous; be glad, ye heavens; leap for joy, ye mountains, for Christ is born! The Virgin sits resembling the cherubim, bearing in her bosom God the Word incarnate; the shepherds marvel at Him Who was born; the Magi bring gifts unto the Master; the angels singing say: Incomprehensible Lord, glory to Thee![^315]
O Virgin Theotokos, who didst bear the Savior, thou hast abolished the first curse of Eve; for thou wast the Mother of the Father's good pleasure, bearing in thy bosom God the Word incarnate; the mystery tolerates no searching out; by faith alone do we all glorify it, crying out with thee, and saying: Ineffable Lord, glory to Thee![^316]
Come, let us sing unto the Mother of the Savior, who after childbearing appeared again a Virgin: Rejoice, O ensouled city of the King and God, in which Christ dwelt and wrought salvation; with Gabriel let us sing, with the shepherds let us give glory, crying: O Theotokos, pray unto Him Who was incarnate of thee, that we may be saved![^317]
The Father was well pleased, the Word became flesh, the Virgin gave birth to God made man; the star proclaims; the Magi worship; the shepherds wonder; and creation rejoices.[^318]
*Glory... Tone 6:*[^319]
Today the invisible nature is united to men from the Virgin; today the immeasurable essence in Bethlehem is wrapped in swaddling clothes; today God by a star leads the Magi unto worship, prefiguring His three-day burial as in gold, myrrh, and frankincense, unto Whom we sing: O Christ God incarnate of the Virgin, save our souls!

*Both now... Tone 2:*
Today Christ is born in Bethlehem of the Virgin; today He Who is without beginning begins to be, and the Word becomes incarnate; the heavenly hosts rejoice, and the earth with mankind makes merry; the Magi offer gifts to the Master; the shepherds marvel at Him Who was born; and we unceasingly cry aloud: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will among men!
*After this, the Great Doxology: "Glory to God in the highest" (see p. 118).[^320] After the Trisagion, the Troparion of the Feast: "Today the Church celebrates" (p. 127);[^321] Glory... Both now...: "Thy Nativity, O Christ" (p. 121);[^322] Litany: "Have mercy on us, O God" (p. 62);[^323] and the Litany: "Let us complete our morning prayer" (p. 63);[^324] and the Dismissal.*[^325]
*If the Synaxis falls on Monday: at "God is the Lord," the Troparion of the Nativity twice: "Thy Nativity, O Christ" (p. 121);[^326] Glory: to the Ancestors of God: "Proclaim the wonders, O Joseph" (p. 129);[^327] Both now: "Thy Nativity, O Christ..." Two regular Kathismata with the Sessional Hymns of the Nativity: "After the Psalms" (p. 99).[^328] Psalm 50, and straightway the Canon: Three Canons for 14: the first of the Nativity with the Heirmos for 6, the Heirmos twice; then the second of the Nativity for 4, and of the Ancestors of God for 4. Katavasia of the Nativity after each Ode.*[^329]
*Canon of the Ancestors of God:*[^330]
*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^331]
I will sing unto Thee, O Lord my God, for Thou hast led Thy people out of the bondage of Egypt, and hast covered the chariots and power of Pharaoh.
As many of us as celebrate the Nativity of Him Who was incarnate for our sake, with joy let us sing of James, the Brother of the Lord, and David, the Ancestor of God.[^332]

The angelic choirs stood before Joseph in Bethlehem, saying: Glory to God in the highest; with them let us sing of Him Who was well pleased to become incarnate.
O David, Ancestor of God, with psalteries, having brought along righteous James, sing of the Lord Who is incarnate from the root of Jesse.
*Theotokion:*
I will sing unto Thee, O Lord my God, for Thou wast born of a mother, the Holy Virgin, and hast shown her to be the hope of our souls.
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*[^333]
O Christ God, Thou Confirmation of those who hope in Thee, confirm us in Thy Orthodox faith, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.
Let us all with the angels sing of Christ God Who was born in the flesh of the Virgin without seed, crying aloud: Glory to God in the highest!
O faithful, with David and James let us worthily honor with hymns the all-festal feast of Him Who emptied Himself for our sake.
As a bridegroom from his chamber, David said the Lord would come forth from the maternal womb; Whom now with James let us sing.
*Theotokion:*
Let us honor Mary, the unwedded Mother of God, for because of her the Sun of Righteousness has shone forth upon us.
*After this, the Kontakion: "He Who was born before the morning star" (see p. 134);[^334] and the Ikos: "The uncultivated Cluster of Grapes" (p. 134).[^335]*
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 2. Podoben: Today the Virgin:*[^336]
Let us worthily hymn righteous Joseph the Betrothed of the Virgin, and with David, James:

for having walked the righteous path, they worthily attained the heavenly mansions; rejoicing with the angels, they ask remission of sins for us.[^337]
*Glory... Both now... Of the Nativity.*[^338]
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^339]
Proclaiming the coming of Thy manifestation on earth, O Christ God, [the Prophet] cried with gladness: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!
With the Magi let us worship Him Who was born, and with the angels and Joseph let us rejoice, crying out in a manner fitting for God: Glory in the highest to Christ God!
Christ, reigning over all kings, being born according to the flesh from the seed of David, accepted chosen James as brother, even as He was pleased.
The Lord, having appeared in the flesh from the Virgin, glorifies together your memory, O holy ones, James, best of the Apostles, and all-blessed David.
*Theotokion:*
Surpassing the ranks of the Cherubim, and having borne in the flesh God Carried in thy arms: Rejoice, O unwedded Mother of God!
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^340]
Thou Who hast shone forth light, and enlightened the morning, and shown the day: Glory to Thee, glory to Thee, O Jesus, Son of God!
O God incarnate, Who wast well pleased to be born of the pure Virgin: Glory to Thee, glory to Thee, cried Joseph!
Thou Who wast born of the Virgin and hast made radiant the memory of David and James: Glory to Thee, glory to Thee, O Jesus, Son of God!

Him Whom the prophet David called by divine inspiration the rain upon the fleece, James proclaimed as Jesus the Son of God.
*Theotokion:*
Unceasingly creation brings to thee, O Virgin, the voice of the angel: Rejoice, O pure Mother of Jesus the Son of God!
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^341]
Prefiguring the three-day burial, the Prophet Jonah cried out, praying in the sea-monster: Deliver me from corruption, O Jesus, King of powers!
The heavenly ranks stood in awe around the cave, and unceasingly sang of the most pure Nativity: Glory in the highest to God Who was born!
Thou didst sit upon the throne, as Thou didst swear, O Master, to the Prophet David, having come forth from the fruit of his loins; and to James Thou didst deliver the throne of the Church, O Christ.
David was shown forth indeed as a king and marvelous prophet, and forefather of the divine Incarnation; while James was shown forth as the first bishop and disciple of Jesus.
*Theotokion:*
We pray thee for thy servants, O Virgin Mother of God: pray to Him Who was incarnate of thee, for we know thee as our only Protectress.
*Kontakion, Tone 3. Podoben: Today the Virgin:*[^342]
Today divine David is filled with gladness, and Joseph with James offers praise; for receiving the crown by kinship with Christ, they rejoice, and hymn Him Who was ineffably born on earth, crying aloud: O Merciful One, save those who honor Thee!
*Ikos:*[^343]
By ineffable counsel the Bodiless One is born in the flesh; the Uncircumscribed is now circumscribed in body; and He saves immutably both natures. He Who by nature is without beginning and alone timeless receives a beginning; He, the all-perfect, is seen as an infant; He Who upholds all things is borne in arms; therefore, as God He crowns by His Nativity those honored by kinship with Him; whom glorifying with faith, unceasingly we cry aloud: O Merciful One, save those who honor Thee!
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^344]
The Abrahamic youths of old in Babylon trampled down the fiery furnace flame, crying aloud with hymns: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!
At the childbearing beyond word of Mary, the Mother of God, the angelic choirs marveled, crying to Joseph: Glory in the highest, and on earth peace!
David having been indeed the friend of the Bridegroom, and James the chamberlain of the divine Church, said: Blessed art Thou, O God, Who wast incarnate for our sake!
O thou king among prophets and prophet among kings, with wise-minded James sing unto us: Blessed art Thou, O Christ, Who wast incarnate for our sake!
*Theotokion:*
In the unburnt bush and the dew-yielding furnace, thou wast prefigured, O most pure Mother, without seed thou didst give birth to God incarnate, O Ever-blessed One.
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^345]
Christ the King, Whom the captive youths confessed in the furnace, saying with a loud voice: All ye works, praise the Lord!
Christ incarnate, Whom the heavenly hosts glorified, Joseph the Betrothed also unceasingly hymns: All ye works, praise the Lord, and supreme exalt Him unto the ages!
Praise Christ born of the Virgin, Who has magnified the sacred memory of David and James as His friends: All ye works, praise the Lord, and supreme exalt Him unto the ages!
*Theotokion:*
Praise Christ the King, Whom Mary the Mother of God bore for us, and after childbearing remained a pure Virgin: All ye works, praise the Lord, and supreme exalt Him unto the ages!
*At Ode 9, instead of "More honorable," the Heirmos of the Canon of the Nativity: "A strange mystery" (see p. 112),[^346] with the Refrains of the Nativity.*
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^347]
Moses beheld the unburnt bush upon the mountain; Joseph heard the ineffable childbearing in the cave, O undefiled Virgin, unwedded Mother: with hymns, O Mother of God, we magnify thee.
All creation served with trembling Him Who was born of the Virgin: the heavens with a star; the earth with a manger; the mountain with a cave; the Magi with gifts; the shepherds with wonders; and the angels with: Glory to God in the highest!

David was indeed made a king from being a shepherd of old; and thou, O James, from the tribe of Judah and the royal tribe, wast shown forth as shepherd of the flock of Him Who was incarnate, the Lamb on high and the Son of God.
Rejoice now, O Bethlehem, having become the city of the new David; and Sion, proclaim the primacy of the churches, the common joy, having now received the King of all and His pleased servants!
*Theotokion:*
The angelic assemblies glorify thee, O Mother of God, and the human race unceasingly honors thee with hymns; for through thy divine childbearing thou hast united earthly things with heavenly; therefore do we honor thee.
*Exapostilarion. Podoben: With the disciples:*[^348]
With glorious James, the Brother of the Lord, let us sing of David the Ancestor of God, and divine Joseph the Betrothed of the Mother of God; for they served the divine Nativity of Christ in the city of Bethlehem in a manner fitting for God, with angels, Magi, and shepherds, singing a hymn to Him as God and Master.[^349]

## Service of the Holy Apostle, First-Martyr, and Archdeacon Stephen
*And of our venerable Father and Confessor Theodore the Branded, and his brother Theophanes.*[^399]
*At Vespers.*
*On "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Apostle, 3; and of the Venerable, 3.*
*Stichera of the Apostle, Tone 4. Podoben: "As one noble...":*
Having enlightened thy mind by the grace of the Spirit, thou didst appear as an angel in form, O Stephen, thy body shining with inward brightness, showing the radiance of thy soul unto those that beheld thee, through which thou didst also obtain the vision of light, the heavens being most gloriously opened unto thee, O beginning and boast of martyrs.
As steps and a ladder for ascent into heaven were the hurling of stones unto thee, mounting whereon, thou didst behold the Lord standing at the right hand of the Father, stretching forth unto thee a crown of thy same name with His life-bearing right hand; near Whom thou standest as a noble victor, and the prince of martyrs.[^400]
Shining with signs and wonders, thou didst extinguish the council of the lawless with thy teachings; and being slain by them, and pelted with stones, thou didst pray for forgiveness for those who slew thee, following the voice of the Savior, into Whose hands thou didst commit thy spirit, O consecrated Stephen.[^401]
*Other Stichera of the Venerable, Tone and model melody the same:*[^402]
Unto the wounds of exile were added wounds unto thee, most grievous banishments, O venerable one, when he who lawlessly held the lawful throne fiercely agitated the laws of faith, casting down the most pure icon of the Savior; whom by divine grace, O servant of Christ, thou didst put to shame.[^403]
Neither prison nor affliction, nor gloomy darkness, nor wounds nor beatings, O father, were able to separate thee from the love of God, O Theodore; neither grievous, lingering, and intolerable sickness, nor scarcity of food, O divinely-speaking one; but unswervingly thou didst endure, foreseeing the recompense of thy labors.[^404]
Instead of sorrow, relief; instead of sickness, delight; instead of darkness, illumination has the Umpire of the contest granted unto thee, O venerable one; and instead of the writings upon thy face, O marvelous one, thou hast been written in the book of life, and thou hast been enrolled in the church of the firstborn on high, and hast come with exceeding joy unto the festival of the angels.[^405]
*Glory: Tone 2:*[^406]
Unto the King and Master of all Who was born on earth, a most radiant crown is offered, not fashioned of precious stones, but blossoming from his own blood. But come, O ye lovers of the martyrs, taking flowers of hymns, let us crown our heads, and singing hymns let us say: O thou who didst enlighten thy soul with wisdom and grace, first-martyr of Christ God, ask peace for us, and great mercy.
*Both now: Same tone:*
"A great and most glorious wonder..." (see p. 88).[^407]
*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly hosts":*[^408]
Him Who shone forth from the Father before all ages, and as Son ever-existing in the bosom of the Father, and now in these latter days sitting upon His Mother's arms as upon a throne, and worshiped by angels, shepherds, and Magi, let us hymn.
*Verse:* From the womb before the morning star have I begotten Thee; the Lord has sworn, and will not repent.[^409]
Assembling in Bethlehem, O ye faithful, let us behold the Creator of all lying in a manger; and those who have brought from the land of the Chaldeans a chosen three-part gift: as unto King and God, and unto Him Who for our sake would be a dead corpse of three days.[^410]
*Verse:* The Lord said unto my Lord: Sit Thou at My right hand.[^411]
Thou wast born of the Virgin in a cave, and the angelic hosts standing round about cried aloud:

Glory in the highest unto Thee, O God, Who wast born in the form of a servant and hast renewed the world, in the womb of the Virgin and unwedded Mother.[^412]
*Glory: Tone 6:*[^413]
Thou wast revealed as first among martyrs and among deacons, O Apostle Stephen, adornment of sufferers, glory of the righteous. For those who celebrate thy venerable memory, as thou standest before the throne of Christ the King of all, ask purification of transgressions and that they may be counted worthy of the heavenly kingdom.
*Both now: Same tone:*
"All the angels rejoice..." (see p. 84).[^414]
*Troparion, Tone 4:*[^415]
With a good fight hast thou fought, O first-martyr and Apostle of Christ, and hast rebuked the impiety of the tormentors; for being stoned by the hands of the lawless, thou didst receive a crown from the right hand on high, and didst call unto God, crying aloud: O Lord, lay not this sin to their charge!
*Glory: Troparion, Tone 8:*[^416]
Guide of Orthodoxy, teacher of piety and purity, luminary of the universe, divinely-inspired adornment of monastics, O Theodore most wise; by thy teachings thou hast enlightened all, O spiritual lyre; entreat Christ God that our souls may be saved.
*Both now: Of the Feast:*
"Thy Nativity, O Christ..." (see p. 121).[^417]

*At Matins.*
*On "God is the Lord," Troparion of the Nativity: "Thy Nativity, O Christ..."[^418] And of the Apostle: "With a good fight..."[^419] Glory: of the Venerable: "Guide of Orthodoxy..."[^420] Both now: of the Nativity: "Thy Nativity, O Christ..."*
*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 2. Podoben: "Today the Virgin":*[^421]
The seedless conception gives birth to the Redeemer; and the Magi come to worship Life; men with angels glorify; the cave and manger offer gifts, hymning the One Who was born, a young child, the pre-eternal God.
*Glory: Both now: the same again.*
*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Joseph marveled":*[^422]
He Who is uncontainable everywhere, how was He contained in the womb? He Who is in the bosom of the Father, how is He in the arms of His Mother? In every way as He knows, as He willed, and as He was well pleased; for being bodiless, He voluntarily took flesh; and He became what He was not for our sake, and not departing from His divine nature, He partook of our compound nature; twofold was Christ born, desiring to fulfill the world on high.
*Glory: Both now: the same again.*
*Magnification:*[^423]
We magnify thee, O holy first-martyr and Archdeacon Stephen, and we honor thy venerable sufferings, which thou didst endure for Christ.
*Verse:* The heavens declare the glory of God, and the firmament proclaims the work of His hands.[^424]
Thou shalt make them princes over all the earth.[^425]

Thy lightnings illumined the world.[^426]
Their sound is gone out into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.[^427]
God stood in the congregation of gods, and in the midst He judges gods.[^428]
He will give strength and power unto His people; blessed is God.[^429]
*Glory: Both now: Alleluia (3).*
*Canon.*[^430]
*First Canon of the Feast, with the Heirmos for 6: "Christ is born..." (see p. 103);[^431] and two Canons of the Saints for 8.*
*Canon of the Apostle, Tone 7. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^432]
He Who shatters battles with a high arm, Christ shook horse and rider into the Red Sea; but He saved Israel as they sang a song of victory.
*Troparia:*
Come, with crowns of hymns let us gloriously crown the church of Christ's first-martyr; and crowned by the divine grace of Stephen, let us sing a song of victory.
Bearing the meek mind of the Master, and the deifying humility born of love, thou didst worthily become the chief of deacons, O Stephen, and an advocate for the widows.[^433]
Thou wast worthily called to the aid of Christ's Apostles, and didst become a faithful deacon, O Stephen of thy same name; where Christ was, thither thou didst pass through blood.

*Theotokion:*[^434]
For mankind's sake, O Christ, having assumed man from the Virgin, Thou wast swaddled as an infant; and Thy first-martyr, pelted by the hurling of stones, puts off the old man.
*Another Canon, of the Venerable, Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^435]
Having traversed the deep of the Red Sea with dry feet, ancient Israel of old conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness by Moses' hands stretched out in the form of a cross.
*Troparia:*
Seeing the inscription upon thy venerable face, the cherubim step back from the tree of life; and the flaming sword most reverently turns its back unto thee, O all-wise Theodore.[^436]
An inscription lay above the head of Christ the King, written upon the wood of the Cross; and thine inscription, O all-wise one, was inscribed upon thy face, written with fitting beauty.[^437]
The Lord was lifted up upon the Cross and pierced in the side; and thou, stretched out upon a bench, didst patiently endure stabbings, marked out as a chosen ram, O initiate of sacred mysteries.[^438]
Adorned with all wisdom, O father Theodore, thou didst openly rebuke the champion of evil heresy; and by the endurance of deeds thou didst terrify his designs.[^439]
*Theotokion:*
Thou wast the receptacle of transcendent Wisdom, O most pure Mother of God, the sole salvation and hymn of the faithful; for unto us thou hast today given birth to divine redemption.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*[^440]
O Thou Who by Thy command didst establish the earth upon nothing, and didst suspend what by gravity is held by no support; on the immovable rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, establish Thy Church, O Thou Who alone art good and the Lover of mankind.
*Troparia:*
Lawfully, as a soldier of Christ, did Stephen array himself against the murderers of God; clad in His invincible power, he rebuked all the evil opposition of the lawless.[^441]
Kindled with zeal for Christ's love and stripped for contests, filled with faith and the divine Spirit, the first-martyr confirmed unto all the divinely-decreed preaching of the fishermen.[^442]
As a preacher of the hidden and truly divine life, having as it were forgotten the present life, the first-martyr preferred a victorious death for the truth, O Master.[^443]
*Theotokion:*
For our sake the Master entered into the earthly cave in a manner of condescension; and Stephen, the leader of the martyrs, departs from human heaviness, conquered by the love of the Master.
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^444]
Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying aloud: Thou art my strength, O Lord, and refuge, and foundation.
*Troparia:*
Having nothing wherewith to contradict thy teachings, O venerable one, he who was full of Hellenic madness turned unto wounds.[^445]

Patiently didst thou surrender thyself to wounds upon thy shoulders and upon thy chest, O divinely-inspired one, for Christ's sake, and to blows upon the head.
By spiritual wisdom and fervency of mind, thou didst put to shame the wicked and lawless one by thy most radiant doctrines.
*Theotokion:*
David, as if seeing the Ark in Bethlehem with prophetic eyes, O Mother of God, beholds thee carrying the transcendent Infant.[^446]
*Kontakion, Tone 3:*
"Today the Virgin gives birth to the Transcendent in essence..." (p. 109).[^447]
*Ikos:*
"Bethlehem has opened Eden..." (p. 109).[^448]
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 1. Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior":*[^449]
Apostle of Christ, first of deacons, wise first-martyr, crown of martyrs, having sanctified the ends of the world by thy sufferings, and enlightened the souls of men by wonders: deliver from all tribulations those who honor thee, O all-praised Stephen.
*Glory: Of the Venerable, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3. Podoben: "Of the divine faith":*[^450]
Thou wast a mouth of boldness, putting the pride of the tormentor to shame with lofty wisdom through the endurance of thy sufferings; for being wounded in the face, O Theodore, for the all-venerable icon of Christ, thou didst confirm all to venerate and glorify it, asking great mercy for us.
*Both now: Theotokion:*[^451]
Beholding her seedless birth-giving, the all-undefiled one marveled, and unto Him Who was born of her said: "Though Thou art clothed in human form, yet deeds proclaim Thee God, O beginningless Child, co-beginningless with the beginningless Father, Who grantest great mercy unto all."
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^452]
Perceiving Thy divine self-emptying, foresightedly Habakkuk cried aloud unto Thee with trembling, O Christ: For the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed ones art Thou come.
*Troparia:*
Emulating the zeal of the angels, and radiantly adorned with an angelic countenance, thou dost behold the divine glory and its illuminations.[^453]
The slanderer shows himself to be judge and tormentor, a manslayer and the father of lies, O Stephen; yet he overcomes himself, spreading nets for his own destruction.[^454]
As first of the martyrs, having opened for us the path into martyrdom, Stephen, bearer of the crown, rejoices, being crowned with the martyrs' crown.[^455]
*Theotokion:*
Having shone forth from the Virgin like the sun, and Stephen having dawned with Thee like the morning star, O Christ, Thou dost adorn the glorious memory of the first-martyr with Thine own brightness.[^456]
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^457]
Beholding Thee lifted up upon the Cross, O Sun of righteousness, the Church stood in her order, worthily crying aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord.
*Troparia:*
Stretched out upon a bench, thou wast branded, O Theodore, because thou didst venerate the icon of Christ and of the Mother of God, through the violence of fury and barbarian cruelty.[^458]
Having served the Master from thy youth, and possessing unceasing longing for Him through temperance, O blessed one, thou wast straightway most radiantly adorned with martyr's blood.[^459]
Like a luminary, as the sun, O God-bearing father, thou didst hasten from the East, emitting the rays of thy confession and of thy high-minded and most valiant opposition.[^460]
*Theotokion:*
Blessed is God the Word Who comes to Bethlehem in His compassion from thine unwedded and most pure womb, O all-pure one; entreat Him now to save those who have recourse unto thee.[^461]
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^462]
O Thou Who deckest Thyself with light as with a garment, unto Thee I rise early, and unto Thee I cry: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou alone art compassionate.
*Troparia:*
Thy first-martyr shone like an angel, having surpassed the heavenly spheres, O Christ, and he was filled with Thine ineffable glory, O Lover of mankind.[^463]
By the rain of his divinely-uttering mouth, the first-martyr cast down the foul murderers; and by countless showers of stones from them, he was crowned as victor.[^464]
How invincible is the boldness of the first-martyr's divine zeal! For even unto blood he courageously stood in array against the murderers of God.[^465]

*Theotokion:*
The first-martyr beheld Him Who came forth from an unwedded Mother, standing in the heavens in the unmovable Godhead and glory of the Father.[^466]
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^467]
Thou, O Lord, art come into the world as my Light, the holy Light that turns from the darkness of ignorance those who praise Thee in faith.
*Troparia:*
Dwelling in a strange land, thou didst patiently endure exile, affliction, wounds, banishments, and bitterest prisons.[^468]
Now thou dost truly rejoice, having received a victorious crowning on the radiant and light-bearing day of the divine first-martyr.[^469]
The promise of piety which thou didst have from childhood, thou didst keep even unto the end, remaining unswerving, O invincible Theodore.[^470]
*Theotokion:*
Thee do we set forth as an invincible weapon against our enemies; thee have we gained as the help and hope of our salvation, O Bride of God.[^471]
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^472]
Calm the sea of passions raging with a soul-corrupting tempest, O Master Christ, and bring me up from corruption, as Thou art compassionate.
*Troparia:*
Stephen, the glorious captain of martyrs, O faithful, having transcended the laws of nature by grace, is illumined with divine glory.[^473]
Having become a most excellent imitator of Thy precious Passion, O Master Christ, Stephen avenges himself on his murderers with blessing.[^474]

Preserve us unpartaking of the defiling act of murder, O Christ, and vouchsafe Thy singers the heritage of the first-martyr, as Thou art compassionate.[^475]
*Theotokion:*
Thy Nativity, O Master Christ, became the beginning of universal salvation, and the foundation of God-befitting confession for the martyrs.[^476]
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^477]
I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of thanksgiving, O Lord, the Church cries unto Thee, having been cleansed from the blood of demons by the blood that for mercy's sake flowed from Thy side.
*Troparia:*
Still dripping with the sweat of thy struggles, thou didst stand before the Judge of the contest, Who governs all creation with righteous judgment, O God-bearing and all-blessed Theodore.[^478]
Nothing separated thee from the love of Christ: neither the wounding of the body, nor the threat of death's condemnation, nor the loss of present goods, O all-blessed Theodore.[^479]
Being a most radiant son of the light and of the day, full of light thou wast translated, increasing toward the divine radiance, unfailing by day and by night, O God-receiver.[^480]
*Theotokion:*
Having found thee alone amidst thorns, as a most pure lily and flower of the valleys, O Mother of God, the spiritual Bridegroom took up His abode in thee.[^481]
*Kontakion, Tone 3. Podoben: "Today the Virgin":*[^482]
Yesterday the Master came unto us in the flesh, and today the servant departs from the flesh; yesterday He Who reigns was born in the flesh, today the servant is stoned with stones; for His sake also the first-martyr and divine Stephen meets his end.
*Ikos:*[^483]
Like a bright star, the first-martyr Stephen dawned today together with the Nativity of Christ, flashing and enlightening the ends of the earth, he only darkened all the malice of the Jews; having convicted them with the wisdom of the word, conversing from the Scriptures and persuading them that Jesus Who was born of the Virgin is the Son of God and God; and he put to shame their impious wickedness, the first-martyr and divine Stephen.
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^484]
The all-extolled Lord of our fathers quenched the flame and bedewed the youths, who sang in unison: Blessed art Thou, O God.
*Troparia:*
Murderously gnashing their teeth, the lawless seized Stephen like wild beasts and slew him, as he sang divinely: Blessed art Thou, O God.[^485]
As with variegated and fair flowers, O Stephen, being adorned with stones, thou didst bring thyself unto Christ the Giver of life, singing: Blessed art Thou, O God.[^486]
How unconquerable was Paul's resistance, when at first he persecuted God's saints, but now brings the Gentiles unto Christ unto ages.[^487]
Carried up by the Holy Spirit, O Stephen, thou didst ineffably behold the Son with the Father, crying to the Trinity: Blessed art Thou, O God.[^488]

*Theotokion:*
Unto Thee Who didst shine forth from the divine pure Virgin, a living crown was offered as unto a King, singing divinely unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, O God.[^489]
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^490]
In the Persian furnace, Abraham's youths, burned more by love of piety than by flame, cried aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord.
*Troparia:*
Kindled with Orthodox zeal, O all-wise one, thou didst boldly cast away the devouring food of foul and cruel heresy, crying: Blessed art Thou, my God and Lord.[^491]
A word of wisdom, a word of dogmas, a word of all-pious teachings, O father, God the Word granted unto thee, as thou didst cry: Blessed art Thou, my God and Lord.[^492]
Having dawned from the East like the sun, thou didst sow on earth the ray of piety, the veneration of Christ's icon, which enlightens and instructs, O father Theodore.[^493]
*Theotokion:*
Adorned, most glorified, radiant with the light of virginity, thou hast appeared as Mother, having borne God in the flesh and swaddled in bands Him Who manifestly swaddles all things.[^494]
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^495]
Unto Thee, the Creator of all, the youths in the furnace, having woven a universal choir, sang: All ye works of the Lord, praise the Lord, and supreme-exalt Him unto all ages.

*Troparia:*
Come, let us mentally shine with the glory of Stephen and sing unto God Who took flesh: All ye works, praise the Lord, and supreme-exalt Him unto all ages.[^496]
For thee, as victor over iniquitous deceit, a crown of sufferings was woven, O Stephen; therefore thou didst cry aloud: Praise the Lord, and supreme-exalt Him unto all ages.[^497]
Christ appeared unto thee in the Father's glory, manifestly announcing to thee the rewards of thy sufferings; therefore thou didst cry aloud: Praise the Lord, and supreme-exalt Him unto all ages.[^498]
The Master's Nativity and the memory of the first-martyr have woven a choir for us; therefore unceasingly we sing the Lord, and supreme-exalt Him unto all ages.[^499]
*Theotokion:*
Thee Who beyond understanding didst give birth in divine-human manner by the Word unto the Lord, while remaining virgin, all ye works do we bless, O Virgin, and supreme-exalt unto all ages.[^500]
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^501]
Daniel, stretching out his hands, stopped the lions' open mouths in the den; and the youths, lovers of piety, girt with virtue, quenched the power of fire, crying aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.
*Troparia:*
Like a lion trusting in the firmness of thy soul, O all-blessed one, thou didst not fear the heretical onslaught of him who was named for fury; spitting upon him, thou didst cry aloud to the people with joy: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.[^502]
Having adorned thy soul with courage, and with chastity thy desire, O blessed one, having acquired a mind filled with understanding, thou didst reach perfection; and with righteousness thou didst direct thy thoughts, and thou dost cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.[^503]
Thou didst dry up the impetuously flowing stream of heresy, O father, by thy chastisements, O venerable one, and by all-wise demonstrations tearing down the subtleties of the impious, and crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.[^504]
*Theotokion:*
God Who created human nature in the beginning is clothed in it from thy most pure and holy womb, O all-undefiled one, being born today in Bethlehem according to prophecy; therefore all of us bless thee, O Mary, Bride of God.[^505]
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^506]
Rejoice, O Isaiah! The Virgin was with child, and brought forth a Son, Emmanuel, God and man, the Orient is His name; Whom magnifying, we call the Virgin blessed.
*Troparia:*
Thou hast surpassed every order of praises, O Stephen, and in every word thou dost truly bear the tokens of victory; for human mind is powerless to weave a worthy crown of praises for thee.[^507]
O thy blessed voice, which thou didst utter, O Stephen! "Master Christ," crying, "impute not the sin unto the murderers; but as God and Creator receive my spirit as a most fragrant sacrifice."[^508]
Thou hast received the recompense of thy sufferings, the victorious crown from the almighty right hand; and standing now before the Sovereign King, O all-blessed one, crown with glory and grace those who praise thee.[^509]

*Theotokion:*
The timeless Son of the Father received the beginning of temporal existence in the flesh from the Virgin; for Whose sake, being slain, Stephen, the imitator of Christ, has now inherited life eternal from the beginning.[^510]
*Another Canon. Heirmos:*[^511]
A cornerstone was cut out from thee, an uncut mountain, O Virgin, without hands, even Christ, Who joined together the severed natures; therefore rejoicing, we magnify thee, O Mother of God.
*Troparia:*
Overcome by divine desire, thou didst easily endure the arrays of persecutions, O divinely-wise one; and having cast down the fury of the persecutor, thou hast received the crown of thy struggles, O father.[^512]
Thou hast found heavenly riches and an enduring habitation instead of bitter banishment, wandering, and journeys; and now thou art nourished, receiving eternal delight.[^513]
Offering hymnody and prayers unto the Master, O father, cease not to pray for my weakness, for our fellow dwellers, and for the brotherhood, so that having lived together we may together attain deification.[^514]
*Theotokion:*
Thee Who beyond mind and word didst bear God the Word today in Bethlehem, O Virgin, and didst swaddle Him in swaddling clothes, as truly the Maiden of God and Mother of God do we magnify.[^515]
*Exapostilarion. Podoben: "In the spirit in the sanctuary":*[^516]
Thou didst behold standing at the right hand of the Father the Word Who is before all beginning, and with ineffable light moreover thou wast filled, O praise of the apostles and glory of the martyrs; and thou wast adorned with a crown by the throwing of stones, and didst receive a divine end.[^517]
*Glory, Both now: Exapostilarion of the Nativity:*[^518]
He was laid in a manger, having become an infant like a child; and the star summoned the triad of the Magi; and the shepherds marveled at the wonder, hastening to see God become man; and the angelic choirs, marveling, cried aloud: Glory in the highest.
*At the Praises, Stichera on 6, Automela.*[^519]
*Tone 1:*
Let us, the faithful, weave from spiritual flowers a martyr's crown for the first-sufferer; for he prepared the way for the martyrs, and in joy cried aloud: Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of God standing at the right hand of the invisible Father.[^520]
*Tone 2:*
First among deacons, first also among martyrs didst thou show thyself, O all-holy Stephen; for thou wast a path unto the saints, and didst bring many martyrs unto the Lord; therefore heaven opened unto thee, and God appeared unto thee; entreat Him that our souls may be saved.[^521]
Thou didst clothe thyself with holiness, O blessed Stephen, first-martyr and protodeacon, fellow companion of the angels; intercede and pray for us unto the Savior, the sinless Lord.[^522]
Stephen, the first-martyr and valiant servant of Christ, the protodeacon, let us worthily[^523] honor; for standing in the midst of the lawless, he beheld the Son at the right hand of the Father.[^524]
*Tone 4:*
O glorious Stephen, citizen of heaven, blessed servant of Christ, offer prayer for our souls.[^525]
*The same tone:*
Stephen, the fair beginning of the martyrs, full of grace and power, working great signs and wonders among the people, was stoned with stones by the lawless; yet he shone like an angel, and beheld the glory of Thee Who wast crucified for our sake, at the right hand of Power; and by the Spirit of grace he was caught up into heaven; and dwelling therefore with the angelic choirs, he prays that our souls may be saved.
*Glory: Tone 6:*
O first-martyr, apostle, and protodeacon, door of the martyrs, glory of the righteous, and praise of the apostles: standing before the judgment-seat thou didst see the heavens opened, and the Son of God standing at the right hand of the invisible Father; therefore shining in countenance like an angel, in joy thou didst cry out concerning those who stoned thee: Impute not this sin unto them; and now ask for those who praise thee in love the cleansing of sins and great mercy.[^526]
*Both now: The same tone:*
An incomprehensible mystery is accomplished today in Bethlehem: the Invisible is seen, the Bodiless becomes incarnate, the Word takes on flesh,[^527] and He Who is becomes what He was not; the Virgin brings forth in the cave a young child, the Creator of nature; the manger is prefigured as the heavenly throne; the cattle typify the standing of the Cherubim; the shepherds marvel, the Magi offer gifts; the angels chanting say: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men; for Emmanuel is unchangeable.[^528]
*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 6. Podoben: "Angelic powers...":*[^529]
All creation keeps festival with joy, and the heavens rejoice with us; for the Creator, becoming incarnate of the Virgin, was seen most gloriously as an infant in the manger in Judean Bethlehem; let us, the peoples, say: Blessed art Thou Who wast born, O our God, glory to Thee.
*Verse:* From the womb before the morning star have I begotten Thee: the Lord has sworn, and will not repent.
That Thou mightest make earthly beings heavenly, Thou didst come upon earth, becoming incarnate of the Virgin, and wast seen as an infant, Thou Who art the pre-eternal Word; Thou didst move the Magi from Persia to worship Thee, O compassionate One; with whom we cry aloud: Blessed art Thou Who wast born, O our God, glory to Thee.
*Verse:* The Lord said unto my Lord: Sit Thou at My right hand.
A young Child for our sake didst Thou appear, O Jesus, having shone forth from the Father before the ages, willing to renew all mankind grown old through transgression; therefore unto Thee do we all cry continually in thanksgiving: Blessed art Thou Who wast born, O our God, glory to Thee.
*Glory: Tone 8:*
Rejoice in the Lord, O crown-bearer Stephen, imitator of the Master; for thou wast also the first-martyr[^530] of Christ our King; and thou didst abolish the deception of the lawless Judeans, praying for us unto the Lord.[^531]
*Both now: The same tone:*
How shall I declare the great mystery? The Bodiless becomes incarnate, the Word takes on flesh, the Invisible is seen, and the Intangible is touched; and He Who is without beginning begins; the Son of God becomes the Son of man: Jesus Christ, the same yesterday and today, and unto ages.[^532]
*At the Liturgy:*
*Prokeimenon of the Nativity, and until the Apodosis: "Let all the earth..." (see p. 122),*[^533] *and of the Apostle, Tone 8:*
Their sound is gone out into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.
*Verse:* The heavens declare the glory of God, and the firmament proclaims the work of His hands.
*The Epistle in the Acts of the Apostles, Pericope 16:*[^534]
In those days, Stephen, full of faith and power, worked great signs and wonders among the people. Then there arose certain of the synagogue, which is called the synagogue of the Libertines, and Cyrenians, and Alexandrians, and of them of Cilicia and of Asia, disputing with Stephen. And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the spirit by which he spoke. Then they suborned men, who said: We have heard him speak blasphemous words against Moses, and against God. And they stirred up the people, and the elders, and the scribes, and came upon him, and seized him, and brought him to the council, and set up false witnesses, who said: This man does not cease speaking blasphemous words against this holy place and the law; for we have heard him say that this Jesus of Nazareth shall destroy this place, and shall change the customs which Moses delivered unto us. And all who sat in the council, looking steadfastly upon him, saw his face as the face of an angel. Then the high priest said: Are these things so? And he said: Men, brethren, and fathers, give ear: The God of glory appeared unto our father Abraham when he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Charran, and said unto him: Get thee out from thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father's house, and come into the land which I shall show thee. Then having come out of the land of the Chaldeans, he dwelt in Charran; and from thence, after his father was dead, He removed him into this land wherein ye now dwell. And He gave him no inheritance in it, no, not so much as to set his foot upon. But Solomon built Him a house. Yet the Most High dwells not in temples made with hands, as the prophet says: Heaven is My throne, and earth is the footstool of My feet. What house will ye build Me? says the Lord; or what is the place of My rest? Has not My hand made all these things? Ye stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Spirit; as your fathers did, so do ye. Which of the prophets did not your fathers persecute? And they slew those who foretold the coming of the Just One, of Whom ye have now become the betrayers and murderers; who received the law by the ordinance of angels, and did not keep[^535] it.[^536] When they heard these things, they were cut to their hearts, and they gnashed on him with their teeth. But [Stephen,] being full of the Holy Spirit, looked up steadfastly into heaven, and saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God, and said: Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing at the right hand of God. Then crying out with a loud voice, they stopped their ears, and ran upon him with one accord, and cast him out of the city, and stoned him; and the witnesses [having laid aside] their garments, laid them down at the feet of a young man named Saul. And they stoned Stephen, praying and saying: Lord Jesus, receive my spirit. And kneeling down, he cried with a loud voice: Lord, impute not this sin unto them; and having said this, he fell asleep.
*Alleluia, Tone 1:*
The heavens shall confess Thy wonders, O Lord, and Thy truth in the assembly of the saints.[^537]
*Verse:* God is glorified in the council of the saints, and is terrible unto all that are round about Him.
*The Gospel according to Matthew, Pericope 87:*[^538]
The Lord spoke this parable: There was a certain householder, who planted a vineyard, and hedged it round about, and dug a winepress in it, and built a tower, and let it out to husbandmen, and went into a far country. And when the time of the fruit drew near, he sent his servants to the husbandmen, to receive the fruits of it. And the husbandmen took his servants, and beat one, and killed another, and stoned another. Again, he sent other servants more than the first, and they did unto them likewise. But last of all he sent unto them his son, saying: They will reverence my son. But when the husbandmen saw the son, they said among themselves: This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and let us seize on his inheritance. And they caught him, and cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him. When therefore the lord of the vineyard comes, what will he do unto those husbandmen? They say unto him: He will miserably destroy those wicked men, and will let out his vineyard unto other husbandmen, who shall render him the fruits in their seasons. Jesus says unto them: Did ye never read in the scriptures: The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is the Lord's doing, and it is marvelous in our eyes?[^539]
*Kinonikon of the Nativity: "The Lord has sent redemption..."*[^540]

Their sound is gone out into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.
