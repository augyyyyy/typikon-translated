*It behooves to know that if the Eve of the Nativity of Christ falls on a Saturday or on a Sunday, the Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom is chanted; but the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great is chanted on the very feast day of the Nativity.*[^54]

*Psalm 103:*[^55]

Bless the Lord, O my soul! O Lord my God, Thou art magnified exceedingly.
Thou hast clothed Thyself with confession and majesty, Who coverest Thyself with light as with a garment.
Who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain, Who coverest His upper chambers with waters.
Who makest the clouds His chariot, Who walkest upon the wings of the winds.
Who makest His angels spirits, and His ministers a flame of fire.

=== LEAF p50 ===
Who hast founded the earth upon the firmness thereof; it shall not be turned back unto ages of ages.
The deep, like a garment, is His vesture; upon the mountains shall the waters stand.
At Thy rebuke they shall flee; at the voice of Thy thunder they shall be afraid.
The mountains ascend, and the plains descend, into the place which Thou hast founded for them.
Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass over, neither shall they turn again to cover the earth.
He sends forth springs in the valleys; between the mountains the waters shall pass.
They give drink to all the beasts of the field; the wild asses await them in their thirst.
By them the birds of heaven shall have their habitation; from the midst of the rocks they shall utter their voice.
He waters the mountains from His higher chambers; the earth shall be satisfied with the fruit of Thy works.
He causes grass to spring up for cattle, and herb for the service of men,
To bring forth bread out of the earth; and wine makes glad the heart of man,
To make his face cheerful with oil; and bread strengthens man's heart.
The trees of the plain shall be satisfied, the cedars of Lebanon which Thou hast planted; there the sparrows make their nests.
The habitation of the heron is the chief among them: the high mountains for the stags, the rock a refuge for the hares.
He made the moon for seasons; the sun knows its going down.
