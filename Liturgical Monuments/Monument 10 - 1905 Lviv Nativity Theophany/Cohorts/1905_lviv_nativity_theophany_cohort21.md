*Troparia of Basil:*
As a faithful sheep of Christ, O Basil, thou didst follow the life-bearing footsteps of the Chief Shepherd; for thou didst surrender thy soul unto the tormentor, O father, valiantly imperiling thyself for the Church, O all-blessed one.[^689]

Having seen the all-sacred assembly of the Church of Christ adorned by thy office as hierarch, O wise one, the all-destroying tormentor, struck as by thunder, fell down; for he could not endure the radiance of the Spirit that was within thee, O Basil.[^690]

Thou wast vouchsafed the seat of the Apostles, the company of the passion-bearers of Christ, the tent of the patriarchs, the sweetness of the righteous, and the joy of the prophets, O Basil; for thou wast a servant of the Theotokos and an initiate of the Trinity.[^691]

Every tongue is at a loss to praise thee worthily, and even a mind beyond the world is bewildered to hymn thee, O Theotokos; yet, being good, accept our faith, for thou knowest our divine love, for thou art the protectress of Christians: thee we magnify.[^693]

*At the Praises, Stichera on 6.*
*Tone 5. Podoben: Rejoice...*
He Who was ineffably begotten as the Word of the Father without severing or corruption, and Who as God from God abides in immutable Godhead, endures circumcision in the flesh; therefore, having come under the law though being above the law, He delivers all from the curse of the law, and grants blessing from on high; therefore, praising His most-good condescension, we sing and thankfully glorify Him, entreating Him to grant our souls great mercy. (Twice)[^697]

Having become a son of God by grace through the rebirth of divine baptism, thou didst confess Him Who by nature and truth is the co-eternal Son of God and Word, consubstantial with the Father and co-unoriginate, O venerable one; and the unstopped mouths of heretics thou didst close with the brightness of thy words; therefore also thou didst take up dwelling in the heavenly kingdoms, reigning together with Christ Who alone reigns by nature, Who richly dispenses great mercy unto the world.[^698]

Within the heavenly temple, as a consecrated hierarch thou hast found room, vested as with a sacred vestment in action and contemplation, the principles of wisdom; and now ministering as priest at the altar on high, O venerable one, and standing before God and celebrating the immaterial service, mercifully remember, O all-blessed Basil, those who celebrate thy sacred and all-honorable memory, praying unto Christ, Who grants great mercy unto the world.[^699]

Wholly consecrated unto God, and in every way dedicated from childhood, illumined with the rays of transcendent wisdom,

thou didst make clear the understanding of existing things, lucidly expounding and wisely narrating, bringing good order to existing things, an action of most divine knowledge; therefore we proclaim thee a divinely speaking and divine teacher, and a light-bearing luminary of the Church, praying unto Christ, Who grants great mercy unto the world.[^700]

Established by the fear of God, thou didst understand the beginning of wisdom, O Basil; by wisdom and by uprightness of life thou didst transcend fear, O father; and having been commingled with God in love, thou becamest most intimate with Him; therefore thou wast instructed in divine ordinances and judgments; thou didst learn the ways of men, and didst adorn the minds of the faithful, illuminating the assembly by spiritual statutes; pray therefore unto Christ to grant our souls great mercy.[^701]

*Glory, Tone 6:*
