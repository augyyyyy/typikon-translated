THE BOOK OF THE MENAION
Month of January

To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; under the reign of the Great King Augustus the Third;[^1] by the blessing of His Grace, Lord Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytskyi,[^2] Exarch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the volition of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk Hypatius Bilynskyi,[^3] Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; through the zeal and at the expense of the monks of the same Order; in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv, printed with types: Published. In the year from the Creation of the world according to the Greek chronographs 7269,[^4] and of the Nativity of Christ 1761.

*Day 1*

...unrighteousness shall fall. The righteousness of upright men shall deliver them, but the lawless are taken by their lack of counsel. When a righteous man dies, his hope does not perish, but the boast of the ungodly perishes. A righteous man escapes from the snare, and in his stead the ungodly is delivered up. In the mouth of the ungodly is a snare to citizens, but the perception of the righteous is prosperous. In the good prosperity of the righteous a city shall be set aright, and at the destruction of the ungodly there is rejoicing. In the blessing of the upright a city shall be exalted, but by the mouths of the ungodly it is overthrown. He who lacks understanding mocks citizens, but a prudent man keeps silence.[^6]

*At the Litiya, the Sticheron of the Temple:*
*Then of Saint Basil, Tone 3:*
*By Germanus:*
Having welcomed Christ into thy soul through thy pure life, the fountain of life, O sacredly blessed Basil, thou didst pour forth rivers of pious teachings upon the inhabited world; watered by them, the faithful people of the Church, offering the fruit of lips confessing Him, bring thanksgiving unto Him Who glorifies thy memory unto ages of ages.

*Another Sticheron:*
The chrism of grace poured out upon thee anointed thee to minister sacredly the Gospel of the heavenly Kingdom, O Basil revealed by God; having become the sweet savor of Christ, with the fragrance of His knowledge thou didst fill the inhabited world. Therefore, favorably receiving the voices of thy servants, entreat great mercy for us who honor thee.

*By Byzantios:*
Clothed in sacerdotal vestments, O Basil, champion of the Trinity, thou didst stand before the judgment seat, enduring danger for the faith; and having displayed steadfast suffering, thou didst put to shame the fury of the prefects raging with the power of impiety. And when he threatened thee with unmerciful dismemberment, having courageously resolved upon this, O father, and become a martyr by volition, thou didst receive the crown of victory from Christ, Who has great mercy.

*Glory, Tone 6:*
Grace was poured out upon thy lips, O venerable father, and thou wast a shepherd of the Church of Christ, teaching the reasonable sheep to believe in the Consubstantial Trinity in one Godhead.

...the royal priesthood, Christ's holy nation, O father, thou didst shepherd with love of wisdom and skill; then with the crown of the Kingdom did the King of kings and Lord of all adorn thee, the co-eternal and co-unoriginate Son united to Him Who begot Him; pray Him to save and enlighten our souls.

Adorned with hierarchical vestments, rejoicing thou didst proclaim the Gospel of the Kingdom, O Basil, as a fountain of orthodox teachings for the Church; enlightened by which, we now theologize and glorify one Godhead in the Father Almighty, and in the Only-Begotten Word of God, and in the Divine Spirit, indivisibly in three Persons: pray Him to save and enlighten our souls.

Dwelling together with the heavenly choirs and taking up thy abode with them, O father Basil, whose manner of life thou didst also emulate through the splendor of the purity of thy life, while yet living in the flesh with those upon earth as though bodiless: entreat Christ our God on behalf of those who delight in thy divinely-inspired teaching, that we may be delivered from tribulations and the darkness of ignorance, and that He may save and enlighten our souls.

*Glory, Tone 6. By Anatolius:*
Having become a lover of wisdom, O venerable one, and preferring that companionship which is toward God, having chosen the contemplation of death as thy consort, thou didst lead a comely life. For having surrounded thyself with the strongholds of temperance against carnal passions, and having kept the nobility of thy soul unenslaved through the rule of divine study, by the wealth of virtue thou didst subject all carnal mindedness to the spirit. Therefore, having hated the flesh and the world and the ruler of this world, as thou standest before Christ, entreat great mercy for our souls.

*Tone 5, Podoben: "Co-unoriginate with the Father" (Собезнача́льное сло́во):*

As the royal adornment of the Church let us all praise Basil, the inexhaustible treasury of teachings; for by them he taught us to revere the Holy Trinity, united indeed in essence, but divided in Hypostases.

*Glory, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior" (Гро́бъ тво́й, сп҃се):*
O all-wise Basil, as thou standest before the Trinity, pray that on the day of judgment grace, mercy, and the cleansing of transgressions may be obtained by us who sing unto thee and honor thy memory, that with mouth and heart we may glorify the one Lover of mankind.

*Both now:*
The Creator and Master of all the world, Who is above with the Father and the Spirit, is circumcised on earth as an eight-day-old infant. Truly divine and marvelous are Thy works: for Thou, O Master, wast circumcised for our sake, as Thou art the fulfillment of the Law.

By the power of divine words thou didst cast down dark heresies; thou didst drown all the arrogance of Eunomius, for thou didst proclaim the Spirit to be God unto men; and by the lifting up of thy hands thou didst put the enemies to death; thou didst drive away all the service of Sabellius, and thou didst abolish all the vain musings of Nestorius, O holy hierarch Basil: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who celebrate thy holy memory with love.

*Glory:*
The grace of thy divine words and the mystical height of thy dogmas became for us a ladder of divine ascent; for having acquired the trumpet of the Holy Spirit, O father, thereby thou didst proclaim the divine teachings. Therefore also thou hast taken up thine abode in a place of green pastures, having received the rewards of thy struggles, O venerable hierarch Basil: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who celebrate thy holy memory with love.

*Both now:*
The Master and Creator of men, having received flesh from thy most pure womb, showed thee to be the protectress of mankind, O all-immaculate one. Therefore we all flee unto thee, asking cleansing of transgressions, O Sovereign Lady, and to be delivered from eternal torment and from every malice of the cruel ruler of this world. Therefore we cry unto thee: pray thy Son and God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who bow down in faith to thy all-holy Nativity.
