[^3010]: *Communion Verse: Rejoice in the Lord*: LXX Psalm 32:1 ("Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous; praise becomes the upright", Slavonic *Ра́дꙋйтеся пра́веднїи ѡ҆ Го́сподѣ*).
[^3011]: *Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee*: The opening pre-Lenten Sunday of the Lenten Triodion cycle.
[^3012]: *Sunday of the Prodigal Son*: The second pre-Lenten preparatory Sunday.
[^3013]: *Meatfare Sunday*: The third pre-Lenten preparatory Sunday, dedicated to the Commemoration of the Second Coming of Christ and the Universal Last Judgment.
[^3014]: *By the rivers of Babylon*: LXX Psalm 136, the third Polyeleos psalm prescribed alongside Psalms 134 and 135 on the pre-Lenten Sundays of the Prodigal Son, Meatfare, and Cheesefare.
[^3015]: *The sun once shone upon the dry land*: Heirmos of Ode 1 of the Canon of the Meeting of the Lord (Church Slavonic *Сꙋ́шꙋ глꙋбороди́тельнꙋю зе́млю*).
[^3016]: *Meatfare Saturday*: The Saturday before Meatfare Sunday, dedicated to the Universal Commemoration of all the Faithful Departed (Soul Saturday). Because of the solemn requiem character of this day, the festive vigil of the Three Hierarchs is transferred forward to the preceding Friday.
[^3017]: *Cheesefare Week*: The week immediately preceding Great Lent (Maslenitsa), during which meat is forbidden but dairy products and eggs are permitted throughout the week.
[^3018]: *Where there is a triode*: Church Slavonic *И҆ди́же трїпѣ́снѡ*, referring to the three-ode canons appointed in the Lenten Triodion for weekdays of Cheesefare Week.
[^3019]: *Cyrus and John*: The Holy Unmercenary Wonderworkers Cyrus (an Egyptian physician from Alexandria who became an anchorite in Arabia) and John (a former Roman soldier from Edessa), who together encouraged the Christian woman Athanasia and her three young daughters (Theodotia, Theoktiste, and Eudoxia) and were beheaded under Diocletian at Canopus in 311.
[^3020]: *Joy of the heavenly ranks*: Church Slavonic model melody *Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ* (Tone 1).
[^3021]: *O most marvelous wonder*: Church Slavonic model melody *Ѽ пресла́внагѡ чꙋдесѐ* (Tone 8).
[^3022]: *When from the Tree*: Church Slavonic model melody *Е҆гда̀ ѿ дре́ва* (Tone 2).
[^3023]: *manfully vanquished*: The physical print shows faint inking or a paper crease between *мꙋ́жески* and *ди́ша*, reading *[побѣ]ди́ша* ("manfully conquered/vanquished").
[^3024]: *Thou Who wast lifted up*: Church Slavonic model melody *Вознесы́йся* (Tone 4).
[^3025]: *good torments*: Church Slavonic *бл҃гое мꙋ́къ*, an epigraphic reading reflecting *[бо]я́знь мꙋ́къ* ("fear of torments") or Greek *δεινὰ τῶν κολάσεων* ("terrors of torments").
[^3026]: *arrows of babes*: Scriptural citation from LXX Psalm 63:8 ("The arrows of babes have become their wounds").
[^3027]: *Today the Virgin*: Church Slavonic model melody *Дѣ́ва дне́сь* (Tone 3).
[^3028]: *He Who adorned the heaven with stars*: Church Slavonic model melody *И҆́же не́бо ѕвѣзда́ми* (Tone 3).
[^3029]: *Theodotia, Eudoxia, and Theoktiste*: The three young daughters of St. Athanasia (aged fifteen, thirteen, and eleven respectively), martyred together with their mother and the holy unmercenaries Cyrus and John at Canopus near Alexandria in 311.
[^3030]: *strangers*: Church Slavonic *приходцы*, meaning pilgrims, sojourners, or newcomers (*προσήλυτοι* / *ἐπήλυδες*).
[^3031]: *Called from on high*: Church Slavonic model melody *Зва́нный свы́ше* (Tone 4).
[^3032]: *1 Corinthians, pericope 153*: 1 Corinthians 12:27–13:8 ("Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular...").
[^3033]: *Matthew, pericope 34 from the half*: Matthew 10:1, 5–8 ("And when He had called unto Him His twelve disciples, He gave them power against unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal all manner of sickness and all manner of disease...").
[^3034]: *Communion Verse*: LXX Psalm 32:1 ("Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous").
[^3035]: *Typographic Tailpiece*: A characteristic Pochaiv monastic woodcut printer's mark bearing the radiate IHS monogram with cross and three nails, standardly employed by the Basilian Fathers of the Holy Dormition Pochaiv Lavra in their liturgical imprints.
[^3036]: *Catchword: Theotokia*: The catchword *Бг҃оро́дичны* at the lower margin indicates the sequence of Dismissal Theotokia for the eight tones found appended to the conclusion of the monthly volumes in the full liturgical set.
[^3037]: *Concluding Folio 224v*: This leaf concludes the text of the Month of January (leaves p1 through p450, folios 1r through 224v), completing the facsimiles and transcription of Volume 5 of the 1761 Pochaiv Menaion.
