[^2982]: *Birthgiver of God*: Church Slavonic *Богородица* (Theotokos).
[^2983]: *John Chrysostom*: Church Slavonic *Іѡаннъ Златоустый*, literally *John of the Golden Mouth*.
[^2984]: *Hieromartyr Hippolytus*: St. Hippolytus, Pope of Rome, commemorated liturgically on January 30 alongside the Three Holy Hierarchs.
[^2985]: *thy virginity*: Church Slavonic *дѣтства* (literally *childhood/maidenhood*, collated with Greek *παρθενίας*, virginity).
[^2986]: *The Word co-unoriginate*: Church Slavonic *Собезнача́льное Сло́во*, model melody of Tone 5 (attested in the text as *Собезначáлное*).
[^2987]: *Gospel according to John, pericope 36*: John 10:9–16, the standard Festal Gospel reading for holy hierarchs ("I am the door... I am the good shepherd").
[^2988]: *John of Euchaita*: Metropolitan John Mauropous of Euchaita (11th century), author of the canonical office to the Three Holy Hierarchs.
[^2989]: *Acrostic*: The Church Slavonic acrostic reads: *Трїсо́лнечнагѡ свѣ́та, трѝ возвыша́ю сїѧ̑нца* ("I praise the three rays of the Tri-sunned light"), composed in Byzantine iambic verse by John Mauropous.
[^2990]: *Katavasia*: Ode 1 Katavasia of the Meeting of the Lord (Church Slavonic *Сꙋ́шꙋ глубороди́тельнꙋю зе́млю*, "The sun once traversed the dry depth-bearing land").
[^2991]: *Katavasia*: Ode 3 Katavasia of the Meeting of the Lord (Church Slavonic *Оу҆твержде́ніе на тѧ̀ надѣ́ющихсѧ*, "O Lord, the confirmation of them that put their trust in Thee, establish the Church...").
