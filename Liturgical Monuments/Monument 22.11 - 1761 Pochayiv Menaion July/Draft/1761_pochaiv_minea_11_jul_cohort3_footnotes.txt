[^21]: The Greek name Hyakinthos (Ὑάκινθος) denotes both the flowering hyacinth and the precious gemstone (jacinth/sapphire, Rev. 21:20); the hymnographer plays on this lapidary imagery throughout the canon.
[^22]: The Church Slavonic acrostic reads: Тѧ̀ свѣ́тлый мꙋ́чениче хвалю̀ ка́мень ("Thee, radiant martyr, as a stone I praise"). In the Greek Menaion, the acrostic by Theophanes compares him to a sapphire (σάπφειρον), rendered in Slavonic by ка́мень ("stone").
[^23]: St. Andrew of Crete (c. 660–740 AD), Archbishop of Gortyna in Crete, born in Damascus and tonsured at the monastery of the Holy Sepulchre / Great Laura of St. Sabbas in Jerusalem (hence "the Hierosolymite"), renowned as the author of the Great Canon of Repentance and numerous festal triodia, heirmoi, and stichera.
[^24]: St. Martha of Antioch (d. 551 AD), the mother of St. Symeon Stylites the Younger of the Wonderful Mountain (feast day May 24).
