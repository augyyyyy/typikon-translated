[^612]: St. Prochorus, disciple of the Apostle Peter and companion of St. John the Theologian on Patmos, was consecrated first bishop of Nicomedia in Bithynia, where he faithfully shepherded the flock until his martyric death.

[^614]: St. Timon was appointed bishop of Bostra (Bozrah) in Arabia Petræa, where he was condemned by the pagans and cast into a furnace, receiving the crown of martyrdom.
[^615]: Unlike his three fellow deacons who met violent martyric deaths, St. Parmenas is recorded to have died peacefully in the Lord in Jerusalem while fulfilling his service at the tables of the poor, mourned and buried by the Apostles themselves.
