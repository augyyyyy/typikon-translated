[^314]: Slavonic: *Мц҃а тогѡ́же, въ в҃і де́нь. Ст҃ы́хъ мч҃нкъ Про́кла и҆ Їла́рїа: И҆ прпⷣбнагѡ ѻ҆ц҃а̀ на́шегѡ Мїхаи́ла малеї́на* (July 12). Proclus and Hilarion of Ancyra suffered under Trajan (early 2nd c.); St. Michael Maleinos (c. 894–961) was a Byzantine monastic founder on Mount Kyminas in Bithynia and spiritual father to St. Athanasius of Athos.
[^315]: Slavonic: *и҆ ꙗ҆́кѡ сѣ́на сла́вы вре́менныѧ, не све́рпа, ше́велѧ є҆сѝ, сла́вы бж҃твенныѧ не забꙋ́дꙋ* (Fol. 55r col. 1–2). An unusual Ruthenian print reading representing Greek: καὶ ὡς χόρτον δόξης προσκαίρου οὐκ ἐφείσω (and as grass didst not spare passing glory); the Pochaiv print exhibits corrupt orthography combining *не сверпа* with *шевеля* and first-person *не забуду*.
[^316]: Rubric: *И҆ ны́нѣ̀, бг҃о, и҆лѝ крⷭтобг҃о* (Fol. 55v col. 1). When the feast falls on a Wednesday or Friday, the Stavrotheotokion is chanted instead of the standard Theotokion.
[^317]: Rubric: *И҆ ны́нѣ̀, бг҃о: и҆лѝ крⷭтобг҃о.* Text incipit: *Ю҆́ница нескве́рнаѧ...* (The undefiled Heifer...). A classic Byzantine Stavrotheotokion based on Jeremiah 50:11 and Patristic typology.
[^318]: Slavonic: *бл҃гоче́* (Fol. 56r col. 1), scribal/typesetter abbreviation in the Pochaiv print for *благоче́стная* (or *благодатная*), Greek: κεχαριτωμένη.
[^319]: Slavonic: *по́ѧсъ ѿве́ргъ* (Fol. 56v col. 2). In Byzantine administrative practice, the military and patrician belt (*cingulum*, ζώνη) was the official insignia of senatorial or high court office; Michael Maleinos was nephew to Emperor Romanos I Lekapenos and of high patrician rank before renouncing the court for the monastic habit.
[^320]: Slavonic: *Подо́бенъ: Премꙋ́дрости* (Fol. 57r col. 1). Canonical melodic model: *Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and Word" (Премꙋ́дрости и҆ Сло́ва)*.
[^321]: Slavonic: *пло́ти не покрыва́ѧ бро́ви* (Fol. 58r col. 1), literally "not covering the brows/eyelids of the flesh," an idiom for keeping unbroken monastic vigil without sleep.
[^322]: Slavonic: *Зако́нѡмъ хва́льнымъ не порабо́тѧтъ* (Fol. 58r col. 2), representing Greek: ἐπαίνων νόμοις οὐχ ὑποπίπτει (the martyrs' courage falls not under / cannot be bound by the conventional rules of laudation).
[^323]: Slavonic: *Малос҃ло́вїе лѣ́ностное ᲂу҆ма̀ ѿгна́въ* (Fol. 58r col. 2). In ascetic literature, *малословие* here refers to mental sluggishness or brevity in contemplation.
[^324]: Slavonic: *Подо́бенъ: Тве́рдыѧ* (Fol. 58v col. 1). Full canonical incipit: *Tone 2, Podoben: "Seeking the highest / The firm and divinely-speaking preachers" (Тве́рдыѧ и҆ бг҃овѣща̑нныѧ проповѣ́датели)*.
[^325]: Slavonic: *водрꙋзи́въ є҆диноѡбра́знѡ сѣ́ни ꙗ҆́кѡ песо́къ* (Fol. 59v col. 1). *Сѣ́ни* (literally tabernacles, booths, cells) refers to the monastic lavriotic dwellings or sketes founded by St. Michael Maleinos on Mount Kyminas.
