BOOK OF THE MENAION
Month of July,
To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit; during the reign of the great
KING AUGUSTUS THE THIRD,[^1]
By the blessing of His Excellency, the Lord
SYLVESTER LUBIENIECKI-RUDNICKI,[^2]
Eparch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the resolve of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk
IPATII BILINSKY,[^3]
Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; through the zeal and expense of the Monks of the same Order, in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv,
PUBLISHED BY TYPE.
In the year from the Creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers 7269;
And from the Nativity of Christ 1761.[^4]

# MONTH OF JULY
*Having 31 days.*

*July 1: The Holy and Wonderworking Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian, Martyred in Rome:*

*Stichera on 6.*[^6]
*Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the Celestial Ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*

*Troparion:*
By marvelous rays drive away every disease of our afflictions, freely extending grace, being enriched with the gifts of the Teacher, Who upon earth took the sicknesses of the earthborn upon His shoulders.

*Troparion:*
Having first been well instructed in the healing art, through faith you cleanse the illnesses of all; and secondly, having armed yourselves spiritually, you divinely drive away also the injuries of spiritual maladies.

*Troparion:*
As you freely received grace from Christ God, O unmercenaries, you freely heal the infirmities of all; not only do you cleanse our illnesses, but as merciful ones you also bestow restoration rationally upon cattle.

*Other Stichera, Tone 4.*
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Hast Given a Sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*

*Troparion:*
With spiritual waters, overflowing like rivers and visibly filled, you water creation with divine signs and the most glorious gifts of healings. And you cast out soul-destroying passions, and heal infirmities, and drive away evil spirits, O God-bearing unmercenaries, intercessors for our souls.

*Troparion:*
Having subdued irrational passions by spiritual powers, O sacred ones, you bestow beneficence upon men and cattle, being enriched by Christ with the gift of healings. Therefore, as we celebrate your sacred and light-bearing solemnity, we ask for cleansing for our souls.

*Troparion:*
Your divine temple has been shown forth as a heaven, radiant

...and saving, having now acquired salvific wonders like stars, and like the bright sun the divine operation of healings: O most blessed Cosmas, O most glorious Damian, servants of the Lord, intercessors for our souls.

*Glory... Tone 6:*
Endless is the grace of the saints which they received from Christ; therefore their relics also, by divine power, unceasingly work wonders, whose names also, when invoked with faith, heal incurable diseases. Through them, O Lord, deliver us also from spiritual and bodily passions, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Both now... Theotokion or Stavrotheotokion:*
*Tone 6, Podoben: "Having Laid Aside All Earthly Hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*
When of old the ewe-lamb, the undefiled and spotless Lady, beheld her Lamb upon the Tree of the Cross, she cried aloud as a mother, and marveling she exclaimed: "O sweetest Child, what is this sight, new and most glorious? How does the ungrateful assembly deliver Thee to Pilate's judgment seat, and condemn unto death the Life of all? Yet I hymn Thine ineffable condescension, O Word."

*At the Aposticha, the stichera of the Octoechos. Glory... Tone 6:*
Ever having Christ working in you, O holy unmercenaries, you perform wonders in the world and heal the sick. For your medicine is an inexhaustible fountain: the more it is drawn, the more abundantly it flows forth; and as it is poured out, it abounds; day by day being depleted, it multiplies, giving unto all and remaining unfailing; and those who draw are filled with healing, while it remains unspent. What then shall we call you? Healing physicians of souls and bodies? Healers of incurable passions, who freely heal all, having received the gift from Christ the Savior, Who bestows upon us great mercy!

*Both now... Theotokion or Stavrotheotokion:*
*Tone 6, Podoben: "Having Risen on the Third Day" (Тридне́венъ):*
Beholding Thee crucified, O Christ, she who bare Thee cried aloud: "What is this strange mystery which I behold, [O my God]?[^7] How art Thou dying in the flesh, suspended upon the Tree, O Bestower of life?"

*Troparion, Tone 8:*
Holy unmercenaries and wonderworkers, Cosmas and Damian, visit our infirmities: freely you have received, freely give unto us.

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord," the Troparion twice.*
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion or Stavrotheotokion.*
*Both Kathismata from the Octoechos. And stichera...*

*And of the saints on 6, Tone 4.*
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
O Thou Who wast born of the Virgin, drown, I pray Thee, the tripartite power of my soul in the depth of dispassion, even as Thou didst the mighty captains of old, that unto Thee as on a timbrel, through the mortification of the body, I may sing a triumphal hymn.

*Troparion:*
O radiant pair of wise unmercenaries, as you stand before the honored Trinity with all the chosen, pray that those who keep your light-bearing memory may be enlightened with the divine brightness of the Spirit.

*Troparion:*
Shown forth as exalted in mind above material things, O wise and holy ones, you received the immaterial radiance of the Spirit. Therefore, by your divine visitations, O unmercenaries, you ever dispel the darkness of diseases.

*Troparion:*
Having acquired an unsleeping eye of the soul toward the divine fulfillment of God's commandments, O glorious, merciful, and God-bearing unmercenaries, by grace you awaken unto good health those sleeping in infirmities.

*Theotokion:*
From thy pure blood, O pure one, thou didst incarnate the Savior and God, Who through the Spirit showed forth unto us the honored unmercenaries as physicians for our souls, O most pure one, and as saving and fervent protectors.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
Not in wisdom, nor in power, nor in riches do we boast, but in Thee, the hypostatic Wisdom of the Father, O Christ: for none is holy save Thee, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*
Your divine temple sacredly pours forth fragrant myrrh and spiritual showers of healings, ever washing away foul-smelling passions.

*Troparion:*
Ever taking up your abode in the heavenly mansions, O all-wise ones, by the grace of the Almighty you ever show forth your tabernacle to be a fountain of healings.

*Troparion:*
Having curbed the passions of the flesh with the bridle of temperance, you most richly received spiritual beams. Therefore with healings you enrich the world.

*Theotokion:*
God, Who rests in His saints, chose thee as the fairest among women, and was well pleased to be born of thee in the flesh, O pure one.

Therefore, having acquired your divine church as a medicine for souls, fleeing unto it we cry: O God-bearing unmercenaries, pray to Christ

*Glory... Tone 8, Podoben: The same ("Of Wisdom"):*
Shedding the rays of healings upon those on earth, O divine wonderworkers, you manifestly drive away the ailments of men, having truly acquired within yourselves the unwaning Sun, Christ our God. Therefore, having worthily assembled, we honor your glorious and precious memory, O holy ones, God-bearing unmercenaries. Pray to Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who honor your holy memory with love.

*Both now... Theotokion:*
As the Virgin and the only one among women who without seed bare God in the flesh, all human generations call thee blessed; for the fire of the Godhead dwelt in thee, and as an infant thou didst nourish the Creator and Lord with milk. Therefore we, both the angelic and human race, worthily glorify thy most holy childbearing, and with one accord cry unto thee: Pray to Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who venerate thy most holy childbearing in faith.

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Beholding her Lamb and Shepherd and Deliverer upon the Cross, the ewe-lamb cried out weeping, and bitterly lamenting she exclaimed: "The world indeed rejoices, having received deliverance through Thee, but my womb burns within me as I behold Thy crucifixion, which Thou endurest through the tender compassion of Thy mercy, O longsuffering Lord. O abyss of mercy and inexhaustible fountain, have compassion and grant remission of transgressions unto those who in faith hymn Thy divine passions!"

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
He Who sits in glory upon the throne of the Godhead, on a swift cloud, Jesus the most divine, is come with an incorruptible palm, and saved those who cry: Glory to Thy power, O Christ!

*Troparion:*
By life-bearing operations and through every good work, you heal the mortal passions of men, O radiant pillars, immovable bulwarks of the true vineyard, divine branches.

*Troparion:*
Let the breasts of the Church, which pour forth the milk of healings, the enlightened unmercenaries who nourish all with divine foods, be praised with joyful hymns!

*Troparion:*
Your temple has been shown forth as a hospital, O holy ones, a tempest-free and saving haven for the storm-tossed, fleeing into which we all receive tranquility and deliverance from cruel afflictions.

*Troparion:*
As shoots of the divine vineyard, pouring forth the wine of healings unto us who are in the mourning of diseases, you fill us with gladness, O unmercenaries.

*Theotokion:*

Having received the grace of healings from God, you freely and fervently heal the passions of our souls and bodies, O all-comely unmercenaries. Therefore Christ, bestowing given health through you unto the faithful, shows you forth to the universe as unfailing beacons. Entreat Him that our souls may be saved!

*Troparion:*
Having drawn an abyss of healings from the Providence on high, you pour forth healings unto all the faithful, O unmercenaries; for supernaturally, through mystical handiwork, offering saving remedies from the treasures of the Spirit for the sufferings of the sick, you heal those who suffer. Therefore, having become an honored temple of the Life-originating Trinity, for It manifestly made Its dwelling in you: pray to It that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 2. By Theophanes:*
Having lived actively in divine love and desire for the things to come, you set aright saving pathways. Therefore, having kept spiritual purity undefiled, you stood against material things unto the end; and being enlightened by the divine Spirit, you freely bestow healing upon the infirm, O sacred pair, light-bearing assembly, enlightened divine yoke of unmercenaries, visiting us in tribulations and sicknesses, and gratuitously healing the infirmities of our souls.

*By Germanus:*
Having been accounted worthy of great gifts, O all-praised ones, you lived in humble conduct upon the earth; and going about everywhere, freely healing the sufferings of the sick, you were revealed as companions of the angels, Cosmas with wise Damian, all-comely brethren: heal our passions also by your prayers!
