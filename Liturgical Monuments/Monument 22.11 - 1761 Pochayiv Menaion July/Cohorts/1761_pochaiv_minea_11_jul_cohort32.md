# APPENDIX: RESURRECTIONAL THEOTOKIA FOR THE EIGHT TONES

*Resurrectional Theotokia for the Eight Tones.*[^657]

*Tone 1. Theotokion [Dogmatikon at "Lord, I have cried"]:*
Let us hymn Mary the Virgin, the glory of the whole world, who blossomed forth from mankind and gave birth unto the Master, the gate of heaven, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful! For she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she, having broken down the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace and opened the Kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the confirmation of faith, we possess as champion the Lord Who was born of her. Be of good courage, therefore, be of good courage, O people of God; for He shall vanquish the enemies, as Almighty!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Lo, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled;[^658] for thou, a virgin, didst give birth, and after childbirth didst remain as before childbirth, for God was He Who was born; therefore nature is newly transformed. But O Mother of God, despise not the entreaties of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple; but as thou bearest the Merciful One in thine arms, have compassion upon thy servants, and entreat that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 2. Theotokion [Dogmatikon at "Lord, I have cried"]:*
The shadow of the law has passed away, now that grace is come; for as the bush burned not when enveloped in fire,[^659] so as a virgin thou didst give birth, and a virgin didst remain. In place of the pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness has shone forth; in place of Moses, Christ, the salvation of our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O new wonder, greater than all ancient wonders! For who has known a mother who gave birth without a husband, and carried in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? It is the counsel of God that was born. Whom as an Infant having borne in thine arms, O most pure one, and possessing maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to entreat for those who honor thee, that He may have mercy and save our souls.

*Tone 3. Theotokion [Dogmatikon at "Lord, I have cried"]:*
How shall we not marvel at thy God-man childbirth, O most pure one? For without having received the experience of man, O all-undefiled one, thou didst give birth in the flesh unto the Son without a father, Who was born of the Father without a mother before the ages; Who in no wise underwent change, or confusion, or division...[^660]
