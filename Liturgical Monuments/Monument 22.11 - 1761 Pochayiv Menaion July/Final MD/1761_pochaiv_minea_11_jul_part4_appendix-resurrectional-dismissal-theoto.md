# APPENDIX: RESURRECTIONAL DISMISSAL THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES

*Resurrectional Dismissal Theotokia of the Eight Tones.*[^675]

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*
When Gabriel said unto thee: Rejoice, O Virgin! with the voice was the Master of all incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, as righteous David said;[^676] thou wast shown forth more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who made His abode in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who set us free through thy childbirth!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
All beyond understanding, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Birthgiver of God; being sealed in purity and preserved in virginity, thou wast known in truth as Mother, having given birth unto the true God: pray Him that our souls may be saved.

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
Thee, who didst mediate the salvation of our race, do we hymn, O Virgin Birthgiver of God; for thy Son and our God, having accepted the Passion through the Cross in the flesh assumed of thee, delivered us from corruption, as He loves mankind.

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto the angels, through thee, O Birthgiver of God, was revealed unto those upon earth: God being incarnate in unconfused union, and having willingly accepted the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised up the first-formed, He saved our souls from death.

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, wall and protection of those who flee unto thee! Rejoice, haven unstormed, and unwedded one, who didst bear in the flesh thy Creator and God: cease not to entreat for those who sing of and worship thy childbirth!

*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Having called Thy Mother blessed, Thou didst come unto the Passion by voluntary will, shining upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: "Rejoice with Me, for the lost drachma is found!"[^677] O our God, Who hast wisely ordered all things: glory to Thee!

*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
As the treasury of our Resurrection, lead up from the pit and depth of transgressions those who trust in thee, O all-hymned one; for *[Fol. 164v]*
...for thou hast saved those guilty of sin, having given birth unto our Salvation; who before childbirth wast Virgin, and in childbirth Virgin, and after childbirth still dost remain Virgin.

*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
O Good One, Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure the Cross, Who by death didst overthrow death, and didst show forth the Resurrection as God: despise not what Thou hast formed with Thine own hand; show forth Thy lovingkindness, O Merciful One; accept the Birthgiver of God who gave Thee birth, who prays for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people![^678]

---

### Notes

[^675]: The Resurrectional Dismissal Theotokia (*Voskresni otpustitelni bogorodychny*), sung after the Resurrectional Troparion at Saturday Great Vespers, Sunday Matins, and Divine Liturgy according to the Tone of the week.
[^676]: Ps. 131:8 (LXX: Ps. 131:8); "Arise, O Lord, into Thy rest; Thou, and the ark of Thy sanctuary."
[^677]: Luke 15:8–10; the Parable of the Lost Coin (drachma), typifying Christ's descent and the seeking out of lost Adam.
[^678]: The Tone 8 Resurrectional Dismissal Theotokion (*Voskresen otpustitelny bogorodychen*), derived from the Oktoechos and chanted at Saturday Great Vespers, Sunday Matins, and Divine Liturgy.
