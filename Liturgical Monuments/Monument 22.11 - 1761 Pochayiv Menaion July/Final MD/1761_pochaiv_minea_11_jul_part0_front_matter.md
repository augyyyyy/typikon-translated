# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 11: July (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-11-july-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

*(Title Page: Book of the Menaion — Month of July)*

BOOK OF THE MENAION
Month of July,
To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit; during the reign of the great
KING AUGUSTUS THE THIRD,[^1]
By the blessing of His Excellency, the Lord
SYLVESTER LUBIENIECKI-RUDNICKI,[^2]
Eparch of the Metropolitanate of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh; by the resolve of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk
IPATII BILINSKY,[^3]
Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great; through the zeal and expense of the Monks of the same Order, in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv,
PUBLISHED BY TYPE.
In the year from the Creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers 7269;
And from the Nativity of Christ 1761.[^4]

*(Copperplate Engraving — Frontispiece Insert Folio)*

*(Full-page copperplate engraving: Depiction of the wonderworking icon of our Most Holy Lady the Mother of God of Pochaiv with the Pre-eternal Infant on her arm, adorned with crowns and aureoles. At the top within a cartouche is an ornate monogram. Below, on a ribbon banderole, is an inscription in Polish:)*
"Wyobrażenie Cudotwornego Obrazu N. P. MARYI Poczaiowskiey Koronowanego Roku 1773 za Klemensa XIII."[^5]
*(Beneath the banner is depicted the apparition of the Mother of God in the burning bush leaving the imprint of the Pochaiv Footstep, flanked by the kneeling figures of Saint Job of Pochaiv on the left and the shepherd Ivan Bosy on the right.)*

### Notes

[^1]: Augustus III of Poland (Frederick Augustus II of Saxony, 1696–1763), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania from 1733 to 1763.
[^2]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Łubieniecki-Rudnicki, ca. 1700–1777), Ruthenian Uniate Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh (1756–1777), who administered the Metropolitanate of Kyiv and Halych as Eparch during the vacancy preceding Felician Wolodkowicz.
[^3]: Ipatii (Hypatius) Bilynsky (1704–1771), prominent Basilian scholar, Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great (OSBM) from 1751 to 1771.
[^4]: The Byzantine Era year 7269 (counting from 5508 BC) corresponds directly to AD 1761.
[^5]: "Depiction of the Wonderworking Image of Our Lady Mary of Pochaiv, Crowned in the Year 1773 under Clement XIII." Although Pope Clement XIV (1769–1774) reigned when the icon was solemnly crowned on September 8, 1773, the Pochaiv engraver's inscription attributes the papal decree or initial brief to Clement XIII (1758–1769).
