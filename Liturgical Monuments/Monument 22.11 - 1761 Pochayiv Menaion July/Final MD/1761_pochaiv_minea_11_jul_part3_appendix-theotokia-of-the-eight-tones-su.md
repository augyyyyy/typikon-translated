# APPENDIX: THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES SUNG THROUGHOUT THE WEEK

*Theotokia of the Eight Tones, sung when there is "Glory..." to the Saint in the Menaion, and "Both now..." according to the Tone of the Saint.*[^664]

*Tone 1.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Joy of the heavenly ranks, mighty protection of men upon earth, O most pure Virgin: save us who flee unto thee, for after God we have placed our hope in thee, O Birthgiver of God.

*On Monday at Matins, Theotokion:*
Holier than all the holy hosts, more honorable than all creation, O Birthgiver of God, Sovereign Lady of the world: save us, O thou who didst bear the Savior, from ten-thousandfold transgressions and tribulations, as thou art good, by thy prayers.

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
The mystery within thee, O all-hymned one, Moses beheld with prophetic eyes: the bush burning and unconsumed;[^665] for the divine fire burned not thy womb, O pure one. Therefore we pray thee, as Mother of our God: ask peace for the world, and great mercy.

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
I have surpassed the harlot and the prodigal, and the robber;[^666] and I have exceeded the publican in transgressions, and the Ninevites. Woe is me! What shall become of me? How shall I, wretched, escape punishment? O pure one, I fall down before thee: have compassion upon me by thy mercy, as thy Son has saved them!

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, joy of the forefathers, gladness of apostles and martyrs, and protection of us thy servants, O Virgin!

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O Virgin Birthgiver of God! Rejoice, praise of the whole universe! Rejoice, most pure, blessed Mother of God!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Truly beyond understanding are thy marvelous great things, O Bride of God, of thy childbirth which all the prophets proclaimed, all most glorious! Thy conception and childbirth, O all-hymned one, are incomprehensible and ineffable, whereby He has saved the world, as He is merciful.

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
Rejoice from us, O holy Virgin Birthgiver of God, pure vessel of the whole universe, unquenchable lamp, receptacle of the Uncontainable, unconquerable temple! Rejoice, of whom was born the Lamb of God, Who takes away the sins of the whole world![^667]

*[Fol. 161r]*
*Tone 2.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, Mary Birthgiver of God, indestructible temple, and yet more holy, as the prophet cries: "Holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness!"[^668]

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
We have placed our hope in thee, O Birthgiver of God, that we may not fall away from our expectation; save us from tribulations, O helper of the perplexed, and scatter the counsels of adversaries; for thou art our salvation, O blessed one!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Impassable gate, mystically sealed,[^669] blessed Virgin Birthgiver of God: accept our supplications, and bring them unto thy Son and God, that through thee He may save our souls.

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, Mary Birthgiver of God, indestructible temple, and yet more holy, as the prophet cries: "Holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness!"

*On Wednesday morning, Theotokion:*
As a fruitful olive tree,[^670] the Virgin budded forth Thee, the Fruit of life, to bear fruit for the world of great and rich mercy.

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
All my hope I place in thee, O Mother of God: preserve me beneath thy protection!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Save thy servants from tribulations, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, for after God we all flee unto thee as an unbreakable wall and protection.

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
Come, let all of us glorify the Mother of Light, calling in unceasing hymns, for she gave birth unto our salvation; and let us offer "Rejoice!" unto her who alone gave birth unto Him Who is before all beginning, God before the ages! Rejoice, thou who didst build up again fallen Eve! Rejoice, most pure, unwedded Virgin!

*Tone 3.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
O Birthgiver of God, protectress of all who pray unto thee: in thee we are bold, and in thee we boast, and in thee is all our hope; pray Him Who was born of thee for *[Fol. 161v]*
...thy unprofitable servants!

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
O firm, pure praise of the heavenly ranks that exist, song of the apostles, and fulfillment of the prophets, O Sovereign Lady: accept our prayers!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
O holy among women, Birthgiver of God, unwedded Mother: entreat Him Whom thou didst bear, the King and God, that He may save us, as He loves mankind!

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
Without seed thou didst conceive of the Holy Spirit, and giving glory we sing of thee: Rejoice, O all-holy Virgin!

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Snatch me who have greatly sinned, O Maiden, by thy great mercy from the tormenting flame; and set me upright by thy supplications, O pure one, guiding me unto the paths of salvation by thy maternal prayers!

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
Without seed thou didst conceive of the Holy Spirit, and giving glory we sing of thee: Rejoice, O all-holy Virgin!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
O holy among women, Birthgiver of God, unwedded Mother: entreat Him Whom thou didst bear, the King and God, that He may save us, as He loves mankind!

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
Without seed thou didst conceive of the Holy Spirit, and giving glory we sing of thee: Rejoice, O all-holy Virgin!

*Tone 4.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, cloud of light! Rejoice, radiant lampstand! Rejoice, jar wherein was the manna![^671] Rejoice, rod of Aaron! Rejoice, unconsumed bush! Rejoice, bridal chamber! Rejoice, throne! Rejoice, holy mountain! Rejoice, refuge! Rejoice, divine table! Rejoice, mystical door! Rejoice, joy of all!

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
O Birthgiver of God, Queen of all, praise of the Orthodox: destroy the haughtiness of heretics, and put to shame the faces of those who do not bow down before, nor honor, O most pure one, thy precious icon!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Deliver us from our necessities, O Mother of Christ God, who didst bear the Creator of all, that *[Fol. 162r]*
...all of us may cry unto thee: Rejoice, single protection of our souls!

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
Preserve thy servants from all tribulations, O blessed Birthgiver of God, that we may glorify thee, the hope of our souls!

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Having thee, O Birthgiver of God, as our hope and protection, we shall not fear the snares of the enemy, for thou savest our souls.

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
Thee have we acquired as a wall, O most pure Birthgiver of God, a calm haven, and a confirmation; therefore pray [for me who am] storm-tossed in this life: guide me, and save me!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
O unquenchable lamp, righteous throne, most pure Sovereign Lady: pray that our souls may be saved!

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
O only pure and most pure Virgin, who didst bear God without seed: pray that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 5.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Dread, most glorious, and great is the mystery: the Uncontainable was contained within a womb; and after childbirth the Mother remained again a virgin, for she gave birth unto God incarnate of her. Unto Him let us cry aloud, unto Him let us say a hymn, singing with the angels: Holy art Thou, O Christ God, Who wast made man for our sake, glory to Thee!

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
O thou who art full of grace, intercede by thy prayers, and ask for our souls a multitude of compassions and the cleansing of many transgressions, we pray!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Assuage the pains of my much-sighing soul, thou who didst wipe away every tear from the face of the earth;[^672] for thou drivest away the sicknesses of men and destroyest the afflictions of sinners; for thee all of us have acquired as hope and confirmation, O all-holy Virgin Mother!

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
O thou who art full of grace, intercede by thy prayers, and ask for our souls a multitude of compassions and the cleansing of many transgressions, we pray!

*[Fol. 162v]*
*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
We call thee blessed, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, and we the faithful glorify thee as is meet: an unshakable city, an impregnable wall, a firm protectress, and the refuge of our souls!

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
We call thee blessed, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, for from thee shone forth Christ, the Sun of righteousness, Who possesses great mercy!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Unto thee we pray as the Mother of God, O blessed one: pray for the salvation of our souls!

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
O wretched soul, what answer hast thou to say unto the Judge at that hour, when thrones shall be set up at the judgment, and the Judge shall come from heaven, having descended with myriads of angels?[^673] When He shall sit upon the judgment seat to make trial with unprofitable servants like unto me, what shalt thou answer? And what shalt thou bring then? Truly nothing, having defiled thy mind and body! Therefore fall down before the Virgin, and cry unceasingly, that He may richly grant thee forgiveness of sins!

*Tone 6.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Let us the faithful sing with the archangel of the heavenly bridal chamber and the gate truly sealed: Rejoice, through whom Christ, the Savior of all, the Giver of life and God, budded forth unto us! Cast down the tormentors, our godless enemies, O Sovereign Lady, by thy hand, O most pure one, hope of Christians!

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
Thou didst receive the archangel's word, and wast shown forth as the cherubic throne, and in thine arms didst bear [Him], O Birthgiver of God, hope of our souls!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
No one that flees unto thee departs from thee put to shame, O most pure Virgin Birthgiver of God, but asks for grace, and receives a gift according unto profitable petition.

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
Of great gifts, O pure Virgin Mother of God, wast thou accounted worthy; for thou didst bear in the flesh One of the Trinity, Christ the Giver of life, unto the salvation of our souls!

*[Fol. 163r]*
*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
I lift up the eye of my heart unto thee, O Sovereign Lady: despise not my small sighing at the hour when thy Son shall judge the world: be thou my protection and helper!

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
Thou art the relief of the afflicted, the healing of the sick, O all-hymned Birthgiver of God: save the city and people, O pacification of the embattled, calm of the storm-tossed, only protectress of the faithful!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
By the prayers of her who gave birth unto Thee, O Christ, and of Thy martyrs, apostles, prophets, hierarchs, venerable ones, righteous ones, and all the saints: give rest unto Thy departed servants!

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
We have come to know God Who was incarnate of thee, O Virgin Birthgiver of God: pray Him for the salvation of our souls!

*Tone 7.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
The "Rejoice!" we cry unto thee with the angel, O Bride of God, calling thee bridal chamber, and gate, and fiery throne, and unhewn mountain, and unconsumed bush!

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
By the prayers of the Birthgiver of God give peace unto our life who cry unto Thee: Merciful Lord, glory to Thee!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, cloud of the spiritual and ineffable Sun, O Sovereign Lady! Rejoice, all-radiant lamp! Rejoice, all-golden lampstand! Through thee, O most pure one, Eve was delivered from the curse; but as having boldness toward thy well-pleased Son and God, through thy maternal prayer cease not to pray, O most pure one!

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
Light hast Thou budded forth from the Virgin, O Christ, and Thou hast enlightened the human race: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
Let us sing of the only most pure Virgin after childbirth as Mother of God the Word, saying: Glory to Thee!

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
Thou wast born ineffably of the Virgin, O Christ, and Thou hast enlightened those that are in darkness, who cry: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
Thou who alone didst receive the Uncontainable, and didst bear God *[Fol. 163v]*
...the Word incarnate: pray that our souls may be saved!

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
Pray, O Virgin, with the apostles and martyrs, that those who have departed may find great mercy at the judgment!

*Tone 8.*

*On Sunday evening, Theotokion:*
Having received the voice of the Archangel Gabriel, let us say: Rejoice, Mother of God, who didst bear Christ, the Giver of life, unto the world!

*On Monday morning, Theotokion:*
The heavenly hosts sing of thee, O unwedded Mother full of grace; and we glorify thy unsearchable childbirth, O Birthgiver of God: pray that our souls may be saved!

*On Monday evening, Theotokion:*
Rejoice, praise of the universe! Rejoice, temple of the Lord! Rejoice, overshadowing mountain! Rejoice, refuge of all! Rejoice, golden lampstand! Rejoice, honorable glory of the Orthodox! Rejoice, Mary, Mother of Christ God! Rejoice, paradise! Rejoice, divine table! Rejoice, tabernacle! Rejoice, all-golden urn! Rejoice, hope of all!

*On Tuesday morning, Theotokion:*
Thy protection, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, is spiritual medicine; for fleeing unto it, we are delivered from spiritual infirmities.

*On Wednesday evening, Theotokion:*
I, O holy Virgin Birthgiver of God, flee unto thy protection; I know that through thee I shall find salvation, for thou art able, O pure one, to help me!

*On Thursday morning, Theotokion:*
Snatch me, O Sovereign Lady, from the hand of the man-slaying serpent,[^674] who desires through cunning to swallow me up utterly; shatter his jaws, I pray thee, and destroy his snares, that having escaped from his talons, I may magnify thy defense!

*On Friday evening, Theotokion:*
O pure Virgin, gate of the Word, Mother of our God: pray that we may be saved!

*On Saturday morning, Theotokion:*
O Sovereign Lady, accept the prayers of thy servants, and deliver us from every necessity and sorrow!

*[Fol. 164r]*

### Notes

[^664]: These are the daily dismissal Theotokia (*Bogorodychny otpustitelni*), appointed to be chanted throughout the liturgical week at Vespers and Matins when a commemoration in the Menaion has a "Glory..." to the saint and "Both now..." is taken according to the Tone of the saint.
[^665]: Ex. 3:2; the burning bush on Sinai seen by the Prophet Moses, prefiguring the Virgin Conception and the Divine Fire of the Godhead.
[^666]: Luke 7:37–50 (the repentant harlot), Luke 15:11–32 (the prodigal son), Luke 23:39–43 (the wise thief), and Luke 18:9–14 (the publican); Jonah 3:5–10 (the repentance of Nineveh).
[^667]: John 1:29; "Behold the Lamb of God, Who takes away the sin of the world."
[^668]: Ps. 64:5–6 (LXX: Ps. 64:4–5); "Holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness: give ear unto us, O God our Savior."
[^669]: Ezek. 44:1–3; the shut eastern gate seen by the Prophet Ezekiel through which none shall enter but the Lord God of Israel.
[^670]: Ps. 51:10 (LXX: Ps. 51:8); "But I am like a fruitful olive tree in the house of the Lord."
[^671]: Heb. 9:4, Ex. 16:33–34, Num. 17:8; the golden urn containing the manna, the rod of Aaron that budded, and the tables of the covenant within the Holy of Holies, all fulfilling their types in the Virgin Mother of God.
[^672]: Isa. 25:8, Rev. 21:4; "And the Lord God will wipe away tears from all faces."
[^673]: Dan. 7:9–10, Matt. 25:31; the dread Last Judgment, the setting up of the thrones, and the coming of the Son of Man with His holy angels.
[^674]: Rev. 12:9, 1 Pet. 5:8; the adversary depicted as the ancient serpent and roaring lion seeking whom he may devour.
