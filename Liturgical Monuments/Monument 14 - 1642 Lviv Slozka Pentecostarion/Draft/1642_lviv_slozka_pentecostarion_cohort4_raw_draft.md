*Synaxarion of Palm Sunday, the Master's Feast*[^119]
*Verses:*
*Seated upon a colt, He Who by His word stretched out the heavens,*
*Seeking to deliver mortals from their irrationality.*

On this manifest and most radiant day we celebrate the Feast of Palms, for the following reason: After the raising of Lazarus from the dead, many, seeing what had come to pass, believed in Christ. But the council of the Jewish Sanhedrin resolved to kill Lazarus himself also. Jesus therefore withdrew, giving opportunity for their malice to abate; but the Jews made a decree to kill Him at the feast of the Passover. A considerable time of withdrawal having elapsed, six days before the Passover, as it is written, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus was who had died; and when a supper was prepared there, Lazarus sat at meat with Him, and his sister Mary poured myrrh upon the feet of Christ.[^120] On the morrow He sends His Disciples to bring an ass and a colt. He Who has heaven for His throne, seated upon the colt, enters into Jerusalem. And the Hebrew children themselves spread their garments before Him, and branches of palms, some cutting them down, others carrying them in their hands, crying aloud and sending forth unto Him: "Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is He that comes in the name of the Lord, the King of Israel!"[^121]

This came to pass as the Holy Spirit moved their tongues unto the glory and high praise of Christ. By palms—that is, branches—they manifested the victory of Christ over death; for in Hebrew *vaïon* signifies a tender branch.[^122] For it was customary that victors in athletic contests or in wars were honored and escorted in triumphal processions with branches of evergreen trees. Mystically, the colt signified us, the people from among the Gentiles, upon whom Christ sat and rested, and was proclaimed Conqueror, Victor, and King of all the earth. Concerning this feast the Prophet Zechariah also spoke: "Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; behold, thy King comes unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of an ass."[^123] And David again spoke concerning the children: "Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings Thou didst perfect praise."[^124] But as Christ entered, all Jerusalem was moved, and the crowds, incited by the chief priests into turmoil, sought to put Him to death.

## Great and Holy Monday: Matins[^173]

*At Matins, the Priest says:* Blessed is our God... *And we say:* Amen. *The Trisagion, and 3 Prostrations. After "Our Father," "Lord, have mercy" (12 times). "Come, let us worship..." (3 times). Then the Six Psalms, and the Litany. After this, the Alleluia in Tone 8, in a loud voice and with sweet melody.*
*Verse 1:* From night my spirit wakes at dawn unto Thee, O God, for Thy commandments are a light upon the earth (Isaiah 26:9 LXX).
*Verse 2:* Learn righteousness, ye that dwell upon the earth (Isaiah 26:9b LXX).
*Verse 3:* Bring more evils upon them, O Lord, bring more evils on the glorious of the earth (Isaiah 26:15 LXX).
*Verse 4:* Thus were we to Thy beloved (Isaiah 26:16–17 LXX).

*Then this Troparion (3 times), Tone 8:*
Behold, the Bridegroom comes at midnight, and blessed is the servant whom He shall find watching; but unworthy again is the servant whom He shall find careless. Beware therefore, O my soul, lest thou be weighed down with sleep, lest thou be given over unto death, and be shut out from the Kingdom; but awake, crying aloud: Holy, holy, holy art Thou, O God: through the Theotokos, have mercy on us![^174] *(Chanted 3 times with a loud voice.)*

*Then the Psalter, with Prostrations.*

*After the 1st Kathisma: Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior" (Гро́бъ тво́й Сп҃се):*
The present day causes the honored Passions to shine forth upon the world as perfect luminaries; for Christ comes to suffer through His goodness: He Who holds all things in His palm is held, that He may be stretched out upon the Tree to save mankind!
*Glory... Both now... the same.*[^175]

*After the 2nd Kathisma: Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*
O invisible Judge, how wast Thou seen in the flesh, and how dost Thou go to be slain by lawless men, condemning our condemnation by Thy Passion? Therefore sending up praise, majesty, and glory unto Thy goodness, O Word, we offer it with one accord!
*Glory... Both now... the same.*

*After the 3rd Kathisma: Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and Word" (Премꙋ́дрости):*
The present day brings brightly the beginnings of the Lord's Passions. Come therefore, ye lovers of the feast, let us meet Him with hymns! For the Creator comes to accept the Cross, interrogations and scourging, judged by Pilate: therefore, struck on the head by a servant, He endures all things to save man. *Glory... Both now... the same.*[^176]
