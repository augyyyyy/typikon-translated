*It should also be known that we have received tradition not to celebrate the Presanctified, nor again the full Liturgy, on this holy day of Great Friday. Therefore, we do not even set a refectory table, nor do we eat on this day of the Crucifixion. But if anyone is exceedingly infirm or very aged, and unable to remain fasting, let bread and water be given him after the setting of the sun. For thus have we received from the holy injunctions of the holy Apostles,[^377] not to eat on Great Friday. For the Lord's word also, which He spoke to the Pharisees:*

=== LEAF p122 ===

*to the Pharisees: That when the Bridegroom shall be taken away from them, then shall they fast in those days. Which the most blessed Apostles also observed, as thou shalt find in the Apostolic Traditions, that is, in the Canons, by examining these things attentively.*

*In the Studite Typikon, moreover, it is commanded that on the holy Great Friday of Holy Great Week, foods are given according to the first week of the Fast.[^378]*

*The Typikon of the Holy Mountain, however, judges that on Great Friday one may eat bread and uncooked vegetables. Even as in another determination it testifies consistently, saying: On Great Friday, if it be possible, it is not proper to eat, as the Fathers command. Yet we do not hold this as an absolute prohibition from not eating sacred things, but it was appointed by the Fathers according to each one's strength. And for this reason, if it be possible to keep these two days in fasting, Friday and Saturday, it is good and acceptable. But if not, at least on Saturday. On Great Friday, then, eat bread and uncooked vegetables, and drink water; yet in this also, as each wills and is able. Only let all things be done with the discernment of the Superior, or of the most experienced.*

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*  
There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and hast established them upon the rock of Thy confession.

"For fear of the Jews Peter hid himself, and all the faithful fled away, forsaking Christ," cried the Virgin, weeping.

*Troparion:*  
"I would receive upon my hands from the Tree Thine inward parts, on which I held Thee as an Infant," said the Pure One; "yet alas, no one gives Him unto me!"

*Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.*

*Theotokion:*  
"Lo, my sweet Light, my Hope and good Life, my God is extinguished upon the Cross! Mine inward parts are inflamed!" cried the Virgin, groaning.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*  
Christ is my power, my God and Lord, the venerable Church divinely sings, crying out from a pure conscience, keeping festival in the Lord.

*Refrain:* Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.

*Troparion:*  
"O unsetting Sun, eternal God, and Creator of all creatures, O Lord, how dost Thou endure suffering upon the Cross?" cried the Pure One, weeping.

*Troparion:*  
Weeping, the Unwedded One said unto the noble Joseph: "Hasten, Joseph, draw near unto Pilate, and ask to take down from the Tree thy Teacher!"

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit:*

*Troparion:*  
Seeing the All-Pure One shedding bitter tears, Joseph was troubled, and weeping, drew near unto Pilate, crying aloud with tears: "Give me the Body of my God!"

*Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.*

*Theotokion:*  
"Beholding Thee wounded, inglorious, and naked upon the Tree, O my Child, mine inward parts are consumed as I weep," said the Virgin Mother.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*  
With Thy Divine light, O Good One, illuminate, I pray Thee, the souls of those who rise early unto Thee with love, that they may see Thee, O Word of God, the true God, recalling them from the gloom of sin.

*Refrain:* Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.

*Troparion:*  
Torn with grief and weeping, and marveling together with Nicodemus, Joseph took Him down, and kissing the spotless Body, wept and groaned, and praised Him as God.

*Troparion:*  
Receiving Him with tears, the Mother that knew not wedlock laid Him upon her knees, entreating Him with tears and kissing Him, bitterly lamenting and crying aloud:

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit:*

*Troparion:*  
"As mine only hope and life, O Master, my Son and my God, in the light of mine eyes had I Thee, Thine handmaid; but now am I bereft of Thee, my sweet and beloved Child!"

*Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.*

*Theotokion:*  
"Pains and sorrows and sighings have overtaken me, woe is me!" cried the Pure One, weeping bitterly, "beholding Thee, my beloved Child, naked and solitary, a dead corpse anointed with sweet spices!"

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*  
Beholding the sea of life surging with the storm of temptations, fleeing unto Thy calm haven, I cry unto Thee: Bring up my life from corruption, O Greatly Merciful One!

*Refrain:* Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.

*Troparion:*  
"Beholding Thee dead, O Lover of mankind, Who didst revive the dead,

*Ikos:*  
Beholding her own Lamb led to the slaughter, Mary followed with hair disheveled, together with the other women, crying aloud thus: "Whither goest Thou, O Child? *Ode 7. Heirmos:*  
The Angel made the furnace pour down dew for the Holy Children, and the command of God consumed the Chaldeans, compelling the tyrant to cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Refrain:* Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.

*Troparion:*  
"Where, my Son and God, is the ancient Annunciation which Gabriel spoke unto me, calling Thee King, Son, and God of the Most High? But now I behold Thee, my sweet Light, a naked, wounded corpse!"

*Troparion:*  
"O Deliverer from afflictions, now receive me with Thyself, my Son and God, that I also, O Master, may descend into Hades with Thee! Leave me not alone, for I can no longer bear to live without seeing Thee, my sweet Light!"

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit:*

*Troparion:*  
With the other Myrrh-bearing women, the Spotless One, weeping bitterly as she saw Christ being carried, said: "Woe is me, what do I behold? Whither goest Thou now, my Son, leaving me alone?"

*Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.*

*Theotokion:*  
Fainting and weeping, the Spotless One said unto the Myrrh-bearing women: "Lament with me and weep bitterly with me, for lo, my sweet Light and your Teacher is delivered unto the Tomb!"

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*  
From the flame didst Thou pour down dew for the Holy Youths, and didst consume the sacrifice of the righteous man with water: for Thou dost accomplish all things, O Christ, whatsoever Thou willest; Whom we supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Troparion:*  
Seeing the Virgin weeping, Joseph was wholly torn with grief, and cried out bitterly: "How shall I, Thy servant, now bury Thee, O my God? In what winding-sheets [Plashchanytsias] shall I wrap Thy Body?"

*Troparion:*  
"Beyond understanding is the strange vision of Thee, the Lord Who sustains all creation! Therefore Joseph, holding Thee dead in his hands, with Nicodemus carries Thee and buries Thee."

*Troparion:*  
"A strange and marvelous mystery do I behold!" cried the Virgin unto her Son and Lord: "How art Thou laid in a poor tomb, Who by Thy command dost raise the dead from the graves?"
