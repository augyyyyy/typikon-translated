O Savior, Who by Thine own hand didst fashion Adam and set the bounds between incorruption and mortality, and by grace didst exchange corruption for life, restoring him unto the first life: do Thou Thyself, O Master, give rest with the righteous in the choir of Thine elect unto Thy servants whom Thou hast translated from us, and having inscribed their names

O Savior, Who by Thy blood hast redeemed mankind, and by Thy death hast delivered us from bitter death, and by Thy Resurrection hast granted us life everlasting: give rest, O Lord, unto all that have fallen asleep in piety, whether in deserts, or in cities, or in the sea, or on land, or in any place: emperors, priests, hierarchs, monks, and laymen, in every age of the whole human race; and account them worthy of Thy Heavenly Kingdom!

Christ, having risen, loosed the bonds of Adam the first-formed, and destroyed the might of Hades: be of good cheer, all ye dead! Death is put to death, and Hades is led captive with it, and Christ is King, Who was crucified and is risen! He has bestowed upon us incorruption of the flesh; He raises us up, and grants us resurrection, and will account all worthy of that glorious joy who with faith and love have warmly trusted in Him!

The tasting of the tree of old in Eden became grief unto Adam, when the serpent vomited forth his poison: for through it entered universal death, devouring man. But the Master having come overthrew the serpent, and granted us resurrection: let us therefore cry aloud unto Him: Spare, O Savior, and give rest with the saints unto those whom Thou hast taken, as Lover of mankind!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
By the prayers of her that bore Thee, O Christ, of Thy martyrs and apostles, prophets, hierarchs, venerable and righteous ones, and all the saints: give rest unto Thy departed servants!

*At the Aposticha, from the Octoechos: Stichera by Theophanes, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ᲊпова́нїе):*[^935]
Possessing incomprehensible tender mercy toward us, and an inexhaustible wellspring of divine goodness, O greatly merciful One: settle in the land of the living those who have passed away unto Thee, O Master! In beloved and longed-for dwellings, granting an inheritance that abides forever! For Thou didst shed Thy blood for all, O Christ, and didst redeem the world with a life-bearing price!

*Verse: Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken, O Lord [LXX Ps. 64:5].*
Having willingly endured life-creating death, Thou didst pour forth life and gavest everlasting sweetness unto the faithful, and divine loving-kindness, O Master, Who alone art all-bountiful and sinless: therein we pray Thee, rank those who have fallen asleep in the hope of the resurrection, granting remission of sins, that Thy Name may be praised above all things, O Christ, and being saved we may glorify Thy love for mankind!

*Verse: And their soul shall dwell among good things [LXX Ps. 24:13].*
Ruling over the living by Thy divine authority, and exercising mastery over the dead, we pray Thee with all our soul, O Christ: do Thou Thyself, O Lover of mankind, give rest unto Thy faithful servants who have departed unto Thee, the only Benefactor, with Thine elect, in places of refreshment, in the splendors of the saints! For Thou art the Desirer of mercy, and savest as God those whom Thou didst create according to Thine image, O Thou Who alone art abundant in mercy!

# PART V: GREAT FEAST OF HOLY PENTECOST AND ALL SAINTS[^937]

## The Holy and Glorious Sunday of Pentecost

*On Saturday evening, at Little Vespers:*

*The Priest having given the blessing, we say: "O Heavenly King..." and the rest.*

*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera on 4, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чино́въ):*[^938]
Now the nations are renewed to proclaim the marvelous works of God in strange tongues and with a vision of fire, for the assurance of all that dwell upon earth, to report openly now as a sign unto the nations: true salvation is come! *(Twice).*

The power from on high which Thou didst promise to send unto Thy disciples, O Christ, having accomplished it, Thou dost send Thy Holy Spirit, showing us that Thou art the Truth, and that those who hope in Thee, O Good One, shall truly never be put to shame!

Having fulfilled the promise of Thine own Comforter, Thou hast sent Him unto the world as in fiery tongues, to consume with divine fire the sins of all, and to grant unto us pure communion with Him, as we faithfully love Him!

*Glory... Both now... Tone 7:*
Having the Comforter Christ God before the Father, now that another Comforter is come upon earth unto us this day, let us worship with faith the Holy Spirit!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^939]
Enlightened in mind with the God-originating radiance, Thy disciples, O Word, essentially received the Holy Spirit!
