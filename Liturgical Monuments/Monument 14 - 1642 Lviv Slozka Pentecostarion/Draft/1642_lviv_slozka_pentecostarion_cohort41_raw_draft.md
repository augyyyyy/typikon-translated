Save, O Comforter, those who faithfully hymn Thy divine coming, Who proceedest through the Son; and cleanse us from every defilement, as Thou art compassionate, and show us worthy of Thine illumination, making us spotless mirrors by Thy most God-beholding light!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
The abyss of prophecies mystically taught concerning thee, foreseeing the mystery of the ineffable and divine incarnation of God the Word, proclaimed thee as Virgin Mother: for thou hast revealed the truest and ancient Light!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^1014]  
The Children nurtured in piety, disregarding the ungodly command, feared not the threat of fire; but standing in the midst of the flame, they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*  
Now is the fulfillment of Christ's promise accomplished:

*Troparion:*  
Of old the senseless agreement of tongues was divided, but now they are manifestly gathered into one composition, through the wonder-working and divine Spirit Himself, One of the transcendent Trinity!

*Glory...*  
*Troparion:*  
At the breath of the Holy Spirit carried from on high, the apostles of Christ proclaimed with marvelous voice the mighty works of God, singing in harmony: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
The three Children in the furnace prefigured the image of thy childbearing: for as they remained unconsumed by the fire, so wast thou preserved pure, having received in thy womb the unendurable Fire, the blessed God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^1015]  
The prefigured image of a supernatural wonder: for the furnace burned not the youths whom it received. So neither did the Fire of the Godhead burn the Virgin's womb into which it entered: therefore let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Troparion:*  
O Holy Spirit, proceeding from the Father, granting holiness unto all who believe in Thee, for Thou art holy and the Bestower of holiness unto men: therefore singing, we cry: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Troparion:*  
Thou grantest the gift of grace as Benefactor unto those who hymn Thee, O Comforter, for Thou art the Giver of good things and an ocean of grace: therefore praising Thee, we sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Glory...*  
*Troparion:*  
True and life-creating is the Spirit, self-moved, self-governing, distributing the distribution of gifts as He wills, sovereign, self-commanding, unoriginate: Whom we now worship and sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
Who marvels not at the exceeding multitude of Thy compassions, O unoriginate Word? For for our sake, being rich, Thou didst become poor, and didst dwell in the womb of the holy Virgin: therefore singing, we cry: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^1016]  
Rejoice, virgin glory! Rejoice, all-pure Mother: whom all creation magnifies with divine hymns!

*Troparion:*  
Behold, Thou hast sent down unto us another Comforter, O Thine Only-begotten Word, co-enthroned with Thy Father!

*Troparion:*  
Save from temptations, O Comforter, those who confess Thee as co-enthroned, and who glorify Thine eternal being!

*Glory...*  
*Troparion:*  
Come unto us, O Comforter, satisfying us with Thy comfort, as we theologically praise Thine ineffable glory!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
O all-immaculate Bride of God, deliver from temptations by thy prayers those who worthily honor thee!
