**Deacon:** And began to wash the disciples' feet, and to wipe them with the towel wherewith He was girded...
*The Superior begins to wash the feet of the brethren, beginning from the Doorkeeper down to the Ecclesiarch or the Economos, and he washes them, wiping the feet of each one of those being washed, and kissing them. And when he comes unto the Economos, the Deacon says:*
**Deacon:** Then came He to Simon Peter; and Peter said unto Him:
**Peter:** Lord, dost Thou wash my feet?[^312]
*And he performs according to the pattern of the Gospel. The Superior answers:*
**Abbot:** What I do thou knowest not now, but thou shalt understand hereafter.
*And the Senior says unto him:*
**Peter:** Thou shalt never wash my feet!
*And the Superior answers:*
**Abbot:** If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with Me.
*Then the Senior rises up a little, as if struck with fear, and lifting up his hands, points with his right hand toward his feet, saying in a quiet voice, so that all may hear:*
**Peter:** Lord, not my feet only!
*And showing his hands, he says:*
**Peter:** But also my hands!
*And again pointing to his head, bowing slightly, he says:*
**Peter:** And my head!
*And the Superior says:*
**Abbot:** He that is washed needs not save to wash his feet, for he is clean every whit; and ye are clean, but not all.
*And when the Deacon has finished the Gospel, the Senior sits down, and the Superior washes his feet also, as he did beforehand unto the rest. And after the washing of all, the Superior, having taken off the towel, girds himself with his own linen cloth, and puts on his vestments. And the Deacon exclaims:*
**Deacon:** That we may be counted worthy to hear the holy Gospel...
*The Superior himself reads the Second Gospel of the Footwashing, saying:*
**Abbot:** The reading from the Holy Gospel according to John (John 13:12–17, pericopa 42):
At that time, when Jesus had washed the feet of the disciples, He took His garments...
*And after the Gospel the Deacon says:*
**Deacon:** Let us pray to the Lord.
**Choir:** Lord, have mercy.

*Prayer of the Superior in the Hearing of All:*
**Abbot:** O Lord our God, according to Thy great mercy, Who didst empty Thyself and take the form of a servant, Who at the time of Thy saving, life-creating, and voluntary Passion didst deign to sup with Thy holy disciples and Apostles, and thereafter, having girded Thyself with a towel and washed the feet of Thy holy disciples, didst give them an example of humility and love one toward another, and didst say: "As I have done unto you, so do ye also one to another": do Thou Thyself now also, O Master, coming into the midst of Thine unworthy servants who follow Thine example, wash away every defilement and impurity of our souls, that having washed away the dust of transgressions that has cleaved unto us, and having wiped one another with the towel of love, we may be able to be well-pleasing unto Thee all the days of our life, and find grace before Thee!

*Exclamation:*
**Abbot:** For Thou art good and the Cleanser of all things, O Christ our God, and unto Thee we send up glory, with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thine all-holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.
**Choir:** Amen.

*And after the conclusion of the Prayer, all the brethren are sprinkled from the basin. Together we chant the Troparion from the Fifth Ode of Great Friday, Tone 6:*
Having washed their feet and having been purified by the communion of the Divine Mysteries, Thy servants, O Christ, now went up from Zion unto the great Mount of Olives, singing unto Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Then: "Blessed be the name of the Lord," and "I will bless the Lord at all times." Antidoron is given, and the Dismissal.*
*Then we enter into the refectory, and eat cooked food with oil (if there is blessing), and drink wine, three cups each, according to the Jerusalem Typikon and that of the Studite Monasteries.[^313] But in Muscovite editions without oil, only dry eating, because Alleluia is chanted. Yet let all things be according to the discretion of the Superior.*

## Great and Holy Thursday: Small Compline

*On the same Great Thursday at Compline, we chant the Three-Ode Canon (Triode), the composition of the Lord Andrew of Crete, Tone 8:*
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^315]
Dispel the darkness of my soul, O Christ God, Giver of light, Who didst drive away the primordial darkness of the abyss, and grant me the light of Thy commandments, that rising early I may glorify Thee!

*Troparia:*
The Supper was adorned, and the Passover was prepared for Thee, as Thou didst say, O Christ: but Judas hastened unto Thy betrayal, and being with Thee, estranged from Thy price, he made agreement through avarice!

Christ voluntarily rises from supper, and is girded with a towel about His loins, and bows His neck, while Peter cries aloud: "Thou shalt never wash my feet, O my Creator: yet wash them!"

Judas the deceitful disciple received the bread in his hands unto betrayal, by which also he accomplished the betrayal against Thee; he stretched forth his feet, which Thou Thyself didst wash and wipe with a towel!

With a deceitful kiss Judas then opened wide his mouth unto Thee, with which he unworthily dipped Thy Body, O Word, crying unto Thee: "Hail, Master!" the kisser and betrayer, servant and deceiver!

Peter, as he beheld the things that were being done, being then seized with fear, was convicted by the question of a wretched maidservant: for he denied Thee, the Lord, not only as Thou didst say, but as Thou, the All-Knowing One, didst know!

The Creator is struck on the cheek, and creation is smitten with His reproach; He is willingly beaten with a reed, and the highest things are bowed down; the Judge is spat upon, and all the foundations of the earth are shaken!

*Glory, Tone 8:*
God is crowned with thorns, He Who adorned all the earth with flowers; He receives stripes, and endures reproach longsufferingly, wearing the purple robe of mockery; and being God, He endures all things, and suffers in His flesh!

*Both now, Tone 8:*
Let John cry aloud, declaring through divine teachings Thine Incarnation: THE WORD BECAME FLESH of the Virgin without change, and remained God by nature as He was, not departing from His Father's bosom!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
He Who covers His upper chambers with waters, Who set the sand as a bound for the sea, and Who upholds all things: Thee the sun sings, Thee the moon glorifies, unto Thee all creation offers a hymn, as the Creator of all unto the ages!

*Troparia:*
O Jesus, Who clothest heaven with clouds, sitting on the throne of glory with the incorrupt Father: Thou, having taken a towel, didst gird Thyself to wash the clay feet, being all fire, O Word, although Thou wast incarnate!

When He had washed all, Jesus reclined and said unto His disciples: "Know ye what I have done unto you? For I have given you all an example of humility, that whosoever desires to be first, let him be the last of all by free choice!"
