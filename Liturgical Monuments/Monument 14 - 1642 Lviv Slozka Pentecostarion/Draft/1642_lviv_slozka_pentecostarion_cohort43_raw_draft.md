## First Sunday after Pentecost: Sunday of All Saints

*On Saturday evening, we celebrate the customary All-Night Vigil.*  
*At Small Vespers: in the Octoechos, for 4: one sticheron, twice. Glory... Both now... Dogmatikon, Tone 8:*[^1041]

For every Maker and Fashioner of our nature, having had compassion on His own image, brought Himself down into ineffable emptying: the Son, Who is in the immaterial bosom of the Father, took up His abode in thy womb, O pure one, and unchangeably became flesh from thee who knewest not wedlock, remaining what He was by nature, God. Therefore, we worship Him as perfect God and perfect man, the same in both

Entreat Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may be saved!

*Stichera to All Saints, Tone 6, Podoben: "All the hope" (Все ᲊпова́нїе):*  
The spiritual orators, the disciples of the Savior, having become instruments through faith, scattered throughout the ends of the earth the venerable preaching, sowing right belief; and by divine husbandry and grace they sprouted the nations, forming hosts of martyrs wondrously depicting Christ's passions on account of many kinds of diverse wounds and fire; and with boldness they pray for our souls! *(Twice)*

Kindled by the fire of the love of the Lord, they despised fire; and as divine coals ignited in Christ, the ranks of the martyrs burned up the audacious insolence of deceit; and they stopped the mouths of wild beasts by divine invocations; and being beheaded, they struck down all the armies of the enemy, and patiently pouring forth streams of blood, they watered the Church that is enlightened through faith!

Throughout all the ends of the earth, let us worthily praise with sacred hymns those who suffered faithfully: the apostles, the martyrs, the God-wise hierarchs, and the sacred assembly of holy women; for earthly beings were united unto heavenly ones, and through sufferings they received freedom from passion through the grace of Christ; and now, shining as bright stars upon us, with boldness they pray for our souls!

The light of the Resurrection shone forth unto those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, even my God Jesus; and having bound the strong one by His Godhead, He despoiled his vessels!

*Theotokion:*  
Thou hast been shown higher than the Cherubim and Seraphim, O Mother of God; for thou alone, O undefiled one, didst receive the uncontainable God in thy womb: therefore all we the faithful bless thee, O pure one, in hymns!

*Troparion:*  
Him that previously was disobedient to the commandment of the Lord, Thou didst bring near unto Thyself Who didst create me; taking his form upon Thyself and instructing him by Thine own obedience, Thou hast restored him by Thy crucifixion!

*Troparion:*  
Foreseeing all things by Thy wisdom, O Lord, and having established the nethermost parts by understanding, Thou didst not deem it unworthy by Thy descent, O Word of God, to raise up him who was fashioned according to Thine image!

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*  
Thou hast been the restoration of Eve our foremother, having given birth to the Prince of Life for the world, Christ, O Mother of God!

*Fourth Canon (All Saints), Ode 3. Heirmos: The heavens were established...*  
*Troparion:*  
Holily robed as hierarchs and pastors, and having pastored Thy rational flock in a manner pleasing to Thee, O Christ, they worthily adorned the word of teaching, being truly enriched from above! *(Twice)*

*Troparion:*  
Adorned with the beauties of the primary creation of good, and appearing as unerrant stars, ye adorned the Church of Christ, O holy ones, embellishing her in diverse ways! *(Twice)*

*Glory...*  
*Troparion:*  
Yielding obedience unto Thy law, the assemblies of the blessed ones, illuminated by manifold virtues, inherited the heavenly mansions, rejoicing; for diverse ranks worthily filled them!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
Virgin Earth, who didst bear for us the God Word from God, O Mother of God: Whom the holy virgins divinely loved, and following after thee, they manifestly followed Him in all things!

*Sessional Hymns, Tone 8:*  
Having endured mountains of tortures and standing as spiritual pillars, the all-lauded martyrs of Christ our God, taken up from the earth to the height, became eyewitnesses of heavenly glory by their translation: therefore, having been worthily enriched with the gifts of the Spirit, ye ever bestow healing! O all-lauded passion-bearers, entreat Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who lovingly celebrate your holy memory!

*Another Sessional Hymn, the same Tone. Glory...*  
Celebrating the holy memory of the Forefathers, Fathers, Patriarchs, Apostles, Martyrs, Hierarchs, Prophets, and the Venerable, Ascetics, and Righteous, and every name written in the Book of Life, O Christ God, we urge them all to prayer, entreating: Pacify Thy world for their sake, as Thou art the Lover of mankind, that we all may cry unto Thee: O God Who art glorified in the council of Thy saints, Thou art truly He Who hath worthily glorified their memory!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
Heavenly Gate and Ark, All-Holy Mountain, and radiant Cloud, unburnt Bush of sufferings, rational Paradise and restoration of Eve, great vessel of the whole universe; for salvation was wrought in thee for the world, and remission of ancient transgressions: therefore we cry unto thee: Entreat Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who piously worship thy most holy childbearing!

*Troparion:*  
Being loathsome, Thou didst greatly love me; by a strange emptying Thou didst descend upon the earth, O compassionate Savior, not rejecting the extremes of my reproach; and remaining in the height of Thine all-pure glory, Thou hast glorified him who was previously dishonored!

*Troparion:*  
Who that beholds, O Master, doth not now marvel at Thee Who hast destroyed death by Thy passions? Corruption is put to flight by the Cross, and by death the wealth of Hades is emptied out through divine power, O Crucified One: a fearful work, O Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:*  
Thou art the boast of the faithful, O unwedded Bride; thou art the intercessor, thou art the refuge of Christians, a wall and a haven; for thou dost bear supplications unto thy Son, O all-blameless one, and dost save from perils those who acknowledge thee in faith and love as the pure Mother of God!

*Second Canon, Ode 4. Heirmos: I have heard, O Lord...*  
*Troparion:*  
The children of the lawless nailed Thee upon the Cross, O Christ God; whereby Thou hast saved those who glorify Thy sufferings, as Thou art merciful!

*Troparion:*  
Having risen from the tomb, Thou didst raise with Thyself all the dead that were in Hades; and Thou didst enlighten them, as Thou art compassionate, who glorify Thy Resurrection!

*Third Canon, Ode 4. The same Heirmos.*  
*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*  
Enlightened, we all praise thee as Mother of God, O all-pure one: for thou hast ever given birth to the Sun of Righteousness, O Virgin!

*Fourth Canon (All Saints), Ode 4. Heirmos: Thou art my Lord, O Christ...*  
*Troparion:*  
The assembly of Thy saints was gathered together for the sake of Thy love, enjoying Thee; and truly and purely in gladness their unbroken choir celebrates with the angels, through Thee, the Cause, the God of all, and the Lord! *(Twice)*

*Troparion:*  
Youthfully, O glorious holy martyrs, ye were adorned with the martyrs' crown through the divine anointing wherewith ye were first adorned in the priesthood, O most wealthy ones: therefore, having worthily received double crowns, ye rejoice eternally in Christ!

*Troparion:*  
All the God-bearing ones subjected the carnal mind unto the truth, by temperance withering its unbridled impulses through labors; and now, enlightened with the light of passionlessness, ye have received the reward of your toils!

*Glory...*  
*Troparion:*  
As the champions of Christ, O martyrs, ye steadfastly and bravely endured extreme torments, as though suffering in alien bodies, O glorious ones; and now, shown to be inheritors of the Kingdom, ye pour forth healings for the faithful!

*Both now... Theotokion:*  
Thou hast been the Bride of God, O Mother of God; for thou hast bodily borne for us His pre-eternal Word: in Whom the holy women also lived, and being adorned through thee with all-various honors, they corrected the fall of our foremother!
