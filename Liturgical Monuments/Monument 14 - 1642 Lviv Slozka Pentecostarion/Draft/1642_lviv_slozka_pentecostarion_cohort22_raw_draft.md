*At "Lord, I have cried", we set 6 Stichera: of the feast, 3; and of the Menaion, 3. Stichera of the feast, Tone 1:*  
*Tone 1, Podoben: "O All-Praised Martyrs" (Прехва́лнїи мꙋ́ченицы):*  
Touch, the Lover of mankind, Who rose on the third day from the dead, says unto Thomas: and behold the wounds of My hands and feet, and the piercing of My side, and know Me to be immutable God, fashioned according to essence in earthly commingling, and having received suffering therein!  
The Twin was filled with awe, beholding the wounds of the feet and the piercing of the hands, and was astonished at the fearful miracles; and touching and seeing the most pure side with his hand, he showed forth unto the nations the third-day rising from the tomb, which is beyond understanding!  
I receive great joy, beholding Thy abundant rising, O my Savior, wherein Thomas was accounted worthy to touch daring things; therefore we confess Thee as simple God and man, bearing two sacred operations, of which Thou wast composed! *Glory: Both now: Tone 4:*  
While the doors were shut, Thou didst come with the unendurable brilliance of Thy Godhead; and standing in the midst of the disciples, Thou didst bare Thy side, showing the signs of the wounds of Thy hands and feet, resolving sorrow and despondency, as Thou didst cry aloud: In what manner ye see the assumption of flesh in Me, O friends, I bear not the nature of a spirit! And unto the doubting disciple Thou didst command to touch with trembling, saying: Having searched out, come, henceforth be not faithless! And he, perceiving Thy twofold nature with his hands, cried aloud with fear, drawn by faith: My Lord and my God, glory to Thee!
