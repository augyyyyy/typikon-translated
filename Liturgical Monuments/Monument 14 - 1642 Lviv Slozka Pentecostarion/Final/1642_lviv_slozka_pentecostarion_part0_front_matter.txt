[Front Outer Binding Board: 17th-century blind-tooled calfskin over wooden boards (dark brown leather over wooden boards with multi-line blind-tooled frame fillets, foliate rolls, corner fleurons, and a central eight-petaled floral rosette). Affixed to the fore-edge of the board are two brass clasp hinges and catches secured with leather straps.]

[Front Pastedown and blank flyleaf. In the upper-left corner of the pastedown is a modern museum pencil accession notation and shelfmark:][^1]
MKDU
KN-6201
SP-26
[The remainder of the opening is blank 17th-century handmade laid paper, without printed text or library stamps.]

[Front flyleaves: blank opening of 17th-century handmade laid paper without printed text, manuscript annotations, pen trials, or library stamps.]

[Front flyleaves: additional blank opening of laid paper preceding the text block, without printed text, foliation, signatures, watermarks, or marginalia.]

[Woodcut Headpiece: The New Testament Holy Trinity (Paternity / Synaxis: God the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit within an oval mandorla surrounded by seraphim), flanked by the Most Holy Theotokos and St. John the Forerunner, with kneeling holy hierarchs and monarchs below, framed by ornate acanthus foliage and floral vases.]

*[Titular cinnabar ligature: The Grace and Completion of the Great Fast]*[^2]

### Notes

[^1]: Museum shelfmark inscription of the Museum of the Kyiv Theological Academy (МКДУ — Музей Київської духовної академії, inventory KN-6201, SP-26).
[^2]: Ornamental titular ligature in cinnabar ink at the head of Folio 1r: 'Благодать и окончаніе Великаго Поста' (The Grace and Completion of the Great Fast). In the 1642 Slozka printing, the Pentecostarion begins on Friday evening of the Sixth Week of Great Lent (the eve of Lazarus Saturday), representing the liturgical hinge where the Great Fast terminates and the festal cycle of Lazarus Saturday and Palm Sunday opens.
