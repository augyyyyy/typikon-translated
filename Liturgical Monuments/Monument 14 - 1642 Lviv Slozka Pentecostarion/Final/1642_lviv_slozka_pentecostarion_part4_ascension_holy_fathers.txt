# PART IV: FEAST OF THE ASCENSION AND SUNDAY OF THE HOLY FATHERS OF NICAEA

## The Ascension of our Lord God and Savior Jesus Christ

*On Wednesday Evening, at Small Vespers*

*The Priest having given the blessing, we say: Trisagion, and the rest according to the custom. But "O Heavenly King" we do not say at any of the services, until the Sunday of Pentecost of the Holy Spirit.[^742]*

*At "Lord, I have cried," we set 4 verses, Tone 6: "The Lord ascended into the Heavens..."; "O Lord, at Thine Ascension..."; "On the holy mountains..."; "O Lord, having fulfilled the mystery of the dispensation...": see below at Great Vespers. Glory... both now and ever... the same: "O Lord, when the Apostles beheld Thee...": there.*

*At the Aposticha: "Ascended into the heavens..." Verse: God is gone up with a shout [LXX 46:6]. "Thou didst ascend, O Christ, unto Thine unoriginate Father..." Verse: Clap your hands, all ye nations [LXX 46:2]. "Thine Angels, O Lord, said unto the Apostles..." Glory... both now and ever... Tone 2: "Thou wast born even as Thou didst will..." Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." Trisagion; and Troparion, Tone 4:*

Thou didst ascend in glory, O Christ our God, having gladdened Thy disciples by the promise of the Holy Spirit, they being confirmed through the blessing, that Thou art the Son of God, the Redeemer of the world!

*Then the Small Litany and Dismissal.*

*Or the Stichera appointed here:[^743]*

*Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly hosts" (Небе́снымъ чино́мъ ра́дованіе):*

*Sticheron 1:*
In Thy tender mercy desiring to cleanse through Thyself the renewal of mankind, and having ascended in the flesh, Thou didst sit on high, O Word of God, at the right hand of the Father; therefore we look to see Thee come again as Judge of the faithful and unfaithful!

*On Thursday of the Ascension:*

*Sticheron 2:*
Unto the radiant Apostles the Angels cried aloud: "Ye men, why gaze ye, beholding Christ the Giver of life ascending on high? In like manner He shall come as Judge of all, being God over all, to render unto each according to the merit of what they have wrought!"

*Sticheron 3:*
O Savior and Lover of mankind, account us who are illuminated by Thine Ascension worthy of immortal and divine glory; illuminate our hearts with Thy light, even of us who sing praise unto Thee!

*Glory... both now and ever... Tone 5:*
Come, ye assemblies of the faithful, having mystically learned the mystery, the unceasing hymn of Christ's disciples: at the Ascension, even as the Apostles, with David let us cry aloud: "God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet!" [LXX 46:6], that He may deliver our race from the heavy oppression of the enemy, and enlighten our souls!

*Then the Prokeimenon of the day.*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме Е҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^744]

*Sticheron 1:*
A wonder of astonishing novelty! Human nature hath ascended into the Heavens, united unto God the Word, the Indweller of everywhere!

*Verse: Clap your hands, all ye nations [LXX 46:2].*

*Sticheron 2:*
A radiant and most bright day of the divine cloudy ascent into the Heavens hath shone forth, enlightening all things!

*Verse: God is gone up with a shout [LXX 46:6].*

*Sticheron 3:*
Even as Thou didst send Thy Holy Spirit from on high unto the disciples, so also send Thy grace upon Thy people, O Christ Savior!

*Glory... both now and ever... Tone and Podoben the same:*
In praise worthy of God, with the Apostles, O Mother of God, we hymn the Lord, beholding Him borne upon the clouds!

*"Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." Trisagion, and after the Our Father: Troparion: "Thou didst ascend in glory..." We sing it. Litany and Dismissal.*

*At Great Vespers*

*We do not chant the Kathisma (Psalmody).[^745] But after the Psalm at Evening, we sing "Lord, I have cried" in Tone 6. And we set 10 verses, and sing Idiomela Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:*
The Lord ascended into the Heavens, that He might send the Comforter unto the world; the Heavens prepared His Throne, the clouds His ascent; the Angels marvel, seeing a Man higher than themselves; the Father awaits Him Whom He hath co-eternal in His bosom; and the Holy Spirit commands all His Angels: "Lift up your gates, O princes!" [LXX 23:7][^746] Clap your hands, all ye nations, for Christ hath ascended thither where He was before! *(Twice)*

*Sticheron 2:*
O Lord, at Thine Ascension the Cherubim were astonished, beholding Thee, God, ascending upon the clouds, sitting upon them; and we glorify Thee, for Thy mercy is good: glory to Thee! *(Twice)*

*Sticheron 3:*
On the holy mountains, beholding Thine Ascension, O Christ, Brightness of the Father's Glory, we sing praise unto the luminous aspect of Thy countenance; we worship Thy Passions, we honor the Resurrection, glorifying Thine illustrious Ascension: have mercy on us! *(Twice) On Thursday of the Ascension:*

*Sticheron 4:*
O Lord, when the Apostles beheld Thee borne upon the clouds, with weeping of tears, O Christ Giver of life, filled with sorrow, lamenting they said: "O Master, leave us not orphans, whom in Thy compassion Thou didst love as Thy servants, being tender-hearted; but send down, as Thou didst promise unto us, Thy Most Holy Spirit, enlightening our souls!" *(Twice)*

*Sticheron 5:*
O Lord, having fulfilled the mystery of the dispensation, taking Thy disciples up to the Mount of Olives, Thou didst ascend, and lo, Thou didst pass through the firmament of heaven, Thou Who for my sake didst become poor for me; and having mounted thither whence Thou wast never parted, send forth Thy All-Holy Spirit, enlightening our souls! *(Twice)*

*Glory... both now and ever... Tone 6:*
Not being parted from the Father's bosom, sweetest Jesus, and having lived with the earthly as man, today from the Mount of Olives Thou didst ascend in glory; and having mercifully lifted up our fallen nature, Thou didst seat it with the Father: therefore the heavenly Ranks of the bodiless hosts, marveling at the wonder, were struck with fear, and seized with trembling they magnified Thy love for mankind; with them we also, the earthly, glorifying Thy condescension toward us and Thine Ascension from us, pray Thee, saying: O Thou Who at Thine Ascension didst fill the disciples and the Mother of God who bore Thee with boundless joy, account us also worthy of the joy of Thine elect, by their supplications, for the sake of Thy great mercy!

*Entrance with the Censer. "Gladsome Light." Prokeimenon of the day.*

*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah [LXX Isaiah 2:2-3]:*[^747]
Thus saith the Lord: It shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the Lord shall be manifest, and the house of God on the tops of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall come unto it. And many peoples shall go and say: "Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, and to the house of the God of Jacob; and He will declare unto us His way, and we will walk in it!"

*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah [LXX Isaiah 62:10-12; 63:1-3, 7-9]:*[^748]
Thus saith the Lord: Go through My gates, prepare My way, and make a way for My people, and cast away the stones from the way; lift up a standard for the nations. For behold, the Lord hath made it heard unto the ends of the earth: Say ye to the daughter of Sion: Behold, thy Savior comes, having His reward with Him, and His work before His face. And He shall call them a holy people, redeemed by the Lord; and thou shalt be called a City sought out, and not forsaken.
Who is this that comes from Edom, the redness of His garments from Bosor? This One that is glorious in His apparel, crying out with great strength: "I speak righteousness, and the judgment of salvation!" Why are Thy garments red, and Thy apparel as from the treading of a full winepress? "I have trodden the winepress alone, and of the nations there was no man with Me."
I remembered the mercy of the Lord, the virtues of the Lord in all things wherein He rewards us. The Lord is a good Judge to the house of Israel; He deals with us according to His mercy, and according to the multitude of His righteousness. And He said: "Are they not My people? Children will not reject Me"; and He became salvation unto them out of all their affliction. Neither an intercessor nor an Angel, but the Lord Himself saved them, because He loved them and spared them; He Himself redeemed them, and took them up, and lifted them up all the days of the age.

*Reading from the Prophecy of Zechariah [LXX Zechariah 14:4, 8-11]:*[^749]
Thus saith the Lord: Behold, the day of the Lord comes, and His feet shall stand in that day upon the Mount of Olives, which is before Jerusalem toward the east; and in that day living water shall go forth out of Jerusalem, half of it toward the former sea, and half of it toward the hinder sea; in summer and in spring it shall be so. And the Lord shall be King over all the earth: in that day shall the Lord be one, and His Name one, encompassing all the earth and the wilderness from Gaba even unto Remmon, over against the ridge of Jerusalem; and it shall be exalted, and shall remain in its place, from the gate of Benjamin even unto the place of the first gate, unto the corner gate, and unto the tower of Hananeel, unto the King's winepresses. Men shall dwell in it, and there shall be no curse any more; and Jerusalem shall dwell securely.

*On Thursday of the Ascension:*
Men shall dwell in it, and there shall be no curse any more; and Jerusalem shall dwell securely.

*At the Litiya, Stichera, Tone 1:*

*Sticheron 1:*
Ascending into the heavens whence also Thou didst descend, leave us not orphans, O Lord: let Thy Spirit come, bringing peace unto the world; show unto the sons of men the works of Thy power, O Lord and Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 2:*
Thou didst ascend, O Christ, unto Thine unoriginate Father, not having been separated from His uncircumscribed bosom; and the powers did not admit an addition to the thrice-holy praise, but recognized Thee, O Lord, as the only Son even after Thine incarnation, the Only-begotten of the Father: in the multitude of Thy compassions, have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 3:*
Thine Angels, O Lord, said unto the Apostles: "Ye men of Galilee, why stand ye looking into Heaven? This is Christ God, Who is taken up in a cloud into Heaven; He shall come again in like manner as ye have seen Him going into Heaven: serve Him in holiness and righteousness!"

*Tone 4:*

*Sticheron 4:*
When Thou didst come unto the Mount of Olives, O Christ, to fulfill the Father's good pleasure, the heavenly Angels were astonished, and the subterranean realms were terrified; and the disciples stood by trembling with joy, as Thou didst speak unto them, while a Throne was being prepared from the opposite parts, waiting for the cloud; and the Heaven, opening its gates, displayed its beauty, and the earth revealed its treasures, as one that knew the descent into hades[^750] and Thine ascent again; but Thy steps were lifted up as were also Thy hands, and Thy mouth abundantly blessed them, even as they heard; the cloud took Thee up, and the inner Heaven received Thee: this work, O Lord, Thou didst accomplish, great and most glorious, for the salvation of our souls!

*Sticheron 5:*
Our Adamite nature that had descended into the lowest parts of the earth, O God, having newly created it through Thyself higher than all Principality and Power, Thou didst lead up today: for having loved it, Thou didst seat it with Thyself; having had compassion, Thou didst unite it unto Thyself; having united it, Thou didst suffer with it; having suffered as impassible, Thou didst also glorify it with Thyself. But the bodiless hosts said: "Who is this fair Man? Yet not man alone, but God and Man, united and manifested!"[^751] Therefore the Angels, astonished, flew in white garments round about the Apostles, and cried unto them that looked on: "Ye men of Galilee, He Who hath departed from us, this Jesus, the God-Man, the Man-God, shall come again as Judge of the living and the dead, granting unto the faithful forgiveness of sins and great mercy!"

*Sticheron 6:*
When Thou didst ascend in glory, O Christ God, while the disciples beheld, the clouds received Thee with Thy flesh; the gates of the heavens were lifted up as they beheld; the angelic choir rejoiced with gladness; the higher Powers cried aloud, saying:

*On Thursday of the Ascension:*
Saying: "Lift up your gates, O ye princes, and the King of glory shall enter in!" But the disciples, marveling, said: "Depart not from us, O Good Shepherd, but send down upon us Thy Most Holy Spirit, guiding and establishing our souls!"

*Glory... both now and ever... Tone 4:*
O Lord, having fulfilled the mystery hidden from the ages and from generations, as Good: Thou didst come with Thy disciples unto the Mount of Olives, having her that bore Thee, the Creator and Maker of all; for she who suffered more than all with motherly sorrow at Thy Passion, ought also to enjoy supreme joy at the glory of Thy flesh: whereof we also partaking, at Thine ascent into the Heavens, O Master, we glorify Thy great mercy shown toward us!

*At the Aposticha, Idiomela Stichera, Tone 2:*

*Sticheron 1:*
Thou wast born even as Thou didst will; Thou didst appear even as Thou didst choose; Thou didst suffer in the flesh, O our God; Thou didst rise from the dead, having trampled down death; Thou didst ascend in glory, filling all things; and Thou didst send unto us the Divine Spirit, that we may hymn and glorify Thy Godhead!

*Verse: Clap your hands, all ye nations [LXX 46:2].*

*Sticheron 2:*
As Thou wast taken up from the Mount of Olives, O Christ, the Powers beholding, cried one to another: "Who is this?" And it was said unto them: "This is the Mighty and Strong One; this is the One mighty in battle; this is truly the King of glory! And why are His garments red, He that comes from Bosor, which is of the Flesh?" But Thou Thyself as God, having sat down at the right hand of majesty, hast sent unto us the Holy Spirit, that He may guide and save our souls!

*Verse: God is gone up with a shout [LXX 46:6].*

*Sticheron 3:*
Thou didst ascend in glory upon the Mount of Olives, O Christ God, before Thy disciples, and didst sit down at the right hand of the Father, filling all things with Thy Godhead; and Thou didst send unto them the Holy Spirit, enlightening and establishing and sanctifying our souls!

*Glory... both now and ever... Tone 6:*
God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet, to lift up the fallen image of Adam, and to send the Spirit Comforter, to sanctify our souls!

*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." Trisagion; and after the Our Father: At the Blessing of the Loaves, Troparion: "Thou didst ascend in glory, O Christ our God..." at the Litiya, thrice. And the Great Reading: in the Acts, three Homilies of Chrysostom on the Apostles.[^752]*

## ON THURSDAY OF THE ASCENSION, AT MATINS

*At "God is the Lord," Troparion of the Feast, thrice.*

*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "The stone being sealed" (Ка́мени зна́менану):*[^756]
While the Angels marveled at Thy strange ascent, and the disciples were struck with awe at Thy fearful assumption, Thou didst ascend in glory as God, and the gates were lifted up for Thee, O Savior! Therefore the Heavenly Powers were amazed, crying aloud: "Glory to Thy condescension! Glory to Thy Kingdom! Glory to Thine Ascension, O only Lover of mankind!" *Glory... both now and ever... the same.*

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3, Podoben: "The beauty of virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства):*[^757]
The pre-eternal and unoriginate God, having renewed human nature which He assumed, mystically ascended today: the Angels running before showed Him unto the Apostles, going into the Heavens with great glory; and having worshipped Him, they said: "Glory to God Who hath ascended!" *Glory... both now and ever... the same.*

*And after the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Пове́лѣнное та́йнѡ):*[^758]
Having ascended unto the Mount of Olives, O Merciful Lord, thence Thou wast taken up, for a radiant cloud received Thee from the earth, while Thy disciples beheld, a fearful wonder, and the Bodiless Hosts cried aloud in the Heavens: "With fear lift up the gates, O highest Princes!" With them all creation hymns Thee, the King and God of all! *Glory... both now and ever... the same. And the reading of the Feast: Encomia, the work of Lord Andrew, or others.[^759] Graduals: 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon, Tone 4: "God is gone up with a shout, and the Lord with the sound of a trumpet!" Verse: "Clap your hands, all ye nations!" "Let every breath..." Gospel from Matthew, Pericope 74 [Mark Pericope 71], the 3rd Resurrection Gospel: "Now when Jesus was risen early the first day of the week..."[^760] Then: "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ..." Psalm 50. Glory... "Through the prayers of the holy Apostles..." Both now and ever... "Through the prayers of the Most Holy Theotokos..." "Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy..."*

*And the present Sticheron, Tone 6:*
Today in the Heavens the supernal Powers, beholding our nature, marveled at the strange manner of Thine ascent: they were in perplexity, looking one to another, saying: "Who is this that comes?" But seeing their Master, they commanded the Heavenly gates to be lifted up! With them let us unceasingly hymn Him Who shall come again from thence with the flesh, as the Judge of all and Almighty God!

*Canon. The work of Lord John the Monk.[^761] Heirmos twice; Troparia on 6. Tone 5.*

*Ode 1. Heirmos:*
"Unto God the Savior, Who guided the people through the sea with dry feet, and drowned Pharaoh with all his host, unto Him alone let us sing, for He hath been glorified!"

*Troparia:*
Let us all people sing a song of victory unto Christ, Who ascended with glory upon the shoulders of the Cherubim, and seated our nature at the right hand of the Father, for He hath been glorified!

Beholding Christ, the Mediator between God and men, in the flesh on high, the angelic choirs were astonished, *On Thursday of the Ascension:*
Were astonished in the heights, and with one accord sang a song of victory: "For He hath been glorified!"

Unto God Who appeared upon Mount Sinai, and gave the Law to the God-seer Moses, Who from the Mount of Olives was taken up in the flesh: unto Him let us all sing, for He hath been glorified!

*Theotokion:*
O all-pure Mother of God, unceasingly pray Him Who was incarnate of thee, and never separated from the bosom of the Father, to save from every distress those whom He fashioned!

*The Second Canon, likewise on 6. Whose acrostic is according to the alphabet; and in the Eighth and Ninth Odes, the acrostic is: (ὨΙΔΗ ІΩСИФ )[Acrostic: Song of Joseph].[^762]*

*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
"I shall open my mouth, and it shall be filled with the Spirit, and I shall pour forth a word to the Queen Mother, and I shall be seen radiantly keeping festival, and rejoicing I shall sing her wonders!"

*Troparia:*
Thou didst rise on the third day, immortal by nature, and didst appear unto the eleven and unto all the disciples; and Thou didst mount up unto the Father, O Christ Creator of all, borne upon a cloud!

David, inspired by God, clearly cries aloud, singing: "The Lord hath ascended unto the heavens with a shout and the sound of the trumpet, and hath reached unto the Father, the Primal Light!"

*Glory...*
O Lord, having renewed by Thy Passion the world that was grown old through many sins, Thou didst ascend, borne upon a cloud unto the supercelestial heights: glory to Thy glory!

*Theotokion:*
Since thou didst bear the Lord of all, O all-immaculate Lady, Who accepted voluntary suffering, and ascended unto His Father, Whom He never left, even though He took on flesh!

*Katavasia, both choirs together:*
"Covered by divine darkness, he that was slow of tongue proclaimed the God-written Law; for having shaken off the mire from the eye of the mind, he beholds the Existing One, and is instructed in the knowledge of the Spirit, praising with divine hymns!"[^763]

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
"By the power of Thy Cross, O Christ, establish my understanding, that I may hymn and glorify Thy saving Ascension!"

*Troparia:*
Thou didst ascend unto the Father, O Christ Giver of life, and didst lift up our race, O Lover of mankind, in Thine ineffable compassion!

The angelic ranks, O Savior, beholding human nature ascending together with Thee, were unceasingly astonished and glorified Thee!

The choirs of Angels were amazed, O Christ, seeing Thee ascended with the body, and sang praise unto Thy holy Ascension!

Thou didst raise up human nature, O Christ, that was fallen through corruption, and by Thine ascent didst lift it up, and didst glorify us together with Thyself!

*Theotokion:*
Intercede unceasingly, O Pure One, with Him Who came forth from thy womb, that they who sing of thee as the Mother of God may be delivered from the deception of the devil!

*Another Heirmos [Canon 2, Ode 3]:*
"Thy hymn-singers, O Mother of God, the living and abundant Fount, who have formed a spiritual choir, establish in Thy divine glory, vouchsafing them crowns of glory!"

*Troparia:*
"Lift up the Heavenly gates, behold, Christ the King of creation hath come, clothed in an earthly body!" the lowest Powers cried unto the highest!

Having sought out Adam, who was deceived by the serpent's deceit, O Christ, as having clothed Thyself in him, Thou didst ascend and sit at the right hand, as co-enthroned with the Father, while Angels sang praise unto Thee!

*Glory...*
The earth keeps festival and dances, and Heaven rejoices today at the Ascension of the Creator of creation, Who formerly by His will united what had been sundered!

*Theotokion:*
Having borne the Destroyer of death, the only immortal God, O Virgin all-immaculate Mother, pray Him ever to put to death my deadening passions and save me!

*Katavasia: "The womb of barrenness..."*

*On Thursday of the Ascension:*
"The womb of barrenness broke its bonds, and the shameful reproach of childlessness, by the sole prayer of the Prophetess Anna of old, who bore a contrite spirit unto the Mighty One and God of knowledge!"[^764]

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "Of the Wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости):*[^765]
Mounted upon the Heavenly Clouds, leaving peace unto those on earth, Thou didst ascend and sit down at the right hand of the Father, being co-essential with Him and the Spirit; for Thou didst appear in the flesh, yet didst remain unchangeable: therefore Thou awaitest the fulfillment of the end, coming to judge all the world! O Righteous Judge and Lord, spare our souls, granting remission of sins, as the merciful God, unto Thy servants! *Glory... both now and ever... the same. And the reading of the Feast.*

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
"I have heard the report of the power of Thy Cross, that paradise was opened thereby, and I cried aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!"

*Troparia:*
Thou didst ascend in glory, O King of Angels, having sent the Comforter unto us from the Father; therefore we cry: Glory to Thine Ascension, O Christ!

As the Savior ascended unto the Father in the Flesh, the angelic hosts were amazed at Him and cried: Glory to Thine Ascension, O Christ!

The angelic Powers cried unto the higher ranks: "Lift up the gates for Christ our King, Whom we hymn together with the Father and the Spirit!"

*Theotokion:*
A Virgin bore, and knew not a mother's estate, yet is she a Mother, and hath remained a Virgin: hymning her, we cry: Rejoice, O Mother of God!

*Another Heirmos [Canon 2, Ode 4]:*
"He Who sits in glory upon the Throne of the Godhead, Jesus the most divine, hath come upon a light cloud, and with His incorrupt hand hath saved them that cry: Glory to Thy power, O Christ!"

*Troparia:*
Jesus the Giver of life, having taken those whom He loved, ascends unto the Mount of Olives, and blessed them, and borne upon a cloud reached the Father's bosom, which He had in no wise left!

The whole world keeps festival, both visible and invisible; the Angels dance with joy, and men unceasingly glorify the Ascension of Him Who in goodness united Himself unto us in the flesh!

Having destroyed the dominion of death as immortal, O Lord, Thou didst grant immortality unto all, O Lover of mankind; and Thou didst ascend in glory, while Thy honorable disciples beheld Thee, O all-powerful Jesus!

*Theotokion:*
Blessed was thy womb, O all-immaculate one, for thou wast accounted worthy ineffably to contain Him Who wondrously emptied the womb of hades: pray Him to save us who hymn thee!

*Katavasia:*

*On the same day, on Thursday of the Sixth Week after Pascha, we celebrate the Ascension of our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ:*  
For while He was yet with the disciples before His Passion, He promised the coming of the All-Holy Spirit, saying: "It is expedient for you that I go away; for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come." And again: "When He comes, He will guide you into all truth." And after the Resurrection...

...from the dead, during forty days He appeared unto them not continuously, but from time to time, eating and drinking with them, and presenting His Resurrection more certainly; and finally He spoke many things concerning the Kingdom of God, commanding them not to depart from Jerusalem, but to remain there, awaiting the reception and coming of the Holy Spirit, and thereby to be baptized; for previously baptism was by water and only once from John, although later Saint Epiphanius of Cyprus related that John the Theologian baptized the Mother of God, and he in turn baptized the other Apostles.[^771] He commanded them to remain in Jerusalem, so that there the preaching of the Gospel might first be confirmed, lest going into foreign regions they might easily be slandered, and because it was necessary that they, like soldiers, should be equipped with the armor of the Spirit, and so go forth to battle against the enemies of Christ. But when the time of the Ascension arrived, He led them out unto the Mount of Olives, which is so called because it was planted with very many olive trees. And He spoke unto them concerning what had taken place in His preaching, and furthermore concerning His incomprehensible Kingdom in the future; and since He saw that they did not wish Him to depart, and His all-pure Mother was also present there, He appointed Angels for them, showing His ascent into the Heavens. And as they beheld, He was taken up from their midst, a cloud receiving Him, and so He was escorted by Angels, who commanded one another that the Heavenly Gates should be lifted up, and who marveled at the crimson of the blood of His flesh. He ascended and sat at the right hand of the Father, having deified the flesh, and—I dare to say—having shown it to be twofold together with the divine nature, on account of which we have been reconciled, the ancient enmity having been abolished. And the Angels in the appearance of men stood by the Apostles, saying: "Men of Galilee, why stand ye wondering, looking up into Heaven? This Jesus, Whom ye see with the flesh as God, shall come again with the flesh in like manner, yet not as formerly, in poverty and silence, but with great glory, as ye now behold Him escorted by Angels." Then the Apostles departed and returned from the Mount of Olives, which is near Jerusalem, being a distance of two thousand and forty paces, for this is a Sabbath day's journey; for so it was ordained by Moses, to walk that many paces on the Sabbath, because the Tabernacle of Witness was that many paces distant from the camp of the Israelites.[^772] For it was permitted for some to walk there on the Sabbath to worship, and in some measure to extend their walking, and therefore it was called a Sabbath day's journey. And some from this supposed that Christ's Ascension took place on a Sabbath, but they erred:

And having returned, the Apostles entered into the Upper Room, in which they had been staying, together with the Myrrh-bearing Women and the Mother of the Word, occupying themselves in fasting, prayers, and supplication, and awaiting the descent of the All-Holy Spirit, according to the promise. O Christ God, Who didst ascend in glory, have mercy upon us, now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Synaxarion on the Seventh Sunday, of the Holy Fathers.*[^829]
*Bless, Father, to read.*
*Verses:*
*O chosen assembly of luminaries, I pray you, hasten to assist:*
*Illumine my earthbound thoughts, that they may be light-bearing!*
*Verses:*
*Having called the Son alien to the Father's all-creative essence,*
*Arius was alienated from the glory of God!*

On this same day, the Seventh Sunday after Pascha, we celebrate the First Council in Nicaea of the three hundred and eighteen God-bearing Fathers, for the following cause: Since our Lord Jesus Christ, clothed in the flesh, was truly the Son of God and became man, and as perfect man and God ascended and sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high: this council of holy Fathers so proclaimed Him, and confessed Him consubstantial and co-honored with the Father; and by these words, after the glorious Ascension, they instituted the present feast, setting forth the assembly of so many Fathers who proclaimed Him who ascended in the flesh to be true God, and in the flesh perfect man.

This Council took place in the days of Constantine the Great, in the twentieth year of his reign, after the persecutions had ceased. Having first reigned in Rome, he afterward rebuilt Byzantium into Constantinople, named after himself, in the year 5818 from the creation of the world. Then they began to speak against Arius: for this Arius was from Libya, and having come to Alexandria, was ordained deacon by the Hieromartyr Peter of Alexandria; but because he began to utter blasphemies against the Son of God, preaching Him to be created from non-existence and far removed from the Father's divine dignity, and using the titles "Wisdom" and "Word" of God in an improper sense, resembling the wicked Sabellius, who taught a single person and single hypostasis in the Godhead, saying that at one time He was Father, at another Son, and at another Holy Spirit. When Arius uttered these blasphemies, the Great Peter excommunicated him from the priesthood, having seen Christ as an infant upon the holy altar clothed in a torn tunic, who said: "Arius hath torn it!" Achilles, who received the patriarchate of Alexandria after Peter, upon his promise again absolved Arius, and moreover ordained him presbyter and appointed him teacher in Alexandria. But when Achilles died, Alexander became patriarch, who, finding Arius uttering yet worse blasphemies, deposed him by a council in Alexandria; for as Theodoret saith, he taught that the nature of Christ was mutable, and was the first to spew forth the heresy that the Lord assumed mindless and soulless flesh. Arius, having taught many his impiety, wrote to and won over Eusebius of Nicomedia, Paulinus of Tyre, Eusebius of Caesarea, and others, and set himself against Alexander. But Alexander sent...

...sending everywhere an account of his blasphemies and his deposition, and stirred up many. The Church indeed was thrown into confusion, and many desiring to embrace Christian teachings were hindered by the contentious doctrine.

Constantine the Great therefore gathered the holy Fathers in Nicaea from all over the world, providing public carriages and conveyances; and he himself came thither. And when all the Fathers were seated, he sat not upon an imperial throne, but upon a humble chair. And when the charges against Arius were declared, he and all who thought with him were put under anathema; and the Word of God was proclaimed by the holy Fathers to be consubstantial, co-honored, and co-unoriginate with the Father. And they set forth the holy Symbol of Faith, bringing it up to: "And in the Holy Spirit, the Lord...", which the Second Council afterward completed. Furthermore, the First Council confirmed the feast of Pascha, when and how we ought to celebrate it, and not with the Jews, as was formerly the custom. They also set forth twenty canons for ecclesiastical order. And the holy Symbol of Faith Constantine the Great, the equal-to-the-apostles, confirmed last of all in red letters. There were present of these holy Fathers: two hundred and thirty-two hierarchs, and eighty-six priests, deacons, and monks: in all, three hundred and eighteen. The most renowned among them were: Sylvester, Bishop of Rome, and Metrophanes of Constantinople, who being infirm, were present through their locum tenentes; Alexander of Alexandria, with Athanasius the Great, then archdeacon; Eustathius of Antioch, and Macarius of Jerusalem; Hosius, Bishop of Cordoba; Paphnutius the Confessor; Nicholas the Myrrh-streamer, and Spyridon of Trimythous, who having overcome a philosopher there, baptized him, showing him the three-in-one Godhead. In the midst of the Council, two bishop-fathers having passed away unto God, Constantine the Great placed the definitions of the holy Council in their caskets and locked them firmly, and found them also sealed and signed by their hands with ineffable words of God!

The Council being finished, since Constantinople, which was then being built, was completed, Constantine the Great summoned all those holy men; who, having walked throughout the whole city and prayed sufficiently, decreed it to be the Queen of cities, and dedicated it to the Mother of the Word. The Emperor having commanded it, the saints departed each to his own home. But while Constantine the Great had not yet departed unto God, holding the scepter together with his son Constantius: Arius came to the Emperor, saying: "I renounce all things, and wish to be united unto the Church of God!" Having written down his blasphemies, he hung them around his neck, and as though submitting to the Council, striking them with his hand, he said he submitted thereto. And the Emperor commanded the Patriarch of Constantinople to receive Arius into communion. Alexander had then succeeded Metrophanes; knowing the evil character of the man, he did not believe him, and prayed to God to reveal unto him whether it were His will to communicate with Arius. When the time arrived wherein he was to concelebrate with him, his prayer became more fervent, beseeching his death. But Arius, on his way to the church, somewhere near the porphyry pillar of the forum, feeling his bowels burst, went into a public privy, and there ruptured, pouring out all his entrails below, suffering the rupture of Judas, being matched with the betrayer of the Word; and for tearing the Son of God away from the Father's essence, he himself was torn asunder and found dead! And thus the Church of God was delivered from that destruction. By the prayers of the three hundred and eighteen God-bearing Fathers, O Christ our God, have mercy on us! Now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.[^830]

## Seventh Sunday after Pascha: Vespers

*At "Lord, I have cried", we set 6 stichoi: of the Holy Fathers, 3; and for the Menaion, 3.*

*Stichera of the Fathers, Tone 6, Podoben: "Unoriginate" (Ненача́емаѧ):*[^854]
From the womb Thou wast born before the morning star, from the Father without mother before the ages, even though Arius glorifies Thee as a creature and not God, with audacity! foolishly confusing Thee, the Creator, with material creatures, hiding for himself fuel for the eternal fire; but the Council in Nicaea proclaimed Thee the Son of God, O Lord, co-enthroned with the Father and the Spirit!

Who rent Thy garment, O Savior? Arius, Thou didst say: who severed the Trinity, the equal-in-honor Principle, into divisions: he denied Thee to be One of the Trinity; he also teaches not to call the Theotokos Mother of God.[^855] But the Council in Nicaea confessed Thee to be the Son of God, O Lord, co-enthroned with the Father and the Spirit!

Arius falls into the pit of sin, having hated to see the light, and by the divine hook he is burst asunder inwardly, violently surrendering his whole being and soul, having become another Judas in disposition and manner! But the Council in Nicaea proclaimed Thee the Son of God, O Lord, co-enthroned with the Father and the Spirit!

Senseless Arius severed the All-Holy Trinity, the single Sovereignty, into three unlawful and unequal essences. Therefore the God-bearing Fathers, having eagerly come together, inflamed with zeal like Elijah the Tishbite, with the spiritual sword cut down the blasphemer accoutred in shame, even as the Spirit spoke!

*Glory... Tone 6:*
Today let us praise the mystic trumpets of the Spirit, the God-bearing Fathers, who sang in the midst of the Church a harmonious melody of theology: one immutable Trinity, essence and Godhead; the overthrowers of Arius and champions of the Orthodox, who ever pray unto the Lord that our souls may be saved!

*Both now... Same Tone:*
On the holy mountains, beholding Thine Ascension, O Christ, the radiance of the Father's glory, we hymn the light-bearing form of Thy countenance, we worship Thy sufferings, we honor the Resurrection, glorifying the glorious Ascension: Have mercy on us!

*Glory... Tone 3:*
Faithful guardians of the apostolic traditions were ye, O holy Fathers: for having orthodoxly proclaimed the consubstantiality of the Holy Trinity, ye synodically cast down the blasphemy of Arius; with him also having refuted Macedonius the fighter against the Spirit, ye condemned Nestorius, Eutyches, and Dioscorus, Sabellius and Severus the headless:[^857] from whose deception ask that we be delivered, praying that our unblemished life be preserved in the faith!

*Both now...:*
"God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet [LXX Ps. 46:6],[^858] to lift up the fallen image of Adam, and to send the Holy Spirit the Comforter, to sanctify our souls!"

Having assumed flesh, Thou didst deify it, O Savior who lovest mankind, and didst vouchsafe it immortal and divine glory in Thy fearful and terrible Ascension: O longsuffering One, illumine our hearts with Thy light, even of us who sing unto Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Tone 5:*
"O Lord, as Thou wast taken up..." (see fol. 344)[^883].

*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart..." and the Dismissal.*

## Wednesday of the Seventh Week after Pascha: Matins

*At "God is the Lord": Troparion of the Feast (thrice).*

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3, Podoben: "Co-unoriginate with the Father" (Собезнача́льномꙋ Сло́вꙋ):*[^884]
From on high from heaven unto those on earth, and lying below in the prison of hades, Thou didst raise up Adam's image together with Thyself, O God, and by Thine Ascension, O Christ, having led him into the heavens, Thou didst make him co-enthroned upon Thy Father's throne, as Thou art merciful and lovest mankind! *Glory... Both now... the same.*

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "That which was commanded mystically" (Повелѣ́нное та́инственно):*[^885]
Having ascended the Mount of Olives, O merciful God, whence Thou didst ascend into the heavens: for a bright cloud from earth took Thee, while Thy disciples beheld the fearful wonder, and the bodiless ones in the heavens cried aloud with fear: "Lift up the gates for the highest hosts!" With them all creation praises Thee, the eternal King and God! *Glory... Both now... the same.*

*Canon of the Feast, and in the Menaion.*

*At the Aposticha: Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^886]
A new and marvelous wonder: for human nature hath ascended into heaven, united unto God the Word, the Ruler of all!

*Verse: Clap your hands, all ye nations; shout unto God with a voice of rejoicing [LXX Ps. 46:2].*[^887]
The radiant and most bright day of the eternal divine ascent into heaven hath shone forth, enlightening all things!

*Verse: God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet [LXX Ps. 46:6].*[^888]
Even as Thou didst send Thy Comforter Spirit from on high unto the disciples, so send down Thy grace upon Thy people, O Christ Savior!

*Glory... Both now... Tone 5:*
"Come, ye faithful, let us praise the Ascension..." (see fol. 344)[^889].

*At the Beatitudes, the Triodion, Tone 3.*
*Ode 3. Heirmos: Thou art the confirmation of those who flee unto Thee, O Lord...*[^890]
*Troparia:*
Human nature, condemned and corrupted by transgression, Thou hast raised up, O Master, higher than the angels, as Thou art compassionate!

Thou hast ascended into the heavens with glory, while the bodiless ones sing unto Thee, O Lord, as Creator of all and our God!

Having ascended into the heavens, O all-good Master, Thou didst send the true Comforter Spirit unto Thy disciples!

*Theotokion:*
Incarnate from thee, O pure one, came forth the Word, righting the fall of the first-fashioned through the compassion of His mercies!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: In the unquenchable fire...*[^891]

Having accomplished by almighty power the dispensation for us, O God, Thou didst ascend while Thy disciples gazed, and cried unto Thee with fear: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord![^892]

"Ye men of Galilee, truly why look ye thus, as He ascends close unto heaven?" the angels said: "even as ye see Him, so again shall He come to judge all creation: Whom we exalt supremely unto the ages!"

"The upright Spirit, the divine Spirit, the Spirit existing indivisibly with Thee, as Thou didst promise, send unto us, O Word of God, carried upon the clouds of glory!" the divine apostles cried: "that we may sing and exalt Thee supremely unto the ages!"

*Theotokion:*
From thee the Wisdom of God built for Himself a temple, and became incarnate by ineffable descent, O Maiden who knewest not wedlock; for thou alone among all generations wast chosen to be the dwelling-place of Christ God!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A new and divine marvel...*[^893]

*Troparia:*
Ascending with glory amid shouting, as the prophet spoke of old: "The Lord and God hath gone up! Sing unto Him, ye nations, sing with faith, and praise Him with trembling, for He hath wrought unsearchable and ineffable things!"

Unto the sorrowing disciples Thou didst say: "I will not be parted from you forever, even though I go unto the Father who sent Me; go ye, teach all the world to know Me as the true God, who have done the most glorious things which ye see and have heard!"

*Glory...*
Having spoken thus unto all, O Christ, immediately a cloud of light took Thee, and while all beheld, Thou didst ascend from earth; and having ascended, Thou abidest with God above all principality and power: therefore we glorify Thy much-hymned power!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Truly hast thou appeared as the receptacle of Light, for from thee God became ineffably incarnate without change, having exalted Adam's nature upon the Cross, and having become the first-born from the dead; and remaining God in the highest, together with the Father and the divine Spirit He is glorified!

## Wednesday of the Seventh Week after Pascha: Vespers

*On the same Wednesday, in the evening, as usual. At "Lord, I have cried", Stichera, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*[^894]
Thou didst suffer as man, being impassible in Thy Godhead, and having risen on the third day, Thou didst despoil death, and having raised up all who had decayed through transgression, Thou didst ascend unto the Father, having promised to send the Comforter unto Thy sacred apostles, O almighty Jesus, Savior of our souls!

"Why stand ye looking into heaven?" the angels, visibly appearing as young men, said unto the initiates of the Word: "This same Jesus whom ye see a bright cloud taking up, shall come in like manner as ye have seen Him, to judge the world, as it is written; go ye therefore and accomplish all that hath been spoken!"

After Thy rising from the tomb, ineffably risen, O almighty Lord, having taken those whom Thou didst love, Thou didst lead them out as far as Bethany, O Word, and coming unto the Mount of Olives, having blessed them, Thou didst ascend while the angels submitted unto Thee, O all-good Jesus, Savior of our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Tone 1:*
"Thine angels, O Lord, said unto the apostles..." (see fol. 348)[^895].

*Then, Stichera in the Menaion.*

*At the Aposticha: Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^896]
"Leave not orphans those whom I have gathered," the Lord said unto His friends, "but I will send you the All-Holy Spirit!"

*Verse: Clap your hands, all ye nations; shout unto God with a voice of rejoicing [LXX Ps. 46:2].*[^897]
The angels cried aloud unto the apostles: "Ye men of Galilee, in like manner as ye have seen Him, so shall He come again!"

*Verse: God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet [LXX Ps. 46:6].*[^898]
Thy disciples, O Word, came down with joy from the Mount of Olives, glorifying and praising Thy divine Ascension!

*Glory... Both now... Tone 4:*
"Ascending into the heavens whence also Thou didst descend..." (see fol. 344)[^899].

*Then: "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart in peace, O Master..." and the Dismissal.*

## Thursday of the Seventh Week after Pascha: Matins

*At "God is the Lord": Troparion of the Feast (thrice).*

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1, Podoben: "When the stone had been sealed" (Ка́мени зна́менанꙋ):*[^900]
Hades having been despoiled by Thee, O Life-giver, and the world having been enlightened by Thy Resurrection, Thou didst ascend in glory, O Savior, holding all things in Thy hand: therefore with the angels we glorify Thee, O almighty Lord: Glory to Thine Ascension, O Christ, glory to Thy kingdom, glory to Thy compassion, O Thou who alone lovest mankind! *Glory... Both now... the same.*

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*
Thou camest in the flesh from heaven, O Word, upon earth, that Thou mightest raise up all with Thyself: Thou didst descend incorporeal upon earth, and didst enter with the flesh, O Christ: therefore the heavenly hosts cried aloud, shouting: Glory to Thy descent, O Christ, glory to Thine Ascension, glory to Thy dispensation, O Thou who alone lovest mankind! *Verse and Both now... the same. Canon of the Feast, on 6; and in the Menaion...*

*At the Aposticha: Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*[^901]
Thou hast accomplished, O Good One, the Father's good pleasure, uniting the things on high with those below, and didst ascend in glory unto the foremost ranks!

*Verse: Clap your hands, all ye nations; shout unto God with a voice of rejoicing [LXX Ps. 46:2].*[^902]
A bright cloud received Thee on high, and the angels served Thy divine Ascension with fear and trembling!

*Verse: God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet [LXX Ps. 46:6].*[^903]
Beholding Thee carried upon a cloud, with the apostles the Mother of God sings God-befitting praise unto Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Tone 7:*
"Thou didst come unto the Mount of Olives..." (see today)[^904]. *Then: "It is good to give thanks unto the Lord..." The First Hour, and the Dismissal.*

*At the Beatitudes, the Triodion, Tone 4.*
*Ode 4. Heirmos: He that sits in glory upon the throne...*[^905]
*Troparia:*
Inaccessible by nature, becoming accessible unto me, clothed with my nature, Thou didst enlighten all my being, and by Thine Ascension didst lead it up, far above every principality and power!

Elijah the charioteer passed into hearing, prefiguring of old Thy divine Ascension, O Word of God: for as upon a chariot, O Savior, carried by a cloud, Thou didst reach the Father's bosom!

While the disciples were terrified at the fearful taking up and marveled, the divine angels proclaimed: "Ye men of Galilee, as ye see, so again shall Jesus come to judge all!"

*Theotokion:*
Having renewed crushed human nature, O most pure one, which had bowed down to corruption, having incarnated God and given birth beyond nature, O unwedded Maiden, unto Him we cry: Glory to Thy power, O Lord! *Ode 8. Heirmos: The children walking...*

*Troparia:*
Walking upon the wings of the winds, borne upon a cloud, He is now received up into the heavens where He was before; He sends the Comforter unto the disciples, as they praise and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

Having willingly endured the Crucifixion and Burial, Thou didst rise on the third day, O almighty One, and appearing unto those that love Thee during forty days, Thou didst make them glad; then having blessed them, Thou didst ascend unto Thy Father with glory!

Beyond human nature and beyond word, O God-loving disciples, the Word, having ascended, sends unto you, as He promised, the vision of the All-Holy and Co-enthroned Spirit, as ye sing: "Praise the Lord and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!"

*Theotokion:*
O shut Gate of God, through which the Most High alone passed, guide me unto the divine paths, and open unto me the doors of grace; for unto thee do I flee, O Virgin, the only protectress of mankind!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A stone cut without hands...*[^906]

*Troparia:*
"Until ye be clothed with power from on high," Thou didst say unto the disciples, "tarry ye gathered together; for behold, I am taken up, and I will send you the Comforter Who is co-enthroned with the Father!"

Come mystically, having all ascended to the height of contemplation, let us behold with spiritual eyes Him Who ascended and sits at the right hand of the Father, as the co-enthroned and co-eternal Word!

*Glory...*
The Sun, shining forth beautifully from the tomb, by grace, like radiant beams unto all the inhabited earth, sent you forth, O apostles, enlightening it and driving away the gloom!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
Spare me, O Christ, when Thou comest with glory to judge the world, dispelling the gloom of my passions by the prayers of her that bore Thee, and make me an inheritor of Thy Heavenly Kingdom!

## On the Same Thursday Evening: Vespers

*The regular Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera of the Feast, without the Old Testament Readings and the Entrance. Likewise on Friday at Matins:*[^907] *The entire service of the Feast unchanged, as indicated for the Feast itself, except the Polyeleos and the Gospel, and we do not sing "More honorable than the Cherubim." And thus is the Apodosis of the Feast of the Ascension of Christ celebrated. And the service of the saint of the day is sung at Compline (see fol. 354)[^908].*

## On Friday of the Seventh Week after Pascha: Liturgy

*At the Beatitudes, the Triodion, Tone 6.*

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Rising early unto Thee...*[^909]

*Troparia:*
Having overthrown the dominion of death by divine power, Thou didst rise on the third day, O my Christ, and didst ascend with glory unto the heavenly places, while Thy disciples sang praises unto Thee as God and Lover of mankind!

"Leave us not orphans, O Bountiful One, but as Thou didst promise, send Thine All-Holy Spirit!" Thy disciples cried aloud unto the Redeemer, as they beheld Thee, O Lover of mankind, ascending from the earth.

Behold, the Spirit is about to descend upon the earth in tongues of fire, so that He may visibly manifest as fiery the clay-fashioned human tongues of the disciples, proclaiming His God-befitting coming!

Clothed in human nature, having renewed the corrupted, O Word, for the sake of boundless compassion, Thou hast given us a strong wall and protection forever, beholding Thee as God and Man!

*Theotokion:*

*Ode 8. Heirmos: According to the Father's law...*[^910]
Having united the separated by Thy tender mercy, O Word all-good, striking down the adversary by the suffering of Thy flesh, Thou didst ascend in glory unto the Father Whom Thou didst never leave, while the disciples watched: therefore with them we bless and supremely exalt Thee unto the ages!

Having had mercy, O Savior, upon those whom Thou didst love, rescuing them from the malicious slander of the enemy, Thou didst ascend from the Mount of Olives, O Master, while Thy disciples looked on and marveled how a cloud received Thee in the flesh, like a Cherubic throne: therefore with them we bless and supremely exalt Thee unto the ages!

"I will not leave you orphans, O blessed sons and heirs of My Kingdom, but I will send from on high the Comforter, teaching you mystically, beyond comprehension and mind, and showing the participation of fire unto mortal beings," Thy disciples cry aloud unto Thee, O Bountiful One: therefore with them we praise and supremely exalt Thee unto the ages!

*Theotokion:*
Beyond the laws of nature, O Virgin, having given birth unto the Lawgiver, Who put on the renewal of the law and bestowed the brightness of grace: therefore, O pure one, free me who am now sorely overcome by the law of sin, that I may sing: "Praise the Lord, all ye works, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!"

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Every tongue is at a loss...*[^911]

*Troparia:*
Having had mercy on cast-off and afflicted human nature, O Thou Who alone art merciful and loving of mankind in grace, Thou didst take upon Thyself human flesh, and by Thy mighty suffering as Savior didst ascend, O Christ, on high unto the Father, having filled all things with glory!

"Why are ye grieving? Why do ye marvel?" the angels cried aloud unto the apostles, standing with them upon the mountain: "This Jesus Whom ye see ascended, so shall He come again in great glory to judge the inhabited world and to give unto each according to his desert!"

*Glory...*
O Thou Who didst give good humility unto Thy sacred disciples through Thy Holy Spirit, as a bountiful Benefactor, preserve them unharmed from the evil one, that having done Thy will, O Christ, we may all be accounted worthy of Thy Kingdom together!

*Both now... Theotokion:*
A receptacle of the Light that enlightened all creation hast thou been shown to be, O alone most pure and all-immaculate Mary, Mother of God: therefore I pray thee, enlighten my passion-tossed soul darkened by the pleasures of this life, that I may walk by day in divine paths!
