*Glory, Tone 6:*[^445]  
Today the great Moses mystically foreshadowed this day, saying: "And God blessed the seventh day." For this is the blessed Sabbath; this is the day of rest, on which the Only-begotten Son of God rested from all His works, keeping the Sabbath in the flesh according to the dispensation of death; and having returned again unto what He was through the Resurrection, He has granted us eternal life, for He alone is good and loves mankind.

*Both now, Theotokion (Dogmaticon), Tone 1:*[^446]  
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of the whole world, who sprang forth from mankind and gave birth unto the Master, the heavenly portal, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful. For she appeared as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she broke down the barrier of enmity, brought in peace, and opened the Kingdom. Therefore, having her as the firm foundation of our faith, we have as our defender the Lord Who was born of her. Be of good courage therefore, be of good courage, O people of God; for He will overcome the enemies, as He is almighty!

*The Ninth Reading (Isaiah 61:10–62:5):*[^457]  
*The Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 61):*  
Let my soul rejoice in the Lord: for He has clothed me with the garment of salvation, and with the robe of gladness has He covered me; as a bridegroom He has put a garland upon me, and as a bride has He adorned me with beauty. And as the earth increases her flower, and as a garden causes her seeds to spring forth; so the Lord God will cause righteousness and joy to spring forth before all nations.  
*(Chapter 62):*  
For Zion's sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem's sake I will not rest, until my righteousness go forth as light, and my salvation burn as a lamp. And the Gentiles shall see Thy righteousness, and all kings Thy glory; and they shall call thee by Thy new name, which the Lord shall name thee. Thou shalt also be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a royal diadem in the hand of Thy God. Thou shalt no more be termed Forsaken; neither shall Thy land any more be termed Desolate: but thou shalt be called My Will, and Thy land the Inhabited Earth; for the Lord delights in thee, and Thy land shall be inhabited. And as a young man lives with a virgin, so shall Thy sons live with thee; and as the bridegroom rejoices over the bride, so shall the Lord rejoice over thee.
