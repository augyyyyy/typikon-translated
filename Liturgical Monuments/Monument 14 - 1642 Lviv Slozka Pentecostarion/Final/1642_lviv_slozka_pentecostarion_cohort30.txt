*Sticheron. Tone 3:*
The co-unoriginate and co-everlasting Son and Word of the Father came unto the Well, the Fountain of healings; and a woman of Samaria came to draw water, seeing whom the Savior said: "Give Me water to drink, and go, call thy husband!" But she, speaking as unto a man and not unto God, striving to conceal it, said: "I have no husband." And the Teacher said unto her: "Thou hast spoken truly: 'I have no husband': for thou hast had five husbands, and he whom thou now hast is not thy husband." And she, astonished at His voice, running into the city, cried unto the crowds, saying: "Come and see Christ, Who grants great mercy unto the world!"

*Glory... Tone 6:*[^659]
At Jacob's well Jesus found the Samaritan woman, and He Who covers heaven with clouds asks water of her. O wonder! He Who is invisible unto the Cherubim converses with a harlot woman, asking water, He Who suspended the earth upon the waters; seeking water, He Who pours forth fountains

*Then the Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. At the Litiya, Stichera of the Holy Monastery according to custom, and of the Mother of God from the Octoechos, Tone 6.*

*Glory... both now and ever... Tone 3:*
Let Heaven and Earth rejoice radiantly today, for Christ appeared in the flesh, having become man, that He might deliver Adam from the universal curse; and He is marvelous in wonders, having come to Samaria and stood before the woman, asking water, He Who clothes the clouds with waters: therefore let all of us the faithful bow down before Him Who willingly became poor by His tender-hearted counsel!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Resurrection. Tone 4:*
O Lord, having ascended upon the Cross, Thou hast destroyed our ancestral curse; and having descended into hades, Thou hast liberated the eternal prisoners, granting incorruption unto the human race: therefore singing we glorify Thy life-creating and saving Resurrection!

*Other Stichera, one each. Verse: The Lord is King, He is clothed with majesty...*
Hung upon the Tree, O only Mighty One, Thou didst shake all creation; and placed in the Tomb, Thou didst raise up them that dwelt in the grave, granting incorruption and life unto the human race: therefore singing we glorify Thy three-day rising! *Verse: For He has established the world, which shall not be moved...*

O Christ, the lawless people, having delivered Thee unto Pilate, condemned Thee in the flesh, showing themselves senseless toward their Benefactor; but willingly having endured burial, Thou didst rise on the third day of Thine own power as God, granting unending life and great mercy unto us!

*Both now and ever...*

How hast Thou been stolen away? What place holds Thy life-bearing body?" *Glory... Tone 8:*
When Thou didst appear in the flesh, O Christ God, in Thine ineffable dispensation, the Samaritan woman, hearing the word of Thee the Lover of mankind, left her water-pot at the Well and ran, saying unto them that were in the city: "Come, see the Knower of hearts! Is not this indeed the expected Christ, Who has great mercy?"

*Both now and ever... of the Feast. Tone 8:*
When the Feast reached its midpoint, as Thou wast teaching, O Savior, the Judeans said: "How knows this man letters, having never learned?" Not understanding that Thou art the Wisdom that ordered the world: glory to Thee!

*Concerning the Saint of the day, rubric on fol. 176v.*[^661]

*If it happens on this Fifth Sunday after Pascha that the Memory of the Holy Great-Martyr George falls, call Pascha 4 [April 23]; or of the Holy Apostle John the Theologian, call 300 [May 8]: then the service of Mid-Pentecost is left aside, and the Service of the Resurrection is sung, and of the Samaritan Woman, and of the Saint: and the rubric is written on the Sunday of the Myrrh-bearers.*[^662]

*After the First Kathisma, Sessional Hymns. Tone 4:*[^665]
Looking upon the entrance of the Tomb, and unable to endure the angelic flame, the Myrrh-bearing women marvelled with trembling, saying: "Has He been stolen away, Who opened Paradise unto the thief? Or is He risen, Who proclaimed His rising before the Passion? Truly Christ is risen, granting life and resurrection unto them that are in hades!"

Thou didst swiftly anticipate, rising from the tomb as immortal, O Savior; by Thy power Thou didst raise up Thy world with Thyself, O Christ our God; Thou didst shatter the dominion of death in Thy might, Thou didst show forth resurrection unto all the world: therefore we glorify Thee, O only Lover of mankind!

*Glory... both now and ever... Theotokion:*
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto the angels was made manifest upon earth through thee, O Mother of God: God being incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having raised the First-fashioned, He saved our souls from death!

*After the Second Kathisma, Sessional Hymns. Tone 4:*
Gabriel, having descended from the heights of heaven and approached the stone where the Rock of life was, clothed in white, cried aloud unto the weeping women: "Cease from the cry of lamentation, ye that now have that which is full of gracious joy! For He Whom ye seek weeping, take courage: having received joy, bow down before the Risen One: take courage, therefore, and let Eve also take courage!"
