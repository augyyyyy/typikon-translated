O Lord our God, Who sittest upon the Cherubim, Who didst stir up the power of Thine own Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, that He might save the world through His Cross, Burial, and Resurrection; Whom, when He came now into Jerusalem unto His voluntary Passion, the people that sat in darkness and the shadow of death met, bearing symbols of the resurrection—branches of trees and boughs of palms—prefiguring the resurrection: Do Thou Thyself, O Master, keep and preserve us also who, in imitation of them on this eve of the feast, carry palm boughs and branches of trees in our hands; that even as those multitudes and children offered "Hosanna" unto Thee, so we also in hymns and spiritual songs may attain unto the life-creating and third-day Resurrection, in the same Christ Jesus our Lord, with Whom Thou art blessed, together with Thy most holy, good, and life-creating Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages. *Heirmos chanted twice, Troparia on 12. Katavasia: both choirs chant the Heirmos together. The composition of Lord Cosmas, bearing the acrostic: "Hosanna unto Christ the Blessed God."*[^109]
*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
The springs of the deep were seen, lacking moisture, and the uncovered foundations of the billowing sea were revealed: for by Thy nod Thou didst rebuke its tempest, and didst save Thy chosen people, as they sang a song of victory unto Thee, O Lord![^110]

*Troparion 1:*
Out of the mouth of innocent babes and sucklings Thou didst perfect praise for Thy servants, to destroy the adversary and avenge through the Passion of the Cross the fall of ancient Adam, raising him up through the Tree, as he sings a song of victory unto Thee, O Lord!

*Katavasia: The springs of the deep... both choirs together.*

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
The hard rock, flowing when cleft at Thy command, poured forth drink for the people of Israel; but Thou, O Christ, art the Rock and Life, wherein the Church is established, crying aloud: "Hosanna! Blessed art Thou that comest!"[^111]

*Troparion 1:*
At Thy command, Hades released trembling Lazarus from among the dead, dead four days: for Thou art the Resurrection and Life, O Christ, wherein the Church is established, crying aloud: "Hosanna! Blessed art Thou that comest!"

*Troparion 2:*
Sing in a manner worthy of God in Zion, O ye people, and offer prayer unto Christ in Jerusalem! He Himself comes in glory with authority, wherein the Church is established, crying aloud: "Hosanna! Blessed art Thou that comest!"

*Hypakoe, Tone 8:*
Singing first with branches, the unbelieving Jews afterward seized Christ God with staves: but we, honoring Him unceasingly with unwavering faith as our Benefactor, ever cry aloud unto Him: Blessed art Thou that comest to recall Adam!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
Christ our God Who comes openly shall come and shall not delay, from the shaded and dense mountain, the Virgin who bore Him without knowing man, as the Prophet said of old: therefore we all cry aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord![^112]

*Troparion 1:*
Let the mountains and all the hills drop down strong gladness upon Olivet, and let the trees of the forest leap for joy: praise Christ, ye nations, and all ye peoples singing praise unto Him, cry aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion 2:*
Reigning unto the ages in strength, the Lord comes clothed with majesty; the beauty and glory of His loveliness in Zion is beyond compare: therefore we all cry aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion 3:*
He Who measured heaven with a span and the earth with the palm of His hand, the Lord is come! For He hath chosen Zion, wherein He was well pleased to dwell and reign, and He loved the people who faithfully sing: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Get thee up into the mountain, O thou that bringest good tidings unto Zion, and lift up thy voice with strength, thou that preachest unto Jerusalem: glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God: peace upon Israel, and salvation unto the nations![^113]

*Troparion 2:*
O Zion, holy mountain of God, and Jerusalem, lift up thine eyes round about, and behold thy children gathered unto thee! For behold, they are come from afar to worship thy King: peace upon Israel, and salvation unto the nations!
