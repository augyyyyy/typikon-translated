*The Paraecclesiarch distributes candles to the brethren. And we sing "Blessed are the undefiled" [Kathisma 17], in Tone 5, with Refrains, in Three Staseis.[^398] At the First Stasis, the Superior censes; and at the second and third, the Priests according to rank.[^399] Then first the Superior sings this Refrain in the midst of the church, having censed in the form of a cross:*

*Refrain:* We magnify Thee, O Christ the Giver of life, Who for our sake wast buried yesterday, Who didst endure [the Passion], and Who gloriously quickenest the dead.[^400]

*First Stasis*

*Verse 1 (Ps. 118:1):* Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.  
*Troparion:* In a tomb wast Thou laid, O Christ, Who art the Life; and the angelic hosts were filled with awe, glorifying Thy condescension.[^401]

*Verse 2 (Ps. 118:2):* Blessed are they that search His testimonies; they will seek Him with their whole heart.  
*Troparion:* O Life, how diest Thou? How dwellest Thou also in a tomb, yet destroyest the kingdom of death, and raisest up the dead from Hades?

*Verse 3 (Ps. 118:3):* For they that work iniquity have not walked in His ways.  
*Troparion:* We magnify Thee, O Jesus the King, and we honor Thy burial and Thy sufferings, whereby Thou hast saved us from corruption.

*Verse 4 (Ps. 118:4):* Thou hast commanded Thy commandments to be kept diligently.  
*Troparion:* O Jesus, King of all, Who didst appoint the measures of the earth, Thou dwellest now in a narrow tomb, raising up the dead from the graves.

*Verse 5 (Ps. 118:5):* O that my ways were directed to keep Thine ordinances!  
*Troparion:* O my Jesus Christ, King of all creation, what didst Thou seek when Thou camest unto those in Hades? Was it to loose mortal mankind?

*Verse 6 (Ps. 118:6):* Then shall I not be ashamed, when I have respect unto all Thy commandments.  
*Troparion:* The Master of all is seen dead, and is laid in a new tomb, Who emptied the graves of the dead.

*Verse 7 (Ps. 118:7):* I will give thanks unto Thee with uprightness of heart, when I have learned the judgments of Thy righteousness.  
*Troparion:* In a tomb wast Thou laid, O Christ, Who art the Life; and by Thy death Thou hast destroyed death, and poured forth life unto the world.

*Verse 8 (Ps. 118:8):* I will keep Thine ordinances; O forsake me not utterly.  
*Troparion:* With malefactors wast Thou numbered as a malefactor, O Christ, justifying us all from the malice of the ancient supplanter.

*Verse 9 (Ps. 118:9):* Wherewith shall a young man direct his way? By keeping Thy words.  
*Troparion:* He that is fairer in beauty than all the sons of men appears as a corpse without form, He that adorned the nature of all things.

*Verse 10 (Ps. 118:10):* With my whole heart have I sought Thee; cast me not away from Thy commandments.  
*Troparion:* How shall Hades endure Thy coming, O Savior, and not rather suffer anguish, being darkened and blinded by the lightning-flashes of the rays of Thy light?

*Verse 11 (Ps. 118:11):* In my heart have I hidden Thy words, that I might not sin against Thee.  
*Troparion:* O my Sweet Jesus and Saving Light, how hast Thou hidden Thyself in a dark tomb? O Thine ineffable and unutterable endurance!

*Verse 12 (Ps. 118:12):* Blessed art Thou, O Lord; teach me Thine ordinances.  
*Troparion:* Both the intelligent nature and the bodiless host are perplexed, O Christ, at the mystery of Thine ineffable and unsearchable burial.

*Verse 13 (Ps. 118:13):* With my lips have I declared all the judgments of Thy mouth.  
*Troparion:* O strange marvels! O new wonders! The Giver of my breath is carried breathless, buried by the hands of Joseph.

*Verse 14 (Ps. 118:14):* In the way of Thy testimonies have I found delight, as in all manner of riches.  
*Troparion:* Thou hast descended into the tomb, yet wast Thou in no wise separated from the Father's bosom, O Christ; strange and marvelous is this together.
