*Synaxarion Reading:*[^282]
On Great and Holy Thursday, the divine Fathers, who have ordered all things well, receiving in succession from the holy Apostles and the sacred and divine Gospels, handed down unto us to celebrate four things: the Holy Washing of the Feet, the Mystical Supper, the Transmission plainly of the Dread Mysteries that are for our sake, the Supernatural Prayer, and the Betrayal itself. For the Jewish Passover was to be sacrificed on Friday; and it was fitting that the type should precede the truth, furthermore that Christ should be slaughtered as our Passover. Therefore our Lord Jesus Christ, having anticipated it, as the divine Fathers say, accomplishes it with His disciples on Thursday evening: for that evening and the whole of Friday are reckoned...

=== LEAF p77 ===

...are reckoned as one single day among the Hebrews; for thus do they count, reckoning by a day-and-night. He accomplished this therefore, and then (as some have said) with the disciples according to the Law, of whom one is the divine Chrysostom: first simply standing, and girded, shod with sandals, and leaning on staves, and all other things that the Law commands, lest He should be thought a transgressor of the Law. For Zebedee prepared these things; for he was the one carrying a pitcher of water, as the great Athanasius says, although others have spoken otherwise concerning this.[^283]
Thereafter, showing unto the disciples the more perfect things, He also transmits the Mystery of our Passover in the Upper Room, night having already come: "For when evening was come," it says, "He reclined at table with the twelve." Behold, this was not the legal Passover; for there was a supper, and reclining, and bread, and water, whereas there all things were roasted with fire and unleavened. And before beginning to sup—for thus says the divine Chrysostom—He rises from supper, and lays aside His garments, and pours water into a basin, Himself performing all things with His own hands: both putting Judas to shame, and at the same time reminding the other disciples not to seek preeminence; even as after the Washing He instructs them, saying: "He that desires to be first, let him be the last of all," setting Himself as an example. And it appears that Christ washed Judas before all, who had sat down more promptly and shamelessly; and afterwards He comes unto Peter. But he, being more fervent than the rest, forbids the Teacher, and leaves it again more forcefully. Having therefore washed their feet, and having shown forth an exceeding exaltation through humility, taking His garments again and reclining, He instructs them to love one another and not to seek preeminence.
And as they were eating, He introduces the beginning concerning the Betrayal. When the disciples were troubled at the word, Jesus said unto John alone secretly: "He unto whom I shall give the bread when I have dipped it, he it is that betrays Me." For if Peter had known the word, being more fervent than the others, he would have killed Judas; and again: "He that dips his hand with Me in the dish"; thus both took place.
Then, having waited a little, taking bread, He says: "Take, eat"; and likewise the Cup: "Drink ye all of it," having said: "This is My Blood of the New Covenant; this do ye in remembrance of Me."

Come, O faithful, with exalted minds let us partake of the Master's hospitality and the immortal Table in the Upper Room, having learned the lofty word from the Word, Whom we magnify!

*Troparion 1 (A):*
"Go," the Word said unto the disciples, "prepare the Passover in the Upper Room, where the mind is established, mystically accomplished for them, with the unleavened word of truth; and magnify the firm goodness!"

*Troparion 2 (D):*
"The Father begat Me, the Wisdom, the Fashioner before the ages, the beginning of His ways, unto works mysteriously accomplished: for being by nature the uncreated Word, I have made Mine own the voices of the nature which I have now received!"

*Troparion 3 (O):*
"As being man in essence, and not in phantom, so God in the manner of exchange, the nature united unto Me: know Me therefore as the one Christ, from which and in which and which are, saving them that believe!"

*Exapostilarion (thrice):*[^291]
I see Thy bridal chamber adorned, O my Savior, and I have no garment that I may enter therein; enlighten the vesture of my soul, O Giver of light, and save me!

*At the Praises, we set 4 verses, and we chant Stichera Idiomela, Tone 2:*[^292]
The rest of the Jewish council runs together, that it may deliver the Maker and Creator of all things unto Pilate. O the lawless! O the faithless! For they prepare Him for judgment Who is coming to judge the living and the dead; they make ready for sufferings Him Who heals the passions! O longsuffering Lord, great is Thy mercy: glory to Thee!

*Verse 1: Praise Him for His mighty acts, praise Him according to the multitude of His greatness:*
Judas the lawless, O Lord, who at the supper dipped his hand into the dish with Thee, stretched forth his hands unto the lawless to take silver pieces; and he that calculated the price of the myrrh was not afraid to sell Thee, the Priceless One: he that stretched forth his feet to be washed deceitfully kissed the Master, that he might betray Him to the lawless! Casting himself away from the apostolic choir, and casting down the thirty pieces of silver, he knew not Thy third-day Resurrection, through which have mercy on us!

*Verse 2: Praise Him with the sound of the trumpet, praise Him with psaltery and harp:*
Judas the betrayer, being deceitful, with a deceitful kiss betrayed the Savior, the Lord and Master of all things; he sold Him unto the Judeans as a slave, as a sheep to the slaughter: so followed the Lamb of God, the Son of the Father, the only Abundantly-merciful One!

*Verse 3: Praise Him with timbrel and dance, praise Him with strings and flute:*
Judas was revealed by his deeds as a slave and deceiver, a disciple and plotter, a friend and devil: for he followed the Teacher, and took counsel against Him unto betrayal; he said within himself: "I will deliver Him up, and I will acquire the gathered possessions!" He sought that the myrrh should be sold, and that Jesus should be taken by deceit; he will give a kiss, he will betray Christ: and as a sheep to the slaughter, so followed the Lamb of God, the only Tenderhearted and Lover of mankind!

*Glory, both now, Tone 2:*[^293]
He Whom Isaiah proclaimed as the Lamb comes to His voluntary slaughter, and He gives His back to stripes, His cheeks to buffeting, and He turned not away His face from the shame of spitting; He is condemned to an ignominious death: all these things the Sinless One willingly accepts, that He may grant unto all the resurrection from the dead!
