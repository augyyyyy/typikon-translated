*Verse:* O God, My God, give ear unto Me: why hast Thou forsaken Me? (Ps. 21:2 [LXX])

*Seventh Gospel from Matthew, pericopa 113 (Matthew 27:33–54):* At that time, the soldiers having come... *Ending:* ...Truly this was the Son of God.
*Then the reading from the Commentary on Matthew.*
*Then Psalm 50.*
*Then the Eighth Gospel from Luke, pericopa 111 (Luke 23:32–49):* At that time, there were also two other malefactors led with Jesus... *Ending:* ...and the women that followed Him from Galilee, beholding these things.

*Canon Triode. The work of Lord Cosmas.*[^333]
*Heirmos (twice). Troparia to 12. And at the end, the Heirmos chanted together by both choirs. The Acrostic or marginal verse of the Triode is:* PRO SABBATON TE, *which is:* Before the Sabbath.
*Tone 6. Ode 5. Heirmos:*
Early in the morning unto Thee do I rise, O Word of God, Who for Thy tender mercy didst give Thyself changelessly unto fallen man, and even unto Thy Passions didst bow Thyself impassibly: grant me peace, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparia:*
Having washed their feet and been purified by the communion of the divine Mystery, Thy ministers, O Christ, now ascended with Thee from Sion unto the great Mount of Olives, hymning Thee, O Lover of mankind!
"Behold," Thou didst say, "O My friends, be not afraid! For now the hour is at hand that I should be taken and slain by the hands of the lawless..."
