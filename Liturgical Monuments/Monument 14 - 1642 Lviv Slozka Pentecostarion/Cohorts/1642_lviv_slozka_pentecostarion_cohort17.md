*Troparion:* None dares to ask for the immaculate Body, hanging visible upon the Tree, breathless, dead,

and wounded, and assailed with revilings, and especially pierced in the side.  
*Troparion:* Peter the first disciple denied Thee, all the disciples abandoned the Teacher; take pity, O noble one, and having asked, take down the Body of my Master, and do thou alone weep, as for a most cherished child, beloved of a mother!  
*Glory:*  
*Troparion:* When Joseph saw Thy weeping Mother, O my Christ, hearing her painful words, he was moved with compassion, and wept with her, and made haste quickly to ask for Thine immaculate Body.  
*Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* Looking upon Thy commandment, Pilate grants Thy divine Body; and taking It down from the Tree with Nicodemus, Joseph bowed toward the earth, and the earth shook, and Hades surrendered its dead in haste.  
*Ode 4. Heirmos: Foreseeing Thy divine...*  
*Troparion:* Embracing Thee, O all-blameless one, she kissed the wounds of Thine immaculate members, saying: "I will die with Thee, for I cannot bear, O Child, to behold the pouring forth of Thine all-pure blood!"  
*Troparion:* Receiving Thee, she that bore Thee laid Thee upon her knees, O Christ, and weeping, kissed Thy hands, wiping away from Thy side the dripping divine blood, O Master!  
*Glory:*  
*Troparion:* Kissing the speechless mouth, she that bore Thee, O Christ, pressed Thee to herself and weeping, kissed Thee, both man and God!  
*Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* The Persian kings brought Thee gifts, O Master, worshipping Thee as God; but now Nicodemus brings burial ointments, O Child, as for a mortal! How shall I bury Thee, O my Christ?  
*Ode 5. Heirmos: Rising early unto Thee...*  
*Troparion:* "I cannot bear, O Child, to hold Thee breathless, Whom I long held playing as a babe in my arms! If Thou descendest unto Adam into Hades, I also will descend with Thee, to learn Thy mysteries!"  
*Troparion:* "Weep not, O Mother," Christ spoke divinely unto her that bore Him, "for thou shalt see Me risen again, and radiantly proclaiming 'Rejoice!' unto thee, and raising up Adam with Eve!"  
*Glory:*  
*Troparion:* "How shall I endure, O Child, the pains of my womb, kissing Thy face which gazed from the Tree, which formerly I saw shining with beautiful comeliness, and enlightening the blind by Thy divine behest?"  
*Both now:*  
*Theotokion:* "Shall I rather go down to see Thy glory in Hades, O my Son, and ascend again? For I cannot bear to hold Thy lifeless body motionless, and to kiss Thy wounded members!"  
*Ode 6. Heirmos: Held fast, but not consumed...*  
*Troparion:* "How hast thou denied the Savior, O Peter, Whom thou didst confess as Christ, the Son of the living God, and hast left Him alone upon the Tree, crucified, without breath and without form; neither hast thou made haste quickly to hide His Body in a tomb?" the pure Mother wept and spoke.  
*Troparion:* "Give me a word, O sweetest Word and Teacher!" the pure one cried unto Thee with weeping; "I cannot bear to lay Thee in the tomb without breath, O my Son, Who art free among the dead! Behold how Thine own Mother weeps without ceasing; for a burning sword has pierced my heart!"
