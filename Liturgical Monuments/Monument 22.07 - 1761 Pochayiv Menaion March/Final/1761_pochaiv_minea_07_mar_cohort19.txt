After the Kathismata, Resurrectional Sessional Hymns with their Theotokia; and reading in the Commentary Gospel; and the rest Resurrectional. Resurrectional Canon with the Heirmos on 4; and of the Triodion on 4; and of the Feast and of the Archangel on 6. Katavasia of the Feast. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the Archangel, and Sessional Hymn; Glory and both now, of the Feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the Feast, and Ikos. At Ode 9, we sing "More honorable." Resurrectional Exapostilarion; Glory, of the Archangel; and both now, of the Feast. At the Praises, Resurrectional Stichera, 4; and of the Feast, 3; and the Idiomelon of the Feast with its verse; then the verse: "Arise, O Lord my God;"[^418] and the Idiomelon of the Triodion, the same; and both now: "Most blessed art thou." Great Doxology. Resurrectional Troparion; Litanies, and dismissal; and the catechesis of the Studite, and customary departure. Glory and both now, Evangelical Sticheron; and the 1st Hour in the narthex. At the Hours, Resurrectional Troparion; Glory, of the Feast; and both now, Theotokion of the Hours. After "Our Father," Kontakion of the Feast. At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes of the Tone on 6; and of the Feast, Ode 4, on 4. After the Entrance, Resurrectional Troparion, and of the Feast, and of the Archangel; Resurrectional Kontakion; Glory, of the Archangel; and both now, of the Feast. Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day, and of the Feast. Instead of "It is truly meet," we sing: "All creation rejoices in thee."*
