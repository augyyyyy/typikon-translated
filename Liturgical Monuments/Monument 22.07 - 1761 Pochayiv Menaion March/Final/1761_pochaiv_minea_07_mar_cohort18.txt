*...or priest says: "And that we may be accounted worthy to hear the Holy Gospel." And the Superior reads the 1st Gospel; and afterwards the Antiphons, and the Gospels of the Holy Passions, according to their order. And after the reading of the 7th Gospel, the reading of the day. After this, Gradual Hymns, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon of the Feast; "Every breath;" Gospel of the Feast; and immediately the 8th Gospel of the Holy Passions. Psalm 50. Sticheron of the Feast. Then the prayer: "O God, save Thy people;" and we: "Lord, have mercy," 12. Canon of the Feast with the Heirmos on 14. Katavasia of the Feast, both choirs together. But where there is a Triodion (three-ode canon): we sing the canon of the Feast with the Heirmos on 6, the Heirmos twice; and the Triodion on 8; Katavasia, the Heirmos of the Triodion, both choirs together. After Ode 3, Kontakion of the day, and Ikos; Glory and both now, Sessional Hymn of the Feast; and reading of the Feast. After Ode 6, Kontakion of the Feast, and Ikos, and Synaxarion. At Ode 9, we do not sing "More honorable," but sing the Refrain of the Feast. Exapostilarion of the Feast; Glory, of the day; and both now, of the Feast. Then the 9th Gospel. At the Praises, Idiomela Stichera of the day; Glory, of the day; and both now, of the Feast. Then the 10th Gospel. After this, "Glory to God in the highest;" Litany: "Let us complete our morning prayer;" and exclamation. After this, the 11th Gospel. Then at the Aposticha, Stichera of the Feast; Glory, of the day; and both now, of the Feast. Then the 12th Gospel. After this: "It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord;"[^396] and after "Our Father," Troparion, Tone 4: "Thou hast redeemed us [from the curse of the law];"[^397] Glory and both now, of the Feast. Litany: "Have mercy on us, O God." Priest: "Wisdom;" and dismissal. And we sing the 1st Hour as was indicated on Great Friday: we say at it the Troparion of the Feast, and of the day, and the Kontakion of the Feast.*

*And at the 5th hour of the day, we make the procession outside the monastery; and having returned, we sing the 3rd Hour, the 6th, and the 9th, with Troparia, and Paremias, with the Epistles, and Gospels, and readings, and with their remaining service. And at them are said the Troparion of the Hours of Great Friday, which is of the day, and of the Feast; Kontakion of the day, and of the Feast; and the rest as was indicated on Great Friday. But Psalm 33 we omit here, and say it at the Liturgy. And at the 8th hour of the day, the bell is struck in the great bell and in all the heavy ones; we begin Vespers, and the priest performs the Proskomedia.
