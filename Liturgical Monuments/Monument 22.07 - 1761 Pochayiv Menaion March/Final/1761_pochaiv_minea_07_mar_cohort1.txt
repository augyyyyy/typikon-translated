**BOOK OF THE MENAION**  
**MONTH OF MARCH**

To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; under the reign of the Great King Augustus the Third;[^1]  
By the blessing of His Excellency, the Lord Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky,[^2] Exarch of the Metropolia of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh;  
By the resolve of the Most Reverend Lord Father Hieromonk Hypatius Bilynsky,[^3] Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great;  
Through the diligence and at the expense of the monks of the same Order, in the Holy Miraculous Lavra of Pochaiv, issued in print.  
In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers: 7269; and from the Nativity of Christ: 1761.[^4]

# MONTH OF MARCH
*March 1: Holy Venerable-Martyr Eudokia:*

*The Month of March, having 31 days.*  
*On the 1st day: the Holy Venerable-Martyr Eudokia.*

*If it be the Fast, we sing Alleluia. But if not: the Troparion.*[^5]  
*At "Lord, I have cried," stichera, Tone 4:*

*Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Hast Given a Sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*

Having first fasted, and having withered the wantonness of the flesh by self-control, by suffering thou didst cast down a second time the crafts of the enemy, and didst obtain the victory over him, O Eudokia, blessed of God. Therefore Jesus, the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls, crowned thee for both these contests.

With the furrows of thy judgments, as a fertile and God-inspired soil, Thou didst sow the divine seed, and yielding a hundredfold harvest of martyrdom, Thou didst truly blossom forth; and Thou hast gathered them into spiritual granaries by the power of the Spirit Who transformed thee, and Who by His grace transformed thee with a better transformation, O all-praised Eudokia.

Thou didst raise the dead by thy life-creating illumination, O glorious Eudokia, having first put to death the bodily passions by the pains of self-control; and now thou dwellest in the heavens with the martyrs, having run well the course of suffering by the help of the Spirit, interceding for all who hymn thee in faith.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion, in the same Tone and Podoben (Гла́съ и҆ подо́бенъ то́йже):*

Rejoice, ray radiant as the sun, throne of the unwaning Sun, who didst dawn the incomprehensible Sun! Rejoice, mind glistening with divine splendors, lightning flash that enlightens

=== LEAF p4 === (Fol. 1v)
the ends of the earth: true golden gleam, all-undefiled and all-immaculate one, who hast shone forth an unwaning light upon the faithful.

*Stavrotheotokion, in the same Podoben (Подо́бенъ то́йже):*

Beholding Christ, the Lover of mankind, crucified, and His side pierced with a spear, the all-pure one wept and cried aloud: What is this, O my Son? What have the ungrateful people rendered unto Thee in return for the good things Thou didst for them? And dost Thou hasten to leave me childless, O most beloved One? I marvel, O compassionate One, at Thy voluntary crucifixion.

*Troparion, Tone 8:*

Having bound thy soul to the love of Christ with uprightness of mind, and having surpassed corruptible, pleasant, and fleeting things by forgetting them as a disciple of the Word, thou didst first put the passions to death through fasting, and second didst put the enemy to shame through suffering. Therefore Christ hath deemed thee worthy of a double crown, O glorious Eudokia; O venerable passion-bearer, pray unto Christ God that our souls may be saved.

*Canon, Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Come, ye peoples, let us sing a song to Christ God, Who divided the sea and guided the people whom He brought out of the bondage of Egypt, for He hath been glorified.

*Troparia:*

Deign, O Word, to sweeten the affliction of my soul through the supplications of Thy passion-bearer Eudokia,[^6] and grant me to sing of her.

Who will not proclaim the abyss of Thy compassion? For Thou didst cause to be saved a stranger who was formerly in the grip of grievous sins.

As one who offered sweet wine instead of evil, she received the teaching of venerable Germanus, which brought forth the fruit of piety.

Having Christ as a spark in thy heart, O all-pure one, through endurance thou didst truly consume deceit and bodily passions like brushwood.

*Theotokion:*

Truly thou hast been shown to be a God-like dwelling, O pure Virgin, being more spacious and more honorable; and now the angelic choir rejoices in thee.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Establish me in Thee, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put sin to death; and plant Thy fear in the hearts of us who hymn Thee.

*Troparion:*

Having obtained Christ as a bulwark and an invincible rampart within her breast, Thy venerable one was not shaken by the assaults of the enemy, but utterly destroyed him.

=== LEAF p5 === (Fol. 3r)
...this honorable body; and those who embrace its fruits are made partakers of sweet fragrance, and seeing it they marvel that in truth a dead body pours forth healings of all diseases.[^7] Therefore come ye all now with zeal, together with me the humble one, and having cleansed our defilement, let us draw forth the grace of miracles!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

When the golden image was worshiped on the plain of Dura, Thy three youths disregarded the godless decree; cast into the midst of the fire, bedewed they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*

The helper of the enemy, who of old ensnared thee, when trampled under thy feet groaned greatly because of the rain of thy God-inspired words, crying: Woe is me, I am vanquished, O all-praised Eudokia, by the lips that unceasingly offer supplication unto God!

Having put to death the desires of thy flesh through much self-control, thou didst grant boldness unto thy mothers in their resurrection unto God, and unto Him here by thine undoubting prayer; for the prayer of those who fear Him slays [the enemy].

Bearing the wounds of Christ upon thy neck, as a God-befitting adornment, thou didst draw Him by the desire of incorruption, therefore He found thee an undefiled bride, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*

Gideon of old prefigured thy divine birthgiving, O pure one, clearly bearing the fleece full of water which he wrung out from the wool; for God the Word took up His abode in thee, O all-pure one. Blessed is the fruit of thy womb!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who descended into the fiery furnace unto the Hebrew youths, and changed the flame into dew, praise ye God as the Lord, all ye works, and supreme-exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*

Having attained with thy mind unto the true Lover, thou didst readily endure bodily wounds and the bitter pain of fire, O all-praised martyr, blessing Christ unto all ages.

With the staff of the Cross thou didst shatter the tempest of afflictions, and didst save the rudder of the soul into the harbors of the Kingdom of God, O glorious one, blessing Christ unto ages.

Thou didst strip off the disfigurement of the passions and didst clothe thy soul with the fair garment of dispassion. Therefore Christ, having betrothed thee as a bride, took thee into the heavenly chambers.

*Theotokion:*

=== LEAF p6 === (Fol. 3v)
...through thee, O Theotokos, our foremother was loosed from condemnation, and lo, now women suffer for Christ; and female nature rejoices, as the first-martyr and virgin of virgins cries aloud.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

God the Word, Who by ineffable wisdom came to renew Adam who had fallen grievously into corruption through eating, and Who for our sake was ineffably enfleshed of the Holy Virgin, with one mind we the faithful magnify in hymns.

*Troparia:*

The sight of thy face, O pure one, having shone forth like the sun with a spiritual radiance, showed forth a marvelous vision unto the unbelievers who beheld it; for having loved Christ as heaven, thou hast worthily been glorified by Him, O all-honored one.

O divine martyr, having brightly fed the lamp of thy soul with the oil of thy blood, rejoicing thou hast now entered into the divine bridal chamber of Christ with the wise virgins; with them remember all who praise thee in faith.

As a sweet-scented rose from the thorns of deceit, O God-wise one, thou didst blossom forth in beauty, and hast perfumed the souls of the faithful, O Eudokia; and thou didst steadfastly repel the deceitful stench of the unbelievers. Therefore we bless thee.

O pure martyr, by thy prayers unto God and the Redeemer, save from all afflictions those who celebrate thy prayerful memory with love, and wash away the soul's defilement from me who gladly hymn thee.

*Theotokion:*

O all-holy Virgin, pray to thy Son, God the Lover of mankind, on their behalf; for we have acquired thee as our hope, and through thy protection we who praise thee in faith are delivered from afflictions, and are saved from transgressions and diseases.

*March 2: Holy Hieromartyr Theodotus of Cyrenia:*

*In the same month, on the 2nd day: the Holy Hieromartyr Theodotus, Bishop of Cyrenia.*  
*Alleluia, or the Troparion.*[^8]  
*At "Lord, I have cried," stichera, Tone 4:*

*Tone 4, Podoben: "As One Valiant among the Martyrs" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*

A most sacred priest, the foundation of the Church, an unshakable pillar hast thou been shown to be, O all-glorious one; a most true wonderworker, a glorious servant of God, and a radiant luminary of the consecrated [priests]...

=== LEAF p7 === (Fol. 2r)
Having desired the beauty of Christ, and consumed by His vine: through suffering thou wast united unto Him, O God-wise and all-glorious one.

Having been cleansed first by Christ's Baptism, and with thy tears, and after this by the blood of martyrdom, thou wast a temple, a threefold place of sanctification.

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst blossom forth as the joy of Anna, censing the King, O Virgin; and the women who rejoiced at thy birthgiving were loosed from the curse through thee.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Speedily Anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*[^9]

Illumined with divine light, thou didst forsake the darkness of deceit; for thou wast enriched with holy divine repose, and filled with divine grace, by a brief word thou didst raise the dead. Therefore, having gloriously finished the contest of martyrdom, thou wast divinely adorned with a crown and didst put the deceiver to shame. O Eudokia, equal-to-the-angels, pray unto Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who honor thy holy memory with love.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

As the only one among women who without seed didst bear God in the flesh, all we the generations of mankind bless thee; for the fire of the Godhead dwelt in thee, and as an infant thou didst feed with milk the Creator and Lord. Therefore the angelic and human race worthily glorify thy most holy birthgiving, and with one voice we cry unto thee: Pray unto Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who worship thy most holy birthgiving in faith.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Beholding the Lamb, the Shepherd and Redeemer upon the Cross, the ewe-lamb cried aloud weeping, and bitterly lamenting, exclaimed: The world indeed rejoices, receiving redemption through Thee, but my womb burns, beholding Thy crucifixion which Thou endurest for the tender mercy of Thy compassion. O long-suffering Lord, abyss of mercy and inexhaustible fountain, have compassion and grant remission of transgressions unto those who hymn Thy divine passions with faith!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I heard, O Lord, the report of Thy dispensation, and I glorified Thee, Who alone art the Lover of mankind.

*Troparia:*

With the river of thy divine blood, O glorious Eudokia, thou didst dry up the abyss of godlessness.

She who of old ensnared the foolish with the nets of fornication, now catches the faithful for the Kingdom of God.

=== LEAF p8 === (Fol. 2v)
The corruptible wealth gathered by thee in injustice, having dispersed it righteously, thou didst inherit the wealth of incorruption.

*Theotokion:*

Thou hast shone forth light, O Lord, unto those in the night and darkness of transgressions, taking flesh from the pure Virgin.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

O Giver of light and Creator of the ages, O Lord, guide us in the light of Thy commandments, for we know no other God beside Thee.

*Troparia:*

The unwaning Light having truly shone upon the glorious one, delivered her from the gloom of passions, and having guided her to the path of dispassion, showed her forth as good unto all.

Having prayed to Christ God the King, as she who bore a son, by invoking His honored name, O most glorious one, thou didst raise up the dead child of the king.

With manly purpose thou didst glorify Christ at the tribunal, confessing God incarnate; and beheaded in the neck by the sword, thou dost dance in choir with the martyrs.

*Theotokion:*

As having thy protection as our hope, O faithful, and surrounded by the tribulations of bitter afflictions, we earnestly flee unto thee, O Lady Virgin.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Wallowing in the abyss of sins, I call upon the fathomless abyss of Thy compassion: Lead me up from corruption, O God!

*Troparia:*

The holy zeal of the faith hath seized thee, O martyr Eudokia: thou didst trample down the pride of the infidels, and didst win the victory of suffering.

Having mingled the streams of martyrdom with drops of blood, thou didst accomplish one cleansing from both.

Formerly filled with the stench of much impurity, she is now filled with sweet fragrance, and joins in the choir of virgins.

*Theotokion:*

O pure Theotokos, who art joy itself, deem him who faithfully cries "Rejoice" unto thee with the angel, worthy of thy joy; and heal my sorrowful soul.

*Kontakion, Tone 4:*

Having contested well in thy contest, and after death sanctifying us through the outpouring of miracles, O praiseworthy one; as we flee in faith into thy divine temple, celebrating we pray thee, O venerable martyr Eudokia: Deliver us from spiritual infirmities, that we may draw forth the grace of miracles.

*Ikos:*

The church of Grotenka,[^10] which planted the garden of immortality in my midst, [presents] this honorable body...

=== LEAF p9 === (Fol. 4r)
...thou wast consecrated, and having planted the tree of life in the midst, [thou didst serve] the Giver of life, O hieromartyr Theodotus.

Stretched upon the tree with ox sinews, and cruelly handled by the guards, and shut up in prison, and thy feet grievously pierced with nails, and laid upon fiery coals, thou wast shown to be unarmed, glorifying Him Who strengthened thee unto ages, O hieromartyr Theodotus.

Through the sinews of thy contests thou didst cast down the enemy and overthrow his power, and having fought most radiantly, thou dwellest now crowned in the highest Kingdom, deemed worthy of forgiveness; and praying that they who celebrate in faith may be saved, [O adornment] of martyrs, Theodotus.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion, in the same Tone and Podoben (Гла́съ и҆ подо́бенъ то́йже):*

Deliver my humble soul from condemnation and from grievous transgressions, O all-holy Bride of God, and from deadly afflictions by thy prayers, that I may obtain justification before the King on the day of trial, which the assemblies of the saints have attained; show me forth cleansed by weeping and the pouring forth of tears before the end.

*Stavrotheotokion, in the same Podoben (Подо́бенъ то́йже):*

When the Ewe-lamb and Thy Mother beheld Thee nailed upon the Cross, O Lord, she was amazed and cried aloud: What is this vision, O desired Son? Is this what the ungrateful and lawless assembly hath rendered unto Thee, who enjoyed Thy many wonders? But glory to Thine ineffable descent, O Master!

*Canon, the acrostic whereof is: "Thou hast fittingly been a divine gift, O blessed one."*[^11] *Tone 4.*  
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Israel of old, having traversed the deep of the Red Sea with dryshod feet, by the crosswise-stretched hands of Moses overcame the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Troparia:*

Direct thy movements unto Him, O martyr, whose venerable commandments thou didst keep: thou wast a hierarch, and didst most manifestly shine forth with the blood of martyrdom.

Having lived divinely and piously, and having put the movements of the flesh to death by ascetic struggles, thou didst receive life, O adornment of hierarchs and completion of martyrs.

The Holy Spirit dwelt in thy most wise heart from thy swaddling clothes, O venerable one, showing thee forth as upright, compassionate, gentle, and a divine hierarch...

=== LEAF p10 === (Fol. 4v)
...and a divine hierarch, O martyr Theodotus.

*Theotokion:*

Human nature, which was formerly deceitfully enslaved by error, O pure one, having given birth to Christ, obtained freedom, glorifying thee with high praise.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying aloud: Thou art my strength, O Lord, my refuge, and my foundation!

*Troparia:*

By the sighs of thy tears and the streams of thy blood, thou didst extinguish the fierce coals of deceit, O martyr and hierarch Theodotus.

Thy pure life showed forth divine sacred actions, and made thee a true witness of Christ's passions.

Standing before the tribunal, and wounded by cruel blows, thou didst not deny the name of Christ, O martyr Theodotus.

*Theotokion:*

Heal my mind, O all-pure one, which hath grown weak from the assaults of the deceitful serpent and is enslaved to sin.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou Who Wast Raised upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йсѧ):*

Having made the robe of thy hierarchal office more radiant by steeping it in thy blood, thou didst take up thine abode in the heavens, O Theodotus, seeing with a pure mind Him Whom thou didst desire. Therefore we glorify thy most sacred memory, and cry aloud: Remember us, as thou standest with boldness before God!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Who can tell the multitude of my evil thoughts, and the surge of unlawful imaginations, O all-immaculate one? Or who can recount the assaults of my bodiless enemies and their malice? But by thy prayer, grant me deliverance from them all!

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Beholding Thee, Who wast begotten of the unoriginate Father, and in the last times born of her in the flesh, upon the Cross, O Christ, she cried aloud: Woe is me, O most beloved Jesus! How is it that Thou, Who art glorified as God by the angels, dost now will to be crucified by lawless men, O Son? I hymn Thy long-suffering!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Beholding Thee lifted up upon the Cross, O Sun of righteousness, the Church stood in her place, worthily crying aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparia:*

Thy body covered with blood from wounds made thy mind firmer and in truth made thee a stronger martyr, crying: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Inflamed with eternal love, thou didst endure the most cruel pains from wounds, as though another were suffering...
