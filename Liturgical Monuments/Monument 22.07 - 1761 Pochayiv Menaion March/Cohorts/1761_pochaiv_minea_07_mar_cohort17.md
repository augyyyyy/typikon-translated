Having flown down from the heavenly spheres to Nazareth, Gabriel came unto the Virgin Mary, crying aloud unto her: Rejoice! Thou shalt conceive the Son, Who is older than Adam, the Creator of the ages and Deliverer of those who cry unto thee: Rejoice, O pure one!

The co-eternal Word of the Father without beginning, not parting from the heights, has now come unto the lowly by reason of supreme tenderheartedness, having accepted mercy for our slipping; and taking Adam's poverty upon Himself, He was fashioned in what was alien.[^363]

Bringing good tidings from heaven unto the Virgin, Gabriel cried aloud: Rejoice! In thy womb thou shalt conceive Him Whom thou containest, yet Whom none can contain; and thou that givest birth shalt be shown forth of Him Who shone forth from the Father before the morning star.[^364]

The transcendent Word, having dwelt in thee in the flesh by the Father's counsel, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, has restored our human race which fell through the ancient curse; therefore we all faithfully cry unto thee with the angel: Rejoice, O Mother of Christ!

*Glory, both now, Tone 2. By Theophanes:[^365]*

The mystery which is from the ages is revealed today, and the Son of God becomes the Son of Man: that, taking the worse, He may impart unto me the better. Adam was deceived of old, and desiring to become God, he was not; God becomes man, that He may make Adam god. Let creation be glad, let nature dance for joy, for the Archangel stands before the Virgin with fear, and brings the "Rejoice," which is the opposite of sorrow: O our God, Who for the mercy of Thy compassion didst become man, glory to Thee!
