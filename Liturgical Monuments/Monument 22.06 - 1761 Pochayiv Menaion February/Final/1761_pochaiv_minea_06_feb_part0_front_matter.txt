# 1761 Pochaiv Menaion — Vol. 6: February (Pochaiv Lavra, 1761) <a id="1761-pochaiv-menaion-vol-6-february-pochaiv-lavra-1761"></a>

*(Title Page: Baroque woodcut ornamental architectural border printed in red and black ink)*

THE BOOK
MENAION
Month of February.
To the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit; in the reign of the Great King Augustus the Third;[^1]
By the blessing of His Excellency, Lord Sylvester Liubieniecki-Rudnytsky,[^2] Exarch of the Metropolitan See of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus', Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh;
By the resolve of the Most Venerable Lord, Hieromonk Ipatii Bilynsky,[^3] Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great;
Through the diligence and at the expense of the monks of the same Order in the Holy Wonderworking Lavra of Pochaiv, printed and published.
In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers 7269;
And from the Nativity of Christ 1761.[^4]

*(Vacat — Blank flyleaf verso) (Fol. 1r. Woodcut headpiece: The Meeting of the Lord with Righteous Symeon, the Theotokos, Joseph, and Anna the Prophetess)*

### Notes

[^1]: King Augustus III of Poland (August III Sas), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania (reigned 1734–1763), Elector of Saxony.
[^2]: Sylvester Liubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Rudnycki-Lubieniecki, consecrated 1752, died 1777), Greek Catholic Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh, and Exarch of the Metropolitan See of Kyiv, Halych, and all Rus'.
[^3]: Ipatii Bilynsky (Hipacy Biliński, OSBM), Protoarchimandrite of the Order of Saint Basil the Great (Basilian Order of St. Josaphat) from 1759 to 1772.
[^4]: The year 7269 from the Byzantine creation era corresponds to 1761 AD (7269 - 5508 = 1761).
