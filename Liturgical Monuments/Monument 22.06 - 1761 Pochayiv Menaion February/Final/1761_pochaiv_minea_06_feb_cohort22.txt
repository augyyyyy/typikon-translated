But if on account of some great necessity, on the memory of great saints having a Polyeleos, it should happen that there is no Presanctified Liturgy: we sing the Hours as said above; after the Ninth Hour, and after the prayer: "O Master Lord Jesus Christ", we say Psalm 102: "Bless the Lord, O my soul, and all that is within me"; Glory: "Praise the Lord, O my soul"; Both now: "O Only-begotten Son"; and "In Thy Kingdom" quickly without singing. At the Beatitudes we say of the Forerunner, Ode 3 and 6; then the Epistle and Gospel of the Forerunner. Thereafter: "Remember us, O Lord"; after "Pardon, remit", and after "Our Father", kontakion of the Temple; Glory, of the Forerunner; Both now, of the Temple of the Mother of God. If there is no temple of the Mother of God: "Protection of Christians"; then "Lord, have mercy", 40, and 3 great prostrations. And at that time the paraecclesiarch strikes two bells; and after the three great prostrations, we say: "Come, let us worship"; and we sing Vespers with prostrations, and the customary Kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried", stichera of the Triodion, prosomoia, 3; and of Saint Tarasius,[^504] 3; Glory... Both now, Theotokion. But if it be towards Wednesday or Friday, we say: Glory... Both now, Stavrotheotokion; then "O Gladsome Light"; Prokeimenon, and reading of the Triodion of the day; at the Aposticha of the day from the Triodion; after "Now lettest Thou depart", and after "Our Father", the troparion: "O Mother of God and Virgin, rejoice"; and the rest according to the Lenten order. After "More honorable than the Cherubim" and after the prostrations, we say the Trisagion; "Lord, have mercy", 12; "All-holy Trinity"; then "Blessed be the name of the Lord", and 3 prostrations; "I will bless the Lord"; "It is truly meet"; and dismissal: at the dismissal he commemorates the incoming saint. After the dismissal we enter the refectory, we eat two cooked dishes with oil, and drink wine. But on Wednesday and Friday we eat two cooked dishes without oil, and drink wine.*
