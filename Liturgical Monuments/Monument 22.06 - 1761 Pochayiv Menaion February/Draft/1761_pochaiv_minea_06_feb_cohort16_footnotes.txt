[^348]: The twelve martyrs correspond in number to the twelve apostles and the twelve minor prophets.
[^349]: The five Egyptian companions of Saint Pamphilus adopted the names of the holy prophets: Samuel, Elias, Daniel, Jeremias, and Isaias.
