[^297]: Pericope 36 of the Gospel according to Matthew is Matthew 10:16–22, the appointed gospel lection for a martyr (*"Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves..."*).
[^298]: The printed heading reads *гла́съ, н҃*, an apparent typographical error for *гла́съ, и҃* (Tone 8), as confirmed by the standard Tone 8 Heirmologion heirmoi (*Колесницегонителѧ фараѡ́на*, etc.) utilized throughout this canon composed by St. Theophanes Graptos.
[^299]: The printed Slavonic text reads *Оу҆́мъ нѐныи*, an evident typographical error either for *Оу҆́мъ нб҃ный* (*The heavenly mind*) or *зе́мный* (*The earthly mind*), expressing the traditional patristic theological axiom that no created intellect can comprehend the mystery of the Incarnation.
[^300]: The printed Church Slavonic text reads *Стѧ́гши*, an obscure form or scribal error perhaps for *Стѧжа́въ* (*Having acquired...*) or *Стѧ́гомъ* (*Under the banner of Christ's martyrs...*).
[^301]: The printed text has *промышле́нїю, ве́мъ:* across the column break, dropping the syllable *зо-* from *зове́мъ* (*we cry*).
[^302]: The printed text reads *Ѿсщїе́тїѧ*, an evident typographical corruption for *Ѿсѣче́нїемъ* (*By his beheading* / *By cutting off*), translating the Greek hymnographic term *Τῇ τομῇ*.
[^303]: The printed Slavonic text reads *Н҆́бо ѕвѣзда́ми:* with an initial *Н* bearing a smooth breathing/titlo, representing *Не́бо ѕвѣзда́ми* (the standard Tone 3 Exapostilarion model, Greek *Ὁ οὐρανὸν τοῖς ἄστροις*).
[^304]: The rubric notes that the commemorated daily saint whose service is superseded by the Apodosis of the Meeting of the Lord (in this case, St. Nikephoros) is transferred to Compline.
[^305]: The printed abbreviation *въ вл҃к:* represents *въ воскресе́нїе* or *въ недѣ́лю госпо́дскую / влады́чню* (*on the Lord's Day / on Sunday*).
