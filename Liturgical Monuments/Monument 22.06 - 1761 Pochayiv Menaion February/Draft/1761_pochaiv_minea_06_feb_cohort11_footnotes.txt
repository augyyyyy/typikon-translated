[^294]: The rubrics cross-reference the common Sessional Theotokia and Cross-Theotokia for Lenten weekdays (or fast periods) provided at the conclusion of the month under February 28 (fol. 106v).
[^295]: The printed text reads Дѣво́мъ (or Дево́мъ), an evident typographical error either for Дѣ́вою (*Through the Virgin*), or for Дрѣ́вомъ / Дре́вомъ (*By the Tree*), the latter being a common patristic motif of Christ renewing mankind through the Wood of the Cross in reversal of Adam's fall.
