After "Our Father": Troparion of the Saint; Glory... Both now... of the feast; but if not: of the feast only; and after the Litany, the First Hour. At the Hours: Troparion of the feast; Glory... of the Saint, if there be one; and Both now... the Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion we recite the Kontakion of the feast, without variation.*
