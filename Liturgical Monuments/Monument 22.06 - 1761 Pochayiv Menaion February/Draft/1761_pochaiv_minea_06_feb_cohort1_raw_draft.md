# MONTH OF FEBRUARY

*Having 28 days; but if it be a leap year, then it has 29.*

*February 1: Forefeast of the Meeting of our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ; and the Holy Martyr Tryphon:*

*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Forefeast, 3, Tone 1. Podoben: "As one valiant among martyrs" (*Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ*):*[^5]

*The radiant Church prepares herself to receive within herself the Lord, Who comes as an infant and spiritually enlightens by grace His most faithful and God-loving assembly; therefore she cries out: "Thou art the glory and praise, and the adornment of my fullness, O Word, Who hast become an infant in the flesh for my sake!"*

*The bridal chamber of many lights, the most honorable tabernacle, the holy and spacious temple, which brings into the inner chambers the Church of the Lord, betrothes herself to His most honorable Church, and prays unceasingly that they who truly glorify her as the Birthgiver of God may be delivered from corruption and perils.*

*Let us now offer a forefeast praise unto Christ, glorifying His descent: for He Who is borne as an infant by virginal hands comes to recline in the arms of Symeon, working the restoration of all mankind, and deifying human nature, as Benefactor and Lord.*

*Other Stichera of the Saint, Tone 4. Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (*Всехва́льнїи*):*[^6]

*O all-lauded Tryphon, thou hast become a most radiant partaker of incorruptible sweetness in the heavens; by valiant struggles thou hast suffered gloriously, and having become a witness of the Truth, pray thou unto Christ to grant unto our souls peace and great mercy.*
