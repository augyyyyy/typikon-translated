## May 8. Holy Apostle and Evangelist John the Theologian[^59]

*Commemoration of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostle and Evangelist, the beloved bosom friend, the virgin, John the Theologian; and of our venerable father Arsenius the Great.*

*At Small Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Yegda ot dreva):*
Come, ye faithful, let us today crown with divine hymns the depth of wisdom and the writer of Orthodox dogmas, the glorious and beloved John; for he thundered forth: *In the beginning was the Word.* Whence he was shown to be loud-voiced, shining as a herald of glad tidings unto the world, most sighted and all-praised.

*Sticheron:*
In truth thou wast shown to be a true friend and great bosom companion unto Christ the Teacher: for leaning upon His breast, from thence thou didst draw forth the dogmas of wisdom, wherewith thou dost enrich the whole earth all around, as God's divine speaker. Therefore Christ's Church, holding these beautiful [teachings], now rejoices in gladness.

*Sticheron:*
Rejoice, O Theologian in truth; rejoice, all-beloved son of the Lord's Mother! For standing beside Christ's Cross, thou didst hear the Master's divine voice, crying unto thee: *Behold now thy mother.* Whence worthily all of us call thee blessed, as a great and beloved apostle of Christ.
