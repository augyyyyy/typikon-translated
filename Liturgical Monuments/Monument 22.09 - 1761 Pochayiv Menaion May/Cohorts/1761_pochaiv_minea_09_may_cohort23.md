## May 29. The Holy Venerable Martyr Theodosia the Virgin[^411]

*Stichera, Tone 8, Podoben: "O Most Marvelous Wonder" (Ѽ пресла́внагѡ чꙋдесѐ):*

*Sticheron 1:* Shining with the radiance of virginity, O venerable Theodosia, and brightly vested in the purple dye of the blood of martyrdom, O all-wise maiden; and having entered into the transcendent bridal chamber of Christ, O all-immaculate maiden, thou rejoicest with the angelic ranks, in truth holding an indestructible festival, O glorious one.

*Sticheron 2:* O virgin martyr, all-lauded Theodosia, having desired Christ with thy whole soul, thou didst endure the wounds of torture, gallantly struck for the sake of thy Lover, and scraped with iron claws along thy ribs. O thy struggles and steadfast resistance, by which thou didst truly cast down the proud one unto the earth!

*Sticheron 3:* Brightly adorned with the beauty of both soul and body, thou didst offer thyself unto Christ, desiring to be crowned by Him with a crown of glory, O all-glorious martyr Theodosia, and worthily to receive upon thy head the truly precious diadem of the Kingdom, having been shown forth as an incorrupt bride, O divinely wise one.

*Stichera of the Theotokos, Tone 8, Same Podoben (Подо́бенъ то́йже):*

*Theotokion 1:* Rejoice, enlightenment of souls, forgiveness of those who sin, amendment of the negligent; rejoice, consolation and joy, helper of the afflicted; rejoice, good one, healing of infirm souls; rejoice, reconciliation with God and mediatress, as thou art holier than all the Seraphim, O pure one.

*Theotokion 2:* Taking flesh from thy blood, the pre-eternal God showed thee forth as protectress unto mankind, O pure one. Therefore deliver thy servants from every calamity and distress, and from the snares of the crafty enemy; and vouchsafe all who glorify and bow down before thee to partake of the radiance of the elect.

*Theotokion 3:* Save me, O most pure Sovereign Lady, who didst ineffably bear the Savior Christ; for thee alone have I acquired as protectress, an invincible rampart, a shelter, joy, and divine consolation of my soul. Save me, deliver me who am wretched from the evil worm and from eternal fire, as thou art the Mother of Christ God:

=== LEAF p222 === (Fol. 121v)

That I may magnify thee with joyful voices, O pure one, and glorify the deep of thy love for mankind. Save me from calamities, and pluck me from the soul-corrupting arrows of the invisible and all-crafty enemy; for against him I set thee forth, O Bride of God, as a firm and invincible weapon, O Mother of Christ God.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

"What is this sight beheld by mine eyes, O Master, Thou Who holdest all creation, lifted up upon the Tree, and dying, Thou Who givest life unto all?" the Theotokos weeping said, when she beheld Him Who ineffably shone forth from her, God and man, lifted up upon the Cross.

*Troparion, Tone 4:*

Acceptably hast thou attained unto virtue, O martyr, and becamest a pure receptacle of Christ and a beloved dwelling of the Holy Spirit; whence thou didst put to shame the enemy who wars against the human race, having suffered valiantly, and thou didst cut down Copronymus[^412] with the sword of faith, O Theodosia; and rejoicing thou hast passed over unto the heavenly bridal chamber. For those who honor thee with faith, entreat great mercy.

*Canon of the Saint, whose acrostic is: "To the martyr Theodosia I sing praise":*[^413]

*Ode 1, Heirmos:* Israel of old, traversing with dryshod feet the Red Sea deep, by Moses' hands stretched out crosswise conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Troparion 1:* God became for thee the path of suffering, O Theodosia, He Who willingly ascended the Cross and cast down the tormentor, causing him to be mocked like a bird through thy torments.

*Troparion 2:* With mighty strength, O all-honored Theodosia, having suffered stoutly upon earth, O all-lauded one, thou didst dash to pieces the prince of delusion; and as a trophy-bearer, O martyr, thou didst receive the crown of glory.

*Troparion 3:* He who of old possessed great boastfulness is now cast down, confounded by thy patient endurance, O martyr; for having received the power of the Cross, thou didst put him to shame in his boundless raging.

*Theotokion:* The serpent craftily deceived me, took me captive, and cast into me the desire to become a god; but having been called back through thee, O most pure one, I am in truth immutably deified.

*Ode 3, Heirmos:* Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying aloud: Thou art my strength, O Lord, my refuge, and my firm foundation!

*Troparion 1:* Divine desire showed thee forth as an honored bride, O Theodosia, a martyr of Christ, having loved His Cross.

*Troparion 2:* Having forsaken the corruptible provision of the flesh, O venerable one, thou livest by the life-creating Spirit, O all-glorious Theodosia.

*Troparion 3:* Heal my mind by thy grace that is full of divine joy, O honored one, and cure my soul which is paralyzed by passions.

*Theotokion:* Thou didst raise up my fallen form, O pure one, having given birth to the Prince of the resurrection of us all, O Sovereign Lady.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "Of Wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости):*

By the streams of thy blood having truly submerged all the strength of the crafty devil, O glorious one, thou didst pass through the immense gulf of torments unharmed. And thou hast manifestly settled in the holy land, worthily receiving the recompense of thy labors, and wast vouchsafed the entrance of the heavenly bridal chamber, O all-lauded Theodosia. Entreat Christ God to grant remission of sins unto those who with love celebrate thy holy memory.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Let us hymn the heavenly gate and the ark, the all-holy mountain, the bright cloud, the unburnt bush, the noetic paradise, the recall of Eve, the great treasure of the whole universe; for salvation was wrought in her for the world, and remission of ancient transgressions. Therefore we cry unto her: Pray unto thy Son to grant remission of sins unto those who piously bow down before thy most holy childbearing.

*Stavrotheotokion:*

Beholding the Lamb, the Shepherd and Redeemer upon the Cross, the ewe-lamb wept and cried out, and bitterly lamenting exclaimed: "The world indeed rejoices, receiving redemption through Thee, but my womb burns, beholding Thy crucifixion which Thou endurest in Thy tender mercy, O long-suffering Lord, abyss of mercy and inexhaustible wellspring! Have compassion, and grant remission of transgressions unto those who hymn Thy divine Passion with faith!"

*Ode 4, Heirmos:* Beholding Thee, the Sun of righteousness, lifted up upon the Cross, the Church stood in her station, worthily crying aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion 1:* The tormentor raged, wishing in his wretchedness to terrify the strength of thy patience with wounds; but thou, O all-lauded one, didst cry aloud: "Glory to my God!"

*Troparion 2:* Thou wast radiantly enriched with a God-given and divine name,[^414] O passion-bearing martyr; for thou becamest a divine gift rejoicing us who honor thee with faith and love.

## May 30. Our Venerable Father Isaac, of the Dalmatian Monastery[^416]

*Stichera, Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the Heavenly Ranks" (Небє́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*

*Sticheron 1:* Beholding the beauty of paradise, and richly enjoying its incorrupt flowers, thou didst blossom forth the divine understanding unto the world; and partaking thereof with spiritual love, O venerable father, we nurture salvation for our souls.

*Sticheron 2:* Having hedged about thy body with abstinence, O father, by prayers and vigils thou didst mortify the stirrings of passion. Therefore the spiritual power that overshadowed thee showed thee forth as a noetic luminary unto the whole world.

*Sticheron 3:* Thou didst illumine the monastic life, O father, shining forth like the sun from the wilderness upon the imperial city; therefore by thy teachings thou didst enlighten the faithful to bless the consubstantial Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

*Stichera of the Theotokos, Tone 1, Same Podoben (Подо́бенъ то́йже):*

*Theotokion 1:* Beset by desperate falls, O pure one, having thee as our only protectress, O Virgin, we cry out gratefully: "Purify us, O all-holy Bride of God; for thou art the refuge of the world and the help of our race!"

*Theotokion 2:* By the staff of thy protection, O pure Mother of God, quickly drive away the beast-like passions of my wretched soul, peacefully guiding my life; and number me with the holy flock of thine elect sheep.

*Theotokion 3:* Overwhelmed by the deep of sins, fleeing unto the tranquil haven of thy most pure prayers, O Mother of God, I cry unto thee: "Save me, reaching forth thy sovereign right hand..."
