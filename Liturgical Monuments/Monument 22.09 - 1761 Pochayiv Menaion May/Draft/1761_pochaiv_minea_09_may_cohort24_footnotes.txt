[^422]: Joseph the Hymnographer (ca. 816–886), prolific Byzantine hymnographer and poet of the Studite tradition, author of numerous canons in the Menaion.
[^423]: An allusion to the Passion of the Holy Martyr Hermias of Comana (Cappadocia, martyred under Antoninus Pius, ca. 166 AD), whose tortures included having his teeth knocked out and the skin flayed from his face.
[^424]: Melodic model: *Вознесы́йся на Кре́стъ во́лею* (*Voznesyisya na Krest voleyu* / *Ὁ ὑψωθεὶς ἐν τῷ Σταυρῷ ἑκουσίως*), the celebrated Kontakion of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross.
[^425]: According to the hagiographical account, Saint Hermias was cast into a glowing fiery furnace, wherein he remained three days unburned and unharmed, praising God.
[^426]: According to the *Synaxarion*, an unnamed pagan sorcerer was hired to prepare a lethal poison for Hermias. When the poison caused the saint no harm, the sorcerer compounded an even stronger draft, which likewise had no effect; astonished by the miracle, the sorcerer renounced idolatry, confessed Christ, and was immediately beheaded, receiving martyrdom before Hermias.
[^427]: Hermias was subjected to severe bodily tortures, including the excision of his tendons and sinews.
[^428]: Melodic model: *Е́же ѡ на́съ испо́лнивъ смотре́нїе* (*Ezhe o nas ispolniv smotreniye* / *Τὴν ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν πληρώσας οἰκονομίαν*), the automelon Kontakion of the Feast of the Ascension of our Lord.
[^429]: An echo of the Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed: *распя́таго же за ны при Понті́йстѣмъ Пила́тѣ* (*crucifixus etiam pro nobis sub Pontio Pilato* / *σταυρωθέντα τε ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν ἐπὶ Ποντίου Πιλάτου*).
[^430]: An ornate baroque woodcut tailpiece produced at the Pochaiv Lavra printshop, marking the completion of the liturgical services for the month of May.
[^431]: The catchword *Бгородичны* (*Theotokia*) at the foot of fol. 118r references the standard supplementary appendices of generic Dismissal Theotokia (*Theotokia apolytikia*) frequently appended to Menaion volumes, which were omitted or left unprinted in this copy where fol. 118v remains blank.
