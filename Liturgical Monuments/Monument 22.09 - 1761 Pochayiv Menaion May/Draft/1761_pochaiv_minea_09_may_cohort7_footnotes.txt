[^68]: Christ bestowed upon St. John and his brother James the surname *Boanerges*, which is 'Sons of Thunder' (Mark 3:17), prefiguring St. John's thundering declaration of the pre-eternal divinity of the Logos.

[^70]: A direct quotation of John 1:17: 'For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.'
[^71]: An allusion to Isaiah 6:6–7, where one of the Seraphim touched the prophet's lips with a live coal taken with tongs from off the altar, cleansing his iniquity and fitting him for prophetic ministry.
[^72]: A direct citation of John 1:18: 'No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.'
[^73]: An allusion to the *Apophthegmata Patrum* (*Arsenius* 30), recounting that Abba Arsenius would stand from Saturday evening in vigil with arms outstretched toward heaven until the morning sun shone upon his face on Sunday.

[^75]: An allusion to the *Apophthegmata Patrum* (*Arsenius* 18), where St. Arsenius, having formerly used sweet perfumes in the imperial palace, mortified his senses by working with palm fronds steeped in water that had grown fetid, offering it as an ascetic counter-balance.

[^77]: A reference to the miraculous vision in the *Apophthegmata Patrum* (*Arsenius* 27), wherein a brother peering through the window of Abba Arsenius's cell saw the venerable elder standing wholly transformed into fire like a flame.
[^78]: Psalm 44:1 (LXX): 'Mine heart hath poured forth a good word' (*Ѿры́гнꙋ се́рдце моѐ сло́во бл҃го*), patristically interpreted as the unoriginate generation of the Son from the Father.
