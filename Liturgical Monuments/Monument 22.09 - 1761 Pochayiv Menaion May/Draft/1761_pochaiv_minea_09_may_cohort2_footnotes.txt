Fol. 6r opens abruptly with the third Sticheron at the Praises for St. Athanasius (*By the radiance of thy divine words...*), whereas Fol. 7 contains the preceding portion of Matins (Canon Odes 7–9 and Praises Stichera 1–2). [^13]: The printed rubric abbreviation *прⷣ* indicates that the preceding stichera are of the Afterfeast of Mid-Pentecost, while the Doxasticon *Glory* is dedicated to St. Athanasius.
[^14]: St. Theodosius of the Caves († 1074) is commemorated on May 3 (his falling asleep) and August 14 (the translation of his relics).
