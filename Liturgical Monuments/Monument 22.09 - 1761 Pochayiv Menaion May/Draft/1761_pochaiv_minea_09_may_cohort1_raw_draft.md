# MONTH OF MAY
## May 1. Holy Prophet Jeremiah

*The month of May has 31 days.*

*On the 1st day: the Holy Prophet Jeremiah.*

*Stichera of the Prophet, Tone 8, Podoben: "Lord, Even if Before the Judgment Seat" (Гдⷭ҇и а́ще и на суди́щи):*

O Lord, before he was formed, Thou didst foreknow glorious Jeremiah, and before birth from the womb Thou didst sanctify him as a prophet, as truly foreknowing the freedom of his mind; by his prayers, as Thou art merciful, save our souls.

O Lord, even though holy Jeremiah was cast into the mire,[^1] yet noetically did he receive lovely fragrance, as though taking up his abode in the mansions of Paradise; by his prayers, as Benefactor, preserve us all.

O Lord, shining with Thy most radiant beams, the prophet was seen to be honorable and blessed, even by adversaries; for the foe is openly put to shame by virtues; and the things to come he foreknew as present risings.

*Glory... Both now... of the feast, or Theotokion, in the same Tone, Podoben: "The same Tone and Podoben" (Гла́съ и҆ подо́бенъ то́йже):*

Descend, O Bride of God, unto the sighing of my contrite heart; receive, O pure Virgin Mary, the lifting up of my hands, as thou art loving toward mankind, and cast me not away, O all-immaculate one; that I may sing and magnify thee who hast magnified our race.

*Stavrotheotokion, Podoben: "The same" (то́йже):*

O beginningless Word of the Father, and my Son, enthroned together with the Spirit: how hast Thou stretched out Thy most pure hands upon the Wood? What is this Thy great poverty now, O most Good? Thus cried the all-immaculate one, standing before Thy Cross.

*Troparion, Tone 2:*

Traversing the water as dry land, and having escaped the Egyptian evil, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Deliverer and God.

*Troparia:*

Foreknown before thy creation, thou wast sanctified from the maternal womb, and wast anointed a prophet unto the nations, crying aloud: Let us sing unto our Deliverer and God.

Having beforehand received understanding of these things, and foreseeing the movements of thy mind, O Jeremiah manifest of God, He establishes thee as a guide unto the people.

Having cleansed the perceptive faculty of thy mind from carnal defilements, thou wast shown forth as a witness of truth, being exceedingly enriched by thy Creator.

*Theotokion:*

Having taken up His abode within thee, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, and having beyond nature given birth in the flesh unto the Word of the Father upon earth, thou hast appeared as Sovereign Lady of all creation.

*Troparia:*

Having sanctified Thy prophet before his birth, O Master, Thou didst send him as a guide unto them that were astray.

The grace of the Spirit taking up its abode upon thy lips, like a most radiant luminary, shone forth upon them that are in darkness.

Unable to endure the myrrh of thy fragrance, the vile assembly of the Israelites[^2] shut thee in a pit.

*Theotokion:*

Through thee, O all-pure one, the supersubstantial and beginningless Son of God was well-pleased to dwell together with mankind.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "Having Risen from the Tomb" (Воскр҃се и҆з̾ гро́ба):*

God inclined His ear unto thee to hear His words, O God-proclaiming prophet; therefore, as present, thou didst make clear unto all things that were not yet. Beholding the Lamb and Shepherd hanging upon the Cross, the undefiled ewe-lamb cried aloud: O my Child, what is this strange and unexpected sight? *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

*Troparia:*

The most radiant illumination of the All-Holy and Divine Spirit, flashing upon thy heart, wrought enlightenment for them that are in darkness.

Having suffered in sympathy more than all the prophets, O all-blessed one accepted of God: vouchsafe mercy unto me who am in despair through many transgressions.

Having become a mediator by evident and divine illumination, by thy mediation calm the storm of my turbulent life.

*Theotokion:*

Entreat God Whom thou didst bear, O all-pure Virgin, by thy prayers to grant forgiveness of transgressions.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Enlighten us by Thy commandments, O Lord, and by Thy high arm grant Thy peace unto us, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparia:*

As lightning Thou didst send Thy prophet, consecrated Jeremiah, unto them that are in darkness, announcing the brilliant things of Christ.

As a chosen arrow hidden in a quiver, O Jeremiah, by His foreknowledge thy Master showed thee forth in the times that were fitting.

Possessing pure spiritual ears, O glorious one, when the Spirit conversed with thee, thou wast vouchsafed to listen, O prophet.

*Theotokion:*

Great and awesome is the mystery of the Virgin: for heaven, having shown forth God unto mankind through her on earth, maternally nurtured Him as an Infant.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The abyss of sins and the storm of transgressions troubles me, and plunges me into the depth of violent despair; but stretch forth Thy strong hand unto me as unto Peter, O Pilot, and save me.

*Troparia:*

Thou didst draw forth enlightenment and grace richly, O God-bearer, having broadened the spiritual capacities of the soul; and filling them, thou hast communally granted radiance unto all who desire it, O prophet.

The sea of this world being tossed up, the turbulent surges of afflictions...

*Theotokion:*

The Word Who was begotten bodiless of the Father before the ages, is born in the flesh in time from thee, O pure one; and in His shadow shall we all live, as Jeremiah prophesied of old.[^3]

*Kontakion, Tone 2, Podoben: "Today the Virgin" (Дв҃а дне́сь):*

Having cleansed thy light-bearing heart by the Spirit, O great prophet and martyr, glorious Jeremiah, thou didst receive the gift of prophecy from on high, and didst cry aloud with a great voice among the nations: This is our God, and none other shall be compared unto Him, Who, having become incarnate, appeared upon the earth.[^4]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Hebrew children in the furnace boldly trampled upon the flame, and turned the fire into dew, crying aloud: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages.

*Troparia:*

Thou art now no longer in hopes, but beholding in deed the fulfillment of the things spoken by thee, O prophet, thou criest aloud: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages.

A divine instrument thou wast shown to be, struck by the hand of the Comforter, uttering faithful voices, and singing: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages.

Writing down thy lamentations, O prophet, thou didst not darken divine gladness, which from infancy thou didst clearly enjoy, crying: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages.

*Theotokion:*

To enter into the womb of the Virgin, as Thou wast well-pleased, desiring to save mankind, Thou didst clearly teach the prophets of the wonder: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Having become conquerors over the tyrant and the flame by Thy grace, cleaving exceedingly unto Thy commandments, the children cried aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord.

*Troparia:*

Into the heavens hast thou ascended rejoicing, O God-inspired Jeremiah, and with the bodiless hosts thou standest before the throne of the King of all, singing: Bless the Lord, all ye works, and exalt Him above all unto the ages.

Having become a preacher of piety, O all-lauded and consecrated one, thou didst rebuke the weakness of impiety, O Jeremiah: Bless the Lord, all ye works, and exalt Him above all unto the ages.

My nature the Giver of life received from thee, and cleansed it from former defilements, O Mother of God, Whom we hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages.

*Theotokion:*

Knowing thee to be a haven of salvation, who believe in thy most pure birthgiving, we pray thee to be delivered from afflictions.

## May 2. St. Athanasius the Great

*On the same month, on the 2nd day: Our Father among the Saints Athanasius the Great, Patriarch of Alexandria.*

*At "Lord, I have cried," stichera on 6, Tone 4, Podoben: "Being Called from on High" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*

Vouchsafed immortal life, when thou didst preach the consubstantial and co-eternal Son and Word, Christ the Giver of life, unto the beginningless Father and Begetter: then with thy fiery tongue, O glorious one, thou didst burn up the substance of heresies, having driven...

=== LEAF p6 === (Fol. 3v)
...having driven Arius away from the Church of Christ. And with divine words thou didst enlighten, O wise one, the hearts of the pious to worship the Trinity in one essence.

Like a river of God, full of spiritual waters, thou wast shown forth, O most marvelous one, overflowing with divine teachings, and watering the entire plenitude of the Church, but drowning the council of heretics in the deep of thy divine words, O father, and in the depth of thy understandings. For thou, O most wise, didst preach Christ as consubstantial with the Begetting Father, O good, divine, and most wealthy Athanasius.

As a mystic teacher and divine hierarch, thou didst endure perils and persecutions patiently, and slander, O blessed one, and long-lasting banishments for the sake of the faith of Christ. Clearly didst thou preach Christ as consubstantial, co-eternal, equal in operation, and co-beginningless with the Primal and Co-beginningless Father. Therefore He Himself adorned thee manifestly with most radiant teachings, and showed thee forth most radiant unto the whole world.

*At the Aposticha, Tone 5, Podoben: "O Venerable Father" (Преподо́бне ѻ҆́тче):*

O venerable father, God-manifest Athanasius, from infancy thou wast known as a luminary of the Church, shining with most radiant visions, and enriched in every manner of virtues; with fire-breathed words thou didst burn up heresies, while guiding all the faithful by the most divine radiance of the Trinity, understood in one Godhead and possessing co-eternal glory: entreat Her, and pray unto Her evermore, to grant oneness of mind, peace, and great mercy unto the world.

O venerable father, God-speaking Athanasius, being aptly named after immortality,[^7] by its mediating path didst thou walk, being manifestly enriched with the splendor of life; having acquired a pure mind, O God-wise one, thou wast found a champion of the Word, and didst breathe according to the All-Holy Spirit, enlightened by Whom thou didst conquer thine enemies, protected by Whom thou wast shown to be invincible, and deified by Whom thou art a partaker thereof, O holy one: ask for all that there be granted oneness of mind, peace, and great mercy.

O venerable father, God-proclaimer Athanasius, having endured perils and frequent afflictions, thou hast now been vouchsafed the delights of Paradise, having entered within the divine mansions; thou hast drawn near unto the tree of incorruptible life...
=== LEAF p7 === (Fol. 4r)
...as a victor, adorned with a righteous crown, O blessed one, and patterned with the diadem of the kingdom on high, and shining with beauty. Pray unto Christ, entreat Christ, O God-wise one, to grant oneness of mind, peace, and great mercy unto the Church.

*Glory... Tone 6:*

Let us all hymn Athanasius, the hierarch of Christ, as having abolished all the teachings of Arius, and clearly proclaiming in all the world the dominion of the Holy Trinity, one God in three persons undivided; Whom he also entreats on behalf of us who in faith celebrate his memory.

*Both now... of the feast.*

*At the Aposticha, of the Triodion. Glory of the Saint, Tone 3. By Germanus:*

Once again the gold-flowing Nile, who is named after immortality,[^8] having arrived in his yearly memory, by fair risings overflows with gold-streaming outpourings of immortality, bearing the fruit of incorruptible food by lofty divine speech; he teaches us to worship the undivided dominion of the Trinity, and pours forth divine teachings, watering the thoughts of the faithful, and prays for our souls.

*Both now... of the feast.*

*Troparion, Tone 3:*

A pillar of Orthodoxy wast thou, confirming the Church with divine dogmas, O hieromartyr Athanasius; for having preached the Son as consubstantial with the Father, thou didst put Arius to shame, O venerable father: entreat Christ God to grant great mercy unto us.

*Canon of the feast, with the Heirmos on 6; and of the Saint, on 6, the acrostic of which is: "Athanasius is the praise of Orthodoxy." A composition of Theophanes. Tone 8.*  
*Ode 1. Heirmos:*

The rod of Moses, working a wonder of old, striking the sea crosswise and dividing it, drowned Pharaoh the chariot-driver, but saved fleeing Israel on foot, as they sang a song unto God.

*Troparia:*

Unto Athanasius I offer praise, and as praising virtue, unto God rather do I offer praise: from Whom is granted unto men the praiseworthy gift of virtue, of which he was a living image and seal.

Full of God-wisdom wast thou, and brighter than the sun by an angelic life, O blessed one; shining forth now, O venerable one, thou hast surpassed the laws of praises that come from us: yet accept, O father, even if it be not according to worthiness, this praise.

As is right, then, we weave a song in thy glorious memory...

=== LEAF p8 === (Fol. 4v)
...memory: but falling short of greater things, O Athanasius, worthy of thy songs, we pray that forgiveness be granted unto us, and ungrudging grace of the Spirit through thee.

In youth thou didst mortify the leaping of carnal passions, having in thy youth acquired the wisdom of elders, well-ordered, O blessed Athanasius: for by desire of wisdom thou didst set aright all thy senses.

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear Him Who was incarnate for our sake, O all-immaculate one, Who before was bodiless, the beginningless Word of the Father, O virginal boast, receptacle of purity, and temple of cleansing, Sovereign Lady of the world, Bride of God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who in the beginning didst establish the heavens with understanding, and didst found the earth upon the waters: establish me, O Christ, upon the rock of Thy commandments; for there is none holy save Thee, O Thou only Lover of mankind.

*Troparia:*

Having cleansed both soul and body from every defilement, O Athanasius, thou wast shown forth as a temple worthy of God: therefore the plenitude of the Trinity rested within thee, O most rich sacred initiate.

Having found thy soul cleansed from passions, as the grace of the Comforter desired, It manifestly shows forth efficacious works within it, and appoints thee, O father, as a most radiant luminary unto the world.

A rule of the hierarchate wast thou shown forth unto hierarchs, and a depiction of active life: while thy word, O most wise one, is clearly an image of contemplation, and the definition of thy theology was shown forth as teaching.

*Theotokion:*

Higher than the Cherubim and Seraphim hast thou appeared, O Birthgiver of God: for thou alone didst receive the uncontainable God in thy womb, O undefiled one. Therefore all we the faithful bless thee in hymns, O pure one.

*After Ode 3, Kontakion and Ikos of the feast. Then the Sessional Hymn of the Saint, Tone 8, Podoben: "Guiding unto Wisdom" (Прему́дрости):*

Having learned the words of wisdom, and overthrown the deceit of Arius, thou didst preach the Orthodox faith unto the world. For thou didst truly make clear that Christ, our Life, the Word of the Father, is enthroned together with Him. Therefore with one mind the Church glorifies the consubstantial Trinity in the unity of the Godhead, O God-bearer Athanasius: entreat Christ God to grant forgiveness of sins unto them that with love honor thy holy memory.

*Glory... Both now... of the feast.*

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou art my strength, O Lord, Thou art my power, Thou art my God...

...my rejoicing, not leaving the bosom of the Father, and visiting our poverty. Therefore with the Prophet Habakkuk I cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparia:*

Into thee the life-bearing breath from on high breathed, which first came in God-befitting manner upon the Spirit of Christ in the upper room and filled the disciples, showing thee forth, O thrice-holy father, as an apostle preaching the Orthodox faith.

Being vehemently kindled with divine zeal, thou didst assist the Council, and before thou wast made a primate, in Nicaea thou didst preach the consubstantiality; therefore Christ appoints thee as chief shepherd and teacher of the Church.

Possessing lips as the rudders of the Church by divine providence, O Athanasius, as with an axe thou didst hew down all the soul-corrupting babblings of the heretics, uprooting them like thorns; and watering the rational seed, thou didst cultivate it in faith.

By thy fiery tongue the akin division of Arius and the confusion of Sabellius were burnt up, which from madness are evil and equally impious, and all the heretical blasphemies together with them, O Athanasius.

*Theotokion:*

A new Adam in place of the first wast Thou from the Virgin, a Deliverer and Savior of all in place of the foremother, and true immortal Life in place of death. Therefore knowing her who bare Thee to be the Birthgiver of God, worthily do we bless her, O pure one.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy presence, O Light unwaning, and alien darkness has covered me, the wretched one? But convert me, and direct my paths unto the light of Thy commandments, I pray Thee.

*Troparia:*

Having Christ speaking in thee, O father, through the sweet-sounding organ of thy tongue, thou didst put to shame the most insane heresy of idols,[^9] guiding those who had gone astray, and turning them unto the true God, O Athanasius.

All destructive heresies thou didst refute, O father, by words and writings, driving diverse deceit out of the world by wise demonstrations, and making plain incontrovertibly the Orthodox faith, O blessed one.

Truly as a great teacher, a teacher of many teachers, O Athanasius, the Church of Christ falsely does not proclaim thee, O father, but reckons thee with the Apostles, as making manifest the blameless word of faith.

*Theotokion:*

Having acquired maternal boldness toward thy Son, O all-pure one...

...pure one, despise not our prayer for thy akin providence toward us; for unto the Master we Christians favorably offer thee as our only cleansing.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine iniquities, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and give ear unto me, O God of my salvation.

*Troparia:*

Apollinarius having appeared as a sower of strange seed, depriving Christ of a rational soul and mind,[^10] was strongly refuted by thee, O all-blessed one, as foolish and corrupted in mind.

The diamond strength[^11] of thy God-inspired words, O blessed one accepted of God, like a sound of thunder clearly resounded in the ears of heretics, and blackened their faces.

Every master of eloquence, lovingly embracing in thy writings what was wisely composed according to the Spirit, and foreseeing the budding heresies that would arise, prophetically turned from them by thy guidance.

*Theotokion:*

The Only-begotten Son, invisible before the ages, in the last days became the first-born, visible in the flesh from thee, O Mother of God, the God of my salvation.

*Kontakion, Tone 2, Podoben: "In the Streams of Thy Blood" (Крове́й твои́хъ):*

Having planted the teachings of Orthodoxy, thou didst cut down the thorns of false glory, multiplying the seed of faith with the rain of the Spirit, O venerable one: therefore we hymn thee, O Athanasius.

*Ikos:*

Pouring forth torrents of thy words, divine teachings unto the world, thou didst water the souls of the faithful, O Athanasius; and thou didst drown the champions of heresy like other Egyptians. Having written for us the God-inscribed law of Orthodoxy, showing forth that the Trinity is to be honored in one Godhead, by Which preserve us from invisible enemies, O deliverer: therefore we hymn thee, O Athanasius.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The fire was put to shame of old by God's condescension in Babylon; therefore the children in the furnace, dancing with joyful step as in a meadow of flowers, sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.

*Troparia:*

Having Orthodoxly preached the Word as equally honored and enthroned together with the Father, the Only-begotten Son, O father named after immortality, straightway thou teachest that the Spirit is enthroned together, consubstantial, and co-eternal.

As a sacredly-manifest teacher and preacher, proclaiming the Orthodox faith brighter than the light of the sun, He granted thee unto the ends of the earth, as a silencer of impiety...
