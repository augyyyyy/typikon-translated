### The 8th Day. The Nativity of our Most Holy Lady Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary.[^143]

4. At Great Vespers, following the Introductory Psalm [Psalm 103 LXX] and the First Stasis of the Psalter [Kathisma 1: *Blessed is the man*], at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we sing 6 Idiomela Stichira: **"Today God Who resteth upon the spiritual thrones"** (*Σήμερον ὁ τοῖς νοεροῖς θρόνοις*); **"Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit"**, **"Both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen"**: the same Idiomelon: **"Today God Who resteth upon the spiritual thrones"**. Entrance with the censer: **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), the Prokeimenon of the day, and the Old Testament Readings [Gen. 28:10–17; Ezek. 43:27–44:4; Prov. 9:1–11]. At the Aposticha: the 3 Idiomela Stichira: **"The universal joy"** (*Ἡ παγκόσμιος χαρά*), etc.; **"Glory"**, **"Both now"**: **"Come, all ye faithful"** (*Δεῦτε ἅπαντες πιστοί*). The Dismissal Troparion: **"Thy Nativity, O Theotokos Virgin"** (*Ἡ γέννησίς σου Θεοτόκε*, Tone 4) thrice, and Dismissal.

5. At Matins: after **"Blessed is our God"** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*), the Trisagion, and Psalm 50 LXX, the Liti is sung; then the Trisagion again, the Dismissal Troparion, and the Hexapsalmos. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparion thrice; the Psalter and the Polyeleos: **"My heart overfloweth with a good word"** (*Ἐξηρεύξατο ἡ καρδία μου λόγον ἀγαθόν*, Psalm 44 LXX). Then **"Let every breath"** (*Πᾶσα πνοή*), and the Matins Gospel: **"Having arisen, Mary went into the hill country"** (*Ἀναστᾶσα δὲ Μαριάμ*, Luke 1:39–49, 56); Psalm 50 LXX (recited); **"Glory"**: **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos, O Merciful One, blot out the multitude of our offenses"** (*Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου πρεσβείαις*); **"Both now"**: repeat the same hymn. Then the verse: **"Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy"** (*Ἐλέησόν με, ὁ Θεός*, Ps. 50:1 LXX), and the Idiomelon: **"This is the day of the Lord: rejoice, ye peoples!"** (*Αὕτη ἡ ἡμέρα Κυρίου*). Both Canons of the Feast are sung with the verse: **"Most Holy Theotokos, save us"** (*Ὑπεραγία Θεοτόκε, σῶσον ἡμᾶς*). After the Third Ode: the Hypakoe: **"The closed gate"** (*Πύλην κεκλεισμένην*); after the Sixth Ode: the Kontakion: **"Joachim and Anna were freed from the reproach of childlessness"** (*Ἰωακεὶμ καὶ Ἄννα*), the Ikos, and the Synaxarion from the Menaion. The Katavasiai: **"Moses, having etched the cross with his rod"** (*Σταυρὸν χαράξας*); and the Ninth Ode is sung with its proper festal refrain and the Katavasia: **"A mystical paradise art Thou, O Theotokos"** (*Μυστικὸς εἶ Θεοτόκε*); then the two Exapostilaria. At the Praises: the Prosomia Stichira in 4; **"Glory"**, **"Both now"**: **"This is the day of the Lord"** (*Αὕτη ἡ ἡμέρα Κυρίου*); the Great Doxology, and the Dismissal Troparion.
