# Month of April: Holy Glorious Great-Martyr George the Trophy-Bearer (Situation I Concl. §§ 2–3, General Rubric)

## Τῇ ΚΓ΄: Τοῦ Ἁγίου Γεωργίου τοῦ Τροπαιοφόρου (Περίπτωσις Α΄ Τέλος §§ 2–3, Γενικὴ Διάταξις) [Violakis, p. 506]

μετὰ τὴν Συναπτὴν οἱ Ἀναβαθμοί, τὸ α΄ Ἀντίφωνον τοῦ δ΄ ἤχου, τὸ Προκείμενον Δικαίως ὡς φοῖνιξ ἀνθήσει, τὸ Πᾶσα πνοὴ καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον Προσέχετε ἀπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων, Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ ἅπαξ, καὶ εὐθὺς Δόξα, Ταῖς τοῦ Ἀθλοφόρου, Καὶ νῦν, Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου, ὁ Στίχος Ἐλέησόν με ὁ Θεὸς καὶ τὸ Ἰδιόμελον Τοῦ μεγάλου Βασιλέως (ζήτει εἰς τὴν Λιτήν)· [Εἰς τὴν Περίπτωσιν Δ΄ § 11, εἰς τὸ ἴδιον μέρος τῆς ἀκολουθίας, ὁ Βιολάκης ἔχει ὡς Ἰδιόμελον Σήμερον ἡ οἰκουμένη πᾶσα.] Σῶσον ὁ Θεὸς τὸν λαόν σου, Κύριε ἐλέησον ιβ΄, Ἐλέει καὶ οἰκτιρμοῖς, καὶ ὁ Κανὼν τοῦ Πάσχα καὶ ὁ τοῦ Ἁγίου ἀνὰ δ΄ μετὰ Στίχου εἰς μὲν τὸν Ἀναστάσιμον Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ Ἀναστάσει σου Κύριε, εἰς δὲ τὸν τοῦ Ἁγίου Ἅγιε τοῦ Θεοῦ πρέσβευε ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν· ἐν τῷ τέλει ἑκάστης Ὠδῆς ἡ Καταβασία, τὸ Χριστὸς ἀνέστη τρίς, Ἀναστὰς ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἅπαξ, καὶ ἡ Αἴτησις· ἀπὸ γ΄ Ὠδῆς ἡ Ὑπακοὴ τοῦ Πάσχα καὶ τὸ Κοντάκιον {τοῦ Πάσχα}· ἀφ᾿ ς΄ τὸ Κοντάκιον καὶ Οἶκος τοῦ Ἁγίου καὶ τὸ Μηνολόγιον, τὸ Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ γ΄, καὶ τὸ Ἀναστὰς ὁ Ἰησοῦς· εἶτα μετὰ τὴν ζ΄ καὶ η΄ Ὠδὴν ψάλλεται ἡ θ΄ τοῦ Πάσχα μετὰ τῶν Μεγαλυναρίων καὶ ἡ τοῦ Ἁγίου, τὸ Ἐξαποστειλάριον τοῦ Πάσχα, τὸ τοῦ Ἁγίου Ἔαρ ἡμῖν ἐξέλαμψεν, αὖθις τὸ πρῶτον. Εἰς τοὺς Αἴνους, Ἀναστάσιμα δ΄, τοῦ Ἁγίου δ΄ καὶ τὰ Στιχηρὰ Πάσχα ἱερόν, Δόξα, Ἀνέτειλε τὸ ἔαρ, Καὶ νῦν, Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα, τὸ Χριστὸς ἀνέστη τρίς, καὶ ἄρχεται ἡ Λειτουργία.

3.    Εἰς τὴν Λειτουργίαν, τὰ Ἀντίφωνα τοῦ Πάσχα, καὶ τὸ Εἰσοδικὸν Ἐν Ἐκκλησίαις εὐλογεῖτε τὸν Θεόν, τὸ Χριστὸς ἀνέστη, ἡ Ὑπακοὴ Προλαβοῦσαι τὸν Ὄρθρον, τὸ Ἀπολυτίκιον τοῦ Ἁγίου καὶ τὸ Κοντάκιον τοῦ Πάσχα, τὸ Ὅσοι εἰς Χριστὸν ἐβαπτίσθητε, ὁ Ἀπόστολος τοῦ Ἁγίου καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον τῆς ἡμέρας Θεὸν οὐδεὶς ἑώρακε πώποτε. Εἰς τὸ Ἐξαιρέτως Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα, Κοινωνικὸν Εἰς μνημόσυνον¹, Χριστὸς ἀνέστη, καὶ Ἀπόλυσις.

    Κατ᾿ αὐτὸν τὸν τύπον ψάλλεται ἡ Ἀκολουθία τοῦ Ἁγίου, ὅταν συμπέσῃ ἡ μνήμη αὐτοῦ τῇ Τρίτῃ, Τετάρτῃ, Πέμπτῃ καὶ τῷ Σαββάτῳ τῆς Διακαινησίμου· ἐξαιρεῖται δὲ μόνον τὸ ἐν τῷ Ἑσπερινῷ τῆς Κυριακῆς τοῦ Πάσχα ἀναγινωσκόμενον Εὐαγγέλιον, ἀλλ᾿ ἀναγινώσκονται ἐν τῷ Ἑσπερινῷ τὰ Ἀναγνώσματα τοῦ Ἁγίου.

# Month of April: Holy Glorious Great-Martyr George the Trophy-Bearer (Situation One Concl. §§ 2–3, General Rubric)

## April 23: The Holy Glorious Great-Martyr George the Trophy-Bearer (Situation One Concl. §§ 2–3, General Rubric) [Violakis, p. 507]

we chant the Anavathmoi: the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4, the Prokeimenon: **"The righteous shall flourish like a palm tree"** (*Δίκαιος ὡς φοῖνιξ ἀνθήσει*, Ps. 91:13 LXX). Then **"Let every breath"** (*Πᾶσα πνοή*) and the Matins Gospel: **"Beware of men"** (*Προσέχετε ἀπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων*, Luke 21:12–19). Then **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (*Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ θεασάμενοι*) once, and immediately "Glory...": **"Through the intercessions of the Trophy-Bearer"** (*Ταῖς τοῦ Ἀθλοφόρου πρεσβείαις*); "Both now and ever...": **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos"** (*Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου*); the Verse: **"Have mercy on me, O God"** (*Ἐλέησόν με, ὁ Θεός*, Ps. 50 LXX) and the Idiomelon: **"Of the great King"** (*Τοῦ μεγάλου Βασιλέως*, look in the Litiya). [In Situation IV, Paragraph 11, at the same place in the service, Violakis has as the Idiomelon: **"Today the whole world"** (*Σήμερον ἡ οἰκουμένη πᾶσα*)]. Then **"O God, save Thy people"**, **"Lord, have mercy"** (12 times), **"Through the mercy and compassion"**, and the Canon of Pascha and that of the Saint to 4 each, with the Verse for the Resurrection Canon: **"Glory to Thy holy Resurrection, O Lord"**, and for the Canon of the Saint, the Verse: **"Holy one of God, intercede for us"**; at the end of each Ode the Katavasia: **"Christ is risen"** (thrice), **"Jesus having risen from the tomb"** (once), and the Little Litany. After Ode 3, the Hypakoe of Pascha and the Paschal Kontakion. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Saint and the Menologion. Then **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (thrice), and **"Jesus having risen from the tomb"**. After Odes 7 and 8, Ode 9 of Pascha is sung with the Megalynaria and that of the Saint. The Exapostilarion of Pascha, that of the Saint: **"Springtime has shone forth upon us"** (*Ἔαρ ἡμῖν ἐξέλαμψεν*), and again the Exapostilarion of Pascha. At the Praises: 4 Resurrection Stichira, 4 of the Saint, and the Paschal Stichira: **"A sacred Pascha"** (*Πάσχα ἱερόν*); "Glory...": **"Spring has dawned"** (*Ἀνέτειλε τὸ ἔαρ*); "Both now and ever...": **"It is the day of Resurrection"** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*); **"Christ is risen"** (thrice), and the Divine Liturgy begins.

3.    In the Divine Liturgy, the Antiphons of Pascha, and the Entrance Hymn [Eisodikon]: **"In the congregations bless God"** (*Ἐν Ἐκκλησίαις εὐλογεῖτε τὸν Θεόν*, Ps. 67:27 LXX). **"Christ is risen"**, the Hypakoe: **"They who were with Mary"** (*Προλαβοῦσαι τὸν ὄρθρον*), the Dismissal Troparion of the Saint: **"Liberator of captives"** (*Ὡς τῶν αἰχμαλώτων ἐλευθερωτής*), and the Kontakion of Pascha: **"Though Thou didst descend into the tomb"** (*Εἰ καὶ ἐν τάφῳ*). Instead of the Trisagion, **"As many of you as have been baptized into Christ"** (Galatians 3:27), the Epistle of the Saint: Acts 12:1–11, and the Gospel of the day: **"No man hath seen God at any time"** (*Θεὸν οὐδεὶς ἑώρακε πώποτε*, John 1:18–28). At "Especially": **"The Angel cried out to her that is full of grace... Shine, shine"** (*Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα*); Communion Hymn: **"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance"** (*Εἰς μνημόσυνον αἰώνιον*, Ps. 111:6 LXX)¹; **"Christ is risen"**, and Dismissal.

    The service of the Saint is celebrated in this manner when his commemoration falls on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday of Bright Week; however, instead of the Gospel read at the Vespers of Easter Sunday, the Old Testament Lessons of the Saint are read at Vespers.

# Month of April: Holy Glorious Great-Martyr George the Trophy-Bearer (Situation Two §§ 4–6: Bright Friday)

## April 23: The Holy Glorious Great-Martyr George the Trophy-Bearer (Situation Two §§ 4–6: Bright Friday) [Violakis, p. 509]

### SITUATION TWO

4.    If the commemoration [of the Saint] falls on Friday of Bright Week, on Thursday evening, at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant 3 Resurrection Stichira, 3 of the Theotokos, and 4 of the Saint. "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": **"Who can tell of thy mighty deeds, O Pure Spring?"** (*Τίς λαλήσει τὰς δυναστείας σου Πηγή*). Little Entrance, **"O Gladsome Light"**, the Prokeimenon: **"I will love Thee, O Lord, my strength"** (*Ἀγαπήσω σε Κύριε ἡ ἰσχύς μου*, Ps. 17:2 LXX) with its verses, and the Old Testament Lessons of the Saint. At the Aposticha: the Resurrection Stichiron: **"Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior"** (*Τὴν Ἀνάστασίν σου Χριστὲ Σωτήρ*) and the 3 Aposticha Stichira of the Theotokos; "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": **"It is the day of Resurrection"** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*); **"Christ is risen"** (once), the Dismissal Troparion of the Saint, again **"Christ is risen"**, and Dismissal.

5.    On Bright Friday at Matins, the service up to the Canons is sung according to the order in Paragraph 2; then the Canons of Pascha, of the Saint, and of the Theotokos, with the verses: for the Resurrection Canon, **"Glory to Thy holy Resurrection, O Lord"**; for the Canon of the Saint, **"Holy one of God, intercede for us"**; and for the Theotokos, **"Most Holy Theotokos, save us"**. After Ode 3, the Hypakoe: **"They who were with Mary"** (*Προλαβοῦσαι τὸν ὄρθρον*), then the Sessional Hymn of the Saint and that of the Theotokos; after Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Saint, the Menologion, and the Synaxarion commentary of the Theotokos [the Life-Giving Spring]. Then **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (thrice). After Odes 7 and 8, we chant Ode 9 of Pascha with the Megalynaria, that of the Saint, and that of the Theotokos with the verse: **"Most Holy Theotokos, save us"**. The Exapostilaria: of Pascha, of the Saint, and of the Theotokos. At the Praises: 2 Resurrection Stichira, 3 of the Theotokos, 3 of the Saint, and the Paschal Stichira: **"A sacred Pascha"** (*Πάσχα ἱερόν*); "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": **"It is the day of Resurrection"** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*); and **"Christ is risen"** (thrice).

6.    In the Divine Liturgy, the Antiphons and Entrance Hymn [Eisodikon] of Pascha; after the Entrance, **"Christ is risen"**, the Hypakoe, the Dismissal Troparion of the Saint, the Kontakion of the Spring: **"From thy unfailing fount"** (*Ἐξ ἀκενώτου σου πηγῆς*) and that of Pascha: **"Though Thou didst descend into the tomb"** (*Εἰ καὶ ἐν τάφῳ*), the Epistle of the Saint [Acts 12:1–11], and the Gospel of the day [John 2:12–22]. At "Especially": **"The Angel cried out to her that is full of grace... Shine, shine"** (*Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα*); Communion Hymn: **"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance"** (*Εἰς μνημόσυνον αἰώνιον*, Ps. 111:6 LXX)*; **"Christ is risen"**, and Dismissal.

* {It is possible that the same rules that apply to the rest of the week apply here as well: that is, if the church in which the Liturgy is celebrated is dedicated to Saint George, then we sing: **"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance"**, and if not, then we sing the Communion Hymn of Pascha: **"Receive the Body of Christ"** (*Σῶμα Χριστοῦ*).}[^321]
