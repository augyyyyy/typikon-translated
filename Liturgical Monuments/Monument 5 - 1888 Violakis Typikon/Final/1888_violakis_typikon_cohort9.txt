## Concerning the Psalter

**20.** According to the ancient typikon order, the Psalter is chanted in verses (*stichologeitai*) before the Kathismata [Sessional Hymns] of each Stichologia; the Polyeleos (whenever appointed) is chanted before the Kathisma of the third Stichologia of the occurring feast. Now, however, in certain places, by pastoral economy of time, on Sundays and festal days the appointed Kathismata of the Psalter for the day, as well as the Polyeleos (when there is a feast having a Litiya) or the Amomos [Psalm 118 LXX] (when it is an ordinary Sunday with a non-celebrated Saint), are read before Matins. The Polyeleos of Feasts of the Lord consists of Psalm 134 [MT 135] **"Praise the Name of the Lord"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸ ὄνομα Κυρίου*) and Psalm 135 [MT 136] **"Give thanks unto the Lord, for He is good"** (*Ἐξομολογεῖσθε τῷ Κυρίῳ*); for Feasts of the Mother of God, the Polyeleos consists solely of Psalm 44 [MT 45] **"My heart hath poured forth a good word"** (*Ἐξηρεύξατο ἡ καρδία μου*); for celebrated Saints, those who possess an *Eklogarion* find in it the appropriate Polyeleos selections for each feast; otherwise, let them recite Psalms 134 [MT 135] and 135 [MT 136]. Psalm 136 [MT 137] **"By the rivers of Babylon"** (*Ἐπὶ τῶν ποταμῶν Βαβυλῶνος*) is read on Meatfare Sunday and Cheesefare Sunday. On each day, two Kathismata of the Psalter are chanted in verses, and during Great Lent three are chanted; but during the First Week of Great Lent the entire Psalter is completed twice, and from Vespers of Palm Sunday until Holy Wednesday it is completed once. The Psalter is not chanted in verses from Holy Thursday until Saturday of Bright Week.[^82]

## Concerning the Kathismata [Sessional Hymns]

**21.** When a Feast of the Mother of God coincides with a Sunday, Kathismata are chanted at the first and second Stichologiae: two Resurrection Kathismata and one of the feast instead of the Theotokia of the Octoechos. At the third Stichologia, only the Kathismata of the feast are chanted. On Afterfeast Sundays and on Apodoses, two Resurrection Kathismata are chanted again at the first and second Stichologiae, and those of the feast are said instead of the Theotokia. There is no Kathisma of the third Stichologia on those occasions. On commemorations of less solemnly celebrated Saints, after the Resurrection Kathismata and Theotokia of the first and second Stichologiae, the Kathismata of the third Stichologia of the Saint are chanted; but on commemorations of more solemnly celebrated Saints having a Litiya and Polyeleos Kathismata—such as the Apostles, the Forerunner, the Three...

## Concerning the Evlogitaria, the Hypakoe, the Gradual Antiphons, and the Prokimenon

**22.** Every Sunday, after the Kathismata [Sessional Hymns], the Resurrectional Evlogitaria (**"Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes"** [*Εὐλογητὸς εἶ, Κύριε*]) are chanted; then the Little Litany is intoned by the Priest with the exclamation **"For blessed is Thy Name, and glorified is Thy kingdom..."** (*Ὅτι ηὐλόγηται τὸ ὄνομά σου...*); thereafter follow the Hypakoe of the Tone, the Gradual Antiphons (*Anavathmoi*), the Prokimenon, and subsequently the Canons. When a Feast of the Mother of God coincides with a Sunday, the Evlogitaria are not chanted; instead, immediately following the Kathismata, the Little Litany and exclamation are made by the Priest; then follow the Hypakoe of the Tone, the Gradual Antiphons, the Prokimenon of the feast, **"Let every breath praise the Lord"** (*Πᾶσα πνοὴ αἰνεσάτω τὸν Κύριον*), and the Matins Gospel of the feast; thereafter **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (*Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ θεασάμενοι*) and Psalm 50 LXX read smoothly without singing (*chuma* / *χῦμα*), **"Glory..."**, **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos, O Merciful One, blot out the multitude of our transgressions"** (*Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου πρεσβείαις...*), **"Now and forever..."**, the same; the verse **"Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy..."** (Psalm 50:1 LXX), and the appointed Idiomelon of the feast; whereafter the Priest intones **"O God, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance..."** (*Σῶσον, ὁ Θεός, τὸν λαόν σου...*), exclamation: **"Through the mercies and compassions and love for mankind of Thine Only-Begotten Son..."** (*Ἐλέει καὶ οἰκτιρμοῖς...*), and the Canons begin. On Apodoses and Afterfeast Sundays of Feasts of the Mother of God or Feasts of the Lord, however, the established order of the Sunday Eothinon [Morning] Gospel is preserved (see the particular Typikon Rubrics for Sunday).[^84]

## Concerning the Verses Sung Before the Troparia of the Canons

**24.** According to the ancient typikon order, at Matins for the Canons of Sundays and of the Saints, and for the Canons read daily, as many verses from the Nine Biblical Canticles (*αἱ ἐννέα Ὠδαί*) were chanted beforehand as there were troparia contained in each Ode of the Canon. Now, however, after the Heirmos of each Ode, verses are chanted before the troparia: for the Resurrection troparia, **"Glory to Thy holy Resurrection, O Lord"** (*Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ Ἀναστάσει σου, Κύριε*); for Marian troparia, **"Most Holy Theotokos, save us"** (*Ὑπεραγία Θεοτόκε, σῶσον ἡμᾶς*); and for the troparia of Saints, **"Saints of God, intercede for us"** (*Ἅγιοι τοῦ Θεοῦ, πρεσβεύσατε ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν*). At the Great Canon [of St. Andrew of Crete]: **"Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me"** (*Ἐλέησόν με, ὁ Θεός, ἐλέησόν με*); before the Martyrika preceding the Funeral Troparia: **"Through the intercessions of Thy Martyrs, O Christ God, grant rest to the souls...

...εἰς τὰ πρὸ τῶν Νεκρωσίμων Μαρτυρικά, *Πρεσβείαις τῶν Μαρτύρων σου, Χριστὲ ὁ Θεός, ἀνάπαυσον τὰς ψυχὰς τῶν δούλων*· εἰς τὰ Νεκρώσιμα, *Μακάριοι οὓς ἐξελέξω καὶ προσελάβου Κύριε*, ἢ *Αἱ ψυχαὶ αὐτῶν ἐν ἀγαθοῖς αὐλισθήσονται*, ἢ *Καὶ τὸ Μνημόσυνον αὐτῶν εἰς γενεὰν καὶ γενεάν*. Σημειωτέον δὲ ὅτι, ἐπειδὴ ἐν ταῖς μεγάλαις Δεσποτικαῖς ἑορταῖς οὐ στιχολογοῦνται κατὰ τὴν ἀρχαίαν τυπικὴν διάταξιν αἱ θ΄ Ὠδαί, διὰ τοῦτο οὐδεὶς Στίχος προψάλλεται τῶν Τροπαρίων πασῶν τῶν Δεσποτικῶν ἑορτῶν (πλὴν τῆς τοῦ Πάσχα, ὅτε λέγεται τὸ *Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ Ἀναστάσει σου Κύριε*), ἀλλὰ μόνον εἰς τὰ δύο τελευταῖα τὸ *Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν*· οὐδὲν ἧττον ὅμως καθ᾽ ἁπάσας τὰς μεθεόρτους ἡμέρας ψαλλομένου τοῦ Κανόνος τῆς προηγηθείσης Δεσποτικῆς ἑορτῆς, προτάττεται τὸ *Δόξα σοι ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν δόξα σοι*.

## ΠΕΡΙ ΤΟΥ ΕΝ ΤΗ Γ΄ ΩΔΗ ΚΑΘΙΣΜΑΤΟΣ

**25.** Ἐν ταῖς Κυριακαῖς, μετὰ τὴν τῆς γ΄ Ὠδῆς Αἴτησιν, ψάλλεται τὸ Κάθισμα καὶ τὸ Θεοτοκίον τοῦ ἐπιλαχόντος Ἁγίου· Θεομητορικῆς δὲ ἑορτῆς τυχούσης, προαναγινώσκεται τὸ Κοντάκιον καὶ ὁ Οἶκος τῆς Ὀκτωήχου, καὶ εἶτα ψάλλεται τὸ Κάθισμα τῆς ἑορτῆς ἅπαξ· τὸ αὐτὸ γίνεται καὶ κατὰ πάσας τὰς Κυριακὰς τοῦ Τριῳδίου καὶ ἐν ταῖς μνήμαις τῶν ἐπισημότερον ἑορταζομένων Ἁγίων.

Amen."** Nevertheless, on all Afterfeast days, when chanting the Canon of the preceding Feast of the Lord, the verse **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee"** is prefixed before its troparia.[^86]

### Concerning the Kathisma [Sessional Hymn] of the Third Ode

**25.** On Sundays, after the Little Litany following the Third Ode of the Canon, the Kathisma and Theotokion of the occurring Saint are chanted. If a Feast of the Mother of God occurs, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Octoechos are read beforehand, and then the Kathisma of the feast is chanted once; the same order is observed on all Sundays of the Triodion and on the commemorations of more solemnly celebrated Saints.
  On Forefeast and Afterfeast Sundays, the Kathisma of the occurring Saint is chanted first, and then that of the Forefeast or Afterfeast.[^87]

## Concerning the Katavasiae

**27.** On all Sundays included in the intervals between the following periods—namely, from the Apodosis of the Feast of the Precious Cross [September 21] until November 21; from the Apodosis of the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord [February 9] until the beginning of the Triodion, as well as on the Second, Fourth, and Fifth Sundays of Great Lent; from the Sunday of All Saints until July 27; and on all commemorations of celebrated Saints coinciding during the weekdays of these periods—the Katavasiae **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*) are chanted. On Sundays falling between the Forefeasts and Afterfeasts of Feasts of the Lord and Feasts of the Mother of God, and on commemorations of celebrated Saints occurring during the week, the Katavasiae of the occurring Feast of the Lord or Mother of God are chanted. On the first four Sundays of the Triodion (if the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord has not undergone its Apodosis), the Katavasiae of the Triodion are chanted, as also on the First Sunday of Lent [Triumph of Orthodoxy]. On whatever day of the week other than Sunday during the weeks of the Triodion the commemoration of a Saint occurs, the Katavasiae **"I shall open my mouth"** are chanted, and at the Divine Liturgy the Kontakion **"O Protection of Christians unashamed"** (*Προστασία τῶν Χριστιανῶν ἀκαταίσχυντε*).[^89]
