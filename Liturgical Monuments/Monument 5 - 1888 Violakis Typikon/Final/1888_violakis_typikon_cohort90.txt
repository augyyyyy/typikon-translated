# Appendix: The Ceremony of the Consecration of a Church (Censing, Anointing, and Conclusion)

## The Ceremony of the Consecration of a Church (Censing, Anointing of Pillars, Altar Lamp, and Dismissal) [Violakis, p. 877]

...and upon that, the Holy Gospel Book; then, he places in its proper place the sacred Artophorion [Tabernacle].* Then takes place the kissing of the Holy Gospel Book. After the kissing of the Gospel, the Bishop takes the censer and censes the Holy Table, the clergy, and the whole church. While the church is being censed, the Choirs chant Psalm 25 [LXX]: **"Judge me, O Lord"** (*Κρῖνόν με, Κύριε*). While the Bishop is censing, one of the Priests follows him carrying the vessel of Holy Myron; upon each one of the pillars and arches, he traces the sign of the Cross with the Holy Myron, anointing them by means of a sponge soaked in Myron attached to the end of a reed. When the censing and anointing are finished, and at the same time the Psalm is completed, the Bishop gives glory in a loud voice, saying: **"Glory to the holy, all-powerful, and life-creating Trinity, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ καὶ παντοδυνάμῳ καὶ ζωοποιῷ Τριάδι, πάντοτε νῦν καὶ ἀεὶ καὶ εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων*).

The Deacon intones the Small Litany: **"Again and again in peace let us pray unto the Lord"** (*Ἔτι καὶ ἔτι ἐν εἰρήνῃ τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*), and the Bishop pronounces the Exclamation: **"For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι πρέπει σοι πᾶσα δόξα...*). The Deacon: **"Let us pray to the Lord"** (*Τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*). The Bishop recites the Prayer: **"O Lord our God, Maker of heaven and earth..."** (*Κύριε ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*), and intones: **"Peace be unto all"** (*Εἰρήνη πᾶσι*). The Deacon: **"Let us bow our heads unto the Lord"** (*Τὰς κεφαλὰς ἡμῶν τῷ Κυρίῳ κλίνωμεν*). The Bishop recites the Prayer: **"We thank Thee, O Lord..."** (*Εὐχαριστοῦμέν σοι, Κύριε*). Then the Deacon exclaims: **"Let us go forth in peace"** (*Ἐν εἰρήνῃ προέλθωμεν*), and the Apostolos is read by the Deacon; the Gospel, on the other hand, is read by the Bishop. After this, a new oil lamp [*κανδήλα và καινή*] is brought to the Bishop with a wick and olive oil. With his own hands the Bishop lights the wick, chanting at the same time in Tone 1, three times, the first verse of the Great Doxology: **"Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will among men!"** (*Δόξα ἐν ὑψίστοις Θεῷ...*), and places the lit oil lamp upon the Holy Altar [Holy Table].†

The Bishop then takes off the white garment [the Savvanon], the cloths [mandylia], and the belt (all of which are distributed to the people as precious sanctified tokens), and permits the church's serving attendants to light the oil lamps and candles throughout the church. At the same time, the Doxastikon [Glory Sticheron] of the Patron Saint of the church is chanted, followed by **"Now and forever..."** (*Καὶ νῦν*): **"While celebrating the commemoration of the Consecration, O Lord, we glorify Thee, the Sanctifier of all!"** (*Τὴν μνήμην τῶν Ἐγκαινίων ἐπιτελοῦντες, Κύριε...*). Then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion of the Patron Saint of the church, and that of the Consecration: **"As the splendor of the firmament on high..."** (*Ὡς τοῦ ἄνω στερεώματος τὴν εὐπρέπειαν*); the Litany by the Deacon: **"Let us complete our morning prayer unto the Lord"** (*Πληρώσωμεν τὴν ἑωθινὴν δέησιν ἡμῶν τῷ Κυρίῳ*);‡ the Exclamation by the Bishop: **"For Thou art a merciful God and lover of mankind, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι ἐλεήμων καὶ φιλάνθρωπος...*)...[^505]

---
* *Note on the Sacred Artophorion:* The Tabernacle (Artophorion), wherein the Holy Mysteries (Reserved Eucharist) are permanently kept for the communion of the sick, is now placed in its permanent position at the center of the Holy Table.  
† *Rubric on the Lighting of the Sanctuary Lamps:* The Bishop himself lights the perpetual lamp (kandela) upon the altar, from which all other lamps and candles in the holy sanctuary and nave are subsequently kindled.  
‡ *Rubric on the Litany of Completion:* The deacon intones the morning litany of supplication, leading directly into the episcopal dismissal of the consecration office.

*(Printed Page 877)*

# Appendix: Ceremony of the Accession of a Patriarch to the Ecumenical Throne

## Ceremony of the Accession of a Patriarch to the Ecumenical Throne (Arrival, Entrance, and Supplications) [Violakis, p. 883]

### CEREMONY OF THE ACCESSION OF A PATRIARCH TO THE ECUMENICAL THRONE

Arriving with his customary entourage in solemn cortège at the Patriarchate, the Patriarch comes before the great portal of the church* where all the clergy have assembled to receive him. The holy Hierarchs who accompany him enter directly into the Holy Sanctuary [Bema]; four of the deacons vest in their sacred vestments; holding the Dikerotrikera, two of them cense the Patriarch as he enters the church, while the other two stand beside him. Preceded by the Primikirios carrying the Divamboulon, and with the Choirs of Chanters singing **"It is truly meet to bless thee, O Theotokos..."** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*), the Patriarch enters into the Holy Sanctuary.

Then the senior Hierarch in precedence [by seniority of episcopal ordination], vested in Epitrachilion and Omophorion, intones the blessing: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*). The Deacon recites the Trisagion Prayers, and the Hierarch exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι σοῦ ἐστιν ἡ βασιλεία...*). After this, the Hierarchs chant the Dismissal Troparion of Pentecost: **"Blessed art Thou, O Christ our God, Who hast revealed the fishermen to be all-wise, by sending down upon them the Holy Spirit, and through them catching the whole world in Thy net: O Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!"** (*Εὐλογητὸς εἶ Χριστὲ ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν...*), and the Kontakion of Pentecost: **"When the Most High came down and confused the tongues, He divided the nations; but when He distributed the tongues of fire, He called all to unity: and with one voice we glorify the All-Holy Spirit!"** (*Ὅτε καταβὰς τὰς γλώσσας συνέχεε...*).†

The Hierarch then intones the Ektenia: **"Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee, hear us and have mercy."** (*Ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς ὁ Θεός...*). The Hierarchs respond: **"Lord, have mercy"** (*Κύριε, ἐλέησον*) three times. The Hierarch: **"Again let us pray for all devout and Orthodox Christians."** (*Ἔτι δεόμεθα ὑπὲρ τῶν εὐσεβῶν...*). The Hierarchs again respond: **"Lord, have mercy"** three times. The Hierarch intones the Exclamation: **"For Thou art a merciful and loving...**[^508]

---
* *Note on the Great Portal:* The main entrance portal leading from the Narthex into the nave of the patriarchal cathedral church.  
† *Editorial Rubrical Concordance:* These two festal hymns are respectively the Dismissal Troparion and the Kontakion of the Great Feast of Holy Pentecost, invoking the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the newly acclaimed Patriarch and Primate.

## Τελετὴ εἰς Ἀνάρρησιν Πατριάρχου (Ἐνθρόνισις, Πολυχρονισμός, καὶ Ἀπόλυσις) [Violakis, p. 884]

Μετὰ ταῦτα ὁ Πατριάρχης ἐνδεδυμένος Μανδύαν, Ἐπιτραχήλιον καὶ Ὠμοφόριον ἵσταται ἐνώπιον τῆς ἁγίας Τραπέζης καὶ ποιεῖ Εὐλογητόν, εἶτα ἀναγινώσκει τὸ Τρισάγιον κτλ. ὡς ἀνωτέρω, καὶ γίνεται ἡ Ἀπόλυσις, μεθ’ ἣν ἱσταμένου τοῦ Πατριάρχου ἐν τῇ Ὡραίᾳ Πύλῃ, προσέρχεται ὁ μέγας Λογοθέτης, καὶ διὰ τῆς συνήθους προσφωνήσεως προσκαλεῖ αὐτὸν ἐπὶ τὸν Οἰκουμενικὸν Θρόνον, ὁ δὲ Πατριάρχης ποιεῖται τὴν εὐχαριστίαν, καὶ ἀκολούθως προσέρχεται αὐτῷ ὁ Μητροπολίτης Ἡρακλείας καὶ μετὰ τὴν συνήθη προσλαλιὰν ἐγχειρίζει αὐτῷ τὴν Πατριαρχικὴν ῥάβδον, ἀσπαζόμενος τὴν δεξιὰν αὐτοῦ, καὶ οὕτως ἡ Α.Θ.Π. ὁδηγουμένη ὑπὸ τοῦ Μεγάλου Λογοθέτου καὶ τῶν διακόνων ἀνέρχεται εἰς τὸν θρόνον καὶ εὐλογεῖ τὸν λαὸν μετὰ τοῦ τιμίου Σταυροῦ, τῶν χορῶν ψαλλόντων τὸ *Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη*· μετὰ τοῦτο ὁ Πρωτοψάλτης μετὰ τοῦ Χοροῦ αὐτοῦ ψάλλει τὸν Πατριαρχικὸν πολυχρονισμὸν *Πολυχρόνιον ποιήσαι Κύριος ὁ Θεὸς τὸν παναγιώτατον καὶ σεβασμιώτατον ἡμῶν αὐθέντην καὶ Δεσπότην, τὸν οἰκουμενικὸν Πατριάρχην, κύριον κύριον [δεῖνα] . . . Κύριε, φύλαττε αὐτὸν εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη*. Μετὰ τὸν πολυχρονισμὸν ὁ ἀπ’ Ἄμβωνος λόγος· εἰς τὸ τέλος οἱ Χοροὶ *Τὸν Δεσπότην καὶ Ἀρχιερέα ἡμῶν*, τοῦ Πατριάρχου εὐλογοῦντος· ἀπὸ τοῦ Βήματος τὸ *Δι’ εὐχῶν τοῦ ἁγίου Δεσπότου ἡμῶν*, καὶ οὕτω λήγει ἡ τελετή.

# Appendix: Ceremony of the Accession of a Patriarch to the Ecumenical Throne

## Ceremony of the Accession of a Patriarch to the Ecumenical Throne (Enthronement, Polychronion, and Dismissal) [Violakis, p. 885]

...God, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen." and the Dismissal: **"May Christ our true God, Who sent down the All-Holy Spirit from heaven upon His holy disciples and apostles in the form of fiery tongues, have mercy on us and save us, for He is good and the Lover of mankind"** (*Ὁ ἐν εἴδει πυρίνων γλωσσῶν...*), concluding without the clause **"Through the prayers of our holy fathers..."** (*ἄνευ τοῦ Δι’ εὐχῶν*).

Following this, the Patriarch, vested in the Mandya, the Epitrachilion, and the Omophorion, stands before the Holy Table and intones: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*). Then the Trisagion Prayers are read as above, and the Dismissal is given. After this, while the Patriarch stands in the Beautiful Gate [Royal Doors], the Great Logothete [Chancellor and Grand Representative of the Patriarchate] comes forward and, by means of the customary formal proclamation, invites the Patriarch to ascend the Ecumenical Throne. The Patriarch expresses his thanksgiving; and immediately following, the Metropolitan of Herakleia approaches him and, after the customary congratulatory address, delivers into his hands the Patriarchal Pastoral Staff, kissing his right hand. Thus His All-Holiness the Patriarch, escorted by the Great Logothete and the deacons, ascends the Throne and blesses the people with the Precious Cross, while the Choirs chant: **"For many years, Master!"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη, Δέσποτα*).

After this, the Protopsaltes [First-Chanter] with his Choir chants the Patriarchal Polychronion: **"May the Lord God preserve for many years our most holy and venerable Master and Lord, the Ecumenical Patriarch, Lord [Name]! O Lord, preserve him for many years!"** (*Πολυχρόνιον ποιήσαι Κύριος ὁ Θεὸς τὸν παναγιώτατον καὶ σεβασμιώτατον ἡμῶν αὐθέντην καὶ Δεσπότην, τὸν οἰκουμενικὸν Πατριάρχην, κύριον κύριον...*). Following the Polychronion, the homily from the Ambo is delivered [customarily by the Great Logothete]. At the conclusion of the address, the Choirs chant: **"Our Master and Chief Priest, O Lord, preserve for many years!"** (*Τὸν Δεσπότην καὶ Ἀρχιερέα ἡμῶν, Κύριε, φύλαττε εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη*), while the Patriarch blesses the assembly. From within the Holy Sanctuary [Bema], the clergy intone: **"Through the prayers of our holy Master, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us and save us. Amen."** (*Δι’ εὐχῶν τοῦ ἁγίου Δεσπότου ἡμῶν, Κύριε Ἰησοῦ Χριστὲ ὁ Θεός, ἐλέησον καὶ σῶσον ἡμᾶς. Ἀμήν*), and thus the ceremony of accession concludes.*...[^509]

---
* *Historical and Ecclesiological Context of the Accession Ceremony:* The accession and enthronement ceremony (*Anarrhesis*) of the Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople is celebrated with ancient Byzantine court and liturgical ceremonial. The Metropolitan of Herakleia exercises the historic right of investiture with the patriarchal crozier by virtue of Herakleia's ancient position as the metropolitan see over Byzantium prior to the establishment of the capital at Constantinople. The Great Logothete represents the imperial civil chancery and lay leadership of the Great Church of Christ.
