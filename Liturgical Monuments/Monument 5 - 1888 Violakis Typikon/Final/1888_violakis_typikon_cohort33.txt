# Month of January: Holy Theophany (January 6)

## Ὁ Μέγας Ἑσπερινὸς καὶ ἡ Λειτουργία τοῦ Μεγάλου Βασιλείου [Violakis, p. 306]

2.    Μετὰ τὴν τῆς Θ΄ Ὥρας ἀπόλυσιν, ὁ Διάκονος *Εὐλόγησον Δέσποτα*, ὁ Ἱερεὺς *Εὐλογημένη ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Πατρὸς κτλ.* ὁ Πατριάρχης (ἢ ὁ Ἀρχιερεὺς) τὸν Προοιμιακόν, εἶτα ὁ Διάκονος τὴν Μ. Συναπτήν. Εἰς τὸ *Κύριε ἐκέκραξα* ψάλλομεν τὰ δ΄ Ἰδιόμελα *Τὸν φωτισμὸν ἡμῶν* εἰς ς΄. *Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν*, *Ὑπέκλινας κάραν τῷ Προδρόμῳ*. Εἴσοδος μετὰ τοῦ Εὐαγγελίου, *Φῶς ἱλαρόν*, καὶ εὐθὺς ὁ Ἀναγνώστης τὰ Ἀναγνώσματα *Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἐποίησεν ὁ Θεὸς τὸν οὐρανὸν καὶ τὴν γῆν*· μετὰ τὸ τέλος ἄρχεται ὁ α΄ Χορὸς ψάλλειν τὸ Τροπάριον *Ἐπεφάνης ἐν τῷ κόσμῳ* ὁλόκληρον· ὁ β΄ ἐπαναλαμβάνει *Ἵνα φωτίσῃς τοὺς ἐν σκότει καθημένους, Φιλάνθρωπε, δόξα σοι*, ὅπερ ἐπαναλαμβάνεται ἑκατέρωθεν ἕως οὗ ὁ Κανονάρχης πληρώσῃ τοὺς Στίχους τοῦ ψαλμοῦ. Εἶτα αὖθις ὁ α΄ Χορὸς *Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν* τὸ Τροπάριον ὁλόκληρον. Μετὰ ταῦτα ὁ β΄ Κανονάρχης ἀναγινώσκει τὸ Ε΄ Ἀνάγνωσμα *Εἶπεν Ἠλίας τῷ Ἐλισσαιέ*· μετὰ τὸ τέλος ὁ β΄ Χορὸς ψάλλει *Ἁμαρτωλοῖς καὶ τελώναις* ὁλόκληρον· ἀπὸ δὲ τοῦ *Ποῦ γὰρ εἶχε τὸ φῶς σου λάμψαι*, ἐπαναλαμβάνεται ἐναλλὰξ ὑπὸ τῶν δύο Χορῶν, ἕως οὗ ὁ Κανονάρχης πληρώσῃ τοὺς Στίχους τοῦ ψαλμοῦ· ὁ α΄ αὖθις Κανονάρχης τὸ ς΄ Ἀνάγνωσμα *Παραγένετο Νεεμὰν βασιλέως Ἀσσυρίων* καὶ μετὰ τὸ τέλος τὴν μικρὰν Συναπτὴν *Τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*, καὶ ψάλλεται τὸ Τρισάγιον, εἶτα λέγεται ὁ Ἀπόστολος, καὶ μετὰ τὸ Ἀλληλούϊα τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον ἐπ’ ἄμβωνος καὶ καθεξῆς ἡ Λειτουργία τοῦ Μ. Βασιλείου. Κοινωνικὸν *Αἰνεῖτε (τὸν) Κύριον* καὶ τὸ *Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς*. Μετὰ τὴν Ὀπισθάμβωνον Εὐχήν, ψάλλονται τὰ Ἰδιόμελα *Φωνὴ Κυρίου* καὶ τελεῖται ὑπὸ τοῦ λειτουργοῦντος Ἱερέως ὁ Ἁγιασμός, ὡς εἴθισται, κατὰ τὴν ἐν τῷ Μηναίῳ διάταξιν. Μετὰ τὸ τέλος τοῦ Ἁγιασμοῦ, ἁγιαζομένων τῶν Χριστιανῶν, ὁ β΄ Χορὸς ψάλλει τὸ Ἰδιόμελον *Ἀνυμνήσωμεν οἱ πιστοί*, καὶ μετὰ τοῦτο ἡ Ἀπόλυσις *Δόξα σοι . . . ὁ ἐν Ἰορδάνῃ ὑπὸ Ἰωάννου βαπτισθῆναι καταδεξάμενος διὰ τὴν ἡμῶν σωτηρίαν Χριστός . . . .* Ἐν δὲ τῇ τραπέζῃ ξηροφαγία.

# Month of January: Holy Theophany (January 6)

## Great Vespers and the Divine Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great [Violakis, p. 307]

2.    After the Dismissal of the Ninth Hour, the Deacon intones: **"Bless, Master"** (*Εὐλόγησον, Δέσποτα*); the Priest: **"Blessed is the kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen"** (*Εὐλογημένη ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Πατρὸς...*); the Patriarch (or Hierarch) reads the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX]; then the Deacon intones the Great Litany. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we sing the 4 Idiomela: **"Our illumination"** (*Τὸν φωτισμὸν ἡμῶν*), repeating the first two to make 6. **"Glory... Both now and ever..."**: **"Thou hast bowed Thy head unto the Forerunner"** (*Ὑπέκλινας κάραν τῷ Προδρόμῳ*). Entrance with the Gospel Book, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), and immediately the Reader reads the Readings: **"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth"** (*Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἐποίησεν ὁ Θεὸς τὸν οὐρανὸν καὶ τὴν γῆν*, Genesis 1:1–13); at the conclusion, the First Choir begins singing the Troparion: **"Thou hast appeared in the world"** (*Ἐπεφάνης ἐν τῷ κόσμῳ*) in its entirety; the Second Choir repeats the refrain: **"To give light to those who sit in darkness; O Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!"** (*Ἵνα φωτίσῃς τοὺς ἐν σκότει καθημένους, Φιλάνθρωπε, δόξα σοι*), which is repeated alternately by both Choirs until the Canonarch completes the Verses of the Psalm [Ps. 66 LXX]. Then immediately the First Choir sings **"Glory to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen"**, and the entire Troparion. After this, the second Canonarch reads the Fifth Reading: **"And Elijah said unto Elisha"** (*Εἶπεν Ἠλίας τῷ Ἐλισσαιέ*, 4 Kingdoms 2:6–14 LXX); at the conclusion, the Second Choir sings: **"Unto sinners and tax-collectors"** (*Ἁμαρτωλοῖς καὶ τελώναις*) in its entirety; from the phrase: **"Where indeed should Thy light have shone"** (*Ποῦ γὰρ εἶχε τὸ φῶς σου λάμψαι*), it is repeated alternately by the two Choirs, until the Canonarch completes the verses of the Psalm [Ps. 92 LXX]; then the first Canonarch recites the Sixth Reading: **"So Naaman, captain of the host of the king of Syria"** (*Παραγένετο Νεεμὰν βασιλέως Ἀσσυρίων*, 4 Kingdoms 5:1–14 LXX). Then the Small Litany: **"Let us pray to the Lord"** (*Τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*), and the Trisagion Hymn is sung; then follows the Epistle [1 Corinthians 9:19–27] and, following the **"Alleluia"**, the Gospel [Luke 3:1–18] is proclaimed from the ambo, and then follows the Divine Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great. Communion Verse: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 148:1 LXX) and **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς τὸ ἀληθινόν*). After the Prayer behind the Ambon, the Idiomela: **"The voice of the Lord is upon the waters"** (*Φωνὴ Κυρίου ἐπὶ τῶν ὑδάτων*) are sung, and the Service of the Great Blessing of the Waters is celebrated, as is customary, according to the order in the Menaion by the celebrating Priest. After the conclusion of the Great Blessing of the Waters, while the faithful are blessed with holy water, the Second Choir sings the Idiomelon: **"Let us, the faithful, praise"** (*Ἀνυμνήσωμεν οἱ πιστοί*), and then the Dismissal: **"Glory to Thee, [O Christ God, our hope, glory to Thee]... May He Who deigned to be baptized by John in the Jordan for our salvation, Christ our true God..."** (*Δόξα σοι... ὁ ἐν Ἰορδάνῃ ὑπὸ Ἰωάννου βαπτισθῆναι καταδεξάμενος διὰ τὴν ἡμῶν σωτηρίαν Χριστὸς ὁ ἀληθινὸς Θεὸς ἡμῶν...*). At table, xerophagy [strict fast without wine or oil] is observed.
3.    On Friday at Matins: after Psalm 50 [LXX], the Lite and the Idiomela of the Aposticha are sung (since they were not sung yesterday evening); then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion: **"When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan"** (*Ἐν Ἰορδάνῃ*), and the Hexapsalmos. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparion: **"When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan"** (3 times); then the Psalter Kathismata and the Polyeleos: **"Praise the name of the Lord"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸ ὄνομα Κυρίου*, Ps. 134 LXX).[^220]

## Ὄρθρος: Τάξις τοῦ Εὐαγγελίου, Κανόνες καὶ Μεγαλυνάρια [Violakis, p. 308]

*εἶδε καὶ ἔφυγεν*, τὸ *Πᾶσα πνοὴ* καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Ὄρθρου, ὁ Ν΄ ψαλμὸς χῦμα, *Δόξα*, *Τὰ σύμπαντα σήμερον ἀγαλλιάσθω*, *Καὶ νῦν* τὸ αὐτό· ὁ Στίχος *Ἐλέησόν με ὁ Θεὸς* καὶ τὸ Ἰδιόμελον· *Θεὸς λόγος ἐπεφάνη ἐν σαρκί*, οἱ Κανόνες ἀμφότεροι ἀνὰ δ΄ ἄνευ Στίχου, εἰμὴ μόνον *Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν* ἐν ἑκάστῃ Ὠδῇ, ἀπὸ γ΄ Ὠδῆς ἡ Ὑπακοὴ *Ὅτε τῇ ἐπιφανείᾳ σου*, ἀφ’ ς΄ τὸ Κοντάκιον, ὁ Οἶκος καὶ τὸ Μηνολόγιον, αἱ Καταβασίαι διπλαῖ, καὶ ψάλλεται ἡ Θ΄ Ὠδὴ μετὰ τῶν Μεγαλυναρίων.

# Month of January: Holy Theophany (January 6)

## Matins: Gospel Order, Canons, and Festal Megalynaria [Violakis, p. 309]

Then the 3 Kathismata, the Anavathmoi (first Antiphon of Tone 4), the Prokeimenon: **"The sea saw and fled, Jordan was driven back"** (*Ἡ θάλασσα εἶδε καὶ ἔφυγεν*, Ps. 113:3 LXX); then **"Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord"** (*Πᾶσα πνοὴ αἰνεσάτω τὸν Κύριον*) and the Gospel of Matins [Mark 1:9–11], and Psalm 50 [LXX] read. **"Glory"**: **"Today let all things greatly rejoice"** (*Τὰ σύμπαντα σήμερον ἀγαλλιάσθω*); **"Both now and ever"**: repeat the same hymn; the Verse: **"Have mercy upon me, O God, according to Thy great mercy"** [Ps. 50:3 LXX], and the Idiomelon: **"God the Word appeared in the flesh"** (*Θεὸς λόγος ἐπεφάνη ἐν σαρκί*). Both Canons are sung with 4 troparia from each Canon without verses except for **"Glory... Both now and ever..."** in each Ode. After the Third Ode, the Hypakoe: **"When at Thy manifestation"** (*Ὅτε τῇ ἐπιφανείᾳ σου*); after the Sixth Ode, the Kontakion: **"Thou hast appeared today to the universe"** (*Ἐπεφάνης σήμερον*), the Ikos, and the Menologion of the day. Both double Katavasiai: **"He uncovered the bottom of the deep"** (*Βυθοῦ ἀνεκάλυψε πυθμένα*) and **"The Lord, working wonders"** (*Στείβει θαλάσσης*), and the Ninth Ode is sung with the Megalynaria:

### For the First Canon

    **"Magnify, O my soul, Her that is more honorable than the heavenly hosts."** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὴν τιμιωτέραν τῶν ἄνω στρατευμάτων.*)  
    **"Magnify, O my soul, Him Who hath come to be baptized in the Jordan."** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὸν ἐν Ἰορδάνῃ ἐλθόντα βαπτισθῆναι.*)  
    **"Magnify, O my soul, Him Who asketh [receiveth] baptism from the Forerunner."** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὸν ὑπὸ Προδρόμου τὸ βάπτισμα αἰτοῦντα [λαβόντα].*)  
    **"Magnify, O my soul, Him to Whom the voice of the Father bore witness."** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὸν ἐκ τῆς Πατρῴας φωνῆς μαρτυρηθέντα.*)  
    *[**"Magnify, O my soul, One of the Trinity Who bowed His head and received baptism."** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὸν ἕνα τῆς Τριάδος, κλίναντα τὸν αὐχένα, καὶ βάπτισμα λαβόντα.*)]\*  
    *[**"O Prophet, come unto Me: stretch out thy hand and baptize Me swiftly."** (*Προφῆτα, δεῦρο πρός με, ἔκτεινον τὴν χεῖρα, καὶ βάπτισόν με τάχος.*)]\*  
    *[**"O Prophet, suffer it to be so now, and baptize Me willing: for I have come to fulfill all righteousness."** (*Προφῆτα, ἄφες ἄρτι, καὶ βάπτισόν με θέλων· πληρῶσαι καὶ γὰρ ἦλθον, πᾶσαν δικαιοσύνην.*)]\*  

### For the Second Iambic Canon

    **"Today the Master bows His head beneath the hand of the Forerunner."** (*Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης, κλίνει τὸν αὐχένα, χειρὶ τῇ τοῦ Προδρόμου.*)  
    **"Today John baptizes the Master in the streams of the Jordan."** (*Σήμερον Ἰωάννης, βαπτίζει τὸν Δεσπότην, ἐν ῥείθροις Ἰορδάνου.*)  
    *[**"Today the Master buries in the waters the sin of mortal man."** (*Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης, νάμασιν ἐνθάπτει, βροτῶν τὴν ἁμαρτίαν.*)]\*  
    *[**"Today the Master receives testimony from on high, that He is the beloved Son."** (*Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης, ἄνωθεν μαρτυρεῖται, Υἱὸς ἠγαπημένος.*)]\*  
    *[**"Today the Master has come to sanctify the nature of the waters."** (*Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης, ἦλθεν ἁγιάσαι, τὴν φύσιν τῶν ὑδάτων.*)]\*[^221]

## Ὄρθρος: Κατακλεὶς τῆς Θ΄ Ὠδῆς καὶ Τελετὴ τοῦ Μεγάλου Ἁγιασμοῦ [Violakis, p. 310]

        *[Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης, τὸ βάπτισμα λαμβάνει, ὑπὸ χειρὸς Προδρόμου.]\**
        *Δόξα. Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τῆς τρισυποστάτου, καὶ ἀδιαιρέτου, θεότητος τὸ κράτος.*
        *Καὶ νῦν. Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὴν λυτρωσαμένην, ἡμᾶς ἐκ τῆς κατάρας.*

*[Σημ. Οἱ ἄνωθι στίχοι ἐντὸς παρενθέσεων καὶ μὲ ἀστερίσκους εὑρίσκονται εἰς τὸ Μηναῖον (ἐκδ. Φῶς, 1960 καὶ Ἀποστολικὴ Διακονία, 1991). Δὲν εὑρίσκονται εἰς τὸ Τυπικὸν ἢ εἰς τὰς τυπικὰς ὁδηγείας τοῦ Μηναίου.]*

    Τὸ Ἐξαποστειλάριον ἐκ γ΄, Εἰς τοὺς Αἴνους, τὰ Ἰδιόμελα *Φῶς ἐκ φωτὸς* εἰς ς΄, *Δόξα*, *Νάματα Ἰορδάνεια*, *Καὶ νῦν*, *Σήμερον ὁ Χριστὸς* καὶ ἡ Μεγάλη Δοξολογία.

    Μετὰ τὸ *Ἅγιος ἀθάνατος, ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς* τοῦ Τρισαγίου τῆς Δοξολογίας, ψάλλονται τὰ γ΄ Ἰδιόμελα τοῦ Ἁγιασμοῦ *Φωνὴ Κυρίου ἐπὶ τῶν ὑδάτων*, *Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν*, *Πρὸς τὴν φωνὴν τοῦ βοῶντος ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ*. Τούτων ψαλλομένων, ἐξέρχεται ὁ Ἱερεὺς ἀπὸ τῆς Ὡραίας Πύλης μετὰ τοῦ τιμίου Σταυροῦ προπορευομένων λαμπάδων καὶ τῶν Διακόνων, ὁ δὲ Πατριάρχης (ἢ ὁ Ἀρχιερεὺς) κατέρχεται τοῦ Θρόνου καὶ ἔρχεται ἐν τῷ μέσῳ τοῦ Ναοῦ, ἔνθα κεῖται ἡ τράπεζα καὶ τὸ δοχεῖον τοῦ Ἁγιασμοῦ, φορῶν ἐπιτραχήλιον καὶ ὠμόφορον. Μετὰ τὸ τέλος τῶν Ἰδιομέλων ὁ Κανονάρχης ἢ ὁ Διάκονος εὐθὺς ἀναγινώσκει τὰς τρεῖς Προφητείας τοῦ Ἁγιασμοῦ καὶ ἀκολούθως τὸν Ἀπόστολον, ὁ δὲ Πατριάρχης τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον· μετὰ τὸ *Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη*, ὁ Μ. Ἀρχιδιάκονος τὴν μεγάλην Συναπτὴν τοῦ Ἁγιασμοῦ, τοῦ Πατριάρχου μεταξὺ τούτου ἀναγινώσκοντος μυστικῶς τὴν Εὐχὴν *Κύριε Ἰησοῦ Χριστέ*· πληρωθείσης τῆς Εὐχῆς συνάμα καὶ τῆς Συναπτῆς, ἐκφωνεῖ τὴν Εὐχὴν *Τριὰς ὑπερούσιε*, ἐπισυνάπτων μετὰ τὸ τέλος ταύτης καὶ τὴν ἑτέραν Εὐχὴν *Μέγας εἶ Κύριε καὶ θαυμαστὰ τὰ ἔργα σου*, ὃ ἐπαναλαμβάνει τρίς, καὶ πᾶσαν δὲ ἐπανάληψιν οἱ Χοροὶ ψάλλουσι τὸ

*[Σημ. 1. [Ὁ Βιολάκης ἔχει λανθασμένως τὸν ἀριθμὸν 4.] Ἐν τισι μέρεσιν ἤρξατο ἡ συνήθεια τοῦ τελεῖν τὸν Ἁγιασμὸν μετὰ τὸ τέλος τῆς Λειτουργίας, τὸ μὲν διότι ἐγίνετο διακοπή τις μετὰ τὸν Ἁγιασμὸν καὶ οἱ πλείους τῶν ἐκκλησιαζομένων ἀνεχώρουν, τὸ δὲ καὶ ὅτι μέγα μέρος τοῦ λαοῦ καθυστεροῦν ἐστερεῖτο τῆς τοῦ Ἁγιασμοῦ τελετῆς.]*

# Month of January: Holy Theophany (January 6)

## Matins Concluded and the Service of the Great Blessing of the Waters [Violakis, p. 311]

    *[**"Today the Master receives baptism at the hand of the Forerunner."** (*Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης, τὸ βάπτισμα λαμβάνει, ὑπὸ χειρὸς Προδρόμου.*)]\*  
    **"Glory to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit: Magnify, O my soul, the power of the Godhead in three Persons and undivided."** (*Δόξα. Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τῆς τρισυποστάτου, καὶ ἀδιαιρέτου, θεότητος τὸ κράτος.*)  
    **"Both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen: Magnify, O my soul, Her who hath delivered us from the curse."** (*Καὶ νῦν. Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τὴν λυτρωσαμένην, ἡμᾶς ἐκ τῆς κατάρας.*)  

*[Note: The Megalynaria above, enclosed in brackets and marked with an asterisk, are found in the Menaion (ed. Phos, 1960 and ed. Apostoliki Diakonia, 1991); they are not found in the Typikon or in the rubrics from the Typikon reprinted in the Menaion.]*

    The Exapostilarion (3 times). At the Praises: the Idiomela: **"Light from Light"** (*Φῶς ἐκ φωτός*) in 6 troparia; **"Glory"**: **"O Savior, Who clothedst Thyself with the waters of Jordan"** (*Νάματα Ἰορδάνεια*); **"Both now and ever"**: **"Today Christ"** (*Σήμερον ὁ Χριστός*), and the Great Doxology.
    [In the Great Doxology, the customary opening line is replaced by the concluding refrain of the Theotokion: "We who have been enlightened cry aloud: Glory to God made manifest, Who hath appeared upon earth and brought light unto the world."]
    In the Great Doxology, after the phrase **"Holy Immortal, have mercy on us"** (*Ἅγιος ἀθάνατος, ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς*) of the Trisagion, we sing the 3 Idiomela of the Blessing of the Waters: **"The voice of the Lord is upon the waters"** (*Φωνὴ Κυρίου ἐπὶ τῶν ὑδάτων*); **"Glory... Both now and ever..."**: **"At the voice of one crying in the wilderness"** (*Πρὸς τὴν φωνὴν τοῦ βοῶντος ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ*). While these hymns are being sung, the Priest exits from the Holy Doors holding the Precious Cross, preceded by candle-bearers and Deacons. The Patriarch (or Hierarch), wearing the epitrachilion and small omophorion, descends from the Throne and comes to the center of the temple, where the table with the vessel of holy water has been prepared. At the conclusion of the Idiomela, the Canonarch or the Deacon immediately reads the 3 Prophecies of the Blessing of the Waters [Isaiah 35:1–10; Isaiah 55:1–13; Isaiah 12:3–6], and then follows the Epistle [1 Corinthians 10:1–4]; the Patriarch proclaims the Gospel [Mark 1:9–11]; after **"Many years"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη*), the Great Archdeacon intones the Great Litany of the Blessing of the Waters Service. In the meantime, the Patriarch reads silently the Prayer: **"O Lord Jesus Christ"** (*Κύριε Ἰησοῦ Χριστέ*); at the conclusion of both the Prayer and the Litany, he exclaims aloud the Prayer: **"O Trinity transcending essence"** (*Τριὰς ὑπερούσιε*), joining to the end of it the second Prayer: **"Great art Thou, O Lord, and marvelous are Thy works"** (*Μέγας εἶ Κύριε καὶ θαυμαστὰ τὰ ἔργα σου*), which he repeats three times, while at each repetition the Choirs sing: **"Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee!"** (*Δόξα σοι Κύριε, δόξα σοι!*)[^222]
