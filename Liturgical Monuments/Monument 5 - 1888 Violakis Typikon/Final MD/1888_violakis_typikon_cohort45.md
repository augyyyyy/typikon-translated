# Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situations XI Concl., XII §§ 19–20)

## March 9: The Holy Forty Martyrs [in the Lake of Sebastea] (Situations XI Concl., XII §§ 19–20) [Violakis, p. 433]

    In the evening, the Akathist Hymn is sung with the Small Compline, as customary; Kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*).

### SITUATION TWELVE

19.    If the Feast occurs on the Saturday of the Akathist: on Friday morning at Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and "Unto the Lord" [Kathisma 18, Pss. 119–133 LXX], at "Lord, I have cried", we chant the Idiomelon: **"As from Jerusalem"** (*Ὡς ἐξ Ἱερουσαλὴμ*) twice, 4 Prosomia of the Theotokos: **"Revealing to thee the pre-eternal counsel"** (*Βουλὴν προαιώνιον*), and 4 of the Martyrs; "Glory...": **"David cried out prophetically in the psalms"** (*Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόα*); "Both now and ever...": **"The mystery which was hidden from all eternity"** (*Τὸ ἀπ᾿ αἰῶνος μυστήριον*); Entrance with the censer, "O Gladsome Light", and the Readings of the day; **"Let my prayer arise"** [Ps. 140:2 LXX], and then the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts follows in due order; Communion Verse: **"O taste and see"** (*Γεύσασθε καὶ ἴδετε*, Ps. 33:9 LXX); **"I will bless the Lord at all times"** (*Εὐλογήσω τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 33:2 LXX), and Dismissal.
    In the evening, the Akathist Hymn is sung with the Small Compline, as customary; Kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*).
20.    On Saturday at Matins: after the Midnight Service, the Trisagion Prayers, **"Taking knowledge of the secret command"** (*Τὸ προσταχθὲν μυστικῶς*), and the Hexapsalmos. At "God is the Lord": **"Taking knowledge of the secret command"**, the Dismissal Troparion of the Saints: **"By reason of the sufferings which the Saints endured for Thy sake, O Lord"** (*Τὰς ἀλγηδόνας τῶν Ἁγίων*), and once again **"Taking knowledge of the secret command"**; then the Psalter; the Kathismata of the Saints: **"The venerable firmament"** (*Τὸ σεπτὸν στερέωμα*) (seek after the 3rd Ode) [these Kathismata, however, belong to the First Stasis of the Matins of March 9, according to the Menaion]; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"The great commander"** (*Ὁ μέγας στρατηγός*); at the 2nd Stichologia: of the Saints: **"Enlisted under Christ"** (*Τῷ Χριστῷ στρατευθέντες*); "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"I reflect upon the judgment"** (*Ἐνθυμοῦμαι τὴν κρίσιν*) [these Kathismata of the Second Stasis follow the 3rd Ode]; and immediately Psalm 50 [LXX] read plainly. Canons: that of the Theotokos: **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*), and that of the Saints; after the 3rd Ode, the Kathisma: **"The great commander"** (*Ὁ μέγας στρατηγός*) once; after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*), and the Ikos: **"An archangel was sent"** (*Ἄγγελος πρωτοστάτης*), the Menologion of the Martyrs, and the Synaxarion of the Triodion; the Katavasiai: **"I shall open my mouth"**; and we chant "More honorable than the Cherubim". Exapostilaria: of the Saints, and that of the Triodion: **"Today the mystery that is from all eternity"** (*Τὸ ἀπ᾿ αἰῶνος σήμερον*). At the Praises: 3 Prosomia of the Theotokos: **"A hidden mystery"** (*Κεκρυμμένον μυστήριον*), and 3 of the Saints: **"Come, O brethren, let us praise in song the band of martyrs"** (*Δεῦτε μαρτυρικὴν ἀδελφοί*); "Glory...": **"O prize-bearing martyrs of Christ"** (*Ἀθλοφόροι Χριστοῦ*); "Both now and ever...": **"A tongue which she knew not"** (*Γλῶσσαν ἣν οὐκ ἔγνω*); Great Doxology, and the Dismissal Troparion: **"By reason of the sufferings which the Saints endured for Thy sake, O Lord"** (*Τὰς ἀλγηδόνας τῶν Ἁγίων*).[^283]

## March 9: The Holy Forty Martyrs [in the Lake of Sebastea] (Situation XII Concl. § 21) [Violakis, p. 435]

21.    At the Divine Liturgy, the Typika and the Beatitudes: 4 troparia from the 3rd Ode of the Canon of the Theotokos, and 4 from the 6th Ode of that of the Saints. At the Entrance: **"O come, let us worship and bow down before Christ; save us, O Son of God, wondrous art Thou in the Saints, who sing to Thee: Alleluia"** (*Δεῦτε προσκυνήσωμεν... ὁ ἐν Ἁγίοις θαυμαστός*), **"Taking knowledge of the secret command"** (*Τὸ προσταχθὲν μυστικῶς*), that of the Saints, that of the Patron Saint of the Church, and the Kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*). # The 25th Day of March: The Annunciation of Our Most Holy Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary

## March 25: The Annunciation of Our Most Holy Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary (Situation I § 1) [Violakis, p. 435]

### SITUATIONS

#### SITUATION ONE

Note: If the Paramony [Eve] of the Feast of the Annunciation falls on a Wednesday or Friday, on which days, according to custom, the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts is celebrated: in the holy parish churches of the cities the Presanctified Liturgy is not celebrated, because an all-night vigil is not kept in them (except on Great and Holy Tuesday and Great and Holy Wednesday, on which the Presanctified Liturgy is celebrated following Small Vespers). In the holy monasteries, however, owing to the distinction of the monastic way of life and because all-night vigils are always celebrated therein, the Presanctified Liturgy is celebrated on the Paramony following Small Vespers, the ancient monastic typika remaining immovable.
