# Month of February: Hieromartyr Charalampos (Situation XIII: Sunday of Orthodoxy §§ 23–25)

## February 10: The Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos (Situation XIII: First Sunday of Great Lent / Sunday of Orthodoxy §§ 23–25) [Violakis, p. 403]

### SITUATION THIRTEEN

23.    If the commemoration occurs on the Sunday of Orthodoxy: on Saturday evening at Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and the reading from the Psalter [Kathisma 1], at "Lord, I have cried", we chant 4 Resurrectional Stichira, 3 of the Triodion, and 3 Prosomia of the Saint; "Glory...": of the Triodion; "Both now and ever...": the first Theotokion of the Tone; Little Entrance, "O Gladsome Light", the Prokeimenon, and the Old Testament Readings of the Saint. At the Aposticha: the Resurrectional Stichira; "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": of the Triodion; the Dismissal Troparia: of the Resurrection, of the Triodion [**"Thy pure icon do we venerate"**, *Τὴν ἄχραντον εἰκόνα σου*, Tone 2], of the Saint, the Theotokion, and Dismissal.

24.    On Sunday at Matins: after Psalm 50 [LXX], the Triadic Canon and the Litiya of the Saint; "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": of the Triodion: **"Rejoice, ye honored prophets"** (*Χαίρετε προφῆται τίμιοι*), and the Triadic hymns: **"It is truly meet"**; then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion: **"Thy pure icon do we venerate"** (*Τὴν ἄχραντον εἰκόνα σου*), and the Hexapsalmos. At "God is the Lord": the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers; then the Psalter and the Polyeleos; the Resurrectional Kathismata in order, and the Kathisma of the third Stichologia of the Saint with the Theotokion; the Evlogitaria, the Hypakoe, the Anavathmoi of the Tone, and the Prokeimenon. The Canons: of the Resurrection, of the Triodion, and of the Saint; after the Third Ode: the Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos, then the Kathisma of the Triodion, that of the Saint, and the Theotokion; after the Sixth Ode: the Kontakion and Ikos of the Triodion, the Menologion of the Saint, and the Synaxarion [Hypomnema] of the Triodion; the Katavasiai: **"The Red Sea's abyssal depth"** (*Θαλάσσης τὸ ἐρυθραῖον πέλαγος*), and the entire order of the Morning Gospel appointed for Sundays of the Triodion; then we chant with verses "More honorable than the Cherubim"; Exapostilaria: that of the Resurrection, that of the Triodion, that of the Saint, and the Theotokion. At the Praises: 2 Resurrectional Stichira, 3 of the Triodion, and 3 of the Saint; "Glory...": of the Triodion; "Both now and ever...": **"Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη*); Great Doxology, and **"Today salvation is come unto the world"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*).

25.    At the Divine Liturgy: the Antiphons of the Sunday (see in the Horologion under the First Sunday of Great Lent [Sunday of Orthodoxy]). After the Little Entrance: the Dismissal Troparia of the Resurrection, of the Triodion, of the Saint, of the Patron Saint of the Temple, and the Kontakion: **"To thee, the champion leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*); the Epistle and Gospel of the Sunday, and the rest of the Liturgy of St. Basil the Great follows; Communion Verse: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 148:1 LXX), "We have seen the true light", and Dismissal.[^268]

# Month of February: Hieromartyr Charalampos (Situation XIV: Second Week of Great Lent §§ 26–28); Concurrence Envelope; February 24 Heading

## Τῇ Ι΄: Τοῦ Ἁγίου Ἱερομάρτυρος Χαραλάμπους (Περίπτωσις ΙΔ΄ §§ 26–28, Ἑορτοδρόμιον) / Τῇ ΚΔ΄: Ἡ Εὕρεσις τῆς Τιμίας Κεφαλῆς τοῦ Προδρόμου [Violakis, p. 404]

### ΙΔ΄.

26.    Εἰ τύχοι τῇ Δευτέρᾳ τῆς Β΄ ἑβδομάδος, τῇ Κυριακῇ ἑσπέρας, εἰς τὸ *Κύριε ἐκέκραξα* τὰ Κατανυκτικὰ τοῦ ἤχου οὐ ψάλλονται, ἀλλὰ τὰ γ΄ Προσόμοια τῆς σειρᾶς τοῦ Τριῳδίου *Δίδου μοι κατάνυξιν*, καὶ τὰ ἕτερα β΄ καὶ γ΄ τοῦ ἁγίου Χαραλάμπους *Ὅλος ἐκ νεότητος*, *Δόξα*, *Ἔθυσας τῷ Θεῷ*, *Καὶ νῦν* Προσόμοιον Θεοτοκίον, Εἴσοδος, *Φῶς ἱλαρόν*, τὸ Μέγα Προκείμενον *Ἔδωκας κληρονομίαν*, μετὰ τῶν Στίχων αὐτοῦ ἐκ τρίτου. Εἰς τὰ Ἀπόστιχα ψάλλεται τὸ Ἰδιόμελον *Δεῦτε ἐκκαθάρωμεν* ἀργῶς, δίς, καὶ τὸ Μαρτυρικόν, *Δόξα*, *Τὸν νοερὸν ἀδάμαντα*, *Καὶ νῦν*, *Ἐκ παντοίων κινδύνων*, *Νῦν ἀπολύεις*, τὸ Τρισάγιον, τὸ Ἀπολυτίκιον τοῦ Ἁγίου, τὸ Θεοτοκίον, καὶ Ἀπόλυσις.

27.    Τῇ Δευτέρᾳ πρωΐ, ἡ Ἀκολουθία τοῦ Ὄρθρου τοῦ Ἁγίου ἀπὸ τῆς Λιτῆς μέχρι τῆς Δοξολογίας, ὡς καὶ τῶν Ὡρῶν, ψάλλεται ὡς διετάχθη ἐν περιπτώσει Η΄ § 14. Ἐν δὲ τῷ Ἑσπερινῷ, μετὰ τὸν Προοιμιακὸν καὶ τὰ Πρὸς Κύριον, εἰς τὸ *Κύριε ἐκέκραξα* ψάλλομεν τὸ τῶν Ἀποστίχων Ἰδιόμελον *Τὴν σωματικὴν νηστείαν* δίς, καὶ τὸ Μαρτυρικόν, τῆς σειρᾶς Προσόμοια γ΄ *Τίνι ὡμοιώθης ταλαίπωρε*, καὶ τοῦ Ἁγίου δ΄ *Ἔδωκας τὸ σῶμά σου*, *Δόξα*, *Φάλαγγα θεοστεφῆ*, *Καὶ νῦν* Προσόμοιον Θεοτοκίον, Εἴσοδος μετὰ τοῦ Εὐαγγελίου, *Φῶς ἱλαρόν*, τὰ Ἀναγνώσματα τοῦ Τριῳδίου, τὸ *Κατευθυνθήτω* καὶ ἡ τῶν ἄρτων εὐλόγησις· μετὰ τὸ *Πλούσιοι ἐπτώχευσαν*, ὁ Ἀπόστολος καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Ἁγίου καὶ καθεξῆς ἡ Λειτουργία τῶν Προηγιασμένων, Κοινωνικὸν *Εἰς μνημόσυνον*, *Εὐλογήσω τὸν Κύριον* καὶ Ἀπόλυσις.

28.    Εἰ τύχοι τῇ Τρίτῃ τῆς αὐτῆς ἑβδομάδος, ἅπασαν τὴν τῆς Ἀκολουθίας διάταξιν ὅρα ἐν περιπτώσει Η΄ § 13, 14, 15. Ἐννοεῖται ὅμως ὅτι ἐκ τοῦ Τριῳδίου λέγονται τὰ τῆς ἡμέρας.

## February 10: The Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos (Situation XIV: Second Week of Great Lent §§ 26–28; Occurrence Envelope) / February 24: Finding of the Head of the Forerunner [Violakis, p. 405]

### SITUATION FOURTEEN

26.    If the commemoration occurs on Monday of the Second Week of the Great Fast: on Sunday evening at Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried", the penitential Katanyktika hymns of the Tone are not chanted; instead we chant the 3 cycle Prosomia of the Triodion: **"Grant me compunction"** (*Δίδου μοι κατάνυξιν*) and the other two, and 3 Prosomia of St. Charalampos: **"Wholly from thy youth"** (*Ὅλος ἐκ νεότητος*); "Glory...": **"Thou didst sacrifice unto God a sacrifice of praise"** (*Ἔθυσας τῷ Θεῷ*); "Both now and ever...": Prosomion Theotokion; Little Entrance, "O Gladsome Light", and the Great Prokeimenon: **"Thou hast given an inheritance to them that fear Thy name"** (*Ἔδωκας κληρονομίαν*, Ps. 60:6 LXX) chanted thrice with its verses. At the Aposticha: the Idiomelon: **"Come, let us cleanse ourselves"** (*Δεῦτε ἐκκαθάρωμεν*) is chanted slowly twice, with the Martyrikon; "Glory...": **"The noetic adamant"** (*Τὸν νοερὸν ἀδάμαντα*); "Both now and ever...": **"From perils of every kind"** (*Ἐκ παντοίων κινδύνων*); then "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart" [Luke 2:29], the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion of the Saint, the Theotokion, and Dismissal.

The hymn **"O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*) is not chanted, because the Presanctified Liturgy is celebrated on the morrow.

27.    On Monday morning: the Office of Matins of the Saint from the Litiya until the Great Doxology, as well as the Hours, are chanted as prescribed in Situation Eight, Paragraph 14. At Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and "Unto the Lord" [Kathisma 18, Pss. 119–133 LXX], at "Lord, I have cried", we chant the Idiomelon of the Aposticha: **"Keeping bodily fasting"** (*Τὴν σωματικὴν νηστείαν*, twice), and the Martyrikon; 3 cycle Prosomia: **"Unto what hast thou become likened, wretched soul"** (*Τίνι ὡμοιώθης ταλαίπωρε*), and 4 of the Saint: **"Thou didst surrender thy body"** (*Ἔδωκας τὸ σῶμά σου*); "Glory...": **"O God-crowned phalanx"** (*Φάλαγγα θεοστεφῆ*); "Both now and ever...": Prosomion Theotokion; Little Entrance with the Gospel Book, "O Gladsome Light", the Old Testament Readings of the Triodion; **"Let my prayer arise"** [Ps. 140:2 LXX] and the Blessing of the Loaves [Artoklasia] with **"The rich have become poor"** [Ps. 33:11 LXX]; the Epistle and the Gospel of the Saint, and then the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts; Communion Verse: **"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance"** (*Εἰς μνημόσυνον αἰώνιον ἔσται δίκαιος*, Ps. 111:6 LXX), **"I will bless the Lord at all times"** (Ps. 33:2 LXX), and Dismissal.

28.    If the commemoration occurs on Tuesday of this same week: for the entire order of the Office see Situation Eight, Paragraphs 13, 14, and 15. It is understood, however, that the hymns appointed from the Triodion are those of the day.

It should be noted that the commemoration of St. Charalampos may occur within the Triodion period from the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee until Tuesday of the Second Week of the Great Fast, as seen in the Heortodromion Table.
