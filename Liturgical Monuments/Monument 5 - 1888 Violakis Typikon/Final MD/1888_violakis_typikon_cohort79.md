# Holy Week: Great and Holy Friday (Vespers of the Taking Down from the Cross Opening) (§ 84 Opening)

Then the Prophecy (Jeremiah 11:18–12:4, 9–10, and 14–15 [LXX]: The Innocent Lamb Led to the Slaughter); the Epistle (Hebrews 10:19–31: A New and Living Way Opened through the Veil of His Flesh); and the Gospel (John 19:23–37: The Parting of the Garments, "It Is Finished", and the Piercing of His Side). Then the Trisagion Prayers, the Kontakion in Tone 8: **"Come, let us all hymn Him Who was crucified for us..."** (*Τὸν δι' ἡμᾶς σταυρωθέντα*), etc., as at the First Hour; the prayer: **"O Master, Lord Jesus Christ our God..."** (*Δέσποτα Κύριε Ἰησοῦ Χριστέ*); and the Dismissal. Then Vespers begins.

**[THE TAKING DOWN OF THE BODY OF CHRIST FROM THE CROSS / APOKATHELOSIS]**

84.    At Great Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX], at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant the five Idiomela of the Aposticha for six verses: 1. **"All creation was altered with fear when it beheld Thee hanging upon the Cross, O Christ..."** (*Πᾶσα ἡ κτίσις ἠλλοιοῦτο φόβῳ*) (twice); 2. **"Why does the impious and lawless people imagine vain things?..."** (*Λαὸς δυσσεβὴς καὶ παράνομος*); 3. **"Today, seeing Thee, O Word, hanging upon the Cross, the blameless Virgin wept..."** (*Σήμερον σὲ θεωροῦσα ἡ ἄμεμπτος Παρθένος*); 4. **"Beholding Thee, the Creator and God of all, lifted up upon the Tree..."** (*Ἐπὶ ξύλου βλέπουσα κρεμάμενον*); 5. **"Today the Master of creation stands before Pilate..."** (*Σήμερον ὁ Δεσπότης τῆς κτίσεως*). "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 6: **"O how did the lawless assembly condemn the King of creation to death!..."** (*Ὢ πῶς ἡ παράνομος συναγωγή*); "Both now and ever...": Theotokion in Tone 6: **"A fearful and marvelous mystery is seen accomplished today: He Who is untouchable is held fast..."** (*Φοβερὸν καὶ παράδοξον μυστήριον*). The Entrance is made with the Gospel Book; **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρὸν*); and immediately the Readings: Exodus 33:11–23 [LXX] (The Lord Speaks with Moses Face to Face, and Moses Beholds the Glory of God from the Cleft of the Rock); Job 42:12–17 [LXX] (The Lord Blesses the Latter End of Job More than His Beginning); and Isaiah 52:13–54:1 [LXX] (The Fourth Servant Song: "Behold, My Servant Shall Prosper"). The Epistle: 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; 2:1–2 (The Word of the Cross is Foolishness to Those Who Perish, but to Us Who Are Saved It Is the Power of God); and the Gospel: Matthew 27:1–38; Luke 23:39–43; Matthew 27:39–54; John 19:31–37; Matthew 27:55–61 (The Composite Passion Gospel of the Crucifixion, the Death, and the Deposition of the Lord) read from the ambo. [At this point, while the words are read: "Now when evening was come, there came a rich man from Arimathea, named Joseph...", the body of the Lord is taken down from the Cross and wrapped in a clean linen sheet. When the Gospel reading is completed, the Priest receives the Sacred Body and carries It into the Sanctuary]. Then the litanies: **"Let us all say with our whole soul..."** (*Εἴπωμεν πάντες*); **"Vouchsafe, O Lord, to keep us this evening without sin..."** (*Καταξίωσον*); **"Let us complete our evening supplication unto the Lord"** (*Πληρώσωμεν τὴν ἑσπερινὴν δέησιν*). After the exclamation: **"Blessed and glorified be the might of Thy kingdom: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εἴη τὸ κράτος*), the choirs assemble by the northern door of the Sanctuary and begin chanting the Aposticha. Six Priests with heads uncovered (not wearing the kamilavkion), vested in phelonia [and epitrachilia], bearing the Epitaphios [Plashchanytsia] upon their heads with reverence, among whom the Great Archimandrite carries also the Holy Gospel Book in his right hand, exit from the Sanctuary preceded by candles, processional fans [hexapteryga], the chanters, the Dean of the church [Primikirios] with the two-branched candlestick [divamvoulon], and the deacons censing; they advance to the middle of the temple, where the Tomb [Sepulchre / Kuvuklion] stands prepared...[^450]

# Holy Week: Great and Holy Saturday (Matins of the Lamentations: The Epitaphios and Stasis I) (§ 85 Opening)

## Great and Holy Saturday: Matins of the Lamentations (The Epitaphios and Stasis I) (§ 85 Opening) [Violakis, p. 771]

85.    Around the Seventh Hour of the night [1:00 a.m.]⁴⁶ the Amomos [Psalm 118 LXX: "Blessed are the undefiled in the way..."] and the Old Testament readings omitted from the Vespers of Great Saturday are customarily read here.⁴⁷ Then the Priest says: **"Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages"**; the Superior [Proestos]: **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. O Heavenly King, Comforter, the Spirit of truth..."** (*Βασιλεῦ οὐράνιε*); the Reader: the Trisagion Prayers; the Priest: **"For Thine is the kingdom..."**; the Reader: the Troparia: **"Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance..."** (*Σῶσον Κύριε*); **"Glory..."**: **"Thou Who wast voluntarily lifted up upon the Cross, O Christ God..."**; **"Both now and ever..."**: **"O awesome and unashamed protection, all-lauded Theotokos..."**; and the Six Psalms [Ps. 3, 37, 62, 87, 102, and 142 LXX]. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*), the Dismissal Troparia in Tone 2: **"The noble Joseph..."** (*Ὁ εὐσχήμων Ἰωσὴφ*) until **"and laid It for burial"** (*κηδεύσας ἀπέθετο*); **"Glory..."**: Tone 2: **"When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal, then didst Thou slay Hades with the brilliance of Thy Divinity..."** (*Ὅτε κατῆλθες πρὸς τὸν θάνατον*) in its entirety; **"Both now and ever..."**: Tone 2: **"Unto the myrrh-bearing women..."** (*Ταῖς μυροφόροις γυναιξί*) until **"foreign to corruption"** (*ἐδείχθη ἀλλότριος*). Then the Small Litany, and the Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] in Tone 1 is chanted: **"In a clean shroud and with spices, the noble Joseph, taking down Thine all-pure Body from the Tree, wrapped It and laid It in a new tomb; and so early in the morning the myrrh-bearing women cried aloud: Show us, O Christ, Thy Resurrection!"** (*Σινδόνι καθαρᾷ*) once, slowly; **"Glory... Both now and ever..."**: Tone 1: **"The angelic choirs were astounded at the sight of Him Who rests in the bosom of the Father being laid in a tomb as a mortal..."** (*Ἐξέστησαν χοροί*) likewise slowly.⁴⁸ Then Psalm 50 [LXX], and the Canon in Tone 6: **"Of old the surge of the sea covered the pursuing tyrant beneath the waves; but the children of those who were saved bury beneath the earth Him Who was hidden..."** (*Κύματι θαλάσσης*) for six Troparia, the Heirmos being repeated slowly at the end of each Ode as Katavasia. After Ode 3, the Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] in Tone 1: **"Thy tomb, O Savior, the soldiers guarding became as dead men; and the angel sat upon the stone proclaiming the Resurrection unto the women..."** (*Τὸν τάφον σου Σωτήρ*) once, and this also slowly.⁴⁹ After Ode 6, the Small Litany, the Kontakion in Tone 2: **"He Who closed the abyss is beheld dead, and wrapped in fine linen and myrrh, the Immortal One is laid in a tomb as a mortal; and the women came to anoint Him with myrrh, weeping bitterly and crying aloud: This Sabbath is most blessed, in which Christ, having fallen asleep, shall rise again on the third day!"** (*Τὴν ἄβυσσον ὁ κλείσας*); the Ikos; the Menologion; and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the day from the Triodion; then follow Odes 7 and 8 of the Canon. The Deacon exclaims: **"The Theotokos and Mother of the Light let us magnify in hymns!"** (*Τὴν Θεοτόκον καὶ Μητέρα τοῦ Φωτός*), and the Ninth Ode of the Canon is chanted. At the conclusion of Ode 9, the Patriarch, fully vested in all his hierarchical vestments, exits from the Sanctuary preceded by the holy Hierarchs; chanting the opening verse of the Lamentations [Enkomia]: **"Life in the tomb art Thou laid, O Christ, and the angelic hosts were astounded, glorifying Thy condescension"** (*Ἡ ζωὴ ἐν τάφῳ κατετέθης Χριστέ*), he advances toward the Epitaphios [Plashchanytsia] and censes it cross-wise, and afterward censes all the people. The Hierarchs, standing in choir formation, chant the Lamentations of the First Stasis until the Patriarch returns to the throne; then the first Troparion is chanted again.

The Small Litany follows, with the exclamation by the Patriarch:
**"For blessed is Thy Name, and glorified is Thy kingdom: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι ηὐλόγηταί σου τὸ ὄνομα*)

and he begins the Second Stasis: **"It is right to magnify Thee, O Life-Giver..."** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστι μεγαλύνειν σε τὸν Ζωοδότην*) together with the Hierarchs...[^452]

## Great and Holy Saturday: Matins of the Lamentations (Stases II and III, Evlogitaria, and Exaposteilarion) (§ 85 Continued) [Violakis, p. 773]

... The Third Stasis having been completed, its first verse is repeated again. Then the Small Litany, with the exclamation by the Patriarch:
    **"For Thou art the King of peace, and the Savior of our souls, O Christ our God, and unto Thee we send up glory: together with Thine unoriginate Father, and Thine All-Holy, Good, and Life-giving Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Σὺ γὰρ εἶ ὁ Βασιλεὺς τῆς εἰρήνης...*)

When these have been completed, the Small Litany follows, with the exclamation by the Patriarch:
    **"For all the powers of heaven praise Thee, and unto Thee they send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι σὲ αἰνοῦσι πᾶσαι αἱ δυνάμεις τῶν οὐρανῶν...*)

# Holy Week: Great and Holy Saturday (Matins Concluded: Praises, Epitaphios Procession, and Dismissal) (§ 85 Concluded)

## Great and Holy Saturday: Matins Concluded (Praises, Epitaphios Procession, Prophecy, and Dismissal) (§ 85 Concluded) [Violakis, p. 775]

... **"Holy is the Lord our God"** (*Ἅγιος Κύριος ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*) three times (this text only); then **"Let every breath"** and the Praises for four verses: 1. **"Today the tomb holds Him Who holds creation in the palm of His hand..."** (*Σήμερον συνέχει τάφος*); 2. **"What is this sight that we behold? What is this present rest?..."** (*Τί τὸ ὁρώμενον θέαμα;*); 3. **"Come, let us behold our Life lying in the tomb..."** (*Δεῦτε ἴδωμεν τὴν ζωὴν ἡμῶν*); 4. **"Joseph asked for the Body of Jesus, and laid It in his own new tomb..."** (*ᾐτήσατο Ἰωσὴφ τὸ σῶμα τοῦ Ἰησοῦ*). "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 2: **"This present day Moses mystically prefigured, saying: And God blessed the seventh day..."** (*Τὴν σήμερον μυστικῶς ὁ μέγας Μωϋσῆς προδιετυποῦτο λέγων*); "Both now and ever...": Theotokion in Tone 2: **"Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos..."** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη ὑπάρχεις, Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*). The Great Doxology is chanted; at its conclusion, the Asmatikon: **"Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us"** (*Ἅγιος ὁ Θεός*) is chanted slowly by the choirs, who precede the Epitaphios [Plashchanytsia] in solemn procession, exiting from and circling back into the church. The order of the procession is as follows: first the priests lead the way, then follow the chanters, and after them the Patriarch with the Hierarchs bearing the Epitaphios upon their heads; thus is conducted the exit from the temple and likewise the return. Having returned into the church, the Patriarch with the rest of the procession enters the Sanctuary, and immediately exclaims: **"Let us attend! Peace be unto all! Wisdom!"** (*Πρόσχωμεν. Εἰρήνη πᾶσι. Σοφία*). Then he chants the Dismissal Troparion in Tone 2: **"When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal..."** (*Ὅτε κατῆλθες πρὸς τὸν θάνατον*), and censes around the Holy Altar Table, processing in front of the Epitaphios; then the Troparion:⁵¹* **"Unto the myrrh-bearing women..."** (*Ταῖς μυροφόροις γυναιξί*) until **"foreign to corruption"** (*ἐδείχθη ἀλλότριος*); next the Troparion: **"The noble Joseph..."** (*Ὁ εὐσχήμων Ἰωσὴφ*) until **"and laid It for burial"** (*κηδεύσας ἀπέθετο*); and the priests place the Epitaphios upon the Holy Altar Table. The Reader outside the iconostasis reads the Troparion of the Prophecy in Tone 2: **"O Christ, Who holdest fast the ends of the earth, Thou didst deign to be held fast in a narrow tomb..."** (*Ὁ συνέχων τὰ πέρατα*); the Prokimenon in Tone 4: **"Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for Thy Name's sake!"** (*Ἀνάστα, Κύριε, βοήθησον ἡμῖν*, Ps. 43:26 [LXX]); the Prophecy: Ezekiel 37:1–14 [LXX] (The Vision of the Dry Bones in the Valley Brought to Life: "Behold, I Will Open Your Graves, and Cause You to Come Up out of Your Graves, O My People"); the Epistle: 1 Corinthians 5:6–8 and Galatians 3:13–14 (Christ Our Passover is Sacrificed for Us, and Has Redeemed Us from the Curse of the Law); and the Gospel: Matthew 27:62–66 (The Chief Priests and the Pharisees Seal the Tomb and Post the Roman Guard) read by the Patriarch before the Royal Doors [Beautiful Gate]. After **"Many years to you, Master!"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη*), he unvests from his hierarchical vestments,⁵²* puts on his monastic mandya, and holding his pastoral staff exits from the Sanctuary and takes his place upon the throne. In the meantime, the Archdeacon recites the Augmented Litany: **"Let us all say with our whole soul..."** (*Εἴπωμεν πάντες*), and **"Let us complete our morning prayer unto the Lord"** (*Πληρώσωμεν τὴν ἑωθινὴν δέησιν*). The Priest gives the exclamation: **"For Thine it is to show mercy and to save us, O Christ our God..."** (*Σὸν γάρ ἐστι τὸ ἐλεεῖν καὶ σῴζειν ἡμᾶς*), and the Dismissal is given by the Priest (as noted yesterday): **"May Christ, our true God, Who for us men and for our salvation voluntarily endured His terrible Passions, the Cross, and voluntary entombment in the flesh, have mercy on us and save us..."**; and then begins...[^454]
