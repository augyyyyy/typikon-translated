## Order of the Sunday Service of Vespers, Matins, and Liturgy

**1.** On Saturday evening, after the Ninth Hour, the Priest having given the blessing,¹[^124] the presiding Bishop (or the Superior) recites the Introductory Psalm [Psalm 103 LXX]; then the Deacon (or in his absence the Priest) intones the Great Litany: **"In peace let us pray to the Lord"** (*Ἐν εἰρήνῃ τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*); and the Priest chants the ecphonesis: **"For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages"** (*Ὅτι πρέπει σοι πᾶσα δόξα*); and the entire First Kathisma of the Psalter [Psalms 1–8 LXX, *Blessed is the man*] is read by the appointed Reader. After this the Deacon intones the Little Litany: **"Again and again, in peace let us pray to the Lord"** (*Ἔτι καὶ ἔτι*); the Priest chants the ecphonesis: **"For Thine is the dominion, and Thine is the Kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages"** (*Ὅτι σὸν τὸ κράτος*).

After this the Choirs begin to chant Psalms 140 and 141 LXX [141 and 142 MT], that is, **"Lord, I have cried unto Thee, hear me"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα πρὸς σέ*) and **"With my voice unto the Lord have I cried"** (*Φωνῇ μου πρὸς Κύριον ἐκέκραξα*), in the Tone of the day up to the verse: **"Deliver me from them that persecute me, for they are stronger than I"** (*Ῥῦσαί με ἐκ τῶν καταδιωκόντων με*). From the verse: **"Bring my soul out of prison"** (*Ἐξάγαγε ἐκ φυλακῆς τὴν ψυχήν μου*), the seven Resurrection Stichira of the Tone and the three Prosomoia of the occurring Saint of the day from the Menaion are sung together with the remaining verses:

For the first Stichiron, the verse:  
**"Bring my soul out of prison, that I may confess Thy Name."** (*Ἐξάγαγε ἐκ φυλακῆς τὴν ψυχήν μου, τοῦ ἐξομολογήσασθαι τῷ ὀνόματί σου*, Ps. 141:8 LXX [142:7 MT]).  
For the second:  
**"The righteous shall wait for me, until Thou shalt recompense me."** (*Ἐμὲ ὑπομενοῦσι δίκαιοι, ἕως οὗ ἀνταποδῷς μοι*, Ps. 141:8 LXX [142:7 MT]).  
For the third:

## The Sunday Midnight Office

**2.** On Sunday at the morning service [the nocturnal Midnight Office], the Priest having given the blessing [**"Blessed is our God..."**], the Superior says: **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. O Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of Truth..."** (*Βασιλεῦ οὐράνιε, Παράκλητε*). The Reader recites the Trisagion Prayers; the Priest: **"For Thine is the Kingdom..."** The Reader: **"Lord, have mercy"** (twelve times), **"Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** **"O come, let us worship and fall down before God our King"** (thrice), and Psalm 50 [Psalm 51 MT] is read. Then the Triadic [Trinitarian] Canon of the Tone is sung; and if the Saint of the day has a Liti, the Liti stichira are appended to the Triadic Canon; if not, immediately after the Triadic Canon the following four Triadic Megalynaria are sung:  
**"It is truly meet to hymn the transcendent Trinity."** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν ὡς ἀληθῶς τὴν ὑπέρθεον ὑμνεῖν Τριάδα*).  
**"It is truly meet to glorify Thee, O God the Word."** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν ὡς ἀληθῶς τοῦ δοξάζειν σε τὸν Θεὸν Λόγον*).

## Concerning Matins

The Priest having given the blessing, the Reader recites the Trisagion Prayers. The Priest: **"For Thine is the Kingdom, and the power, and the glory..."**; the Reader recites the Royal Troparia: **"O Lord, save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance..."** (*Σῶσον, Κύριε*); **"Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit"**, **"O Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross of Thine own will..."** (*Ὁ ὑψωθεὶς ἐν τῷ Σταυρῷ*); **"Both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen"**, **"O awesome and unashamed Protection, overlook not our supplications, O good and all-lauded Theotokos..."** (*Προστασία φοβερά*). The Priest intones: **"Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee: hear us and have mercy"**; and the ecphonesis: **"For a merciful and loving God art Thou, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages"**; the Choir: **"{Amen.} In the Name of the Lord, Father, bless"**; the Priest intones: **"Glory to the Holy, consubstantial, life-creating, and undivided Trinity, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages"**; the Choir responds by chanting: **"Amen"**; and the Superior begins the Six Psalms [Psalms 3, 37, 62, 87, 102, 142 LXX], all present listening in total silence and compunction, and let no one either by coughing or by any other disturbance disrupt the prayerful compunction, as the ancient Typika enjoin.

After the Six Psalms, the Priest intones the Great Litany and chants the ecphonesis: **"For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship..."** And thus the Choirs begin chanting four times: (1) **"God is the Lord, and hath appeared unto us; blessed is He that cometh in the Name of the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος καὶ ἐπέφανεν ἡμῖν*) in the Tone of the day, with the appointed verses, beginning from the second **"God is the Lord"**:  
(2) **"O give thanks unto the Lord, and call upon His holy Name."** (*Ἐξομολογεῖσθε τῷ Κυρίῳ, καὶ ἐπικαλεῖσθε τὸ ὄνομα τὸ ἅγιον αὐτοῦ*, Ps. 117:1 LXX [118:1 MT]).  
(3) **"All nations compassed me about, and in the Name of the Lord I warded them off."** (*Πάντα τὰ ἔθνη ἐκύκλωσάν με, καὶ τῷ ὀνόματι Κυρίου ἠμυνάμην αὐτούς*, Ps. 117:10 LXX [118:10 MT]).  
(4) **"This is the Lord's doing, and it is marvelous in our eyes."** (*Παρὰ Κυρίου ἐγένετο αὕτη, καὶ ἔστι θαυμαστὴ ἐν ὀφθαλμοῖς ἡμῶν*, Ps. 117:23 LXX [118:23 MT]).  

Then follow the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers, but the first one is repeated; if, however, the Saint of the day is celebrated, we chant the Resurrection Dismissal Troparion once, then **"Glory to the Father..."** the Dismissal Troparion of the Saint, **"Both now and ever..."** the Theotokion as at Vespers. After the Dismissal Troparia, the Psalter kathisma selections and the Polyeleos are read (if the Saint of the day is celebrated), but if not, then the Amomos [Psalm 118 LXX] is read.[^127]
