# Great Lent: Order of Lenten Vespers and Presanctified Opening (§§ 20–21 Opening)

## Order of Lenten Vespers (Aliturgical) and Presanctified Opening (§§ 20–21 Opening) [Violakis, p. 687]

20.    At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant three Prosomia of the Triodion and three of the Saint of the following day from the Menaion; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Prosomion Theotokion (or Stavrotheotokion on Tuesday or Thursday evening); **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), the Prokimena and Old Testament Readings of the day; **"Vouchsafe, O Lord"** (*Καταξίωσον, Κύριε*); **"Let us complete our evening prayer"** (*Πληρώσωμεν τὴν ἑσπερινὴν δέησιν*); the Exclamation: **"Blessed and glorified be the majesty of Thy kingdom..."** (*Εἴη τὸ κράτος*); and the Idiomelon of the Aposticha twice¹² and the Martyrikon once; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Theotokion (or Stavrotheotokion); **"Now Thou dost dismiss Thy servant"** (*Νῦν ἀπολύεις*); the Trisagion Prayers; **"Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*) etc., sung with melody; **"Beneath thy tender compassion"** (*Ὑπὸ τὴν σὴν εὐσπλαγχνίαν*) read in a recitativo (*chuma*); **"Lord, have mercy"** (40 times); "Glory... Both now and ever..."; **"More honorable than the Cherubim"** (*Τὴν τιμιωτέραν τῶν Χερουβίμ*); **"In the name of the Lord, bless, father"** (*Ἐν ὀνόματι Κυρίου, εὐλόγησον, πάτερ*); the Priest exclaims: **"He Who is, Christ our God, is blessed..."** (*Ὁ ὢν εὐλογητός*); the Reader: **"O Heavenly King, strengthen our faithful civil authorities..."** (*Ἐπουράνιε Βασιλεῦ*); then the three great prostrations with the Prayer of Saint Ephraim the Syrian; then the Trisagion Prayers again, **"Lord, have mercy"** (12 times), and the prayer: **"All-Holy Trinity, one-natured Might..."** (*Παναγία Τριάς, τὸ ὁμοούσιον κράτος*); **"Blessed be the name of the Lord"** (*Εἴη τὸ ὄνομα Κυρίου*) thrice; "Glory... Both now and ever..."; Psalm 33 [LXX]: **"I will bless the Lord at all times"** (*Εὐλογήσω τὸν Κύριον ἐν παντὶ καιρῷ*); and the Dismissal.

    This order of the Service of Vespers is performed when there is no Presanctified Liturgy. But when the Presanctified Liturgy is celebrated, then after the prayer **"All-Holy Trinity, one-natured Might..."**, the Priest makes the Dismissal of Matins and immediately exclaims: **"Blessed is the kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογημένη ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Πατρὸς . . .*); and Vespers with the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts begins in the following manner:

**The Vespers with the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts**

21.    The Deacon (if there is one): **"Bless, Master!"** (*Εὐλόγησον, Δέσποτα*). The Priest: **"Blessed is the kingdom of the Father..."** (*Εὐλογημένη ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Πατρὸς . . .*). And the Proestos reads the Introductory Psalm (Ps. 103 [LXX])...[^410]

22.    If an officially commemorated Saint falls on fasting days: if on Wednesday, at Vespers the following are chanted: the Idiomelon of the day twice, the Martyrikon once, the three Prosomia of the sequence of the Triodion, and the four Prosomia of the commemorated Saint (not the Saint of the following day). After **"Let my prayer arise"** (*Κατευθυνθήτω*), the Prokimenon, Epistle [Apostolos], and Gospel of the commemorated Saint are read, and the Communion Hymn (*Κοινωνικόν*) is chanted: **"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.     If the commemoration falls on a Friday, we chant the Idiomelon of the day twice, the four Martyrika of the Tone, and the four Stichera of the Saint; then "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": the first Theotokion of the Tone of the week; and the rest of the service proceeds as above. We note the following two exceptions: 1) On Friday of the First Week of Lent (commemoration of Saint Theodore the Tyro and the miracle of the Kolyva), after the Idiomelon twice, four Stichera of Saint Theodore and four of the commemorated Saint are chanted; 2) On Friday of the Fifth Week of Lent (eve of the Akathist Saturday), after the Idiomelon twice, four Stichera of the Theotokos: **"Counsel eternal"** (*Βουλὴν προαιώνιον*) and four of the appointed commemorated Saint are chanted.
    If a commemorated Saint falls on Monday, Tuesday, or Thursday, the Idiomelon of the Matins Aposticha of the day is chanted twice, the Martyrikon once, three Prosomia of the sequence from the Triodion, and four Stichera of the commemorated Saint; "Glory...": of the Saint; "Both now and ever...": Prosomion Theotokion; and the remainder of the Presanctified Liturgy proceeds as outlined above.
