Εἰς τὸ Ἐξαιρέτως, Ἄξιόν ἐστιν, Κοινωνικὸν Εἰς μνημόσυνον αἰώνιον, Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς καὶ Ἀπόλυσις.

## July 11: The Holy, Glorious Great-Martyr and All-Praised Euphemia (Heading, §§ 1–3, Patriarchal Note) [Violakis, p. 603]

### July 11: The Holy, Glorious Great-Martyr and All-Praised Euphemia

1.    In Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and the First Stasis of the Psalter [Kathisma 1, Pss. 1–8 LXX], at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*, Ps. 140 LXX), we chant the 6 Prosomoia Stichira and the "Glory..." {of the Saint}; "Both now and ever...": **"Who would not call Thee blessed"** (*Τίς μὴ μακαρίσει σε*). Little Entrance, **"Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), the Prokeimenon, and the Old Testament Readings (these are found on July 27). At the Aposticha: the 3 Prosomoia Stichira; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"O Theotokos, Thou art the vine... with the prize-bearer"** (*Θεοτόκε σὺ εἶ ἡ ἄμπελος . . . μετὰ τῆς ἀθληφόρου*). The Dismissal Troparion: **"Thou didst greatly gladden"** (*Λίαν εὔφρανας*); "Both now and ever...": **"Thou who didst intercede for us"** (*Σὲ τὴν μεσιτεύσασαν*), and Dismissal.

2.    At Matins, after Psalm 50 [LXX], the Litiya; then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion of the Saint, and the Six Psalms. At "God is the Lord": the Dismissal Troparion twice and the Theotokion once; then the Psalter reading and the Polyeleos. The Canon of the Theotokos: **"Traversing the deep water"** (*Ὑγρὰν διοδεύσας*) and that of the Saint. After Ode 3, the Sessional Hymn of the Saint and the Theotokion: **"We shall not be silent"** (*Οὐ σιωπήσομεν*); after Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Saint, and the Menologion. The Katavasiai: **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*); then we chant the Magnifications: **"More honorable than the Cherubim"** (*Τὴν τιμιωτέραν*). The Exapostilarion of the Saint and the Theotokion. At the Praises: the 3 Prosomoia Stichira of the Saint in 4 [repeating the first one]; "Glory...": **"Let every tongue be stirred"** (*Πᾶσα γλῶσσα κινείσθω*); "Both now and ever...": **"Sovereign Lady, accept"** (*Δέσποινα πρόσδεξαι*); Great Doxology, and the Dismissal Troparion.

3.    In the Divine Liturgy, the Typika and the Beatitudes from the Third and Sixth Odes of the Canon of the Saint. After the Little Entrance, the Dismissal Troparia: of the Saint, of the Patron Saint of the Church, and the Kontakion: **"Protection of Christians"** (*Προστασία τῶν Χριστιανῶν*). Epistle of the Saint: **"Brethren, working together with Him, we entreat you"** (*Ἀδελφοί, συνεργοῦντες παρακαλοῦμεν*, 2 Cor. 6:1; see the 16th Sunday), and the Gospel of the Saint: **"At that time, one of the Pharisees asked Him"** (*Τῷ καιρῷ ἐκείνῳ, ἠρώτα τις τῶν Φαρισαίων*, Luke 7:36; see Tuesday of the 4th Week of Luke). At "Especially" [Megalynarion], **"It is truly right"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*); Communion Hymn: **"In everlasting remembrance shall the righteous be"** (*Εἰς μνημόσυνον αἰώνιον*, Ps. 111:6 LXX); **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς*), and Dismissal.
    This is the order celebrated at the Great Church, where the holy relics of the Saint are kept in the Chapel of the Pammakaristos.[^368]
