# Great Lent: Monday of the First Week (Matins and Presanctified Order, §§ 18–19 Opening)

## Monday of the First Week of the Fast [Lent]: Order of Matins and Vespers, with the Liturgy of the Presanctified (§§ 18–19 Opening) [Violakis, p. 677]

18.    On Monday of the First Week at Matins, the Priest enters the Holy Sanctuary, vests in the Epitrachilion, and taking the censer, stands before the Holy Table and, censing cross-wise, says: **"Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν πάντοτε νῦν καὶ ἀεὶ καὶ εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων, Ἀμήν*). The Reader recites the Trisagion Prayers. The Priest exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom"** (*Ὅτι σοῦ ἐστιν*). The Reader: **"Lord, have mercy"** (*Κύριε, ἐλέησον*) 12 times. And immediately the Priest proclaims: **"Glory to the holy, consubstantial, life-creating, and undivided Trinity..."** (*Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ καὶ ὁμοουσίῳ . . .*). The Proestos [Superior or Leader] reads the Six Psalms [Exapsalmos] (the people listening in all silence and reverence); and the remainder of Matins follows as prescribed below.

19. Immediately at its conclusion, we recite the Penitential Troparia: **"Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us"** (*Ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς, Κύριε, ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς*) and the remaining two Troparia from the Horologion.
