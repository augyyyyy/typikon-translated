Following the initial Megalynarion *Ἀκατάληπτόν ἐστι* (inaugurated on leaf p380), the ninth ode of the Canon of the Meeting (composed by Saint Cosmas of Maiuma and embellished by the Byzantine sticheraric tradition) presents an alphabetical series of ten nine-syllable/twelve-syllable Megalynaria addressed alternately to the Theotokos, Christ, and the Righteous Symeon: (1) concluding verse of initial incipit: *Ἀγγέλοις καὶ βροτοῖς, Μητροπάρθενε ἁγνή* ["...unto angels and mortal men, O pure Virgin Mother"]; (2) *Ἀγκαλίζεται χερσίν, ὁ Πρεσβύτης Συμεών* ["Symeon the Elder takes in his arms the Maker of the Law and Master of all"]; (3) *Βουληθεὶς ὁ Πλαστουργός, ἵνα σώσῃ τὸν Ἀδάμ* ["The Creator, wishing to save Adam, took up His dwelling in thy pure and virgin womb"]; (4) *Γένος ἅπαν τῶν βροτῶν, μακαρίζει σε Ἁγνή* ["All the race of mortals blesseth thee, O Pure Virgin, and in faith glorifieth thee as Mother of God"]; (5) *Δεῦτε ἴδετε Χριστόν, τὸν Δεσπότην τοῦ παντός* ["Come and behold Christ, the Master of all, Whom Symeon beareth today in the temple"]; (6) *Ἐπιβλέπεις πρὸς τὴν γῆν, καὶ ποιεῖς τρέμειν αὐτήν* ["Thou lookest upon the earth and makest it tremble; how then can I, aged and weary, hold Thee in mine arms?"]; (7) *Ζήσας ἔτη Συμεών, ἕως εἶδε τὸν Χριστόν* ["Symeon lived for many years until he beheld Christ, and cried aloud unto Him: 'Now do I seek release'"]; (8) *Ἡ λαβὶς ἡ μυστική, ἡ τὸν ἄνθρακα Χριστόν* ["Thou art the mystic tongs, Mary, who hast conceived in thy womb Christ the live Coal" — drawing upon Isaiah 6:6]; (9) *Θέλων ἐνηνθρώπησας, ὁ προάναρχος Θεός* ["Of Thine own will Thou didst become man, O God without beginning, and art brought into the temple, forty days of age"]; (10) *Κατελθόντ’ ἐξ οὐρανοῦ, τὸν Δεσπότην τοῦ παντός* ["Symeon the priest received the Master of all, Who came down from heaven"]. Rubrical expansion of the 4 Troparia of Ode 9 to 6 with their proper appointed Megalynaria: Troparion 1 *Ἐν νόμῳ σκιᾷ* with Megalynarion *Λάμπρυνόν μου τὴν ψυχήν*; Troparion 2 with Megalynarion *Μητροπάρθενε ἁγνή, τί προσφέρεις*; Troparion 3 *Τοῖς πρὶν νεογνῶν* with Megalynarion *Νῦν ἀπόλυσιν ζητῶ*; Troparion 4 with Megalynarion *Ὃν οἱ ἄνω λειτουργοί*; Doxastikon (*Δόξα*) with Triadikon *Ἡ τῇ φύσει μὲν μονάς* ["O Thou Who art by nature One, but in Persons Three..."] paired with Megalynarion *Ἀπέδωκάς μοι*; and Theotokion (*Καὶ νῦν*) *Θεοτόκε ἡ ἐλπίς* ["O Theotokos, Hope of all Christians, shelter, guard, and preserve those whose hope is in thee..."] paired with Megalynarion *Ἱεροπρεπῶς ἀνθωμολογεῖτο*.
