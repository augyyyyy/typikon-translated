[^510]: Ceremony of the Return of a Patriarch to the Ecumenical Throne: Arrival, Reception, and Supplications (§ Return of a Patriarch 1). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p901 (printed page 886) and facing English leaf p902 (printed page 887). The Constantinopolitan ceremonial order for the reinstallation and return (Epanodos) of the Ecumenical Patriarch to the patriarchal throne: Arriving at the Patriarchate with his customary entourage, the Patriarch halts before the Great Portal of the cathedral church and vests in the Mandya. The accompanying Hierarchs enter directly into the Holy Sanctuary (Bema). Preceded by the Primikirios carrying the double-candled Divamboulon and four deacons (two of whom cense the Patriarch as he enters), the Choirs chant "It is truly meet to bless thee, O Theotokos...". The Patriarch stands in the midst of the church and imparts a blessing, but without carrying the patriarchal staff. Two Hierarchs escort him to ascend the Patriarchal Cathedra; the Metropolitan of Herakleia approaches, delivers the traditional greeting, bestows the Patriarchal Staff upon him, kisses his right hand, and enters the Sanctuary. The Great Logothete stands at the Parathrone. The Great Ecclesiarch (Grand Master of Ceremonies) invests the Patriarch with Epitrachilion and Omophorion. The Great Archdeacon exclaims "Bless, Master", the Patriarch intones the blessing, the Archdeacon reads the Trisagion Prayers, and the Patriarch gives the Exclamation. The First Choir chants the Dismissal Troparion of Saint George in Tone 4 ("As the liberator of captives..."), and the Second Choir chants the Theotokion ("Open unto us the gate of compassion..."). The Great Archdeacon intones the Ektenia of Fervent Supplication ("Have mercy on us, O God..."), with petitions for all devout Orthodox Christians, for the Archbishop, and for preservation of the city and temple. The Patriarch intones the Exclamation "Hear us, O God our Savior...", the Protopsaltes sings the Patriarchal Polychronion, and the customary allocution is delivered from the Ambo.

[^511]: Ceremony of the Return of a Patriarch: Conclusion and Final Dismissal (§ Return of a Patriarch 2). Conclusion of the ceremonial of patriarchal return: As the allocution concludes, the Choirs chant the episcopal acclamation "Our Master and Chief Priest, O Lord, preserve for many years!", while the Patriarch imparts his blessing upon the clergy and faithful. From within the Holy Sanctuary (Bema), the clergy chant the concluding prayer: "Through the prayers of our holy Master, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us and save us. Amen.", concluding the solemn office of the Patriarch's return to the Ecumenical Cathedra.

[^512]: Ceremony of the Indiction in the Great Church on September 1: Assembly, Troparia, and Litany (§ Ceremony of the Indiction 1). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p905 (printed page 890) and facing English leaf p906 (printed page 891). The solemn ecclesiastical order for the beginning of the New Church Year (Indiction) on September 1 in the Ecumenical Patriarchate: On September 1, the Patriarch descends to the patriarchal cathedral church for Vespers, and on the morning of the feast attends Matins and the Divine Liturgy, seated at the Parathrone wearing the monastic Epanokalymmavchion and holding the unadorned staff (Chazranion). Following the Divine Liturgy, the synodal Hierarchs withdraw from the Sanctuary into the Great Synodikon. After the distribution of the Antidoron to the faithful, the Patriarch proceeds in solemn procession to the Great Synodikon, preceded by the processional Exapteryga (seraphic fans), processional torches, the Primikirios bearing the Divamboulon, and the Choirs of Chanters. In the Synodikon, the Patriarch vests in Epitrachilion and Omophorion. The Great Archdeacon exclaims "Bless, Master", the Patriarch gives the blessing "Blessed is our God...", the Archdeacon recites the Trisagion Prayers, and the Patriarch gives the Exclamation. The First Choir chants the Dismissal Troparion of the Indiction in Tone 2 ("O Creator of the universe..."), the Second Choir chants the Dismissal Troparion of Saint Simeon the Stylite in Tone 1 ("Thou hast become a pillar of endurance..."), and the First Choir chants the Theotokion in Tone 1 ("Rejoice, full of grace, Virgin Theotokos..."). The Great Archdeacon and Second Deacon alternate in chanting the petitions of the Fervent Ektenia for all Orthodox Christians, for the Archbishop, for deliverance of the church and city from every calamity, and for the hearing of prayer, answered by threefold responses of "Lord, have mercy" by the Choirs. The Patriarch pronounces the Exclamation "Hear us, O God our Savior...", leading to the Dismissal.

[^513]: Ceremony of the Indiction Concluded: Synodal Act, Signatures, and Hand-Kissing (§ Ceremony of the Indiction 2). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p907 (printed page 892) and facing English leaf p908 (printed page 893). Solemn execution of the Synodal Act of the Indiction: As the Dismissal concludes, the Choirs chant "Our Master and Chief Priest, O Lord, preserve for many years!". The Ecumenical Patriarch reads aloud the official Synodal Act (Praxis) of the Indiction, promulgating the entrance into the new ecclesiastical year and imparting synodal absolution and blessing upon all devout and Orthodox Christians of every rank and degree throughout the world. Following the reading, the Choirs chant the Patriarchal Polychronion. The Patriarch signs the formal registry of the Indiction, followed by all the members of the Holy Synod in order of episcopal seniority, all vested in Epitrachilion and Omophorion. During the signing, the Choirs chant the festal Idiomelon hymns appointed for the Indiction. Following the signing, all hierarchs, clergy, dignitaries, and faithful approach to kiss the right hand of His All-Holiness, concluding the patriarchal rite of the New Year.

[^514]: Horologion of the Great Church for the Beginning and Ending of Services of the Great Feasts (§ Feasts Horologion 1). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p909 (printed page 894) and facing English leaf p910 (printed page 895). Ancient Byzantine horary schedule governing patriarchal celebrations and bell-tolling in the Great Church of Constantinople: The rubric establishes the temporal coordination between the bell-ringing (simantron/peal), the descent of the Patriarch from the patriarchal apartments into the cathedral, and the final Dismissal for the major winter dominical solemnities. The ancient Byzantine system calculates twenty-four hours beginning at sunset (twelve hours of night followed by twelve hours of day). On December 24 (Christmas Eve), when the Royal Hours are conjoined to the Liturgy of Saint Basil, the bell tolls at the 12th Hour of the Night (6:00 a.m.), the Patriarch descends at the 1st Hour of the Day (7:00 a.m.), and the Dismissal occurs at the 4th Hour of the Day (10:00 a.m.); if the Hours are chanted alone (on Saturday or Sunday Paramony), bells toll at the 1st Hour of the Day (7:00 a.m.), the Patriarch descends at the 2nd Hour (8:00 a.m.), and Dismissal occurs at the 4th Hour (10:00 a.m.). On the Nativity of Christ (December 25), Matins bells toll at the 9th Hour of the Night (3:00 a.m.), the Patriarch descends at the 10th Hour and a half of the Night (4:30 a.m.), and the Divine Liturgy ends at the 1st Hour and a half of the Day (7:30 a.m.). On January 1 (Circumcision and Saint Basil), Matins bells toll at the 1st Hour of the Day (7:00 a.m.), the Patriarch descends at the 2nd Hour and a half (8:30 a.m.), and Dismissal occurs at the 4th Hour and a half (10:30 a.m.). On January 5 (Theophany Eve), when Hours are joined to Saint Basil's Liturgy, bells toll at the 11th Hour of the Night (5:00 a.m.), the Patriarch descends at the 12th Hour of the Night (6:00 a.m.), and Dismissal occurs at the 3rd Hour of the Day (9:00 a.m.); if the Hours are chanted alone, bells toll at the 1st Hour of the Day (7:00 a.m.), the Patriarch descends at the 2nd Hour (8:00 a.m.), and Dismissal occurs at the 4th Hour (10:00 a.m.).
