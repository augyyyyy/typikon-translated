Small Compline of Lazarus: Paragraph 60 details Small Compline on Friday evening: After "It is truly meet" (Axion estin), the Canon of Lazarus by Saint Cosmas the Melodist is sung with the refrain "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee!". (Hē pantōn chara Christos), "Lord, have mercy" (40 times), the Prayer of the Hours: "Thou Who at all times and at every hour...", and the nocturnal prayer to the Mother of God: "O spotless, undefiled, incorrupt, unblemished, pure Virgin..." (Aspile, amolynte). Dismissal with the Litany of Mutual Forgiveness: "Let us pray for the peace of the world...", and "Through the prayers of our holy fathers...". Saturday Matins Opening: Paragraph 61 opens Matins of Lazarus Saturday: After Psalm 50, the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion in Tone 1: "In confirming the common Resurrection before Thy Passion, Thou didst raise Lazarus from the dead, O Christ God..." Lazarus Matins Concluded: Paragraph 61 concludes Matins of Lazarus Saturday: Following the Small Litany after the Evlogitaria, the second Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] from the Triodion in Tone 5 is chanted: "The fountain of wisdom and foreknowledge..." (Hē pēgē tēs sophias) twice; and immediately "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ..." (Anastasin Christou theasamenoi) and Psalm 50 recited plain (chyma). Both Canons of Lazarus (the first by Saint Theophanes the Branded and the second by Saint Cosmas the Melodist) are chanted through Ode 6 with the refrain "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee!". After Ode 3, the Sessional Hymns from the Triodion; after Ode 5, the Four-Ode Canon [Tetraodion] of Ode 6, followed by the Kontakion in Tone 2: "Christ Who is the Truth and Joy of all..." (Hē pantōn chara) and its Oikos: "Since Thou didst bind Hades, O Immortal One...". The Menologion from the Menaion and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the Triodion detailing the raising of Lazarus four days dead as the harbinger of Christ's Resurrection and the general resurrection of mankind. Katavasiai: "Having traversed the water as if it were dry land..." (Ygran diodevsas). In place of "More honorable than the Cherubim", Ode 9 of both Canons is chanted with their appointed verses, and the Katavasia: "Confessing thee in truth to be the Mother of God..." (Kyriōs Theotokon). Then "Holy is the Lord our God" twice (the Psalm verse "Exalt ye the Lord our God..." is omitted). Exapostilaria: "By Thy word, O Word of God, Lazarus now leapeth forth..." (Logō sou Loge tou Theou) twice, and "Through Lazarus hath Christ already despoiled thee, O Death..." (Dia Lazarou se Christos) once. At the Praises [Lauds], eight Idiomela in Tone 1: 1. "Christ, the Resurrection and Life of mankind...", 2. "Thou didst raise Lazarus four days dead from Hades, O Christ...", 3. "Martha and Mary said unto the Savior...", 4. "Confirming the reality of Thy divine nature, O Christ...", 5. "After four days Thou didst raise Thy friend in Bethany, O Christ...", 6. "Martha cried aloud unto Mary: The Teacher is present!...", 7. "Lazarus that was dead four days didst Thou raise up in Bethany...", 8. "Even as Thou saidst unto Martha, O Lord: I am the Resurrection...". "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 2: "A great and marvelous wonder is fulfilled today!..." (Mega kai paradoxon thauma); "Both now and ever...": "Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos...". Great Doxology, and the Sunday Troparion "Today salvation is come unto the world" (Sēmeron sōtēria). Divine Liturgy of Lazarus Saturday: Paragraph 62 codifies the Divine Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom on Lazarus Saturday: Typika (Pss. 102 and 145 LXX) and Beatitudes [Makarismoi] with Troparia from Ode 3 of the first Canon and Ode 6 of the second Canon. After the Little Entrance, the Entrance Troparion: "O come, let us worship and fall down before Christ! Save us, O Son of God, Who didst rise from the dead, who sing unto Thee: Alleluia!", the Dismissal Troparion: "In confirming the common Resurrection...", and the Kontakion: "Christ Who is the Truth and Joy of all...". In place of the Trisagion Hymn: the baptismal proclamation "As many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ. Alleluia!" (Gal. 3:27), witnessing to the ancient practice of conferring Holy Baptism on Lazarus Saturday. Epistle pericope: Hebrews 12:28–13:8 LXX (Wherefore we receiving a kingdom which cannot be moved...); Gospel pericope: John 11:1–45 (The Raising of Lazarus). At "Especially [among the all-holy]...", the Heirmos of Ode 9: "With all peoples let us come and glorify the pure Theotokos..." (Tēn hagnēn endoxōs timēsōmen). Communion Hymn [Koinōnikon]: "Out of the mouths of babes and sucklings hast Thou perfected praise. Alleluia!" (Ps. 8:2 LXX).

Palm Sunday Matins: Paragraph 64 details the solemn order of Matins on the Great Feast of Palm Sunday (The Entry of our Lord into Jerusalem): Following the nocturnal Litē, the Trisagion Prayers, the second Dismissal Troparion in Tone 4: "Buried with Thee through Baptism, O Christ our God..." (Sēmeron ho Christos eiserchetai en polei Bēthania); "Both now and ever...": the same; then the verse Ps. 50:1 LXX ("Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy"), and the Idiomelon in Tone 6: "Today the grace of the Holy Spirit hath gathered us together...". Canon of Palm Sunday: the renowned acrostic iambic and trochaic masterpiece by Saint Cosmas the Melodist in Tone 4: "The springs of the deep appeared dry..." (Ōphthēsan hai pēgai tēs abyssou) in six Troparia without biblical verses, except "Glory... Both now and ever..." at the final two Troparia. In place of the Exaposteilarion, "Holy is the Lord our God" is chanted three times in Tone 4. The Superior [Proestos] then solemnly reads the Prayer over the Palms and Branches: "O Lord our God, Who sittest upon the Cherubim...". Immediately the First Choir begins "Let every breath praise the Lord" (Ps. 150:6 LXX) and the Praises [Lauds].
