At "Lord, I have cried" (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant the six Stichera of the Octoechos in Tone 5: "Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant unto us remission of sins; for Thou alone art He Who didst manifest the Resurrection unto the world!" (*Τὰς ἑσπερινὰς ἡμῶν εὐχὰς*) in order; "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 5: "Him Who is co-eternal with the Father and the Spirit, let us praise..." (*Τὸν τῷ Πατρὶ συνάναρχον*); "Both now and ever...": Dogmatic Theotokion in Tone 1: "The Joy of all the world, who sprang forth from mankind and gave birth unto the Master..." (*Τὴν παγκόσμιον δόξαν*). Entrance with censer, and the Prokimenon of the day: "Thy mercy, O Lord, shall pursue me all the days of my life!" (*Τὸ ἔλεός σου, Κύριε*, Ps. 22:6 LXX). At the Aposticha: the Resurrectional Apostichon Sticheron in Tone 5: "By Thy Passion, O Christ, hast Thou darkened the sun, and by the light of Thy Resurrection hast Thou enlightened all things..." (*Τῷ πάθει σου Χριστέ*), then the Stichera of Pascha: "A sacred Pascha is shown forth unto us today..." (*Πάσχα ἱερόν*) with their verses; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 5: "It is the day of Resurrection: let us be radiant for the festival, and let us embrace one another!..." (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*), then "Christ is risen from the dead..." (three times), and Dismissal. Paragraph 36: Wednesday morning Matins of the Apodosis of Pascha: After the Midnight Office of Pascha (see Paragraph 2, Note 8), the Priest censing in front of the Holy Table exclaims: "Glory to the holy, consubstantial, life-creating, and indivisible Trinity, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.", and chants "Christ is risen from the dead..." (*Χριστὸς ἀνέστη*) as yesterday; then the Litany, and the Paschal Canon is chanted with four Troparia for each ode including the Katavasia. Then we chant "Christ is risen from the dead..." (three times), the hymn: "Jesus, having arisen from the tomb, as He foretold, hath given unto us life eternal and great mercy!" (*Ἀναστὰς ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἀπὸ τοῦ τάφου*), the Litany, and the Exclamation at the end of each Ode. After the 3rd Ode, the Ypakoe: "Coming before the dawn, Mary and the women found the stone rolled away from the tomb..." (*Προλαβοῦσαι τὸν Ὄρθρον*) (recited plainly); after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion: "Though Thou didst descend into the grave, O Immortal One..." and the Ikos of Pascha, then "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ, let us worship the holy Lord Jesus..." (*Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ θεασάμενοι*) (three times), and "Jesus, having arisen from the tomb..." (*Ἀναστὰς ὁ Ἰησοῦς*); then the 7th and 8th Odes of the Canon are chanted. Then the deacon or priest proclaims: "The Theotokos and Mother of the Light, honoring her in hymns, let us magnify!" (*Τὴν Θεοτόκον καὶ Μητέρα τοῦ Φωτός*), and the 9th Ode is chanted with the Megalynaria of Pascha. The Exaposteilarion: "Having slept in the flesh as a mortal, O King and Lord, on the third day Thou didst arise..." (*Σαρκὶ ὑπνώσας ὡς θνητός*) (three times). At the Praises: the four Resurrectional Stichera in Tone 1: "We praise Thy saving Passion, O Christ, and glorify Thy Resurrection!" (*Ὑμνοῦμέν σου Χριστέ*) and the Stichera of Pascha: "A sacred Pascha is shown forth unto us today..." (*Πάσχα ἱερόν*) with their verses; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 5: "It is the day of Resurrection: let us be radiant for the festival..." (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*), then "Christ is risen from the dead..." (three times), and the Divine Liturgy begins.
