[^210]: Conclusion of Situation Three (Apodosis date, § 30) and Situation Four: Nativity falling on Monday (§§ 31–35). Physical leaf p301 (printed page 286) and facing English leaf p302 (printed page 287) conclude the rubrics for Situation Three (Γ΄), where December 25 falls on a Sunday. Paragraph 30 assigns the Apodosis of the Feast to the following Saturday. The text notes the print erratum "ἤτοι τῇ Λ΄ τοῦ μηνός" (that is, on the 30th of the month), which is bracketed and corrected to "ὀρθότερον εἶναι τῇ ΛΑ΄ τοῦ μηνός" (more correctly, on the 31st of the month), as December contains 31 days. On Friday evening and Saturday morning (Dec 22–23, § 32), the Service of the Ten Holy Martyrs of Crete (Dec 23) is combined with the Forefeast from the Menaion; Dismissal Troparion *Ἑτοιμάζου, Βηθλεέμ*, Kontakion *Ἡ Παρθένος σήμερον τὸν προαιώνιον Λόγον*; Divine Liturgy includes the Epistle (Galatians 3:8–12) and Gospel (Luke 13:19–29) of the Saturday before the Nativity, and Communion Verse Psalm 64:5 LXX (*Μακάριοι οὓς ἐξελέξω* / "Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen"). On Saturday evening and Sunday morning (Dec 23–24, § 33), the Sunday before the Nativity (Holy Ancestors) is celebrated as in Situation Three §§ 7–9. On Sunday evening (Dec 24, § 34), Great Festal Vespers is chanted as in Situation Three § 25. On Monday (Dec 25, § 35), Festal Matins is sung as in Situation One §§ 7–8, together with the Divine Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great.
