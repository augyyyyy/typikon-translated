### Notes Concerning the Epistle and Gospel Readings

1.    If after the Sunday after the Lights only one Sunday remains before the First Sunday of the Triodion: on that Sunday we read the Epistle of the 32nd Sunday [1 Timothy 4:9–15] and the Gospel of Zacchaeus [15th Sunday of Luke, Luke 19:1–10].

2. 3. 4.    If four Sundays intervene: on the first we read the Epistle of the 29th Sunday [Colossians 3:4–11] and the Gospel of the Ten Lepers [12th Sunday of Luke, Luke 17:12–19]; on the second we read the Epistle of the 31st Sunday [1 Timothy 1:15–17] and the Gospel of the 13th Sunday of Luke [The Rich Ruler, Luke 18:18–27]; on the third Sunday we read the Epistle of the 32nd Sunday [1 Timothy 4:9–15] and the Gospel of Zacchaeus [15th Sunday of Luke, Luke 19:1–10]; and on the fourth we read the Epistle of the 17th Sunday [2 Corinthians 6:16–7:1] and the Gospel of the Canaanite Woman [17th Sunday of Matthew, Matthew 15:21–28].
