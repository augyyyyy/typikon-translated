# Great Lent: Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday of Second Week (§§ 32–34 Opening)

## Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday of the Second Week of Great Lent (§§ 32–34 Opening) [Violakis, p. 707]

**Wednesday of the Second Week**

32.    At Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm (Ps. 103 [LXX]) and the Eighteenth Kathisma of the Psalter: **"Unto the Lord in my distress..."** (*Τὰ Πρὸς Κύριον*, Pss. 119–133 [LXX]), at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*, Ps. 140 [LXX]), we chant the Idiomelon: **"Fasting spiritually, O brethren..."** (*Τὴν πνευματικὴν ἀδελφοὶ*) twice, then the Martyrikon once, the three Prosomia of the sequence from the Triodion, and four from the Menaion for the Saint of the following day; "Glory... Both now and ever...": the Prosomion Theotokion from the Menaion. The Old Testament Readings of the day: Genesis 4:16–26 [LXX] and Proverbs 5:15–6:3 [LXX]. Then: **"Let my prayer arise"** (*Κατευθυνθήτω*), and the rest of the Divine Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts follows in regular sequence.

**Friday of the Second Week**

33.    At Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm and the Eighteenth Kathisma, at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant the Idiomelon: **"Now is the acceptable time..."** (*Νῦν καιρὸς εὐπρόσδεκτος*) twice, the four Martyrika of the occurring Tone, and four Stichera from the Menaion; "Glory...": the Doxastikon of the Departed [Nekrosimon] of the occurring Tone; "Both now and ever...": the first Dogmatic Theotokion of the Tone of the week; the Old Testament Readings of the day: Genesis 5:32–6:8 [LXX] and Proverbs 6:20–7:1 [LXX]. Then: **"Let my prayer arise"** (*Κατευθυνθήτω*), and the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts is celebrated.  
    In the evening, Small Compline (*To mikron Apodeipnon*) is served, and the Second Stasis of the Oikoi of the Most Holy Theotokos (Oikoi 7–12) is read, etc., as is customary (see Paragraph 26 above).

**Saturday of the Second Week**

34.    On Saturday, at Matins, after the Midnight Office and the Six Psalms, at **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*),[*] the Dismissal Troparia: **"Apostles, Martyrs, and Prophets..."** (*Ἀπόστολοι Μάρτυρες*); "Glory...": **"Remember, O Lord, as Thou art good, Thy servants..."** (*Μνήσθητι Κύριε*); "Both now and ever...": **"Holy Mother of the ineffable Light..."** (*Μήτηρ ἁγία*). Then the designated Sixteenth Kathisma of the Psalter: **"The Lord said unto my Lord..."** (*Εἶπεν ὁ Κύριος τῷ Κυρίῳ μου*, Pss. 109–117 [LXX]); then the Martyrika Kathismata with the Kathisma of the Departed and the Theotokion of the Tone of the week. Then the Amomos [Psalm 118 (LXX), the Seventeenth Kathisma] in two Stases (see the order of service on Saturday of the Souls, Paragraph 7). Then the Evlogitaria of the Departed: **"Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes... The choir of the saints have found the fountain of life..."** (*Εὐλογητὸς εἶ, Κύριε*), and the Priest commemorates the departed pious and Orthodox Christians. Then the following Troparia are chanted: **"Give rest, our Savior, with the righteous unto Thy servants..."** (*Ἀνάπαυσον Σωτὴρ ἡμῶν*); "Both now and ever...": **"Thou Who dawned upon the world from a Virgin, Christ God..."** (*Ὁ ἐκ Παρθένου ἀνατείλας*). Psalm 50 is read; the Canon of the Saint of the day from the Menaion alone until the Fifth Ode; from the Sixth Ode, the Triodia Canons begin until the Ninth Ode, preceded by the Canon of the Saint. Then the Heirmos of the Eighth Ode: **"The One beginningless from the Father..."** (*Τὸν ἐξ ἀνάρχου τοῦ Πατρός*). Then we chant with the Biblical Canticle verses **"More honorable than the Cherubim"** (*Τὴν τιμιωτέραν*). The Heirmos of the Ninth Ode: **"Thee, the immortal fountain..."** (*Σὲ τὴν ἀθάνατον πηγήν*). Then we sing the Exaposteilarion of the Departed: **"Thou Who rulest the dead and the living..."** (*Ὁ καὶ νεκρῶν καὶ ζώντων δεσπόζων*) and: **"In thee we boast, O Theotokos..."** (*Ἡμεῖς ἐν σοὶ καυχώμεθα, Θεοτόκε*). At the Praises (Lauds): the Martyrika of the occurring Tone of the week...[^420]

# Great Lent: Second Saturday Concluded and Second Sunday of Lent (§§ 34 Concluded, 35, 36 Opening)

## Second Saturday Concluded and Second Sunday of Great Lent (§§ 34 Concluded, 35, 36 Opening) [Violakis, p. 709]

... "Glory...": the Doxastikon of the Departed; "Both now and ever...": the Theotokion. Then: **"To Thee belongs glory"** (*Σοὶ δόξα πρέπει*). The Aposticha of the occurring Tone and "Glory... Both now and ever..." (for all of these see the end of the Triodion). Then: **"It is good to confess unto the Lord"** (*Ἀγαθὸν τὸ ἐξομολογεῖσθαι*, Ps. 91:2 [LXX]), the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion: **"Apostles, Martyrs..."** (*Ἀπόστολοι Μάρτυρες*), and the Divine Liturgy begins, in which we sing the Antiphons: **"It is good to confess unto the Lord"**. After the Little Entrance: the Dismissal Troparia: **"Apostles, Martyrs..."** and that of the Patron Saint of the Church, and the Kontakion: **"As first-fruits of our nature, O Lord, the world offers unto Thee the God-bearing Martyrs..."** (*Ὡς ἀπαρχὰς τῆς φύσεως*). At "Especially": **"It is truly meet"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*); the Communion Hymn: **"Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen and taken unto Thyself, O Lord. This order is to be observed on every Saturday until the Fourth Week of Lent.

**Second Sunday of Great Lent**

After the Sixth Ode: the Resurrection Kontakion and Oikos of the occurring Tone, the Menologion from the Menaion, and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the Triodion for Saint Gregory Palamas. The Katavasiai: **"I shall open my mouth, and it shall be filled with the Spirit..."** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*) and the entire order of the Matins Gospels for Sundays of the Triodion. The Choirs singing the Asmatikon come down from their stalls and move to the North Door of the Sanctuary. The Priest, fully vested, censes the Holy Table and the Precious Cross, which is placed on the Holy Table upon a tray decorated with flowers and three candles. Having completed the censing, he raises the tray over his head and exits from the North Door of the Sanctuary, preceded by candle-bearers, the six-winged fans [hexapteryga], two censers, and the Primikerios holding the Divamvoulon. When the Priest reaches the center of the church, where the Tetrapod stands already decorated, he makes three circles around it; then, facing Eastward, he exclaims: **"Wisdom! Let us attend!"** (*Σοφία, ὀρθοί*), and places the tray with the Cross upon the Tetrapod, censing in a cross-wise manner and chanting: **"Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance..."** (*Σῶσον, Κύριε, τὸν λαόν σου*); which is repeated by each of the two Choirs in turn. This is repeated once by each of the Choirs. While the Patriarch blesses the people, the Choirs sing: **"For many years, Master!"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη, Δέσποτα*).
