[^487]: Order of Service of the Akathist Hymn in the Patriarchal Church: Opening Rubrics (§ Appendix 1). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p855 (printed page 840) and facing English leaf p856 (printed page 841). Ceremony of the Akathist Hymn on the Friday evening of the Fifth Week of the Great Fast: At the Ninth Hour [3:00 p.m.], the Ecumenical Patriarch descends with the customary patriarchal entourage into the Narthex, where he vests with the Mandyas [Hierarchical Mantle] and receives the Pastoral Staff (*Pateritsa*). He enters the Nave, blesses the congregation, and ascends the Patriarchal Throne. While the Choirs sing "For many years, Master!" (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη, Δέσποτα*), the Great Archimandrite, the Great Archdeacon, and the Second Deacon receive Keron (*καιρόν*)—the formal liturgical blessing and permission to officiate from the Patriarch. The Great Archimandrite intones the opening Exclamation: "Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen." (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*), and the Patriarch begins: "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee!", "O Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of truth...". The designated Reader-Priest, standing opposite the Patriarch near the Primikirios, reads the Trisagion Prayers and Small Compline. Following "It is truly right" (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*), the First Choir chants the Troparion in Tone 8: "Taking knowledge of the secret command" (*Τὸ προσταχθὲν μυστικῶς*) slowly and melodiously. During this hymn, the officiating Hierarchs exit the Bema through the South Deacon's Gate, led by the Great Ecclesiarch, and take their places in the Soleas according to seniority. [Printed Note 1: Receiving Keron (*καιρόν*) is the formal blessing of the highest-ranking hierarch authorizing clergy to participate in the sacred office. Printed Note 2: Patriarchal court offices (*offikia*) such as Primikirios and Megas Logothetis originated in Byzantine administrative governance and became honorary ceremonial titles in the post-Byzantine Great Church of Christ].

[^488]: Order of Service of the Akathist Hymn in the Patriarchal Church: First Stasis and Opening Odes (§ Appendix 2). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p857 (printed page 842) and facing English leaf p858 (printed page 843). The Great Ecclesiarch, having made a reverent bow (*σχῆμα*) to the Patriarch, returns to the Bema with the Great Archdeacon and Second Deacon. As the Second Choir repeats "Taking knowledge of the secret command" (*Τὸ προσταχθὲν μυστικῶς*), the vested Deacons with Dikerotrikera, led by the Ecclesiarch, exit the Beautiful Gate and flank the Patriarchal Throne. Midway through the hymn, the Patriarch descends, enters the Sanctuary preceded by the Primikirios, Ecclesiarch, and Deacons, kisses the Holy Table, and is vested with Epitrachilion and Omophorion. The Primikirios stands before the Festal Icon of the Theotokos on the Soleas. As the First Choir chants "Taking knowledge..." for the third time at a moderate tempo, the Patriarch exits the Sanctuary, censes the Holy Icon three times, and solemnly intones the First Stasis of the Oikoi (Oikoi 1–6: A–Z / Ἀ–Ζ), censing three times at the end of each Ikos. He then kisses the Icon, blesses the people with the Trikirion while the Choirs sing "For many years, Master!", and returns to the Sanctuary to unvest from the Epitrachilion and Omophorion before re-ascending his Throne. The Choirs sing Odes 1 and 3 of the Canon ("I shall open my mouth and it shall be filled with the Spirit" / *Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*), followed by the slow, solemn singing of the Kontakion in Tone 8: "To the Champion General" (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*) by both Choirs. During its penultimate verse, the Great Ecclesiarch invites the first officiating Hierarch to enter the Bema. [Printed Note 3: The reverent sign (*σχῆμα*) consists of placing the right hand over the chest and bowing slightly. Printed Note 4: The Dikerion symbolizes the two natures of Christ (divine and human), and the Trikirion symbolizes the three Persons of the Holy Trinity].

[^489]: Order of Service of the Akathist Hymn in the Patriarchal Church: Stases 2–4, Canon Odes 4–9, and Divamvulon (§ Appendix 3). Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p859 (printed page 844) and facing English leaf p860 (printed page 845). The first officiating Hierarch kisses the Patriarch's hand, enters the Sanctuary preceded by the Ecclesiarch, and vests with Epitrachilion and Omophorion. Preceded by the Third Deacon and another Deacon with Dikerotrikera, he exits to the Festal Icon, censes the Patriarch and the Icon three times each, and intones the Second Stasis of the Oikoi (Oikoi 7–12: H–M / Η–Μ). Upon conclusion, he kisses the Icon, bows to the Patriarch, returns to the Bema, unvests, and remains inside. The Choirs sing Odes 4, 5, and 6 of the Canon. During Ode 6, the Ecclesiarch invites the second Hierarch, who receives Keron from the Patriarch, vests in the Bema, and towards the end of the fast "To the Champion General" (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*), exits with the Deacons to intone the Third Stasis of the Oikoi (Oikoi 13–18: N–P / Ν–Π). He unvests in the Bema. The Choirs sing Odes 7, 8, and 9 of the Canon; during Ode 7, the two vested Deacons cense the congregation. During Ode 9, the third Hierarch receives Keron, vests, exits towards the conclusion of "To the Champion General", and intones the Fourth Stasis of the Oikoi (Oikoi 19–24: T–Omega / Τ–Ω, concluding with the repetition of Ikos 1). The remaining Hierarchs in attendance come before the Patriarch in order of seniority, bow, kiss the Festal Icon, and enter the Bema. Following the final rapid singing of "To the Champion General", the Reader recites the Trisagion and Kontakion, and the Primikirios comes forward with the Divamvulon (a large double-branched candlestick) and stands opposite the Patriarchal Throne.
