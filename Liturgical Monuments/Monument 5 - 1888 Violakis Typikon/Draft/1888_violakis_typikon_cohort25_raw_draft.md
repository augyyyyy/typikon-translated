At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers; then the Psalter and the Polyeleos. The Kathismata: one of the Resurrection and one of the Apostle with the Theotokion. At the Third Stichologia: the Kathisma of the Apostle and the Theotokion; the Sunday Evlogitaria, the Hypakoe, the Anavathmoi of the Tone, and the Prokeimenon. The Canons: the Resurrection Canon and the first Canon of the Apostle: **"My darkened soul"** (*Τὴν τεθολωμένην μου ψυχήν*). After the Third Ode: the Resurrection Kontakion and Ikos (recited plain), then the Kathisma of the Apostle and the Theotokion; after the Sixth Ode: the Kontakion and Ikos of the Apostle, and the Synaxarion from the Menaion. The Katavasiai: **"Christ is born, give ye glory"** (*Χριστὸς γεννᾶται*), and the entire order of the Sunday Morning Gospel. Then we sing the biblical canticle with **"More honorable than the Cherubim"** (*Τὴν τιμιωτέραν*). Exapostilaria: the Resurrection Exapostilarion, that of the Apostle, and the Theotokion. At the Praises: 4 Resurrection Stichira and 4 of the Apostle; **"Glory"**: instead of the Sunday Eothinon, that of the Apostle is sung; **"Both now and ever"**: **"Most blessed art Thou, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη*); the Great Doxology, and **"Today salvation has come into the world"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*).[^184]
