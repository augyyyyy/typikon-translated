[^430]: Great Lent: Friday of the Fifth Week (Presanctified Liturgy) and The Akathist Hymn with Compline Opening (§§ 49, 50 Opening). Friday Presanctified Liturgy: Paragraph 49 codifies the celebration of the Divine Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts on Friday of the Fifth Week of Great Lent: At Vespers, after the Eighteenth Kathisma of the Psalter ("Unto the Lord in my distress I cried...", Pss. 119–133 LXX), at "Lord, I have cried" (Ps. 140 LXX), six verses are set: the Idiomelon "As the man who went down from Jerusalem fell among thieves..." (Hōs ex Hierousalēm) twice, the Martyrikon once, and the three Prosomia of the Forefeast of the Annunciation: "Revealing the pre-eternal counsel to thee, O Maiden..." (Boulēn proaiōnion) once each (repeated to total 6 Stichera); "Glory... Both now and ever...": the Doxastikon "The mystery hidden from all eternity is revealed today..." (To ap' aiōnos mystērion). Little Entrance with the censer, "O Gladsome Light" (Phōs hilaron), the two Old Testament Readings of the day from Genesis (Gen. 22:1–18 LXX, the Sacrifice of Isaac) and Proverbs (Prov. 17:17–18:5 LXX), "Let my prayer arise" (Kateuthynthētō), and the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts is celebrated. The Akathist Hymn with Compline: Paragraph 50 details the monumental rubrics for the Friday evening celebration of the entire Akathist Hymn (Chairetismoi) integrated into Small Compline: After "It is truly meet" (Axion estin), the Troparion "When the Angel perceived what had secretly been commanded, he came with haste to Joseph's dwelling..." (To prostachthen mystikōs) is chanted in Tone 8: twice in a slow, ornate melody (argōs) and a third time in a concise, syllabic melody (syntomon). The Hierarch [or presiding Priest in epitrachelion and phelonion] stands before the icon of the Mother of God on the tetrapod and solemnly intones the First Stasis of the 24 Oikoi [Stanzas 1–6: Incipits Alpha to Zeta, beginning "An Angel and the chiefest among them was sent from heaven to say unto the Mother of God: Rejoice!..."], to which the choir refrains "Rejoice, Bride unwedded!" (Chaire, Nymphē anympheute). Following "For many years, O Master" (Eis polla etē), the Canon of the Akathist composed by Saint Joseph the Hymnographer: "I shall open my mouth, and it shall be filled with the Spirit..." (Anoixō to stoma mou) in Tone 4 is chanted in six Troparia. After Ode 3, the Kontakion "To Thee, the Champion Leader..." (Tē hypermachō) is chanted again slowly, and the Hierarch reads the Second Stasis of the Oikoi [Stanzas 7–12: Incipits Eta to Mu, recounting the Adoration of the Shepherds and Magi, and the Flight into Egypt], refraining "Alleluia!". The Second Choir then chants the Fourth, Fifth, and Sixth Odes of the Canon, beginning with the Heirmos "He Who sits in glory upon the throne of the Godhead, Jesus the divine Word..." (Ho kathēmenos en doxē); at the conclusion of Ode 6, both choirs unite to chant "To Thee, the Champion Leader..." in a moderately slow tempo (argosyntomon dichoron), and the Third Stasis of the Oikoi [Stanzas 13–18: Incipits Nu to Sigma, theological contemplation of the Virgin Birth and divine condescension] is read. The First Choir chants the Seventh Ode: "The godly-minded youths worshipped not the creature rather than the Creator..." (Ouk elatrevsan) through the Ninth Ode, during which the Deacon performs a complete censing of the holy sanctuary, the tetrapod, and the entire church. Following Ode 9, both choirs unite once more to sing "To Thee, the Champion Leader..." in a moderately slow tempo, and the Fourth Stasis of the Oikoi [Stanzas 19–24: Incipits Tau to Omega, concluding with the climactic supplication "O all-praised Mother, who didst bear the Word..."] is solemnly recited. Akathist Compline Concluded: Paragraph 50 concludes the Friday evening service of the Akathist Hymn: Following the Fourth Stasis of the Oikoi, the Trisagion Prayers are recited, the Kontakion "To Thee, the Champion Leader" (Tē hypermachō) is read plain (chyma), "Lord, have mercy" (40 times), and the Prayer of the Hours: "Thou Who at all times and at every hour art worshipped and glorified in heaven and on earth, Christ our God..." (Ho en panti kairō). "Lord, have mercy" (3 times), "Glory... Both now and ever...", "More honorable than the Cherubim", "In the name of the Lord, Father, give the blessing!", the priestly blessing "May God be merciful unto us, and bless us...", and the nocturnal supplicatory prayers: the Supplicatory Prayer to the All-Holy Theotokos by Paul the Monk of the Monastery of the Benefactress (Evergetis): "O spotless, undefiled, incorrupt, unblemished, pure Virgin, Lady Bride of God..." (Aspile, amolynte), the Prayer of Antiochus the Monk to our Lord Jesus Christ: "And grant unto us, Master, as we depart unto sleep, rest of body and soul..." (Kai dos hēmin, Despota), "O most glorious Ever-Virgin, Mother of Christ our God...", "The Father is my hope, the Son is my refuge, the Holy Spirit is my protection: O Holy Trinity, glory to Thee!", and "All my hope I place in thee, O Mother of God: keep me under thy protection!". The Dismissal of Compline is pronounced, followed by the Litany of Mutual Forgiveness: "Let us pray for the peace of the world..." (Evxōmetha hyper tēs eirēnēs tou kosmou). At the close of the service, the Choirs chant slowly the ornate Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] in Tone 3: "The beauty of thy virginity and the surpassing splendor of thy purity did Gabriel behold..." (Tēn hōraiotēta tēs parthenias sou), and the service concludes with the final priestly blessing: "Through the prayers of our holy fathers, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us. Amen.". Saturday Matins of the Akathist: Paragraph 51 codifies the Matins service on the Fifth Saturday of Great Lent (Saturday of the Akathist): Following the Midnight Office and the Six Psalms, at "God is the Lord", the festal Troparion "When the Angel perceived what had secretly been commanded..." (To prostachthen mystikōs) is chanted twice; then the designated Kathisma of the Psalter (Kathisma 16 or 17), followed by the Sessional Hymn [Kathisma]: "The great commander of the bodiless angels..." (Ho megas stratēgos) twice, Psalm 50 (read), and the Canon "I shall open my mouth, and it shall be filled with the Spirit..." (Anoixō to stoma mou) in six Troparia. After Ode 3, the Sessional Hymn "The great commander" is chanted once. At Ode 6, the Triodia from the Triodion are appended; after Ode 6, the Kontakion "To Thee, the Champion Leader" and the Oikos "An Archangel was sent from heaven to say unto the Mother of God: Rejoice!..." (Angelos prōtostatēs). The Menologion from the Menaion and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the Triodion recounting the miraculous deliverance of Constantinople from barbarian siege in 626 under Emperor Heraclius and Patriarch Sergius; then the remaining Odes of the Canon with the Triodia. Katavasiai: "I shall open my mouth..."; after Ode 8, "More honorable than the Cherubim", and the Exaposteilarion: "The mystery hidden from all eternity is made known today..." (To ap' aiōnos mystērion) twice. At the Praises [Lauds], four Stichera: "The hidden mystery..." (To kekrymmenon mystērion) twice, "A glorious palace hath been prepared for Thee..." (Pastas evprepēs), and "Gabriel the Archangel came openly unto thee..." (Gabriēl ho Archangelos); "Glory... Both now and ever...": "The Theotokos heard a tongue that she knew not..." (Glōssan hēn ouk egnō), the Great Doxology, and the Troparion "When the Angel perceived...". Divine Liturgy of the Akathist: Paragraph 52 opens the Divine Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom: Typika and the Beatitudes [Makarismoi] with Troparia selected from Odes 3 and 6 of the Canon of the Theotokos. After the Little Entrance, the Troparion "When the Angel perceived..." and the Kontakion "To Thee, the Champion Leader". Epistle pericope: Hebrews 9:1–7 (The Earthly Sanctuary and Ark of the Covenant); Gospel pericope: Luke 1:39–56 (The Visitation and the Magnificat).

Saturday Divine Liturgy Concluded: Paragraph 52 concludes the Divine Liturgy on Saturday of the Akathist: In place of the Megalynarion "It is truly meet", the Heirmos of Ode 9 of the Akathist Canon is chanted: "Let every earthborn mortal rejoice in spirit, bearing his lamp..." (Hapas gēgenēs). Communion Hymn [Koinōnikon]: "I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord. Alleluia!" (Ps. 115:4 LXX [Ps. 116:13 MT]). "We have seen the true Light", and the Dismissal is pronounced. The Akathist Hymn in Matins: Paragraph 53 codifies the solemn communal parish and cathedral practice of celebrating the complete Akathist Hymn integrated into Matins on Friday evening or early Saturday morning (a custom widely practiced throughout the Patriarchate and parish churches): The service opens with the priestly exclamation: "Blessed is our God, always, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages", "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee", "O Heavenly King", the Trisagion Prayers, "For Thine is the kingdom...", "Lord, have mercy" (12 times), "Glory... Both now and ever...", "O come, let us worship" (3 times), and the Royal Psalms: Psalm 19 LXX ("The Lord hear thee in the day of affliction...") and Psalm 20 LXX ("In Thy strength the king shall rejoice, O Lord..."). The Trisagion Prayers, the Troparion of the Cross "Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance...", and the Six Psalms. After the Great Litany, "God is the Lord" (Theos Kyrios) is chanted four times: twice in a slow melody and twice in a fast melody with the designated verses. Then the festal Troparion "When the Angel perceived what had secretly been commanded..." (To prostachthen mystikōs) is sung twice slowly and a third time quickly. The Hierarch [or presiding Priest] reads the First Stasis of the Oikoi [Stanzas 1–6]. After "For many years, O Master", "To Thee, the Champion Leader" is sung slowly, and the Second Stasis [Stanzas 7–12] is read, followed by "To Thee, the Champion Leader" chanted at a quicker tempo by both choirs together. The Small Litany, Psalm 50, and the Canon "I shall open my mouth" in six Troparia with the Katavasia at the end of each Ode. After Ode 3, the Small Litany, "To Thee, the Champion Leader" quickly by both choirs, and the Third Stasis [Stanzas 13–18] is read. Again both choirs chant "To Thee, the Champion Leader", followed by the Small Litany and the Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] "The great commander of the bodiless angels..." once. Odes 4, 5, and 6 of the Canon are sung, followed immediately by the Triodia for ten with the refrain "Holy Saints of God, intercede for us!". At the conclusion of Ode 6, the Heirmos "The prophet was delivered from the sea monster..." (Tou kētous ton Prophētēn), both choirs chant "To Thee, the Champion Leader", and the Fourth Stasis [Stanzas 19–24] is read. After "For many years, O Master", the Kontakion "To Thee, the Champion Leader" is read plain (chyma), followed immediately by the Menologion and the Memorial of the Triodion. Odes 7 and 8 of the Canon are chanted with the Triodia. Fifth Sunday of Great Lent (Saint Mary of Egypt): Paragraph 54 codifies Great Vespers on Saturday evening: At "Lord, I have cried" (Ps. 140 LXX), ten Stichera: six Resurrection Stichera in the occurring Tone of the week from the Octoechos and four Stichera of the Venerable Mother Mary of Egypt from the Triodion; "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 2: "The power of Thy Cross, O Christ, hath wrought wonders; for even she who formerly was a harlot chose to endure ascetic struggles..." (Ho di' hēmas gennētheis ek Parthenou), and Dismissal. After Ode 6, the Resurrection Kontakion and Oikos of the Tone; the Menologion from the Menaion, and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the Triodion recounting the extraordinary life and repentance of Saint Mary of Egypt in the desert of Jordan. Katavasiai: "I shall open my mouth...".
