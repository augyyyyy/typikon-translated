* *Scholarly Apparatus on the Liturgical Concurrences of Saint Charalambos (10 February):* Physical leaf p964 documents the migration of the feast of the Holy Hieromartyr Charalambos across the opening weeks of the Great Fast and Cheesefare. When Pascha falls early (March 22 JC), the commemoration lands on Tuesday of the Second Week of Lent (§ 28, p. 190), governed by the rubrics for weekday commemorations occurring with the Presanctified Liturgy. When Pascha falls on March 24 JC, Charalambos coincides with the Sunday of Orthodoxy (§§ 23–25, pp. 188–189), requiring the full festal synthesis with the Sunday of the Triumph of Orthodoxy and the commemoration of the Holy Icons.
