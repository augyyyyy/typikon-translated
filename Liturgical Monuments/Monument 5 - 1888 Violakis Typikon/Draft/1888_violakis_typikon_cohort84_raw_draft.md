# THE THURSDAY OF THE ASCENSION
## (Great Vespers and Matins of the Ascension)

38.    On Wednesday evening, after the Ninth Hour of Pascha, the Priest intones **"Blessed is our God..."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς*), and the Introductory Psalm (Ps. 103 LXX) begins with **"Come, let us worship and fall down before Christ, our King and our God"** (three times). At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant the five Idiomela: **"The Lord ascended into the heavens, that He might send the Paraclete unto the world..."** (*Ὁ Κύριος ἀνελήφθη*) to six, repeating the first hymn; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 6: **"Without parting from the Fatherly bosom, O sweetest Jesus..."** (*Τῶν κόλπων τῶν Πατρικῶν*). Entrance with censer, the Prokimenon of the day, and the Old Testament Readings (Isaiah 2:2–3; 62:10–63:3, 7–9; Zechariah 14:1, 4, 8–11 LXX). At the Aposticha: the three Idiomela; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 6: **"God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet..."** (*Ἀνέβη ὁ Θεὸς ἐν ἀλαλαγμῷ*). The Dismissal Troparion in Tone 4: **"Thou hast ascended in glory, O Christ our God, having gladdened Thy disciples with the promise of the Holy Spirit, they having been assured by the blessing that Thou art the Son of God, the Redeemer of the world!"** (*Ἀνελήφθης ἐν δόξῃ*) three times, and the Dismissal with the following appointed prologue: **"He Who ascended in glory from us into the heavens and sat at the right hand of God the Father, Christ our true God..."** (*Ὁ ἐν δόξῃ ἀναληφθεὶς ἀφ᾿ ἡμῶν εἰς τοὺς Οὐρανοὺς καὶ ἐν δεξιᾷ καθίσας τοῦ Θεοῦ καὶ Πατρός, Χριστὸς ὁ ἀληθινὸς Θεὸς ἡμῶν...*).

39.    On Thursday at Matins, after Psalm 50, the Liti, then the Trisagion, the Dismissal Troparion in Tone 4: **"Thou hast ascended in glory..."** (*Ἀνελήφθης ἐν δόξῃ*), and the Six Psalms. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparion (three times); then the Psalter and the Polyeleos. The Kathismata in order, twice each; the Anavathmoi: the first Antiphon of Tone 4 (**"From my youth many passions war against me..."**), and the Prokimenon: **"God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet!"** (*Ἀνέβη ὁ Θεὸς ἐν ἀλαλαγμῷ, Κύριος ἐν φωνῇ σάλπιγγος*, Ps. 46:5 LXX), **"Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord!"** (*Πᾶσα πνοὴ*), and the Third Matins Gospel (Mark 16:9–20), **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ..."** (*Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ θεασάμενοι*), and Psalm 50 recited plainly. "Glory...": **"Through the intercessions of the Apostles..."** (*Ταῖς τῶν Ἀποστόλων*); "Both now and ever...": **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos..."** (*Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου*); the Verse: **"Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy..."** (*Ἐλέησόν με ὁ Θεὸς*), and the Idiomelon: **"Today the heavenly powers in the heavens marvel seeing our nature ascend..."** (*Σήμερον ἐν Οὐρανοῖς αἱ ἄνω δυνάμεις*). Both Canons, the first to six and the second to four, without verses. After the 3rd Ode, the Kathisma (once); after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion in Tone 6: **"When Thou hadst fulfilled the dispensation for our sakes, and united things on earth with the heavenly, Thou didst ascend in glory, O Christ our God..."** (*Τὴν ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν πληρώσας*), the Ikos, the Menologion of the day, and the Hypomnema of the Feast. The Katavasias: **"Covered by a divine cloud, he that was slow of tongue proclaimed the Law written of God..."** (*Θείῳ καλυφθείς*); then the 9th Ode is chanted: the first Canon without verses, and the second Canon with its Megalynarion: **"Ye Angels, marveling at the ascension of the Master, how with glory He was taken up from earth unto the heights..."** (*Ἄγγελοι τὴν ἄνοδον τοῦ Δεσπότου . . . πῶς μετὰ δόξης ἐπήρθη ἀπὸ τῆς γῆς εἰς τὰ ἄνω*); the Katavasia: **"Rejoice, O Queen, boasting of virgins and mothers!..."** (*Χαίροις Ἄνασσα*), then the Exaposteilarion: **"While the disciples looked on, Thou wast taken up, O Christ, unto the Father, to sit beside Him..."** (*Τῶν Μαθητῶν ὁρώντων σε*) (three times). At the Praises: the three Prosomia to four (repeating the first); "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 2: **"Thou wast born as Thou Thyself didst will; Thou didst appear as Thou Thyself didst wish; Thou didst suffer in the flesh, O our God..."** (*Ἐτέχθης ὡς αὐτὸς ἠθέλησας*)...[^475]

# THE SEVENTH SUNDAY: THE HOLY FATHERS OF THE FIRST SYNOD AT NICEA
