[^258]: Variable Calendrical Cases for the Apodosis of the Meeting (Cases 1–5) and Cheesefare Sunday Evening Vespers (§ 20 incipit): Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p397 (printed page 382) and facing English leaf p398 (printed page 383). The Typikon provides a rigorous 5-case combinatorial matrix for fixing the date of the Apodosis of the Meeting based upon its interaction with the movable pre-Lenten Triodion cycle: Case 1 [α΄]: Feast occurs between Sunday of Publican and Pharisee and Friday of that week -> Apodosis on regular 8th day (February 9); Case 2 [β΄]: Feast occurs between Saturday eve of Prodigal Son and Tuesday of Meatfare Week -> Apodosis celebrated early on Friday before the Saturday of Souls, because the ancestral Office of the Dead on Meatfare Saturday is inviolable and cannot be displaced for an Apodosis; Case 3 [γ΄]: Feast occurs between Wednesday of Meatfare Week and Saturday of Souls -> Apodosis postponed to Tuesday of Cheesefare Week; Case 4 [δ΄]: Feast occurs between Meatfare Sunday and Tuesday of Cheesefare Week -> Apodosis on Friday of Cheesefare Week; Case 5 [ε΄]: Feast occurs between Wednesday and Saturday of Cheesefare Week -> Apodosis celebrated on Cheesefare Sunday per §§ 16–18. When the Feast itself falls on Cheesefare Sunday (celebrated per §§ 11–13), the Apodosis is celebrated that same evening at Forgiveness Vespers (§ 20): "Lord, I have cried" combines 3 Stichira of the Feast and 3 of the Righteous Symeon; Great Prokeimenon *Μὴ ἀποστρέψῃς* (Psalm 68:18 LXX); Aposticha Idiomelon *Ἔλαμψεν ἡ χάρις σου* and festal Theotokion; proper festal Dismissal followed immediately by the Forgiveness rite without the customary priestly blessing *Δι’ εὐχῶν*.

[^259]: Cheesefare Sunday Evening Forgiveness Vespers Conclusion (§ 20 concl.) and Situation IV: Clean Monday Concurrence (§§ 21–22): Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p399 (printed page 384) and facing English leaf p400 (printed page 385). Paragraph 20 concludes the Forgiveness Vespers rite marking the opening of Great Lent: following the festal Dismissal of the Meeting (omitting *Δι’ εὐχῶν*), the Choirs chant the penitential Troparion *Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*, 40 *Κύριε ἐλέησον*, *Ὁ ὢν εὐλογητός*, the Prayer of Saint Ephrem with Great Prostrations [Metanias], the Deacon/Priest litany *Εὐξώμεθα ὑπὲρ εἰρήνης τοῦ κόσμου*, and the hymn *Πάντων προστατεύεις* while clergy and faithful exchange the kiss of peace and mutual forgiveness, formally concluding the Apodosis of the Feast. Paragraphs 21–22 formulate Situation IV: when February 2 coincides with Clean Monday (the first day of Great Lent): on Cheesefare Sunday evening at Vespers (§ 21), "Lord, I have cried" combines 3 Triodion fasting Idiomela (*Ἐγκρατείᾳ τὴν σάρκα*) and 3 festal Stichira (*Λέγε Συμεών*), Great Prokeimenon Psalm 133:1 LXX (*Ἰδοὺ δὴ εὐλογεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*), Old Testament Readings of the Feast, and festal Aposticha; on Clean Monday at Matins (§ 22), the festal Litiya and "God is the Lord" lead into Kathismata across three Stichologiai combining Triodion fasting hymns (*Τῆς νηστείας τῇ θείᾳ ἀπαρχῇ*, *Τὴν πάνσεπτον ἐγκράτειαν*) with festal Kathismata; Anavathmoi (Antiphon 1 of Tone 4), Prokeimenon (Psalm 44:18 LXX), and festal Morning Gospel; Canon to 6 with festal Megalynaria of Ode 9 (*Ἀκατάληπτόν ἐστι*) and festal Exapostilarion *Ἐν πνεύματι τῷ Ἱερῷ* thrice; Praises combining festal Stichira Prosomia *Νόμον τὸν ἐν γράμματι* and festal Doxastikon *Ὁ ἐν χερσὶ πρεσβυτικαῖς*, concluding with the Great Doxology.
