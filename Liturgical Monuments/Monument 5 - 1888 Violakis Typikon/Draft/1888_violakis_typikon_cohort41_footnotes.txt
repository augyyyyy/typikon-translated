[^260]: Feast of the Meeting of our Lord Jesus Christ (February 2) — Clean Monday Liturgy Conclusion (§ 23), Apodosis of the Meeting (§ 24), and February 10: Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos (Heading & Situation I, § 1): Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p401 (printed page 386) and facing English leaf p402 (printed page 387). Paragraph 23 completes the rubrical order for the Divine Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom when the Meeting coincides with Clean Monday (the first day of Great Lent): festal Antiphons from Psalm 44 LXX, Entrance Hymn Psalm 97:2 LXX, Dismissal Troparion *Χαῖρε κεχαριτωμένη*, Kontakion *Ὁ μήτραν παρθενικήν*, festal Epistle and Gospel, Ode 9 Megalynarion *Θεοτόκε ἡ ἐλπίς . . . Ἐν νόμῳ σκιᾷ*, Communion Verse Psalm 115:4 LXX (*Ποτήριον σωτηρίου*), and proper festal Dismissal with Christological incipit *Ὁ ἐν ἀγκάλαις τοῦ Δικαίου Συμεὼν βασταχθῆναι καταδεξάμενος διὰ τὴν ἡμῶν σωτηρίαν...*; the dispensation permitting wine and oil on Clean Monday is explicitly confirmed. Paragraph 24 establishes the Apodosis of the Meeting on Clean Monday evening: Vespers is chanted without variation per § 1, omitting Old Testament Readings; Dismissal Troparion thrice, Dismissal, and formal Apodosis; Great Compline is omitted out of festal reverence, replaced by Small Compline recited in cells in city churches, while monasteries observe their founder Typika (per Patriarchs Constantios I and Gregory VI). The general occurrence rule notes the Meeting may fall between the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee and Clean Monday per the 34 Paschalia table. Commemoration of February 10 (Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos) begins with Situation I (§ 1): occurrence on any weekday from the beginning of the Triodion through Cheesefare Friday is governed entirely by the Menaion; Matins Katavasiai *Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*; Liturgy Kontakion *Προστασία τῶν Χριστιανῶν*.

[^261]: February 10: Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos — Situation II: Concurrence on Sunday Outside the Triodion (§ 2) and Situation III: Concurrence on First Four Sundays of the Triodion (§§ 3–4, § 5 incipit): Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p403 (printed page 388) and facing English leaf p404 (printed page 389). Paragraph 2 governs Situation II when February 10 falls on a Sunday outside the Triodion cycle: the Saint's Office is combined with the Resurrectional Office as customary for ranked feasts on Sundays; Praises Doxastikon is the Eothinon Gospel Sticheron; Epistle of the Saint, Gospel of the Sunday; Kontakion *Προστασία τῶν Χριστιανῶν*; Communion Verse Psalm 148:1 LXX (*Αἰνεῖτε*). Paragraphs 3–5 govern Situation III when the feast occurs on one of the first four Sundays of the Triodion (Publican and Pharisee, Prodigal Son, Meatfare, or Cheesefare): on Saturday evening at Great Vespers (§ 3), 10 Stichira at "Lord, I have cried" combine 4 Resurrectional, 3 Triodion, and 3 of the Saint; Doxastikon of the Triodion, first Dogmatic Theotokion of the Tone; Old Testament Readings of the Saint; Aposticha Resurrectional with Doxastikon of the Saint and Theotokion of the Triodion; Dismissal Troparia of Resurrection, Saint, and Theotokion. On Sunday at Matins (§ 4): after Psalm 50 LXX, Triadic Canon, festal Litiya of the Saint, Hexapsalmos, and "God is the Lord"; Psalter and Polyeleos; Resurrectional Kathismata with 3rd Kathisma of the Saint; Evlogitaria, Hypakoe, Anavathmoi of the Tone, Prokeimenon; Canons of Resurrection, Triodion, and Saint; after Ode 3, Resurrectional Kontakion/Ikos and Kathismata of the Saint, Triodion, and Theotokion; after Ode 6, Triodion Kontakion/Ikos, Menologion of the Saint, and Sunday Synaxarion; Triodion Katavasiai; Eothinon Gospel sequence; *Τὴν τιμιωτέραν*; Exapostilaria of Resurrection, Triodion, Saint, and Theotokion; Praises with 3 Resurrectional, 2 Triodion, and 3 Saint Stichira; Triodion Doxastikon, *Ὑπερευλογημένη*, Great Doxology, and *Σήμερον σωτηρία*. Paragraph 5 begins the Divine Liturgy order with Typika and Beatitudes (4 of Tone, 4 of Ode 6 of Triodion Canon).

[^262]: February 10: Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos — Situation III Divine Liturgy Conclusion (§ 5 concl.) and Situation IV: Anticipated Celebration on Friday Before Saturday of Souls (§§ 6–7, § 8 incipit): Detailed epigraphic synthesis of physical leaf p405 (printed page 390) and facing English leaf p406 (printed page 391). Paragraph 5 concludes the Divine Liturgy for Situation III: following the Little Entrance, Troparia of the Resurrection, the Saint, the Temple Patron, and Triodion Kontakion; Epistle of the Saint, Sunday Gospel; *Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*; Communion Verse Psalm 148:1 LXX (*Αἰνεῖτε*), and Dismissal. Paragraphs 6–8 resolve Situation IV when February 10 coincides with Meatfare Saturday (Saturday of Souls): the commemoration of St. Charalampos is anticipated and celebrated on Friday together with the Apodosis of the Meeting so as not to displace the ancestral Office of the Dead. On Thursday evening at Great Vespers (§ 6): at "Lord, I have cried", 3 festal Idiomela of the Meeting and 3 Prosomia of the Saint; Doxastikon of the Saint, festal Theotokion; Old Testament Readings of the Saint; Aposticha festal Idiomela with Doxastikon of the Saint and festal Theotokion; Dismissal Troparia of the Feast, the Saint, and the Feast again. On Friday at Matins (§ 7): after Psalm 50 LXX, Litiya of the Saint, Trisagion, festal Troparion, and Hexapsalmos; "God is the Lord" with Troparia as at Vespers; Psalter and Polyeleos of the Saint; Kathismata of the Saint with festal Kathismata in place of Theotokia; Anavathmoi (Antiphon 1 of Tone 4), Prokeimenon Psalm 91:13 LXX (*Δίκαιος ὡς φοῖνιξ*), festal Morning Gospel; Psalm 50 LXX, *Ταῖς τοῦ Ἀθλοφόρου* ("Through the intercessions of the prize-bearing champion"), Idiomelon *Φάλαγγα θεοστεφῆ*; Canons of Feast and Saint; Kathismata after Ode 3; Kontakion/Ikos and Menologion of the Saint after Ode 6; Katavasiai *Χέρσον ἀβυσσοτόκον* and Ode 9 Megalynaria of the Meeting (*Ἀκατάληπτόν ἐστι*); Exapostilaria of Feast, Saint, and Feast; Praises (3 Feast, 3 Saint), Doxastikon of the Saint, festal Theotokion, Great Doxology, and festal Dismissal Troparion. Paragraph 8 opens the Divine Liturgy with festal Antiphons and Entrance Hymn Psalm 97:2 LXX (*Ἐγνώρισε Κύριος*).
