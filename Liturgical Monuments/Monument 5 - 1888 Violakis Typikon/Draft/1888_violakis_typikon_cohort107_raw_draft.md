**HOURS** (*῞Ωρα, ἡ, pl. ῏Ωραι, αἱ*; Slav. *Chasy*): Though predominantly celebrated in holy monasteries, the Hours are services regularly celebrated in parish churches in cities and towns. The First, Third, and Sixth Hours are chanted before Matins (*Orthros*), and the Ninth Hour before Vespers. The text of these Hours is found in the liturgical book *The Great Horologion* (*῾Ωρολόγιον τὸ Μέγα* / *Chasoslov*), preceding the Service of Matins (*Orthros*). A distinct solemn cycle called the Great (or Royal) Hours is celebrated on the Eve of the Nativity of Christ, the Eve of Theophany, and on Great and Holy Friday. Their texts are contained in the festal propers of those days.
