### THE CEREMONY OF THE CONSECRATION

The [presiding] Bishop must have prepared in advance all the various items, as prescribed in the Great Euchologion (*Sluzhebnik / Trebnik*), that are required for the dedication and Consecration. On the eve of the service, the Bishop proceeds with the Clergy to the new church and, before Vespers, arranges the Holy Relics in three portions upon the Holy Table. When the Holy Relics have been placed on the Diskarion [Paten] and covered with a Veil over the Asterisk [star], the Bishop intones: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*). This is followed by the Trisagion Prayers and the Exclamation **"For Thine is the kingdom..."** (*Ὅτι σοῦ ἐστιν ἡ βασιλεία*), and the following Troparia are chanted: **"Thy Martyrs, O Lord, in their sufferings..."** (*Οἱ μάρτυρές σου, Κύριε*), **"Champions of the Lord..."** (*Ἀθλοφόροι Κυρίου*), **"Let us all implore Christ's Martyrs..."** (*Τοὺς Μάρτυρας Χριστοῦ*), and the Troparion of the day. Then, the Kontakion: **"As first fruits of nature..."** (*Ὡς ἀπαρχὰς τῆς φύσεως*); **"Glory..."** (*Δόξα*), **"Godlike choir of Martyrs..."** (*Μαρτύρων θεῖος χορός*); **"Now and forever..."** (*Καὶ νῦν*), **"Theotokos, thou art the true vine, together with the Champions..."** (*Θεοτόκε, σὺ εἶ ἡ ἄμπελος*), and the Dismissal. If there is another consecrated church nearby, the Bishop transfers the Holy Relics to it before Vespers and places them on the Holy Table, where a candle burns through the whole night. Vespers and an All-night Vigil are chanted in the church that has received the Relics of the Saints.* If, however, there is no nearby consecrated church, the whole Office takes place in the new church, in accordance with the prescribed order.

In the morning, Matins is chanted according to the typikon order of service of the occurring day (see the detailed orders in Cases 1–6 above). After **"Let every breath..."** (*Πᾶσα πνοή*) and **"Praise the Lord..."** (*Αἰνεῖτε*), the Bishop descends...[^500]

# Appendix: The Ceremony of the Consecration of a Church (Vesting, Wax-Mastic, and Procession)

## The Ceremony of the Consecration of a Church (Vesting, Wax-Mastic Preparation, and Procession) [Violakis, p. 869]

...down from his Throne into the middle of the church and, having knelt down facing East, he reads the two preparatory prayers of the Consecration by the most holy Patriarch Kallistos, which are found in the Euchologion (*Sluzhebnik / Trebnik*). He then rises and venerates the holy Icons and prepares as usual for the Divine Liturgy while **"Our Master and Chief Priest..."** (*Τὸν δεσπότην καὶ Ἀρχιερέα*) is chanted, followed by the Stichira of the Praises (*Ainoi*), the Doxastikon, the Great Doxology, the Dismissal Troparion of Matins, and the Dismissal.

Then the various ingredients of the wax-mastic (*κηρομαστίχη*) are poured into a vessel [cauldron] which is then placed on the fire. When they have melted, the vessel is kept close to the fire so that what it contains does not get cold and congeal. Likewise, other vessels are filled with water and put on the fire, so that they are heated for the washing of the Holy Table. Heavy paper strips are then tied around the lips of the central Column,* so that they extend about two finger-breadths [an inch] above the lip. The ring of papers is filled inside with crushed marble, so that the wax-mastic does not run over the edge.

When everything else needed is ready, the Bishop gives the blessing: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*), and at once Psalm 142 [LXX] is recited: **"O Lord, hear my prayer..."** (*Κύριε, εἰσάκουσον τῆς προσευχῆς μου*), followed by the Small Litany intoned by the Deacon: **"Again and again in peace let us pray unto the Lord"** (*Ἔτι καὶ ἔτι ἐν εἰρήνῃ τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*). The Bishop then exclaims: **"For holy art Thou, our God, and unto Thee do we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι Ἅγιος εἶ, ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*). Then the Deacon: **"Let us pray to the Lord"** (*Τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*); then the Bishop recites the Prayer and intones: **"Peace be unto all"** (*Εἰρήνη πᾶσι*). The Deacon: **"Let us bow our heads unto the Lord"** (*Τὰς κεφαλὰς ἡμῶν τῷ Κυρίῳ κλίνωμεν*); the Bishop recites the Prayer in a low voice [*μυστικῶς*] and pronounces the Exclamation: **"Blessed and glorified be the majesty of Thy kingdom, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εἴη τὸ κράτος τῆς βασιλείας σου...*).†

After this Exclamation, the Bishop lifts the Diskarion [Paten] on which are the Holy Relics onto his head and, preceded by the Priests with the Holy Gospel Books, the Cross, the Ripidia (*Exapteryga*, six-winged fans) and the Candles, with the Chanters chanting the Troparia: **"O Holy Martyrs, who have fought the good fight and received your crowns..."** (*Ἅγιοι Μάρτυρες οἱ καλῶς ἀθλήσαντες*), and **"Glory to Thee, O Christ God, the Apostles' boast, the Martyrs' joy..."** (*Δόξα σοι, Χριστὲ ὁ Θεός*), he leaves the church (as do all the people) and goes in solemn procession around it, while the Idiomela are chanted: **"Be renewed, be renewed, O new Jerusalem..."** (*Ἐγκαινίζου, ἐγκαινίζου, ἡ νέα Ἱερουσαλήμ*), and **"Of old when renewing the temple of testimony..."** (*Πάλαι μὲν ἐγκαινίζων τὸν τῆς σκηνῆς ναόν*)...[^501]

---
* *Note on the Central Column:* The central column supporting the Holy Table, in which the Holy Relics are deposited. There are also four additional columns supporting the Holy Table at each corner. They symbolize the Four Evangelists, while the central supporting column symbolizes Christ.  
† *Rubric for External Church Translation:* If the Service is not conducted in the new church, but in another already consecrated church, at this hour the Bishop, vested in his archieratical vestments (as well as all the Clergy in their sacred vestments), carries upon his head the Holy Relics and transfers them in solemn procession to the new church, where after the customary circling around the new church, he enters it to celebrate the Consecration and following that the Divine Liturgy; it is prescribed that the Divine Liturgy be celebrated in that [the new] church.

When the Bishop arrives in front of the main western doors of the church, he deposits the Holy Relics upon the Tetrapod; the Deacon proclaims the Apostolos, and then the Bishop proclaims the Holy Gospel. With the doors of the church shut, the Bishop exclaims: **"Lift up your gates, ye princes; and be ye lifted up, O eternal gates, and the King of glory shall enter in!"** (*Ἄρατε πύλας, οἱ ἄρχοντες ὑμῶν, καὶ ἐπάρθητε, πύλαι αἰώνιοι, καὶ εἰσελεύσεται ὁ Βασιλεὺς τῆς δόξης*, Ps. 23:7 LXX). From inside the church, someone standing there responds: **"Who is this King of glory?"** (*Τίς ἐστιν οὗτος ὁ Βασιλεὺς τῆς δόξης;*). The Bishop replies: **"The Lord strong and mighty, the Lord mighty in battle!"** (*Κύριος κραταιὸς καὶ δυνατός, Κύριος δυνατὸς ἐν πολέμῳ*, Ps. 23:8 LXX). The foregoing questions and answers are repeated three times. After this, the Bishop, taking the Diskarion [Paten] with the Holy Relics, signs the doors three times in the form of a Cross. When the doors have been opened, the Bishop enters the church with the Clergy and all the people with them, while the Dismissal Troparion of the Consecration is chanted in Tone 4: **"As the splendor of the firmament on high, Thou hast revealed the beauty of the holy dwelling of Thy glory here below, O Lord: establish it unto the ages of ages, and accept our supplications unceasingly offered therein through the Theotokos, O Life and Resurrection of all!"** (*Ὡς τοῦ ἄνω στερεώματος τὴν εὐπρέπειαν...*).

Having entered the Holy Sanctuary [Bema], the Bishop deposits the Holy Relics into the silver reliquary box prepared for them and, pouring Holy Myron upon them, secures them with the greatest care and attention, exclaiming three times: **"May the memory of the Founders of this holy House be eternal!"** (*Αἰωνία ἡ μνήμη τῶν κτιτόρων τοῦ ἁγίου Ναοῦ τούτου*). The people respond each time: **"Eternal be the memory!"** (*Αἰωνία ἡ μνήμη*).

Amen."** (*Εἰς τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ Πατρὸς καὶ τοῦ Υἱοῦ καὶ τοῦ ἁγίου Πνεύματος, Ἀμήν*). With the water poured forth and the cakes of soap, he washes the Holy Table and wipes it with new natural sponges. Because the Column cannot be washed by hands alone, he cleanses and dries it with the sponge. * *Note on the Consecration of Antimensia:* Antimension (pl. *Antimensia*): A consecrated linen cloth depicting the Deposition / Burial of Our Lord Jesus Christ (the *Plashchanytsia*), containing sewn relics of martyrs.
