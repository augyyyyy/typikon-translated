Paragraph 69 introduces Situation XXIII: concurrence on Holy and Great Friday or Holy and Great Saturday: the Feast is transferred and celebrated on Easter Sunday itself together with the Resurrection Office. Violakis transcribes the canonical decree of the Ecumenical Patriarchate: "The Holy and Great Church of Christ, having received this tradition from Patriarchs and Hierarchs from a century ago to the present, observing the disagreement and confusion occurring throughout all the churches in cities and villages, took thought especially concerning the great solemnity of the Annunciation. And indeed, for the cessation of the apparent anomaly, especially in the ears of the simple folk who do not perceive deeply the things of the Spirit, the Church ordained that, if the Feast falls on these two sorrowful days [Holy Friday or Holy Saturday], it should be transferred to the supreme day of Pascha itself named after the Lord. In order that joyous things may be sung and melodized together with joyous things, the Church by economy prevented the mixing of the bright with the sorrowful, in order also that no disharmony might be shown between: 'Today He is hung upon the tree' (*Σήμερον κρεμᾶται ἐπὶ ξύλου*) and: 'Today are the glad tidings of joy' (*Σήμερον χαρᾶς εὐαγγέλια*), and between: 'Pouring out tears of lamentation over Thee, O Pure One' (*Δακρυρρόους θρήνους ἐπὶ σὲ Ἁγνὴ*) and: 'Gabriel to the Virgin the glad tidings' (*Ὁ Γαβριὴλ τῇ Παρθένῳ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον*). But these provisions, as has been said, pertain only to the churches in cities and villages..."
