Ἑσπερινῷ, μετὰ τὸν Προοιμιακὸν καὶ τὸ Ψαλτήριον, εἰς τὸ Κύριε ἐκέκραξα ἀναστάσιμα Στιχηρὰ δ΄, καὶ τῆς ἑορτῆς ς΄, Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν, Ἀπεστάλη ἐξ οὐρανοῦ, Εἴσοδος, Φῶς ἱλαρόν, καὶ τὰ Ἀναγνώσματα τῆς ἑορτῆς. Καθίσματα ἐν μὲν τῇ α΄ καὶ β΄ Στιχολογίᾳ ἀνὰ δύο Ἀναστάσιμα καὶ ἀνὰ ἓν τῆς ἑορτῆς, ἐν δὲ τῇ γ΄ μόνα τὰ τῆς ἑορτῆς, ἡ Αἴτησις, καὶ μετ᾿ αὐτὴν ἡ Ὑπακοὴ τοῦ ἤχου καὶ οἱ Ἀναβαθμοί, τὸ Προκείμενον τῆς ἑορτῆς Εὐαγγελίζεσθε ἡμέραν ἐξ ἡμέρας, τὸ Πᾶσα πνοή, καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον Ἀναστᾶσα Μαριάμ, τὸ Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ καὶ ὁ Ν΄ ψαλμὸς χῦμα, Δόξα, Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου, Καὶ νῦν, τὸ αὐτό, ὁ Στίχος Ἐλέησόν με ὁ Θεὸς καὶ τὸ Ἰδιόμελον Εὐαγγελίζεται ὁ Γαβριήλ, Κανόνες ὁ Ἀναστάσιμος καὶ ὁ τῆς ἑορτῆς· ἀπὸ γ΄ Ὠδῆς τὸ ἀναστάσιμον Κοντάκιον μετὰ τοῦ Οἴκου, εἶτα τὸ Κάθισμα Ὁ Λόγος τοῦ Θεοῦ ἅπαξ· ἀφ᾿ ς΄ Κοντάκιον καὶ Οἶκος τῆς ἑορτῆς καὶ τὸ Μηνολόγιον, αἱ Καταβασίαι Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου, καὶ ἡ θ΄ τῆς ἑορτῆς· Ἐξαποστειλάρια τὸ Ἀναστάσιμον, καὶ τὰ τῆς ἑορτῆς· εἰς τοὺς Αἴνους, Ἀναστάσιμα δ΄ καὶ τῆς ἑορτῆς δ΄, Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν, Τὸ ἀπ᾿ αἰῶνος μυστήριον, Δοξολογία Μεγάλη καὶ Σήμερον Σωτηρία.

# Month of March: The Annunciation of the Theotokos (Situation IX Concl. §§ 27–29)

## March 25: The Annunciation of Our Most Holy Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary (Situation IX Concl. §§ 27–29) [Violakis, p. 457]

    in Situation Three, §§ 13 and 14.
27.    On Saturday at Great Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and the reading of the Psalter [Kathisma 1, Pss. 1–8 LXX], at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant 4 Resurrection Stichira and 6 of the Feast; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"He was sent from heaven"** (*Ἀπεστάλη ἐξ οὐρανοῦ*). Entrance, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), and the Readings of the Feast. At the Aposticha: the Resurrection Stichira; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"Today are the glad tidings of joy"** (*Σήμερον χαρᾶς εὐαγγέλια*); the Resurrection Dismissal Troparion, that of the Feast twice, and Dismissal.
28.    On Sunday at Matins, after **"Blessed is our God"** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεός*), the Trisagion Prayers, and Psalm 50 [LXX] (the Triadic Canon being omitted, the Liti of the Feast is chanted); then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion of the Feast, and the Hexapsalmos. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers, the Psalter reading of the day, and the Polyeleos of the Feast. Kathismata: at the First and Second Psalter readings [Stichologiai], two of the Resurrection and one of the Feast; at the third reading, those of the Feast alone; the Litany, and after it the Hypakoe of the Tone and the Anavathmoi; the Prokeimenon of the Feast: **"Proclaim His salvation from day to day"** (*Εὐαγγελίζεσθε ἡμέραν ἐξ ἡμέρας*, Ps. 95:2 LXX). Then **"Let everything that hath breath"** (*Πᾶσα πνοή*), and the Gospel: **"Mary arose"** (*Ἀναστᾶσα Μαριάμ*, Luke 1:39–49, 56). Then **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (*Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ θεασάμενοι*), Psalm 50 [LXX] read plainly; "Glory...": **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos"** (*Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου*); "Both now and ever...": repeat the same; the Verse: **"Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy"** (*Ἐλέησόν με ὁ Θεός*, Ps. 50:3 LXX), and the Idiomelon: **"Gabriel announces"** (*Εὐαγγελίζεται ὁ Γαβριήλ*). Canons: that of the Resurrection and that of the Feast. After the 3rd Ode, the Resurrection Kontakion with the Ikos, then the Kathisma: **"The Word of God"** (*Ὁ Λόγος τοῦ Θεοῦ*) once; after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Feast, and the Menologion. The Katavasiai: **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*), and the 9th Ode of the Feast. Exapostilaria: the Resurrection Exapostilarion and those of the Feast. At the Praises: 4 Resurrection Stichira and 4 of the Feast; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"The mystery hidden from all eternity"** (*Τὸ ἀπ᾿ αἰῶνος μυστήριον*); Great Doxology, and **"Today is salvation"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*).
29.    At the Divine Liturgy: the Antiphons of the Feast; the Entrance Hymn (Eisodikon): **"Proclaim His salvation from day to day... Save us, O Son of God, Who art risen from the dead..."** (*Εὐαγγελίζεσθε ἡμέραν ἐξ ἡμέρας . . . ὁ ἀναστὰς ἐκ νεκρῶν*); the Resurrection Dismissal Troparion, that of the Feast, and the Kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*). Epistle and Gospel of the Feast. At "Especially": **"O earth, announce good tidings of great joy . . . As a living ark of God"** (*Εὐαγγελίζου γῆ χαρὰν μεγάλην . . . Ὡς ἐμψύχῳ Θεοῦ κιβωτῷ*); Communion Verse of the Feast [Ps. 131:13 LXX]; **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς*), and Dismissal. The Office of the Venerable Father [John of the Ladder] is chanted at Compline.
    The Apodosis of the Feast takes place that evening as previously prescribed in Situation Three, § 18.[^295]

## March 25: The Annunciation of Our Most Holy Lady, the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary (Situation X §§ 30–31) [Violakis, p. 459]

### SITUATION TEN

30.    If the Feast occurs on Monday of the 5th Week [the printed text adds: "and the 6th Week", which is a typographical error; it must be the 5th Week, since Situations 11–15 treat the intervening days]: on Saturday at Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and the reading of the Psalter [Kathisma 1, Pss. 1–8 LXX], at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant 4 Resurrection Stichira, 3 of the Forefeast, and 3 of the Triodion; "Glory...": **"A tongue which she knew not"** (*Γλῶσσαν ἣν οὐκ ἔγνω*); "Both now and ever...": the first Theotokion of the Tone. Entrance, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), and the Prokeimenon. At the Aposticha: the Resurrection Stichira; "Glory...": of the Triodion; "Both now and ever...": **"Gabriel announces"** (*Εὐαγγελίζεται ὁ Γαβριήλ*); the Resurrection Dismissal Troparion, that of the Triodion, that of the Forefeast, and Dismissal.
31.    On Sunday at Matins, after Psalm 50 [LXX], the Triadic Canon and the Triadic hymn: **"It is truly meet"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*); the Trisagion Prayers, the Forefeast Dismissal Troparion, and the Hexapsalmos. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Resurrection Dismissal Troparion, that of the Triodion, and that of the Forefeast; the Psalter and the Amomos [Kathisma 17, Ps. 118 LXX]. The Resurrection Kathismata, the Evlogitaria, the Anavathmoi, and the Prokeimenon of the Tone, then the Hypakoe (canonically, the Hypakoe precedes the Anavathmoi). Canons: that of the Resurrection and that of the Forefeast, with the Verse: "Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee." After the 3rd Ode, the Forefeast Kathisma: **"Today the entire creation"** (*Σήμερον ἅπασα*) once; after the 6th Ode, the Resurrection Kontakion and Ikos, the Menologion, and the Synaxarion of the Triodion. The Katavasiai: **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*), and the entire order of the Sunday Matins Gospel as prescribed for the Sundays of the Triodion; then **"More honorable than the Cherubim"**. Exapostilaria: the Resurrection Exapostilarion, that of the Triodion, and that of the Forefeast. At the Praises: 4 Resurrection Stichira and 4 of the Forefeast: **"Having compassion on His handiwork, the Creator"** (*Κατοικτιρήσας τῷ πλάσματι ὁ πλάστης*); "Glory...": of the Triodion; "Both now and ever...": **"Most blessed art thou"** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη*); Great Doxology, **"Today is salvation"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*), and following in order the Divine Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great, in which the Typika and the Beatitudes are sung. Kontakion: **"Upon the descent of the All-Holy Spirit"** (*Ἐπελεύσει Πνεύματος τοῦ Παναγίου*); Epistle and Gospel of the Sunday; Communion Verse: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον ἐκ τῶν οὐρανῶν*, Ps. 148:1 LXX); **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς*), and Dismissal.
    On that Sunday evening and Monday morning, the Office of the Feast is chanted together with the Divine Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom, as prescribed in Situation One, §§ 3–5.
    The Apodosis of the Feast is celebrated that evening as prescribed in Situation Three, § 18.[^296]
