# Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation I §§ 1–2)

## Τῇ Θ΄: Τῶν Ἁγίων Μ΄ Μαρτύρων (Περίπτωσις Α΄ §§ 1–2) [Violakis, p. 416]

### ΠΕΡΙΠΤΩΣΕΙΣ

#### Α΄.

1.    Εἰ τύχοι ἡ μνήμη τῶν Μαρτύρων τῇ Τετάρτῃ τῆς Α΄ ἑβδομάδος τῶν Νηστειῶν, τῇ Τρίτῃ πρωΐ, μετὰ τὸν Προοιμιακόν, εἰς τὸ Κύριε ἐκέκραξα ψάλλομεν τὰ γ΄ Προσόμοια τῆς σειρᾶς τοῦ Τριῳδίου καὶ τῶν ἁγίων Προσόμοια Φέροντες τὰ παρόντα γενναίως γ΄. Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν, Προσόμοιον Σταυροθεοτοκίον, Φῶς ἱλαρόν, καὶ τὰ Ἀναγνώσματα τῆς ἡμέρας. Εἰς τὰ Ἀπόστιχα, τὸ Ἰδιόμελον τῶν Ἀποστίχων δίς, καὶ τὸ Μαρτυρικόν· Δόξα, Καὶ νῦν, Σταυροθεοτοκίον, Νῦν ἀπολύεις, τὸ Τρισάγιον, τὸ Θεοτόκε Παρθένε κτλ. καὶ Ἀπόλυσις.

2.    Τῇ Τετάρτῃ πρωΐ, μετὰ τὸν Ν΄ ψαλμόν, ἀντὶ Λιτῆς λέγονται τὰ Ἰδιόμελα τῶν Αἴνων καὶ ἓν Δόξα τῶν Ἁγίων μετὰ τοῦ καταλλήλου Θεοτοκίου· εἶτα τὸ Τρισάγιον, τὸ Ἀπολυτίκιον τῶν Ἁγίων καὶ ὁ Ἑξάψαλμος. Εἰς τὸ Θεὸς Κύριος, τὸ Ἀπολυτίκιον τῶν Ἁγίων δὶς καὶ τὸ Θεοτοκίον· εἶτα τὸ Ψαλτήριον τῆς ἡμέρας, τὰ Καθίσματα τῶν Ἁγίων κατὰ σειράν, οἱ Ἀναβαθμοί, τὸ α΄ Ἀντίφωνον τοῦ δ΄ ἤχου, τὸ Προκείμενον Θαυμαστὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἐν τοῖς Ἁγίοις αὐτοῦ, τὸ Πᾶσα πνοὴ καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Ὄρθρου τῶν Ἁγίων, ὁ Ν΄ ψαλμὸς χῦμα, Δόξα, Ταῖς τῶν ἀθλοφόρων, Καὶ νῦν, Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου, ὁ Στίχος Ἐλέησόν με ὁ Θεὸς καὶ τὸ ἰδιόμελον Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόα. Ὁ Κανὼν τῆς Θεοτόκου Ὑγρὰν διοδεύσας καὶ ὁ τῶν Ἁγίων· ἀπὸ γ΄ Ὠδῆς τὸ Κάθισμα κτλ. ἀφ᾿ ς΄ Κοντάκιον καὶ Οἶκος τῶν Ἁγίων, καὶ τὸ Μηνολόγιον· Καταβασίαι Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου, εἶτα στιχολογοῦμεν Τὴν τιμιωτέραν, τὰ Ἐξαποστειλάρια, οἱ Αἶνοι, Δόξα, Ἀθλοφόροι Χριστοῦ, Καὶ νῦν, Μακαρίζομέν σε, Δοξολογία Μεγάλη, τὸ Ἀπολυτίκιον τῶν Ἁγίων, καὶ ἀναγινώσκονται αἱ Ὧραι λιταί, ἐν αἷς λέγομεν Ἀπολυτίκιον Τὰς ἀλγηδόνας τῶν Ἁγίων, καὶ Κοντάκιον Πᾶσαν Στρατιάν. Εἰς τὴν Θ΄ Ὥραν ψάλλονται οἱ Μακαρισμοὶ καὶ τὰ λοιπὰ ὡς διετάχθησαν ἐν τῇ μνήμῃ τοῦ ἁγίου Χαραλάμπους ἐν περιπτώσει Η΄ § 14.

# Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation I §§ 1–2)

## March 9: The Holy Forty Martyrs [in the Lake of Sebastea] (Situation I §§ 1–2) [Violakis, p. 417]

### SITUATIONS

#### SITUATION ONE

1.    If the Commemoration of the Martyrs occurs on Wednesday of the First Week of the Great Fast: on Tuesday morning, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX], at "Lord, I have cried", we sing the 3 Prosomia of the cycle of the Triodion and 3 Prosomia of the Saints: **"Bravely enduring the sufferings of the present life"** (*Φέροντες τὰ παρόντα γενναίως*); "Glory... Both now and ever...": Prosomion Stavrotheotokion; "O Gladsome Light", and the Readings of the day. At the Aposticha: the Idiomelon of the Aposticha twice, and the Martyrikon; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Stavrotheotokion; then **"Now let Thy servant depart"** (*Νῦν ἀπολύεις*, Luke 2:29), the Trisagion Prayers, **"Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*), etc., and Dismissal.

2.    On Wednesday morning: after Psalm 50 [LXX], instead of the Liti we chant the Idiomela of the Praises and one "Glory..." of the Saints with its appropriate Theotokion; then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion of the Saints, and the Hexapsalmos. At "God is the Lord": the Dismissal Troparion of the Saints twice, and the Theotokion; then the Psalter of the day, the Kathismata of the Saints in order, the Anavathmoi (the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4), the Prokeimenon: **"Wondrous is God in His Saints"** (*Θαυμαστὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἐν τοῖς Ἁγίοις αὐτοῦ*, Ps. 67:36 LXX); then "Let every breath praise the Lord" and the Matins Gospel of the Saints; Psalm 50 [LXX] read plainly; "Glory...": **"Through the intercessions of the prize-bearers"** (*Ταῖς τῶν ἀθλοφόρων*); "Both now and ever...": **"Through the intercessions of the Theotokos"** (*Ταῖς τῆς Θεοτόκου*); the Verse: "Have mercy on me, O God" [Ps. 50:3 LXX] and the Idiomelon: **"David cried out prophetically in the psalms"** (*Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόα*). The Canon of the Theotokos: **"Traversing the deep water"** (*Ὑγρὰν διοδεύσας*) and that of the Saints; after the 3rd Ode, the Kathisma, etc.; after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Saints, and the Menologion; Katavasiai: **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*); then we chant with verses "More honorable than the Cherubim"; the Exapostilaria, the Praises; "Glory...": **"O prize-bearers of Christ"** (*Ἀθλοφόροι Χριστοῦ*); "Both now and ever...": **"We bless thee, O Virgin"** (*Μακαρίζομέν σε*); Great Doxology, the Dismissal Troparion of the Saints, and the plain Hours are read, at which we say the Dismissal Troparion: **"By reason of the sufferings which the Saints endured for Thy sake, O Lord"** (*Τὰς ἀλγηδόνας τῶν Ἁγίων*), and the Kontakion: **"Every army"** (*Πᾶσαν στρατιάν*). At the 9th Hour, the Beatitudes and the rest of the service are chanted as prescribed in the commemoration of the Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos under Situation Eight, Paragraph 14.[^275]

# Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation II Concl.; Situation III § 6; Situation IV § 7)

## Τῇ Θ΄: Τῶν Ἁγίων Μ΄ Μαρτύρων (Περιπτώσεις Β΄ Τέλος, Γ΄ § 6, Δ΄ § 7) [Violakis, p. 420]

### Γ΄.

6.    Εἰ τύχοι τῇ Παρασκευῇ τῆς αὐτῆς ἑβδομάδος, τῇ Πέμπτῃ πρωῒ ὁ Ἑσπερινὸς ψάλλεται, ὡς προδιετυπώθη ἐν περιπτώσει Α΄ § 1. Τῇ δὲ Παρασκευῇ πρωῒ ὁ Ὄρθρος μέχρι τῆς Δοξολογίας, καὶ αἱ Ὧραι ψάλλονται, ὡς διετάχθησαν ἐν περιπτώσει Α΄ § 2. Ἐν τῷ Ἑσπερινῷ μετὰ τὸν Προοιμιακὸν καὶ τὰ Πρὸς Κύριον, εἰς τὸ Κύριε ἐκέκραξα ψάλλομεν τὸ Ἰδιόμελον Δεῦτε πιστοὶ δίς, δ΄ Προσόμοια τῶν μαρτύρων, καὶ δ΄ τοῦ ἁγίου Θεοδώρου. Κοινωνικὸν Εἰς μνημόσυνον, Εὐλογήσω τὸν Κύριον καὶ Ἀπόλυσις. Τὸ ἑσπέρας ἀναγινώσκονται οἱ Οἶκοι τῆς Θεοτόκου, ὡς εἴθισται, Κοντάκιον Πίστιν Χριστοῦ.

    Τῇ Παρασκευῇ τῆς Β΄, Γ΄ καὶ Δ΄ ἑβδομάδος, ἡ αὐτή ἐστι τῆς Ἀκολουθίας διάταξις (ἐξαιρουμένων ἐν τῷ Ἑσπερινῷ τῆς Προηγιασμένης τῶν Στιχηρῶν τοῦ ἁγίου Θεοδώρου), ψάλλεται δὲ τὸ Ἰδιόμελον τῆς ἡμέρας δίς, τὰ δ΄ τοῦ ἤχου Μαρτυρικὰ καὶ δ΄ τῶν Ἁγίων. Δόξα, τῶν Ἁγίων, Καὶ νῦν, τὸ α΄ Θεοτοκίον τοῦ ἤχου κτλ. ὡς ἀνωτέρω.

7. # Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation IV Concl.; Situation V Opening §§ 8–9)

## Τῇ Θ΄: Τῶν Ἁγίων Μ΄ Μαρτύρων (Περιπτώσεις Δ΄ Τέλος, Ε΄ Ἀρχὴ §§ 8–9) [Violakis, p. 422]

Τὸ ἑσπέρας ἀναγινώσκονται οἱ Οἶκοι τῆς Θεοτόκου, ὡς εἴθισται, Κοντάκιον Πᾶσαν στρατιάν.

Τῷ δὲ Σαββάτῳ πρωῒ ὁ Ὄρθρος μετὰ τῆς Λειτουργίας τοῦ Χρυσοστόμου ψάλλονται, ὡς διετάχθησαν ἐν τῇ τοῦ ἁγίου Χαραλάμπους ἑορτῇ, ἐν περιπτώσει ΙΒ΄ § 21. Εἰς τὴν Λειτουργίαν, ὁ Ἀπόστολος τοῦ Τριῳδίου καὶ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον τῶν Ἁγίων. Κοινωνικόν, Εἰς μνημόσυνον, Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς καὶ Ἀπόλυσις.

### Ε΄.

8.    Εἰ τύχοι ἡ μνήμη τῶν Ἁγίων τῇ Α΄, Β΄, Γ΄, Δ΄ καὶ Ε΄ Κυριακῇ τῶν Νηστειῶν, τῷ Σαββάτῳ ἑσπέρας μετὰ τὸν Προοιμιακὸν καὶ τὸ Ψαλτήριον, εἰς τὸ Κύριε ἐκέκραξα ψάλλομεν Ἀναστάσιμα τοῦ τυχόντος ἤχου δ΄, τοῦ Τριῳδίου γ΄, καὶ τῶν Ἁγίων γ΄. Δόξα τοῦ Τριῳδίου, Καὶ νῦν τὸ α΄ Θεοτοκίον τοῦ ἤχου, Εἴσοδος Φῶς ἱλαρὸν καὶ τὸ Προκείμενον. Εἰς τὰ Ἀπόστιχα τὰ ἀναστάσιμα Στιχηρά, Δόξα τῶν Ἁγίων Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόων, Καὶ νῦν, (ἐν τῇ Β΄, Δ΄ καὶ Ε΄ Κυριακῇ Ὢ θαύματος καινοῦ, ἐν δὲ τῇ Α΄ Κυριακῇ καὶ τῇ Γ΄ τὰ Καὶ νῦν τοῦ Τριῳδίου), τὸ ἀναστάσιμον Ἀπολυτίκιον, τῶν Ἁγίων, τοῦ Τριῳδίου καὶ τὸ Θεοτοκίον (ἐν δὲ τῇ Α΄ καὶ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ, τὸ ἀναστάσιμον Ἀπολυτίκιον, τῶν Μαρτύρων καὶ ἀντὶ Θεοτοκίων τὰ τοῦ Τριῳδίου Τὴν ἄχραντον εἰκόνα σου, ἢ τὸ Σῶσον Κύριε τὸν λαόν σου), καὶ ἡ Ἀπόλυσις.

9. Εἰς τὸ Θεὸς Κύριος τὰ Ἀπολυτίκια ὡς εἰς τὸν Ἑσπερινόν, εἶτα τὸ Ψαλτήριον καὶ ὁ Πολυέλεος, τὰ ἀναστάσιμα Καθίσματα κατὰ σειράν, καὶ τὸ τῆς γ΄ Στιχολογίας τῶν Ἁγίων (ἐν δὲ τῇ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ τὸ τελευταῖον τῶν Ἁγίων, καὶ ἀντὶ Θεοτοκίου, τὸ Ἐν Παραδείσῳ μὲν τὸ πρίν), εἶτα τὰ Εὐλογητάρια, ἡ Ὑπακοή, οἱ Ἀναβαθμοὶ τοῦ ἤχου καὶ τὸ Προκείμενον. # Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation IV Concl.; Situation V Opening §§ 8–9)

## March 9: The Holy Forty Martyrs [in the Lake of Sebastea] (Situation IV Concl., Situation V Opening §§ 8–9) [Violakis, p. 423]

In the evening, the Ikoi of the Theotokos [the Akathist Stasis] are read, as is the custom, with the Kontakion: **"Every army"** (*Πᾶσαν στρατιάν*).

On Saturday morning, Matins with the Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom are sung, as prescribed in the Feast of the Holy Hieromartyr Charalampos under Situation Twelve, Paragraph 21. At the Liturgy, the Epistle of the Triodion [Heb. 1:1–12] and Gospel of the Saints; Communion Verse: **"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance"** (*Εἰς μνημόσυνον αἰώνιον*, Ps. 111:6 LXX); "We have seen the true light", and Dismissal.

### SITUATION FIVE

8.    If the Commemoration of the Saints occurs on the First, Second, Third, Fourth, or Fifth Sunday of the Great Fast: on Saturday evening, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and the Psalter [Kathisma 1], at "Lord, I have cried", we sing 4 Resurrectional hymns of the Tone of the week, 3 of the Triodion, and 3 of the Saints; "Glory...": of the Triodion; "Both now and ever...": the first Dogmatic Theotokion of the Tone occurring; Little Entrance, "O Gladsome Light", and the Prokeimenon. At the Aposticha: the Resurrectional Stichira; "Glory...": of the Saints: **"David cried out prophetically"** (*Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόων*); "Both now and ever...": on the 2nd, 4th, or 5th Sunday: **"O extraordinary marvel"** (*Ὢ θαύματος καινοῦ*); on the 1st and 3rd Sundays, the Theotokia of the Triodion; the Dismissal Troparia: of the Resurrection, of the Saints, of the Triodion, and the Theotokion (on the 1st and 3rd Sundays: the Dismissal Troparion of the Resurrection, that of the Martyrs, and instead of the Theotokion the Dismissal Troparia of the Triodion: **"We venerate Thy most pure icon"** [*Τὴν ἄχραντον εἰκόνα σου*] on the 1st Sunday, or **"Save, O Lord, Thy people"** [*Σῶσον Κύριε τὸν λαόν σου*] on the 3rd Sunday), and Dismissal.

9.    On Sunday morning at Matins: after Psalm 50 [LXX], the Triadic Canon; then the Liti; "Glory...": of the Saints: **"In songs and hymns let us praise"** (*Ἐν ᾠδαῖς ἀσμάτων*); "Both now and ever...": **"O Theotokos, thou art the true vine"** (*Θεοτόκε σὺ εἶ ἡ ἄμπελος*); on the 1st Sunday, "Both now and ever...": **"Rejoice, O venerable prophets"** (*Χαίρετε Προφῆται τίμιοι*); on the 3rd Sunday: **"All creation beholding Thee"** (*Ὁρῶσά σε ἡ κτίσις ἅπασα*); and the Triadic hymns: **"It is truly meet"**; then the Trisagion Prayers; the Dismissal Troparion of the Martyrs (this is replaced on the 1st Sunday with **"We venerate Thy most pure icon"**, and on the 3rd Sunday with **"Save, O Lord, Thy people"**); and the Hexapsalmos. At "God is the Lord": the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers; then the Psalter and the Polyeleos; the Resurrectional Kathismata in order, and the Kathisma of the third Stichologia of the Saints (on the 3rd Sunday, the last Kathisma of the Saints, and instead of the Theotokion: **"In Paradise of old"** [*Ἐν Παραδείσῳ μὲν τὸ πρίν*]); then the Evlogitaria, the Hypakoe, the Anavathmoi of the Tone, and the Prokeimenon. The Canons: of the Resurrection, of the Saints, and of the Triodion (on the 3rd Sunday: the Canon of the Triodion and that of the Saints); after the 3rd Ode, the Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos, then the Kathismata of the Triodion, of the Saints, and the Theotokion; after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion and...[^278]

# Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation V Concl. §§ 9–10; Situation VI § 11)

## Τῇ Θ΄: Τῶν Ἁγίων Μ΄ Μαρτύρων (Περιπτώσεις Ε΄ Τέλος §§ 9–10, Ϛ΄ § 11) [Violakis, p. 424]

Οἶκος, τὰ τοῦ Τριῳδίου, τὸ Μηνολόγιον τῶν Ἁγίων καὶ τὸ Ὑπόμνημα τῆς Κυριακῆς, αἱ Καταβασίαι ἐν μὲν τῇ Α΄ καὶ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ τοῦ Τριῳδίου, ἐν δὲ τῇ Β΄, Δ΄ καὶ Ε΄ Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου. Εἶτα Στιχολογοῦμεν Τὴν τιμιωτέραν, Ἐξαποστειλάρια τὸ Ἀναστάσιμον, τοῦ Τριῳδίου, τῶν Ἁγίων καὶ τὸ Θεοτοκίον. Εἰς τοὺς Αἴνους, ἐν μὲν τῇ Α΄ καὶ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ Ἀναστάσιμα β΄, τοῦ Τριῳδίου γ΄ καὶ τῶν Μαρτύρων γ΄, ἐν δὲ τῇ Β΄ μετὰ τὰ Ἀναστάσιμα ἔρχονται τὰ τῶν Μαρτύρων καὶ ἕπονται τὰ τοῦ Ἁγίου μετὰ τῶν ἁρμοδίων Στίχων· καὶ ἐν τῇ Δ΄ καὶ Ε΄ Ἀναστάσιμα ε΄ καὶ τῶν Μαρτύρων γ΄, ὧν οἱ Στίχοι ἐν τοῖς δυσὶ τελευταίοις Τροπαρίοις Θαυμαστὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἐν τοῖς Ἁγίοις αὐτοῦ, καὶ Τοῖς Ἁγίοις τοῖς ἐν τῇ γῇ αὐτοῦ ἐθαυμάστωσεν ὁ Κύριος, Δόξα, τοῦ Τριῳδίου, Καὶ νῦν, Ὑπερευλογημένη, Δοξολογία Μεγάλη καὶ τὸ Σήμερον Σωτηρία.

10.    Εἰς τὴν Λειτουργίαν, τὰ Τυπικὰ καὶ οἱ Μακαρισμοὶ τοῦ ἤχου εἰς δ΄ καὶ τῶν Ἁγίων δ΄ (ἐν δὲ τῇ Α΄ καὶ τῇ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ τὰ Ἀντίφωνα ὡς εἰσιν ὡρισμένα· μετὰ τὴν Εἴσοδον, ἐν μὲν τῇ Α΄ καὶ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ τὸ ἀναστάσιμον Ἀπολυτίκιον, τοῦ Τριῳδίου τῶν Ἁγίων καὶ τὸ τοῦ Ἁγίου τοῦ Ναοῦ· ἐν δὲ ταῖς λοιπαῖς Κυριακαῖς, τὸ Ἀναστάσιμον τῶν Ἁγίων, τοῦ Τριῳδίου, καὶ τὸ τοῦ Ἁγίου τοῦ Ναοῦ. Κοντάκιον Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ, Ἀπόστολος τῶν τοῦ Μ. Βασιλείου· Κοινωνικὸν ἐν τῇ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ Ἐσημειώθη ἐφ᾿ ἡμᾶς, ἐν ταῖς λοιπαῖς Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον, Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς, ἐν δὲ τῇ Γ΄ Κυριακῇ Σῶσον Κύριε, καὶ Ἀπόλυσις

### Ϛ΄.

11.    Εἰ τύχοι ἐν Δευτέρᾳ τῆς Β΄, Γ΄, Δ΄, Ε΄, καὶ ΣΤ΄ ἑβδομάδος, τῇ Κυριακῇ ἑσπέρας, εἰς τὸ Κύριε ἐκέκραξα ψάλλομεν Στιχηρὰ ς΄, τοῦ Τριῳδίου Προσόμοια γ΄ καὶ γ΄ τῶν Ἁγίων. Δόξα, Τὴν λίμνην ὡς παράδεισον, Καὶ νῦν Θεοτοκίον Ἔργοις σκοτεινοῖς, Εἴσοδος Φῶς ἱλαρόν, καὶ τὸ Μέγα Προκείμενον. Εἰς τὰ Ἀπόστιχα τὸ Ἰδιόμελον ἀργῶς δίς, καὶ τὸ Μαρτυρικὸν ἅπαξ. Δόξα Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόα, Καὶ νῦν Τὴν πᾶσαν ἐλπίδα μου, Νῦν ἀπολύεις, τὸ Τρισάγιον, τὰ Ἀπολυτίκια Τὰς ἀλγηδόνας τῶν ἁγίων, Τοῦ Γαβριὴλ φθεγξαμένου σοι, καὶ Ἀπόλυσις. Τὸ Θεοτόκε παρθένε οὐ ψάλλεται διὰ τὸ τελεῖσθαι αὔριον Προηγιασμένην.

# Month of March: The Holy Forty Martyrs (Situation V Concl. §§ 9–10; Situation VI § 11)

## March 9: The Holy Forty Martyrs [in the Lake of Sebastea] (Situation V Concl. §§ 9–10, Situation VI § 11) [Violakis, p. 425]

Ikos of the Triodion, the Menologion of the Saints, and the Synaxarion [Hypomnema] of the Sunday. The Katavasiai: on the 1st and 3rd Sundays, those of the Triodion; on the 2nd, 4th, or 5th Sunday: **"I shall open my mouth"** (*Ἀνοίξω τὸ στόμα μου*). Then we chant with verses "More honorable than the Cherubim"; Exapostilaria: of the Resurrection, of the Triodion, of the Saints, and the Theotokion. At the Praises: on the 1st and 3rd Sundays, we chant 2 hymns of the Resurrection, 3 of the Triodion, and 3 of the Martyrs; on the 2nd Sunday, after those of the Resurrection, we chant those of the Martyrs and then those of the Saint [St. Gregory Palamas] with the appropriate verses; on the 4th and 5th Sundays, we chant 5 hymns of the Resurrection and 3 of the Martyrs, with the following Verses at the last two: **"Wondrous is God in His Saints"** (*Θαυμαστὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἐν τοῖς Ἁγίοις αὐτοῦ*, Ps. 67:36 LXX) and **"In the saints that are in His earth, the Lord hath magnified all His good pleasure"** (*Τοῖς ἁγίοις τοῖς ἐν τῇ γῇ αὐτοῦ ἐθαυμάστωσεν ὁ Κύριος*, Ps. 15:3 LXX); "Glory...": of the Triodion; "Both now and ever...": **"Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη*); Great Doxology, and **"Today salvation is come unto the world"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*).

10.    At the Divine Liturgy: the Typika and the Beatitudes of the Tone in 4, and 4 of the Saints (on the 1st and 3rd Sundays, we chant the Antiphons as they are appointed); after the Little Entrance, on the 1st and 3rd Sundays, the Dismissal Troparia: of the Resurrection, of the Triodion, of the Saints, and that of the Patron Saint of the Temple; on the other Sundays, the Dismissal Troparia: of the Resurrection, of the Saints, of the Triodion, and that of the Patron Saint of the Temple. Kontakion: **"To thee, the champion leader"** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*); the Epistle of the Saints and Gospel of the Sunday, and then the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great; Communion Verse: on the 3rd Sunday, **"The light of Thy countenance is signed upon us, O Lord"** (*Ἐσημειώθη ἐφ᾿ ἡμᾶς τὸ φῶς τοῦ προσώπου σου, Κύριε*, Ps. 4:7 LXX); on the other Sundays: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 148:1 LXX); "We have seen the true light", but on the 3rd Sunday **"Save, O Lord, Thy people"** [Ps. 27:9 LXX], and Dismissal.

### SITUATION SIX

11.    If the Feast occurs on Monday of the Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth, or Sixth Week: on Sunday evening, at "Lord, I have cried", we sing 6 Stichira: 3 Prosomia of the Triodion and 3 of the Saints; "Glory...": **"The lake as paradise"** (*Τὴν λίμνην ὡς παράδεισον*); "Both now and ever...": Theotokion: **"By dark works"** (*Ἔργοις σκοτεινοῖς*); Little Entrance, "O Gladsome Light", and the Great Prokeimenon. At the Aposticha: the Idiomelon is sung twice slowly, and the Martyrikon once; "Glory...": **"David cried out prophetically in the psalms"** (*Προφητικῶς ἀνεβόα*); "Both now and ever...": **"All my hope I place in thee, O Mother of God"** (*Τὴν πᾶσαν ἐλπίδα μου*); **"Now let Thy servant depart"** (*Νῦν ἀπολύεις*, Luke 2:29), the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparia: **"By reason of the sufferings which the Saints endured for Thy sake, O Lord"** (*Τὰς ἀλγηδόνας τῶν Ἁγίων*), and **"When Gabriel said unto thee"** (*Τοῦ Γαβριὴλ φθεγξαμένου σοι*), and Dismissal. The hymn **"Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*) is not sung because tomorrow the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts will be celebrated.[^279]
