For the Presentation of Our Lord, the commemoration coincides with Clean Monday (the first day of the Great Fast) when Pascha falls on its earliest possible date of March 22 JC (§§ 21–24, pp. 180–181), and with Cheesefare Sunday when Pascha falls on March 23 JC (§§ 11, 13, 20, pp. 175–179). The printed English edition on p. 947 exhibits typographical errors in Column 1 ("29 (9)" and "30 (10)" for 29 and 30 January) and shifts the Column 4 rubrical references upward by one row beginning at January 18. These defects are corrected in the critical translation.

[^541]: Physical leaf p964 (printed page 949), conclusion of the concurrence table for February 2 (The Presentation of Our Lord into the Temple) and opening of February 10 (The Holy Hieromartyr Charalambos, part 1). For the Presentation, the feast reaches its latest possible pre-Lenten concurrence on the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee when Pascha falls on April 13 JC (§§ 11–13, pp. 175–176). For Saint Charalambos, the table traces the feast across the First and Second Weeks of the Great Fast. When Pascha occurs on March 22 JC (April 4 GC), Charalambos coincides with Tuesday of the Second Week of Lent (§ 28, p. 190); when Pascha falls on March 24 JC, the feast coincides with the Sunday of the Triumph of Orthodoxy (§§ 23–25, pp. 188–189); and when Pascha falls on March 25 JC, the saint's office coincides with the Saturday of Saint Theodore the Recruit (§§ 20–22, pp. 187–188).

[^542]: Physical leaf p966 (printed page 951), continuation of the Table of Feasts covering February 10 (Saint Charalambos, conclusion) and February 24 (The First and Second Finding of the Venerable Head of the Holy Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John, part 1). The facing pages 950–951 in the printed edition exhibit a systematic typographical aberration in Column 1: the compositor miscalculated the Julian-to-Gregorian divergence as 16 days instead of 13, printing "21 (6)" through "31 (16)" instead of 21 (3 February) through 31 (13 February). For Saint John the Baptist (February 24), the earliest possible Triodion occurrence places the feast on Tuesday of the Fourth Week of the Great Fast (§§ 6–8, pp. 191–192) when Pascha falls on March 22 JC.

[^543]: Physical leaf p968 (printed page 953), continuation of the Table of Feasts for February 24 (Finding of the Head of the Forerunner, part 2). The printed English edition on p. 953 displays severe typographical defects: in the upper table, Column 4 cross-references are shifted by one row and line 17 contains the garbled compositing error "9 (1926-8 (191)". In the middle table under April Paschal dates, the English translator accidentally skipped Wednesday on line 24 (Julian Jan 24), shifting every subsequent weekday back by one row and omitting line 31 entirely. In this critical edition, the correct sequence of days and the missing line 31 have been restored from the Greek archetype on leaf p967. The table documents the concurrence of the Forerunner's feast with the Sunday of the Adoration of the Cross (§ 1, p. 190), the Second Sunday of Lent (§§ 13–15, pp. 193–194), the Sunday of Orthodoxy (§§ 10–11, pp. 192–193), Cheesefare Sunday (§ 3, p. 191), and Meatfare Sunday (§ 2, p. 191).
