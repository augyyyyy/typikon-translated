[^520]: Physical leaf p922 (printed page 907), rubric on concelebration of multiple hierarchs. The text outlines liturgical precedence: the senior hierarch presides from the cathedra wearing the Mandya, while concelebrating hierarchs enter the sanctuary directly to vest, receiving the episcopal blessing from the senior hierarch before beginning the vesting prayers.

[^521]: Physical leaf p924 (printed page 909), rubric on communion of concelebrating hierarchs and distribution of exclamations. In Constantinople practice as codified by Violakis, the presiding hierarch blesses, consecrates the Holy Gifts, and commemorates during the Great Entrance. Concelebrating hierarchs self-commune of the Holy Bread and Holy Chalice, while concelebrating priests and deacons receive the Holy Mysteries from the hands of the hierarch lowest in episcopal seniority (*ὁ τῇ τάξει ἔσχατος τῶν συλλειτουργούντων Ἀρχιερέων*).

[^522]: Physical leaf p926 (printed page 911), start of the Order of the Burial of Priests (*Τάξις εἰς κεκοιμημένον Ἱερέα*). The service commences at the deceased priest's house with the Trisagion prayers and Psalm 90, followed by the solemn procession to the temple, during which the clergy and chanters chant the slow funeral Troparia in Tone 2 (*Ὁ βάθει σοφίας*).

[^523]: Physical leaf p928 (printed page 913), the First and Second Stases of the Amomos (Psalm 118 [LXX 118, MT 119]) at the Funeral of a Priest. The refrains alternate: in the First Stasis (*Ἄμωμοι ἐν ὁδῷ*), the refrain is *Ἀλληλούϊα* (Alleluia); in the Second Stasis (*Αἱ χεῖρές σου ἐποίησάν με*), the refrain is *Ἐλέησόν με, Κύριε* (Have mercy on me, Lord). Each stasis concludes with a Little Ektenia for the departed.

[^524]: Physical leaf p930 (printed page 915), the Third Stasis of the Amomos (*Καὶ ἐλέησόν με*) with refrain *Ἀλληλούϊα*, followed immediately by the Funeral Evlogitaria in Tone 5 (*Εὐλογητὸς εἶ, Κύριε, δίδαξόν με τὰ δικαιώματά σου* / *Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes*). The final Theotokion *Χαῖρε σεμνὴ* is chanted before the Small Ektenia for the deceased and the funeral Sessional Hymn in Tone 5.
