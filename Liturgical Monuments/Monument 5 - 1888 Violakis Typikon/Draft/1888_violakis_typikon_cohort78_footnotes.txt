Paragraph 80: Great and Holy Thursday Evening (The Service of the Twelve Gospels of the Holy Passions): In the evening at the First Hour of the night (7:00 p.m.), the Priest gives the initial blessing, the Superior [Proestos] says "Glory to Thee, our God...", "O Heavenly King", the Reader recites the Trisagion Prayers, the Priest "For Thine is the kingdom...", the Reader "Lord, have mercy" (12 times), "Glory... (Hote hoi endoxoi mathētai) twice slowly and once rapidly. (Eis polla etē), and ascends the episcopal throne. The choirs then alternate in chanting the Antiphons, beginning with Antiphon 1 in Tone 8: "The rulers of the peoples took counsel together against the Lord and against His Anointed" (Archontes laōn) through Antiphon 3, concluding with the Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] in Tone 7: "Sustaining the disciples at the supper, Thou didst say: One of you will betray Me..." (En tō deipnō tous mathētas).

At the Fifteenth Antiphon, the solemn rite of the Procession of the Holy Cross (exodos tou Stavrou) is enacted according to universal Greek usage: the Priest, vested in phelonion and carrying the large carved wooden Crucifix upon his shoulder, preceded by processional fans [rhipidia], candles, and the hand-censer, exits the Sanctuary through the northern door and processes around the nave of the temple, chanting in a solemn recitative intonation: "Today is hung upon the Tree, He Who suspended the earth upon the waters..." (Sēmeron krematai epi xylou) three times, the choir repeating the verse after him while the faithful kneel in adoration; the Crucifix is then planted firmly in the center of the temple on the Tetrapod. The First Choir begins the solemn Triodion Canon of Great Friday in Tone 6 by Saint Cosmas the Melodist: "Early in the morning my soul rises unto Thee, O Word of God..." (Pros se orthrizō), with four Troparia for each Ode without biblical verses, the Heirmos being repeated slowly at the end of each Ode as Katavasia. After Ode 5, the Small Litany, the Kontakion in Tone 8: "Come, let us all hymn Him Who was crucified for us; for Mary beheld Him upon the Tree and said: Even though Thou dost endure the Cross, Thou art my Son and my God!" (Ton di' hēmas stavrōthenta), the Ikos, the Menologion, and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the Triodion on the Holy and Salvific Passions. Ode 9 is chanted with the exclamation "The Theotokos and Mother of the Light let us magnify in hymns!", followed by the Small Litany and the solemn Exaposteilarion in Tone 3: "The wise thief in a single hour Thou didst account worthy of Paradise, O Lord; enlighten me also by the wood of the Cross, and save me!" (Ton lēstēn authēmeron) three times. The Ninth Gospel is proclaimed: John 19:25–37 (Christ Entrusts His Mother to the Beloved Disciple, "It Is Finished", and the Piercing of His Sacred Side). (Ps. 150:2 LXX) and "Every member of Thy holy flesh endured dishonor for our sake..." (Ps. 150:4 LXX).
