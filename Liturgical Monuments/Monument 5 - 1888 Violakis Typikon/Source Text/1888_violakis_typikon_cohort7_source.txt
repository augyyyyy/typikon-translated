CONCERNING THE ARTOKLASIA

  11. If there is to be an Artoklasia {Blessing of the Loaves}, after the Exclamation “Blessed and glorified be the majesty of your kingdom,” the Second Choir sings a hymn from the Liti, during which the Deacon and Priest exit from the North Door holding candles in their hands, and they stand in the center of the Naos [church], where the loaves, the wine and the oil have been placed. Then the Deacon says: “Have mercy on us, O God ...,” “Again we pray for the preservation of this Holy Church and for this city,” etc., the choirs sing reverently and with subdued voice “Lord, have mercy” as the order of service prescribes. The Priest: “Hear us, O God our Saviour....” The Great Prokimena are chanted also in the Vespers of Bright Week, as they are prescribed in the Pentikostarion. Then “Peace be with you all.” Choir: “And with your spirit.” Deacon: “Let us bow our heads ...” Choir: “To You, O Lord.” Priest (or Hierarch): “O Master, all-merciful Lord,” then the Priest censes crosswise circling around the table with the Loaves, chanting “O Theotokos, Virgin ....” In all Feasts of the Lord and the Theotokos as well as the Commemorations of Saints, if an Artoklasia is held “O Theotokos, Virgin” is always sung except during Bright Week when it is replaced with “Christ is Risen.” After this is completed, the Deacon says: “Let us pray to the Lord.” Choir: “Lord, have mercy.” The Priest blesses the loaves, saying the prayer “O Lord Jesus Christ our God ....” Then “The rich became poor” is sung three times, and after that the Aposticha.

CONCERNING THE APOLYTIKIA

  15. On Saturday Vespers, the Resurrection Apolytikia of the Octoichos are chanted before any Apolytikia of the Feasts of the Theotokos which may occur on a Sunday before the Apolytikia of a Post-feast Sunday, and before the Apolytikia of the Leave-takings of Feasts of the Lord or Theotokos. On the major Feasts of the Theotokos and their Leave-takings, the Resurrection Apolytikion is chanted once and the Feast’s twice. On Post-feast Sundays, the Apolytikion of the Resurrection and of the feast are chanted once each, although at Matins the Resurrection Apolytikion is chanted twice and the feast’s once. If the Feast of the Annunciation occurs on the Third Sunday of Lent, the Apolytikia are sung in this order: that of the Resurrection, of the Cross and of the Feast; if the Feast of the Annunciation occurs on the Saturday of Lazarus: the Feast’s Apolytikion is sung twice and that of Lazarus once; if the Feast occurs on Palm Sunday: the Feast’s, Lazarus’s and the Feast’s again. On a Pre-feast Sunday of either the Lord or the Theotokos at Vespers we sing the Apolytikia of the Resurrection and the Pre-feast once each; in the morning at “God is the Lord”: the Resurrection Apolytikion twice and the Pre-feast’s once. The commemoration of a celebrating Saint occurring on a Post-feast Sunday or Leave-taking of a Feast of the Lord or Theotokos, the Apolytikia are chanted in the following order: that of the Resurrection, of the Saint, and of the Feast.
