CONCERNING THE MATINS OF SUNDAY
MATINS GOSPEL

  28.    On Sundays, after the Katavasia of the 8th Ode, the Deacon (or in his absence the Priest) exclaims "Let us pray to the Lord." The Choir: "Lord, have mercy!" three times. The Priest recites the prescribed Matins Gospel; then "Having beheld the Resurrection" is recited. Psalm 50 "Have mercy on me, O God" is chanted; Glory "Through the intercessions of the Apostles," Now and ever "Through the intercessions of the Theotokos." The Verse "Have mercy on me, O God" and "Jesus having risen." Then the Priest:10 "Save, O God, your people,... Through the mercy...." On the Sundays of the Triodion after Psalm 50, instead of Glory, "Through the intercessions of the Apostles," the following Idiomela of the Triodion are sung: Glory, "Your repentance open to me," Now and ever "Guide me to salvation." The Verse "Have mercy, O God" with the Idiomelon "The multitude of my committed sins." Then the Priest intones "Save, O God, Your people." Then the Choir chants, verse by verse, the "More honorable."10

CONCERNING THE DOXASTIKON OF THE PRAISES

  32.    On all the Sundays in which the Eothinon (morning) Gospel is read in Matins, we chant the hymn called the Eothinon as the Doxastikon of the Praises, in as much as the eleven Eothina Doxastika, as well as the Exapostilaria, are a synopsis of the eleven Matins Gospels. When a Feast of the Theotokos or its Leave-taking or the Leave-taking of a Feast of the Lord occurs on Sunday, instead of the Eothinon Doxastikon {or, simply, Eothinon}, the Doxastikon {Glory Hymn} of the Feast is sung. CONCERNING THE [NOW AND EVER] THEOTOKION
FOLLOWING THE DOXASTIKON OF THE PRAISES

  33.    On Sundays, after the Eothinon Doxastikon of the Praises, at Now and ever is chanted (always in Mode II) the Theotokion "You are truly most blessed," even if a feast of the Theotokos or its Leave-taking or the Leave-taking of a Lord's feast is celebrated.
