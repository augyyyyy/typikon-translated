This Gospel is read by several Hierarchs and Priests, breaking at the end of the 10th verse and continuing with the 16th, i.e., they go from "there they will see me" to "now the eleven disciples" until the very end of the Gospel reading. In some churches, however, both in cities and monasteries, the practice is to read the Second Eothinon [Morning Gospel] "When the Sabbath was passed" (Mark 16:1-8).
