46. For the reason that many in the cities have difficulty in leaving their homes around midnight [in order to attend the Matins Service at the Seventh Hour, that is, at 1 a.m.], for the sake of “ikonomia” and for the ease of all, the custom arose of celebrating this Matins Service at the same hour as that of the Matins of the Bridegroom, that is, at the First Hour of the night (7 p.m.). The Liturgy itself was put off until the next morning. As a result, the great length of one single liturgical event presenting both Matins and the Liturgy was alleviated by celebrating these services as two separate events done at different times.

{“Today the tomb holds Him Who holds,” “What is this sight we behold? What is the present rest? “Come you and behold our Life lying in the tomb,” “Joseph asked for the Body of Christ and he laid it in a new tomb,”} Glory, “{Moses the Great} secretly forefigured this present day {saying}.” Now and ever, “Most blessed.” The Great Doxology and at the end the Melismatic [Asmatikon] “Holy God” is sung by the choirs which precede the Epitaphios in a procession, exiting from and returning to the church. The order of procession is as follows: first the Priests, then the chanters, and after them the Patriarch with the Hierarchs carrying the Epitaphios. Returning to the Church with the rest of the procession, the Patriarch enters the Holy Sanctuary [Vima] and immediately exclaims “Let us attend. Peace be with you. Wisdom.” Then he chants “When You had gone down to death,” while he proceeds to cense the Epitaphios which is being carried around the Holy Altar Table by the Hierarchs, then the hymn⁵¹* “The angel stood by the tomb” is chanted until “Christ has shown Himself foreign {to corruption}” and then the hymn “Noble Joseph taking down Your undefiled body” until “laid it in a tomb.” Then the Priests place the Epitaphios upon the Altar Table. Then the Reader reads the Troparion of the Prophecy “O Christ, Who holds fast the ends of the earth”; the Prophecy {Ezekiel 37:1-14}. The Apostolos {1 Corinthians 5:6-8 and Galatians 3:13-14}. The Gospel {Matthew 27:62-66} is read by the Patriarch in front of the Beautiful Gate. After “For many years, [Master],” he unvests from his Hierarchical vestments,⁵²* puts on the Mantle [Mandya], and holding the pastoral staff exits from the Sanctuary and stands at the Throne. In the meantime the Archdeacon says the Litany “Let us say with all our soul,” “Let us complete our morning supplication.” The Priest: “For to You belong {mercy and salvation}” and the Dismissal by the Priest (as yesterday): “May Christ, our True God, Who for us men and for our salvation voluntarily endured His terrible Passion, the Cross, and the entombment in the flesh, have mercy upon us.”
