We begin the Ninth Hour as we did the previous three Hours {but with Psalms 69, 70, and 86; Glory, “The thief said, as he beheld the Author of life, hanging upon the Cross.” Now and ever, “O good Lord, for our sakes You were born of a Virgin and have endured crucifixion.”} The Second Choir sings the first Idiomelon {“A dazzling wonder it was to see the Maker of heaven and earth.”} Then the First Choir sings the second Idiomelon “When the transgressors nailed the Lord of glory upon the Cross,” preceded by the Verse “They divided my garments among them.” The Second Choir sings the conclusion to the above Idiomelon: “...with the Father and the Spirit.” The First Choir sings “Glory to the Father {and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit}.” The Second Choir, “Now and ever {and to the ages of ages. Amen.}” At this point the Canonarch, coming to the middle of the Church, {stands facing the Crucifix and} intones in the manner of the Apostolos the Stichiron:
Today is hung upon the Tree, He Who suspended the Land in the midst of the waters... (3 times)
{A Crown of thorns crowns Him, Who is the King of Angels.
He is wrapped about with the purple of mockery, Who wrapped the Heavens with clouds.
He received buffetings, Who freed Adam in Jordan.
He was transfixed with nails, Who is the Bridegroom of the Church.
He was pierced with a spear Who is the son of the Virgin.}
We worship Your Passion, O Christ. (3 times)
