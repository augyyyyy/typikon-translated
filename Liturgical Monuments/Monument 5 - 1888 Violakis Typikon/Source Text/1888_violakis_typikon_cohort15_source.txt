ORDER
OF
THE SUNDAY SERVICE
OF VESPERS, MATINS
AND LITURGY

On Saturday evening, after the 9th hour, the Priest having given the blessing,¹ the presiding Bishop (or the Proistamenos) says the Introductory Psalm, then the Deacon (or in his absence the Priest) the Great Litany "In peace let us pray to the Lord"; and the Priest the Exclamation "For to You belong all glory" and the entire 1st Kathisma of the Psalter is read by the designated Reader; after this the Deacon says the Small Litany "Again and again"; the Priest chants the Exclamation "For Yours is the dominion." After this the Choirs begin to chant Psalms 140 and 141 [141 and 142 RSV], that is, "Lord, I have cried unto You" and "With my voice I cry to the Lord" in the Mode of the day up to "Save me from my persecutors." From the Verse "Bring me out of prison," the seven Resurrection Stichira of the Mode and the three of the Saint of the day are sung together with the remaining verses.

    For the 1st Stichiron, the Verse:
    Bring me out of prison, so that I may give thanks to your name. [Psalm 142:7 RSV]
    For the 2nd: The righteous will surround me, for you will deal bountifully with me. [Psalm 142:7 RSV]
    For the 3rd: Out of the depths I cry to you, O Lord. Lord, hear my voice. [Psalm 130:1 RSV]

1. At every Exclamation of the Priest ending in "Amen," the Choir is obligated as representative of the people to say with melodiousness and piety "Amen"; otherwise it fails in its duty as chanter.
