CEREMONY
OF THE RETURN [REINSTALLMENT] OF THE PATRIARCH
TO THE ECUMENICAL THRONE

   The Patriarch, as indicated above, arriving with his customary entourage at the Patriarchate, comes and stands before the great gate of the Church* and vests with the Mandya [Mantle]. The Holy Hierarchs who have accompanied him enter immediately into the Holy Sanctuary. The Choirs, preceded by the Primikirios with the Divamvulon and 4 deacons, two of them censing the Patriarch as he enters the Church, sing "It is meet." The Patriarch, standing in the middle of the Church, blesses, but he does not carry the Patriarchal Staff. Two of the Hierarchs help him ascend the Throne; and then the Metropolitan of Hiraklia comes forward, and after the customary welcoming address bestows the Patriarch with the Patriarchal Staff, kissing his right hand, and then enters the Holy Sanctuary. The Great Logothetis stands at the Parathrone [a smaller throne near the Patriarchal throne]. Then the Great Ecclesiarch [Director of Ceremonies] vests the Patriarch with the Epitrachilion and the Omophorion. The Great Archdeacon exclaims "Bless, Master." The Patriarch: "Blessed is our God"; the Archdeacon recites the "Trisagion." The Patriarch: "For Yours is." The First Choir sings the Apolytikion of Saint George "As the liberator of captives," and the Second Choir sings "Open to us the gates of compassion." The Great Archdeacon: "Have mercy on us, O God, according to Your great mercy. Again let us pray for all devout and Orthodox Christians. For Our Archbishop . . . That this Holy Church may be kept," etc. The Patriarch: "Hear us, O God." At the end the First-Chanter sings the Polychronion, then the homily [is given usually by the Great Logothetis] from the Pulpit. At the conclusion, the Choirs sing

THE CEREMONY OF THE INDICTION,
IN THE GREAT CHURCH,
ON SEPTEMBER 1

   On September 1, the Ecumenical Patriarch comes down to the Patriarchal Church at Vespers and, in the morning, for Matins and the Liturgy. He stands at the Parathrone [a smaller throne near the Patriarchal throne] wearing the Epanokalymmafchon [a priest's cap with a black veil draped behind it] and carrying a Chazranion [a plain, unadorned staff]. After the Liturgy, the holy Hierarchs who were listening to the Liturgy from within the Holy Sanctuary, proceed to the Great Synodikon [the room where the Holy Synod meets], where, after the distribution of the Antidoron, the Patriarch also proceeds to the Great Synodikon, preceded by the Exapteryga [six-winged fans] and Candles, the Primikirios with the Divamvulon [two-wicked Candle], the Chanters, and vests with the Epitrachilion and the Omophorion. Then the Great Archdeacon exclaims "Bless, Master"; the Patriarch: "Blessed is our God"; the Archdeacon recites the Trisagion; the Patriarch: "For Yours is." The First Choir sings the Apolytikion "Creator of all the Universe," the Second Choir "You became a column of endurance." The First Choir sings again "Hail, O Woman, full of grace." Then the Great Archdeacon: "Have mercy on us, O God, according to Your great mercy."
The Choirs: "Lord, have mercy" (3 times)
The Second Deacon: "Again let us pray for all devout [and Orthodox Christians]."
The Choirs: "Lord, have mercy" (3 times).
The Great Archdeacon: "For our Archbishop."
The Choirs: "Lord, have mercy" (3 times).
The Second Deacon: "For this Holy Church may be kept."
The Choirs: "Lord, have mercy" (3 times).
The Great Archdeacon: "Again we pray that the Lord God hears us."
The Choirs: "Lord, have mercy" (3 times).
The Patriarch as customary: "Hear us, O God," etc., and the Dismissal
