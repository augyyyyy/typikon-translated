After the Entrance: the Resurrection Apolytikion, then “Your pure Image,” the Apolytikion of the Saint of the Church, the Kontakion “To the Champion General.” The Apostolos (Hebrews 11: 24-26, 32-40) and the Gospel of the Sunday (John 1:43-51) and the rest of the Liturgy of Saint Basil. The Kinonikon “Praise [the Lord],” “We have seen the [True] Light.” The prayer behind the Amvon²³ and the Dismissal. After the Dismissal, the Patriarch exits from the Holy Vima [Sanctuary], and ascends the Throne; the other Hierarchs and co-celebrating Priests also exit and take their respective places. Then, the Great Archdeacon exclaims “Bless Master!” and the Patriarch says the “Blessed [is our God always now and ever.]” The Archdeacon: the Trisagion. The Patriarch: “For Yours [is the Kingdom]” and, along with the other Hierarchs sings “With the souls of the righteous,” etc. He then commemorates all the departed, through time, Orthodox Christians as do the co-celebrating Hierarchs.and Priests. The Choir sings “Our Master and Hierarch” and the Dismissal follows.
