CONCERNING THE EXAPSALMOS

  18. The Priest, after the Dismissal of the Midnight Service, enters the Sanctuary, and standing in front of the Holy Altar Table proclaims with piety and fear of God: “Blessed is our God”; the Reader the Trisagion; Priest: “For Yours is.” Reader: the hymns “Save O Lord,” Glory, “O You who was elevated on the Cross,” Both now and ever,” “O Protection.” Priest: “Have mercy on us O God etc.; at the end: “For You are merciful and a lover of man...” Reader: “In the Name of the Lord Bless Father.” Priest: “Glory to the Holy and Consubstantial...” etc.; the Proistamenos {Senior Priest} says the Exapsalmos in a penitential manner, the people listening with all silence and reverence. The Exapsalmos is never omitted except during Bright Week and the Leave-taking of Easter.
