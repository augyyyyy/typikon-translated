14.    On Saturday evening at Great Vespers, at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*, Ps. 140 LXX), we chant 6 Resurrection Stichira and 4 of the Triodion; "Glory...": **"Adam sat opposite Paradise, weeping over his nakedness and crying"** (*Ἐκάθισεν Ἀδάμ*); "Both now and ever...": the first Theotokion of the Tone. At the Aposticha: the Resurrection Stichira; "Glory...": **"Adam was driven out of Paradise"** (*Ἐξεβλήθη Ἀδὰμ τοῦ Παραδείσου*); "Both now and ever...": **"My Maker and Deliverer"** (*Ὁ ποιητὴς καὶ λυτρωτής μου*). After the Third Ode, the Resurrection Kontakion with its Ikos followed by the Sessional Hymns of the Triodion with their Theotokion. After the Sixth Ode, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Triodion, the Menologion, and the Synaxarion [Postscript] of the Triodion. Katavasiai: **"When Israel traversed on foot as on dry land"** (*Ὡς ἐν ἠπείρῳ πεζεύσας ὁ Ἰσραήλ*), and the entire order of the Sunday Matins Gospel as prescribed for Sundays of the Triodion period. Exaposteilaria: that of the Resurrection and the two of the Triodion. At the Praises: 5 Resurrection Stichira and 3 of the Triodion; "Glory...": **"The time is come, which is the beginning of spiritual struggles and triumph over demons"** (*Ἔφθασε καιρός*); "Both now and ever...": **"Most blessed art Thou"** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη*); Great Doxology, and **"Today salvation"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*).

# Triodion: Sunday of Cheesefare (Concl. Liturgy § 16; Forgiveness Vespers § 17)

## Sunday of Cheesefare: Concl. Liturgy (§ 16); Forgiveness Vespers (§ 17) [Violakis, p. 675]

...At "Especially" [Megalynarion], the choir chants **"It is truly right"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*); the Communion Hymn: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 148:1 LXX); then **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς*), and Dismissal.

17.    On the evening of this same Sunday [Forgiveness Vespers opening the Holy and Great Forty Days Fast], at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*, Ps. 140 LXX), we chant 4 Penitential Stichira of the Tone of the week (these are found at the end of the Triodion among the Penitential Hymns of the Eight Tones); then 3 Stichira from the order of the Triodion and 3 from the Menaion; "Glory... Both now and ever...": the Prosomoion Theotokion from the Menaion. Entrance with the censer because of the Great Prokeimenon (this Entrance with censer is performed at all Sunday evening Vespers of Great Lent); **"Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), the Great Prokeimenon: **"Turn not away Thy face from Thy servant, for I am in trouble; hear me speedily. Attend unto my soul, and deliver it"** (*Μὴ ἀποστρέψῃς τὸ πρόσωπόν σου ἀπὸ τοῦ παιδός σου*, Ps. 68:18–19 LXX) with its verses (see § 9 of the Protheoria). Then the Litany of Fervent Supplication: **"Let us all say with all our soul and with all our mind"** (*Εἴπωμεν πάντες*) up to **"For our Archbishop [Name]"**, and immediately the Exclamation: **"For Thou art a merciful God..."** At the Aposticha: the Idiomelon: **"Thy grace hath shone forth, O Lord"** (*Ἔλαμψεν ἡ χάρις σου, Κύριε*) twice, and the Martyrikon: **"O Christ God, Who art glorified in the assembly of Thy Saints"** (*Ὁ ἐνδοξαζόμενος ἐν ταῖς μνείαις τῶν Ἁγίων σου*); "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"The ranks of Angels praise thee"** (*Τῶν Ἀγγέλων αἱ τάξεις σε*). Then **"Lord, now lettest Thou Thy servant depart"** (*Νῦν ἀπολύεις*, Luke 2:29), and after the Trisagion Prayers, the following Dismissal Troparia: **"O Theotokos Virgin, rejoice, Mary full of grace"** (*Θεοτόκε Παρθένε*), **"O Baptizer of Christ"** (*Βαπτιστὰ τοῦ Χριστοῦ*); "Glory...": **"Intercede for us, O holy Apostles and all Saints"** (*Ἱκετεύσατε ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν*); "Both now and ever...": **"Beneath thy compassion we take refuge, O Theotokos"** (*Ὑπὸ τὴν σὴν εὐσπλαγχνίαν*).⁵ Then **"Lord, have mercy"** (40 times). The Priest intones: **"He Who is blessed, Christ our God, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** The Reader recites: **"O Heavenly King, strengthen the faith, establish the rulers..."** Then we make three great prostrations with the Prayer of Saint Ephraim the Syrian, and the Dismissal is given without **"Through the prayers"** (*Δι᾿ εὐχῶν*). Following the Dismissal, the Second Choir chants **"All who under thy protection take refuge, O good one"** (*Πάντων προστατεύεις ἀγαθή*). While this hymn is being chanted, the clergy and the faithful approach and venerate the hand of the Patriarch (or Hierarch, or Priest), receiving mutual forgiveness and reconciliation; and only after this reconciliation is concluded is **"Through the prayers of our holy Fathers..."** proclaimed.

This liturgical order must be observed on every Sunday evening throughout Great Lent up to the Fifth Sunday of the Fast.[^404]
