Εἴσοδος, *Φῶς ἱλαρόν*, τὸ Προκείμενον καὶ τὰ Ἀναγνώσματα τοῦ Ὁσίου. 40.    Τῇ Κυριακῇ εἰς τὸν Ὄρθρον, μετὰ τὸν Ν΄ ψαλμόν, ὁ Τριαδικὸς κανὼν καὶ τὰ Τριαδικὰ *Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*, εἶτα τὸ Τρισάγιον, τὸ *Ἐν Ἰορδάνῃ* καὶ ὁ Ἑξάψαλμος. Εἰς τοὺς Αἴνους, Ἀναστάσιμα δ΄ καὶ μεθέορτα δ΄ (ὅπου δ’ ἐπισημότερον ἑορτάζεται ὁ Ὅσιος, λέγονται ἀντὶ τῶν μεθεόρτων οἱ Αἶνοι τοῦ Ἁγίου εἰς δ΄). *Δόξα* τὸ Ἑωθινόν, *Καὶ νῦν*, *Ὑπερευλογημένη*, Δοξολογία Μεγάλη καὶ τὸ *Σήμερον σωτηρία*.

# Month of January: St. Theodosios the Cenobiarch Concurrence (§ 38–41)

## Commemoration of Our Righteous Father Theodosios the Cenobiarch [Violakis, p. 333]

...his office is chanted together with that of the Afterfeast according to the order of the Menaion; if the feast falls on a Saturday, the Epistle of the Saint [2 Corinthians 4:6–15] and the Gospel of the Saturday after the Lights [Epiphany, Luke 16:10–15] are read.

39.    If the feast falls on the Sunday after the Lights: at Vespers on Saturday evening, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and Kathisma 1 of the Psalter, at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant 10 Stichira: 4 Resurrectional, 3 of the Afterfeast: **"Didst thou see how I am invested"** (*Εἶδες πῶς περίκειμαι*), and 3 of the Righteous Saint; **"Glory"**: of the Afterfeast; **"Both now and ever"**: the first Theotokion of the Tone. Entrance with the censer, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), the Prokeimenon of the day, and the Old Testament Readings of the Righteous Saint. At the Aposticha: the Resurrectional Stichira; **"Glory"**: of the Righteous Saint; **"Both now and ever"**: of the Afterfeast; Dismissal Troparia: the Resurrectional Dismissal Troparion, that of the Righteous Saint, that of the Feast, and Dismissal.

40.    On Sunday at Matins: after Psalm 50 [LXX], the Triadic Canon and the Triadic hymns: **"It is truly meet"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*); then the Trisagion Prayers, the Dismissal Troparion: **"When Thou, O Lord, wast baptized in the Jordan"** (*Ἐν Ἰορδάνῃ*), and the Hexapsalmos. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers; then the Psalter and the Amomos [Psalm 118 LXX]; the Resurrectional Kathismata and those of the Feast in place of the Theotokia; at the third Stichologia [Stasis], the Kathisma of the Righteous Saint and that of the Afterfeast; the Evlogitaria, the Hypakoe of the Tone, the Anavathmoi, and the Prokeimenon. Canons: the Resurrectional Canon, that of the Feast, and that of the Righteous Saint; after the Third Ode, the Kathisma of the Righteous Saint and that of the Afterfeast; after the Sixth Ode, the Resurrectional Kontakion and Ikos, and the Menologion of the Saints of the day; Katavasiai: **"The Lord, working wonders"** (*Στείβει θαλάσσης*), and the entire order of the Resurrectional Morning Gospel; then we recite the Magnificat: **"More honorable than the Cherubim"** (*Τὴν τιμιωτέραν*); the Resurrectional Exapostilarion, that of the Righteous Saint, and that of the Afterfeast: **"O the wonder of Thy divine works!"** (*Βαβαὶ τῶν θείων ἔργων σου*). At the Praises: 4 Resurrectional Stichira and 4 of the Afterfeast (where the Righteous Saint is celebrated with greater solemnity, instead of the afterfeast stichira, the 4 Praises of the Saint are chanted); **"Glory"**: the appointed Resurrectional Eothinon Doxastikon; **"Both now and ever"**: **"Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos"** (*Ὑπερευλογημένη ὑπάρχεις*); Great Doxology, and **"Today is salvation come into the world"** (*Σήμερον σωτηρία*).

41.    At the Divine Liturgy: the Typika and the Beatitudes of the Tone for 4 troparia, and 4 from the Sixth Ode of the Canon of the Feast; after the Little Entrance: the Resurrectional Dismissal Troparion, that of the Feast, that of the Righteous Saint, and that of the Holy Patron of the Temple; Kontakion: **"Thou hast appeared today to the universe"** (*Ἐπεφάνης σήμερον*); the Epistle of the Righteous Saint [2 Corinthians 4:6–15] and the Gospel of the Sunday after the Lights [Epiphany, Matthew 4:12–17]. At **"Especially"**: **"It is truly meet"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*); Communion Verse: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 148:1 LXX); **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς τὸ ἀληθινόν*), and Dismissal.[^233]

# Month of January: Sunday after the Lights with the Triodion (§ 42–44)

## Sunday after the Lights with the Triodion [Violakis, p. 335]

*Note: It is necessary to know that Holy Pascha can occur as early as March 22 and as late as April 24; consequently, the period of the Lenten Triodion may begin in the earliest possible instance on January 11, and in the latest possible instance on February 13. Concerning this, see the Paschal Tables [Heortodromion].*

42.    If the period of the Lenten Triodion begins on January 11 and this coincides with the Sunday after the Lights [Epiphany], the office is chanted as follows: at Vespers on Saturday evening, after the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX] and Kathisma 1 of the Psalter, at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant 10 Stichira: 4 Resurrectional, 3 of the Triodion, and 3 of our Righteous Father Theodosios; **"Glory"**: **"Almighty Lord"** (*Παντοκράτορ Κύριε*); **"Both now and ever"**: the first Theotokion of the Tone. Entrance with the censer, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), the Prokeimenon of the day, and the Old Testament Readings of the Righteous Saint. At the Aposticha: the Resurrectional Stichira; **"Glory"**: of the Righteous Saint; **"Both now and ever"**: of the Triodion; Dismissal Troparia: the Resurrectional Dismissal Troparion, that of the Righteous Saint, that of the Feast, and Dismissal.

43. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparia as at Vespers; then the Psalter and the Amomos [Psalm 118 LXX]; the Resurrectional Kathismata with those of the Afterfeast in place of the Theotokia; the Evlogitaria, the Hypakoe of the Tone, the Anavathmoi, and the Prokeimenon.
