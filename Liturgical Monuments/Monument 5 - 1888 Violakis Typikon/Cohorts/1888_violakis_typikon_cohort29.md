## Great Vespers and the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great [Violakis, p. 267]

6.    The Deacon: **"Master, bless"** (*Εὐλόγησον, Δέσποτα*); the Priest: **"Blessed is the kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit"** (*Εὐλογημένη ἡ βασιλεία*); the Patriarch (or the Hierarch) recites the Introductory Psalm [Psalm 103 LXX]. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we sing the 4 Idiomela: **"Come, let us rejoice in the Lord"** (*Δεῦτε ἀγαλλιασώμεθα τῷ Κυρίῳ*) in 6 (repeating the first two). **"Glory... Both now and ever..."**: **"When Augustus reigned alone upon the earth"** (*Αὐγούστου μοναρχήσαντος ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς*). Entrance is made with the Holy Gospel; **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), and immediately the Canonarch intones the First Old Testament Reading: **"In the beginning God made the heaven and the earth"** (*Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἐποίησεν ὁ Θεὸς τὸν οὐρανὸν καὶ τὴν γῆν*, Genesis 1:1–13). At the conclusion, the First Choir begins chanting the Troparion: **"Having been born secretly in a cave"** (*Λαθὼν ἐτέχθης ὑπὸ τὸ σπήλαιον*), and the Second Choir responds with the refrain: **"And brought the Magi unto Thee"** (*Καὶ μάγους σοι προσήνεγκεν*); this same refrain the Choirs repeat alternately on each side until the Canonarch completes the verses of the Psalm; then the First Choir chants **"Glory... Both now and ever..."** and sings the entire Troparion again from the beginning. Thereupon the Second Canonarch reads the Seventh Reading: **"A Child is born unto us, a Son is given unto us"** (*Παιδίον ἐγεννήθη ἡμῖν*, Isaiah 9:6–7); at the conclusion, the Second Choir chants the Troparion: **"Thou didst dawn forth, O Christ, from a Virgin"** (*Ἀνέτειλας Χριστὲ ἐκ Παρθένου*), and the conclusion thereof: **"Having guided the Magi by a star"** (*Μάγους ὁδηγήσας*) is repeated by both Choirs until the Canonarch completes the verses of the Psalm as above; then the Second Choir chants **"Glory... Both now and ever..."**, repeating the entire Troparion; and the First Canonarch reads the Eighth Reading: **"The Lord spoke again to Ahaz"** (*Προέθετο Κύριος λαλῆσαι τῷ Ἄχαζ*, Isaiah 7:10–16; 8:1–4, 9–10). After these readings, the Little Litany: **"Let us pray to the Lord"** (*Τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*), etc., and the Trisagion Hymn is sung; then the Epistle [Hebrews 1:1–12], the Alleluia [Ps. 109:1 LXX], and the Holy Gospel [Luke 2:1–20] proclaimed from the Ambo by the Deacon. At the conclusion, the Patriarch (or the Hierarch) blesses the people, the Choirs chanting: **"Many years, Master"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη, Δέσποτα*); otherwise, there is said: **"Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee"** (*Δόξα σοι, Κύριε, δόξα σοι*), and the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great follows in order; Communion Verse: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 148:1 LXX), **"We have seen the true light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς*), and the Dismissal with the proper festal incipit: **"May He Who was born in a cave and lay in a manger for our salvation, Christ our true God..."** (*Ὁ ἐν σπηλαίῳ γεννηθεὶς καὶ ἐν φάτνῃ ἀνακληθεὶς διὰ τὴν ἡμῶν σωτηρίαν Χριστὸς ὁ ἀληθινὸς Θεὸς ἡμῶν...*).

# December 26: Matins Concluded & Liturgy / Sunday after Nativity & St. Stephen

## Matins and the Divine Liturgy of the Synaxis (Concluded) [Violakis, p. 275]

Hypakoe recited plain; after the Sixth Ode, the Kontakion: **"He Who was begotten of the Father before the morning star"** (*Ὁ πρὸ Ἑωσφόρου Πατρὸς ἐκλάμψας*), the Ikos, and the Synaxarion of the Feast. The Katavasiai: **"He saved the people"** (*Ἔσωσε λαόν*); then the Ninth Ode of both Canons with their Megalynaria, and the Katavasia: **"Easier for us would it be"** (*Στέργειν μὲν ἡμᾶς*). The Exapostilarion: **"Our Savior, the Dayspring from on high, hath visited us"** (*Ἐπεσκέψατο ἡμᾶς*) thrice. At the Praises: the 4 Idiomela: **"Be glad, O ye righteous"** (*Εὐφραίνεσθε, δίκαιοι*); **"Glory"**: **"Today the invisible nature"** (*Σήμερον ἡ ἀόρατος φύσις*); **"Both now and ever"**: **"Today Christ is born in Bethlehem of the Virgin"** (*Σήμερον ὁ Χριστὸς*) up to the cue words **"and we cry aloud without ceasing: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will among men."** (Note: the concluding phrase of the above Theotokion is sung by the choir as the opening proclamation of the Great Doxology); after which we sing the Dismissal Troparion: **"Thy Nativity, O Christ our God"** (*Ἡ γέννησίς σου*).
11.    In the Divine Liturgy: the Antiphons of the Feast as yesterday. Introit (Entrance Hymn): **"From the womb before the morning star have I begotten Thee"** (*Ἐκ γαστρὸς πρὸ Ἑωσφόρου ἐγέννησά σε*, Ps. 109:3 LXX). The Dismissal Troparion: **"Thy Nativity, O Christ our God"**, and the Kontakion: **"Today the Virgin gives birth unto Him Who is transcendent in essence"** (*Ἡ Παρθένος σήμερον*). Instead of the Trisagion: **"As many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ. Alleluia"** (*Ὅσοι εἰς Χριστὸν ἐβαπτίσθητε*, Gal. 3:27). The Epistle of the Saturday after the Nativity of Christ: **"O my son Timothy, follow after righteousness"** (*Τέκνον Τιμόθεε, δίωκε δικαιοσύνην*, 1 Tim. 6:11–16; note: the Greek text has "Titus", which is a typographical error for 1 Timothy 6:11–16); and the Holy Gospel likewise: **"At that time, great multitudes followed Jesus"** (*Τῷ καιρῷ ἐκείνῳ ἠκολούθησαν τῷ Ἰησοῦ ὄχλοι πολλοί*, Matt. 12:15–21). At the Megalynarion: **"Magnify, O my soul... Easier for us would it be"** (*Στέργειν μὲν ἡμᾶς*). Communion Verse: **"The Lord hath sent redemption unto His people. Alleluia"** (*Λύτρωσιν ἀπέστειλε Κύριος τῷ λαῷ αὐτοῦ*, Ps. 110:9 LXX); the Dismissal Troparion: **"Thy Nativity, O Christ our God"**, and Dismissal.

*Note.* On other days (that is, outside the occurrence of this Feast on a Saturday or Sunday), the Epistle read is: **"Brethren, both He that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one"** (*Ἀδελφοί, ὁ ἁγιάζων καὶ οἱ ἁγιαζόμενοι*, Heb. 2:11–18); and the Holy Gospel: **"When the wise men had departed"** (*Ἀναχωρησάντων τῶν Μάγων*, Matt. 2:13–23).
