**Wednesday of the First Week**

24. "Glory... Both now and ever...": Prosomion Theotokion from the Menaion. Entrance with the censer, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), and the Old Testament Readings of the day: Genesis 1:24–2:3 [LXX] and Proverbs 2:1–22 [LXX]. Then the Dismissal. During the distribution of the antidoron, Psalm 33 [LXX]: **"I will bless the Lord at all times"** (*Εὐλογήσω τὸν Κύριον ἐν παντὶ καιρῷ*) and Psalm 144 [LXX]: **"I will extol Thee, my God, my King"** (*Ὑψώσω σε, ὁ Θεός μου*) are read. Then: **"Let my prayer arise"** (*Κατευθυνθήτω*), and the rest of the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts in regular sequence.

## Third Antiphon, Divine Liturgy Conclusion, Solemn Memorial, and Lenten Vespers (§§ 30 Concluded, 31) [Violakis, p. 705]

**Third Antiphon**

*Verse 1:* **"Let the heavens and the earth praise Him, the seas, and everything that creepeth in them!"** (*Αἰνεσάτωσαν αὐτὸν οἱ οὐρανοὶ καὶ ἡ γῆ...*, Ps. 68:35 [LXX]) | *Refrain:* **"Thy pure icon do we venerate, O Good One, asking forgiveness of our offenses, O Christ God..."** (*Τὴν ἄχραντον Εἰκόνα σου προσκυνοῦμεν, Ἀγαθέ...*)  
*Verse 2:* **"This is the day which the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it!"** (*Αὕτη ἡμέρα Κυρίου, ἀγαλλιασώμεθα καὶ εὐφρανθῶμεν ἐν αὐτῇ.*, Ps. 117:24 [LXX]) | *Refrain:* **"Thy pure icon do we venerate, O Good One, asking forgiveness of our offenses, O Christ God..."**  
*Verse 3:* **"O Lord my God, I will give thanks unto Thee forever!"** (*Κύριε ὁ θεός μου εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα ἐξομολογήσομαί σοι.*, Ps. 29:13 [LXX]) | *Refrain:* **"Thy pure icon do we venerate, O Good One, asking forgiveness of our offenses, O Christ God..."**  

After the Little Entrance: the Resurrection Dismissal Troparion, then the Dismissal Troparion of the Holy Icons: **"Thy pure icon do we venerate, O Good One..."**, the Dismissal Troparion of the Patron Saint of the Church, and the Kontakion: **"To Thee, the Champion Leader, do we, thy servants, dedicate a hymn of victory..."** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ στρατηγῷ*). The Epistle [Apostolos] of the Sunday of Orthodoxy (Hebrews 11:24–26, 32–40) and the Gospel pericope of the Sunday (John 1:43–51), and the rest of the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great in regular order. The Communion Hymn: **"Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest. Alleluia"** (*Αἰνεῖτε τὸν Κύριον ἐκ τῶν οὐρανῶν...*, Ps. 148:1 [LXX]); **"We have seen the true Light"** (*Εἴδομεν τὸ φῶς τὸ ἀληθινόν*). The Prayer behind the Ambo²³ and the Dismissal.

After the Dismissal, the Patriarch exits from the Holy Sanctuary and ascends the throne in the nave; the other Hierarchs and concelebrating Priests also exit and take their respective places. Then the Great Archdeacon exclaims: **"Bless, Master!"** (*Εὐλόγησον, Δέσποτα*), and the Patriarch pronounces: **"Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν πάντοτε...*). The Archdeacon recites the Trisagion Prayers. The Patriarch exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom..."** (*Ὅτι σοῦ ἐστιν ἡ βασιλεία*), and along with the other Hierarchs chants: **"With the spirits of the righteous made perfect, give rest to the souls of Thy servants, O Savior..."** (*Μετὰ πνευμάτων δικαίων τετελειωμένων...*). He then commemorates all the departed Orthodox Christians throughout the ages, as do the concelebrating Hierarchs and Priests. The Choir sings: **"Our Master and Hierarch, O Lord, preserve for many years!"** (*Τὸν Δεσπότην καὶ Ἀρχιερέα*), and the Dismissal follows.
