...occurs on one of the days between the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee and the Sunday of Cheesefare:

**1]**    From the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee until the Friday of that same week, on whichever day the Feast may fall, the Apodosis takes place on the eighth day, that is, on the 9th of the month.
