# Η ΘΕΙΑ ΛΕΙΤΟΥΡΓΙΑ

## ΠΕΡΙ ΤΩΝ ΕΝ ΤΗι ΛΕΙΤΟΥΡΓΙΑι ΨΑΛΛΟΜΕΝΩΝ ΚΟΝΤΑΚΙΩΝ

**40.** Τὰ ἐν ὅλῳ τῷ ἐνιαυτῷ καθ’ ὡρισμένας περιόδους ψαλλόμενα ἐν ταῖς Ἱεραῖς Λειτουργίαις Κοντάκια ἔχουσιν ὡς ἑξῆς. # The Divine Liturgy

## Concerning the Kontakia Chanted in the Liturgy

**40.** Throughout the entire year during designated liturgical seasons, the Kontakia chanted at the Holy Liturgies are appointed as follows. From November 8 until the Apodosis [November 25] of the Feast of the Entrance [Presentation] of the Theotokos, we chant the Kontakion: **"The most pure Temple of the Savior, the most precious bridal chamber and Virgin, the sacred treasury of the glory of God, is led today into the house of the Lord..."** (*Ὁ καθαρώτατος Ναὸς τοῦ Σωτῆρος*). On the Eve of the Feast of the Entrance [November 20], even if it falls on a Sunday, we chant the Forefeast Kontakion: **"Filled with joy today is the whole world, on the auspicious feast of the Theotokos..."** (*Εὐφροσύνης σήμερον*). From the Apodosis of the Feast of the Entrance of the Theotokos [November 26] until Christmas Eve [December 24]: **"Today the Virgin cometh unto the cave to give birth in an ineffable manner to the Pre-eternal Word..."** (*Ἡ Παρθένος σήμερον τὸν προαιώνιον λόγον*). On December 9 [Conception of Saint Anna]: **"Today the universe rejoiceth at the conception of Anna, brought about by God..."** (*Ἑορτάζει σήμερον ἡ οἰκουμένη*). From the Feast of the Nativity of Christ [December 25] until its Apodosis [December 31]: **"Today the Virgin giveth birth to the Transcendent One, and the earth offereth a cave to the Unapproachable..."** (*Ἡ Παρθένος σήμερον τὸν ὑπερούσιον τίκτει*). On January 1 [Circumcision of our Lord and Commemoration of Saint Basil the Great]: **"The Lord of all endureth circumcision, and in His goodness cutteth off the transgressions of mortals..."** (*Ὁ τῶν ὅλων Κύριος περιτομὴν ὑπομένει*), being the Kontakion of the Circumcision. From January 2 until the Eve of Theophany [January 5]: **"In the streams today of the Jordan the Lord stood, crying out unto John: Fear not to baptize Me..."** (*Ἐν τοῖς ρείθροις σήμερον τοῦ Ἰορδάνου*), being the Kontakion of the Forefeast of Theophany. From the Feast of Theophany [January 6] until its Apodosis [January 14]: **"Thou hast appeared today unto the universe, and Thy light, O Lord, is signed upon us..."** (*Ἐπεφάνης σήμερον τῇ οἰκουμένῃ*). From the Apodosis of the Feast of Theophany [January 15] until the Apodosis of the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord [February 9]: **"Thou Who didst sanctify the virginal womb by Thy birth, and didst bless the hands of Simeon as was meet..."** (*Ὁ μήτραν παρθενικήν*). On the Eve of the Meeting of the Lord [February 1], the Forefeast Kontakion: **"A heavenly choir of heavenly angels, bending down toward the earth, beholdeth now the Firstborn of all creation borne into the Temple..."** (*Οὐράνιος χορός*). From the opening of the Triodion (if the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord hath already reached its Apodosis), we chant the Kontakion of the occurring Sunday of the Triodion;¹³ from the First Sunday of the Great Fast [Sunday of Orthodoxy] through the Fifth Sunday of the Fast: **"To Thee, the Champion Leader, we Thy servants dedicate a feast of victory and thanksgiving, as ones delivered from sufferings, O Theotokos..."** (*Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ*); on the Saturday of Lazarus: **"Christ, the Joy of all, the Truth, the Light, the Life, and the Resurrection of the world..."** (*Ἡ πάντων χαρά*); on Palm Sunday: **"Enthroned in heaven, upon earth borne on a colt, O Christ God..."** (*Τῷ θρόνῳ ἐν οὐρανῷ*); on Great and Holy Thursday: **"The traitor having taken bread into his hands, secretly reacheth forth his hands and receiveth the price of Him Who fashioned man with His hands..."** (*Τὸν ἄρτον λαβὼν εἰς χεῖρας ὁ προδότης*). [Translator's Note: It must be observed that since on Great and Holy Thursday we celebrate the Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great conjoined with Vespers, Kontakia are not chanted at the Little Entrance]. On Great and Holy Saturday: **"He Who closed the abyss is beheld dead, and wrapped in fine linen and spices, the Immortal One is laid in a tomb as mortal..."** (*Τὴν ἄβυσσον ὁ κλείσας*) [the same observation concerning the Liturgy conjoined with Vespers made for Great and Holy Thursday applies likewise to Great and Holy Saturday]. From Easter Sunday [Pascha] until its Apodosis, we chant: **"Though Thou didst descend into the grave, O Immortal One, yet didst Thou destroy the power of Hades..."** (*Εἰ καὶ ἐν τάφῳ κατῆλθες*), which is chanted likewise on Thomas Sunday (yet on the remaining days of this week until the Apodosis of Thomas Sunday we chant: **"With his inquisitive right hand, Thomas probed Thy life-giving side, O Christ God..."**, *Τῇ φιλοπράγμονι δεξιᾷ*); on the Wednesday of Mid-Pentecost until its Apodosis...

## ΠΕΡΙ ΤΟΥ ΤΡΙΣΑΓΙΟΥ ΥΜΝΟΥ

**41.** Ἐν ἑκάστῃ τελουμένῃ Λειτουργίᾳ (ἐξαιρουμένης τῆς τῶν Προηγιασμένων) ψάλλεται ὁ Τρισάγιος ὕμνος· ἐν δὲ ταῖς ἑξῆς Μ. Δεσποτικαῖς ἑορταῖς ἤτοι, ταῖς δυσὶν ἡμέραις τῆς τε ἑορτῆς τῶν Χριστουγέννων καὶ τῆς τῶν Θεοφανείων, τῷ Σαββάτῳ τοῦ Λαζάρου, τῷ Μ. Σαββάτῳ, τῇ Κυριακῇ τοῦ Πάσχα, ὡς καὶ ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ Διακαινησίμῳ ἑβδομάδι, τῇ Ἀποδόσει τοῦ Πάσχα, τῇ Κυριακῇ τῆς Πεντηκοστῆς καὶ τῇ Δευτέρᾳ τοῦ Ἁγίου Πνεύματος, ψάλλεται ἀντὶ τοῦ Τρισαγίου τὸ *Ὅσοι εἰς Χριστὸν ἐβαπτίσθητε*, ἐν δὲ τῇ ἑορτῇ τῆς Ὑψώσεως τοῦ Σταυροῦ καὶ τῇ Κυριακῇ τῆς Σταυροπροσκυνήσεως ψάλλεται *Τὸν σταυρόν σου προσκυνοῦμεν Δέσποτα*.

## Concerning the Trisagion Hymn

**41.** At every celebrated Divine Liturgy (with the exception of the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts, where it is omitted) the Trisagion Hymn [**"Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us"**] is sung. On the following Great Feasts of the Lord, namely: the two days of the Feast of the Nativity of Christ [December 25–26] and that of Theophany [January 6–7], on the Saturday of Lazarus, Great and Holy Saturday, Easter Sunday [Pascha], as well as throughout all of Bright Week, on the Apodosis of Pascha, on the Sunday of Pentecost, and on Monday of the Holy Spirit, instead of the Trisagion we sing the baptismal hymn: **"As many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ. Alleluia"** (*Ὅσοι εἰς Χριστὸν ἐβαπτίσθητε, Χριστὸν ἐνεδύσασθε. Ἀλληλούϊα*). On the Feast of the Exaltation of the Precious Cross [September 14] and on the Third Sunday of the Great Fast [Sunday of the Veneration of the Precious Cross], we chant: **"Thy Cross we worship, O Master, and Thy holy Resurrection we glorify"** (*Τὸν σταυρόν σου προσκυνοῦμεν Δέσποτα, καὶ τὴν ἁγίαν σου ἀνάστασιν δοξάζομεν*).[^105]

## Concerning the Cherubic Hymn

**44.** In every Divine Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom or Saint Basil the Great, the Cherubic Hymn: **"We who mystically image the Cherubim, and sing the Thrice-Holy Hymn unto the Life-creating Trinity, let us now lay aside all earthly care"** (*Οἱ τὰ Χερουβὶμ μυστικῶς εἰκονίζοντες*) is sung. But on Great and Holy Thursday and Great and Holy Saturday, even though the same Liturgy of Basil the Great is celebrated, the following distinction exists: on Holy Thursday, instead of **"We who mystically image the Cherubim,"** the choir chants the Troparion: **"Of Thy mystical supper, O Son of God, accept me today as a communicant; for I will not speak of Thy mystery to Thine enemies, neither like Judas will I give Thee a kiss, but like the thief will I confess Thee: Remember me, O Lord, in Thy kingdom"** (*Τοῦ δείπνου σου τοῦ μυστικοῦ*). On Great and Holy Saturday, we chant: **"Let all mortal flesh keep silence, and with fear and trembling stand, and ponder nothing earthly within itself; for the King of kings and Lord of lords cometh forth to be slain and given as food unto the faithful"** (*Σιγησάτω πᾶσα σὰρξ βροτεία*). If the Feast of the Annunciation falls on Holy Thursday, the Liturgy of Chrysostom is celebrated, yet **"Of Thy mystical supper"** is sung. In the Holy Liturgies of the Presanctified Gifts, we always chant: **"Now the heavenly powers invisibly minister with us; for lo, the King of Glory entereth; behold, the mystical and perfected sacrifice is escorted in. Let us draw near with faith and love, that we may become partakers of eternal life. Alleluia, alleluia, alleluia"** (*Νῦν αἱ δυνάμεις τῶν οὐρανῶν*).[^108]

## Concerning "It is Meet and Right" [The Megalynarion / Hirmos of Ode 9]

**45.** In every Liturgy of Saint John Chrysostom, on whatever day it may be celebrated—on all Sundays, on Afterfeast Sundays of Dominical and Marian Feasts, as well as on commemorations of celebrated Saints occurring during Afterfeasts on which at Matins we chant the Magnificat **"More honorable than the Cherubim"** (*Τὴν τιμιωτέραν*)—at the exclamation **"Especially [for our most holy, most pure, most blessed and glorious Lady, Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary]"** (*Ἐξαιρέτως*) the choir sings: **"It is truly meet to bless thee, the Theotokos, ever-blessed and most blameless and the Mother of our God. More honorable than the Cherubim, and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim, who without corruption gavest birth to God the Word, the very Theotokos, thee do we magnify"** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν ὡς ἀληθῶς*). On Feasts of the Lord and of the Theotokos and on their Apodoses occurring on a Sunday, instead of **"It is truly meet,"** the Katavasia [Hirmos] of the Ninth Ode of the Feast is chanted along with its proper Megalynarion. On the Feast of the Holy Transfiguration [August 6], instead of the Ninth Ode Katavasia **"Thy childbirth was shown forth incorrupt"** (*Ὁ τόκος σου ἄφθορος ἐδείχθη*), it is now the custom (even though the ancient Typikon makes no mention of this) to chant the troparion from the Seventh Ode: **"Now the unheard of has been heard, for the Son of the Virgin hath revealed His glory..."** (*Νῦν τὰ ἀνήκουστα ἠκούσθη*). In the Liturgies of Saint Basil the Great, instead of **"It is truly meet,"** the Megalynarion [Hirmos] chanted is the ancient Kathisma: **"All of creation rejoices in thee, O thou full of grace: the angelic hierarchy and the human race; hallowed temple and rational paradise, virgin boast, from whom God took flesh and became a child, He Who is our God before the ages..."** (*Ἐπὶ σοὶ χαίρει, Κεχαριτωμένη*).

   It must be noted that on the commemorations of Saints occurring after Thomas Sunday until the Apodosis of Pascha on any day except Sunday, after **"Especially,"** the choir sings **"It is truly meet"** and not the Paschal Megalynarion **"The Angel cried unto her that is full of grace: O Pure Virgin, rejoice! And again I say: Rejoice! Thy Son is risen from the grave on the third day... Shine, shine, O New Jerusalem..."** (*Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα*), which has already been established to be sung exclusively on Sundays; because on these ferias we do not sing [at Matins] the **"More honorable"**...[^109]
