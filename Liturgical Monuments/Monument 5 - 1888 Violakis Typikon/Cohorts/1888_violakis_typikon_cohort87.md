# Appendix: The Order of the Service of the Holy Passion in the Patriarchal Church

## The Order of the Service of the Holy Passion in the Patriarchal Church [Violakis, p. 849]

# THE ORDER OF THE SERVICE OF THE HOLY PASSION

## AS SERVED IN THE PATRIARCHAL CHURCH

---

On Holy and Great Thursday evening, about the Twelfth Hour [6:00 p.m.], the bells ring. About a half hour later His All-Holiness the Patriarch comes down into the Church with his customary entourage. Preceding him are four staff-bearing ushers announcing the arrival of the Patriarch. Then all the sacred clergy of the parish priests of the Church, with the Great Archimandrite, walk row by row before the Patriarch, as does also the Great Ecclesiarch carrying the Patriarchal Pastoral Staff (*Pateritsa*). After these comes the Patriarch holding a Chazranion [an unadorned wooden walking staff].* Walking with the Patriarch are the Great Protosynkellos [Grand Chancellor] on his right side and the Great Archdeacon on his left; then follow the Second Deacon, the Third Deacon, and the rest of the Deacons. When they arrive before the Great Gate of the Church, the Patriarch is vested by the Great Archdeacon with the Mandyas [Hierarchical Mantle]. The Great Ecclesiarch gives the Pastoral Staff to the Protosynkellos, who in turn hands it over to the Patriarch, kissing his right hand. In this same order, all of them enter into the Church, preceded by the Great Primikirios [Leader of Processions and participant in the chanting of the chanters], who carries the great candlestick with two bulbous knobs [the Divamvulon]. Arriving in the middle of the Nave, the Patriarch blesses the people, while the Choirs chant **"For many years, Master!"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη, Δέσποτα*)...

---
* *Note on the Chazranion:* A plain walking staff appropriate in its lack of ornamentation to the solemnity of Great and Holy Week.[^491]
