# Pentekostarion: The Sunday of Holy Pascha (Paschal Matins Concluded: Ode 9 Megalynaria, Praises, and Liturgy Opening)

## The Sunday of Pascha: Paschal Matins Concluded (Ode 9 Megalynaria and Praises) [Violakis, p. 787]

    **"Magnify, O my soul, Him Who suffered willingly, and was buried, and arose from the grave on the third day!"** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου τὸν ἐθελουσίως παθόντα*)
    Heirmos: **"Shine, shine, O new Jerusalem, for the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee! Dance now and be glad, O Zion, and do thou, O pure Theotokos, rejoice in the Resurrection of Him Whom thou didst bear!"** (*Φωτίζου, φωτίζου ἡ νέα Ἱερουσαλήμ*)...

    **"Magnify, O my soul, Christ the Giver of life, Who arose from the grave on the third day!"** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου τὸν ἐξαναστάντα τριήμερον*)
    [The same Heirmos repeated].

    **"Christ is the new Pascha, the living sacrificial Victim, the Lamb of God Who taketh away the sin of the world!"** (*Χριστὸς τὸ καινὸν Πάσχα*)
    Troparion: **"O Thy divine, O Thy beloved, O Thy sweetest voice! For Thou hast truly promised to be with us unto the end of the age, O Christ; and holding this as an anchor of hope, we the faithful rejoice!"** (*Ὢ θείας, ὢ φίλης*)...

    **"Today the whole creation is glad and does rejoice, for Christ has risen, and Hades has been despoiled!"** (*Σήμερον πᾶσα κτίσις ἀγάλλεται καὶ χαίρει*)
    [The same Troparion repeated].

    "Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit": **"Magnify, O my soul, the dominion of the Undivided Godhead of Three Hypostases!"** (*Μεγάλυνον ψυχή μου, τῆς Τρισυποστάτου*)
    Troparion: **"O great and most sacred Pascha, Christ! O Wisdom, and Word of God, and Power! Grant us to partake of Thee more clearly in the unwaning day of Thy Kingdom!"** (*Ὢ Πάσχα τὸ Μέγα*)...

    "Both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen": **"Rejoice, O Virgin, rejoice; rejoice, O blessed one; rejoice, O most glorified one, for thy Son has arisen from the grave on the third day!"** (*Χαῖρε Παρθένε, χαῖρε*)
    [The same Troparion repeated].

    Then we chant the Katavasia: **"The Angel cried unto her who is full of grace: O pure Virgin, rejoice, and again I say rejoice; for thy Son has arisen from the grave on the third day. Shine, shine, O new Jerusalem..."** (*Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα*). The Little Litany [Petition], and the Exaposteilarion: **"Having slept in the flesh as a mortal, O King and Lord, on the third day Thou didst rise again, raising up Adam from corruption and abolishing death: O Pascha of incorruption, salvation of the world!"** (*Σαρκὶ ὑπνώσας ὡς θνητὸς*) [three times].

    At the Praises, we set eight stichoi and chant the four Resurrectional Stichera in Tone 1: **"We hymn Thy saving Passion, O Christ, and glorify Thy Resurrection!"** (*Ὑμνοῦμέν σου Χριστὲ τὸ σωτήριον Πάθος*)... and the four Stichera of Pascha in Tone 5 with their verses of Psalm 67 LXX: **"A sacred Pascha is shown forth unto us today..."** (*Πάσχα ἱερόν*). Then we chant "Glory... Both now and ever...": Paschal Doxastikon in Tone 5: **"It is the day of Resurrection: let us be radiant for the festival, and let us embrace one another. Let us say, Brethren, even to those who hate us; let us forgive all things at the Resurrection, and thus let us cry:"** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*) ³ until the words **"and thus let us cry"**, at which point the Second Choir sings the customary melody of **"Christ is risen from the dead, trampling down death by death, and upon those in the tombs bestowing life!"** (*Χριστὸς ἀνέστη*); then the First Choir chants the same, and again the Second Choir repeats it, and the Divine Liturgy begins.[^460]

In certain local traditions, however, the veneration takes place immediately at the beginning of the chanting of the Canon for the greater convenience of both the clergy and the congregation.
