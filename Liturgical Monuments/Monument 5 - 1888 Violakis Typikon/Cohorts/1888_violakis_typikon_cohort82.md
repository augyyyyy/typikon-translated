# THE SUNDAY OF ANTIPASCHA
## (Sunday of Thomas)

9.    On Saturday evening, the Ninth Hour of Pascha is recited (as noted in Paragraph 2, Note 8). After **"Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."**, **"Christ is risen from the dead..."** is sung once from the Sanctuary and once by each of the choirs, and immediately we recite the Introductory Psalm (Ps. 103 LXX) and the First Stasis of the First Kathisma of the Psalter (Pss. 1–3 LXX). At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant the six Idiomela of the Feast in order; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"When the doors were closed, the disciples being assembled, Jesus entered..."** (*Τῶν θυρῶν κεκλεισμένων*); and the Saturday Evening Great Prokimenon: **"The Lord is King, He is clothed with majesty!"** (*Ὁ Κύριος ἐβασίλευσεν*, Ps. 92:1 LXX) with its verses. At the Aposticha: the three Idiomela; "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 8: **"O Lover of mankind, great and unsearchable is the multitude of Thy compassions!..."** (*Φιλάνθρωπε μέγα καὶ ἀνείκαστον*). The Dismissal Troparion in Tone 7: **"While the tomb was sealed, Thou, O Life, didst shine forth from the grave, O Christ God..."** (*Ἐσφραγισμένου τοῦ μνήματος*) three times, and Dismissal.

10.    On Sunday at Matins, after Psalm 50, we chant the Triadic Canon in Tone 1, then the Liti of the Feast, and the Triadic hymn: **"It is truly right to glorify Thee, God the Word..."** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*), then the Trisagion, the Dismissal Troparion, and the Six Psalms. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparion three times; then the Psalter and the Polyeleos (Pss. 134–135 LXX). The three Kathismata twice each (Resurrectional Evlogitaria are not chanted today since it is a Master Feast of the Lord, as the ancient Typika note) and immediately the Anavathmoi of Tone 4 (First Antiphon: **"From my youth..."**) and the Prokimenon in Tone 4: **"Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem"** (*Ἐπαίνει Ἱερουσαλὴμ τὸν Κύριον*, Ps. 147:1 LXX) [Note 9] and the Canon of the Feast with six Troparia without verses. After the 3rd Ode, the Ypakoe in Tone 6: **"As Thou didst stand in the midst of Thy disciples, O Savior, giving them peace: come also unto us and save us!"** (*Ὡς ἐν μέσῳ τῶν Μαθητῶν σου*); after the 6th Ode, the Kontakion in Tone 8: **"With his inquisitive right hand, Thomas explored Thy life-giving side, O Christ God..."** (*Τῇ φιλοπράγμονι δεξιᾷ*), the Ikos, the Menologion of the day, and the Hypomnema of the Feast. The Katavasias of Pascha (**"It is the day of Resurrection..."**) and the entire order of the Matins Gospel (First Eothinon: Matthew 28:16–20); then the 9th Ode of the Canon is sung, the Katavasia: **"The Angel cried unto her who is full of grace... Shine, shine, O new Jerusalem..."** (*Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα . . . Φωτίζου, φωτίζου*). Then **"Holy is the Lord our God"** (twice), and the Verse: **"Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship at His footstool, for He is holy!"** (*Ὑψοῦτε Κύριον τὸν Θεὸν ἡμῶν*, Ps. 98:5 LXX) [Note 10] and the Exaposteilaria: **"With my hand have I explored Thy side, O Compassionate One..."** (*Ἐμῇ χειρὶ ἠρεύνησα*) — the first one twice and the second one once. At the Praises: the three Prosomia to 4, "Glory... Both now and ever...": Tone 6: **"Eight days after Thy Resurrection, O Jesus our King..."** (*Μεθ᾿ ἡμέρας ὀκτώ*). Great Doxology and the Dismissal Troparion.

At "Especially", the Ninth Heirmos with Megalynarion: **"The Angel cried unto her who is full of grace: O pure Virgin, rejoice, and again I say rejoice; for thy Son has arisen from the grave on the third day. Shine, shine, O new Jerusalem, for the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee! Dance now and be glad, O Zion, and do thou, O pure Theotokos, rejoice in the Resurrection of Him Whom thou didst bear!"** (*Ὁ Ἄγγελος ἐβόα . . . Φωτίζου, φωτίζου*). Communion Hymn: **"Receive the Body of Christ; taste of the immortal Fountain. Alleluia!"** (*Σῶμα Χριστοῦ*), **"Christ is risen from the dead..."**, **"Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth, and unto the ages"**, and Dismissal.

19.    On Friday evening of that week, at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant the first three Stichera of the Sunday of the Myrrh-bearers and three Stichera of the Menaion; "Glory...": **"The Myrrh-bearing Women, reaching Thy tomb at deep dawn..."** (*Αἱ Μυροφόροι Γυναῖκες*); "Both now and ever...": **"The shadow of the law passed away when grace arrived..."** (*Παρῆλθεν ἡ σκιὰ τοῦ νόμου*). At the Aposticha: the four Resurrectional Stichera; "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment..."** (*Σὲ τὸν ἀναβαλλόμενον τὸ φῶς*). The three Dismissal Troparia of the Myrrh-bearers and Dismissal.

    In the morning the Service is chanted as prescribed in the Pentekostarion, Epistle, Gospel and Communion Hymn of the day; and at "Especially", **"It is truly right to bless thee, O Theotokos..."** (*Ἄξιόν ἐστιν*).

# THE FOURTH SUNDAY AFTER PASCHA
## (Sunday of the Paralytic)

20.    On Saturday evening, following the Introductory Psalm (Ps. 103 LXX) and the Psalter (First Kathisma: "Blessed is the man", Pss. 1–3 LXX), at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we chant seven Resurrectional Stichera in Tone 3, and three of the Paralytic, repeating the first; "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 5: **"Jesus went up to Jerusalem to the Sheep's Pool, which is called in Hebrew Bethesda, having five porches..."** (*Ἀνέβη ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα*); "Both now and ever...": Dogmatic Theotokion in Tone 3: **"How can we not marvel at thy childbirth, wherein God and man are united, O all-honored Lady?..."** (*Πῶς μὴ θαυμάσωμεν*). At the Aposticha: the Resurrectional Apostichon Sticheron in Tone 3: **"Thou Who by Thy Passion, O Christ, didst darken the sun, and by the light of Thy Resurrection didst enlighten all things: accept our evening hymn, O Lover of mankind!"** (*Ὁ τῷ πάθει σου Χριστέ*), then the Stichera of Pascha: **"A sacred Pascha is shown forth unto us today..."** (*Πάσχα ἱερόν*) with their verses; "Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 8: **"In Solomon's porch lay a multitude of the sick, and at Mid-feast Christ found the Paralytic who had been lying there thirty and eight years..."** (*Ἐν τῇ στοᾷ τοῦ Σολομῶντος*); "Both now and ever...": Tone 5: **"It is the day of Resurrection: let us be radiant for the festival..."** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*). The Dismissal Troparion in Tone 3: **"Let the heavens rejoice, let earthly things be glad; for the Lord hath wrought might with His arm!..."** (*Εὐφραινέσθω τὰ Οὐράνια*); the Theotokion in Tone 3: **"Thou Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure the Cross, O Good One, and didst despoil death by death, and as God didst reveal the Resurrection: overlook not those whom Thou hast fashioned with Thine own hand!..."** (*Σὲ τὴν μεσιτεύσασαν*), and Dismissal.

21. At **"God is the Lord"** (*Θεὸς Κύριος*): the Dismissal Troparion in Tone 3 (twice) and the Theotokion (once), the Psalter and the Amomos (Psalm 118 LXX / 17th Kathisma). The Kathismata of the First and Second Stasis of the Kathisma [Stichologia], the Resurrectional Evlogitaria (**"Blessed art Thou, O Lord, teach me Thy statutes"**), the Ypakoe of Tone 3, the Anavathmoi of Tone 3, and the Prokimenon in Tone 3: **"Chant unto our God, chant! Chant unto our King, chant!"** (*Ψάλατε τῷ Θεῷ ἡμῶν, ψάλατε*, Ps. 46:7 LXX). The Canon of Pascha with its two Theotokia of Saint John of Damascus. After the 3rd Ode, the Kathisma in Tone 3: **"A word alone paralyzed the limbs of the sick man..."** (*Ῥῆμα Παράλυτον*); "Glory... Both now and ever...": Kathisma in Tone 4: **"Awed by the beauty of thy virginity and the exceeding brilliance of thy purity, Gabriel cried aloud unto thee, O Theotokos: What praise worthy of thee shall I offer?..."** (*Τὴν ὡραιότητα*). After the 6th Ode, the Kontakion in Tone 3: **"As of old Thou didst raise up the Paralytic, O Lord, by Thy divine presence: so raise up my soul, paralyzed by grievous sins and shameful deeds!..."** and the Ikos of the Paralytic, the Menologion of the day, and the Hypomnema of the Sunday of the Paralytic.
