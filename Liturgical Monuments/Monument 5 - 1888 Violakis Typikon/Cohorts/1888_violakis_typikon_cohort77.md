## Great and Holy Monday: Matins Opening and Rubrics (§§ 66 Concluded, 67 Opening) [Violakis, p. 747]

At the rite of Mutual Forgiveness, the Stavrotheotokion is chanted: **"When the Virgin beheld Thy unjust slaughter, O Christ..."** (*Σφαγήν σου τὴν ἄδικον*); the Dismissal: **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee... May the Lord, coming unto His voluntary Passion for our salvation, Christ our true God..."** (*Δόξα σοι ὁ Θεὸς . . . ἐρχόμενος ὁ Κύριος ἐπὶ τὸ ἑκούσιον πάθος διὰ τὴν ἡμῶν σωτηρίαν*),³⁷ and: **"Through the prayers of our holy fathers, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us. Amen."** (*Δι' εὐχῶν*).

**GREAT AND HOLY MONDAY AT MATINS**

67.    The Priest: **"Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεός*); the Superior [Proestos]: **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. O Heavenly King, Comforter, the Spirit of truth..."** (*Δόξα σοι ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν, δόξα σοι. Βασιλεῦ οὐράνιε*); the Reader: the Trisagion Prayers; the Priest: **"For Thine is the kingdom..."** (*Ὅτι σοῦ ἐστιν*); the Reader: **"Lord, have mercy"** (12 times); **"Glory... Both now and ever..."**, **"O come, let us worship and fall down before our King and God..."** (3 times) and the Royal Psalms: Psalm 19 [LXX]: **"The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble..."** (*Ἐπακούσαι σου Κύριος*), and Psalm 20 [LXX]: **"The king shall rejoice in Thy strength, O Lord..."** (While these Psalms are being read slowly, the Priest censes the people with the hand-censer [*katzion*]). After the Trisagion Prayers, the Priest: **"For Thine is the kingdom..."**; then the Troparia: **"Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance..."** (*Σῶσον Κύριε*); **"Glory..."**: **"Thou Who wast voluntarily lifted up upon the Cross, O Christ God..."**; **"Both now and ever..."**: **"O awesome and unashamed protection, despise not our petitions, O Good One..."** (*Προστασία φοβερὰ καὶ ἀκαταίσχυντε*). The Dean or presiding Reader [Proistamenos] recites the Six Psalms. After the Great Litany, the Great Alleluia is chanted with its appointed verses: **"From night until morning my spirit seeks Thee early, O God, for Thy commandments are a light upon the earth"** (*Ἐκ νυκτὸς ὀρθρίζει*, Isa. 26:9 [LXX]), twice in slow melody and twice in brief melody. Then the Dismissal Troparion: **"Behold, the Bridegroom comes in the middle of the night, and blessed is the servant whom He finds watching..."** (*Ἰδοὺ ὁ Νυμφίος ἔρχεται*) three times (twice slowly and once rapidly). At the end of the first time we say: **"...through the protection of the Bodiless Hosts, save us!"** (*Προστασίαις τῶν Ἀσωμάτων σῶσον ἡμᾶς*);³⁸ at the second time we join the commemoration of the Patron Saint of the Church, saying: **"...through the intercessions of Saint [N.] (or: the Saints), save us!"** (*Πρεσβείαις τοῦ Ἁγίου ἢ τῶν Ἁγίων σῶσον ἡμᾶς*); and at the third time: **"...through the Theotokos, have mercy on us!"** (*Διὰ τῆς Θεοτόκου ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς*). Then the Kathismata of the Psalter,³⁹ and next the three appointed Sessional Hymns [Kathismata] of the Triodion are chanted slowly in sequence: the first two once each, and the third twice. Immediately the Priest exclaims: **"And that we may be accounted worthy to hear the Holy Gospel..."**, and...[^440]

---
37. This same Dismissal is maintained until Great and Holy Wednesday at Matins and at the Divine Liturgy.
38. On Great and Holy Tuesday: "...through the intercessions of the Forerunner, save us"; on Great and Holy Wednesday: "...by the power of Thy Cross, save us."
39. The Psalter of the day is customarily omitted in most holy churches during these Matins services, either for the sake of brevity or because five Kathismata are appointed for each of the first three days of Holy Week, and it is read privately in the morning by the Priest; nevertheless it is devoutly to be wished that at least a portion of the five Kathismata be read in the hearing of the people also, inasmuch as the Psalter has ever been regarded as the foundation of our ecclesiastical prayers and the daily spiritual sustenance of every Christian, as was noted in the Introductory Chapter [Protheoria] § 3, footnote 3.

# Holy Week: Great and Holy Monday (Matins Concluded) (§ 67 Concluded)

## Great and Holy Monday: Matins Concluded (§ 67 Concluded) [Violakis, p. 749]

... reads the Gospel [Matthew 21:18–43: The Cursing of the Barren Fig Tree and the Parable of the Wicked Vinedressers]. After the Gospel, Psalm 50 is read; then the Three-Ode Canon [Triodion] in six Troparia in each Ode, and again the Heirmos slowly as Katavasia. The Small Litany, the Kontakion: **"Jacob lamented the loss of Joseph..."** (*Ὁ Ἰακὼβ ὠδύρετο*), the Ikos, the Menologion, and the Memorial [Hypomnema] of the Triodion. Then the Eighth Ode with its Heirmos; and instead of **"Glory..."**, we say: **"We bless the Lord: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit"** (*Εὐλογοῦμεν Πατέρα, Υἱὸν καὶ Ἅγιον Πνεῦμα τὸν Κύριον*). Before the Heirmos is repeated as Katavasia, we sing: **"We praise, we bless, and we worship the Lord"** (*Αἰνοῦμεν, εὐλογοῦμεν καὶ προσκυνοῦμεν τὸν Κύριον*). Then the Deacon exclaims: **"The Theotokos and Mother of the Light let us magnify in hymns!"** (*Τὴν Θεοτόκον καὶ μητέρα τοῦ φωτός*), and the Ninth Ode is chanted with its Heirmos again. At the conclusion, the Small Litany and the Exaposteilarion: **"I see Thy bridal chamber adorned, O my Savior, and I have no wedding garment that I may enter therein; enlighten the robe of my soul, O Giver of Light, and save me!"** (*Τὸν νυμφῶνά σου βλέπω, Σωτήρ μου, κεκοσμημένον*) three times. At the Praises:⁴⁰ we set 4 verses, and we chant the two Idiomela of the Praises (twice each): [**"O Lord, coming unto Thy voluntary Passion..."** (*Κύριε, ἐρχόμενος πρὸς τὸ πάθος*) and **"O Lord, coming unto Thy Passion, Thou didst establish the hearts of Thy Disciples..."**]. "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"O Lord, coming unto Thy Passion, Thou didst say unto Thy Disciples: Watch and pray..."** (*Κύριε, ἐρχόμενος πρὸς τὸ πάθος, τοὺς ἰδίους ἐστήριξας Μαθητάς*). The Small Doxology: **"To Thee belongs glory, O Lord our God..."** (*Σοὶ δόξα πρέπει*) read plain. After the litany: **"Let us complete our morning prayer unto the Lord"** (*Πληρώσωμεν τὴν ἑωθινὴν δέησιν*), the First Choir begins the Aposticha. Then: **"It is good to give praise unto the Lord, and to sing unto Thy name, O Most High"** (*Ἀγαθὸν τὸ ἐξομολογεῖσθαι*), the Trisagion Prayers, the Kontakion: **"Jacob lamented the loss of Joseph..."**, **"Lord, have mercy"** (12 times);⁴¹ the Priest: **"Blessed is He Who is, Christ our God, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages"** (*Ὁ ὢν εὐλογητός*); the Reader: **"Confirm, O God, the holy Orthodox faith..."**; then the Prayer: **"O Heavenly King, strengthen our faithful civil authorities..."** (*Ἐπουράνιε βασιλεῦ*); the three Great Prostrations with the Prayer of Saint Ephrem the Syrian, and the Dismissal: **"May the Lord, coming unto His voluntary Passion for our salvation, Christ our true God..."** (*Ἐρχόμενος ὁ Κύριος ἐπὶ τὸ ἑκούσιον πάθος*), and: **"Through the prayers of our holy fathers, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us. Amen."** (*Δι' εὐχῶν*).⁴²[^441]

# Holy Week: Great Monday (Hours, Presanctified, Compline) and Great Tuesday (Matins, Hours, Presanctified, Compline Opening) (§§ 68, 69, 70, 71, 72, 73, 74 Opening)

## Great Monday (Hours, Presanctified, Compline) and Great Tuesday (Matins, Hours, Presanctified, Compline Opening) (§§ 68, 69, 70, 71, 72, 73, 74 Opening) [Violakis, p. 751]

68.    In the morning, the five Kathismata of the Psalter are read [Kathismata 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8]; then the plain Hours with the Dismissal Troparion: **"Behold, the Bridegroom comes..."** (*Ἰδοὺ ὁ Νυμφίος ἔρχεται*) and the Kontakion: **"Jacob lamented the loss of Joseph..."** (*Ὁ Ἰακὼβ ὠδύρετο*). At the Ninth Hour, the Beatitudes [Makarismoi] are chanted; and after the Prayer: **"All-Holy Trinity, Consubstantial Might..."** (*Παναγία Τριάς*), the Dismissal is given, and Vespers begins.

69.    At Vespers, after the Introductory Psalm (Ps. 103 [LXX]) and the Eighteenth Kathisma: **"Unto the Lord"** (*Πρὸς Κύριον*, Pss. 119–133 [LXX]), at **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*, Ps. 140 [LXX]), we chant the six Idiomela of the Praises and the Aposticha. "Glory... Both now and ever...": **"Finding the Egyptian woman another Eve, the serpent hastened through speech to trip up Joseph..."** (*Δευτέραν Εὔαν τὴν Αἰγυπτίαν εὑρὼν ὁ δράκων*). Little Entrance with the Holy Gospel, **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρόν*), and the Old Testament Readings of the day: Exodus 1:1–20 [LXX] and Job 1:1–12 [LXX]. Then **"Let my prayer arise"** (*Κατευθυνθήτω*), and immediately after it: **"Wisdom! Stand upright! Let us hear the Holy Gospel"**, and the Gospel is read [Matthew 24:3–35: The Discourse on the Mount of Olives], and in sequence the rest of the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts.

70.    In the evening, Great Compline is read, as is customary. After the Doxology: **"Glory to God in the highest..."** (*Δόξα ἐν ὑψίστοις Θεῷ*), the Second Choir begins the Three-Ode Canon [Triodion]; after the Heirmos of the Ninth Ode: **"Him that was prefigured upon the mountain..."** (*Τὸν προδηλωθέντα ἐν ὄρει*), the Trisagion Prayers, and following this: **"O Lord of hosts, be with us..."** (*Κύριε τῶν δυνάμεων*), and the rest of Great Compline. At the rite of Mutual Forgiveness, we chant: **"When the Virgin beheld Thy unjust slaughter, O Christ, she lamented: Woe is me, my Child!..."** (*Σφαγήν σου τὴν ἄδικον, Χριστέ*).

**GREAT AND HOLY TUESDAY**

71.    The service of Matins of Great and Holy Tuesday is chanted unalterably according to the order set forth in Paragraph 67. (See the order for Great and Holy Monday).
