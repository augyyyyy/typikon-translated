## Concerning the Prokimenon of Vespers

**9.** At Saturday Vespers, even if Feasts of the Mother of God or of the Lord occur (with the exception of the two feasts of the Nativity of Christ and Theophany), the Prokimenon chanted is **"The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty"** (*Ὁ Κύριος ἐβασίλευσεν*, Psalm 92:1 LXX);[^66] except for the afterfeast Vespers of Great Feasts of the Lord falling on a Saturday, or on any other day of the week, at which are always chanted the Great Prokimena: **"Who is a god as great as our God? ## Concerning the Artoklasia

**11.** If an Artoklasia (Blessing of the Loaves) is held,[^68] after the exclamation **"Blessed and glorified be the majesty of Thy kingdom..."** (*Εἴη τὸ κράτος τῆς βασιλείας σου...*), a chosen Sticheron from the Litiya is chanted by the Second Choir; meanwhile, the Deacon and the Priest exit through the north door [of the iconostasis] holding candles in their hands, and stand in the middle of the temple before the Tetrapod, where the loaves, wheat, wine, and oil have been prepared. The Deacon then intones the litany: **"Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy..."** (*Ἐλέησον ἡμᾶς ὁ Θεός...*), **"Again we pray for the preservation of this holy temple, this city (or village)..."** (*Ἔτι δεόμεθα ὑπὲρ τοῦ διαφυλαχθῆναι τὴν ἁγίαν ἐκκλησίαν ταύτην...*), etc. The choirs chant **"Lord, have mercy"** reverently and in a subdued voice as the rubrics appoint. The Priest intones: **"Hear us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth..."** (*Ἐπάκουσον ἡμῶν ὁ Θεός...*). (Great Prokimena are likewise chanted at the Vespers of Bright Week, as set forth in the Pentecostarion). The Priest: **"Peace be to all."** Choir: **"And to thy spirit."** Deacon: **"Let us bow our heads unto the Lord."** Choir: **"To Thee, O Lord."** The Priest (or the Hierarch): **"O Master, abundant in mercy..."** (*Δέσποτα Πολυέλεε...*). Then the Priest censes crosswise, circling the Tetrapod with the loaves and chanting: **"O Mother of God and Virgin, rejoice, Mary full of grace, the Lord is with thee; blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, for thou hast borne the Savior of our souls"** (*Θεοτόκε Παρθένε...*). On all Feasts of the Lord and of the Mother of God without exception, as well as on commemorations of Saints, if an Artoklasia is held, **"O Mother of God and Virgin"** is always chanted. But throughout Bright Week, instead of **"O Mother of God and Virgin,"** **"Christ is risen from the dead"** (*Χριστὸς ἀνέστη*) is chanted. This being completed, the Deacon says: **"Let us pray to the Lord."** Choir: **"Lord, have mercy."** The Priest blesses the loaves, reciting the prayer: **"O Lord Jesus Christ our God, Who didst bless the five loaves in the wilderness and therewith feed five thousand..."** (*Δέσποτα Κύριε Ἰησοῦ Χριστὲ ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν...*). Then **"The rich have become poor and gone hungry, but they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing"** (*Πλούσιοι ἐπτώχευσαν*, Psalm 33:11 LXX) is chanted thrice, and after it, the Aposticha.

## Concerning the Doxastikon of the Aposticha

**13.** On Feasts of the Mother of God coinciding on Sunday, on their Afterfeast Sundays, on Feasts of the Lord and their Forefeasts and their Apodoses, on the Third Sunday of Great Lent [Veneration of the Precious Cross], on the Sunday of the Myrrh-Bearers, on the feast of the Holy Ancestors of God Joachim and Anna [September 9], and on the Archangels [November 8 (when it falls on Sunday)], only the Doxastikon of the Aposticha of the occurring feast is chanted, without a Theotokion.[^70] When the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord or its Apodosis coincides on one of the first four Sundays of the Triodion: for *Glory...*, that of the Aposticha of the Triodion is chanted; for *Now and forever...*, that of the feast. When the Feast of the Annunciation coincides on the Third Sunday of Great Lent or on Palm Sunday: the Aposticha and Doxastikon of the Triodion are chanted; for *Now and forever...*, that of the feast. If it coincides on Pascha Sunday, then at Vespers: *Glory...*, of the feast; *Now and forever...*, **"It is the day of Resurrection"** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα*). On commemorations of Saints occurring on a Sunday: for *Glory...* of the Aposticha, that of the Saint is chanted; for *Now and forever...*, the Theotokion from the Octoechos in the same Tone [Tone 1–8] as the Doxastikon of the Saint. But when the commemorations of celebrated Saints coincide on an Afterfeast Sunday or on the Apodosis of a Feast of the Lord or of the Mother of God: for *Glory...* of the Aposticha, that of the Saint is chanted; for *Now and forever...*, that of the occurring feast. If the Saint occurring on Sunday has no Doxastikon at the Aposticha, for *Glory...*, *Now and forever...*, the Theotokion of the Aposticha of the Tone is chanted.

## Concerning "Now Let Your Servant Depart" and the Trisagion

**14.** Daily at Vespers before the Dismissal, **"Now let Your servant depart in peace, O Master, according to Thy word"** (*Νῦν ἀπολύεις*, Luke 2:29–32) is said with the Trisagion Prayers.[^71] But throughout Bright Week and at the Vespers of the Apodosis of Pascha, after **"It is the day of Resurrection,"** instead of the Trisagion Prayers, **"Christ is risen from the dead"** (*Χριστὸς ἀνέστη*) is chanted thrice according to its customary melody (and not according to the melody of **"It is the day of Resurrection"**), and the Dismissal immediately follows.

## Concerning the Dismissal Troparia

**15.** At Saturday Vespers, the Resurrection Dismissal Troparia of the Octoechos are chanted before the Dismissal Troparia of Feasts of the Mother of God coinciding on Sunday, of Afterfeast Sundays, and of the Apodoses of Feasts of the Lord and of the Mother of God.[^72] They are chanted as follows: on the principal Feasts of the Mother of God and on their Apodoses: the Resurrection Troparion once, and that of the feast twice. On Afterfeast Sundays: both the Resurrection Troparion and that of the feast once; but at Matins, the Resurrection Troparion is said twice and that of the feast once. If the Feast of the Annunciation coincides on the Third Sunday of Great Lent, there are chanted: first the Resurrection Troparion, second that of the Cross, and third that of the feast; on Lazarus Saturday: that of the feast twice, and that of Lazarus once; on Palm Sunday: that of the feast, that of Lazarus, and again that of the feast. On Forefeast Sundays of Feasts of the Lord and of the Mother of God, at evening the Resurrection Troparion and the Forefeast Troparion are chanted once each; but in the morning at **"God is the Lord,"** the first is doubled. When the commemoration of a celebrated Saint occurs on an Afterfeast Sunday or on the Apodosis of a Feast of the Lord or of the Mother of God, there are chanted: first the Resurrection Troparion, then that of the Saint, and last that of the occurring feast.
