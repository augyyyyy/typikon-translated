# Holy Week: Great and Holy Saturday (Vespers with the Divine Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great Opening) (§ 86 Opening)

## Great and Holy Saturday: Vespers with the Divine Liturgy of Saint Basil the Great (Opening) (§ 86 Opening) [Violakis, p. 777]

**VESPERS WITH THE LITURGY**  
**[OF SAINT BASIL THE GREAT]**

86.    The Archdeacon: **"Bless, Master!"** (*Εὐλόγησον Δέσποτα*). The Priest: **"Blessed is the kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογημένη ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Πατρὸς*). The Patriarch recites the Introductory Psalm [Ps. 103 LXX: "Bless the Lord, O my soul..."]. At **"Lord, I have cried"** (*Κύριε ἐκέκραξα*), we set eight verses and chant four Resurrectional Stichera and four Idiomela of the day:
    1. Tone 1: **"Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins, for Thou alone art He Who hast manifested the Resurrection unto the world"** (*Τὰς ἑσπερινὰς ἡμῶν εὐχάς*);
    2. Tone 1: **"Surround Zion, O ye peoples, and encompass her, and give glory within her unto Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered us from our iniquities"** (*Κυκλώσατε λαοὶ Σιών*);
    3. Tone 1: **"Come, ye peoples, let us hymn and worship Christ, glorifying His Resurrection from the dead; for He is our God, Who hath delivered the world from the error of the enemy"** (*Δεῦτε λαοὶ ὑμνήσωμεν*);
    4. Tone 1: **"By Thy Passion, O Christ, we have been set free from passions, and by Thy Resurrection we have been delivered from corruption: O Lord, glory to Thee!"** (*Τῷ πάθει σου Χριστέ*);
    5. Tone 8: **"Today Hades groaning cries aloud: Woe is me, if only I had not received Him Who was born of Mary; for coming upon me He has dissolved my power, and broken the gates of bronze; as God, He has raised up the souls that I held fast of old! Glory to Thy Cross and Thy Resurrection, O Lord!"** (*Σήμερον ὁ ᾅδης στένων βοᾷ*);
    6. Tone 8: [The same Troparion is repeated];
    7. Tone 8: **"Today Hades groaning cries aloud: My power is dissolved; I received a dead Man as one of the dead, but could not hold Him at all, for with Him I lose those over whom I had ruled; all those whom I held fast for ages I have vomited forth! But He Who was crucified has raised up the dead! Glory to Thy Cross and Thy Resurrection, O Lord!"** (*Σήμερον ὁ ᾅδης στένων βοᾷ· Κατελύθη μου ἡ ἐξουσία*);
    8. Tone 8: **"Today Hades groaning cries aloud: My dominion is swallowed up; the Shepherd has been crucified, and has raised up Adam; those over whom I reigned are taken from me; whom I swallowed down in my might, I have surrendered utterly; the Crucified One has emptied the graves; the might of death has no power! Glory to Thy Cross and Thy Resurrection, O Lord!"** (*Σήμερον ὁ ᾅδης στένων βοᾷ· Κατεπόθη μου τὸ κράτος*).

"Glory...": Doxastikon in Tone 6: **"Moses the great mystically prefigured this present day, saying: And God blessed the seventh day; for this is the blessed Sabbath, this is the day of rest, whereon the Only-Begotten Son of God rested from all His works..."** (*Τὴν σήμερον μυστικῶς ὁ μέγας Μωϋσῆς*); "Both now and ever...": Dogmatic Theotokion in Tone 1: **"Let us praise the Virgin Mary, the glory of the whole world, who sprang forth from mankind and gave birth to the Master, the gate of heaven..."** (*Τὴν παγκόσμιον δόξαν*).

The Entrance is made with the Holy Gospel Book; **"O Gladsome Light"** (*Φῶς ἱλαρὸν*); there is no Prokimenon, but immediately the Readings:
    1. Genesis 1:1–13 [LXX] (The Creation of the World: Heaven and Earth, the Light, the Firmament, the Earth, the Seas, and Plant Life);
    2. Jonah 1:1–4:11 [LXX] (The Entire Book of the Prophet Jonah: The Three Days and Three Nights Prefiguring the Burial and Resurrection of the Lord);
    3. Daniel 3:1–23 [LXX] (The Three Holy Youths in the Babylonian Furnace of Fire);
    4. Then the Prayer of Azariah [Daniel 3:26–45 LXX / Apocrypha]: **"Blessed art Thou, O Lord, God of our fathers, and praised and glorified is Thy Name unto the ages..."** (*Εὐλογητὸς εἶ, Κύριε, ὁ Θεὸς τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν*);
    5. The Hymn of the Three Youths [Daniel 3:52–90 LXX / Song of the Three Holy Children] through verse 56: **"Blessed art Thou in the firmament of heaven, and praised and glorified unto the ages"** (*Εὐλογημένος εἶ ἐν τῷ στερεώματι τοῦ οὐρανοῦ, καὶ ὑπερύμνητος καὶ ὑπερυψούμενος εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας*).

At this point, all stand and we begin chanting in Tone 1 the remainder of the Hymn of the Three Youths [from verse 57]: **"Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; hymn and exalt Him above all forever!"** (*Τὸν Κύριον ὑμνεῖτε καὶ ὑπερυψοῦτε εἰς πάντας τοὺς αἰῶνας*). After each of the intermittent verses intoned by the Canonarch through verse 88: **"Bless the Lord, Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael; hymn and exalt Him above all forever!"**, the following is sung as a refrain: **"Hymn and exalt Him above all forever!"** (*Ὑμνεῖτε καὶ ὑπερυψοῦτε εἰς πάντας τοὺς αἰῶνας*). The concluding verses from verse 88 and the final doxology are chanted as follows:
    **"O ye Apostles, Prophets, and Martyrs of the Lord, bless ye the Lord; hymn and exalt Him above all forever!"**, with the Refrain:
    **"We bless the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit: the Lord!"** (*Εὐλογοῦμεν Πατέρα, Υἱὸν καὶ Ἅγιον Πνεῦμα, τὸν Κύριον*)...[^455]

## Ritual Order of the Services of the Pentekostarion: The Sunday of Pascha (Midnight Office) [Violakis, p. 783]

**RITUAL ORDER**  
**OF THE SERVICES**  
**OF THE PENTIKOSTARION**

**THE SUNDAY OF PASCHA**

Around the Fifth Hour of the night [11:00 p.m.], we enter into the church. The Priest: **"Blessed is our God always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Εὐλογητὸς ὁ Θεός*); the Patriarch: **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. O Heavenly King, Comforter, the Spirit of truth..."** (*Βασιλεῦ οὐράνιε*); the Reader: the Trisagion Prayers, **"Lord, have mercy"** [12 times], **"Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."**, **"Come, let us worship and fall down before Christ, our King and our God"** [three times], and Psalm 50 [LXX: "Have mercy on me, O God..."] is read plainly. Then the Canon in Tone 6: **"Of old the surge of the sea covered the pursuing tyrant beneath the waves..."** (*Κύματι θαλάσσης*) is chanted for six Troparia, with the refrain: **"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee"**; and after each Ode, the Heirmos is chanted slowly as Katavasia; the Sessional Hymn [Kathisma] is not chanted.

At the Fourth Ode, the deacons, vested in their sacred vestments, exit from the Sanctuary with the two-branched and three-branched candlesticks [dikirotrikira]. The Patriarch, having venerated the holy icons and having prepared himself according to custom for the Divine Liturgy, enters the Sanctuary and vests in all his hierarchical vestments, as do all the concelebrating Hierarchs.

After the Ninth Ode of the Canon, the Trisagion Prayers are recited from within the Sanctuary; then the Dismissal Troparion in Tone 2: **"When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal, then didst Thou slay Hades with the brilliance of Thy Divinity..."** (*Ὅτε κατῆλθες πρὸς τὸν θάνατον*); the Augmented Litany and the Dismissal are given.

Then the Patriarch,¹ preceded by the concelebrating Hierarchs, exits from the Sanctuary, chanting slowly with them the Sticheron in Tone 6: **"Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, the angels hymn in the heavens: enable us also on earth to glorify Thee with a pure heart"** (*Τὴν Ἀνάστασίν σου Χριστὲ Σωτήρ*), which is repeated also by the choirs until they [the procession of the clergy] reach the appointed platform [dais / ambo] from...[^458]

... which the Holy Gospel is to be read; the Archdeacon then exclaims: **"And that we may be accounted worthy to hear the Holy Gospel, let us pray unto the Lord God"** (*Καὶ ὑπὲρ τοῦ καταξιωθῆναι ἡμᾶς*). The Patriarch reads the Holy Gospel: **"In the end of the Sabbath, as it began to dawn toward the first day of the week..."** (*Ὀψὲ σαββάτων τῇ ἐπιφωσκούσῃ*)² [Matthew 28:1–20. This is the same Gospel read at the morning Liturgy; see footnote 2 below], and the choirs sing: **"Many years to you, Master!"** (*Εἰς πολλὰ ἔτη, Δέσποτα*).

Then taking the censer, the Patriarch censes the Holy Gospel Book three times and exclaims:
**"Glory to the holy, and consubstantial, and life-creating, and undivided Trinity, always, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ καὶ ὁμοουσίῳ καὶ ζωοποιῷ καὶ ἀδιαιρέτῳ Τριάδι*).
Then he sings the Paschal Troparion in Tone 5:
**"Christ is risen from the dead, trampling down death by death, and upon those in the tombs bestowing life!"** (*Χριστὸς ἀνέστη ἐκ νεκρῶν, θανάτῳ θάνατον πατήσας, καὶ τοῖς ἐν τοῖς μνήμασι, ζωὴν χαρισάμενος*) [three times];
and the choirs repeat it six times, while the Patriarch censes and intones the four Paschal Verses of Psalm 67 LXX:
    1. **"Let God arise, and let His enemies be scattered; let them also that hate Him flee before Him!"** (*Ἀναστήτω ὁ Θεός*, Ps. 67:2 LXX);
    2. **"As smoke vanisheth, so let them vanish; as wax melteth before the fire!"** (*Ὡς ἐκλείπει καπνός*, Ps. 67:3 LXX);
    3. **"So let the wicked perish at the presence of God; and let the righteous be glad!"** (*Οὕτως ἀπολοῦνται οἱ ἁμαρτωλοί*, Ps. 67:3 LXX);
    4. **"This is the day which the Lord hath made; let us rejoice and be glad in it!"** (*Αὕτη ἡ ἡμέρα ἣν ἐποίησεν ὁ Κύριος*, Ps. 117:24 LXX).

After these verses, the Patriarch intones **"Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit"**, and **"Both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** At the conclusion, the Patriarch chants the same Troparion [**"Christ is risen from the dead..."**] while censing the Hierarchs and the rest of the participants; then the Archdeacon recites the Great Litany: **"In peace let us pray unto the Lord"** (*Ἐν εἰρήνῃ τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν*), and the Patriarch sings the Exclamation: **"For unto Thee is due all glory, honor, and worship: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι πρέπει σοι πᾶσα δόξα*).

And thus we enter into the church with this processional order. The Patriarch begins to sing the Canon: **"It is the day of Resurrection: let us be radiant, O ye peoples!"** (*Ἀναστάσεως ἡμέρα λαμπρυνθῶμεν λαοί*) with the holy Hierarchs, with six Troparia in each Ode, each hymn preceded with the Verse: **"Glory to Thy holy Resurrection, O Lord!"** (*Δόξα τῇ ἁγίᾳ Ἀναστάσει σου, Κύριε*); the last two Troparia are preceded by **"Glory..."** and **"Both now and ever..."** respectively.

After each Ode, we chant the Katavasia, followed by the Little Litany and the Exclamation:
    - After the 1st Ode: **"For Thine is the dominion, and Thine is the kingdom..."** (*Ὅτι σὸν τὸ κράτος*);
    - After the 3rd Ode: **"For Thou art our God, and unto Thee we send up glory..."** (*Ὅτι σὺ εἶ ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν*);
    - After the 4th Ode: **"For Thou art a good God, and lovest mankind..."** (*Ὅτι ἀγαθὸς καὶ φιλάνθρωπος*);
    - After the 5th Ode: **"For sanctified and glorified is Thine all-honorable and majestic Name..."** (*Ὅτι ἡγίασται καὶ δεδόξασται*);
    - After the 6th Ode: **"For Thou art the King of peace, and the Savior of our souls..."** (*Σὺ γὰρ εἶ ὁ Βασιλεύς*);
    - After the 7th Ode: **"Blessed and glorified be the might of Thy kingdom..."** (*Εἴη τὸ κράτος*);
    - After the 8th Ode: **"For blessed is Thy Name, and glorified is Thy kingdom..."** (*Ὅτι ηὐλόγηταί σου τὸ ὄνομα*);
    - After the 9th Ode: **"For all the powers of the heavens praise Thee, and unto Thee they send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and forever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen."** (*Ὅτι σὲ αἰνοῦσι πᾶσαι αἱ δυνάμεις τῶν οὐρανῶν*).

After the 3rd Ode, we read the Ypakoe plainly: **"Anticipating the dawn, Mary and the women found the stone rolled away from the tomb..."** (*Προλαβοῦσαι τὸν ὄρθρον*); after the 6th Ode, we recite the Kontakion, the Ikos, and the Memorial [*Hypomnema* / Synaxarion] of the day with its verses. Then is recited: **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ, let us worship the Holy Lord Jesus, the only sinless One..."** (*Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ θεασάμενοι*) [three times] — once by the Patriarch and twice by the concelebrating Hierarchs. After **"Jesus having risen from the tomb, as He foretold, hath given us eternal life and great mercy"** (*Ἀναστὰς ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἀπὸ τοῦ τάφου*), the 7th and 8th Odes are sung, after which the Great Archdeacon exclaims: **"The Theotokos and Mother of the Light let us magnify in hymns!"** (*Τὴν Θεοτόκον καὶ Μητέρα τοῦ Φωτός*), and the 9th Ode is sung with the appointed Megalynaria...[^459]
