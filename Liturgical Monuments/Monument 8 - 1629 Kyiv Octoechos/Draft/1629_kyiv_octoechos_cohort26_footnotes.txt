[^254]: The completion of Ode 9 of the Thursday Matins canons (to the Holy Apostles and to St. Nicholas of Myra) on Fol. 248v–249r applies the architectural typology of Christ the cornerstone (1 Peter 2:6; Psalm 117:22 LXX: *Kamenie izbrannoye na polozhenago vo glavu ugla kamene*) to the apostolic foundation of the Church. Fol. 249r concludes with the opening of the Exapostilarion of the Apostles (*Petra i Pavla, Andreya, i Yakova, Vartholomeya...*), enumerating the apostolic college across the leaf break. Codicological note: Quire signature `Ѡ҃ ѡ҃ г҃` marks the lower margin of Fol. 249r, which bears the printed folio number `см҃є` (245r).

[^255]: On Fol. 249v–250r, the Thursday Aposticha concludes with the martyrion citing Psalm 115:6 LXX (*Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints* / *Chestna smert svyatykh Tvoyikh Gospodi*). Fol. 250r introduces the Thursday Beatitudes (*Blazhenna*) in Tone 4 with the classical patristic contrast between Adam's expulsion via the tree of transgression (Genesis 3) and the Penitent Thief's entrance into paradise via the tree of the Cross (Luke 23:42–43: *Dreva radi Adam raya byst izselen, dreva zhe radi krestnago razboynik v ray vselisya*). Quire signature `Ѡ҃ ѡ҃ д҃` appears at the foot of Fol. 250r (printed `см҃ѕ` / 246r), completing quire `Ѡ҃ ѡ҃`.

[^256]: Fol. 251r marks the beginning of quire `Ц҃ ц҃` (printed `см҃з` / 247r) and the liturgical transition from Thursday (Apostles and St. Nicholas, Tone 4) to Friday (Precious Cross, Tone 5). The concluding Beatitudes stichera include an explicit Trinitarian hymn addressed to the consubstantial and indivisible Trinity (*Troitse nerazdelnaya*), followed by a Marian theotokion honoring the Mother of God as the "unfading crown of the passion-bearers." The Friday Vespers stichera open under an ornamental typographical headpiece, invoking Psalm 95:12 LXX (*Then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice before the Lord* / *Vsya dreva dubravnaya da vozraduyutsya*).

[^257]: Fol. 251v continues the Friday Vespers hymnography of the Cross, venerating the instrument of redemption and citing Psalm 131:7 LXX (*Let us worship at the place where His feet stood* / *Mestu idezhe stoyaste noze Gospodni*). Fol. 252r introduces the Staurotheotokia, deploying the vivid eucharistic and botanical typology of the Virgin as the mystical Vine bearing Christ as the "unplanted Cluster" (*grozd nenasazhdenny*) wounded by the soldier's spear (John 19:34). Codicological note: Quire signature `Ц҃ ц҃ в҃` marks Fol. 252r (printed `см҃и` / 248r); the running header on this page mistakenly retains the previous section's `Гласъ, д҃` before the printer rectifies the headline to `Гласъ, е҃` on subsequent leaves.

[^258]: The compunctious Staurotheotokion on Fol. 252v–253r develops a profound interior dialogue of repentance: the soul's accumulation of sinful habits is likened to putrefying bodily wounds and sores (cf. Isaiah 1:6: *prilagayushchi bezumno svoim yazvam strupy mnozhayshaya*), urging immediate recourse to the Mother of God prior to the arrival of the righteous Judge. Fol. 253r concludes with the Stavrotheotokion to the model melody *Raduysya zhikonosny* (abbreviating *Raduysya, Zhivonosny Krestye* / Greek *Chaire zoēphore stauros*). Quire signature `Ц҃ ц҃ г҃` marks Fol. 253r (printed `см҃ѳ` / 249r).

[^259]: Fol. 253v features the Friday Aposticha sticheron in Tone 5 (*Gospodi, pri Moisei inogda prorotse*), paralleling the Old Testament type of Moses extending his hands to defeat Amalek (Exodus 17:11) with the Christian empire's victory under St. Constantine the Great through the sign of the Cross. On Fol. 254r, the Matins Sessional Hymn after the first Kathisma versification (*Mesto lobnoye ray byst*) transforms Golgotha into a new Eden where the planted Cross immediately produces the Life-giving Cluster. Quire signature `Ц҃ ц҃ д҃` appears at the foot of Fol. 254r (printed `сн҃` / 250r), marking the fourth signed recto of quire `Ц`.

[^260]: Fol. 254v contains the Matins Staurotheotokion evoking the cosmic paradox of the Crucifixion: the Virgin beholds stretched upon the Wood the very Lord Who "stretched out heaven like a curtain" (Psalm 103:2 LXX: *prostry bozhestvenne nebo yakozhe kozhu*). Fol. 255r commences quire `Ч҃ ч҃` (printed `сна` / 251r), advancing through the second Kathisma Sessional Hymns in Tone 5 which celebrate Christ's voluntary Passion as the total annulment of the ancestral culinary curse of Eden (*drevnyuyu klyatvu yazhe radi snedi*).

[^261]: Fol. 255v introduces the Friday Matins Canon to the Precious and Life-giving Cross in Tone 5 to the heirmos *Spasitelyu Bogu* (*Unto the Savior God Who guided the people through the sea with dry feet...*; Exodus 15:1). The preceding Sessional Hymn model melody *Sobe.* abbreviates *Ton synanarchon Logon* (*Sobeznachalnoye Slovo* / *The co-unoriginate Word*). The Theotokion bridging Fol. 256r and 256v sets the mystery of the Incarnation into high dogmatic relief, juxtaposing Christ as the "new Child" (*novo otrocha*) with His eternal identity as the Danielic "Ancient of Days" (*Vetkhi denmi*; Daniel 7:9). Fol. 256r is signed `Ч҃ ч҃ в҃` (printed `сн҃в` / 252r).

[^262]: Ode 3 of the Friday Canon of the Cross (Fol. 257r) is chanted to the heirmos *Siloyu Kresta Tvoyego* (*By the power of Thy Cross establish my heart*). The second troparion articulates the paschal typology of Exodus 12:7, 13, wherein Christ the slaughtered Lamb marks the spiritual doorposts (*pragi*) of the faithful's souls with His divine blood. The martyria depict the martyrs' sufferings as wine pressed from the fruit of the living Vine, gladdening the Church. Quire signature `Ч҃ ч҃ г҃` marks Fol. 257r (printed `сн҃г` / 253r).

[^263]: Ode 4 of the Friday Canon of the Cross (Fol. 257v–258r) is framed by the prophetic heirmos of Habakkuk 3:1–2 LXX (*Uslyshakh slukh tvoy i uboyakhsya* / *O Lord, I heard Thy report and was afraid*). The hymn contrasts the temporal setting of the "Sun of righteousness" (*solntse pravednoye*) upon Golgotha with the spiritual dawning of the "unwaning Light" (*svet nevecherniy*) upon creation. Fol. 258r completes the signed rectos of quire `Ч` with signature `Ч҃ ч҃ д҃` (printed `сн҃д` / 254r).
