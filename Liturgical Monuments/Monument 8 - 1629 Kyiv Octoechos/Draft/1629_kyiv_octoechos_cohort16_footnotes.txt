[^154]: Folios 148v–149r contain the concluding Resurrection Stichera and the Anatolian (*Восточны*) stichera of Sunday Great Vespers in Tone 7 at "Lord, I have cried", culminating in the opening of the famous Dogmatic Theotokion (*Мати убо познася паче естества, Богородице*). The hymnography emphasizes the paradoxical twofold nature of Christ (first bodiless as the Word, then incarnate and put to death as man, rising in divine power), and David's prophecies in Zion (Psalms 49:2, 109:2 LXX).
[^155]: Folios 149v–150r conclude the Sunday Great Vespers of Tone 7 (with the completion of the Dogmatikon and the Aposticha stichera) and transition on Fol. 150r to the Sunday Midnight Office (*В неделю на полунощници*) with the opening of the Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity (*Канѡнъ святей живоначальней Троице*). Note the famous theological aphorism in the Dogmatikon: *идеже бо хощетъ Богъ, побеждается естества чинъ* ("for where God so wills, the order of nature is overcome"). The Midnight Trinity Canon is composed to the Heirmos *Маниемъ ти* (*By Thy beck*).
[^156]: Folios 150v–151r contain Odes 1 and 3 of the Sunday Midnight Office Trinity Canon in Tone 7. The hymnographer articulates high Trinitarian theology: the Father as the unoriginate Mind (*Оумъ безначалный*) begetting the co-eternal Word and causing the co-unoriginate Spirit to shine forth, and as the unoriginate root (*ѿ корене безначална*) from which the Word sprouts like a shoot (*стебль*). Christological typology is prominent in the Ode 1 Theotokion, where the Burning Bush (*купина*) seen by Moses prefigures the virginal conception without corruption.
[^157]: Folios 151v–152r span Odes 4 and 5 of the Sunday Midnight Office Trinity Canon, including the Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ: Помилуй, Троице святая*) after Ode 3. Note the antinomic Trinitarian phrasing on Fol. 152r: *нераздѣлно тя раздѣляемъ* ("indivisibly we distinguish Thee"), reflecting the classical Cappadocian synthesis of the undivided divine essence (*ousia*) existing in three distinct hypostases.
[^158]: Folios 152v–153r contain Odes 6 and 7 of the Sunday Midnight Office Trinity Canon, divided by the mid-canon Sessional Hymn (*Тажъ Сѣдална: Троице единосущная*). Fol. 152v displays notable technical theological vocabulary from St. John of Damascus's *De Fide Orthodoxa*: distinguishing the Persons solely by their hypostatic/existential properties (*различіе имуще точію, бытными коимиждо свойствы* — Greek ἰδιώμασι ὑποστατικοῖς), namely unbegottenness in the Father, generation in the Son, and procession in the Holy Spirit.
[^159]: Folios 153v–154r span Odes 8 and 9 of the Sunday Midnight Office Trinity Canon. In Ode 8, the refrains draw from the Song of the Three Holy Children (Daniel 3:57–88: *Вся дѣла Господня Господа пойте* / "Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord"). The hymnographer's personal petition on Fol. 154r asks the Lord to make the worshiper a "temple of Thy thrice-radiant ray" (*Храмъ мя твоея тріесіянныя сотвори луча*), safe from unseen spiritual demons and fleshly passions.
[^160]: Folios 154v–155r mark the completion of the Sunday Midnight Office Trinity Canon and the transition to Sunday Matins in Tone 7 at "God is the Lord" (*На Богъ Господь*). Fol. 155r features the seminal Resurrection Troparion of Tone 7 (*Разруши крестомъ своимъ смерть* / *Κατέλυσας τῷ σταυρῷ σου τὸν θάνατον*), accompanied by its Resurrection Theotokion celebrating Mary's perpetual virginity (*яже прежде рождества дѣва, и въ рождествѣ дѣва, и по рождествѣ паки дѣва*), followed by the first Kathisma Sessional Hymn (*Знаменану гробу* / *Τῆς ζωῆς ἐν тῷ τάφῳ*).
[^161]: Folios 155v–156r contain the second Kathisma Sessional Hymn, the Sunday Hypakoe (*Иже нашъ зракъ воспріемый* / *Ὁ τὴν ἡμετέραν μορφὴν ἀναλαβών*), and the opening Antiphons of the Tone 7 Sunday Songs of Ascents (*Степенна* / *Ἀναβαθμοί*). The text paraphrases Psalm 125:1–6 LXX ("When the Lord turned back the captivity of Zion... They that sow in tears shall reap in joy") and Psalm 126:1 LXX ("Except the Lord build the house, they labor in vain that build it"), reinterpreting the house allegorically as the "spiritual house" (*домъ душевный*).
[^162]: Folios 156v–157r conclude the Sunday Songs of Ascents (Antiphons 2 and 3), give the Sunday Matins Prokeimenon (*Воскресни, Господи Боже мой* / Psalm 9:33 LXX) and Gospel rubric, and open the Sunday Matins Canons of Tone 7. Antiphon 3 draws directly from Psalm 127:3 LXX ("Thy children like young olive plants round about thy table"). The opening Resurrection Canon is set to the Heirmos *Маніемъ ти на земны образъ* (*Νεύσει σου πρὸς γεώδη*), recounting the drying of the Red Sea (Exodus 14).
[^163]: Folios 157v–158r present the concluding troparia of Ode 1 for both the Cross-Resurrection and Theotokos Canons, followed by the opening of Ode 3 of the Sunday Matins Resurrection Canon in Tone 7. On Fol. 157v, the Cross-Resurrection troparion meditates on the "two life-bearing streams" (*Два источника живоносна*) flowing from Christ's pierced side (John 19:34: blood and water, typifying the Holy Mysteries of Baptism and the Eucharist). The Ode 3 Heirmos on Fol. 158r (*В начатцѣхъ небеса*) paraphrases Psalm 32:6 LXX: "By the word of the Lord the heavens were established, and all the host of them by the breath of His mouth."
