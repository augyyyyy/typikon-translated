[^104]: Fol. 98v–99r (Leaf p101). Saturday Vespers, "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of Tone 5 (concluded). The closing stichera draw upon the Harrowing of Hades and Isaiah 49:9 ("Saying to them that are in bonds: Go forth; and to them that are in darkness: Show yourselves"). Fol. 98v witnesses the spelling *ѡ҆кры́йтесѧ* (*открыйтеся*), and Fol. 99r records the polemical stichera refuting the bribery of the Roman guard (*кꙋсто́дїа*, Matthew 28:11–15) by noting the absurdity of stealing an anointed, naked corpse while leaving the burial linens behind.

[^105]: Fol. 99v–100r (Leaf p102). Saturday Vespers, Tone 5 (Dogmatikon Theotokion and Aposticha). Fol. 99v contains the classic Dogmatikon *Въ чермнѣ́мъ мо́ри* (*In the Red Sea*), typologically contrasting Moses dividing the sea with the Archangel Gabriel announcing the virginal conception of Emmanuel, and Israel passing dryshod with Mary remaining an incorrupt virgin. Fol. 100r opens the Aposticha celebrating Christ's pierced side pouring forth streams of remission, life, and salvation.

[^106]: Fol. 100v–101r (Leaf p103). Conclusion of Saturday Vespers and opening of the Sunday Midnight Office in Tone 5. Fol. 100v contains the Resurrection Troparion *Собезнача́лное сло́во* (*The Co-unoriginate Word*) and the Marian dismissal theotokion *Цр҃кви и҆ вра́та сꙋ́щи*. Fol. 101r begins the Midnight Office Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity with the Ode 1 heirmos incipit *Конѧ и҆ всадника* (*Horse and rider*, Exodus 15:1 / Ode 1 of Moses), employing the compound theological epithets *трислꙋ́чнаго* (*thrice-radiant*) and *є҆динстве́ннаго* (*unitary*).

[^107]: Fol. 101v–102r (Leaf p104). Sunday Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy Trinity, Tone 5 (Odes 1–3). Fol. 101v witnesses the metaphor of divine compunction as *бж҃тве́нное пи́во твое́ѧ любвѐ* (*the divine drink of Thy love*, Greek πόμα) and invokes the Gideon-fleece typology (Judges 6:37–40; Psalm 71:6 [LXX 71:6 / MT 72:6]: *ꙗ҆́кѡ же на̀ рꙋно̀ сни́де безшꙋ́ма*). Fol. 102r features the theophany of the Angel of Great Counsel in the burning bush (Exodus 3:2; Isaiah 9:6) and introduces the Sessional Hymn modeled on *Собезнача́лное сло́во*.

[^108]: Fol. 102v–103r (Leaf p105). Sunday Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy Trinity, Tone 5 (Odes 4–5). Fol. 102v interprets the vision of Daniel 7:9–14 (the Ancient of Days and the Son of Man coming before Him) as a prophetic revelation of the Trinity. Fol. 103r marks a codicological quire transition where gathering Ч concludes on Fol. 102v and gathering Ш begins on Fol. 103r, accompanied by the Habakkuk theophany (Habakkuk 3:3: *Гора̀ чⷭ҇тна́ѧ и҆ присѣ́нна* / *the overshadowing mountain*).

[^109]: Fol. 103v–104r (Leaf p106). Sunday Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy Trinity, Tone 5 (Odes 6–7). Fol. 103v applies Psalm 35:10 (LXX 35:10 / MT 36:9: "In Thy light shall we see light") to the trinitarian mystery of the Father's light, the Son's manifestation, and the Holy Spirit's illumination. Fol. 104r contains the Sessional Hymn weaving Psalm 6:2 ("rebuke me not in Thine anger") and Psalm 142:2 ("enter not into judgment with Thy servant") into personal penitential supplication.

[^110]: Fol. 104v–105r (Leaf p107). Sunday Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy Trinity, Tone 5 (Odes 8–9). Fol. 104v cites the Hospitality of Abraham (Genesis 18) as the primary biblical theophany revealing the Trinity under human appearance. Fol. 105r articulates the classical Eastern Orthodox pneumatological doctrine: the Holy Spirit is *животворѧ́щїй* (*life-creating*), *и҆сходѧ̀ и҆ ѻ҆ц҃а̀* (*proceeding from the Father*), and *сн҃ом же ꙗ҆влѧ́ем* (*manifested through the Son*).

[^111]: Fol. 105v–106r (Leaf p108). Conclusion of the Midnight Office and opening of Sunday Matins in Tone 5. Fol. 105v records the Seraphic Trisagion vision of Isaiah 6:1–3. Following the First and Second Kathismata (*На̀ а҃ / в҃, стїхоло́гїи*), the Sessional Hymns on Fol. 106r juxtapose the human guards keeping watch over the earthly tomb above with Christ descending below to raise the dead from the ages (*верхꙋ̀ во́ини гро́бъ стрежа́хꙋ: низꙋ́ же ѿ вѣ́ка ме́ртвыѧ воскреси́лъ є҆сѝ*).

[^112]: Fol. 106v–107r (Leaf p109). Sunday Matins, Tone 5 (Hypakoe, Anavathmoi, and Prokeimenon). Following Psalm 118 (*По непоро́чна*), Fol. 106v introduces the Hypakoe *А҆́гг҃льскимъ зра́комъ* and the three Antiphons of the Gradual Hymns (*А҆нтифо́ны*), paraphrasing the Gradual Psalms (Psalms 119–121 [LXX 119–121 / MT 120–122]). Fol. 107r marks the transition to quire Щ and records the Sunday Matins Prokeimenon in Tone 5 (Psalm 9:33 [LXX 9:33 / MT 10:12]: *Воскрⷭ҇ни гдⷭ҇и бж҃е мо́й...*).

[^113]: Fol. 107v–108r (Leaf p110). Sunday Matins, Tone 5 Canons (Resurrection, Cross-Resurrection, and Theotokos, Ode 1). Following the Matins Gospel and Psalm 50, Fol. 107v opens the Resurrection Canon of St. John of Damascus (*Конѧ̀ и҆ вса́дника*), interpreting the crown of thorns (*терконо́сное є҆ѵре́йское собо́рище*) as releasing the curse of thorns of Genesis 3:18. Fol. 108r opens the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection and the Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.
