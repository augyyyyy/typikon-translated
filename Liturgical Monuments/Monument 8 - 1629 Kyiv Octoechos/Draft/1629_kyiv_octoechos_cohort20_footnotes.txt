The stichera are dedicated to the incorporeal heavenly hosts, whose commemoration occurs on Mondays throughout the Byzantine liturgical year.
[^201]: In Fol. 195v (Page 198, left), the rubric 'Въ пн҃елокъ на Оу҆́трени, по а҃ Каді́смѣ: Сѣ́: Гла́съ а҃' inaugurates the weekday cycle in Tone 1, confirming the codicological division of the 1629 Kyiv Octoechos wherein Sunday resurrectional services for all eight tones (fol. 1–194) are followed by the general weekday hymnography starting with Tone 1 Monday. The first Sessional draws upon Psalm 50:7 LXX ('Conceived in transgressions am I') and the parable of the eleventh-hour laborers (Matthew 20:1–16).

Fol. 197r line 12 introduces the Monday Canon of Repentance in Tone 1, set to the ancient Exodus heirmos 'Thy victorious right hand' (Exodus 15:6 LXX).
