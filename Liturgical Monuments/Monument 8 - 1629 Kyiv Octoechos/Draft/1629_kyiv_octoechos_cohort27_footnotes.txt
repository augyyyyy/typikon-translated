[^264]: Fol. 258v concludes Ode 4 of the Friday Theotokion canon and initiates Ode 5 of the Friday Canon of the Cross in Tone 5, chanted to the heirmos *Utrennyuyushche vopiyem Ti Gospodi* (*Rising early we cry aloud unto Thee, O Lord: Save us, for Thou art our God, beside Thee we know no other*; cf. Isaiah 26:9). Fol. 259r inaugurates quire `Ш҃ ш҃` (printed `с҃н҃є` / 255r), containing the martyria praising the indivisible fortitude of the martyrs whose bodies were severed by swords while their love for Christ remained uncut.

[^265]: Ode 6 of the Friday Canon of the Cross in Tone 5 is chanted to the Jonahic heirmos *Obyde mya bezdna* (*The abyss hath surrounded me, the whale became a grave unto me; but I cried unto Thee, the Lover of mankind...*; Jonah 2:3, 6 LXX). Fol. 259v troparion 2 weaves together the finding of the lost drachma (Luke 15:8–10) with the solar eclipse at Golgotha, describing the flesh of Christ ignited like a lamp upon the Wood to recover fallen humanity buried beneath passions. Fol. 260r is signed `Ш҃ ш҃ в҃` (printed `с҃н҃ѕ` / 256r), concluding Ode 6 and introducing Ode 7 with heirmos *V peshchi ognennoy* (*In the furnace of fire the holy Children sang...*).

[^266]: Fol. 260v–261r carries the conclusion of Ode 7 and the commencement of Ode 8 of the Friday Canon of the Cross in Tone 5 to the heirmos *Iz Otsa prezhde vek* (*Him Who was begotten of the Father before the ages, the Son and God...*; cf. Daniel 3:57). The second troparion of Ode 8 contrasts the proud demonic serpent cast down by the Tree with fallen humanity raised up through humble repentance. Signature `Ш҃ ш҃ г҃` on Fol. 261r (printed `с҃н҃з` / 257r) marks the third signed leaf of quire `Ш`.

[^267]: Ode 9 of the Friday Canon of the Cross in Tone 5 is introduced on Fol. 262r by the Marian heirmos *Tya pache uma* (*Thee Who beyond mind and word gavest birth in time to the timeless One...*). Troparion 2 directly cites Psalm 21:17–19 LXX (*Iskopasha rutse moi i noze moi, ischetosha vsya kosti moya*), linking the crucifixion between lawless men and the offering of gall and vinegar (Psalm 68:22 LXX) to the fulfillment of messianic prophecy. Quire signature `Ш҃ ш҃ д҃` on Fol. 262r (printed `с҃н҃и` / 258r) marks the fourth signed recto of quire `Ш`.

[^268]: Fol. 263r contains the Friday Matins Exapostilarion of the Cross (*Raduysya kreste presvyatyy, miru steno neoborimaya*), an expansive panegyric invocating the Holy Cross across ecclesial and civic estates (kings, warriors, apostles, faithful). The following Staurotheotokion on Fol. 263r articulates the maternal lament over the "primordial Light" (*svete nachalneyshiy*) setting upon Golgotha. The Aposticha Stichera of the Cross in Tone 5 then begin at the foot of Fol. 263r (printed `с҃н҃ѳ` / 259r).

[^269]: Fol. 263v–264r concludes Friday Matins with the Aposticha and Staurotheotokion portraying the Mother of God standing beside the Cross. The hymn explicitly cites the prophecy of Symeon from Luke 2:35 (*nynye bo ispolnisya glas izhe reche Symeon: serdtse tvoye neporochnaya oruzhiye proydet*), contrasting her painless virginal birthgiving with her agonizing compassion at the Crucifixion. Fol. 264r subsequently introduces the Friday Liturgy Beatitudes in Tone 5 (*Razboynik poznav na kreste* / Luke 23:42). Signature `Ш҃ ш҃ ѕ҃` on Fol. 264r (printed `с҃ѯ` / 260r) marks the sixth folio of quire `Ш`.

[^270]: Fol. 264v transitions from the Friday Beatitudes of Tone 5 to Friday Vespers (the commencement of Saturday of Tone 6), marked by the Aposticha/Lord I have cried rubric: *V pyatok vecher na Gospodi vozzvakh Stikhiry, svyatym Muchenikom, i svyatitelem, i Prepodobnym, Glas 6, podoben: Vse upovaniye* (*On Friday evening, at "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of the Holy Martyrs, Hierarchs, and Venerable Monks, Tone 6, model melody: Having placed all their hope*). The rubric opens with a decorative woodcut initial 'В'. Fol. 265r is signed `Ш҃ ш҃ з҃` (printed `с҃ѯа` / 261r).

[^271]: Fol. 265v includes cross-reference rubrics for Friday Vespers in Tone 6, citing the Dogmatic Theotokion *Kto tebe ne blazhit* (*Who shall not call Thee blessed, O all-holy Virgin?*) by directing the chanter to Tone 2 Sunday Great Vespers (*pn v v ko ve* = *poi v vtornik k vecheru* / seek in Tone 2 Vespers). Fol. 266r contains the Saturday Nekrosimon (*Nachatok mi i sostav*), an ontological hymn of Christian anthropology tracing human creation from earthly dust and divine inbreathing (Genesis 2:7), praying for the repose of the departed in the "tents of the righteous." Quire signature is omitted on Fol. 266r (printed `с҃ѯв` / 262r), representing the eighth leaf of quire `Ш`.

[^272]: Fol. 266v–267r contains Saturday Matins in Tone 6, opening with the first and second Kathisma Sessional Hymns. The Theotokion of the first Kathisma (*Izhe blagoslovennuyu narekiy matyer sebe*) links Christ's voluntary Crucifixion with the parable of the lost drachma (Luke 15:8–9), depicting Christ displaying the reclaimed soul of Adam to the rejoicing angelic hosts. Fol. 267r inaugurates quire `Ъ҃ ъ҃` (printed `с҃ѯг` / 263r), containing the Second Kathisma Sessional Hymns and the famous Nekrosimon *Voinstinnu suyeta vsyacheskaya* (*Truly all things are vanity, and life is but a shadow and a dream*; cf. Ecclesiastes 1:2, Psalm 38:7 LXX).

[^273]: Fol. 267v introduces Saturday Matins Canon of the All Saints (Martyrs, Hierarchs, Venerable Monks, Righteous) in Tone 6 to the heirmos *Chudotvornyy Farao potople* (*He Who marvelously drowned Pharaoh in the Red Sea...*; Exodus 15). The troparia celebrate the martyrs who put to shame the impious tormentors and arrived at the unwaning light of Christ.

[^274]: Fol. 268r continues Saturday Matins with troparia celebrating the holy women who overcame the demonic dragon by their manly ascetic struggles (*trudy muzheskimi*). It then introduces the second Canon for the Departed with the heirmos *Vodu proshed yakozhe sushu* (*Having traversed the water as dry land...*), whose rubrical tone designation in the Kyiv 1629 print gives `Гла́, и҆` (an evident misprint for Tone 6 / `Гла́, ѕ҃`, or referencing the standard Tone 8 heirmos model). Signature `Ъ҃ Ъ҃ в҃` on Fol. 268r (printed `с҃ѯд` / 264r) marks the second signed recto of quire `Ъ`.
