[^275]: Fol. 268v–269r completes Ode 1 and contains Ode 3 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6 (Canon of All Saints to the heirmos *Na tverdem very tvoeya kameni* / *Upon the firm rock of Thy faith*, and Canon for the Departed to *Nebesnomu krugu* / *O Lord, Builder of the vault of heaven*). Fol. 269r is signed `Ъ҃ Ъ҃ г҃` (printed `с҃ѯє` / 265r), representing the third signed recto of quire `Ъ`. The troparia celebrate the martyrs who by their fortitude preserved the "divine sheep of Christ" and the holy women who joined the celestial choir before the Mother of God.

[^276]: Fol. 269v–270r contains Ode 4 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6, chanted to the heirmoi *Uslyshakh Gospodi slukh tvoy* (*I heard, O Lord, Thy report, and was afraid; I considered Thy works and was astonished*; Habakkuk 3:2 LXX) and *Tymi Khriste Gospod* (*Thou art my Lord, O Christ, Thou art my strength*). Fol. 270r (printed `с҃ѯѕ` / 266r) bears signature `Ъ҃ Ъ҃ д҃`, marking the fourth signed leaf of quire `Ъ`. The hymnography compares the holy ascetics to glowing coals (*ugliye*) consumed by love for God that burn away the material passions of this world.

[^277]: Fol. 270v–271r contains Ode 5 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6 to the heirmoi *Vozsiyavyy svet vsemu miru* (*Thou Who didst shine forth light upon the whole world*) and *Voskuyu mya otrinul yesi* (*Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy face, O Light unwaning?*; cf. Psalm 42:2 LXX). A noteworthy codicological development occurs on Fol. 271r (printed `с҃ѯз` / 267r), which introduces a fresh quire signature `Ы҃ Ы҃` directly following `Ъ҃ Ъ҃ д҃`, indicating that quire `Ъ` was set as an irregular 4-leaf gathering (binio) rather than the standard 8-leaf quaternion.

[^278]: Fol. 271v–272r spans the conclusion of Ode 5 and the commencement of Ode 6 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6, with Ode 6 chanted to the heirmos *Kitom pozhert* (*Swallowed by the whale of the abyss...*; Jonah 2:1–10). Fol. 271v contains an expansive eschatological supplication asking Christ to settle the faithful departed in the bosom of Abraham (Luke 16:22) where the voice of those who feast is sweet (Psalm 41:5 LXX). Fol. 272r (printed `с҃ѯи` / 268r) is signed `Ы҃ Ы҃ в҃`, the second signed recto of quire `Ы`.

[^279]: Fol. 272v–273r spans Ode 6 and Ode 7 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6 to the heirmoi *Ochisti mya Spase* (*Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are my transgressions...*; cf. Jonah 2:3) and *Svyatii otrotsy* / *Deti yevreyskiya* (*The holy Children in the furnace...*; Daniel 3). Fol. 272v features a vivid Anastasis troparion describing Christ tearing apart the belly of hostile death and releasing all who were bound in Hades. Fol. 273r (printed `с҃ѯѳ` / 269r) is signed `Ы҃ Ы҃ г҃`.

[^280]: Fol. 273v–274r carries Ode 8 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6, chanted to the heirmoi *Svyatii otrotsy v Vavylone* (*The holy children in Babylon, burning with divine zeal...*; Daniel 3) and *Muchenicheskym* (*In the martyrs' fire...*). The troparia celebrate the glorious company of the martyrs, prophets, high priests, venerable fathers, and holy women who intercede before the heavenly throne for the preservation of Christ's flock. Fol. 274r (printed `с҃о` / 270r) is signed `Ы҃ Ы҃ д҃`, concluding the signed rectos of quire `Ы`.

[^281]: Fol. 274v–275r contains Ode 9 of the Saturday Matins Canons in Tone 6, chanted to the heirmoi *Priyemshaya radost angelom* (*She that received the joy through the angel...*; Luke 1:28) and *Ustrashisya vsyak slyshay* (*Every ear shuddered at the ineffable condescension of God...*). Fol. 275r inaugurates quire `Ь҃ Ь҃` (printed `с҃оа` / 271r), continuing the sequence of 4-leaf quire demarcations (`Ъ`, `Ы`, `Ь`). The troparia praise the martyrs illumined by Trinity rays who put down tyrannical impiety.

[^282]: Fol. 275v–276r marks the conclusion of Saturday Matins Canon in Tone 6, followed by the Exapostilaria (*Prorotsy preslavnii* and the Nekrosimon *Izhe mertvymi i zhivymi obladaya*), leading into the Matins Praises (Ainoi) at *Na khvalitekh*. The Theotokion on Fol. 275v gives an explicit Chalcedonian Christological confession of Christ as "both God and man, twofold in nature but not in person" (*suguba yestestvom, no ne sostavom*). Fol. 276r (printed `с҃ов` / 272r) is signed `Ь҃ Ь҃ в҃`.

[^283]: Fol. 276v–277r contains the Praises Stichera of the Martyrs in Tone 6 and transitions to the Aposticha Penitential Stichera (*Stikhiry pokayanny*, Tone 6, model melody: *Vse upovaniye* / *Having placed all their hope*). Fol. 276v features the celebrated Nekrosimon contrasting the poison of the ancient serpent in Eden which brought universal death upon Adam's line with the life-giving advent of Christ Who trampled death. Fol. 277r (printed `с҃ог` / 273r) is signed `Ь҃ Ь҃ г҃`.

[^284]: Fol. 277v–278r concludes Saturday Matins in Tone 6 with the final Aposticha Nekrosima and Theotokion, and introduces the Saturday Liturgy Beatitudes in Tone 6 (*V Subbotu Blazhenna, Glas Shestyy*). The concluding Theotokion on Fol. 278r reiterates the two natures in one hypostasis (*vo dvoyu yestestvo, vo yedinom zhe sostavye*) and Christ's preservation of the Virgin's blameless integrity in and after birthgiving. Fol. 278r (printed `с҃од` / 274r) is signed `Ь҃ Ь҃ д҃`.
