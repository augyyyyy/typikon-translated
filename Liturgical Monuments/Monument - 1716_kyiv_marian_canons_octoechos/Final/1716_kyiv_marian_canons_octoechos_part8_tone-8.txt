## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 8)

*On Saturday at Small Compline,*[^252]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

Let us sing unto the Lord, Who led His people through the Red Sea, for He alone has been gloriously glorified.[^253]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Come mystically, O brethren, as from the beginning unto the Most Holy Theotokos let us the faithful today offer a new song, praising her majesty.

*Troparion:*

Moses, having of old been enlightened in mind by the vision of God, was clearly instructed concerning thy godlike conception surpassing nature, O Pure Virgin, which was revealed unto him in the Bush. Glory:[^254]

[f. 183vA] Unto thee do I present the deeds of my heart; and do thou favorably offer the writing, being near, having placed thee as a shelter of divine intercession before Christ the Master. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Incline thine ear unto me, O Pure One; with orthodox faith turn thy precious face unto me who flee unto thee with love; and unto me who bow down in fear, hear my suppliant voice.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou art the confirmation of those who flee unto Thee, O Lord; Thou art the light of the darkened, and my spirit sings of Thee.[^255]

*Troparion:*

The Patriarch's Ladder of old prefigured thee, O All-immaculate One: for it revealed the angelic descent, the divine condescension of God unto us in thy womb.[^256]

*Troparion:*

Rejoice, O Tribe of Judah, which Jacob foretold, that from his tribe deliverance should spring forth, Jesus Christ: Whom thou, O Most Pure One, having brought forth, hast glorified. Glory:[^257]

*Troparion:*

Encompassed by sins, I have found thee to be a haven for salvation, O Most Holy Theotokos, our hope and help; therefore guide me unto repentance. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Having thee near unto the Master, O All-glorious Sovereign Lady, I have laid upon thee in faith the book of my deeds: cease not to have compassion upon me.

[f. 184rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

From the overshadowing Mountain of the only Theotokos, O Word, the Prophet godlike perceived that Thou wast about to become incarnate, and with fear he glorified Thy power.[^258]

*Troparion:*

As if clothed in golden fringes, O Bride of the Father, having received grace, adorned with the beauty of virginity, thou hast appeared as the Mother of the Son of God.[^259]

*Troparion:*

As the true Zion, Christ the Word was pleased to take thee unto Himself as a divine habitation, having chosen thee, O chosen Theotokos, for the renewal of the whole world. Glory:[^260]

*Troparion:*

Rejoice, fair palace of the Word, virginal bridal chamber of the King! Rejoice, praise of all the Bodiless Powers! Rejoice, help of mankind! Both now:

*Theotokion:*

They estrange themselves from God, and therefore perish, who reject the icons of thy Son, O Mother of God, Virgin Theotokos, through which those who honor thee are saved.[^261]

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Dispel the gloom of my soul, O Giver of Light, Christ God, having driven away the primordial darkness of the abyss; and grant me the light of Thy commandments, O Word, that rising early I may glorify Thee.[^262]

*Troparion:*

Having come together with the divine Gabriel, let us faithfully cry unto the Theotokos: Rejoice, O holy Virgin, full of grace, the Lord is with thee, Who for thy sake consumed sorrow and bestowed rejoicing.

[f. 184vA] *Troparion:*

Gideon beheld thy most holy womb, O Pure Virgin, into which the Word descended as dew, and was incarnate by the Holy Spirit, being undivided from the Godhead of the Father. Glory:[^263]

*Troparion:*

Thou art a helper unto the world and a protectress unto sinful men, O Virgin Mother of God, and unto those who flee unto thee with faith and love, a saving transformation, and the loosening of many transgressions. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Seedlessly hast thou budded forth, before every creature, the Offspring of the Son from the Father, timeless and beginningless, by the Divine Spirit, O Pure Mother of God: the likeness of Whose appearance all of us revere.[^264]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Held fast by many sins, receive me who flee unto Thy tender mercies, O Compassionate One, as the Prophet, O Lord, and save me.[^265]

*Troparion:*

Thee who art the mirror of virginity and the honorable receptacle of the Godhead do we praise with songs, O Virgin unacquainted with wedlock.

*Troparion:*

God was incarnate in thy womb, passionlessly and awesomely, O Bride of God: as in a new scroll written by the Father's Finger. Glory:[^266]

[f. 185rA] We possess thy protection as cleansing, and as our certain hope and defense, O Pure Virgin: put not thy servants to shame, O Sovereign Lady. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

By thine intercession, O Bride of God, calm the unceasing surging of passions, and guide us unto the haven of stillness.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "To thee, the Champion Leader" (Возбра́нной Воево́дѣ):*

To thee, the Champion Leader, do we thy servants dedicate a song of victory and thanksgiving as ones delivered from evil, O Theotokos; but as thou hast might unassailable, deliver us from all dangers, that we may cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Bride unwedded![^267]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The fire was put to shame of old in Babylon by God's condescension; therefore the children, with joy-stepping feet in the furnace as in a bridal chamber, danced, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers![^268]

*Troparion:*

Thou hast appeared as a temple unto the incomprehensible God, O Most Pure Virgin; by thy prayers show me also to be a temple of His divine grace, and preserve me unharmed.

*Troparion:*

A Holy Mountain art thou, overshadowed by God, a rich Mountain, O All-immaculate One, a fertile Mountain with divine splendors, a Mountain wherein God was pleased to dwell. Glory:[^269]

[f. 185vA] Wholly as the Pure One hast thou received the Word being incarnate in thy womb; entreat Him, O Most Pure One, to cleanse me from transgressions, who flee unto thee with a contrite heart and pure faith. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou hast given birth unto One of the Trinity, O Theotokos, Who became unchangeably united in the flesh, being twofold in nature: the visible likeness of Whose form and image we revere.[^270]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who was foretold on the holy mountain, and in the fiery bush revealed unto Moses the mystery of virginity, sing of the Lord and supremely exalt Him unto all ages.[^271]

*Troparion:*

Thou hast appeared unto the Prophet as the censer of the divine coal that takes away sins, O Virgin Theotokos, Mother of our God.[^272]

*Troparion:*

Daniel foresaw thee as a great Mountain, O Virgin Theotokos, from which the Precious Stone, Christ, was cut out in the flesh, and cast down the temples of idol deceit. Glory:[^273]

*Troparion:*

The great whale of grievous sin and the despair of my passions seek to swallow me; but hasten beforehand and save thy servant, O Sovereign Lady. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

He Who conversed with men through thee, being God of majesty, was pleased to receive the form of a man: Whose countenance we revere in writings, O Virgin.[^274]

[f. 186rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Confessing thee as truly the Theotokos, saved by thee, O Pure Virgin, with the Heavenly Hosts do we magnify thee.[^275]

*Troparion:*

The wisest one in the Song of Songs sings of thee, O Virgin Theotokos, as an enclosed garden and a sealed fountain by the Divine Spirit; therefore as the Garden of Life, Christ is incarnate.[^276]

*Troparion:*

The Prophet proclaimed thine ineffable Childbearing, foreseeing a sealed Book, whose mystery of the incarnation of thy childbearing no one comprehended. Glory:[^277]

*Troparion:*

Unto thee with compunction of soul do we all pray, despise not our supplications, O Sovereign Lady; but be unto us a benign protection, and hear our prayer. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

I fall down before the image of thy Son and before thine own, and those who doubt concerning honoring them I cast away as the falsehoods of Manes, O Virgin Theotokos: therefore orthodoxly do I conclude the song.[^278]

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 8)

*On Sunday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

The wonderworking rod of Moses of old, striking crosswise and dividing the sea, submerged Pharaoh the chariot-driver; and it saved Israel who fled on foot, singing a song unto God.[^279]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Most glorious things were spoken of thee, O Most Pure One, who alone hast become the glory of the faithful; therefore by thy prayers show me to be a partaker of future glory, who glorify thee, O Most Holy and All-hymned Virgin.[^280]

*Troparion:*

Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O Most Pure One, through Whom all of us earthborn were delivered from the curse, O Blessed and All-honorable One! O ineffable wonder! O incomprehensible report! The salvation of all the faithful! Glory:

*Troparion:*

A multitude of angels ever entreats Thee, O Lord, and the choir of Apostles, the company of Prophets, the assembly of Martyrs, [f. 187rA] and of Thy Venerable Ones: for the Theotokos' sake, grant us forgiveness of transgressions, as Thou art the Lover of mankind. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thee, O Most Pure One, who didst receive in thy womb the uncontainable Fire, do I entreat in faith: deliver me and snatch me from the fire of Gehenna, and free me from eternal torment by thy favorable prayers.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

O Lord, Artificer of the celestial vault and Builder of the Church: do Thou establish me in Thy love, O Height of desires, Confirmation of the faithful, only Lover of mankind.[^281]

*Troparion:*

Having fallen away from a pure life, O Most Pure One, I have become likened unto the beasts, and am wholly condemned; do thou who didst give birth unto the Judge deliver and save me from every condemnation.[^282]

*Troparion:*

Grant me sighing, O Pure Maiden, and tears of soul, and perpetual compunction, that I may weep over the passions that hold me fast through ignorance, O all-blessed one. Glory:

*Troparion:*

The Heavenly Ranks, the ministering spirits, the company of Prophets, and Thine Apostles and Martyrs, unceasingly pray Thee, O Compassionate One: grant Thy people remission of transgressions. Both now:

[f. 187vA] *Theotokion:*

Since thou art merciful, O Most Pure One, entreat the Merciful One to account me worthy now of divine tender mercy; for having acquired merciless ways without compassion, I have become worthy of the eternal fire.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou art my strength, O Lord, Thou art my power; Thou art my God, Thou art my rejoicing, Who left not the Father's bosom, but in mercy visited our poverty; therefore with the Prophet Habakkuk I cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind![^283]

*Troparion:*

Tear up the spiritual handwriting of my sins, O Most Pure One, with the divine Spear that pierced the divine Ribs of Him Who was pleased to be made man from thee; and pray Him to inscribe me in the Book of the Saved, me who foolishly became estranged from Him.[^284]

*Troparion:*

That with thankful voices I may glorify thee, and with divine love magnify thee, O All-immaculate One, who by thy great childbearing dost draw near all that were estranged: account me also, who am exceedingly estranged and have grievously fallen, worthy of divine forgiveness. Glory:

*Troparion:*

The laws of nature were abolished in thee, for beyond nature thou didst give birth unto God the Word; therefore in faith I pray thee, O All-immaculate One: save by thy prayers him who hath sinned exceedingly beyond human nature, and who, having estranged himself from God, has turned back. Both now:

[f. 188rA] *Theotokion:*

The enemy hath wounded all my soul with the darts of sin, and hath defiled my heart with pleasures, and led me away from the righteous path; therefore, O Virgin, I cry unto thee: having turned me back, heal and save me.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Why hast Thou cast me away from Thy presence, O Light never-setting, and an alien darkness hath covered me, the wretched one? But turn me back, and guide my paths unto the light of Thy commandments, I pray Thee.[^285]

*Troparion:*

Generations of generations now call thee blessed, O most blessed Virgin, and the Heavenly Powers praise the only blessed among women, the restoration of fallen Adam, as having united the earthly with the heavenly.

*Troparion:*

Pray, O good one, having given birth unto the Good Word Who delivered us from unreason, that I may remain whole, and obtain an unshaken standing, walking well and doing what is well-pleasing unto God. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O Word Who didst shed Thy Blood upon the Cross, for the sake of the exceedingly great abyss of Thy tender mercy, dry up the abyss of my passions; and for the Theotokos' sake account me worthy to be well-pleasing unto Thee, my God, in compunction. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

O Mother Virgin Maiden, Bride of God, who in the flesh didst give birth unto the Incomprehensible One, the Glory of angels and the Wound of demons: account my heart, which hath been wounded by sin, worthy of divine healings.

[f. 188vA] *Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine iniquities, and bring me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and hear me, O God of my salvation.[^286]

*Troparion:*

Human nature was shown mercy through thee, O merciful Sovereign Lady, for thou didst give birth unto the Merciful One, O Most Pure One; therefore I pray thee: have mercy upon my soul.

*Troparion:*

The Bodiless Ranks pray Thee, O Christ, together with Thy Martyrs, Prophets, and Apostles: for the sake of her who gave Thee birth, save Thy people from every tribulation. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O untilled Earth that didst give birth unto the Husbandman and the Creator of all: show my soul, which hath been frozen cold by passions, to be fruitful with divine understanding. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

I have transgressed the commandments that lead me unto God, and unwillingly have become enslaved unto evil passions; therefore I ever pray thee, O Virgin: snatch me from their dominion.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "To thee, the Champion Leader" (Возбра́нной Воево́дѣ):*

Let us the faithful praise another piety for the hosts, and a praiseworthy height for our souls, the Virgin Maiden Mother: Rejoice, thou who in thy womb didst conceive the Creator of angels; rejoice, thou who didst incarnate God the Word, the cleansing of those who fall; rejoice, O Bride unwedded![^287]

[f. 189rA] *Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The fire was put to shame of old in Babylon by God's condescension; therefore the children, with joy-stepping feet in the furnace as in a flower-garden, danced, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers![^288]

*Troparion:*

Ineffable is thy childbearing, and of fearful and ineffable things hast thou accounted us worthy, O Theotokos; therefore deliver me from fearful torment, and fill me with ineffable joy, that worthily I may sing of thee, the all-hymned one.

*Troparion:*

Having spent my life in slothfulness, I have become unfruitful; and I am terrified of the trial and of the unquenchable fire of Gehenna. O Theotokos who didst give birth unto the unendurable Fire: incline to deliver me from Gehenna by thy prayers. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Exceeding all wonders is the great wonder of thy Childbearing, O Pure Virgin; therefore I cry unto thee, Mother of God: make marvelous thy tender mercies upon me, and deliver me from the wrath to come, and save me. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Unto me who have lived in slothfulness, and contradicted Thy holy laws and Thy precious commandments, O Christ: show compassion, O Savior, for the sake of her who gave Thee birth, as Thou art a beneficent God and plenteous in mercy.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The Chaldean tyrant fiercely kindled the furnace sevenfold for the godly; but having seen them saved by a higher power, he cried aloud unto the Creator and Redeemer: Ye children, bless; ye priests, hymn; ye people, supremely exalt Him unto all ages.[^289]

[f. 189vA] *Troparion:*

Quicken my deadened soul, raise it up fallen, heal it wounded, bring peace unto my mind, calm the waves of temptations, O Virgin, and save me who cry aloud: Ye children, bless; ye priests, hymn; ye people, supremely exalt Him unto all ages.

*Troparion:*

As the Thief I cry unto Thee: Remember me, O Lover of mankind; I weep as the Harlot, and cry: I have sinned as of old the Prodigal; receive me, the repentant sinner, for the Theotokos' sake, that with fervor I may cry unto Thee: Ye priests, hymn; ye people, supremely exalt Him unto all ages. Glory:[^290]

*Troparion:*

As servants we cry unto thee, O all-hymned Theotokos, with the countless noetic Powers, Prophets and Venerable Ones, Apostles and Martyrs, jointly praying for all men who divinely sing: Ye priests, hymn; ye people, supremely exalt Him unto all ages. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

More honorable art thou, O all-immaculate Theotokos, than the ranks of transcendent angels; for thou didst give birth unto their Creator and Lord from a virginal unwedded womb, in two essences, unconfusedly and unchangeably, one Hypostasis incarnate, O Theotokos.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Exceedingly fearful is the report of the ineffable condescension of God: how the Most High voluntarily descended even unto the flesh, having become man from the virginal womb; therefore thee, the Most Pure Theotokos, do we the faithful magnify.[^291]

[f. 190rA] *Troparion:*

O divine Bed of Solomon, which threescore valiant men surround [the words of the Divine Scripture], upon which God rested: preserve me unharmed by thy power, who am ever surrounded by myriads of demons, O Pure and Perpetual Virgin.[^292]

*Troparion:*

O thou who distillest divine sweetness, having given birth unto the Sweetness of all: sweeten my soul which hath been embittered by the venom of the serpent, ever alienating me from bitter harm by thine intercession, O unashamed protectress of the faithful. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O Jesus, Sun of Glory: enlighten my wretched soul through the prayers of her who gave Thee birth, the Pure Virgin Maiden of God, and of the Bodiless Servants, Thine honorable Apostles, Hierarchs, Prophets, Martyrs, the Elect, and Thy Venerable Ones. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

I am terrified, O only King, of Thy second and fearful coming, being wholly condemned and in perplexity, O Lord; therefore before the end I cry unto Thee: For the sake of her who gave Thee birth, spare me and save me, O Merciful, Good, Lover of mankind.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 8)

*On Monday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

Having traversed the water as upon dry land, and having escaped from the evil of Egypt, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Redeemer and God.[^293]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee, O Mother of God, as the prayerful fountain of waters pouring forth streams unto the faithful for souls and bodies, all of us unceasingly glorify thee.

*Troparion:*

Beyond nature hast thou given birth unto the transcendent Word of God, O Virgin, and hast delivered nature from corruption; therefore free me from irrational and unnatural passions. Glory:

*Troparion:*

The creepers of carnal passions now surround me and afflict me grievously: visit thy servant quickly, O Virgin, and save me from those that oppress me. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

He Who upholds all creation, bearing and saving it, was seen circumscribed in thine arms, O Pure One, and hath renewed the human race.

[f. 191rA] *Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou art the confirmation of those who flee unto Thee, O Lord; Thou art the light of the darkened, and my spirit sings of Thee.[^294]

*Troparion:*

Allay the disturbance of the grievous disease that now holds me fast, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, and free me from transgressions and passions.

*Troparion:*

Unsearchable is the abyss of thy compassions, O Most Pure One: whereby do thou deliver me also from transgressions and disease. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Make haste and visit me that am diseased, O Most Pure One, and deliver me from grievous illness and all affliction. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Magnify the mercy and intercession of thy prayers upon me, O Most Pure One, and deliver me from misfortunes and griefs.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation; I have understood Thy works, and I have glorified Thy Divinity.[^295]

*Troparion:*

Ever show unto my heart, O Most Pure One, a propitiation, the remembrance of the saints, and the cleansing of transgressions.

*Troparion:*

Grant me salvation of soul and body, O All-immaculate One; and unto him that is sick grant healing, and deliverance from grievous things. Glory:

[f. 191vA] *Troparion:*

Bring to naught the wiles of the subtle demons, O Most Pure One, and the uprising of passions; and grant strength unto us the infirm. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

From thy womb, O Most Pure One, the Sun of Righteousness shone forth, and hath enlightened the world: with Whose divine rays do thou also enlighten me.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Why hast Thou cast me away from Thy face, O Unwaning Light, and a strange darkness hath covered me, the wretched one; but turn me, and unto the light of Thy commandments direct my paths, I pray Thee.[^296]

*Troparion:*

By thy Childbearing the bonds of death have been loosed, and the dominion of corruption hath been bound, O Most Pure and All-hymned Virgin; therefore swiftly loose also the fetters of my transgressions, and heavy sorrows.

*Troparion:*

From the wiles of demons and human malice deliver me, O Sovereign Lady, and swiftly heal the disease of soul and body, O thou who alone didst give birth unto the Physician of all flesh and spirits, the Savior and Lord. Glory:

*Troparion:*

As the tongs of the Divine Coal, O Most Pure One, I pray thee: burn up every impassioned lust of thy servant, and make tolerable the grievous infirmities of diseases, and dry up the torrents of sorrows. Both now:

[f. 192rA] *Theotokion:*

Thy Son, O All-immaculate Sovereign Lady, grants thee as salvation unto all who have believed in Him, and who proclaim Him as God, manifestly incarnate of thee; therefore save me from diverse perils and tribulations.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine iniquities; and lead me up from the abyss of evils, I pray Thee: for unto Thee have I cried, and hear me, O God of my salvation.[^297]

*Troparion:*

Look upon me with thy merciful eye, O Sovereign Lady, and deliver me swiftly from grievous diseases, and from every affliction of mankind now, and from snares and falling.

*Troparion:*

From senseless and impassioned disposition, and from the envy of unworthy men, and from all malice and worldly affliction, deliver me, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, by thy supplications. Glory:

*Troparion:*

He Who fashioned us was fashioned in thee, O Virgin, delivering human nature from corruption; therefore deliver me, O Pure One, from the temptations that hold me fast, by thy prayers. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst appear as a most pure temple of the uncontainable God, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady: by thy prayers show me also to be a temple of His grace, and preserve me unharmed. [f. 193vA] of the surging sea, destroy the assault of all sorrows, and by thy prayers grant me stillness unto all ages.[^298] Glory:

*Troparion:*

He Who formed the earthly body of man, and then placed within him an immortal soul, is fashioned in thee, O Virgin, and renews him again; through thy prayers show Him to be propitious and favorable unto me, and grant strength and speedy deliverance unto all that are in sorrow. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

With light dost thou ever illuminate those who glorify thee, O Most Pure Maiden, from whom the Unwaning Light shone forth; and from the terror of night and darkness, and from the barkings of demons, dost thou deliver thy servants; from which snatch me also, and by thy prayers save me from the evils that encompass me.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Confessing thee to be truly the Theotokos, we who through thee are saved, O Pure Virgin, with the Heavenly Hosts magnify thee.[^299]

*Troparion:*

Exalt the royal horn, O Most Pure One: grant strength unto the faithful against the heathens, and establish the scepters of the Tsardom.

[f. 194rA] *Troparion:*

Cast down the haughty, and vanquish the armies of foreigners that desire wars, O Pure Virgin, and free thy servants unharmed from them, O Maiden. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O sole champion of Orthodox Christians who glorify thee: by thy prayers put swiftly to shame the armed Hagarenes.[^300] Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Instruct, O Virgin, the companies of the faithful, strengthening them against enemies visible and invisible; and save them from every affliction.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after Our Father: Troparia, and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON TUESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 8)

*On Tuesday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

Having traversed the water as upon dry land, and having escaped from the evil of Egypt, the Israelite cried aloud: Let us sing unto our Redeemer and God.[^301]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Holden by many temptations, unto thee do I flee, seeking salvation: O Mother of the Word and Virgin, save me from cruel and grievous things.

*Troparion:*

The assaults of passions disturb me, and many sorrows fill my soul; bring peace, O Maiden, by the calm of thy Son and God, O All-immaculate One. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Thee who didst give birth unto the Savior and God do I entreat, O Virgin: deliver me from perils; for unto thee do I now flee, reaching out with soul and mind. Both now:

[f. 195rA] *Theotokion:*

Diseased in body and in soul, vouchsafe me divine visitation and providence from thee, O only Mother of God, as thou art good and the Parent of the Good One.

*Second Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

Let us sing unto the Lord, Who led His people through the Red Sea, a song of victory, for He hath been glorified.[^302]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Rejoice, deliverance from the ancient curse, and fountain of blessing! Rejoice, Mother of Life, destruction of Hades, slaying of death! Rejoice, loosing of sorrow, spacious receptacle of joy! Rejoice, O all-hymned Theotokos!

*Troparion:*

Chariot of the Word, noetic and animate, rejoice, O All-immaculate One! Rejoice, virginal chariot of many names, chariot prefigured by myriads! Rejoice, higher than the Cherubim of the Theophany, and surpassing the Seraphim! Glory:

*Troparion:*

Most hallowed above the Noetic Powers, rejoice, O Bride of God! Rejoice, higher than all created nature! Rejoice, Palace of God! Rejoice, Fiery Throne!

[f. 195vA] *Troparion:*

Rejoice, many-lighted Lampstand! Rejoice, Sovereign Lady celebrated by myriads! Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, O undefiled one, who didst bud forth the Rod from the Root of Jesse; rejoice, thou who of old didst prefigure the budding of Aaron's rod, mysteriously and profoundly: for as that rod budded forth flower and almond, so hast thou blossomed forth Christ.[^303]

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

## ON WEDNESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 8)

*On Wednesday at Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

Armed Pharaoh did the wonderworking staff of Moses drown of old, striking the sea crosswise and dividing it; but it saved Israel fleeing on foot, chanting a song unto God.[^330]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Thou hast given birth unto the incarnate Word of God, Who was bodiless before, beyond nature, O All-immaculate Theotokos and Virgin, Who lived in the world as the God-man. Therefore we all glorify thee, who art our protection after God.

*Troparion:*

Adorned with diverse colors,[^331] O woman full of the grace of God, thou hast given birth beyond mind and word unto the Father's Word, Who became flesh through unutterable tender mercy, O blessed one, having remained an undefiled Virgin. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Having sprouted, O pure one, from the human root,[^332] thou hast given birth beyond mind and understanding unto the King Christ, the Word of God, incarnate of thy pure blood, shown forth in two natures, yet single in hypostasis. Both now:

[f. 202vA] *Theotokion:*

Whosoever proclaims thee to be the Theotokos, O Most Pure One, bears victory over great heresies: for thou, O Mother of God, hast inchangeably given birth in the flesh unto the Word of God, Who is co-eternal, higher than all creation.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who in the beginning didst establish the heavens in understanding, and didst found the earth upon the waters: establish me, O Christ, upon the rock of the Church; for there is none holy save Thee, O Lover of mankind.[^333]

*Troparion:*

God dwelt in thy holy womb and became incarnate, as He alone knows, O Mother of God, and He saved us by His life-bearing Passion; therefore do we glorify thee as the door of salvation.

*Troparion:*

Having become incarnate on earth, the supremely divine One, Who breathed the breath of life into all men, lived among men, having come forth from thy womb without seed; therefore all we the faithful in songs bless thee, O Pure One. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Having conceived in thy womb without wedlock Him Who was born of the Father before all ages, O Virgin, thou hast brought forth[^334] for us at once God and man, perfect in both natures and indivisible. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

All of us know thee, O Pure One, as the Rod that budded the Flower of incorruption, Christ, and as the golden Censer, bearing in thine hand the Coal of divine nature, O Virgin blessed of God.[^335]

[f. 203rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou art my strength, O Lord, yea, Thou art my power; Thou art my God, Thou art my rejoicing, Who, without leaving the Father’s bosom, hast visited our poverty in Thy mercy. Therefore with the Prophet Habakkuk I cry: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind![^336]

*Troparion:*

The Ark that received the Law written by God prefigured from afar the Life-originating Word in thy womb, unutterably conceived, O All-immaculate One, richly nourishing the souls of those who chant: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Thou alone, O Mother of God, didst become the true cause of salvation, lending flesh from thy most pure blood unto the Creator and Builder, whereby the gates of death were crushed, and life was bestowed upon men. Glory:

*Troparion:*

That He might in every way renew the image buried by passions, the Wealth of grace dwelt in the Virgin's womb, whereof Thou, the Wisdom of God, didst build Thyself a temple; wherein having lived among men, O Compassionate One, Thou hast saved the ends of the world. Both now:

[f. 203vA] *Theotokion:*

After God we have acquired thee as protectress, O All-immaculate One: for thou didst become the Mother of God, the Creator and Builder, Who took our form, and saved it from corruption and perils, and glorified it with divine glory.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy countenance, O Never-setting Light, and an alien darkness hath covered me, a wretch? But turn me back, and guide my ways unto the light of Thy commandments, I pray Thee.[^337]

*Troparion:*

As having conceived God, the Builder, Creator, and Lord, O Most Pure One, all generations of men call thee blessed, and the bodiless noetic leaders of the ranks glorify thee as Mother unto God.

*Troparion:*

The hosts of the heavenly Powers bless thee, O Most Pure One: for through thee alone earthly beings were united with heavenly beings; we hymn thy Childbearing. Glory:

*Troparion:*

In an ineffable manner, O Virgin, the Word dwelt in thee and became man, He Who shone forth from the Father before the ages, and He delivers men from corruption, leading them unto incorruptible life, as the only compassionate One. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou hast adorned thyself with virginal beauties, O Most Pure Virgin Maiden, and hast loosed the ugly shame of the first Eve by giving birth unto Christ, [f. 204rA] Who grants the garment of immortality unto those who chant unto thee.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine iniquities, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and do Thou hear me, O God of my salvation.[^338]

*Troparion:*

He Who upholds all things by His almighty power is clothed from thee in the weakness of the flesh, O All-immaculate Virgin, for the benefaction of men, as the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

The all-effecting Spirit came upon thee, O All-immaculate One, and the Word of God dwelt in thee, and became flesh ineffably, remaining unchanging. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Supremely glorious is the miracle of thy compassions: show compassion upon me, O Most Pure Theotokos, and appear unto me as a fervent helper and salvation in temptations. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

A dwelling-place of God, divine in truth and most honorable, we piously hymn thee, O Most Pure Mary: for thou wast accounted worthy to contain God, Whom nothing can contain and Who is unsearchable.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

*Troparion:*

Grant me easily, O Theotokos, to cross the surge of this life, and temptations, and the onset of passions, calming them as good, and guiding me unto the virtue of the heavenly journey: that as my Benefactress I may unceasingly magnify thee. Glory:

[f. 206rA] *Troparion:*

O Pure and most glorious Mother of God, save from temptations those who chant unto thee with love, graciously fulfilling their supplication: for having given birth unto God, whatsoever thou desirest to do thou doest without hindrance, and hast power, O Virgin; therefore we all magnify thee. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Show forth the precious Church triumphing over youthful heresies,[^343] which Christ Thy Son, O Full of Grace, acquired as good with His own precious Blood; and save her from perils and from the oppression that holds us fast, O Sovereign Lady.

*Theotokion:*

Upon thee have I placed all my hope, O Virgin Mother: preserve my soul, thou who didst give birth unto God, my Savior.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Why hast Thou cast me off from Thy countenance, O Never-setting Light, and an alien darkness hath covered me, a wretch? But turn me back, and guide my ways unto the light of Thy commandments, I pray Thee.[^348]

*Troparion:*

Heal the wholly incurable passions of my soul, O Maiden, and rekindle my lamp that hath been extinguished through sloth, and guide me unto the way of repentance, O Virgin, that in faith and love I may glorify thee.

*Troparion:*

My shameful deeds condemn me before the trial, O thou who hast been gladdened of God, and I am uniquely condemned; but stand before me, as thou art the Protectress of all, and deliver me from cruel condemnation. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Deliver me from captivity, and from the soul-corrupting turmoil that holds me fast, and from cruel passions that unceasingly slay me, O Most Pure Maiden, protectress of sinners, ready helper of men. Both now:

[f. 208rA] *Theotokion:*

Set me upright, O Most Pure One, who am wholly slain by the sting of death through transgression, thou who didst conceive the Life for the world, the Redeemer and King; and guide me unto the light.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine iniquities, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray Thee; for unto Thee have I cried, and do Thou hear me, O God of my salvation.[^349]

*Troparion:*

Make me alive, who am slain by many transgressions, O Most Pure Virgin Theotokos, who didst unutterably give birth unto the Life of men; and guide me to do the Lord's will.

*Troparion:*

We all the faithful have acquired thee as protectress and rampart, being ever submerged in the depth of evils, turmoils, and griefs, O Theotokos, only refuge of the faithful. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Thou who by thy holy budding didst wither the gardens of godlessness, O All-immaculate One: consume the enemy's malice that ever grows within me, O Most Pure One. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Sanctify my mind and enlighten my heart, O Holy Mother of God, and change the evils that hold me fast, that I may glorify thee as my firm protectress.[^350]

O marvel of wonder! O fearful mystery! cried the Ewe-lamb, beholding her Son upon the Tree of the Cross [f. 208vA] stretched out: What is this, O immortal Word of God! How art Thou seen dead! And Thou shakest the earth, as Almighty: Yet do I hymn Thy fearful and divine condescension.[^351]

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Troparion:*

*Theotokion:*

Have compassion and save me, O Virgin, who didst give birth unto the compassionate Word of God; and with the light that is in thee illuminate my soul, and deliver me eternally from cruel craftiness.

## ON FRIDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 8)

*On Friday at Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos:*

Let us sing unto the Lord, Who led His people through the Red Sea; for He alone hath been gloriously glorified.[^358]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Put to death the wisdom of my flesh,[^359] O Most Pure Virgin who didst give birth unto Life; and deliver me from all torment.

*Troparion:*

With the supremely bright ray of Him Who shone forth from thee, O Virgin, enlighten the pupils of my soul, that I may glorify thee. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Pray unto the Master, O only Holy Theotokos, that on the day of judgment I may obtain salvation and divine brightness. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

O Most Pure Theotokos, assuage the pains of my heart, thou who didst without travail give birth unto Christ beyond mind and word.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou art the confirmation of those who flee unto Thee, O Lord; Thou art the light of the darkened, and my spirit sings of Thee.[^360]

[f. 210vA] *Troparion:*

Deliver me by thy prayers, O Most Pure One, from the enemy's arrows, and from the assault of thoughts; spare my heart.

*Troparion:*

Heal the wounds of my soul, O All-immaculate One, and soothe the turmoil of my heart, O Mother of God, by thy prayers. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Calm the ceaseless rising of my thoughts, O Pure Sovereign Lady, and take away all sorrow from my soul, thou who didst give birth unto Joy. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Rejoice, salvation of those who flee unto thee, O Virgin Mother of Christ; rejoice, praise of Apostles and Martyrs.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation; I have understood Thy works, and I have glorified Thy Divinity.[^361]

*Troparion:*

Heal the wounds of my soul, O Word of God, Who wast wounded for my sake as the Lover of mankind; and enlighten the darkness of my mind.[^362]

*Troparion:*

The slumber of my sloth and the sleep of sin have overtaken me, O Virgin; but by thy vigilant prayer, raise me up unto repentance. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O fountain that didst pour forth the water of forgiveness, O All-immaculate One: water my heart that is dried up by all manner of transgressions. Both now:

[f. 211rA] *Theotokion:*

O Most Pure Bride of God, Sovereign Lady of the world: save me, changing what is from ages past, and driving away the turmoil of passions.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Early in the morning we cry unto Thee, O Lord, save us: for Thou art our God, and beside Thee we know no other.[^363]

*Troparion:*

Look down, O Most Pure One, and hear my voice, and cleanse all mine iniquities, O Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

Thou who didst give birth unto the Never-setting Sun, O Theotokos: wholly enlighten me who am darkened by passions, that I may glorify and praise thee, O All-immaculate One. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Have compassion upon my soul, O Most Pure Maiden, and deliver it from judgment and eternal torment. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Heal my diseased soul, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, who didst give birth unto Him Who takes away the diseases of all.[^364]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The abyss of sins and tempest of transgressions troubles me, and thrusts me into the depth of sudden despair; but stretch forth unto me Thy mighty hand as unto Peter, O Helmsman, and save me.[^365]

*Troparion:*

Put to death, O Theotokos, the disorderly motions of my flesh, and me who am slain by malice do thou make alive by thy living Childbearing, that I may glorify thee as the cause of human renewal.

[f. 211vA] *Troparion:*

Having given birth unto the Abyss of compassions, O Virgin Theotokos, save my soul sorrowful from this life, and open unto me the gates of spiritual joy; for upon thee alone have I placed my hope. Glory:

*Troparion:*

That I may joyfully hymn thy majesties, O All-pure Virgin, and the great grace of thy wonders, change thou by thy prayers the unruly thoughts that ever torment me. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou hast given birth beyond nature unto Him Who is inseparable from the Father's bosom, and Who through thee conversed with men, O Virgin; but as thou art the boast and confirmation of us all, O Theotokos, have compassion upon those who flee unto thee.

*Lord, have mercy (3). Glory: Both now:*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*

The Word of the Father came down upon earth, and a radiant Angel said unto the Theotokos: Rejoice, blessed one, who alone hast preserved the Bridal Chamber, having received the conception of the Pre-eternal God and Lord, that as God He might save the human race from deception.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Hebrew children in the furnace boldly trampled upon the flame, and changing the fire into dew, they chanted: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, unto the ages.[^366]

*Troparion:*

Woe is me, how shall I escape the torments awaiting me, having lived evilly here! How shall I appear, wretched one, before the fearful Judge; [f. 212rA] but do thou, O Mother of God and Sovereign Lady, be my helper then.

*Troparion:*

Quench the flame of my passions, and calm the tempest of my heart, O pure Mother of God; and deliver me, O All-pure One, from the torment of demons and from eternal fire. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Behold my affliction, O Virgin, which the multitude of mine evils hath brought on, and grant me rest before my departure, imploring thy Son by thy maternal prayers. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

The flame of the passions of my heart quench with thy dew, O Virgin, and snatch me, O Maiden, from the fearful fire, and from eternal judgment, and from demonic torment.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Bless the Creator Father, O children equal in number to the Trinity; praise God, the Word Who descended and transformed the fire into dew; and highly exalt throughout the ages the All-Holy Spirit Who gives life unto all.[^367]

*Troparion:*

Put to death, O Virgin, all our bodily motions, who by thy life-bearing Childbearing hast made dead the serpent;[^368] and pray that we may obtain the life that grows not old, that we may hymn thee unto the ages.

*Troparion:*

Thee do I ever call upon for help, O Mother of God; put me not to shame, who have acquired shameful deeds, [f. 212vA] but have compassion upon me, O Most Pure One, and snatch me from the flame, and deliver me from eternal torments. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O full of grace, be revealed as my Helper in the distress of evils, delivering me from the assaults of demonic snares, that I may ever bless thee, O Theotokos, Protectress of all. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

O Most Pure Maiden, Boast of the Apostles, Glory of the Martyrs: make me a partaker of eternal glory as I cry: Bless the All-Holy Spirit unto all the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Thou hast surpassed the ordinances of nature, having conceived the Creator and Lord, and hast become the door of salvation unto the world; therefore, O Theotokos, we unceasingly magnify thee.[^369]

*Troparion:*

By thy never-waning Light, O door of the light, drive away the grievous darkness of my soul, and show unto me the radiant paths of repentance, that walking in them, I may escape the murk of sin and unceasingly magnify thee.

*Troparion:*

Cleanse Thy servants, O Good One, and grant remission of transgressions, and deliver us from the eternal flame, and make us partakers of Thy Kingdom, [f. 213rA] O Word of God, for in desiring to save the human race, Thou didst endure the Cross. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Grant me easily, O Theotokos, to pass through the course of this life, calming as a good one the risings of temptations and passions, and guiding me unto the virtue of the heavenly Kingdom and divine rest, that being saved, I may glorify thee. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Heal my soul, diseased with grievous passion, as a merciful one, O thou who didst give birth unto the most good God, and deliver me from those who afflict me again, O Most Pure One, and from those who abide in cruel malice, that being saved, I may fervently magnify thee who hast magnified our race.[^370]

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after Our Father:*

*and straightway we say these Troparia:*
