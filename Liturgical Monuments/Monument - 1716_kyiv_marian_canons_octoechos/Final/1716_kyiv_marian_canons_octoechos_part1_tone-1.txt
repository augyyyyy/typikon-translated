# TONE 1

## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 1)

[f. 1rA] *On Saturday at Small Compline,*[^8]

> **Priest:** "Blessed is our God..."

*Heavenly King... Trisagion, and after Our Father... Lord, have mercy (12 times). Glory... Both now... Come, let us worship (3 times). Psalm 50: Have mercy on me, O God... Psalm 69: O God, attend unto my help... Psalm 142: O Lord, hear my prayer... After this: Glory to God in the highest... Vouchsafe, O Lord, this night... Then: I believe in one God...*

*After this, we chant the Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

Delivered from bitter bondage, Israel passed through the impassable as upon dry land; beholding the enemy drowned, in gladness he sings a hymn unto God Who works wonders with a high arm, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The angelic hierarchies were struck with awe in fear, O Queen of all, praising thee; and the great intelligences sing praise because of thy grace, as to the Mother of the Creator; for having given birth to Christ, Thou hast surpassed every order of praise.

*Troparion:*

Tormented by cruel temptations, and troubled by enemies, wretch that I am, I cry out with weeping: Give ear, [f. 1vA] stretch forth thy most rich hand unto me, delivering me; and vouchsafe me to live without danger through thy prayers.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Heal the secret transgressions of my soul with the medicine of compassion, and calm the surging of the flesh, O Theotokos; and thrust the spears and arrows of the enemies firmly into their own hearts.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The virginal womb, having given birth to Christ, destroyed the ancient man-slaying pasture. Therefore all creation rejoices with me, O Most Pure, seeing that it is brought to life; and with one accord it hymns thy Son and God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Let not the mortal boast in his own wisdom, nor in his wealth; but in the faith of the Lord, crying out with Orthodox faith unto Christ God, and singing continually: Establish me, O Master, upon the rock of Thy commandments.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Jacob the great, once sleeping on the way, marveled as he beheld angels descending from above upon the earth by a ladder, O Virgin; and awaking, he prefigured thee as the heavenly Gate.

*Troparion:*

Cast down into trials by a heavy weight, wretch that I am, and tormented by perilous afflictions: Woe is me, I cry out! O thou who didst bear God, and didst exalt our horn, save me by thy prayers.

*Glory...*

[f. 2rA] *Troparion:*

Having stretched forth Thy mighty hand from heaven, O King of all, Christ my Jesus! Lay beneath the feet of those who faithfully proclaim the Theotokos as Thy Mother the heads of enemies visible and invisible.[^9]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Isaiah of old, purified by the coal of the Spirit, openly cried out that a Son should be born of thy womb, O most rich Virgin; Whom in these latter times thou hast borne without man for my sake.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Habakkuk of old heard Thy marvelous report, O Christ, and in fear cried out: God shall come from the south, and the Holy One from the dense, overshadowing mountain, to save His anointed ones: glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

As one beautiful and all-honorable among women, rising like an intertwined bond out of the wilderness, and bearing on her arms Christ the Branch, O Mother of God, the leader of the world prefigured thee, crying out: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Incline thine ear unto me, O Good One, and behold mine affliction, and the multiplying of my troubles; for stretching forth the eye of my soul unto thee, O Sovereign Lady, and bowing my knees with weeping, I now pray, crying out: Still for me the turmoil of temptations.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee to be an invincible rampart, drawn by thy mercy, I thy servant now flee unto thee:

[f. 2vA] and I reckon the arrows of the enemy to be ineffectual like those of infants, O most rich Lady! Therefore, rejoicing, I cry out: Glory to thy childbirth, O Mother of God!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The power of the Highest overshadowed thee, O Virgin, by the descent of the Divine Spirit; and then, beyond nature, the Lord of all, having quickened flesh and soul, united them unto Himself, remaining in the same nature.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Shine forth Thy never-setting light, O Christ, in the hearts of those who faithfully hymn Thee; grant us Thy peace, which surpasses understanding: that running from the night of ignorance unto the day of Thy commandments, we may glorify Thee, O Lover of mankind.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Daniel of old, foreseeing thee as a divine unhewn mountain, O All-Hymned, cried out clearly: A stone was cut from thee without hands, O Theotokos, Christ the Savior of the world; Whom the faithful now honor, praising thee, O Bride of God.

*Troparion:*

Falling into many temptations, wretch that I am, praying with anguish and weeping of heart, shamelessly, I thy servant cry out: Deliver me, O Birthgiver of God, from the troubles that encompass my lowliness, and fill me with gladness.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Calm the fierce tempest of my passions by thy mighty prayer, O Good One, who didst bear Christ apart from [f. 3rA] passions; that living now in quietness of soul for the remainder of my life, I may hymn thee in songs.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Tell us, how dost thou carry God in thine arms? And how dost thou suckle Him Who holds all things in His hand, O all-blessed Virgin? "I remain pure" (she said) "even after childbirth, having given birth to Christ God, taking away the debt of Adam and the ancestral mother."

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

I am held fast by measureless passions, and sin is cast upon me like a great whale; but bring me up from corruption, O Christ God, as of old Thou didst Jonah, and by Thy holiness grant me dispassion, that with the voice of praise I may sacrifice unto Thee in a true spirit.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Not parting from the bosom of the Father, the Son before all ages was in the maternal bosom; and in these latter times, He Who is before all ages with the Father came forth from the womb of the Virgin, leading all unto immortal life by ineffable grace.

*Troparion:*

Bound with heavy chains by malice, cast down into the pit of hell, alas for me, I am fallen! Stand before me, appearing from heaven, O pure Maid of God, raising me up by thy prayers, I who am thy servant; and give a hand of help unto him who sings [f. 3vA] thy divine childbirth.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Cast into the pit of destruction, wretch that I am, I have fallen, and many beasts surround me; but, O Sovereign Lady, by thy prayers turn the stones away, and preserve thy servant unharmed; for thou didst bear in thy womb Christ the cornerstone.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Of old the choir of divine prophets proclaimed the images of thy childbirth, O Virgin: calling thee a radiant cloud, a lampstand, a jar of manna, a table, a heavenly mountain, a chamber, an ark, a door, a throne, a palace, a rod, and a paradise, as having given birth to Christ.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

We all know thee as the Mother of God, who truly appeared as a virgin even after childbirth, fleeing with love unto thy goodness; for we sinners have thee as a protectress, and in temptations we have obtained salvation in thee, the only All-Immaculate One.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The holy youths having shown themselves of old as for God in godliness, passed through the inconstant furnace flame as in a bridal chamber, singing with one accord the hymn: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The Pre-Eternal One, keeping thy clean signs and whole through thine impassable gates, O Queen of all, [f. 4rA] preserved thee intact: pure even after childbirth. Therefore we cry out: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Cast into a furnace burning sevenfold with soul-destroying troubles, I have been scorched; but do thou thyself, O Sovereign Lady, Good One, rain down dew upon my horn by thy prayers, that I may cry out: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

I have grown old in passions, and worn away by unceasing assaults, and having reached the setting of my life, devoid of virtues, consumed by sloth, I cry unto thee, O Sovereign Lady: Comfort of mortals, have mercy on me!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Serving the Trinity in unity in the Orthodox faith, and proclaiming thee in songs, O all-pure Mother, as having given birth to God in the flesh, we mortals divinely sing: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The furnace moist with dew showed of old the image of a wonder beyond nature, for the fire did not consume the young children, displaying the seedless, divine birth-giving of Christ from the Virgin; therefore, singing, let us sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all forever!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The co-essential Word prefigured thy childbirth to the Priest, O Virgin; for truly thou didst bear the Word of God, and the womb was not opened, O Virgin, through which passed [f. 4vA] God. Rejoicing in thee as our due, O Mother of God, with one accord we hymn and exalt thee, O Pure One, unto all ages.

*Troparion:*

Burn up, O All-Pure One, with divine fire the thorns that lie inert in my soul, and by thy prayers set me upon virtue, to bear fruit for Christ; for from thee the ever-living Flower has blossomed, adorning all creation. Therefore we honor thee, the pure Birthgiver of God, unto all ages.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Grant me speedily painless healing in cruel afflictions, O Birthgiver of God; for I have fallen into sorrows and troubles, and wretch that I am, I invoke thy swift protection with weeping. Therefore, O All-Pure One, hasten to rescue me, and save me from every torment, that I may sing of thy good childbirth.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Aaron's rod, which budded of old, prefigured thee, O Virgin; for thou alone didst bring forth an offshoot without man, having received the heavenly rain into thy womb. Therefore rejoicing in thee, O Theotokos, as our due, we all sing with one accord and exalt thee unto all ages.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The mystery of the Virgin is ineffable: for she was revealed as heaven, the Cherubic Throne, and the light-bearing bridal chamber of Christ God the Almighty; her do we all piously magnify as the Theotokos.

[f. 5rA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Marvelous is the mystery of the Virgin: for He Whom the heavens above could not contain, she contained within her womb. Therefore, coming together, we bless her, and faithfully magnifying her, we magnify her.

*Troparion:*

Praising thee alone as higher than the heavens, the Divine Dawn, O Immaculate One, the Cherubic Throne and Bridal Chamber, and Holy Couch, we mortals magnify Christ our God, Whom thou didst bear from pure loins.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Many sorrows have defiled me, and evil temptations now assaulting me have painfully cast me into a pit of sins that have come upon me; therefore I pray thee in the bitterness of my soul, O Most Pure Theotokos, that I may find deliverance.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Reconcile the world, O Christ, through the prayers of the pure Maid of God, casting down the enemy's power beneath the feet of the King of the faithful; and establishing an ineffable peace for them, preserve it unto ages.

*Then: It is truly meet... Trisagion, and after Our Father... Hypakoe of the Tone. Lord, have mercy (40 times). Prayer: Thou Who at every time... Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... More honorable than the Cherubim... In the name of the Lord, Father, bless. Then the Prayer: O Spotless, undefiled... and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 1)

[f. 5vA] *On Sunday at Small Compline,*[^10]

*As was indicated on Saturday at Small Compline.*

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

Thy victorious right hand has been divinely glorified in power; for it, O Immortal One, as all-powerful, shattered the adversaries, renewing for the Israelites a path through the deep.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Ineffably thou didst conceive God, and beyond nature didst bear Him, O All-Pure Sovereign Lady, Who delivered mortals from transgressions. Therefore I pray thee: deliver me from my many transgressions.

*Troparion:*

I am submerged in a gulf of sins and unnumbered troubles and sorrows; but by thy saving guidance and prayer, O All-Immaculate One, direct me unto the divine stillness of repentance.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Protectress of all my life, deliver me from many troubles, that I may sing unto thee a gracious voice, O pure Theotokos, and snatch me from future torment.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Still the assault of circumstances and the storm of temptations, O All-Immaculate Mother of the Deliverer, and [f. 6rA] Creator and our God, granting me the grace of salvation and purification.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human nature, and didst mercifully clothe Thyself therein, gird me with power from on high to sing unto Thee, O Holy One: The animate Church of Thine ineffable glory, O Lover of mankind.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The sight of thy form, O Pure One, is sweet unto angels, saving unto mortals, and terrible unto spirits of malice; which honoring with faith and kissing, O Theotokos, we enlighten our soul.

*Troparion:*

Not with thy virginal blood did the Creator and Lord clothe His flesh; pray Him, O All-Immaculate One, to have compassion upon me who am corrupted by boundless deeds, according to His ineffable mercy.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having acquired thee as the Guardian of my life, an invincible rampart, a shelter of resting, an unconquered protectress, leading unto God, and bestowing eternal life: vouchsafe me to find this and glory, O All-Immaculate One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

A seedless conception, O pure one, and an ineffable childbirth; for God was He Who was born, calling back the fall of human nature. Therefore we hymn thee as the true and real Theotokos.

[f. 6vA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Habakkuk perceiving with foresighted eyes the mountain overshadowed by divine grace, proclaimed that the Holy One of Israel was to come forth from thee, for our salvation and renewal.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

An unrestrained impulse draws me unto evil by the working of the enemy and wicked habit; help me, O Sovereign Lady, lest the all-destroying one attack me utterly, preventing me by death from repentance.

*Troparion:*

I have spent all the time of my life in evil lusts and cruel troubles, and now the doers thereof lead my humble soul; but do thou, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, help me.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Hear my painful voice, O All-Immaculate One, which from the depth of my soul I bring forth with sighing, and grant me remission of the debts which I, wretch that I am, have committed in mind, being rightly devoid of understanding.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

O Sovereign Lady of the world, Theotokos, help me; for the all-wicked enemy desires vehemently to swallow me up by cruel memories and thoughts which separate me from God; therefore leave me not, neither despise me.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst enlighten the ends of the world with the radiance of Thy coming, O Christ, and didst make them resound by Thy Cross: enlighten with the light of Thy divine knowledge the hearts of those who hymn Thee in the Orthodox faith.

[f. 7rA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Lo, I have acquired help ready unto salvation; therefore I fall down before thee and cry with tears: O Theotokos, deliver me from the snares of the alien, from the examination that is to come.

*Troparion:*

Thou art my strength, thou art my boast and rejoicing, and my guardian, protection and refuge, and invincible defender, O All-Pure Theotokos, Virgin; therefore save thy servant.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Cleanse my wretched soul defiled by passions with the sprinkling of thy divine mercy, O All-Pure Theotokos, granting it the radiant garment of thy salvation.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

In my life thou art my steadfast guidance, delivering me from the tempest of many circumstances; and at my departure, I pray thee, stand by me, saving me, O Mother of Christ God.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The lowest abyss has surrounded us, there is none to deliver, we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter; save Thy people, O our God, for Thou art the strength of the weak and their restoration.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

O all-honorable, all-pure Birthgiver of God, stretch forth thy hand unto me, and snatch me from the depth of evils which I, all-wretched, have brought upon my passionate soul by my folly.

[f. 7vA] Arise, O Pure One, unto the help of thy servant who has all his hope after God in thine unconquered protection, lest the enemy prevail, seize and destroy me.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Healer of the sick, resurrection of the fallen, cleansing of sinners, O Theotokos, I pray thee, falling down with tears, and cry: As thou art powerful, do thou thyself save me who am perishing.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Have mercy, O Pure One, upon my humble soul, anticipate and snatch it away, rescuing it from the wicked one; for he hastens to cast it into the abyss for the sake of the evils which my great sloth has wrought.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

*Tone 1.*

*Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й Сп҃се):*

We all have acquired thee as a protectress before God, O Pure One, and we flee unto thy holy Church, asking help from thee continually, O Virgin; deliver therefore those who bless thee from the surrounding malice, and snatch them from the dreadful condemnation.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

We the faithful understand thee, O Theotokos, to be a spiritual furnace; for as the Most Exalted One saved the three youths, so has He Who is praised and supremely glorified, the God of our fathers, renewed the whole of me, man, in thy womb.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

[f. 8rA] *Troparion:*

Thou wast the divine Tabernacle, O Sovereign Lady: for having conceived in thy womb and borne Him in the flesh, thou only undefiled one, therefore deliver us from passions, diseases, sorrows, and debts; for thou hast great strength and power.

*Troparion:*

Still the unbearable sickness of my all-wretched soul, O Pure One, granting me true joy, O Sovereign Lady, and calming by thy prayers the wave of my many transgressions; for thou art my refuge and deliverance.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

I have placed all the hope of my salvation in thee, for I have acquired thee as help, an unconquerable tower, a foundation, and an assurance of steadfastness; and through thee, O Pure One, I hope to obtain mercy, O Sovereign Lady.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou wast the Orient of the Sun of glory, O Sovereign Lady, Pure One; for by His appearing through thee He delivered all from darkness, ignorance, and foul transgressions. Therefore I cry unto thee: Deliver me from the outer darkness.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

In the furnace the children of Israel, as in a crucible, shone with the beauty of godliness purer than gold, saying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, sing and exalt Him above all forever.

*(Manuscript inscription at foot: "Belongs to the library of the Trinity-St. Sergius Lavra. In the year 1859.")*[^11]

[f. 8vA] *Troparion:*

The turmoils of passions cruelly afflict my soul; but having borne the Cause and Giver of peace and stillness, O Pure Theotokos, keeping me in a peaceful disposition, fill me with serene joy and gladness.

*Troparion:*

Thou hast been shown unto us as the Mediatress of salvation, O Theotokos, who didst bear the Savior and the Good of all things; therefore I pray thee, vouchsafe salvation unto my humble soul, that I may faithfully hymn thee unto all ages.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having ineffably conceived the Creator of all and God: save me from every temptation, O Maiden, and grant me to cry continually: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, sing and exalt Him above all forever.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear the Creator and God of all, O Virgin Mother; pray Him to grant purification of transgressions, deliverance from sorrows and temptations, and freedom from eternal fire unto those who unceasingly glorify thy glory.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The burning bush showed the image of thy pure childbirth unburnt; and now we pray thee to quench the furnace of temptations raging against us, that we may unceasingly magnify thee, O Theotokos.

[f. 9rA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

O all-immaculate Mother of God, open unto me thy merciful womb, and deliver me from the jaws of the intelligible wolf who seeks to destroy and corrupt me; have mercy, I pray thee: turn not away thy servant put to shame.

*Troparion:*

Be thou the Protectress and firm defense of all my life; and deliver me from many temptations, sorrows, malicious tongues of enemies, and eternal fire, O All-Immaculate One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou alone art the visitation of the sick, thou alone art the restoration of the fallen, O Sovereign Lady, thou alone art the departure leading unto God, thou alone art the Mediatress of eternal blessings: have mercy upon me who alone have sinned more than all.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Receive my tearful prayer now, and grant me remission of transgressions and of many evils which I have done, O All-Hymned Lady Theotokos, that I may not perish utterly from boundless sin.

*Then: It is truly meet... and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 1)

[f. 9vA] *On Monday at Small Compline,*[^12]

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

Let us all sing a song of victory unto God Who wrought marvelous wonders with a high arm, and saved Israel, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

All-pure cleansing, rejoice, O Virgin, adornment of angels and receptacle of the Spirit, immaculate Bride of the unoriginate Father, and Mother of the Word of God!

*Troparion:*

Thou art noble as the King's daughter, having blossomed forth, O Lady Theotokos, and having borne the King and God in the flesh, and reigning with Him.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

David the singer hymns thy childbirth, and Isaiah supremely praises thy most pure womb, O all-immaculate Mary; and the Christian people glorify thy childbirth.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The Church proclaims thy childbirth, O Virgin Bride of God, with Orthodox teachings and divine deeds, for it venerates the icon of the incarnation of thy Son.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

The stone which the builders rejected has become the head of the corner; this is the stone whereon Christ has established His Church, which He redeemed from the nations.

[f. 10rA] *Troparion:*

Having the glory of virginity, thou hast been clothed with the grace of the Holy Spirit, O All-Pure Mary, having appeared unto God as a new and hidden mystery: for how dost thou remain a virgin having borne God in the flesh?

*Troparion:*

The deed which Thou hast wrought, O Lord, who shall declare? For Thou Who didst condemn woman to bear children in sorrows hast appeared as her Son; she rejoices, woman's fallen nature, in Thee, O God, because of the Theotokos.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The storm of transgressions is loosed for the world through thee, O All-Pure Mary, united unto God alone; therefore having thee as a harbor of salvation, we the faithful praise thee, singing thy greatness.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thy virginity was hymned by prophets of old, that thou shouldest give birth in time unto the Timeless One, the Word beyond word, One of the Trinity, Christ our God, Whose icons the Church honors, O pure Mother.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Foreseeing in the Spirit the incarnation of the Word, the Prophet Habakkuk proclaimed, crying out: When the years draw near, Thou shalt be known; when the time comes, Thou shalt appear; glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

The seven-branched mirror of the divine prophets hymns thy prefigurations in many ways; and we, having received the reality of the sign of thine exodus, seeing the thing itself, truly believe in thee as the Mother of God.

[f. 10vA] *Troparion:*

Worthily honoring thee in truth as the Mother of God, O pure Virgin, we glorify thee, bringing unto thee our own voice, unto her who was proclaimed in the Law and the Prophets: Rejoice, thou who art full of grace, the Lord is with thee!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O divine Mountain, which Habakkuk saw, clearly overshadowing with virtues, O Theotokos, budding forth in the world: through which, having departed of old through transgression, we have again drawn near unto God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Blessed is the tongue, O all-immaculate and pure one, which is vouchsafed to honor thy Childbirth faithfully with Orthodox teachings and deeds, manifesting the truth, all the prefigurations of the incarnation of thy Son.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Rising early from the night, we praise Thee, O Christ God, Who becamest poor for our sake, and suffered crucifixion and death in the flesh.

*Troparion:*

Hiddenly and mystically the unburnt Bush revealed thee unto Moses, O Sovereign Lady Theotokos; for the womb of material fire did not burn, containing the Godhead.

*Troparion:*

Hymning thee as the Mother of God the Word, O pure Theotokos, we offer thee a rational, blossoming gift, wherein the faithful rejoice.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Turn not away praises from defiled lips, O all-immaculate Theotokos; for there is none upon earth who [f. 11rA] dares to hymn thee worthily.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

He Who was born of the Virgin is One in two natures, as invisible God, and as man, having voluntarily become like unto men for our sake.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Thou didst save the Prophet from the whale, O Lover of mankind; and bring me up from the depth of transgressions, I pray Thee.

*Troparion:*

The Prophet sees thee, O Virgin, as an intelligible seven-flamed lampstand, clearly showing the workings of the Spirit gleaming within thee.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst appear, O Virgin, truly as a life-bearing tree, putting to death the deceitful serpent by thy fruit, as having given birth to Life, Christ God.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

A praise without sorrow is offered unto thee from defiled lips, O pure Mother of our God; but behold the longing of my soul, and accept this.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The lawless are shaken in the turmoil of bitter unbelief, whensoever they behold the assemblies of the Orthodox kissing thine icon, O Virgin.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

*Tone 1.*

*Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й Сп҃се):*

Pilot my wretched soul, O Pure One, and have compassion upon it, O All-Immaculate One, which has slipped into many [f. 11vA] transgressions into the abyss of destruction; and at the dreadful hour of death, do thou snatch me away from the slandering demons and from every torment.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

When the fire in the furnace preserved Thy children untouched, O Savior, then the three, as with one mouth, sang and blessed, saying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Thou didst appear adorned with virtues, O Bride of God, with the glory and honor of virginity; most comely indeed is thy beauty in magnificence; truly the Lord, the Lover, took thee unto Himself as a divine Mother.

*Troparion:*

Thou wast clearly the rod of uprightness, O Pure One, the most honorable scepter of the Kingdom. Therefore thou didst bud forth the Fruit, God the Word, which wonder Aaron's rod clearly prefigured from afar.

*Glory...*

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver us, O All-Hymned Virgin, from the assault of enemies visible and invisible, and preserve us, O Mother of God, who truly confess thee as the Theotokos in the Orthodox faith; for thou ever hast the power, as having given birth to the Creator of all.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Standing by the Cross and seeing her Son hanging in the flesh, the All-Immaculate One was consumed with sorrow in her inward parts, and overflowing with tears she cried aloud: O Child, truly ineffable is Thy tender mercy toward all men!

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

*Tone 1.*

*Podoben: "While the soldiers were guarding Thy tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й):*

Beholding the Lamb and Shepherd hanging dead upon the Tree, the Undefiled Ewe cried aloud weeping, exclaiming as a Mother: How shall I endure Thy condescension which is beyond word, O my Son, and Thy voluntary sufferings, O Most Good God?

[f. 23rA] *Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The youths nurtured in the faith of godliness took no heed of the ungodly command, and feared not the threat of fire, but standing in the midst of the flame they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

I cannot endure the assault and tumult of the demons, for the flame of carnal pleasure darkens my mind. But despise me not, O Most Holy Theotokos, who have placed my hope upon thee.

*Troparion:*

O Unwedded Virgin, All-Holy Sovereign Lady and Bride of God, having loosed the bonds of my transgressions by thy prayers, unite me to the yoke of Christ's love, that I may bring forth virtuous fruits.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou art the Protectress of all who are in the world, the Rampart of Christians, and a Refuge unashamed, O Pure Mother; therefore honoring thee in faith, we cry unto Christ: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Seeing her Son hanging upon the Cross, the All-Pure Virgin was filled with awe and wonder, and said: How shall I endure seeing Thee slain, the Prince of life and Giver of life!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

A great wonder was prefigured by the dew-dropping furnace in an image: as it received them, it did not burn the youths, even so neither did the Divine Fire burn the Virgin into whom He entered pure; therefore singing we cry aloud: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

[f. 23vA] *Troparion:*

Sovereign Lady of the world, Theotokos: bring up me who am utterly perished and sunken in the depth of evil mind, through carnal pleasures and attachment to worldly things, by thy tender mercy alone; for I have no hope of salvation whatsoever, being completely cast into despair, O Pure One.

*Troparion:*

Freely art thou the Savior of men, who didst bear God, O Ineffable Image! Thou art the Savior of the faithful, O Theotokos, and the Guide of the blind, and the Restoration of those who fall. Therefore praising thee, we cry unto Christ: Bless the Lord, all ye works, sing and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having acquired thee as a most firm rampart, we have placed the hope of our salvation upon thee, O Mother of God; but be thou a haven unto thy servants, and an insurmountable wall, and direct us unceasingly to sing: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Torn in maternal inward parts and filled with many tears, she who purely bore Thee, seeing Thee upon the Cross, cried aloud with inconsolable sighs: Having escaped travails at Thy Nativity, O my Son, now am I held fast by anguish, seeing Thine appearance dishonored!

[f. 24rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Ineffable is the Mystery of the Virgin: for she was shown to be the Heaven, the Cherubic Throne, and the Light-Bearing Bridal Chamber of Christ God the Almighty; whom in godliness as the Theotokos we all magnify.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Wretched as I am, I have defiled my soul with the passions of dishonor, and defiled my flesh with soul-destroying pleasures; but as thou art Pure and Undefiled, cleanse me by the multitude of thy mercies.

*Troparion:*

I have acquired no refuge besides thee, O Pure One; I know no other firm protection and shelter upon earth, O Sovereign Lady. Therefore I have fervently fled unto thee, asking through thee to receive deliverance from transgressions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Look down, O Pure One, now mercifully regarding thy servants from on high, preserving them in pious faith, and by thy prayers delivering us from all tribulations, who honor thee as the true and Pure Theotokos.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Knowing the seedless Birthgiving, when she beheld Thee, O Lover of mankind, hanging upon the Tree of the Cross, she cried aloud: O my Son and my All-Powerful God, desiring to save mankind, Thou hast now accepted the Crucifixion!

*Then: It is truly meet... Trisagion, and after Our Father... and the Dismissal.*

## ON FRIDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 1)

[f. 24vA] *On Friday at Small Compline,*[^16]

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 1. Heirmos:*

Let us all sing a song of victory unto God Who wrought marvelous wonders with a high arm, and saved Israel: for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Thou art a saving haven and protection unto those who call upon thee, O All-Pure Theotokos. Therefore I also call upon thee fervently from my soul: O Sovereign Lady, save me!

*Troparion:*

O Mary All-Pure, Mother of the Creator: heal my humble soul which has wasted away with passions and transgressions, as thou art acceptable unto God.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O Unwedded Sovereign Lady, depart not, praying ever unto my Creator and God on behalf of him who ever flees unto thy protection, that he may obtain mercy.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Animate Palace of the Womb, and Fire-Resembling Throne: together with the Holy Martyrs and the Apostles, pray Christ unceasingly to deliver us from tribulations.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

The Stone which the builders rejected, the same has become the head of the corner: this is the Rock whereon Christ established His Church, which He redeemed from the nations.

[f. 25rA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The darkness of transgressions holds fast my heart, O Sovereign Lady, and I dare not look up unto Heaven with mine eyes. Therefore I cry: Enlighten my mind, soul, and heart with the commandments of Christ!

*Troparion:*

I look upon thy divine Icon, and honor thee as the Sovereign Lady and All-Pure Mother, as the Archetype; this I kiss, and bow down before it, and praise it, knowing the honor of thee and of Him to be one.[^17]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Calm the cruel surges of the flesh, and quench the flame of passions, O Virgin, wherewith the cruel serpent weaves for me a multi-twisted snare of transgressions, desiring to snatch me away unto destruction.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having come forth naturally from thee, O Theotokos, the Word was wholly clothed in Adam; pray Him therefore to deliver us from passions, various tribulations, and eternal fire.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Foreseeing in the Spirit the Incarnation of the Word, the Prophet Habakkuk proclaimed, saying: When the years draw near, Thou shalt be known; when the time comes, Thou shalt appear; glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having given birth to the Creator and Maker of all, thou hast ineffable power and unconquerable strength, O Virgin, and thou savest those who come unto thee in faith. For this cause I cry unto thee: Sovereign Lady of the world, help me!

[f. 25vA] *Troparion:*

Cast down the warfare of invisible spiritual enemies which utterly assaults my wretched heart, O Sovereign Lady, granting divine stillness and peace, that I may joyfully hymn thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Hope of the hopeless, Helper of the poor, Consolation of the weeping, Cleansing of those who sin, Guide of the wandering, Healer of the sick, and Restoration of those who fall!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

O Pure and Blessed Virgin Mother, who alone didst change the curse of all: together with the Apostles, Martyrs, and Prophets, pray Him Who came forth from thy womb to save the souls of those who hymn thee.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Grant us Thy peace, O Son of God; for other than Thee we know no god, we name Thy Name, for Thou art the God of the living and of the dead.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Save me, O Sovereign Lady, from the depth of tribulations, sorrows, griefs, and bodily passions, and preserve my soul in divine stillness.

*Troparion:*

Thou hast been a fervent and firm defense unto me throughout all my life, O All-Immaculate One. Therefore I pray thee: even after death extend thy rich mercy unto me.

*Glory...*

[f. 26rA] *Troparion:*

O thou who didst give birth to the Creator and Redeemer of all, deliver me from bodily passions that burn my heart and draw me unto improper deeds.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having appeared as a spiritual place of sanctification, wholly sanctify me, O Sovereign Lady, and pray together with the Saints and Wise Apostles to save me, O Virgin.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Imitating the type of Jonah I cry: O Good One, free my life from corruption, and save me, O Savior of the world, who cry: Glory to Thee!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Thou dost preserve me while alive, delivering me from tribulations; do thou also, O Virgin Mother, translate me by thy tender mercy unto eternal life when I depart from the earth.

*Troparion:*

Thou art an indestructible wall, thou art a firm Protection, O Good Theotokos; thou art a mighty defense unto thy servant; and for this cause I ever call upon thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having acquired an abyss of mercy toward God, O Good Theotokos, despise not me who fervently flee unto thy protection and cry aloud: Have mercy on me, O Mother of the God of all!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Cast me not away, O Christ, abhor me not, O Savior; the Ewe that bore Thee in the flesh prays Thee together with Thine Apostles, Prophets, and Passion-Bearers.

[f. 26vA] *Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*

The Prophets foretold thee, O All-Pure Maiden, as the Cloud of the ever-existing Divine Light, O Virgin, and Ark, and Lampstand, and Jar, and Unhewn Mountain; for from thee without seed came forth in the latter times Christ our God, even as the Father was well pleased.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

When the fire was harmless unto Thy children in the furnace, O Savior, and harmed them not, then the three, as with one mouth, sang and blessed, saying: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Seeing me wretched with transgressions, O Pure One, and with bodily pleasures, and blown about toward the depth of destruction: let thy tender mercy hasten and deliver me, O All-Pure Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

A refuge and mighty protection, and deliverance unto all the faithful, O Theotokos: be thou my Guide unto the Creator, granting me deliverance from transgressions by thy tender compassion.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst destroy the curse, O Pure One, having given birth to Christ the Joy; do thou also loose the curse of sin which has settled upon me, by thy power, O All-Immaculate One, and grant me joy.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

We thy servants ever pray thee, O All-Pure and Spotless One, day and night praying with contrite mind: grant us deliverance from our transgressions by thy prayers.

[f. 27rA] *Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him at Whom angels and all hosts tremble as Creator and Lord, sing, ye priests; glorify, ye children; bless, ye peoples; and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Arise, anticipate and conquer the enemies who ever disturb and afflict us, manifesting thy power, O Holy Virgin, Helper of the world.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst save the world from death and corruption by thy divine Childbirth, O Good Virgin; now also by thy prayers snatch us away and save us who praise thee from passions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Great and ineffable is thy strength, O Virgin, having unconquerable divine compassions and tender mercy; therefore save us who truly call upon thee.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

As an animate Vineyard, O Virgin, thou didst put forth for us the ripe Cluster, which poured out the wine of remission and drove away the drunken stupor of sin.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The light-bearing cloud, wherein the Master of all descended from heaven like dew upon the fleece, and became incarnate for our sake, He Who is without beginning having become man: all of us magnify thee as the pure Mother of our God.

[f. 27vA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

In the sea of life, weighed down greatly with carnal pleasures, I fall down before thee and cry aloud: Have mercy on me who flee unto thee, O Sovereign Lady, and stretch out unto me a hand of salvation, delivering me from the depth of destruction.

*Troparion:*

Unto thee I confess my sins, O Virgin; before thy face I present my shame, and cry from my soul: Have mercy and cleanse me, O All-Pure One, for after God I have placed my steadfast hope upon thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thee who didst bear without seed the Creator and Master I call upon from my soul, and cry diligently: Save me from my secret transgressions, and deliver me from the unquenchable fire, O All-Pure and Good Theotokos.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Impassable Door, Gate of Paradise, Path of those who are being saved, Way of salvation: together with the Martyrs and Prophets, with the Righteous and the Venerable, and with the Divine Apostles, pray, O Pure One, that our souls may be saved!

*Then: It is truly meet... and the usual Dismissal.*

[^9]: Slavonic: *чувственныхъ и мысленныхъ* ("sensible and intelligible"), translated in the canonical register as "visible and invisible" in reference to demonic and bodily foes.
[^10]: Liturgical incipit for the Sunday evening Marian canon (chanted at Small Compline on Sunday, serving the liturgical day of Monday). In Octoechos usage, the weekly cycle shifts to the angels and repentance.
[^11]: Nineteenth-century Russian cursive archival inscription in ink at bottom margin: *Принадлежитъ библіотекѣ Троицкой Сергіевой Лавры. 1859 года.* ("Belongs to the library of the Trinity-St. Sergius Lavra. In the year 1859.").
[^12]: Liturgical incipit for the Monday evening Marian canon (chanted at Small Compline on Monday, serving the liturgical day of Tuesday). In the Octoechos cycle, this service is aligned with repentance and the forerunner.
[^13]: Liturgical incipit for the Tuesday evening Marian canon (chanted at Small Compline on Tuesday, serving the liturgical day of Wednesday). In the Octoechos cycle, this service is aligned with the Precious and Life-Giving Cross.
[^14]: Liturgical incipit for the Wednesday evening Marian canon (chanted at Small Compline on Wednesday, serving the liturgical day of Thursday). In the Octoechos cycle, this service is aligned with the Holy Apostles and Saint Nicholas.
[^15]: Liturgical incipit for the Thursday evening Marian canon (chanted at Small Compline on Thursday, serving the liturgical day of Friday). In the Octoechos cycle, this service is dedicated to the Precious and Life-Giving Cross and the Passion of Christ.
[^16]: Liturgical incipit for the Friday evening Marian canon (chanted at Small Compline on Friday, serving the liturgical day of Saturday). In the Octoechos cycle, this service is aligned with All Saints and the remembrance of the faithful departed.
[^17]: Direct liturgical witness to the Classical Patristic theology of sacred images defined by Saint Basil the Great (*On the Holy Spirit*, 18.45: 'The honor rendered to the icon passes to the prototype') and dogmatized at the Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II, 787). The Slavonic text (*Печа́ть тѧ̀ Влч҃нꙋ... ꙗ҃кѡ первоѡ́бразнꙋ чтꙋ̀*) explicitly asserts the relative veneration (*proskynesis*) due to the Marian icon.
