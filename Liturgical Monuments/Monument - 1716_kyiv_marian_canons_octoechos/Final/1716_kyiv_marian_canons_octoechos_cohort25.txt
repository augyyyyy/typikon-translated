*Daily Examination of Conscience before Sleep (Contemporary 18th-century manuscript addition on facing unnumbered recto):*[^443]

Before thou liest down upon thy bed, consider all these things and pass through them in thy memory:

1. First: Give thanks unto Almighty God, that He hath granted thee by His grace to pass through the past day in life and in health, saying:  
I give thanks unto Thee, O Lord God, my most good Giver of life, that in this past day, wherein many have passed from this life unto death, and especially unto eternal death, Thou hast been well-pleased to preserve me, Thine unworthy servant, whole and unharmed until this night. Blessed, therefore, be Thy Name across all days and nights, and unto ages.

2. Second: Settle accounts with thine own self, and make an examination of thy conscience, going over and recounting in detail all the daytime hours, beginning from the time when thou didst arise from thy bed and bringing to thy remembrance: whither thou didst go, what thou didst do unto anyone, and what thou didst counsel, and all thy deeds, words, and thoughts produced by thee from morning even until evening, keeping all these things in mind...[^444]
