# 1716 Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra Theotokarion / Eight-Tone Marian Canons (Богородичник / Канони Богородиці Осмогласні, Київ: Друкарня Лаври, 1716) <a id="1716-kyiv-pechersk-lavra-theotokarion-eight-tone-marian-canons"></a>

[p1LA] *(Flyleaf — Archival binding sheet)* [p1LA] [Front pastedown / unnumbered archival flyleaf with 18th-century manuscript notations. At top is an early cursive Cyrillic inscription: "From the books of Elijah the Deacon..." In the middle are archival ink shelfmarks: "No. 4n.", "Year 1795, No: 3." At the bottom is a cursive inscription: "Of the Metropolitan..."][^1]

[p1RA] [Title Page within an architectural woodcut border featuring medallions of the Holy Dormition, Saint Prince Vladimir, Saint Andrew the First-Called, the Venerable Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves, Holy Martyrs Boris and Gleb, and a panoramic cityscape of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra with the engraver's monogram and date 1707:][^2]

*Canons to the Most Holy Theotokos in the Eight Tones,*

appointed to be read at Compline for every day according to the Church Typikon.

Written by Saint John of Damascus.

Printed in types in the Holy Great Miraculous Lavra of the Caves in Kyiv.

In the year from the Nativity of Christ: 1716.

[p2LA] [Full-page woodcut engraving: The Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos. At the top is inscribed: "The Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos." The All-Pure Virgin rests upon the bier; behind the bier the Savior in glory holds her all-pure soul in the likeness of an infant in swaddling bands; around stand the Holy Apostles and Angels; in the foreground stands a candlestick with a burning candle; at lower right is the engraver's signature: "MARKO," and at left: "In the year 17...". In the lower cartouche beneath the engraving is inscribed the Troparion of the Dormition:][^3]

*Troparion:* In giving birth thou didst preserve virginity, and in thy dormition thou didst not forsake the world, O Theotokos; thou hast passed over unto life, being the Mother of Life, and by thy supplications thou deliverest our souls from death.

### Notes

[^1]: The front flyleaf contains ink provenance shelfmarks and calligraphic cursive ownership notes of the eighteenth century, including references to Deacon Elijah ("З книгъ Іллїѧ дїакона...") and the archival cataloguing record "1795 года Но: 3."
[^2]: The architectural woodcut border of the master title page depicts Christ Pantocrator flanked by angels at the summit, the Dormition of the Mother of God directly beneath, lateral vertical registers with medallic portraits of Saint Prince Vladimir, Princess Olga, the Holy Passion-Bearers Boris and Gleb, the Venerable Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves, and Saint Andrew the First-Called, and a detailed panoramic engraving of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra with churches and bastions at the base, signed with the engraver's monogram "A. K." and dated 1707 ("Аѱо҃з.").
[^3]: This full-page woodcut frontispiece is the work of the renowned Kyivan Lavra master woodcut artist Marko (signed "МАРКО" in the lower right foreground), depicting the Dormition with Christ receiving the soul of His Mother amid the assembled Apostles. Beneath the engraving is printed the Troparion of the Dormition in Tone 1 (*Въ Рождествѣ Дѣвство сохранила еси...*).
