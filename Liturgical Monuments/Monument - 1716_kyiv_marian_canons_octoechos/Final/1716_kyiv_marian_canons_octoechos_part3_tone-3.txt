# TONE 3

## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 3)

[f. 55rA] *On Saturday at Small Compline,*[^32]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos:*

He Who of old by divine nod gathered the waters into one gathering and divided the sea for the people of Israel, this is our God, exceedingly glorified: unto Him alone let us sing, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

As the Mother of God revealed by the Divine Spirit and the good pleasure of the Father, we the faithful crown thee with songs of praise, O Most Pure Bride of God; and with the Archangel we kiss thee in songs for our salvation.

[f. 55vA] *Troparion:*

From the ribs of Adam was Eve fashioned by the ancient creation of God; but from the womb of the Theotokos appeared Christ the Son our God, Who unchangeably became man, the Pre-Eternal having become subject to time.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

He Who condemned the womb of Eve in sorrows to bring forth children in pain, settled in thy womb, O Virgin Pure, the Son our God: He appeared in the flesh beyond speech and loosed the debt of our first mother.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having fallen into the depth of despair through burdens, we diligently cry unto thee, O Parent of God: O Sovereign Lady, help those submerged by the deeds of crafty transgressions, for after God we have thee as our only hope.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who broughtest all things from non-being, being created by the Word and perfected by the Spirit, O Supreme Almighty Ruler: establish me in Thy love!

*Troparion:*

Aaron's rod that budded without being watered prefigured thee, O Most Pure Theotokos, who without seed didst give birth unto God incarnate immutably.

*Troparion:*

The Prophet foresaw thee in the Spirit as a lampstand carrying the Divine Fire, O Most Pure One, shedding fragrance and eternal life unto those in the world.

*Glory...*

[f. 56rA] *Troparion:*

Let us approach with Gabriel the Archangel, saying unto the Virgin in songs: Rejoice! For through thee was loosed the ancestral curse of condemnation.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having thee as a wall of salvation, O Most Pure Theotokos, we sinners are saved: despise not, O Sovereign Lady, nor reject our supplications.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou hast placed Thy steadfast love toward us, O Lord, for Thou didst deliver Thine Only-Begotten Son unto death for our sake: therefore we cry unto Thee, praising: glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Habakkuk the beholder of God, seeing thee of old, O Most Pure One, proclaimed thee to be the spiritual mountain of virtues overshadowed by the Spirit: for from the south the Word came from thee, having assumed flesh.

*Troparion:*

Daniel saw thee in the Spirit as a great unhewn mountain, manifestly revealing the purity of thy virginity, O Sovereign Lady: from which the Stone was cut, Christ the Word, casting down the deceit of idols.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

David foretold thee as a marvelous and fertile mountain: for the Only-Begotten Son from the Father was well pleased to dwell in thee, O Pure One, being incarnate: therefore in the Spirit we cry unto thee: Rejoice!

*Both now...*

[f. 56vA] *Theotokion (continued):*

Being all-good and a fervent protectress unto sinners and the humble, O Parent of God, Most Pure Sovereign Lady: save thy servants from perils, and afflictions, and sins.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

As Isaiah saw figurately God upon an exalted throne, borne aloft by the Angels of glory, he cried aloud: Woe is me, wretched one! For I have foreseen God incarnate, the Never-Setting Light, Who rules over peace.

*Troparion:*

The Rod of Jesse, the Virgin Mary, brought forth the unfading Flower, God the Unoriginate, without seed from the Divine Spirit and the Father, Who rules over the sovereign kingdoms of the gentiles, upon Whom the nations have hoped.

*Troparion:*

The Prince of Peace came, to reign upon the throne of David, God incarnate of thee, O Theotokos. O wonder! Loosing the womb, He honored the princes of Moab, and revealed thee who didst bear Him as Queen.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Calling upon thy spotless beauty, from which Christ clothed Himself in flesh without seed, O Virgin, Isaiah cries aloud, proclaiming: The Lord of Glory comes upon a light cloud, and driving away the darkness of deceit, He will enlighten us!

*Both now...*

[f. 57rA] *Troparion (continued):*

Having conceived of the Holy Spirit the Word consubstantial with the Father, O Virgin, thou didst give birth unto Him in two natures, being perfect God and perfect man: Whose appearance in the flesh we honor with faith.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The uttermost abyss of sins has encompassed me, and my spirit fails: but stretching forth Thy high arm, O Master, save me, O Helmsman, as Thou didst save Peter!

*Troparion:*

The all-wise one prefigured in songs the image, O All-Hymned One: upon which God, incarnate hypostatically of Thee, rested, and ineffably glorified Thee as He was born.

*Troparion:*

A silver-woven garment wast thou unto the Word, O Virgin All-Hymned: for having taken flesh from thee as a divine Purple, He reigned, being divinely arrayed.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst become the receptacle of the divine union, O Bride of God, brighter than gold: for through thee God became as man, and God conversed with men as man.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Put to death the heresy of malice of those who act impiously toward thee, O All-Hymned Virgin: for they melt with envy, beholding the most glorious likeness of thine icon.[^33]

[f. 57vA] *Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:*[^34]

In the warmth of faith I cry unto thee, O Theotokos, with unworthy lips and a defiled heart: save me who am submerged in sins, have compassion upon me slain by despair: that being saved I may cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Virgin, help of Christians!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

To serve the golden image with Persian honor the three children refused to bow down, singing in the midst of the furnace of fire: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

The bush and the flame joined congruously together and both appearing unconsumed manifestly prefigured thee, O Virgin: for thou didst bear God, and remainest a Virgin.

*Troparion:*

The fleece and dew shown in change unto Gideon prefigured thy childbearing: for thou alone, O Virgin Mother, didst carry in thy womb the Divine Word as rain.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The fire of my sin creates a flame for me worse than Gehenna: quench this by thy mercy, O Pure One, guiding me unto the light through repentance.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Honoring the appearance of thy likeness, O Most Pure Theotokos: as the prototype, we all have thee ever as a protectress and favorable covering before God.

[f. 58rA] *Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The Babylonian furnace burned not the children, nor did the fire of the Godhead kindle the Spirit: therefore with the children we faithfully cry aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

The Father chose thee, an Unwedded Bride: finding thee as a most radiant lily amid thorns, shining with the brightness of beauty, by the Divine Spirit, as a dwelling for the Son.

*Troparion:*

The wall of the heavenly Hosts, the All-Immaculate Virgin, unto salvation do I praise throughout the ages: for she carried their Creator in her womb, Who by unconfused union took flesh from thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having preserved an instealed virginity, O Virgin, thou didst truly appear as the Mother of the Son of God: having become the Bride by the good pleasure of the Father, and an incorruptible receptacle for the glory of the Spirit.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

God, immaterial and invisible by nature, simply and ineffably beyond nature is born man of the Holy Virgin: seen as twofold in one hypostasis, in which He is seen in songs.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

A new and divinely-befitting wonder: for the closed virginal portal the Lord manifestly passes through, naked at His entry, and bearing flesh [f. 58vA] *Heirmos (continued):*

God appeared at His going forth, and the portal remains closed: her unceasingly, as the Mother of God, do we magnify!

*Troparion:*

From a holy root, a Holy Fruit: for unto barren parents cleansed by the Law of God, the Maiden budded forth, bringing forth the undefiled Life, the Theotokos; and Anna rejoices, having received in old age the Child, the Mother of God, whom we glorify.

*Troparion:*

New and God-revealing is the childbearing of thy holy and God-bearing womb, O Pure One: for therein by the Father's finger the Holy Son was inscribed as an image in man, incarnate by the Holy Spirit: Whom indivisibly as God and man we magnify.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Every judgment cries out against me: for the deeds of sinful actions raise a menacing cry against me, and all my soul cries out, convicted by them, and trembles before the flame of Gehenna: from which before the end, deliver me by thy prayers, O Sovereign Lady.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

After childbirth thou wast revealed incorrupt, O Pure One: for unchangeably didst thou, O Theotokos, bear in the flesh the Creator of all, man beyond nature, without departing from the essence of the Father; and again thou didst remain a Virgin by the Divine Spirit: therefore glorifying thee, we offer this song.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 3)

[f. 59rA] *On Sunday at Small Compline,*[^35]

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos:*

He Who of old by divine nod gathered the waters into one gathering and divided the sea for the people of Israel, this is our God, exceedingly glorified: unto Him alone let us sing, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

O only One of great renown, Most Holy Virgin Mary, more glorious than the Cherubim, Daughter of David and Beauty of Jacob, Mother of our God: exceedingly magnified is thy glory, and all we praise thee with faith.

*Troparion:*

Him Who by His word stretched out the heavens like a curtain, thou didst carry in thine arms as a man: implore Him unchangeably, O Pure One, to deliver my soul from irrational passions, and from every affliction.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Foreordained before all ages, O Virgin, Most Pure Purple of the King of Glory, in which He clothed Himself in the last times: cover the coats of skins of our forefather Adam, and cover me now.

*Both now...*

[f. 59vA] *Theotokion (continued):*

Sin working corruption of old in the human race, O Virgin, didst thou dry up as a restless fire, having given birth unto Christ: therefore dry up the torrents of passions of my soul by thy prayers.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who broughtest all things from non-being, being created by the Word and perfected by the Spirit, O Supreme Almighty Ruler: establish me in Thy love!

*Troparion:*

By fiery Godhead is the Master and Lord, thy Son, named, O Pure One: therefore deliver me from passionate bondages, O Sovereign Lady, by thy prayers.

*Troparion:*

From the tempest of carnal passions deliver me thy servant swiftly, O Most Pure One, who didst give birth unto the Calm of salvation and the Fountain of dispassion.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst become a radiant lampstand of the Never-Setting Light, O Most Pure One, and didst enlighten the world: therefore by thy rays disperse the cloud of my soul.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Revive me who am dead in soul from the multitude of evil passions, O Sovereign Lady, who ineffably didst give birth unto Christ the Giver of life.

[f. 60rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou hast placed Thy steadfast love toward us, O Lord, for Thou didst deliver Thine Only-Begotten Son unto death for our sake: therefore we cry unto Thee thankfully: glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

The warfare of carnal mind has come upon me and takes captive my soul: but O thou who didst give birth unto the Peace surpassing all understanding, grant me thy peace, O Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

Thou didst pour forth unto the world intelligible myrrh from thy womb: therefore free my soul from defilements and passions, and grant me, O Most Pure One, the myrrh of dispassion through thy prayers.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee manifestly as the Golden Jar that carried the Manna of life, the God-Man Christ, I pray: nourish my soul that faints with hunger, and refresh it with the stream of the Spirit.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Eve indeed introduced death of old through intemperance; but through thy pure virginity thou hast brought back the true Life: therefore deliver me from the death of sin through thy prayers.

[f. 60vA] *Ode 5. Heirmos:*

He Who is invisible appeared upon earth, and He Who is incomprehensible lived voluntarily with men, and rising early unto Thee, we sing Thy praise, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Having become our Redemption, thy Son, O Virgin, delivered all from corruption: therefore deliver me from carnal mind, and from the assault of passions.

*Troparion:*

With the fire of thy prayers, O Theotokos, burn up the thorns of passionate thoughts: thou who with the tongs of the live coal didst cleanse mankind.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst become the Heifer of God, [who didst bear] for our sake the divine Bullock sacrificed out of love for mankind: by Whose divine Blood, cleanse my heart.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Of old I acquired the comely garment of virtues, whereof I stripped myself through my slothfulness; but do thou, O Virgin, now clothe me in brightness by thy prayers.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The uttermost abyss of sins has surrounded me, and my spirit fails; but stretching forth Thy high arm, O Master, save me, O Helmsman, even as Peter.

*Troparion:*

The grievous craftiness of malice has shaken the temple of my soul; but having given birth unto Christ Who established the earth upon the waters, establish [me] by thy prayers.

[f. 61rA] *Troparion:*

Thou didst loose the debt of earthborn mortals, having given birth unto the Pearl of great price: therefore, O Most Pure One, swiftly loose the bonds of my transgressions, and of passions and afflictions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

God the all-good, having been born from thy womb, has given thee as a refuge and divine protection unto the faithful: therefore, O Most Pure One, cover me also with thy hands, and preserve me.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Being harmfully engulfed often by the waves of life, I, wretched one, and ever tempest-tossed by the enslavements of the enemy, now cry unto thee, O Theotokos: help me.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3.*

*Podoben: "Of the divine faith" (Боже́ственныя вѣ́ры):* [^36]

Thou didst become the divine Tabernacle of the Word, O only Most Pure Virgin Mother, who in purity didst surpass the Angels: cleanse me, who am defiled above all men by carnal transgressions, with divine waters through thy prayers, granting great mercy, O Pure One.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Thou Who of old didst bedew the three pious youths in the Chaldean flame, illuminate us also with the radiant fire of Godhead, as we cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

[f. 61vA] *Troparion:*

Assemblies of crafty demons have surrounded me, and carnal multitudes of passions; but having given birth unto Christ Who is mighty in battles, deliver me from frequent sorrows and impending assaults.

*Troparion:*

The One Who is unapproachable in essence to the Cherubim, and fearful to the angelic ranks, dwelt in thee, O Pure One, and renewed us: through Whom show me, O Sovereign Lady, to be fearful unto the invisible enemies.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

A spiritual paradise didst thou become, O Virgin, which put forth the Tree of life: whereof Adam having partaken was delivered from the mortal fruit: therefore sweeten me now also, and deliver me from the taste of passions.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Deliver [me] from the mire of bodily passions and demonic temptations, O thou who didst give birth unto the Redeemer of mankind, the highest dwelling and consecrated temple of the God of our fathers.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Gathered together in the intolerable fire, standing before [it], the pious youths, unharmed by the flame, sang the divine hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

[f. 62rA] *Troparion:*

O Cloud of light, [who didst bear] Him Who made the great luminaries in the world, both day and night: disperse every darkness of all my passions and transgressions by thy rays, and show me to be a son of the light, O Mother of God.

*Troparion:*

By the Rain that came down from heaven into thy womb, O Theotokos, enlighten and bedew my soul and my heart, and quench the flame of passions and afflictions, that I may fervently glorify thee unto all ages.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Without corruption, O Pure One, thou didst give birth unto the Wisdom of God, Who united into one flesh all things from non-being, both heavenly and earthly nature: by Whose word grant me wisdom and understanding, that I may now sing of thy divine Childbearing.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

May my soul and body be bedewed by thy prayers, O Sovereign Lady of the world, and swiftly deliver me from the spears and lightning, the diseases of sins, and the malice of passions: for thou art healing unto all men.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Upon Mount Sinai Moses beheld thee in the bush unburned, having conceived in thy womb the divine Fire; and Daniel beheld thee as the unhewn Mountain; and Isaiah cried: A Rod budded forth from the root of David!

[f. 62vA] *Troparion:*

Thou didst appear as a loom divinely woven for Christ, from which the Spirit prepared the garment of most divine-princely flesh: clothe me therefore in the garment of chastity, O Pure One, by thy prayers.

*Troparion:*

O Spiritual Vineyard, who didst bud forth for us the Divine Cluster, wherefrom we are watered with the water of incorruption: pour forth unto my soul wine of compunction and cleansing, by thy prayers.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O pure and holy Bridal Chamber, through whom God in nature was betrothed unto the nature of men, O Mother: unite me unto thy Son and make me His own in divine life, by thy prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having given birth unto Christ the Fountain of incorruption, O Virgin, thou didst transform the pasture of corruption of human nature: put to death therefore in me the scorching and tempestuous rush of passions, by thy prayers.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual. And the Dismissal.*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 3)

*On Monday at Compline,*[^37]

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos:*

He Who of old by divine beckoning gathered the waters into one gathering, and divided the sea for the people of Israel: this is our God, exceedingly glorified; unto Him alone let us sing, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having spent my life in slothfulness, I have drawn near unto the end of my days; but do thou thyself, O Most Pure One, remember me in the final time, and grant me compunction as I pray, that I may weep bitterly over my boundless transgressions.

*Troparion:*

Having foolishly defiled the beauty made after the image of God by the passions of my flesh, O Virgin, I was terrified of God's indignation and of the dreadful threat of fire; unto thee have I fled, O tenderhearted Mother: have mercy on me.

*Glory...*

[f. 63vA] *Troparion:*

Bending my knees, I, wretched one, ask for thy help, O Most Holy Virgin; do thou give ear unto my ailing soul, and disperse the impending cloud of my sorrow with the ray of thy prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Grant the passionate furrows of my soul to be richly watered with tears, and vouchsafe me, O Sovereign Lady, to bear fruit a hundredfold, and fill my heart with every joy, that I may glorify thee.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst bring all things from non-existence, which are built by the Word and accomplished by the Spirit, O Almighty Most High, establish me in Thy love.

*Troparion:*

Grant me a rain-cloud of tears, O Good Theotokos, wherewith quench the furnace of my passions, and wash away from me every defilement of soul.

*Troparion:*

In iniquities I have defiled the nobility of my soul, O Most Pure One, and I tremble at the inquiry, when the Word shall examine the dignity of my image.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The tempest of transgressions having now seized me has brought me down into the depth of despair: but stretch forth thy hand unto me, O Pure One, and guide me unto repentance.

*Both now...*

[f. 64rA] *Theotokion:*

Deliver thy servant from gehenna and every other threat in the hour of judgment, O All-Immaculate One, and make [me] a partaker of the Kingdom of thy Son and God.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou hast placed Thy steadfast love toward us, O Lord, for Thou didst deliver Thine Only-Begotten Son unto death for our sake: therefore we cry unto Thee thankfully: glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

The wounds of shameful deeds have reached even unto my wretched soul, O Pure One; therefore held fast by thoughts of clay, I cry aloud with pain: despise me not, O Sovereign Lady, thy servant.

*Troparion:*

Spiritual beasts have now unmercifully surrounded me, striving unsparingly to snatch away my humble soul, O All-Immaculate One; but by thy Son, O Most Pure One, crush their soul-corrupting jaws.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Be thou an intercession unto thy servant, O Sovereign Lady, we pray, and from the expected threat snatch thy servants, O Pure One, that we may cry unto thee giving thanks: Glory to thee, Queen of all!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

When the Bridegroom shall come by night to judge the earth: then vouchsafe me, O Most Pure One, to go forth to meet Him with a shining lamp, and to be ready for His coming.

[f. 64vA] *Ode 5. Heirmos:*

He Who is invisible appeared upon earth, and He Who is incomprehensible lived voluntarily with men, and rising early unto Thee, we sing Thy praise, O Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Many dogs have truly surrounded me; an assembly of wicked spirits has encompassed me; but do thou, O Most Pure One, now destroy their counsels.

*Troparion:*

The wicked one has now dug a pit for me, and strives to cast me therein; but by thy right hand, O Sovereign Lady, let him fall into the pit that he made.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Let me not be rebuked by the fury of thy Son at the time of His Coming, nor chastised by His wrath, O All-Hymned One: do thou save me by thy prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Behold my weakness, O Pure One; behold the humiliation of my wretched soul, and the uprising of incorporeal enemies, and deliver me from their harm.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The uttermost abyss of sins has surrounded me, and my spirit fails; but stretching forth Thy high arm, O Master, save me, O Helmsman, even as Peter.

*Troparion:*

By the beckoning of the Creator of all, when my soul is about to part from the flesh, O All-Immaculate One, set me free from the bonds of those who hate me, O All-Hymned Theotokos.

[f. 65rA] *Troparion:*

Dry up the turbid rivers of my wicked deeds, O Mother of God, with streams of compunction, and guide me unto the water of relief in the Day of Judgment.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou knowest the weakness of my soul, and the infirmity of my mind, O Most Pure One, and the impotence of my flesh: therefore save thy servant, for I have acquired thee as an unconquerable Protectress.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Grant me a stream of spiritual tears, O Maiden, wherewith I may wash away the mire of my transgressions, and the tumult of passions, O All-Immaculate One, and bodily defilements.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3.*

*Podoben: "Beholding the beauty of thy virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства):* [^38]

O thou who didst carry the Merciful One in thy womb: have compassion upon him that flees beneath thy protection, and asks from his soul for thy divine help, O Sovereign Lady, and vouchsafe him mercy; when we shall stand before all creation, deliver [us], O Pure One, from eternal fire and every condemnation.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Enduring the molten body [of the idol] made through Persian deceit, the three children bowed not down, singing in the midst of the fire: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

[f. 65vA] *Troparion:*

The wounds of transgressions have poured out upon me even unto my soul, O Pure One, and a wretched ruin has surrounded me: but snatch me from this distress.

*Troparion:*

Sprinkle me, O Pure One, with the Blood from the ribs of thy Child, according to the multitude of thy [mercies], O Mother, and wash me with streams of tears, and cleanse me from every defilement.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Grant contrition unto my soul and humility unto my heart, O All-Pure One, that I may be delivered from the oppressive craftiness of those who continually and unsparingly hunt after me.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

As the merciful Mother of God, O Sovereign Lady, vouchsafe mercy also unto thy servants who cry aloud with faith unto thy Son: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Gathered together in the intolerable fire, standing before [it], the pious youths, unharmed by the flame, sang the divine hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Crush the arrows of the archer, O Pure One, by the dominion of thy Son, and let his iniquity come down upon his own crown, that I may sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

Illuminate my darkened heart with thy light, O Maiden, and with the armor of light open unto me the gates of light, as I cry:

[f. 66rA] Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and praise and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having fallen asleep unto death, I lie in the tomb of despair; but do thou thyself raise me up, O Virgin, and vouchsafe me to sing vigilantly: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, praise and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Cease not, O Pure One, praying for those who honor thee in compunction, that they may be delivered from the snares of the devil, crying unto thy Son: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, praise and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

In the shadowy writing of the Law we believers perceive a figure: for every male child that opens the womb is holy to God. Therefore the First-born Word, the Son of the Unoriginate Father, first-born from an unwedded Mother, do we magnify.

*Troparion:*

The surge of passions tempest-tosses my soul, and the turmoil of foul thoughts, and the storm of wicked men continually troubles me; but as thou art the lover of mankind, O Virgin, swiftly deliver me from the distress of those who hold me fast.

*Troparion:*

O humble soul, depart from thy wicked deeds, and cease from malice, and [f. 66vA] anger not God, but receive His commandments diligently: for thou hast the Mother of God directing thy ways.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

As thou didst bear the Lord of all, free me from passions and grievous sins, and enrich me with good deeds according to thy great tenderheartedness: that rejoicing, I may magnify thee with love, O All-Immaculate One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The end draws near, O my soul; the judgment seat is at the doors; depart from shameful deeds, and take hold of a good life: for thou hast the Theotokos as a Champion, delivering thee from every affliction.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after the Our Father: Troparia, and the rest as usual. And the Dismissal.*

## ON TUESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 3)

*On Tuesday at Compline,*[^39]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos:*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 3. Heirmos:*

He Who of old by divine beckoning gathered the waters into one gathering, and divided the sea for the people of Israel: this is our God, exceedingly glorified; unto Him alone let us sing, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having brought forth the Author of salvation and the Giver of life beyond all understanding, thou didst loose the bond of the condemnation of Eve our Fore-mother. Therefore all creation blesses thee, O Pure Mother of God.

*Troparion:*

Snatch me, who am afflicted with grievous sicknesses in soul and body, O Most Holy Virgin, from the depth of sorrow, delivering me from all dire straits: for thou, O Most Pure One, art the fountain of tenderheartedness.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

I have stripped myself of the garment of gladness, falling into sorrow and infirmity, and have been grievously wounded by enemies; anticipate [them] and deliver me, O Sovereign Lady, from the delusion that holds me fast: for thou art my refuge and hope.

*Both now...*

[f. 79vA] *Theotokion:*

Having acquired thee as a steadfast Protectress, O Bride of God, I flee beneath thy protection, praying: Despise me not, thy servant, weighed down by many passions, infirmities, and sorrows: but heal me by thy prayers, O Mother of God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst bring all things from non-existence, which are built by the Word and accomplished by the Spirit, O Almighty Most High, establish me in Thy love.

*Troparion:*

I, wretched one, am in perplexity and painful sorrow, beholding death following after me. Therefore, O Most Pure One, save me by thy prayer!

*Troparion:*

Behold, having wasted my life in evil and profligate deeds, [my conscience] accuses me and submerges me in despair; O Pure One, save me!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The abyss of thy compassions, poured out everywhere, O Sovereign Lady, for the sake of goodness alone, anoints with oil and soothes all that ever sing thy praise.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having taken [humanity] from the first-fruits of our nature, O Bride of God, Thy Son and Lord unites those that sing thy praise, through thy prayers.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou hast placed Thy steadfast love toward us, O Lord, for Thou didst deliver Thine Only-Begotten Son unto death for our sake. Therefore we cry unto Thee thankfully: glory to Thy power, O Lord!

[f. 80rA] *Troparion:*

Having placed all my hope upon thee, O Sovereign Lady, I pray and fall down from my soul: deliver [me] from death-bearing sicknesses, leading me up unto the life of salvation: as thou didst bear Life!

*Troparion:*

Heal me, O Pure One, by thy prayers, thy wretched servant, paralyzed by the appearance of grievous things: for thou didst bear the Physician, rich in mercy, our God.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver me who have fallen into grievous passions, and grant me the garment of gladness through thy divine visitation: for thou, O Virgin, didst bud forth Joy that surpasses every mind.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou hast been shown forth as another Heaven, O Theotokos, having ineffably shone forth the Sun of righteousness upon earth, O Pure One, through Whom the Ever-Existing Light of divine knowledge has shone upon us.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Unto Thee I rise early, O Creator of all, Who art Peace surpassing every mind: for Thy commandments are light; guide me in them!

*Troparion:*

Things causing death and passions have drawn near because of my measureless sins, O Mother of God; I diligently pray that thou mayest be the deliverance from my many evils.

*Troparion:*

I stretch forth my feeble hands unto thee, O Pure One: for I, wretched one, am weakened by carnal things. But do thou [f. 80vA] heal me of my many wounds, granting healing.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

I have stripped off the garment of health, and have put on a sorrowful robe in sicknesses; I pray unto thee, O Sovereign Lady: clothe me in health.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

As the sacred beauty of Martyrs and Apostles, and the protection of the faithful, O Theotokos, we all worthily glorify thee.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Bring me up from corruption, O Lord my God, Jonah cried, and I cry unto Thee: deliver me, O Savior, from the depth of my many evils, and guide me unto the light of Thy commandments.

*Troparion:*

A multitude of corrupting sicknesses has fallen upon me from my measureless transgressions, and encompassed me with deadness; but do thou thyself, O Theotokos, deliver me from them, and snatch me from every distress.

*Troparion:*

Having been cast down into the depth of transgressions and sicknesses, and measureless sorrows, and being utterly submerged in them: do thou, O Mother of God, stretch forth thy hand and bring me up unto salvation.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

We know thee as a firm foundation, O Theotokos, and a mighty help in sorrows. Therefore, fleeing unto thy protection, I implore thee: deliver me from fierce calamities and from grievous sicknesses.

*Both now...*

[f. 81rA] *Theotokion:*

As the Furrow that sprouted the Divine Ear of Grain, whereby the hearts of the faithful are nourished by faith and noetic hunger has ceased, let us sing of the Ark of God, the Virgin Mother of God.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:*[^47]

Having become flesh from thy womb, unsearchable of eternal nature, yet God became man and remained [God], He Who after childbirth preserved thee as Virgin Mother all-immaculate, even as before childbirth, the only Lord: Him do thou diligently implore to grant us great mercy.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Thou Who of old didst refresh the three pious youths in the Chaldean flame, with the light-bearing fire of Godhead illumine us also, who cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Have tenderhearted compassion upon me, O Virgin Bride of God, who have slipped into the depth of despair; I pray thee to be my calm, O Most Pure One: for thou art ever the Haven of salvation unto those in distress.

*Troparion:*

Ineffably beyond understanding thou didst bear Christ, the Wisdom and Power of God. Therefore, being mighty in prayer and generous, grant me, O Theotokos, Hope of the faithful, the saving waters of healing.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having enriched the ends [of the earth] with thy Divine Childbirth by the sweetness of nourishment, free me from corrupting illness and bitter passions, and grant [me] salvation, O All-Glorious Mother of God.

*Both now...*

[f. 81vA] *Theotokion:*

He Who prepared thee as His mystical gate and a marvelous protectress unto those on earth, through thee, O Pure One, has granted unto men a door that leads all up unto eternal life, as King and God of all.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The Babylonian furnace did not burn the youths, nor did the divine fire consume their spirit; therefore with the children we cry in faith: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

Having acquired an unsearchable abyss of transgressions, now am I tossed by waves, O Pure One, submerged in sicknesses. Therefore, stretching forth a hand of help unto me, bring me up from the depth of sicknesses, I pray.

*Troparion:*

Thou hast appeared, O Pure Virgin, lauded Theotokos, as an earthly Throne unto God, and even more as the Heavenly Door. Therefore open unto me the door of salvation, O Mother of God, and set me free from the darkness of passions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

As a vine thou didst put forth the Untilled Cluster, O Pure One, that gushes forth wine making glad the souls of those that sing His praise in faith, O All-Immaculate One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou who remained incorrupt after childbirth, O All-Immaculate One: by thy prayers, O Pure One, deliver from corruption thy servants who cry in faith with singleness of soul: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto all ages!

[f. 82rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Moses saw thee in the bush upon Mount Sinai, who didst unburnt conceive the Divine Fire in thy womb; and Daniel saw thee as the Uncut Mountain, [and] the Budded Rod, cried Isaiah, from the root of David.

*Troparion:*

O Good Virgin, Joy and Refuge from the world, Haven of the faithful, and Deliverance from sorrows: do thou stand by me in the hour of death, and deliver me from the demons seeking to tear me apart.

*Troparion:*

Having conceived in thy womb the Creator and God, O Virgin Mother Pure, by the power abiding in thee cast down the ragings of demons, and exalt the horn of those that sing of thy divine dominion and faithfully venerate thine icon.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having made our nature new, He Who took flesh of thee, O Pure Virgin, makes [it] new and renews [it] by thy maternal prayer. Therefore, as our radiant and vigilant Protectress and true Theotokos, we unceasingly magnify thee in hymns.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou art the joy of Angels, thou art the beauty of the Righteous, thou art the hope of the faithful, thou art our preservation, thou art the Bridge leading those who magnify thee in faith and love unto ageless life.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

[f. 82vA] *(Woodcut: Full-page engraving of the Mother of God holding the Christ Child within an ornate arch, with the Holy Spirit as a Dove above, an inscription cartouche below, and the Russian imperial double-headed eagle at the base)*

### Notes

[^32]: Saturday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Saturday evening belongs liturge-historically to the Resurrectional Sunday vigil office of Tone 3, opening the weekly cycle of the Third Tone.
[^33]: The phrase *пресла́вное твоегѡ́ о́браза ви́дѧще подо́бїе* ("beholding the most glorious likeness of thine icon") reflects the standard Byzantine-Ruthenian iconophile theology affirmed at the Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II), defending the veneration of sacred icons against iconoclastic heresy.
[^34]: The Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Tone 3 at Small Compline is an idiomelon sung to its own melody without a preceding *podoben* rubric.
[^35]: Sunday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Sunday evening belongs liturge-historically to Monday, the beginning of the liturgical week, which commemorates the Holy Incorporeal Bodiless Hosts (Angels) and the penitential call of the Octoechos.
[^36]: The Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) follows the melodic model *Tone 3, Podoben: "Of the Divine Faith"* (Боже́ственныя вѣ́ры), a standard prosomoion of the Third Tone.
[^37]: Monday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Monday evening liturgically begins Tuesday, dedicated in the weekly liturgical cycle of the Octoechos to the Holy Forerunner and Baptist John.
[^38]: Melodic model: *Tone 3, Podoben: "The Beauty of Thy Virginity"* (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства), one of the most prominent Marian automela of Tone 3.
[^39]: Tuesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Tuesday evening forms the liturgical eve of Wednesday, which commemorates throughout the Byzantine Octoechos the Holy Cross and the Betrayal of the Lord.
[^40]: This Sessional Hymn to the Cross (*Крестобогородичен* / Stavrotheotokion) of Tone 3 is sung as an idiomelon to its own melody without a preceding *podoben* rubric.
[^41]: Wednesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Wednesday evening liturgically forms the eve of Thursday, dedicated throughout the Byzantine Octoechos to the Holy Apostles and Saint Nicholas of Myra in Lycia.
[^42]: The hymnographer evokes the Dominical parable of the barren fig tree (Luke 13:6–9; Matt. 21:19), in which the unyielding tree symbolizes the impenitent soul deserving of being cut down and cast into fire.
[^43]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Wednesday Compline is an idiomelon sung to its own Third Tone melody without an automelon rubric.
[^44]: Thursday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Thursday evening liturgically begins Friday, the day dedicated across the Byzantine liturgical cycle to the Passion of the Lord and the Adoration of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross.
[^45]: This Sessional Hymn to the Cross (*Крестобогородичен* / Stavrotheotokion) of Thursday Compline is sung as an idiomelon without a podoben model, meditating upon the Mother of God lamenting beneath the Cross on Friday eve.
[^46]: Friday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Friday evening liturgically inaugurates Saturday, dedicated across the Octoechos to all Saints and to the memory of all departed Orthodox Christians.
[^47]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Friday Compline in Tone 3 is an idiomelon sung to its own melody, confessing the unconfused two natures of the incarnate Word and the perpetual virginity (*semper virgo*) of the Theotokos.
