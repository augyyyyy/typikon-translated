## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 5)

*On Saturday at Compline,*[^64]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 5. Heirmos:*

Christ shook out into the Red Sea the horse and rider, He Who crushes wars with a high arm, and saved Israel, as he sang a song of victory.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

We are all at a loss to offer a song unto thee according to thy worth, O Sovereign Lady: for thy glory is above all; yet, O Bride of God, reject not the supplication offered unto thee with fear and love.

*Troparion:*

Having drawn near unto the water of thine inexhaustible Fountain, O Virgin Theotokos, we flee, crying aloud: O only Giver of joy, Most Pure One, ask peace for thy churches.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

God, Who was well pleased to take flesh from thee, O Pure One, appointed thee a haven unto those in calamities [f. 109vA] therefore falling down before thee, we cry aloud: Grant thy help unto thy servants.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

May thy prayer, O Most Pure Virgin, be relief unto thy servants, driving away passions, loosing sins, and cleansing every disease, O Birthgiver of God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and didst suspend its unsupported weight: establish Thy Church upon the immovable Rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, Who alone art Good and the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Thou art the hope of earthborn mortals, and our help, joy, protection, and refuge, O Sovereign Lady, Mother of Life; therefore we pray thee: send down thy help unto all who sing of thee, O Most Pure One.

*Troparion:*

Being sick and held fast by grievous misfortunes, O Compassionate God of all, Who seest all: we bring this thy Most Pure Mother unto Thee in supplication, O Savior; loose the bonds of our transgressions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O thou who alone didst give birth unto the Upholder, Creator, and Lord of all, while remaining a virgin: grant salvation from on high unto thy servants, who truly glorify thee as the Mother of God, O Bride of God.

*Both now...*

[f. 110rA] *Theotokion:*

Fill me with life-giving waters, O Sovereign Lady, who didst pour forth the divine Water unto the world; and calm the grievous torrents of my iniquities and the waves of my heart, by thy divine tranquility.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Perceiving Thy divine dispensation prophetically, O Christ, Habakkuk cried aloud unto Thee with trembling: Thou hast come for the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed.

*Troparion:*

As being higher than all creatures, God gave thee unto those on earth, as the cause of mediation unto Him, O All-Hymned Theotokos.

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee to be the divine Jacob, O Virgin Mother, we who honor thee diligently pray thee: shut not the doors of thy mercy unto thy servants, O Theotokos.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Knowing Thee to be the truly all-adorned garment of God, O unwedded Mother, we who honor Thee ask: clothe us in the garment of forgiveness.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

At thy Childbirth, O Most Pure One, the whole world was filled with joy: from whence the great Gabriel cried aloud unto thee from on high: Rejoice, O Virgin Mother Mary!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment: I rise early at dawn unto Thee, and cry unto Thee: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou alone art tenderhearted.

[f. 110vA] *Troparion:*

Truly we are chastened, yet not according to the full measure of the debt of our transgressions; but O Virgin, Most Pure Mother, turn away all the wrath of thy Son from us.

*Troparion:*

Having given birth without knowing wedlock unto the Only God, Who brought forth light out of darkness, O Pure One: diligently pray Him to send down divine light upon thy servants.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thy prayers have censed us, O Pure One, and as the divine Bride from Lebanon foretold by Solomon: shed fragrance upon thy servants, O Mother of the Creator.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having given birth seedlessly unto Christ, our Righteousness and Deliverance: thou didst make the nature of our forefather free from the curse, O Theotokos.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Calm the sea of my passions, O Master Christ, which is troubled by a soul-destroying storm, and bring me up from corruption, as Thou art compassionate.

*Troparion:*

O Sovereign Lady Theotokos, who didst give birth unto the Creator: ask forgiveness for thy servants, and raise us up mighty to sing of thee.

*Troparion:*

Be a help unto us, thy servants, O Pure Sovereign Lady, who faithfully pray unto thee, as by thy prayers; and raise us up mighty to sing of thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having power according to thy dignity, O Pure Sovereign Lady: look down with thy merciful eye, and raise us up, thy servants, from corruption.

*Both now...*

[f. 111rA] *Theotokion:*

...unceasingly pouring forth a stream of compassions unto those that ask, O Good One: rain down also upon me the light of the commandments of thy Son, O All-Immaculate One.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^65]

O Pure Virgin, have mercy upon us who flee unto thy protection and unto thee fervently, and who ask thy warm mediation: for thou canst save all, as being the good Mother of the Most High God, ever encompassing us with thy maternal prayers, O Virgin Full of God's Grace.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Lord of the fathers, Who is exalted above all, quenched the flame, and bedewed the children who sang in unison: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

O Inscrutable Mercy of God, Christ: have compassion upon Thy servants for the sake of her who gave birth unto Thee, who unceasingly sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

We implore Thy grace, O Lord: heal our wound for the sake of her who gave birth unto Thee, of those who sing with fear: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Look down with thy merciful eye, O Mother of God, and deliver thy servants from every affliction, who sing with faith: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Both now...*

[f. 111vA] *Theotokion:*

...Working evil, O Sovereign Lady, we fell away from thee; but we immediately found thy help, O Most Pure One, when we cried: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who was begotten of the Father before the ages, the Son and God, and in the latter times incarnate of the Virgin Mother: ye priests, sing praises; ye people, exalt Him above all unto all ages!

*Troparion:*

O our Sovereign Lady, Giver of good things: grant unto thy servants healing in passions, that we may unceasingly sing of thee, O Maiden, and exalt thee unto the ages.

*Troparion:*

Having ineffably given birth unto the Redeemer, O Pure One, and inexplicably nursed Him, remaining a Virgin: pray Him on behalf of those who sing of thee and glorify thee unto all ages.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Unto thee, the radiant Lampstand of the Redeemer, having woven a beauteous choir, we sing: All ye works of the Lord, sing unceasingly of the Virgin Mother, and exalt her above all unto the ages!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

O Pure Ewe-Lamb, Virgin Mother, Maiden: make me pure from bodily passions, that I may be delivered from the snares of the deceitful one, hymning thee, O Full of God's Grace.

[f. 112rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Dance, O Isaiah! For the Virgin held in her womb and brought forth a Son, Emmanuel: both God and Man, the Orient is His name: Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed, O Virgin!

*Troparion:*

The song is indeed completed, but hope in Thee, O Master Christ, is unfailing toward Thee, the Creator, as is also Thy grace; yet grant firm strength on both sides unto Thy servants, through the prayers of her who gave birth unto Thee.

*Troparion:*

Thou art strength unto the ailing, and near unto those who suffer, O Pure One, as the true Mother of Life. Therefore, fleeing unto thee, we have found relief from all sorrowful things, O Sovereign Lady, and have been saved by thy protection.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Beholding thy divine form iconographically, O Theotokos, we see thee in it as though visibly present, utterly abhorring the folly of heretics upon earth; falling down before which, we receive healing.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

We sinners have known thee, O Pure One, as an abyss of healings and a deep sea of graces. Therefore we pray thee: deliver from dire misfortunes those who hasten unto thy protection, O only Most Pure One.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: Troparion, and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 5)

*On Sunday at Compline,*[^66]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 5. Heirmos:*

Christ shook out into the Red Sea the horse and rider, He Who crushes wars with a high arm, and saved Israel, as he sang a song of victory.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

A truly fearful wonder is accomplished in thee, O Virgin: for Him Whom nothing can contain, thou didst hold contained within thy womb, and didst inexplicably give birth unto Him, remaining ever a Virgin.

*Troparion:*

Rain down upon me the forgiveness of transgressions, O Sovereign Lady, who didst ineffably contain the Heavenly Rain that came unto thee, and didst remain a Virgin after incomprehensible Childbirth.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O only Blessed and Full of God's Grace, who didst give birth unto ineffable joy for mortals [f. 113rA] take away the sorrows of my soul, I pray thee, and make my heart glad.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

All desire, and the sweetness of life, shone forth from thee because of exceedingly abundant grace, O All-Immaculate Virgin: pray Him that he who now unceasingly glorifies thee may be saved.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and didst suspend its unsupported weight: establish Thy Church upon the immovable Rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, Who alone art Good and the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

The things prescribed by the shadow of the law have passed away: for thou didst give birth unto Christ the Lawgiver, O Most Pure One, Who ordained the grace of purification and enlightenment for us, and took us out from the curse, O All-Immaculate Virgin.

*Troparion:*

God was born of thee, O Pure One, becoming incarnate, and was seen in the flesh, Who was formerly invisible: diligently pray Him, therefore, O Maiden, to deliver me from visible and invisible enemies, as I radiantly glorify thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

A fierce wave of passions tosses me about, the deep of evil spirits holds me fast, sinful shame covers my breast, O Mother of God: do thou establish me, who radiantly sing of thee.

*Both now...*

[f. 113vA] *Theotokion:*

Having the divine Gabriel as Leader of the ranks, reverently, with joyful faith, we offer praise in unison, faithfully unto the Virgin Mother who knew not wedlock, the Theotokos, by whom we are delivered from misfortunes, sorrows, and diseases.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Discerning Thy divine dispensation, farsighted Habakkuk cried aloud unto Thee with trembling, O Christ: Thou hast come for the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed.

*Troparion:*

Thou who within didst weave from thy virginal blood a garment of incorruption for Him Who clothes heaven with clouds, O Virgin: clothe me with it, who was formerly stripped naked by deception.

*Troparion:*

Thy womb, O Virgin, ineffably became the holy Palace of the King and God: dwelling in which, He made us Churches.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Have compassion, as thou art compassionate, upon my most wretched soul, O All-Immaculate Birthgiver of God, which is grievously blackened and defiled by passions and sin.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst bud forth as a Scepter of incorruption from the root of Jesse, O Pure One, and without a man, O Pure One, didst give birth unto the Child, the God of Adam and David, together both Creator and Lord.

[f. 114rA] *Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment: I rise early at dawn unto Thee, and cry unto Thee: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou alone art tenderhearted.

*Troparion:*

Remaining a Virgin of incorruption after Childbirth, thou bringest all corrupted mortals unto life, and clothest them with incorruption, O All-Immaculate One.

*Troparion:*

Anew hast thou given birth as young, O Most Pure One, unto Him Who timelessly shone forth from the Father before the ages: pray Him for peace, O Bride of God.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

May I find thee as a Helper, O Virgin, who snatchest me away in the hour of receiving judgment, when I shall stand before the Judgment Seat of Him Who was born of thee.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Behold, my companion is good and spotless, from Lebanon, O Virgin Bride of God: through the Holy Spirit the Incarnation of the Son of God was made known in thee.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Calm the sea of my passions, O Master Christ, which is troubled by a soul-destroying storm, and bring me up from corruption, as Thou art compassionate.

*Troparion:*

Without knowing man thou didst give birth, O Theotokos, unto Emmanuel, Who had compassion upon our lowliness. Therefore we ever dutifully glorify thee.

[f. 114vA] *Troparion:*

Beyond timeless mind, beyond the word of the Creator, O Most Pure One, thou didst give birth unto Him Who delivers from all corruption those who sing of thee as Theotokos.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having given birth unto the Good One and Creator, O Full of Grace, Most Pure Sovereign Lady named of God: sweeten my afflicted soul.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

A new and ancient praise we offer unto thee, O All-Immaculate One: for we have nothing better for praise than to chant unto thee with Gabriel: Rejoice!

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^67]

Before the Judgment I now weep, pondering my wicked and grievous deeds, and the depth of transgressions that has possessed me from my youth, weighing down my mind; but by thy protection, O Pure One, grant me forgiveness, and vouchsafe me to obtain salvation.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Lord of the fathers, Who is exalted above all, quenched the flame, and bedewed the children who sang in unison: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

Deliver me, O Most Pure One, from evil despondency, and from the darkening of passions, and from ancient condemnation, that with faith I may glorify thee.

*Troparion:*

Put my passions to death, O thou who didst give birth unto Life; raise me up, O Bride of God, who lie in the tomb of insensibility [f. 115rA] that with love I may glorify thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Arrayed in flesh, the Bodiless God, O All-Immaculate One, thou didst bring forth, Who delivers us that sing with fear: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having found thy pure prayer as a freely given healing, O Virgin, we ask for help, the good transformation of the soul, and health of body.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Unto Thee, the Creator of all, the youths in the furnace, having woven an all-beauteous choir, sang: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

O Portal of Light, open unto me the light-bearing gates of repentance, showing me the straight path of righteousness, leading up into the entrances of the divine will.

*Troparion:*

Anew upon earth, O Virgin Mother Maiden, hast thou given birth unto the Son, co-unoriginate with the Father, Who through ineffable compassion was likened unto us that were corrupted by sins.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

All-chosen, all-beauteous, whom God loved, whom He chose, hast thou appeared, O Virgin, ever All-Glorified. Therefore we sing of thee, O Pure One, unto the ages.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

All-pure, and full of divine glory, my companion, said the Spirit of God: proclaiming thee [f. 115vA] O Pure One, whom we hymn in songs, chanting: It is meet for thee to rejoice, who didst give birth unto God and Man!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Dance, O Isaiah! For the Virgin held in her womb and brought forth a Son, Emmanuel: both God and Man, the Orient is His name: Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed, O Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Cast not away now the sighs of those who hasten unto thee, O All-Immaculate One, but look upon us who are in sorrows: turn mourning into joy, and compunction into gladness, for those who sing of thee in hymns.

*Troparion:*

Thou art an immovable Foundation, O Pure One, having given birth unto the Savior, Who founded the earth upon the waters by divine commands: pray Him to establish upon Him us who radiantly glorify thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thy Childbirth is beyond mind, O Theotokos, for conception without a man was in thee, and thy Childbirth was virginal: for He Who was born is God: Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed who gavest birth unto Him.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Fearful art Thou, O Lord, and who shall then stand before Thy threat, O Christ, Only King, when Thou dost execute judgment? Therefore spare and save me, O Savior, by the acceptable supplications of her who gave birth unto Thee.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 5)

*On Monday at Compline,*[^68]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 5. Heirmos:*

Christ shook out into the Red Sea the horse and rider, He Who crushes wars with a high arm, and saved Israel, as he sang a song of victory.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Grant me a contrite spirit, O Good One, humility of heart, purity of mind, amendment of life, remission of transgressions, and fountains of tears, O All-Immaculate One.

*Troparion:*

Behold my affliction and compunction, and heal the ancient scars of transgressions, and grant me time for repentance, and the confession of sins.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

I weep for myself before my departure, pondering the abyss of my evils, O All-Immaculate One. Therefore I pray thee: entreat thy Son to deliver me from torments.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having time for repentance, depart from every evil, O my soul, and unto thy Creator cry aloud with tears: O my God, save me by the supplications of her who gave birth unto Thee!

[f. 116vA] *Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and didst suspend its unsupported weight: establish Thy Church upon the immovable Rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, Who alone art Good and the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

The princes of my passions have encompassed me, O Most Pure One, and me, who was created according to the image and likeness of God, they have filled with shame; but deliver me from their harm, who sing of thee in compunction.

*Troparion:*

Subtly the enemy strives to ensnare me, desiring to show me forth as fuel for eternal fire, O Most Pure One; but destroy his cunning wiles and counsels, that with joy I may glorify thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

In the lowest pit of many transgressions the enemies of the righteous have deceitfully cast me; being now helpless and wholly wounded, I invoke thy prayer, O All-Immaculate Sovereign Lady: save me!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

In sloth have I, the wretched one, spent my life; now I have drawn near unto the gates of death, and tremble at the snares of the enemy, crying aloud unto thee: deliver me from these unaccustomed trials, that being saved, I may glorify thee.

[f. 117rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Discerning Thy divine self-emptying, farsighted Habakkuk cried aloud unto Thee with trembling, O Christ: Thou hast come for the salvation of Thy people, to save Thine anointed.

*Troparion:*

I have been wounded by the arrows of sin, and now bear wounds all over my body; therefore I cry unto thee, O Most Pure One: by the swiftness of thy prayers, heal the wounds of my soul.

*Troparion:*

Have mercy upon thy servants, O Pure One: for we have acquired thee as Intercessor with God, and we pray to be delivered from every necessity and from eternal torment.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou art my hope, and shield, and confirmation, O Good One, and deliverance from grievous ills, and enlightenment of my soul, and praise, and wall, and stronghold.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Prodigally have I squandered the wealth of good works which Christ as the Good One bestowed upon me; despise me not, therefore, O Most Pure Maiden, who am perishing of hunger.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou Who clothest Thyself with light as with a garment: I rise early at dawn unto Thee, and cry unto Thee: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou alone art tenderhearted.

[f. 117vA] *Troparion:*

...Behold, O Sovereign Lady, the infirmity of my humble soul, and the weakness of my flesh, and the captivity of my mind, and save me who am beyond hope.

*Troparion:*

Ever pray God, Whom thou didst bear, to save all of us who confess thee to be Theotokos and glorify thy Childbirth, O Most Pure One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Who does not call thee blessed, O All-Immaculate One? For thou didst ineffably give birth unto the Creator of the whole world, the Savior Christ and Only Master.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Send down upon me streams of tears, O Most Pure One, with which having washed away the foulness and mire of my sins, I may sing of the abundance of thy grace.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Calm the sea of my passions, O Master Christ, which is troubled by a soul-destroying storm, and bring me up from corruption, as Thou art compassionate.

*Troparion:*

Into the depth of transgressions and iniquities have I been cast; but stretch forth thy hand, O All-Immaculate One, and bring me up from the hell of despair.

*Troparion:*

Thou knowest the multitude of sins and thoughts that trouble me. Therefore make haste and deliver me from them, O Most Pure One.

*Glory...*

[f. 118rA] *Troparion:*

...Spare me Thy servant, O Master Christ, through the prayers of her who purely gave birth unto Thee, when Thou shalt come to judge the world which Thou didst create.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Into the depth of transgressions has my soul been dragged down, O Pure Sovereign Lady, having become food for demons; therefore save me who am beyond hope.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^69]

Swiftly heal the grievously painful passions of my soul and the diseases of my flesh, stop the wanderings of my mind, O All-Immaculate One, and in the quiet of thought vouchsafe me to offer pure prayers unto the King of all, O Theotokos, and to ask forgiveness of transgressions.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Lord of the fathers, Who is exalted above all, quenched the flame, and bedewed the children who sang in unison: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

When I take into my mind my unseemly deeds and the multitude of transgressions, O All-Immaculate One, I fear and tremble; from them free me by thy prayers, O Virgin.

*Troparion:*

The sleepless worm and the unquenchable fire consume me continually and eat away my soul: may I not be delivered up unto them, O Most Pure Theotokos!

*Glory...*

[f. 118vA] *Troparion:*

...From outer darkness and fearful torment deliver me, thy servant, O All-Immaculate Virgin, who cry aloud unto thy Son: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

My flesh indeed have I defiled with shameful passions, and my mind have I darkened with foul thoughts; but have compassion, O Pure One, and save me, thine unprofitable servant.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Unto Thee, the Creator of all, the youths in the furnace, having woven an all-beauteous choir, sang: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Unto thee, O Theotokos, I bring the infirmity of my humble soul, and the disease of my heart, and the deception of my mind I make known, asking thy help, O Maiden.

*Troparion:*

Be merciful unto Thy servants, O Word, through the prayers of her who gave birth unto Thee, and save those who sing: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O Mother who didst give birth unto the Creator, have mercy upon all who sing with faith: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Deliver me from outer darkness and from the unsleeping worm, O Good Maiden, by thy prayers: for thou didst give birth unto the Creator of the world, Who does whatsoever He wills.

[f. 119rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Dance, O Isaiah! For the Virgin held in her womb and brought forth a Son, Emmanuel: both God and Man, the Orient is His name: Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed, O Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Diligently pray God, Whom thou didst ineffably bear, O Pure Sovereign Lady, to deliver us from misfortunes and sorrows and from the coming fearful Judgment, and vouchsafe us the radiance of His saints.

*Troparion:*

More than all have I sinned, having transgressed Thy life-creating commandments, O Christ, and I have become like the unreasoning beasts; but by the prayers of Thy Mother, O Word, pluck me not out from this life unrepentant.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

As having given birth unto the Abyss of mercy, God the Word: have mercy, O Good One, upon the souls of all who flee beneath thy protection; for we all have acquired thee as an unashamed Intercessor with God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Ask for us cleansing and forgiveness of transgressions, and relief from all necessities, and amendment of life that is now pure and radiant: that we may glorify thine abundant grace, O Mother of God.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON TUESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 5)

*On Tuesday at Compline,*[^70]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 5. Heirmos:*

Christ shook out into the Red Sea the horse and rider, He Who crushes wars with a high arm, and saved Israel, as he sang a song of victory.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Accept, O Theotokos, the prayers faithfully offered unto thee from the depth of the heart of thy servants, and bringing them unto thy Son, grant forgiveness of transgressions.

*Troparion:*

As truly being a Benefactress who enriches earthborn mortals and the Portal of salvation leading into life: cease not ever to intercede for all, O Virgin Mother of God, who pray unto thee.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Be inclined now by the supplications of those who sing of thee: as being a mighty help unto salvation, deliver those who honor thee from misfortunes by thy prayers, O Sovereign Lady, and preserve them whole.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Come quickly to our aid and stand before us warmly, O Most Pure Maiden; deliver and free from sorrow those who are compunctionate, who are sorely held by grievous afflictions.

[f. 120rA] *Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and didst suspend its unsupported weight: establish Thy Church upon the immovable Rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, Who alone art Good and the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Having illuminated the dark cloud of my heart with the divine lightning radiant with thy light, O Mother of God: show me forth as a son of the day, enlightened by virtues, O Guide of light unto our souls.

*Troparion:*

*Theotokion:*

By the ministry of thy prayers, O Virgin, consume the stripes, wounds, and festering sores of sin for me, and grant strength unto thy servant.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Calm the sea of passions, Master Christ, fiercely raging with a soul-destroying storm, and bring me up from corruption, as Thou art merciful.

*Troparion:*

Thou who didst give birth unto the Light, the Maker of luminaries: enlighten my soul now, and deliver me from the darkening of passions, O All-Hymned One.

[f. 131vA] *Troparion:*

Entreat Thy Son, O Birthgiver of God, Who of old sweetened the waters of Marah,[^85] swiftly to deliver me from grievous passion and bitterness.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

A torrent of passions troubles my soul, O Most Pure One; but dry it up by thy prayers, and consume the evil thoughts.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Delivering mankind, Christ came forth from Zion,[^86] and from thy womb, O all-immaculate Sovereign Lady; through Him deliver me also from adversities and sorrows.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^87]

Desponding in every way, O passionate soul, hast thou forgotten thy God Who has mercy upon thee, and despising His commandments, dost thou end thy life in prodigality with a harmed mind? Depart from evil, crying aloud unto the Birthgiver of God: Have mercy upon my despairing soul!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Lord God of the fathers, Who is exalted above all, quenched the flame and bedewed the children who sang in harmony: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:*

The demonic hosts are terrified and tremble at the invocation of thy name, O Most Pure One; delivering me from them, save and preserve me, sheltering me from every harm.

[f. 132rA] *Troparion:*

Ineffable is thy glory, O Virgin, for thou didst give birth unto the Lord of Glory; therefore vouchsafe me the glory of thy Son and my God, through thy prayers.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Incline Thyself unto the supplications of thy servant, O Sovereign Lady, and swiftly deliver me from misfortunes and sorrows, and from all temptations visible and invisible.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having been wholly muddied and defiled by the passions of shame, O Most Pure One, wash me with purity, and make me bright, O Virgin, by the sprinklings of thy prayers.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Unto the Creator of all, the youths in the furnace, having woven an all-beauteous choir, sang: All ye works of the Lord, sing the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

Make the barren womb of my soul barren and dried up to the bearing of passions, and rain down upon me the divine dew, O light cloud,[^88] that I may bear the fruits of repentance.

*Troparion:*

Calm the wave of passionate thoughts and the storm, O Most Pure One, by thy prayers, and guide me unto the course of dispassion: that I may warmly glorify thee unto all ages.

*Glory...*

[f. 132vA] *Troparion:*

Spiritual Door, and closed gate through which God alone passed:[^89] shut and bar the doors of my passions, and open unto me the gates of hope.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Take from me the burden of transgressions, O Virgin Mother: who alone hast ineffably given birth unto the Lamb of God and the Word,[^90] Who takes away all the sin of the whole world.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Dance, O Isaiah! For the Virgin held in her womb and brought forth a Son, Emmanuel: both God and Man, the Orient is His name:[^91] Whom magnifying, we call thee blessed, O Virgin!

*Troparion:*

Having defiled my soul as a man with many transgressions, and having been muddied with carnal passions, I now entreat thee earnestly and implore thee, O Virgin: cleanse me, O Pure One, by thy prayers, from every malice.

*Troparion:*

Thou hast given birth unto the God of mercy and compassions, the Lover of mankind, the only Good, Long-suffering, and Tenderhearted: show Him propitious unto me in good season, O Pure One, by thy supplications, and grant remission of transgressions.

*Glory...*

[f. 133rA] *Troparion:*

Raise up thy servant, O Sovereign Lady, who am now held fast by the slumber of despondency and asleep in pleasures, unto the virtues of good deeds, by thine unsleeping maternal prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

With blessings I bless thee, O Most Pure One, and warmly magnify thee: bless therefore me, thy singer, and deliver me from all grievous and sorrowful things, and preserve me unconquered by thy hands.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

[^64]: Saturday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Saturday evening liturgically inaugurates the Sunday of Tone 5, opening the weekly cycle of the Octoechos with the celebration of the Lord's Resurrection and the veneration of the Most Holy Theotokos.
[^65]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Saturday Compline in Tone 5 extols the Most Holy Theotokos as the fervent protection and merciful Mother of God the Most High.
[^66]: Sunday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Sunday evening liturgically introduces Monday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Bodiless Hosts (Angels) and repentance.
[^67]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Sunday Compline in Tone 5 expresses penitential mourning before the impending Judgment, entreating the Virgin's intercession for forgiveness.
[^68]: Monday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Monday evening liturgically introduces Tuesday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Holy Forerunner and Baptist John and to repentance.
[^69]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Monday Compline in Tone 5 implores the Mother of God to heal all soul-passions and bodily sicknesses and to quieten straying thoughts for pure prayer.
[^70]: Tuesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Tuesday evening liturgically introduces Wednesday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the commemoration of the Betrayal of Christ and the veneration of the Precious Cross.
[^71]: An allusion to Isaiah 6:6–7, where the live coal (*оугль*) taken from the altar purges the prophet's lips, patristically typifying Christ in the womb of the Virgin.
[^72]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Tuesday Compline in Tone 5 extols the Virgin through whose Son's Cross the power of idols and demons was overthrown.
[^73]: An allusion to the Song of Songs (Song 1:9, 15; 2:2, etc.), where the Bride is addressed as "my near one" or "my love" (*ближняя моя*), interpreted mariologically of the Theotokos.
[^74]: Wednesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Wednesday evening liturgically introduces Thursday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Holy Apostles and Saint Nicholas the Wonderworker.
[^75]: An allusion to the Parable of the Barren Fig Tree (Luke 13:6–9) and Christ's cursing of the barren fig tree (Matthew 21:18–19; Mark 11:12–14), a recurring patristic type of unrepentant sloth.
[^76]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Wednesday Compline in Tone 5 extols the Theotokos as the impregnable bulwark, the defeat of hostile barbarians, and the defense of the Orthodox faithful against visible and demonic adversaries.
[^77]: Thursday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Thursday evening liturgically introduces Friday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the commemoration of the Crucifixion and Passion of our Lord Jesus Christ.
[^78]: A patristic-typological title (*Трїста́та*) applied to the Mother of God as the mystical Tabernacle containing the Trinity's uncontainable Word, echoing the Exodus imagery of Exodus 14:7 and 15:4.
[^79]: The Stavrotheotokion (*Крестобогородиченъ*) Sessional Hymn of Thursday Compline in Tone 5 laments the voluntary Crucifixion of Christ between the two thieves as beheld by His grieving Mother.
[^80]: A precise dogmatic formula reflecting the Christological definitions of the Sixth Ecumenical Council (Constantinople III, 680–681), affirming two natural wills and two natural operations (*энергии*) in Christ, undivided and unconfused.
[^81]: Friday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Friday evening liturgically introduces Saturday, dedicated across the Octoechos to All Saints, the Holy Martyrs, and the commemoration of all the faithful departed.
[^82]: An allusion to the Magnificat (Luke 1:48): "For behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed."
[^83]: An allusion to Psalm 45:5 (LXX) / Psalm 46:4: "The streams of the river make glad the city of God, the holy place of the tabernacle of the Most High," applied mariologically to the Theotokos.
[^84]: An allusion to Isaiah 45:8: "Drop down dew, ye heavens, from above, and let the clouds shower righteousness," typifying the Virgin through whom the Sun of Righteousness descended.
[^85]: An allusion to Exodus 15:23–25, where the bitter waters of Marah were sweetened by the tree cast in by Moses, patristically typifying the Cross and the sweetening of bitter human passion through Christ.
[^86]: An allusion to Psalm 13:7 (LXX), Psalm 52:6, and Isaiah 59:20 (cf. Romans 11:26): "There shall come out of Zion the Deliverer."
[^87]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Friday Compline in Tone 5 urges the soul steeped in sin and despondency to turn in repentance to the Mother of God before the end.
[^88]: An allusion to Isaiah 19:1: "Behold, the Lord rides upon a swift cloud," interpreted by the Church Fathers of the Virgin Mary bearing Christ.
[^89]: An allusion to Ezekiel 44:1–3, the prophecy of the closed eastern gate through which only the Lord God of Israel enters and goes out, a premier Old Testament type of Mary's perpetual virginity.
[^90]: An allusion to John 1:29: "Behold the Lamb of God, which takes away the sin of the world."
[^91]: An allusion to Isaiah 7:14 and Matthew 1:23 (Emmanuel, "God with us"), combined with Zechariah 6:12 and Luke 1:78, where the Messiah is called "the Orient" or "Dayspring from on high" (*Востокъ* / Ἀνατολή).
