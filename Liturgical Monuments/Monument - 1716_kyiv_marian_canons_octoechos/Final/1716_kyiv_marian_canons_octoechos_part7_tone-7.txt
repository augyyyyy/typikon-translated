# TONE 7

## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 7)

*On Saturday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 7. Heirmos:*

Unto Him Who shattered wars by His arm, and engulfed the captains and horsemen: let us sing unto Him, as unto our Redeemer and God, for He is glorified![^178]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

As an adornment of song, and a hymn worthy of God, the gifts of thanksgiving do we offer unto thee, crying: Rejoice! now, O Pure One: for from sorrow thou hast bestowed joy upon us.

*Troparion:*

The grace of thy mercy, and the strength of thy protection, we shall not pass over in silence, O Most Pure Virgin, but proclaim it now: that from grievous perils thou hast saved us.

*Glory:*

[f. 159vA] *Troparion:*

...being delivered from diverse temptations and sorrows by thy maternal prayers, O Most Pure One: harmoniously and fervently singing all voices of thanksgiving unto thee, we cry aloud a song of victory.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Unbrokenly adorned with diverse virtues as with golden ornaments and the graces of the Spirit, O Pure One, as the Bride of the Father, thou didst truly appear as the Mother of God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

O Thou Who didst establish the Heavens by Thy word, and founded the base of the earth upon many waters: establish my mind unto Thy will, O only Lover of mankind.[^179]

*Troparion:*

The majesty of divine joy, as our rejoicing from the beginning, do we offer with voices of thanksgiving, fervently honoring thee as our Protectress.

*Troparion:*

Being delivered from afflictions through thee, and having obtained joy for thy sake, O Unwedded Mother: we all glorify thee as a good bestower and all-praised protectress.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Receiving relief from transgressions and trials by thy divine prayer, O Mother of Christ God, we faithfully sing of Thee with voices of thanksgiving as the cause of all good things.

*Both now:*

[f. 160rA] *Theotokion:*

She who is the spring of immortal joy continually pours forth streams of gladness and herself saves all: as our ever-living benefaction, the Mother of Christ God.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

The virtue of Thine ineffable wisdom, O Christ God, covered the Heavens by Thy dispensation, O Lover of mankind.[^180]

*Troparion:*

A worthy song, Rejoice, O Pure Virgin, do we offer rejoicing, having been delivered from afflictions by thy prayers.

*Troparion:*

Gratefully unto thee, O Pure Virgin, with the hands of the soul, we offer singing, leaping with divine songs, having been delivered from many sorrows.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Many troubles and passionate sins have risen up against us: but by thy divine protection, O Pure One, snatch us away.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Blessed truly are they that honor thee, O Most Pure Theotokos: for through thee have we been delivered from sin and sorrow.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

O Thou Who didst drive away the night of passions, cause the light of understanding to shine upon me: O Thou Who didst dispel the primordial darkness of the deep, bringing forth the first-created light unto the world, O Creator of all.[^181]

*Troparion:*

Thou didst bear Him Who takes away the sins of the faithful, O Virgin, through Whom the world was saved from trials and sicknesses. Therefore [f. 160vA] we also cry unto thee: Rejoice, having been delivered from sorrows.

*Troparion:*

Encompassed by diverse temptations, O Pure Sovereign Lady, by sorrow and affliction and grievous distress, and bereft of gladness, I have found thee as my hope. Glory:

*Troparion:*

As a treasury of salvation for us, thy faithful servants, O Pure One, thou drivest away temptations, and keepest us unharmed. Therefore, partakers of thy many good things, we thank thee with songs. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

We thank thee, having been delivered through thee from many sins, and weaknesses, and sicknesses, and grievous diseases, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady: for thou art a firm hope unto thy faithful servants.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

I fell into the depth of transgressions, O Good One; as Jonah from the whale, I cry unto Thee: Bring up my life from corruption, and save me, O Lover of mankind.[^182]

*Troparion:*

Angelic tongues cannot worthily sing thy praises, O Pure One; yet we now, undertaking it as servants, offer unto thee Gabriel's Rejoice!

*Troparion:*

From the depth of sorrow and distress into which we fell through our sins, we have been delivered through thee from want and temptations, O Virgin Theotokos Pure. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Thou hast made the whole world, O Pure One, rightly to give thanks, and praise, and glorify thy grace with piety:

[f. 161rA] for through thee have we been delivered from troubles and sorrows. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

By night and by day, openly and secretly, we flee beneath thy protection, O Most Pure Maiden, faithfully glorifying thee.

*Lord, have mercy (thrice). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

O Lord, we are Thy people, and the sheep of Thy pasture: as Shepherd, turn us back who have strayed; gather us who are scattered by corruption; have mercy on Thy flock, O Lover of mankind; have compassion on Thy people, through the prayers of the Theotokos, O only Sinless One.[^183]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Cast into the fiery furnace, the holy youths changed the fire into dew, and singing thus they sang: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers![^184]

*Troparion:*

A thanksgiving rejoicing do we offer unto thee, O Mother of God: for truly delivered through thee from every evil undertaking, we harmoniously sing unto thee: Blessed art thou!

*Troparion:*

In the evening weeping lodged with us amid sorrows and expectation of evils: but through thy glorious protection, O Virgin, being deified, we found joy in the morning; for thou hast saved us.[^185] Glory:

[f. 161vA] *Troparion:*

Having all acquired thy Protection before God as a divine refuge, in trials and persecutions and sins we flee unto thee, and through thee receive relief, O Most Pure One. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

The grace of thy prayer, O Pure One, we proclaim with lips and spirit, O glorious Lady: for for thy sake are we all delivered from temptations and storm and heavy sorrows, and from the passion of sin.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who is fearful unto the Cherubim, and marvelous unto the Seraphim, and Creator of the world: ye priests, servants, and spirits of the righteous, praise the Lord![^186]

*Troparion:*

Having been delivered from the storm of sin, and from passions and trials by thy prayers, O Good Theotokos, with thanksgiving voice we cry unto thee: Rejoice! as having passed through thee from sorrow into joy.

*Troparion:*

Despise not those held fast in sickness and temptations, O Good One: but hearing our poor supplication, liberate us from great afflictions, that we may faithfully sing of thy prayer, O Pure One. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O Cause of the remission of transgressions, raise us up now from sorrows, trials, and passions of men, and unseemly temptation; and through thy divine supplications, O Theotokos, deliver us from them most gloriously. Both now:

[f. 162rA] *Theotokion:*

Thy compassions are ever sent down upon all, O Christ, truly by the grace and supplications of her who bore Thee: for from Thee do we Christians receive Thy mercy, O Compassionate Savior.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

A Mother above custom, and a Virgin beyond custom, the only blessed among women: with hymns, ye faithful, let us worthily magnify her.[^187]

*Troparion:*

A rejoicing according to worth, with a voice of acclamation, do we offer unto thee now, O Virgin, with the Angel Gabriel, having been delivered from diverse temptations by thy prayers, O Theotokos.

*Troparion:*

Joy and gladness and divine rejoicing, O Virgin who knewest not man, God's own joy hath multiplied for us: for behold, we who were weeping bitterly now rejoice through thy prayers. Glory:

*Troparion:*

I will sacrifice a sacrifice of praise, and with tongue and voice fervently render unto thee a song of thanksgiving, O Virgin: so that praying unto thee in the day of sorrow, I am delivered through thee. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

We rejoice together with thy divine Nativity with piety, O Most Pure One: for it poured forth joy unto us from trials and sorrows. Therefore do we also thankfully offer a song unto thee, faithfully singing thy praise.

*It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after Our Father: the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

[f. 162vA] *(Woodcut: Decorative headpiece depicting the Nativity of the Most Holy Theotokos, with Saint Anne reclining and maidservants washing the newborn Virgin)*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 7)

*On Sunday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 7. Heirmos:*

At Thy nod, the nature of water, which before was poured forth, was changed into an earthly form, O Lord; therefore, traversing dryshod, Israel sings unto Thee a song of victory.[^188]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The ranks of the Angels truly honor thee as Mother of the God of all and their strength, O Most Pure One: but even now from my clay lips, receive a song of supplication.

*Troparion:*

Put to death the malice of my flesh which is still alive, O Most Pure One who didst give birth to the true Life, and unto thy servant grant deliverance from passions and transgressions, O Pure One. Glory:

*Troparion:*

The timeless Word, Sun from Sun, which shone forth incomprehensibly, shone forth in time: by His rays, O Virgin, enlighten my soul and give it understanding. Both now:

[f. 163rA] *Theotokion:*

A word of praise ever operative is fitting for thee, O Theotokos: yet we pray thee, O Pure One, save those who flee unto thee from every worldly sorrow.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

In the beginning, O Lord Savior, Who didst establish the Heavens by Thy all-powerful Word, and all their host by the all-creating and Divine Spirit: establish me upon the immovable rock of Thy confession.[^189]

*Troparion:*

Paying my debts, as befits a servant I have acknowledged thy graces, O Sovereign Lady, and fervently sing of thee, O Most Pure Mother of the Word and Redeemer of all: but even now deliver me from worldly distresses.

*Troparion:*

Adam who was slain of old by transgression, hast thou visibly accounted worthy of a better life, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, who didst give birth to the Life-originating Word: but even now liberate me from mortal sin. Glory:

*Troparion:*

The law of passions has grown hardened in my flesh, and now grieves the spiritual law, and unites my mind unto most grievous lusts, O Most Pure One: but grant me swiftly the peace of dispassion through thy prayers. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Ineffably having conceived in thy womb Christ, the Sustainer of all, O Most Pure One, [f. 163vA] thou hast loosed the substance of the earthborn from the gates of hades: but even now tear asunder the much-entangled bonds that corrupt me.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Not leaving the Father's Heavens, Thou didst descend upon the earth, O Christ God; I heard the mystery of Thy dispensation, and I glorified Thee, O only Lover of mankind.[^190]

*Troparion:*

By night I wrestle with the darkness of grievous things, having darkened my mind; O Gate of Light, illuminate me by thy rays, and deliver me swiftly from bitterness and sorrow.

*Troparion:*

Constrained by a multitude of sins, and greatly bound by the cords of temptations, I call upon thy help now, O Sovereign Lady, that I may be delivered from every torment. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Destroy the counsels of the gatherings of evil, fierce, envious, and crafty men, O All-immaculate One: and deliver me from every sorrow of this life, and affliction. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Cut down with the sword of thy prayers the turnaround of deceitful words and workings against me, O Sovereign Lady, and destroy them swiftly, and deliver me from every sorrow.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Night is without light, O Christ, unto unbelievers, but unto the faithful there is enlightenment in the sweetness of Thy words: therefore early do I rise unto Thee, and sing of Thy Divinity.[^191]

[f. 164rA] *Troparion:*

Being an invincible Wall unto the faithful, be a protection unto thy servant, O Virgin, and deliver me from every dire sorrow and affliction.

*Troparion:*

I am exceedingly sick, grievously afflicted on both sides, O Most Pure One: heal by thy prayers the weakness of soul and body which I have undergone. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Into the deep of thy compassions, O Virgin, I, thy servant, flee now, that I may be delivered from the hostile counsel holding me fast, and from affliction. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Exalted is the glory of thy prayers everywhere, O Virgin Mother Most Pure: through them deliver me now from every assault of this life.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Sailing upon the storm of life's billows, sinking with the ship through sins, and cast unto the soul-corrupting beast: as Jonah, O Christ, I cry unto Thee: Bring me up from the death-bearing depth![^192]

*Troparion:*

The sayings and words of all faithfully praise thee, O Pure One, for the debt of all men was visibly loosed by thy Nativity. But even now receive the prayer of thy singers, and deliver us from temptations and sorrows.

*Troparion:*

Sick as I am, and pricked by the passionate and sinful thorns, seeking healing from thee, O Most Pure One: deliver me from every sickness and affliction, and save me from temptations and sorrows. Glory:

[f. 164vA] *Troparion:*

Having glorified the nature of the earthborn, thou didst lead it up to Heaven, O Theotokos, having become the Palace of the King of Glory. Therefore bring me up from the abyss of many transgressions, and temptations, and passions. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst give birth to the Word incarnate beyond mind and word, O Pure Virgin, Who delivered us from all irrationality. Therefore with divine words do we unceasingly sing of thee and faithfully glorify thee.

*Lord, have mercy (thrice). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

O Sovereign Lady, thy Son is angered against thy sinful servants, for we have moved the Compassionate One unto wrath: but turn Him unto mercy, O Pure One; take compassion, O Mother of God, entreat the Tender-hearted One by thine unceasing supplications, and deliver the captives.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Cast into the fiery furnace, the holy youths changed the fire into dew, and singing thus they sang: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers![^193]

*Troparion:*

Adorned with fringes of purity, O Most Pure Maiden: turn my soul now from defiled malice unto divine beauty, O Blessed Sovereign Lady, Protectress of our race.

*Troparion:*

Demonic malice holds me fast exceedingly now, and they kindle within me the fire of passions; but rather by thy rays burn them utterly by thy compassions, O Protectress of our race. Glory:

[f. 165rA] *Troparion:*

In praying unto thy Son for us, O Most Pure Mother of God, cease not at all by thy prayers to snatch thy servants from the trial of human sorrows. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

The Transubstantial Son immutably took flesh from thee, O Virgin, and became Man, that He might save those who sing: Blessed, O Most Pure One, is the Fruit of thy womb!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Refreshed in the furnace, the youths cry out: Let all creation bless the Word Who created all things, and exalt Him above all unto all ages.[^194]

*Troparion:*

Passionate pastures and manifold troubles truly encompass thy servant, O Sovereign Lady: but deliver me from every temptation thereof.

*Troparion:*

May thy mighty hand come upon us now, O Sovereign Lady, and deliver us from the expected human afflictions. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Break the snares of crafty and evil-counseling men, O Pure Birthgiver of God, and deliver thy servant from every dire necessity. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

O only divine haven existing for the faithful, O Virgin Pure: I pray thee, deliver me who am in the sea of life from the bitter storm.

[f. 165vA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Rejoice, radiant Bride, Mother of the Giver of Light; Rejoice, thou who didst contain the Incontainable in thy womb; Rejoice, thou who art more honorable than the Cherubim themselves, having given birth to the Savior of our souls.[^195]

*Troparion:*

I have pranced like an unbroken steed away from the Master's commandments; by the bridles of thy prayers, O Most Pure One, may I be restrained unto the path of swift ordering.

*Troparion:*

I have been dragged down exceedingly by senseless stumbling unto the working of my crafty sin: deliver me, O Theotokos, from a worse change. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O thou who didst appear as the first healing of human nature, heal the crushing of Adam and Eve: heal me also, O Theotokos, through thy supplications. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Grant me a chaste mind, a pure heart, and a vigilant understanding, that I may keep, O Sovereign Lady, the most pure commandments of my God.

*It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after Our Father: the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

[f. 166rA] *(Woodcut: Decorative headpiece depicting a winged cherub head surrounded by floral branches)*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 7)

*On Monday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 7. Heirmos:*

Unto Him Who shattered wars by His arm, and engulfed the captains and horsemen: let us sing unto Him, as unto our Redeemer and God, for He is glorified![^196]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Thee, the cause of our salvation and divine redemption, do we entreat, O Most Pure One: pray that we may be saved.

*Troparion:*

I am wholly in grievous temptations, in troubles and transgressions; I cry unto thee, O Most Pure One: save me, thy servant. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Thee, the pure Virgin and Mother of the Creator, do we entreat: deliver us, the faithful, from every affliction. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

O thou who beyond word didst give birth in the flesh to the Word, Christ our God, the Author of life: entreat Him that we may be saved.

[f. 166vA] *Ode 3. Heirmos:*

The Church of Christ is established in faith, which unceasingly with hymns sings, crying: Holy art Thou, O Lord, Who savest our souls![^197]

*Troparion:*

Having walked the path of all-ruinous malice, I found not the way of my salvation: guide me unto it, O All-immaculate Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

My strength and establishment, and help, and only hope do I inscribe thee, O All-immaculate One: be thou my Protectress in the day of my departure. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Ever praying unto thee, O All-immaculate One: stand by me at the hour of my death, and deliver thy servant from torment. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Incomprehensibly born of the Virgin, our Savior appeared as He willed, and Thou hast renewed the world, O Lord.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I heard the report of Thee and was afraid; I understood Thy works and was amazed, O Lord.[^198]

*Troparion:*

Having transgressed the commandments of my Master, I have appeared an easy prey unto enemies: deliver me, O Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

At the hour of death deliver and save my passionate soul from the demons, O Most Pure One. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Loose the bonds of my transgressions, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, and be Mediatress unto eternal life for me through thy prayers. Both now:

[f. 167rA] *Theotokion:*

We sing of thee as Virgin even after childbearing, and glorify thee, O Most Pure One, as Mother of our God.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Rising early unto Thy glory and praise, O Christ, we ever praise the sign of Thy Cross, which Thou hast given us as a weapon of help.[^199]

*Troparion:*

Haven unto those grievously tossed in the storm, Gate of salvation unto those saved through faith: O Good Virgin, save me, thy servant.

*Troparion:*

Show forth thy manifold compassions richly upon me, thy servant, O Virgin Theotokos, delivering me from the fearful future judgment. Glory:

*Troparion:*

The unstable night of passions holds fast my soul and sends it into the ruinous abyss; with the light of thy prayer, O Theotokos, illuminate and save me, thy servant. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

O Most Pure Virgin Full of Grace, who in time beyond word didst give birth to the Word: entreat Him to save our souls.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Jonah cried from the belly of hades: Liberate my life from corruption! And we cry unto Thee: O Almighty Savior, have mercy on us![^200]

*Troparion:*

In temptations we ever have thee as a firm protectress, in afflictions strong salvation, rest in sorrow, and help in troubles, O Virgin.

[f. 167vA] *Troparion:*

Appear unto me as guardian in this life, O Most Pure One; deliver me from demons at the hour of death, and give me rest after death. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Thou art the beauty of the honorable Holy Angels, the rejoicing of all men, O Theotokos Virgin: guide me unto life. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Heal my wretched soul, O All-immaculate One, which is incurably sick with the deceits of this life and the closing of many doors.

*Lord, have mercy (thrice). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

In the tender compassion of Thy mercy, O Christ God, Thou wast pleased to become incarnate of the Holy Virgin: for her sake preserve our life, as Lover of mankind.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Thou didst cool the burning furnace, O Savior, and saved the youths who cried aloud and said: Blessed art Thou unto ages, O Lord God of our fathers![^201]

*Troparion:*

Calling to mind the multitude of my transgressions, I fall into despair, O All-immaculate Virgin. Therefore do I cry unto thee: Help me, that I may not perish utterly.

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee to be the Mother of Life, O Pure One, I cry unto thee: Deliver me from the death of the soul, and account me worthy of eternal life. Glory:

[f. 168rA] *Troparion:*

Deliver from troubles, passions, sicknesses, affliction, and the sins of this life, and from the unquenchable eternal fire, those who faithfully honor thee with hymns, O All-immaculate One. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Every tongue glorifies thee, O Pure One, who art the glory and praise of our race, and Guide of the strayed, O all-hymned Theotokos.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who is fearful unto the Cherubim, and marvelous unto the Seraphim, and Creator of the world: ye priests, servants, and spirits of the righteous, praise the Lord![^202]

*Troparion:*

Behold, I, most wretched one, have spent my life in slothfulness: and now I am in perplexity and have drawn near unto the end; help me, O Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

Refuge of sinners, uprighting of the fallen art thou, O Sovereign Lady: therefore do I flee unto thy protection; save me. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Possessing unslumbering prayer, O All-immaculate Sovereign Lady of the world, deliver from the future judgment those who in faith venerate thine icon. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Every tongue sings of thee, glorifying thee: for thou, O Virgin Bride of God, didst give birth to the All-hymned God; entreat Him unceasingly to save the souls of those who sing of thee.

[f. 168vA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

A Mother above nature, and a Virgin in nature, the only blessed among women: with divinely inspired hymns, ye faithful, let us magnify her.[^203]

*Troparion:*

All who flee in faith unto thy tender mercy are delivered from the sorrowful troubles of this life. Therefore I also have fled unto thy protection, O Theotokos.

*Troparion:*

Mighty Protectress, invincible wall unto the sorrowful, O Pure One: save me from the passions of sins, and from the everlasting fire. Glory:

*Troparion:*

With the radiant beams of the Word Who shone forth from thee, O Pure Virgin, illuminate me and save me as thou art Good, and snatch me from torments. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou bearest in thy holy hands Him Who bears all things: entreat Him, O Pure One, to save us who are not held fast by the adversary's wickedness.

*It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after Our Father: the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON TUESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 7)

*On Tuesday at Small Compline,*

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 7. Heirmos:*

At Thy nod, the nature of water, which before was poured forth, was changed into an earthly form, O Lord; traversing wholly dryshod, Israel sings unto Thee a song of victory.[^204]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Still the storm of my pleasures and passions by the calm of thy divine supplication, O All-immaculate One, that with a tranquil heart I may glorify thine ineffable Childbearing.

*Troparion:*

Behold, thy Childbearing became the desire and sweetness of salvation, O Sovereign Lady Theotokos: drive away from thy servant every bitterness arising from the passions of transgressions. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Deliver me, O Most Pure One, from demonic deception, and affliction, and harm, and temptation: that in faith I may glorify thee, who after God art my protectress and protection. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst appear unto Moses at the bush, O Word of God, as a fire that burns and yet consumes not at all:

[f. 169vA] prefiguring Thy Childbearing of the Virgin, for whose sake Thou didst fashion man.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

In the beginning, O Lord Savior, Who didst establish the Heavens by Thy all-powerful Word, and all their host by the all-creating and Divine Spirit: establish me upon the immovable rock of Thy confession.[^205]

*Troparion:*

Entreat fervently the King Who was lifted up upon the Cross and cast down hades, on behalf of those who ever faithfully sing of thee with sacred voices and hymns, O Hope of the world and Defense of the faithful.

*Troparion:*

Higher than the Heavens, and more glorious than the Cherubim, having conceived thou didst give birth to God: therefore entreat Him to deliver me from transgressions and fearful condemnation, by thy merciful supplication and thy power beyond word. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O thou who through thy Childbearing hast changed men from the corruption of death, O Sovereign Lady: deliver me from the corruption of passions and sicknesses, and grievous transgressions, and grant me unaging life through thy divine intercession. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Immutably made like unto men in all things, O Word of God, Thou didst visibly come forth from the Pure Virgin, and unto all didst reveal the all-radiant Godhead, immutable in hypostases in one undifferentiated Essence.[^206]

[f. 170rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Not leaving the Father's Heavens, Thou didst descend upon the earth, O Christ God; I heard the mystery of Thy dispensation, and I glorified Thee, O Lover of mankind.[^207]

*Troparion:*

Stretch forth thy mercy and compassion upon thy servants, O Pure One, who ever pray unto thee, delivering them from fearful torment through thy prayers.

*Troparion:*

With thy pure prayer and fervent faith I flee unto thee, O All-immaculate One: be thou the cleansing-place of my many transgressions, delivering and saving me. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Mediatress of our joy hast thou appeared by thy Childbearing, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady: entreat Him, O All-immaculate One, to deliver me from temptations, and to account me worthy of eternal life. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Not parting from the Father's Bosom, yet incarnate of the Virgin, O Christ God: preserve the flock of Thy dispensation, which worships in godly manner.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Night is without light, O Christ, unto unbelievers, but unto the faithful there is enlightenment in the sweetness of Thy words: therefore early do I rise unto Thee, and sing of Thy Divinity.[^208]

*Troparion:*

I pray thee, O Pure Virgin, deliver my soul from dark sin, and illuminate it with divine [f. 170vA] beam of repentance, through the mercy of thy supplication.

*Troparion:*

Being in the cruel abyss of tribulations and oppressive passions, O Most Pure Consolation: by thy prayers and protection deliver and save those who praise thee. Glory:

*Troparion:*

Having conceived God in a virginal womb and given birth, O Most Pure Theotokos: deliver those who sing of thee from all eternal condemnation. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Knowing thee to be the Mother of God, O Pure One, and our Protectress: we present thee as the Mediatress of our salvation unto the bountiful Lord.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Given from us unto the Master as a gift from the firstfruits of human nature, receive our supplications, O Most Pure One, returning unto us the fair gifts of thy help: that unceasingly we may sing of Him Who was born of thee, and supremely exalt Him unto the ages.

[f. 182rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Mother of God and Virgin, giving birth and remaining a virgin still: this is not a work of nature, but of God's condescension; therefore, as the only one accounted worthy of divine wonders, thee, O Pure One, do we unceasingly magnify.[^250]

*Troparion:*

Under thy Protection, O Virgin, have I now fled; save me who am overwhelmed by a multitude of transgressions and perishing, O thou who didst give birth unto the Savior and Creator, and deliver me from eternal condemnation.

*Troparion:*

Entreat God the Creator and Lord, Who was incarnate from thy most pure blood, on our behalf, O Pure One, that in tender mercy He may have compassion upon thy despondent people. Glory:

*Troparion:*

O Most Holy Pure Theotokos, deliver me, who am worthy of the unquenchable fire, the outer darkness, the eternal weeping, and condemnation, through thy fervent intercession unto the Lord. Both now:

*Theotokion:*

Preserved under thy grace, O Theotokos, I remain unconsumed by the assaults of adversary foes; therefore, as a divine confirmation and fortress, I sing of thee, and bow down, and magnify thee.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*
