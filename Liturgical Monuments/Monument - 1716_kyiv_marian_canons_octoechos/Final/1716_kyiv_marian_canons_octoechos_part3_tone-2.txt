# TONE 2

## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 2)

[f. 28rA] *On Saturday at Small Compline,*[^18]

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*(Which let those who are slandered, in sorrows and in tribulations chant)*

*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

In the deep of old, the overwhelming power drowned all Pharaoh's host; and the Incarnate Word consumed all-wicked sin: the supremely glorious Lord, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Readily attentive unto all in afflictions, and helping in sorrow, O Good Theotokos: grant grace unto those who dare to hymn thee, O Sovereign Lady, Joy of the sorrowful!

*Troparion:*

Having acquired an abundant grace, O Sovereign Lady: by thy bold prayer marvelously deliver me from tribulations, thy poor servant, O Joy of the sorrowful!

*Glory...*

[f. 28vA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Deliver us, we pray thee, who flee unto thee, O Theotokos, from enemies visible and invisible, and destroy every counsel of those who war against us.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Take away the reproach of men and the slanders of conspirators from me, O Theotokos, I pray thee, that with zeal I may glorify the Lord Whom thou didst nourish.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Establish us through Thyself, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put transgressions to death, and plant Thy fear in our hearts, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Destroy the conspiratorial wiles of enemies, O All-Immaculate Theotokos, fail not with thy prayers, guarding us who praise thee.

*Troparion:*

Look down, O Pure One, with thy merciful eye, and deliver me from every plot of enemies visible and invisible, blinding the pupils of their eyes.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Quench the evil assault of enemies which burns like fire, who ever seek to destroy us, O Virgin, with the dew of thy prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Unquenchable Lamp, Ray shining ever: enlighten me who am held fast by the night of sorrows by thy prayers, O thou who didst give birth to the Sun of glory, Christ!

[f. 29rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I heard, O Lord, Thy glorious dispensation, and I glorified Thee, O Merciful One, Thine incomprehensible power.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

We pray thee, the bridge of salvation, sleepless prayer, firm protection: have tender mercy and behold our unbearable grief, diseases, afflictions, passions, and visit us in good season, O Mother of God, granting speedy joy!

*Troparion:*

We are not deprived, O Sovereign Lady, of thy protection in sorrows. Therefore now also help us speedily, who are grievously burdened, stretching forth thy hand, O Pure One; be the healing of our diseases, O Mother of God, granting speedy joy!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The lawless have not hoped in thee, O Sovereign Lady, but have hoped in a boastful tongue, in a deceitful tongue, which is ever enviously sharpened, roaring unjustly to shed the blood of a neighbor; but do thou, O Pure One, break their jawbones!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Subdue, O Sovereign Lady, the exalted neck of the enemies who boast greatly, and their counsels and concealments of malice, who prompt their hearts unto evil things against me all day long; but grant strength

[f. 29vA] *Theotokion (continued):*

and victory unto those who call upon thee, O Mother of God, granting speedy joy!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

The glowing coal having appeared, the Sun shone forth from the virginal womb, granting the enlightenment of the knowledge of God unto those who wander in darkness.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

True Intercessor, Hope of Christians, O Full of Grace: accept supplications from us who diligently call upon thee and pray unto thee.

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee as the Fount of life, O Pure One, which pours out the waters of immortality, we earthborn call thee blessed.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The enemy has armed himself subtly against us, desiring to destroy us with his tongue as with a sword; but anticipate him by thy might, O Birthgiver of God!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Who shall recount the abyss of thy protections, O Pure One, and the strength of thy power? Therefore speedily anticipate us who are in distress.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy tender mercy: bring me up from corruption, O my God!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Being the Mediatress of compassions, appear now unto those who call upon thee, and deliver us from all tribulations and perils, O Bride of God!

[f. 30rA] *Troparion:*

Destroy the evil deeds of the enemy, and stop the slanders of the unjust, O Blessed and All-Pure One, delivering the innocent from sorrows.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Surrounded by cruel sins and overwhelmed by perilous distresses, we flee under thy divine Protection, O Mother of Christ God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having given birth to the Incomprehensible Lord, thou wast shown to be a Virgin again after childbirth. O the marvelous wonder wrought in thee, O Bride of God!

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*

Fervent prayer, and saving insurmountable rampart, fountain of mercy, refuge for the world: earnestly we cry unto thee: O Theotokos and Sovereign Lady, anticipate us, and deliver us from perils, who alone art a speedy protectress.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Thy marvelous Nativity, O Christ, from the Virgin prefiguring clearly, Thou didst preserve the youths unburnt in the furnace, who sang in hymns: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Ark of tender mercy, O Pure Virgin! for thou loosest measureless griefs and tribulations of those who call upon thee in distress and circumstance. Therefore now also help those who praise thee, O Blessed One.

[f. 30vA] *Troparion:*

Manifest thy speedy protection, manifest it, for thou art able as the Mother of God, unto those who call upon thee from the heart, falling down with tears: speedily soothe also the suffering of thy servants without delay.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The mouths of men, like cruel lions, having concealed a grave, were opened bitterly to swallow me up: but do thou, O Theotokos, having appeared as the hope of the hopeless, cast down their strength, O Blessed One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Let the enemies see and be put to shame, let them understand and behold thy power contending against them on our behalf: and cast them down into the lowest pit, O Blessed One, hope of the hopeless.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who descended into the fiery furnace unto the Hebrew youths, and changed the flame into dew, God: sing, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him supreme unto all ages.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Our refuge, and the rejoicing of the world, O Mother of God: make haste with tenderheartedness, and speedily grant thy grace unto us who are afflicted, and protect thy servants, O Good One.

[f. 31rA] *Troparion:*

A cruel counsel have the assemblies of the godless cruelly taken against us, as of old Ahithophel;[^19] but we cry aloud: by thy prayers break it in pieces; cast down their strength, O Theotokos.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Unto those who call upon thee, O Theotokos, from the soul in perplexity, in every tribulation, and in diverse sicknesses, and cruel perils: speedily give ear, O Sovereign Lady, delivering them ever by thy prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

May thy name, O Theotokos, be glorified on earth: for He Who is born of thee granted thee as a mighty hope and rampart unto us sinners; for through thee every breath has recourse unto God.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Every tongue is at a loss to praise thee worthily, and even an intellect from the world above is in perplexity to hymn thee, O Theotokos: nevertheless, being Good, accept our faith, for thou knowest our divine love; for thou art the protectress of Christians, and thee do we magnify.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Let every tongue that devises deceit be stopped; let lying lips be silenced, and mouths that speak iniquity unjustly against the Righteous One, through pride and hostile envy together, O Theotokos, by thy prayer and that of the saints of Christ.

[f. 31vA] *Troparion:*

Watchful in prayers, the pure Birthgiver of God, do we all invoke, held fast by sickness and sorrow, crying aloud: O Pure Sovereign Lady, speedily deliver thy servants ever from the afflictions that hold us fast, for after God we have no other protectress.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

A great refuge unto those who sail, a calm haven unto those tossed by storms art thou, O Theotokos. Therefore we also have recourse unto thee, crying aloud: Let us not be put to shame, O Mother of true life: but with Thanksgiving and fervor let us magnify thee.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Accept, O All-Pure Maiden, this divine hymn, bestowing grace upon those who hope in thee, and unceasingly ask peace to be sent unto the Churches, and unto our sovereign ruler victories and triumphs: that every Christian nation may magnify thee.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: after the Our Father: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 2)

[f. 32rA] *On Sunday at Small Compline,*[^20]

*As indicated on Saturday at Small Compline.*

*Supplicatory Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

Of old the all-surpassing power drowned the entire host of Pharaoh in the deep; and the Incarnate Word consumed all-destructive sin: for the Lord is exceedingly glorious, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Being formed out of thee on our behalf, the Unwaning Sun manifestly showed thee to be a rational and most luminous Heaven. Therefore deliver me from the stench of passions: that with love I may hymn thee, the only Mother and Virgin.

*Troparion:*

Having carried the noetic Coal, as with tongs,[^21] and having cleansed human nature, I pray thee, O Sovereign Lady: cleanse the filth of my many transgressions, and deliver me from defiling passions by thy prayers.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having given birth to Christ, the Fountain of mercy, O All-Pure One, and having opened Eden unto men: open unto me the doors of thy mercy, O Theotokos, and grant forgiveness of sins, O Sovereign Lady of the world.

*Both now...*

[f. 32vA] *Theotokion:*

Sunder the bonds of my transgressions, O Sovereign Lady, praying unto thy Son and God, and cast down the sin that torments me ever, O Virgin: that being saved I may hymn thee always, O All-Immaculate One.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Having established me upon the rock of faith, Thou hast enlarged my mouth against my enemies; for my spirit rejoices in singing: There is none holy as our God, and there is none righteous beside Thee, O Lord!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Drive away the mist of my passions and my darkening by the rays of thy prayers, and enlighten me, O All-Pure Sovereign Lady, who didst give birth to Christ Who shone forth before the morning star, the Unwaning Sun from the Sun.

*Troparion:*

By thy childbearing, O All-Pure One, thou hast raised up the fallen and shattered tabernacle of David thy forefather. Therefore raise me up also, O Theotokos, thy servant who am shattered by passions and transgressions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Be unto me a weapon, O Virgin, driving away sins that consume my soul, and show me forth as a second Paradise, free from assault and guarded by thee, bearing the flowers of the Spirit.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having stretched out the hand of tender compassion, receive me, O Mother of God, and snatch from every condemnation him who flees unto thee and falls down before thee, and calls upon thy help.

[f. 33rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I hymn Thee, O Lord, for I heard Thy report and was afraid; for Thou didst come unto me, seeking me who had strayed: therefore I glorify Thy great condescension unto me, which is my renewal.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having given birth to the Fountain of dispassion, heal me who am wounded by many passions, O All-Immaculate Maiden, and snatch me from eternal fire, O only God-graced One.

*Troparion:*

By the remedy of thy prayers, O Sovereign Lady, and with plaster and oil, heal now the bruises of my soul and sinful wounds, O Pure One: as having given birth to the divine ember of nature.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Loose the bonds of my passions, O Sovereign Lady, and tear asunder the chains of my sins with the weapon of thy prayers, who didst give birth to Christ Who grants deliverance unto those bound in hades.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Heal the passions of my soul which have most wretchedly darkened me, and enlighten my mind, driving far away, O Pure One, the darkness of demons that grieve me and spread nets to bring me into distress.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Enlightenment of those lying in darkness, and salvation of the despairing, O Christ my Savior: early unto Thee do I awake, O King of peace; enlighten me with Thy radiance, for we know no other God beside Thee.

[f. 33vA] *Troparion:*

As thou didst deliver the world from the ancient curse, O Sovereign Lady, having given birth to Christ, the blessing for the world: deliver me from improper thoughts and from every sorrow; for thou alone art the rejoicing of the faithful.

*Troparion:*

A demonic host surrounds the city of my soul, and attempts vehemently to despoil it, O All-Glorious Sovereign Lady, City of the King of all; defend me with the towered walls of thy prayers, and save me.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

A cruel tempest of corruptions of evil spirits troubles my mind: anticipate speedily, and calm it, who didst give birth to Christ the Almighty, the only Helmsman of all creation.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Make the light of repentance shine upon me, O Mother of God, who am shut up in deadly darkness; stretch out a helping hand unto him who lies prostrate, and by thy prayers raise me up, O Pure One, to do the statutes of God.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy tender mercy: bring me up from corruption, O God!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having given birth to eternal Life, O All-Pure One: when my soul departs from the body, put death to death by thy prayers.

[f. 34rA] *Troparion:*

The man-hating serpent hunts to devour me; render him powerless, O Sovereign Lady, as thou didst bear Him Who crushed the heads of the serpents.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Me who have walked crooked paths, direct unto the straight path, who didst give birth to the Way of life, Christ our God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Heal the passions of my soul, and overcome the perplexity of grief, O All-Pure One, who didst bear God for the salvation of men.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*

Being a fountain of tender mercy, vouchsafe mercy unto us, O Theotokos; look upon the people who have sinned; manifest thy power as always: for hoping in thee, we cry aloud unto thee, Rejoice! as once did Gabriel, the Supreme Commander of the Bodiless Powers.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The all-wise youths served not the golden image, but went themselves into the flame, and mocked their gods. Amid the flame they cried aloud, and an Angel bedewed them, saying: The prayer of your lips has been heard.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

A wave of passions troubles my mind and shakes my soul: but do thou who didst bear the Lord, Who by divine command bridles the surging sea, O Virgin:

[f. 34vA] *Troparion:*

turn it unto the calm of dispassion, and stay the agitation of the flesh.

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee as the portal of the noetic Orient, which appeared from Heaven, I thy servant pray: may I enter Heaven through thee, O Virgin. Therefore receive me, O Sovereign Lady, and guide me by the light.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

By His voluntary crucifixion upon the Cross, Christ thy Son put to shame the principalities of darkness, and destroyed the corrupting power of death: through Whom, O All-Pure Sovereign Lady, put to death the passionate mind of my flesh.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Cease not to pray, O Pure One, unto God Who was born of thee, for those who flee with faith unto thee and ask deliverance, the only Protectress of the world, hope and defense of the hopeless, a constant help unto those distressed in cruel afflictions.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Looking not upon the golden image, the all-glorious youths, seeing the immutable living image of God, sang amid the flame: Let all material creation hymn the Lord, and exalt Him supreme unto all ages.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

O Tabernacle fashioned by God, being the Holy of Holies, having within thee the Great High Priest Who revealed the type of the ancient tabernacle: show forth my heart, O Virgin, as a holy Tabernacle of Christ the King of all of us on high.

[f. 35rA] *Troparion:*

O divine Ark who didst carry the tablets of the Covenant, thy Son Christ Who cannot be circumscribed, O Virgin, the All-Pure Word of God: grant through thy prayers that He may be written upon the tablet of my soul by the finger of God.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

From thy womb, O All-Immaculate Sovereign Lady, was cut the precious Stone as from a mountain, and as the only Almighty Ruler He crushed the steps of every delusion: through Whom do thou now destroy the images of passions of my soul, having crushed the jaws of noetic enemies.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

With living waters do thou refresh my heart which has withered through the flame of sin, O Virgin Theotokos, preserving me ever in compunction as with fear I sing: Let all creation hymn the Lord, and exalt Him supreme unto all ages.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The Luminary, God Who shone forth before the sun, Who came unto us in the flesh from the Virgin's womb, ineffably incarnate: O Blessed and Pure One, thee, O Theotokos, do we magnify.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The Sun of righteousness, Christ, didst thou cause to shine forth, O Pure One, and wast shown to be a most luminous Heaven, through Whom the night of my passions [f. 35vA] *Troparion:*

scatter by thy prayers, and enlighten my soul, and illuminate my heart compunctiously.

*Troparion:*

Judge, O Theotokos, and plead the cause against my cruel adversary, and deliver me from his affliction: for thou didst bear the Good and Righteous Judge, Who in the depths of the Godhead condemned death.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

He Who as God brought the incorporeal beings into existence, was incarnate of thee, O Virgin Maiden. Therefore deliver me thy servant from the encirclement of bodily passions and from carnal wisdom by thy prayers.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou didst loose the sorrow of Eve, having given birth to the New Adam, Who created Adam's nature of old: therefore also loose the handwriting of my transgressions, delivering me from tribulations, sorrows, and passions, O Virgin Unwedded.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 2)

[f. 36rA] *On Monday at Small Compline,*[^22]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

Of old the all-surpassing power drowned the entire host of Pharaoh in the deep; and the Incarnate Word consumed all-destructive sin: for the Lord is exceedingly glorious, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having blossomed an ever-blooming wreath of praise in the Spirit, the Church, O Pure One, offers it unto thee, the Bride of God, in song, singing with the Archangel Gabriel: Rejoice! and purely crowning thee, O Queen full of grace.

*Troparion:*

Having cultivated the life-bearing Vine, Christ God, O All-Hymned Mother of God, holy land of the Father, thou hast appeared beyond understanding, O God-graced One, and hast watered the whole world with a life-bearing drink, O Pure One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Cruelly surged about and drowning in the transgressions of the whole world, thou didst bear, O All-Pure One, God and Lord of all of us, and Christ the Helmsman, Who faithfully directs us unto the saving haven.

*Both now...*

[f. 36vA] *Theotokion:*

A refuge and city of salvation, all we the faithful pray unto thee, Mary, Mother of God, and warmly beseech thee: accept our supplications, of thy faithful servants, and loose us all from the condemnation of transgressions.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Establish us through Thyself, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put to death transgressions; and plant Thy fear in our hearts, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The beginning of the turning away of God from men was the deception of Eve; but the Holy Theotokos brought God back unto us again.

*Troparion:*

The glorious Joachim gives birth unto thee for the world, the treasure of life, O All-Hymned One, and rejoices in goodly offspring concerning thee: as hope having come through barren parents.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

From Anna joy blossomed forth unto the world: for as a Virgin thou didst bear the King, and our race rejoices together with thee, having been loosed from the ancient curse.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The bush in Sinai prefigured thy marvelous Nativity, O Virgin: for thou wast unburnt by the fire of the Godhead, having received Him in thy womb, O Pure One.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I heard, O Lord, Thy glorious dispensation, and I glorified Thy unsearchable power, O Compassionate One.

[f. 37rA] *Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

As a ladder set up, in a manner befitting God, did Jacob foresee thee, O Virgin, upon the summit whereof God was established.

*Troparion:*

The angelic condescension, which is the coming of the Word unto us, was manifested through thee, O All-Hymned One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Blessed are thy womb, O All-Hymned One, and thy breasts, O Blessed Virgin: for for their sake we all have found life.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Mystically we hymn thee, O Mother of God, with Orthodox voices: as the Church preserved by thy prayers.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Enlightenment of those lying in darkness, and salvation of the despairing, O Christ my Savior: early unto Thee do I awake, O King of peace; enlighten me with Thy radiance, for I know no other God beside Thee.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

As more honorable than the Ark of the Law do we hymn thee, O Theotokos Mary: for thou, O All-Hymned One, didst carry the Creator and God of all, as the tablets.

*Troparion:*

As the throne of the Word of God do we glorify thee, O Theotokos: upon which God, sitting as man, appeared and became higher than the Cherubim.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou didst loose, O Maiden, all the human race from bitter bondage, and with the freedom of Christ didst honor female nature in thy divine Nativity.

*Both now...*

[f. 37vA] *Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear a Son, O Virgin, and women manifestly conquer the enemy: therefore they flee unto thee, O Maiden, holding their children.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy tender mercy: bring me up from corruption, O God!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

O Pure Theotokos, thou art joy unto the world; faithfully with the Angel we cry unto thee, Rejoice! O Virgin: vouchsafe us also thy joy, and destroy our grief.

*Troparion:*

The dwelling of unassailable joy, thee, O Ever-Virgin Mother, do we faithfully praise: vouchsafe us also to be the dwelling of the unassailable Most High.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Higher than the Heaven of heavens didst thou appear, O Bride of God, in all divine glory: for from thee our God shone forth and appeared unto us.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Now has female nature rejoiced; now has grief ceased, joy has blossomed: for the Mother gave birth unto Joy, the Savior Christ God.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 2.*

*Podoben: "Being the wellspring of loving-kindness" (Милосе́рдія сꙋ́щи и҆сто́чникъ):*

Beholding the cruel slothfulness of my soul and the exhaustion of my heart, O Mother of God, heal them by thy prayers, and vouchsafe me the portion of the saved,

[f. 38rA] *Sessional Hymn (continued):*

delivering me from darkness and torment: for thou art my only hope and consolation.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Unto the golden image served in the plain of Dura, the three youths paid no heed regarding the ungodly command, but cast into the midst of the flame, being refreshed, they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

The fleece of Gideon of old prefigured the descent of the Word of God upon thee, O Pure One: for as dew didst thou receive conception, O Virgin unwrathful. Therefore we all cry unto thee: Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O Pure One.

*Troparion:*

New and fearful, faithful and marvelous are thy mysteries, O Mary, Mother of our God: for through thee we all were reconciled unto God and the Sovereign Lord. Therefore with the Angels we now sing unto thee: Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O Pure One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Gideon of old foretold thy divine Childbearing clearly, O Virgin: he brings the bowl filled with water from the wringing of the fleece; for the Word in a divine manner dwelt wholly within thee, O All-Pure One: Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O Pure One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having given birth to God and the Savior of all, O Mary, thou hast become the restoration of the despairing, and unto sinners

[f. 38vA] *Theotokion (continued):*

cleansing, renewal, and hope unto the hopeless, and help unto those who hymn thee: Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O Pure One.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Who descended into the fiery furnace unto the Hebrew youths, and changed the flame into dew, God: sing, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him supreme unto all ages.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

A New Paradise hast thou appeared unto us, O All-Pure Theotokos, having brought forth not the tree of death, but of life, as a Garden that seedlessly budded forth the Lord: by Whom all we are nourished with immortal life.

*Troparion:*

The entire Church of Christ hymns thy Nativity, O Theotokos: for all sinful men are saved, fleeing with love unto thee: for Christ came upon earth to save men.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

The foremother is freed from condemnation through thee, O Virgin Theotokos, and now women suffer for Christ, and female nature rejoices: as Thecla the protomartyr cries aloud.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

None has perished, O Pure One, who in Orthodox manner acquired the hope of faith toward thee, O Virgin Mother of God: except only those who through envy turn away from thee, and do not bow down before thine inscribed image.[^23]

[f. 39rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

The Luminary, God Who shone forth before the sun, Who came unto us in the flesh, ineffably incarnate from the Virgin's womb: O Blessed and Pure One, thee, O Theotokos, do we magnify.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Incline thine ear unto me, O Most Holy Virgin, who praise thy Nativity with faith, in words of praise; and having accepted the song of my lips as the widow's mites, ask forgiveness for my sins.

*Troparion:*

Thy beauty shines, the brightness of purity flashes, O Pure One, and more than these shines forth thy Nativity: for God, the Creator of the sun and of all creation, was born of thee. Therefore all we magnify thee.

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*

Come, ye people, let us sing a song unto Christ God, Who divided the sea and guided the people whom He brought forth from the bondage of Egypt, for He has been glorified.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Thou art a fountain of life, O Virgin Mother Pure, who didst bear the Author and Lord: having implored the Life of all, save those who praise thee with faith.

*Troparion:*

Having thee as an all-powerful support and protectress, O Most Pure One, we who confess thee to be the Theotokos are saved from the worldly tempests of this life, O All-Immaculate Virgin.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Having given birth to the Fountain of immortality, heal me who am wounded by passions, O Maiden, and snatch me away from eternal fire, O only woman full of the grace of God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou art ever a refuge unto the faithful, O Virgin, and a mighty help unto those who run unto thee: save us from every need and harm of the adversary.

[f. 51vA] *Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Establish us within Thyself, O Lord, Who by the Tree didst put to death transgressions; and plant Thy fear in our hearts, as Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion:*

Truly as the Golden Censer, and the Jar of Manna, and the Divine Mountain, and the all-comely Palace of God do we name thee, O Virgin.

*Troparion:*

Being the temple and sacred dwelling of the Word, O Theotokos: be thou ever unto me the cleansing of transgressions, O Most Holy Virgin.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Neither earthly tongue nor mind of the bodiless hosts is able to recount thy childbearing: for beyond nature and understanding didst thou, O Theotokos, give birth unto the Creator.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Be thou the support, and refuge, and protection, O Virgin Parent of God, unto those who flee unto thee with faith and confess thee to be the Mother of God.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I heard, O Lord, Thy glorious dispensation, and I glorified Thy unsearchable power, O Compassionate One.

*Troparion:*

Thee as a great help have we Christians acquired, O Theotokos: snatch us away from cruel perils.

*Troparion:*

O Mother unwedded, Sovereign Lady, who didst conceive God in thy womb: deliver all of us from assaults and afflictions.

*Glory...*

[f. 52rA] *Troparion:*

As an invincible wall and mighty hope have we the faithful acquired thee in assaults, O Pure Theotokos.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having acquired thy prayer as a firm stair, O Sovereign Lady, we are delivered from diverse afflictions.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

O Giver of light and Creator of the ages, O Lord: direct us in the light of Thy commandments, for we know no other God beside Thee.

*Troparion:*

Incarnate of thee, O Parent of God, have we the faithful known the Son born without seed, true God, and man by nature: therefore do we glorify thee.

*Troparion:*

Fleeing always under thy protection and defense with faith, O Most Pure One, we are delivered through thee, O Theotokos, from every grievous assault.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver us from assaults and the tempest of thoughts, O Most Pure One, from every wrath, every sin, and famine and pestilence, and eternal torment, O Virgin.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Being our protectress and salvation, and hope for Christians, O Sovereign Lady: save those who ever faithfully hymn thee with love, O All-Hymned Virgin.

[f. 52vA] *Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy tender mercy: bring me up from corruption, O my God!

*Troparion:*

Thou Who of Thine own will workest all things, having of Thine own will settled in the womb of the Mother unwedded, didst enrich with incorruption those ailing with corruption, as Thou art merciful.

*Troparion:*

Higher than the heavenly Hosts and holier art thou, O All-Immaculate One, having beyond nature contained the Uncontainable Word within thy womb.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Direct me unto the path of repentance, O Sovereign Lady, who have strayed on the way of life and frequently fall into sins through waywardness.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Despise not our supplications, O Pure One, for we have set our hope on thee: for thou art a refuge unto thy servants, O Sovereign Lady, and the cleansing of souls.

*Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now... Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*[^30]

The Word without seed didst thou conceive, and the only Son, Christ, didst thou bear: a new Heaven didst thou bring forth as a Child, Thine own Creator: therefore do we magnify thee, O Theotokos!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Unto the golden image served in the plain of Dura, the three youths paid no heed regarding the ungodly command, but cast into the midst of the flame, being refreshed, they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

[f. 53rA] *Troparion:*

The Intelligible Son consubstantial with the Father didst thou incarnate by the power of the Most Holy Spirit, O Sovereign Lady full of the grace of God: implore Him unceasingly to have compassion upon those who sing: Blessed art thou, who didst bear God in the flesh!

*Troparion:*

O Virgin Pure, Mother unwedded, Holy, Blessed, Restoration of the falling, Deliverance of sinners: save me, the prodigal, save me, crying unto thy Son: Blessed art thou, who didst bear God in the flesh!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Being a firm haven, an awesome protectress, and an insurmountable wall through prayers unto thy Son for those distressed in needs and storm-tossed in sorrow, O Theotokos: save thy servants from manifold temptations.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou alone art the hope and help of the faithful, O Parent of God: make haste to help thy servants who are submerged in sorrows, perplexed on every side, and in sicknesses, and who flee unto thee with love from the whole soul.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Unto God Who descended into the fiery furnace unto the Hebrew children and changed the flame into coolness: sing, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him supreme unto all ages.

[f. 53vA] *Troparion:*

Being a Living Fountain, as thou didst give birth unto the Water of life: bedew my soul that is melted by the flame of sin, O Virgin Theotokos, that I may glorify thee unto all ages.

*Troparion:*

Those put to death and brought down unto mortal and corruptible dust, thou alone hast raised up, having given birth unto Christ our God, the Author of life, O Sovereign Lady, Virgin Pure and Blessed.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver me, O Pure Sovereign Lady, from eternal fire and condemnation, and rescue me from men who work evil and seek to trip me up: that I may ever bless thee, whom all creation divinely blesses.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

In the likeness of man was the All-Divine seen from thee, O Pure Virgin: implore Him unceasingly to have mercy upon us who live in malice and tremble before eternal torment.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

God the Word of God, Who by ineffable wisdom came to renew Adam, who evilly fell into corruption through the eating of food, and Who for our sake was ineffably incarnate of the Holy Virgin: with one accord we the faithful magnify Him in songs.

*Troparion:*

Vouchsafe me the divine love of mankind, O Maiden, who ineffably didst give birth unto God, the only Lover of mankind, Who borrowed flesh from thee:

[f. 54rA] *Troparion (continued):*

deliver me from the coming flame and from every torment, who praise thee with love.

*Troparion:*

Having acquired thee as a mighty Protectress, as a hope and wall, and foundation, and firm protection, and invincible support, and stormless haven, and sole sovereign refuge: we are saved, O All-Hymned One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O Virgin, Parent of the Light: drive away the clouds of my soul, and grant me to behold clearly, O Sovereign Lady, the saving beauty that shone forth ineffably from thy most pure womb as a light unto the nations, O All-Hymned One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having given birth to the Divine Light: enlighten my heart darkened by many assaults of passions and alienating thought, O Maiden, ever granting me drops of tears that cleanse the defilements of sin, O Virgin.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: after the Our Father: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

### Notes

[^18]: Liturgical incipit for the Saturday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Saturday, serving the liturgical day of Sunday). The rubric explicitly directs this canon to be chanted by those suffering under slander, sorrow, and tribulations (*его́же и҆ ѿ ѡ҆клевета́нїѧ въ печа́лехъ и҆ напа́стехъ сꙋ́щїи да пою́тъ*).
[^19]: Typological allusion to the traitorous counselor Ahithophel (2 Sam. 15:31; 17:1–23), whose cunning conspiracy against King David was brought to naught through prayer ('O Lord, turn the counsel of Ahithophel into foolishness'). In the liturgical context of this canon against slander and unjust persecution, Ahithophel serves as the archetypal conspirator whose evil design is shattered through Marian intercession.
[^20]: Liturgical incipit for the Sunday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Sunday, serving the liturgical day of Monday). In the weekly Octoechos cycle, Monday is dedicated to the Bodiless Powers of Heaven (the Holy Angels and Archangels).
[^21]: Direct Scriptural and liturgical typological witness to the Seraphic tongs and the burning coal of Isaiah 6:6–7. In Patristic Mariology, the tongs (*λαβίς* / *клѣщѣ*) prefigure the Theotokos, who bore the uncontainable fire of the Divine Word without being consumed.
[^22]: Liturgical incipit for the Monday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Monday, serving the liturgical day of Tuesday). In the weekly Octoechos cycle, Tuesday is dedicated to the Holy Forerunner and Baptist John, the archetype of repentance and monastic asceticism.
[^23]: Direct liturgical witness to the Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II, 787) and Orthodox iconodulic dogma. The Slavonic text (*ра́звѣ то́чїю за́вистїю ѿмета́ющїеся ѿ тебѐ, и҆ твое́мꙋ непокланѧ́ющїся ѡ҆́бразꙋ начерта́нномꙋ*) explicitly anathematizes the iconoclastic rejection of the inscribed Marian image (*ѡ҆́бразъ начерта́нный* / *χαρακτήρ* / *εἰκών*), declaring that perdition belongs solely to those who refuse relative veneration (*proskynesis*) to the sacred icon of the Mother of God.
[^24]: Liturgical incipit for the Tuesday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Tuesday, serving the liturgical day of Wednesday). In the Octoechos cycle, Wednesday is consecrated to the commemoration of the Betrayal of Judas and the Passion of Christ on the Precious Cross.
[^25]: The printed Slavonic text reads 'въ разтрѣхова́нїе' (or 'разстрѣхова́нїе'), an unusual Ruthenian Church Slavonic formation denoting extreme perturbation, terror, or scattering into sinful distractions (from *страхъ* / *стрѣх-* or *грѣхъ*).
[^26]: The heading specifies the Small Compline service for Wednesday in Tone 2 (*Въ сре́дꙋ на павече́рнїи*).
[^27]: The melodic model heading in the printed text gives the abbreviated incipit *Подо́бенъ: Бж҃їю бы́хомъ:*, corresponding to the canonical Tone 2 automelon *Боже́ственныя бы́хомъ о́бщницы* / *Θείας γεγόναμεν* ("We have become partakers of the divine [nature]").
[^28]: The heading specifies the Small Compline service for Thursday in Tone 2 (*Въ четве́ртокъ на па́вече́рнїи*).
[^29]: In the Ruthenian liturgical tradition, Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Friday evening forms the vigil eve of Saturday, which is dedicated particularly to the All-Holy Theotokos and to the memory of all departed faithful.
[^30]: The incipit of this Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) is an automelon/idiomelon without a specified *podoben* model in the print witness, retaining its direct Tone 2 melody.
[^31]: Fol. 54v bears a full-page woodcut engraving of the Kyiv-Pechersk Mother of God (*Печерська Пресвята Богородиця*) enthroned with the Christ Child, flanked by Archangels Michael and Gabriel, with the Lavra's founders, Venerable Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves, kneeling at her feet.
