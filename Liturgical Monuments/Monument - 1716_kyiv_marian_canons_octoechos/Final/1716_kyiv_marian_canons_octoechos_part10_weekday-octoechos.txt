# WEEKDAY OCTOECHOS

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE

*ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE:*

*Troparion of the Temple, Tone 1:*

In thy childbearing thou didst preserve thy virginity, and in thy falling asleep thou didst not forsake the world, O Theotokos; thou hast passed over unto life, being the Mother of Life, and by thy prayers thou deliverest our souls from death.[^371]

*And of the Day, Tone 4:*

O Leaders of the heavenly hosts, we the unworthy do ever beseech you, that by your prayers you may shelter us under the roof of the wings of your immaterial glory, preserving us who fall down diligently and cry:

[f. 214rA] Deliver us from perils, as Commanders of the higher Powers.[^372]

*Then unto our Venerable Father Anthony of the Caves, Troparion, Tone 4:*

Having departed from worldly turmoil, and by renunciation of the world having followed the Gospel of Christ, and having lived an angel-like life, thou didst attain unto the quiet haven of the Holy Mountain of Athos; whence, by the blessing of the Fathers having come unto the hills of Kyiv, and having laboriously accomplished thy life there, thou didst enlighten thy homeland; and having shown unto a multitude of monastics the paths leading unto the heavenly Kingdom, thou didst bring them unto Christ;[^373] [f. 214vA] Whom do thou beseech, O Venerable Anthony, that He may save our souls.

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*

Having committed thyself unto God, Who was beloved above all from thy youth, O Venerable One, thou didst follow Him with love from all thy soul; and having accounted the corruptible things of the world as naught, thou didst make a cave in the earth, and therein didst struggle valiantly against the snares of the invisible enemy, shining forth like a radiant sun unto all ends of the earth; whence rejoicing thou hast passed over unto the heavenly bridal chambers; and standing now with the Angels before the Master's throne, remember us who honor thy memory, that we may cry unto thee: Rejoice, Anthony our Father!

[f. 215rA] *Unto our Venerable Father Theodosius of the Caves, Troparion, Tone 8:*

Having been exalted in virtue, having loved the monastic life from youth, and having valiantly attained unto thy desire, thou didst settle in the Cave, and didst adorn thy life with fasting and radiance; in prayers thou didst abide as one bodiless, shining forth in the land of Rus' like a radiant luminary,[^374] O Father Theodosius; beseech Christ God that our souls may be saved.[^375]

*Kontakion, Tone 4:*

Today let us honor the star of Rus', which shone forth from the East and came unto the West; for it illumined all this land with wonders and goodness, and [f. 215vA] enriched all of us with the ascetic labor and grace of the monastic rule of the blessed Theodosius.[^376]

*Troparion, Tone 4:*

O God of our Fathers, Who dealest ever with us according to Thy gentleness, withdraw not Thy mercy from us; but by their supplications order our life in peace.[^377]

*Troparion, Tone 4:*

Thy Church, having been adorned with the blood of Thy Martyrs throughout the whole world as with purple and fine linen, through them cries unto Thee, O Christ God: Send down Thy compassions upon Thy people, grant peace unto Thy commonwealth, and unto our souls great mercy.[^378] Glory:

[f. 216rA] *Kontakion, Tone 8:*

With the Saints give rest, O Christ, unto the souls of Thy servants, where there is no sickness, nor sorrow, nor sighing, but life unending.[^379] Both now:

*Theotokion:*

By the prayers, O Lord, of all the Saints and of the Theotokos, grant us Thy peace, and have mercy upon us, as Thou alone art compassionate.

*(Ornamental headpiece)*

Apostles, Prophets, and holy Martyrs, Venerable and Righteous ones, who have well finished the course and kept the faith, having boldness before the Savior, we beseech you: pray unto Him as good on our behalf, that He may save our souls.[^385]

*Glory: Both now:*

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*

Unto Thee, O Lord, the Creator of creation, the inhabited world offers [f. 218vA] the God-bearing Martyrs as firstfruits of nature; by their prayers keep Thy Church and Thy commonwealth in deep peace for the sake of the Theotokos, O only Most Merciful One.[^386]

## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE

*ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE:*

*The Hypakoai of the current Tone are read.*

*Hypakoe, Tone 1:*

The repentance of the Thief opened Paradise, and the weeping of the Myrrhbearers announced Joy, for Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.[^387]

*Hypakoe, Tone 2:*

After the Passion the women went unto the Tomb to anoint Thy Body, O Christ God, and they saw Angels in the Tomb and were dismayed; [f. 219rA] for they heard a voice from them: The Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.[^388]

*Hypakoe, Tone 3:*

Astonishing by his appearance, shedding dew by his words, the radiant Angel said unto the Myrrhbearers: Why seek ye the living in the tomb? He is risen, having emptied the graves! Understand the Incorruptible One as the Changer of corruption; say unto God: How fearful are Thy works! For Thou hast saved the human race.[^389]

*Hypakoe, Tone 4:*

Anticipating the dawn of Thy glorious Rising, the Myrrhbearers preached unto the Apostles, O Christ, that Thou art risen as God, granting great mercy unto the world.[^390]

[f. 219vA] *Hypakoe, Tone 5:*

Troubling the mind with angelic appearance, but enlightening the soul with divine Rising, the Myrrhbearers announced good tidings unto the Apostles: Proclaim among the nations the Resurrection, the Lord working together with wonders, granting us great mercy.[^391]

*Hypakoe, Tone 6:*

By Thy voluntary and life-creating Death, O Christ God, having shattered the gates of hades as God, Thou hast opened ancient Paradise unto us; and having risen from the dead, Thou hast delivered our life from corruption.[^392]

*Hypakoe, Tone 7:*

Thou Who didst assume our likeness, and didst endure Crucifixion in the flesh: save us [f. 220rA] by Thy Resurrection, O Christ God, as the Lover of mankind.[^393]

*Hypakoe, Tone 8:*

The Myrrhbearers standing before the Tomb of the Giver of life sought the Master, the Immortal One among the dead, and having received from the Angel the joy of good tidings, they announced unto the Apostles that Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world.[^394]

*Be it known also this: If there occur a Saint having a Polyeleos, or only a Doxology, outside of Sunday, we read their proper Kontakion. We chant: Lord, have mercy (40). Then the Prayer of Saint Basil the Great:*[^395]

Thou Who at all times and at every hour, in heaven and on earth, art worshipped and glorified, O Christ God,[^396] good, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy; Who lovest the righteous and hast mercy upon sinners; [f. 220vA] Who callest all unto salvation through the promise of good things to come: do Thou Thyself, O Lord, receive also our supplications at this hour, and direct our life in goodness unto Thy commandments. Sanctify our souls, purify our bodies, correct our thoughts, sober our understanding and chasten it; and deliver us from all affliction, evils, diseases, and soul-passions; encompass us about with Thy holy Angels, that preserved and guided by their encampment, we may attain unto the unity of the faith and unto the knowledge of Thine unapproachable glory; for Thou art blessed unto the ages. Amen.

*Lord, have mercy (3). Glory: Both now: More honorable than the Cherubim:*

*In the Name of the Lord bless, father.*

> **Priest:** *God be bountiful unto us, and bless us...*

*Then:*

[f. 221rA] *Prayer to the Most Holy Theotokos,*

*of Paul the Monk, of Evergetis, that is, of the Monastery of the Benefactress:*[^397]

*(Woodcut: The Most Holy Theotokos holding the Christ Child, with radiant crowns and halos)*

O spotless, undefiled, incorrupt, most pure Sovereign Lady, Bride of God, who by thy glorious childbearing didst unite God the Word unto men, and didst join the fallen nature of our race unto the heavenly; thou who art the only Hope of the hopeless, and the Helper of the oppressed, the ready Protection of those who flee unto thee, and the Refuge of all Christians: disdain not me, a sinner and defiled, who have made myself wholly unprofitable by foul thoughts, words, and deeds,[^398] [f. 221vA] and through indolence of habits having become a slave unto pleasures: but as the Mother of God Who lovest mankind, lovingly have compassion upon me, a sinner and prodigal, and accept my supplication offered unto thee from defiled lips; and implore Thy Son and our Master and Lord, having assumed boldness with thy maternal boldness, that He may open also unto me the loving compassion of His goodness, and overlooking my countless transgressions, may turn me unto repentance, and show me forth as an approved doer of His commandments. And be thou ever present with me as merciful, compassionate, and loving goodness in this present life, an ardent Protectress and Helper, repulsing the ranks of adversaries, and guiding me unto salvation; [f. 222rA] and at the time of my departure guarding my wretched soul, and driving far from it the dark forms of evil demons; and in the dreadful day of righteous judgment delivering me from eternal torment, and showing me forth as an heir of the ineffable glory of Thy Son and our God:[^399] which may I obtain, my Sovereign Lady, Most Holy Theotokos, through thy mediation and protection, by the grace and love for mankind of Thine Only-begotten Son, our Lord and God and Savior Jesus Christ, unto Whom is due all glory, honor, and worship, with His unoriginate Father, and His All-Holy, Good, and Life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

[f. 222vA] *Then this Prayer, of Saint Antioch the Monk:*[^400]

And grant unto us, O Master, as we go to sleep, rest of soul and body, and preserve us from the gloomy sleep of sin, and from all dark and nocturnal voluptuousness. Quench the assault of passions, and extinguish the burning darts of the evil one which are craftily cast against us; appease the rebellions of our flesh, and lull to sleep all our earthly and material minding. And grant unto us, O God, a watchful mind, a chaste thought, a sober heart, a light sleep, and free from every satanic phantom. And raise us up at the time of prayer, established in Thy commandments, [f. 223rA] and having within ourselves the remembrance of Thy judgments continually; and grant us a night-long doxology, to chant and bless and glorify Thine all-honorable and majestic Name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*O most glorious, Ever-Virgin Theotokos, Mary, Mother of Christ our God, receive our prayers and present them unto thy Son and our God, that for thy sake He may save and enlighten our souls.*[^401]

*Then, the Prayer of Saint Joannicius:*[^402]

God is our hope, and Christ is our refuge, and the Holy Spirit is our protector: O Holy Trinity, glory to Thee!

*And straightway the chanters chant these Troparia, Tone 6:*

*Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us... Glory:*

*Lord, have mercy on us, for we have hoped in Thee... Both now:*

*Open unto us the doors of compassion...*[^403]

[f. 223vA] *After the chanting, the Priest says this Litany:*[^404]

> **Priest:** "Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee, hear and have mercy."

> **Choir:** *Lord, have mercy (3).*

> **Priest:** "Again we pray that this holy Monastery (or: this city), and every Monastery, and every city, countryside, and land may be preserved from famine, pestilence, earthquake, flood, hail, fire, sword, the invasion of aliens, and civil war; and that our good and man-befriending God may be merciful, propitious, and easily entreated, and turn away all wrath stirred up against us, and deliver us from His impending righteous chastisement, and have mercy on us."

> **Choir:** *Lord, have mercy (40).*

*Exclamation:*

[f. 224rA] **Priest:** Hear us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth and of those who are far off upon the sea; and, O Merciful One, be merciful unto our sins, and have mercy on us: for a merciful God art Thou, and the Lover of mankind, and unto Thee do we send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages.

> **Choir:** "Amen."

> **Priest:** "Glory to Thee, O Christ God,[^405] our hope, glory to Thee."

> **Choir:** *Glory: Both now: Lord, have mercy (3). Bless.*

> **Priest:** *Dismissal: If it be Sunday: He Who rose from the dead... If on other days:* Christ our true God, through the prayers of His most pure Mother, of our venerable and God-bearing Fathers, and of all the Saints, have mercy on us and save us, as He is good and the Lover of mankind.[^406]

*If it be Holy Week, or after "God is our hope...", the Priest says this Prayer:*[^407]

[f. 224vA] O Master, great in mercy, Lord Jesus Christ our God: through the prayers of our all-immaculate Lady, Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary; by the power of the precious and life-creating Cross; by the protection of the honorable bodiless Heavenly Powers; of the honorable and glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John; of the holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles; of the holy and victorious Martyrs; of our venerable and God-bearing Fathers; of the holy and righteous Ancestors of God, Joachim and Anna; and of Saint *[Name]*, whose temple it is; and of all Thy Saints: make our prayer acceptable; grant us remission of our transgressions; cover us with the shelter of Thy wings; and drive away from us every enemy and adversary. Pacify our life, O Lord; [f. 225rA] have mercy on us and on Thy world, and save our souls, as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind.

*Then he says this:*[^408]

> **Priest:** "Let us pray for our Pious Sovereign, Tsar and Grand Prince *[Name]:*"[^409]

> **Choir:** *God preserve and strengthen the realm of his kingdom.*

> **Priest:** "For his Christ-loving armed forces:"

> **Choir:** *God give them good success.*

> **Priest:** "For the founders of this holy Monastery (or: of this holy Temple):"

> **Priest:** "For the Most Holy Patriarch of all Rus' *[Name]:*"[^410]

> **Choir:** *God have mercy on him and save him.*

> **Choir:** *God have mercy on him and save him.*

> **Priest:** "For our all-honorable Father Archimandrite *[Name],* and all our Brotherhood in Christ:"

[f. 225vA] **Choir:** *God have mercy on them and save them.*

> **Priest:** "For our fathers and brethren sent away on service:"

> **Priest:** "Let us remember our Orthodox Tsars;[^412] and the founders of this holy Monastery; our parents and kinsfolk; and our fathers and brethren departed before us, who lie here and the Orthodox everywhere."

*The Priest makes a prostration unto the ground before the Brethren, saying:*

> **Priest:** "Bless, holy fathers, and forgive me, a sinner, whereinsoever I have sinned all the days [f. 226vA] of my life, and this day, in deed, word, and thought, and in all my senses."

**Brethren:** *God forgive thee and have mercy on thee, holy father.*

*The Priest responds:*

> **Priest:** "By His grace may God forgive and have mercy on you."

## PRAYERS FOR THOSE GOING TO SLEEP

*Prayers for Those Going to Sleep:*[^414]

*If a Priest: Blessed is our God... If not, then: Through the prayers... Trisagion, and after Our Father: the Troparia: Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us... Then: Lord, have mercy (12). And read these prayers in order, with attention and contrition:*

*Prayer 1, of Saint Macarius the Great, unto God the Father:*[^415]

O Eternal God and King of all creation, Who hast vouchsafed me to arrive even unto this hour: forgive me the sins which I have committed this day in deed, word, and thought; and cleanse, O Lord, my humble soul from all defilement [f. 227vA] of flesh and spirit; and grant unto me, O Lord, to pass this sleep in peace during this night, that rising from my humble bed, I may be well-pleasing unto Thine All-Holy Name all the days of my life, and trample down the enemies that war against me, bodily and bodiless; and deliver me, O Lord, from vain thoughts that defile me, and from evil desires: for Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, with Thine Only-begotten Son, and Thine All-Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Prayer 2, unto our Lord Jesus Christ, of Saint Antioch:*[^416]

O Almighty Word of the Father, Jesus Christ, Who art Thyself perfect: for the sake of Thy manifold mercy, never depart from me, Thy servant, but [f. 228rA] ever rest in me, O Jesus, the good Shepherd of Thy sheep; deliver me not over unto the rebellion of the serpent, and leave me not unto the desire of Satan, for the seed of corruption is in me.[^417] Do Thou therefore, O Lord God worshipped, Holy King Jesus Christ, preserve me while I sleep with the unwaning light, by Thy Holy Spirit, wherewith Thou didst sanctify Thy disciples. Grant also unto me, Thine unworthy servant, O Lord, Thy salvation upon my bed: enlighten my mind with the light of the understanding of Thy holy Gospel; my soul with the love of Thy Cross; my heart with the purity of Thy word; my body with Thy passionless Passion; preserve my thought with Thy humility; and raise me up in due time unto [f. 228vA] Thy doxology: for Thou art glorified above all with Thine unoriginate Father, and with the All-Holy Spirit, unto the ages. Amen.

*Prayer 3, unto the All-Holy Spirit:*[^418]

O Lord, Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of truth: have compassion and have mercy upon me, Thy sinful servant, and loose unto me, unworthy one, and forgive all whereinsoever I have sinned against Thee this day as a man, or rather not as a man, but even worse than a beast; my voluntary sins and involuntary, known and unknown, which are from youth and from evil habit, which come of presumption and faintheartedness. If I have sworn by Thy Name, or blasphemed It in my thought; or reproached [f. 229rA] anyone in my wrath; or grieved anyone, or became angered at anything; or lied, or slept unseasonably; or if a poor man came unto me, and I despised him; or if I grieved my brother, or quarreled; or judged anyone; or became haughty, or grew proud; or when standing at prayer, my mind was moved by the malice of this world; or thought perversity; or overate, or drank to excess, or laughed mindlessly; or thought evil; or saw another's beauty and was wounded thereby in heart; or spoke unfitting things; or mocked my brother's sin: while mine own transgressions are countless; or neglected prayer; or did some other evil thing, remember it not; for all these things and greater than these have I done. Have mercy upon me, O my Creator, [f. 229vA] O Master, upon me, Thy fainthearted and unworthy servant; and remit unto me, and loose, and forgive me, as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind; that in peace I may lie down, fall asleep, and take rest, prodigal, sinful, and wretched though I am; and that I may worship, hymn, and glorify Thine all-honorable Name, with the Father and His Only-begotten Son, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Prayer 4:*[^419]

O Lord our God, whereinsoever I have sinned this day in word, deed, and thought, as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind, forgive me. Grant me peaceful and untroubled sleep. Send Thy Guardian Angel to overshadow and preserve me from every evil: for Thou art the Guardian of our souls and our bodies, and unto Thee do we send up glory:

[f. 230rA] to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Prayer 5:*[^420]

O Lord our God, in Whom we believe, and Whose Name we call upon above every name: grant unto us as we go to sleep relaxation of soul and body; preserve us from every phantom and dark pleasure; stop the assault of passions; quench the burning rebellion of the flesh; grant us to live chastely in deeds and words, that embracing a virtuous conversation, we may not fall away from Thy promised blessings: for blessed art Thou unto the ages. Amen.

*Prayer 6, of Peter the Monk of the Monastery of Studios, unto the Most Holy Theotokos:*[^421]

Unto thee, Most Pure Mother of God, do I wretched fall down and pray: Behold, O Queen, that unceasingly [f. 230vA] I sin, and anger Thy Son and my God; and though I often repent, I am found a liar before God; and repenting, I tremble lest the Lord strike me, yet after a little while I do the same again. Knowing this, my Sovereign Lady, Queen and Theotokos, I pray thee to have mercy, to strengthen me, and to grant that I may do good works; for I know, my Sovereign Lady Theotokos, that I utterly hate mine evil deeds, and with all my mind I love the law of my God: but I know not, O All-pure Queen, how it is that what I hate, that I also love, and good things I transgress.[^422] Suffer not, O All-pure One, my will to be accomplished, for it is not pleasing unto God; but let the will of Thy Son and my God be done, that He may save me, and give me understanding, and grant the grace of the Holy Spirit, that from henceforth I may cease from foul deeds, and for the rest live in the commandments of Thy Son:

[f. 231rA] unto Whom is due all glory, honor, and dominion, with His unoriginate Father, and His All-Holy, Good, and Life-creating Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Prayer 7:*[^423]

O Good Mother of the Good King, Most Pure and Blessed Theotokos Mary: pour out the mercy of Thy Son and our God upon my passionate soul, and by thy prayers guide me unto good deeds, that I may pass the remaining time of my life without blemish, and through thee may find Paradise, O Virgin Theotokos, who alone art pure and blessed.

*Prayer 8, unto the Holy Guardian Angel:*[^424]

O Angel of Christ, my holy Guardian and protector of my soul and body: forgive me all whereinsoever I have sinned this day, and deliver me from every malice of the opposing [f. 231vA] enemy, that I in no sin may provoke my God to anger; but pray for me, a sinful and unworthy servant, that thou mayest show me forth worthy of the goodness and mercy of the All-Holy Trinity, and of the Mother of my Lord Jesus Christ, and of all the Saints. Amen.

*Kontakion of the Theotokos:*[^425]

To thee, our Champion General, we thy servants ascribe the victory: having been delivered from evils, we offer hymns of thanksgiving unto thee, O Theotokos; but as thou hast invincible might, deliver us from all dangers, that we may cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Bride unwedded!

*O most glorious, Ever-Virgin Theotokos Mary, Mother of Christ our God: receive our prayers, and present them unto thy Son and our God, that for thy sake He may save and enlighten our souls.*

*All my hope do I place in thee, O Mother of God; [f. 232rA] keep me under thy protection.*

*O Virgin Theotokos, despise me not a sinner, in need of thy help and thy protection: for in thee hath my soul hoped, and have mercy upon me.*

*The Father is my hope, the Son is my refuge, and the Holy Spirit is my protector: O Holy Trinity, glory to Thee!*

*Then, "It is truly meet...": Glory: Both now: Lord, have mercy (2). O Lord, bless: and the Dismissal:*

> **Priest:** "O Lord Jesus Christ our God, through the prayers of Thine all-pure Mother, and of our venerable Fathers Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves,[^426] and of all the Saints, have mercy on us. Amen."

*Prayer of Saint John of Damascus, pointing toward one's bed, say:*[^427]

O Master, Lover of mankind, shall this bed indeed be my tomb? Or wilt Thou yet enlighten my wretched soul with day? Behold, the tomb lies before me; behold, death [f. 232vA] stands before me; I fear Thy judgment, O Lord, and the endless torment, yet I cease not doing evil; Thee, the Lord my God, do I ever provoke to anger, and Thine all-pure Mother, and all the Heavenly Powers, and my holy Guardian Angel. For I know, O Lord, that I am not worthy of Thy love for mankind, but am worthy of all condemnation and torment: but, O Lord, whether I will or will not, save me. For if Thou savest the righteous, it is no great thing, and if Thou hast mercy upon the pure, it is nothing marvelous, for they are worthy of Thy mercy. But upon me a sinner make Thy mercy marvelous; herein show forth Thy love for mankind, lest mine evil overcome Thine ineffable goodness and compassion; and even as Thou wilt, order all things concerning me.

*A prostration unto the face of the earth, after the whole Rule.*[^428]

[f. 233rA] *(Woodcut: The Holy Trinity enthroned in glory among clouds, God the Father depicted with papal-style tiara holding a sceptre, God the Son holding the Cross, and the Holy Spirit as a Dove radiating light above, surrounded by Seraphim and Cherubim)*[^429]

I worship Thee, O Most Holy Trinity, consubstantial, life-creating, and undivided, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; I believe in Thee and confess, I glorify, give thanks, praise, honor, and extol Thee, and I pray: have mercy upon me, Thine unprofitable creature, for Thy Name's sake.

*Say this thrice, with a prostration. Thereafter:*

Most Holy Lady, Virgin Theotokos, Mother of my Lord and God, who art the only protectress, help, and hope of my salvation after God: blessed art thou among women, and blessed [f. 233vA] is the fruit of thy womb, with Whom I call thee blessed, honor, magnify, and pray thee: remember me, my Mediatress, in thy maternal prayers unto God.

*Prostration 1:*

O All-honorable and Life-creating Cross of the Lord, by the power of Christ crucified upon thee, strengthen me against all visible and invisible enemies, preserving me from temptations of the flesh and of the soul.

*Prostration 1:*

All ye holy Heavenly Ranks: Angels, Archangels, Principalities, Authorities, Powers, Dominions, Thrones, Cherubim, and Seraphim: enlighten, sanctify, and preserve me by your intercession and protection.

*Prostration 1:*

O holy Angel of Christ, Guardian of my soul [f. 234rA] and of my body, cover me with the wings of thy goodness from every evil operation of the demons, guiding me into the path of salvation, and pray to God for me a sinner.

*Prostration 1:*

Holy, glorious Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist of the Lord, John, and all ye holy Prophets, Forefathers, and Fathers: pray to God for me a sinner, that I may be saved by your prayers.

*Prostration 1:*

Holy, glorious, and all-praised Apostles of Christ, the Twelve and the Seventy: pray to Christ the Lord, Who gave unto you the Holy Spirit to remit sins, that by the same Holy Spirit He may remit mine offences, kept bound through the years.[^430]

*Prostration 1:*

Holy hierarchs of Christ: Basil the Great, Gregory [f. 234vA] the Theologian, and John Chrysostom, Athanasius and Cyril, Nicholas, Spyridon, Peter, Alexis, and Jonah, Russian wonderworkers,[^431] and the other Hierarchs, Pastors, Teachers, Priests, and Confessors of Christ: pray to God for me a sinner.

*Prostration 1:*

Holy Great-Martyrs of Christ: Stephen, George, Demetrius, Theodore Stratelates, and Theodore the Recruit, Procopius, Artemius, Roman and David, Russian wonderworkers,[^432] and holy female martyrs Thecla, Barbara, Catherine, and Irene, and the other martyrs and confessors of Christ: pray to God for me a sinner.

*Prostration 1:*

Our venerable and God-bearing Fathers Anthony [f. 235rA] and Theodosius, Euthymius, Sabbas, Macarius, Paul, Onuphrius, and Joannicius, Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves,[^433] and our venerable Mothers: Mary, Theodora, Euphrosyne, and the other holy Fathers and Mothers: pray to God for me a sinner.

*Prostration 1:*

Holy Unmercenaries and Wonderworking Physicians: Cosmas and Damian, Cyrus and John, Panteleimon, Hermolaus, Agapitus,[^434] and the other wonderworkers: pray to God for me a sinner.

*Prostration 1:*

All ye holy Saints of God, whose souls even as they are in the hand of God, so also their names are written in the Heavens: pray to God for me a sinner.

[f. 235vA] *Troparia of Compline:*[^435]

Enlighten mine eyes, O Christ God, lest I sleep the sleep of death, lest mine enemy say: I have prevailed against him.[^436]

*Glory:*

Be Thou the protector of my soul, O God, for I walk in the midst of many snares; deliver me from them, and save me, O Good One, as the Lover of mankind.

*Both now:*

The most glorious Mother of Christ, and holier than the holy Angels, let us praise unceasingly with heart and lips, confessing thee to be the Theotokos: for truly hast thou borne for us God incarnate, and dost pray unceasingly for our souls.

*Then kiss thy cross which thou wearest upon thy breast, and cross thy bed therewith from head to foot, likewise also on all sides, saying the Prayer to the Precious Cross:*[^437]

[f. 236rA] *Prayer to the Precious and Life-Creating Cross of the Lord:*[^438]

Let God arise, and let His enemies be scattered; and let them that hate Him flee from before His face. As smoke vanishes, so let them vanish; as wax melts before the fire, so let demons perish from the face of them that love God and mark themselves with the sign of the Cross, and say with gladness: Rejoice, O All-honorable and Life-creating Cross of the Lord, who drivest away demons by the power of our Lord Jesus Christ Who was crucified upon thee, Who descended into Hades and trampled down the power of the devil, and gave thee unto us, His honorable Cross, for the driving away of every enemy and adversary.[^439]

O All-honorable and Life-creating Cross of the Lord: help me with the Holy Lady, Virgin Theotokos, and with all the Saints, unto ages. Amen.

[^434]: *Agapitus*: Saint Agapitus the Unmercenary Physician of the Kyiv Caves (*Агапитъ Печерский*, d. 1095), invoked alongside the universal unmercenary physicians Cosmas and Damian, Cyrus and John, and Panteleimon.

[^437]: *Kiss thy cross...*: An ancient monastic and lay rubrical practice for retirement before sleep, blessing the bed on all four sides with the pectoral cross (*наперсный крестъ*) while reciting the Prayer to the Venerable Cross.
[^438]: *Prayer to the Precious and Life-Creating Cross of the Lord*: A traditional prayer based on Psalm 67:2–3 [Masoretic 68:1–2], combined with an apostrophe to the Cross (*Радуйся Пречестный и Животворящій Кресте Господень*).
