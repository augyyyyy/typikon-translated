[^41]: Wednesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Wednesday evening liturgically forms the eve of Thursday, dedicated throughout the Byzantine Octoechos to the Holy Apostles and Saint Nicholas of Myra in Lycia.
[^42]: The hymnographer evokes the Dominical parable of the barren fig tree (Luke 13:6–9; Matt. 21:19), in which the unyielding tree symbolizes the impenitent soul deserving of being cut down and cast into fire.
[^43]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Wednesday Compline is an idiomelon sung to its own Third Tone melody without an automelon rubric.
[^44]: Thursday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Thursday evening liturgically begins Friday, the day dedicated across the Byzantine liturgical cycle to the Passion of the Lord and the Adoration of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross.
[^45]: This Sessional Hymn to the Cross (*Крестобогородичен* / Stavrotheotokion) of Thursday Compline is sung as an idiomelon without a podoben model, meditating upon the Mother of God lamenting beneath the Cross on Friday eve.
[^46]: Friday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Friday evening liturgically inaugurates Saturday, dedicated across the Octoechos to all Saints and to the memory of all departed Orthodox Christians.
