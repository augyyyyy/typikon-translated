[^425]: *Kontakion of the Theotokos*: The celebrated prooimion of the Akathist Hymn (*Взбранной Воеводѣ*, Greek: *Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ στρατηγῷ*), customarily appointed at the conclusion of Compline (*Повечерїе*) in the Byzantine and Ruthenian traditions.
[^426]: *Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves*: The insertion of the founders of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra (*Антѡнїѧ и Ѳеодо́сїѧ Пече́рскихъ*) within the daily dismissal formula reflects the liturgical recension of the Kyiv Lavra printing house.
[^427]: *Prayer of Saint John of Damascus*: This nocturnal prayer of compunction (*Владыко Человѣколюбче, не ужели мнѣ одръ сей гробъ будетъ;*) is traditionally ascribed to Saint John of Damascus and forms a standard element of the Prayers before Sleep in the Ruthenian and Slavic Horologion.
[^428]: *Prostration unto the face of the earth*: Church Slavonic: *Поклоненїе до лица земли, по всемъ Правилѣ*. This rubric indicates a full metania / great prostration (*земной поклонъ*) to be made at the completion of the prescribed cell prayer rule.
[^429]: *Engraving of the Holy Trinity*: The woodcut presents the New Testament Trinity (*Сопрестолїе* / *Paternitas*), characteristic of 17th- and 18th-century Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra book engraving, showing God the Father with a tri-radiant or pontifical tiara and sceptre beside the Son, with the Holy Spirit hovering above.
[^430]: *Kept bound through the years*: Church Slavonic: *моѧ̑ лѣ́ты оу҆держа́ннаѧ... согрѣше́нїѧ*. Alluding to the apostolic authority to bind and loose sins (St. John 20:23), asking that accumulated sins retained over many years be loosed through the grace of the Holy Spirit.
[^431]: *Peter, Alexis, and Jonah, Russian wonderworkers*: The Three Moscow and All-Rus Hierarchs (*Пе́тре, А҆леѯі́е и҆ І҆ѡ́но, Рѡссі́йстїи чꙋдотво́рцы*), canonized in the 15th and 16th centuries, whose veneration was standard across East Slavic service books.
[^432]: *Roman and David, Russian wonderworkers*: The baptismal names of the holy Passion-bearers Boris and Gleb (*Рома́не и҆ Дѣ́де* [a misprint or recensional variant in the 1716 Kyiv edition for *Давы́де* / *Дв҃де*]), sons of Grand Prince Vladimir the Great.
[^433]: *Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves*: Church Slavonic: *А҆нтѡ́нїе и҆ Ѳеодѡ́сїе Пече́рстїи*. Re-invoked here specifically among the monastic fathers of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra.
[^434]: *Agapitus*: Saint Agapitus the Unmercenary Physician of the Kyiv Caves (*Агапитъ Печерский*, d. 1095), invoked alongside the universal unmercenary physicians Cosmas and Damian, Cyrus and John, and Panteleimon.
[^435]: *Troparia of Compline*: These three troparia (*Просвѣти ѻчи мои*, *Заступникъ дши моєй*, *Преславную Хр̄тову Мтерь*) constitute the ancient nocturnal troparia appended to Compline and the midnight office in the Byzantine Horologion.
[^436]: *Enlighten mine eyes...*: Psalm 12:4–5 [Masoretic 13:3–4]: "Lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death; lest mine enemy say, I have prevailed against him."
[^437]: *Kiss thy cross...*: An ancient monastic and lay rubrical practice for retirement before sleep, blessing the bed on all four sides with the pectoral cross (*наперсный крестъ*) while reciting the Prayer to the Venerable Cross.
[^438]: *Prayer to the Precious and Life-Creating Cross of the Lord*: A traditional prayer based on Psalm 67:2–3 [Masoretic 68:1–2], combined with an apostrophe to the Cross (*Радуйся Пречестный и Животворящій Кресте Господень*).
[^439]: *Psalm 67:2–3 [Masoretic 68:1–2]*: "Let God arise, and let His enemies be scattered; let them also that hate Him flee before Him. As smoke is driven away, so drive them away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God."
[^440]: *Colophon of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra*: The concise printer's dedication and doxological closure (*Сотворшему Дѣвѣ Богоматери величїе Силному Богу...*) of the 1716 Kyiv edition of the Octoechos-Theotokarion (*Богородичникъ*).
[^441]: *Who hath done great things...*: An explicit quotation of the Magnificat (St. Luke 1:49): "For He that is mighty hath done to me great things; and holy is His name" (Church Slavonic: *ꙗ҆́кѡ сотвори́ ми вели́чїе Си́льный, и҆ ст҃о и҆́мѧ є҆гѡ̀*).
[^442]: *Printer's Mark of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra*: The formal baroque ornamental tailpiece vignette characteristic of the Archimandrite and typography of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra in the early 18th century, marking the formal end of the printed codex.
[^443]: *Manuscript Addition*: An early 18th-century cursive Cyrillic inscription written by a contemporary reader/monastic on the facing unnumbered recto leaf (Fol. [237r]) immediately following the printed colophon, providing practical ascetic guidance for the daily nocturnal examination of conscience (*испытанїе совѣсти*).
[^444]: *Settle accounts with thine own self*: Church Slavonic: *Положѝ сло́во съ сами́мъ тобо́ю*. A classical patristic idiom derived from the monastic tradition of daily self-examination (cf. Saint John Chrysostom, *Homily on the Statues*, and Saint Dorotheus of Gaza, *Discourses*), enjoining a methodical review of all deeds, words, and thoughts before sleep.
