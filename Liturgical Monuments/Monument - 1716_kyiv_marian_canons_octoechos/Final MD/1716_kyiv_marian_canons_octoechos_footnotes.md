[^4]: Archimandrite Ioannikii Sennutovych served as Archimandrite of the Holy Dormition Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra from 1715 until his death in 1729, overseeing an active period of liturgical printing and scholarly publication at the Lavra's historic press.

[^17]: Direct liturgical witness to the Classical Patristic theology of sacred images defined by Saint Basil the Great (*On the Holy Spirit*, 18.45: 'The honor rendered to the icon passes to the prototype') and dogmatized at the Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II, 787). The Slavonic text (*Печа́ть тѧ̀ Влч҃нꙋ... ꙗ҃кѡ первоѡ́бразнꙋ чтꙋ̀*) explicitly asserts the relative veneration (*proskynesis*) due to the Marian icon.

[^18]: Liturgical incipit for the Saturday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Saturday, serving the liturgical day of Sunday). The rubric explicitly directs this canon to be chanted by those suffering under slander, sorrow, and tribulations (*его́же и҆ ѿ ѡ҆клевета́нїѧ въ печа́лехъ и҆ напа́стехъ сꙋ́щїи да пою́тъ*).

[^19]: Typological allusion to the traitorous counselor Ahithophel (2 Sam. 15:31; 17:1–23), whose cunning conspiracy against King David was brought to naught through prayer ('O Lord, turn the counsel of Ahithophel into foolishness'). In the liturgical context of this canon against slander and unjust persecution, Ahithophel serves as the archetypal conspirator whose evil design is shattered through Marian intercession.

[^20]: Liturgical incipit for the Sunday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Sunday, serving the liturgical day of Monday). In the weekly Octoechos cycle, Monday is dedicated to the Bodiless Powers of Heaven (the Holy Angels and Archangels).

[^21]: Direct Scriptural and liturgical typological witness to the Seraphic tongs and the burning coal of Isaiah 6:6–7. In Patristic Mariology, the tongs (*λαβίς* / *клѣщѣ*) prefigure the Theotokos, who bore the uncontainable fire of the Divine Word without being consumed.

[^22]: Liturgical incipit for the Monday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Monday, serving the liturgical day of Tuesday). In the weekly Octoechos cycle, Tuesday is dedicated to the Holy Forerunner and Baptist John, the archetype of repentance and monastic asceticism.

[^23]: Direct liturgical witness to the Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II, 787) and Orthodox iconodulic dogma. [^24]: Liturgical incipit for the Tuesday evening Marian canon of Tone 2 (chanted at Small Compline on Tuesday, serving the liturgical day of Wednesday). In the Octoechos cycle, Wednesday is consecrated to the commemoration of the Betrayal of Judas and the Passion of Christ on the Precious Cross.
