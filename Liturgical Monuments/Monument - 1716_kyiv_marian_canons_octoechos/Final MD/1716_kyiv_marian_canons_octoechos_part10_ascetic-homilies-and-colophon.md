# ASCETIC HOMILIES AND COLOPHON

[f. 236vA] *(Woodcut: Elaborate typographic headpiece band with floral and geometric arabesques)*

*Colophon of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra Octoechos-Theotokarion:*[^440]

Unto the Mighty God Who hath done great things for the Virgin Mother of God,[^441] let this canonical blessed hymnody be unto honor and glory, and unto those that read and hear, unto spiritual profit. Amen.

*(Woodcut: Typographic printer's mark and heraldic emblem of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra printing house, depicting a crowned cherub with outspread wings beneath an imperial baldachin draped with festoons, wreaths, and pendant tassels)*[^442]

[p246RA] *Daily Examination of Conscience before Sleep (Contemporary 18th-century manuscript addition on facing unnumbered recto):*[^443]

Before thou liest down upon thy bed, consider all these things and pass through them in thy memory:

First: Give thanks unto Almighty God, that He hath granted thee by His grace to pass through the past day in life and in health, saying:

I give thanks unto Thee, O Lord God, my most good Giver of life, that in this past day, wherein many have passed from this life unto death, and especially unto eternal death, Thou hast been well-pleased to preserve me, Thine unworthy servant, whole and unharmed until this night. Blessed, therefore, be Thy Name across all days and nights, and unto ages.

Second: Settle accounts with thine own self, and make an examination of thy conscience, going over and recounting in detail all the daytime hours, beginning from the time when thou didst arise from thy bed and bringing to thy remembrance: whither thou didst go, what thou didst do unto anyone, and what thou didst counsel, and all thy deeds, words, and thoughts produced by thee from morning even until evening, keeping all these things in mind...[^444]

---

### Notes

[^440]: *Colophon of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra*: The concise printer's dedication and doxological closure (*Сотворшему Дѣвѣ Богоматери величїе Силному Богу...*) of the 1716 Kyiv edition of the Octoechos-Theotokarion (*Богородичникъ*).
[^441]: *Who hath done great things...*: An explicit quotation of the Magnificat (St. Luke 1:49): "For He that is mighty hath done to me great things; and holy is His name" (Church Slavonic: *ꙗ҆́кѡ сотвори́ ми вели́чїе Си́льный, и҆ ст҃о и҆́мѧ є҆гѡ̀*).
[^442]: *Printer's Mark of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra*: The formal baroque ornamental tailpiece vignette characteristic of the Archimandrite and typography of the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra in the early 18th century, marking the formal end of the printed codex.
[^443]: *Manuscript Addition*: An early 18th-century cursive Cyrillic inscription written by a contemporary reader/monastic on the facing unnumbered recto leaf (Fol. [237r]) immediately following the printed colophon, providing practical ascetic guidance for the daily nocturnal examination of conscience (*испытанїе совѣсти*).
[^444]: *Settle accounts with thine own self*: Church Slavonic: *Положѝ сло́во съ сами́мъ тобо́ю*. A classical patristic idiom derived from the monastic tradition of daily self-examination (cf. Saint John Chrysostom, *Homily on the Statues*, and Saint Dorotheus of Gaza, *Discourses*), enjoining a methodical review of all deeds, words, and thoughts before sleep.
