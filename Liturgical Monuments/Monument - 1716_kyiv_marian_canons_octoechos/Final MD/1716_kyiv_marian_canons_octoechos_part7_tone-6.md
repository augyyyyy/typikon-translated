# TONE 6

## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 6)

*On Saturday at Compline,*[^92]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos:*

When Israel walked upon the dry land through the abyss with their footsteps, beholding the pursuer Pharaoh engulfed, they cried aloud: Let us sing a song of victory unto God!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having inexhaustible grace as divine, O unwedded Mother: despise not those who flee unto thee, ever saving them from misfortunes and sorrows.

*Troparion:*

Lo, a time of despondency has come upon me; arise, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, and give me a helping hand: for thou hast filled the world with divine gladness.

*Glory...*

[f. 134vA] *Troparion:*

Grant me swiftly thy mighty protection, O Birthgiver of God, as thou art our Protectress, who am held fast by afflictions, and preserve me unharmed from them.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Death hastens unto my soul, and the temptations of those who hate me encompass me like asps to destroy me; but do thou thyself save me.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established us upon the rock of Thy confession.[^93]

*Troparion:*

As thou didst give birth unto Life, O Birthgiver of God, make alive my soul that is slain by transgressions.

*Troparion:*

Preserve from every temptation those who flee unto thee, O Birthgiver of God, our Hope.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

From the adversities that rise up bitterly against me, save me, O all-immaculate Mother of my Lord.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

O thou who didst grant salvation unto the world through thy divine Childbirth: deliver me from misfortunes.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

The Prophet Habakkuk heard of Thy coming, O Lord, and was afraid, that Thou wast about to be born of a Virgin and appear unto men, and he said: I heard the report of Thee and was afraid: Glory to Thy power, O Lord![^94]

[f. 135rA] *Troparion:*

Through poverty the strength of my soul has grown faint, and a grievous darkness has fallen upon me, arising from the onset of transgressions: look down, O Birthgiver of God, as the light-bearing Cloud of God, and enlighten me, I pray.

*Troparion:*

Turn the storm of my evils and the turmoil of my transgressions into the calm of salvation, as thou art a harbor, O all-immaculate one, and deliver me from their destruction, I pray: for roaring as lions they seek to swallow me up.[^95]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

By night and by day, on earth and at sea, and in every place, being a firm salvation, O Birthgiver of God, invincible Protection, save me: for after God we Christians truly place our hope in thee.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

From great and diverse transgressions and adversities thou ever savest me. Therefore, knowing Thee to have given birth unto the Lord, I pray, and unto Thee I flee, the invincible Help of the afflicted: lead me out from misfortunes by thy supplications.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

O Christ Who didst make light shine upon all the world: enlighten my heart, crying aloud unto Thee from the night, and save me.

*Troparion:*

We glorify thee as the protection of our salvation, O immaculate Mother of the Word, not fearing the snares of men.

[f. 135vA] *Troparion:*

Having thee as an insurmountable wall, we are delivered from temptations and from the uprising of sorrows, O Most Pure One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Deliver me from the evil tongues of men, O Pure One: for as a razor have they been sharpened,[^96] bitterly seeking to destroy my soul.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Earnestly falling down before thee, I pray: as thou art the Mother of my Creator, set me free from the misfortunes that beset me.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Swallowed up by the sins of this life, I cry aloud unto Thee, O Christ: As Jonah from corruption, set me free!

*Troparion:*

Having tasted bitterness, I am estranged from divine sweetness, O Most Pure One; therefore I cry unto thee: grant me help.

*Troparion:*

The darkening of passions has made me a slave of corruption; therefore, O thou who didst give birth unto the Light, set me free, O Sovereign Lady.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

In harmony and with faith I confess thee, O Most Pure One, and offer thee a sacrifice of praise, having been delivered through thee from sorrows.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Mouths of iniquity have opened against me, O Sovereign Lady; therefore, as our Protectress, swiftly set me free from them.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6:*[^97]

Steadfast Protectress of Christians, unfailing Mediatress before the Creator: despise not the voices of sinners in prayer, but in thy goodness anticipate us for our help, who in faith cry aloud unto thee: hasten unto prayer and make speed unto intercession, ever protecting those who honor thee, O Birthgiver of God.

[f. 136rA] *Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Angel made the furnace drop dew for the holy children, but the command of God burned the Chaldeans, compelling the tyrant to cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers![^98]

*Troparion:*

Temptations encompass my soul now like fat bulls, O Most Pure Virgin;[^99] do thou thyself set me free from them.

*Troparion:*

Diligently defending those who are in misfortune and in sorrow, ever grant joy, O Birthgiver of God.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Surround me with defense, O Virgin, who am wholly condemned by despair of my deeds and by sorrows: for thou art the indestructible protection of all.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

By thy representation we are delivered from misfortunes and sorrows, and we find great and inexhaustible riches.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Out of the flame Thou didst pour dew for the saints, and the righteous man's sacrifice Thou didst burn up with water:[^100] for Thou, O Christ, doest all things whatsoever Thou wilt: Thee we exalt above all unto the ages!

[f. 136vA] *Troparion:*

I am consumed by the malicious afflictions of men, and I pray thee, O Birthgiver of God: deliver me from their vain counsel.[^101]

*Troparion:*

I have been overcome in sorrows by my temptations, and I pray thee, O Sovereign Lady: save me unharmed from them.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Save me, O Pure One, from the deceitful man, and from the tongue and lips, and from tyranny and every constraint.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Seduced in my disposition, being without defense, I cry unto the Birthgiver of God: Deliver me from all evils!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Thou who didst receive joy through the Angel, and didst give birth unto thy Creator: O Virgin, save those who magnify thee!

*Troparion:*

Be a prayer for us who are in the sorrow of this life, O Birthgiver of God, and save from misfortunes those who now flee unto thee.

*Troparion:*

Truly on earth and at sea thou alone hast appeared, O Pure One, as an indestructible protection unto those who flee unto thee with a humble-minded soul.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Unto me who have foolishly enslaved myself to all manner of grievous falls, grant freedom through thy supplications, O All-Hymned One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having acquired thee as hope and firm protection, O pure one, magnifying thee as the Birthgiver of God, I finish the song in faith.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 6)

*On Sunday at Compline,*[^102]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos:*

When Israel walked upon the dry land through the abyss with their footsteps, beholding the pursuer Pharaoh engulfed, they cried aloud: Let us sing a song of victory unto God!

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Drive away the cloud of passions from my mind, O Pure One, and make the Light shine upon me, O Most Pure One who didst give birth unto the Son, the Radiance of the Father,[^103] that I may sing of thee.

*Troparion:*

As Gabriel unto the Virgin, come, ye faithful, let us also cry aloud with soul-felt reverence: Truly adorned one, who didst give birth unto Joy, Rejoice, O Most Pure One![^104]

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Carrying in thy womb in the last days the Son, Who before the ages was impassibly begotten of the Father, O Most Pure One, thou hast brought forth Him twofold, God and Man.[^105]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Before thy Childbirth thou didst give birth unto the Only-begotten without corruption, and a Virgin thou didst remain after...

[f. 137vA] *Theotokion:*

...Childbirth, O Most Pure One: therefore we the faithful cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established us upon the rock of Thy confession.

*Troparion:*

There is none undefiled as thou, O Most Pure Birthgiver of God, who didst give birth unto the Creator and Sovereign Lord of all. Therefore unto thee all we faithful thankfully cry: Rejoice!

*Troparion:*

A fierce surging of passions and the bitterness of many falls trouble me, casting me truly into the depth of my evils; give me a hand, O Pure One, and save me.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Dispel the darkness of my transgressions, O Virgin, and make radiant the light of dispassion, O Pure One, who hast ineffably brought forth Life, the unapproachable Sun.[^106]

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having cleansed me from transgressions and defilement by the fire of the fear of God, O Maiden, clothe me with the radiant garment of virtues, and unite me with the Choir of the saints.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Christ is my power, God and Lord, the venerable Church cries aloud in godly manner, celebrating in the Lord with a pure mind.[^107]

[f. 138rA] *Troparion:*

O thou who didst give birth unto the Light, dispel the darkness of my many transgressions by thy radiance, O all-immaculate Sovereign Lady, who hast made the Sun of Righteousness shine upon the faithful.[^108]

*Troparion:*

Unto thee, O Most Pure One, I now offer the hope of my infirmity: that from thee I may go away unailing, and having received salvation, I may sing: Rejoice!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Grant us thy rich compassions, O Most Pure One, who run with faith and love into thy Temple, and deliver us from all temptations and sorrows.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The Word dwelt within thee, O Most Pure One, delivering the world from the fury of passions, O Sovereign Lady, and vouchsafing the Kingdom of Heaven unto those who sing of thee.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

By Thy divine light, O Good One, enlighten with love the souls of those who rise early at dawn unto Thee, I pray: to see Thee, O Word of God, the true God, calling them back from the darkness of sin.[^109]

*Troparion:*

By the divine radiance of Him Who shone forth from thy loins, enlighten my soul sleeping in darkness, O Virgin, and drive away the mist of sin, illuminating me with thy light.

*Troparion:*

Heal my soul that has fallen among thieves, O Good One,[^110] mercifully dropping the oil and wine of thy tenderheartedness, unstintingly granting it unto me.

*Glory...*

[f. 138vA] *Troparion:*

Heal our bodily infirmities and those of the soul, who flee unto thee, O All-Hymned One: for thou art the strength of those who run to the protection of thy prayer, O Birthgiver of God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The divine Holy Spirit, having dwelt within thy womb, O Most Holy and Pure One, and overshadowed thee, showed thee forth as the House of the Trinity, by the Father's good pleasure conceiving the Word and bringing Him forth.[^111]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Beholding the sea of this life surging with the storm of temptations, and fleeing to Thy calm haven, I cry aloud unto Thee: Bring up my life from corruption, O Greatly Merciful One![^112]

*Troparion:*

Dispel the darkness of my wretched soul, and enlighten me, O Most Pure One, with the Light that shone forth from thy womb and illuminated all human nature.

*Troparion:*

Deliver me, O Pure One, from the snare of enemies visible and invisible, and grant me the shelter of thy mighty protection: for unto thee, O Virgin, have I now fled.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

He Who created all things was incarnate in the last days from thy holy womb, and having restored human nature that had fallen grievously from Adam, He renewed the world.[^113]

*Both now...*

[f. 139rA] *Theotokion:*

The majesty of the holy Angels sings of thee, and I pray thee, O all-immaculate one: take away from me the unfitting fantasies of demons, preserving my heart in stillness.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6:*

The assaults of the adversaries wage war against thine inheritance, O Pure Sovereign Lady; destroy their craftiness, and cast down to the earth their haughty insolence: for thou art the Protectress of those who faithfully pray unto thee.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Angel made the furnace drop dew for the holy children, but the command of God burned the Chaldeans, compelling the tyrant to cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Having enlightened me who was in the darkness of ignorance with unapproachable Light, O Most Pure One, guide me unto the light to cry aloud faithfully and piously unto thy Son: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:*

Drive away the dark cloud from my soul, O Birthgiver of God, illuminating me with the light of the Creator of all: for thou art the receptacle of the Co-eternal Light, Who shone forth impassibly from the Father.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Incline Thyself unto our supplications, O Pure Birthgiver of God, being moved mercifully by prayer unto God, and make Him merciful unto us, who ever hope in thee, O Good One.

*Both now...*

[f. 139vA] *Theotokion:*

Unto me who am defiled by the passions of wicked deeds, grant a place of cleansing, O Pure One: drops of tears, washing away the fountain of mire of the shame of my wicked sin.[^114]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Out of the flame Thou didst pour dew for the saints, and the righteous man's sacrifice Thou didst burn up with water: for Thou, O Christ, doest all things whatsoever Thou wilt: Thee, the Lord, we exalt above all unto the ages!

*Troparion:*

O thou who didst in the flesh bring forth the Master of all: snatch me, O Maiden, from the assaults of passions, who sing with perpetual love unto the Creator of all unto all ages.

*Troparion:*

As I lie upon a bed of pain, raise me up by thy visitation, O Maiden, and grant me health of soul and body, who sing of Christ unto the ages.

*Troparion:*

Quench the flame of my passions by the dew of thy prayers, O Sovereign Lady, and save me, even as of old Thy Son delivered the Children who exalted Him above all unto the ages.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

I am not terrified by the barking of the adversary,[^115] having my hope and expectation in thee, O Good One: for thou ever protectest, saving from misfortunes those who praise thee.

[f. 140rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

It is not possible for men to see God, upon Whom the ranks of Angels dare not gaze; but through thee, O Most Pure One, the incarnate Word was manifested unto us: Whom magnifying, with the heavenly hosts we call thee blessed.[^116]

*Troparion:*

Deliver him who flees unto thee in faith from all evils, O Most Pure Virgin, and grant health unto me who have fled beneath thy protection, O Maiden, and deliverance from grievous ills: that with pious voice I may ever glorify thee.

*Troparion:*

Thou hast dissolved the curse of sorrow, O Pure One,[^117] and hast poured forth joy upon the world by thy Childbirth, having brought forth the Fountain of blessing; therefore praising thee, the All-Hymned, we all, the faithful, ever call thee blessed in piety.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thy womb became a receptacle for the Light, O All-Hymned Birthgiver of God, Who shone forth from the Father before all ages; therefore, all of us glorifying thee as the Birthgiver of God: Him we magnify as thy Son and God.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou alone, O Pure One, hast brought forth in time the timeless Son Who shone forth from the Father,[^118] through the overshadowing of the divine Holy Spirit. Therefore, O Most Pure One, as the unwedded Birthgiver of God, all we faithful confess thee with tongue and heart.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON MONDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 6)

[f. 140vA] *On Monday at Small Compline, Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos:*

Traversing the deep on foot as if on dry land, and beholding Pharaoh the pursuer drowned, Israel cried out: Let us sing unto God a song of victory![^119]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Bowed down by a multitude of temptations and grievous deeds, I bend the neck of my soul and body unto thee, O Pure One, and earnestly cry out unto thee: Do thou set me upright!

*Troparion:*

All-immaculate Maiden, mighty help of the faithful and hope of Christians: deliver me from bodily pleasures and passions that war against me.

*Glory:*

O Purest One, who gavest birth to the noetic Light, the Guide of those who are in darkness: enlighten my soul and illuminate my mind, dispelling the gloom of passions and transgressions.

*Both now:*

[f. 141rA] *Theotokion:*

Unto my darkened soul, beset by improper thoughts, dispel the gloom, O Theotokos, Sovereign Lady, by the light of the holy commandments of Him Who was incarnate from thee.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established us upon the rock of Thine acknowledgment.[^120]

*Troparion:*

Having hoped in thee, O Pure and Most Pure One, may I not fall away from expectation; but as the merciful Mother of the God Who loves mankind, deliver me from the snares of the enemy.

*Troparion:*

O Mary, Mother of God, gate of salvation through which the Creator of all alone did pass: open unto me the divine gates of repentance, O Most Pure One.

*Glory:*

Be thou a haven and guardian unto me, O Pure One, who am ever tempest-tossed by the grievous surges of passions in the sea of life, O only Ever-Virgin.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

O Mary, Mother of God, enlighten my soul, which is grievously darkened by many sins, wounded by the arrow of the evil one, and grown faint.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

"Christ is my power, God and Lord," the venerable Church in godly manner calls out, crying: feasting in the Lord from a pure understanding.

[f. 141vA] *Troparion:*

Being my strength, O Most Pure One, and refuge, and impregnable rampart, and intercessor before God: deliver me from the everlasting flame and from Gehenna.

*Troparion:*

O All-honorable Mary, dispel the turmoil of passions from my mind and the storm of temptations; for thou hast given birth to the Fountain of dispassion, O Mother, Ever-Virgin.

*Glory:*

O Pure and All-immaculate One, who becamest a spacious receptacle for the Divine indwelling of Purity: consume the defilement and uncleanness of my soul.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Thee, the only Most Pure and only undefiled One, do I implore, O Maiden: cleanse with thy prayers me who am defiled by pleasurable passions and have mired my soul in misery.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

By Thy divine light, O Good One, lovingly illumine, I pray, the souls of those who awake early unto Thee, that they may see Thee, O Word of God, as true God, calling them forth from the gloom of sins.[^121]

*Troparion:*

Defiled by thoughts and mired by many falls, O Mother of God, I implore thee, the only undefiled One: take pity and save me by thine intercession.

*Troparion:*

With thy divine radiance, O Good One, enlighten my soul darkened by pleasures, and direct it unto the paths of salvation, O thou who alone hast given birth to Christ the Savior.

*Glory:*

[f. 142rA] *Troparion:*

Tear asunder the fetters of my sins by thine intercession and protection, O Virgin, and fill my miserable soul with the joy of divine stillness, and deliver it from darkness.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Having now acquired thee alone as a divine refuge, O Good One, I faithfully fall down before thee and cry: Be thou my protection and shelter of salvation, O Sovereign Lady of the world, and save me.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Beholding the sea of life surging with the tempest of temptations, I run to Thy calm haven and cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O Greatly Merciful One![^122]

*Troparion:*

Guilty of sins and grievously wounded in my wretchedness, fleeing unto thee, most merciful Mother of God, I pray: Destroy the assaults of my transgressions.

*Troparion:*

O gracious Gate, who hast opened the heavenly gate unto the faithful: open unto me the radiant gate of repentance, O Sovereign Lady, and deliver me from the gates of death.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Establish me who am bowed down by passions from the assaults of the adversary, O Mother of God, as thou hast given birth to the Fountain of dispassion; for unto thee have I fled, the Consoler of my miserable soul.

*Both now:*

[f. 142vA] *Theotokion:*

Look upon me the humble one, O Sovereign Lady, and save me who am without hope; for thou art my expectation and protection, my life and the light of my heart, O Theotokos, and my confirmation.

*Lord, have mercy (thrice). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6:*

Truly thou art the strength of the helpless, O Most Pure Virgin Mother; therefore, we the humble are exalted through thee, in thee we hold our head high; unto all thou art a Protection, and an intercessor before God.[^123]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

An Angel made the furnace pour forth dew for the holy Children, while the command of God consumed the Chaldeans; and it persuaded the tyrant to cry out: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers![^124]

*Troparion:*

We ever have thee as our cleansing before God, O All-immaculate One. Therefore, O Most Pure One, entreat Him to deliver from fearful torments yonder all who truly confess thee to be the Theotokos.

*Troparion:*

Raise me up who am held fast by sloth unto the doing of divine deeds, O Most Pure One, strengthening me against the enemies who ever sorely battle against me and beguile me with contrary thoughts.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Leave me not to perish, O Virgin, nor suffer me to become food for the fierce serpent who ever thirsts for my destruction; but grant me thy rich mercy.

*Both now:*

[f. 143rA] *Theotokion:*

Cease not to pray to our God Who loves mankind, O All-immaculate One, that we may receive complete forgiveness, O Pure One, and may attain the prepared heavenly good things and joy that cannot be taken away.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

From the flame Thou didst pour forth dew for the holy ones, and with water Thou didst burn up the sacrifice of the righteous one; for Thou, O Christ, dost accomplish all things whatsoever Thou willest: therefore we exalt Thee, the Lord, unto all ages.[^125]

*Troparion:*

Through thy childbearing, O All-honorable One, show my barren heart to be fruitful in good works, by entreating God.

*Troparion:*

Bring a supplication unto God, O Virgin, to save our souls, O full of the grace of God, and deliver us from the reception of passions and the evil temptation of demons.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Unto thy protection do I flee, O All-immaculate One, and I now present thee as the Protectress of my life: deliver me, O Maiden, from the fearful judgment, the trial, and the eternal fire.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Establish my soul which hath been shaken by the malice of the enemy, O Pure Virgin, and rescue me from the fiery torment and the painful portion of the goats.[^126]

[f. 143vA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

It is impossible for men to see God, upon Whom the ranks of Angels dare not gaze; but through thee, O Most Pure One, the incarnate Word appeared unto us, Whom magnifying with the heavenly hosts, we bless thee.[^127]

*Troparion:*

The surging of passions troubles me, and with pleasures drowns me, O all-immaculate Virgin, who didst bear the Helmsman, Christ: stretch forth a hand of help unto me and save me, the only salvation of those who with faith bless thee.

*Troparion:*

O Bridal Chamber and Throne of the Reigning One, Mountain of God, and elect City, and Paradise, all-radiant Cloud of the Sun: enlighten my soul, driving away the cloud of my sins, O full of divine grace.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

O Pure Virgin, graced of God, Gate of Light: open the gates of my soul, locking out the entrance of sin, lest the hand of the deceiver seize me, leading me grievously unto all-ruinous and fearful torment.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Arouse thyself, my soul, and watch in prayers and in all-good cares, driving away the sleep of despondency, having ever as an alert protectress the Pure Mother of God, the all-bountiful.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON TUESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 6)

[f. 144rA] *On Tuesday at Small Compline, Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos:*

Traversing the deep on foot as if on dry land, and beholding Pharaoh the pursuer drowned, Israel cried out: Let us sing unto God a song of victory![^128]

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Thus I lament over my defiled life and the multitude of my measureless evils; what to confess I am at a loss to say and am terrified! Help me, O Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

Whence shall I begin to speak of my wicked and grievous falls, passionate as I am? Woe is me, what shall become of me hereafter! But O Sovereign Lady, before the end have mercy on me.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

The hour of death and the fearful Judgment Seat I ever contemplate, O Most Pure One, and being convicted, I tremble sorely; but help me.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Beholding me now stripped of divine virtues, and far departed and estranged from God, the destroyer of good things rushes to swallow me up; hasten, O Sovereign Lady!

[f. 144vA] *Ode 3. Heirmos:*

There is none holy as Thou, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established us upon the rock of Thine acknowledgment.[^129]

*Troparion:*

Filthily have I defiled my soul with my measureless evils, passionate that I am, O Theotokos, Sovereign Lady; and whither shall I go hereafter, being utterly possessed by despair?

*Troparion:*

Slain by the snares and huntings of the deceitful enemy, O All-immaculate Sovereign Lady, quicken me; for thou, O Theotokos, didst bear the hypostatic Life of all, that I, thine unworthy servant, may piously hymn thee, the all-praiseworthy.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

There is no other among men who has committed such improper deeds, nor was any born in the world as I, O Good One, darkened in mind, who have also defiled divine Baptism.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

I have reached the extremity of evils, O Most Holy Virgin; help me quickly! Heaven and earth cry bitterly because of my improper, unbecoming deeds.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

"Christ is my power, God and Lord," the venerable Church in godly manner calls out, crying: feasting in the Lord from a pure understanding.

*Troparion:*

The ranks of Angels and the hosts of the Heavenly Powers are in awe of the dominion of thy Son, O Pure One; yet now I, despairing, am possessed by fearless impudence.

[f. 145rA] *Troparion:*

All creation was greatly amazed and terrified, beholding me doing grievous, evil, and improper things, and it marvels at the great mercy of thy Son.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Grievously have I defiled the bodily church, and into the temple of the Lord, wherein men enter with trembling, I the prodigal enter without shame; woe is me!

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

Waking early from the night, O Lover of mankind, enlighten me, I pray, and guide me in Thy commandments, and teach me, O Savior, to do Thy will.[^169]

*Troparion:*

I have kindled for myself the fire of Gehenna by unbecoming deeds, and have grievously drawn down the wrath of God: help me, O Pure One, and forsake me not.

[f. 156vA] I continually ask for remission of transgressions, yet unceasingly commit most lawless things, and embitter thee, O Most Pure One: O Cleansing of the faithful, take pity on me.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

Him Whom thou didst bear of thy blood, O Pure One, perfect Man and true God, Jesus: entreat Him that we may be delivered from eternal fire.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

O Gate impassable, I pray: open unto me the gates of true repentance, and show me the pathways unto salvation, O Pure One, the Directress of all.[^170]

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

I cried with my whole heart unto the merciful God, and He heard me from the lowest hell, and brought up my life from corruption.[^171]

*Troparion:*

The waves of unbecoming thoughts engulf me now: but do thou, O Sovereign Lady, guide me by thy tender mercy unto the calm haven of true repentance.

*Troparion:*

Having acquired thee as hope and unconquerable rampart, and firm protectress, O Maiden, we are delivered from transgressions, grievous passions, and every harm.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

I fall down before thee, O Pure Virgin, and cry with weeping, O Theotokos: deliver my wretched soul from the future judgment and the eternal fire.

*Both now:*

[f. 157rA] *Theotokion:*

Thou art strength, O Most Pure One, unto those weak in soul through thy prayers: therefore despise me not who am sick in soul, but heal me.

*Lord, have mercy (thrice). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6:*

Through the tender mercy of Thy compassions, O Christ, Thou didst descend upon earth, and being incarnate of the Virgin, Thou didst sanctify all: all those who are of earth Thou didst draw up unto Heaven. Therefore, hoping in Thee, we shall never err, but are delivered through Thee in this present age: for Thou art our Savior, O Creator, our God.[^172]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

We have sinned, we have transgressed, and have not been justified before Thee, neither have we kept nor done as Thou hast commanded us: yet betray us not utterly, O God of the fathers![^173]

*Troparion:*

When my wretched soul must endure separation from the body, and there shall be none to deliver or comfort it: stand by me then, O Sovereign Lady, and deliver me from the malice of demons.

*Troparion:*

I fall down before thee, and offer fervent drops of tears: I know thy loving long-suffering and meekness: do thou have mercy on me now, O Pure One, forgive and save me.

*Glory:*

[f. 157vA] *Troparion:*

Have mercy, O Virgin, on my estranged and wretched soul; see also the unstable burning of my flesh, and grant me saving help and deliverance.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

The Son and Word, co-unoriginate and consubstantial with the Father, took from thee our flesh immutable, endowed with mind and animated by a soul, as He Himself knows, and in it renewed our nature unto what is better, O Pure Virgin.[^174]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Him Whom the heavenly hosts glorify, and before Whom the Cherubim and Seraphim tremble: let every breath and creation sing, bless, and exalt Him above all unto all ages.[^175]

*Troparion:*

I am at a loss contemplating my deeds, and tremble before the Judge of the fearful judgment seat: what answer shall I, the wretched one, bring then! O Sovereign Lady of the world, be thou my Protectress.

*Troparion:*

Turn me not away, O Sovereign Lady, from thy face when I look upon thine image; but be merciful unto me, and deliver me from the condemnation that is at hand.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

O Bride of God, Mary unwedded, deliver me, thy servant, from every malicious harm, I cry unto thee: and in the age to come stand by me, only Protectress of Christians.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

To show me, being perfectly cleansed, a partaker of His divine Kingdom, that He might deify mankind, Thy Son, O Sovereign Lady, appeared from thee as perfect Man:

[f. 158rA] Him do thou entreat, O Pure One.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

O thou who didst receive the Joy of the Angels and didst give birth to thy Creator: O Virgin, save those who magnify thee.[^176]

*Troparion:*

Thou alone art the Protectress of men, O Most Pure One, thou art the rampart of Christians, O Pure One; thee do I now set forth as Mediatress before Christ for me the lowly one: that through thy prayers He may have mercy on me, the wretched one.

*Troparion:*

Nightly works have I committed, and the night of torment is about righteously to cover me who am stripped of light, and to receive every torment of hades: but O Pure Virgin, who didst give birth to the Judge and God, deliver me from every torment.

*Glory:*

*Troparion:*

The time of my life have I spent in evil deeds, and have drawn near to the gates of hades, and go thither utterly unprepared: help me, O Good Theotokos, for in thee have I placed my hope.

*Both now:*

*Theotokion:*

O divinely magnificent Sovereign Lady, Adornment of Angels, Glory of Martyrs: pray together with them that we may find mercy and remission of debts, and that all who perform divine works may be enabled well to finish the course of our life.

*It is truly meet: Trisagion: and after Our Father: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*
