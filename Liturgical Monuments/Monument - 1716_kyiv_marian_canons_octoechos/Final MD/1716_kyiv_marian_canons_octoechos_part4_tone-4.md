## ON SATURDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 4)

*On Saturday at Compline,*[^48]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 4. Heirmos:*

Israel of old traversed the abyss of the Red Sea with unwetted feet; and by Moses' arms outstretched in the likeness of the Cross, he conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

O thou who alone protectest in temptations and sorrows, as thou art All-Good, accept from the heart the supplications of those that fervently flee unto thy protection, O Most Pure One.

*Troparion:*

Having found thee as a storm-free Haven, I, unreasoning one, driving away tempestuous and distressful assaults, sing unto thee a hymn of thanksgiving, O Mother unwedded.

*Glory...*

[f. 83vA] Beholding me with thy maternal and gentle eye, O Mother of God, held fast in tribulation and sorrow, swiftly set me free: for I call upon thee for help.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Unto me who am grievously bowed down by distressing sorrows, O Sovereign Lady: as the only merciful, Good Protectress of thy servants, stretch forth a hand of supplication, and deliver me from fierce calamities.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

The bow of the mighty has grown weak, and the feeble have girded themselves with strength; therefore my heart has been established in the Lord.

*Troparion:*

Having acquired thee as a strong weapon and a rampart, I conquer the hosts of adversaries, and sing of thy greatness, O Theotokos Unwedded.

*Troparion:*

Thou dost destroy the furnace of griefs and quench the scorching heat of despair; for who is such as thou, O Virgin Theotokos, our hope?

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Give ear unto the voice of thy servant who needs thy help, O Mother of God, my hope: hear me, and snatch me from temptations.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The torment of the multitude of transgressions has come upon us, bearing ruinous death. But save thy servants, O Theotokos: for thou art able.

[f. 84rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Beholding Thee lifted up upon the Cross, O Sun of righteousness, the Church stood in her order, worthily crying: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:*

Overcoming all those who war against me and fiercely strive to seize my soul, preserve me, O Sovereign Lady, have mercy, and save me: for unto thee do I flee, thy servant.

*Troparion:*

Delivering me from a deceitful tongue, O my Good Protectress, show me unharmed also by worldly deeds: for thou canst do much, as thou art the Mother of the Creator.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Knowing thee as a painless Healer, I, feeble one, call in spirit and with lips: heal me, O Sovereign Lady, have mercy, and save me: for unto thee do I flee, thy servant.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Leave me not to be betrayed unto temptations, O Mother of our God: but from every sorrow and human malice preserve me unharmed. For thou art a Helper unto us all.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou, my Lord, hast come into the world as Light, the Holy Light Who turnest back from the gloom of ignorance those who praise Thee in faith.

*Troparion:*

Direct, O Pure One, the prayer of thy servant unto the Lord thy Son: that I may find release from my many transgressions.

[f. 84vA] *Troparion:*

Deliver me from passions and calamities, O Bride of God: for God has truly appointed thee as purification for my lowliness.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Thou art my protection and continual praise, O Sovereign Lady Theotokos: for thou dost in no wise despise those who flee unto thee.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Have mercy, O Pure One, upon those that honor thy Childbirth, and deliver [us] from torment and human bitterness: for thou hast the power so to do.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries unto Thee, having been cleansed from demonic blood by the Blood that mercifully flowed from Thy side.

*Troparion:*

Thou thyself art strength unto me, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, in the unexpected sorrows that sorely encompass me, and I cry unto thee: for thou art a great Protectress unto thy servant.

*Troparion:*

Heal the wounds of my soul, O Most Pure One; defend me, O Virgin Maiden, and deliver thy servant from slander, from assault, and from the perversity of the enemy.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Crush the unjust assailants who attack me, who ever flee unto thee, and leave me not to perish: for all things are possible unto thee, O Pure One, as the Maiden of God.

*Both now...*

[f. 85rA] *Theotokion:*

Overcome the raging wave of my soul: for a multitude of transgressions, temptations, and sorrows, O Sovereign Lady, have risen up against me; but do thou thyself save me.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*[^49]

Having darkened my prodigal mind with many transgressions, I cry unto thy mighty protection, O Theotokos: enlighten the pupils of my soul, make the radiant dawn of repentance shine upon me, and clothe me in the armor of light, O Pure Mother of God.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

In the furnace the fire slew the Chaldeans, but the God of righteousness gave rest unto the righteous Abrahamic children; exceedingly praised Lord and God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

Swiftly destroying the violence of the Hagarenes with the sword of thy prayers, O Mary, preserve thy people and thy flock, who cry unto thy Son: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou![^50]

*Troparion:*

O Tabernacle of equal majesty, accept me who flee unto thee, lest the enemy who desires to destroy me seize me, as I cry: Exalted God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O Mother of God, Mary, swiftly anticipate thy servant, who is submerged in the tempestuous waves of temptations [f. 85vA] and has no helper, yet cries unto thee: O Hope of the ends of the earth, have mercy upon me!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Dispel now human reasonings, O Good Theotokos, since they are the cause of sins, through thy divine prayers; and deliver thy servants from grievous temptation and from every harm.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

O Redeemer of all, Almighty One, having descended into the midst of the flame, Thou didst bedew those who acted piously, and didst teach them to sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

A lawless nation has come upon us, boasting to destroy thy servants; having consumed them, O Most Pure One, shelter those that cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

Thy many compassions mercifully save us, O only Mother of God, from sinful judgment and diverse temptations: for having given birth unto God, thou hast mercy upon His world.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Since thou art my strength and help, I fear not the rage of enemies: but I sing of thee, O Sovereign Lady, and cry unto thy Son: Bless the Lord, all ye works!

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Have tenderhearted compassion now upon my supplication, and grant me joy instead of sorrow: that I may sing of thee, O Sovereign Lady, and cry unto thy Son: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

[f. 86rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Eve through the disease of disobedience brought in the curse, but thou, O Virgin Theotokos, through the blossoming of thy bearing in the womb didst make blessing bud forth unto the world: therefore we all magnify thee.

*Troparion:*

Having sharpened a weapon against us, the deceitful, lawless Arabian takes counsel; but do thou, O Virgin Theotokos, by the power of the Cross and of thy prayers, arm thy servants against him: therefore we proclaim thy glory.[^51]

*Troparion:*

Strength against enemies was given unto thee, O Sovereign Lady, and deliverance from calamities unto me. What then shall I offer unto thee, I am at a loss; nevertheless, the thanksgiving that I have I offer unto thee: accept this now, and save me.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

All-radiant Mother of the Creator of all, Consolation of the sorrowful, Protectress of the drowning, Defender of the overwhelmed: do thou preserve me unto the end of my life.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Unto me who am oppressed by many sins and calamities, despise me not now, O All-Lauded One; I offer unto thee a sacrifice of praise, crying diligently unto thee: Holy Theotokos, help me, for glorifying thee I conclude my song.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

## ON SUNDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 4)

*On Sunday at Compline,*[^52]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 4. Heirmos:*

Israel of old traversed the abyss of the Red Sea with unwetted feet; and by Moses' arms outstretched in the likeness of the Cross, he conquered the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Accept from the soul my supplication, O Most Pure Sovereign Lady, who didst bear God in the flesh: for unto thee have I fled as a mighty Helper, that I may not be disappointed of my hope.

*Troparion:*

I fall down before thee as a servant, O Most Pure Mother of God: as thou hast great boldness, deliver me from all sorrows by thy prayers, interceding with thy Son.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Submerged by the waves of the sea of life, and held fast by fierce temptations, I have hastened unto the storm-free haven of thy protection; therefore deliver me, O Theotokos, from fierce evils.

*Both now...*

[f. 87rA] *Theotokion:*

Look down upon thy servant with thy tenderhearted and quiet eye, and make haste to hear, O Good One, fulfilling the supplication of thy servant, O Most Pure One, and destroying deceitful counsels.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Thy Church makes glad in Thee, O Christ, crying: Thou art my strength, O Lord, my refuge, and my establishment.

*Troparion:*

A Helper against enemies and an Ally in battles, O Sovereign Lady, art thou unto the faithful, and a Refuge unto those in grief.

*Troparion:*

Implore Him Whom thou didst bear in the flesh, the God of all, as thy Son: to grant me release from my evils and from worldly assaults.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Look down mercifully upon our lowliness, O Sovereign Lady: that we thy servants may be delivered from impending wrath.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Being ever saved by thy protection from all sorrows, O Good Sovereign Lady, we offer praise unto thy Son.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

I heard report of Thee, O God, and was afraid; I understood Thy works, and was filled with wonder, O Lord: for the earth was full of Thy praise.

*Troparion:*

As thou hast boldness unto thy Son, O Pure Mother of God, from present [f. 87vA] temptation deliver me, destroying the persistent counsels of the enemies that ever war against me.

*Troparion:*

Ye choirs of the higher Ranks, Martyrs, and Righteous Ones with Apostles, the divine assembly of Prophets, and the Venerable, together with the Theotokos and Mother, implore Christ for our sake.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Accept Thy most glorious Mother, O Christ, praying for the world, crying mercifully unto Thee: O My Son, accept My supplication, and stay the wrath that overhangs the earth.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

I fall down before thee, O Theotokos, and pray from the depth of my heart: turn away the present temptation from me, that having been delivered from fierce evils, I may offer a hymn unto thy brightness.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

The ungodly shall not see Thy glory, O Christ; but we, rising early from the night, sing of Thee, O Only-Begotten, the Radiance of the Father's glory of the Godhead, O Merciful One.

*Troparion:*

O Hope of the earthly and Protection, Pure One, have tenderhearted compassion upon our lowliness, we pray, and free us from impending wrath.

*Troparion:*

Holding thy prayer as an insurmountable rampart, O Pure One, we cry unto thee: Have tenderhearted compassion, O Sovereign Lady, and drive away visible enemies.

*Glory...*

[f. 88rA] *Troparion:*

Who ever praise the hope that we have in thee, O Most Pure One, may we not be put to shame; we pray with tears, and bow down before thy goodness.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

With thy most pure hand, O All-Lauded One, scatter the enemies that rise up against us: that the wretched ones may understand that we have placed our hope in thee.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Prefiguring the three-day burial, the Prophet Jonah cried, praying in the sea-monster: From corruption save me, O Jesus, King of powers!

*Troparion:*

Cleanse our sins, O Lover of mankind, we pray, through the prayers of her that seedlessly bore Thee: for for our sake, O Word, Thou didst pour out Thy Precious Blood.

*Troparion:*

An evil assembly has gathered against us of those who unrighteously war against us, O Bride of God; but cast them down, as thou didst of old through Samson.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Hear our supplication, O Sovereign Lady, we pray, and calm the storm-tossed waves of diverse sicknesses, whereby enemies have gathered against us.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Thou Who didst turn water into wine in Cana of Galilee, O Christ, transform my sorrow into joy, as Thou art generous, and change weeping into gladness, through compassion for the sake of the Theotokos.

[f. 88vA] *Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*[^53]

Having darkened my prodigal mind with many transgressions, I cry unto thy mighty protection, O Theotokos: enlighten the pupils of my soul, make the radiant dawn of repentance shine upon me, and clothe me in the armor of light, O Pure Mother of God.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Deliver us not up utterly for Thy Name's sake, neither break Thou Thy covenant, and take not Thy mercy away from us, O Lord God of our fathers, praised above all unto the ages.

*Troparion:*

Being in diverse temptations and sorrows, unto thee, O Pure One, my salvation, have I now fled, crying: May I not turn back put to shame from my expectation, but hear and deliver me from the nets of those that hunt me.

*Troparion:*

As a servant I, wretched one, bow my neck, and cry from my heart; stretching forth my hands, I bend the knees, and implore thee, Most Pure Virgin: to be delivered from the sorrows that ever come upon me from the assault of the stranger.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

O Most Glorious and Pure Mary, praise of the earthly, grant thy help unto us who pray and piously bow down before thy Childbirth: for we have acquired no other hope or protectress besides thee.

*Both now...*

[f. 89rA] *Theotokion:*

Having borne God beyond understanding and beyond all nature, O Mary unwedded, Protectress of the faithful: deliver those that honor thee pure and unharmed from sudden temptations, and from human enemies, both visible and invisible.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

Stretching forth his hands, Daniel shut the yawning mouths of the lions in the pit; and the youths, lovers of piety, girded with virtue, quenched the power of the fire, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:*

I dare not lift up my hands unto thy Son, O Pure One: for I am wholly defiled. Therefore do I venture to flee unto thee, O Sovereign Lady: intercede with the merciful and forgiving God, that we may be delivered from adversary enemies who afflict us.

*Troparion:*

Mind and heart and soul have I placed upon thee, O Most Pure One. Therefore have tenderhearted compassion, O Pure Sovereign Lady, falling down before the Merciful One on behalf of me, lowly and impatient: that He may save me from every distress, and plague, and sorrow.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

With Thine armor, as Thou art mighty, overthrow those that war against us, O Lord, and grant victory, O Master, unto those that hope in Thee in faith: the Theotokos implores Thee, together with the Forerunner John, and the Choir of Apostles, and Thy Martyrs.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The joy of the Annunciation did Gabriel once bring unto thee, O Pure Virgin, and the mourning of our First Mother [f. 89vA] was dissolved by thy Childbirth. Therefore, having cleansed the despondency of my soul, do thou thyself show me unashamed through thy prayers.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

A cornerstone was cut out without hands from an unquarried mountain, even from thee, O Virgin: Christ, Who joined together the sundered natures. Therefore, rejoicing, we magnify thee, O Theotokos.

*Troparion:*

Show forth thy help in affliction, O Virgin Theotokos; diligently incline thine ear, and hear those that cry fervently, and change fierce evils, delivering us by thy prayers.

*Troparion:*

I am wholly held fast by sloth, and am in the depth of despair because of my transgressions. Therefore stretch forth a hand unto me, O Virgin Mother, even as Christ unto Peter, and deliver me from the depth of transgressions.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Subdue, O Virgin, an unbridled and heartless tongue, sharpened like an arrow, that seeks to shoot and slay me, and melt it like wax, and make its counsel vain.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Dispel all the counsels of those armed against us, O Mother of God the Most High, and fill with joy those that hope in thee: that we all may fervently proclaim thy protection.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and the rest as usual: and the Dismissal.*

Beholding me with thy merciful and gentle eye, O Mother of God, held fast in tribulation and sorrow, swiftly set me free: for I call upon thee for help.

*Both now...*

[f. 90vA] *Theotokion:*

Both Eve and Adam were delivered from condemnation through thee, O Pure One; with them, turn now the sorrowful tears of us who fall down before thee into joy, and free us from misfortunes.

The bow of the mighty has grown weak, and the feeble have girded themselves with power; therefore my heart is glad in the Lord.

## ON TUESDAY AT SMALL COMPLINE (TONE 4)

*On Tuesday at Compline,*[^56]

*Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.*

*Ode 1, Tone 4. Heirmos:*

I will open my mouth, and it shall be filled with the Spirit, and I will utter a word unto the Queen and Mother: and I will appear radiantly keeping festival, and rejoicing I will sing of her wonders.

*Verse: Most Holy Lady Theotokos, save us.*

*Troparion:*

Having seedlessly given birth in the flesh unto the immeasurable Wisdom and Power of God, even Christ, O All-Immaculate One: thou hast acquired the dominion of strength and divine majesty by thy Childbirth, O Mother of God.

*Troparion:*

O thy Childbirth beyond understanding, O Most Pure One, whereby the multitude of the earthly was enriched with incorruption, and now worthily blesses thee, the Mediatrix of renewal!

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Heal thy servant, sick in soul and body, O Virgin, by thine overshadowing: for I have known thee as the Protectress of all who are in grief, as having given birth unto our salvation.

*Both now...*

[f. 94rA] *Theotokion:*

Fiercely submerged in the abyss of griefs and tempest of immeasurable tribulations, stretch forth unto me the hand of salvation, O Sovereign Lady, and lead me forth from the depth of evils.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*

Establish thy singers, O Theotokos, thou living and plentiful fount of incorruption, who have spiritually formed a choir; and in thy divine glory, make them worthy of crowns of glory.

*Troparion:*

Thou dost manifestly sprinkle streams of healings upon the sick, O Virgin: for the Lord, being [the Lord of] mercy, Who was born of thee beyond understanding, showed thee as a fountain of compassion, O Sovereign Lady.

*Troparion:*

A beautiful bridal chamber of the divine Word and a divine bridal hall beyond nature didst thou become, O Virgin Mother. Therefore open unto me the mercy of thy compassions, leading me up unto salvation.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

All my strength has been brought to death by the multitude of immeasurable evils: for I am tossed by a tempest of many griefs; help me, O Theotokos, who didst give birth unto Life and consolation unto those who weep.

*Theotokion:*

*Troparion:*

Despairing of all help, I have fled unto thee, O All-Immaculate One, a sovereign Defender and Protection: cast me not away from thy divine protection.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Adorned with golden robes as a Queen, thou standest now at the right hand of the King and God, O Sovereign Lady, praying for thy servants.

[f. 106rA] *Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Sitting in glory upon the Throne of the Godhead, Jesus, the Most Divine, came upon a light cloud, from the Most Pure Virgin, and saved those that cry: Glory to Thy power, O Christ!

*Troparion:*

Held fast by despondency, wholly darkened by the gloom of passions, and enslaved to sin: free me, O Maiden Blessed of God, and reconcile me unto thy Son and our God.

*Troparion:*

Quicken me who am slain by the malice of the deceiver, O All-Immaculate One, as thou didst give birth unto Life, for unto thee have I fled; and raise me who am buried in the depth of falls, as thou art merciful, O All-Immaculate One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

I have sunk down into the tomb of my measureless pleasures, and am held fast by despondency and sloth: but O Good One who didst give birth unto the resurrection of all, quicken me and save me.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The Prophet of old named thee the sacred, divine Mountain, O Pure Virgin, wholly overshadowed by virtues: from which the saving Word appeared, unto the recreation and enlightenment of our souls.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

The ungodly shall not behold Thy glory, O Christ; but waking early at dawn out of the night, we sing of Thee, O Only-Begotten, the Radiance of the Father's glory of the Godhead, O Merciful One.

[f. 106vA] *Troparion:*

May my humble soul rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, may it fear thy Son and perform His commandments from the heart; guide me by thy prayers, O All-Immaculate and Most Pure One.

*Troparion:*

With all my heart, O Good Sovereign Lady, confessing the Redeemer, bless my impassioned soul, driving away the ignorance of my heart, O All-Immaculate One.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Preserve my soul as the apple of the eye under the shelter of thy wings, O Good and Most Pure One, and deliver me from the torment and torture of the wicked spirits.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Show thyself as delivering thy servant from the assaults of passions and from the wicked demons, O Most Pure One, for he has acquired thee as a mighty protection and an invincible Defender.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

Prefiguring the three-day Burial, the Prophet Jonah, praying in the whale, cried aloud: Deliver me from corruption, O Jesus, King of hosts!

*Troparion:*

Cast down him who ever wars against me, O Sovereign Lady, and dispel the wicked cloud of my thoughts, O Mother of God, holy Portal of the full Sun.

*Troparion:*

Cause the dawn of repentance to shine upon me, O Sovereign Lady, and dispel the clouds of my evil thoughts, which are drawn [f. 107rA] and corruption.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Assuage the fierce tempest of my passions, and consume the storm of evil thoughts, O firm Haven of all and Protection, All-Immaculate One.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

The transgressions of my soul have multiplied more than the sand of the sea, and like a heavy burden they weigh me down: but O Virgin, having had compassion before the end, save me.

*Then, Lord, have mercy (3 times). Glory... Both now...*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*[^63]

Warm protection in calamities, and our helper, and reconciliation unto God: through whom we were delivered from corruption, the Theotokos do we, the faithful, call blessed.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

The Abrahamic children of old in Babylon trampled down the furnace flame, crying aloud with praises: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:*

Thee, O Pure Theotokos, do I faithfully invoke: preserve my humble heart, and deliver me from the flame of torment and eternal darkness.

*Troparion:*

Tear up the handwriting of my transgressions, O Pure Virgin, with the Spear of thy Son, and deliver me, I pray thee, from every possession at the hour of my end.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Quench, O Pure Virgin, with the streams of my tears and the shower of thy compassions, the furnace which [f. 107vA] the multitude of my sins prepares for me through demonic action.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Deliver us from the misfortunes that come upon us, O only Mother of the Word, Ever-Virgin, and from every torment, who truly and understandingly sing of thee unto all ages.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

O Almighty Redeemer of all, having descended into the midst of the flame, Thou didst bedew those who practiced piety, and didst teach them to sing: All ye works, bless the Lord, the Lord!

*Troparion:*

Heal my ailing soul, enlighten my darkened mind, and snatch me from the fire and from the unutterable torments of punishment, O Virgin Theotokos, Bride of God.

*Troparion:*

Having bowed down with irrational passions, I have descended into the depth of perdition, and have wrought the burning of the flame there: deliver me from them, O Virgin Theotokos, thy servant.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

Dry up the abyss of my sins, having given birth unto the Abyss of loving-kindness, the Redeemer and Lord, and loose the bonds of my sins before the end, O thou who didst give birth unto the Savior of all.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having become incarnate from thy womb, the Lord rules over creation, and shows thee forth, O Virgin, as the Lady of all things visible and invisible: glorifying thee as His Immaculate Mother.

[f. 108rA] *Ode 9. Heirmos:*

Eve through the disease of disobedience brought in the curse, but thou, O Virgin Theotokos, through the blossoming of thy bearing in the womb didst make blessing bud forth unto the world: therefore we all magnify thee.

*Troparion:*

Show forth my heart, which has been barren of divine virtues, to be fruitful, O Most Pure Virgin Theotokos: thou who wast born of a barren woman by the will of Him Who nourishes all, that I may sing of thee, the All-Hymned One.

*Troparion:*

Illuminate me with thy light, O All-Immaculate One, who didst give birth unto the Unapproachable Light, dispel the clouds of my passions, snatch me from the dark gloom, and make me worthy of the divine light: that I may sing of thee, O Virgin Mother.

*Glory...*

*Troparion:*

By the sprinkling of thy compassions, O Pure One, wash away the foulness of my heart, and vouchsafe me ever to pour forth streams of tears, subduing the torrents of passions and delivering me from torment.

*Both now...*

*Theotokion:*

Having given birth unto the Blessed God, O Blessed Theotokos: swiftly free me now, O Sovereign Lady, from evil carnal love, and subject me, who am perishing in sloth, unto the divine will.

*Then, It is truly meet: Trisagion, and after the Our Father: and the rest as usual, and the Dismissal.*

### Notes

[^48]: Saturday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Saturday evening liturgically inaugurates Sunday, the Day of the Holy Resurrection, opening the liturgical week of the Fourth Tone (*Гласъ 4*).
[^49]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Saturday Compline in Tone 4 is sung as an idiomelon to its own Fourth Tone melody, imploring the Theotokos to dispel spiritual darkness and clothe the soul in the Paul-inspired "armor of light" (Rom. 13:12).
[^50]: The troparion explicitly evokes the historical Ruthenian and Ukrainian context of Crimean Tatar and Ottoman Turkish ("Hagarene") slave raids and invasions, petitioning the Mother of God to intervene with the "sword of her prayers" to protect the Orthodox flock.
[^51]: "Arabian" (*Аравлянинъ*, Greek *Ἄραψ*) functions in Byzantine and Ruthenian hymnography as an ethnonymic designation for Islamic Ottoman forces, reflecting the historical raids threatening the southern frontiers of Ruthenia and the wider Christian East.
[^52]: Sunday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Sunday evening liturgically inaugurates Monday, dedicated across the weekly cycle to the Bodiless Hosts (the Holy Angels and Archangels).
[^53]: This Sessional Hymn is repeated verbatim from the preceding service (Saturday Compline, Fol. 85r), serving here again as the meditative midpoint kathisma of Sunday Compline in Tone 4.
[^54]: Monday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Monday evening liturgically introduces Tuesday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Holy Prophet, Forerunner and Baptist of the Lord, John.
[^55]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Monday Compline in Tone 4 petitions the Theotokos as the unceasing mediatrix before Christ the Lover of mankind, imploring her to accept the evening supplications of her servants.
[^56]: Tuesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Tuesday evening liturgically inaugurates Wednesday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Betrayal of the Lord and the veneration of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross.
[^57]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Tuesday Compline in Tone 4 is a Stavrotheotokion (*Крестобогородиченъ*), meditating upon the maternal grief of the Virgin Mary at the Cross and the prophetic fulfillment of Symeon's words concerning the sword piercing her soul (Luke 2:35).
[^58]: Wednesday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Wednesday evening liturgically introduces Thursday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Holy Apostles and to Saint Nicholas the Wonderworker, Archbishop of Myra in Lycia.
[^59]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Wednesday Compline in Tone 4 addresses the Theotokos as the invincible Defender (*Предста́тельница нера́тная*), imploring her swift rescue from dangers before the concluding odes of the canon.
[^60]: Thursday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Thursday evening liturgically inaugurates Friday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the Passion of Christ and the veneration of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross.
[^61]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Thursday Compline in Tone 4 is a Stavrotheotokion (*Крестобогородиченъ*), portraying the maternal lamentation of the Mother of God standing before the Cross of her Son.
[^62]: Friday Small Compline (*Павечеріє мале*) on Friday evening liturgically introduces Saturday, dedicated across the Octoechos to the remembrance of All Saints and the commemoration of the faithful departed.
[^63]: This Sessional Hymn (*Сѣдаленъ*) of Friday Compline in Tone 4 extols the Mother of God as our warm protection in afflictions and our reconciliation unto God (*къ Богу премѣненіє*).
