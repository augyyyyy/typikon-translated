## On the Fifth Sunday of the Great Fast: Commemoration of our Venerable Mother Mary of Egypt[^1158]

*The order of service is similar to that of the other Sundays: in which we also sing the Menaion office of the saint of the day.*
*The later fathers, however, handed down that on this Sunday we sing the office of our venerable mother Mary of Egypt.*

*At the Lamplighting Psalms, after the Introductory Psalm and the usual kathisma of the Psalter, at "Lord, I have cried," we set 10 verses. And we sing 3 Resurrectional stichera and 3 Anatolian stichera of the Octoechos, and 3 stichera of the venerable woman, repeating them to make 6:*

*Tone 6, Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*

The pollution of past defilements held thee back from beholding the precious sights; but thy perception and thy God-wise conscience concerning what had been committed wrought thy turning unto better things. For looking upon the Icon of the blessed Maiden of God, having repented of all thy former transgressions, O all-lauded one, with boldness thou didst venerate the precious Wood.

Having with joy venerated the holy places, from thence thou didst receive the most saving viaticum of virtue, and didst run exceedingly well on thy noble journey. And crossing the stream of the Jordan, thou didst eagerly dwell in the habitation of the Forerunner, and by thy way of life didst darken the fierceness of the passions, boldly refining away, O ever-memorable mother, the properties of the flesh.

Having settled in the wilderness, thou didst banish from thy soul the images of thy passions, inscribing upon thy soul the most Godlike depiction: the forms of virtues. And so greatly didst thou shine forth, O blessed one, that thou didst even walk lightly over the waters, and was lifted up from the earth in thy supplications unto God. And now, O all-glorious Mary, standing before Christ with boldness, pray for our souls!

*Glory...*

=== LEAF p412 ===
*Glory: Tone 4:*
The power of Thy Cross, O Christ, hath wrought a marvel, for even she who was formerly a harlot strove in ascetic contest; therefore, casting aside weakness, she valiantly stood against the devil; therefore, bearing the trophy of victory, she prays for our souls!

*Both now: Theotokion of the Tone [Tone 4, first Theotokion / Dogmatikon].*

*At the Aposticha, the Stichera of the Octoechos.*

*Glory, of the venerable woman: Tone 2:*
The snares of the soul and the passions of the flesh hast thou severed with the sword of abstinence; the thoughts of transgression hast thou suppressed by the silence of discipline; and with the streams of thy tears thou didst water all the wilderness, and didst make the fruits of repentance bud forth for us; therefore we celebrate thy memory, O venerable mother!

*Both now: Theotokion, in the same Tone: "O new wonder!"*[^1159]

*Dismissal Troparion: The Resurrectional Troparion of the Tone; Glory, of the venerable woman, Tone 8:*
In thee, O mother, that which was according to the image was preserved in safety; for taking up the cross, thou didst follow Christ, and by thine actions thou didst teach to despise the flesh, for it passes away, but to care for the soul, a thing immortal; therefore, O venerable Mary, thy spirit rejoices together with the Angels!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Thou Who for our sake wast born of a Virgin...

*The same also at "God is the Lord."*
*And the rest of the order of service as usual.*

*At Matins:*

*After the whole usual order of service, 3 Canons: the Resurrectional Canon with its Theotokia for 6; the present Canon of the Triodion for 4; and for the venerable woman for 4.*[^1160]

*Canon on the Rich Man and on Lazarus:*[^1161]

*Ode 1, Tone 8. Heirmos: "Having traversed the water:"*
Thou hast left me unto the riches of pleasures, like unto the rich man who rejoiced each day in sweetness; therefore I pray Thee, O Savior: deliver me from the fire, even as Thou didst deliver Lazarus!

=== LEAF p413 ===
I have clothed myself in pleasures, O Savior, even as he who put on fine linen and gold and golden raiment; but send me not into the fire, as Thou didst send him!

The rich man of old exulted in wealth and sweet pleasure in this corruptible life, therefore was he condemned unto torments; but Lazarus the poor was bedewed with refreshment.

*Theotokion:*
The ranks of Angels and men, O unwedded Mother, praise thee unceasingly; for thou didst bear in thine arms the Creator of them both as an Infant.

*Second Canon of the venerable woman, for 4:*

*Ode 1, Tone 6. Heirmos: "The wave of the sea:"*
Unto me who celebrate in love thy light-bearing and divine memory, send down light, O venerable woman, as thou standest before Christ the unapproachable Light, delivering me from all temptations of this life!

He Who went down into Egypt in the flesh, uncircumscribed and pre-eternal, shows thee forth from Egypt as an all-radiant star, the Lord Who knows all things before their being.

Knowing not the divine commandments, O honorable one, thou didst defile the divine image of God; yet by divine providence thou didst cleanse it again, O all-lauded one, having been deified by thy divine deeds, O venerable mother.

*Theotokion:*
O the great compassion and the ineffable condescension of Thee, my God! How by the intercessions of Thy Mother hast Thou likened her who was formerly a harlot unto the Angels, as pure and undefiled!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: "Thou art the confirmation:"*
Even as Thou didst save Lazarus from the flame, O Christ, so deliver me, Thine unworthy servant, from the fire of Gehenna!

I am the rich man in passions and pleasures, O Lord, and Lazarus the poor in lack of virtues; but save me!

The rich man clothed himself in purple and fine linen through pleasures and sins; for this cause is he tormented in the flames.

*Theotokion:*
Grant us help by thy prayers, O All-Pure One, repelling the assaults of grievous adversities!

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "Thee Who upon the waters:"*
Unto her who drew near unto the gates of destruction through unseemly deeds, He Who of old broke down the gates of Hades by the power of His Divinity opens the gates of repentance unto thee, O all-honorable one, being Himself the Door of Life.

Her who was formerly a weapon of sin, O Long-suffering and Compassionate One, Thou didst show forth, through the veneration of the weapon of the divine Cross, as conquering all the weapons and wiles of the demons!

=== LEAF p414 ===
He Who of old shed His own blood as the ransom for all makes thee pure by drops of tears, thee who was diseased with the grievous leprosy of evil deeds, He Who gave being in every way unto all!

*Theotokion:*
Higher than any word is that which hath come to pass in thee, O Virgin; for the Word of the Father dwelt in thee in a manner befitting God, granting by a single word the remission of sins unto all who transgress.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Wisdom and the Word" (Премꙋ́дрость и҆ Сло́во):*
Having bridled all the surges of the flesh by ascetic labors, thou didst show forth the courageous disposition of thy soul; for longing to behold the Cross of the Lord, O all-honorable one, thou didst crucify thyself unto the world. Therefore, O all-blessed one, thou didst lovingly direct thyself toward the desire for the angelic life. Pray thou unto Christ God that He grant remission of sins unto them that honor with love thy holy memory!

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Let us praise the Heavenly Gate and Ark, the All-Holy Mountain, the Radiant Cloud, the Unburnt Bush, the Spiritual Paradise, the Recalling of Eve, the Great Treasure of all the universe; for salvation was wrought in her for the world, and remission of ancient transgressions. Therefore we cry aloud unto her: Pray unto thy Son and God that He grant remission of sins unto them that piously worship thine all-holy Offspring!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: "I heard, O Lord:"*
The rich man was delighting in meats and making merry in clothing; but Lazarus was desiring to be fed with the crumbs from his table.

The dogs licked with their tongue the sores of Lazarus the poor, being more compassionate to the needy man than the mind of the rich man.

Of old before the gates of the rich man, O Savior, lay Lazarus tormented by the wounds of poverty, and for this is he now glorified!

*Theotokion:*
Him Whom thou didst bear, O All-Pure One, pray that they who praise thee may be saved from the bondage of the deceiver, for thou alone art our protectress!

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "Thy divine emptying upon the Cross:"*
As the Creator of human nature, as the Fountain of mercy and the Riches of compassion, O Lover of mankind, Thou didst show compassion upon her who fled unto Thee, and didst snatch her away from the destructive beast!

Having hastened to behold the Cross, O Mary, thou wast illumined with the illumination of the Cross by the divine command of Him Who conversed through the Cross, having crucified thyself unto the world, O laudable one!

Though she was formerly the cause of evil, a deceitful pleasure unto many, yet having shone forth as the sun, the venerable woman appeared as a guide unto all that sin!

=== LEAF p415 ===
*Theotokion:*
Thou hast surpassed even the heavenly mind, being the spiritual Heaven of the King of all; for outside the laws of nature, O Pure One, thou didst bear the Lawgiver and Creator of all!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: "Why hast Thou cast me off:"*
When the rich man saw Lazarus in the bosom of Abraham, rejoicing in light and glory, he cried aloud: Father Abraham, have mercy on me who am condemned in the fire, and grievously tormented in my tongue!

Thou didst enjoy the riches of life, rejoicing, Abraham said unto the rich man; for this cause art thou tormented here eternally, being in the fire, but Lazarus the poor rejoices in unending joy!

I have become rich through the deceit of life, even as the rich man who spent his whole life in pleasures, O Lover of mankind; but I entreat Thy compassions, that I may be freed from the fire, even as Lazarus was saved.

*Theotokion:*
Having acquired maternal boldness toward thy Son, O All-Pure One, despise not, we pray, a kin-like providence for us; for we Christians offer thee, and thee alone, as an acceptable propitiation unto the Master.

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "Of Thy manifestation, O Christ:"*
Moses was glorified of old on Sinai, having mystically seen the back parts of God, depicting an astonishing mystery; and now Mary, having warmly fallen down before the most pure image of the jar that received the Manna, leads an angelic life.

Desiring to see the temple of Thy majesty after the manner of the Psalms, and the spiritual habitation of Thy glory, she who had defiled Thy temple: by the spiritual prayers, O Christ, of Her that was Thy temple without knowing man, make me a temple of the Creative Spirit!

She who with the hook of the flesh caught the eyes of many through fleeting pleasure, making them food for the devil, was herself truly caught by the divine grace of the Precious Cross, having become most sweet food for Christ!

*Theotokion:*
Taught the mystery concerning thee through mystical divine utterances, O All-Pure One, the choir of prophets foretold thee in diverse manners; and Mary, having fallen down before the most pure image of the jar that received the Manna, is now a surety for sinners unto God!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: "Cleanse me, O Savior:"*
The rich man condemned himself to the fiery flame by a life of pleasure; but Lazarus the poor, having chosen poverty in this life, was accounted worthy of unending joy.

Lazarus was accounted worthy of the bosom of Abraham, enjoying eternal life; but the rich man was condemned to the fire, suffering in soul and body.

The rich man is condemned to the fire because of Lazarus; condemn not me, the wretched one, I pray Thee, O Lord Lover of mankind; but make me worthy of Thy light, even as Lazarus!

=== LEAF p416 ===
*Theotokion:*
May we be delivered from grievous transgressions by thy prayers, O pure Mother of God; and may we obtain the divine illumination of the Son of God, Who was ineffably incarnate of thee!

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "The lowest abyss:"*
The angelic hosts rejoice, O Mary, beholding in thee a way of life equal to their own, O venerable woman, and they shout glory unto the Lord.

The legions of dark demons are dismayed at the steadfastness of thy patient endurance, seeing how a woman, gloriously naked and all alone, was able to prevail.

Thou hast shone forth like the sun, O all-lauded Mary, and with wonders didst illumine all the wilderness; therefore shine upon me also with thy light!

*Theotokion:*
The Angels, enlightened by the glory of thy Nativity, cried aloud, O Virgin: Peace on earth unto all of us, and good will among men!

*Kontakion, Tone 3, Podoben: "Today the Virgin" (Дѣ́ва дне́сь):*
Though formerly filled with all manner of defilement, today through repentance she is shown forth as the Bride of Christ; imitating the angelic way of life, she destroys the demons with the weapon of the Cross; for this cause hast thou been revealed as a Bride of the Kingdom, O Mary all-glorious!

*Ikos:*
The ewe-lamb and daughter of Christ let us praise now in hymns, the ever-memorable Mary, who appeared as a lamb from Egypt, and having escaped from all their delusion, she alone was offered to the Church as a perfect growth, striving through abstinence and prayer beyond the measure of human nature; therefore the one Sovereign Ruler exalted the life and deeds of Mary all-glorious!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: "The Children coming forth from Judea:"*
Even as Job of old lay in a dunghill of worms and filth, so also did Lazarus sit before the gates of the rich man, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

Lazarus, rolling of old before the gates of the unmerciful man, desired the crumbs of his table, but no one gave unto him; and instead of these, he found the bosom of Abraham.

Deliver me, I pray, O my Christ, from the portion of the unmerciful rich man; and joining me unto Lazarus the poor, make me worthy to cry unto Thee in thanksgiving: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*
Having been incarnate from a virginal womb, Thou didst appear for our salvation; therefore, knowing Thy Mother to be the Theotokos, we cry in thanksgiving: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "The Children in Babylon:"*
The greatest among the fathers, the wise Zosimas, wandering through the wilderness, was accounted worthy to behold the venerable woman; and he cries: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

=== LEAF p417 ===
"Why, O Father, hast thou come to see a strange dwelling of every active virtue?" the venerable woman cried unto the elder; and she cries: "Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!"

Having put to death the surges of thy passions, O blessed one, thou hast now sped unto the haven of dispassion, crying: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:*
Thou didst conceive ineffably, remaining a virgin, O All-Pure One, and didst bear unto the world salvation, Christ our God; therefore we, all the faithful, magnify thee with hymns!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: "Sevenfold the furnace:"*
Of old the rich man, the wretched one, was brightly arrayed in scarlet raiment, and fine linen, and purple; but Lazarus the poor lay grievously at his gates, desiring to be filled with the crumbs falling from the table, and no one gave unto him; therefore he reigns together with Christ in glory!

Lazarus lay at the gates of the rich man, rotting in body with sores, and he desired to be satisfied with food, and no one gave unto him; but even the dogs compassionately licked his rot and sores with their tongue; therefore was he accounted worthy of the paradise of delight!

I have grown rich in pleasures, even as the rich man of old, clothing myself every day in purple; and I, O Many-Merciful One, condemn myself unto the sweetness of this life by pleasures and delusions; therefore I pray Thee, O Christ: deliver me from the eternal fire, unto all ages!

*Triadikon:*
The Thrice-Radiant Godhead, the single shining Ray from one three-hypostatic Nature: the Unoriginate Father, the Consubstantial Word with the Father, and the Co-existing, Consubstantial Spirit: bless, ye children; praise in song, ye priests; let the people supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "Be astonished in fear, O heaven:"*
Thou Who searchest the depths of the heart, Who foreknowest all that is ours before being: Thou didst snatch away from the violent life her who fled unto Thee, O Savior, who urgently and unceasingly cries unto Thy love for mankind: Ye priests, bless; ye people, supremely exalt unto all ages!

O the honorable change, the turning of thyself unto that which is better, O honorable one! O divine love, which hated carnal pleasures! O fervent and divine faith, O all-lauded Mary! Whom we faithfully praise and supremely exalt unto the ages!

Thou hast found the recompense of thy sufferings and the reward of thy labors, O honorable Mary, whereby thou didst cast down the murderous enemy; and now with the Angels thou criest aloud, unceasingly chanting a hymn and supremely exalting Christ unto the ages!

*Theotokion:*
Renew me entirely for the sake of goodness in thy womb, O Pure One, having not corrupted the properties of His nature, as Master of all the ages; therefore as the cause of our salvation we sing of thee in hymns unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: "Thee, who knewest not wedlock:"*
Make me poor Lazarus, O Christ, I pray Thee, destroying my desires for pleasures, as Thou art God by nature; and make me rich in virtues, that in faith with hymnody I may magnify Thee!

=== LEAF p418 ===
Rich and unmerciful, in my mind I have despised Thy commandments through lack of faith, O Lover of mankind, being cast down grievously before the gates; but as Thou art compassionate and overflowing in mercy, raise me up, even as of old Thou didst raise Lazarus, Thy friend of four days!

We have all learned well the instructive parable; let all of us who are faithful therefore hate the unmercifulness of the rich man, that we may escape torment and ever rejoice in the bosom of Abraham!

*Theotokion:*
Thee who didst hold in thine arms the invisible God Who is praised in the heavens by all creation, and Who through thee ever grants salvation unto us, we magnify in faith!

*Another [Canon of the Saint]. Heirmos: "Weep not for Me, O Mother:"*
Easily didst thou endure the toil of the wilderness, O mother, being strengthened by the mighty power of Christ; for the foul thoughts that assailed thee, thou didst extinguish by streams of divine tears, O pure one, summit of ascetics, boast of the venerable!

With the most radiant beams the Virgin and Pure One, who alone bore Christ the Light, enlightens thee; she makes thee fearsome unto enemies, O honorable one, and manifests thee clearly unto all, O Mary, beauty of sufferers, confirmation of the venerable!

Having wisely abandoned all earthly things, thou wast shown to be an honorable habitation of the Spirit; pray thou unto Christ, the only Deliverer, that they who faithfully celebrate thy divine memory may be freed from worldly evils!

*Theotokion:*
Having hidden thyself beyond nature from the laws of nature, O Virgin, thou didst bear a new Child upon earth, O Pure One, Who is the Lawgiver and the Ancient of Days, the spiritual Heaven of the Creator of all; therefore with faith and love we call thee blessed!

*Exapostilarion:*
*The Resurrectional Exapostilarion of the Tone.*
*Then for the venerable woman, Podoben: "Thou Who didst adorn heaven with stars" (Нб҃о ѕвѣзда́ми):*
Having thee as an image of repentance, O all-venerable Mary, pray thou unto Christ that in this time of the Fast it may be granted unto us, so that in faith and love we may praise thee in hymns!

*Theotokion:*
O sweetness of the Angels, joy of the afflicted, protectress of Christians, O Virgin Mother of the Lord: intercede for us, and deliver us from eternal torments!

*At the Praises, 4 Resurrectional Stichera and 4 Anatolian Stichera of the Octoechos.*

*Then we say the Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up [Psalm 9:33 LXX].*

*And we sing the Idiomelon, Tone 1:*
The Kingdom of God is not food and drink, but righteousness and abstinence with holiness; therefore neither shall the rich enter into it, but those who place their treasures into the hands of the poor. These things David the prophet also teaches, saying: Righteous is the man that is merciful all the day long, who delights in the Lord...

...and walking in the light he will not stumble; all these things were written for our instruction, that while fasting we may practice kindness, and the Lord will give us heavenly things instead of earthly!

*Glory, the same again.*
*Both now:* Most blessed art thou...
*Great Doxology, and Dismissal. Glory, both now: the Evangelical Sticheron, and the First Hour.*[^1162]

## On the Fifth Sunday of the Great Fast, at Vespers

*At "Lord, I have cried," we set 6 verses, and we sing 4 penitential stichera of the Tone from the Octoechos, and prosomoia of the Triodion:*

*Tone 1, Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (Прехва́льнїи мꙋ́ченицы):*
Having become poor, O Christ, Thou rich One, Thou didst enrich men with immortality and radiance; therefore, enrich me who have become impoverished by the pleasures of life with virtues, and join me unto Lazarus the poor, delivering me from the torment of the rich man and from the Gehenna that lies before me!

I have become grievously rich in wickedness, and have loved sweetness, and have enjoyed the pleasures of this life, O Lord, and I am condemned to the fire of Gehenna; wasting away in mind from hunger, cast down before the gates of divine deeds, like Lazarus despised, have compassion upon me, O Master!

*Another Sticheron, in the same Tone, Podoben: "A radiant cloud" (Ѻ҆́блакъ тѧ̀):*[^1166]
Eagerly beginning the sixth week of the venerable Fasts, let us, the faithful, offer unto the Lord a prefestal hymn of the Palms, as He comes in glory by the power of His Divinity to Jerusalem to put death to death; let us therefore piously prepare the emblems of victory, the branches of virtues, crying aloud: "Hosanna" unto the Creator of all!

*And 3 stichera of the Menaion.*
*Glory, both now: Theotokion.*
*Entrance: "O Gladsome Light."*
*Prokeimenon, Tone 8: "Thou hast given an inheritance" [Psalm 60:6 LXX], with its verses (see fol. 175)[^1167].*
*And the rest as usual.*

=== LEAF p420 ===
*At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the day, twice: Tone 1:*
Marvelous is the Savior's disposition of love toward mankind for our sake: possessing the knowledge of things to come as though present, He set forth the life of Lazarus and of the rich man; contemplating the end of both, let us flee the unmercifulness and inhumanity of the one, and let us emulate the patience and greatness of soul of the other, so that, being comforted with him in the bosom of Abraham, we may cry aloud: O righteous Judge, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Martyrikon:*
By the prayers of all the Saints and of the Theotokos, O Lord, grant us Thy peace, and have mercy on us, as Thou alone art compassionate!

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
O joy of the heavenly ranks, strong defense of men upon earth, O All-Pure Virgin: save us who flee unto thee, for in thee, after God, have we placed our hope, O Theotokos!

*Theotokion:*
