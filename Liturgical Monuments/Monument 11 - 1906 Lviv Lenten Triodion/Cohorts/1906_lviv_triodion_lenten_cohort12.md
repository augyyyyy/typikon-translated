My wealth, O Savior, have I squandered in wantonness; I am devoid of godly fruits, and hungry I cry: O Father of compassions, prevent me and have compassion upon me!

*Troparion:*
I am he who fell among thieves in my thoughts; all of me has now been wounded by them, and is filled with sores; but do Thou Thyself, standing by, O Christ Savior, heal me!

*Troparion:*
The priest, foreseeing me, passed by on the other side, and the Levite, seeing me naked in afflictions, despised me; but O Jesus, Who didst shine forth from Mary, do Thou stand by and have compassion upon me!

*To the Venerable Mother Mary:*
Grant me radiant grace from divine providence from on high to escape the gloom of passions, and to sing zealously, O Mary, the fair corrections of thy life!

*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Trinity Hymn:*
O Trinity above all essence, worshipped in Unity: take from me the heavy burden of sin, and as Thou art compassionate, give me tears of compunction!

*Now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen. Theotokion:*
O Mother of God, the hope and protection of those who hymn thee: take from me the heavy burden of sin, and as pure Sovereign Lady, receive me in repentance!
