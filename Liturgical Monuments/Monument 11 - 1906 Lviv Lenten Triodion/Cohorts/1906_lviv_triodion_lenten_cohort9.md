## Monday Evening of the First Week of the Holy Great Fast[^263]

*At "Lord, I have cried," we set 6 stichera, Tone 2. Composition of Lord Joseph:*[^264]
*Model: All things have passed away:*
I have committed every sin, I have surpassed all in prodigality; if I wish to repent, I have no streams of tears; if I live in slothfulness even now, I am guilty of torment; but grant me amendment, O only Good God, and have mercy on me.

*Model:*
Grant me showers of tears, O Christ, on the fair day of the Fast, so that I may lament and wash away the defilement that comes from pleasures, and may appear cleansed before Thee when Thou, O Lord, shalt come from heaven as Judge to judge mankind, as Judge and only Righteous One.

*Another [sticheron], Tone 5. Of Lord Theodore the Studite:*[^265]
*Model: O venerable Father:*
Come ye eagerly, having the strong armor of the Fast as a shield, let us the faithful turn back every snare of the enemy's deception; let us not be burned with the pleasures of passions, neither let us fear the fire of temptations, wherewith Christ the Lover of mankind crowns us with the honors of endurance; therefore praying with boldness, we fall down crying aloud, asking peace and great mercy for our souls.

*And in the Menaion, 3.*
*Glory, and now and ever, Theotokion: "O gladsome Light:"*
*Then the prokeimenon, Psalm 3, Tone 6:*[^266]
