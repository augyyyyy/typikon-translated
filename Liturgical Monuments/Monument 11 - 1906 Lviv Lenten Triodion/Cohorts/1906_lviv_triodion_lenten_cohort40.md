Rejoice, thou who fillest the nets of the fishermen!  
Rejoice, thou who drawest up from the depth of ignorance;  
Rejoice, thou who illuminest many in understanding!  
Rejoice, ship of those who desire to be saved;  
Rejoice, haven of the voyages of life!  
Rejoice, O Bride unwedded!

*Kontakion 10 [S]:*  
Desiring to save the world, the Adorner of all came unto it of His own promise; and being the Shepherd as God, for our sake He appeared as a man among us; for having called like by like, as God He hears: Alleluia!
