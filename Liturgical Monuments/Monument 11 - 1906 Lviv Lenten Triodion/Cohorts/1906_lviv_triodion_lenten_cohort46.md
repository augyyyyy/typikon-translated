*COMPUNCTIVE STICHERA*[^1244]

*Chanted on Sunday evening, at "Lord, I have cried": and the Sessional Hymns, with the Martyria, chanted at Matins; unto this also the Praises Stichera, with those at the Aposticha, chanted on Saturdays, from the Oktoechos, according to the Tone.*

*At "Lord, I have cried", Penitential Stichera, Tone 1:*
Great is the abyss of my transgressions, O Savior, and grievously am I submerged by mine iniquities; reach out Thy hand unto me, as unto Peter, and save me, O God, and have mercy on me!

Seeing that in wicked thoughts and in deeds Thou hast condemned me, O Savior, grant me the thought of repentance, O God, that I may cry unto Thee: Save me, O Good Benefactor, and have mercy on me!

Another age awaits thee, O my soul, and the Judge desires to expose thy secret and grievous deeds; remain not therefore in the things of this world, but anticipate the judgment, crying aloud unto the Judge: O God, cleanse me and save me!

Despise me not, O my Savior, who am held fast by sinful sloth; raise up my thoughts unto repentance, and show me to be an approved laborer of Thy vineyard, granting me the reward of the eleventh hour, and great mercy!

*Then three from the Triodion, and three for the Saint from the Menaion.*

*ON MONDAY AT MATINS*
*After the first stichology, Compunctive Sessional Hymns, Tone 1:*
In iniquities was I conceived, prodigal that I am, and I dare not look up unto the height of heaven; yet daring upon Thy love for mankind, I cry: O God, cleanse me and save me!

*Verse: O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger, nor chasten me in Thy wrath.*[^1245]
If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall I appear, a sinner? I have not borne the burden and the heat of the day; yet number me with the laborers of the eleventh hour, O God, and save me!
