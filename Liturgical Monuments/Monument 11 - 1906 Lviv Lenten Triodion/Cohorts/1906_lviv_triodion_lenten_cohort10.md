*And thus the priest begins to say: "Let us pray for our most holy universal Pontiff [Name], Pope of Rome," and the rest.*[^294] *And after these as usual, a verse from the superior, and toward him from all likewise a bow; and thus we depart unto our cells.*
*This order takes place throughout the entire Holy Forty Days at compline. Be it known, when the superior says the verse, we respond: "Bless," having received forgiveness. And it is proper for the ecclesiarch to watch this diligently, that when they dismiss compline, there be the token of day, that is, before darkness.*[^295]
*This then, as was prescribed above, having diligently examined one day of the holy fasts, we have set before your love, the amount of chanting, and the prostrations portrayed in it from the holy Fathers, which it is not seemly to despise, not even for a great lawgiver.*[^296]
*And the number of day-and-night prostrations made in church is 300, besides the Midnight Office. We have received also in Palestine that each chant the Psalter in his cells: and those who are able accomplish it day and night; others thrice in the week, and others twice. And to make prostrations, as many as the superior shall determine for each according to his strength.*[^297]

## Tuesday of the First Week of the Holy Fast at Matins[^298]

*After the second poetic recitation of the Psalter, Sessional Hymns, Tone 2:*
*Model: Most blessed art thou:*
Most blessed is the grace of the most honorable Fast: for by it Moses was glorified, and received the Law written upon tablets; and the young youths showed themselves stronger than fire; having quenched therein the blazing carnal passions, crying aloud unto Christ the Savior: Grant illumination unto us all, and deliver us from Gehenna!
*Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Another [sessional hymn], the same Tone. Model: Being the fountain of tender mercy:*
The time of repentance has arrived: show the fruits of temperance, O my soul! Look upon those that repented before thee, cry aloud unto Christ: I have sinned, save me, even as Thou didst save, O Master, the publican who sighed from his heart, O Good One, Who alone art rich in mercy!
