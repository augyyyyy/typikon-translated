*Decree of John the Faster:*[^1470]
We find in certain Typika that on this day of this precious feast there is sung: "Let my prayer be set forth." And this we do not know, in what manner it was determined. For some, out of arbitrary will and lack of instruction, have appended many things. But as for me, I judge that this ought not to be admitted at the complete Liturgy of Chrysostom, both at the Sixth Hour and when the Proskomide has already taken place. For it says: On whatever day the feast of the divine Annunciation occurs, the Liturgy of Chrysostom is celebrated, and at the refectory there is dispensation for fish, on whatever day it occurs. For this we received from the divine Fathers, to eat fish on the day of the divine Annunciation. But "Let my prayer be set forth" began to be sung in the Liturgy of the Presanctified, and at the Sixth Hour; for it is not fitting that any sign of mourning be sung on this day, for we honor it as the fountainhead of our salvation.
