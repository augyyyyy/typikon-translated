THE LENTEN TRIODION
or
THE THREE-ODE CANON
in Greek:
TRIODION.

Unto the glory of the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating, and Undivided Trinity,
of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,

this Book was printed

through the diligence and at the expense of
THE STAUROPEGION INSTITUTE
at the Church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God.[^1]

[Library oval stamp: "Lviv Theological Academy / Library"]

IN LVIV
At the Typography of the same Stauropegion Institute,
in the year 1906.

[Handwritten dedication in Ukrainian cursive at the bottom margin:][^2]
"For Most Reverend Kyr I. Prasko — gratefully, Fr. Ihor. Melbourne, 29.VII.1968."

[Library stamp at upper right: "THEOLOGICAL ACADEMY / LIBRARY / 502049"]

[Headpiece: decorative vignette depicting the Holy Cross in radiant beams, flanked by grapevine foliage]

PRAYER
to Our Lord Jesus Christ.[^6]

O God, King of ages, and Creator of all creation: Thou art our only hope; Thou art the ever-existing, unsearchable God in love for mankind, eternal. Thou art the Giver of all life, Who upholdest our temporary and mortal life, unto daily and nightly instruction, and Thou dost grant us all things for the reception of good things, unto the salvation of our souls and bodies. Since Thou art the God Who lovest mankind, Who grantest rest unto every toil-loving ascetic labor, receive the present strivings toward the world to come as an odor of spiritual fragrance, according to the saying: "The heavens are Thine, and the earth is Thine; and Thine own of Thine own we offer unto Thee alone upon this Tablet." Do Thou therefore, as the Lover of mankind, receive the unworthy lifting up of our hands; grant unto us the revelation of Thy incomprehensible mysteries through Thy sovereign, incomprehensible Wisdom, which was sent from Thee unto us for our salvation, that we may know Thee, the God and Father of us all, and may delight in Thine ineffable mysteries. Grant unto us in Thy Wisdom, which is the Son and Word of God, through which Word the heavens were established, to abide patiently, and in Her to behold Thee, our God and Father, to be well-pleasing unto Thee through Her, guarding ourselves against the spiritual Pharaoh, and praying earnestly concerning this unto Thee, our God on high: so that in the water of tears we may drown the hypocrisy of the Pharisee, and, shamelessly fleeing vainglory, may escape it, and may overcome our visible and invisible enemies, and may proclaim Thy glory before men; that we may be accounted worthy to be crowned by Thee, the God Who lovest mankind, with the crown of victory, and in our eternal Fatherland and in Thy Kingdom to glorify Thee, such a great Benefactor, together with Thine Only-begotten Son, our Savior, Who was eternally begotten of Thee, and with Thy Holy, Good, and Life-Creating Spirit, unto endless ages of ages. Amen.
