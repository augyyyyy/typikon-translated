O holy Mother of God among women, unwedded Mother, pray unto the King and God Whom thou didst bear, that He may save us, as He is the Lover of mankind.

*Then: "It is good to give praise:" We join also the First Hour, as usual. And the Dismissal.*

*On the same Cheesefare Thursday evening:*
*From the beginning we begin prostrations, and the rest of the whole service, as was also indicated above for Cheesefare Tuesday.*

*At "Lord, I have cried," stichera as usual; Glory, and now and ever: Theotokion. "O Gladsome Light:" And the Prokeimenon of the day.*

*At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the day, Tone 2:*
The Cross of the Lord, unto those who worship it without delusion, is the sprouting of every pleasure and the law of abstinence: for they who gaze unceasingly upon Him Who was nailed upon it crucify the flesh with its passions and lusts;[^128] of whom let us also strive to be, and by a pure fast let us be joined unto the love of mankind of Him Who condescended unto us through suffering, and bestowed upon our nature His dispassion, Who possesses great mercy.

*We repeat this with the verse: "Unto Thee have I lifted up mine eyes:" Verse: "Have mercy on us, O Lord:" And the Martyrion:*
Not having desired earthly pleasure, the passion-bearers were accounted worthy of heavenly good things, and became companions of the Angels: O Lord, by their prayers have mercy on us and save us.

*Glory, and now and ever: Stavrotheotokion. Model: "When from the tree:"*
When the pure Lady beheld the ripe Cluster, Whom she bore untilled in her womb, hanging upon the tree, weeping she exclaimed and cried aloud: "O Child, pour forth sweetness, whereby all intoxication of passions is removed, O Benefactor, for the sake of me who bore Thee, with divine consolations, as Thou art tender-hearted."

*Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart: "O Virgin Mother of God:" And the rest, making great prostrations. Then the Dismissal.*

*Compline we chant Great with prostrations, as was indicated for Tuesday: fol. 35.*[^129]

*On Cheesefare Friday at Matins, after the Six Psalms we chant Alleluia, to the tone of the Trinity Hymn of the Tone.*

*After the 1st kathisma psalmody, Sessional hymns of the Cross of the Octoechos.*

*After the 2nd kathisma psalmody, Sessional hymn of the Triodion, Tone 7. Model: "As having:"*
Exalt the horn of the Church, O precious Cross, cast down heretical pride by thy power, and exalt the choirs of the Orthodox: grant us all to anticipate thy fore-announcement, to worship Thee, the footstool of Christ: for in Thee do we boast, O blessed Tree.

*Glory: the same. And now and ever, Stavrotheotokion:*
As thou hast compassion upon our lowliness, and lookest upon earthborn men prone to stumbling, have mercy, O blessed Mother of God, continue praying that we may not perish grievously: pray, as the all-pure one, unto the God of reconciliation, that our souls may be saved, O most holy Virgin.
