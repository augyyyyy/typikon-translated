## On Thursday of the Fifth Week of the Great Fast[^1040]

*The klepalo is struck at the 4th hour of the night.*[^1041] *And when we have gathered into the church, the beginning is made by the priest according to custom. And we say: "O Heavenly King," the Trisagion, and after "Our Father": "Lord, have mercy," 12. Glory... Both now... "Come, let us worship," and the other two Psalms, 19 and 20 [LXX 19 and 20], and the usual Troparia, and the Litany. And thereafter we say the Six Psalms, and after the Psalms we sing "Alleluia," and the Triadica of the Tone, as is the custom. And we read the stichology of one Kathisma, the eighth.*[^1042]  
*Then we say the Sessional Hymns of the Octoechos. And we read the Life of Venerable Mary in two staseis.*[^1043]  
*Then Psalm 50 [LXX 50].*  
*We do not sing the biblical Odes.*[^1044]  
*And immediately we begin the Great Canon, slowly, and with a contrite heart and voice, making three metanias at every troparion.*[^1045]

*The Great Canon, the work of our Holy Father Andrew of Crete, of Jerusalem:*[^1046]

A Helper and Protector has He become unto me for salvation; He is my God, and I will glorify Him, the God of my father, and I will exalt Him: for gloriously has He been glorified!

*Refrain: Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*[^1048]

Having rivaled the transgression of first-formed Adam, I have recognized myself stripped naked of God, and of the everlasting kingdom and delight, on account of my sins.[^1049]

Instead of sensible Eve, an intelligible Eve has arisen within me: the passionate thought in the flesh, showing forth sweet things, yet ever tasting of the bitter drink.

Worthily was Adam driven out of Eden, because he kept not Thy one commandment, O Savior; what then shall I suffer, who ever reject Thy life-giving words?

Surpassing the murder of Cain, by deliberate choice have I become a murderer of my soul's conscience, giving life to the flesh and warring against my soul by my wicked deeds.

I have not resembled the righteousness of Abel, O Jesus; never have I offered Thee an acceptable gift, nor divine actions, nor a pure sacrifice, nor a blameless life.

Like Cain, we also, O wretched soul, have offered together unto the Creator of all defiled deeds, and a blemished sacrifice, and a useless life; therefore we also have been condemned.

Having quickened clay into life, O Fashioner, Thou didst place within me flesh and bones, and breath and life; but, O my Creator, my Redeemer, and Judge, receive me who repent!

I declare unto Thee, O Savior, the sins which I have committed, and the wounds of my soul and body, which murderous thoughts within have laid upon me like robbers.

Though I have sinned, O Savior, yet I know that Thou art the Lover of mankind: Thou chastisest mercifully, and hast fervent compassion; Thou seest him that weeps, and runnest to meet him like the Father, calling back the prodigal.

Cast down before Thy gates, O Savior, at least in old age cast me not away empty into hades; but before the end, as Thou art the Lover of mankind, grant me remission of transgressions!
