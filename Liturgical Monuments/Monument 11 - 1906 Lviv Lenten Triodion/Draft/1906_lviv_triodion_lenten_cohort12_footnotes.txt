[^315]: Great Canon, Ode 9, Tone 6, Heirmos: "Seedless conception" (*Безсѣ́меннагѡ зача́тїѧ*), with troparia contemplating the temptations of Christ in the wilderness (Matt. 4:1–11), John the Baptist crying in the wilderness and warning Herod of his unlawful marriage to Herodias (Matt. 14:3–12), Christ blessing marriage at Cana of Galilee (John 2:1–11), healing the paralytic carrying his bed (Mark 2:1–12), raising the widow's son at Nain (Luke 7:11–17), healing the centurion's servant (Matt. 8:5–13), conversing with the Samaritan woman (John 4:5–26), healing the woman with the flow of blood (Luke 8:43–48), cleansing lepers, and restoring sight to the blind and strength to the lame.

[^317]: Clean Wednesday Matins: Triodion Canon, Ode 8, Tone 2, by St. Joseph, Heirmos: "Once in Babylon the fiery furnace" (*Пе́щь и҆ногда̀ ѻ҆́гненнаѧ въ Вавѷлѡ́нѣ*), and the second Ode 8, Tone 2, by St. Theodore the Studite, Heirmos: "In the bush to Moses" (*Въ кꙋпинѣ̀ Мѡѷсе́ю*), extolling the life-giving stream poured from the Savior's pierced ribs and summoning all creation to bless Christ hanging upon the Tree between the thieves.
[^318]: Clean Wednesday Matins: Triodion Canon, Ode 9, Tone 2, by St. Joseph, Heirmos: "The unoriginate Father" (*Безнача́льна Роди́телѧ*), heralding the Fast shining brighter than the sun, and the second Ode 9, Tone 2, by St. Theodore, Heirmos: "Pure and most pure Mother and Virgin" (*Чи́стꙋю и҆ пречи́стꙋю*), worshipping Christ's Cross, reed, nails, spear, and life-creating Sufferings.
[^319]: Clean Wednesday at the Sixth Hour (*На ѕ҃-мъ часѣ̀*): Troparion of prophecy, Tone 4 (*Thou knowest our frame, Thou knowest our weakness*, Ps. 102:14), Prokeimenon from Psalm 9 (*I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart*), and the prophecy of Isaiah (Isa. 2:2–11) regarding the exaltation of the mountain of the Lord's house, universal peace when nations beat their swords into plowshares, the denunciation of idolatrous riches and divination in the house of Jacob, and the humbling of human arrogance before the terrifying glory of the Lord.
[^320]: Wednesday of the First Week of the Holy and Great Fast at Vespers with the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts (*Въ сре́дꙋ пе́рвыѧ седми́цы ст҃а́гѡ вели́кагѡ поста̀ ве́чера, є҆гда̀ быва́етъ Преждеѡсвѧще́ннаѧ*): Rubrics prescribing small litanies by the priest at each antiphon of the Psalter stichology, ten stichera on *Lord, I have cried* beginning with the Idiomelon of the day, Tone 8 ("Fasting bodily, brethren, let us fast also spiritually...", Isa. 58:6–7), the Martyrikon (*мече́мъ бо преклони́ша вы̑и*), and prosomoia by St. Joseph (Tone 2, *As lightning, O Christ, sending the apostles as rays of light...*, and *Ascending upon the divine chariot through virtues, Elijah shone forth through fasting...*) and St. Theodore (Tone 5, *Divine apostles, fervent intercessors for the world...*).
[^321]: Clean Wednesday Vespers: Stichera conclusion from the Menaion, the Entrance with the censer, *O Radiant Light* (*Свѣ́те ти́хїй*), the first Prokeimenon from Psalm 11 (*Thou, O Lord, shalt keep us*), followed by the first Genesis reading (Gen. 1:24–2:3) recounting the creation of beasts, cattle, creeping things, the creation of man in the image and likeness of God, the dominion of man, and the hallowing of the seventh day of rest; concluded by the second Prokeimenon from Psalm 12 (*Look upon me and hear me, O Lord my God*), the rubrical blessing with censer and candle (*The Light of Christ illumines all*), and the opening of the Proverbs reading (Prov. 2:1–5).
[^322]: Clean Wednesday Vespers and Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts: The reading from Proverbs (Prov. 2:6–22) detailing the Lord granting wisdom, guarding the paths of the just, and uprooting the wicked from the earth; followed by the solemn chanting of *Let my prayer arise* (Ps. 140:2) with kneeling prostrations by the choirs and people, the Entrance of the Presanctified Gifts (*Now the celestial powers...*) during which all fall prostrate in divine adoration (*поклоне́нїе бг҃оподо́бное*) before Christ truly present in the consecrated Gifts, the Kinonikon (*Taste and see that the Lord is good*, Ps. 33:9), the distribution of antidoron, and the final dismissal.
