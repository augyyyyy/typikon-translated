*For on Wednesday and on Friday of Cheesefare Week we chant Alleluia, and make prostrations evening and morning, see as was said above, and perform the Fast according to rule, even if it happen to be of any Saint who has troparia: but the Inter-Hours we do not chant.[^103] If, however, it be the feast of the Meeting of the Lord, or the temple feast of any Saint, we chant the troparia, and make no prostrations, save only three great prostrations.*

*After the first stichology, sessional hymns of the Octoechos, of the Cross.*

*After the second stichology, sessional hymn of the Triodion:*
*Tone 2. Model: "Thou art a well of compassion:"*
Be pleased, O Christ God, by Thine ineffable love for mankind, that we humble ones may pass through the pre-purification of the fasts in peace: overthrow the wiles of the enemy, and by Thy Cross save all, O Thou Who alone knowest the secrets of the heart.

*Glory: the same. Now and ever, Stavrotheotokion. Model:*
Standing before Thy Cross, she that bare Thee without seed, unable to endure beholding Thee suffering unjustly, wailed with weeping, and cried unto Thee: "How dost Thou suffer, Who art impassible in nature, O sweetest Son? I praise Thine utter goodness!"

*Here we read the Homily on Fasting of St. Basil the Great, of which the beginning is: "Comfort ye, comfort ye:"[^104] and Psalm 50.*

*Canons in the Octoechos to the Cross, with the heirmos on 6; and in the Menaion to the Saint on 4; and in the Triodion on 4. At the end of the odes comes the triodion, and we chant the canon of the Triodion with the heirmos on 6; and the triodion on 8, and at the end the heirmos of the triodion. In these odes the canon of the Octoechos and of the Menaion is omitted. And we chant the Menaion in the upper odes thus: Ode 1 of the canon of the Octoechos with the heirmos on 6; and in the Menaion of the Saint, Odes 4 and 6 on 6; and in the Triodion on 6. In like manner we chant Ode 8 with Ode 6, and Ode 9 with Ode 7. Canon of the Triodion.*

*Ode 1, Tone 4. The work of master Andrew of Crete.[^105] Heirmos:*
*I will sing unto Thee, O Lord my God, for Thou didst lead Thy people out of Egyptian bondage, and didst cover the chariots of Pharaoh and his power.*

The Lord, fasting after the manner of men for our model,[^107] overcomes the tempter, showing forth what is ours, and prescribing bounds for us.

Divine Moses on Sinai was accounted worthy by abstinence to speak with God face to face: let us faithful emulate him!

Purify Thy people, O Lord, and having looked down as God with a merciful eye, visit all by the multitude of Thy mercy.

*Theotokion:*
Unto thee do we all flee, O Mother of God, as a firm protection, and moving thee unto prayer, that thou mayest deliver thy flock from every affliction.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*My heart is established in the Lord my God: therefore the weak have girded themselves with power.*

Marvelous Enoch by abstinence was translated from the earth:[^106] imitating his example, let us be translated from corruption unto life.

By prayers and fastings let us entreat the Redeemer: for the Creator rejoices together at the repentance of His creations.

Prepare thyself, O soul, and pre-purify thyself before the Passion of Christ, that through the Resurrection thou mayest keep spiritual feast with Him!

*Theotokion:*
As thou gavest birth unto God, pray unceasingly for us: for unto thee, O Mother of God, do we sinners flee.

*Then the triodion of Joseph. Ode 3, Tone 2. Heirmos: "Upon the rock of faith:"*
Thou didst stretch forth Thy palms upon Thy Cross, Thou didst put the curse to death by Thy mortification, Thou didst give life unto men by Thy Passion; therefore we sing of Thy divine crucifixion, O Jesus, Lover of mankind, our God.

Having been put to death by the eating of the tree, by the Tree of Thy Cross we were made alive; this then we offer unto Thee in prayer: send down grace and mercy upon us, O Benefactor and Master abundant in mercy.

The vestibules of fasting have been opened, the racecourse of abstinence draws near; let us rise up with fervent zeal, receiving the God-given gift that quenches the burning heat of transgressions.

The blessed season of the Fast, emitting a ray of repentance, has shone forth; let us approach honorably, dispelling the deepest darkness of sluggishness, all of us rejoicing.

Fasting of the saints philosophically, let us preach abstinence from passions; let us cry aloud in tears, saying: Grant us, O Lord, grace that works together toward Thy desires, O Thou abundant in mercy.

Having received the gifts of the Fast, let us glorify Him Who gave them for salvation, and let us accomplish this laboriously, that we may receive forgiveness of our sins from the hand of the Creator.

*Theotokion:*
Calm the disturbance of my passions, heal the wounds of my soul, raise me up from the sleep of despondency by thine intercession and mediation, O most holy Sovereign Lady, Virgin Mother.

*Another. Heirmos, the same Tone: "My barren mind:"*
When Thou, O Savior, wast willingly lifted up upon the Cross, all earthly creation was shaken, and the veil of the temple was torn in twain.

Thou wast wounded upon the Cross, O my Jesus, for my sake, and pierced in the ribs, O God: whence in faith I worship Thy divine power.

*Glory, Trinity Hymn:*
Bowing my knees to the earth, I worship the Father, I glorify the Son, and I hymn the Spirit, co-enthroned Principle and Tri-hypostatic.

*Now and ever, Theotokion:*
Incomprehensible is the mystery of thy childbearing, O Pure One: for without knowing man dost thou, O Mary, give birth unto God, and remainest incorrupt.

Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee!
