[^278]: St. Andrew of Crete (c. 660–740), born in Damascus, monk of the Brotherhood of the Holy Sepulchre in Jerusalem, deacon of the Great Church in Constantinople, and Archbishop of Gortyna in Crete, hymnographer and creator of the Great Canon.

[^295]: The ecclesiarch's daylight rule: Great Compline in Great Lent must be concluded while natural daylight remains (*знаменїю дне, сирѣчь прежде мрака*), ensuring monastics return to their cells in quiet contemplation before total darkness.

[^297]: Daily monastic measure of 300 prostrations to the ground in church throughout Great Lent (excluding Midnight Office), and the Palestinian monastic rule for cell recitation of the complete 150-psalm Psalter either daily, thrice weekly, or twice weekly.
