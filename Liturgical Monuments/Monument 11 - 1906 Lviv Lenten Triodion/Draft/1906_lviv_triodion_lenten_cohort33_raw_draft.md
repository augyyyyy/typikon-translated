*At the Sixth Hour, Troparion of the Prophecy, Tone 3:*[^958]  
For our iniquities have risen up against us, arise, O Lord, help us; for Thou art our Father, and besides Thee we know no other!

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 83 [LXX 83:4, 2], Tone 4:*[^959]  
Thine altars, O Lord of hosts, my King and my God!  
*Verse: How beloved are Thy dwellings, O Lord of hosts!*
