## On Tuesday of the Second Week of the Great Fast at Matins[^421]

*The Trinity Hymns.*  
*After the first reading of the Psalter, the penitential sessional hymns of the Octoechos, with their Theotokia.*  
*After the second reading of the Psalter, we sing the following sessional hymns, Tone 8.*  

*Tone 8, Podoben: "Thou hast risen" (Воскре́слъ є҆сѝ):*[^422]  
Restraining the passions with the bridle of the blameless fast, let us all strive to wing the mind unto honorable divine visions with most perfect faith, that we may despise the earthly sweetness of life, and obtain heavenly life and divine radiance.

*Glory: the same. Both now and ever: Theotokion:*  
Into a grievous abyss of despondency have I fallen, from the multitude of my deceitful and lawless deeds; I have come into perplexity, and am now held fast by sorrow. O Sovereign Lady, Mother of God, do thou save me, do thou help me; for thou art the propitiation of sinners and saving cleansing.

*After the third reading of the Psalter, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8. Podoben: "The shepherd's pipe" (Па́стырскаѧ свирѣ́ль):*[^423]  
Having set forth the fast as a mystical table, He urges us richly to be filled: let us eat the ever-living gifts of the Spirit as food; let us drink the God-flowing stream of tears as drink, and rejoicing, let us unceasingly offer praise unto God.

*Glory: the same. Both now and ever: Theotokion:*  
From every threat and affliction of man save us, O all-hymned Virgin; for we who flee unto thee, O Mother of God, and unto our God Who was born of thee, possess thee as protection and intercession: do thou entreat Him that we may be delivered from tribulations and afflictions.

*The Canon from the Menaion, and the Triodion of Lord Joseph.*  
*And the biblical ode from the Psalter is read.*  

*Ode 2, Tone 8. Heirmos:*[^424]  
**"See, see that I am your God..."**  

*Troparia:*  
Sigh, weep, O my soul, turn back, repent: the day draws near, the Judge is at the doors; make ready for the defense, and cry aloud: I have sinned against Thee, O Compassionate, Merciful, and Good One, have compassion on me!

*Troparia:*  
Having come to hate the all-destroying frenzy of sin, O my soul, take delight diligently in the fullness of the fast; make the saving commandments thy food, which procure through faith the delight of eternal good things.

Ye Angels, Powers, Principalities, Archangels, Dominions, Thrones, Authorities, Cherubim, and Seraphim: pray ye to the Giver of grace and God, that He grant unto us forgiveness of debts and turning away of passions.

*Theotokion:*  
O all-immaculate one, who alone didst bear the unoriginate Word incarnate of thee without change, remaining after childbirth the unwedded Virgin: pray thou for all, that our life may be delivered from corruption.

*Another canon, by Lord Theodore, same Tone. Heirmos: "See, see:"*[^425]  
We have seen, we have known, that the season which Thou hast appointed for us is good for repentance: but in this season, O God, receive our prayers, and direct them before Thee, O Compassionate One, as good incense, as an acceptable sacrifice.

I am terrified and afraid when I consider how much I have sinned: how shall I behold Thee? How shall I appear before Thee, O Terrible One? And how shall I stand before Thy tribunal which cannot be bribed? Therefore spare me, O Compassionate One, in the hour when Thou shalt judge all the earth!

*Glory: Trinity Hymn:*  
O All-Perfect Unity, Supremely Divine, in Three Hypostases: Unbegotten Father, and Only-Begotten Son, and Spirit proceeding from the Father, yet made manifest through the Son: One Substance and Nature, Dominion, Kingdom, save us all!

*Both now and ever: Theotokion:*  
The wonder of thy childbearing is ineffable, O Mother and Virgin: for how dost thou both give birth, and yet remainest pure; how dost thou bear an Infant, and knowest no experience of man at all? Even as He knows Who was incarnate of me surpassing nature: the Word of God Who was born.

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*  
Thou didst deliver the Ninevites who repented and fasted from the threatened wrath; but the Sodomites who strayed in satiety Thou didst burn with fire: yet deliver me, O Christ, from their condemnation!

*Heirmos:*  
See, see that I am your God, Who established for you an acceptable first-fruits, a tithing of the days of the whole year; and this have I given unto My people for the deliverance from passions, as the beginning of salvation.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^426]  
**"The God-proclaiming children..."**  

*Troparia:*  
O Thou Who didst justify the publican when he sighed, Who didst have compassion on the harlot weeping from her heart: save and have compassion, O Savior, on the souls of them that sing to Thee!

Let us bring forth fruitful deeds of virtue unto the Lord by fasting, departing from harmful thoughts, that we may obtain divine sweetness.

The choirs of angels, the assemblies of apostles, and the ranks of martyrs entreat Thee, O Jesus: grant Thou forgiveness unto Thy people!

*Theotokion:*  
The unquarried mountain, the Virgin, the light-bearing lampstand, the easily traversed ladder, the dwelling-place of God: let us the faithful bless her!

*Another canon [by St. Theodore]. Heirmos: "Upon the holy mountain:"*[^427]  
On the appointed day of abstinence, come, let us all sound the trumpet in songs: and as those feasting upon divine sweetness, let us sing in hymns unto the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all the ages!

Who consumed the man of God of old? A fierce wild beast, when through the deceit of the false prophet he partook of the food of disobedience: take heed therefore, O my soul, lest the serpent of gluttony deceive thee!

*Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord.*  
*Trinity Hymn:*  
As Unity in essence I sing of Thee; as Trinity in Persons I honor Thee, O Father, and Son, and Most Holy Spirit: the unoriginate dominion of Thy Kingdom I glorify unto the ages!

*Both now and ever: Theotokion:*  
Thou hast appeared as the Mountain of God, O Mother of God, in which Christ dwelt and made those who sing divine temples: Sing ye unto the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all the ages!

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*  
Honoring the fast as the guardian of purity and the measure of dispassion, come, ye people, let us embrace it with love, singing unto the Lord, and supremely exalting Him unto all the ages!

*We praise, we bless, we worship the Lord.*  
*Heirmos:*  
Him Who was glorified on the holy mountain, and in the bush through fire revealed the mystery of the Ever-Virgin unto Moses: sing ye unto the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^428]  
**"That which was prefigured on the mountain..."**  

*Troparia:*  
The good fast that enriches the heart makes the ripe ear of virtues divinely spring forth: let us love it, receiving the gathering of the holy days.

Beholding me defiled in many sins, bound fast and condemned, O Merciful One, have compassion through Thy mercy, and save me by the prayers of Thy saints!

Woe is me, O all-wretched soul! How wilt thou answer? What fear shall seize thee when the Judge sits and ten thousand times ten thousand angels stand before Him? Strive therefore: before the end, repent!

*Theotokion:*  
Chariot of the King, bright cloud, fruitful mountain, curdled mountain, O thou who knewest not wedlock: we pray thee, O Virgin, heal the passions of our souls!

*The same Heirmos.*  
*Troparia:*  
Let us fast with a true fast unto the Lord, as we abstain from foods, keeping ourselves distant from the tongue, wrath, falsehood, and every other passion, that we may purely behold Pascha.

Thou didst cause Samuel to blossom as fruit, O fast; thou didst nurture Samson the great and valiant hero; thou makest priests and prophets complete: do thou sanctify us, O honorable fast!

*Glory: Trinity Hymn:*  
O Lord, with the bright radiances of Thy Thrice-Radiant Monarchy shine upon our mind, that from manifold deceit we may turn unto the unitive deification.

*Both now and ever: Theotokion:*  
Thou art our weapon and wall, O Mother of God; thou art the protection of those who flee unto thee; thee do we now move unto prayer, that we may be delivered from our enemies.

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*  
Possessing the Holy Spirit as the rich host of the fast, let us be filled with His gifts and richly take delight, praising Him as our God.

*Heirmos:*  
The Childbirth of the Ever-Virgin, which was prefigured on the mountain unto the Lawgiver in the fire and the bush, for the salvation of us the faithful, with unceasing hymns we magnify.

*Exapostilarion of the Tone.*  

*At the Aposticha, Idiomelon, Tone 5:*[^429]  
Why dost thou despond, O my soul, serving sin? And why, being sick, dost thou not hasten unto the Physician? Behold the acceptable time, behold now the true day of salvation: arise, wash thy face with tears of repentance, and illuminate thy lamp with the oil of well-doing, that thou mayest find cleansing from Christ God, and great mercy.  
*(Twice.)*  

*For the Martyrs:*  
Thy passion-bearers, O Lord, having imitated the angelic ranks as if bodiless, endured tortures, possessing a unanimous hope, the enjoyment of promised good things: by their prayers, O Christ our God, grant peace unto Thy world, and unto our souls great mercy.

*Glory, and now and ever, Theotokion:*  
We bless thee, O Virgin Mother of God, and we the faithful glorify thee as is due: the unshakable city, the impregnable wall, the firm protectress, and the refuge of our souls.

*At the Sixth Hour, Troparion of the Prophecy, Tone 6:*[^430]  
We are not able worthily to hymn Thee; but we pray and entreat: destroy us not with our lawlessnesses, O Lord, Who art easily reconciled!

*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Isa. 5:7–16):*[^432]  
Thus says the Lord: The vineyard of the Lord of Sabaoth is the house of Israel, and the man of Judah a new and beloved plant: I waited for it to execute judgment, but it executed lawlessness, and not righteousness, but a cry. Woe unto those who join house to house, and bring field near to field, that they may take away something from their neighbor: shall ye dwell alone upon the earth? For these things were heard in the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth: For though houses be many, into desolation shall great and good houses become, and there shall be none dwelling in them. For where ten yoke of oxen plow, it shall yield one jar; and he that sows six artabae shall reap three measures. Woe unto those that rise up early in the morning and follow strong drink, who wait until evening: for the wine shall inflame them. For with harp, and psaltery, and timbrels, and pipes they drink wine; but upon the works of the Lord they look not, and the works of His hands they consider not. Therefore My people have been taken captive, because they know not the Lord; and there was a multitude of dead because of famine and thirst for water. And Hades enlarged its soul, and opened its mouth without ceasing; and down shall go their glorious, and their great, and their rich, and their destroyers, and he that rejoices therein. And man shall be brought low, and a man shall be dishonored, and the eyes that look high shall be humbled. And the Lord of Sabaoth shall be exalted in judgment, and the holy God shall be glorified in righteousness.

## On Tuesday of the Second Week of the Great Fast at Vespers[^434]

*At "Lord, I have cried," Stichera of Lord Joseph, Tone 1. Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (Прехва́льнїи мꙋ́ченицы):*[^435]  
Having stretched out Thine all-pure hands upon the Cross, O Christ, gathering the ends of the earth, therefore I cry unto Thee: gather my scattered mind, taken captive and dragged along by the passions, and show me to be a partaker of Thy sufferings, cleansed of all through abstinence.

Fasting sharpened the children of old, and showed them stronger than the burning fire, as it is written: O my humble soul, fast thou, kindling within thyself the Master's love, whereby thou shalt be able to escape the future Gehenna and burn up all-destructive passions.

*Another sticheron, of Lord Theodore, Tone 3. Podoben: "O valiant martyrs" (До́блїи мꙋ́ченицы):*[^436]  
The time of the fast is cheerful; therefore, having been richly filled with light-like purity, and pure love, radiant prayer, and every other virtue, let us brightly cry aloud: O Cross of...

=== LEAF p186 ===
...Christ, the honorable one, that hast made the sweetness of life to grow: account us all worthy to worship thee with a pure heart, granting unto us cleansing and great mercy.

*And of the Menaion, 3.*

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 27 (LXX), Tone 6:*[^437]  
Save, O Lord, Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance.  
*Verse:* Unto Thee, O Lord, will I cry, O my God.

*Reading from Genesis (Gen. 4:8–15):*[^438]  
Cain said unto Abel his brother: Let us go out into the field. And it came to pass, when they were in the field, that Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and slew him. And the Lord God said unto Cain: Where is Abel thy brother? And he said: I know not; am I my brother's keeper? And the Lord said: What hast thou done? The voice of thy brother's blood cries unto Me from the ground. And now cursed art thou from the earth, which has opened its mouth to receive the blood of thy brother from thy hand. When thou tillest the ground, it shall not add to give its strength unto thee: groaning and trembling shalt thou be upon the earth. And Cain said unto the Lord God: My guilt is greater than can be forgiven me. If Thou drivest me out this day from the face of the earth, and from Thy face I shall be hidden, and I shall be groaning and trembling upon the earth, then it will come to pass that everyone that finds me will kill me. And the Lord God said unto him: Not so; whosoever slays Cain, vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold. And the Lord God set a mark upon Cain, lest anyone finding him should kill him.

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 28 (LXX), Tone 7:*[^439]  
The Lord will give strength unto His people; the Lord will bless His people with peace.  
*Verse:* Bring unto the Lord, ye sons of God.

*Reading from Proverbs (Prov. 5:1–15):*[^440]  
My son, attend unto my wisdom, and apply thine ear unto my words, that thou mayest keep good counsel; and the sense of my lips gives commandment unto thee. Give no heed unto an evil woman: for honey drops from the lips of an harlot woman, who for a season pleases thy palate; but afterward thou shalt find her more bitter than gall, and sharper than a two-edged sword. For the feet of folly lead down those who deal with her with death into Hades, and her steps are not established; for she enters not upon the paths of life, and her ways are straying and unwise. Now therefore, my son, hear me, and make not my words hidden: remove thy way far from her, and come not near unto the doors of her houses, lest she give thy life unto others, and thy living unto the unmerciful; lest strangers be filled with thy strength, and thy labors enter into the houses of strangers; and thou repent in thine end, when the flesh of thy body is consumed, and say: How have I hated instruction, and mine heart declined from reproofs? I heard not the voice of him that instructed me, and inclined not mine ear unto him that taught me; I was well-nigh in all evil in the midst of the church and congregation. My son, drink waters out of thine own vessels, and from the spring of thine own wells.

O Lord Who wast crucified in the flesh, and didst crucify with Thyself our old man, and wast pierced with a spear in Thy side, and didst pierce through the serpent, the slayer of men: nail my flesh unto Thy fear, and wound my soul with Thy love, that contemplating Thy Passion, I may with abstinence accomplish the appointment of the fast, restraining not only the belly, but also the other entrances of sin; and sacrifice unto Thee contrition of heart and humility of spirit for my former transgressions, from which do Thou deliver me, O Lover of mankind!
