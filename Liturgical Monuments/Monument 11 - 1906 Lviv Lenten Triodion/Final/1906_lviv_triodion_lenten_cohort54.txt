*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on Wednesday of the Fifth Week of the Fasts:*[^1479]
We chant the service of the Forefeast of the Annunciation: The stichera and the canon on Monday in order; while that of the saint from the Menaion we chant at Compline; because the Great Canon is transferred from Thursday to Tuesday of that same week. And we chant the Great Canon with the Tuesday triode, on account of the feast of the Most Holy Theotokos; because on Wednesday, which is the feast itself, the kathismata are not recited, and prostrations do not take place; and we chant the Apodosis of the feast on Thursday; therefore, on Wednesday evening, the stichera are chanted with prostrations, and the Great Canon is not sung on Thursday, for it was sung beforehand on Tuesday.

On Wednesday of the Fifth Week of the Fasts, we chant the entire service of the feast in the evening and in the morning with the Triodion according to the Typikon, as indicated previously for a fasting day. And the Apodosis of the Annunciation we chant on Thursday at Matins with the apostolic triode, as shown under Monday of the Fourth and Fifth Weeks of the Fast, for the Apodosis of the Feast of the Annunciation.

*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on Thursday of the Fifth Week of the Fasts, when the Great Canon is sung:*[^1480]
The Great Canon of lord Andrew is transferred on account of the feast, and we sing it on Tuesday of that same week with the Tuesday triode; while the apostolic triode we chant with the feast of the Annunciation; and afterwards, the Katavasia of the Triodion.

*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on Friday of that same week:*[^1481]
We chant the Forefeast of the Annunciation, the stichera and the canon beforehand on Wednesday in order, on account of the Great Canon; and the service of the saint in the Menaion we sing at Compline.
