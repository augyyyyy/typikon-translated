Steadfastness in martyrdom and patience in pains didst thou show, O passion-bearer of Christ; and swift again unto us dost thou show thy help, O Theodore.

*Glory:*
Wickedly rejecting from his soul the pious faith of the fathers, the tyrant, rushing upon an impious counsel against God, wished to go continually.

*Both now and ever, Theotokion:*
The prophesies of the men of old have manifestly been fulfilled in thee, O Virgin Mother; for thou didst bear the true God and the true Man, above nature and awe-inspiringly.
