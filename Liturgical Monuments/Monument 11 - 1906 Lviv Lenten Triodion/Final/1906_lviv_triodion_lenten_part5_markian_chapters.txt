# PART V: MARKIAN CHAPTERS (RUBRICS FOR THE ANNUNCIATION COINCIDING WITH LENT AND HOLY WEEK)

## Order of the Markian Chapters

*How to chant together with the Triodion on the Holy and Great Fast for the Saints and the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos, whenever it occurs on any day.*[^1422]

*It is fitting to know: If the Feast of the Three Hierarchs—Basil the Great, Gregory the Theologian, and John Chrysostom—occurs on the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee, or of the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare Sunday:*[^1423]

We chant their entire service in all respects similarly to the way that the Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner is indicated above. See below.

*If the Feast of the Three Hierarchs occurs on Meatfare Saturday:*

We chant their service on the preceding Friday, without any changes.

*If the Feast of the Three Hierarchs occurs on Monday or Tuesday of Cheesefare Week:*

We chant as for the Finding of the Precious Head of John the Forerunner, in all respects similarly.

*If the Feast of the Three Hierarchs occurs on Wednesday of Cheesefare Week:*

We chant their service on Tuesday of that same Cheesefare Week.

*If it occurs on Friday:*

We chant their service on Thursday.

*[Typographic Divider Vignette]*

*It is fitting to know: If the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on the Sunday of the Pharisee, or of the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare, or Cheesefare:*[^1424]

On Saturday at Small Vespers, the Resurrection stichera, and of the Theotokos according to custom.

At Great Vespers, 6 Resurrection stichera, and 1 Anatolian; and 3 of the Triodion; and 3 of the Forefeast.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

3 Resurrection stichera; and 4 of the Triodion; and 3 of the Forefeast. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* Theotokion of Tone 1.

At Litiya, the sticheron of the temple; and of the Forefeast, the aposticha of Vespers. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the Forefeast, the aposticha of Matins.

At the Aposticha, the Resurrection stichera. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Blessing of the Loaves, Troparion: **"O Theotokos Virgin"**, twice; and of the Forefeast, once; and **"Blessed be the Name of the Lord"**, thrice; and the reading.

*Where there is no vigil:*

After **"Now lettest Thou"**: the Resurrection Troparion. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At Compline, after **"Our Father"**: we say the Kontakion of the Forefeast. *Glory, both now:* of the Triodion.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**: the Resurrection Troparion, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast; and the rest through to the Resurrection Canon.

Resurrection Canon with its Heirmos to 4; and of the Theotokos to 2; and of the Forefeast to 4; and of the Triodion to 4. Katavasia of the feast.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

Resurrection Canon to 4; and of the Forefeast to 4; and in the Triodion to 6. Katavasia of the Triodion.

After Ode 3, the Kontakion of the Forefeast, and the Ikos; and the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Triodion.

At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**; the Resurrection Exapostilarion. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Praises, 5 Resurrection stichera; and 3 of the Triodion, with their refrains.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday or Cheesefare Sunday:*

We chant 4 Resurrection stichera, and 4 of the Triodion, with their refrains. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**. Great Doxology; the Resurrection Troparion; the litanies; and the dismissal. Then *Glory, both now:* the Evangelical sticheron; and the First Hour in the narthex.

At the Hours, the Resurrection Troparion. *Glory:* of the Forefeast. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, we say alternately the Kontakion of the Forefeast and of the Triodion.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the Tone to 4; and of the Triodion, Ode 3 to 4; and of the Forefeast, Ode 6 to 4. After the Little Entrance, the Resurrection Troparion, and of the Forefeast. *Glory:* Kontakion of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the Forefeast. Prokeimenon of the Tone; the Epistle; the Gospel; and the Communion Hymn of the day.

*If the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Meatfare Saturday:*[^1425]

Then we chant the service of the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord beforehand on Friday of that week, together with the Holy Martyrs Cyrus and John, and Tryphon.

*If the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Monday, or Tuesday, or Thursday of Cheesefare Week:*

At **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 stichera of the Forefeast, and 3 of the Saint. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast. After the Trisagion, the Troparion of the Saint. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At Compline, after **"It is truly meet"**, the Kontakion of the Forefeast.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the Forefeast, twice. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

After Kathisma 1, the litany, and the Sessional Hymn of the Forefeast. After Kathisma 2, the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion, without litany. Canon of the Forefeast with its Heirmos to 8; and of the Saint to 4.

*Where the Triodion triode arrives:*

We chant the Canon of the Forefeast with its Heirmos to 4; and 3 troparia of the Martyr to 2; and of the Triodion to 8. Katavasia of the Triodion.

After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos, and the Sessional Hymn of the Saint. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Forefeast.

After Ode 9, the Exapostilarion of the Saint. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

After **"It is good to give thanks"** and after the Trisagion, the Troparion of the Martyr. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Hours, the Troparion of the Forefeast. *Glory:* of the Martyr. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After **"Our Father"**, we say the Kontakion of the Forefeast without change.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes from the Canon of the Forefeast, Ode 3; and of the Martyr, Ode 6. After the Little Entrance, the troparia of the Forefeast, and of the temple of the Saint, and of the Martyr. Then the Kontakion of the temple of the Saint. *Glory:* of the Martyr. *Both now:* of the Forefeast. Prokeimenon, Epistle, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day and of the Saint.

*If the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Wednesday or Friday of Cheesefare Week:*

On Tuesday evening at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 stichera of the Forefeast, and 3 of the Saint. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon, with the usual refrains. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast. After the Trisagion, the Troparion of the Saint. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast. The litany; and 3 great prostrations; and the dismissal.

We chant Great Compline, beginning at **"When I called"**; at which we chant the first morning triode of the Triodion. After **"It is truly meet"** and after the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the Forefeast; and 3 great prostrations; and the prayer; and the usual dismissal.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the Forefeast, twice. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

After Kathisma 1, the Sessional Hymn of the Forefeast. After Kathisma 2, the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion, twice. *Glory, both now:* Stavrotheotokion, without litany.

Canon of the Forefeast with its Heirmos to 6; and of the Saint to 4; and of the Triodion to 4.

*When the triode arrives:*

We chant the Canon of the Forefeast with its Heirmos to 4; and 3 troparia of the Saint to 2; and of the Triodion to 8: the appointed ode of the current canon to 4, and of the second triode to 4. The first triode was sung beforehand at Compline.

After Ode 3, the Kontakion of the Saint, and his Sessional Hymn. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion of the Forefeast, and the Ikos.

After Ode 9, the Exapostilarion of the Saint. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Aposticha, the sticheron of the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast. The litany; and 3 great prostrations, without dismissal; and the First Hour.

At the First Hour, the Troparion of the Forefeast. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the Forefeast. After **"More honorable"**, 3 great prostrations; and the dismissal; and after the dismissal, the Litiya.

Likewise at the other Hours: the Troparion of the Forefeast and of the Saint. At the Sixth Hour, the usual troparion and the reading. After the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the Forefeast; and at each Hour, 3 great prostrations.

Vespers of the feast takes place at its own time. In this manner we chant the service of the Forefeast if it also occurs on Friday, evening and morning without change. At Compline, the fourth morning triode of Tone 6.

*If the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Saturday:*

In the evening, the kathisma of the day. At **"Lord, I have cried"**, 6 stichera: 3 of the Forefeast, and 3 of the Fathers from the Triodion. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* Theotokion of Tone 1. Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day; and the reading of the Triodion; and the prokeimenon: **"Let Israel hope in the Lord"**.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the Forefeast. After **"Now lettest Thou"** and after the Trisagion, the Troparion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast. The litany; and the dismissal.

At Compline, after **"It is truly meet"** and after the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the Forefeast, twice. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

After the Kathismata, both Sessional Hymns of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the Forefeast.

Canon of the Forefeast with its Heirmos to 6, the heirmoi twice; and of the Fathers to 8. Katavasia: the Heirmos of the Triodion. The Heirmos of the Triodion is sung once for the ode; and at the end we conclude with that same Heirmos.

After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Forefeast; and the Sessional Hymn of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Fathers.

The Exapostilarion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the Forefeast.

At the Praises, of the Fathers to 4. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the Forefeast. Great Doxology; and the rest of Matins.

At the Hours, the Troparion of the Forefeast. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. We say alternately the Kontakia of the Forefeast and of the Fathers.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the Forefeast, Ode 3 to 4; and of the Fathers, Ode 6 to 4. After the Little Entrance, the Troparion of the Forefeast, and of the Fathers. *Glory:* Kontakion of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the Forefeast; and all the other service of the day and of the Fathers. The service of the Martyr Tryphon is chanted at Compline, or whenever the superior determines.

*It is fitting to know: If the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee, or of the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare, or Cheesefare:*[^1426]

On Saturday at Small Vespers, the Resurrection stichera of the Tone to 4. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. At the Aposticha, 1 Resurrection sticheron; and the festal aposticha of Great Vespers, with their refrains. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. The Resurrection Troparion. *Glory, both now:* of the feast; and the dismissal.

At Great Vespers, we chant the entire kathisma **"Blessed is the man"**. At **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 Resurrection stichera of the Tone; and 3 of the Triodion; and 4 of the feast.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

3 Resurrection stichera; and 4 of the Triodion; and 3 of the feast. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast. Entrance; and 3 readings of the feast.

At Litiya, the stichera of the feast, Tone 1: **"The ancient of days"**, and the other two. Then the praises stichera of the Triodion: the first sticheron, Tone 3: **"Of the boastful Pharisee"**; the second, Tone 8: **"Boasting of works"**.

*If it is the Sunday of the Prodigal Son:*

After the stichera of the feast, we chant 3 praises stichera of the Triodion: the first sticheron, Tone 2: **"The voice of the prodigal"**; the second, Tone 8: **"Having wasted prodigally"**; the third, Tone 6: **"Good Father"**.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

After the stichera of the feast, we chant 2 praises stichera: the first, Tone 6: **"I ponder that day"**; the second: **"O what an hour it will be then"**.

*If it is Cheesefare Sunday:*

We chant at Litiya 2 praises stichera of the Triodion: the first, Tone 5: **"Woe is me"**; the second: **"The stadium of virtues"**.

*Glory:* we chant on all these Sundays of the Pharisee, Prodigal Son, Meatfare, and Cheesefare the doxastikon of the Triodion written for Litiya; *Both now:* of the feast. (For this reason it was appointed for the praises stichera of the Triodion to be sung at Litiya at Vespers, so that not a single sticheron of the Triodion should be left unsung.)[^1427]

At the Aposticha, the Resurrection stichera. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast. At the Blessing of the Loaves, the Troparion of the feast, thrice; and the reading from the Apostolic Epistles.

*Where there is no vigil:*

After **"Now lettest Thou"**: the Resurrection Troparion. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. At Compline, after the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the Triodion. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. We chant the Midnight Office according to custom with the Triadic Canon.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**: the Resurrection Troparion, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Resurrection Sessional Hymns with their Theotokia; and the reading from the Commentary Gospel. The Polyeleos of the feast; and the troparia: **"The assembly of the Angels"**.

*If it is the Sunday of the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare, or Cheesefare:*

We append also the third Psalm: **"By the waters of Babylon"**. Then the Megalynarion of the feast, and the Sessional Hymns of the feast, once each; and the reading of the feast. The Hymns of Ascent of the Tone; the Prokeimenon of the feast; **"Let every breath"**; the Gospel of the feast; **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"**; and Psalm 50; the sticheron of the feast.

Resurrection Canon with its Heirmos to 4; and of the Triodion to 4; and of the feast to 6. Katavasia of the feast, both choirs together.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

Resurrection Canon to 4; and of the Triodion to 6; and of the feast to 4. After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos, and the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast; and we read the Synaxarion. At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**; the Resurrection Exapostilarion. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 4 Resurrection stichera; and 4 of the feast, with the refrains of the feast. *Glory:* of the feast. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

At the Praises, 4 Resurrection stichera; and 4 of the feast with the Doxastikon, with the refrains of the feast. And after the completion of the stichera of the feast, we say the verse: **"Arise, O Lord my God"**; and we chant the Idiomelon sticheron of the Triodion, Tone 8: **"Daniel the Prophet"**. *Glory*, Tone 1: **"Let us purify ourselves, brethren"**. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**.

*If it is Cheesefare Sunday:*

At the Praises, we chant as on Meatfare Sunday, and we say the verse likewise: **"Arise, O Lord my God"**; and we chant the Idiomelon sticheron of the Triodion, Tone 6: **"Adam is driven out of Paradise"**. *Glory*, the same Tone: **"The season hath arrived"**. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos"**. Great Doxology; the Resurrection Troparion; and the usual dismissal. And the procession into the narthex; *Glory, both now:* the Evangelical sticheron; and the First Hour.

At the First Hour, and at the others, the Resurrection Troparion. *Glory:* of the feast. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After **"Our Father"**, we say alternately the Kontakia of the feast and of the Triodion.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the Tone to 4; and of the Triodion, Ode 3 to 4; and of the feast, Ode 6 to 4. Introit of the feast. We chant the Resurrection Troparion, and of the feast. *Glory:* Kontakion of the Triodion. *Both now:*

*Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon of the Tone, or of the Triodion, and of the feast; the Epistle; Alleluia; the Gospel; and the Communion Hymn, first of the day, and of the feast.

*If the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Meatfare Saturday:*[^1428]

The service of the dead is chanted, evening and morning, on the preceding Saturday, or on Meatfare Thursday. On the Saturday itself, the entire service of the feast alone is chanted without change.

*If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Monday, or Tuesday, or Thursday of Cheesefare Week:*

In the evening: at Small Vespers, at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 4 stichera of the feast. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the feast; the litany; and the dismissal.

At Great Vespers and at Matins, the entire service of the feast is celebrated without change, as was indicated above in order; for the Triodion is not chanted together with the feast, on whatever day it occurs during Cheesefare Week, except on Saturday and Sunday, save for the idiomela stichera of the day which are said at Small Vespers, as indicated.

*Where there is no all-night vigil:*

We chant at Great Vespers according to what is prescribed above. At the Aposticha, *Glory:* of the Triodion, the Idiomelon of the day; *Both now:* of the feast. At the Praises, after the stichera of the feast, *Glory:* of the Triodion, the Idiomelon of the day; *Both now:* of the feast. Great Doxology. At the Hours and at the Divine Liturgy, the entire service of the feast; the Epistle and Gospel of the day are read beforehand.

*If the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Wednesday or Friday of Cheesefare Week:*

At Small and Great Vespers, and at Matins, the entire service is celebrated as indicated for Cheesefare Monday, without change. At the end of Vespers and Matins, and of the Great Hour, there are 3 great prostrations. Only at the Hours do we say the troparia and Kontakion of the feast. At the Sixth Hour, the Troparion of the Prophecy is said, and the Prophecy is read. The Liturgy of John Chrysostom is celebrated at its proper time. We chant Vespers according to custom at its proper time. After the customary psalm, the kathisma of the day.

*Where there was an all-night vigil:*

We do not say the kathisma; but after the customary psalm, we say: **"Lord, I have cried unto Thee"**. Then 8 stichera of the feast, and 3 of Saint Symeon. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon of the day, and the Prophecy. Then the prokeimenon: **"Let Israel hope in the Lord"**. At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the feast; and the dismissal.

*If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Monday:*

We chant the morning triode at Compline on Sunday. And the morning Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion we chant on the feast, at the Praises, at *Glory*.

*If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Tuesday:*

We chant the Tuesday triode on Wednesday of Cheesefare at Compline. And the morning Idiomelon of the day we chant on the feast, at the Praises, at *Glory*.

*If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Wednesday:*

We chant the Wednesday canon and triode on Thursday of Cheesefare at Compline. And the morning Idiomelon of the day we chant on the feast, at the Praises, at *Glory*.

*If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Thursday:*

We chant the Thursday triode on Tuesday of Cheesefare at Compline.

*If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Friday:*

We chant the Friday canon with the triode on Wednesday of Cheesefare at Compline.

*If the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Saturday:*

On Friday evening, at Small Vespers, 4 stichera of the feast. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon and Prophecy of the day, and after it the prokeimenon: **"Let Israel hope in the Lord"**. At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the feast; and the dismissal.

At Great Vespers, we chant **"Blessed is the man"**, the first antiphon. At **"Lord, I have cried"**, we set 8 verses; and we chant 6 stichera of the feast, and 4 of the Fathers. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day, and the readings of the feast. At Litiya, the stichera, and *Glory, both now:* of the feast. At the Aposticha, the stichera of the feast. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. At the Blessing of the Loaves, the Troparion of the feast, twice; and of the Fathers, once; and the reading of the feast.

*Where there is no all-night vigil:*

We chant at Great Vespers all according to what is prescribed above: the Prokeimenon, the reading of the day, and the prokeimenon: **"Let Israel hope in the Lord"**. Then the readings of the feast. At the Aposticha of the feast; then the refrain of the Fathers: **"Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones"**; and we chant their Idiomelon: **"The multitudes of the monastics"**. *Glory:* the Idiomelon of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the feast, twice. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Sessional Hymns of the feast, and the reading of the feast. After the Polyeleos, the Sessional Hymn of the feast. Hymns of Ascent, the first antiphon of Tone 4; the Prokeimenon; **"Let every breath"**; the Gospel; and after Psalm 50, the sticheron of the feast.

Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 8, and of the Fathers to 6. Katavasia of the feast. After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Fathers; and their Sessional Hymn, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast; and we read the Synaxarion. At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**; the Exapostilarion of the feast. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 of the Fathers. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast.

Great Doxology; Troparion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the feast; the litanies; and the dismissal. And the usual procession into the narthex; and the First Hour.

At the Hours, the Troparion of the feast. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After **"Our Father"**, the Kontakion of the feast.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the feast, Ode 3 to 4; and of the Fathers, Ode 6 to 4. Introit of the feast. Troparion of the feast and of the Fathers. *Glory:* Kontakion of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Hymn, first of the feast, and of the Fathers.

*If the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Monday of the First Week of the Fast:*

We chant the service of the feast on Cheesefare Sunday according to what is prescribed above.

*It is fitting to know: If the Afterfeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on the Sunday of the Pharisee, or of the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare:*[^1429]

At Small Vespers, at **"Lord, I have cried"**, and at the Aposticha, the stichera of the Octoechos, according to custom.

At Great Vespers, at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 4 Resurrection stichera; and 3 of the Triodion; and 3 of the feast.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

3 Resurrection stichera; and 4 of the Triodion; and 3 of the feast. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* Theotokion of Tone 1.

At Litiya, the appointed stichera of the feast from the aposticha of that day; and the praises stichera of the Triodion. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Resurrection stichera. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Blessing of the Loaves, Troparion: **"O Theotokos Virgin"**, twice; and of the feast, once; and the reading.

At **"God is the Lord"**, the Resurrection Troparion, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Resurrection Sessional Hymns with their Theotokia; and the reading from the Commentary Gospel. After the Evlogitaria, the troparia: **"The assembly of the Angels"**.

*If it is the Sunday of the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare:*

We chant the Polyeleos, and we append also the third Psalm: **"By the waters of Babylon"**; the Hymns of Ascent; and the Prokeimenon of the Tone; **"Let every breath"**; the Resurrection Gospel; **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"**; and the rest.

Resurrection Canon with its Heirmos to 4; and of the Theotokos to 2; and of the Triodion to 4; and of the feast to 4. Katavasia of the feast.

*If on Meatfare Sunday:*

Resurrection Canon to 4; and of the Triodion to 6; and of the feast to 4. Katavasia of the Triodion. After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast. *Glory:* Sessional Hymn of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Triodion. At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**. Then the Resurrection Exapostilarion. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 4 Resurrection stichera; and 4 idiomela of the feast, with its refrains.

*If it is Meatfare Sunday:*

We say the verse: **"Arise, O Lord my God"**; and we chant the Idiomelon sticheron of the Triodion: **"Daniel the Prophet"**. *Glory*, of the Triodion: **"Let us purify ourselves"**. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**.

*If it is the Sunday of the Pharisee, or of the Prodigal Son:*

We do not say the verse of the Triodion; but after the stichera of the feast, we say *Glory:* of the Triodion; *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**. Great Doxology. After the Trisagion, the Resurrection Troparion; the litanies; and the dismissal; and the usual procession into the narthex. *Glory, both now:* the Evangelical sticheron; and the First Hour.

At the Hours, the Resurrection Troparion. *Glory:* of the feast. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After **"Our Father"**, we say alternately the Kontakia of the Triodion and of the feast.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the Tone to 4; and of the Triodion, Ode 6 to 4; and of the feast, the appointed ode to 4. After the Little Entrance, the Resurrection Troparion, and of the feast. *Glory:* Kontakion of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Hymn of the day.

*If the Afterfeast of the Meeting occurs on Meatfare Saturday:*

The service of the Afterfeast is omitted, and we chant the regular service of the Saturday of the Dead.

*If the Afterfeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Monday, or Tuesday, or Thursday of Cheesefare Week:*

At **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 of the Saint. *Glory:* of the Saint, if he has one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the Saint, if there is one. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. The litany; and the dismissal.

At Matins: at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the feast, twice. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Sessional Hymns of the feast.

Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 8; and of the Menaion to 4.

*Where the triode occurs:*

We chant the Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 4; and of the Menaion, 3 troparia to 2; and of the Triodion to 8. If the Saint has a Kontakion and Ikos, we say them after Ode 3. Then the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion, written after Kathisma 2. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the feast. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast. After Ode 9, the Exapostilarion of the feast. *Glory:* of the Saint; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 4 stichera of the feast from the aposticha of that day. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the idiomela of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After **"Our Father"**, the Troparion of the Saint. *Glory, both now:* of the feast; but if not, of the feast only. And after the litany, the First Hour.

At the Hours, the Troparion of the feast. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, we say the Kontakion of the feast without change.

*If the Afterfeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Week on Wednesday or Friday:*

There are no prostrations, save only at the end of each Hour, 3 great prostrations.

In the evening, at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 of the Saint from the Menaion. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the Saint, if he has one; *Glory, both now:* of the feast. If not, of the feast. The litany; and 3 great prostrations; and the dismissal.

We chant Great Compline, beginning at **"When I called"**; at which we chant the first morning triode of the Triodion. After **"It is truly meet"** and after the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the feast. After **"More honorable"**, 3 great prostrations; and at the dismissal, 3 prostrations. Likewise at the Midnight Office, after **"More honorable"**, 3 great prostrations.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the feast, twice. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Sessional Hymns of the feast.

Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 6; and of the Menaion to 4; and of the Triodion to 4.

*When the triode arrives:*

We chant the Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 4; and of the Menaion to 2, the first two troparia as one; and of the Triodion to 8: the appointed ode of the current canon to 4, and of the second triode to 4; the first triode was sung beforehand at Compline.

After Ode 3, the Kontakion of the Saint; then the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* of the feast. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast, and the Prologue. After Ode 9, the Exapostilarion of the Saint, if there is one; *Glory, both now:* of the feast. If not, of the feast twice.

At the Praises, prosomoia stichera of the feast to 4 from the aposticha of that day. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory:* of the Saint, if there is one; *Both now:* of the feast. If not, *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After the Trisagion, the Troparion of the Saint, if there is one; *Glory, both now:* of the feast. If not, of the feast only. The litany; and 3 great prostrations; and the First Hour.

At the First Hour, the Troparion of the feast. *Glory:* of the Saint. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the feast. After **"More honorable"**, 3 great prostrations, and the rest; and the complete dismissal, and 3 prostrations.

Likewise at the other Hours we say the Troparion of the feast; and after the Trisagion, the Kontakion of the feast. After **"More honorable"**, 3 great prostrations. At the Sixth Hour, the Troparion of the Prophecy, and the reading; then the prokeimenon. After the Ninth Hour, we say the Beatitudes swiftly without singing, and the rest.

On Wednesday evening, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 prosomoia of the Saint from the Menaion. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Then the prokeimenon, and the Prophecy of the day.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Thereafter the litany: **"Have mercy on us, O God"**; and 3 great prostrations; and the prayer: **"All-Holy Trinity"**; and **"Blessed be the Name of the Lord"**, thrice; and Psalm 33; and the dismissal; and 3 prostrations.

*If the Afterfeast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Saturday:*

On Friday evening, after the dismissal of the Hours, we begin Vespers immediately; and after the usual kathisma, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 of the Fathers. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon, and the Prophecy of the day. Then: **"Vouchsafe, O Lord"**; at the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the feast; the litany; and the dismissal. At Compline, the Kontakion of the feast.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the feast, twice. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. After their Kathismata, the Sessional Hymns of the Fathers, twice each. *Glory, both now:* of the feast; and the reading of the feast, or of the Fathers.

Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 6, and of the Fathers to 8. Katavasia of the feast. After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast, and the Sessional Hymn of the Fathers, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Fathers. The Exapostilarion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 3 stichera of the feast from the aposticha of that day, and 3 of the Fathers. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Great Doxology; Troparion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the feast; the litanies; and the dismissal. And the usual procession into the narthex; and the First Hour.

At the Hours, the Troparion of the feast, and of the Fathers. We say alternately the Kontakia of the feast and of the Fathers.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the feast, the appointed ode to 4; and of the Fathers, Ode 6. After the Little Entrance, the Troparion of the feast, and of the Fathers, and of the temple of the Saint. Then the Kontakion of the temple of the Saint. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon, Alleluia, and Communion Hymn of the feast, and of the Fathers; the Epistle and Gospel of the day, and of the Fathers.

*Instruction concerning the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord, on whatever day the Meeting occurs, or on whatever day its Apodosis occurs:*[^1430]

If the Feast of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on the Sunday of the Prodigal Son, or on Monday, or on Tuesday of Meatfare Week, the feast is taken leave of on Friday of that same week; for on Meatfare Sunday there is no Apodosis.

If it occurs on Wednesday, or on Thursday, or on Friday, or on Saturday of Meatfare Week: its Apodosis is observed on Tuesday of Cheesefare Week.

If it occurs on Meatfare Sunday, or on Monday of Cheesefare Week: its Apodosis is observed on Thursday of that same week.

If it occurs on Tuesday or on Wednesday of Cheesefare Week: its Apodosis is observed on Saturday of that same week.

If it occurs on Cheesefare Thursday, or on Friday, or on Saturday: its Apodosis is observed on Cheesefare Sunday.

If the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Sunday: then we celebrate the feast for one day only.

*If the Apodosis of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on the Sunday of the Pharisee, or of the Prodigal Son, or Cheesefare:*

On Saturday at Small Vespers, the Resurrection stichera of the Tone according to custom. At the Aposticha, the Resurrection sticheron, and the festal aposticha of Great Vespers.

At Great Vespers, at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 Resurrection stichera; and 3 of the Triodion; and of the feast with the Doxastikon, 4. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* Theotokion of Tone 1. If it is a temple of the Meeting: *Both now:* of the feast.

At Litiya, the stichera of the feast; and the praises stichera of the Triodion, except the Doxastikon. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Resurrection stichera. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Blessing of the Loaves, Troparion: **"O Theotokos Virgin"**, twice; and of the feast, once.

At **"God is the Lord"**, the Resurrection Troparion, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Resurrection Sessional Hymns and their Theotokia; and the reading. And after the reading we chant the Polyeleos: the Psalm **"Praise ye the Name of the Lord"**, and the other Psalm **"O give thanks unto the Lord"**.

*If it is the Sunday of the Prodigal Son or Cheesefare:*

We chant also the third Psalm: **"By the waters of Babylon"**, with the Alleluia; and the rest of the Resurrection service through to the Canon.

Resurrection Canon to 4; and of the Triodion to 4; and of the feast to 6. Katavasia of the feast.

After Ode 3, the Kontakia and Sessional Hymn of the Triodion. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast.

At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**; the Resurrection Exapostilarion. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 4 Resurrection stichera; and of the feast with the Doxastikon, 4. *Glory:* of the Triodion. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**.

*If it is Cheesefare Sunday:*

After the stichera of the feast, we say the verse: **"Arise, O Lord my God"**; and we chant the Idiomelon sticheron of the Triodion for the day. *Glory:* another sticheron. *Both now:* **"Most blessed art thou"**. And after the dismissal, *Glory, both now:* the Evangelical sticheron; and the First Hour.

At the Hours, the Resurrection Troparion. *Glory:* of the feast. *Both now:* Theotokion of the Hours. After **"Our Father"**, we say alternately the Kontakion of the feast and of the Triodion.

At the Divine Liturgy: the Beatitudes of the Tone to 4; and of the Triodion, Ode 3 to 4; and of the feast, Ode 4 to 4. After the Little Entrance, the Resurrection Troparion and of the feast. *Glory:* Kontakion of the Triodion. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon of the day, and of the feast.

On Meatfare Saturday the Apodosis of the Meeting does not take place; likewise on Meatfare Sunday.

*If the Apodosis of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Tuesday or on Thursday of Cheesefare Week, we chant thus:*

In the evening, at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 6 stichera of the feast. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon, with the usual refrains. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the feast; and the dismissal.

The service of the Saint of the day is chanted at Compline, or as the superior desires.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the feast, thrice. After the Kathismata, the Sessional Hymns of the feast.

Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 14. Where the triode occurs, we chant the canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 6, and of the Triodion to 8.

After Ode 3, the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion, written after Kathisma 2. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast.

At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**; the Exapostilarion of the feast, twice.

At the Praises, 4 stichera of the feast. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the feast; the litany; and the dismissal.

At the Divine Liturgy, the Beatitudes of the feast, Ode 1 to 8. After the Little Entrance, the Troparion of the feast. *Glory, both now:* Kontakion of the feast. Prokeimenon, Alleluia, and Communion Hymn of the feast; the Epistle and Gospel of the day.

On Wednesday and Friday the Apodosis of the Meeting does not take place.

*If the Apodosis of the Meeting of the Lord occurs on Cheesefare Saturday:*

On Cheesefare Friday evening: at **"Lord, I have cried"**, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 of the Fathers. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Prokeimenon and Prophecy of the day, and after it the prokeimenon: **"Let Israel hope in the Lord"**.

At the Aposticha, the Idiomelon of the Triodion for the day, twice, and the Martyrikon. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Troparion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

At Matins, at **"God is the Lord"**, the Troparion of the feast, twice. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. After the Kathismata, the Sessional Hymns of the feast.

Canon of the feast with its Heirmos to 8, and of the Fathers to 6. Katavasia of the feast.

After Ode 3, the Kontakion and Ikos of the Fathers; and their Sessional Hymn, twice. *Glory, both now:* of the feast.

After Ode 6, the Kontakion and Ikos of the feast.

At Ode 9, we chant **"More honorable"**; the Exapostilarion of the feast. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast.

At the Praises, 3 stichera of the feast, and 3 of the Fathers. *Glory:* of the Fathers. *Both now:* of the feast. Great Doxology; Troparion of the Fathers. *Glory, both now:* of the feast. And the usual procession into the narthex, and the First Hour, and the final dismissal.

At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes of the feast, Ode 4, on 4; and of the Fathers, Ode 6, on 4. After the Entrance, the troparion of the feast, and of the Fathers. Glory: Kontakion of the Fathers; Both now: Kontakion of the feast. Prokeimenon of the feast, and of the Fathers. The Apostle and the Gospel of the day, and of the Fathers. Alleluia of the feast, and of the Fathers; Communion verse of the feast, and of the Fathers.

*It is fitting to know: If the Finding of the Precious Head of John the Forerunner occurs on Meatfare or Cheesefare Sunday:*[^1431]
It begins from Wednesday of Meatfare Week, and ascends unto Tuesday of the First Week of the Great Fast.
On Saturday at Small Vespers: Resurrection stichera, 4; and the Theotokion of the Tone. At the Aposticha: Resurrection sticheron, 1; and the Aposticha of the Forerunner. After "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart": Resurrection troparion; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Resurrection Theotokion. Small Litany, and the dismissal.
At Great Vespers: At "Lord, I have cried," Resurrection stichera, 3; and of the Triodion, 4; and of the Forerunner, 3.
And if it is Cheesefare Sunday: We chant Resurrection stichera, 3; and Anatolian, 1; and of the Triodion, 2; and of the Forerunner, 3. Glory: Of the Triodion; Both now: The first Theotokion of the Tone. At the Litiya: The sticheron of the Temple; and of the Triodion Praises, the first verse, Tone 6: "I meditate upon that day"; the second, the same Tone: "O what a manner of hour then, and day!"
And if it is Cheesefare Sunday, we likewise say of the Triodion the first verse, Tone 5: "Woe is me! Adam cried out with weeping"; the second, the same Tone: "The stadium of the virtues is opened." Glory: Of the Forerunner, Tone 6: "The treasury of divine gifts"; Both now: Of the Triodion, the Doxasticon written at the Litiya. At the Aposticha, Resurrection stichera; Glory: Of the Forerunner, Tone 2: "As of divine [mysteries]"; Both now: Of the Triodion. At the Blessing of the Loaves: Troparion, "O Theotokos Virgin," three times. And a reading from the Apostolic Epistles.
At Matins: At "God is the Lord," the Resurrection troparion, twice; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: The first Theotokion in the same Tone. After the Kathismata: Resurrection sessional hymns, with their Theotokia. And a reading from the Explanatory Gospel. Then: "Praise ye the name of the Lord," and "Give thanks unto the Lord"; we say also the third psalm: "By the rivers of Babylon," with Alleluia. Then we chant the selected psalm of the Forerunner, and the troparia: "The angelic assembly"; and both sessional hymns, once each. Glory: Sessional hymn after the Polyeleos; Both now: Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner. The Anabathmoi, and the Prokeimenon of the Tone. "Let every breath"; Resurrection Gospel. "Having seen the Resurrection of Christ"; Resurrection canon with the heirmos on 4; and of the Triodion on 6; and of the Forerunner on 4. Katavasia of the Triodion.
If it is Cheesefare Sunday: Resurrection canon, with the heirmos on 4; and of the Theotokos on 2; and of the Triodion on 4; and of the Forerunner on 4. Katavasia of the Triodion. After Ode 3: Kontakion and Ikos of the Forerunner, and his sessional hymn, twice. Glory, both now: Of the Triodion. And a reading of the Forerunner. After Ode 6: Kontakion of the Triodion, and the Ikos; and the Synaxarion. At Ode 9 we chant "More honorable"; Resurrection exapostilarion. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Of the Triodion. At the Praises: Resurrection stichera, 4; and of the saint with the Doxasticon, 4, with his refrains. And after the stichera of the saint, we say: "Arise, O Lord my God"; and we chant the automelon sticheron of the Triodion, Tone 6: "Daniel the prophet"; Glory, Tone 1: "Let us purify ourselves beforehand, brethren"; Both now: "Most blessed art thou."
And if it is Cheesefare Sunday: We likewise say the refrain: "Arise, O Lord my God"; and we chant the automelon sticheron of the Triodion, Tone 5: "Adam sat over against Paradise"; Glory, the same Tone: "The time has drawn near"; Both now: "Most blessed art thou." Great Doxology. After the Trisagion: Resurrection troparion; the litanies, and the dismissal, and the customary departure into the narthex. Glory, both now: Gospel sticheron, and the First Hour. At the Hours: Resurrection troparion; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the "Our Father": The kontakion of the Triodion and of the Forerunner, alternating, we say. At the Liturgy: The Beatitudes of the Tone, on 4; and of the Triodion, Ode 3, on 4; and of the Forerunner, Ode 6, on 4. After the Entrance: Resurrection troparion, and of the Forerunner. Glory: His kontakion; Both now: Of the Triodion. Prokeimenon, Apostle, Gospel, and Communion verse: First of the day, and of the Forerunner.

*If the Finding of the Precious Head of John the Forerunner occurs on Meatfare Saturday:*[^1432]
The entire feast of the Forerunner is sung beforehand on Meatfare Friday, without change.

*If the Finding of the Precious Head of John the Forerunner occurs on Monday or Tuesday of Cheesefare Week:*[^1433]
On Sunday evening we chant: "Blessed is the man," the first antiphon; [and on the remaining days of Cheesefare Week, the appointed Kathisma is said.] At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera of the Forerunner, on 6. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Resurrection Theotokion, in the same Tone. Prokeimenon of the day, and the Old Testament readings of the Forerunner. At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Theotokion. Troparion of the Forerunner; Glory, both now: Resurrection Theotokion, and the litany.
At Matins: At "God is the Lord," the troparion of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: Theotokion. And the customary Kathismata. After the first Kathisma: Sessional hymn of the Triodion; and a reading of the Forerunner. Polyeleos, and the sessional hymn of the Forerunner, twice, and his Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner. The Anabathmoi, the first antiphon of Tone 4; Prokeimenon of the Forerunner. "Let every breath"; Gospel of the Forerunner. Then Psalm 50; sticheron of the Forerunner. Canon of the Theotokos, with the heirmos on 6; and of the Forerunner on 8. Katavasia: "I will open my mouth." But where there is a triode, we chant the canon of the Forerunner, with the heirmos on 6; and of the Triodion on 8. After Ode 3: Sessional hymn of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: Theotokion, and a reading of the Forerunner. After Ode 6: Kontakion of the Forerunner, and the Ikos, and the Prologue. After Ode 9: Exapostilarion of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: His Theotokion. At the Praises: Stichera of the Forerunner, on 4. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Theotokion. At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. Glory: Of the Forerunner: "He who previously upon a platter"; Both now: Theotokion; and the customary prichal, and the dismissal. And the First Hour: Troparion of the Forerunner; and after the Trisagion, his kontakion. Likewise also at the other Hours. At the Liturgy: The Beatitudes of the Forerunner, Odes 3 and 6; Prokeimenon, Apostle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion verse of the day, and of the Forerunner.

*And if the Finding of the Precious Head of John the Forerunner occurs on Wednesday of Cheesefare Week:*[^1434]
We chant the entire service of the Forerunner at Vespers and Matins on Tuesday of Cheesefare Week.

*And if on Friday:*
We chant his service on Thursday.

*If the Finding of the Precious Head occurs on Cheesefare Thursday:*[^1435]
On Wednesday at the Hours, after "Remit, forgive," and after the prostrations, there is ringing of the bells according to custom, and immediately we say: "Come, let us worship," three times, and the customary Psalm; and the litany, and the stichera according to what is prescribed. After the Entrance: Prokeimenon and the Old Testament readings of the day; and after these, the Prokeimenon: "Let Israel hope in the Lord." Then the Old Testament readings of the saint, 3. After this: "Vouchsafe, O Lord." At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Theotokion. After "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart," the Trisagion. After the "Our Father": Troparion of the Forerunner; Glory, both now: Resurrection Theotokion, and the litany: "Have mercy on us, O God"; and 3 great prostrations. After this, the prayer: "All-Holy Trinity"; and "Blessed be the name of the Lord," three times, with prostrations, and Psalm 33. Then: "It is truly meet," and the dismissal. And we enter into the refectory and dispense for cheese and eggs.

*If the Finding of the Precious Head of John the Forerunner occurs on Cheesefare Saturday:*[^1436]
On Friday evening, we chant: "Blessed is the man," the first antiphon. At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera of the Forerunner, podoben, 3; and of the Fathers, 3. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Theotokion, the first of the Tone. Entrance, Prokeimenon of the day, and the Old Testament reading; and after it the Prokeimenon, Tone 6: "Let Israel hope in the Lord." Then the Old Testament readings of the Forerunner. "Vouchsafe, O Lord, in this evening"; and the litany: "Let us complete our evening prayer." At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. Then the refrain: "The righteous man shall be glad"; and we chant the automelon sticheron of the Forerunner, Tone 6: "He who previously upon a platter"; Glory: Of the Fathers, Tone 8: "The multitudes of monastics"; Both now: Theotokion, in the same Tone. Troparion of the Forerunner; Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Theotokion: "The mystery hidden from the ages." We chant the litany: "Have mercy on us, O God"; and 3 great prostrations. Then: "All-Holy Trinity"; and "Blessed be the name of the Lord," three times, and Psalm 33: "I will bless the Lord"; and the dismissal. In the refectory, we dispense for cheese and eggs.
At Compline: We chant the canon of the departed in the narthex from the Octoechos of the Tone, or the Theotokion. After "It is truly meet," and after the Trisagion: Kontakion of the Forerunner; Glory, both now: From the Triodion, of the Fathers.
At Matins: At "God is the Lord," troparion of the Forerunner, twice; Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: The first Theotokion. After the Kathismata: Sessional hymns of the Fathers and their Theotokia; and a reading of the Fathers. Polyeleos, and the sessional hymns of the Forerunner, all once each. Prokeimenon; "Let every breath"; and the Gospel of the Forerunner; Psalm 50; sticheron of the Forerunner. Canon of the Forerunner, with the heirmos on 6; and of the Fathers on 8. Katavasia: The heirmos of the Triodion. We also recite the biblical canticle of Ode 9 of the Triodion; but in the Psalter we do not say that canticle. After Ode 3: Kontakion of the Forerunner, and the Ikos, and his sessional hymn. Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner. After Ode 6: Kontakion of the Fathers, and the Ikos, and the Synaxarion. After Ode 9: Exapostilarion of the Forerunner; Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Theotokion. At the Praises: Stichera of the Forerunner, 3; and of the Fathers, 3. Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Theotokion. Great Doxology; troparion of the Forerunner; Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Theotokion; and the departure into the narthex, and the First Hour. At the Hours: Troparion of the Forerunner; Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the "Our Father": The kontakion of the Forerunner and of the Fathers, alternating, we say.
At the Liturgy: The Beatitudes of the Forerunner, Ode 3, on 4; and of the Fathers, Ode 6, on 4. After the Entrance: Troparion of the Temple, and of the Forerunner, and of the Fathers. Then the kontakion of the Temple, and of the Forerunner. Glory: Of the Fathers; Both now: Of the Temple of the Theotokos; or: "Steadfast Protectress of Christians." Prokeimenon, Apostle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion verse: First of the Forerunner, and afterwards of the Fathers. The appointed daily Apostle and Gospel we read combined.

*And if the Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner occurs on Monday of the First Week of the Fasts:*[^1437]
The entire service of the Forerunner is sung on Cheesefare Sunday.

His service is sung on Cheesefare Sunday, or on Saturday of the First Week of the Fasts.

Glory: Of Theodore; Both now: The first Theotokion of the preceding Tone, without Entrance. Prokeimenon, and the readings of the day, and of the Forerunner. At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. *For us, Small Compline is sung according to the decision of the Synod of Lviv held in the year 1891.*[^1439]

After the first Kathisma: Both sessional hymns of the Forerunner; Glory, both now: Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner. After the second Kathisma: Sessional hymn of Theodore; Glory, both now: Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner, or of Theodore. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Of the Theotokos of the Temple, or: "Steadfast Protectress of Christians." Prokeimenon, Alleluia, and Communion verse: Of the Forerunner and of Theodore, one. The Apostle and the Gospel: First for Saturday, and of Theodore combined, then of the Forerunner.

*And if the Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner occurs on Monday of the Second, or Third, or Fourth Weeks of the Fasts:*[^1441]
On Sunday evening we chant: "Blessed is the man," the first antiphon. At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera on 10; of the Triodion of the day, on 4, repeating the first; and of the Forerunner, on 6; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: The first Theotokion. Entrance, Great Prokeimenon of the Triodion; and the Old Testament readings of the Forerunner, 3. Then: "Vouchsafe, O Lord, in this evening"; and "Let us complete our evening prayer." At the Aposticha of the Triodion: Idiomelon of the day, twice; and the Martyr-hymn; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Resurrection Theotokion, according to the Tone. After the "Our Father": Troparion of the Forerunner; Glory, both now: Resurrection Theotokion. Litany: "Have mercy on us, O God"; and 3 great prostrations, and the dismissal. The Midnight Office we chant according to custom, with the Kathisma and the customary troparia. After "More honorable," 3 great prostrations.
At Matins: At "God is the Lord," the troparion of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: Theotokion. After the first and second Kathismata we say the sessional hymns of the Triodion; the litanies are not said; and a reading in Ephrem. After the third Kathisma, the priest says the small litany; and both sessional hymns of the Forerunner; and a reading of the Forerunner. Polyeleos, and the sessional hymn of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner. The Anabathmoi, the first antiphon of Tone 4. Prokeimenon, and the Gospel of the Forerunner. Then Psalm 50; sticheron of the Forerunner. Canon of the Forerunner, with the heirmos on 8. Katavasia: "I will open my mouth." But where there is a triode: We chant the canon of the Forerunner, with the heirmos on 6, and the triode on 8. Katavasia of the Triodion. After Ode 3: Sessional hymn of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: Theotokion; and a reading of the Forerunner. After Ode 6: Kontakion and Ikos, and the Prologue. Exapostilarion of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, both now: Theotokion. At the Praises: Stichera of the Forerunner, on 4; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: His Theotokion. Then the superior or the reader says: "Glory to Thee Who hast shown us the light." After this, the litany: "Let us complete our morning prayer." Then at the Aposticha of the Triodion: Idiomelon of the day, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now:

Theotokion. After this: "It is a good thing to give thanks," we say once. After the "Our Father": Troparion of the Forerunner; Glory, both now: Resurrection Theotokion. Litany: "Have mercy on us, O God"; and 3 great prostrations, without dismissal, and the First Hour.
At the Hours we say the troparion of the Forerunner; and after the Trisagion, his kontakion; and at the end, 6 great prostrations. And after the prostrations, the Trisagion we do not say, but we say: "O Christ, the true Light"; and the dismissal. The Third, Sixth, and Ninth Hours we chant with Kathismata, at their appropriate time, with a reading. At the Sixth Hour, the Old Testament reading of the day is read, according to its order.
The prayers of Basil the Great after the Hours we say without change, namely: "O Master, God the Father," and "O God and Lord of hosts," and "O Master, Lord Jesus Christ." When we chant the Polyeleos, at the Hours we do not strike the bell. After the Kathisma: Alleluia, three times; then: "Lord, have mercy," three times; and the reader says: "Glory," the troparion of the Forerunner; "Both now," the Theotokion of the Hours. After the "Our Father": Kontakion of the Forerunner. After "More honorable," 3 great prostrations. After the Ninth Hour, the Beatitudes we say quickly without chanting. After "Remit, forgive": Kontakion of the Temple; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Of the Temple of the Theotokos. But if there is no Temple of the Theotokos: "Both now: Steadfast Protectress of Christians." After this: "Lord, have mercy," 40. Glory, both now: "More honorable than the Cherubim"; and 10 great prostrations. Then the prayer: "All-Holy Trinity." And we begin Vespers according to custom: The superior or reader says: "Come, let us worship," three times. And we chant Vespers with prostrations, according to the Lenten order. After the customary Kathisma, at "Lord, I have cried," stichera on 10: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn; and podoben of the Triodion, 3; and of the Forerunner, 4. Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: The customary Theotokion according to the Tone. But if toward Wednesday or Friday, we say the Stavrotheotokion, according to the Tone of the Doxasticon. Entrance with the Gospel. "O Joyful Light"; and the Prokeimenon, and the readings of the day. "Let my prayer be set forth"; and 3 great prostrations. Small litanies we do not say. Prokeimenon, Apostle, Alleluia, Gospel of the Forerunner. Communion verse: "In everlasting remembrance"; and of the Forerunner. The Liturgy of the Presanctified according to custom.
At the dismissal, the priest commemorates first the celebrated saint, afterwards the saint of the coming day. Of the saint occurring in order, we chant the canon at Matins according to order; and his stichera that are written in the evening at "Lord, I have cried," we say them at the Praises.
And if, by reason of any great necessity, on the commemoration of great saints having a Polyeleos, it should not happen that the Presanctified Liturgy is celebrated, we chant the Hours as said above.[^1442] After the Ninth Hour, and after the prayer: "O Master, Lord Jesus Christ," we say also the psalm: "Bless the Lord, O my soul"; Glory: "Praise the Lord, O my soul"; Both now: "Only-begotten Son and Word"; and "In Thy Kingdom," quickly without chanting. At the Beatitudes we say of the Forerunner, Odes 3 and 6. Then the Apostle and the Gospel of the Forerunner. After this: "Remember us, O Lord." After "Remit, forgive," and after the "Our Father": Kontakion of the Temple; Glory: Of the Forerunner; Both now: Of the Theotokos of the Temple. But if there is no Temple of the Theotokos: "Steadfast Protectress of Christians." Then: "Lord, have mercy," 40. And 3 great prostrations, and at that time the paraecclesiarch tolls two bells.[^1443]

At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera of the Triodion, podoben, 3; and of Saint Tarasios, 3.[^1444] Glory, both now: Theotokion. But if toward Wednesday or Friday, we say: Glory, both now: Stavrotheotokion. Then: "O Joyful Light"; Prokeimenon, and the reading of the Triodion of the day. At the Aposticha, of the day from the Triodion. After "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart," and after the "Our Father": Troparion: "O Theotokos Virgin, rejoice"; and the rest according to the Lenten order. After "More honorable," and after the prostrations, we say the Trisagion; "Lord, have mercy," 12; "All-Holy Trinity." Then: "Blessed be the name of the Lord," and 3 prostrations; and "I will bless the Lord"; "It is truly meet"; and the dismissal. At the dismissal he commemorates the saint of the coming day. After the dismissal we enter into the refectory, and eat two cooked dishes with oil, and drink wine. On Wednesday and Friday, however, we eat two cooked dishes without oil, and drink wine.

*Instruction concerning the Holy Forty Martyrs:*[^1449]
*If the commemoration of the Holy Forty Martyrs occurs on Saturday of the First, Second, Third, or Fourth Weeks of the Fasts:*
The entire service is sung as indicated above for the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner, without change.

*If the Holy Forty Martyrs occurs on Wednesday of the Fourth Week:*[^1450]
Their service is sung on Tuesday, on the eve, as indicated for the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner on fasting days; on Wednesday, however, we chant the service of the Cross.

*If the Holy Forty Martyrs occurs on Thursday of the Great Canon:*[^1451]
Their service is sung on Tuesday of that week; and on Wednesday evening we chant the Great Canon with its readings.

*If the Holy Forty Martyrs occurs on the Saturday of the Akathist:*[^1452]
Their service is sung on the Fifth Sunday of the Fasts, as indicated for the Second Sunday of the Fasts.

*Instruction concerning the Forefeast of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos:*[^1453]
*If the Forefeast of the Annunciation occurs on any day of the week, except Saturday and Sunday:*
On the eve, at the Ninth Hour, we say the troparion and kontakion of the day from the Triodion. After the prayer of Saint Ephrem: "Come, let us worship," three times, with 3 prostrations; and the reader begins the prooemiac Psalm. And we chant Vespers with Kathisma and prostrations. At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera on 10; idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn; and podoben of the Triodion, 3; and of the forefeast, 4. Glory, both now: Of the forefeast. Entrance, Prokeimenon, and the readings of the day. "Let my prayer be set forth"; and the rest of the Presanctified. But if there is no Presanctified, at "Lord, I have cried," stichera on 6: Podoben of the Triodion, 3; and of the forefeast, 3. Glory, both now: Of the forefeast. Entrance, "O Joyful Light"; Prokeimenon and the readings of the day. Then: "Vouchsafe, O Lord." At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn. ## Instruction Concerning the Feast of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos[^1461]

It begins from Thursday of the Third Week of the Holy Fasts, and ascends unto Wednesday of Bright Week.
And if it is a fast day, we begin Vespers without Kathisma, and without prostrations. At "Lord, I have cried": We set 10 verses, and we chant in the Triodion the idiomelon of the day, twice; and the podoben, 3; and of the feast, 2, repeating two, Tone 6, podoben: "Having set aside all": "The pre-eternal Counsel"; Glory, both now, the same Tone: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance. "O Joyful Light"; Prokeimenon. And the readings of the day; and of the feast, 3: First, from Genesis: "Jacob went forth from the well"; second, from the Prophecy of Ezekiel: "It shall come to pass from the eighth day"; third, from Proverbs: "Wisdom has built a house for herself." Then: "Let my prayer be set forth"; and three great prostrations; and the rest of the Presanctified. But if there is no Presanctified: At "Lord, I have cried," stichera: Podoben of the Triodion, 3; and of the feast, 5; Glory, both now: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance, Prokeimenon, and readings of the day; and of the feast, 3. Then: "Vouchsafe, O Lord." At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day, twice; and the Martyr-hymn; Glory, both now: "Today is the joy." After "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart": Troparion of the feast. Litany, and three great prostrations, and the dismissal. In the refectory, we dispense for wine and oil, on whatever day it occurs, unto the glory of the Most Holy Theotokos.

*It is necessary to know: If the Annunciation occurs on any Saturday of the Fast:*[^1462]

*Decree of John the Faster:*[^1470]
We find in certain Typika that on this day of this precious feast there is sung: "Let my prayer be set forth." And this we do not know, in what manner it was determined. For some, out of arbitrary will and lack of instruction, have appended many things. But as for me, I judge that this ought not to be admitted at the complete Liturgy of Chrysostom, both at the Sixth Hour and when the Proskomide has already taken place. For it says: On whatever day the feast of the divine Annunciation occurs, the Liturgy of Chrysostom is celebrated, and at the refectory there is dispensation for fish, on whatever day it occurs. For this we received from the divine Fathers, to eat fish on the day of the divine Annunciation. But "Let my prayer be set forth" began to be sung in the Liturgy of the Presanctified, and at the Sixth Hour; for it is not fitting that any sign of mourning be sung on this day, for we honor it as the fountainhead of our salvation.

After "Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart": Troparion of the Archangel; Glory, both now: Of the feast. Litany, and 3 great prostrations, and the dismissal. In the refectory, there is great consolation for the brethren: We eat fish, and drink wine, on whatever day it occurs, once during the day, except on Saturday and Sunday.

*If the Annunciation occurs on Monday of the Second, Third, or Sixth Week of the Holy Fasts:*[^1476]
On Sunday at Small Vespers: Stichera of the feast, on 4; Glory, both now: Of the feast. At the Aposticha: Idiomelon of the day from the Triodion, twice; and the Martyr-hymn; Glory, both now: Of the feast. Troparion of the feast. Small Litany, and the dismissal.
At Great Vespers: After the customary Psalm, we chant: "Blessed is the man," the first antiphon. At "Lord, I have cried": Stichera on 10: Podoben of the Triodion, 3; and of the feast, 6. Glory, both now: "The Archangel Gabriel was sent." Entrance, Prokeimenon of the day; and 3 readings of the feast; and then the litany: "Let us all say"; then: "Vouchsafe, O Lord"; and "Let us complete our evening prayer."
At the Litiya: Stichera of the feast; Glory, both now: Of the feast. At the Aposticha: Stichera of the feast with the refrains of the feast; Glory, both now: Of the feast. At the Blessing of the Loaves: Troparion of the feast, three times; and a reading of the feast. At Matins, we chant the service of the feast, as prescribed above on a fast day in order.

*And if the Annunciation occurs on Cross-Veneration Wednesday:*[^1477]
The stichera, the Doxastica, and the canon of the Cross are left aside, and are sung on Monday or on Friday of that same week. Here, however, we chant the entire service of the Annunciation with the Wednesday triode, as indicated on the first day in order. And at Matins at the First Hour, the veneration of the Cross takes place according to the rite.

*And if the Annunciation occurs on the Fourth or Fifth Sunday of the Fasts:*[^1478]
On Saturday at Small Vespers: Stichera of the feast, on 4; Glory, both now: Of the feast. At the Aposticha: Resurrection sticheron, 1; and of the feast, the Aposticha of Great Vespers, with their refrains; Glory, both now: Of the feast. Troparion of the feast. Little Litany; and the Dismissal. At Great Vespers: **"Blessed is the man..."**, the entire kathisma. At "Lord, I have cried...", Resurrection stichera, 3; and Anatolian, 1; and of the feast, 6; Glory, both now, in Tone 6: **"He was sent from heaven..."** Entrance; Prokeimenon of the day; and 5 readings of the feast. At the Litiya, stichera of the feast; Glory, in Tone 8; Both now, in Tone 2. At the Aposticha, Resurrection stichera; Glory, both now: Of the feast. At the blessing of the loaves, troparion of the feast, thrice; and a reading from the apostolic epistles, or of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord...", Resurrection troparion, twice; Glory, both now: Of the feast. After the kathismata, Resurrection sessional hymns, with their Theotokia; and a reading from the Commentary Gospel. Then the Polyeleos, and the sessional hymns of the feast, all once; and a reading of the feast. The Graduals of the Tone; Prokeimenon of the feast; "Let every breath..."; and the Gospel of the feast; "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ..."; Psalm 50; Sticheron of the feast.

Resurrection canon with its Heirmos, on 4; and of the Triodion, on 4; and of the feast, on 6. Katavasia: The Heirmos of the feast. After the 3rd Ode, sessional hymn of the feast, and a reading of the feast. After the 6th Ode, we chant the Kontakion of the feast and the Ikos; and we read the Prologue. At the 9th Ode, we chant "More honorable..."; Resurrection Exapostilarion, and of the feast. At the Praises, stichera on 8: Resurrection, 4; and of the feast, 4, with their refrains; and with "Glory...", we chant the refrain: "Arise, O Lord my God..."; and we chant the idiomelon sticheron of the day; Glory, again the same; Both now: "Most blessed art thou..."; the Great Doxology. After the Trisagion, Resurrection troparion; the litanies; and the Dismissal. Then, Glory, both now: Gospel sticheron. And holy oil from the lamp of the Most Holy Theotokos is given to the brethren, while we sing the idiomelon sticheron of the feast. Procession into the narthex, and the First Hour. At the First Hour, Resurrection troparion; Glory: Of the feast; Both now: Theotokion of the Hours. After the Trisagion, Kontakion of the feast; and the rest; the complete Dismissal.

And at the second hour of the day, we make a procession outside the monastery, as indicated in order. And having returned, we chant the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great. The Beatitudes of the Tone, on 6; and of the feast, Ode 3, on 4. After the Entrance, troparia: Resurrectional, and of the feast; Glory, both now: Kontakion of the feast; Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Verse: first of the Sunday, then of the feast.

*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on Wednesday of the Fifth Week of the Fasts:*[^1479]
We chant the service of the Forefeast of the Annunciation: The stichera and the canon on Monday in order; while that of the saint from the Menaion we chant at Compline; because the Great Canon is transferred from Thursday to Tuesday of that same week. And we chant the Great Canon with the Tuesday triode, on account of the feast of the Most Holy Theotokos; because on Wednesday, which is the feast itself, the kathismata are not recited, and prostrations do not take place; and we chant the Apodosis of the feast on Thursday; therefore, on Wednesday evening, the stichera are chanted with prostrations, and the Great Canon is not sung on Thursday, for it was sung beforehand on Tuesday.

On Wednesday of the Fifth Week of the Fasts, we chant the entire service of the feast in the evening and in the morning with the Triodion according to the Typikon, as indicated previously for a fasting day. And the Apodosis of the Annunciation we chant on Thursday at Matins with the apostolic triode, as shown under Monday of the Fourth and Fifth Weeks of the Fast, for the Apodosis of the Feast of the Annunciation.

*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on Thursday of the Fifth Week of the Fasts, when the Great Canon is sung:*[^1480]
The Great Canon of lord Andrew is transferred on account of the feast, and we sing it on Tuesday of that same week with the Tuesday triode; while the apostolic triode we chant with the feast of the Annunciation; and afterwards, the Katavasia of the Triodion.

*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on Friday of that same week:*[^1481]
We chant the Forefeast of the Annunciation, the stichera and the canon beforehand on Wednesday in order, on account of the Great Canon; and the service of the saint in the Menaion we sing at Compline.

*And if the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos occurs on the Saturday of the Akathist:*[^1482]
On Friday evening, at "Lord, I have cried...", stichera on 10: In the Triodion, the idiomelon of the day, twice; and of the feast, on 8; Glory, both now: Of the feast: **"He was sent..."** Entrance; and the readings of the day; and 5 of the feast. After "Let my prayer be set forth...", 3 prostrations; and the rest of the Presanctified Gifts. And at "Blessed be the name of the Lord...", 3 prostrations; and at the Dismissal, prostrations.

And at the fourth hour of the night, the klepalo is sounded for the great bell, and afterwards for all the bells. And having gathered in the temple, when the priest has given the blessing, we chant Great Compline. And the priest censes according to custom, and after "Glory to God in the highest...", we go forth into the narthex and make the customary litiya, and we chant 3 idiomelon stichera of the feast, in Tone 1; Glory, both now: **"Gabriel announces the good tidings..."** And the customary prayers are recited by the priest. And upon the conclusion, we enter again into the temple; and we chant at the Aposticha the idiomelon stichera with their refrains; Glory, both now: **"Today is joy..."** At the blessing of the loaves, troparion of the feast, thrice; and a reading of the feast.

At Matins, at "God is the Lord...", troparion of the feast, thrice. After the 1st kathisma, the Kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader..."**, and the other 3; and 3 ikoi; and again the 1st kontakion; and the reading of the Akathist. After the 2nd kathisma, 3 kontakia and 3 ikoi; and again the 1st kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader..."**, as shown previously. And the reading of the Akathist; and the Polyeleos; and all the sessional hymns of the feast; and a reading of the feast. The Graduals, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4; Prokeimenon of the feast; "Let every breath..."; Gospel of the feast;

Psalm 50; Sticheron of the feast. Canon of the feast, with the Heirmos, on 8; and of the Akathist, on 6; Katavasia of the feast, both choirs together.

The four-ode canon[^1483] we do not chant here, but we sing it beforehand on Thursday at Compline. After the 3rd Ode, 3 kontakia and 3 ikoi; and again the 1st kontakion. Then the sessional hymn, and a reading of the feast. After the 6th Ode, 3 kontakia and 3 ikoi. Then the kontakion: **"O all-hymned Mother..."**, thrice, and the 1st ikos; and the kontakion: **"To thee, the Champion Leader..."** And we read the Prologue and the Synaxarion. At the 9th Ode, we do not chant "More honorable...", but we sing the refrains of the feast, as indicated on the feast itself; and for the canon of the Akathist, the refrain to the troparia is: "Most Holy Theotokos, save us." Then at the descent[^1484] we chant the refrain and the Heirmos: **"As the living ark of God..."**; and a bow. Exapostilarion of the feast.

At the Praises, stichera of the feast, on 4: *Tone 1, Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чино́въ):* Glory, both now: **"Let the heavens rejoice..."** Great Doxology. Troparion of the feast; litanies; and the Dismissal. And holy oil is given to the brethren according to custom; and the Catechesis of Theodore the Studite; and the First Hour in the narthex. At the First Hour, troparion of the feast. After the Trisagion, kontakion of the feast, and the rest; and the Dismissal. And a procession outside the monastery. At the Divine Liturgy, the Beatitudes of the feast, Odes 3 and 6, on 8; Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, Gospel, and Communion Verse of the feast.

### Notes

[^1422]: The *Markian Chapters* (*Главы́ Ма́рковы*, *Kaphalaia tou Markou*), attributed to Hieromonk Mark of the Monastery of St. Sabas or Bishop Mark of Otranto, provide precise combinatorial rubrics for resolving coincidences between immovable feasts of the Menaion and movable liturgical days of the Triodion and Pentecostarion.
[^1423]: The Feast of the Three Holy Hierarchs (St. Basil the Great, St. Gregory the Theologian, and St. John Chrysostom, January 30) coinciding with the Sundays of the Publican and Pharisee, the Prodigal Son, or Meatfare Sunday. The rubrics refer to the order prescribed for the First and Second Findings of the Precious Head of St. John the Baptist (February 24).
[^1424]: Rubrics for the Forefeast of the Meeting of the Lord (February 1) when it falls on the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee, the Prodigal Son, Meatfare Sunday, or Cheesefare Sunday.
[^1425]: Meatfare Saturday is dedicated universally to the commemoration of all departed Orthodox Christians (Saturday of Souls), while Cheesefare Saturday commemorates all the holy ascetic fathers and mothers who shone forth in the monastic life.
[^1426]: Rubrics for the Great Feast of the Meeting of the Lord (*Hypapante*, February 2) coinciding with the Pre-Lenten Sundays (Publican and Pharisee, Prodigal Son, Meatfare, or Cheesefare).
[^1427]: An important liturgical provision of the Sabbaite and Ruthenian Typika: when the Great Feast of the Meeting of the Lord coincides with a Pre-Lenten Sunday, the praises (*lauds*) stichera of the Triodion are transferred to the Litiya at Vespers on Saturday evening so that none of the appointed Sunday Triodion hymnography is omitted.
[^1428]: Because a Great Feast of the Lord cannot admit funeral commemorations, when the Meeting of the Lord falls on Meatfare Saturday, the entire universal Memorial Service for the Departed is transferred either to the preceding Saturday or to the preceding Thursday of Meatfare Week.
[^1429]: The Afterfeast (*Poprazdnstvo*) of the Meeting of the Lord, encompassing the days following February 2 during which the festal hymns continue to be sung alongside the daily services of the Octoechos, Menaion, and Triodion.
[^1430]: The Apodosis (*Otdanie*, Greek *Apodosis*) of the Meeting of the Lord: according to the Typikon, the apodosis cannot be celebrated on Meatfare Sunday or during Clean Week; its celebration is therefore adjusted according to the day of the week on which the feast falls, concluding at the latest on Cheesefare Sunday.
[^1431]: The First and Second Findings of the Precious Head of the Holy Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John (*Честно́е ѡ҆брѣ́тенїе честны́ѧ Главы̀ І҆ѡа́нна Предте́чи*), celebrated on February 24. These Markian chapters govern the complex liturgical intersections that occur when February 24 coincides with the movable cycle of the Pre-Lenten period and Great Lent, ranging from Meatfare Wednesday through the Tuesday of the First Week of the Great Fast, and subsequent Lenten weekdays and Sundays.
[^1432]: Meatfare Saturday is dedicated universally to the general commemoration of all departed Orthodox Christians (the Universal Memorial Saturday of Souls, *Сꙋббѡ́та мѧсопꙋ́стнаѧ ᲂу҆со́пшихъ*). Because the solemn ancestral requiem office cannot be displaced or easily combined with a joyous saintly festival of Polyeleos rank, the feast of the Forerunner is transferred in its entirety to the preceding Friday.
[^1433]: On Monday and Tuesday of Cheesefare Week, although the strict dietary transition has begun (meat is excluded, but dairy and eggs are permitted throughout), the liturgical order remains festive, permitting the celebration of the Polyeleos, Great Doxology, and the readings of the Forerunner alongside the movable Triodion hymnography.
[^1434]: On Wednesday and Friday of Cheesefare Week, the services already assume an aliturgical, penitential character: prostrations are appointed, the Prayer of St. Ephrem is recited, and the Divine Liturgy is not celebrated. Consequently, the festal celebration of the Forerunner cannot be held on these days and is transferred to Tuesday or Thursday respectively.
[^1435]: On Cheesefare Thursday, because the eve of the day (Wednesday afternoon) contains Lenten elements, Vespers is served after the Ninth Hour and the Prayer of St. Ephrem, with readings and Great Prokeimena. At the monastic refectory, a dispensation for cheese and eggs is granted in honor of the feast.
[^1436]: On Cheesefare Saturday, the Church commemorates all the Holy Ascetic Fathers and Mothers who shone forth in the monastic life (*Па́мѧть всѣ́хъ прпⷣбныхъ ѻ҆тє́цъ въ по́двизѣ провозсїя́вшихъ*). When the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner coincides with this day, the hymnography of the Ascetic Fathers is harmonized with that of the Forerunner.
[^1437]: Pure Monday (*Чи́стый понедѣ́льникъ*), the opening day of Clean Week and of the Great Fast, is kept with the strictest fasting and mournful penitence; no festal celebration is permitted. The entire service of the Forerunner is therefore transferred backward to Cheesefare Sunday.
[^1438]: When the feast falls on any weekday of Clean Week (Tuesday through Friday), the penitential services of the Great Canon of St. Andrew of Crete cannot be displaced; the festal service of the Forerunner is transferred either backward to Cheesefare Sunday or forward to the First Saturday of Lent (commemoration of the Miracle of the Kolyva of the Great Martyr Theodore the Tyro).
[^1439]: The Provincial Synod of Lviv (*Synodus Provincialis Ruthenorum Galiciae*), convened under Metropolitan Sylvester Cardinal Sembratovych in 1891 (Slavonic year `҂аѡч҃а` = 1891 AD), which codified and standardized liturgical practices across the Greek Catholic Metropolitanate of Kyiv-Halych and the Archeparchy of Lviv, resolving local rubrical adaptations, here sanctioning Small Compline instead of Great Compline.
[^1440]: The First Sunday of Great Lent (Sunday of the Triumph of Orthodoxy, *Недѣ́лѧ правосла́вїѧ*). The service of the Forerunner is combined with that of the Triumph of Orthodoxy following the rubrics prescribed for the Third Sunday of Lent (Veneration of the Cross), omitting only the ceremony of the veneration of the Cross.
[^1441]: When the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner falls on Monday of the Second, Third, or Fourth Weeks of Great Lent, Great Vespers with the Great Prokeimenon is served on Sunday evening, followed by Matins with Polyeleos and the celebration of the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts on Monday.
[^1442]: An exceptional rubrical contingency: when, due to grave necessity or the absence of a priest authorized to serve the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts on a major feast with Polyeleos, the Presanctified Liturgy cannot be celebrated, the Typika (with Typika Psalms, Beatitudes, and festal Epistle and Gospel) are chanted in place of the Liturgy.
[^1443]: The *paraecclesiarch* (*параекклисїа́рхъ* / church warden / candle-lighter) tolls two bells (*зна́менаетъ къ два̀ кампа́на*) to signal the monastic assembly's transition from the Typika to Great Lenten Vespers.
[^1444]: St. Tarasios, Patriarch of Constantinople (commemorated February 25), defender of the holy icons at the Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II, 787 AD), whose commemoration immediately follows the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner (February 24).
[^1445]: When the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner occurs on an ordinary fasting weekday of the Second, Third, or Fourth Weeks of Lent, Vespers is united with the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts in the afternoon, or served standalone without the Liturgy if none is celebrated.
[^1446]: On the Second or Third Saturday of Great Lent (traditionally appointed as ancestral memorial Saturdays of Souls), the celebration of the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner displaces the requiem service and is combined with the hymns of the Octoechos and the Triodion.
[^1447]: The Second Sunday of Great Lent (commemoration of St. Gregory Palamas, Archbishop of Thessalonica). The Sunday Resurrectional service is combined with the hymns of the Triodion and the Forerunner, with Polyeleos, Magnification, and the Great Doxology.
[^1448]: The Third Sunday of Great Lent (Sunday of the Veneration of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross, *Недѣ́лѧ крⷭ҇топокло́ннаѧ*). The service harmonizes the Resurrectional hymns, the veneration and hymns of the Precious Cross, and the feast of the Forerunner.
[^1449]: The Holy Forty Martyrs of Sebaste (*Святи́и четыре́десѧть му́чєницы въ Севасті́йстѣмъ є҆́зерѣ му́чєнически сконча́вшїисѧ*), commemorated on March 9. When March 9 falls on the First, Second, Third, or Fourth Saturdays of Great Lent, the order of services follows precisely the rubrics established for the Finding of the Head of the Forerunner.
[^1450]: On Wednesday of the Fourth Week of Lent (Mid-Lent / Wednesday of the Cross), the commemoration of the Forty Martyrs is transferred backward to Tuesday, because Wednesday is dedicated pre-eminently to the Veneration of the Precious Cross.
[^1451]: On Thursday of the Great Canon (Fifth Week), the Forty Martyrs are transferred backward to Tuesday of that week because the entire Thursday office is occupied by the Great Canon of St. Andrew of Crete and the Life of St. Mary of Egypt.
[^1452]: On the Saturday of the Akathist (Fifth Saturday of Great Lent), the Forty Martyrs are transferred to the Fifth Sunday of the Fast, so that the festive Akathist Hymn to the Mother of God may be sung without curtailment.
[^1453]: The Forefeast of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos (March 24) coinciding with weekdays of Great Lent (Monday through Friday), combining the forefestal hymnography with the Lenten Triodion at Vespers and the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts.
[^1454]: Forefeast of the Annunciation coinciding with the Third or Fourth Saturday of Great Lent. A celebratory chime of two small bells (*трезво́нъ въ дво́и ма́лыѧ колокола̀*) is sounded, and the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts is celebrated on Friday evening.
[^1455]: Forefeast of the Annunciation on the Third Sunday of Lent (Veneration of the Cross). The hymnography of the Precious Cross is joined with the Resurrection and Forefeast, taking precedence over the daily Triodion commemorations.
[^1456]: Forefeast of the Annunciation on Mid-Lent Wednesday (Cross-Veneration Wednesday). Prostrations are made at all services, the Cross is venerated at the First Hour, and the Triodion cross hymnography takes prominence alongside the forefestal stichera and canon.
[^1457]: Forefeast of the Annunciation on Thursday of the Fourth Week of Great Lent. The Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts is celebrated on Wednesday evening, followed by the combined forefestal and Triodion office on Thursday morning.
[^1458]: Forefeast of the Annunciation on the Fourth or Fifth Sunday of Great Lent. The customary secondary commemorations of St. John Climacus (Fourth Sunday) or St. Mary of Egypt (Fifth Sunday) are postponed or transferred to another day at the discretion of the ecclesiarch.
[^1459]: Forefeast of the Annunciation coinciding with the Fifth Saturday of Great Lent (Saturday of the Akathist), combining the Akathist hymnography with that of the forefeast.
[^1460]: Forefeast of the Annunciation on Lazarus Saturday. The forefestal service is transferred to Great and Holy Thursday because Lazarus Saturday is dedicated exclusively to the Raising of Lazarus by the Lord.
[^1461]: The comprehensive Markian Chapters governing the Feast of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos (March 25) coinciding with the movable cycles of Great Lent, Lazarus Saturday, Palm Sunday, Passion Week, Holy Pascha, and Bright Week. In the Ruthenian Typikon, the movable date of the Annunciation can range from Thursday of the Third Week of Lent to Wednesday of Bright Week.
[^1462]: The general order of Old Testament readings for the Annunciation when it falls on a Saturday or Sunday of Lent: five festal Old Testament readings are appointed (three at Vespers and two at the Liturgy, or all five at Great Vespers on the eve of Saturday/Sunday).
[^1463]: An ancient monastic procession (*лїті́а*) outside the monastery walls with crosses and banners, conducted at the Third Hour of the day on the Annunciation.
[^1464]: St. John Chrysostom's renowned festive homily for the Annunciation of the Theotokos, beginning *"Again the joy of the Annunciation..."* (*Πάλιν χαρᾶς εὐαγγέλια* / *Па́ки ра́дость Бл҃говѣ́щенїа*).
[^1465]: Psalm 103:4 (LXX) [Psalm 104:4 Masoretic]: "Who makes His angels spirits, and His ministers a flame of fire"; and Hebrews 1:7.
[^1466]: Hebrews 2:11–18 (Pericope 306): "For both He that sanctifies and they who are sanctified are all of one: for which cause He is not ashamed to call them brethren..."
[^1467]: Psalm 71:6, 17 (LXX) [Psalm 72:6, 17 Masoretic]: "He shall come down like rain upon the fleece; and as drops falling upon the earth... His name shall be blessed unto ages."
[^1468]: Luke 1:24–38 (Pericope 3): The Conception of St. Elizabeth and the Annunciation of the Archangel Gabriel to the Most Holy Theotokos.
[^1469]: Psalm 131:13 (LXX) [Psalm 132:13 Masoretic]: "For the Lord hath chosen Zion; He hath desired it for His habitation."
[^1470]: An authoritative canonical and rubrical decree attributed to St. John the Faster (*Ioannes Nesteutes*), Patriarch of Constantinople (582–595 AD), defending the celebration of the full Divine Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom and the dispensation for fish on the Annunciation during Great Lent, and forbidding penitential laments such as *"Let my prayer be set forth"* (*Да испра́вится моли́тва моя̀*) when the full Liturgy is served.
[^1471]: Rubrics for when, due to grave pastoral necessity or lack of a priest, the Divine Liturgy cannot be celebrated on the Annunciation; fish and wine are still permitted at the monastic table once during the day.
[^1472]: The Synaxis of the Archangel Gabriel (*Собо́ръ ст҃а́гѡ а҆рха́ггела Гаврїи́ла*), commemorated on March 26, the day following the Annunciation.
[^1473]: Annunciation coinciding with the Third or Fourth Saturday of Great Lent. On Friday evening, the Presanctified Liturgy is combined with Vespers of the Annunciation, and Great Compline is chanted at the first hour of the night.
[^1474]: Annunciation on the Third Sunday of Great Lent (Sunday of the Veneration of the Precious and Life-Giving Cross). The Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great is celebrated with the Hymn to the Cross (*"Before Thy Cross we bow down, O Master..."*) in place of the Trisagion.
[^1475]: An inviolable rubrical rule of the Byzantine and Ruthenian Typika: whenever the Annunciation falls on any weekday of Great Lent, the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great is celebrated without exception (*быва́етъ Літургі́а вели́кагѡ Васїлїа непосмѣ́ннѡ*), united with Vespers on fasting days, or in its regular order on Saturday and Sunday.
[^1476]: Annunciation on Monday of the Second, Third, or Sixth (Palm) Weeks of Great Lent.
[^1477]: Annunciation on Mid-Lent Wednesday (Cross-Veneration Wednesday). The stichera, Doxastica, and canon of the Cross are postponed to Monday or Friday of that same week, while the Annunciation with the Triodion Mid-Lent triode is celebrated on Wednesday; veneration of the Cross takes place at the First Hour.
[^1478]: Annunciation on the Fourth or Fifth Sunday of Great Lent.
[^1479]: When the Annunciation falls on Wednesday of the Fifth Week of Great Lent, the Great Penitential Canon of St. Andrew of Crete (ordinarily appointed for Thursday Matins of the Fifth Week) is transferred backward to Tuesday Matins of that same week. Because Wednesday is the festal day of the Annunciation, kathisma readings and prostrations are suspended, and the Apodosis of the feast is celebrated on Thursday; thus the Great Canon cannot be sung on Thursday and is anticipated on Tuesday.
[^1480]: When the Annunciation falls on Thursday of the Fifth Week of Great Lent—the appointed day of the Great Canon of St. Andrew of Crete—the Great Canon is likewise transferred to Tuesday of that week and chanted with the Tuesday triode. On Thursday itself, the festal office of the Annunciation is celebrated, united with the apostolic triode from the Triodion.
[^1481]: When the Annunciation falls on Friday of the Fifth Week of Great Lent, the Forefeast of the Annunciation (ordinarily kept on Thursday, March 24) is transferred backward to Wednesday, because Thursday is fully occupied by the celebration of the Great Canon.
[^1482]: The concurrence of the Annunciation (March 25) with the Saturday of the Akathist (the Fifth Saturday of Great Lent). This creates a magnificent liturgical synthesis of the two paramount Byzantine celebrations of the Mother of God, uniting the four strophes of the Akathist Hymn with the festal Annunciation hymnography at Compline, Matins, and the Divine Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom.
[^1483]: The four-ode canon (*Четверопѣ́снецъ*) of the Triodion for the Saturday of the Akathist, composed by St. Joseph the Hymnographer (Odes 6, 7, 8, and 9). Because the Matins of the combined feast is already exceptionally dense with the full Akathist and the Annunciation canon, this triode/tetrode is transferred to Thursday Compline.
[^1484]: The descent (*схо́дъ* / *katabasis* / gathering into the center of the nave): The liturgical rubrics direct both choirs to descend from their respective kliros stalls and assemble together in the middle of the nave to solemnly chant the Ninth Heirmos (*"As the living ark of God..."*) and make a unified prostration.
[^1485]: The concluding colophon of the 1906 Lviv Stauropegion Lenten Triodion: *Коне́цъ и҆ Бг҃ꙋ Сла́ва* ("The End, and to God be the Glory"), printed on page 528 (physical leaf 532) beneath a decorative woodcut tailpiece. This marks the complete conclusion of the entire monument, issued by the Stauropegion Institute of Lviv under the archpastoral blessing of Metropolitan Andrey Sheptytsky of Halych, Bishop Hryhoriy Chekhovych of Peremyshl, and Bishop Hryhoriy Khomyshyn of Stanyslaviv.
