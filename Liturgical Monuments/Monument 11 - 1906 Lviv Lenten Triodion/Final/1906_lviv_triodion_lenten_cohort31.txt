*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Chapter 29:13–23):*[^882]  
Thus says the Lord: This people draws near unto Me with their mouth, and with their lips they honor Me, but their heart is far from Me; but in vain do they worship Me, teaching commandments and doctrines of men. Therefore, behold, I will proceed to remove this people, and I will remove them, and I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and hide the understanding of the prudent. Woe unto them that make counsel deeply, and not by the Lord! Woe unto them that take counsel in secret, and their works shall be in darkness, and they will say: Who has seen us? And who shall understand us or what we do? Shall ye not be counted as the clay of the potter? Shall the thing formed say unto him that formed it: Thou hast not formed me? Or the work to him that made it: Thou hast not made me wisely? Is it not yet a very little while, and Lebanon shall be turned like Mount Carmel, and Carmel shall be counted as a forest? And in that day the deaf shall hear the words of this book, and the eyes of the blind out of darkness and out of gloom shall see. And the poor shall rejoice because of the Lord with gladness, and men that were in despair shall be filled with joy. For the lawless man has failed, and the proud has perished, and all that commit iniquity in malice are cut off; and they that make men to sin in word, and they will lay a stumbling-block for all that reprove in the gates, for they have turned aside the righteous in unjust things. Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the house of Jacob, which He separated from Abraham: Jacob shall not now be put to shame, neither shall Israel now change his face! But when their children see My works, for My sake they shall sanctify My name, and they shall sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and shall fear the God of Israel.

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 78 [LXX 78:9, 1], Tone 5:*[^883]  
Help us, O God our Savior!  
*Verse: O God, the heathen are come into Thine inheritance.*

*And the veneration of the Cross takes place after the dismissal of the Hours, after the final Trisagion, and we sing the stichera, Tone 2: "Come, ye faithful" (see fol. 293):*[^884]

*And the precious Cross is lifted up, and they carry it into the skeuophylakion.*
