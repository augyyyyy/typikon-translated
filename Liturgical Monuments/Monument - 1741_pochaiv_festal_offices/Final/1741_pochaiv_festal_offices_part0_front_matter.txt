# 1741 Pochaiv Lavra Festal Offices / Vozslidovaniia Prazdnykom (Возслідованія праздником, Почаїв: Друкарня Лаври, 1741) <a id="1741-pochaiv-lavra-festal-offices-vozslidovaniia-prazdnykom"></a>

*(Outer front binding cover: 18th-century half-leather binding with marbled paper in brown and ochre tones over pasteboards and brown leather spine).*

*(Flyleaf and blank endpaper opening. In the upper-left corner of the endpaper is a handwritten pencil archival shelfmark and inventory accession number:)*[^1]
MKDU
KM-2206
SF 255/1-2
*(The remainder of the opening is blank 18th-century laid paper, without printed text).*

*(Flyleaf — Archival binding sheet).*

*(Woodcut title cartouche: title page set within an elaborate Late Baroque architectural cartouche, crowned by a cherub and flanked by two angels; below are floral garlands, acanthus foliage, and a pedestal).*

*TITLE PAGE:*

SERVICES FOR THE FEASTS
OF THE MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST,
OF THE COMPASSION OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS,[^2]
AND OF THE BLESSED HIEROMARTYR JOSAPHAT,[^3]
PRESCRIBED BY THE HOLY SYNOD OF ZAMOŚĆ.[^4]
Printed in the Monastery of Pochaiv,
of the Order of Saint Basil the Great.[^5]
In the Year of the Lord 1741.[^6]

*(In the lower margin is a red ink accession stamp: 1976-229 / r, a notation in red pencil: 1741, and an 18th-century manuscript inscription in Polish:)*
Ta to Xięga Voslědowan...
... Soboru Zamayskiego Wydana[^7] *(Flyleaf — Archival binding sheet. In the upper-left corner is a handwritten pencil notation: SF-255/1).*

### Notes

[^1]: Archival shelfmark of the Museum Book Depository of Ukraine (MKDU / *Музейна книга-депозитарій України*): inventory number KM-2206, collection fund SF 255/1-2.
[^2]: *Compassion of the Most Holy Theotokos* (*Состраданіе Пресвятыя Богородицы* / *Compassio Beatae Mariae Virginis*, also known as Our Lady of Sorrows), instituted for the Ruthenian Church by the Synod of Zamość (1720, Title III, §4).
[^3]: Saint Josaphat Kuntsevych, Archbishop of Polotsk, martyred in Vitebsk on November 12, 1623; beatified by Pope Urban VIII in 1643.
[^4]: The Provincial Synod of Zamość (*Synodus Provincialis Zamoscena*), held in 1720 under Metropolitan Leo Kishka, standardized Ruthenian liturgical usage and decreed the universal celebration of these festal offices throughout the Kyivan Metropolia.
[^5]: The printing house of the Holy Dormition Lavra in Pochaiv was transferred to the Basilian Order (OSBM) in the 1730s and became the preeminent publisher of liturgical books in Western Ukraine.
[^6]: Church Slavonic alphanumeric dating `҂аѱма` corresponds to 1741 (`҂а` = 1000, `ѱ` = 700, `м` = 40, `а` = 1).
[^7]: 18th-century Polish manuscript provenance inscription indicating the book's contents and origin: *"This is the Book of Services... issued [by the authority of] the Synod of Zamość..."*
