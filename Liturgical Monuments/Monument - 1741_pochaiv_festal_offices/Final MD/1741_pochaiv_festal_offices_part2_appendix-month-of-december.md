# APPENDIX: MONTH OF DECEMBER

*ON THE TWENTY-FIRST DAY*
*Forefeast of the Nativity of Christ, and the Holy Martyr Juliana.*
*On the same day, our father among the saints Peter, Metropolitan of Kyiv and All Rus.*[^52]

*At Vespers: "Blessed is the man," 1st Stasis. At "Lord, I have cried," 8 Stichera: 3 of the Forefeast, 2 to the holy woman, and 3 to the Saint. Tone 2, Podoben: "With what crowns of praise" (Ки́ми похва́льными):*
*Sticheron 1:*
With what crowns of praise shall we crown the hierarch: the crown of the priesthood, the guide of piety, the luminary and adornment of the Church; the inexhaustible fountain of divine healings, the outpourer of spiritual gifts, the river of many wonders, gladdening the Rus' land with his streams; Peter, our fervent defender and guardian?
*Sticheron 2:*
With what humble lips shall we sing unto the saint: marvelous in wonders, companion of the incorporeal hosts, sanctified from his swaddling-clothes; the unshaken pillar of the Church, the joy of our land, the rule of righteousness, the nourisher of orphans, the protector of the wronged, and the unmercenary physician of the sick?
*Sticheron 3:*
Come, ye assemblies of the faithful, let us clap our hands, praising in song the divine Peter, the consolation of the sorrowful and protector of us all; the boast of the Rus' land and adornment of all the priesthood; truly a river of mercy, zealous for the humility of Christ the God: Who grants unto the world great mercy.
*Glory... Tone 1:*
Having appeared as a manifestation of the Divine, enlightened with brightness, O Peter, of steadfast mind: thou didst flee Egyptian laws as a shadow by grace and truth, divinely-wise father, being sprinkled; whence having become a receptacle of the Most Holy Spirit, thou didst receive the gift of wonders, and didst enrich us thy children: and now standing with the Chief Hierarchs before Christ, pray for our souls.
*Now and ever... Of the Forefeast, Theotokion. Prokeimenon of the day, and three Readings: "The memory of the just," "Though the righteous," and "The righteous man is praised..." (seek at fol. 100)[^53]. At the Litiya: of the Forefeast; or these Stichera to the holy martyr are sung at Small Vespers, if there be a vigil.*

*AT THE APOSTICHA, Stichera, Tone 2, Podoben: "O House of Ephratha" (До́ме Є҆vфра́ѳовъ):*
*Sticheron 1:*
A spiritual house of the outpouring of grace Peter hath appeared; coming together now, let us sing unto him.
*Stichos: Thy priests shall clothe themselves with righteousness, and Thy venerable ones shall rejoice (Ps. 131:9).*
*Sticheron 2:*
A holy meadow, O Peter our boast, glory of priests, rule of monastics, establishment of the Church.
*Stichos: The venerable ones shall rejoice in glory (Ps. 149:5).*
*Sticheron 3:*
Come now, our song of thy wonders, O father [text breaks off at quire end] *(Outer rear binding cover: 18th-century half-leather binding with marbled paper in brown, ochre, black, and coral-red tones over pasteboards and brown leather spine).*

### Notes

[^52]: St. Peter, Metropolitan of Kyiv and All Rus (c. 1260–1326), born in Volhynia, who transferred the metropolitan see from Vladimir to Moscow in 1325. This single leaf (bearing the printed folio number `рѕ҃і` = 116) represents an independent supplementary liturgical printing bound into the codex by the Pochaiv Lavra binder.
[^53]: Internal folio cross-reference in the original printed book: `И҆щѝ Ли́стъ, ст` (*seek at fol. 200 / fol. 100*), directing the reader to the common readings for Hierarchs in the complete Menaion / Trebnik corpus.
