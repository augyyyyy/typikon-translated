*Ikos:*
Who can praise Thy saints one by one, O Blessed One! If I count them, they are multiplied more than the sand; but do Thou, O Master Christ, Who countest the multitude of the stars and callest them all by their names, receive their supplications for us, and grant me Thy compassions upon Thy people: for we know that they have great boldness toward Thy majesty, and intercede with Thee as ever pray-ers for our souls.
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
*Thou Who didst dew the children in the furnace, and didst preserve the Virgin uncorrupt after childbirth: blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.*
*Troparia:*
Thou Who hast counted me worthy to offer a childlike song, O God-adorned choir of Pechersk fathers, through your instruction may I sing passionlessly with Christ-loving people: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Of the Near Caves.*
Pimen the Much-Ailing, who not only passed through this life in illness with thanksgiving, but didst also grant holy rest unto others: relieve me of sickness, that I may sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
Pure gold and silver tried sevenfold with gifts, O righteous favorite, thou wast a doer of the righteous, and giving all into the working of noetic virtues, thou didst pass over: by which having purified thy soul, thou didst present it unto God like gold, and singest: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
O glorious Nestor, who didst reveal unto us by thy writing the times and years of the most marvelous Church, the lives and virtues of the first fathers, and wast a zealous emulator of them in deed: together with them, bless thee now, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
Eustratius, beauty of martyrs, thou didst appear as a second Elijah the charioteer: but he was taken as unto heaven, whereas thou didst ascend into the very heavenly gates in a fiery chariot, a voice calling thee from the majesty of glory, where with a voice of rejoicing thou singest: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
Glorious in gifted wonders, O blessed fathers, Helladius and Luke the steward, as having been good workers of the blessed commandments, now have ye received retribution from the torrent of delight, and joyfully sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
*Glory:*
The Spirit having come, bears witness with our spirit, O God-bearing fathers, that ye are children of God and heirs of Christ, Whom ye served, and with Whom ye are glorified, singing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
The Holy Spirit came upon thee, and the power of the Most High having overshadowed thee, showed thee to be a pure Mother both in and after childbirth: through whom being delivered from alien bondage, all generations bless thee, for the blessed God of our fathers hath blessed thee.
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
*Hymn the Lord Who was glorified on the holy mountain and revealed the mystery of the Ever-Virgin in the bush by fire unto Moses, and exalt Him above all unto all ages.*
*Troparia:*
The God-bearing Fathers, the great luminaries whom Thou didst cause to shine upon the holy Pechersk Mount, whence they extended their gracious rays and illumined all the Russian land with wonders: therefore do we hymn the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto the ages.
