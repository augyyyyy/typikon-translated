According to Discourse 25 of the Kyiv-Pechersk Paterikon, St. Gregory was gifted with discernment and prophecy; when Prince Rostislav Vsevolodovich was marching on an expedition against the Polovtsi in 1093, his servants mocked the elder, who prophesied that the prince and his warriors would drown in the Stuhna River. Enraged, Rostislav had Gregory bound hand and foot with a heavy stone tied around his neck and cast into the Dnipro River. The prophecy was fulfilled shortly thereafter at the Battle of the Stuhna.
