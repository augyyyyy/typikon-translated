# MONTH OF MAY

*On the 10th Day: Service to our Father among the Saints, Simon, Bishop of Vladimir and Suzdal.*[^200]

*At Vespers, "Blessed is the man," the first antiphon.*
*On "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 5. Podoben: "Rejoice, life-bearing Cross" (Ра́дꙋйсѧ живоно́сный):*[^201]
Rejoice, adornment of the Church, beauty of hierarchs, most sacred father, good shepherd: who having loved Jesus the Chief Shepherd from thy youth, with thy whole soul didst ever serve Him; us who with passions *Service to the Hierarch*

...us who are enslaved by passions, who from the soul fall down before thee, restore unto the former freedom by thy prayers.

Rejoice, most fervent lover of Christ thy Lord, O worthily-marvelous Simon: who by the warmth of spiritual grace being exceedingly kindled into the love of God, didst despise all the fair things of this world, and didst captivate thy mind into the obedience of Christ; therefore now, dwelling as free in free habitations, free our minds that are captivated by the assaults of the enemy, O blessed father, and deliver us from them through thy mediation with God, as we pray and honor thy memory, O father.

Them that were held fast by the turmoils of this life, and were going astray upon the paths of passions, O hierarch Simon, by wise words, instructing and guiding them by thy wise teaching, thou didst lead unto the path of salvation, having become their guide unto the heavenly height; where now dwelling thyself with the angels, direct our souls into life and salvation by thine invisible governance.

*Glory: Tone 6:*
Thy sweet tongue for teaching even now stirs up the hearts of the slothful, and calls them unto the doing of the Lord's commandments; and thy wilderness flute proclaims God-revealed words, whereby, as by a ladder, they that hear are lifted up and brought unto God; therefore, O Simon, venerable father, as having boldness toward Christ God, cease not praying that we may be enlightened with the light of understanding and good deeds, who lovingly honor thy holy memory.

*Both now: Theotokion.*
*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. Three readings for a hierarch from the Discourses of Venerable Ephraim the Syrian (see fol. 47v).*[^202]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "All-praised martyrs" (Прехва́льнїи мч҃нцы):*[^203]
As a rational trumpet of theology and an honorable mirror of virtues, as an initiate of grace, having come together today with hymns we praise thee, O Simon most sacred, and we pray thee: pray thou unto Christ God to grant our souls peace and great mercy.

*Verse:* Thy priests shall clothe themselves with righteousness, and Thy venerable ones shall rejoice.[^204]

*To Simon, Bishop of Suzdal*

Having received illumination from the noetic Light, O blessed one, thou wast a radiant beacon in the midst of the ascetic choir; from whence by election from on high being entrusted with the episcopal throne, thou wast a good shepherd of the rational sheep of Christ God; before Whom now standing, pray thou to grant our souls peace and great mercy.

*Verse:* The mouth of the righteous shall meditate on wisdom, and his tongue shall speak of judgment.[^205]
By thy many labors, O consecrated and blessed hierarch, thou hast ascended unto the unwaning Light, and dwellest rejoicing with thy first founders;[^206] with whom also pray for us, that we may be vouchsafed to abide in eternal life with thee and with all the saints.

*Glory: Tone 6:*
The image of thy radiant life, O most sacred Simon, directed many unto virtues, and the river of thy saving teaching stirred up unto vigilance souls held fast by despondency, and raised the slothful unto the praise of God; therefore honoring thy all-honorable memory, we fall down and pray, that through thy guidance we may direct our life in peace, and receive great mercy from Christ God.

*Both now: Theotokion: Save from perils thy servants, O all-holy Mother of God:*

*Troparion, Tone 4:*
O true writer of the lives of the venerable fathers of the Caves, all-blessed hierarch of Christ Simon: with them diligently pray unto Christ God to direct our life in peace, and to inscribe us in the books of life, saving our souls.[^207]
*Glory, both now: Theotokion.*

*At Matins, on "God is the Lord," Troparion to the Hierarch, twice. Glory, both now: Theotokion.*
*After Kathisma 1, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast appeared" (Ꙗ҆ви́лсѧ є҆сѝ):*[^208]
As with a gold-woven robe, by thy orthodox teaching the Church of Vladimir is clothed, and the ranks of monastics are adorned by thy guidance; while laboring in the one priesthood, they ever glorify thee, O hierarch Simon.
*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
O most pure Virgin, Mother of God: despise not us who run unto thy goodness and seek thy speedy defense, but incline unto supplication, help in our needs, and deliver our souls from perils.

*After Kathisma 2, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3. Podoben: "The beauty of virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства):*[^209]
A noetic treasure from our first-founding fathers *Service to the Hierarch*

...having received, O venerable father Simon, all-wise hierarch, thou didst multiply it into threefold talents, and left it abundantly unto them that came after thee; and thyself as a good servant didst enter into the joy of thy Lord; of which also vouchsafe us who honor thy memory with praises, through thy prayers.[^210]

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
The talents entrusted unto us by God we have ruined through sloth, and we are ashamed to appear empty-handed before our Lord: unto thee do we flee, O Lady; grant at least one mite from the unsearchable treasury of thy mercy, that we be not put to shame in the day of the dread examination, and may unceasingly magnify thee with praises.

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5. Podoben: "Co-unoriginate Word" (Собеꙁнача́лное Сло́во):*[^211]
The wise hierarch and unswerving shepherd, faithful writer of the lives of the saints and their pious emulator, Simon most sacred, come, ye people, let us bless in hymns: for he now stands on high before the throne of God amid the unceasing vision of the radiant ones, and unceasingly prays for them that lovingly sing his memory and glorify Christ God.

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
O sacred Mother of the Word co-unoriginate with the Father, speedy helper of sinners and intercessor for them that are in perils: stretch forth thy most pure hands, wherewith thou didst embrace and carry in time as an infant Him Who is timeless, and entreat His goodness for us who are much-sinning, that we may be delivered from impending needs and sorrows, that we may magnify thy blameless childbirth.

*Gradual Hymns, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 3.*
*Prokeimenon:* My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart understanding.[^212]
*Verse:* Thy priests shall clothe themselves with righteousness, and Thy venerable ones shall rejoice.
*Let every breath praise the Lord.*
*The Gospel from John, pericope 36.*[^213]
*After Psalm 50, Sticheron, Tone 6:*
O venerable father, thou wast an example unto them that live the monastic life: for who beheld thy boundless humility and was not corrected in his life? Or who, having seen thy murmuring-free obedience, did not marvel at thy patient endurance? Thou didst teach all by thyself, and even after thy departure thou ceasest not to guide and instruct thy sheep with diligent admonition; therefore as our shepherd and teacher, and companion in character with the venerable, honoring thy memory, we bless thee.

*To Simon, Bishop of Suzdal*

*Canon of the Mother of God, with the Heirmos to 6, and of the Hierarch to 6.*
*Canon to the Hierarch, Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^214]
The wonderworking rod of Moses, striking the sea crosswise and dividing it, submerged the chariot-mounted Pharaoh; while it saved fugitive Israel fleeing on foot, as they sang a song unto God.

*Troparia:*
Direct me, I pray, O Christ, as I strive to sail across the sea of the virtues of Thy hierarch in order to hymn them with praises; and having calmed my mind from the tempestuous thoughts of this life, save me as I sing a song unto Thee my God.

Rude and senseless though I am, yet drawn by love toward thee, I make bold to praise thy memory, O Simon, hierarch of Christ; do thou assist me by thy prayers, and be my guide as I now send up song concerning thee unto my God.

Who shall not marvel, reading thy life in hymns and seeing thy zeal to emulate the first Caves fathers? Therefore I also, marveling, humbly send up a song concerning thee unto my God.

*Glory:*
Having received the angelic habit in the blessed Caves Lavra, thou didst also embrace the angelic life, following Anthony the Great and Theodosius the marvelous; with whom now living thou rejoicest, unceasingly singing hymnody unto God.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
I have changed my habit, yet I have not changed my deeds, O Mother of God; by the grace of Him Who was born of thee and saved the human race, according to thy grace save me as together concerning thee I send up my song unto God.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*[^215]
O Lord, Artificer of the celestial vault and Builder of the Church: do Thou confirm me in Thy love, O summit of desires, confirmation of the faithful, O only Lover of mankind.

*Troparia:*
Thy virtues shone forth like the stars of heaven, O father, with whose rays enlighten us also who walk in the darkness of passions, that concerning thee we may sing unto the Creator of the heavenly vault: Glory to Thee, O only Lover of mankind.

Wishing to be a partaker of the unwaning Light, thou didst depart from the captives of the world, and having received the angelic habit, didst angelically offer praise unto God, crying: Glory to Thee, O only Lover of mankind.

Thou didst come unto the great fathers: Anthony and Theodosius of the Caves *Service to the Hierarch*

...thou didst appear, and like a radiant star didst shine forth in this life, O venerable one, through humility and purity; marveling at which, we sing: Glory to Thee, O only Lover of mankind.

*Glory:*
Thy obedience, O blessed one, was like a mirror in the midst of the assembly of the brethren, and through this thou didst especially draw near unto Jesus Who became obedient even unto death;[^216] with Whom now living in the midst of heavenly citizens, thou singest: Glory to Thee, O only Lover of mankind.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
I hear the commandments of my Lord, yet I disobey them; but do thou, O pure Virgin who didst say: Behold the handmaid of the Lord,[^217] make me a servant of obedience, that I may glorify thee, the only Mother that loves mankind.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*[^218]
Having found the ladder of virtues, thou didst ascend the mountain of dispassion, and seeing the deceit of this world, thou didst renounce the voluptuous kingdom which passes away in the twinkling of an eye, and bowedst not before the world-ruler, but before the only King of ages; therefore with songs we honor thee, O Simon, most blessed father.

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Having thee as the hope of salvation and helper in needs, O Virgin Bride of God, we fear not the assaults of enemies, but in thee we take courage: for thou art our strength and confirmation.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^219]
Thou art my strength, O Lord, Thou art my power, Thou art my God, Thou art my rejoicing, Who didst not leave the bosom of the Father, yet visitedst our poverty: therefore with the Prophet Habakkuk I cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparia:*
By vigilance and action drawing near unto the noetic Light, O venerable one, thou didst show thyself radiant in life, through which many being guided glorified God, saying: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind!

The report of thy deeds went forth into diverse lands, O father, and many desired to have thee as leader and instructor, that being guided by thee, they might glorify the Heavenly Father, Who glorified thee with such grace.

More than others the Church of Vladimir eagerly called thee, O blessed one, that by thy coming thou mightest gladden her that was troubled, and shepherd her with the rod of thy governance.

*Glory:*
The citizens of Suzdal also set thee forth, forming choirs of spiritual songs, and rejoicing they elevate thee to the archiepiscopal throne; having ascended which, thou wast a good shepherd unto them.

*To Simon, Bishop of Suzdal*

*Both now: Theotokion:*
O sacred Mother of the Good Shepherd: gather thy rational sheep scattered by the noetic wolf, and do thou thyself shepherd us close at hand, that no longer may the wolf of hell dare to approach thy flock.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^220]
Why hast Thou cast me away from Thy presence, O unwaning Light, and an alien darkness has covered me, the wretched one? But turn me back, and direct my ways unto the light of Thy commandments, I pray.

*Troparia:*
Unto thee, O Simon, even as unto that beloved disciple Simon,[^221] Jesus the Chief of shepherds entrusted the pasturing of sheep, which having received, thou didst well guide unto the heavenly height; whereto also direct my ways by thy prayers.

From youth thou didst love the Master of all, from Whom in return for love thou didst receive to shepherd the sheep redeemed by His precious blood; of this same Good One I also am a sheep: look upon me, O hierarch Simon, and direct my ways, I pray thee.

The pastoral pipe of thy teachings, O father, gladdened the rational sheep, so that no longer did they prefer corruptible food, feeding on spiritual pasture; nourish me who hunger with thy teaching, and direct my ways, I pray thee.

*Glory:*
With the rod of governance thou didst well guide the flock entrusted unto thee, setting thyself as an example unto them, which written instruction I also read, yet am not able to emulate it; do thou, O good shepherd, that I may become an imitator thereof, direct my ways by thy prayers.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
O nature more sacred than all earth-born through thy most holy childbirth, O Virgin Mary: lift up our mind crawling among earthly trifles unto higher things, and by thy prayers direct our paths to seek them.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^222]
Cleanse me, O Savior, for many are mine iniquities, and lead me up from the depth of evils, I pray: for unto Thee have I cried, and do Thou hear me, O God of my salvation!

*Troparia:*
Sanctified by the grace of the Holy Spirit, as a faithful sacred initiate of mysteries, thou didst serve the throne of God on earth in holiness and righteousness, O Simon; therefore now living with the faithful servants in the joy of thy Lord, pray, and be an intercessor with God for my salvation.

Hearing the call, O hierarch, thou wast vouchsafed as one who took upon himself the promised labors: for attending unto pastoral deeds *Service to the Hierarch*

...equal unto the angels, thou didst never abandon monastic ascetic labors; therefore now living in the honor of hierarchs, pray for me that I may not be deprived of my salvation.

Ever remembering the labors of the venerable Caves fathers, O holy one, thou didst learn to emulate them, showing thyself of like character and like-minded with them; thou didst pass from the lesser to the better, that thou mightest be bound with a double crown.

*Glory:*
Through the doing of the Lord's commandments thou didst mortify thy flesh, yet by the grace of God thou didst quicken thy soul, and through knowledge having found the spiritual ladder, thou didst ascend by noetic elevation unto God; before Whom standing, pray for them that sing of thee, O blessed Simon.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Action through sloth and knowledge through the darkening of passions have I ruined, and possessing neither, I have fled from the bond of good ordering; yet unto thee do I flee, O Mother of God: as thou art strong and all-powerful, correct both, and be the helper of my salvation.

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*[^223]
Though thou wast worthily vested with the vesture of hierarchs, yet thou wast not exalted thereby, but desiredst rather as a hierarch to submit before the brethren of the Caves Lavra, O humble-minded Simon; therefore, not as possessions left outside, but as a treasure within the holy cave wast thou vouchsafed to be laid, and now standing in heaven before the Lord, pray for us that honor Thee.[^224]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^225]
The fire was put to shame by God's condescension in Babylon of old: therefore the youths in the furnace with joyful step, dancing as in a meadow, sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*
The grace of God worked in thee, O hierarch: for streams of holy teachings ever went forth from thy mouth, watering the hearts of the slothful, who budding forth fruits of virtues by thy sprinkling cried aloud: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

Thou didst sow the seed of the word of God in the garden of thy flock, planting the lily of purity, prayer like the incense of Lebanon, mercy toward neighbors like an olive tree, and thoughts ever concerning God like a cypress: which things having done thyself, thou didst instruct others also to do.

Having well inscribed the virtues of the Caves fathers upon the tables of thy heart, thou didst not hide them under the bushel of worldly turmoil, O father, but moved by zeal, didst turn thy whole mind to write down their lives for the profit of others; which we now reading *To Simon, Bishop of Suzdal*

...cry aloud: Blessed is the God of our fathers![^226]

*Glory:*
Like a wise bee, having gathered noetic flowers, the lives of those near to thee, thou didst give them unto us that live after thee like a sweet honeycomb, wherewith sweetening our souls, with thee, O Simon, we sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Having tasted of the honeycomb of passions, I still have lips filled with bitterness: do thou, O Lady who didst bear the most sweet Jesus, sweeten my soul by thy grace, that I may sing: Rejoice, dweller of holy sweetness and blessing of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^227]
Sevenfold did the Chaldean tyrant frantically kindle the furnace for the pious; but having seen them saved by a greater power, he cried unto the Creator and Deliverer: Ye youths, bless; ye priests, hymn; ye people, supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
The head of the seven-headed serpent thou didst crush by the sevenfold spiritual gifts, O father, and wounded, he trembles even until now at thy name; which remembering on this present day, concerning thee we cry: Ye people, supremely exalt Christ unto all ages!

Through love toward the Most Holy Mother of God and the Caves fathers, thou seemedst to be separated from them in body only, yet in spirit thou didst ever abide with them and emulate their life; therefore visit us in thy love, and pray that we may supremely exalt Christ unto all ages.

Truly, even as Simon was a most fervent lover of Christ Himself, so thou also, O hierarch Simon, didst show thyself a zealous lover toward the Mother of God and her monastery; and with those whom thou didst love, with them thou now dwellest unto ages.

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.*
Accounting thy glory and honor not equal to the dust of the Caves monastery, thou didst desire, O father, that it were better for thee to be cast out for one day as dead in the house of the Mother of God, than to be glorious upon a throne for a thousand years, by reason of thy marvelous humility; therefore we bless thee unto all ages.[^228]

*Both now: Theotokion:*
By thy help, O pure one, I came unto cohabitation with the Caves fathers; yet having fallen through the sight of laxity I turned aside, and the enemy draws me back again into the world unto former things. O Mother of God, shame me into abiding here and taking heed to my salvation, lest falling away I perish unto ages.

*Service to the Hierarch*

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
Heaven was dismayed at this, and the ends of the earth were amazed, that God appeared in the flesh unto men, and thy womb became more spacious than the heavens: therefore thee, O Mother of God, do the ranks of angels and men magnify.

*Troparia:*
Emulating that life of thine, O most blessed Simon, we glorify thy struggles, and we honor thy memory, which now with the angels we magnify.

Leaving lower things, thou didst depart unto the highest; and thy body according to thy desire was brought from thy throne into the cave, and shows how great love thou hadst toward this place, wherein we all magnify thee.[^229]

The cave rejoices holding thy relics, O hierarch, and convenes assemblies of monks and feast-lovers, that with songs of praise all may magnify thy memory on this present day.

*Glory:*
Thou didst depart thyself, O honorable one, yet thy memory abides with us without departing: as thy children burning with love toward thee, we ever remember and magnify thee.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Abide with us without departing, O most pure Virgin, with our fellow citizens in memory of thy eternal servant; strengthen the people, drive away sloth, and enlighten our understanding, that unitedly we all may magnify thee and thy Son.

*Exapostilarion:*
Let us praise the God-wise father who drank in all-wise words: for his honorable teachings are like the morning dew, sweet honeycomb, bread of life, heavenly nectar, a wellspring of living water, drinking of which one is filled with spiritual sweetness, and glorifies thy memory, O most blessed Simon.[^230]

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Having brought forth, O Virgin, the wellspring of living water higher than the heavens, which cannot be comprehended: give drink unto me who thirst, O thy mother, that refreshed I may glorify thee.

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*
Thou wast an initiate of sacred mysteries, O blessed one, while living in the body in this world; and translated unto a better life, thou wast vouchsafed the very Vision of God, and clearly seest the Inaccessible Light: of which do thou make us also worthy to behold, by thy fervent prayers unto God.[^231]

They that attend unto thy words and cleave unto thy admonition *To Simon, Bishop of Suzdal*

...unto thy admonition, O venerable Simon, perceive profit in their life, and appear filled with correction; they receive spiritual deeds and draw grace through thy teaching: wherewith enrich us also who on this present day honor thy memory with praises.

A divine instrument of the Holy Spirit, and a trumpet proclaiming spiritual corrections, the primate of Suzdal, Simon of sacred utterance, let us the people today honor with songs, bowing down before the reliquary of his relics: for as a hierarch and fervent intercessor he stands before the throne of the Master, praying that we may be saved.

*Glory: Tone 6:*
At the radiance of thy life and thy episcopal direction, angels and men marveled together: for in election thou wast shown from on high to be an initiate of sacred mysteries, and wast an excellent mirror unto ascetics; therefore as a worthy servant of God, O Simon, most blessed hierarch, with love in songs we magnify thee.

*Both now: Theotokion: O Mother of God, thou art the true vine:*

*Great Doxology, and dismissal.*
*At the Liturgy, the common service to a Hierarch.*
