# MONTH OF OCTOBER: ON THE 5TH DAY

*Memory of Our Venerable Fathers Damian the Presbyter, Matthew, and Jeremiah the Clairvoyant:*
*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried": Stichera, on 6. Tone 4. Podoben: "As One Valiant" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*
Held fast by spiritual and bodily ailments, with the oil of thy mercy, as having a sacred anointing from on high, O Damian our companion, speedily heal me who cry unto thee from the heart; and through warm prayers unto God, grant humility unto my mind, and cleanse the festering wounds of my soul, that with clean *Service to the Venerable Fathers of the Caves.*
...clean lips I may offer praise unto God Who is glorified in His saints.
Having illumined thy soul by the grace of the Spirit received, O all-praised Jeremiah, thou didst receive a gift from God, to announce things future as though present; and likening thyself unto the marvelous prophet, unerringly didst thou foretell all things, and didst foresee the secrets of the human heart: by thine illumination enlighten our hearts also, and ask from Christ God great mercy!
Matthew, preacher of the Word of God, thou didst follow Him, O marvelous one; for as that Evangelist boldly reproved the unrepentant people, so also didst thou, O blessed one, put to shame the craftily woven snares of the subterranean cunning Ethiopians; and now we pray thee, O father, noetically cleanse our minds, drive away the demonic legions, and deliver our souls from afflictions!
*Glory: Tone 2.*
Come, O ye people, let us honor with praises the three most blessed fathers, intercessors before the Trinity: Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew; and let us offer doxology unto God Who was in their minds, Who gave His favorites as a help unto the Russian land and as intercessors for our souls.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
*At the Aposticha, all from the Octoechos.*
*Glory: Tone 6.*
Bright and radiant hath shone forth today your memory, O our blessed fathers; for brightly do ye shine with the rays of wonders upon those who come unto you and ask of you warm protection; therefore do we also fall down before your relics, eagerly kissing them, and we glorify God Who gave you such great gifts, with which enrich us also, and entreat great mercy from Christ!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
*Troparion, Tone 4:*
Having illumined your hearts with the light of the commandments of Christ, ye drove away the passionate gloom; therefore did ye three-numbered fathers, Damian, Jeremiah with Matthew, become a dwelling of the Trinity, from Whom having received grace, ye heal the infirm, announce things to come, and being partakers with the angels, pray unto Christ God to grant us communion with the saints!
*CANON*
*Tone 2. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*Unto God the Savior, Who guided the people in the sea with dry feet, and drowned Pharaoh with all his host, unto Him alone let us sing: for He hath been glorified.*
*Troparia:*
Unto God the Savior let us offer praise, glorifying His favorite, all-blessed Damian, the wise physician of the infirm; and let us cry aloud unto Him Who gave such gifts: for He hath been glorified!

*To Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew.*
With unceasing zeal didst thou imitate the angel-like life of thy teacher, instructed by whom thou wast adorned with virtue, abiding unceasingly in praise throughout all nights without sleep, and crying unto God: for He hath been glorified!
Standing now with the rest before God the Savior, as having boldness, pray through grace to establish the hearts of those who bless thee with faith, O divinely inspired Jeremiah, and who cry unto the Savior: for He hath been glorified!
*Glory:*
The all-seeing eye of God, beholding thy pure heart, O blessed Matthew, gave thee the gift to know the secrets of men and to reveal them for the profit of all, that guided by thee we may sing unto God: for He hath been glorified!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O noetic Sun, holy and consecrated Virgin, illumine our minds darkened by passions and pleasures, that enlightened by thee, we may sing unto God: for He hath been glorified!
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*By the power of Thy Cross, O Christ, establish my mind, that I may hymn and glorify Thy saving Ascension.*
*Troparia:*
Strengthened by the power of God, thou wast gentle and silent, O blessed one, ever with warm prayers unto God, and exercising thyself in diligent reading of the Holy Scriptures, and thereby being established in the Lord.
The word of God was thy food and drink, with which thou didst feed thy soul, O venerable one; while thy body was fed only with bread and water, and upon this didst thou nourish thyself until the day of thy death, subduing unto the spirit all rising passions.
Having completed thy years in ripe old age, O Jeremiah, thou didst receive the gift to foresee the secrets of the heart, and in private didst reprove the cunning thoughts of men, and didst instruct them to beware of the counsel of the devil.
*Glory:*
Having come unto the first-founded Pechersk fathers, thou didst dwell with them, and living as a monk didst cut off all bodily lusts; therefore didst thou receive a gift from God, openly to behold the faces of the subterranean Ethiopians, O marvelous Matthew!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
The cunning demons who transform themselves into manifold forms and work in our members, do thou, O Theotokos, terrify and drive away by the visitation of thy countenance, that without disturbance we may sing of thy saving deliverance!

*Service to the Venerable Fathers of the Caves.*
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
As having received twofold grace from God, Thou healest twofold passions: spiritual ones through diligent prayers, and bodily ones through anointing with holy oil; do Thou heal us who suffer from both ailments, and drive away the passions that oppress us, and pray unto Christ God that those who honor thy holy dormition may obtain everlasting life.
*Glory: Both now... Theotokion:*
All-spotless Bride, spacious dwelling of the Word of God: cleanse me who am a foul dwelling of passions, and show me forth as a radiant habitation of virtues, drive away the darkness of iniquities, and by thy prayers count me worthy to partake of the Unwaning Light, that I may sing of thine ineffable mercy!
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
*I heard the report of the power of the Cross, that Paradise was opened thereby, and I cried aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!*
*Troparia:*
Thou didst appear as a sweet-smelling cluster of the vineyard of the Mother of God, watered by the dew of the teachings of the venerable fathers, and didst distil the wine of healings unto all who flee unto thee with faith, O Damian, servant of God.
Walled about with the weapon of faith and the flame of love, O blessed one, thou didst drive away the enemies who harm men with diseases; for thy diligent prayer, passing through the clouds, drove away sicknesses, O Damian worthy of wonder.
As a foreseer of human thoughts, thou didst observe those turning their backs unto the enemies and wishing to depart from this holy enclosure, and with words mixed with love thou didst instruct them, O Jeremiah, to stand firmly in the contest, and to be unshakable against the enemies.
*Glory:*
With hidden eyes, O Matthew, thou didst once behold in church a demon walking about in the likeness of a warrior, and casting sticky flowers upon the brothers' cloaks, so that, darkened with sleep, they would depart before the blessing; therefore didst thou instruct them to depart without laziness and to wall themselves about with the weapon of the Cross.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Encompassed by demonic legions and assailed by flattering passions, strengthen me, O Mother of God, and grant me grace to conquer their deceptive wiles, that I may flee beneath thy pure help!
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
*Rising early, we cry unto Thee, O Lord: Save us! For Thou art our God, and beside Thee we know no other.*
*Troparia:*
Seeing those who came in sickness, thy blessed instructor Theodosius commanded thee to offer prayer; and thou, being humble and obedient *To Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew.*
...obedient, didst count thyself unworthy of the gift of healing; yet fulfilling the command of thy father, with prayer and oil thou didst bestow health upon the sick.
As a good athlete, thou didst walk well along the path of life, confirming thyself through monastic struggles; and when thou didst attain unto thy end, having completed what was lacking through sickness, purer than gold thou didst depart unto painless repose.
Through the clairvoyance given unto thee by God, thou didst see wondrously, O blessed one; without hesitation thou didst declare unto everyone, and thy word was fulfilled in truth; therefore, as the glory of prophets and composer of hymns, we worthily bless thee, O Jeremiah.
*Glory:*
Thou didst never go forth from the church before the conclusion of the singing, but wast always the last, having an insatiable desire to abide in the doxology of God; therefore God glorified thee before angels and men, O God-bearing Matthew!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Knowing thee to be the holy mountain of God and the house of glory of the Most High, O pure Virgin, falling down in humility I pray: lead me up unto the height of a virtuous life, and make me a pure temple for the reception of divine gifts!
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
*Cleanse me, for the abyss of my sins seethes within me; yet I cried aloud unto Thee, O Lover of mankind, and Thy right hand saved me, O Lord.*
*Troparia:*
Having lived upon earth along the right paths, at thine end thou didst mingle thy prayer unto the Crowner with tears, asking of His goodness that He deprive thee not of the Heavenly Kingdom and the fellowship of thy father, the venerable Theodosius.
Desiring not to be deprived of partaking of the hymn-singing with the unapproachable light, with humility thou didst send up prayers unto Christ, and didst obtain what thou didst ask; for the wise Creator informed thee by marvelous incorruption, so that with good hope thou mightest depart to rest in the repose of the righteous.
In deep old age thy youth was spiritually renewed, O venerable Jeremiah; for having left behind the old bodily vesture, thou didst depart in soul into a new habitation, unto the ancient prophets, to behold the glory of God.
*Glory:*
Sitting once beneath the church bell, thou didst behold a great throng of gloomy demons, and having questioned them, didst learn that they were going unto a certain brother; therefore thou thyself didst lovingly instruct many of the brethren how to beware of them: teach us also, therefore, O blessed one, that they may not enter into *Service to the Venerable Fathers of the Caves.*
...into the doors of our hearts!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Deliver me, O Sovereign Lady, from the assaults of demonic passions, lest those cunning throngs enter into the house of my soul; bar the noetic doors of my heart with fear, and grant me to watch and take heed unto my salvation!
*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
As the ancient prophets, having received the grace of prophecy, unerringly foresaw by the Spirit and foretold things about to come to pass, so also in our life did ye foretell the evils coming upon us because of our sins; and now, that evils may not overtake us, entreat from Christ God the amendment of our deeds and great mercy!
*Ikos:*
Having imitated your namesakes, and having lived angelically upon earth, ye were counted worthy of great gifts, O Jeremiah and Matthew; for foretelling things to come, ye brought many unto correction, put all the demons to shame, and astonished men; and now, living after this temporal life in the endless one with the choirs of angels, ask from Christ God the amendment of our deeds and great mercy!
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
*Thou Who didst save the hymn-singing children in the fiery furnace: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers.*
*Troparia:*
An angel of God stood before thee, O master, father Damian, in the likeness of the venerable Theodosius, and falling upon thy breast kissed thee lovingly, as one beloved of God, and together with angels, and thou didst sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
Seeking an apparition, behold the wonder: "He unto Whom they prayed to the Lord hath sent me to inform thee that according to thy petition it shall be unto thee, and thou shalt be settled in the Kingdom of God with the saints." Having heard these words, joyfully didst thou cry aloud, O Damian: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
What is sweeter, and what is greater, than to live with the saints unto the ages? Where Christ lives in the Unwaning Light, there also is a place of repose prepared for thee, O Damian; pray that we also may obtain it who sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
Numbered in the choir of the saints, in honor with the prophets, in kinship with the angels, thou wast taught of God, O Jeremiah; praying unto Whom thou standest before Him, that we also may honor Him who sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
*Glory:*
Having love for true salvation, O Matthew, thou didst reveal all hidden things for the profit of all, and didst instruct those coming unto thy cell to weep over their sins *To Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew.*
...sins, and to sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Delivered through thee from all demonic assaults, O most pure one, unceasingly unto Him Who was born of thee do we cry: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
*Him Who was begotten of the Father before the ages as Son and God, and in these latter times was incarnate of the Virgin Mother: praise Him, O ye priests, and exalt Him above all, O ye people, unto all ages.*
*Troparia:*
When he who was sent unto thee from the Father in brightness appeared, thou didst recognize him to be thy instructor, the God-bearing Theodosius; and having called him, thou didst make known that he stood speaking, and what he promised thee, O Damian; and he marveled at what was told, and exalted the Lord above all unto the ages.
With joy didst thou ask concerning the promises from thine instructor and father, saying: "Shall it be, O father, as thou hast promised me upon my petition?" And he answered: "I know not, child, of what thou askest me; for an angel of God was he who promised thee, and he shall settle thee there where he sings with the saints unto the Lord unto the ages."
Joyful and filled with good hope at the certainty of the promise, thou didst depart unto the radiant places, escorted by radiant angels, with whom also thou singest unto the Lord unto the ages.
All thy knowledge was for the instruction of those being saved, O venerable Matthew; not even the abbot rejected thine instruction; through which being elevated he quickly attained blessedness, where the righteous exalt the Lord above all unto the ages.
*Glory:*
After many revelations thou didst depart in deep old age, O Matthew, to stand with uncovered face before the throne of God; pray also for us, O father, that through thee we also may be counted worthy to behold the Lord, and to exalt Him with praises unto the ages!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Being one in essence of the Trinity, Christ the Word took up His abode in thy womb, O pure Virgin, and appeared in the flesh consubstantial with us: Whom blessing, we exalt Him above all unto the ages!
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
*Thee Who beyond mind and word art the Mother of God, Who in time didst ineffably bear the Timeless One, we the faithful magnify with one mind.*
*Troparia:*
What shall I call thee, this blessed place? A heavenly firmament, whereon the trinity of fathers shines like stars through wonders, and illumines those who celebrate their honored memory and devoutly bless them with hymns!
Having passed from the temporal light unto the eternal, O God-bearing ones, by your radiant light our hearts *Service to the Venerable Fathers of the Caves.*
...illumine our hearts, and entreat by your prayers that, walking in the light of the Lord's commandments, we may with you behold the Unwaning Light.
Having received the grace of healings from God, O holy ones, while yet in this life: speedily heal us who are held fast by passions; for with faith we flee unto your relics, and praying we magnify you with hymns.
*Glory:*
Let us glorify God Who glorified His saints, Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew, who having lived in their bodies like angels glorified Him; Whom we also pray that, honoring their memory today and magnifying them with praises, He deprive us not of His heavenly glory together with them.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Remembering my end, when the time shall come for me to be loosed from the bonds of the flesh, I tremble, for the day of judgment is coming: from which who shall deliver me, if not thou, O pure Virgin? Therefore do I ever flee unto thee with praise, that through thine intercession we may find mercy on the day of judgment.
*Theotokion:*
Thy temple here is holy, and marvelous in truth; for being thyself the temple of God's glory, by thy providence thou didst build it, and didst adorn it with the angel-like life of the holy fathers: transform our unsightly appearance into goodly beauty, that the King may desire the beauty of our souls, and pour out His great mercy upon us.
*Exapostilarion (Photogogic):*
Having lived holily upon earth, ye are holy in heaven also, and with the angelic choirs ye stand before God: from Whom having received the reward of your labors, forget us not, but taking us by the hand, guide us into the right way, that through your intercession we may find mercy on the day of judgment.

*To Damian, Jeremiah, and Matthew.*
*At the Praises (Lauds): Stichera.*
*Tone 3. Podoben: "Thou hast Given a Sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*
A radiant reception-vessel of the All-Holy Spirit were ye, O all-blessed fathers, and enlightened by Him, in God-beholding manner ye were enriched with the gift of prophecy, and acquired foreknowledge of things to come and true understanding; therefore do we pray you: pray for us unto Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls.
As true prophets, by unerring indications ye foretold all things about to come to pass, and proclaimed them, O hymn-singing fathers: Matthew and Jeremiah, with Damian worthy of wonder; for we know that the Holy Spirit enlightens pure hearts and grants the grace of revelation; therefore we glorify Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls Who glorified you.
Kindled with divine love and desire of the blessings to come, ye willingly traversed the narrow path, counting its afflictions as nothing; therefore now rejoicing in a place of refreshment, give of your gifts unto those who honor you with love, and pray for us, O Damian and Jeremiah with Matthew, unto Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls!
*Glory: Tone 6.*
Come, O ye faithful, let us bless the trinity of venerable fathers: the one as having received the gift of healings, the other as filled with true prophecy, and the other as clairvoyant of unseen things; honoring the day of whose feast, we glorify our God, and we pray that He grant peace unto the world and save our souls!
