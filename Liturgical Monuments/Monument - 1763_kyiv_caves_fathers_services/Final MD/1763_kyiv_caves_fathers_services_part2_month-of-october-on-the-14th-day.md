# MONTH OF OCTOBER: ON THE 14TH DAY

*Memory of Our Venerable Father Nikola Sviatosha, Prince of Chernihiv:*
*At Vespers, Blessed is the man: the 1st antiphon.*
*On "Lord, I have cried": Stichera, on 6. Tone 8. Podoben: "O Most Marvellous Wonder" (Ѽ пресла́внагѡ чꙋдесѐ):*
O God-wise, blessed Nikola: seeing that the fashion of this world passes away, and that all things are subject to corruption, thou didst quickly exchange the fashion of thy glory, didst leave behind the diadem and the sceptre, and looking toward the Heavenly Kingdom, didst choose to walk the narrow path; and now dwelling in the heavenly mansions *To Nikola Sviatosha.*
...dwelling in the heavenly mansions, pray unto Christ God to grant us the blessedness of the everlasting Kingdom.
O venerable Nikola: desiring according to thy name to acquire a victorious life also, thou didst conquer this world, didst leave behind the throne of princely rule, and didst count wealth, glory, and ease as refuse; and clothed in the poor monastic habit, didst follow Christ Who became poor for our sake: Whom now standing before, pray diligently for us, that He grant peace and great mercy unto our souls!
O holy father Nikola most blessed: foreseeing with thy noetic eye that kings and princes are deprived of their glory and go down naked into the grave, thou didst fear the fragile authority of this age, following in the footsteps of Christ, for in humility thou didst serve Him; therefore didst thou obtain unplundered blessedness, where remember us also who honor thy memory!
*Glory: Tone 2.*
Foreseeing Thy Kingdom, O Christ God, and Thy dominion which abides unto the ages, Thy favorite Nikola cast away the sceptre and diadem, and departed from manifold cares, fleeing unto the tranquil life of monastics: he became a true and faithful servant, having served Thee, his Savior, in holiness and righteousness.
*Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion: The shadow of the law hath passed away... Then, the Entrance; Prokeimenon of the day; and 3 Readings for Venerable Ones (see fol. 1v).*[^30]
*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Of the Heavenly Ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*
Kindled with divine desire, ever-memorable father Nikola, thou didst despise worldly glory, and having loved the monastic life, wast of one mind and manner with the venerable fathers; with whom now dwelling, pray for those who honor thy dormition!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord.* (Ps. 111:1 [LXX])[^31]
Thou didst wholly enslave thyself unto Christ, winging all thy desire toward Him; for the sake of Whose love thou didst renounce this world, having loved Him Who loved the human race: therefore we bless thee with hymns, and with love venerate the shrine of thy relics.
*Verse: Blessed are all they that fear the Lord.* (Ps. 127:1 [LXX])[^32]
O thou who lookest upon all thy children who now honor thee and the people who guard thee: disappoint not our hope in thy protection, O father, but mightily intercede for all, and assist in perils and tribulations, granting what is profitable through thy prayers!

*Service to the Venerable One.*
*Glory: Tone 5.*
O venerable father, thou gavest no bound unto thy repose until thou hadst cleansed the passions of thy heart: fulfilling Christ's commandments, thou didst abandon home, kinsmen, and friends; fleeing unto the tranquil haven of the Mother of God, thou didst bring low all thy greatness and glory beneath the feet of all; thou didst blossom in fasting, didst mortify the body, and being ever directed by the Spirit of God, didst show thyself a son and heir of the Ever-Existing Light: where now dwelling, pray that we also may find mercy on the day of judgment.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who art full of grace, intercede through thy prayers, and entreat for our souls a multitude of compassions and the cleansing of our many transgressions, we pray thee.
*At Matins, on "God is the Lord": Troparion, Tone 2:*
Having abandoned the fatherland and the glory of thy princely rule, thou didst eagerly follow Christ, the Prince of humility, O our father Nikola; therefore didst thou receive from Him an everlasting kingdom and glory in Heaven, where rejoicing, remember us who honor thy memory with faith!
*Glory: Both now... Theotokion: All beyond comprehension...*
*Following the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thy Tomb" (Гро́бъ тво́й):*
The Russian people are filled with gladness, and thy memory makes joyful, O blessed one, having thee as a speedy helper in afflictions and deliverance in all sicknesses; therefore, as having received grace from God to drive away diseases by a word, disappoint us not, and assist even now all who honor thy venerable dormition.
*Glory: Both now... Theotokion:*
Knowing thee to be a fountain of healings, O Virgin, in warmth of heart we run unto thee, thy sinful servants: do thou, having motherly care for us, forget not those afflicted with painful tribulations; but stretching forth thy hands, pray unto thy Son, that we may receive speedy relief from them.
*Following the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast Appeared" (Ꙗ҆ви́лся є҆сѝ):*
Having hated the passing things in the light of earth, O blessed one, and fervently loved the ever-existing things in Heaven, which after thy many labors thou didst inherit: where dwelling, pray ever unto Christ for all of us who honor thy memory in hymns.

*To Nikola Sviatosha.*
*Glory: Both now... Theotokion:*
By thy holy childbirth, O Virgin, thou didst renew the nature of earthborn men that was corrupted by passions, and didst raise it from death unto incorruptible life: therefore, as is our duty, O pure Mother, we ever bless thee, as being blessed by all generations of the faithful.
*Following the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Being Commanded Mystically" (Пове́лѣнное та́йнѡ):*
Let thy holy forefather rejoice today, the sovereign Vladimir sitting upon the height of the throne, for from his root a good branch hast thou budded forth, O most blessed Nikola: bearing upon thyself spiritual fruit as an example unto all who wish to be saved and to follow Christ in humility, even as thou didst, O venerable father; and now as thou rejoicest in glory, the fruit of thy monastic labors abides unto the ages.
*Glory: Both now... Theotokion:*
Let David the hymnographer rejoice today, for from his root hath grown up for us the noetic tree, the Virgin Mary, bearing the fruit, Christ our God: by Whom the ends of the whole world are nourished and become partakers of everlasting life; therefore do all we the faithful devoutly bless her as Mother of God.
*Sticheron following the Gospel, Tone 6:*
Great was the humility of thy life, O our father; for having left behind all earthly things—lineage and the throne of thy princely rule—thou didst love Christ alone, and serving Him with a pure conscience, didst live angelically upon earth: thou didst drive away demonic legions, having ascended unto the height of virtues. Blessed art thou who didst so work for Christ, and we, having known thy labors, bless thee with love!
*CANON*
*To the Venerable One.*
*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*I shall open my mouth, and it will be filled with the Spirit, and I shall pour forth a word unto the Queen Mother, and I shall appear radiantly keeping festival, and rejoicing I shall sing her wonders.*
*Troparia:*
Open my lips, O my Christ, send down the Paraclete, that I may be able to sing the versification of Thy favorite, the height of virtues, unto which in purity of heart he ascended through humility.
In truth wast thou named a favorite, for being full of holiness, thou didst depart from every irreverent action, and having winged thy mind toward the vision of God, didst know all fragile things in the world to be transient.
Thou didst remember those who were before thee: from how great a glory they vainly fell away, and were harvested with dishonor by the sickle of death: from which that thou mightest be delivered *Service to the Venerable One.*
...that thou mightest be delivered, thou didst abandon the princely throne on earth, and didst receive in Heaven a throne that abides ever with the saints.
*Glory:*
Thou didst emulate blessed Joasaph of India; for as he abandoned an earthly kingdom and sought the heavenly, so also didst thou, O blessed one, having abandoned the burdensome princely rule, receive an everlasting one.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Deprive me not of the Kingdom on high, O Sovereign Lady Who rulest over heavenly and earthly beings; for seeking this, I have departed, being conquered by the passions that ever war against me; yet unto thee do I flee with unwavering hope.
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*Establish spiritually Thy hymnographers, O Theotokos, living and ungrudging fountain, who have formed a choir for thee; in thy divine glory count them worthy of crowns of glory.*
*Troparia:*
That thou mightest be a servant of God alone, thou didst not wish to serve the world, departing from which, like a second Abraham, thou didst leave behind thy home and land; and having come, as into Haran, unto the most honored Pechersk monastery of the Mother of God, thou didst see in active deed the actions and visions of the blessed fathers ascending unto Heaven.
Having once been master of thine own princely realm, for the love of thy Lord thou didst make thyself a servant, serving the servants of Christ in the kitchen: that thou mightest follow Him Who for our sake was obedient unto death.
Clothed in purple and holding a sceptre in hand, thou wast not ashamed to carry firewood upon thy shoulders and to chop it with thine own hands: that thou mightest yield all thy members in servitude unto the Master on high, from Whom thou didst receive as reward everlasting life.
*Glory:*
Living in turbulent worldly wisdom, thy brothers, O blessed one, thought thy work in the kitchen to be dishonorable; therefore they tried to pervert thee, that thou shouldest leave this behind; but loving this obedience, thou didst ask for thyself yet another year to abide therein.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Persuade me, O my Sovereign Lady, who obey not the commandments of my Lord nor preserve the rules of obedience, to work them; and strengthen me in the remaining time to amend myself completely in both: lest having finished my life in slothfulness, I receive punishment according to my deeds with the unprofitable servants.
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8:*
As a true hearer of the Gospel word, a lover of monks, and an emulator of the venerable, come, O brethren, with hymnographic praises let us sing of blessed Nikola: for he enslaved his body *To Nikola Sviatosha.*
...enslaved his body unto God, that he might free the soul from passions; and having shone forth like the sun with the rays of his virtues, he illumines all of us who sing: Glory to God Who calls His servants unto everlasting salvation!
*Glory: Both now... Theotokion:*
Show me to be a citizen of the Kingdom on high, O pure Virgin, who flee unto thy protection and ask of thee warm intercession: that having found grace through thee, I may sing: Glory to God Who saves the human race!
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
*He Who sits in glory upon the throne of the Godhead, in a light cloud, Jesus the Most Divine, is come with an incorruptible hand, and hath saved those who cry aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Christ!*
*Troparia:*
Formerly sitting in glory upon a throne, with nobles standing before thee, thou wast not ashamed, O blessed one, to sit at the gates of the toll-gatherers for three years, faithfully guarding their entrance against those wandering idly.
Whereas formerly at table those fond of delicacies stood before thee, and many servants served thee as a lord: afterward thou didst not abhor making thyself a servant unto the servants of Christ, serving them and setting forth food given by God.
As one who has ascended by steps unto the height of noetic glory has repose after labors: so also didst thou, O venerable one, by the counsel of the abbot and brethren having passed through all obediences in good order, wast constrained to live in silence with peace in the cell, and to take heed unto thy salvation.
*Glory:*
As a labor-loving servant multiplying the talent for his Master, thou didst give no rest unto thy much-labored body, but with thine own hands didst plant an orchard: and the brethren enjoyed the fruits of thy labors.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Do thou, O Theotokos, arouse me who am sluggish unto virtuous struggle, and grant me to plant a spiritual orchard with noetic trees, to water it with tears of compunction, and to bring forth a hundredfold fruit unto my Master, that I also with His faithful servants may receive from His hands the reward of my labors.
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
*All creation was amazed at thy divine glory: for thou, O Virgin who knewest not wedlock, didst hold in thy womb the God of all, and didst bear the Timeless Son, granting peace unto all who hymn thee.*
*Troparia:*
All marveled at thy perseverance, O blessed one, for thou wast never seen idle, but hadst work in thy hands, and in thy lips ever the Jesus Prayer: and by this didst thou wound the demon who attacks through slothfulness.

*Service to the Venerable One.*
That thou mightest become a partaker of the heavenly food, thou didst cast away the love of delicacies: for thou didst taste of nothing else, save only a little of the common food at the appointed time at table; and thus didst thou preserve the purity of soul and body.
After thy many monastic labors, thou didst receive a gift from God, to heal diseases by a single word and to assist in afflictions: I pray thee, change also my sickness into health speedily!
*Glory:*
With craftily woven nets the enemy sought to turn thy purchased soul from the good path of labor-loving, through the malice of a certain physician who had once served thee; but with patience thou didst persuade him that he should take heed not unto thee, but unto his own salvation.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
In heart and soul have I gone astray, and cast away the grace given me, walking in unbecoming paths: but before I fall into the hands of the enemies, do thou, O Mother of God, direct me unto my former blessedness: for I am a sheep of thy saved fold.
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
*Celebrating this divine and all-honored feast of the Mother of God, come, O God-wise, let us clap our hands, and glorify God Who was born of her.*
*Troparia:*
Reaching forth with divine desire toward the love of God, thou didst sweetly endure all afflictions that came upon thee, O blessed one, considering what was spoken: that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory that shall be revealed in us.[^33]
When Peter the physician urged thee to cease from monastic labors, chastely didst thou answer many times, O holy one: "Cease turning me away from my humble endeavor; for neither affliction, nor famine, nor distress shall separate me from the love of Christ,[^34] for Whose sake I endure all things that come upon me!"
That thou mightest find Christ the Everlasting Life, thou didst mortify thy body with much abstinence and labor, and wast not deprived of what thou didst desire: for behold, thou now livest with the saints in the heavenly courts!
*Glory:*
Having Christ as the only Physician, thou didst never permit thyself to be medically treated in sickness, but didst say with the Apostle: *For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain;*[^35] and by a word didst thou thyself heal others of their diseases.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
By thy visitation heal my soul that faints from many sins, O Sovereign Lady, and water me with the water of thy grace: that being freed from spiritual and bodily diseases, I may ever work for thee, my Sovereign Lady, with zeal.

*To Nikola Sviatosha.*
*Kontakion, Tone 4:*
Having counted all the good things of this world and corruptible riches as nothing, thou didst enrich thyself with many wonders and signs from Christ God: standing before Whom in gladness, remember us who honor thy memory with love, that we may cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Nikola most marvelous!
*Ikos:*
Unto thee who didst live angelically upon earth, and now rejoicest in the heavens with the angelic ranks in the everlasting light, O blessed one, we offer these praises in hymns:
Rejoice, thou who didst despise nobility of lineage and princely rule!
Rejoice, thou who didst trample upon riches and vainglory!
Rejoice, poor and needy one, who, leaving all, didst follow the Lord!
Rejoice, thou who didst cast down pride and cleave unto humility!
Rejoice, thou who didst pass through all obediences for many years!
Rejoice, thou who didst abide in them with all humility!
Rejoice, thou who didst not abhor the kitchen and the gate!
Rejoice, thou who didst reject living with thy brothers, the princes!
Rejoice, thou who didst thyself help in infirmity, and didst instruct the physician!
Rejoice, thou who through thy prayers didst intercede for the salvation of the physician, and didst foresee thine own death and his!
Rejoice, thou who after thy death didst bestow healing upon thy brother!
Rejoice, marvelous and speedy helper unto all who call upon thee warmly!
Rejoice, O Nikola most marvelous!
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
*The God-wise ones served not the creature rather than the Creator; but manfully trampling upon the threat of fire, they rejoiced, singing: Blessed art Thou, O all-hymned Lord and God of our fathers!*
*Troparia:*
"From how great a glory hast thou departed, O prince!" Peter said unto thee, and reproached thee, O blessed one, saying that thou wast working for the servants and drawing near unto death; but thou didst answer him: "I have not destroyed my honor, but have rather been glorified by these things, following Christ Who came to serve all, unto Whom also I cry: Blessed is the God of our fathers!"
When Peter wished to heal thee in sickness, he himself fell sick, whom thou didst heal not with herbs, but with prayer, and didst instruct him not to hope in the art of physicians, but to trust only in God, crying: Blessed art Thou, O all-hymned God of our fathers!
Completing thine obedience among the brethren, thou didst foresee thine own end after three months: therefore thou didst call Peter, and didst persuade him to take upon himself the monastic habit in thy place, and to serve God instead of thee, Who is blessed unto the ages.

*Service to the Venerable One.*
Having heard of the departure of thy life, Peter fell down with tears at thy feet, O venerable one, weeping that he was being deprived of his master; but thou, having raised him up, didst comfort him, and didst promise that he should not sorrow over this, but cry: Blessed is the God of our fathers!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having no amendment of my life on account of foul deeds, who shall not bewail me? For I depart on an unknown path; I have turned aside from the way of the commandments of thy Son. Therefore with tears I call upon thee, O Sovereign Lady: show me the way, and lead me in truth, as I sing: All-hymned art thou, O Theotokos, and blessed from generation to generation!
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
*The childbirth of the Theotokos saved the pious children in the furnace: then prefigured, but now in deed accomplished; it stirs up the whole world to sing unto Thee: Praise the Lord, O ye works, and exalt Him above all unto all ages.*
*Troparia:*
Having lived in pious wisdom, and noetically foreseeing thy departure, O venerable Nikola, thou didst prepare for thyself a place of repose, which thou didst bestow upon Peter at his supplication, saying: "Be it unto thee, if thou wilt, and even as the Lord wills, Who is exalted above all unto the ages!"
Having boldness before God, and unwavering hope in God, thou didst receive the gift of clairvoyance, through which knowing that after three days Peter would depart unto God, thou didst command him to prepare for departure; and according to thy word he arose, having received the Holy Mysteries, and departed rejoicing and singing: Exalted is God above all unto the ages!
Having lived for thirty years after the death of that physician, and having faithfully served the Lord, thou didst pour forth as a fountain fruits, with which thou didst depart, O blessed one, unto the reposed fathers,[^36] singing unto the Lord, and exalting Him above all unto the ages.
*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.*
Having heard of thy glorious and honored dormition, the people gathered from the whole city to give thee the last kiss, and they buried thee with tears, who wast borne with the joy of angels into the bosom of Abraham, that there with all the saints thou mayest stand before the Lord unto the ages.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Temporal life is drawing to a close; woe is me! How shall I be presented, the most wretched of all men? The Judge waits, the books are opened, and according to my deeds it is certain that I must expect to inherit evils; yet before I depart hence, together with Thine obedient favorite Nikola, pray diligently unto Thy Son that I may inherit a portion of the good things!

*To Nikola Sviatosha.*
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
*Let every earthborn mortal leap for joy, enlightened by the Spirit, and let the nature of the bodiless minds celebrate, honoring the sacred festival of the Mother of God, and cry aloud: Rejoice, O all-blessed Theotokos, pure Ever-Virgin!*
*Troparia:*
Let every earthborn mortal imitate the life of the God-praised father; for he despised temporal glory upon earth, and having now entered into eternal glory he rejoices, having an immovable throne in the heavenly mansions, where he also prays for us, that we may be heirs of the blessed life.
"Whosoever loves Me, follows Me, that he may be with Me": having heard these divine words,[^37] O most blessed father, having loved God, thou didst follow Him, and didst acquire nothing in the world above His love; therefore now thou livest with Christ in unapproachable light.
He Who said: "Them that glorify Me will I glorify,"[^38] fulfilled the promise upon thee, O father; for not only did He glorify thee thyself with gift-bestowals, but also thy cross, headrest, and hairshirt are glorified by thy brother, and remembered even unto this day, for glorious things were wrought through them.
*Glory:*
From generation to generation thy memory is glorified, O venerable father; the Church praises thy labors and ascetic deeds together with us, and the shrine containing thy relics in the holy cave reveals them, and convokes all to pray unto thee now, as a fervent intercessor and protector in our infirmity.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Glorified by all generations, and honored by the All-Holy Trinity, unto us who praise thee in hymns, O pure Virgin, be an intercessor and a speedy protector against the assaults of enemies, that we may ever sing unto thee: Rejoice, O Theotokos, Ever-Virgin!
*Exapostilarion:*
Thou wast a temple of the All-Holy Spirit, and the Father coming with the Son made an abode in thee, and through communion in the divine things showed thee to be a citizen of paradise: wherein now being nourished from the fertile tree, remember also us who with faith venerate the shrine of thy relics, O venerable father!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
A most holy temple wast thou, O Virgin, unto the Word Who was incarnate of thee; alas, make me, a temple of great sinning, to be a temple of the lawful commandments of thy Son through thine intercession, that having become a dwelling of God's grace, I may worthily hymn thee, as one who canst do all things through God, O Maiden of God!

*Service to the Venerable One.*
*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Of the Heavenly Ranks" (Небе́сныхъ чинѡ́въ):*
The great accomplishments of the fasting labors of the great Caves fathers drew thee, O blessed Nikola, unto the fasting company, whose life thou didst zealously emulate; and now living with them in Heaven, pray for those who hymn thee. *(Twice)*
Upon the holy mountain, where the Mother of God placed her abode, thou didst blossom like a leafy palm tree; by the branches of thy labors, cover from affliction all who call upon thee for help, and grant healing from thy fruits unto those who piously honor thy most honorable memory, O all-blessed Nikola!
Today at thy memory, O venerable one, the Caves Lavra rejoices, and the honorable assembly of monastics, having come unto thy tomb, spiritually keeps festival in psalms, praising God Who dwells in unapproachable light and is praised in the saints, with whom thou also now livest, receiving the reward from Him.
*Glory: Tone 2:*
Being a goodly branched tree, like a cedar in Lebanon,[^39] thou wast planted in the house of the Mother of God, O most blessed father Nikola, and nourished at the living waters from the teachings of the Caves fathers; by thy shade overshadow the people who flee unto thee, protect them from barbarian invasions, casting down their uprisings; for the Lord set thee as prince in the land of Rus'; therefore falling down we pray: preserve the realm of Rus' in peace, and guard well this monastery by thy prayers!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Save thy servants from perils, O most holy Theotokos Virgin; for next to God we all flee unto thee, as unto an unbreakable wall and intercession. *The Great Doxology, and Dismissal.*

*In the Month of October, on the 27th Day:*
*Service to the Venerable Nestor the Chronicler of the Caves.*
*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried", Stichera, Tone 4. Podoben: "As One Valiant among Martyrs" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*
Having recorded the times and years of memorable deeds, the ascetic struggles and labors of the God-bearing fathers, O blessed Nestor, thou didst burn with love for noetic desire, and having shown thyself a good disciple, thou didst follow in the footsteps of the founders; and while yet in this life thou didst choose *Service to the Venerable One.*
...the good portion, which is also with thee now: make us also partakers thereof by thy prayers, who zealously hymn thy memory.
Having unfeignedly written in a book the writings of divine words and the ascetic struggles of those who lived angelically, thou didst write in the book of thy heart the memory of the future recompense; therefore thou hast thy name written in the Book of Life. But, O most blessed father, pray for us, that we also may be found written in the Book of Life, who honor the commemoration of thy memory.
Desiring to possess the understanding of spiritual wisdom, O venerable one, thou didst come unto the great fathers, from whom thou didst learn heavenly comprehensions; therefore having lived angelically in the flesh, thou didst shine forth as a noetic morning; and now being in the day and a partaker of the light, cease not to pray diligently, that those who hymn thy most honorable memory with faith may be delivered from snares and perils.
*Glory: Tone 6:*
Having prepared thy heart as a tabernacle for the Holy Spirit, O Nestor our father, thou didst adorn thy soul with purity and humility, with the choice of voluntary poverty, with vigilance, obedience, and unceasing supplication; and with the reed of thy chastity thou didst write the God-befitting lives of the original Caves fathers; having unceasing desire toward God, thou didst find Him Whom thou didst seek. And now, as thou hast boldness before God, pray that our souls may be saved.
*Both now... Theotokion or Stavrotheotokion.*
*At the Aposticha, Stichera from the Octoechos.*
*Glory: Tone 5:*
Thou didst fulfill the prophetic saying, O venerable Nestor, proclaiming unto us by writing the works of God, saying with David in the psalm: "I remembered the works of the Lord, and Thy wonders from the beginning, O God,"[^40] which recount the things that took place in the beginning of the monastery of Thy Mother; make known unto the latter generation Thy power in the venerable ones: having learned these things from thy writings, O most blessed one, we kiss thy labors, we bless thy memory with hymns, and unto Him Who is in the highest we offer psalmody: "Thou art the God Who workest wonders!"[^41]
*Both now... Theotokion according to the tone, or Stavrotheotokion.*
*Canon to the Same.*
*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*The choirs of Israel with unwetted feet passed through the Red Sea and the watery deep; and beholding the enemy captains and horsemen submerged therein, with gladness they sang: Let us sing unto our God, for He is glorified!*
*Troparia:*
O monastic choirs, in the yearly memory of the God-bearing father hasten zealously unto the holy cave, and venerating with love the shrine of the relics of Nestor wise in God, sing unto God, for He is gloriously glorified in His saints!

*To Nestor the Chronicler.*
Unto me, who unite myself unto the monastic choirs and dare this day to hymn thy memory, though I am more sinful than all men, grant help through thy prayers, O holy Nestor; for God is marvelously glorified in thee!
Desiring to be numbered among the angelic choirs, thou didst join thyself, O father, unto the choirs of those who lived like angels in the flesh; taught by them the angelic life, thou didst ascend unto noetic desire; therefore do we honor thee, for God is marvelously glorified in thee!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O ye choirs of the saints and venerable fathers, standing together with the angelic choirs, glorify her who bore the Word Who is holier than all the holy; and as ye glorify the Mother of God today, help also us earthborn mortals; for she is marvelously glorified by all!
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*The bow of the mighty hath grown weak, and the weak have girded themselves with strength; therefore my heart is established in the Lord.*
*Troparia:*
The resolve of thy mind was drawn like a bow, O father, for living in this vale of weeping, thou didst ever have an ascent in thy heart unto God; help also me, who lead my life ever in sorrows, O Nestor, pray for me, that my heart may rejoice in the Lord!
Being seventeen years of age, having embraced poverty, thou didst lofty-mindedly receive the stature of a perfect man; for thou didst shine forth as with the gray hairs of old age through good deeds; direct these things also in me, O blessed one, and my heart shall rejoice in the Lord!
*Glory:*
Thou didst acquire profound purity and humility, O father, as two wings unto the mountain of monastic virtues, whence having observed that the fashion of this world passes away in the twinkling of an eye, thou didst wing thy heart all the more into the love of God.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having given birth unto the Orchard of purity, by His fruit thou hast richly nourished those who believe thee to be the Mother of God; unto me also who with faith bow down before thee, O unwedded Bride of God, grant to taste at least a little of this fruit, that my sorrowful heart may rejoice!
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*
Clad in virtuous ways, and having in youth partaken in a God-knowing manner of all things, thou didst mortify the lusts of the flesh by strict fasting; thou didst quicken the spirit through hope in eternal good things; having lived angelically upon earth, thou now dwellest with the angels; therefore do we honor thee, O blessed Nestor!

*Service to the Venerable One.*
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
An ineffable image of fearful wonders do I behold in thee, O most pure Virgin, for thou didst conceive God, and didst contain the Incomprehensible One in thy womb; but how this mystery came to pass, I examine not, but rather believe, and with fear bless thee, crying: Rejoice, spacious dwelling of the uncontainable God!
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
*I have heard of Thy glorious dispensation, O Christ God, that Thou wast born of the Virgin, to deliver from deception those who cry: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!*
*Troparia:*
Having heard the glorious things handed down on the Caves mountain, thou didst come thither, O venerable one; and seeing the grace of God descending with fire and dew at the beginning of the founding of the church, thou didst cry aloud with fear: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!
Making a greater and more perfect tabernacle in thy soul for the Holy Spirit, by the affectionate reception of the commandments from the God-bearing fathers thou didst gild it, O father Nestor; therefore, as into a beautiful bridal chamber, the Father with the Son and the Spirit came and made an abode in thee.
*Glory:*
Having received the teaching of the God-bearing fathers like sweet milk, thou didst fulfill the word in deed, O blessed one, and wast filled with wisdom from on high; reading which also in thy writings, we gladden our souls with the knowledge of God's grace that came to pass on these hills.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Sweet milk of repentance, and heavenly nectar of compunction, dost thou grant, O pure Maiden, unto those who bring hymns with love unto thee; which I also, gripped with fear, yet moved festive-wise, bring unto thee, O Sovereign Lady, and pray: sweeten my heart, O thou who didst bear my sweet Jesus Christ!
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
*O Thou Who didst spread out the light, the firstborn light, so that in the light the works hymn Thee, O Christ Creator: in Thy light guide our paths!*
*Troparia:*
Having removed thyself from the love of the deceptive world, with divine desire thou didst seek, O Nestor, to follow the footsteps, ways, and teachings of the great fathers; grant that we also may cleave unto these, and in the light of their deeds, through thy prayers, guide our paths!
Encompassed with deep humility, O venerable one, thou didst cast down and trample underfoot the high-walking enemy of vainglory; through thine intercession before God, O father, grant humility also unto us, and in it guide our paths!
*Glory:*
Having directed thy life angelically, all regarded thee as an angel of God; therefore Thou wast counted worthy to receive the rank of the diaconate *To Nestor the Chronicler.*
...to receive the rank of the diaconate, wherein having well accomplished thy ministry, guide also our paths in truth, O Nestor!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Having borne the Way of life, O all-holy Virgin, as one compassionate thou guidest onto paths of righteousness those walking on the paths of temptations; and for me who go astray, having given a good guide unto the wandering, in His light guide my paths!
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
*When I was afflicted, I cried unto the Lord, and the God of my salvation heard me.*
*Troparia:*
Having ordered thy rank in holiness and righteousness, thou didst make thyself wholly a temple for the Holy Spirit; and having entered into the temple not made with hands, thou standest before the throne of the Most High: help me also who desire to be there, by thy prayers, O father, I pray thee, the suppliant of my salvation!
Thou wast a beautiful bud of the celestial Cultivator of the beautiful garden; for adorned with beautiful deeds, thou didst bring forth good fruits, whose fragrance of grace we now perceive; I pray thee therefore, O blessed one: grant them as a gift unto me also from God my Savior!
*Glory:*
From the labor of thine ascetic struggles, O venerable Nestor, having shed many drops of sweat, thou didst water the field of thy soul, and having grown up the sheaves of virtues, thou now reapest sheaves, living unto the ages in the heavenly mansions: pray Christ my Savior that I also may be therein!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O holy land watered by the morning Spirit, rejoice, for thou didst sprout forth for us a tree of goodly leaf-shade; bring me who am scorched by the heat of passions beneath its shade, O Sovereign Lady, I pray thee, as the mediatress of my salvation!
*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
Being a disciple of the God-bearing Theodosius, and a true imitator of his life, Thou wast counted worthy to be the first eyewitness of his honorable relics; which having translated with the others in God-befitting manner, Thou didst inherit with them the Heavenly Kingdom: pray the Lord that we also who honor Thee may obtain it!
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*
*The Abrahamic children in Babylon of old trampled upon the flame of the furnace, crying aloud with hymns: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!*
*Troparia:*
Following in Abraham's footsteps, O holy one, by the fulfillment of the commandments thou didst offer a pure mind unto the Master, as he did Isaac; therefore departing from the distress of this life, thou didst pass into the land flowing with endless sweetness: where pray that I also may be, O Nestor, companion of our God-bearing fathers!

*Service to the Venerable One.*
Having departed from this passionate world, thou didst enter into the passionless world, where living in peace with the sons of peace, depart not from us who are in this world, praying unto thy Creator that we may be fellow partakers with thee, singing: Blessed art Thou, O God!
Thou didst not incline thy heart unto words of malice in this tumultuous life, O venerable one; therefore, having untroubledly passed through the malice of the aerial spirits, thou didst reach the heavenly gates: which hasten to open also unto us by thy prayers, for thou art blessed.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
I turned aside from the path of righteousness, and walked not with my feet on the right ways, but wandering over the precipices of passions, I was bound fast, captive by mine endurance of sin; yet even limping I run unto thy goodness, and pray thee, O Sovereign Lady: heal me, for thou art the medicine of my soul!
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
*In Babylon the children, kindled with zeal through strength, manfully trampled upon the tormentor and the threat of the flame, and cast into the midst of the fire, being bedewed, they sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!*
*Troparia:*
Strengthened with spiritual power, O God-wise one, Thou didst fly with the wings of thy virtues unto the things on high, and by their flight Thou now livest where years fail not: pray that we also may attain thither, who sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!
From the house of the Mother of God thou didst pass, O Nestor, into the house of God, the Jerusalem that contains Him, and didst dwell as a fellow citizen with the angels in the number of heavenly citizens; intercede for us also, that we may be numbered with them, who sing unto the Lord in hymns at Thy memory.
*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.*
O Trinity, one Nature: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, one Kingdom, having power and authority: deliver us from the power of the prince of darkness, through the prayers of our God-bearing Caves fathers, with whom we also bring Nestor today in supplication unto Thee, blessing Thee the Lord unto the ages!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Standing before the throne of the Trinity, O Mother and Virgin, and ever beholding the glory of the Ever-Existing Godhead: pray for us sinners, that we also may not be deprived of heavenly glory, who praise thee in hymns, and the Lord Who was born of thee!
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
*Thy childbirth was shown to be incorruptible: God came forth from thy womb, as a flesh-bearer He appeared upon earth and lived with men; therefore, O Theotokos, we all magnify thee.*
*Troparia:*
Thou hast departed into the mansions of the righteous, and in spirit standest before the Ever-Existing Light *To Nestor the Chronicler.*
...standest before the Ever-Existing Light: look down therefore upon us who dwell in the villages of this tumultuous world, and fight for those who are in battles, O blessed Nestor; for now with hymns we magnify thee!
With those with whom thou didst acquire love while living in the body, with them also having departed in spirit thou dwellest inseparably, as thy relics show, O venerable one, lying with the saints in the cave; stand with them praying unto God for us who magnify thy memory!
*Glory:*
The Lord works mercy and truth: and do thou, O most blessed one, imitating Him, be a merciful intercessor for our salvation, that for thy sake we also may find grace and not fall away from eternal glory, as we magnify thy holy dormition with hymns.
*Both now... Theotokion:*
The Master of all accepts thine intercession, O most pure Virgin, and receives thy prayers for the human race: pray therefore also for us thy servants, O all-pure one, that we may obtain mercy on the day of judgment; having thee as mediatress, we bow down and magnify thee!
*At the Aposticha, Stichera from the Octoechos.*
*Glory: Tone 2:*
By preparatory monastic actions, O venerable one, thou didst make thyself an honorable vessel of the Holy Spirit, from Whom thou didst receive inexhaustible grace to fight for all who flee unto thee with faith in afflictions. And now unto us who fall down before the shrine of thy relics, be an unceasing helper, invisibly warring against those who war against us, and pray the Creator of all and God to grant us forgiveness of sins and to obtain mercy on the day of judgment, who bless thy memory, O God-wise Nestor!
*Both now... Theotokion or Stavrotheotokion.*
