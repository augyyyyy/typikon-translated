Sevenfold did the Chaldean tyrant frantically kindle the furnace for the pious; but having seen them saved by a greater power, he cried unto the Creator and Deliverer: Ye youths, bless; ye priests, hymn; ye people, supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
The head of the seven-headed serpent thou didst crush by the sevenfold spiritual gifts, O father, and wounded, he trembles even until now at thy name; which remembering on this present day, concerning thee we cry: Ye people, supremely exalt Christ unto all ages!

Through love toward the Most Holy Mother of God and the Caves fathers, thou seemedst to be separated from them in body only, yet in spirit thou didst ever abide with them and emulate their life; therefore visit us in thy love, and pray that we may supremely exalt Christ unto all ages.

Truly, even as Simon was a most fervent lover of Christ Himself, so thou also, O hierarch Simon, didst show thyself a zealous lover toward the Mother of God and her monastery; and with those whom thou didst love, with them thou now dwellest unto ages.
