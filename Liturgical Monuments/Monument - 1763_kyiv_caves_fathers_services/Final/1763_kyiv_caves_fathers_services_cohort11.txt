When the golden image was served on the plain of Dura, the three children disregarded the godless command, and cast into the midst of the flame, being bedewed they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*
The blessed fathers, desiring to act prudently, revealed their thoughts one to another, and both discerned whether their thoughts were well-pleasing unto God. Emulating them, O brethren, test your thoughts by fraternal discernment, which of them are from God.[^470]

Emulating the struggles and labors of the venerable Theodore...

*Service to the Venerable Ones*

...let us not give our labor unto others; but even as he himself carried firewood unto his cell, so let us also labor for ourselves, if we will that the enemy entangle us not through idleness.[^471]

The demons hating the blessed one wrought mischief against him; for as much as he with great labor carried timber up the mountain, so much did they cast down again from the mountain; but while assigning labor unto the elder, they themselves endured labor and torment.[^472]

Trepidation unto the heart is unceasing prayer from a pure heart, wherewith the blessed Theodore bound the malicious spirits, and they became obedient unto him as captives, carrying timber for the rebuilding of the ancient monastery that had burned down before.[^473]

*Glory:*
Tormented and dishonored by many labors, the crafty spirits cried aloud against the blessed ones, boasting and saying: Cruel adversaries unto us are Theodore and Basil, with whom we shall not cease to war until we deliver them unto death!—not knowing that they would be the cause of a most honorable crown for them.[^474]

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Beholding me strengthened by thee, O Sovereign Lady, the demons gnash their teeth, unceasingly warring against me until they overcome; but do thou, my Protectress, deliver me not into their hands, but rescue me from their assaults!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^475]
Not accepting the golden likeness, the thrice-blessed youths, beholding the unchangeable and living image of God, cried aloud in the midst of the fire: Let all creation sing of the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
By the semblance of all their crafty malice, the demons began themselves to incite men, that they might destroy thee, O Theodore; therefore having hired slanderers with much reproach, they slandered thee unto the judge, O father; but thou, taking no thought for this, didst cry aloud: Let all creation sing of the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages![^476]

In the likeness of the blessed Basil, the demon came again unto a certain inhuman boyar, and slandered Theodore concerning the found treasure, saying that he wished to flee with it into another land; but that wicked one knew not that the saints possess Christ alone as their treasure, Whom they supremely exalt with praises unto ages.[^477]

In the likeness of Basil, the wicked boyar brought the demon unto the prince, who lied unto him also concerning the treasure against thee, O father, and advised that they seize thee, and by many tortures extort the gold from thee; but thou, O blessed one, fearing nothing, didst arm thyself strongly against the adversary who is estranged from God forever.

**[Right Page — Fol. 104r]**
*To Theodore and Basil of the Caves*

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord!*
With fury, as upon a hunt, coming himself against a certain strong warrior, the prince seized thee, O blessed one; and having brought thee into his house, at first with flatteries questioned thee concerning the treasure; unto whom thou didst reply, O father: I found a treasure, and saw a multitude of golden vessels, which I buried again, as it was unprofitable for me, and I prayed unto the Lord that I might forget it forever![^478]

*Both now: Theotokion:*
I have buried the talent entrusted unto me, as a slothful servant, and in no wise remember that I must give an account concerning its increase unto my Master; therefore I pray thee, All-Hymned, All-Pure Virgin: help me to make the talent well-pleasing, that I may find my Lord merciful forever![^479]

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^480]
Thou Who alone art Desire, Who art Sweetness, O Word of God, Son of the Virgin, God of gods, holy and all-holy Lord: therefore do we all magnify Thee with her that bore Thee!

*Troparia:*
Desiring to be loosed from carnal bonds and to be with Christ, thou wast bound by the prince guiltlessly; and being severely tormented, thou didst torment the tormenting enemy, O most blessed Theodore![^481]

From thy flesh streams of blood flowed, and reddened the hair-shirt, wherein clad as in a most precious purple robe, Thou didst enter into the heavenly bridal chamber, where with her that bore God Thou dost magnify the Trinity!

Being suspended and choked with smoke, and then severely scorched with fire, thou wast, O blessed one; but the right hand of God preserved thee whole, so that the fire did not even touch thy hair-shirt; but standing in the flame as in a cool place, thou didst glorify Christ with her that bore Him.[^482]

Believing the demonic deception, the prince dared to go against the mystical victor over passions, Basil, and wishing to have a witness against Theodore, questioned him concerning the hidden gold; but thou without wavering didst reprove him and them that stood by, as deceived by the demon.[^483]

*Glory:*
Being heated with wine, not enduring thy reproof, the foolish prince wounded thee with an arrow, O father Basil; and thou, O blessed one, didst prophesy unto him, saying: Thou thyself shalt be wounded by the very same arrow...

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Our enemies have armed themselves, O Sovereign Lady, and compassed us round about, shooting lustful arrows at us; but do thou, O Protectress, being a strong city, an impregnable wall, and a mighty shield, defend and preserve them that magnify thee with thy Son!

*Exapostilarion:*[^485]
Cast into prison, O blessed fathers, after many tortures, the Lord led your souls out of carnal bonds, and brought them into the unwaning Light, to confess His holy name, where rejoicing with angelic choirs, remember us also who honor your yearly memory!

*Glory, both now: Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
Ensnared by sin-loving nets by the adversary, and cast into the prison of heavy blindness, I see not the sun of the commandments of my Lord; but unto thee, O Pure Virgin, do I cry: Cause the Sun of repentance to dawn upon me, and warm my frozen heart with the rays of thy goodness, that I may glorify thy speedy help!

*At the Praises, Stichera, Automela, Tone 1:*[^486]
Who can worthily praise your labors and ascetical struggles, O most blessed fathers? For in bodily frames ye lived as bodiless ones, and having winged your minds toward God, ye flew up unto the heavens; and now standing before the throne of the Most High, earnestly pray to bring peace unto the world and to save our souls!

Who will not marvel at your patient endurance, O fathers, how being guiltlessly slandered by the enemy, ye endured many torments from the enraged prince, and having endured unto the end, ye invisibly conquered him that warred against you, and received the crown of victory from the hand of the Lord; from Whom pray that we also may receive this!

Blessed is the earth that drank in your blood, O noble confessors of Christ; blessed is the cave that preserves your relics as a precious treasure, lying not under a bushel, but openly in the tomb, wherein while yet in the body, in souls ye stand before the angelic choirs; and upon earth ye drive away crafty spirits, from whom preserve us also, and pray unto Christ God to save our souls![^487]

*To Theodore and Basil of the Caves*

*Glory: Tone 3:*
Come, brethren, let us praise our most blessed fathers, Basil with the marvelous Theodore; for having first renounced every passionate attachment, and striving to attain unto dispassion, they took no thought for this life, and chose for the sake of their Lord to suffer death from the money-loving prince; and appearing as victors, they conquered the warrior and adversary enemy, and are now crowned with glory and honor by the Most High Master, Whom they also unceasingly entreat to grant peace unto the world, and great mercy unto our souls!

*Both now: Of the Feast, or this Theotokion:*
O blessed Hope of the world, Virgin: forget not us that sing of thee, but pray ever for thine unprofitable servants, that we may be delivered from all perils and tribulations!

*Month of August, on the 17th Day:*
*Service to our venerable father Alypius the Iconographer of the Caves.*
*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 4. Podoben: "Called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*[^489]
Having the image of the glory of God ever in thy virtuous mind, O most blessed father Alypius, desiring to show thy soul beautiful unto the Triune Glory by renewal, whence having received the pigments of spiritual grace, thou wast an exceptional wonderworker; and Whose countenance thou didst depict in thy heart, with Him thou now reignest; entreat Him for us, O venerable one, to grant us peace, and the enjoyment of His glory![^490]

**[Right Page — Fol. 106r]**
*To Alypius the Iconographer of the Caves*

Desiring to be a beholder of the face of God, thou didst set in thy mind, O father, ever to behold the sweetest countenance of Jesus; therefore having laid the stone of ultimate humility, washing in the baths of unceasing prayers, mingling them with streams of tears, thou didst inscribe upon the fleshy table of the heart the vision of the invisible glory of the All-Holy Trinity; standing before Whom now, pray for us, O father, that we may obtain mercy and the sweet vision of the face of God![^491]

Being inflamed with the desire to depict the images of the saints, the uncircumscribed glory of God, thou didst depict with the pigments of good deeds upon the tablets of thy heart the image of unceasing love toward the All-Pure Mother of God; Whom having served by thine art, thou wast vouchsafed wonderworking grace, and didst receive the reward of unending recompense; and now dwelling with the angels, pray for us, O venerable one, to bring peace unto the world and to save our souls![^492]

*Glory: Tone 8:*
The boastful serpent who once through the deceit of tasting dishonored the human image, is now filled with shame before thee, O father, who hast received divine nobility from God; for he could not bear to look upon the icons of thine art, which dispel the darkness with wonderworking rays, and grant the gifts of healing unto all that run unto them with faith: make us also partakers of these, on this present day celebrating thy memory, O wise Alypius![^493]

*Both now: Of the Feast.*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Feast.*
*Glory: Tone 3:*
Having the spiritual depiction of thy soul luminous, O our venerable father Alypius, thou wast vouchsafed bright grace even sensibly, which the painting of thine icons gave forth, granting healing unto them that needed in diseases; therefore we pray thee, all-blessed one: correct the deformity of our souls by virtues, and heal our passionate sores with the pigments of thy prayers, even as thou didst the leper of old, that we may praise thee with hymns, O beauty of the venerable![^494]
