# MONTH OF NOVEMBER

[Woodcut Engraving: The Venerable Varlaam, Abbot of the Kyiv Caves, standing in monastic habits surrounded by scenes of his life: his tonsure, his wife entreating him, his father beating him, and his holy repose]

*On the 19th Day: Service to Our Venerable Father Varlaam, Abbot of the Caves.*
*At Small Vespers, Stichera, Tone 5. Podoben: "Rejoice, O Container of Ascetics" (Ра́дꙋйсѧ по́стникѡвъ):*
O venerable father Varlaam, having taken the yoke of Christ upon thy shoulders, thou didst manfully endure the assault of the enemy, and having traversed the narrow way without turning back, thou didst attain the reward of the high calling; but as of old, O praise of monastics, unceasingly pray Christ to grant great mercy unto our souls!
O God-wise father Varlaam, having loved Christ from thy youth, and having enlightened thy heart with the divine rays of the All-Holy Spirit, thou wast truly radiant; therefore fulfill the petitions for profitable salvation of those who run unto thy temple, and pray Christ God to grant great mercy unto us!
O father Varlaam, having taken up thy cross upon thy shoulder, and having affectionately traversed the beautiful journey of Christ thy King, thou hast stood before those who dwell in the holy heavenly city; whence look down now upon those who honor thine ascetic struggles, O father, and pray Christ God that He may guide our steps into the way of peace!
*Glory: Tone 8:*
Having adorned thy life with much abstinence, O venerable one, thou didst show the way leading into life by the acquisition of virtues; having rejected the world, and despised all the beautiful things in the world, by a severe life thou didst wear away thy flesh, and by humility didst trample upon the proud serpent; therefore dwelling in Heaven, pray that our souls may be saved.
*Both now... Theotokion.*
*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 2. Podoben: "House of Ephratha" (До́ме є҆ѵфра́ѳовъ):*
Thou didst despise the passing life upon earth, O blessed one, that thou mightest obtain the endless life!
*Verse: Blessed is the man that fears the Lord; he shall greatly delight in His commandments.* (Ps. 111:1 [LXX])[^42]
For the sake of the love of Christ, O venerable Varlaam, Thou didst mortify the tormenting mindset of the flesh.

*To Varlaam, Abbot of the Caves.*
*Verse: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.* (Ps. 115:6 [LXX])[^43]
By thy prayers that clothe us, help in afflictions those who affectionately hymn thy memory, O God-blessed one!
*Glory: Tone 2:*
Having first entered into the cave, O venerable one, from thy labors for the sake of virtues thou didst pass unto the heavenly city, and didst attain the repose of the righteous and the rejoicing of the saints; therefore with them pray diligently for all who honor thy memory with faith, to be delivered from every hostile adversity, O God-wise Varlaam!
*Both now... Theotokion.*
*At Great Vespers.*
*Blessed is the man: the 1st antiphon.*
*On "Lord, I have cried", Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Being Called from on High" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*[^44]
Beholding the ever-flowing fountain of honey-sweet words proceeding from the eyes of the great father, O blessed Varlaam, as a hart thirsty for the flowing streams of living waters thou didst come with insatiable love; whence having watered thy soul with compunction, thou didst despise all the beautiful things of this world, and by words didst follow the Word: before Whom now standing with gladness *Service to the Venerable One.*
...standing before Him, pray that our souls may be saved and enlightened!
When the great luminaries hid beneath the confines of the holy cave, thou didst perceive them with noetic eyes, O venerable one; then, meeting them in youth with the light of virtues in thy heart, and kindled with divine fire to dwell together with them, thou didst leave behind the house of thy parents and thy friends, and becamest a fellow partaker with monastics; and now being numbered with the light-bearing choirs in Heaven, enlighten us earthborn mortals with the rays of grace, and grant gifts, praying Christ that our souls may be saved and enlightened!
When the God-blessed father showed unto thee the divine love of the good things to come, and the ever-flowing glory in the celestial bridal chamber, thou didst immediately count earthly good things as refuse: having despised riches, glory, the greatness of thy lineage, beautiful chambers, and thy betrothed bride, thou didst love Christ alone, and imitating His poverty, thou didst traverse the narrow path in ascetic struggles and labors, and after thy repose didst inherit the desired things: enjoying which now, pray unceasingly to grant peace unto the world, and to save our souls!
*Glory: Tone 8:*
Living upon earth in a corruptible composition, thou didst emulate the life of the bodiless hosts, O venerable father Varlaam: by the casting away of thy glory thou didst astonish men, by poverty and abstinence thou didst put the demons to shame, by purity thou didst gladden the angels, and by thy great ascetic struggles thou wast an example unto monastics; therefore worthily do we bless thee, adorned by the Master on high: Whom ever pray, O blessed one, to grant peace unto our EMPRESS,[^45] and great mercy unto our souls!
*Both now... Theotokion: The King of Heaven... The Entrance; Prokeimenon of the day; and 3 Readings for Venerable Ones (see fol. 1v).*
*At the Litiya, Stichera, Tone 1:*
Today the holy cave joyfully keeps festival, possessing thy holy relics, O blessed Varlaam; for they grant health unto those who run with faith unto thy venerable shrine; whence being enlightened, we say: count us worthy of thy gifts, O blessed one, and preserve thy flock by thy prayers, granting salvation unto our souls!
O venerable father Varlaam, having loved from youth the beauty of Christ the Master, and having an unwavering desire toward Him, thou didst leave behind fatherland and lineage, didst hate riches and glory, and following Him alone, didst live like an angel in the cave *To Varlaam, Abbot of the Caves.*
...therefore standing before Him now in Heaven with the angels, with boldness pray for our souls!
*Tone 1:*
Strengthened by the all-holding right hand, O venerable father Varlaam, thou didst acquire abstinence in food, and through unceasing prayer and nocturnal vigilance wast counted worthy to become a fellow partaker with blessed Anthony; with streams of tears thou didst drown all the demonic armies; therefore Christ also, Whom thou didst desire and for Whose sake thou didst bear the heat of the day, glorified thee with the gift of wonders: Whom pray that our souls may be saved!
*Tone 3:*
Come, all ye assemblies of monastics, with psalms and hymns let us piously praise today the honorable festival of the venerable one: who, even though he shone forth later, was counted worthy of the honor of the ancient fathers, and prays Christ that our souls may be saved!
An honorable receptacle of the All-Holy Spirit wast Thou, O venerable Varlaam; for imitating the bodiless hosts while in the flesh, Thou didst emulate their life; therefore Thou didst preserve the crowns of restraint in the right hand; and though Thou hast departed from earth, yet living unto God Thou standest before Him with the saints, and prayest for our souls!
*Glory: Tone 8:*
Having hated the beauties of this world from youth, O venerable one, from the heart thou didst love the One Who is fairer than the sons of men, and cleaving unto Him with all thy soul, as one bodiless upon earth living in a mortal and corruptible body, thou didst obtain the reward of incorruption; and now clearly beholding the Holy Trinity, pray diligently, O venerable one, to grant peace unto the world and save our souls!
*Both now... Theotokion: O Unwedded Virgin...*
*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 2. Podoben: "What shall We Call You, O Saints?" (Что́ ва́съ нарече́мъ ст҃і́и):*[^46]
What shall we name thee, O venerable one? An angel existing in the flesh, for thou didst live angelically upon earth? A luminary unto the venerable, for thou wast astonished at their struggles? Thou didst put the demons to shame, having subjected the spirit unto the bodily passions,[^47] and now standing before Christ, pray that our souls may be saved and enlightened!
*Verse: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.* (Ps. 115:6 [LXX])
What shall we proclaim thee, O glorious one? A living pillar, for thou didst abide unshaken in temptations? A river of living water *Service to the Venerable One.*
...pouring forth streams of grace; an excellent instructor of monks, equal in manner unto the bodiless hosts, and a fervent intercessor for our souls!
*Verse: The righteous man shall flourish like a palm tree, and like a cedar in Lebanon shall he be multiplied.* (Ps. 91:13 [LXX])[^48]
What shall we name thee, O father? Instructor of the uninstructed, fountain unto those in sickness, fragrant sprout of the cave, establishment of monks, ever-flowing wellspring of wonders, good ascetic, and speedy intercessor unto all who come unto thee with faith!
*Glory: Tone 8:*
O venerable father, having loved God from youth, thou didst prefer nothing above Him; having escaped the turmoil of the world and of this life, thou didst reach the tranquil haven of the father of the cave, and having settled in it as into a most beautiful bridal chamber, thou didst betroth thy soul unto the Master alone, having pre-purified it with fasting and prayers; therefore thou becamest a beautiful palace of the Holy Trinity, before Whom standing now with gladness, remember those who honor thy most honorable memory!
*Both now... Theotokion: O Theotokos, thou art the true vine...*
*At Matins, on "God is the Lord", Troparion, Tone 4:*
Having left behind the beautiful things of the world and its glory, thou didst run into the cave, where from strength thou didst advance unto strength, and becamest a leader of Christ's flock, O all-blessed Varlaam; which having shepherded well, shepherd also us from all evils, that we may bless thee!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion according to the tone.*
*Following the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5. Podoben: "The Word Co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́льное Сло́во):*
Come, ye assemblies of monastics, and beholding in the fathers the glory of God, open your lips in praise, blessing your Creator and Master Who is glorified in His saints, and sing in psalmody, saying: Unto us this honorable friend of Thine, O Christ God, their principalities are exceedingly strengthened; strengthen them, O Savior and Redeemer of our souls!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
My heart is established to hymn, O all-immaculate Virgin, thy most pure childbirth, whereby thou didst deliver us from the ancestral curse, and didst raise fallen human nature unto the height; therefore, as is our duty, we hymn and magnify thee!
*Following the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1. Podoben: "The Angelic Choir" (Ли́къ а҆́гг҃лскїй):*[^49]
Spiritual wings of knowledge and action thou didst receive from the words of the great father, O God-wise Varlaam, and having lifted up thy mind with them unto the height, thou didst escape *To Varlaam, Abbot of the Caves.*
...thou didst escape like a bird from the snare of the hunters, and didst attain the highest blessedness, where the choirs of the righteous abide; glory to Him Who gave thee this true desire; glory to Him Who gave thee victory over the passions; glory to Him Who brought thee into the eternal dwelling!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
O pure Virgin who didst receive the Holy Spirit into thy womb, and didst bear the Word begotten of the Father before the ages: bring us, who have made ourselves like unto irrational deeds, unto understanding, and grant direction unto our life, that we may ever magnify thee!
*Following the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8. Podoben: "Having Ascended the Wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости возше́дъ):*[^50]
Having enlightened the heat of divine words with brightness, thou didst leave behind the things that are in the darkness of this life, and having run unto the noetic Light, in the flesh thou didst receive an immaterial life with the bodiless hosts; whence having pre-purified thyself by the grace of the Spirit, thou wast an instructor of monastics, O God-wise Varlaam; therefore praising thee we cry aloud: pray Christ God to grant remission of transgressions unto those who honor thy holy memory with love!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
I have fallen into the mire of sinful pursuit, and there is no hope of rising for me, for I am grievously submerged by despair; but as thou didst bear the Hope of salvation, look down upon me thy servant, and deliver me from sin that destroys me, and from soul-corrupting passions, and from the perpetual malice of the deceitful enemy, O unwedded Theotokos; pray Christ God to grant me remission of sins, for I thy servant have thee as my hope!
*Songs of Ascents [Anabathmoi]: The 1st Antiphon of Tone 4.*[^51]
*Prokeimenon, Tone 4:*
*God is wonderful in His saints, the God of Israel.* (Ps. 67:36 [LXX])[^52]
*Verse: In the congregations bless ye God, the Lord from the springs of Israel.* (Ps. 67:27 [LXX])[^53]
*Gospel for a Venerable One. Sticheron following the Gospel, Tone 6:*
The glory and adornment of the venerable, citizen of Zion on high, earthly angel and heavenly man, fellow helper of fasters, and praise of those living in virginity, leader and beauty of monastics: come, ye assemblies of feast-lovers today, let us sing with them, having woven praiseworthy crowns with love, let us form a spiritual choir at the honorable tomb of our God-bearing father, and let us bring worthy hymns, singing in psalmody: Precious is thy death in the sight of the Lord, O venerable Varlaam! For though in spirit thou hast departed unto God, yet in body thou ever abidest with us, and fillest with spiritual gifts those who venerate thy relics; and now as one who hast acquired great boldness before the Lord, pray for those who hymn thine honorable dormition *Service to the Venerable One.*
...thine honorable dormition: to grant us likemindedness, strengthening unto the Orthodox EMPRESS,[^54] and confirmation unto the whole world!
*CANON to the Venerable One.*
*Tone 1. Ode 1. Heirmos:*
*Israel, delivered from bitter bondage, traversed the impassable sea as dry land; and beholding the enemy drowned, they sang a hymn unto God their Benefactor, through wonderworking with a high arm, for He is glorified!*
*Troparia:*
Seeing many captive to the bitter bondage of the cares of this world, O blessed one, thou didst consider that there is no deliverance from it, unless one follows the footsteps of Christ the Deliverer; therefore thou wast an unslothful zealot of Him. By thy vigilant prayer, O father, stir up also me who am despondent to praise thy struggles well, that I may quickly sing a hymn unto God, for He is glorified!
Nourished by the honey-sweet words from the great father, thou didst not desire to look back any longer, but forgetting what was behind, thou didst reach forth like a winged creature toward what was ahead, minding only one thing: to seek God with all thy heart, and ever to sing unto Him a hymn, for He is glorified!
Seeing the warm love of the venerable Anthony toward God, as a flame kindled in thy heart, O blessed one, thou didst return unto thy house rejoicing, as having found a treasure; but the resolve of thy will thou didst leave at the feet of the God-blessed father; thereafter running back unto him, thou didst ever sing unto God: For He is glorified!
*Glory:*
Taking heed unto the light of the God-inspired Anthony, Thou didst burst the hindering nets of worldly love, and running unto him, Thou wast a constant hearer of his teaching, having obtained with him unto God, for He is glorified!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Drive away by thine intercession the afflictions and enemy assaults that ever vex me, O pure Virgin; and put me not to shame from mine expectation, O Mother of the Lord; for thou alone with thy Son art the hope of the hopeless.
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
*The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is the stone whereupon Christ established the Church, which He redeemed from the nations.*
*Troparia:*
Thou, O Christ, the unhewn stone, wast the head of the corner; and upon Thee Thy venerable one, desiring to build a spiritual house, laid the walls of the resolve of humility, poverty, and other virtues; therefore having come unto the great father, he prayed him, saying: Establish me on the rock of the Lord's commandments, O venerable one!

*To Varlaam, Abbot of the Caves.*
Coming in glory and riches with many servants unto the holy cave, thou didst cast down under the feet of the elder all thy beautiful ornaments, and thyself, together casting down the pride of the deceitful one.
Knowing, O father, that a noetic treasure was hidden in the holy cave, thou didst sell all the wealth brought by thee and thyself unto Anthony; and like a prudent merchant, thou didst buy that field wherein thou didst enrich thyself with this, through which we also glorify thee after death!
*Glory:*
Having understood, O venerable one, that the wealth, glory, and all honor of this age are like a shadow that quickly passes away, therefore thou didst count all things as refuse, that thou mightest gain Christ alone, and didst love to walk the path of monastics toward Him!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O pure Dawn, who didst bear the Cultivator of the Highest: brush aside and cleanse the path of my life that is slandered by lawless passions, that the path of virtues may appear unto me, and a right spirit may be renewed in my inward parts!
*Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*
Seeing thy desire to be unfeigned, the great one among the fathers placed upon thee the helmet of salvation, clothed thee in the breastplate of righteousness, and numbered thee in the monastic regiment; and thou, a soldier appointed unto the Heavenly Kingdom, didst fight well against visible and invisible enemies; having conquered whom, thou didst receive the crown of heavenly honor: which pray that we also may obtain, O venerable Varlaam, who honor thy holy memory with love!
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Grant help from on high against the enemies that war against me, O Virgin Bride of God, and teach me, as one who knowest their crafty snares, to conquer them; for none shall vanquish them unless thou assistest those who especially honor thy most immaculate childbirth with love!
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
*The seer Habakkuk heard Thy marvelous report, O Christ, and cried aloud with fear: God shall come from Teman, and the Holy One from the mountain overshadowed with dense thicket, to save His anointed ones: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!*
*Troparia:*
When the three warring adversaries heard that thou wast stripped of their love, when thou didst clothe thyself in the robe of salvation, they raged against thee, and stirred up fearful things against thee through thy father; but thou didst place thy hope in the power of the Lord.
Thy foolish father ran unto the flock at the cave with many servants, and having dispersed all, he dragged thee away, who wast bound with warmest love unto God, hoping in Whom thou didst cry aloud: *Service to the Venerable One.*
...Glory to Thy power, O Lord!
Dragging thee outside, O blessed one, thy father stripped off the holy mantle and threw it into the ravine, and likewise the helmet of salvation, which was upon thy head, he tore off; then he clothed thee in bright and costly raiment; but thou, despising all these things, didst unswervingly obey the angelic life, calling aloud and crying: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!
*Glory:*
Clothed by force in a most radiant garment, thou didst trample it into the mire, together with it trampling upon the proud enemy, who flits about with various guises unto those who hasten to sing: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Strip me, covered with the shame of grievous iniquities, O Theotokos, and make clean my defiled garment by thy grace, granting me repentance and compunction, that I may thankfully stand before thee, crying: Rejoice, garment of boldness for the naked, O Sovereign Lady!
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
*Shine forth Thine unwaning light, O Christ, unto all who diligently sing of Thee, granting us peace which surpasses understanding; therefore from the night of ignorance running by day in Thy light, we glorify Thee, O Lover of mankind!*
*Troparia:*
Led by force into the house in bright robes, O blessed one, the unwaning light of God illumined thy heart, therefore thou didst not look upon the deceptive light, but beholding God alone before thee, thou didst zealously glorify Him as the Lover of mankind!
Placing the Master before thee Who calls unto the immortal supper, O venerable one, thou didst count the table of delicacies as nothing, but looking down with thine eyes, thou didst lift up thy mind unto the height, contemplating the banquet of the Heavenly King and His blessedness, which is the true feast in the Heavenly Kingdom!
Preparing a beautiful chamber in thy heart for the Spirit, thou didst not sit in a place of renown in thy palace, but in a single corner thou didst choose to select a narrow living-place to glorify the Lord, the Lover of mankind!
*Glory:*
Thou didst not incline thy heart unto the deceitful words of thy seductress, nor didst thou ascend onto her couch, taking heed unto what was spoken: "The venerable ones shall rejoice in glory upon their beds,"[^55] of which thou art now a partaker and heir!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
Raise me up lying upon the couch of evil deeds, O Theotokos, and by thy guidance direct my life, that I may spend the remaining time in repentance, and glorify thee, O blessed Maiden, pure Mother!
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
*Held fast in the depth of sins am I, O Christ, and tempest-tossed in the sea of life; but as Jonah from the monster, raise me up also from passions, and save me!*

*To Varlaam, Abbot of the Caves.*
*As Thou didst receive Jonah, grant me also to endure the beast, that in the voice of praise and in the spirit of salvation I may sacrifice unto Thee.*
*Troparia:*
Wholly encompassed by the afflictions of temptation, thou didst call unto the Lover of mankind, O venerable one, that He deliver thee from the snare of the hunters; and thou didst remain for three days fasting, nourishing thy soul with prayer alone, and saying with the prophet: "Raise me up from corruption, O God, and the remaining years I shall sacrifice unto Thee in an incorruptible spirit!"[^56]
Concerning thee, O blessed one, when in so great an affliction, diligent prayer was made by the holy ones in the cave; for the righteous cried, and the Lord heard them,[^57] and delivered thee from sorrow, inclining the heart of thy father unto mercy, who had compassion on thy patience, and having affectionately kissed thee, dismissed thee in peace unto the holy cave!
A mournful voice was heard then in the house of thy father: thy parents wept for thee, their beloved son; thy wife bitterly lamented thee, her betrothed; the manservants and maidservants groaned with weeping for their master, and all escorted thee with tears; but thou, having departed rejoicing as from Egypt into the Promised Land, offered thanksgiving unto God, for He delivered thee from the cruel ruler of the world!
*Glory:*
Glory to Thee, the one God glorified in the Holy Trinity, for Thou hast saved Thy servant from the deceitful adversary: from whom save also us through the prayers of Thine obedient favorite, who honor his memory!
*Both now... Theotokion:*
O thou who art the best helper of those submerged in passions, stretch forth a hand unto the wretched, that having been saved through thee, in a true spirit we may offer praise unto thee with thine Only-Begotten Son and God!
*Kontakion, Tone 2:*
Thou wast not terrified by thy father's threat, thou didst not submit unto his flatteries, neither wast thou cast down by womanly deceit, O most honored Varlaam, but didst remain unconquered by them, hoping in thy Lord: Whom pray for our souls!
*Ikos:*
Beholding thy voluntary suffering, who shall not be astonished, O most blessed Varlaam? For thou didst expose demonic snares, and manfully resisting lustful passions, how greatly didst thou suffer from adversity, that thou mightest present thy pure soul unto God: Whom also pray diligently for us, O father, that we may be delivered from carnal passions *Service to the Venerable Father*

...and the plundering of invisible enemies, having become our helper through thy help and thine unceasing prayers unto God for our souls.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^58]
For them that passed through the furnace as a bridal chamber, He quenched the furnace flame; they that were of old the holy youths for the sake of piety were clearly shown forth, and singing in concord, they chanted a hymn: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparia:*
Thou didst pass through the furnace of temptation, O venerable father, and remaining unburned by it, thou didst rather burn up the enemy that invisibly waged war against thee, having been helped against him by the prayers of thy director; for he cried out unceasingly: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

Anthony marveled at thy longsuffering, that warring against bitter Belial, thou didst not fear his multifaceted snares, wherewith though thou wast hindered, yet by endurance and humility didst thou conquer, and didst chant the hymn of the youths: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

When thou didst return with victory unto the fathers in the cave, all were filled with joy, for they beheld thee bearing a crown; and thy elder also rejoiced, receiving thee in health; therefore they also cried out: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Glory:*
Anthony was refreshed, for upon the root of his teachings thou didst grow up in virtues as a fair-flowering tree, O venerable one, and the shoot of thy youth brought forth elder-like grace as fruit; marveling, he cried out thanksgiving: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Both now:*
The tree of my life that rots away with sins, do thou sprinkle with the dew from thy grace, O pure Virgin, that it may at least put forth small branches, and bring forth for this reason the fair fruit of humility and prayer, crying out: Rejoice, acquisition of the immortal fruit!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^59]
The dew-bearing furnace of old showed forth the image of a supernatural wonder; for the fire burned not the young children, revealing Christ's seedless birth. Therefore singing, let us chant: Let all creation bless the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparia:*
A wonder! For the gift began to operate while thou wast yet in thy youth; which Anthony beholding, placed thee as leader in his stead over the brethren, and thou wast a good shepherd unto the fathers that sing and supremely exalt the Lord unto ages.

Receiving the beginning of rulership, thou didst receive also a new beginning unto thy life, O father; for thou didst add labors unto labors, and ascetic feats unto watchful vigil; so that all that desire to sing unto the Lord unto ages might have thee as an example.

*To Varlaam, Hegumen of the Caves*

A good director and father wast thou, O blessed one; for as thou thyself didst never alter thy rule, so also didst thou instruct the brethren and beseech them to have diligence for salvation, and to care for the soul, the immortal thing, if they desire to supremely exalt the Lord with the angels unto ages.

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.*

Seeking the noetic Jerusalem, thou didst journey as a pilgrim unto the sensible one, sweetly enduring every labor on the path for Christ's sake; and having affectionately kissed His life-giving tomb, thou didst return, singing unto the Lord unto ages.

*Both now:*
All-hymned Mother, Lady of the heavenly ranks, Queen of heaven and earth, turn me away from earthly things, and grant me to seek the things on high; and vouchsafe that in the Jerusalem above I may sing to thee, and supremely exalt the Lord unto ages.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^60]
The ineffable mystery of the Virgin: for she showed herself to be the heaven, and the cherubic throne, and the radiant bridal chamber of Christ God the Almighty. Her do we piously magnify as the Mother of God.

*Troparia:*
Enjoying ineffable joy, O father, now with the angelic ranks thou standest before the never-waning light; and guide us by thy prayers to walk in the light of the Lord's commandments.

From this life thou hast removed unto eternal life, O venerable father, and hast received the place of resting. Keep our life, tempest-tossed by the assaults of adversaries, unharmed; and grant us by thy guidance to attain the dwelling-place of paradise.

Returning from the city of Constantine, thou didst depart with thy soul into the most radiant city of the heavenly King; and thy relics thou didst leave in the monastery of the Holy Mountain, which thou didst command to be brought into the cave, where they now lie incorrupt, pouring forth a fountain of miracles unto them that have recourse thereto.

*Glory:*
O Thou Who art glorified in Thy saints, O only God, glorify all them that faithfully bow down before Thy magnificent glory; for lo, we bring unto Thee in prayer Thine agreeable servant, for whose sake save us all.

*Both now:*
We hymn thee, O Virgin, with unceasing mouths, and as is meet we call thee blessed; for thou alone art blessed among women, having given birth unto the Father's Only-begotten Word: with Whom we, the faithful, together magnify thee.

*Exapostilarion:*
Wounded in thy soul, O venerable father, with the sweet longing for the love of Christ, and desiring to be filled with it, from the corrupt world *Service to the Venerable Father*

...with its lusts thou didst turn away; leaving home and parents, thou didst neglect thy betrothed; therefore thou hast received a hundredfold, and dost inherit life eternal: whereof, that we also be not deprived, grant help by thy prayers.

*Theotokion:*
O fair bridal chamber, Mary, Bride of God, longing for whose beauty the Most High, by the operation of the All-Holy Spirit, dwelt within thy most consecrated womb and took flesh for our sake: make my soul a bridal chamber for the indwelling of the heavenly King, that I, thine unprofitable servant, may receiving, magnify thee.

*On the Praises, Stichera, Tone 4. Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*[^61]
A life unspotted, endurance, meekness, and unfeigned love toward God, great abstinence, divine compunction having acquired, O father, like an angel didst thou live in the flesh, O blessed Varlaam, intercessor for our souls.

A house for thy soul, a house prepared beforehand for the Holy Spirit, thou didst despise the beauty of this world; and having laid an immovable foundation upon the rock of the Lord's commandments, thou didst complete it by the height of humility, and becamest a dwelling of the Most Holy Trinity: Whom do thou entreat for us, to give beauty unto our souls, and to make us receivers of spiritual grace.

By the renunciation of the world and carnal things, thou didst bring the fruits of virtues unto God from the noetic tree of thy life, O most blessed father; receiving from Him the reward of thy labors, thou dost now delight in the tree of life in paradise: whereof to be vouchsafed its sweetness, do thou entreat Jesus the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls.

*Glory, Tone 8:*
The great director of monastics among the fathers, seeing thy warmth toward God, O father, directed by many virtues, entrusted unto thee the God-gathered flock, which having well pastured upon the pastures of thine instruction, thou didst lead whole unto Christ God, the Chief Shepherd: Whom do thou now entreat, to preserve His spiritual flock in peace, and to protect it from destroying wolves.

*Both now. Theotokion:*
O Sovereign Lady, accept the supplication of thy servants, and deliver us from every distress and sorrow.

*Great Doxology, and Dismissal. At the Liturgy, the service to the venerable one, Common.*
