# MONTH OF MARCH

*On the 28th Day: Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr Eustratius of the Caves.*[^152]

*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly ranks" (Нб҃ныхъ чинѡ́въ):*[^153]
O thou who formerly didst crucify thyself willingly unto the world by the cutting off of all passions, thou now afterward having become a partaker of eternal suffering, sufferest the passion of ultimate mortification, being nailed upon a cross by the Jews, like a lamb *Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr*

...like a ladder ascending unto heaven: whence look down, O blessed Eustratius; lift up our minds unto heavenly things, and pray unto Christ God to grant us the cutting off of all passions.

A second Elijah on a chariot wast thou shown forth through zeal for Christ, O marvelous one: for even as he rebuked the madness of the king, so thou also didst reprove the God-slaying deed of the foolish Jew, and wast crucified by him in the body upon a cross; but thy soul ran on a chariot of fire unto heavenly realms, where thou rejoicest, illuminated by the unwaning Light: of which let us not be deprived, through thy prayers.

Depicting the passion of thy Master, thou wast crucified upon a cross by a Jew, by whom being pierced in the ribs with a spear, having mounted a chariot of fire, thou didst signify thy flaming love toward God, before Whom now standing in joy, pray thou for them that hymn thee, O blessed father Eustratius.

*Glory: Tone 2:*
In heaven unto eternal glory, O venerable one, having formerly inflamed thy body with abstinence, and afterward having thy hands and feet nailed by the Jews, thou didst find the ladder of desire, whereby having ascended unto the heavenly hosts, the majestic voice called unto thee: This is a good citizen of heavenly Zion: wherein now rejoicing with the saints, remember us that honor thy holy memory, that we also may become heirs thereof with thee.

*Both now: Theotokion: The shadow of the law has passed:*

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. And the readings:*
*Three from the Common of the Venerable (see fol. 30v).*[^154]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 4. Podoben: "Called from on high" (Вꙁва́нный):*[^155]
O all-praised venerable-martyr Eustratius, thou didst set forth thy life in care for virtues; for with hymnody the faithful praise Christ in faith, obeying thy teachings: standing before Him, entreat the Holy Spirit that they may be established in peace.

*Verse:* Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.[^156]
O God-inspired martyr Eustratius, thy life, equal unto the angels, was sanctified on earth, and thine honorable confession was brought unto the heavenly ones like a sweet-smelling sacrifice and good incense; and now pray thou to grant our souls peace and great mercy.

*Verse:* Blessed is the man that fears the Lord; in His commandments he will desire exceedingly.[^157]

*To Eustratius of the Caves*

Never, O venerable-martyr Eustratius, having become conqueror of passions, didst thou yield to the tormentor: dancing together with the crown-bearers, O blessed one, remember all them that celebrate thy memory; and now pray thou unto Christ to grant our souls peace and great mercy.

*Glory: Tone 3:*
The Jews understood not the words of the prophets, going astray from the womb, speaking a lie against our Son, and by the reproach of the curse condemned Him to the death of the cross; but thou, O blessed one, having been clothed with divine grace, didst show forth the blessing given unto the whole world by the crucifixion: therefore the lawless ones nailed thee to the cross, and pierced thy ribs with a spear, not knowing that for thy cross thou receivest recompense from God, while the ungodly shall quickly perish: they inherited the curse by the tree, but thou the enjoyment of the tree of life: of which let us also be partakers with thee, as we glorify the Son and Lord in thy memory, O father Eustratius.

*Both now: Theotokion: Look down upon the supplications of thy servants:*

*Troparion, Tone 7:*
Thou wast shown forth as the praise of ascetics and martyrs, O venerable Eustratius: for having first mortified the flesh by abstinence, thou didst follow Christ, bearing thy cross; and afterward being thyself lifted up upon a cross, and pierced in the ribs with a spear, thou didst commit thy soul into the hands of God; being borne up unto heaven on a chariot of fire, thou wast doubly crowned by Christ God and the Savior of our souls.
*Glory, both now: Theotokion.*

*At Matins, on "God is the Lord," the same Troparion.*
*After Kathisma 1, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "The angelic choir" (Ли́къ а҆́гг҃лскїй):*[^158]
Assimilating thyself unto thy Master by passion, thou didst magnificently take up the wood of the cross, and by thy God-inspired lips didst put to shame the blaspheming Jew: therefore the ungodly one pierces thy ribs with a spear, while thou rejoicest, ascending unto thy Lord; pray unceasingly unto Him for us, who out of love honor thy sufferings, O noble and wise Eustratius.
*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
An angelic hymn the unwedded Virgin brings unto thee: Rejoice, thou who art full of grace and blessed among women, who didst receive in thy womb *Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr*

...who didst receive in thy womb the Word of God seedlessly, transforming men from seraphic works.

*After Kathisma 2, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5. Podoben: "Co-unoriginate Word" (Собеꙁнача́лное):*[^159]
The citizen of the heavenly Jerusalem, the true witness and good soldier of Christ, noble and wise Eustratius, companion of the venerable and fellow-dancer of the martyrs, let us honor with hymns, O ye people, singing his memory: for standing before Christ God, he unceasingly prays for our souls.

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
O Queen of the heavenly Jerusalem, sole immaculate Mother among women, crown of virgins and consolation of the saints, unceasing intercessor before the Holy Trinity: remember us who have wandered far, O Mother of God, and deliver from perils them that hymn thee.

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 1. Podoben: "Make haste to anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*[^160]
Kindled by the flame of burning love toward the crucified Christ, Thou didst not fear the God-slaying Hebrew breathing fury, but didst reprove him manfully, and didst proclaim from the Scriptures the fulfillment of the legal passover, grace having arrived: for this cause Thou wast nailed upon a cross by the Jews, and having received the crown of confession, didst enter into the heavenly courts, where forget not us also, O father Eustratius.

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Unto us who are burned by the flame of passions from destructive assaults, make haste quickly to send down the water of grace, O all-pure Virgin, and quench the fire that burns in our members, that leading this life in quietness, through thee we may radiantly enjoy the illumination of Christ.

*Gradual Hymns, the 1st Antiphon of Tone 4.*
*Prokeimenon:* Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His venerable ones.
*Verse:* What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He has rendered unto me?[^161]
*Let every breath praise the Lord.*
*The Gospel from Mark, pericope 37.*
*After Psalm 50, Sticheron, Tone 2:*
Today the hill of the Caves rejoices, possessing thy relics like a priceless treasure, and is illuminated by the beams of thy sufferings, bringing flowers of spiritual songs unto thee and saying: O servant of Christ and our father Eustratius, standing before the Master of all, unceasingly pray unto the Lord for us, to send down great mercy upon our souls.

*To Eustratius of the Caves*

*Canon to the Venerable-Martyr, Tone 1. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^162]
Thy victorious right hand has been divinely glorified in power, O Immortal One: for being almighty, it shattered adversaries, fashioning a new path through the deep for the Israelites.

*Troparia:*
Desiring today, and seeking to be a partaker of light forever, in the day of the passions, O blessed one, thou wast lifted up upon a cross, and wast perfected in the passion of Christ, having conquered the world; and now being a partaker of ineffable glory, rejoicing, thou singest a victorious song.

Nothing in this tumultuous world didst thou ever love as the Cross of Christ, and having it as a boast, with the Apostle thou didst cry aloud: God forbid that I should boast, save only in the Cross of the Lord, wherein rejoicing, thou singest a victorious song.

Nailed unto the cross by the Jews, O all-blessed father, thou didst nail all corruptible passions, so that with the Apostle thou didst say: The world is crucified unto me, and I unto the world; therefore worthily now rejoicing with the heavenly hosts, thou singest a victorious song.

*Glory:*
Departing from the voluptuous Egypt of this world, upon the all-honorable Cross thou didst traverse its sea untroubled; and writing against it with thy nailed palms, thou didst drown the noetic Pharaoh with his aerial warriors, and now rejoicing, singest a victorious song.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Departing from this voluptuous world as from Egypt, I return unto former things, pleasing myself by feeding on husks: grant me, O Virgin Bride of God, to turn away at least a little from these, that being free I may bring praise unto thee, singing a victorious song.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*[^163]
O Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human nature, and didst mercifully take form therein, gird me from on high with power, that I may cry aloud unto Thee: Holy art Thou, O animate temple of Thine ineffable glory, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparia:*
Thou didst follow Him Who said: Come unto Me, all ye that labor, O father, and by His Cross didst disperse the noetic enemies, who being conquered unto the end were confounded with shame: pray thou also for us, that we may stand against their warfare, for upon thee are we established.

Laying the cross upon Thy shoulder, O holy one, by unchangeable faith with love thou didst cleave unto the One, that thou mightest gain Christ, and according to Thy desire didst find Him by the narrow path *Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr*

...which thou didst traverse without stumbling, having the Cross as dominion and confirmation.

Clothed in the monastic habit, thou wast stripped of the world, O blessed one, and having lightly traversed the path into the promised land, into the Monastery of the Caves, which flows like milk and honey with the teachings of the God-bearing fathers, wherewith being nourished, thou wast strong: by their teachings grant that we also may be confirmed.

*Glory:*
By the word of prayer and fasting having subjected the murderous enemy unto the spirit, and by humility and obedience having conquered the noetic Amalek, thou wast pleasing unto thy Master, O father, and from Him didst receive the Cross, an invincible weapon, wherein we also are confirmed.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Tossed by the enemy's assaults, having no confirmation, I, the passionate one, flee unto thee, O Mother of God: for thou art an unbreakable wall unto the faithful, wherein we are confirmed.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:*[^164]
Come, ye people, let us hymn the memory of the victorious sufferer, passing the day of his rejoicing with odes and songs: for from this valley of weeping he passed unto unending joy, and standing visibly before the throne, he cries aloud unto us: Become emulators of me, even as I am of Christ, Who grants His abundant mercy unto all.

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Having a passionate life in the world, I find myself tossed by deadly winds; O thou that didst bear Christ the Helmsman, direct my life unto the calm haven, I pray, and grant me thy rich mercy.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^165]
Beholding with foresighted eyes thee as a mountain overshadowed by the grace of God, Habakkuk proclaimed that the Holy One of Israel should come forth from thee, for our salvation and renewal.

*Troparia:*
Sole venerable one, for the sake of the incontinence of the first-fashioned man who introduced hell, thou didst betake thyself unto abstinence and severe fasting, and therefrom didst acquire the name of faster: for by the word of God rather than by meats wast thou nourished, singing unto Christ: For Thou art almighty.

Having been taken captive by the Polovtsians by the permission of the Most High, with apostolic words Thou didst cry aloud: He is free who has captivated his mind unto the obedience of Christ; therefore in affliction Thou didst not faint, but rather marveling at the judgments of God, didst glorify Christ: For Thou art almighty.

*To Eustratius of the Caves*

Always bearing upon thy body the image of Jesus Who was betrayed, thou wast sold into the Greek land in the city of Korsun unto a certain God-slaying Jew, and he strove to turn thee away from the love of God, for which thou wast ready even unto death, singing: Christ is risen, for He is almighty.

*Glory:*
Shut up in prison with other Christians, and tormented with hunger, O holy one, enduring sweetly, thou didst say: Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Neither hunger, nor death, nor distress: for all these things are small compared to the glory that shall be revealed in us, who sing: Christ is risen, for He is almighty.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Having been taken captive by noetic enemies, and being already near the prison of hades, unto thee, O Compassionate One, do I cry aloud: O Mother of God, when thou shalt stir up the captivity of my soul, then shall my tongue be filled with joy, and shall sing: Thy Son is risen, for He is almighty.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^166]
O Christ Who didst enlighten the ends of the world with the radiance of Thy coming, and illuminated them with Thy Cross: enlighten with the light of Thy divine knowledge the hearts of them that sing of Thee in orthodox manner.

*Troparia:*
Oppressed by love for the Cross, O noble Eustratius, resisting them that compelled thee alone to apostatize from thy Christ, Who is the Sun of righteousness, bringing life unto all that cry aloud.

By thy teaching, O father, all the captives who were in prison spat upon the murderous intent of the God-slaying Hebrew, and chose rather to die of hunger and thirst than to deny Christ, Who gives life unto all.

A regiment of fifty martyrs slain by hunger and thirst by the Jew, thou didst send before thee unto thy Christ, O blessed one, thyself intending afterward to suffer the same for the love of Him Who gives life unto all.

*Glory:*
The wretched one, seeing that by the death of all the prisoners in the dungeon he had buried his gold and his hope of drawing them into his impiety, purposed to crucify thee upon a cross, unto which thou didst run hastily, ever calling aloud: O my Christ, lead forth my soul from the prison of the flesh!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
O all-blessed Virgin, have compassion by thy grace upon my soul that perishes in sin, and satisfy it with angelic bread, and water it from the stream of the Church's sweetness, that I may find peace, a comfort for my soul.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^167]
The lowest abyss has surrounded us, and there is none to deliver; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter *Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr*

...we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter: save Thy people, O our God, for Thou art the strength of the weak and their restoration.

*Troparia:*
Like a water-beast's belly was the womb of the lawless Jew, which sought to swallow thee up and bring thee into the number of the darkened Jewish assembly; but thou, O father, didst boldly stand against his gaping jaws, and didst overcome him, being strengthened by the help of the Most High.

On the day when the Jews raged and hung the Giver of life upon the cross, then the wretched Hebrew crucified thee also: that with Him with Whom thou didst suffer, with Him thou mightest be glorified, O blessed one, in unwaning light, having received recompense from His Son.

Remembering the curse with mockery, the foul God-slayers, even as their fathers did unto Christ, so mockingly nailed thee to the wood of the cross, O father; but the wood of the cross was foolishness unto them, but unto thee salvation and the power of God.

*Glory:*
Thou didst reprove the foul feasts of the Jews, hanging upon the cross, O holy one, not fearing their curse, as thou didst possess Christ God Who was nailed in the flesh and enlightened the world by His three-day resurrection.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Thou that didst destroy the ancestral curse by thy blessed childbirth, O pure Mother: I pray thee to vouchsafe me the blessing of God's chosen ones, O blessed and glorified one.

*Kontakion, Tone 8:*[^168]
As a zealot of the Master's passions and an agreeable follower of the ascetics, having instructed the captives by thy teaching, thou didst bring them unto Christ, O Eustratius; and deliver us who are captive unto passions by thy supplications, and show us to be emulators of thine honorable life, who cry aloud: Rejoice, thou that didst sweetly drink the cup of Christ's sufferings!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^169]
Thee, O Mother of God, we the faithful perceive as a noetic furnace: for as the Supremely Exalted One saved the three youths, so the Almighty One renewed the world in thy womb, the praised and supremely glorified God of our fathers.

*Troparia:*
Nailed upon the cross, O father, thou didst reprove the foolish Jewish command from prophetic writings, and didst not desire to see the legal passover, desiring to be a partaker of the Passover which was sacrificed for us in the world, Christ God the blessed.

Citing from the prophets the destruction of the curse that came from the tree, O blessed one, and preaching the entrance of life in the Life hanging upon the wood of the cross before their eyes, thou didst confound the Jews, teaching them to sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*To Eustratius of the Caves*

Unto the Jews that crucified thee thou didst prophesy, O venerable father: Vengeance will soon come upon you for my blood and for that of the other Christians whom ye have innocently slain, and your feasts shall be turned into mourning: for the Lord hates your piety; unto whom thou wast prepared to sing: Blessed art Thou, O Christ God!

*Glory:*
Thy words were sharp arrows unto the lawless ones, wherewith being pierced they could not endure, but pierced thy ribs with a spear; and thou didst depart unto Him Who poured forth life unto the whole world from His pierced side, to sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Pierced with the spear of sin by fierce Belial, make haste to apply the plaster of thy mercy, O Virgin Mother and Bride of God, that being healed I may ever sing unto thee, O Lady: Blessed art thou!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^170]
In the furnace the Israelite youths, as in a smelter, shone purer than gold with the beauty of piety, saying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; sing and supremely exalt Him unto all ages.

*Troparia:*
Honor, ye people, the good sufferer, who having formerly crucified all his members mentally within himself, afterward, being visibly crucified by the Jews, cried aloud unto Christ: I bear Thy wounds upon my body, and I sing of Thee unto all ages.

When thou wast borne up unto heaven, O father, on a chariot and horses of fire, the choirs of martyrs and righteous ones came forth to meet thee, and receiving thy God-praised wounds of hands and feet, and thy pierced side, they received thee into the habitation of the saints, to praise the Lord unto ages.

A voice was brought unto thee from the majestic glory, O holy one: Behold, the good heavenly citizen, of whom the whole world was not worthy, ascends from this earthly world unto the heavenly habitations, to sing unto the Lord unto all ages.

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: the Lord.*
The lawless Hebrew, wishing to hide the priceless treasure of thy relics, O most blessed one, cast them into the sea; but by the providence of God they were found here in the cave: bowing before which, we glorify the Lord Who glorified thee with incorruption and wonders, unto all ages.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
O priceless treasure of the most precious Jesus, who didst proceed from maternal loins: deliver my soul from the captivity of the voluptuous world, that I may sing of thee *Service to the Holy Venerable-Martyr*

...here, and in the unwaning light unto the ages.

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^171]
The unburnt bush showed forth an image of thy pure childbirth: and now we pray thee to extinguish the furnace of temptations against us, that we may unceasingly magnify thee, O Mother of God.

*Troparia:*
Shine, shine, O Monastery of the Caves of the Mother of God: for in thee marvelous Eustratius grew up as a flourishing tree, adorned with the sweet-smelling flowers of suffering: that we also may become partakers of his beauty, we bow before his relics and magnify him with hymns.

God Who reveals secrets showed the wretched murderers unto the pious Emperor,[^172] how the Jews hypocritically kill Christians in secret; and unrighteousness came upon their head: for now they openly received retribution according to thy prophecy, O most blessed father.

Thy blood, poured forth innocently by the Jews, O father, like Abel's cried out unto God, Who by a righteous judgment brought vengeance for it; and thy tormentor, having been hanged upon a tree, received the lot of Judas in Gehenna; while the Lord took thee as His child, Whom bowing down before today we magnify.

*Glory:*
Thy blood, O holy one, and thy unfailing prophecy afterward brought many unto faith: for seeing the fulfillment thereof, having truly repented they believed in Christ, and having been baptized they lived well, and were vouchsafed blessed life; of which let not us also be deprived, as we bow before the shrine of thy relics and magnify thee.

*Both now: Theotokion:*
O blameless Mother, who didst embrace eternal Life in thy womb: direct us from temporal life unto eternal life through thy mediation, and grant unto all to behold the unwaning day of the Kingdom of thy Son.

*Exapostilarion:*
Foreseeing eternal life, O blessed one, thou didst take no thought for the temporal, bravely enduring all things inflicted by the Jews; and refusing to taste of the legal passover, thou wast nailed to the cross, and ascending from earth upon a fiery chariot, didst testify: Christ is the Passover of incorruption, the salvation of the world.

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Thou art the foreshadowed sanctuary of the Ever-Existing Light, O Virgin Bride of God: stand forth unto us that dwell in darkness, and direct our life, that without stumbling we may attain to Eustratius of the Caves*

...to where Christ is, the Passover of incorruption, the salvation of the world.

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 6:*
Having reddened thy robe, dyed purple with martyr's blood, O venerable one, worthily hast thou entered into the Holy of Holies, O glorious one, where is the unwaning light, where is divine glory, where is the voice of them that feast: from Whom for the labors of thy suffering thou hast received the victories, and an unfading crown, and everlasting good glory, and the life of paradise, O all-wise Eustratius; with boldness pray for our souls. *(Twice)*

The tempests of torments did not shake thy soul, nor did the long confinement in the dark prison hide thee, O glorious sufferer; but like a light thou didst shine forth in darkness as a beacon unto the pious, whom thou didst reveal as sons of the day through the laver of baptism, by rebirth of water and salvation, and by divine abiding: as a God-pleasing sufferer, O all-wise Eustratius.

Who shall worthily recount the victories of thy virtues? What lips shall utter thy sufferings and endurance? For in both thou didst well-please Christ, O Eustratius; and now cease not praying, O servant of God, to save our souls, as having great boldness, O ever-memorable one.

*Glory: Tone 8:*
The Cherub, seeing thee ascending unto heavenly realms on a fiery chariot, with his flaming sword did not forbid thee to taste of the tree of life, but with joy received thee, O martyr of Christ, Eustratius. Therefore now enjoying the food of paradise, forget not us that honor thy memory, but stretch forth thy palms, wherewith thou wast nailed to the tree, and pray for all unto the Trinity and Christ, that we also may become partakers with thee, O most blessed father.

*Both now: Theotokion.*
*Great Doxology, and dismissal.*

[^173]: Woodcut engraving of Saint Niphont, Bishop of Novgorod (*С: Нїфо́нтъ Епⷭ҇копъ Новгородскій*), Discourse 14 of the Kyiv-Pechersk Paterikon. Tonsured at the Kyiv Caves Monastery where he shone in obedience and asceticism, he was consecrated Bishop of Novgorod in 1130. A zealous champion of canonical Orthodoxy, he refused to sanction the uncanonical marriage of Prince Svyatoslav Olgovich, firmly withstood Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich's attempt to appoint Clement Smolyatich as Metropolitan of Kyiv without the blessing of the Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople, and suffered imprisonment in the Caves for his fidelity to the canons. Returning to the Caves before his death, he was granted a vision of St. Theodosius shortly before falling asleep in the Lord on April 8, 1156; his incorrupt relics repose in the Near Caves.
