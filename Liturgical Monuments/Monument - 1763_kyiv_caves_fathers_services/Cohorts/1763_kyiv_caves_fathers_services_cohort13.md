*RESURRECTIONAL THEOTOKIA OF THE EIGHT TONES:*
*At Vespers, at "Lord, I have cried," and at the Aposticha.*[^701]

*Tone 1. At "Lord, I have cried":*[^702]
Let us sing of the Virgin Mary, the glory of the universe, who budded forth from men and bare the Master, the heavenly gate, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful! For she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead: she broke down the middle wall of enmity, brought in peace, and opened the Kingdom! Having her therefore as the confirmation of faith, we have as champion the Lord born of her. Take courage therefore, take courage, O people of God: for He will conquer the enemies, as He is almighty!
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^703]
Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for a Virgin hast thou given birth, and after giving birth hast remained as before childbirth: for He that was born was God; therefore nature also was transformed anew. But O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants brought unto thee in thy temple: but as bearing the Compassionate One in thy hands, take pity upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved!
*Tone 2. Theotokion:*[^704]
The shadow of the law hath passed away now that grace hath come: for as the bush was not consumed while burning with fire, even so a Virgin hast thou given birth, and a Virgin hast thou remained; instead of a pillar of fire, the Sun of Righteousness hath shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ, the Salvation of our souls!
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^705]
O new wonder of all ancient wonders! For who hath known a Mother having given birth without a husband, and bearing in her arms Him that contains all creation? It is the good pleasure of God that was born; Whom as an Infant having borne in thy hands, O Pure One, and having motherly boldness toward Him: cease not praying for them that honor thee, to have compassion and to save our souls!
