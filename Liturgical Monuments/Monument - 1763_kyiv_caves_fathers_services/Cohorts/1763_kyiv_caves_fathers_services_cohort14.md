*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^706]
How should we not marvel at thy God-man childbirth, O all-honorable one? For without having accepted the trial of man, O all-immaculate one, thou hast given birth without a father unto a Son in the flesh, Who was born before ages from the Father without a mother, Who in no wise endured change, or mingling, or division, but preserved intact the property of each essence. Therefore, O Mother and Virgin, Lady, entreat Him that the souls of them that in an orthodox manner confess thee as the Mother of God may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^707]
Without seed from the Divine Spirit, yet by the will of the Father, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who from the Father without a mother was before ages; and for our sake having become without a father from thee, thou hast given birth unto Him in the flesh, and hast nourished the Infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray, that our souls may be delivered from perils!

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*[^708]
He that for thy sake is the ancestor of God, the Prophet David, harmoniously proclaimed concerning thee unto Him that worked great things for thee: At Thy right hand stood the Queen![^709] For as a Mother who is mediatress of life did God show thee forth, Who was well-pleased without a father to become man from thee, that He might renew His own image corrupted by passions; and having found the sheep that went astray upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His own will might join it with the heavenly hosts; and that Christ, Who possesses great and rich mercy, might save the world, O Mother of God!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^710]
Look down upon the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, quenching the fierce uprisings against us, delivering us from all sorrow: for thee alone do we have as a firm and sure establishment, and thine intercession have we acquired, that we who call upon thee may not be put to shame, O Lady; make haste unto the entreaty of them that faithfully cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice, O Lady, the help of all, joy and protection, and the salvation of our souls!

Who shall not call thee blessed, O Most Holy Virgin? Who will not sing of thy most pure childbirth? For the only-begotten Son Who shone forth timelessly from the Father, the Same came forth from thee who art pure, having become incarnate in an ineffable manner: being God by nature, and for our sake having become man by nature, not divided into two persons, but in two natures unconfusedly known. Entreat Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may be shown mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^715]

Therefore unto thee, O all-immaculate one, as unto the Mother of God and Virgin in truth, we cry aloud unceasingly: Rejoice, like the angel! Therefore, knowing thee all as the Mother of God, we fervently pray thee: Entreat Him that our souls may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*[^717]

*RESURRECTIONAL THEOTOKIA,*
*Dismissal [Theotokia] after the Troparia of the Saints, of the Eight Tones:*[^720]

*Tone 1. Theotokion:*[^721]
When Gabriel announced unto thee, O Virgin: Rejoice! with the voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy ark, as the righteous David said:[^722] thou wast shown forth more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator! Glory to Him Who made His abode in thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who delivered us through thy childbirth!

*Tone 2. Theotokion:*[^723]
All beyond mind, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Mother of God: sealed in purity and preserved in virginity, thou wast known as a true Mother, having given birth unto the true God! Entreat Him that our souls may be saved!

*Tone 3. Theotokion:*[^724]
Thee, who didst intercede for the salvation of our race, do we sing of, O Mother of God and Virgin: for having assumed flesh from thee, Thy Son and our God, having accepted suffering through the Cross, delivered us from corruption, as the Lover of mankind!

*Tone 4. Theotokion:*[^725]
The mystery hidden from the age and unknown unto angels, through thee, O Mother of God, was revealed unto them that are upon the earth: God becoming incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake; whereby having raised up the first-created, He saved our souls from death!

*Tone 5. Theotokion:*[^726]
Rejoice, impassable Gate of the Lord! Rejoice, wall and protection of them that flee unto thee! Rejoice, tempest-tossed haven, and unwedded one, who hast given birth in the flesh unto thy Creator and God! Cease not to intercede for them that sing of and worship thy childbirth!
