[^74]: The volume is preserved in its contemporary eighteenth-century plain paper wrapper, typical of the modest, utilitarian paper bindings used by the Pochaiv Lavra printing house for separate festal liturgical publications (*vozsliduvaniia*) distributed to parishes and monasteries.
