*Here is set the singing which takes place on the present Feast of the Most Holy Eucharist before the early or brief Liturgy.[^6]*

*The Priest after the Proskomide, having opened the Ark, makes a bow; and having taken the Ciborium with the Most Pure Mysteries, turns toward the people and sings:*

> **Priest:** "☩ O saving Victim!"[^7]

*Then the* **Choir:** Gate opening unto heaven! Hostile battles stir up against us: grant us strength and help, to escape their enmity.

> **Priest:** "☩ Unto the one Lord in Trinity."

*And he places the Ciborium upon the Altar, in the prepared place.*

*Then the* **Choir:** Be eternal glory, Who grants endless life unto us in His Kingdom. Amen.

*After this he begins the Liturgy in the usual manner.*

***************************************************

*After Vespers.*

*The Priest, vested in all the priestly vestments, having approached before the high altar, bows low before the Holy Mysteries, and having taken Them, turns toward the people and begins:*

> **Priest:** "☩ Unto Thy most great Mystery,"[^8]

*Then the* **Choir:** We bow our necks: the Old Testament has passed away, glorious grace we behold; faith likewise supplies that which our senses cannot comprehend.

> **Priest:** "☩ Unto the Father, and unto the Begotten,"

*Then the* **Choir:** Be sung praise and jubilation: honor, glory, power, dominion, and adoration; unto Him Who proceeds from both, equal praise.[^9]

***************************************************

*Then is joined the Trisagion and the hour of supplications, or Supplications, and the other chants.*

> **Priest:** "Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us."

*Then the* **Choir:** Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal.

**Priest again:** Holy God, Holy Mighty. **Choir:** Holy God, Holy Mighty.

> **Priest:** "☩ From plague, famine, fire, and sword, deliver us, O Lord."

*Then the* **Choir:** From plague, famine, fire, and sword, deliver us, O Lord.

*(Know: Thou mayest employ the following three petitions when there is distress from murrain of cattle, or excessive rain, or fierce solar heat:*

*1st Petition:* **Priest:** ☩ From the murrain of cattle:

> **Choir:** "Deliver us, O Lord."

*2nd Petition:* **Priest:** ☩ From immeasurable and unseasonable downpour of rain:

> **Choir:** "Deliver us, O Lord."

*3rd Petition:* **Priest:** ☩ From fierce solar heat.

> **Choir:** "Deliver us, O Lord.*)*"

> **Priest:** "☩ From sudden and unexpected death, preserve us, O Lord."

> **Choir:** "From sudden and unexpected death."

> **Priest:** "We sinners beseech Thee, O God: give ear unto us, O Lord."

> **Choir:** "We sinners [beseech] Thee, O God."

> **Priest:** "Deliver us from every evil, by the intercessions of the Birthgiver of God."

> **Choir:** "Deliver us from every evil."

# PART I: OFFICE FOR THE FEAST OF THE MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST

[Woodcut Headpiece: Symmetrical ornamental frieze with foliate branches and blossoms:]

*Celebrated on the Thursday after the Sunday of All Saints.[^10]*

*On Wednesday evening at "Lord, I have cried", we set 8 stichera. Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Revealing the great mystery of Thy becoming man unto the initiates of Thy mysteries at supper when Thou didst recline, O Lover of mankind, Thou didst say: Eat the Bread of life: This is My Body; and drink the Blood of imperishable life.

*Verse 3:*

*Sticheron 2:* The upper room received the shadow, above which Christ accomplished that which is superior: both the bloodless supper and the rational service; and the sacred table there was the spiritual altar of the perfected Mysteries.

*Verse 2:*

*Sticheron 3:* O blessed Bread, O consecrated Bread! Giving thanks unto the Father Who begot Thee, and taking the Cup, Thou didst give it unto the disciples, crying out: Take, eat, This is My Body; and drink the Blood of imperishable life.

*Verse 1:*

*Sticheron 4:* The Lord fed His disciples with immortal food; before His Passion He gave them the Angelic Bread, foretold above the manna: "Your fathers," said the Lord, "did eat manna in the wilderness, and are dead; This is the Bread which comes down from heaven: if any man eat of this Bread, he shall not die, but shall live unto ages" [cf. John 6:49-51]: of which do Thou now make us worthy to partake, for the sake of the prayers of Thy Mother, O God.

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 4:* Come, ye faithful feast-lovers, let us clap our hands today at the world-saving triumph of the Bread of life, and of the Lamb of God Who takes away the sins of the world and our transgressions, not in the old leaven of malice, but in the unleavened bread of purity and truth [cf. 1 Cor. 5:8]. Having cleansed ourselves, therefore, let us worthily partake of Him, that we may obtain His eternal Kingdom.

*Entrance, and the Prokeimenon of the day. Then the Readings:*

*Reading from Exodus [Exodus 19:10-18]:*

The Lord said unto Moses: Go down, testify unto this people, and sanctify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments. And let them be ready against the third day; for on the third day the Lord will come down upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people. And thou shalt set bounds unto the people round about, saying: Take heed to yourselves, that ye go not up into the mountain, or touch any part of it. Whosoever touches the mountain shall surely die. There shall not a hand touch it, for he shall surely be stoned or shot through with an arrow; whether it be man or beast, he shall not live. When the voice of the trumpet sounds and the cloud departs from the mountain, they shall come up to the mountain. And Moses went down from the mountain unto the people, and sanctified them; and they washed their garments. And he said unto the people: Be ready, for three days do not approach unto your wives. And it came to pass on the third day in the morning, that there were thunders and lightnings, and a thick cloud upon Mount Sinai, and the voice of the trumpet exceeding loud; so that all the people that were in the camp trembled. And Moses brought forth the people out of the camp to meet with God; and they stood at the nether part of Mount Sinai. And Mount Sinai was altogether on a smoke, because God descended upon it in fire: and the smoke thereof ascended as the smoke of a furnace, and all the people were greatly amazed. And the voices of the trumpet sounded long, and grew exceeding loud. Moses spoke, and God answered him by a voice.

*Reading from the First Book of Kings [1 Kings / LXX 1 Kingdoms 21:1–6; MT 1 Samuel 21:1–6]:[^11]*

In those days, David came to Nob[^12] unto Ahimelech the Priest: and Ahimelech was astonished at meeting Him, and said unto Him: Why art thou thus alone, and no man is with thee? And David said unto the Priest: The King commanded me a matter this day, and said unto me: Let no man know anything of the business whereabout I send thee, concerning which I have given thee command. And I have appointed my young men unto such and such a place. Now therefore, is there anywhere under thy hand five loaves of bread? Give me whatsoever thou findest. And the Priest answered David, and said: There is no common bread under my hand, but there is holy bread under my hand, kept by those who are pure from women, and they eat thereof. And David answered the Priest, and said: Yea, we have been pure from women about these three days, since the day when we came forth on our journey. And all the young men are clean: and this journey is clean, seeing that today [our vessels] are sanctified by reason of my vessels. And Ahimelech the Priest gave Him the showbread; for there was no other bread there, save only the loaves of the offering that had been taken away from before the face of the Lord, and he gave them unto Him on that day.

*Reading from the Third Book of Kings [3 Kings / LXX 3 Kingdoms 17:8–16; MT 1 Kings 17:8–16]:[^13]*

The word of the Lord came unto Elijah, saying: Arise, go hence eastward, and hide thyself by the brook Cherith, that is before the Jordan. And thou shalt drink of the brook; and I have commanded the ravens to feed thee there. And Elijah went and did according unto the word of the Lord: for He went and sat by the brook Cherith, that is before the Jordan. And the ravens brought him bread in the morning, and flesh in the evening; and He drank water of the brook. And it came to pass after a while, that the brook dried up, because there had been no rain upon the earth. And the word of the Lord came unto Elijah, saying: Arise, go to Zarephath of Sidon, and dwell there: behold, I have commanded a widow woman there to sustain thee.

So he arose and went to Zarephath to the gate of the city. And, behold, the widow woman was there gathering sticks: and Elijah called after her, and said unto her: Bring me, I pray thee, a little water in a vessel, that I may drink. And as she was going to fetch it, Elijah called after her, and said: Bring me, I pray thee, a morsel of bread in thine hand. And the woman said: As the Lord my God lives, if I have ought in my storehouse, but only a handful of meal in a barrel, and a little oil in a cruse: and, behold, I am gathering two sticks, that I may go in and dress it for me and my children, that we may eat it, and die. And Elijah said unto her: Fear not, go and do not according to thy saying: but make me thereof a little cake first, and bring it unto me, and after make for thee and for thy children. For thus says the Lord God of Israel: The barrel of meal shall not fail, neither shall the cruse of oil diminish, until the day that the Lord God sends rain upon the face of all the earth. And the woman went and did according to the word of Elijah, and gave unto him, and ate herself and her children. And from that day the barrel of meal failed not, neither did the cruse of oil diminish, according to the word of the Lord, which He spoke by the hand of Elijah.

*At the Litiya, Stichera. Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Before the sacred Ark, the forefather David leaped and danced; but we today worship Him as God, Whose humanity the Ark prefigured: for instead of manna, here is the incarnate Essence; instead of the stone tablets, Christ the Lord, the Lawgiver, is beheld in the Mysteries, saving our souls.

*Sticheron 2:* The Old Testament possessed the Ark overlaid round about with gold, which, when the stranger entered the shrine with the idol of conciliation, cast it down unto the earth, and shattered its head and both the palms of its hands. But Thou, O Christ our God, the Lover of mankind, how dost Thou enter into the shrine of defiled, wretched souls?

*Sticheron 3:* O Thou Who alone hast immortality, and dwellest in light unapproachable: how wilt Thou unite Thyself unto an idol of sinful darkness? But, O Light, Who enlightenest every man: defile not, and cast not out into the outer darkness our dark and sinful souls; but enlighten them by Thy grace, we pray Thee, for the sake of Thy full compassion.

*Sticheron 4:* O supernatural Bread, O innocent Lamb Who takest away the sins of the whole world: for the salvation of mankind Thou hast transformed bread into Thy Flesh; and in the Bread Thou hast hidden Thy Divinity, Thy Flesh, and Thy Soul by divine power: unto Whom all we faithful today, in festal joy, with compunction and faith, bow down.

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 2:* Drawing near with fear today unto the mystical Supper and the divine Table, ye faithful, desiring to drink of the immortal fountain: yet trembling and fear seize us, because of the multitude of the many poisons of our sins, from which desiring to be cleansed, we pray by the intercessions of our Sovereign Lady.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "Joy of the heavenly hosts" (Не́бныхъ чинѡ́въ):*

*Sticheron 1:* Let us hymn the heavenly, living Bread, ineffably descending unceasingly from heaven, saying with one accord: Rejoice, O Body of Christ, whereby we all are delivered; and rejoice, O divine and holy Blood, of which we have drunk!

*Verse: Man ate the bread of Angels; He sent them food in abundance [LXX Ps. 77:25a].[^14]*

*Sticheron 2:* At the Supper Thou gavest Thyself unto Thy disciples and friends for food, (O marvel!), and holding the Bread: For this is My Body; and taking the Cup, Thou didst say: Drink ye all of My Blood!

*Verse: And He rained down manna upon them to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven [LXX Ps. 77:24].[^15]*

*Sticheron 3:* O divine, all-venerable, and most precious Body! Whereby the Word made man, O Savior, unto Thy servants in sorrow, distress, and hope, and all manner of pilgrimage, hast granted the heavenly Kingdom and life, for the sake of Thy Mother.

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 8:* O most glorious wonder! The living and divine Bread, having come down from heaven, was willingly given for food at the mystical Supper; for having taken Bread and blessed it, He gave it unto the disciples, saying: Take, eat, This is My Body; likewise also the Cup. Therefore let each man examine himself, and so let him eat and drink, unto salvation and life eternal.

*Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart: and Trisagion. Troparion, Tone 7:*

O supernatural Bread, Whom Angels and Archangels desire to behold with trembling, today Thou art mystically seen upon the altar and given unto men for food: beholding Thy most rich mercy toward us, O Christ our God, seeing Thee, Who art God in the flesh, though corruptible in outward sight, we confess Thee, saying: Vouchsafe us to be partakers, that we may enjoy eternal life.

*Dismissal:*

May Christ our true God, Who for the sake of His exceeding great mercy, at His mystical Supper, gave us His own Flesh to eat and His own Blood truly to drink unto salvation, through the prayers of His all-pure Mother, of the holy, glorious twelve Apostles, and of all the Saints, have mercy on us and save us, for He is a good God and the Lover of mankind.

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord", Troparion: "O supernatural Bread", thrice.*

*After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:* Christ our great and all-venerable Passover was eaten as bread: and having been slain as a sheep, He was offered for us as an acceptable sacrifice. Reverently worshiping His all-pure Body and precious Blood, with compunction we say: Abide Thyself ever with us, and none shall prevail against us.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:* Without seed, O Virgin Mary, Thou didst receive with thy flesh Christ our God, Who was conceived from thy womb by the operation of the Holy Spirit; and Who, having given [Himself] unto His disciples at the mystical Supper, said: Take, eat, This is My Body; and drink, This is My Blood. Him do thou entreat with maternal boldness, that He burn not us who receive communion, but save our souls.

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*

Christ blessed the heavenly Bread and the Cup for all, showed it unto the Father Who begot Him and, giving it unto His disciples who were present, said: He that eats My Flesh and drinks My Blood hath eternal life, eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day. Therefore we unworthy ones today festively worship the all-pure Mysteries of Thy Flesh and Blood, boldly believing that Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God, Who didst come into the world to save sinners.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:* Without seed from the Holy Spirit thou didst conceive, and gavest birth unto a Son, Christ our God: Whose all-pure Flesh, as God and our Redeemer, we worship unto the face of the earth, and desire to be partakers thereof; and unto thee as Mother we diligently flee, O Birthgiver of God, and with compunction we pray thee: Receive our prayers, and convey them unto thy Son and our God, that He may save our souls.

*Polyeleos, and Megalynarion:*

We magnify Thee, O Life-giving Christ, Who for our sake hast transformed bread into Thy Flesh and wine into Thy Blood; and thereby we openly worship Thee as the Incarnate God.

*Verse 1: Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised, in the city of our God, in His holy mountain [LXX Ps. 47:2].*

*Verse 2: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He hath rendered unto me? [LXX Ps. 115:3].*

*Verse 3: I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord [LXX Ps. 115:4].*

*Verse 4: He hath made a remembrance of His marvelous works; the Lord is gracious and full of compassion; He hath given meat unto them that fear Him [LXX Ps. 110:4–5].*

*Verse 5: And He rained down manna upon them to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven [LXX Ps. 77:24].*

*Verse 6: Man ate the bread of Angels; He sent them food in abundance [LXX Ps. 77:25a].*

*Glory... Now and ever... Alleluia, thrice.*

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3. Podoben: "Awed by the beauty of thy virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства):*

O pre-eternal and unoriginate God, having assumed human nature, Thou didst come unto the prepared upper room with Thy disciples; where having finished the old passover, O our Savior, Thou didst institute the new mystical Passover: for having taken bread into Thy Flesh, and the cup into Thy Blood, Thou didst transform them and give them unto us for food and drink. Beholding today this true mystery, with compunction we say: Glory to Thy loving-kindness, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory... Now and ever... The same.*

*Anavathmoi: 1st Antiphon of the 4th Tone.*

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4: He that eats My Flesh and drinks My Blood hath eternal life.*

*Verse: Man ate the bread of Angels; He sent them food in abundance. Gospel from John, pericope 24 [John 6:48–54].[^16]*

*The Lord said unto the Jews who came unto Him: My Flesh is meat indeed... Ending: He shall live unto ages.*

*Sticheron after the Gospel, Tone 6:* Today the Angels are astonished, beholding Thee, the Incarnate God, hidden in the Mysteries, and worshiped with fear by man, and giving Thyself for food: with them also we who are on earth hymn Thee as the Lover of mankind, saying: Vouchsafe us to partake of Thine all-pure Flesh and Blood, O merciful Lord.

*Canon, Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:* Come, ye people, let us sing a song unto Christ God:[^17]

*Refrain: Glory to Thy Flesh and Blood, O Christ!*

May Thy holy Body, O compassionate Lord, and Thy precious Blood, be unto me the Bread of eternal life and the healing of multifaceted sicknesses.

Vouchsafe me, O Master, to receive and drink with longing and faith Thine all-pure Blood and Thy most holy Body.

*Glory:* Defiled with unbecoming deeds, wretched that I am, I am unworthy, O Christ, of communion in Thine all-pure Body and divine Blood: whereof do Thou make me worthy.

*Now and ever:* O good earth, blessed Bride of God, who didst bud forth the unplowed Wheat that saves the world: make me worthy to be saved as I eat thereof.

*Katavasia: I shall open my mouth:*

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* On the rock *Heirmos:* Establish me upon the rock of faith:

Grant me drops of tears, O Christ, purifying the defilement of my heart; that cleansed with a good conscience, in faith and fear I may approach, O Master, unto the communion of Thy divine Gifts.

Unto remission of transgressions may Thine all-pure Body and divine Blood be unto me, and unto the communion of the Holy Spirit, and unto life eternal, O Lover of mankind, and unto estrangement from passions.

*Glory:* Vouchsafe me, O Lord, to partake without condemnation of Thy Body and of Thy precious Blood, and to glorify Thy goodness.

*Now and ever:* All-pure table of the living Bread, Who for the sake of the Mother descended from on high and granted life unto the world: make me also now, unworthy as I am, worthy with fear to taste thereof, and to live.

*Katavasia: O Mother of God, thou living fountain:*

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "While the stone was sealed" (Ка́мени знамена́ну):*

O Lord and Master, instead of the lamb according to the commandment of Moses, Thou didst mystically give Thyself unto Thy disciples, the blameless Lamb, for food unto us, unto remission of sins, and unto sanctification of our souls. Nourished whereby, we pray: Let not the communion of Thy Holy Mysteries be unto us for judgment or condemnation, by reason of our sins and iniquities.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:* O Mother and Virgin, Birthgiver of God, entreat the Lamb and Shepherd, the Son Whom thou didst bear, that He may save and enlighten our souls.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* Thou didst come forth from the Virgin, neither an envoy:

Being incarnate, O most merciful One, Thou didst desire for our sake to be slain as a sheep for the sins of men: therefore I pray Thee, purify my transgressions also.

Heal the wounds of my soul, O Lord, and sanctify all of me; and vouchsafe me, wretched as I am, O Master, to become a partaker of Thy mystical divine Supper.

*Glory:* Standing and sighing with fear and trembling, I lift up the eyes of my heart on high unto Thee, O Savior, Who didst stretch out Thy divine hands upon the Cross: whereby grant me the food of Thy good pleasure, strengthening and confirming me in Thy Passion.

*Now and ever:* Render Thy compassionate Son gracious unto me also, O Sovereign Lady, and keep me, His unworthy servant, undefiled and blameless, that having received within me the spiritual Pearl, I may be sanctified.

*Katavasia: He Who sits in glory:*

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* O light of those who lie in darkness:

As Thou didst foretell, O Christ, so may it be unto Thy base servant: abide in me, as Thou hast promised; for behold, I eat Thy divine Body, and drink Thy Blood.

O Word of God and God, may Thy divine Body be a coal unto me for the illumination of the darkened, and Thy Blood the purification of my defiled soul.

*Glory:* Possessing a defiled soul and unclean lips, I dare not now draw near unto Thee, O Christ, and receive Thy Body: but do Thou make me worthy.

*Now and ever:* O Mary, Mother of God, venerable dwelling of sweet fragrance: by thy prayers make me a chosen vessel, that I may partake of the Holy Mysteries of thy Son.

*Katavasia: All creation was astonished:*

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* Held fast in the abyss of sins:

Sanctify, O Savior, my mind, soul, heart, and body; and vouchsafe me, O Master, to approach without condemnation unto Thine awesome Mysteries.

May I be estranged from passions, and vouchsafed to possess an increase of grace and confirmation of life, through communion in Thy Holy Mysteries, O Christ.

*Glory:* Grant me with fear and trembling to draw near unto Thy divine Mysteries, O Christ; and to receive Thy holy Body, and Thy pure and holy precious Blood.

*Now and ever:* O holy Word of God, sanctify all of me who now approach unto Thy divine Mysteries, through the entreaties of Thy holy Mother.

*Katavasia: Celebrating this divine and all-venerable feast:*

*Kontakion, Tone 4:* Behold, Christ sets forth [Himself] as food unto all: come, and falling down let us worship Christ God, Who is hidden in these Mysteries, crying out with compunction: O Master, burn not us unworthy ones by communion, but rather burn up our sins, and purify our souls.

*Ikos:* Approaching the mystical Table with fear, all of us, let us receive with pure souls the divine Bread, the cup of salvation; let us bow down low in our consciences, that we may enjoy eternal life, by the prayers of the Mother of God.

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* The youth of old in Babylon:

May the communion of Thine immortal Mysteries now, O Christ, I pray, be unto me a fountain of good things, light, life, and dispassion, serving as an advocate for advancement and increase of the most divine virtue, O only good One, that I may glorify Thee.

That I may be delivered from passions, enemies, necessities, and every tribulation, with trembling, love, and reverence, O Lover of mankind, I now draw near unto Thine immortal and divine Mysteries, singing unto Thee: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Glory:* Wretched soul, passionate soul, tremble beholding the most glorious mysteries! Sighing, shed tears and beat thy breast, crying out and saying: O God, cleanse me as the prodigal!

*Now and ever:* O thou full of God's grace, who beyond understanding didst bear Christ the Savior: I, thine unclean servant, earnestly entreat thee, pure one: unto me who desire now to draw near unto the all-pure Mysteries, purify all of me from defilement of flesh and spirit.

*Katavasia: The godly-minded worshiped not creation:*

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* Scorning the golden idol:

Vouchsafe me, O God my Savior, now to become a partaker of Thy heavenly, awesome, and holy Mysteries, and of Thy divine and mystical Supper.

Having fled unto Thy compassion, O good One, with fear I cry unto Thee: Abide in me, O Savior, and I, as Thou didst say, in Thee; for behold, trusting in Thy mercy, I eat Thy Body, and drink Thy Blood.

*Glory:* I tremble as I receive the Fire, lest I be burned up like wax and like grass! O the compassion of God! O the awesome Mystery! How do I, who am clay, partake of the true Body and Blood, and am made incorruptible!

*Now and ever:*

*Now and ever:* The divine Bread of life was truly baked in thy womb, O Mother of God, keeping thine undefiled womb unhurt: therefore as our foster-mother, we sing of thee unto all ages.

*Katavasia: The godly youth in the furnace:*

*Ode 9. Before the Heirmos we sing the Refrain:*

*Magnify, O my soul, the Lord, Who mystically gave His Flesh for food. Another Refrain: Magnify, O my soul, Christ God, Who gave His Flesh unto men for salvation.*

*Heirmos: Thou art all desire:*

Christ is present: see and perceive that He Who of old was the Lord, for our sake and following us, offered Himself once as a sacrifice unto His Father; and is now ever slain for those who receive communion.

May I be sanctified in soul and body, O Master, may I be enlightened, may I be saved, may I become Thy dwelling through communion in the sacred Mysteries, having Thee living within me with the Father and the Spirit, O most merciful Benefactor.

*Glory:* May Thy holy Body and Thine all-pure Blood, O my Savior, be unto me as fire and as light, consuming the material of sin, burning up the thorns of passions, and wholly enlightening me who worship Thy Divinity.

*Now and ever:* God was incarnate of thy pure blood: therefore every generation hymns thee, O Sovereign Lady, and the spiritual multitudes glorify thee; for through thee they openly beheld Him Who rules over all, having become man in humanity.

*Katavasia: Let every earthborn mortal leap for joy:*

*Exapostilarion:*

While the disciples were sitting at the mystical Supper and beholding Thee, O Lord, Thou didst take bread into Thy holy hands, and lifting up Thine eyes unto heaven unto God the Father, giving thanks, blessing, and breaking it, Thou gavest it unto Thy holy disciples: grant it also unto us, Thy sinful servants.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:* This is He, O all-pure Virgin Birthgiver of God, Who is worshiped in these Mysteries, Whom thou didst bear before in thy maternal womb, and thereafter in thine arms. Him do thou entreat for us, that He may save our souls.

# PART II: OFFICE FOR THE FEAST OF THE COMPASSION OF THE MOST HOLY BIRTHGIVER OF GOD

*Celebrated on the Friday of the Second Week after the Sunday of All Saints.[^36]*

# PART III: OFFICE FOR THE FEAST OF THE BLESSED HIEROMARTYR JOSAPHAT [^49]

[Woodcut Portrait: Oval medallion depiction of the Holy Hieromartyr Josaphat in archiepiscopal vestments with omophorion and mitre, holding a blessing cross in his right hand:]

*Celebrated on the 16th day of the Month of September.[^50]*

*If it fall on a Sunday, on Saturday at Vespers we chant the kathisma: "Blessed is the man": All of the Resurrection. At "Lord, I have cried", Resurrection Stichera on 4, and to the Saint on 6. But if it fall on another day, we sing the Stichera of the Feast of the Exaltation [of the Cross], and to the Saint, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "As noble among martyrs" (Прихва́льнїи мꙋ́ченицы):*

*Sticheron 1:* Having stained thy sacred vestment with martyr's blood, thou didst enter into the Holy of Holies, O praiseworthy one, where are the voices of them that feast, from whom thou receivest the victories of thy labors: both an unfading crown, and thine eternal beauty, and the life of paradise, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.[^51]

*Sticheron 2:* A bloodless sacrifice thou didst offer on the altar unto God, as a divine hierarch, as one incorporeal; an acceptable sacrifice, thou didst bring thyself by blood as a true hieromartyr unto Christ, O divinely speaking and all-venerable one: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 3:* Having cast down the schismatic assembly by thy hierarchic word, thou didst confirm in the faith the hearts of the unconfirmed; and having passed over unto life eternal, having suffered for unity, thou wast a witness of the Lord, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 4:* Having stained thy sacred vesture with the blood of thy sacred flesh, O holy Father, a double crown from Christ hast thou received, O most wealthy one, and with the Angels thou dancest in the heavens, before the throne of the Holy Trinity: Whom now entreat, to grant unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 5:* O Hieromartyr Josaphat, thou didst purely desire an honorable life, having become a delineation of Jesus the Almighty; and unto unity thou didst guide the people by thy precious words and by grace: thou hast passed unto timeless things, having washed thyself in martyr's blood, O divinely blessed one: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 6:* Entering in understanding into the unwaning cloud of ineffable light, as hieromartyr and pastor, higher than the unutterable mysteries of Christ: for being enlightened as a martyr, and mystically taught as a pastor, therefore with double crowns of supernal glory wast thou adorned: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Glory... Tone 6:* A lawful priest even unto thy demise wast thou, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; for thou didst celebrate the divine and ineffable mysteries, and pouredst out thy blood for Christ God: an acceptable sacrifice thou didst offer thyself: therefore having boldness unto Him, earnestly pray for those who celebrate with faith thy most venerable memory, and honor it: that thy flock and the whole world may be delivered from tribulations, from every invasion of aliens, and from internal strife, distress, and cruelty.

*Reading from Proverbs [Proverbs 10:7, 6; 3:13–16; 8:32–35; 4:5–9; 1:23; 2:1–5]:*

For the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold. Give ear unto me therefore, O ye children: for I will speak of honorable things; and blessed is the man that keeps my ways. For I wisdom have built a dwelling; counsel and knowledge and understanding have I called upon. Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength. I love them that love me; and those that seek me shall find grace. O ye simple, understand subtilty: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart. Give ear unto me again; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things. For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips. All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them. They are all plain to him that understands, and right to them that find knowledge. For I teach you the truth, that your hope may be in the Lord, and ye may be filled with the Spirit.

*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon [Wisdom 4:7–14; 5:1–5; 1:13–15; 2:1–3, 12–24]:*

Though the righteous be praised, the people shall rejoice: for the memory of him is immortal, because it is known with God, and with men: and his soul pleased the Lord. Love wisdom, therefore, O ye men, and ye shall live: desire her, and ye shall be instructed: for the beginning of her is love, and the observation of her laws. Honor wisdom, that ye may reign unto ages. I will tell you, and will not hide God's mysteries from you: for He is the guide of wisdom, and the director of the wise: and the artificer of every understanding and work. And in all understanding doth wisdom teach: for in her is an understanding spirit, holy, one only, manifold, subtle, the brightness of the everlasting light, and the unspotted mirror of the power of God, and the image of His goodness: she makes men friends of God, and prophets. She is more beautiful than the sun, and above all the order of stars: being compared with the light, she is found before it. She delivered them that waited upon her from travail: and guided them in right paths. She gave them knowledge of holy things, and preserved them from their enemies, and gave them strong victory: that they might know that godliness is stronger than all. And vice shall not prevail against wisdom, neither shall judgment pass away without reproving the wicked.

For they said within themselves, reasoning not aright: Let us oppress the righteous man, let us not spare his holiness, nor reverence the ancient gray hairs of the aged. Let our strength be the law of justice: let us lie in wait for the righteous, because he is not for our turn, and he is clean contrary to our doings: he upbraids us with our offending the law, and objects to our infamy the transgressions of our education. He professes to have the knowledge of God: and he calls himself the child of the Lord. He was made to reprove our thoughts: he is grievous unto us even to behold: for his life is not like other men's, his ways are of another fashion. We are esteemed of him as counterfeits: he abstains from our ways as from filthiness: he pronounces the end of the just to be blessed. Let us see if his words be true: and let us prove what shall happen in the end of him. Let us examine him with despitefulness and torture, that we may know his meekness, and prove his patience. Let us condemn him with a shameful death: for by his own words shall he be respected. Such things they did imagine, and were deceived: for their own malice hath blinded them. As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not: neither considered they that Thou art the only God, Who hast the power of life and death, and savest in the time of tribulation, and deliverest from all evil: Who art merciful and compassionate, and givest grace unto Thy saints, and with Thy right arm resistest the proud:

...resistest the proud:

Though the righteous be overtaken with death, he shall be in rest. For the righteous that is dead shall condemn the ungodly which are living. For they shall see the end of the wise, and shall not understand what God in His counsel hath decreed of him: for the Lord shall cast down the ungodly speechless on their face, and He shall shake them from the foundations, and they shall be utterly laid waste in grief, and their memorial shall perish. They shall come with fear to the thought of their sins, and their iniquities shall convince them to their face. Then shall the righteous man stand in great boldness before the face of such as have afflicted him, and made no account of his labors. When they see it, they shall be troubled with terrible fear, and shall be amazed at the strangeness of his salvation. And they repenting and groaning for anguish of spirit shall say within themselves: This was he, whom we had sometimes in derision, and a proverb of reproach, we fools! We accounted his life madness, and his end to be without honor: how is he numbered among the children of God, and his lot is among the saints? Therefore have we erred from the way of truth, and the light of righteousness hath not shined unto us, and the sun rose not upon us. We wearied ourselves in the way of wickedness and destruction: yea, we have gone through deserts, where there lay no way: but as for the way of the Lord, we have not known it.
