# PART I: OFFICE FOR THE FEAST OF THE MOST HOLY MYSTERY OF THE EUCHARIST

*Celebrated on the Thursday after the Sunday of All Saints.[^10]*

*On Wednesday evening at "Lord, I have cried", we set 8 stichera. Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Revealing the great mystery of Thy becoming man unto the initiates of Thy mysteries at supper when Thou didst recline, O Lover of mankind, Thou didst say: Eat the Bread of life: This is My Body; and drink the Blood of imperishable life.

*Verse 3:*

*Sticheron 2:* The upper room received the shadow, above which Christ accomplished that which is superior: both the bloodless supper and the rational service; and the sacred table there was the spiritual altar of the perfected Mysteries.

*Verse 2:*

*Sticheron 3:* O blessed Bread, O consecrated Bread! Giving thanks unto the Father Who begot Thee, and taking the Cup, Thou didst give it unto the disciples, crying out: Take, eat, This is My Body; and drink the Blood of imperishable life.

*Verse 1:*

*Sticheron 4:* The Lord fed His disciples with immortal food; before His Passion He gave them the Angelic Bread, foretold above the manna: "Your fathers," said the Lord, "did eat manna in the wilderness, and are dead; This is the Bread which comes down from heaven: if any man eat of this Bread, he shall not die, but shall live unto ages" [cf. John 6:49-51]: of which do Thou now make us worthy to partake, for the sake of the prayers of Thy Mother, O God.

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 4:* Come, ye faithful feast-lovers, let us clap our hands today at the world-saving triumph of the Bread of life, and of the Lamb of God Who takes away the sins of the world and our transgressions, not in the old leaven of malice, but in the unleavened bread of purity and truth [cf. 1 Cor. 5:8]. Having cleansed ourselves, therefore, let us worthily partake of Him, that we may obtain His eternal Kingdom.

*At the Litiya, Stichera. Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Before the sacred Ark, the forefather David leaped and danced; but we today worship Him as God, Whose humanity the Ark prefigured: for instead of manna, here is the incarnate Essence; instead of the stone tablets, Christ the Lord, the Lawgiver, is beheld in the Mysteries, saving our souls.

*Sticheron 2:* The Old Testament possessed the Ark overlaid round about with gold, which, when the stranger entered the shrine with the idol of conciliation, cast it down unto the earth, and shattered its head and both the palms of its hands. But Thou, O Christ our God, the Lover of mankind, how dost Thou enter into the shrine of defiled, wretched souls?

*Sticheron 3:* O Thou Who alone hast immortality, and dwellest in light unapproachable: how wilt Thou unite Thyself unto an idol of sinful darkness? But, O Light, Who enlightenest every man: defile not, and cast not out into the outer darkness our dark and sinful souls; but enlighten them by Thy grace, we pray Thee, for the sake of Thy full compassion.

*Sticheron 4:* O supernatural Bread, O innocent Lamb Who takest away the sins of the whole world: for the salvation of mankind Thou hast transformed bread into Thy Flesh; and in the Bread Thou hast hidden Thy Divinity, Thy Flesh, and Thy Soul by divine power: unto Whom all we faithful today, in festal joy, with compunction and faith, bow down.

*Glory... Now and ever... Tone 2:* Drawing near with fear today unto the mystical Supper and the divine Table, ye faithful, desiring to drink of the immortal fountain: yet trembling and fear seize us, because of the multitude...
