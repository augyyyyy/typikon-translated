# PART III: OFFICE FOR THE FEAST OF THE BLESSED HIEROMARTYR JOSAPHAT [^49]

[Woodcut Portrait: Oval medallion depiction of the Holy Hieromartyr Josaphat in archiepiscopal vestments with omophorion and mitre, holding a blessing cross in his right hand:]

*Celebrated on the 16th day of the Month of September.[^50]*

*If it fall on a Sunday, on Saturday at Vespers we chant the kathisma: "Blessed is the man": All of the Resurrection. At "Lord, I have cried", Resurrection Stichera on 4, and to the Saint on 6. But if it fall on another day, we sing the Stichera of the Feast of the Exaltation [of the Cross], and to the Saint, Stichera, Tone 1. Podoben: "As noble among martyrs" (Прихва́льнїи мꙋ́ченицы):*

*Sticheron 1:* Having stained thy sacred vestment with martyr's blood, thou didst enter into the Holy of Holies, O praiseworthy one, where are the voices of them that feast, from whom thou receivest the victories of thy labors: both an unfading crown, and thine eternal beauty, and the life of paradise, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.[^51]

*Sticheron 2:* A bloodless sacrifice thou didst offer on the altar unto God, as a divine hierarch, as one incorporeal; an acceptable sacrifice, thou didst bring thyself by blood as a true hieromartyr unto Christ, O divinely speaking and all-venerable one: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 3:* Having cast down the schismatic assembly by thy hierarchic word, thou didst confirm in the faith the hearts of the unconfirmed; and having passed over unto life eternal, having suffered for unity, thou wast a witness of the Lord, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 4:* Having stained thy sacred vesture with the blood of thy sacred flesh, O holy Father, a double crown from Christ hast thou received, O most wealthy one, and with the Angels thou dancest in the heavens, before the throne of the Holy Trinity: Whom now entreat, to grant unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 5:* O Hieromartyr Josaphat, thou didst purely desire an honorable life, having become a delineation of Jesus the Almighty; and unto unity thou didst guide the people by thy precious words and by grace: thou hast passed unto timeless things, having washed thyself in martyr's blood, O divinely blessed one: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Sticheron 6:* Entering in understanding into the unwaning cloud of ineffable light, as hieromartyr and pastor, higher than the unutterable mysteries of Christ: for being enlightened as a martyr, and mystically taught as a pastor, therefore with double crowns of supernal glory wast thou adorned: and now pray, that there may be granted unto the Churches unity, and peace, and great mercy unto our souls.

*Glory... Tone 6:* A lawful priest even unto thy demise wast thou, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; for thou didst celebrate the divine and ineffable mysteries, and pouredst out thy blood for Christ God: an acceptable sacrifice thou didst offer thyself: therefore having boldness unto Him, earnestly pray for those who celebrate with faith thy most venerable memory, and honor it: that thy flock and the whole world may be delivered from tribulations, from every invasion of aliens, and from internal strife, distress, and cruelty.

*If on Sunday, Dogmatikon of the Tone. If on another day, Now and ever... of the Feast [of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross], Tone 6:[^52]*

The four-quartered world is sanctified today, as Thy four-part Cross is lifted up, O Christ our God, and the horn of our faithful King, who raises it with Thee: whereby we shatter the horns of enemies. Great art Thou, O Lord, and marvelous in Thy works; glory to Thee!

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. Then the Readings:[^53]*

*Reading from Proverbs [Proverbs 10:7, 6; 3:13–16; 8:32–35; 4:5–9; 1:23; 2:1–5]:*

The memory of the just is with praises, and the blessing of the Lord is upon his head. Blessed is the man that finds wisdom, and the mortal that gets understanding. For the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold. She is more precious than rubies: and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her. Length of days is in her right hand; and in her left hand riches and honor. Out of her mouth goes forth righteousness: and law and mercy she carries upon her tongue. Give ear unto me therefore, O ye children: for I will speak of honorable things; and blessed is the man that keeps my ways. For my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and the will is prepared from the Lord. Therefore do I entreat you, and put forth my voice to the sons of men. For I wisdom have built a dwelling; counsel and knowledge and understanding have I called upon. Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength. I love them that love me; and those that seek me shall find grace. O ye simple, understand subtilty: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart. Give ear unto me again; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things. For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips. All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them. They are all plain to him that understands, and right to them that find knowledge. For I teach you the truth, that your hope may be in the Lord, and ye may be filled with the Spirit.

*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon [Wisdom 4:7–14; 5:1–5; 1:13–15; 2:1–3, 12–24]:*

Though the righteous be praised, the people shall rejoice: for the memory of him is immortal, because it is known with God, and with men: and his soul pleased the Lord. Love wisdom, therefore, O ye men, and ye shall live: desire her, and ye shall be instructed: for the beginning of her is love, and the observation of her laws. Honor wisdom, that ye may reign unto ages. I will tell you, and will not hide God's mysteries from you: for He is the guide of wisdom, and the director of the wise: and the artificer of every understanding and work. And in all understanding doth wisdom teach: for in her is an understanding spirit, holy, one only, manifold, subtle, the brightness of the everlasting light, and the unspotted mirror of the power of God, and the image of His goodness: she makes men friends of God, and prophets. She is more beautiful than the sun, and above all the order of stars: being compared with the light, she is found before it. She delivered them that waited upon her from travail: and guided them in right paths. She gave them knowledge of holy things, and preserved them from their enemies, and gave them strong victory: that they might know that godliness is stronger than all. And vice shall not prevail against wisdom, neither shall judgment pass away without reproving the wicked.

For they said within themselves, reasoning not aright: Let us oppress the righteous man, let us not spare his holiness, nor reverence the ancient gray hairs of the aged. Let our strength be the law of justice: let us lie in wait for the righteous, because he is not for our turn, and he is clean contrary to our doings: he upbraids us with our offending the law, and objects to our infamy the transgressions of our education. He professes to have the knowledge of God: and he calls himself the child of the Lord. He was made to reprove our thoughts: he is grievous unto us even to behold: for his life is not like other men's, his ways are of another fashion. We are esteemed of him as counterfeits: he abstains from our ways as from filthiness: he pronounces the end of the just to be blessed. Let us see if his words be true: and let us prove what shall happen in the end of him. Let us examine him with despitefulness and torture, that we may know his meekness, and prove his patience. Let us condemn him with a shameful death: for by his own words shall he be respected. Such things they did imagine, and were deceived: for their own malice hath blinded them. As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not: neither considered they that Thou art the only God, Who hast the power of life and death, and savest in the time of tribulation, and deliverest from all evil: Who art merciful and compassionate, and givest grace unto Thy saints, and with Thy right arm resistest the proud:

...resistest the proud:

Though the righteous be overtaken with death, he shall be in rest. For the righteous that is dead shall condemn the ungodly which are living. For they shall see the end of the wise, and shall not understand what God in His counsel hath decreed of him: for the Lord shall cast down the ungodly speechless on their face, and He shall shake them from the foundations, and they shall be utterly laid waste in grief, and their memorial shall perish. They shall come with fear to the thought of their sins, and their iniquities shall convince them to their face. Then shall the righteous man stand in great boldness before the face of such as have afflicted him, and made no account of his labors. When they see it, they shall be troubled with terrible fear, and shall be amazed at the strangeness of his salvation. And they repenting and groaning for anguish of spirit shall say within themselves: This was he, whom we had sometimes in derision, and a proverb of reproach, we fools! We accounted his life madness, and his end to be without honor: how is he numbered among the children of God, and his lot is among the saints? Therefore have we erred from the way of truth, and the light of righteousness hath not shined unto us, and the sun rose not upon us. We wearied ourselves in the way of wickedness and destruction: yea, we have gone through deserts, where there lay no way: but as for the way of the Lord, we have not known it.

*At the Litiya, Sticheron of the Feast [of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross], Tone 1:[^54]*

Today, even as Constantine spoke unto them that stood by, the fulfillment of David hath been received: For behold, the holy footstool of Thy feet we worship, and hoping upon the wings of Thy wall, O all-bounteous One, by Thy spear: let the light of Thy countenance be signed upon us. Exalt the horn of Thy Orthodox people by the Exaltation of Thy precious Cross, O Christ, Who art abundant in mercy.

*Stichera to the Saint, Tone 1:*

With sacred and divinely speaking voices, come, all ye faithful, let us spiritually celebrate the memory of the Hieromartyr Josaphat: for he cut down the thorns of schism, and planted for souls saving teachings, bearing abundant fruit of virtues.

Celebrating the divine Gospel, and sealing the divine teachings with his blood, for the sake of the sheep scattered by discord, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: thou didst dry up the heretical torrent, and as a victor didst ascend into heaven, receiving the crown of victory.

Ascending by degrees, the Church acquired thee by thy sacred struggles, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: which keeping unharmed and unassailed by hostile wolves, she proclaims thy valiant deeds and magnifies thee, O blessed one.

*Glory... Tone 5:* Celebrating this thy radiant festival, the Church rejoices, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, faithfully honoring thee: who as a pastor cease not from grace, offering prayer for our sins.

*Now and ever... of the Feast, Tone the same [Tone 5]:* Come, ye people, beholding the marvelous wonder, let us bow down before the power of the Cross: for even as the tree in paradise budded forth death, so this Tree hath blossomed forth life, having the sinless Lord nailed upon it: from Whom all nations receiving incorruption cry aloud: O Thou Who by the Cross hast abolished death, and delivered us, glory to Thee!

*If it fall on a Sunday, we sing at the Aposticha: of the Resurrection. Glory... to the Saint: Now and ever... of the Feast. But if on another day, we sing all to the Saint.*

*At the Aposticha, Stichera to the Saint, Tone 1. Podoben: "As one noble" (Ꙗ҆́кѡ до́блѧ):*

*Sticheron 1:* As a most radiant sun, thou didst spiritually shine forth upon the firmament of the Church, enlightening souls darkened by the malice of the enemy with the radiance of thy words and thy divinely wise teaching, and with thine angel-like life: and thou didst guide the erring into the true path, and brought them unto the union of the Orthodox Catholic Church:[^55] therefore rejoicing today, we celebrate thy radiant and holy memory.

*Verse: Thy priests, O Lord, shall be clothed with righteousness [LXX Ps. 131:9a].[^56]*

*Sticheron 2:* Equal in honor unto the Apostles wast thou, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, having received a mind equal in zeal: thou didst not fear the cruelty of ungodly persecutors, but manly and with boldness didst preach the union of faith, and enduredst multifaceted wounds and torments: and now a great reward for thy longsuffering hast thou received from the one struggle-bestower Christ our God, Whom pray earnestly for all who honor thy holy memory.

*Verse: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints [LXX Ps. 115:6].[^57]*

*Sticheron 3:* Offering an honorable liturgy, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, and an undefiled sacrifice unto Christ God, a whole-burnt offering thou didst completely offer thyself unto Him: thou didst suffer manly for the sake of His love, and pouredst out thy blood: and as a true shepherd and heir of Christ, thou didst not cease crying aloud: Father, keep all in Thy name, that they all may be one [cf. John 17:11, 21].[^58] And now, all-blessed Josaphat, pray that unity may be granted unto the Churches, and that we all who abide in unity may be saved.

*Glory... Tone 4:* Thy good works shone forth as the sun, in heaven and on earth, O servant of Christ, all-blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat: for gloriously didst thou preach unto us the true and blameless faith of Christ, for whose sake also thou laidst down thy life as a good shepherd, as a true heir of Christ our God, the Chief of shepherds: Whom entreat, O all-blessed hierarch, in thy memory, O God-bearer, for those who celebrate with love the all-venerable memory of thy suffering.

*Now and ever... of the Feast, Tone 2:* He whom Moses of old prefigured in himself, when having cast down Amalek he vanquished him:

...and David the psalmist, crying aloud, commanded to worship at Thy footstool: before Thy precious Cross, O Christ God, today we sinners bow down: with unworthy lips hymning Thee Who didst will to be nailed upon it, we pray: O Lord, with the thief vouchsafe us Thy Kingdom.

*If on Sunday, Resurrection Troparion: Glory... to the Saint: Now and ever... of the Feast. But if on another day, Troparion to the Saint, Tone 4:[^59]*

A radiant luminary didst thou appear, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: for as a good shepherd, thou laidst down thy life for the sheep, being slain by schismatic enemies; and thou didst enter into the Holy of Holies, dwelling with the incorporeal hosts: therefore we entreat thee, O much-suffering one: pray unto Christ, the Chief of shepherds, to number us also among the flock that stands at His right hand, and to save our souls.

*Glory... Now and ever... Troparion of the Feast: "Save, O Lord, Thy people".*

*At Matins, after the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8. Podoben: "That which was mystically commanded" (Пове́лѣнное та́йнѡ):* Having forsaken all earthly things, and having lived in the world as an angel in bodily purity, by the Spirit thou wast a communicant of the angels: having mortified present bodily passions, thou appearedst as a servant of the Holy Trinity, O all-blessed one: therefore thou healest the passions of the sick, and drivest away evil spirits by the grace of Christ, O our God-bearing father, Hieromartyr Josaphat: pray unto Christ God, to grant forgiveness of sins unto those who celebrate with love thy holy memory.

*Glory... Now and ever... of the Feast:* Joshua the son of Nun mystically prefigured of old the image of the Cross, when he stretched out his hands crosswise, O my Savior, and the sun stood still until he cast down the enemies that opposed Thee, O God: for now it set seeing Thee upon the Cross, and having destroyed the dominion of death, Thou didst raise up the whole world.

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4. Podoben: "Joseph was amazed" (Оу҆диви́ся І҆ѡ́сифъ):* An altar of blood was thy heart hidden within, and bloodless sacrifices celebrating thou didst offer unto God: and thou sufferedst valiantly from ungodly enemies, and having endured multifaceted torments, thou wast offered as an acceptable sacrifice unto Christ our God Who sacrificed Himself for us, O all-blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, praised by all: Whom earnestly pray evermore, for all who with love hymn thee.

*Glory... Now and ever... of the Feast:* Moses having stretched out his hands unto the height of heaven, prefigured the Cross, the divine weapon of the faithful, whereon Christ nailed our sins: therefore the enemy wept suffering, and feeling pain in his senses said: The ancient spear hath pierced me in the midst of my heart: Christ is the releaser of the bonds of Hades.

*If it fall on a Sunday, Resurrection Sessional Hymns: Then, "Praise ye the name of the Lord": Then:*

*Megalynarion:* We magnify thee, O Hieromartyr of Christ Josaphat, and we honor thy precious sufferings, which thou didst endure for the sake of unity.

*Verse 1: Hear this, all ye nations [LXX Ps. 48:2a].*

*Verse 2: My mouth shall speak of wisdom [LXX Ps. 48:4a].*

*Verse 3: Lord, I have loved the beauty of Thy house [LXX Ps. 25:8a].*

*Verse 4: The mouth of the righteous shall meditate wisdom [LXX Ps. 36:30a].*

*Verse 5: The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance [LXX Ps. 111:6b].*

*Verse 6: Blessed are they that dwell in Thy house [LXX Ps. 83:5a].*

*Glory... Now and ever... Alleluia, thrice.*

*After the Polyeleos, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:* Having cast down the pride of schismatic enemies, as a good shepherd and a mighty warrior didst thou appear: a crown of victory hast thou received from the right hand of the Most High, the Chief of shepherds, Christ our God: therefore we praise thee today as a good shepherd and victorious sufferer, celebrating in faith thy solemn memory: wherein pray that we may be saved from visible and invisible enemies, O Hieromartyr of Christ Josaphat, much-suffering one.

*Glory... Now and ever... of the Feast, Tone 8. Podoben: "That which was mystically commanded" (Пове́лѣнное та́йнѡ):* In paradise of old the tree stripped me bare, by tasting whereof the enemy brought in death; but the Tree of the Cross, bearing the garment of life unto men, was erected upon the earth, and the whole world was filled with all joy: which beholding exalted, with faith let us cry aloud together unto God: Thy house is full of glory!

*If it be not Sunday, Gradual Antiphon, Tone 4, 1st Antiphon. Prokeimenon, Tone 4: Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints. Verse: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He hath rendered unto me? Gospel from John, pericope 35 from the half [John 10:1–9].[^60] After Psalm 50, Glory: Through the prayers of the Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, O merciful One, blot out the multitude of my transgressions. Now and ever: Through the prayers of the Most Holy Birthgiver of God: Then the Sticheron to the Saint, Tone 6: "A lawful priest even unto thy demise": written at "Lord, I have cried": see fol. 13v.[^61]*

*If it fall on a Sunday: Resurrection Antiphons, Prokeimenon, Gospel, and the rest are sung: then the Resurrection Canon with Heirmoi, and of the Birthgiver of God, and of the Feast, and to the Saint, according to the Typikon. Katavasia: of the Feast. But if on another day, we sing the Canons of the Feast and to the Saint.*

*Canon to the Holy Hieromartyr Josaphat, Tone 2. Ode 1. Heirmos: Come, ye people, let us sing a song unto Christ:* Refrain: *Blessed Hieromartyr Josaphat, pray unto God for us.*

As a divinely shining star, the spiritual Sun showed thee forth upon the height of His Church: for thou didst enlighten the universe with thy sufferings and wonders.

From thy youth being dedicated unto God, O Josaphat, by acceptable virtues thou didst enlighten my soul, and didst make clear the world by precious sufferings for the sake of unity.

Thou didst shine forth radiantly in the Church of Christ with a double brightness, O newly manifest Hieromartyr: therefore the Benefactor of all hath crowned thee with a double crown in sacred splendor. *Theotokion:*

O all-pure Birthgiver of God, who beyond nature gavest birth unto the co-eternal and pre-eternal Word, we hymn thee. *Katavasia: Tracing a cross, Moses with his rod:*

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Establishing me upon the rock of faith:*

An undefiled sacrifice, streaming and bloodless, offering unto the Lord, thou didst also immaculately offer thyself unto slaughter by blood, O Hieromartyr Josaphat.

Imitating Christ, for the sake of unity thou laidst down thy life, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, and the walls of schism didst thou tear down.

Filled with wisdom, thou didst confound schismatics, O blessed Josaphat, having hypostatic divine wisdom in thy heart, wherewith with a crash thou didst strike their feeble boastings. *Theotokion:*

Grant us help by thy prayers, O all-pure one, driving away the assaults of fierce tribulations. *Katavasia: The rod is an image of the mystery:*

*Kontakion and Ikos of the Feast [of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross]: "Lifted up upon the Cross":*

*Sessional Hymn to the Saint, Tone 4. Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):* Thou didst burn up the deception of schismatics, O Hieromartyr Josaphat; thou didst clarify the faith well by unity, and enlightenedst the world, therefore a wonderworking sufferer didst thou appear, a pillar of the Church, and a true shepherd: pray unceasingly unto Christ, that we all may be saved.

*Glory... Now and ever... of the Feast, Tone and Podoben: The same.* In thee, O thrice-blessed and life-giving Cross, the people rejoicing celebrate with immaterial choirs: ranks of hierarchs reverently sing praise, the multitude of monastics and ascetics bow down, and Christ Who was crucified we all glorify.

*Ode 4. Heirmos: I have heard, O Lord, the report of Thy coming:*

Thou didst guide the flock of Christ unto the light of unity, O Hieromartyr, and drovest away the darkness of schisms; and having suffered for unity, receiving the martyr's crown, thou criedst: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

The drawing near unto the thrice-radiant Godhead shone upon thee as an unwaning light, and made thee a pure vessel of divine gifts, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, therefore thou singest: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

Walking by the path of virtue, O Josaphat, thou reachedst the mansions on high, O blessed one, adorned with the anointing of priesthood, and shining with the blood of martyrdom. *Theotokion:*

Enlighten my darkened soul with thy light, O all-pure one, who didst bear the light of one hypostasis. *Katavasia: I have heard, O Lord, the mystery of Thy dispensation:*

*Ode 5. Heirmos: O Giver of light and Creator of ages:*

God is my light and the giver of understanding, cried Josaphat before the schismatics, not fearing their threats: therefore struck on the head with a halberd,[^62] he now rejoices in the everlasting light of the Lord.

Riches and radiant glory, length of days and incorruptible life hath Christ the true Wisdom granted unto thee, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, for from infancy thou lovedst Him.

Thou fleddest from those who kissed thee deceitfully unto the nest of the mute, and wast joined with the choirs of martyrs: remember us who abide in unity, who joyfully celebrate thy memory, O blessed Josaphat. *Theotokion:*

Still the turbulent storm of my passions, O Birthgiver of God, who didst bear God, the Sustainer and Lord. *Katavasia: O thrice-blessed Tree:*

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Held fast in the abyss of sins:*

Into the depth of the river Dvina did the lawless schismatic people cast thy holy relics,[^63] yet a pillar from heaven showed them as immaterial: and the people taking them with joy, gave thanks unto God for great signs and wonders.

Come, all ye united, let us solemnly honor the divine turtledove of the Spirit, the trumpet clearly preaching the divine mysteries of unity, the hierarch of the throne of Polotsk, the theological tongue.

We bless thee as the guide of unity and the fountain of healings, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, confirmation of the Church and citizen of heaven. *Theotokion:*

A temple of God, the Ark, an animate bridal chamber, and the Heavenly Gate do we faithful proclaim thee, O Birthgiver of God.

*Katavasia: In the belly of the aquatic beast:*

*Kontakion to the Saint, Tone 4:* Enlightened from youth by a fiery ray from Christ crucified, imitating the angels in monastic life, and having piously lived in hierarchic rank, thou clearly preachedst unity; by thy martyr's blood thou quenchedst the kindle of the schismatics, and receivedst a crown from Christ: therefore remember us who cry unto thee: Rejoice, unshakable pillar of unity!

*Ikos:* As an angel didst thou appear upon earth, clothed in flesh, proclaiming the ineffable mysteries of God: and thou instructedst us to cry unto thee, O divinely speaking one, these things: Rejoice, whereby the darkness of schismatics was darkened; Rejoice, whereby the light of unity arose; Rejoice, angel of the uncreated Godhead; Rejoice, true reprover of created and material deceit; Rejoice, for thou didst well declare the glory of God; Rejoice, sun that appeared as a luminary; Rejoice, cup that dispenses divine drink; Rejoice, for thou receivedst the martyr's crown; Rejoice, for in the heavens thou standest before the Holy Trinity, and dancest with the angels; Rejoice, for with the martyrs thou art glad; Rejoice, whereby truth blossoms forth; Rejoice, unshakable pillar of unity!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The furnace of old bubbling with fire:*

With drops of thy blood, O all-glorious one, thou quenchedst the deceitful furnace of the schismatics, and in life wast established crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

The sword and arrows of the heretics didst thou shatter, and the pride of the mindless schismatics truly didst thou scatter like a great falling stone, O Hieromartyr, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

By thy words and dogmas was the united faith sealed, and the fury of joy of the schismatics ceased, O glorious Josaphat; the destruction of unity was abolished, and the power of the mindless was broken. *Theotokion:*

A rod didst thou bud forth, O all-blessed Virgin, from the root of Jesse, bearing the fruit of salvation unto those who cry with faith unto thy Son: O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou! *Katavasia: The mindless decree of the ungodly tyrant:*

*Ode 8. Heirmos: The fiery furnace of old:*

With praises is thy memory celebrated, O divinely speaking one, for from divine inspiration thou learnedst the knowledge of things that are, and knewest beforehand thy suffering and departure to the highest: and angels conducted thy soul unto heaven, joyfully singing unto the Creator: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and exalt Him unto ages.

Thou standest now, divinely blessed, before the throne of the Almighty, as a true son and of one mind, O all-blessed Josaphat, first hierarch of Polotsk, irreproachable defender of the united Church: therefore by righteous testimony and peace hast thou sealed her unto ages.

By thy hand and by thy wise voices is the concord of schismatics vanquished and distressed, O Hieromartyr Josaphat, and as smoke their rotten madness vanished like smoke, through thy thunder-voiced and theological tongue. *Theotokion:*

Beyond nature thou gavest birth, O Virgin, unto the Creator and Savior, God the Word, Whom we exalt unto all ages. *Katavasia: Bless the Lord, ye children equal in number to the Trinity:*

*Ode 9. Heirmos: God, God the Word:*

Being wholly filled with supreme wisdom, O all-glorious one, thou enlightenedst the world with the light of unity; thou conceivedst the divine fear, and broughtest forth the words of the Spirit: therefore thou becamest the most radiant dwelling of the Holy Trinity: therefore we bless thee.

Armed with love and hope, and walled about by faith, thou destroyedst the snares and deceits of the enemy; and becoming a victor, thou now livest as a crown-bearer with the divine choirs of angels and prophets around the throne of the King of all in heaven.

Having boldness unto God, remember, O all-blessed Josaphat, those who joyfully celebrate this thy divine festival, O Hieromartyr, and who honor with faith thy precious and sacred suffering: and deliver us from all tribulations and distresses.

*Theotokion:* Shatter the arms of them that oppose the Church of Christ, for the sake of the Birthgiver of God, and confirm our King,[^64] that we may unceasingly magnify thee. *Katavasia: A mystery art thou, O Birthgiver of God:*

*EXAPOSTILARION.*

Mercilessly the enemy severed thy head, O blessed hierarch, whom Christ God in the highest crowned with a crown of victory: O much-suffering luminary of the universe unto ages, ornament of archbishops, glory of martyrs, and praise of all venerable ones.

*Glory... Now and ever... of the Feast:* The Cross is the preserver of all the universe: the Cross is the beauty of the Church: the Cross is the might of kings: the Cross is the confirmation of the faithful: the Cross is the glory of angels and the wound of demons.

*At the Praises, Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Wholly from youth dedicating thyself together unto Christ, being above the flesh and worldly love, a sacred monk didst thou become, O most sacred Father Josaphat; and a vessel of the Holy Spirit, thou madest thy soul and body pure: living on earth as an angel among youths, in obedience and poverty, in love toward God and neighbor, in prayers and all-night vigils, thou becamest a pattern in time for monastics, and a mirror of every virtue: thou wingedst thy mind unto heavenly things, to behold the ineffable beauty of Christ the King and God, Whom having loved with all thy heart, and strengthened by His power, thou enduredst many sorrows and torments: Whom with boldness entreat for our souls.

*Sticheron 2:* Sitting upon the hierarchic throne, O blessed one, thou adornedst thyself with all virtues, being a guide unto repentance and salvation by teaching and deeds: therefore armed with purity and fervent prayers, with fasting and all-night vigils, and drawing near like the Master Christ unto suffering and the death of the Cross, lying upon the earth crosswise making prayer, thou preparedst thyself for suffering, O Hieromartyr Josaphat: therefore ungodly enemies, falling upon thee as merciless beasts, dragging thee as an innocent lamb, unjustly tore thee in pieces, who sufferedst valiantly and with boldness prayest for our souls.

*Sticheron 3:* The ungodly people, thine enemies, as David the psalmist said in the Psalms, they that hate thee made a tumult, they lifted up the head against thee, O blessed Josaphat, by the devil's will, and consulting an ungodly counsel they said: Come, let us destroy him from among men, and his name shall be remembered no more [cf. LXX Ps. 82:3–5].[^65] And having consulted together with one mind, the mighty fell upon thee, and severed thy precious head, beating thee mercilessly and cruelly with stones, and poured out thy blood like water throughout the whole city: likewise having tied stones to thy neck and holy feet, they cast thee down from the hill into the swift current of the river. Behold thy courage and longsuffering! Therefore thou hast received, O much-suffering Josaphat, an unfading crown.

*Glory... Tone 7:* Having loved Christ God from infancy, O all-blessed Josaphat, having forsaken the world for the sake of His love, and all the fair things in the world, in obedience, purity, and monastic poverty thou followedst all the days of thy life: having made slumber of works for our sake unto Christ God, monastically thou pleasedst Him: light as a good bird, thou madest thyself ready for suffering like a martyr; and for the sake of the Union of the Church of God, thou enduredst patiently many sorrows from ungodly people: being beaten and struck, and wounded throughout thy whole body the entire day with many wounds, and having thy head severed, thou wast vouchsafed crowns from Christ God: Whom entreat, O all-blessed one, for those who with love celebrate thine ever-honorable memory, that we may be delivered from visible and invisible enemies, from passions and assaults of the devil, and from the future judgment, and that our souls may be saved.

*Now and ever... of the Feast, Tone the same [Tone 7]:* Today the Cross of the Lord comes forth, and the faithful receive it with longing, and take healings of soul and body and of every disease. This let us kiss with joy and fear: with fear, on account of sin, being unworthy; and with joy, on account of the salvation which Christ God, Who was nailed upon it, grants unto the world, having great mercy.

*Great Doxology. And the Dismissal.*
