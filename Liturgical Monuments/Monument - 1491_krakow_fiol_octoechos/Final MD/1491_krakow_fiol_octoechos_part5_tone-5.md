# TONE 5

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 5)

[p114LA] *On Saturday evening, at "Lord, I have cried," we set 8 stichoi, and sing the Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 5:*[^125]

*Sticheron:* By Thy precious Cross, O Christ, Thou hast put the devil to shame; and by Thy Resurrection Thou hast blunted the sting of sin, and saved us from the gates of death: we glorify Thee, O Only-begotten One!

*Sticheron:* He that grants resurrection unto the human race was led as a sheep to the slaughter: the princes of Hades were terrified of Him, and the mournful gates were lifted up; for the King of glory, Christ, entered, saying unto the prisoners: Go forth! and unto them that sit in darkness: Be enlightened!

*Sticheron:* A great marvel! The invisible Creator, through love of mankind, having suffered in the flesh, is risen immortal! Come, families of the nations, let us worship Him; for being delivered from deception by His tender mercy, we have learned to sing of one God in three Persons!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian:*

*Sticheron:* An evening worship do we offer unto the never-setting Light, Who at the end of the ages, as in a glass, shone forth in the flesh upon the world, and descended even unto Hades, and dispelled the darkness there, and showed the light of the Resurrection unto the nations: O Giver of light, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* Glorify Christ, the Author of our salvation; for when He rose from the dead, the world was saved from deception: the choir of Angels rejoices, demonic deception flees, fallen Adam [p115LA] arises, and the devil is brought to naught![^126]

*Sticheron:* Those of the guard were instructed by the lawless: Conceal the Resurrection of Christ; take silver and say: While we were asleep, the dead man was stolen from the tomb. Who ever saw or who ever heard of a dead man being stolen, especially one anointed and naked, leaving his grave clothes in the tomb? Be not deceived, O Judeans! Learn the prophetic proclamations and understand that He is truly the Redeemer of the world and Almighty!

*Sticheron:* O Lord Who didst lead Hades captive and abolish death, O our Savior, Who hast enlightened the world by Thy precious Cross: have mercy on us!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:* In the Red Sea the image of the unwedded Bride was inscribed of old: there Moses was the divider of the water; here Gabriel was the minister of the miracle: then Israel crossed the deep dryshod; now the Virgin hath given birth to Christ without seed. The sea after the passage of Israel remained impassable; the Immaculate One after the birth of Emmanuel remained incorrupt: O Thou Who art and Who wast before, and hast appeared as Man, O God, have mercy on us!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 5:*

*Sticheron:* Thee, Christ the Savior, Who wast incarnate and yet wast not parted from heaven, do we magnify in the voice of song: for Thou didst accept the Cross and death for our race, as the Lord Lover of mankind, overthrowing the gates of Hades and rising on the third day, saving our souls!

*Other Stichera, Alphabetical:*

*Sticheron:* From Thy pierced [p116LA] side, O Life-Giver, Thou didst pour forth a stream of forgiveness unto all, life and salvation; and accepting death in the flesh, Thou didst grant us immortality; and dwelling in the tomb, Thou hast freed us, and gloriously raised us up together with Thyself as God: therefore do we cry unto Thee: O Lord Lover of mankind, glory to Thee![^127]

*Sticheron:* Awesome is Thy Crucifixion and Thy descent into Hades, O Lover of mankind: for leading it captive, Thou didst gloriously raise up with Thyself the ancient prisoners as God, and opened Paradise, and accounted them worthy to receive it: therefore unto us also who glorify Thy third-day rising, grant cleansing of sins, and vouchsafe us to be dwellers of Paradise, as the only Compassionate One!

*Sticheron:* Having accepted death in the flesh for our sake, and risen from the dead on the third day: heal the passions of our flesh, and raise us up from grievous transgressions, O Lover of mankind, and save us!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Thou art the temple and the door, the palace and the throne of the King, O all-pure Virgin; through thee my Redeemer, Christ the Lord, appeared unto those sleeping in darkness, being the Sun of Righteousness, willing to enlighten those whom He fashioned in His image with His own hand: therefore, O all-hymned one, as thou hast motherly boldness toward Him, pray unceasingly that our souls may be saved!

*Troparion of the Resurrection, Tone 5:* Let us the faithful praise and worship the Word, co-unoriginate with the Father and the Spirit, Who was born of the Virgin for our salvation; for He was well pleased to ascend the Cross in the flesh, and to endure death, and to raise [p117LA] up the dead by His glorious Resurrection![^128]

*Theotokion:* Rejoice, impassable gate of the Lord! Rejoice, rampart and protection of those who flee unto thee! Rejoice, stormless haven of our salvation, who in the flesh didst bear Thy Creator and God! Cease not to intercede for those who sing of and worship in faith thy childbirth!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 5)

*Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, Tone 5. Ode 1. Heirmos: The horse and the rider... (Коня и всадника...):*

*Troparion:* Singing of the dominion of the unitary and three-sunned splendor, we cry aloud: Enlighten our mind, O Almighty God, and raise it up, O Master, unto Thine ineffable glory!

*Troparion:* On high the Angels and transcendent intellects unceasingly glorify in three counsels the three-sunned Unity, identically fashioned, supersubstantial and almighty.

*Troparion:* Grant unto my soul the sweetest, light-giving divine drink of Thy love, O Trinity, all-radiant Unity, a divine cleansing compunction, O Master, the only mercy of all creation!

*Theotokion:* As dew upon the fleece came down from heaven, as rain into thy womb, without seed, O all-pure one, the Word without experience of man.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Establish... (Утверди...):*

*Troparion:* Having established the noetic essences of intellects as unceasing singers of Thy Godhead, O all-radiant God-originating Unity: receive also the praise and supplication of the faithful born of earth, as Thou art merciful!

*Troparion:* Thou Who art naturally unreceptive to any addition, from the singers on earth receive praise, O unfathomable [p118LA] fountain of Thy goodness, and grant remission of sins, O Sun of Glory, Who art most pure![^129]

*Troparion:* Glorify the Father, and the Son, and the Spirit, in the boundless splendor of the Godhead, and Thee, the unitary, thrice-radiant God of all, even as the Prophets and Apostles clearly taught concerning Thee!

*Theotokion:* Thou hast appeared unto us, O Father, together with the Son and the Spirit, as the Angel of Great Counsel of the Almighty, manifesting Thy dispensation through the Virgin, O Word of God, through Whom Thou hast enlightened us!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 5.*

*Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́льное Сло́во):*

*Theotokion:* O Virgin Theotokos, thou hast given birth unto One of the Trinity, O most beautiful Paradise, salvation of the earthly: protect with thy covering those who piously sing of thee; for thou didst bear Him of Whom the Prophets spoke, for thou didst carry Him Who upholds all things!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Thy divine... (Божественное Твое...):*

*Troparion:* Knowing the thrice-radiant light of the one Lordship, Daniel beheld God the Judge upon the throne, the vision foreshadowing the Son and the Spirit.

*Troparion:* Let the choir of the faithful sing with their lips of the supersubstantial God in three Hypostases, yet unitary in essence, and make them worthy of angelic glory!

*Troparion:* One dominion, one Godhead in three Persons do I indivisibly glorify: O Father, Son, and Spirit, enlighten my wretched [p119LA] soul![^130]

*Theotokion:* Thee, the pure mountain which Habakkuk beheld of old, from whence the Holy One came forth, and who foresaw the conception of thy childbirth, O Virgin!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Clothed with light... (Одеяйся свѣтом...):*

*Troparion:* Thou Who in Thy goodness didst create man according to Thine image, make an abode in me, O my thrice-radiant God, as Thou art good and compassionate!

*Troparion:* Do Thou guide me, O three-sunned Unity, unto the paths of divine salvation, not of the flesh, which essence is in us of unnumbered strength.

*Troparion:* O indivisible Light, contemplation of the forms divided in essence, thrice-radiant and never-setting: enlighten my heart with light!

*Theotokion:* Even as the Prophet, beholding thee of old, O all-pure one, saw an unentered door facing toward the east, an uncomprehended dwelling!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Surging... (Вълнящееся...):*

*Troparion:* The thrice-radiant essence of the God-originating Hypostases, shining unitarily, as co-fashioned and of equal power, according to essence and will.

*Troparion:* Sufficiently did the Prophet declare unto the morning light: beholding by the Spirit the one God of all, yet not a single Person.

*Troparion:* Having the unceasing power of essence in three Hypostases, one dominion and power, whereby every creature consists and is renewed.

*Theotokion:* Deliverance from transgressions and temptations, O unitary and thrice-radiant divine Power: send down Thy peace upon Thy singers through the prayers of the Mother of God!

*Sessional Hymns, Tone 5.*

*Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́льное Сло́во):*

*Troparion:* Have mercy on me, O my Savior, have mercy on me [p120LA] and reprove me not in Thy wrath, O Merciful One, neither enter into judgment with Thy servant; for I know in truth my foul transgressions which surpass the sands: therefore crying unto Thee with sighs, have mercy on me![^131]

*Troparion:* Despairing, I have fled unto Thee, the hope of all and refuge of sinners; humbly I cry unto Thee: I have sinned! I abide in evil, unfeeling and wretched: have mercy on me, turn me back before the end, and deliver me from eternal torment!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: Highly exalted... (Превозносимый...):*

*Troparion:* Having the bottomless abyss of mercy, unfathomable, O Compassionate One, have mercy on him that sings unto Thee, the one thrice-radiant God, unto the ages!

*Troparion:* Mystically singing and honoring the incomprehensible, one-hypostatic and thrice-radiant God: grant unto Thy servants cleansing of transgressions!

*Theotokion:* Thou hast brought forth a branch, from the unoriginate Father the Flower of the Godhead, an ever-existing shoot, O Virgin, Who grants life unto all mankind!

*Theotokion:* Thou wast shown forth as the propitiation of nature, thou alone blessed one, who didst bear in thine arms God Who sits upon the shoulders of the Cherubim, Who grants life unto all mankind!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Unto Thee, the Creator of all, the youth...*

*Troparion:* That Thou mightest reveal of old the mystery of the one Lordship, O my God, the Trinity of Persons appeared in human likeness unto Abraham, who sang of Thy dominion unto ages!

*Troparion:* Vouchsafe me to behold Thy beneficent rays, O [p121LA] unapproachable, O Father Almighty, Word, and Spirit, that I may ever be well-pleasing unto Thee, O Lord of all![^132]

*Troparion:* Thou hast shone forth the thrice-radiant glory, O all-hymned One, Christ and Lord, calling all to hymn the one Godhead in three Persons unto the ages.

*Theotokion:* The gold-gleaming lampstand foreshadowed thee, who didst receive the ineffable, unapproachable Light; with light thou enlightenest the earthly, Whom we highly exalt unto the ages.

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Rejoice, O Isaiah! For a Virgin hath conceived...*

*Troparion:* Earthly hymns cannot fittingly hymn Thee, O unoriginate Trinity; but as Thou art good and the Lover of mankind, since we dare by faith, O God-originating and co-enthroned Unity, we offer glory and praise unto Thy dominion!

*Troparion:* Equally honoring Thee, the Seraphim hymn Thee with most holy lips as the one-originate and thrice-radiant God; with them receive also us sinners, O Lord, who sing of and magnify Thy dominion!

*Troparion:* Look upon our debts, Thou Who in three God-originating Hypostases art the one true God of glory, and grant comfort unto Thy servants, through the prayers of the all-pure one and Mother of God, holy above all!

*Theotokion:* As pure and immaculate Virgin, thou hast given birth unto the Savior Who delivers us from corruption, the immutable God; but now also send down upon us, O all-pure one, remission of our transgressions!

*Then 4 [hymns] of the other [choir], and the rest as indicated in the first Tone.*

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 5)

*At "God is the Lord," Troparion, Tone 5: [written at Vespers]. After the first stichology:*

[p122LA] *Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:* Let us praise the Cross of the Lord, and honor His holy burial with hymns, and glorify His Resurrection; for as God He raised the dead from the tombs, and brought to naught the dominion of death and the strength of the devil, and shone light upon those in darkness![^133]

*Sessional Hymn:* O Lord, Thou wast called dead, Who didst slay death; and Thou wast laid in a tomb, Who didst empty the tombs; above, soldiers guarded the tomb, while below Thou didst raise up those who slept from the ages: O Almighty and Incomprehensible Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Who can declare thy greatness? For thou didst bear the Creator of all creation, Who suffered for our sake, and rose from the dead, and raised [mankind], O Pure One, from the corruption of deceitful passions: O ecclesial throne of the Lord, chosen earth, and overshadowed mountain, amendment of sinners!

*After the second stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 5:* O Lord, the lawless condemned nailed Thee in their midst, and with a spear they pierced Thy side, O Merciful One; and Thou didst accept burial, shattering the gates of Hades; and Thou didst arise on the third day. The women ran to see Thee, and having seen Thee, they proclaimed the Resurrection unto the Apostles: O highly exalted Savior, Whom the Angels hymn, blessed art Thou, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sessional Hymn:* O Lord, on the third day of Thy Resurrection, at the veneration of the Apostles, Peter cried aloud unto Thee: The women showed boldness, but I was afraid; the thief confessed Thee, but I denied Thee: wilt Thou call [p123LA] me henceforth Thy disciple, or wilt Thou make me once more a fisher of the deep? But receive me in repentance, O Christ God, and have mercy on me![^134]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* O unwedded Mother, pure Theotokos, who didst turn Eve's sorrow into joy, we the faithful sing of and worship thee, for thou hast led us up from the ancient curse; and now pray unceasingly, O all-pure one, that we may be saved!

*Hypakoe, Tone 5:* Perplexed in mind by the angelic vision, yet enlightened in soul by the divine Resurrection, the Myrrh-bearers brought good tidings unto the Apostles: Proclaim the Resurrection among the nations, as the Lord works together with miracles, and grants unto us great mercy!

*Hymns of the Degrees, Tone 5. Antiphon 1. Verse:* When I am afflicted, David-like do I sing unto Thee, O my Savior: deliver my soul from a deceitful tongue!

*Verse: Blessed is the life of those who dwell in the wilderness, who are borne aloft by divine desire!*

*Glory... Both now...:* By the Holy Spirit all things visible and invisible are upheld; for being truly Sovereign, He is truly One of the Trinity.

*Antiphon 2. Verse:* Arise unto the mountains, O my soul; go thither, whence thy help shall come!

*Verse: May Thy right hand, O Christ, outstretched unto me, preserve me from all deception!*

*Glory... Both now...:* Theologizing, let us say unto the Holy Spirit: Thou art God, life, desire, light, and intellect; Thou art goodness, Thou reignest unto the ages!

*Antiphon 3:*

[p124LA] *Verse: Over those who said unto me: Let us go into the courts of the Lord, I was filled with great joy, sending up prayers!* [^135]

*Verse: In the house of David fearful things are accomplished: for there is a fire burning every shameful intellect.*

*Glory... Both now...:* By the Holy Spirit is the life-originating dignity, from Whom every living creature is inspirited, as from the Father and together with the Word.

*Prokeimenon, Tone 5: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up (LXX Ps. 9:33). Verse: I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart (LXX Ps. 9:2). Let every breath... The Matins Gospel: The Resurrection of Christ... Psalm 50: Have mercy on me, O God...*

*Canon of the Resurrection, Tone 5. Ode 1. Heirmos: The horse and the riders... (Коня и всадники...):*

*Heirmos:* The horse and the riders in the Red Sea, He Who shatters wars with a high arm, Christ shook off, and saved Israel, as they sang a triumphal hymn.

*Troparion:* Thee, O Christ, the thorn-bearing synagogue of the Hebrews, having preserved no love toward Thee, the Benefactor, crowned with thorns as Ancestor, Who didst annul the penalty of thorns!

*Troparion:* Thou didst raise me who had fallen into the pit, bowing down, O Life-Giver; having sought me out who was foul-smelling, and having endured the stench without experiencing sin, O Christ, Thou hast perfumed me with divine myrrh!

*Theotokion:* The curse is dissolved, and sorrow hath ceased: for the blessed and joyfully graced one hath shone forth joy and blessing unto the faithful, bearing Christ, the Flower, unto the whole universe!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Unto the Savior... (Спасителю...):*

*Troparion:* Nailed in the flesh unto the Cross of Thine own will, Thou hast freed by the wood him that had fallen through the ancient denial: let us sing unto Him alone, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* Nailed in the flesh upon the Cross, by the wood [p125LA] having destroyed the dominion of death, and descended into Hades, Thou art risen from the dead, O God, and hast reconciled the world in peace![^136]

*Theotokion:* O all-pure Mother of God, unceasingly pray unto Him Who was incarnate of thee, and Who was not parted from the bosom of the Father, to deliver our souls from every assault!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The same (Спасителю...):*

*Troparion:* The Light dwelt in thee, O all-pure one, and enlightened the world with the splendors of the Godhead: pray unto Christ to enlighten all who praise thee, O Virgin Mother!

*Troparion:* As thou wast adorned with the beauty of virtues, O rejoiced one, working good beauty, thou didst receive spiritual radiance, O all-pure one, [bearing] Him Who adorned all things!

*Troparion:* The Sinaitic bush foreshadowed thee of old, remaining unconsumed, O Theotokos, when touched by fire: for as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and as a Virgin thou didst remain, beyond comprehension, O Bride of God!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Establishing the earth upon nothing... (Утверждей землю ни на чемже...):*

*Heirmos:* Thou Who didst establish the earth upon nothing by Thy command, and suspended its uncontainable weight: on the immovable rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, establish Thy Church, O only Good One and Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:* Gall they sought [to give Thee], they who had sucked [honey from the rock] in the wilderness for Him Who poured it forth; they brought Thee vinegar, O Christ, instead of manna; such thankfulness did the senseless children of Israel render unto Thee!

*Troparion:* Those who of old were covered by the luminous cloud laid Christ the Life in the tomb; but rising by Thine own sovereign power, Thou didst grant unto all the faithful the spiritual radiance mystically overshadowing from above!

*Theotokion:* Thou, O Mother of God, without [p126LA] wedlock didst become [Mother] unto Him Who shone forth from the incorrupt Father, and without maternal pangs: therefore do we orthodoxly proclaim thee Theotokos, for thou didst bear the incarnate Word![^137]

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: By the power of the Cross... (Силою Креста...):*

*Troparion:* Risen from the tomb, O Christ, and delivering from the corruption of death: we hymn Thy third-day Resurrection, O Life-Giver!

*Troparion:* To anoint Thy body, O Christ, the Myrrh-bearers came unto Thy tomb, and finding it not, they returned with joy, glorifying Thy rising!

*Theotokion:* Pray unceasingly, O Pure One, unto Him Who came forth from thy loins, to deliver from the deceit of the devil those who praise thee as the Mother of God!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The same (Силою Креста...):*

*Troparion:* The ladder whereby the Most High came down unto us to restore corrupted nature, He visibly showed thee naturally unto all: for through thee the Pre-good One was well pleased to unite Himself unto the world!

*Troparion:* The mystery commanded of old by the Spirit, foreseen before the ages by all-seeing God, in these last of ages received fulfillment in thy womb, O all-pure one, and was manifested!

*Troparion:* The ancient condemnation of the curse was dissolved through thy mediation, O pure Virgin; for from thee God appeared, pouring forth blessing unto all as the Pre-good One!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Perceiving Thy divine condescension with prophetic insight, O...*

*Heirmos:* Perceiving Thy divine dispensation, marveling, Habakkuk cried aloud unto Thee with trembling, O Christ: Thou art come for the salvation of Thy people, to save Thy servants!

*Troparion:* The bitter waters of Marah [p127LA] as foreshadowing the image of Thy most pure Cross, O Lord, putting to death the condemnation of sin, Thou didst sweeten by the wood![^138]

*Troparion:* The Cross instead of the tree of knowledge, gall instead of food, the soldiers brought unto Thee, O Christ, while Thou didst pour out Thy divine blood, incorruptible in death!

*Theotokion:* Without wedlock thou didst conceive, incorruptibly receiving in the womb, and thou didst bear God in the flesh, and after childbirth thou wast preserved a Virgin!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: I heard, [O Lord,] the report... (Услышах слух...):*

*Troparion:* When Thy Cross was planted upon Calvary, O Christ, then the gates of Hades were shattered, and the eternal gatekeepers cried aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:* When Thou didst descend, O Christ, unto the bound as dead, Thou didst raise up with Thyself the dead from the ages, and they cried aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Theotokion:* O Virgin, thou didst give birth, and knewest not man, but wast known as the Mother of God, and remainedst a Virgin, as we praise thee: Rejoice, in hymns, O Theotokos!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The same (Услышах слух...):*

*Troparion:* With heart and mind, with soul and lips do we faithfully confess thee truly to be the Theotokos, O Pure One, receiving unfeigned salvation, O Virgin, through thy prayers!

*Troparion:* He Who created all things out of nothingness was clothed in flesh from thee, as the Benefactor was well pleased, for the salvation of those who sing of thee with faith and love, O all-pure one!

*Troparion:* Praising thy childbirth, O all-hymned one, the transcendent choirs rejoice in hymnody, beholding thee as the Theotokos nurturing an infant, O ecclesial Virgin!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Clothed with light as with a garment, unto Thee do I rise early and cry: Enlighten my darkened soul, O Christ, as Thou art merciful! (Одеяйся свѣтом яко ризою, к Тебѣ утренюю и к Тебѣ вопїю: душу мою просвѣти омраченную, Христе, яко Милостивъ!):*

[p128LA] *Troparion:* The glorious God, in marvelous form, hangs willingly upon the tree, stripped of all honor, providing for me ineffable divine glory![^139]

*Troparion:* Thou hast shown forth ever-shining incorruption, O Christ, having incorruptibly tasted mortal corruption in the flesh, rising from the tomb on the third day!

*Theotokion:* Thou who didst bear Christ without seed as salvation and righteousness unto us, freest ancestral dominion from the curse!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Rising early... (Утренюю...):*

*Troparion:* By Thy three-day burial Thou didst overthrow Hades, and manifestly didst raise all, as a merciful Lord!

*Troparion:* By Thy Cross, O Passionless One, Thou hast abolished death, O Christ God, and slain our sins!

*Theotokion:* Cease not to pray for us unto Him Whom thou didst bear, O Theotokos, that He may save the souls of us who praise thee, O Holy One!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The same (Утренюю...):*

*Troparion:* All the Prophets proclaimed beforehand concerning thee, that thou shouldest be the Mother of God, O all-pure Theotokos; for thou alone wast holy and seen to be immaculate!

*Troparion:* A luminous cloud, raining down living water unto us, having rained down the incorruptible Christ, known by unhoped-for tokens, O Pure One!

*Troparion:* As undefiled in immaculate beauty, He purely loved thy virginity to remain incorrupt, He Who dwelt within thee as the only Good and Compassionate One!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Surging with the soul-destroying tempest, O Master Christ, calm the sea of passions and lead me up from corruption, as Thou art merciful! (Волнящееся бурею душепагубною, Владыко Христе, похотное море укроти, и от тли возведи мя, яко Милостивъ!):*

*Troparion:* Into corruption fell the forefather, O Master Christ, having tasted of the forbidden food, and was raised unto life through Thy Passion [^140]

*Troparion:* As Life Thou didst draw near unto Hades, O Master Christ, and becoming incorruptible unto the corruptible, Thou didst grant Resurrection unto all!

*Theotokion:* As a Virgin thou didst give birth, and after childbirth thou remainedst Pure; and in thine arms, O true Virgin-Mother, thou didst carry Him Who upholds all things!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: The abyss encompassed me... (Обыде мя бездна...):*

*Troparion:* Thou didst stretch out Thy palms upon the Cross, gathering the scattered nations, O Christ our God, by Thy life-bearing Cross and Resurrection!

*Troparion:* Thou didst overthrow death, and shattered the gates of Hades, and released Adam who was bound; and they cried aloud: Thy right hand hath saved me, O Lord!

*Theotokion:* The unburnt bush, the spiritual mountain and ladder, and the heavenly gate, do we worthily glorify, O glorious Mary, praise of the Orthodox!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The same (Обыде мя бездна...):*

*Troparion:* The Cause of all things, Who granted existence unto all, accepted a cause by being incarnate for our sake, O immaculate Mother of God!

*Troparion:* Pouring forth a soul-nourishing fountain of healing, O Sovereign Lady, unto those who come in faith beneath thy glorious shelter, we brightly glorify thee!

*Troparion:* Thou hast given birth unto the Cause of salvation, the Life-Giver, granting eternal deliverance unto those who confess thee in truth to be the Theotokos!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: Highly exalted Lord of the fathers, Who quenched the flame and refreshed the children, who sang in one voice: Blessed art Thou, O God! (Превозносимый отцев Господь, пламень угасивый, отроки прохладивый, единогласно поющыя: Боже, благословенъ еси!):*

*Troparion:* Clothed in the flesh, Thou didst shatter by divine power upon the fishhook [of Thy flesh] the snares of the devil; Thou hast raised up those who cry unto Thee: Blessed art Thou!

[p130LA] *Troparion:* United in one Person, the Unapproachable One is concealed in the flesh within the tomb, unsearchable, unto Whom we sing: Blessed art Thou, O God![^141]

*Theotokion:* O all-immaculate one, thou didst give birth unto the incarnate God, one [Person] in two natures, unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: He Who in the fiery furnace... (Иже в пещи огненнѣй...):*

*Troparion:* By the wood of the Cross Thou didst tear down the deceit of idols: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:* Risen from the tomb, having raised the dead from Hades: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Theotokion:* As we all possess thee as salvation, O Theotokos, pray unto Him Who was incarnate of thee to save us!

*Troparion:* Remaining immaculate and unchanged according to fleshly constitution, yet changed in condescension as merciful toward thee, the Holy One, the only blessed God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* As an immaculate bride, O Theotokos and Sovereign Lady, do we harmoniously glorify thee, the throne and dwelling of thy Master, Whom we all hymn!

*Troparion:* Mother of the King of all, sanctified by the Spirit, O Virgin, didst thou become unto Him Who created thee, unto Whom we all sing: Blessed art Thou, O God!

*Troparion:* At Thine all-effecting descent, O Christ, Hades was mocked, and spewed forth the dead whom of old it had swallowed up through deceit, who sing and highly exalt Christ unto the ages!

*Troparion:* Thee, beyond comprehension [p131LA] who didst bear the Word in God-manly manner, being a Virgin: Hymn the Lord, all ye works, and highly exalt Him unto the ages![^142]

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Him Who before the ages of the Father... (Отца прежде вѣк...):*

*Troparion:* Nailed upon the Cross of Thine own will, O Christ, stretching out Thy palms, and destroying the dominion of death: Ye priests, hymn, and highly exalt Him!

*Troparion:* Him Who descended into Hades and abolished death, O Christ, and rose in glory, and raised the dead: Ye priests, hymn, and highly exalt Him!

*Troparion:* With us the bodiless host also sings, O Virgin, of thine incomprehensible childbirth, forming a united choir in love, highly exalting thee!

*Troparion:* Bright water of the immortal Spirit, coming forth from thee, wash away the filth of long ages for those who sing and highly exalt in faith!

*Troparion:* The living in truth and light-bearing throne and tables of grace confess thee, O Virgin, who didst receive the Word from thee; highly exalt Him!

*Troparion:* Thy God-flowing blood, poured forth, O Master Christ, from Thy most pure and life-creating ribs, a sacrifice for [p132LA] the destruction of idols; and the whole earth was filled with Thy praise![^143]

*Theotokion:* Not an incorporeal God, nor again a mere man, did the pure and immaculate one bear thee, but a complete man and truly perfect God, manifesting two natures!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Thee, beyond comprehension... (Тя паче ума...):*

*Troparion:* Thou didst accept passions in the flesh, and having delivered the whole world from deceit, O Merciful One, with unceasing hymns we magnify Thee!

*Troparion:* Thee, Who didst rise from the tomb on the third day, and cast down Hades, and enlighten the world, we the faithful unanimously magnify!

*Theotokion:* Rejoice, O Virgin, Mother of Christ God, Whom thou didst bear; pray unto Him to grant remission of sins unto those who praise thee in faith!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: The same (Тя паче ума...):*

*Troparion:* From thy pure blood was curdled the supernatural flesh for the Creator of all and for the only-begotten Son of His Father, not changing what He was, but taking an animate and ensouled nature, O Theotokos!

*Troparion:* Thou didst halt the circuitous, mortal, and restrained onset, having in truth given birth in the flesh, beyond comprehension, unto eternal Life, which when bitter Hades touched, it was shattered, O most holy Virgin Mother!

*Epistle [Rubric: To the Hebrews; Witness: Ephesians 4:7 & 1 Corinthians 12:7–11]:* Brethren, unto each one of us grace is given according to the measure of the gift of Christ. But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to profit withal: for to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom, and after this the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; to another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit; to another the working of powers and power; to another prophecy; to another kinds of tongues. But all these things the one and same Spirit works, dividing unto each one as He wills.

*Gospel [Rubric: Gospel according to John; Witness: Matthew 10:1, 5–6]:* At that time, Jesus called His twelve disciples, and gave them power over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every infirmity and every disease among the people. These twelve Jesus sent forth, saying: Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and enter not into a city of the Samaritans; but go rather unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel; [p137LA] and as ye go, preach, saying: The Kingdom of heaven is at hand! Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers; freely ye have received, freely give![^148]

*End. Praises.*

## SUNDAY EVENING VESPERS (TONE 5)

*On Sunday Evening, Stichera at "Lord, I have cried," Penitential, Tone 5, Prosomoia:*

*Sticheron 1:* Possessing fountains of tender mercy, and pouring forth an abyss of compassions and torrents of bounty: O most good Father, Word and Son, and Holy Spirit, uncreated Nature: receive our supplication and prayer, granting forgiveness unto all who are in transgressions, as Thou art a compassionate God and Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 2:* Thou possessest by nature as God a heart of bounties and a treasury of goodness; therefore we pray Thee, O Christ our Savior, and falling down we ever call aloud, crying: Grant unto Thy servants remission of debts and transgressions, and forgiveness of all things wherein we have all sinned, as Thou art a compassionate God and Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 3:* Desiring to save all as God, for whose sake Thou didst appear in human form as man: save us who worship Thy commandments; for Thou camest not, O Lover of mankind, to save the righteous, but those bound in transgressions; and us, who are shackled by many sins, do Thou release by the grace of divine baptism, as Thou art a compassionate God and Lover of mankind!

*Other Stichera to the Bodiless Powers.*

*Podoben: "The same" (то́йже):*

*Sticheron 4:* Enlightened by the radiant rays of Light, the ranks of the Bodiless [p138LA] hymn the Master of all, the All-Bountiful, Who made all things out of non-existence by wisdom, the Archangels with the Angels: by their prayers, as the only All-Good God, send peace unto all the world, and great mercy unto our souls![^149]

*Sticheron 5:* Ever beholding the face of God, enlightened by Him with thrice-radiant beams: enlighten us, and deliver us from the passions and temptations of the ages, O holy Archangels: by their prayers, O God, grant peace unto all the world, and great mercy unto our souls!

*Sticheron 6:* The Seraphim and Cherubim, and all Angels and Archangels, Thrones, Principalities, Dominions, and Powers unceasingly glorify the Divine Trinity, knowing one Lordship, with immaterial lips: pray ye unto Christ to grant peace unto all the world, and great mercy unto our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Fearful and most glorious is the great mystery: the Uncontainable was contained within thy womb, and as Mother after childbirth thou remainedst still a Virgin; for thou didst bear God Who came down and was incarnate for our sake! Unto Him let us cry aloud, unto Him let us sing a hymn, chanting with the Angels: Holy art Thou, O Christ God, Who wast made man for our sake; O Lord, glory to Thee!

*At the Aposticha, Penitential Stichera, Tone 5:*

*Sticheron 1:* O Lord, sinning I cease not; made worthy of Thy love for mankind, I understand not: overcome my darkening, O only Blessed One, and have mercy on me!

*Sticheron 2:* O Lord, I fear Thy dread judgment, yet doing evil I cease not; who at the judgment-seat [p139LA] fears not the judge? Or who that desires healing provokes the physician, as I do, O longsuffering Lord? Have compassion upon the weakness of my soul, and have mercy on me![^150]

*Martyrikon:* Disregarding the things of the earthly age, and manfully daring unto tortures of your own will: O blessed ones, ye missed not your hope, but became inheritors of the heavenly Kingdom, O all-praised Martyrs! Having boldness unto the merciful God, ask peace for all the world, and great mercy for our souls!

*Theotokion:* O rejoice-filled Mediatress: by thy prayers, without ceasing, ask a multitude of bounties and cleansing from our many stumbling-blocks!
