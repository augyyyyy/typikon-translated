# TONE 8

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 8)

[p189LA] *On Saturday Evening, at "Lord, I have cried", set 8 stichoi, and sing the Resurrection Stichera, Tone 8, Samoglasny:*

*Sticheron 1:* Evening hymn and spiritual service we offer unto Thee, O Christ, for Thou wast well pleased to have mercy upon us by [Thy] Resurrection!

*Sticheron 2:* Lord, O Lord, cast us not away from Thy presence, but as Thou wast well pleased, have mercy upon us by [Thy] Resurrection!

*Sticheron 3:* Rejoice, O Holy Sion, Mother of the Churches, dwelling-place of God! For thou didst first receive the remission of sins by the Resurrection!

*Eastern Stichera:*[^210]

*Sticheron 4:* The Word of God the Father, begotten before the ages, in the latter times incarnate of the unwedded one, willingly endured crucifixion and burial for three days, and saved man who of old had been dead, by His Resurrection!

*Sticheron 5:* Thy Resurrection from the dead we glorify, O Christ, for the sake whereof Thou didst free the race of Adam from the torment of Hades, and granted unto the world life eternal and great mercy!

*Sticheron 6:* Glory to Thee, O Christ Savior, Only-begotten Son of God, Who wast nailed unto the Cross and didst rise from the tomb on the third day!

*Sticheron 7:* Thee do we glorify, O Lord, Who for our sake willingly didst endure crucifixion; Thee do we worship, O Almighty Savior: cast us not away from Thy presence, but hear us and save us, for the sake of Thy Resurrection, O Lover of mankind!

[p190LA] *Glory... Both now... Dogmatikon Theotokion:* The King of heaven, for His love toward mankind, appeared on earth and lived among men; for having taken flesh from the pure Virgin, and having come forth from her with the assumption [thereof], He is one Son, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis. Therefore, proclaiming Him truly as perfect God and perfect Man, we confess Christ our God: entreat Him, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera:*

*Sticheron 1:* Thou didst ascend upon the Cross, O Jesus, Who camest down from heaven; Thou didst come unto death, O Immortal Life; unto those in darkness the True Light, and unto all the fallen the Resurrection: O our Savior, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera according to the Alphabet:*[^211]

*Sticheron 2:* Let us glorify Christ, Who is risen from the dead; for He took soul and body, and by the Passion severed both: for Thy most pure Soul, descending into Hades, which also He despoiled, while in the tomb Thy most pure Body knew not corruption, O Redeemer of our souls!

*Sticheron 3:* With psalms and hymns let us glorify Christ, Thy Resurrection from the dead, wherewith Thou didst free us from the torment of Hades, as God granting unto us life eternal and great mercy!

*Sticheron 4:* O Master of all, incomprehensible Creator of heaven and earth, enduring [crucifixion] upon the Cross, Thou didst pour forth dispassion unto us; and accepting burial, and rising in glory, Thou didst raise Adam with a mighty hand: glory to Thy Resurrection on the third day, wherewith Thou hast granted us life eternal, and cleansing of sins, as the only Lover of mankind!

*Glory... Both now...*

[p191LA] *Theotokion:* O unwedded Virgin Mother, who ineffably didst conceive God in the flesh, Mother of the Most High God: receive the supplications of Thy servants, O most immaculate one, who grantest unto all cleansing of transgressions; receive now also our humble supplications, and pray unceasingly that we all may be saved!

*Troparion, Tone 8:* From the heights didst Thou descend, O Compassionate One; Thou didst endure three-day burial, that Thou mightest free us from passions: O our Life and Resurrection, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Resurrection Theotokion, Tone 8:*[^212] He Who for our sake was born of the Virgin, and endured crucifixion, O Good One, Who by death didst despoil death, and manifest the Resurrection as God: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thy hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; accept the Theotokos who bore Thee, who intercedes for us; and save, O our Savior, a despairing people!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 8)

*Canon to the Holy Trinity, Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos: Arming... (Въоружена):*[^213] Unto the Thrice-radiant King and Builder and Provider of all, and unto the one God in one Godhead existing, offering single and common divine glory unto the One Beginning, singing the Thrice-Holy hymn:

*Troparion:* Having directed our journey on high by river-like divine revelations, let us glorify the God-originating Essence in the unity of co-eternal, unoriginate glory in Three Persons: Father, and Son, and Holy Spirit, all-effecting and all-powerful!

*Troparion:* Abraham, having become the sacred initiate of mysteries, in a holy manner of old entertained the Creator of all, God and Lord, in three Persons, and recognized the Three-Hypostatic, single Dominion!

*Theotokion:* O unwedded one, having given birth unto Christ, Who assumed our nature, O most pure one [p192LA] and remained without change both [natures]: entreat Him unceasingly to grant remission of my sins!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: The Affirmation...* Thee, the unapproachable God and King of glory, high upon the throne the heavenly [powers] beheld, Whom Cherubim and Seraphim glorify with unceasing hymns, the One Principle and Three-Hypostatic!

*Troparion:* The Word, Who from the Father alone was born as from Mind, and the Spirit proceeding incomprehensibly by all human thoughts: receiving through scriptural teachings, we revere the One God, Thrice-radiant!

*Troparion:* He Who is, the unbegotten Father, having timelessly begotten of His own Essence the Son, the Savior, Light from Light, brings forth the co-originating Light and Spirit, all-effecting and equal in honor!

*Theotokion:* Thou didst appear as the Church, O pure Virgin, Mother of the King of all, Who omnipotently and most wisely ordered and established the habitations; Whom entreat for me with thy maternal boldness!

*Sessional Hymn to the Trinity, Tone 8:*[^214] The power of the Thrice-radiant and thrice-shining unoriginate Principle let all us faithful hymn, and with one desire let all the angelic heavenly choirs and earthly ecclesiastical ranks assemble to cry: "Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O most good God: glory and praise unto Thy dominion!"

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Having given birth unto the ineffable and changeless God, Who ever delivers from sins and falls [p193LA] grievous, receive from Him change of sorrow, O good one, and by thy morning prayers [deliver me], that now with thanksgiving I may cry unto thee, O Mary, Mother of God, according to the mercy which thou hast obtained, O most immaculate one!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Thou art Christ God, Thou art...*[^215]

*Troparion:* The Dawns of the Godhead Thou didst manifest unto those in darkness, brightly dispersing all the soul's night, and the Sun of righteousness shone forth: simple indeed in Essence, but thrice-radiant in Persons, Whom we hymn in everlasting glory!

*Troparion:* Him Who is hymned by the mouths of the Seraphim, we faithful also glorify with our lips: the one Lord of glory, singing unto Thee in unity and Hypostases; O Compassionate One unto all, grant unto Thy servants, O only Good One, remission of sins!

*Troparion:* All-holding, all-knowing, invisible Essence, most bountiful, compassionate, and long-suffering, honorable and God-originating Trinity: forget not me, Thy servant, unto the end, nor annul the covenant which Thou didst establish with Thy servants, for the sake of Thine ineffable mercy!

*Theotokion:* Fair alone did the all-effecting and God-originating Word find thee from the ages, loving thy beauty, and dwelt within thee, renewing all human nature: Whom entreat unceasingly to deliver us from every torment!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Why hast Thou cast me off...*[^216]

*Troparion:* The co-eternal Three Persons of glory, the divine Essence dividing and uniting Thee as the One God, we faithfully cry: O One-Principle Holy Trinity, deliver Thy servants [p194LA] from every affliction and sorrow!

*Troparion:* I weep for the weakness of my mind, how against my will I suffer the change of lust; therefore I cry unto Thee: O Holy God-originating Trinity, direct me unto good by Thy mercy!

*Troparion:* Weighed down with the slumber of sin, and cast down unto death by sinful sleep; but as the Lover of mankind, most good and all-merciful, O Holy God-originating Trinity, have compassion and have mercy!

*Theotokion:* O Mother of God, Maiden Virgin most pure and immaculate, graced of God: by thy prayers entreat thy Son and God, O all-honorable one, swiftly to deliver thy servants now from passions!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Cleanse me, O Savior...*[^217]

*Troparion:* Imitating the ranks of heavenly minds, O all-ruling Trinity, supersubstantial unto all, we glorify Thee with thrice-holy hymns with our faithful lips!

*Troparion:* Worshipping and honoring the Three-Hypostatic [Godhead], Who created man according to His own image and brought him into being out of nothingness as was spoken of old, I sing and magnify!

*Troparion:* Almighty God, One and unapproachable, dwell within me according to Thine ineffable mercy, O Thrice-radiant Master: enlighten me and give me understanding, as God and compassionate!

*Theotokion:* Having become a pure temple unto God, show me forth as a temple of divine grace, O most pure Lady; preserve me unharmed by thy prayers!

*Kontakion, Tone 8, Podoben (*подобенъ*):*[^218] Confessing in truth, ye faithful, the Father without beginning, and the Son without beginning, and the Divine Spirit: indivisible [p195LA] uncircumscribed, and ineffable, O simple, holy, and divine Trinity, singing unto Thee with the Angels: "Holy art Thou, O Father and Son, with the Holy Spirit in Persons! Have mercy upon those whom Thou didst create according to Thine image, O Master!"

*Theotokion:* We ever give thanks unto God for thee, we magnify [thee] faithfully and worship, unceasingly hymning thy childbirth, O thou full of grace; we unceasingly hymn thee, saved, O all-merciful Virgin: that by God [thou mayest deliver us] from the snatching of demons and from the trial of the dread Day of Judgment, that we thy servants may not be put to shame!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The condescension of God...*[^219]

*Troparion:* By Thine ineffable wisdom, show forth the order of goodness, that Thy servant may freely be shown mercy; look upon my prayer, and ever free and deliver [me], O Trinity-Unity God, granting deliverance from sins and passions!

*Troparion:* Father, the unbegotten Mind, and the Word born of Him, and Holy Spirit, being co-ruling: we hymn Thee as Thrice-radiant!

*Troparion:* Unoriginate Trinity, indivisible in Essence, enthroned together in unity, inseparate in glory, primordial Essence and Kingdom: save those who believe in Thee!

*Theotokion:* Fallen into mortification through sins, O most pure one, I flee unto thee with faith, O Most Pure, Originator of life: by thy prayers make thy servant alive, and deliver from all temptations and passions!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Sevenfold...*[^220]

*Troparion:* Being the unwaning Light, thrice-shining and thrice-radiant, One Principle, One Dominion [p196LA] simple, incomprehensible God: enlighten now my dark-figured heart, make it radiant and light-bearing to hymn Thee!

*Troparion:* With sacred divine wings the Seraphim cover their faces in godly fear, not enduring the glory of the thrice-radiant beauty of the God-originating, co-ruling and God-beginning Thrice-Holy Trinity; yet we also, daring, hymn and glorify unto ages!

*Troparion:* Lordly Origin without beginning, all-powerful and all-effecting goodness, and all-perfect Beginning, deifying and unceasing, innocent Cause, creative, intentional, provident, and saving unto all: we glorify the Unity in Essence, the Trinity, unto ages!

*Theotokion:* The unwaning Sun shone upon earth, for Whose sake, from thy virginal birth, O most pure Lady, He delivered mankind from idolatrous darkness: therefore enlighten with light and preserve thy servants who hymn thee!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Thee, unwedded...*[^221]

*Troparion:* O King of glory and All-Holder of all, primordial Essence, from the beginning and Life-originating, Who wast well-pleased to bring [us] forth from non-being, compassionate, loving mankind: we sinners now glorify Thee, the unoriginate Trinity, asking remission, profound humility, and oneness of mind for the Churches!

*Troparion:* O Single Lordship thrice-shining, single God-Head in Three Persons: accept Thy singers in goodly beauty, and deliver us from transgressions and grievous temptations [p197LA] grant humility, and unto the Churches goodly order and assembly!

*Troparion:* Distinguishing Thee in Persons of Hypostasis, Thrice-Holy, in one Dominion we ever worship; all things standing by Thy word of command: grant forgiveness of sins, that we may worthily glorify Thee!

*Theotokion:* In the womb of the pure Virgin, O Christ, Thou didst make Thy dwelling; Thou wast manifestly shown forth unto all as God and Man, without change, without division, in truth: O God, ever protect Thy servants to be [Thee] unto ages, by the prayers of her that bore Thee; grant peace and humility unto all the world!

*Triadika Hymns, Tone 4: In truth... and the rest:*[^222]

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 8)

*At Matins, at "God is the Lord", the Resurrection Troparion; repeat it.*

*After the First Stichologia, Sessional Hymns:*[^223]

*Stanza 1:* Wonderfully was Thy tomb sealed, and an Angel rolled away the stone from the doors; and the women, having beheld Thee risen from the dead, announced the good tidings unto Thy disciples in Sion: that the Life of all is risen, and has broken the bonds of death: Lord, glory to Thee!

*Stanza 2:* With ineffable brightness appearing unto the Myrrh-bearing women, the Angel, arrayed in radiant garments of joy, spoke the word: "The sad dominion of death is destroyed, the joy of incorruption has shone forth, and having risen from the dead, the Savior has saved the human race!"

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Let us hymn the heavenly gate, and ark, the most holy mountain, radiant cloud, the unburnt bush, the rational paradise, and the deliverance of Eve, the great hope of the whole universe [p198LA] for salvation for the world was wrought in her, and remission of ancestral transgressions; therefore unto her [we cry]: O Mother of Christ God, grant remission of transgressions, for we thy servants have thee now as our Protectress!

*After the Second Stichologia, Sessional Hymns, Tone 8:*

*Stanza 1:* Risen from the dead art Thou, the Life of all, and a radiant Angel said unto the women: "Cease from tears and announce the good tidings unto the Apostles; cry aloud, singing: Christ the Lord is risen, Who was well-pleased as God to save the human race!"

*Stanza 2:* Unto that [sepulchre] the women brought burial [myrrh], and they heard the angelic voice from the tomb: "Cease from tears, receive joy, cry aloud, singing: Christ is risen, Who was well-pleased as God to save the human race!"

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*[^224] All creation rejoices in thee, O thou full of grace: the angelic assembly and the human race; hallowed temple and rational paradise, virginal boast, of whom God was incarnate and became a child, [yet] changeless, our God Who exists before the ages: for He made thy womb a throne, and made thy womb more spacious than the heavens; all creation rejoices in thee, O thou full of grace: glory to thee!

*Hypakoe:* The Myrrh-bearing women, standing at the tomb of the Life-Giver, sought the Master among the dead, the Immortal One; and receiving the joy of the good tidings from the Angel, they announced the good tidings unto the Apostles: Christ is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Hymns of Degrees, Tone 8. Antiphon 1:*[^225]

*Stanza 1:* From my youth the enemy tempts me and inflames me with pleasures [p199LA] but trusting in Thee, O Lord, I vanquish that one!

*Stanza 2:* Ye that hate Sion, let them become as the grass before it is plucked up; for Christ shall cut down their necks with the cutting of torment!

*Glory... Both now...*

*To the Holy Spirit:* To live ever: Light of Light, great God; with the Father and with the Word let us hymn Him!

*Antiphon 2:*

*Stanza 1:* When I call, O Lord, give ear, and incline Thine ear unto me crying, and cleanse me before Thou takest me away hence!

*Stanza 2:* Unto his mother [the earth] [turning], everyone having hope shall not be terrified then, when Thou shalt judge all by fire and torment!

*Glory... Both now...*

*To the Holy Spirit:* Theology of the Thrice-Holy Unity: the Father without beginning, from Whom was born the timeless Son, and the Spirit co-enthroned, identical in form, shining forth with the Son from the Father!

*Antiphon 3:*

*Stanza 1:* Let my heart be covered with Thy fear, being humble-minded, lest being lifted up it fall away from Thee, O All-Compassionate One!

*Stanza 2:* Everyone entering into earth as into his mother shall immediately depart thither, to receive either torments or honors for how he lived!

*Glory... Both now...*

*To the Holy Spirit:* Everyone who divinely beholds and speaks forth higher wonders, singing of the One God in Trinity; for even if Thrice-shining, He is of one authority in the Godhead!

*Antiphon 4:*

*Stanza 1:* Behold now, what is good or what is pleasant, but for brethren to dwell together? For concerning this the Lord promised life eternal!

*Stanza 2:* Concerning His raiment, He Who clothes the lilies of the field commands the world to take no thought for anything!

*Glory... Both now...*

*To the Holy Spirit:* In Whom abiding not everyone has, Giver of peace [p200LA] for He is consubstantial with God the Father and the Son!

*Troparion:* The Crucified is risen; the boastful one is fallen; the fallen and broken one is restored, and darkness is driven away, and incorruption has blossomed forth; for by life death was swallowed up: Ye children, bless...

*Troparion:* The Thrice-radiant Godhead, shining in one ray from the one three-hypostatic nature: the unoriginate Father, the Word consubstantial with the Father, and the reigning consubstantial Spirit, we hymn: Ye children, bless; ye priests...

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Heirmos: Unto the Victor...*[^240]

*Troparion:* Having stretched out His hands upon the Tree, naked, calling [all] to be warmed by His noble nakedness: Bless and supremely exalt...

*Troparion:* Having honored Him Who raised fallen Adam from the nethermost parts unto high-enthroned glory: Bless, all ye works...

[p208LA] *Theotokion:* Thou didst appear, O Virgin, as daughter of fallen Adam; for thou art the divine [mother] of Him Who renewed our nature, Whom we hymn: Bless...

*Another, to the Most Holy Theotokos. Heirmos: The King of Heaven, Whom...*

*Troparion:* Quench the assault of the adversaries and the fiery burning arrows, that we may hymn thee, pure one, unto the ages!

*Troparion:* Beyond nature hast thou given birth to the Creator and Savior, God the Word, O Virgin; therefore do we hymn thee, pure one, unto the ages!

*Theotokion:* With radiant splendor, He Who is unapproachable Light, dwelling within thee, O Virgin, showed thee forth as light unto all ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^241] Thee, unwedded Mother of God the Most High; thee, who beyond mind didst give birth by the Word to the truly existing God, higher than the all-pure Hosts, in unceasing doxologies do we magnify!

*Troparion:* Thee, Who didst raise up fallen Adam, glorifying him with Thyself in the heavens, O Christ, as King of all and Lord, in unceasing hymns do we magnify!

*Troparion:* Thee, God crucified for our sake of Thine own will, Who didst abolish the dominion of death, O Christ, and didst deliver [us] from the bonds of Hades, in unceasing blessings do we magnify!

*Theotokion:* Thee, who didst bear in thine arms the invisible God, Who is hymned on high by the Hosts, and through thee ever granting us salvation in the resurrection, do we magnify!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Heirmos: In truth [Theotokos]...*[^242]

*Troparion:* The Tree of Thy Cross was revealed, O Christ, upon which stretching out Thy divine palms, Thou didst plant righteousness in the world, O Christ!

*Troparion:* Descending of Thine own will with Thy Cross into the nethermost parts of the earth, O Merciful God, for Thy tender mercy's sake Thou hast magnified [us]...

[p209LA] *Theotokion:* Receptacle of Light, O blessed of God, for the sake of the Radiance enlighten my heart, driving away the cloud of despondency, and chasing away the darkness of sin, O thou who didst give birth to the Sinless One!

*Another, to the Theotokos, of the same Heirmos:*

*Troparion:* Thy memory fills with joy and gladness those who draw near, pouring forth healings, who piously confess thee as Theotokos!

*Troparion:* With hymns do we praise thee, O highly favored of God, and unceasingly offer: "Rejoice!", for thou hast poured forth joy upon the world!

*Theotokion:* Comely did He become through thy flesh, O Theotokos, all-pure and incorrupt, Mediator of life unto those who hold fast, who magnify thee in faith!

*Holy is the Lord our God (LXX Ps. 98:9). Evangelical Exapostilarion, Reading 1, Resurrectional.*

*At the Praises, Stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 8:*[^243]

*Sticheron 1:* Lord, even though Thou didst stand at the judgment seat, judged by Pilate, yet Thou didst not withdraw from the throne, sitting with the Father; and having risen from the dead, Thou didst free the world from the servitude of the stranger, as only Compassionate One and Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 2:* Lord, Thou hast given us Thy Cross as a weapon against the devil; for he trembles and shakes, not able to look upon its power; for it raises the dead and has abolished death: therefore we worship Thy burial and Thy resurrection!

*Sticheron 3:* Lord, even though the Jews laid Thee in the tomb as dead, yet soldiers guarded Thee as a sleeping king; and as the treasury of life they sealed it with a seal: and Thou didst rise and grant incorruption unto our souls!

*Sticheron 4:* Lord, when Thou wast nailed upon the Cross and didst stretch out Thy hands [p210LA] then didst Thou abolish the dominion of death by Thy strength, and having descended into Hades didst free the souls, co-raising and enlightening our souls by Thy resurrection!

*Anatolian Stichera:*[^244]

*Sticheron 5:* Thou didst suffer on the Cross, being impassible in Thy Godhead; Thou didst receive burial for three days, that Thou mightest free us from the servitude of the enemy, and having made us immortal, make us alive, O Christ, through Thy resurrection, O Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 6:* I worship and supremely glorify, and hymn, O Christ, Thy rising from the tomb, whereby Thou hast freed us from the insoluble bonds of Hades, and hast granted unto the world, as God, incorruption and eternal life, as Good One and Lover of mankind!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for Thy name's sake (LXX Ps. 43:26).*

*Sticheron 7:* Him Who is risen from the dead let us glorify: God the Word, Who appeared dwelling in Himself upon earth, for our sake received reproach from the lawless and blows on the cheek; Whose kingdom He destroyed as Almighty God: therefore He was nailed to the Cross in the flesh, for our sake as Man, and opened Paradise unto the thief as Creator of all, Deliverer and Savior of our souls!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron 8:* Being the incomprehensible Creator of heaven and earth, Thou didst suffer upon the Cross, that Thou mightest pour forth impassibility unto me, as God; Thou didst receive three-day burial, and didst rise in glory, co-raising Adam as Almighty!

*Then the Evangelical Sticheron, Reading 1. Both now... Most blessed art thou (Theotokion).*

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 8)

*At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 8: Remember us, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy kingdom...*

[p211LA] *Troparion:* O Christ, Savior of the world, as Thou didst remember the thief upon the Tree, vouchsafe also unto me, O only Compassionate One, Thy heavenly kingdom!

*Troparion:* Nailed of Thine own will upon the Tree, O our Savior, by the Tree Thou hast delivered Adam from the curse, restoring as Compassionate One that which was in His image unto the habitation of Paradise![^245]

*Troparion:* Today, having risen from the tomb, Thou didst pour forth incorruption unto all the dead, and granting joy unto the myrrh-bearing women, Thou didst rise as Merciful!

*Troparion:* Today Thou hast abolished the dominion of death by Thy power, O Giver of life, and hast freed the souls from the dead, [O Thou Who art the] Resurrection, our only Savior!

*Troparion:* As in heaven the multitude of Angels, so also on earth the human race celebrates Thy most holy Resurrection, [praising] Thy goodness!

*Troparion:* Rejoice, ye wise myrrh-bearing women, who first beheld the Resurrection of Christ and announced joy unto the Apostles and the Resurrection unto the whole world!

*Glory:* Trinity without beginning and indivisible Essence, co-enthroned Unity, unseparated in glory, transcendent Nature: save those who believe in Thee!

*Both now:* Thee in truth, O Virgin Mother of God, let all the faithful with one accord hymn, as strong champion and intercessor, and the salvation of our souls!

*Prokeimenon [Tone 8: Pray, and give glory unto the Lord our God (LXX Ps. 75:12). Verse: Known in Judea is God; in Israel His name is great (LXX Ps. 75:2)].*[^246]

*Epistle to the Corinthians [1 Cor. 1:10–18]:*[^247] Brethren, I beseech you by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you; but that ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment. For it has been declared [p212LA] unto me of you, my brethren, by them of the household of Chloe, that there are contentions among you. Now this I say, that every one of you says: "I am of Paul," and "I of Apollos," and "I of Cephas," and "I of Christ." Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were ye baptized in the name of Paul? I thank my God that I baptized none of you, but Crispus and Gaius, lest anyone should say that I baptized in mine own name. And I baptized also the household of Stephanas; besides, I know not whether I baptized any other. For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the Gospel: not in wisdom of words, lest the Cross of Christ should be made of none effect. For the word of the Cross is to them that perish foolishness; but unto us who are saved it is the power of God.

*Alleluia: Come, let us rejoice in the Lord (LXX Ps. 94:1). Verse: Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving (LXX Ps. 94:2).*[^248]

*Gospel according to John [John 15:17–16:2]:*[^249] The Lord said unto His disciples: These things I command you, that ye love one another. If the world hate you, know that it hated Me before it hated you. If ye were of the world, the world would love its own; but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hates you. Remember the word that I said unto you: The servant is not greater than his lord. If they have persecuted Me, they will also persecute you; if they have kept My word, they will keep yours also. But all these things will they do unto you for My name's sake, because they know not Him Who sent Me. If I had not come and spoken unto them, they would not have had sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin. He that hates Me hates My Father also. If [p213LA] I had not done among them the works which none other man did, they would not have had sin; but now have they both seen and hated both Me and My Father. But this comes to pass, that the word might be fulfilled that is written in their law: "They hated Me without a cause." But when the Comforter is come, Whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, Who proceeds from the Father, He shall testify of Me; and ye also shall bear witness, because ye have been with Me from the beginning. These things have I spoken unto you, that ye should not be offended. They shall put you out of the synagogues; yea, the time comes, that whosoever kills you will think that he offers service unto God.
