# TONE 7

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 7)

*On Saturday Evening, at "Lord, I have cried", set 8 stichoi, and sing the Resurrection Stichera, Tone 7:*

*Sticheron 1:* Come, let us rejoice in the Lord, Who destroyed the dominion of death and enlightened the human race, crying aloud with the bodiless hosts: O Creator and our Savior, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 2:* Having endured the Cross and burial for our sake, O Savior, by death as God Thou didst put death to death; therefore we worship Thy Resurrection on the third day: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 3:* The Apostles, beholding the Resurrection of the Creator, marvelled, singing the angelic praise: this is the glory of the Church, this is the wealth of the Kingdom! O Thou Who didst suffer for our sake, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian, Automela:*

*Sticheron 4:* Though Thou wast seized, O Christ, by lawless men, yet Thou art my God, and I am not ashamed; though Thou wast smitten on the back, I deny Thee not; though nailed to the Cross, I hide it not: in Thy Resurrection do I boast, for Thy death is my life; O Almighty One and Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 5:* Fulfilling David's prophecy, O Christ, Thou didst reveal Thy majesty in Zion unto Thy disciples, [p165LA] showing Thyself praised and glorified ever with the Father and the Holy Spirit: pre-eternal as the bodiless Word, and in these last days incarnate for our sake, slain as man, and risen in glory as the Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 6:* Thou didst descend even unto Hades, O Christ, as Thou didst will, and didst overthrow death as God and Master, and didst rise on the third day, raising Adam with Thyself from the insoluble bonds of Hades and from corruption, as he cried aloud and said: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 7:* Thou wast laid in the tomb as one asleep, and didst rise on the third day as mighty in strength, raising Adam with Thyself from mortal corruption, as the Almighty One!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Thou wast known as Mother beyond nature, O Mother of God, yet remainedst a Virgin beyond word and understanding; no tongue can declare the wonders of thy childbirth: for most glorious was thy conception, O pure one, and incomprehensible was the manner of thy giving birth; for wherever God so wills, the order of nature is overcome: therefore knowing thee to be the Mother of God, all of us pray fervently unto thee: Unceasingly intercede for our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera:*

*Sticheron 1:* The Savior of the world is risen from the tomb, and has raised man together with His own flesh: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Other Stichera, Alphabetical:*

*Sticheron 2:* Come, let us worship Him Who is risen from the dead and has enlightened all things; for by His Resurrection on the third day He has delivered us from the tyranny of Hades, granting unto us life and great mercy!

*Sticheron 3:* Having descended into Hades, O Christ, Thou didst put death to death, and rising on the third day, [p166LA] Thou didst raise us who were fallen, as we cry aloud: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O compassionate Lord!

*Sticheron 4:* Awesome didst Thou appear, O Lord, lying in the tomb as one asleep, and rising on the third day as mighty, Thou didst raise Adam with Thyself, who cried: Glory to Thine all-powerful rising, O Lord and Lover of mankind!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Fleeing unto thy protection, O Sovereign Lady, all of us earthborn cry aloud unto thee: O Mother of God, our hope, deliver us from our countless transgressions, and save our souls!

*Resurrection Troparion, Tone 7:* Thou didst destroy death by Thy Cross; Thou didst open Paradise to the thief; Thou didst change the lamentation of the myrrh-bearers into joy, and commanded the Apostles to proclaim that Thou art risen, O Christ God, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Theotokion:* As the treasury of our resurrection, O all-praised one, lead up from the pit and depth of transgressions those who hope in thee: for thou didst save those guilty of sin, having given birth unto our Salvation; who before childbirth wast a Virgin, and in childbirth a Virgin, and after childbirth still a Virgin!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 7)

*At Midnight Office, Canon to the Holy Trinity, Tone 7. Ode 1. Heirmos:* By Thy nod the nature of the waters, previously fluid, was transformed upon earth; therefore Israel, having marched dry-shod, sings unto Thee a song of victory!

*Troparion:* Circumcise the eyes of my understanding, and my heart and clay lips, O all-holy Trinity of all, that I may joyfully sing praise unto Thee, the Giver of ages!

*Troparion:* That Thou mightest show forth the abundance of Thy goodness, Thou didst fashion man, O one uncircumscribed Trinity, an honorable image [p167LA] of Thy Godhead:[^180] do Thou, O Creator, renew him!

*Troparion:* The pre-eternal Mind, and His co-eternal Word, and the co-unoriginate Spirit, shining forth, taught us to worship the one God, consubstantial in essence, in three hypostatic Persons!

*Theotokion:* In the bush of old, the divine Word appeared unto Moses as a purifying fire, yet burned it not, prefiguring the incarnation from the Virgin, whereby He renewed the earthborn!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: In the beginning...*

*Troparion:* Hymning the single, thrice-radiant, and all-creating Master, we ask forgiveness of transgressions: O almighty God, despise not those who faithfully glorify Thy goodness!

*Troparion:* The Word of God, consubstantial, sprang forth as an Offshoot from the Father, as from an unoriginate root, and the divine Spirit, co-essential: therefore, ye faithful, let us glorify the one Lordship in threefold forms!

*Troparion:* Equally honored and co-grown is the tri-hypostatic nature, divided without division, a single-originate Trinity; all of us the faithful glorify Thee, and worshipping we ask forgiveness of transgressions!

*Theotokion:* Beyond expression, without earthly likeness, the Word of God came forth manifestly from the pure Virgin, and showed forth unto all the thrice-shining Godhead, an indivisible essence in immutable hypostases!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:* I have sinned, have mercy, O Holy Trinity, upon Thy servants who flee unto Thee, the Compassionate One; and forgive, for Thou art merciful!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* [p168LA] From malice and transgressions deliver our souls, O most pure Mother of God, and cleanse from sins those who sing of thee, [having given birth unto] the unchangeable God![^181]

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Foreseeing Thy coming...*[^182]

*Troparion:* All-sustaining, thrice-radiant, divine Unity, the Savior of all: preserve now Thy singers, and save them from afflictions and all malice!

*Troparion:* Marked by the words of Thine incomprehensible and thrice-radiant Godhead, sweetly do we praise Thee, O Lord and Lover of mankind, and glorify Thy power!

*Troparion:* On earth and in heaven with the bodiless choirs, we praise the indivisible Trinity in Unity, as reigning unto all ages!

*Theotokion:* Without departing from the Father's glory, Thou didst descend into our lowest estate, incarnate as the transcendent God, and hast exalted us unto divine glory, as Thou art merciful!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* Light-originating Essence, Trinity in Unity: strengthen us in Thy love![^183]

*Troparion:* The Mind, the Word, and the Spirit: one Godhead and thrice-radiant Unity; deliver from temptations and eternal sorrows those who glorify Thee!

*Troparion:* Taking form, O Word of God, having assumed human nature from the holy Virgin, Thou didst teach us to praise the Trinity in Unity, co-enthroned and consubstantial!

*Theotokion:* By thy childbirth beyond understanding, O most pure one, the condemnation of the first parents was loosed, and the first-formed regained entry into Paradise, loudly [p169LA] praising thee!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* With equal glory and dominion, the Monarchy of the Godhead, existing immutable in three hypostases, having the distinctions of glory within Itself, according to the natural properties of each![^184]

*Troparion:* The noetic ranks of angelic orders praise the thrice-radiant Godhead; with them also we, with clay lips, sing of the one Creator of all things, crying aloud and faithfully glorifying!

*Troparion:* Indivisible Trinity, one and co-essential, in three Persons one Essence: we worship Thee, O Father, Son, and Spirit, one Essence and one Power, one God, consubstantial Trinity!

*Theotokion:* Beyond expression by word, the Word, born of His Father as the sun before all ages, in these last days shone forth from the Virgin; we proclaim the one God in three Persons, uncircumscribed!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:* Tri-hypostatic and thrice-personal Unity: have mercy upon those whom Thou didst create, not rejecting them; burning away the malice of our souls, and enlightening the souls and hearts of those that sing of Thy goodness: O our God, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Brighter than fire and more active than flame is the mercy of thy goodness, O Sovereign Lady, burning away the sins of men and enlightening the hearts of those that sing of thee, magnifying thy greatness, O thou who hast been gladdened by God!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The Children of old showed the fiery furnace...*

*Troparion:* Following the words of the divine prophets, we glorify Thee alone as the one God in threefold form, crying thus: [p170LA] Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* With clay lips, singing the song of the immaterial orders, we praise the glory of the Trinity in the unity of essence, crying thus: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* Unoriginate Trinity, indivisible in essence, co-enthroned in unity, inseparable in glory, pre-beginning Nature and Kingdom: save those who sing unto Thee in faith!

*Theotokion:* The Creator and Builder of the earthly creation, O most pure one, manifestly became incarnate of thee, and men, made alive, cry aloud: Blessed is the fruit of thy womb, O most pure one![^185]

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Unconsumed by the fire...*

*Troparion:* Thou Who upholdest all creation, O Trinity in Unity, ruling over all, Who embracest the whole earth: preserve those whom Thou hast drawn by Thy love, who ever cry: All ye works of the Lord, bless the Lord!

*Troparion:* Illumine Thy singers and servants with the thrice-radiant rays of the sun, with divine-working victories; and beholding the beauty of the one Trinity, grant that we may sing as far as we are able: All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!

*Troparion:* Make the beacons of Thy Church thrice-radiant, O Benefactor and Lover of mankind, and unite them with Thy communicants; show them to be unassailable by invisible foes and fleshly passions, O Master, one Godhead of the people, that they may unceasingly cry: All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!

*Theotokion:* The God-originating Light shone forth from thy womb, O Virgin Mother unwedded and spotless, illumining the whole world [p171LA] the whole world with thrice-radiant light, and [renewing] the earth:[^186] Praise the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^187] To speak with a loud voice and to sing worthily [of Him] Whom in the highest heavens the Seraphim [unceasingly][^188] praise, we of the earth are unable; but nevertheless, as Master of all and Lover of mankind, Thee do we magnify!

*Troparion:* Deliver Thy faithful singers from bodily sicknesses and spiritual passions, O singular, indivisible Trinity; preserve them unharmed, and free them from all worldly temptations!

*Troparion:* O co-equal, thrice-radiant Godhead, having pre-eternal dominion, and in the immutable beauty of essential goodness: grant remission of transgressions unto Thy servants, and deliverance from defiled passions!

*Theotokion:* Assuming a mind, a soul, and a bodily union from thy womb, O Mother of God, O spotless one, the Word of God truly appeared as Man, and having deified the essence of men, He was manifestly shown forth!

*Then: It is truly meet... and the rest.*[^189] *At Sunday Matins, at "God is the Lord," Troparion of the Resurrection: After the 1st Stichologia, Sessional Hymn, Tone 7:*

When the tomb was sealed, O Life, Thou didst shine forth from the grave, O Christ God; and while the doors were shut, Thou didst come unto the disciples, O Resurrection of all, renewing an upright spirit in us through them, according to Thy great mercy!

*Sessional Hymn:* Life lay in the tomb, and seals were upon the stone; as a sleeping king the soldiers guarded Christ, and angels glorified Him as immortal God; and the women cried aloud: The Lord is risen, [p172LA] granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Rejoice, thou that art full of grace, O Virgin Theotokos, haven and help of the human race! For from thee was the Redeemer of the world incarnate; thou alone art Mother and ever-blessed Virgin; entreat Christ God to grant peace unto the world, and great mercy unto our souls!

*After the 2nd Stichologia, Sessional Hymn:* Unto the tomb ran the women, carrying myrrh with tears, while the soldiers were guarding Thee, the King of all; they said among themselves: "Who shall roll away the stone for us?" The Angel of Great Counsel[^190] is risen, having trampled down death: O almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sessional Hymn:* Thou Who by Thy three-day burial didst despoil death, and by Thy life-bearing rising didst raise up corrupted man: O Christ God, have mercy upon us!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Entreat unceasingly, O Virgin Theotokos, Christ God, Who was crucified for our sake and rose again, and destroyed the dominion of death, that He may save our souls!

*After the Blameless [Psalm 118], Hypakoe, Tone 7:* Thou Who didst take our form, and endure crucifixion in the flesh: save me by Thy Resurrection, for Thou art the Lover of mankind!

*Graduals, Tone 7. Antiphon 1:* O Savior, Who didst turn back the captivity of Zion from deceit: give life also unto me, delivering me from the bondage of passions!

They that sow in affliction in the south, with fastings and tears, shall thus reap joyful sheaves of nourishment unto life eternal!

*Glory... Both now... Through the Holy Spirit:* [He is] the Fountain of divine treasures; from Him come wisdom, understanding, and the fear [of God]; unto Him be praise, glory, honor, and dominion!

*Antiphon 2:*

[p173LA] Except the Lord build the house of the soul, in vain do we labor; for without Him, neither deed nor word is accomplished!

By Thy Holy Spirit, the fruits of the womb, moved [by Him], are turned unto the paternal commandments for adoption!

*Glory... Both now... Through the Holy Spirit:* All things have their being [from Him]; for before all things He is God, the Unapproachable Light, and the Life of all!

*Antiphon 3:* They that fear the Lord, having found the paths of life, are now and ever blessed with incorruptible glory!

Beholding thy children around thy table like young shoots, rejoice and be glad, O Chief Shepherd, bringing them unto Christ!

*Glory... Both now... Through the Holy Spirit:* The depth of gifts, the richness of glory, the great abyss of judgments; of one glory, He is co-worshipped God[^191] with the Father and the Son!

*Prokeimenon: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up!* *Verse: I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart.* *Let every breath praise the Lord.* *Resurrection Gospel, as assigned.* *Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ.* *Psalm 50: Have mercy on me, O God.* *Troparion after Psalm 50, and the rest.*[^192]

*Canon of the Resurrection, Tone 7. Ode 1. Heirmos:* By Thy beckoning, the naturally flowing watery substance on earth changed its form, O Lord; therefore Israel, journeying dryshod, sings unto Thee a song of victory!

*Troparion:* The tyranny of death is condemned, when by unjust death on the Tree Thou wast condemned, O Lord; therefore the prince of darkness, not overcoming Thee, was justly cast out!

*Troparion:* Hades drew near unto Thee, and not having power to grind Thy body with teeth, his [p174LA] jaws were shattered; therefore, O Savior, having destroyed the pangs of death, Thou didst rise from the tomb on the third day!

*Theotokion:* Thou didst loose the sorrow of the first mother Eve, for having escaped pangs, without knowledge of wedlock thou didst give birth; therefore, manifestly knowing thee as the most pure Theotokos, all we call thee blessed!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Let us sing...* From the life-bearing and pierced side gushed forth two fountains; let us sing unto Him, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* Having lain in the tomb, and risen on the third day, Christ, granting incorruption unto the dead: let us sing unto Him, for He is glorified!

*Theotokion:* Rejoice, O Mother of Christ God, who ineffably in the flesh didst bear the Word of God, Who suffered as another for His own servants!

*Another Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos. Same Heirmos:* O Virgin who didst bear the Abyss of compassion: illumine my soul with thy light-bearing radiance, that I may worthily sing, O most pure one, of the power of thy wonders!

*Troparion:* Beholding us wounded by the arrow of sin, O Word, as Benefactor Thou didst have compassion; therefore, ineffably uniting Thyself, O most pure one, through the flesh which He, the Divine One, received from thee!

*Troparion:* And human nature was seized by death, corrupt and harmed, O most pure Sovereign Lady; but thou, having conceived Life, didst lead it back from corruption unto life!

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* In the beginning, by the all-powerful Word, Thou didst establish the heavens, O Lord and Savior, and by the all-creating Spirit of God all their host; establish me upon the immovable rock of Thy confession!

*Troparion:* Thou didst mount the Tree [p175LA] of Thine own will; for our sake Thou didst suffer sores, O truly Merciful Savior, and didst endure wounds, mediating salvation for the world and the faithful, therefore unto Thy mercy, O Almighty One, we have recourse!

*Troparion:* Thou didst heal the wounds of the soul bitten by the serpent's gnawing, O Christ, and didst show light in darkness to him who of old descended into corruption; for descending through the Cross into Hades, Thou didst raise me up with Thyself!

*Theotokion:* By the prayers of Thy Mother who knew not wedlock, grant peace unto the whole world, O Savior, and bestow victories upon our Prince[^193] against contrary adversaries, and vouchsafe incorruptible glory unto those that hymn Thee!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Thou Who didst give power from on high unto the disciples:* Let us give majesty unto the Lord, Who rose gloriously from the tomb and enlightened the whole world!

*Troparion:* Thou didst fill the universe with joy by Thy Resurrection, O Christ, for Thou alone art good and the Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:* O most pure Mother and Virgin, alone all-praised: entreat Thy Savior that He may save our souls!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Same Heirmos:* With evil crawling, [the serpent] of Eden ensnared me with the love of divinity and cast me down to earth; but as Thou art merciful by nature and compassionate, Thou hadst pity; the Grace [of God] made its dwelling in thy womb, assimilating Himself unto me in compassion, O Virgin!

*Troparion:* Blessed is the Fruit of thy womb, O Virgin, O Theotokos, joy of all; for thou didst bear gladness unto the whole world, and true rejoicing, destroying sinful sorrow, O Bride of God!

*Troparion:* Thou didst bear for us eternal Life and Light, O Virgin Birthgiver of God, [p176LA] the return of ancient men unto the Father, therefore, adorning ourselves with faith, we confess [this] grace!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* Leaving not the Father's bosom, Thou didst descend to earth, O Christ God; I have heard the mystery of Thy dispensation, and I glorified Thee, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:* Giving Thy shoulders unto wounds, incarnate of the Virgin; for the servant who sinned, the guiltless Master is beaten, loosing my transgressions!

*Troparion:* Standing before judges, judged by lawbreakers as a judge is received, and receiving a blow from a defiled hand: He that created man and manifestly judges the earth in righteousness!

*Theotokion:* O true Child of God, entreat thy Creator and God, O spotless one, that He may guide into the haven of salvation the flock that glorifies thee!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: The prophet beholding...*[^194] Knowing no sin, O Lord, having known it not, Thou didst take upon Thyself that which was ours, appearing as an infant, and slaying the senseless, deceived tyrant!

*Troparion:* Having received corruption like Adam, O Lord my God, Thou didst abolish by Thy death him that held the power of death, and didst deliver men from deceit!

*Theotokion:* Having come forth from the shadowed, pure mountain, the Virgin, Thou wast enrolled with servants, freeing us from bondage, as Master of all; therefore we glorify Him that was born of thee!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Same Heirmos:* Chosen by all and shown forth good unto God, all-hymned by the brightness of the pre-creation: illumine with thy light-shedding those that sing of thee!

*Troparion:* Thou didst bear God on earth, O pure one, [p177LA] incarnate of thy most pure blood, Who delivers from grievous transgressions those who with love magnify and honor thee, O most pure one!

*Troparion:* The defiled rational nature receives the seed of divine operation, for He came forth from thee beyond understanding, O Virgin: the mystery of thy birthgiving, O most pure and all-blessed one!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* The night is without light unto the faithless, but unto the faithful there is illumination in the sweetness of Thy words; therefore early in the morning we sing unto Thee, praising Thy Divinity!

*Troparion:* For the sake of servants Thou wast betrayed, O Christ, and didst endure blows, mediating freedom for those who sing of Thee; early in the morning we sing unto Thee, praising Thy Divinity!

*Troparion:* By divine power, O Christ, and by the weakness of the flesh, Thou didst cast down the strong one, and by Thy Resurrection, O Savior, didst show me to be victor over death!

*Theotokion:* Thou didst give birth unto God, O pure Mother, Who was incarnate of thee in a manner befitting God, O all-praised one; for thou knewest not a man's bed, but didst bring forth from the Holy Spirit!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Early in the morning unto Thee...* Grant Thy peace, O merciful Lord, and let Thy precious Cross enlighten our souls, as we praise Thee as merciful!

*Troparion:* Thou Who didst make light shine upon the world by Thy Resurrection, and by Thy rising, O Christ, didst raise up him who had fallen through the enemy's deceit: save those who faithfully praise Thee!

*Theotokion:* Without seed thou didst knowingly conceive God, O unwedded Mother, Who was born before the ages of the Father; entreat Him ever, O Theotokos, that He may save us!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: O Lord my God...*

[p178LA] The ladder which Jacob saw, fixed unto the heights, was an image of thee who knewest not wedlock; through thee God lived among men, O most pure Sovereign Lady!

*Troparion:* Having received eternal redemption through thee, O Virgin, with diligence we cry aloud unto thee: "Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady!" and rejoicing in thy light, we hymn thee with songs!

*Troparion:* Finding thee alone among thorns like a lily, O Virgin, shining with the brightness of purity, the Bridegroom brought thee unto Himself as an all-hymned Bride with the flower of virginity!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^195] Voyaging in the tempest of life without wind, and being submerged like a ship by sins, and cast about by the soul-corrupting whirlpool, crying like Jonah, O Christ: "Lead me up from the death-bearing deep!"

*Troparion:* The earthly congregation having of old been sold by polytheistic deceit, Thou, the Son of God, didst come in the form of a servant, and wast lifted up upon the Cross, O Christ, that Thou mightest deliver [us] from corruption, as Thou art merciful!

*Troparion:* In the spiritual chamber of the body not made with hands, destroyed by passions, when it seemed to be neglected, the apostolic choir, having hope, suddenly worshipped and proclaimed everywhere Him Who is risen!

*Theotokion:* Who among men can declare the image of thine ineffable birthgiving, O spotless one, O Virgin Bride of God, for our sake? For the ineffable Word united Himself [to flesh] from thee, and the flesh remained unchangeable!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: I cried unto Thee...* When Thou wast crucified, O Master, the whole earth was shaken, and [p179LA] as Thou art good, Thou didst heal my grievous transgressions!

*Troparion:* Death was abolished, and Hades was overthrown; for Christ arose [from the dead], preparing the resurrection of men!

*Theotokion:* O most pure Sovereign Lady, strong hope of Christians: preserve those who sing of thee with love, as one loving the truth!

*Another Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos. Same Heirmos:* The prophetic prophecies manifestly proclaimed by legal types that thou wouldst give birth unto the Blessing of all creation, Who in many and manifold ways did good unto those who faithfully sing of thee!

*Troparion:* Adam the first-formed, who of old was frightened away by the counsel of the murderer of man from the divine food of paradise, thou didst manifestly lead back, O unwedded one, having given birth unto Him Who delivered us from his transgression!

*Troparion:* The Divine Light, Who by His all-effective power brought all things into being out of nothing, came forth from thy womb, O pure one, and shone with divine radiance upon those sitting in the darkness of death!

*Kontakion: Protection of Christians unashamed...*[^196]

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* Of old the Children showed the fiery-burning furnace to be breathing dew, singing to the one God and saying: "O God of our fathers, Who art supremely exalted, blessed art Thou!"

*Troparion:* By the tree he who committed disobedience is put to death, but by the obedience of Christ he is formed anew; for for my sake the all-glorious Son of God is crucified!

*Troparion:* All creation hymns Thee Who didst rise from the tomb, O Christ, for being Life, Thou didst blossom forth in Hades, the [p180LA] Resurrection of the dead and all-glorious Light unto those in darkness!

*Theotokion:* Rejoice, daughter of corruptible Adam! Rejoice, only Bride of God! Rejoice, through whom the curse was banished, for thou didst bear God; entreat Him, O pure one, to save us all!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: In the Chaldean furnace...* Of Thine own will Thou wast crucified upon the Cross, that Thou mightest free men from corruption; therefore, pouring forth hymns, [we cry]: "Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!"

*Troparion:* Thou didst rise from the dead, O Giver of life, and trampling down the dominion of death, Thou didst raise with Thyself the eternal prisoners, who cry: "O God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!"

*Theotokion:* A Virgin gives birth without a man, and after childbirth remains a Virgin; for she gave birth unto God, singing unto Whom we cry aloud: "[O God] of our fathers!"

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Same Heirmos:* Having raiment all-adorned and gilded, thy Creator and Lord loved thee, O Virgin: "O God of our fathers, Who art supremely exalted!"

*Troparion:* Illumine me, O Virgin! Of old, the live coal[^197] which Isaiah foresaw typically prefigured thy birthgiving: "O God of our fathers, Who art supremely exalted, blessed art Thou!"

*Troparion:* Beholding the figures of all thy divine birthgiving, the divine prophets joyfully cried aloud: "O God of our fathers, Who art supremely exalted, blessed art Thou!"

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* The bush unconsumed by fire in Sinai, communing [therewith], showed forth God unto slow-tongued and stammering Moses; and the Children cast into the fire with divine zeal showed forth singers: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!"

[p181LA] *Troparion:* Having become the Deliverer of the bound,[^198] O passionless Lord, Thou didst endure judgment, delivering the condemned; for as in Adam we died, so also all in Christ shall we live: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord, and supremely exalt Him!"

*Troparion:* He again translated us from corruption unto incorruption; holding the power, having cast [the enemy] down into dark and lightless habitations, He shatters the malice of the strong one and spoils his house: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord, and supremely exalt Him!"

*Theotokion:* Thy pure and most immaculate childbirth, O Virgin, cleansed the defiled and unclean parts of the universe, and became the cause of our reconciliation[^199] unto God, O most pure one; therefore we hymn thee as Theotokos: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord, and supremely exalt Him!"

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: The Children being called...*

*Troparion:* Without beginning unto all, O Holy Trinity; for without time was the beginning of being: the Begetter God and Father, not prior in time unto the Son and not prior unto the Spirit; and appearing worshipped as Three in one Essence, and of one Godhead indivisible, is not God praised unto ages: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!"

*Troparion:* Thou wast pierced in the ribs, with drops of divine Blood cleansing the earth defiled by the bloodshed of idolatrous wrath: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto ages!"

*Theotokion:* O Virgin, who didst cause Christ, the Luminary before the sun, to shine forth upon the world, and delivering from corruption, enlightening with the knowledge of God those who cry: "Supremely exalted art Thou unto ages!"

*Another Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos. Same Heirmos:*

*Troparion:* With the light of thy childbirth, thou didst strangely and timelessly illumine the universe [p182LA] [the universe], O Birthgiver thereof; for truly upon thine arms thou dost bear God Who ever enlightens those that faithfully cry: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!"

*Troparion:* We ever piously praise thy womb which contained God ineffably incarnate, Who gave unto the faithful the radiant enlightenment of the knowledge of God, who ever cry: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!"

*Troparion:* With the rays of thy light make radiant those who hymn thee, O pure Theotokos, Birthgiver of Light; for thou wast shown forth as the dwelling-place of Light, brightly enlightening those who ever cry: "All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!"

*Ode 9. Heirmos:* Rejoice, radiant Bride, Mother of the Giver of light! Rejoice, thou who didst contain in thy womb Him Who cannot be contained! Rejoice, thou who art more honorable than the Cherubim themselves, having given birth unto the Savior of our souls!

*Troparion:* With the saving Blood, His own precious Blood, wherewith He redeemed us who had fallen through deceit, and destroyed the deceitful torment of the fierce enemy: therefore we unceasingly magnify Thee!

*Troparion:* Thou didst endure death by the spear,[^200] O Compassionate One, and didst come forth from the tomb as a bridegroom from the bridal chamber, and didst grant us incorruption: therefore we unceasingly magnify Thee!

*Theotokion:* We have thee as a calm haven, O Theotokos, unto which we flee when laboring in life, being delivered by thy prayer from the enemy's assaults, and we remain unharmed!

*Another Canon of the Cross. Heirmos: Incorrupt...*[^201] Ye that ascribe suffering unto the Godhead, be ashamed, ye [p183LA] [fools];[^202] for [He is] God of glory, crucified in the flesh, yet not crucified in the essence of the Godhead, Whom in two natures we magnify!

*Troparion:* Ye who believe not in the resurrection of bodies, coming to the tomb of Christ, learn that the Giver of life, having become dead in the flesh, arose, for the assurance of the final resurrection, Whom we magnify!

*Troparion:* Not by Godhead is the Trinity [divided], but by Hypostases; nor by confusion of Persons; but honoring one Godhead we understand division, and again making distinction, Which we unceasingly magnify!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Same Heirmos:*

*Troparion:* Thou wast shown forth as the Mediatress of eternal joy and gladness unto us, O Ever-Virgin Maiden, having given birth unto the Redeemer; in spirit and in truth worshipping Him as God Who delivers [us]!

*Troparion:* Thy forefather [David] remembers thee, O most pure one, as secret [tabernacle] of the divine covenants,[^203] having contained God beyond nature, Who sat upon the Tree in holiness, Whom unceasingly we faithful magnify!

*Troparion:* For truly thou wast higher than all creation alone; for thou didst bear in the flesh for our sake the Creator of all; therefore as Mother of the Master of all, thou bearest for all the primordial victory!

*Exapostilarion. Incipit: Having appeared...*[^204]

*At the Praises, Sunday Stichera, Tone 7, Samoglasny:*

*Sticheron 1:* Christ is risen from the dead, and the bonds of death are destroyed! Proclaim, O earth, great joy! Praise, ye heavens, the glory of God!

*Sticheron 2:* Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ, let us worship the holy Lord Jesus, the only Sinless One!

[p184LA] *Sticheron 3:* Unceasingly worshipping Christ's Resurrection, for He has saved us from our iniquities, Holy Lord Jesus, Who didst show forth the Resurrection!

*Verse: What shall I render unto the Lord for all that He has rendered unto me?*

*Sticheron 4:* For our sake God was among men; for the sake of corrupted nature the Word was made flesh and dwelt among us; unto the unthankful the gracious Benefactor, unto the captives the Deliverer, unto those sitting in darkness the Sun of righteousness; upon the Cross passionless, in Hades light, in death life, the Resurrection for the fallen: unto Whom let us cry: Our God, glory to Thee!

*Eastern Stichera:*[^205]

*Sticheron 5:* Thou didst shatter the gates of Hades, O Lord, and didst destroy the dominion of death by Thy mighty power, and didst resurrect the dead that had sat from the ages in darkness, by Thy divine and all-glorious Resurrection, as King of all and Almighty God!

*Verse: Come, let us rejoice in the Lord, let us be glad in His Resurrection (cf. LXX Ps. 94:1):*

*Sticheron 6:* For He resurrected the dead from the unbreakable bonds of Hades, and granted unto the world life eternal and great mercy!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for Thy name's sake (LXX Ps. 43:26):*

*Sticheron 7:* From the depth of Hades [the thief] cried aloud from afar unto Thee, the Creator, and was counted worthy of Paradise; Thine ears, O Lord, were attentive unto his voice; without voice receiving him as suppliant, Thou didst clothe Thyself in the whole nature, not partaking of passions, yet partaking of death, and rising, Thou didst raise with Thyself the fallen one, O Lover of mankind!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron 8:* Why did ye reject the Chief Cornerstone, O lawless Jews? Which God placed in Sion [p185LA] Who from the stone poured forth water in the wilderness, and for us poured forth immortality from His ribs? This is the Stone Which was cut out from the virginal mountain without hand of man: the Savior of man coming upon the clouds of heaven, the Ancient of Days, as Daniel said: His Kingdom is eternal![^206]

*Evangelical Sticheron. Incipit: Having appeared...*

## SUNDAY LITURGY: THE BEATITUDES (TONE 7)

*At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 7:*

*Troparion 1:* Fair was the fruit to look upon and good for food that killed me of old; but Christ is the Tree of Life, of Which eating I die not, but cry with the thief: "Remember me, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom!"

*Troparion 2:* Thou wast lifted up upon the Cross, O Compassionate One, and didst tear up the handwriting against Adam, and didst save the human race from deceit: therefore we hymn Thee, O Benefactor Lord!

*Troparion 3:* Bitterness was poured forth and Hades was shaken at the hearing thereof, of old at the Passion of Christ upon the Cross; Thou didst pour forth the sweetness of incorruption: therefore we cry: "Glory to Thy rising!"

*Troparion 4:* Thou didst nail our sins unto the Cross, and by death didst put death to death, raising the dead from the dead: therefore we worship Thy Resurrection on the third day!

*Troparion 5:* In the tomb as dead Thou wast laid, O Life of all, O Christ, and didst shatter the gates of death, and didst rise in glory on the third day as mighty, and didst enlighten all: we cry aloud to worship Thy Resurrection on the third day!

*Troparion 6:* The Lord, having risen on the third day from the dead, granted peace unto His disciples, and having blessed them, said: "Lead all unto [p186LA] My Kingdom!"

*Trinity Troparion:* Indivisible Trinity, one single Essence, thrice-radiant Substance: we worship Thee, O Father, Son, and Spirit, one Godhead and one Power, one consubstantial Trinity: "Remember me, O Lord, when [Thou comest into Thy Kingdom]!"

*Theotokion:* Thou didst bear the Son and Word of the Father in the flesh upon earth, as He alone knows; therefore, O Virgin Mother, being made divine[^207] through thee, we cry aloud unto thee: "Rejoice, Hope of Christians!"

*Prokeimenon, Tone 7: The Lord will give strength unto His people (LXX Ps. 28:11).* *Verse: Bring unto the Lord, ye sons of God (LXX Ps. 28:1).*

*Epistle to the Galatians [Gal. 5:22–6:2]:*

Brethren: The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, patience, goodness, gentleness, unfeignedness; against such there is no law. And they that are Christ Jesus' have crucified the flesh with the passions and lusts. If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit; and let us not be desirous of vainglory, despising one another, envying one another. And if a man be overtaken in any fault, ye who are spiritual, restore such a one in the spirit of gentleness, considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted. Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.

*Alleluia. Verse 1: It is good to give thanks unto the Lord (LXX Ps. 91:2). Verse 2: To proclaim Thy mercy in the morning (LXX Ps. 91:3).*

*Gospel according to Matthew [Matt. 11:27–30]:*

The Lord said unto His disciples: "All things are delivered unto Me of My Father; and no man knows the Son, but the Father; neither does any man know the Father, [p187LA] save the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son will reveal Him. Come unto Me, all ye that labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you, and learn of Me; for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For My yoke is easy, and My burden is light."

## SUNDAY EVENING VESPERS (TONE 7)

*On Sunday Evening, Stichera at "Lord, I have cried", Penitential, Tone 7, Podoben: "No longer am I hindered" (Оуже не възбранни):*[^208]

*Sticheron 1:* Me who sin all the day long and grieve Thee, the Good Master, Who art merciful by nature: receive me returning as the prodigal, and make me as one of Thy hired servants, O Heavenly Father!

*Sticheron 2:* In laziness I have passed through the measure of life, and I, the wretched one, have drawn near unto the end, and I think not of the judgment that awaits me, nor of falling away from God; but do Thou, turning me, save me, and snatch me away from all these things!

*Sticheron 3:* From Gehenna and the gnashing of teeth deliver my humble soul, O Lord, and from all eternal condemnation, O All-Good One, that with faith I may hymn Thee as merciful God and Lover of mankind by nature!

*Other Stichera of the Bodiless Powers.*

*Podoben: "The same Tone and Podoben" (Гла́съ и҆ подо́бенъ то́йже):*

*Sticheron 1:* A flaming fire Thine Angels, O Lord; Thou hast shown them forth unto us as fervent defenders, swift helpers, O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 2:* Save us, O Master, by the prayers of Thine Angels, [p188LA] washing away our stumbling-blocks, cleansing defiled sins, O Almighty: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 3:* Seraphim and Cherubim, Angels, Principalities, Dominions, singing with the brightness of all Angels, with Authorities: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* From thee, O most pure Theotokos Virgin, was ineffably born Christ our God, truly being pre-eternal God and Man, Son of both, ever-existing; and Who, having become [incarnate] for our sake, saves everyone: Whose nature is of two essences,[^209] the one showing power by miracles, and the other shining forth through passions; therefore the same One dies as Man and rises as God: entreat Him, O pure unwedded one, that our souls may be saved!

*At the Aposticha, Penitential Stichera, Tone 7:*

*Sticheron 1:* As he that fell among thieves and was wounded, so also have I fallen through my sins, and my soul is wounded: unto whom shall I flee, guilty that I am, except unto Thee, the Merciful, the Physician of souls? Pour out upon me, O God, Thy great mercy!

*Sticheron 2:* As Thou art the Sun of righteousness, illumine the hearts of those that hymn Thee: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Martyrikon:* Glory to Thee, O Christ God, praise of the Apostles, joy of the Martyrs, whose preaching was the Consubstantial Trinity!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Pacify our life by the prayers of the Theotokos, as we cry unto Thee: O Merciful One, glory to Thee!
