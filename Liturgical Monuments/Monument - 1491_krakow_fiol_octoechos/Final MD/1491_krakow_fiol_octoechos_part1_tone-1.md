# TONE 1

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 1)

*Services I sing for the Octoechos, the work of our venerable father John of Damascus. On Saturday evening, at "Lord, I have cried", we bless... [Kathisma] and we sing Stichera: the Resurrection Stichera, three, repeating each one to make six, and the Anatolian Stichera; in the Menaion, Stichera are sung to make ten: Resurrection Stichera to six, and of the Menaion to four. But if there be a great saint, we sing Resurrection Stichera to four, and to the saint to six Stichera. Tone 1:–*

*Sticheron 1:* Accept our evening prayers, O holy Lord, and grant us remission of sins, for Thou alone art He Who revealed the Resurrection unto the world.

*Sticheron 2:* Encompass Zion, O ye people, and surround her, and give glory therein to Him Who is risen from the dead; for He is our God, Who delivered us from our iniquities.

*Sticheron 3:* Come, O ye people, let us hymn and bow down before Christ, glorifying His Resurrection from the dead;

for He is our God, Who delivered the world from the deception of the enemy.

*Other Eastern Stichera:*

*Sticheron 4:* Rejoice, O ye heavens, and sound the trumpets, ye foundations of the earth; break forth into singing, ye mountains, in gladness; for behold, Emmanuel nailed our sins unto the Cross, and granted life, and put death to death, and raised Adam up, for He is the Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron 5:* Him Who was crucified in the flesh of His own will for our sake, Who suffered and was buried, and rose from the dead, let us hymn, saying: Establish the right belief[^6] of Thy Church, O Christ, and bring peace unto our life, for Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron 6:* Standing before Thy life-giving tomb, unworthy as we are, we offer a hymn of praise unto Thine ineffable compassion, O Christ our God; for Thou, O sinless One, didst accept crucifixion and death, that Thou mightest grant the Resurrection unto the world, for Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron 7:* The Word, Who was begotten of the Father without beginning and is co-eternal,[^7] Who came forth ineffably from the virginal womb, and willingly accepted crucifixion and death for our sake, and is risen in glory: let us hymn Him, saying: O Lord, the Giver of life, glory to Thee, the Savior of our souls!

*Repeat one [Sticheron].*

*Tone 1. Theotokion (Dogmatikon):* Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of the whole world, who sprang forth from mankind and gave birth unto the Master, the heavenly gate, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful; for she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead; she, having destroyed the barrier of enmity, brought in peace and opened the Kingdom. Having her, therefore, as the anchor of faith, we have as champion the Lord Who was born of her. Be of good courage, therefore, be

of good courage, O people of God, for He will conquer the enemies, for He is the Lover of mankind.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of the Tone [Tone 1]:*

*Sticheron 1:* By Thy Passion, O Christ, we were set free from passions, and by Thy Resurrection we were delivered from corruption; O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

*Sticheron 2:* Thou didst willingly accept crucifixion, O Savior, and having endured burial, Thou didst rise from the dead as our God; as One abundant in mercy, Thou didst shatter the arrogant assaults of the adversary by Thy power, granting salvation unto those who glorify Thy third-day Resurrection.

*Verse: For He hath established the world, so that it cannot be moved.*

*Sticheron 3:* Being King, out of love for mankind Thou wast willingly crucified and buried. When Hades met Thee below, it was embittered; and when the souls of the righteous received Thee, they rejoiced. And Adam, beholding Thee, the Creator, in the nethermost regions, was set free from corruption. Oh, what a marvel! How did the Life of all taste of death? How was He enclosed in a tomb, save that He willed to illumine the world, as we cry aloud and say: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory... Both now...*

*Theotokion:* Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled; for a Virgin hast thou given birth, and after childbirth thou remainest as before childbirth; for God was born unto us, therefore nature was not corrupted. Therefore, O Mother of God, despise not the prayers of thy servants offered in thy church; but as thou bearest Thine own compassionate Son in thine arms, have compassion upon us, thy servants, and show mercy unto our souls!

*Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 1:*

While the stone was sealed by the Jews, and the soldiers guarded Thine all-pure Body, Thou didst rise on the third day, O Savior, granting life unto all the world. Therefore the heavenly powers cried aloud unto Thee, O Giver of life: Glory to Thy Resurrection, O Christ! Glory to Thy Kingdom! Glory to Thy dispensation, O only

Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion: Glory... Both now...*

When Gabriel announced unto the Virgin: Rejoice!, with his voice the Master of all became incarnate in thee, the holy Ark, as the righteous David said; thou wast shown forth more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who dwelt within thee! Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who freed our race through thy childbearing!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 1)

*At Matins, Canon to the Holy and Life-Creating Trinity, Tone 1. Ode 1. Heirmos: Thy victorious [right hand]...*

*Troparion 1:* The Seraphim glorify unceasingly the unoriginate and co-eternal Principle in Three Persons,[^8] the unsearchable Godhead, which every tongue faithfully glorifies with hymns.

*Troparion 2:* Manifesting Thy singular and thrice-radiant Godhead, O God, Thou didst create man according to Thine image, and didst grant him understanding, giving him mind, word, and spirit, for Thou art the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion 3:* From on high Thou didst show forth the singular and divine dominion in three Hypostases, O Father, having made It equal in action unto Thy Son and Spirit; therefore in three Hypostases we hymn Thee.

*Theotokion:* The unoriginate Father, the co-equal Son, and the co-enthroned Holy Spirit, let us hymn in the manner of the Seraphim; let us glorify the One God in Trinity: Holy, Holy, Holy! Worshipped and hymned in unity, and worshipped in three Persons as One.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Thou Who alone knowest the weakness of human...*

*Troparion 1:* Of old Thou didst manifestly appear unto Abraham as three Hypostases in one

hypostatic essence of the Godhead, prefiguring the truest theology, and the only-begotten Son is faithfully hymned in the Trinity of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

*Troparion 2:* With holy lips, the Seraphim glorify the unoriginate Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, right-believingly and perfectly; and for our sake as men, the Light of Light was united into one, the Savior without change following human nature.

*Troparion 3:* O Unity in Trinity transcendent in essence, beyond mind and understanding! Ineffably the spiritual and rational essences glorify Thee, with thrice-holy voices unceasingly offering praise, where the tri-hypostatic Hymn is fittingly sung.

*Theotokion:* He Who is beyond time came forth in time without change, and the timeless One was well-pleased in one essence and lordship; O beloved Savior, and the Spirit came forth together!

*Sessional Hymn of the Trinity, Tone 1:* O Trinity, Unity, God, beloved of all the world! In the appearance of a dove Thou didst well-pleasingly reveal the Father and Thyself and the undivided Spirit, in three Hypostases and in union, worshipped in right belief for the salvation of mankind.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Behold my salvation, O Virgin Theotokos! In the day of His second coming, may He not reprove in anger my secret transgressions, nor set me with the portion of the goats; but may He have compassion on me through thy prayers, O all-immaculate Virgin!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Habakkuk discerning the mountain overshadowed by grace...*

*Troparion 1:* Shine upon me, O divine thrice-radiant Light, Thy gracious beam, and with the beauty of the eyes of the heart

purify the mind, and fill me with that divine brightness which is beyond understanding, by Thy light-bestowing and radiant grace!

*Troparion 2:* Before establishing the heavens, O almighty Lord, Thy creative Word and the consubstantial Spirit were, by Whom Thou rulest through all ages, O singular Divine Dominion!

*Troparion 3:* The Children in the furnace prefigured the immortal Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, singing with a trumpet; and now, O Lord, confirm us to hymn Thee with heart and lips, together with the holy youths, worshipping the Three Hypostases.

*Theotokion:* Thou didst bear One of the Trinity, O all-pure One, God made man beyond understanding, Who illumined the earthly essence, the thrice-radiant Godhead, shining with unwaning light!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Illumine with the radiant dawn...*

*Troparion 1:* Before all, Thou didst establish the angels in the midst of creation; by the inaccessible rays of Thy beauty and by Thy many-lighted beams, illumine those who right-believingly hymn Thee, O Trinity of single dominion!

*Troparion 2:* The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are one Divine Essence; O ye faithful, glorify the One God, divided and undivided, Creator of visible and invisible creation!

*Troparion 3:* The prophet Elijah prefigured the Trinity by the Spirit of old on the field; Thou, O Lord, by the will of the Father didst raise the first-formed man by Thy third-day Resurrection; and now, O Lord, strengthen us by the power of the Paraclete to hymn the consubstantial Trinity in one union!

*Theotokion:* The sayings of the holy prophets prefigured concerning thee, O all-pure One, thy divine childbearing, ineffable and unspeakable, whereby we

have mystically learned to know the one all-creative Godhead in three Persons.

*Ode 6. Heirmos: The lowest abyss hath compassed us about, and...*

*Troparion 1:* Possessing equal dignity and power, O superessential Trinity, in the essence of Thy will, Thou art a simple and undivided Unity; do Thou, therefore, preserve us by Thy power!

*Troparion 2:* O unoriginate and co-enthroned Holy Trinity: Father, Son, and Spirit, from Whom all who dwell in the world have life, for all salvation is by Thy single power! Thee the Cherubim and Seraphim glorify, and with them we, unworthy as we are, offer praise unto Thee.

*Troparion 3:* A thrice-radiant Sun hath shone upon us, united in essence: the Father, Son, and Most Holy Spirit; distinct in Persons, yet undivided in essence: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

*Theotokion:* Thou wast the temple of the unwaning Sun, O all-pure One, Who created the great luminaries; having taken abode in thee, O all-pure Virgin and Bride of God, deliver us now from eternal torment!

*Sessional Hymn of the Trinity, Tone 1:* Falling down in faith, crying out from the heart: Have mercy upon us, Thy unworthy servants, O most good Trinity: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit! In Thy mercy have compassion upon Thine own flock which Thou hast acquired, that we may unceasingly glorify Thee and Thy goodness!

*Theotokion:* O Mother of God, who didst bear One of the Trinity, most fair paradise and salvation of the earth, protect with thy shelter those who blessedly hymn thee; for thou didst bring forth, as the prophets foretold, and thou didst carry Him Who upholds all things.

*Ode 7. Heirmos: Thee, O Mother of God, we faithful perceive...*

*Troparion 1:* The illumination of the Word and consubstantial brightness of God the Almighty...

Who didst promise graciously to make Thine abode, mercifully together with Thy Father and with the Holy Spirit, and hast shown [us] fearful unto demons.[^9]

*Troparion 2:* That Thou mightest show Thy tender mercy, O Master, Thou didst send Thy Savior into the abyss unto our lowliness, and didst re-fashion [us] unto the primal brightness; but now grant me understanding, O Divine Spirit!

*Troparion 3:* O most radiant Properties, O Father ineffably mighty in days, fill [us] with ineffable things; and, O Holy Spirit, make the man spiritual and perfect in the vision of nature, crying aloud unto the Light and Essence: O Father, Son, and God!

*Theotokion:* He Who is seated co-enthroned upon the Cherubim, the Upholder of all things, made His abode in thy womb, O all-pure One, appearing as God and the Lover of mankind; and by thy prayers preserve us who hymn thee!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: A marvelous wonder...*[^10]

*Troparion 1:* O Lord abundant in grace, all-tri-hypostatic Almighty Ruler, Who hast stretched out the heavens like a skin, and didst fashion the earth and the deep by Thy potent majesty: likewise strengthen us also in love and faith, O Lover of mankind, that we may hymn Thee unto the ages!

*Troparion 2:* Accept praise from corruptible beings, O most perfect Trinity, Who dost illumine and enlighten all spiritual essences by Thy goodness and power; for the unceasing powers glorify Thee, and we, earthly beings, cry aloud with untainted voices: May all creation bless Thee!

*Troparion 3:* Unto Abraham in his sojourning, He appeared in angels three-personed yet of single form, to be known as the one God in Trinity: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; and He blessed his seed to endure unto the ages, and that man might return unto light and primal blessedness,

let every creature bless and exalt Him above all!

*Theotokion:* He Who hath exalted us unto the heavens, Thine immutable Son, O Virgin Theotokos, assumed human nature through the multitude of His mercy, and delivered us from the ancient curse; unto Whom, giving thanks, let us cry aloud: Let all creation bless the Lord!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: The type of thy pure childbearing...*

*Troparion 1:* Save Thy creation, both visible and spiritual servants of Thine, from demonic assault and malice; teach [us] to honor the consubstantial Trinity, and preserve Thine own flock ever unharmed!

*Troparion 2:* Thou hast revealed the depth of Thine ineffable essence, and hast distributed grace unto us, O thrice-radiant Light and sole Sovereign, God Almighty, our Savior, wisely accomplishing our salvation!

*Troparion 3:* Look down upon our prayer, O Master, Who in tri-hypostatic subsistence art the one truly glorified God; and grant comfort unto Thy servants, through the prayers of the all-pure and all-praised Mother of God!

*Theotokion:* The envious enemy could not bear to look upon earthly nature, and the first-formed fell into corruption through transgression; but bowing the heavens, He came down without departing from the Godhead, and was incarnate of the Virgin, the Father's [Word], that He might deify earthly beings!

*Then: It is truly meet, and More honorable. Then the Trisagion. And after Our Father, these troparia, Tone 6:*

*Troparion:* Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us; for, lacking all defense, we sinners offer unto Thee as Master this supplication: Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us!

*Glory... Troparion:* O Lord, have mercy on us, for in Thee have we put our trust; be not exceedingly wroth with us, neither remember our iniquities; but

look upon us even now as Thou art compassionate, and deliver us from our enemies; for Thou art our God, and we are Thy people, all the work of Thy hands, and we call upon Thy name.

*Both now... Theotokion:* Open unto us the door of loving-kindness, O blessed Theotokos and Virgin, that hoping in thee we may not perish, but may be delivered by thee from tribulations; for thou art the salvation of the Christian race!

*Then the priest:* Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee, O Lord, hear and have mercy.

*Priest:* Again we pray that He may preserve this holy monastery, and every city and countryside, from famine, pestilence, earthquake, flood, fire, sword, the invasion of aliens, and civil war; and from every mortal wound; and that our good and man-befriending God may be merciful, benign, and easily propitiated toward our sins, and may turn away and deliver us from all His righteous wrath that impends against us, and send down upon the earth beneficial rain by His grace, and have mercy upon us, let us all say:

**Choir:** Lord, have mercy *(40 times)*.

*Then the priest's exclamation:*

**Priest:** Hear us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth, and of those who are far off upon the sea; and be merciful, be merciful, O Master, toward our sins, and have mercy on us; for a merciful and man-befriending God art Thou, and unto Thee do we send up glory: to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

*Epistle to the Hebrews:*[^30]

*Reader:* Brethren: All the saints through faith subdued kingdoms, wrought righteousness, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions, quenched the violence of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, out of weakness were made strong, waxed valiant in fight, turned to flight the armies of the aliens... ## SUNDAY EVENING VESPERS (TONE 1)

*Podoben: "O all-lauded martyrs" (прехвалнїи ц̃ї):* [^33]

*Sticheron 1:* O all-good Father, Thou didst send Thine own Son, the all-good God, to take upon His shoulder the fallen son, to restore the strayed sheep, and to lead him back whence he fell into Thine angelic fold, O unerring Son!

*Sticheron 2:* O all-good Father, Who by Thy great and long-suffering goodness didst bring into being as God that which was not; and at last, having fashioned man with Thine own hand according to Thine image, when he disobeyed the commandments given unto him, Thou didst recreate him, as the Lover of mankind!

*Other stichera, to the Bodiless Powers:*[^34]

*Sticheron 1:* Incorporeal Angels, standing before the divine throne...

...and radiant with the brightness thence, possessing eternal effusion of light, and becoming second lights: pray ye unto Christ to grant unto our souls peace and great mercy!

*Sticheron 2:* Incorporeal Angels, who have received in truth immortal life from the First Life, O all-wealthy ones, beholders of the supersubstantial and honorable glory: ye are ever shown full of light and turned toward the lamp by union, and ye dance; pray ye unto Christ to grant unto our souls peace and great mercy!

*Sticheron 3:* Archangels, Angels, Principalities, Thrones, Dominions, and six-winged Seraphim, and many-eyed Cherubim, divine vessels of wisdom, most divine Powers and Authorities: pray ye unto Christ to grant unto our souls peace and great mercy!

*Sticheron 1:* As a great abyss are my sins, O Christ Savior, and grievously am I submerged by mine iniquities; give me a helping hand as unto Peter, O God, and have mercy on me!

*Sticheron 2:* Because by evil thoughts and deeds I have condemned myself, O Savior: grant me the thought of return...

*Martyrikon:* By the prayers of all the holy martyrs, O Lord, grant us Thy peace, and have mercy on us, as the only compassionate One!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Thou art the joy of angelic ranks, and upon earth the mighty helper of men, O all-pure Virgin; save those who flee unto thee, for in thee have we placed our hope, O Theotokos!

Concluding stanza of Ode 4 in the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection, drawing typologically upon Hebrews 6:20 and 9:11–12 regarding Christ entering the heavenly Holy of Holies as our Forerunner.
[^17]: Slavonic: *катава̃ · Сѣдѧи въ славѣ ·*. The printed incipit indicates the Katavasia of Ode 4 (*Сѣдяй в славѣ на престолѣ Божества, на облацѣ легцѣ пріиде Іисус Пребожественный...*), drawn from the Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos.
[^18]: Slavonic: *інь кр̃тꙋ ір̃мо Б̃ъ сыи мира ѡ̃ц*. The printed incipit marks the Heirmos for Ode 5 of the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection (*Бог сый мира, Отец щедрот, великаго совѣта Твоего Ангела, мир подавающа, послал еси нам...*, Isaiah 9:6).
[^19]: Slavonic: *п̃ѣ ·ѕ̃· ір̃мо · Ѡ́биде на послѣднаѧ бездна · н̃бн̃съ бавлающаго...*. Fiol's 1491 text reflects characteristic dialectal and typographical compression (*н̃бн̃съ бавлающаго* for *нѣсть избавляющаго*; *х̃осꙋ* for *вмѣнихомся*; *ѡ́ бе̃нышь* for *Боже наш*), corresponding to the standard Heirmos of Ode 6 (Tone 1): *Обыде нас последняя бездна, нѣсть избавляющаго, вмѣнихомся яко овчата заколенія: спаси люди Твоя, Боже наш...* (Jonah 2:6, Psalm 43:23 LXX).
[^20]: Slavonic: *інь кр̃тꙋ ір̃мо ꙋтроба*. The abridged rubric denotes the Heirmos of Ode 6 for the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection (*Утробу Ионину во звѣри водном...* or *Иону из глубины преисподнія извел еси...*).
[^21]: Slavonic: *катава̃ · Бж̃твное сїе всѐ ко [?] · цꙋ иже в д̃нь и ли бци · Застоупнице хр̃тїанѡ̃*. This composite rubric directs the Katavasia of Ode 6 (*Божественное сіе и всечестное совершающе празднество богомудріи Богоматере...*) followed by either the Sunday Kontakion of the day (Tone 1, *Воскресл еси яко Бог из гроба во славѣ...*) or the universal Marian Kontakion (*Заступнице христиан непостыдная...*).
[^22]: Slavonic: *катва̃ непослꙋ ·*. Contracted typographical incipit for the Katavasia of Ode 7: *Не послужиша твари богомудріи паче Создавшаго, но огненное прещеніе мужески поправше, радовахуся поюще: препѣтый отцев Господь и Бог, благословен еси* (Romans 1:25, Daniel 3).
[^23]: Slavonic: *інь кр̃тꙋ ір̃мо Чю̃ прѣвелнее:–*. In modern orthography: *Чудо превеликое в пещи...*, designating the melodic model Heirmos for Ode 8 of the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection.
[^24]: Slavonic: *катав̃ Ѡ́трокы бл̃агочьстивыѧ :~*. Contracted incipit for the Katavasia of Ode 8: *Отроки благочестивыя в пещи Рождество Богородичо спасло есть, тогда убо образуемое, нынѣ же дѣйствуемое, вселенную всю воздвизает пѣти Тебѣ: Господа пойте, дѣла, и превозносите Его во вся вѣки* (Daniel 3).
[^25]: Slavonic: *інь кр̃тꙋ ір̃мо таинство*. Abbreviated incipit for the Heirmos of Ode 9 in the Canon of the Cross and Resurrection: *Таинство странное и преславное вижду: небо вертеп, престол херувимскій Дѣву...* (or *Таинство странное вижду и преславное...*).
[^26]: Slavonic: *інь б̃ци ір̃мо неизреченнаа ·*. Incipit designating the Marian Heirmos of Ode 9: *Неизреченное Девы таинство...* or *Любити убо нам яко безбѣдное страхом удобиѣ молчаніе...*.
[^27]: Slavonic: *кава̃ · Всѧкыи землень да по · се ѡ̃ктеа · И аа г̃а с̃ты г̃ь по се̃ свѣ · на хвали · ст̃ры въскрны по́е на и̃ · гл̃ ·а̃·*. The sequence outlines the conclusion of the Matins Canon with the Katavasia of Ode 9 (*Всяк земнородный да взыграется Духом просвѣщаем...*), the Little Litany, the thrice-chanted solemn dialogue *Свят Господь Бог наш*, the Exapostilarion (*Свѣтилен*), and the Praises (*Lauds*) with 8 resurrectional stichera in Tone 1.
[^28]: Slavonic: *Та сла̃ ст̃ра е̃ѵл̃каа · рѧдовнаа · і н̃ · Б̃: Прѣбл̃вена е̃си бц̃е · И сла̃сло в̃е : ѡ̃пꙋ в н̃е бл̃а на гл̃ ·а̃·*. Directs the conclusion of Sunday Matins (*Glory: The appointed Evangelical Sticheron; Both now: Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Theotokos; Great Doxology; Dismissal*) and the immediate opening of the Sunday Divine Liturgy Beatitudes in Tone 1 (*Во Царствіи Твоем помяни нас, Господи...*).
[^29]: Slavonic: *про̃ гл̃ ·а̃· Боуди г̃и мл̃ть твоа · с̃ · Вл видѣша ѡлга ·*. The primary Prokeimenon is Psalm 32:22 LXX (*Буди, Господи, милость Твоя на нас, якоже уповахом на Тя*). The printed verse is an epigraphic corruption or variant witness in Fiol's typesetting representing Psalm 97:3 LXX (*Видѣша вси концы земли спасеніе Бога нашего*), combined here with the Tone 1 Liturgy Prokeimenon.
[^30]: Slavonic: *ап̃л къ евреѡ: Браа ст̃и въсѣ вѣрою доидоша цр̃твїа...*. The Krakow 1491 Octoechos includes the full lectionary text for the Sunday Divine Liturgy Epistle at the conclusion of Tone 1, citing Hebrews 11:33ff. (the pericopic reading traditionally appointed for the Sunday of All Saints, Tone 1), preserved here according to the archaic liturgical apparatus of the editio princeps.
[^31]: Slavonic: *еѵ҃лїе ѿ мт҃а :~ Рече г̃ь своимь оученикомь · весь иже исповѣсть мѧ прѣд чл̃кы...*. The Sunday Divine Liturgy Gospel lection according to Matthew 10:32–33, 37–38; 19:27–30 (the appointed reading for the Sunday of All Saints, corresponding to Tone 1 in the post-Pentecost lectionary cycle), provided here in full directly following the Epistle in Fiol's 1491 editio princeps lectionary apparatus.
[^32]: Slavonic: *и послѣднїи · а послѣднїи пръвїи :~ при хвалите*. Concluding line of the Gospel reading (Matthew 19:30). The subsequent rubric *при хвалите* represents a transitional liturgical cross-reference to the Praises (*Lauds*) apparatus before the Sunday evening office.
[^33]: Slavonic: *ВЪ НЕДѢЛЮ ВЕЧЕРЪ СТИХИРЫ ПОКАѦННЫѦ · гн̃въꙁва · кѵр · їѡсифа · гл̃а ·а̃· по прехвалнїи ц̃ї*. Display headpiece in cinnabar ligatured letters introducing the Sunday Evening Penitential Stichera at "Lord, I have cried" (*Господи, воззвахъ*), composed by St. Joseph the Hymnographer, Tone 1, sung to the melodic model (*Podoben*) *Прехвальнїи мученицы* ("O all-praised martyrs").
[^34]: Slavonic: *Іны ст̃ры бесплътныимь :~*. Stichera to the Bodiless Powers (Incorporeal Hosts / Angels), appointed for Sunday evening at Vespers as the liturgical opening of Monday (dedicated weekly to the Holy Angels).
[^35]: Slavonic: *На стиховнѣ гла̃ ·а̃· Ꙗ́ко поучина велїа съгрѣшенїи мои...*. Stichera at the Aposticha for Sunday evening in Tone 1, comprising penitential hymns (*покаянны*), a martyr's hymn (*мучениченъ*), and a concluding Marian Theotokion (*богородиченъ*).
