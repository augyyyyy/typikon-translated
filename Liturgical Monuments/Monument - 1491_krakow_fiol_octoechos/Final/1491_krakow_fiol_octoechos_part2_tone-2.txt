# TONE 2

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 2)

*On Saturday evening, at "Lord, I have cried", we set 8 verses, and sing resurrectional stichera, Tone 2:*[^36]

*Sticheron 1:* Come, let us worship God the Word, begotten of the Father before the ages, and incarnate of the Virgin Mary; for having endured the Cross, He surrendered Himself unto burial as He Himself willed; and having risen from the dead, He hath saved me, a wandering man!

*Sticheron 2:* Christ our Savior, having nailed unto the Cross the handwriting that was against us, tore it asunder and abolished the dominion of death. Therefore we worship His third-day Resurrection!

*Sticheron 3:* With the archangels let us hymn the Resurrection of Christ; for He is the Redeemer and the Savior of our souls, and in fearful glory and mighty power He comes again to judge the world which He hath fashioned!

*Anatolian stichera:*

*Sticheron 4:* Thee, Who wast crucified and buried, an angel proclaimed as Master, and...

...said unto the women: Come and see where the Lord lay; for He is risen as He said, as almighty! Therefore we worship Thee, the only Immortal One: O Life-giver Christ, have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 5:* By Thy Cross, O Christ, Thou hast abolished the curse of the tree; and by Thy burial Thou hast put to death the dominion of death; and by Thy Resurrection Thou hast enlightened the race of mankind. Therefore we cry aloud unto Thee: O Benefactor, Christ our God, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 6:* Unto Thee, O Lord, the gates of death opened in fear, and the gatekeepers of Hades, seeing Thee, were terrified; for Thou didst shatter the brazen gates and crush the iron bars, and didst lead us out from darkness and the shadow of death, and tore asunder our chains!

*Sticheron 7:* Chanting a saving hymn, we send it forth from our mouths: Come, all ye, into the house of the Lord, let us cry aloud, saying: O Thou Who wast crucified upon the tree and art risen from the dead, Who art in the bosom of God, cleanse our sins!

*Dogmatikon Theotokion:*[^37]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* The shadow of the law hath passed away when grace arrived; for as the bush burned with fire yet was not consumed, so as a virgin thou didst give birth and didst remain a virgin. In place of a pillar of fire, the Sun of righteousness hath shone forth; in place of Moses, Christ, the Salvation of our souls!

*At the Aposticha, Resurrectional Stichera, Tone 2:*

*Sticheron 1:* Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, hath enlightened all the universe and recalled Thine own creation; O almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 2:* When Thou, O Christ, wast nailed unto the Cross...

...the form of creation was changed; and the soldiers, displaying inhumanity, pierced Thy side; and the Jews asked that the tomb be sealed, not knowing Thy power. But for the tender mercy of Thy compassions, having accepted a three-day burial and Resurrection, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 3:* An angel brought "Rejoice" unto her that is full of grace before Thy conception, O Lord; and an angel also rolled away the stone of Thy glorious tomb at Thy Resurrection. The one indeed proclaimed signs of joy in place of sorrow; while the other preached unto us the Master and Life-giver in place of death. Therefore we cry aloud unto Thee: O Benefactor of all, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* O new wonder, greater than all ancient wonders! For who hath known a mother giving birth without a man, and carrying upon her arm Him Who upholds all creation? It is the Word of God that was born, Whom as an infant, O all-pure one, thou didst bear in thine arms; and having maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to pray for those who honor thee, to have compassion and save our souls!

*Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 2:*[^38]

When Thou didst descend unto death, O Life Immortal, then didst Thou slay Hades with the brilliance of the Godhead; and when Thou didst raise up the dead from the nethermost depths, all the heavenly powers cried aloud: O Life-giver, Christ God, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* All beyond thought, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Theotokos! Sealed in purity...

...and preserved in virginity, thou art recognized to be the true Mother, having given birth unto the true God; entreat Him to save our souls!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 2)

*Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, Tone 2. Ode 1. Heirmos: In the deep He submerged of old Pharaoh...*[^39]

*Troparion 1:* One essence in two essences we confess Christ: begotten of the Father before the ages and not made, in the last times incarnate without seed of the pure Virgin, and having renewed the human race by His rising!

*Troparion 2:* Strange and marvelous was the birth before the ages from the unoriginate Father, and again from the Virgin without father, which the heavenly hosts glorify with fear; and we the earthly hymn Thy Nativity, and worship Thy rising!

*Troparion 3:* Thou art the source from the root, being the cause from the Father unto the Son... unto the consubstantial and three-personed Godhead: enlighten my heart with the brilliance of communion, by Thy beneficent radiance!

*Theotokion:* In thee have I placed my hope and my wretched soul; give me a helping hand, O Sovereign Lady: deliver me from corruption and save me by thy tender mercy!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: The desert hath blossomed as a lily...*[^40]

*Troparion 1:* We glorify the one essence in the thrice-holy God; we worship the undivided Trinity: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, that It may uphold and preserve the Christian race without stumbling!

*Troparion 2:* From the unoriginate Father the Word descended into her, taking upon Himself a twofold nature, becoming at once God and man...

...that He might renew man by His Resurrection!

*Troparion 3:* Establish upon the rock of faith, and broaden by Thy love the teaching of my heart, [and] of Thy servants, O three-personed Unity; for Thou art our God, and hoping in Thee we shall not be put to shame!

*Theotokion:* I have despaired of my life, O all-pure Sovereign Lady; vilely have I lived upon earth, both in words and in deeds, and I bow down before thee: have mercy on me, I cry unto thee; cast me not away in the time of sorrow!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 2.*

*Podoben: "Seeking the highest" (Въ вышнихъ ища :~):* [^41]

O Most Holy Trinity, have mercy on those whom Thou hast fashioned; deliver them from fire and all condemnation; for Thou canst do all things, being compassionate, and there is none so merciful. Therefore we fall down before Thee, crying: Forgive those who have sinned, O Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:* O all-pure Virgin, have mercy on thy servants; deliver from fire and every torment those who cry day and night unto thy Son, O all-hymned one; despise not Thy flock, O Christ, through the prayers of her that bare Thee!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: I hear Thee by report, O Lord...*[^42]

*Troparion 1:* Perfect from Perfect, God begotten of God and not made, not in time, and born before the ages; He was pleased to become flesh, partook of incorruption, and rose on the third day!

*Troparion 2:* In Unity let us glorify the Trinity, and the Trinity in Unity, an undivided Godhead: the Father with the unoriginate, and the Savior co-enthroned, with the All-Holy Spirit let us glorify!

*Troparion 3:* In singing the unoriginate and unapproachable Unity in three Persons, having, as it were, a vision...

...distinct in hypostases, yet united and undivided in Godhead and glory!

*Theotokion:* O undefiled Sovereign Lady, with trembling we hymn thee: give ear unto my voice, and drive away the demonic darkening, O pure one, that rises up against me; for thou art my protector and mediatress, and holy salvation!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Enlighten...*[^43]

*Troparion 1:* One kingdom and dominion in three hypostases let us glorify without division; for the Father unoriginately begat the Only-begotten Son, co-eternal and consubstantial, Whom, having risen from the dead, let us glorify!

*Troparion 2:* With the archangels let us sing praise unto the Trinity, undivided in Godhead, yet divided in known personal properties: the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, in the thrice-perfect Godhead glorify the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit!

*Troparion 3:* In Moses' bush, as He appeared in visible darkness, the Father's Word was called an angel, manifesting His coming unto us, for Whose sake Thou hast proclaimed the dominion of the divine origin of the One in three hypostases!

*Theotokion:* Hope of all the ends of the earth, O blessed Virgin Theotokos: in the time of my life deprive me not of rich compassions, but abundantly grant me thy tender mercy, that I also with faith may venerate thine image, O all-pure one!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Held fast in the abyss of sin...*[^44]

*Troparion 1:* One God in three properties, distinct in persons, yet undivided in essence...

...giving life unto the ends [of the earth] by His Nativity!

*Troparion 2:* One Light in three Persons, in the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, three indivisibly, and glorified in three properties, and known as worshipped in unity!

*Troparion 3:* The unbegotten Mind is the Father, and wisely shining forth as the image is the Word, the co-essential unoriginate Son; and the co-enthroned Spirit, [Who wrought] the Incarnation of the Creator in two essences according to the Word!

*Theotokion:* Having given birth unto the Light-giver of the world, enlighten my darkened soul, O Lady; entreat Christ Who was crucified and rose from the tomb on the third day!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*[^45]

Rising at midnight, with heart and lips we offer an angelic hymn unto Thee: enlighten us by Thy Resurrection; in the day of great joy, the day of enlightenment, save us who sing unto Thee, O Holy Trinity, have mercy on our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* All creation worthily hymns thee, O Virgin Mary Theotokos; yet human mind cannot attain to praise thee worthily, for the divine fire made its abode in thee: burn up our transgressions, and save those who hymn thee!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The godless command...*[^46]

*Troparion 1:* One essence in three hypostases, O Most Holy consubstantial Trinity, venerated by us, branches planted by God from the divine Source, perfectly inseparable: Father, Son, and Spirit, worshipped together!

*Troparion 2:* Illumined by all-radiant beams, O Most Holy Trinity, in one dominion preserve Thy singers...

...from adverse assaults, that we may unceasingly glorify Thee: O Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, glorified in one Godhead!

*Troparion 3:* Theologizing the three-leaved glory of the one Lordship, the Seraphim stand before Thee with awe-inspired reverence, glorifying the three-hypostatic Godhead; with them, rightly rejoicing, we sing: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit!

*Theotokion:* Thou art the refuge of Christians, O holy one, a rampart and divine protection, protectress unto all of us who hymn thee with love: deliver Thy servants from passions, O good one, that we may hymn thee and bless: Blessed art Thou, [O God] of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: The fiery furnace once...*[^47]

*Troparion 1:* Knowing the uncreated and creative Trinity in three hypostases: O Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, by Whom all creation subsists, preserved by the word of God; therefore renew the first-formed [Adam] by Thy Resurrection!

*Troparion 2:* O sovereign and all-perfect Trinity, having brought all things by the Word out of non-being, giving life by the Spirit: for his sake Thou didst descend to deliver the human race by the third-day rising!

*Troparion 3:* Unharmed, the most glorious children, having seen the immutable and living image of God, sang in the midst of the flame: Let the consubstantial creation sing!

*Theotokion:* Him before Whom all things tremble, thou hast carried upon thine arms, O pure Virgin, and dost nurse Him: a strange mystery and awesome marvel, that of thee was born the Creator of Adam and my God; entreat Him, O pure one, to save...

...all who in song glorify Thee unto ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: The Lord, Who is co-unoriginate with the Father...*

*Troparion 1:* Ruling over all and holding all things together, O most radiant Trinity, through prayers enlighten my darkened soul; O Word Who art unoriginate, absolve my transgressions, that glorifying Thy holy Resurrection, we may magnify Thee!

*Troparion 2:* By the Word Thou hast established [all things], O Father, in wisdom, and by the Holy Spirit: heaven and earth and all the airborne creatures; Thou hast quickened the ministering spirits, and formed man with Thine own hands; willingly enduring crucifixion, by Thy rising Thou hast renewed our nature!

*Troparion 3:* O God of threefold light, enlighten the hearts of those who hymn Thee; O Holy Spirit of three rays, by Thy light grant unto Thy servants that all may know and behold Thy will, which is good and perfect, and magnify Thy mercy!

*Theotokion:* Grant salvation of the soul from every calamity and distress, O Thou Who art praised in three Persons, ineffable, unique, and almighty: preserve Thine own flock, and deliver [us] for the sake of the Theotokos!

*Then: It is truly meet... as [usual on Sunday]. After that, Trisagion. After Our Father, the troparia written after the Trinity [canon] of the First Tone, and the litany.*[^48]

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 2)

*On Sunday at Matins, at "God is the Lord", Troparion, Tone 2: When Thou didst descend unto death... (twice); and as at Vespers [the Theotokion] (once). Then after the first stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*[^49]

The noble Joseph, taking down Thy most pure Body from the Cross, wrapped it in a clean shroud...

...and with sweet spices laid it closed in a new tomb; but on the third day Thou didst rise, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Who has seen or who has heard a mother without a man bearing her Creator, and nursing Him Who gives food unto all flesh? O marvel! The Cherubic throne has thy womb appeared to be, O Theotokos; O joyful one, pray for our souls!

*And after the second stichology, Sessional Hymn, Tone 2:*[^50]

Unto the myrrh-bearing women standing by the tomb, the Angel cried aloud: Myrrh is fitting for the dead, but Christ has shown Himself a stranger to corruption! Cry aloud singing that He is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Escaping the laws of nature by thy divine childbearing, thou hast united virginity [with motherhood]; for thou alone didst bear the One Who was timelessly begotten of the Father before ages: therefore we magnify thee as Theotokos!

*Hypakoe:* After the Passion, the women going to the tomb to anoint Thy body, O Christ God, saw an Angel in the tomb and were amazed; for they heard a voice from him, that He is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Degrees, Tone 2. Antiphon 1:*[^51]

Unto heaven I lift up the eyes of my heart: unto Thee, O Savior, save me by Thine illumination!

Have mercy on us who sin against Thee greatly at every hour, O my Christ; grant me the manner of repentance before the end!

*Glory... Both now...:* Unto the Holy Spirit it belongs to reign, to sanctify, and to shake creation; for He is God, consubstantial with the Father and the Word!

*Antiphon 2:*

If the Lord had not been in us, who could have been kept whole from the enemy and man-slayer?

Unto their teeth surrender not Thy servant, O Savior; for like sheep they move against me, for they are my enemies!

*Glory... Both now...:* Unto the Holy Spirit belong the origin of life and honor; for as being God He upholds and preserves all created things by the Father through the Son!

*Antiphon 3:*

They who trust in the Lord are like the holy mountain, who shall in no wise be shaken by the assaults of the enemy!

In iniquities let not those who live divinely stretch forth their hands; for Christ will not give the rod unto His inheritance!

*Glory... Both now...:* By the Holy Spirit every wisdom flows: thence grace unto the Apostles, and by sufferings the martyrs are crowned and the prophets see!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 2: Arise, O Lord my God... Verse: O Lord my God, in Thee have I put my hope. Let every breath praise the Lord. Resurrection Gospel. Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ. Psalm 50. Prayer of the Sunday. Through the intercessions of the Apostles... Both now... Through the intercessions of the Theotokos...*

*On Sunday, Resurrection Canon, work of Cosmas the Monk, Tone 2:*[^52]

*Ode 1. Heirmos: In the deep once Pharaoh's host was overwhelmed by the heavily armed power; but the Word made flesh destroyed sins by His almighty strength: the most glorious Lord, for He is glorified!*

*Troparion 1:* The ruler of the world, O Good One, unto whom we were enrolled because we obeyed not Thy commandment, is condemned by Thy Cross: for drawing near unto Thee as unto one mortal, he fell from the dominion of his power, and was shown to be weak...

...and stripped of his power, he was clothed in infirmity!

*Troparion 2:* Deliverer of the human race, and Author of incorruptible life, Thou didst come into the world: by Thy Resurrection having torn asunder the shroud of death, Him we glorify, for gloriously is He glorified!

*Theotokion:* Shown to be higher than all creation, visible and invisible, O most pure one; for thou didst bear the Creator as He willed to become incarnate in thy womb: Whom entreat with boldness to save our souls!

*Another Canon to the Cross, work of Sir John of Damascus. Ode 1. Heirmos: Come, ye people, let us sing a song...*[^53]

*Troparion 1:* Borne upon the Cross willingly, O Christ God, Thou didst slay the destroyer, and pour shame upon his wickedness, and having renewed mankind: for He is glorified!

*Troparion 2:* Commanded with joy, the myrrh-bearing women said unto His disciples: Proclaim My victory over the dead unto ages: for He is glorified!

*Theotokion:* Who can worthily recount of thee the conception beyond word? For thou didst bear God in the flesh, O most pure one, Who appeared for our salvation!

*Another Canon to the Most Holy Theotokos. Heirmos the same:*[^54]

*Troparion 1:* The immaterial ladder of old, and the path strangely made dry through the sea, foreshadowed thy childbearing, O pure Lady, which we sing: for He is glorified!

*Troparion 2:* The Power of the Most High, the perfect hypostasis of the Wisdom of God, having become incarnate incorruptibly of thee, drew near unto mankind: for He is glorified!

*Theotokion:* He passed through the impenetrable closed door of thy womb, the Sun of Righteousness, O most pure one, shining forth upon the world: for He is glorified!

*Ode 3. Heirmos: The wilderness hath blossomed as a lily, O Lord, the barren Church of the Gentiles by Thy coming, wherein my heart is established.*[^55]

*Troparion 1:* Creation was altered at Thy Passion, beholding Thee in humble form mocked by the lawless, Who didst establish all things by Thy divine nod!

*Troparion 2:* Having formed me of dust in Thine image with Thine own hand, and broken again into the dust of death by sin, O Christ, having descended into Hades, Thou hast raised me together by Thy Resurrection!

*Theotokion:* The angelic ranks were filled with wonder, O most pure one, and human hearts were awed at thy childbearing; therefore we, the faithful, honor thee as Theotokos!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: Establish me upon the rock of faith...*

*Troparion 1:* Pouring forth water and blood together from the immortal fountain of the divine side, Thou hast given drink and made me glad, and I sing: There is none holy like our God!

*Troparion 2:* Having raised up the fallen [man], and taken me, the lost one, upon His shoulders, risen He brought me back; and I sing: There is none holy like our God, none righteous!

*Theotokion:* Thou alone beyond all the earth wast granted great things above nature; for the God Whom no creation can contain, thou hast contained in thy womb; therefore we all acknowledge thee as Theotokos!

*Another Canon to the Most Pure Theotokos. Heirmos the same:*

*Troparion 1:* He Who is above all times, as the Creator and Maker of time...

...was willingly formed of thee as an infant, O Virgin!

*Troparion 2:* Let us hymn the womb more spacious than the heavens, for whose sake Adam lives rejoicing in the heavens!

*Theotokion:* After childbearing, O undefiled one, thou didst remain even as before childbearing; for thou didst bear God Who saved man by the Tree, O most pure Lady and Protectress of our race!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Coming from the Virgin, neither an intercessor nor an angel, but Thou Thyself, O Lord, didst become incarnate and saved all of me, a man; therefore I cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!*[^56]

*Troparion 1:* Standing before the tribunal as one condemned, my God, without crying out, O Lord, Thou didst send forth judgment unto the nations, and by Thy Passion, O Christ, Thou hast wrought salvation for the world!

*Troparion 2:* By Thy Passion the weapons of the enemy failed, and by Thine adversary-crushing descent into Hades the strongholds were destroyed, and the dominion of death was overthrown!

*Theotokion:* Thee, the haven of salvation and unshakable wall, all we faithful [have acquired], O Lady Theotokos; for by thy prayers thou dost deliver our souls from afflictions!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: Hearing the report of Thee...*

*Troparion 1:* Alone as in a winepress, O Lord, Thou didst tread down Thine enemies; for seeing Thy manly raiment dyed with strength, the all-destroying enemy fell, and fallen Adam was raised up!

*Troparion 2:* Behold, He came unto the forefather, leading forth the bound: having clothed Himself with our nature as a warrior with armor, He led me forth whole from the all-painful enemy, a captive!

*Theotokion:* Thou alone, O Theotokos, didst become higher than all creation; for thou didst bear the Creator and hold Him in thine arms...

...save those who praise thee, O blessed and all-pure Virgin!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos: Lo, the divine mountain is exalted, the house of the Lord above the hosts, the Theotokos manifestly.*

*Troparion 1:* Transcending the laws of nature, having alone borne the Ruler of creation, thou wast accounted worthy of the divine calling!

*Troparion 2:* Marvel! How the fire did not consume thy holy womb, prefiguring the bush of old, O pure one! Marvelous and awesome is thy childbearing, wherein the apostolic choir was magnified!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: Thou wast a Mediator between God and men, O Christ God; through Thee, O Master, we have found access from the night of ignorance unto the primal light, Thy Father.*[^57]

*Troparion 1:* As a cedar, O Christ, Thou didst crush the pride of enemies, O Master, willingly consenting to be lifted up in Thy flesh upon the cypress, the pine, and the cedar!

*Troparion 2:* In the nether pit, O Christ, they laid Thee dead, without breath, Who by Thy wound healed my festering wounds, O Savior, and didst resurrect with Thyself those who slept in the tombs!

*Theotokion:* Entreat Thy Son and Lord, O pure Virgin, [granting] deliverance to the captive, and to bestow peace from adverse assaults upon those who hope in thee!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: Giver of light...*

*Troparion 1:* Seeking the drachma lost in darkness, Thou didst find it, O Lord: for upon the Cross in the flesh Thou didst shine forth Thy light; desiring the resurrection of the fallen, Thou callest Thine own friends, saying: Rejoice with Me! Therefore we praise Thy Resurrection, O Christ...

...therefore we praise Thy Resurrection, O Christ!

*Theotokion:* Having given birth to Christ, the all-blessed Creator of all, rejoice, O pure one! Rejoice, thou who hast borne the Light for all! Rejoice, thou who didst contain the uncontainable God!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Heirmos the same:*

*Troparion 1:* Ye clouds, drop down the sweetness of gladness upon those on earth; for a Child is given, the Son Who is before ages, the Most High incarnate of the Virgin!

*Troparion 2:* Through our bodily life the Light shone forth, and dissolved the gloom of sin: at the last incarnate of the Virgin, O all-blessed one, the Most High!

*Theotokion:* Thou wast adorned beyond all angelic majesty, having conceived God, the Creator of the angels, O most pure Mother, having taken flesh and being ensouled and incarnate, O undefiled one!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Held fast in the abyss of sins, I call upon the unfathomable abyss of Thy compassion: from corruption, O my God, raise me up!*[^58]

*Troparion 1:* As an evildoer the Righteous One was condemned, and with two lawless men is nailed to the Tree, granting remission unto the guilty by His own blood!

*Troparion 2:* Unto the first-formed by one man, Adam, of old death entered into all the world; and by the one Son of God resurrection was bestowed!

*Theotokion:* Without knowing man thou didst bear God, and dost remain ever-virgin, revealed as the true dawn of the Godhead, shining forth the images of thy God!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: Abyss of sins...*

*Troparion 1:* Hades was abolished by Thy power, O Christ, and we earthborn were delivered from the nether...

...realms: by His own Resurrection He has bestowed life!

*Troparion 2:* The grave was emptied by Thy power, O Christ: descending into Hades, O Life-giver, Thou didst resurrect all by Thy dominion!

*Theotokion:* A Son came forth from thee, O Theotokos, Who created thee before the ages, the Son before all creation: Whom entreat ever for the whole world!

*Another Canon to the Most Pure Theotokos, the same [heirmos]:*

*Troparion 1:* Human nature, enslaved to sin, O holy Lady, through thee received freedom; for thy Son as a lamb is slain for all!

*Troparion 2:* We cry unto thee, O truly-existing Mother of God: deliver Thy servants who have angered [Him], for thou alone hast boldness unto thy Son!

*Theotokion:* Establish our faithful princes, O Lord, and grant them victory over adversaries, as almighty Master, born of the pure, unwedded womb!

*Kontakion of the day, if they have one; and the choir says [it].*[^59]

*Ode 7. Heirmos: The god-opposing command of the lawless tyrant raised up a lofty flame; but Christ spread out spiritual dew upon the pious children: He is blessed, the God of our fathers!*

*Troparion 1:* Thou couldst not bear, O Master, to see the human race tormented by mortal sin; having come, O Savior, Thou hast renewed [it] by Thine own blood, having become truly man: Blessed and all-glorious is God!

*Troparion 2:* Beholding Thee, the doorkeepers of Hades were terrified, O Christ, seeing Thee clothed in the garment of vengeance; not by the will of the tyrant, but his slave, O Master: for He came to deliver him: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:* Thou art known as the Mother of the holy mysteries,[^60] as the only one who gave birth to the immutable God, O undefiled Virgin, unwedded Mother; unto all the faithful Thou hast poured forth incorruption through this Thy divine childbearing: [Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers]!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Ode 7, Heirmos:* When the golden image on the plain of Dura was worshipped,[^60] the Three Children despised the godless decree; cast into the midst of the fire, bedewed they sang: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* When Thou wast nailed to the tree of the Cross, from Thy most pure side Thou didst pour forth life-giving blood, and Hades then was slain; and they that were put to death cried aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* Having overthrown the eternal gates, O Christ, and broken the bonds of the first-fashioned, Thou didst run, saying unto the corrupt one: "Arise straightway, crying unto Me, the Creator: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!"

*Troparion to the Trinity:* Undivided in three Persons, known by the faithful as One in essence: O Father, Word, timeless Son, and Most Holy Spirit: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Ode 7, Heirmos: The same as to the Cross.*

*Troparion:* Jacob indeed beheld Thee as in a nocturnal vision of riddles; but from Thee He was truly manifested in the flesh with radiant brightness unto them that sing: O transcendent God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:* Prefiguring in Thee the image of ineffable salvation, He wrestled with Jacob, He Who by His own will united Himself unto men: O transcendent God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:* Driving away the abominable ignorance of the Gentiles, and with unhesitating faith crying out unto the thrice-hymned Trinity: O transcendent God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

The furnace of fire once in Babylon divided its operation by the divine decree,[^61] consuming the Chaldeans, but cooling the faithful, who sang: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord; hymn and exalt Him above all forever!

*Troparion:* Beholding the garment of Thy flesh crimsoned with Thy Blood, O Christ, the angelic ranks marveled with trembling at Thy longsuffering, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:* Thou didst clothe Thyself in mortality, and through Thy Resurrection Thou didst grant immortality unto all; therefore, praising Thee, we hymn Thee, an elect people, crying: Bless the Lord, all ye works!

*Theotokion:* Having conceived without seed and ineffably given birth unto Him Who is inseparable from the Father, Who dwelt godworthily in Thy womb, O most pure Birthgiver of God: therefore we praise Thee as salvation unto us all.

*Another Canon to the Cross. Ode 8, Heirmos: In the light-bearing furnace...*

*Troparion:* Thou didst descend into Hades by Thy power, O Savior, and seizing the plunder of the all-destroying beast, Thou didst raise up together all who were awaiting Thee, who shared the likeness of Adam in captivity, ceaselessly crying: Bless the Lord!

*Troparion:* Having risen with boldness, the angel proclaimed with a most radiant voice unto those held in bondage, and brought transcendent joy to the holy ones, the glad tidings unto the Apostles: "Rejoice!" crying: Hymn the Lord, all ye works, and exalt Him above all forever!

*Troparion to the Trinity:* A Unity in three Hypostases, O Most Holy consubstantial Trinity, we have recognized Thee as God: the divine source, divinely budding, perfect, undivided: Father, Son, and Spirit worshipped by all!

[O Father, Son, and Spirit worshipped by all!]

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Ode 8, Heirmos: To the same.*

*Troparion:* He appeared on earth for the sake of the people, and dwelt with men through ineffable goodness and power; unto Him all we faithful sing, crying: Let all creation hymn Him Who gave it being, and exalt Him above all forever!

*Troparion:* Truly proclaiming Thee as the Birthgiver of God, O pure one, we glorify Thee; for Thou didst give birth to the One of the Trinity incarnate, Whom together with the Father and the Spirit we all hymn: Let all creation bless the Lord, and exalt Him above all unto ages!

*Troparion:* Beyond all word, Him Who was begotten of the Father before the ages, Thou hast ineffably given birth unto Him incarnate for us, O all-hymned Virgin, Whom we hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:* The Son of the unoriginate Begetter, God and Lord,[^62] incarnate of the Virgin has appeared unto us, to enlighten them that are in darkness, and to gather together them that are scattered; therefore, O all-hymned Birthgiver of God, we magnify Thee.

*Troparion:* Planted at the extremity of the ages, O Christ, the thrice-radiant tree of Thy precious Cross, watered with water and divine blood as from a fountain of Thy divine side, has blossomed forth life!

*Troparion:* Having been crucified as all-powerful, Thou didst destroy the mighty one who held sway below in the stronghold of Hades, and having raised human nature, Thou didst seat it upon the Father's throne; therefore, worshipping Thee as Thou comest, we magnify Thee.

*Theotokion:* O ever-Virgin, who didst give birth unto the Savior: grant peace unto us, Thy servants; grant mercy unto Thy people and steadfast love, and establish Thy servants in eternal rest, O most pure one.

[and establish Thy servants in eternal rest, O most pure one.]

*Another Canon to the Cross and Resurrection. Ode 9, Heirmos: What is this Thy grandeur...*

*Troparion:* Thou wast gloriously promised from of old, whereas formerly the wretched one tasted of the figure, yet now the sinner has communion in the renewed life, not being consumed; continually we magnify Thee.

*Troparion:* Let all us faithful sing a triumphal hymn: Having risen by divine power, destroying the dominion of death and striking down the Ishmaelite enemy,[^63] grant unto Thy servants victories over our adversaries!

*Theotokion:* O Birthgiver of God, great refuge of Christians: we flee unto Thy protection and Thy fervent intercession; receive us into the satiety of divine gladness, O pure one, that we the faithful, ceaselessly praising Thee, may magnify Thee.

*Another Canon to the Theotokos. Ode 9, Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* A rod of strength was given unto corruptible nature: the Word of God within Thy womb, O pure one; and He raised it up when it had slipped into Hades; therefore, as the Birthgiver of God, we all magnify Thee.

*Troparion:* Even as Thou wast well pleased, O Master, receive mercifully the prayer of Thy Mother; for through Thy bounty all things are filled, that we all may magnify Thee as Benefactor.

*Theotokion:* The angelic ranks, O most pure one, ceaselessly glorify Thy childbearing; standing in their ranks and gazing with fear, filled with His gladness, we ceaselessly magnify Thee.

[filled with His gladness, we ceaselessly magnify Thee.]

*Then the Resurrection Exapostilarion. The appointed Resurrection Stichera at the Praises, Tone 2:[^64]*

*Sticheron:* Let every breath and all creation glorify Thee, O Lord; for by the Cross Thou hast abolished death, that Thou mightest manifest unto men Thy Resurrection from the dead, as the only good One and Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:* Let the Jews tell how the soldiers lost the King whom they guarded; why did the stone not preserve the Rock of life? Either let them surrender Him that was buried, or worship Him that is risen, saying with us: Glory to the multitude of Thy compassions; O our Savior, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* Rejoice, ye peoples, and be glad! For an angel sat upon the stone of the tomb, and he brought us the glad tidings, saying: "Christ is risen from the dead, the Savior of the world, and He has filled all things with fragrance; rejoice, ye peoples, and be glad!"

*Anatolika:*

*Sticheron:* Let the gentile peoples praise Christ our God, Who willingly endured the Cross for our sake, and spent three days in Hades; let them worship Thy Resurrection from the dead, through which the ends of the whole world have been enlightened.

*Sticheron:* The women poured out myrrh with tears upon Thy tomb, and their mouths were filled with joy when it was said unto them: "The Lord is risen!"

*Sticheron:* Thou wast nailed to the Cross by the lawless, O Christ, and numbered among the dead, O immortal Word; and Thou didst rise on the third day as God in glory, granting unto the whole world eternal life and great mercy.

[and Thou didst rise on the third day as God in glory, granting unto the whole world eternal life and great mercy.]

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

*Sticheron:* Truly, O ye lawless ones, in sealing the stone ye made us worthy of a greater wonder; the guard has understanding: today He came forth from the tomb! They said: "Say that while we were sleeping His disciples came and stole Him." And who would steal a dead man, especially one anointed and naked? And He rose by His own authority as God, leaving His grave-clothes in the tomb. Come, behold, O ye Jews, how He did not break the seals, having trampled down death, and granted life and great mercy unto the human race!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron:* Let us offer radiant adoration unto Him Who is risen from the dead and has enlightened all things from the corruption of Hades through His three-day Resurrection.

Brethren, being made free from sin, become servants of righteousness. *Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Е҆гда̀ ѿ дре́ва):* [^67]

*Sticheron:* Possessing a fountain of tender compassion, an unsearchable abyss of mercies, and streams of bounties: O all-good Father, O Word and Son of the Father, and O Holy Spirit, uncreated Nature: receive our supplication and our prayer, granting forgiveness unto all of us who are in sins, as Thou art a compassionate God and Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:* Thou hast by nature, as God, a heart of compassion and a wealth of goodness; therefore we pray Thee, O Christ our Savior, and falling down we ever cry out, saying: Grant unto Thy servants remission of their many transgressions, and forgiveness of those sins which we all have committed, as Thou art a compassionate God and Lover of mankind.

*Sticheron:* Desiring to save all, O Savior, as God, for whose sake Thou didst appear as man unto men: save us who bow down before Thy commandments; for Thou camest not, O Lover of mankind, to save the righteous, but to loose those bound in transgressions and burdened with sins, by the grace of divine baptism, as Thou art a compassionate God and Lover of mankind.

*Other Stichera to the Bodiless Powers:*

*Sticheron:* Thrones, Cherubim, Seraphim, Dominions, Powers, and Authorities, Angels, Archangels, and Principalities: they who were brought into being by the incorruptible Godhead, sing with unceasing harmony, teaching all to honor the one essence of the Trinity in three Persons, undivided and unconfused.

*Sticheron:* The incorporeal ranks of the immaterial choirs, overcome by the direct rays of the divine Princeliness, impart divine splendors unto the lower ranks according to their order, and offer them by the law of love according to their worthiness and the purity of each with diligence.

[and offer them by the law of love according to their worthiness and the purity of each with diligence.]

*Sticheron:* Directing the soul on high to the heights, having the eye of the heart and mind stretched out with divine love in our souls at all times, that being illuminated by the rays from thence, we may escape the darkness of the passions, and learn to stand with fear before the throne with the angels unto the Creator, and be transfigured with light in this age.

*Theotokion:* The multitude of angels, O most pure one, praises Thy throne; for as it is fire-fashioned, it burns the inhabited world; Thou art the divine bridge that leads from earth ever unto Him, crying unto Thee: "Rejoice, full of grace!" with Archangel Gabriel in harmony, for Thou didst give birth unto the fountain of joy.

*Sticheron:* I have sinned against Thee, O Christ Savior, like the prodigal son; receive me who am running back, and have mercy on me, O God.

*Sticheron:* I cry unto Thee, O Christ Savior, with the voice of the publican: Be merciful unto me, even as unto him, and have mercy on me.

*Martyrion:* The passion-bearers, having loved none of earthly pleasures, were made worthy of heavenly good things and became fellow citizens of the angels: O Lord, by their prayers have mercy on us and save us.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Rejoice, Mary, Birthgiver of God, invincible temple, yea, holy one, as the prophet sings: "Holy is Thy temple, wonderful in righteousness" (Psalm 64:5 LXX).

Troparion from Ode 9 of Canon 2 (to the Cross and Resurrection). Note the explicit liturgical invocation against the "Ishmaelite enemy" (*Измаиля врага погубивъ*), reflecting historical Byzantine hymnography composed during the Arab and Ottoman incursions and faithfully preserved in early Slavic monuments. Physical leaf p54 bears quire signature *е̃і* (15) at the lower right.
[^64]: Slavonic: *та̃ с̃вѣ въскр̃сна · рѧдовьнаꙗ на хвалите · ст̃ры · въскр̃сны · гла̃ в̃ ·*. Rubric appointing the Sunday Resurrection Stichera at the Praises (*Lauds* / *Хвалитехъ*), Tone 2, concluding with the Anatolian stichera (*Восточны*).
[^65]: Slavonic: *сл̃а ст̃ра · е̃ѵ̃лска рѧдовнаꙗ · в н̃е на лѵ̃ргїи бл̃жены · глась в̃ ·*. Rubric appointing the Glory at the Praises (with the appointed Evangelical Sticheron) and the Sunday Beatitudes (*Блаженны*) at the Divine Liturgy in Tone 2, invoking Christ's mercy unto the Good Thief (Luke 23:42–43). Physical leaf p56 bears quire signature *ѕ̃і* (16) at the lower right.
[^66]: Slavonic: *в н̃е а̃п̃ль къ корен · ѻ́ · ч̃ · пр̃о · Пенги людїе моꙗ... Братїе, сподобньше сѧ ѡ̃ грѣха · поработнете сѧ правдѣ...*. Lectionary rubrics for the Divine Liturgy. Fiol's 1491 editio princeps contains a scribal/typesetting error in the rubric label, designating the Epistle as "to the Corinthians" (*къ корен...*), whereas the printed text is unmistakably the 4th Sunday after Pentecost reading from Romans 6:18–22 (*Pericope 116*). This is followed by the Gospel reading of the calming of the storm from Matthew 8:23–27.
[^67]: Slavonic: *въ тѫжь н̃е · в̃е · ст̃ры · покаанны · гла̃ в̃ · е̃гда ѡ̃ дрѣва ·*. Vespers of Sunday evening (inaugurating the liturgical cycle of Monday in Tone 2), appointing Penitential Stichera sung to the automelon *Tone 2, Podoben: "When from the Tree" (Егда ѿ дрѣва)*, followed by Stichera to the Bodiless Powers (*безплотнымъ*).
[^68]: Slavonic: *на стїхѡ · ст̃ры · покаанны · Съгрѣших ти х̃е сп̃се ꙗ́ко блꙋдныи сн̃ь...*. Aposticha Stichera for Sunday evening, addressed to Christ in the voice of the Prodigal Son (Luke 15:11–32) and the Publican (Luke 18:13), followed by a Martyrion and the Sunday Vespers Theotokion quoting Psalm 64:5 LXX.
