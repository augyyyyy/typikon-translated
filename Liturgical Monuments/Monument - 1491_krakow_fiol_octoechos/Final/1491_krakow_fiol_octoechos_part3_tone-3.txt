# TONE 3

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 3)

[p60LA] *On Saturday evening, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 3, automela:[^69]*

*Sticheron:* By Thy Cross, O Christ Savior, the dominion of death was destroyed, and the deception of the devil was abolished; and the human race, saved by faith, ever offers a hymn unto Thee.

*Sticheron:* All things were enlightened by Thy Resurrection, O Lord, and paradise was opened again; and all creation praising Thee, ever offers a hymn unto Thee.

*Sticheron:* I glorify the Father and the power of the Son, and I hymn the authority of the Holy Spirit: the undivided, uncreated Godhead, the consubstantial Trinity, reigning unto ages of ages.

*Anatolika:*

*Sticheron:* We bow down before Thy precious Cross, O Christ, and we hymn and glorify Thy Resurrection; for by Thy wound we all were healed.

*Sticheron:* We hymn the Savior Who was incarnate of the Virgin; for He was crucified for our sake, and rose on the third day, granting unto us great mercy.

*Sticheron:* Descending unto them that were in Hades, Christ proclaimed the glad tidings, saying with boldness: "Be of good courage, now have I conquered! I am the Resurrection, I will bring you forth, having destroyed the gates of death!"

*Sticheron:* Standing unworthily in Thy most pure house, we send up an evening hymn, crying out from the depths: O Christ God, Who hast enlightened the world by Thy Resurrection [p61LA] [deliver Thy people from the hand of Thine enemies, O Lover of mankind!]

*Sing once; repeat; Glory... Both now... Theotokion (Dogmatikon), Tone 3:[^70]*

How shall we not marvel at thy theandric giving birth, O all-pure one? For without having accepted the temptation of man, O all-blameless one, thou didst bear in the flesh without father Him Who was begotten of the Father without mother before all ages, Who underwent no manner of change, nor mingling, nor division, but preserved the property of each essence intact. Therefore, O Virgin Mother and Lady, entreat Him that our souls may be saved, who in Orthodox manner confess thee to be the Birthgiver of God.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 3:[^71]*

*Sticheron:* By Thy Passion, O Christ, Thou didst darken the sun, and by the light of Thy Resurrection Thou hast enlightened all things: receive our evening hymn, O Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:* Thy life-giving Resurrection, O Lord, enlightened the whole universe, and recalled Thine own creation that was corrupted; therefore, having all been delivered from the curse of Adam, we cry aloud: O almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* Being God changeless, Thou wast changed by suffering in the flesh; and creation, unable to bear seeing Thee hanging upon the Cross, bowed down in fear, and groaning, praised Thy long-suffering; and having descended into Hades, Thou didst rise on the third day, granting life unto the world and great mercy.

*Sticheron:* That Thou mightest deliver our race from death, O Christ, Thou didst endure death; and having become the first-born [p62LA] from the dead, Thou didst raise with Thyself them that have acknowledged Thee as God; and Thou hast enlightened us: O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Without seed, from the Holy Spirit, by the will of the Father, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who was before all ages from the Father without mother; and for our sake, having come from thee without father, thou didst bear Him in the flesh and feed Him as an infant with milk; therefore pray without ceasing, that our souls may be delivered from dangers.

*Resurrection Troparion, Tone 3:[^72]* Let the heavens be glad, and let the earthly rejoice; for the Lord has shown strength with His arm: He has trampled down death by death, and become the first-born from the dead; He has delivered us from the belly of Hades, and granted great mercy unto the world.

*Twice. Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Thee who didst mediate salvation for our race, we hymn, O Virgin Birthgiver of God; for having accepted the suffering of the Cross in the flesh assumed from thee, Thy Son and our God has delivered us from corruption, as the Lover of mankind.

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 3)

*Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, Tone 3. Ode 1. Heirmos: He Who of old gathered the waters...[^73]*

*Troparion:* Incomprehensible sole Sovereign Dominion, unoriginate, eternal, all-seeing God: make us worthy of the thrice-radiant shining of Thy glory, that angels and men may now magnify Thee and unceasingly hymn Thee with thrice-holy songs from angelic mouths.

*Troparion:* Sacredly the ranks of bodiless powers hymn Thee as creative and authorial cause, O thrice-radiant Unity and most luminous Light; with them in harmony sing [p63LA] the assemblies of mankind, and faithfully glorify Thee with faithful mouths.

*Troparion:* Mind, Word, and Spirit: the theologians mystically depict in personal images the passionless generation, knowing the Word to be co-unoriginate from the unbegotten Father, the divine sole origin of all things, together with the divine procession of the Spirit.

*Theotokion:* As Lover of mankind by nature, having assumed human nature, God the Word, the all-beginning Trinity, light of sole origin, glorified everywhere in the one Godhead, revealed Himself in thee who didst bear Him, O undefiled Virgin.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: He Who brought all things out of nothing...*

He Who of old commanded the universe to be established from non-being, having instructed us typologically, prefigured the Trinitarian hypostasis of the sole Godhead, the Sovereign Dominion.

*Troparion:* Corruptible nature of earth hymns Thee, the one thrice-radiant ineffable Creator, crying aloud: O Master, deliver me who am wholly afflicted!

*Troparion:* In harmony following the words of the prophets, and the renowned apostles, preachers of the faith, the faithful glorify Thee, the equally operating Trinity, unto ages of ages.

*Theotokion:* Descending from the high throne, elevating man as Lover of mankind, through thee, O most pure one, the Trinitarian light has shone upon the world.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3.*

*Podoben: "Beholding the beauty of thy virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства твоегѡ̀):* [^74]

[p64LA] those who glorify Thy dominion, as Thou alone art merciful, knowing the weakness of our heart.

*Twice. Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* The incomprehensible, uncircumscribed, and consubstantial Word of the invisible Father, thou didst mystically contain within thy womb; through thy birthgiving we have known how to glorify in the world the one and undivided Godhead of the Trinity; therefore with thanksgiving we cry unto thee: Rejoice, full of grace, the Lord is with thee!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Thou hast set firm love toward us: the...* [^75]

*Troparion:* A twofold-numbered shoot from the Father as from a root, the Son sprouted forth, and the upright Spirit; undivided sproutings planted by God and flowers equal in power, as three-hypostatic divine luminaries.

*Troparion:* The assemblies of noetic beings unceasingly hymn Thee, the uncircumscribed God; with them we also glorify Thee, saying: O supersubstantial Trinity, preserve Thy servants, as Thou art merciful!

*Troparion:* Thou hast drawn us unto Thy love, O only merciful One, O Word of God, Who for our sake wast ineffably incarnate; having mystically received the one thrice-radiant Godhead, therefore we glorify Thee.

*Theotokion:* Look down upon me who am infirm, O undefiled one, and destroy my grievous and incurable passions, that I may magnify thee who hast magnified all mankind.

*Ode 5. Heirmos: As Isaiah saw...*

As Isaiah saw typologically God upon a throne high and lifted up, receiving gifts of glory from angels, "O wretched me!" he cried aloud: "I have foreseen God becoming incarnate, the unwaning Light, ruling over the world!"

[p65LA] *Troparion:* Isaiah saw in unison the Sovereign Origin, God glorified in three persons by the most pure voices of the seraphim; and he was sent to proclaim the true thrice-radiant essence, the three-personal unity.

*Troparion:* Thou Who before didst bring forth from non-being all visible and invisible nature, O thrice-sunned Trinity: hymning Thee as the one God, deliver the faithful from every temptation, and make us worthy of Thy glory!

*Theotokion:* O light-bearing bridal chamber of God, having come forth from the pure Virgin, we hymn with love and bless Christ Who was born of thee in two essences and wills, One of the Trinity unto glory.

*Ode 6. Heirmos: The abyssal...[^76]*

*Troparion:* Abraham, being a stranger, was made worthy typologically to receive God in three hypostases under the forms of men.

*Troparion:* Direct the hands of Thy servants toward the unapproachable Light, O Trinity, unwaning Sun; and grant the illumination of Thy glory unto our souls, as far as it is possible to behold Thine ineffable glory.

*Troparion:* Unto the Father, and the Son, and the divine Spirit we the faithful bow down, unto the uncreated Trinity in three persons; and in one Godhead ever without beginning, in three hypostases, we faithfully glorify.

*Theotokion:* Thou hast opened the gates unto the Light Who was born of thy womb, that seeing the all-radiant [p66LA] ray of the Godhead, all may glorify thee, the all-hymned Lady.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 3 (sung twice):[^77]* When Thou comest, O God, upon earth to judge all, have compassion, as Thou art merciful, upon those whom Thou didst fashion from earth after Thine own image, and deliver all from righteous condemnation, and send us not into the fire of Gehenna, for Thou alone art Christ, the only merciful One.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Grant me sighing, O Lady, as unto the publican, and tears as unto the harlot, that offering these unto thee, I may cry aloud: Have mercy on me, O all-merciful one, and drive away my affliction by thy goodness, and grant me showers of compunction; for unto thee have I fled, O all-merciful one!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: As of old...*

*Troparion:* Show me to be a temple of Thy thrice-radiant and all-luminous Godhead, O Master, free from evil sinful darkening and passions, O Light-giver, truly with us: Blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:* We proclaim the one radiance of the Godhead in three hypostases, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, in undivided properties: Blessed art Thou!

*Theotokion:* Thou didst appear upon earth, having become man, the Creator of all, in a manner befitting God, having come forth from the pure Virgin, from her most pure womb, and Thou hast deified us: Blessed, O all-pure one, is the fruit of thy womb!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Incomparably...*

*Troparion:* From the unoriginate Father as from a root, the Word and the Spirit, being co-unoriginate, having appeared as the sproutings of the supersubstantial divine Origin, reveal the Trinity: one glory and power, [p67LA] which we hymn and glorify unto all ages.

*Troparion:* Thou dost order by Thine unfeared commands the heavenly ranks to hymn Thee unceasingly with thrice-holy divine songs, O Father, Word of like image, and Spirit, thrice-radiant sole dominion of power: with them we hymn Thee unto all ages.

*Theotokion:* The foretold prophetic riddles, having seen thy giving birth from afar, glorify Thee, O Birthgiver of God; for without seed, beyond nature, Thou didst give birth, O Lady; and in harmony we hymn Him, glorifying the Lord Who was born of Thee.

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A new marvel...[^78]*

*Troparion:* Beholding the thrice-radiant glory, the God-beholding bodiless ranks, as they soar with wings desiring the heights, and as they fan around the unapproachable Light, cry unceasingly; with them we also glorify in harmony: O sole Trinity, we glorify Thee!

*Troparion:* With unchanging love, having received from Thee upon earth an intelligent and rational soul, hymning Thee, O Master, God Almighty, one essential nature in truth, and in three persons: as many as praise Thee, preserve this soul from the whole heart, O Merciful One, as Thou art almighty!

*Troparion:* Having proclaimed to the world the Trinity, one in essence in three hypostases, and having driven away the deception of polytheism from the earth, having taught men to glorify without deception the Trinity...

[p68LA] *Theotokion:* Show me to be a temple of the divine light-vision of the divine Origin, and to serve purely the Creator of all, a noetic beholder of ineffable glory, by the prayers of the Mother of the Only-begotten, whom we worthily ever magnify.

*Rubric:* Then: *It is truly meet...* and the rest, as indicated in Tone 1, after the Canon.[^79]

*On Sunday, at "God is the Lord", Troparion, Tone 3:* *Let the heavens be glad...* twice; *Both now...* Theotokion: written backwards (see fol. 62r). After the first Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:

*Sessional Hymn:* Christ is risen from the dead, He has become the first-fruits of them that slept, and the first-born of creation, and the Creator of all things that have come to be: He has renewed in Himself the corrupted nature of our race; no longer dost thou rule, O death, for the Master of all has destroyed thy dominion.

*Twice. Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* The heavenly rejoiced with love, and the earthly trembled with fear, when the most pure voice came upon thee, O Birthgiver of God; for one festival was made for both, when the Bodiless One brought joy unto thee; therefore with the angel we cry unto thee: Rejoice, O pure one, dwelling place of God!

*After the second Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3:* Having tasted death in the flesh, O Lord, Thou didst cut short the bitterness of death by Thy Resurrection; Thou didst strengthen man again by victory over the first curse:

[p69LA] O Defender, Christ God, glory to Thee!

*Twice. Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

*Tone 3.*

*Podoben: "Beholding the beauty of thy virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства твоегѡ̀):* [^80]

*Ypakoe:* Astonishing by his appearance, refreshing with his words, the radiant angel said unto the myrrh-bearing women: "Why seek ye the Living One in the tomb? He is risen, emptying the tombs! Understand Him Who changes corruption to be changeless; say unto God: How awesome are Thy works, for Thou hast saved the human race!"

*Hymns of Ascent, Tone 3. Antiphon 1:*

*Sticheron:* Thou didst deliver the captivity of Zion from Babylon: lead me also from the passions unto life, O Word!

*Sticheron:* They that sow in the south with divine tears shall reap ears of life ever with joy.

*Glory... Both now... Unto the Holy Spirit:* Unto the Holy Spirit all thanksgiving shines forth, even as unto the Father and the Son, in Whom all things live and move.

*Antiphon 2:*

*Sticheron:* Except the Lord build the house of virtues, in vain do we labor; but if He shelter the soul, no one shall destroy our city.

*Sticheron:* Through the Spirit the fruit of the womb is fashioned by the mercy of the saints, as they are holy fathers at all times.

*Glory... Both now... Unto the Holy Spirit:* By the Holy Spirit every holiness and wisdom is foreseen; for He enriches [p70LA] every creature: let us serve Him, for He is God, even as the Father and the Word.

*Antiphon 3:[^81]*

*Sticheron:* Blessed are they that fear the Lord; walking in the way of the commandments, they shall eat every living fruit.

*Sticheron:* Round about thy table be glad, O Chief Shepherd, beholding thine offspring bearing branches of good deeds.

*Glory... Both now... Unto the Holy Spirit:* By the Holy Spirit is every wealth of glory; from Him is grace and life unto all creation: for He is hymned together with the Father and with the Word.

*On Sunday, Canon of the Resurrection, Tone 3. Ode 1. Heirmos:*

He Who of old by divine beckoning gathered the waters into one gathering, and divided the sea for the people of Israel: He is our God, most glorious; unto Him alone let us sing, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* Having condemned the earth to bring forth thorns unto the transgressor in sweat, this our God in the flesh received a crown of thorns from the hand of lawless men; and having dissolved the curse, He glorified it.

*Troparion:* A Victor over death by overcoming, He Who appeared overcome by death; for having accepted the passion of an animate flesh, this our God [p71LA] [He grappled with the tyrant, and] raised all: for He is glorified![^82]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

All nations glorify thee as the true Birthgiver of God, who didst bear without seed; for having entered into thy sanctified womb, this our God, Who took substance according to our likeness, was born of thee as God and man.

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Heirmos: Even as the divided sea...*

*Troparion:* Thou didst accept buffeting for my sake, O Master, procuring freedom for the guilty, and redemption through Thy blood for the liberated; therefore we sing a hymn unto our marvelous God, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* "Why seek ye the Living One as dead at the tomb?" the angel cried unto the women: "Why have ye come seeking? Behold, Christ is risen!" Therefore let us sing a hymn unto our marvelous God, for He is glorified!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Let us faithful piously hymn and honor the pure Virgin who after childbirth remained incorrupt, having borne without man, entreating thee, the Birthgiver of God, who praise thee with faith.

*Another Canon, Ode 1, to the Most Holy Birthgiver of God. Heirmos: The same, Podoben: "Every nature bends the knee" (Всяко естество):*

Every nature bends the knee, O immaculate Lady, unto Him Who was incarnate of thee, together with earthly beings, both noetically and super-cosmically: for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* By Thy good pleasure toward thee, O Good One, for without envy the Giver, the Divine Spirit, came upon thee, and the Word took flesh from thee, O Maiden: for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* Thou hast borne the Word, Co-worker with Him that begot Him, Who deified human nature; entreat Him on our behalf, that we who have grown weak through long-standing sins may conquer the strong wiles of the enemy through divine consolation.

*Ode 3. Heirmos: He Who brought all things out of nothing...*

[p72LA] ...fashioned by the Word, and perfected by the Spirit, O Almighty Most High: establish us in Thy love![^83]

*Troparion:* By meekness the impious one was put to shame, when he fell into the pit that he had made; and by Thy Resurrection, O Christ, Thou hast exalted the horn of the humble.

*Troparion:* The preaching of piety has covered the nations, as the water covers the sea, O Master: having risen from the tomb, Thou hast revealed unto us the Trinitarian Light.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Glorious things are spoken of thee, O animate city of the ever-reigning King; for through thee, O Lady, He has dwelt with earthly beings.

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 3. Heirmos: Thy mystery...*

*Troparion:* Coming upon earth in the likeness of flesh without sin, Thou didst condemn the ancient slaying by the tree of corrupting knowledge, O Master!

*Troparion:* Crucified for our sake and numbered among the dead, willingly enduring three days' burial, O Lord and Savior, by Thy rising Thou didst grant resurrection unto the world, as Good and the Lover of mankind: glorified!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

May thy pure prayer be our confirmation and consolation, O undefiled and pure Virgin after childbirth: pray, O most pure one, send consolation unto the world through thy prayers!

*Another Canon, to the Birthgiver of God. Ode 3. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* As a rod from the root of Jesse, prophetically budding forth, O Virgin, the flower Christ has shone forth from thee: Holy art Thou, O Lord!

*Troparion:* That Thou mightest make earth-born men partakers of the divine nature, Thou wast clothed in flesh from the Virgin; for Thou didst condescend without change, O Lord!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou didst bear the Divine Word beyond speech, Who has loosed the irrationality of man: entreat Him diligently, that I who am enslaved to irrational [p73LA] passions may be set free, O only Ever-Virgin![^84]

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*

Thou hast placed toward us steadfast love, O Lord, for Thou didst give Thine Only-begotten Son unto death for our sake; therefore, praising Thee, we cry aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:* In Thy mercy, O Christ, Thou didst endure wounds and stripes, and in Thy longsuffering Thou didst suffer reproach and buffeting, enduring also spitting, whereby Thou hast wrought salvation, O Christ: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion:* With a mortal body, O Life, Thou wast numbered among the dead, for the misery of the poor and the groaning of the needy: destroying the corrupter, Thou hast gloriously raised all with Thyself!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Remember, O Christ, the flock which Thou didst purchase with Thy Passion: receiving the merciful prayers of Thy glorious Mother, visit our affliction and deliver us by Thy power!

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 4. Heirmos: It covered...*

*Troparion:* Thou wast suspended upon the Tree, Who didst suspend the earth upon the waters, O Creator of all; and Thy side being pierced with a spear, Thou didst pour forth blood and water, redemption and cleansing for all!

*Troparion:* Thy side being pierced healed my wounds; struck by the hand, Thou didst obtain freedom for me; tasting gall, Thou didst deliver me from the wounding of the deceiver, O Christ!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

The Most High was incarnate from thy pure blood: beholding Him unrighteously exalted upon the Tree, O most pure one, weeping with tears and groaning, she magnified His tender mercy!

*Another Canon, to the Birthgiver of God. Ode 4. Heirmos: The same (He Who set the mountains in the scales):*

...by the measure [p74LA] of divine wisdom, Thou, the Stone cut without hands from the Virgin: Glory to Thy power, O Lover of mankind![^85]

*Troparion:* Thou hast healed our diseased nature, O Master, by the most swift uniting from the Virgin, O Son and Word, through Thy precious Godhead!

*Troparion:* Participating in our nature, O Lord, having united the desired inheritance, the Word from the Virgin brought to pass the bodily composition.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Unto Thee I rise early, the Creator of all, Who exceedest every mind in peace; for Thy commandments are light: guide me in them!

*Troparion:* Betrayed by Hebrew envy unto an unjust judge, the All-seeing One, Who judgest all the earth righteously, delivered ancient Adam from condemnation!

*Troparion:* Grant Thy peace unto Thy churches, O Christ, by the unconquerable power of Thy Cross, Thou Who art risen from the dead: save our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Holy Tabernacle broader than all creation, for thou didst receive within thee the incomprehensible Word, O only manifested Ever-Virgin!

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 5. Heirmos: Upon earth...*

*Troparion:* Pierced in Thy divine side with a spear, O Christ, finding fashioned man, through whom perdition had come unto all men, Thou didst become an Intercessor and set him free from corruption!

*Troparion:* Equal to the Father in essence, O Savior, Thou didst raise up the sanctified temple of Thy assumed and all-pure body from the dead, O Lover of mankind!

[p75LA] *Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Corrupted before through transgression, our nature, having incorruptibly borne Christ, thou hast renewed, O most pure Lady, Protectress of our souls![^86]

*Another Canon, to the Birthgiver of God. Ode 5. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* The Word of God is thy Son, O Virgin, the Creator of the first-fashioned Adam, not a creation; although He took flesh endowed with a rational soul from thee!

*Troparion:* Equal to the Father is thy Son, O Virgin, the Word of God, perfect in hypostasis in two natures, and He became perfect man!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou wast shown forth after childbirth without corruption, for God was born of thee as man: beholding Him crucified, unable to bear maternal grief, thou didst cry aloud!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*

The lowest abyss of sins has encompassed me, and my spirit faints; but stretching forth Thy high arm, O Master, save me, O Helmsman, as Thou didst Peter!

*Troparion:* The abyss of mercy and compassions has encompassed me, O compassionate Savior: for having become incarnate, O Master, and taking the form of a servant, Thou didst deify it and glorify it with Thyself!

*Troparion:* He Who put death to death received slaying; making alive him that was slain, through the expectation of Thy Resurrection, O Son of God, He conquered the enemy!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

O most pure one, who alone beyond understanding didst mediate between the Creator and mankind: entreat thy Son to be merciful unto thy sinful servants and to be their Defender!

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 6. Heirmos: Lead up from corruption...*

*Troparion:* Who didst endure crucifixion and death, and didst rise on the third day from the tomb: humble the arrogance of the adversary [p76LA] and guide my life, O Merciful One![^87]

*Troparion:* Exalting me who had fallen, O Christ Lover of mankind, Thou Who didst rise from the dead on the third day: revive me who am afflicted and grievously corrupted, and direct my life, O Lord Merciful!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Our hope and confirmation: guide and direct the faithful people upon the path of life, O Lady, who piously praise and ever honor thee!

*Another Canon, to the Most Holy Birthgiver of God. Ode 6. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Formed in our likeness from the incorrupt Virgin is He Who is inseparable in form from the Father, becoming an image in reality, the changeless God and Lord!

*Troparion:* The timeless Word, having received a birth in time, O Christ, delivered Adam from the curse, descending into extreme poverty, desiring to save the straying nations through the birthgiving of the blameless Mother of God!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Deliver me, O most pure one, from the abyss of sins and the assaults of passions: for thou art a harbor and an abyss of wonders unto them that flee unto thee in faith!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

Even as of old Thou didst bedew the three pious children in the Chaldean furnace, with the radiant fire of Thy Godhead enlighten us also who cry aloud: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* The radiant veil of the temple was rent at the crucifixion of the Creator, revealing the hidden scripture in truth unto the faithful who cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* From Thy pierced side, drops of life-creating blood pouring forth by Thy dispensation upon the earth, Thou hast renewed those upon the earth [p77LA] who cry: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers![^88]

*Troparion (Trinity):*

Let us glorify the Good Spirit together with the Father and with the Only-begotten Son, honoring, O ye faithful, one Principle in three Hypostases, one Godhead, crying: Blessed art Thou, O God of our fathers!

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 7. Heirmos: The rod and the fire...*

*Troparion:* By Thy Passion Thou hast restored Adam's nature upon the Tree: led as a sheep to the slaughter, O Christ, for them that cry: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* Numbered among the dead and laid in the tomb, Thou didst raise up all the dead of Hades, O Master, who hymn Thee with songs: O God, blessed art Thou!

*Glory... Both now... (Trinity):*

As many of the faithful as have learned to honor the one undivided Godhead in three Persons, consubstantial and the same, we cry in faith: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Another Canon, to the Birthgiver of God. Ode 7. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* The Lord of glory and Sovereign of the heavenly hosts, Who sits with the Father, was borne by virginal hands: bless, O blessed Virgin, thy childbearing!

*Troparion:* Thou hast destroyed the deadly poison through conversation with the Virgin, becoming divinely constituted flesh: Blessed art Thou, we cry unto Thee, O God of our fathers!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

We all know thee to be the Birthgiver of God, who didst bear God, One of the Trinity incarnate: blessed, O most pure one, is the fruit [p78LA] of thy womb![^89]

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*

United in the unendurable fire, the youths who stood for piety, unharmed by the flame, sang the divine hymn: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, and highly exalt Him unto all ages!

*Troparion:* The temple's splendor was rent when Thy Cross was erected on Golgotha, and creation bowed down, singing with fear: Bless the Lord, all ye works!

*Troparion:* Christ is risen from the tomb, and by divine power Thou hast restored him that fell through the ancient deception, who cries and says: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou hast been revealed as an animate temple of God, a receptacle and ark, containing the Creator of all mankind, O most pure Mother of God; and worthily all works glorify thee, who wast preserved a Virgin after childbirth!

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 8. Heirmos: Serving Him that lived...*

*Troparion:* Lifted up upon the Cross, O Christ, and pierced in the side by lawless men, Thou didst pour forth the fountain of incorruption unto us; and having become partakers of life: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Troparion:* To anoint Thee as dead, the myrrh-bearing women came early in the morning, and offered weeping instead of myrrh; unto whom He Who Himself said "Rejoice!" commanded them to announce joy unto the disciples: Bless all ye works!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Behold, the pure Virgin conceives in her womb, as the radiant Isaiah foretold, saying unto the unwedded one: Bear the Savior of God, Whom all creation serves with fear, crying: Bless!

*Another Canon, to the Birthgiver of God. Ode 8. Heirmos: The same.*

[p79LA] *Troparion:* As Mother, thou hast surpassed the noetic ranks, and having drawn near unto God: We bless thy childbearing, O blessed Virgin, and highly exalt it![^90]

*Troparion:* Sacred voices of the God-inspired heralds from afar glorify thee as Mother of God and Virgin, through whom being deified we sing and highly exalt the Pure One unto the age!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Thou hast revealed natural beauty most excellent, having shone upon the flesh of the Godhead, O pure Virgin; therefore we sing of thee and highly exalt the Pure One unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*

A new and God-befitting wonder: through the shut virginal gate manifestly enters the Lord, bare at His entrance, yet flesh-bearing and God in His going forth; and the gate remains shut: her unceasingly as Mother of God do we magnify!

*Troparion:* Awesome is it to behold Thee, the Creator, lifted up upon the Tree, O Savior of God, God suffering in the flesh for His servants, and lying breathless in the tomb, loosing the dead from Hades: therefore, O Christ, as Almighty do we magnify Thee!

*Troparion:* Thou hast saved our forefather from corruption of death, O Christ; Thou wast laid dead in the tomb, and hast blossomed forth life, raising the dead, and by Thy hand hast raised corruptible nature unto the light and clothed it in divine incorruption: therefore, as the Ever-living Source of light do we magnify Thee!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

O pure receptacle of Light [p80LA] God, Who was ineffably and changelessly incarnate of thee, having lifted up Adam's nature upon the Cross, became the first-born from the dead; and remaining God in the highest, together with the Father and the Divine Spirit, is glorified![^91]

*Another Canon, to the Cross. Ode 9. Heirmos: In the bush with fire...*

*Troparion:* By Thy Passion, O Savior, Thou hast granted incorruption and life unto the faithful, and by Thy life-creating rising Thou didst resurrect our earthly nature, seating it on high; therefore, praising Thee joyfully and unceasingly, do we magnify Thee!

*Troparion:* Great and awesome is the King in radiance, and our Savior Christ over all the earth; for He Himself has shone forth as unwaning light, giving divine dominion and sure victory unto them that bow down before Him!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

Though bound in body and afflicted, I pray thee, loose me by thy kindness, not remembering my cruel, countless evils, O Virgin, that protected by thy prayers I may not perish!

*Another Canon, to the Birthgiver of God. Ode 9. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Satiety of thy praises, O Virgin, never overtakes the pious faithful; for they ever receive the desired divine and spiritual love, magnifying thee as the Mother of God!

*Troparion:* Thou hast given us as unashamed intercessor her that bore Thee, O Christ: through her prayers Thou grantest mercy unto us, the Spirit, the Giver of grace, Who proceeds from the Father through Thee.

[p81LA] *Troparion:* Finding thee fair in soul, fair in thoughts, and fair in body, the Fair One was incarnate from thy virginal flanks, as He knew, adorning that which was formless, O Virgin: Whom pray thou to save us![^92]

*Then the Exapostilarion: Evangelical Resurrection [Exapostilarion].*

*At the Praises, Resurrection Stichera, Tone 3:*

*Sticheron:* Come, all ye nations, understand the power of the awesome mystery: for Christ our Savior, Who was in the beginning the Word, was crucified for us, and was willingly buried, and is risen from the dead, that He might save all things: let us bow down before Him!

*Sticheron:* Thy guards declared all the wonders, O Lord, but the assembly of vanity filled their right hands with gold, thinking to conceal Thy Resurrection, which all the world glorifies: have mercy on us!

*Sticheron:* All things were filled with joy when they received proof of the Resurrection: Mary Magdalene came unto the tomb and found an angel sitting upon the stone, shining in his raiment, and saying: Why seek ye the Living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen, as He said, going before you into Galilee!

*Sticheron:* In Thy light, O Master, shall we see light, O Lover of mankind; Who art risen from the dead, granting salvation unto the human race, that all creation may hymn Thee, the only Sinless One: have mercy on us!

*Other Stichera, Anatolian:*

*Sticheron:* The myrrh-bearing women brought morning song with tears, O Lord: having sweet spices for a fragrance, unto Thy tomb [p82LA] they came, seeking eagerly to anoint Thy most pure body. But the angel sitting upon the stone proclaimed good tidings unto them: Why seek ye the Living among the dead? For having trampled down death, He is risen as God, granting great mercy unto all the faithful![^93]

*Sticheron:* Having come unto Thy tomb on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene, not finding Thy body, cried out weeping with lamentation: Woe is me, my Savior! How has the King of all been stolen? But the pair of light-bearing angels within the tomb cried out: Why weepest thou, O woman? I weep, she said, because they have taken away my Lord from the tomb, and I know not where they have laid Him. And turning back, when she saw Thee, she straightway cried out: My Lord and my God, glory to Thee!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord, help us.*

*Sticheron:* Finding Peter hiding, Mary Magdalene cried out: Why have ye forsaken the One Christ Who was straightway with you? Ye took no pity on Him as a friend, nor did ye remember Him as Master: shall the sting of death assemble you? Nay, come, behold the empty tomb of Him Who sits in the most pure bosom of the Father, and bow down before Him, Who has great mercy!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron:* The angel, shining upon Thy life-giving tomb, said unto the myrrh-bearing women: The Deliverer has emptied the tombs and overthrown Hades, and is risen on the third day, as the only Mighty and Almighty One!

*Glory... The Evangelical Sticheron.*

[p83LA] *On Sunday, at the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:* When forefather Adam rejected Thy commandment, O Christ, Thou didst drive him out of Paradise; but when the thief, O Compassionate One, confessed Thee upon the Cross, Thou didst establish him therein, crying: Remember me, O Savior, in Thy Kingdom![^94]

*Troparion:* When Thou wast crucified, O Christ, all creation was changed, seeing Thee, its Master, in human nature without earthly father; therefore with the thief we cry: Remember us, O Savior!

*Troparion:* When we had sinned, Thou didst condemn us by the curse of death, O Giver of life, our God; but having suffered in Thy body, O Sinless Lord, Thou didst bring the dead to life, who cry: Remember us in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:* Moses, having stretched out his hands upon the mountain, prefigured the Cross and vanquished Amalek; and we, the faithful, having received it as a mighty weapon against demons, faithfully cry: Remember us, O Lord!

*Troparion:* Risen from the dead on the third day, Thou hast raised us up together by Thy Resurrection, O Lord, and by Thy death Thou hast destroyed all the power of death, O Lord; therefore faithfully we cry out: Remember us!

*Troparion:* Thou didst first appear unto the myrrh-bearing women when Thou hadst risen from the dead, O Savior, crying aloud: Rejoice! And through them announcing Thine arising unto Thy friends, O Christ; therefore with faith we cry: Remember us, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:* Unto the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Divine Spirit do we the faithful bow down, unto the uncreated Trinity, and in three Persons, one Godhead forever; with the bodiless [p84LA] hosts, we faithfully glorify the Godhead in three Hypostases![^95]

*Both now... Theotokion:* Rejoice, heavenly gate, through which the incarnate Creator passed, preserving thee sealed; rejoice, light cloud, bearing the divine rain, Christ; rejoice, throne and heavenly ladder; rejoice, mountain unhewn!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 3: Great is our Lord, and great is His power.*

*Verse: Praise the Lord, for the Lord is good.*

*Epistle for the 3rd Sunday, to the Romans:*

Brethren: Glory and honor and peace unto everyone that works good, to the Jew first, and also to the Greek; for there is no respect of persons with God. For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law; for not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified, in tribulations; knowing that tribulation produces patience, and patience experience, and experience hope; and hope puts not to shame, because the love of God is shed abroad in your hearts by the Holy Spirit Who is given unto us. For when we were yet without strength, in due time Christ died for the ungodly. For scarcely for a righteous man will one die; yet perhaps for a good man someone would even dare to die. But God commends His love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us. Much more then, being now justified by His blood, we shall be saved from wrath through Him. For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of His Son, much more, being reconciled, we shall be saved in His [p85LA] life![^96]

*Alleluia: In Thee, O Lord, have I put my hope...*

*Verse: Be Thou unto me a God of defense...*

*The Gospel from Matthew:*

At that time, when Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion approached Him, entreating Him and saying: Lord, my servant lies at home paralyzed, suffering terribly. Jesus says unto him: I will come and heal him. The centurion answered and said unto Him: Lord, I am not worthy that Thou shouldest come under my roof; but only speak the word, and my servant shall be healed. For I am a man under authority, having soldiers under me; and I say to this one: Go, and he goes; and to another: Come, and he comes; and to my servant: Do this, and he does it. When Jesus heard it, He marveled, and said unto them that followed Him: Truly I say unto you, not even in Israel have I found such faith. And I say unto you that many shall come from east and west, and shall sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the Kingdom of heaven; but the sons of the Kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth. And He said unto the centurion: Go thy way; as thou hast believed, so be it done unto thee. And his servant was healed in that hour.

*Kinonikon: Praise [the Lord from the heavens]...*

## SUNDAY EVENING VESPERS (TONE 3)

*On Sunday evening, Penitential Stichera, Tone 3, without [the Stichera to] the Cross:*

*Sticheron:* Greatly sinning against Thee and grieving Thee, the good Master and merciful Nature, Who receivest them that turn from evil: make me, who foolishly transgressed, as one of Thy harlots [p86LA] I have spent my life in negligence, and have drawn near unto the end, wretch that I am; and I consider not the impending judgment; but having turned me back from my fall, O Savior, rescue me from the snares![^97]

*Sticheron:* Deliver my afflicted soul, O Lord, from the tomb and the gnashing of teeth, and from every other prepared torment, O Most Good One, that in faith I may sing of Thee, the merciful God and Lover of mankind by nature!

*Stichera to the Bodiless Hosts, the same [Tone]:*

*Sticheron:* Thou hast presented the bodiless choirs, O Lord, the wealth of Thy goodness unto all, and hast brought them from non-existence into being, who now with unceasing voices glorify Thy glory which is beyond understanding!

*Sticheron:* Great is the splendor of Thine angels, O Christ: the bodiless ones run swiftly, preserving the Church by the power that comes from Thee, O Master, and they pray unto Thee for the universe!

*Sticheron:* Beholding the beauty of Thy three-sun and unique Godhead, the hosts that serve Thee reveal unto those in the world the brilliance thereof, and having immaterially adorned themselves, they grant us divine illuminations!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* O most pure one who rulest over all creatures, my mind is terribly overwhelmed by passions: preserve me by thy fervent protection and maternal prayer, and make me a servant unto thy Son and Savior!

*At the Aposticha, Penitential [Stichera]:*

*Sticheron:* To offer a faithful hymn as incense unto Thee, O Christ, and with good spiritual songs: have mercy, and save our souls!

*Sticheron:* Save me, O Lord my God, for Thou art the salvation of all: the storm of passions troubles me, and the burden of iniquities sinks me to the deep [p87LA] give me a helping hand, and lead me up unto the light of compunction, as the only Compassionate One and Lover of mankind![^98]

*To the Martyrs:* Great is the power of Thy Cross: for it was set up in one place, yet it operates in all the world; and from fishermen it showed forth Apostles, and from the Gentiles Martyrs, that they may pray for our souls!

*Theotokion:* O Mother of God, Protectress of all who pray unto thee, in thee do we place our hope, and in thee do we boast, and in thee is all our trust: pray thou unto Him Who was born of thee for thine unprofitable servants!
