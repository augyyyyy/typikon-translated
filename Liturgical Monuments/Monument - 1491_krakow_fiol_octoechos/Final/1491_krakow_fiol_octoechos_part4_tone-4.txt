# TONE 4

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 4)

*On Saturday evening, at "Lord, I have cried," set 8 [stichera], and we sing Tone 4:*

*Sticheron:* Bowing down unceasingly before Thy life-creating Cross, O Christ God, we sing and glorify Thy third-day Resurrection; for thereby hast Thou renewed corrupted human nature, O Almighty One, and hast renewed for us the heavenly ascent, as the only Good One and Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:* Thou hast loosed the sentence of the tree of disobedience, O Savior, by being willingly nailed to the tree of the Cross; and having descended into Hades, Thou didst burst the bonds of death as God; therefore do we bow down before Thy Resurrection from the dead, joyfully crying aloud: O Almighty Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* Thou hast shattered the gates of Hades, O Lord, and by Thy death hast destroyed the kingdom of death, and hast set free the human race from corruption...

...granting life and incorruption unto the world, and great mercy![^99]

*Other Stichera, Anatolian:*

*Sticheron:* Come, ye peoples, let us sing of the Savior's third-day arising, whereby we have been delivered from the unbreakable bonds of Hades, and having received incorruption and life, we cry: O Thou Who wast crucified, and buried, and art risen, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:* Angels and men, O Savior, sing of Thy third-day Resurrection, whereby the ends of the universe have been enlightened, and we all have been delivered from the servitude of the enemy, crying and saying: O Life-Creator, Almighty Savior, save us by Thy Resurrection, O only Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron:* Thou hast smashed the gates of brass, and broken in pieces the bars of iron, O Christ God, and hast raised up fallen mankind; therefore with one accord we cry aloud: Risen from the dead, O Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* O Lord, Thy begetting of the Father was timeless and co-eternal; and Thine incarnation of the Virgin was ineffable and unutterable unto men; and Thy descent into Hades was terrible unto the devil and his angels: for having trampled down death, Thou didst rise on the third day, granting incorruption unto men and great mercy!

*Repeat 1. Glory... Both now...*

*Theotokion [Dogmatikon]:* The Prophet David, who through thee became the ancestor of God, in song cried out concerning thee unto Him that did great things for thee: At Thy right hand stood the Queen. For God, Who was well-pleased to become man of thee without father, showed thee forth as Mother, the mediatress of life, that He might fashion anew His own image, corrupted by passions; and having found the stray sheep, taking it upon His shoulders, He brings it unto the Father...

...and according to His own will unites it with the heavenly powers, and saves the world, O Mother of God, Christ Who has great and abundant mercy![^100]

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron:* Ascending the Cross, O Lord, Thou didst cleanse our ancestral curse, and descending into Hades, Thou didst set free the eternal prisoners, granting incorruption and life unto the human race; therefore singing, we glorify Thy life-creating and saving Resurrection!

*Other Alphabetical Stichera:*

*Sticheron:* Suspended upon the Tree, O only Mighty One, Thou didst shake all creation; and laid in the tomb, Thou didst raise up those dwelling in the tombs, granting incorruption and life unto the human race; therefore singing, we glorify Thy third-day arising!

*Sticheron:* A lawless people, O Christ, delivering Thee unto Pilate, condemned Thee to be crucified, showing themselves ungrateful concerning Thy benefits; but having willingly endured burial, by Thine own sovereign power Thou didst rise on the third day as God, granting us endless life and great mercy!

*Sticheron:* With tears the women reached the tomb, seeking Thee; and not finding Thee, weeping with lamentation, they cried out, saying: Woe to us, our Savior, King of all! How hast Thou been stolen away? And what place holds Thy life-bearing body? But the angel answered them, saying: Weep not, but go and preach that He is risen, granting us joy, as the only Compassionate One!

*Theotokion:* Grant comfort unto thy servants, O all-immaculate one, putting an end to our cruel adversities and delivering us from every affliction; for thee alone do we have as a firm and sure...

...foundation, and we have acquired thy protection: let us not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady, who call upon thee; hasten to the supplication of those who faithfully cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, Helper of all, Joy and Protection of our souls![^101]

*Troparion, Tone 4:* Having learned from the angel the radiant proclamation of the Resurrection, and having cast away the ancestral condemnation, the Lord's female disciples boasting said unto the Apostles: Death is overthrown! Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Twice. Glory... Both now...*

*Theotokion:* The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown even unto the angels: through thee, O Mother of God, was revealed unto those upon earth as God, incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-fashioned, He saved our souls from death!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 4)

*Canon to the Most Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, whose acrostic is: "The fourth song I offer unto Thee." Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos: The Red Sea's deep...*

*Troparion:* Let us glorify the Trinity in One Principle, forming a Unity, a Unity by nature, co-eternal and co-enthroned, entreating Whom we say: Save us who with faith honor Thee!

*Troparion:* He was anointed by the Father with the Spirit of joy, the deifying oil, as the Son, having been manifested as the three-hypostatic Principle of the one Godhead.

*Troparion:* Unable to endure gazing upon the beauty of the unapproachable glory of Thy radiance, O Three-Sun Unity, O Trinity, the seraphim veil themselves with wings, and unceasingly glorify Thee with thrice-holy hymns!

*Theotokion:* Having unutterably given birth to the Creator of all...

*Troparion:* ...giving birth, O most pure one, delivering ancient Adam from the curse; and through thee have we come to know the one God in three Hypostases.[^102]

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Not in wisdom...*

*Troparion:* Power from on high Thou hast sent unto Thy disciples of old, as Thou didst send from the Father the Comforter, O Christ: it showed forth as one Thy three-sun essence.

*Troparion:* When Thou didst appear unto Patriarch Abraham in the form of men, O Trinity in Unity, Thou didst reveal the unchanging nature of Thy goodness and dominion.

*Troparion:* O God Who art believed on as One in three forms, manifestly uncircumscribed, and incomprehensible unto all minds: deliver our souls from every affliction!

*Theotokion:* Having been directed by the all-wise guidance of Thy Son, we glorify the unique, three-radiant Godhead, and we bless thee, O Ever-Virgin!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 4.*

*Podoben: "Speedily anticipate" (Ско́рѡ предварѝ):*

By a single nod and by the most pure counsel of Thy Father and of the Spirit, Thou didst create all things, O Christ, Many-Merciful; and Thou didst accept crucifixion for the human race: therefore, giving thanks, we cry aloud unto Thee, O Merciful One: Have mercy on Thy flock, O Lover of mankind!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* All-hymned, unwedded Mother of Christ God, we glorify thy childbearing, O only merciful one, whereby we have been delivered from the deception of idols and from every calamity, O Sovereign Lady: therefore in faith we cry aloud unto thee: Have mercy on thy flock, O all-hymned one!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Sitting in...*

*Troparion:* The supersubstantial Trinity in the unity of Godhead and supreme dominion, with the seraphim...

...we glorify Thee, the indivisible Nature, unconfused, of equal power, O God of glory, Who art incomprehensible![^103]

*Troparion:* Separated ineffably in the Persons of the Godhead, yet united by the dominion of one sovereignty, uncomposed and uniquely uncircumscribed, we hymn Thee, the Creator of all creation!

*Troparion:* The Unoriginate Mind ineffably begot the Word, and the Divine Spirit proceeded with equal power; therefore do we proclaim the consubstantial Trinity, God the Master of all!

*Theotokion:* Appearing in ancient times under types, He announced beforehand the Word incarnate from thee, O Virgin; and in the last times He appeared unto men, truly revealing the one Principle in three Hypostases!

*Ode 5. Heirmos: All things were amazed...*

*Troparion:* Having understood by faith the all-operative Godhead, we sing of the one impassible essence, in three life-originating and united Hypostases: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the co-eternal Nature!

*Troparion:* O Three-Sun Light, shining with the essential radiance of Thy unique hypostatic Godhead, O Uncreated Nature, Giver of light, Source of every light-giving beam: grant that I may behold Thine ineffable beauty!

*Troparion:* As the only essential Creator, Sustainer of all, and Ruler of boundaries, Truth and Life-Giver, we pray unto Thee in faith, O Master, O Three-Sun One: preserve [us]!

*Theotokion:* Desiring to deify man who had decayed of old, [the Word] was incarnate from thee...

...in the image of divine knowledge, and becoming man, He revealed the one Principle in three Persons.[^104]

*Ode 6. Heirmos: Cried aloud...*

*Troparion:* Manifestly the Father is revealed as resting, and the Spirit seen in Christ when He was baptized; therefore do we glorify the one Principle of the Trinity!

*Troparion:* As [Isaiah] saw Thee, with thrice-holy voices we hymn Thee as sitting upon a high throne; he recognized the three-sun Hypostases of the one Godhead!

*Troparion:* Having an exalted heart, O Exalted King in three Hypostases, show us also forth as Thy servants, that we may intelligently behold the brightness of Thy glory, O Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:* Thou didst deign to fashion anew our nature, appearing from the Virgin, O Savior and God; as the Lover of mankind, Thou hast made the human race partakers of divine glory!

*Kontakion, Tone 4.*

*Podoben: "Thou Who wast lifted up upon the Cross" (Вознесы́йся на Крⷭ҇тъ во́лею):*

Incomprehensible God and unapproachable, one God by nature: O Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, cover and preserve us who cry aloud in faith in Thy holy church, night and day; and deliver us not, O Master, unto the enemies who seek our destruction!

*Ikos:* Despise us not, O all-hymned Virgin: with thanksgiving we sing of thee from our heart; make us, thy servants, worthy of thy mercies, who cry aloud and say: O Most Holy Mother of God and Virgin, anticipate and deliver us from enemies visible and invisible, and from every dire threat, for thou art our protection!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: In the fiery furnace the Persian children of...*

*Troparion:* Imitating upon earth the ranks of heaven, the intelligent powers and the radiance of glory...

...we praise Thee divinely in one single-operative Hypostasis in three Persons![^105]

*Troparion:* The pure Prophet of old spoke of Thee under types, the Creator before the ages of ages, having revealed the ineffable God with the Unoriginate One, the divine three-person Hypostases!

*Troparion:* O Word, invisible by essence, all-creative: Thou didst appear unto men, becoming man from the holy Mother of God, taking flesh unto the participation of Thy Godhead!

*Theotokion:* Thou hast appeared as the east of the noetic Sun, Who appeared bodily, and thou enlightenest all, O Mother of God, Ever-Blessed Virgin!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: The Children...*

*Troparion:* O Light, single in principle and three-radiant, Unoriginate Essence, Incomprehensible Beauty: dwell within us, and show us forth as light-bearing temples of Thy Godhead, who cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works!

*Troparion:* Indivisible Trinity and unconfused Unity: deliver me from various passions and darkness and transgressions, and illumine me with Thy beneficent rays, that I may behold Thy glory, and hymn Thee, the God of glory!

*Troparion:* Supersubstantial Mind, the Father; and Son, the co-express Word; and Spirit co-enthroned: one Essence and Power, co-eternal Nature and ineffable Majesty: O Trinity in Unity, preserve Thy flock, O Lover of mankind!

*Theotokion:* O pure one, thou containest the vessel holding the divine Manna; with luminous torches the faithful cry unto thee: Rejoice, thou who art full of grace, joy of Apostles...

...and rejoicing of Martyrs![^106]

*Ode 9. Heirmos: Let every earthborn mortal...*

*Troparion:* Now do I lift up my heart and mind, and the vows of soul and body, unto Thee, my Creator and Deliverer, O only Unoriginate and Three-Radiant One: give ear and save me, Thy servant, from all temptations and afflictions!

*Troparion:* Lift up our minds and thoughts unto Thee on high; enlighten us with most pure beams, O Father, Word, and Spirit: unapproachable, co-express Light, Sun of glory, Sovereign of light, that we may ever glorify Thee, God single in principle and in three Hypostases!

*Troparion:* Unto the Father, and the Son, and the divine Spirit do we the faithful bow down, unto the uncreated Trinity in three Persons in one Godhead ever; with the bodiless powers, we faithfully glorify the Godhead in three Hypostases!

*Theotokion:* Save me who believe in Thee, O Lord, and who proclaim the unoriginate and co-eternal single essence, and three divine and co-express Persons; and make me worthy of Thy divine glory, by the intercessions of Thy most pure Mother!

*Then "It is truly meet," and the rest as indicated before. At "God is the Lord," the Troparion; and search before for the Trinity Kathismata.*

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 4)

*After the 1st Kathisma Reading, Resurrection Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*

*Sessional Hymn:* Having looked upon the entrance of the tomb, and unable to endure the angelic flame, the myrrh-bearing women marveled with trembling, saying: Has He been stolen, Who opened Paradise unto the thief? Or is He risen, Who before His Passion preached the...

...Resurrection? Truly the Lord is risen, granting life and resurrection unto those in Hades![^107]

*Twice. Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Awesome and truly most glorious wonder: how the Virgin gave birth unto Thee, the Creator, and after childbearing remained again a Virgin! By the birth from her, strengthen the faith, and tame the nations, and grant peace unto the whole world, as the Lover of mankind!

*After the 2nd Kathisma Reading, Sessional Hymn, Tone 4:*

*Sessional Hymn:* By Thy voluntary counsel Thou didst endure crucifixion, and mortal men laid Thee in a new tomb, Who by Thy word didst establish the ends of the earth; therefore the alien enemy was bound, and death was cruelly spoiled, and all those in Hades cried aloud: Christ the Life-Giver is risen, abiding unto the ages!

*Repeat. Glory... Both now... Theotokion, Podoben: "Joseph marveled" (Удивися Иосиф):*

Joseph marveled, seeing that which was beyond nature; he considered in his mind the rain upon the fleece in thy seedless conception, O Mother of God, the bush burning with fire, Aaron's rod that budded; and bearing witness, thy betrothed and guardian cried aloud unto the priests: A Virgin brings forth, and after childbearing remains again a Virgin!

*Hypakoe, Tone 4:* Having run unto Thy glorious arising, the myrrh-bearing women announced good tidings unto the Apostles, that Christ God is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!

*Hymns of Ascents, Antiphon 1, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron:* From my youth many passions war against me; but do Thou Thyself help me and save me, O my Savior!

*Sticheron:* Ye who hate Zion, be put to shame by the Lord...

...for ye shall be withered as grass by fire![^108]

*By the Holy Spirit:* Every soul is quickened, and by purity exalted, shining mystically in the Trinitarian Unity.

*Antiphon 2:*

*Sticheron:* I have cried fervently unto Thee, O Lord, from the depth of my soul; let Thine ears be attentive unto my divine supplication!

*Sticheron:* Whosoever has acquired hope in the Lord is higher than all who cause affliction.

*By the Holy Spirit:* Streams of grace gush forth, watering all creation unto revitalization.

*Antiphon 3:*

*Sticheron:* Let my heart be lifted up unto Thee, O Word, and let nothing of the world's beauties entice me unto weakness!

*Sticheron:* As one has love toward his mother, even more fervently are we bound in loving affection toward God.

*By the Holy Spirit:* The riches of divine theology, contemplation of God, and wisdom: for therein does the Word reveal all the Father's decrees.

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4: Arise, O Lord, help us.*

*Verse: Which we have heard with our ears...*

*Let every breath [praise the Lord]... The Evangelical Resurrection [Gospel]. Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ... Psalm 50: Have mercy on me, O God...*

*Resurrection Canon, Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*

Having traversed the deep of the Red Sea with dry feet, ancient Israel of old vanquished the might of Amalek in the wilderness through the hands of Moses stretched out in the form of a cross.

*Troparion:* Thou hast raised up our fallen state, correcting upon the most pure wood of the Cross the ancient all-destructive ruin, O Master, healing it as Good and Almighty!

...Almighty![^109]

*Troparion:* Bodily in the tomb, but in Hades with Thy soul as God, in Paradise with the thief, and on the throne wast Thou, O Christ, with the Father and the Spirit, filling all things, Thyself uncircumscribed!

*Theotokion:* Without seed by the Holy Spirit, by the Father's will, thou didst conceive the Son of God, and in the flesh didst give birth unto Him Who from the Father is without mother, and for our sake from thee without father!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: I shall open my mouth...*

*Troparion:* Healing the ruin of mankind, renewed by divine Blood, and crushing by might him that had crushed Thy creation of old...

*Troparion:* The resurrection of the dead was wrought by Thy slaying, and strength was given over unto mortification, submitting with eternal life, as we bow down before God incarnate!

*Theotokion:* Higher in beauty than the heavenly hosts, the living temple of God was thine, O Virgin, who didst bear in thy womb our God, the most pure mountain!

*Another Canon to the Most Holy Mother of God. Heirmos: Three staths...*

*Troparion:* The peoples trembled, the nations were troubled, and mighty kingdoms bowed down, O holy one, from the fear of thy childbearing; God the King came, overthrowing the tyrant, and delivered the world from corruption!

*Troparion:* He that dwells on high made thee a habitation for Himself in His descent unto mankind, O Christ, and showed thee unshaken; for alone after childbearing did she preserve virginity, keeping the birth of the Creator!

*Troparion:* Written in the unwritten Father, on the tree of old, and in the Mother's bosom Christ is bodily enrolled; Who after childbearing is preserved as truly Virgin beyond understanding...

...unto Whom let us sing aloud, unto our God, for He has been glorified![^110]

*Ode 3. Heirmos: Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying: Thou art my strength, O Lord, my refuge and my firm foundation!*

*Troparion:* The Tree of life, the noetic true Vine, hangs upon the Cross, gushing forth incorruption unto all!

*Troparion:* How great, how awesome is He Who destroyed the boasting of Hades! As incorrupt God, He is now risen bodily from the tomb!

*Theotokion:* Thou alone on earth wast shown forth as the mediatress of supernatural blessings, O Mother of God: therefore with joy do we cry unto thee!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: Thy singers...*

*Troparion:* Filled with serpentine venom, the jaws struck against Thee, O Savior; and the Creator and Master of all with nails crushed his hand, for there is none holy besides Thee, O Lord!

*Troparion:* Thou didst willingly appear, O Lover of mankind; laid in the tomb, O Life-Giver, Thou didst open the gates of Hades unto eternal souls, for there is none holy besides Thee, O Lord!

*Theotokion:* Thou wast shown forth as the untilled field, giving birth to the living Ear of wheat, becoming the mediatress of immortality unto all that partake of the Holy One Who holily rests in the holy place!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Descending from on high...*

*Troparion:* The earthly nature is cleansed, having conversed with the unbearable divine Fire, as in resurrection, O most pure Virgin, unto Him Who preserved unharmed from thee the unbaked Bread!

*Troparion:* Truly drawing near unto God, as higher than all the angelic ranks, alone in the beauty of virginity, as Mother of the Ruler of all...

...Almighty![^111]

*Troparion:* Marveled at beyond mind, they cannot comprehend thy childbearing, O Virgin; for in thy womb, O pure one, thou didst conceive the Word, the Fore-First, Who established every word!

*Ode 4. Heirmos: Beholding Thee, the Sun of righteousness, lifted up...*

*Troparion:* Thou didst come healing by Thy Passion; on the Cross Thou dost suffer in Thy most pure flesh, in which Thou wast willingly clothed: therefore we cry aloud unto Thee: Glory to Thy power!

*Troparion:* Leaving deadened death in the tomb, my Deliverer, bound today, is risen: unto Him let us cry aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Theotokion:* Unwedded didst thou give birth, O Virgin, and after childbearing wast preserved; therefore unceasingly with joy, O Sovereign Lady, in uncondemned faith we cry unto thee!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: Unto the untraceable divine counsel...*

*Troparion:* Israel that received the law understood Thee not, God Who gavest the law; transgressing the law, they nailed Thee to the Cross as lawless, unworthy of the Lawgiver!

*Troparion:* Thy divine soul, having despoiled the dwellings of Hades, raised up together the dead of ages, while Thy life-bearing body became the source of incorruption unto all!

*Theotokion:* Thee, Ever-Virgin, truly the only Mother of God do we glorify, whom the seer Moses beheld prefigured, the unburnt bush that cleaved unto the fire, O most pure one!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Sitting in [glory]...*

*Troparion:* He lived with men, the Invisible becoming visible, in the likeness of the Son...

...comprehended Godhead, and being depicted from thee, O Virgin, alien unto bitterness to those who know thee as the true Mother of God.[^112]

*Troparion:* Immaterially did the Virgin receive the Immaterial One, become an Infant from her amidst material things; therefore in two natures is God recognized as flesh-bearer, and above earthly nature.

*Troparion:* A Virgin after childbearing: He Who dwelt in thee, O Virgin, and was born of thee without seed, the Word, yet remained with the Father; and in childbearing He preserved thee a Virgin, as Master and Creator.

*Resurrection Canon, Tone 4. Ode 5. Heirmos:*

Thou, O Lord, art my light, come into the world, a holy Light turning from the darkness of ignorance those who sing praise unto Thee in faith!

*Troparion:* Thou, O Lord, didst mercifully come down unto earth; Thou didst raise up fallen human nature, being lifted up upon the Tree!

*Troparion:* Thou hast taken from me, O Christ, the condemnation of sins; Thou hast destroyed the pangs of death, O Bountiful One, by Thy divine Resurrection!

*Theotokion:* Thee as an invincible weapon against enemies do we set forth; thee as the fortress and hope of our salvation do we possess, O Bride of God!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: All things were astonished...*

*Troparion:* Senseless Hades received Thee wholly into his mouth, seeing Thee nailed upon the Tree and pierced with a spear, thinking Thee a mere man without living soul; but his teeth were crushed by Thy divine might! He beheld the temple of Thy body destroyed, yet the tomb corrupted [Thee] not, being laid therein by Thine own will; he took both into his grasp, soul and...

...body, while the other sent them down into death.[^113]

*Theotokion:* Fulfilled and brought to an end were the prophetic sayings: for thou, O Virgin, without knowing a man's bed, didst receive in thy womb God Who is over all, and didst give birth unto the ageless Son, granting peace unto all that sing praise unto thee!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: Arisen...*

*Troparion:* As a house of glory, and the holy mountain of God, a Bride, a bridal chamber, a temple of sanctification, God the Savior lived in thee, making us upright and all-immaculate.

*Troparion:* Thou didst accept human covering, O Christ, without seed, most purely, inconfusedly, endued with a rational and noetic soul, by active will possessing sovereign mastery.

*Troparion:* The virginal womb put to shame the tyrant's mind; for as an Infant He touched with His hand the asp's soul-destroying den, and cast down the proud apostate, subduing him under the feet of our faithful Prince!

*Ode 6. Heirmos: I shall sacrifice unto Thee with a voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries unto Thee, having been cleansed from the blood of demons by the blood that mercifully flowed from Thy side!*

*Troparion:* Thou didst ascend upon the Cross, having girded Thyself with power, grappling with the tyrant; casting him down from the heights, Thou didst raise Adam up together with Thee by Thine invincible might!

*Troparion:* Thou didst rise most radiantly from the tomb, O Christ; Thou didst scatter all enemies by divine might, and filled all peoples with joy!

*Theotokion:* Than which of all ancient...

...marvels more marvelous, that as a Virgin in thy womb thou didst unwedded receive the Word Who holds all things together, containing Him without constraint![^114]

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: I shall sacrifice...*

*Troparion:* The senseless one opened his throat and swallowed me, enlarging his jaws; but having descended, O Christ, Thou didst bring up my life, O Lover of mankind!

*Troparion:* Death was vanquished by death, overthrown it fell, and incorruption was granted unto us; unto the women He proclaimed the joy of immortality!

*Theotokion:* A dwelling of the unapproachable Light of the Godhead wast thou shown forth, O pure Maiden, which the heavenly ranks cannot behold without trembling!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Of old the deceitful serpent took me captive and put me to death through my foremother Eve; but now He that fashioned me, for thy sake hath recalled me from corruption!

*Troparion:* The abyss of ineffable mercies showed thee forth as chosen, O compassionate Maiden; through the marvels wrought in thee the divine Pearl shone forth!

*Theotokion:* O all-praised, the thought of the Apostles and praise of martyrs, the fair and marvelous proclamation of prophets: together with them pray that we be delivered from every affliction!

*Ode 7. Heirmos: In the furnace of fire the Abrahamite children of old, burned rather with longing for piety than with the flame, cried aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!*

*Troparion:* Unto incorruption was mankind led up by the divine shed Blood, singing with thanksgiving: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy...

...glory, O Lord![^115]

*Troparion:* As life-bearing, as more splendid than Paradise truly, and radiant above any royal bridal chamber was Thy tomb shown forth, O Christ, the fountain of our resurrection!

*Theotokion:* Rejoice, consecrated divine dwelling of the Most High! For through thee was joy given unto those that cry unto thee, O Mother of God: Blessed art thou among women, O all-immaculate Sovereign Lady!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: They served not the creature...*

*Troparion:* Humbled upon the Tree, O Christ, pouring out Thy blood upon the earth, Thou didst cast down the adversary: O all-praised God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Troparion:* By Thy might raise up the horn of us who serve Thee, O Christ, Who art risen from the dead, granting rich and divine wealth: O all-praised God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Trinity Troparion:* Following the divine word, we glorify one Godhead in three Persons indivisible, in one flame: O all-praised God of our fathers, blessed art Thou!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: ...*

*Troparion:* Virginal love draws me unto singing, the fire within my heart, to sing unto the Virgin Mother: Blessed art thou...

*Troparion:* One Lordship do I honor in three Persons indivisible, singing the three-hypostatic essence: Blessed art Thou, O Lord God of our fathers!

*Theotokion:* Thou wast shown forth as the orient of the noetic Sun Who appeared bodily, enlightening all, O Mother of God, Sovereign Lady ever-blessed!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: Daniel, stretching out his hands, stopped the yawning mouths of lions in the pit; and the children, lovers of piety, having girded themselves with virtue, quenched the power of fire...*

...crying aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, praise the Lord![^116]

*Troparion:* Stretching out Thy hands upon the Cross, Thou didst gather all nations unto Thyself, and showed forth one Church, O Master, singing praise unto Thee, earthly and heavenly together harmonious singing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, praise the Lord!

*Troparion:* In white guise appeared the Angel of the Resurrection unto the women, flashing with unapproachable light: Why cry ye at the tomb seeking the Living as dead? Behold, Christ is risen, unto Whom we sing: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Theotokion:* Thou alone among all generations wast shown forth, O Virgin, all-pure Mother; thou wast the habitation of the Godhead, O all-immaculate, unconsumed by the fire of the divine Light: therefore do all of us bless thee, O Mary, Bride of God!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: The Children...*

*Troparion:* Beholding the unjust slaying, creation wept in terror, the sun was clothed in darkness; and we unceasingly cry aloud unto Thee: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, praise the Lord!

*Troparion:* Having descended in might unto Hades, Thou didst make paths of resurrection unto all; Thou didst ascend carrying Adam upon Thy shoulders and brought him unto God: therefore we cry aloud: Praise the Lord!

*Trinity Troparion:* Knowing one God... we proclaim Thee the Word of the unoriginate Father... the Holy Spirit as Witness, worshiping the Trinity together: Bless the Lord!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* He that fashioned thee from the rib of Adam is incarnate of thy virginity, singing unto Whom...

...we cry: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord, praise the Lord![^117]

*Troparion:* All the ends of the earth beheld the mystery in thee, O Virgin, singing of Thine incarnate salvation: Bless the Lord!

*Troparion:* The Children equal in number, O Savior... the virginal company trampled the flame, the youths crying: Bless the Lord!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: A cornerstone was cut out from an unhewn mountain, from thee, O Virgin, even Christ, Who hath joined together the separated natures: therefore rejoicing do we magnify thee, O Mother of God!*

*Troparion:* Restoring me wholly, O Savior, having endured crucifixion in the flesh, O Christ, only Merciful One!

*Troparion:* Thy disciples, beholding Thy tomb opened, and the God-bearing winding sheets emptied by Thy resurrection, spoke with the Angels: Truly Christ is risen!

*Trinity Troparion:* The unity of the divine essence in three Hypostases: the unoriginate Father, the Word, and the divine Spirit do we praise, the incarnate Word, one God in two natures!

*Another Canon to the Cross. Heirmos: As an animate ark...*

*Troparion:* Thou didst ascend, O Christ, upon the Tree of the Cross, and didst call all nations, willing to save the corrupt nature of Adam; therefore with thanksgiving we cry unto Thee: Glory to Thy Crucifixion, O Lord!

*Troparion:* Early the Myrrh-bearing women came unto Thy tomb with haste, weeping to anoint Thee, O Christ; and He appeared risen from...

...the dead, granting this joy unto all that glorify Thy Resurrection![^118]

*Theotokion:* Through thee, O pure Mother of God, have we known the God of all, and we have become sons of the Most High, ever crying unto thee: Rejoice, thou that art full of grace, the Lord is with thee!

*Another Canon to the Mother of God. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Passing as a lily clothed in purity, O most pure one, by the Holy Spirit, shining in the midst of thorns and filling with fragrance those who sincerely magnify thee!

*Troparion:* Having assumed corrupt earthly nature from thine all-immaculate womb, the Incorrupt One showed it forth in Himself as incorrupt in His tender mercy: therefore do we magnify thee as the Mother of God!

*Theotokion:* O thou that rulest over all creatures, grant unto thy people victorious vanquishing, putting the unsubmissive under the feet of our Prince, that we may magnify thee as the Mother of God!

*Praises Stichera, Tone 4:*

Thou Who didst endure crucifixion and death, O Savior, and didst rise from the dead, O Almighty Lord: Glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* By Thy Cross, O Christ our Savior, deliver us from the snare of the enemy; and risen from the dead, raise us up who have fallen from sins, stretching forth Thy hand, O Lord Lover of mankind, by the intercessions of Thy saints!

*Sticheron:* By Thy Cross, O Christ, we have been set free from the ancient curse; and by Thy death Thou didst bring to naught the devil who tormented our nature, and by Thy Resurrection...

...Thou didst fill all things with joy: therefore do we cry unto Thee: O Thou Who art risen from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee![^119]

*Sticheron:* Thou didst accept death in the flesh, and didst grant us immortality, O Savior; and Thou didst dwell in the tomb that Thou mightest free us from Hades, raising us up together with Thyself: Thou didst suffer indeed as man, yet didst rise as God: therefore do we cry unto Thee: Glory to Thee, O Life-Giver, Lord, only Lover of mankind!

*Other Stichera of the Resurrection:*

*Sticheron:* The rocks were split, O Savior, when Thy Cross was set up at the Place of the Skull; the gatekeepers of Hades were terrified when Thou wast laid in the tomb as dead; for having abolished the power of death, Thou didst grant incorruption unto the dead by Thy Resurrection, O Life-Giver, Savior, Lord: Glory to Thee!

*Sticheron:* Rejoice today, all creatures! Rejoice, for Christ is risen, having shattered the brazen gates and broken the iron bars, and brought Adam forth from the gates of death, and freed mankind from corruption! Where is thy victory, O Hades? Let us the faithful cry aloud: Where is thy sting, O death? Thou hast destroyed the arrogance of Hades, O Master; for as Ruler and Lord of all, Thou didst expose the devil by the Cross, and didst grant resurrection unto the dead, and didst make known ineffable life in the Gospel, being the only Good and Lover of mankind!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

*Sticheron:* Where is Jesus Whom ye kept watching? Tell us, O Judeans: Where is He Whom ye laid in the tomb and sealed the stone? Give up the dead Man, Whom ye deprived of life! Give up the Buried One, or believe in Him Who is risen! For even if ye keep silence...

...the stone rolled away from the tomb shall cry aloud of the Lord's Resurrection! Great is Thy mercy, great is the mystery of Thy dispensation, O our Savior: Glory to Thee![^120]

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron:* Where are the seals of the tomb? Where are Pilate's soldiers guarding the sepulchre? O how was the treasure stolen? How was the Buried One moved? Slander not the Resurrection of Christ! The Lord was willingly crucified, and He is risen even as He willed, granting life and great mercy unto the inhabited world!

*Glory: Evangelical Sticheron. Both now: Most blessed art thou, O Virgin Mother of God...*

*At the Liturgy. The Beatitudes, Tone 4:*

*Troparion:* On account of the tree was Adam banished from Paradise; but through the tree of the Cross did Christ establish the thief in Paradise: for the one, having tasted, transgressed the commandment of the Creator; but the other, being crucified, confessed Thee as God, crying: Remember us, O Savior, in Thy Kingdom!

*Troparion:* Living in Paradise, Adam transgressed; but the thief, beholding Thee nailed upon the Cross, O Master, confessed Thee as God, seeking the heavenly Kingdom, and with these words cried unto Thee, O Lord Lover of mankind, our God: Remember us!

*Troparion:* Ascending upon the Cross, Thou didst destroy the power of death and wiped out the handwriting of old against us, O Master; as Thou didst accept the repentance of the thief, grant it also unto us, O Lover of mankind, who in faith cry unto Thee, O Lord, merciful Christ: Remember us!

*Troparion:* Ascending upon the Cross, Thou didst destroy the dominion of death; laid in the tomb...

...Thou didst raise up all those in Hades, becoming the Firstborn from the dead, and eternal life was bestowed: therefore by Thy Resurrection save us, O our Deliverer: Remember us![^121]

*Troparion:* Thy disciples, O Christ, having been eyewitnesses of Thy sufferings and burial and divine resurrection, caught the nations and as fishes brought all in faith: with them we cry unto Thee: Remember us!

*Trinity Troparion:* The Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit do we all with one mind glorify, worthily praying unto the Unity of the Godhead in three Persons, abiding unconfusedly, simple, indivisible, and unapproachable; worshiping Him we sing, being delivered from torment: Remember us!

*Theotokion:* Thy Mother, O Christ, who in the flesh bore Thee without seed, and remained in truth an incorrupt Virgin: her do we bring unto Thee in prayer, O Master; grant forgiveness of sins as Merciful unto those that cry unto Thee: Remember us, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom!

*Prokeimenon, Tone 4: How magnified are Thy works, O Lord! In wisdom hast Thou made them all (LXX Ps. 103:24).*

*Verse: Bless the Lord, O my soul: O Lord my God, Thou art very great (LXX Ps. 103:1).*

*Epistle: To the Hebrews [2 Cor. 6:16–18]:*

Brethren, ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said: I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be My people. Therefore come out from among them, and be ye cleansed, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you, and will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be My sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty...

...having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.[^122]

*Alleluia, Tone 4. Kinonikon: Praise... Gospel according to Mark [Mk. 8:34–9:1]:*

The Lord said: Whosoever will come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow Me. For whosoever will save his life shall lose it; but whosoever shall lose his life for My sake and the Gospel's, the same shall save it. For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul? Or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul? Whosoever therefore shall be ashamed of Me and of My words in this adulterous and sinful generation, of him also shall the Son of Man be ashamed, when He comes in the glory of His Father with the holy angels. And He said unto them: Truly, truly, I say unto you, that there be some of them that stand here, who shall not taste of death, till they have seen the kingdom of God come with power.

*Kinonikon: Praise the Lord from the heavens... (LXX Ps. 148:1).*

## SUNDAY EVENING VESPERS (TONE 4)

*On Sunday evening, Stichera of Repentance, Tone 4. Podoben: "Give us..." (Даждь нам):*

*Troparion:* I have sinned, O Lover of mankind, according to nature as man; grant me forgiveness, as Thou art beyond man and above nature: for having fallen beneath the laws of nature in mind, Thou didst accept to become man for our sake; possessing supreme love for mankind, have mercy on him that turns unto Thee!

*Troparion:* Thou hast set...

...forth, O Pre-good Savior Christ, repentance for sinners and for the unrighteous: the parable of Judas, the thief and the prodigal, Manasseh and the harlot, the persecutor and the tax collector, and the publican; so that it is not easy to despair, beholding Thy pre-good love for mankind, unto those who turn: and I become of good hope, that Thou mayest come and have mercy![^123]

*Troparion:* Grant me compunction, and estrangement from evil, and perfect amendment, who am now sunk in bodily passions and far removed from Thee, O God, King of all, having no hope: save me, the prodigal and lost one, for the sake of Thy mercy, for Thou art Almighty God, and the Savior of our souls!

*Tone 4, Podoben: "As noble among the martyrs" (Ꙗко добля въ мученицѣхъ):*

*Troparion:* Receptive habitations of divine brightness and all-radiant vessels hast Thou made the angelic hosts and divine compositions, beholders and dwellers of Thy glory, O Immortal One, having established them in strength to accomplish Thy word and fulfilling Thine all-powerful and all-good desire.

*Troparion:* Willing to reveal the abyss of goodness, O Good and Unoriginate One, Thou didst first create by Thine almighty intellectual nod the angelic choirs, powers, and ranks; for truly it was fitting for the Good to pour forth and proceed, so that many might be recipients of Thy goodness, O Master.

*Troparion:* Six-winged Seraphim, many-eyed Cherubim, most exalted...

...Thrones surround Thee, proclaiming in their midst the glory of Thine all-effecting strength: Dominions, Principalities, and Powers, Archangels and Angels, divine Powers, praising Thy glory, O Almighty, and praying unto Thee for us.[^124]

*Theotokion:* Transcending the angelic ranks, O all-immaculate one, ever pray together with the Angels unto Him Who rules over all creation, that He grant us remission of transgressions, and deliverance from passions, and make us then spiritually singers of His glory and heirs of incorruptible delight.

*At the Aposticha, Stichera of Repentance, Tone 4:*

*Sticheron:* I desired to wash away the handwriting of my transgressions with tears, O Lord, and for the rest of my life to please Thee through repentance; but the enemy deceives me and wars against my soul: O Lord, before I perish utterly, save me!

*Sticheron:* Who that is tempest-tossed and flees unto this haven is not saved? Or who that is sick and falls down before this medicine is not healed, O Creator of all and Physician of the ailing? O Lord, before I perish utterly, save me!

*Sticheron:* Glorifying and crying aloud of Thy passions, O Christ God, and moved to compunction by them: send down upon us Thy great mercy!

*Theotokion:* Rejoice, cloud of light! Rejoice, golden lampstand! Rejoice, vessel that contained the manna! Rejoice, rod of Aaron! Rejoice, unburnt bush! Rejoice, bridal chamber! Rejoice, throne! Rejoice, holy mountain! Rejoice, refuge! Rejoice, divine table! Rejoice, mystical door! Rejoice, joy of all!

Leaf p113 concludes the Stichera to the Bodiless Powers with their Theotokion (*Прѣвышши чины ангельския, всенепорочная...*), presents the Aposticha Penitential Stichera in Tone 4 (*Хотѣх слезами омыти моих прегрѣшений, Господи, рукописание...*, *Кто обуреваем притекает к пристанищу сему и не спасается...*, *Прославляя вопия страсти Твоя, Христе Боже...*), and ends with the Marian Salutations Theotokion (*Радуйся, свѣта облаче; радуйся, свѣщниче златый; радуйся, ручко в нейже манна...*).
