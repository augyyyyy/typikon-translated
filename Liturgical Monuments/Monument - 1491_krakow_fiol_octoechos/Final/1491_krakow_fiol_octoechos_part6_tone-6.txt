# TONE 6

## SATURDAY EVENING: VESPERS (TONE 6)

*On Saturday Evening, at "Lord, I have cried," set 8 stichoi, and sing the Resurrection Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Having the victory over Hades, O Christ, Thou didst ascend upon the Cross, that Thou mightest raise with Thyself those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death: free among the dead, and pouring forth life from Thine own light: O Almighty Savior, have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 2:* Today Christ, having trampled down death, is risen even as He said, and has granted rejoicing unto all the world, that all, crying aloud, may send up a hymn: O Fountain of life, Unapproachable Light, Almighty Savior, have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 3:* Whither shall we sinners flee from Thee, O Lord, Who art throughout all creation? In heaven, there Thou Thyself dwellest; in Hades, Thou didst trample down death; into the depths of the sea...

...there also is Thy hand, O Master! Unto Thee do we flee, and falling down before Thee, we pray, O Thou Who didst rise from the dead: O Savior, have mercy on us![^151]

*Anatolian Stichera:*

*Sticheron 4:* In Thy Cross, O Christ, do we boast, and Thy Resurrection we hymn and glorify; for Thou art our God, for beside Thee we know no other!

*Sticheron 5:* Glory to Thy power, O Lord, for Thou didst destroy the dominion of him that held the power of death; Thou hast renewed us by Thy Cross, and granted unto us life and incorruption!

*Sticheron 6:* Truly blessing the Lord, we sing of His Resurrection; for He endured crucifixion, and by death He destroyed death!

*Sticheron 7:* Thy burial, O Christ, having shattered the bonds of Hades, loosed them; and Thy Resurrection from the dead enlightened the whole world: O Lover of mankind, glory to Thee!

*At the Entrance, Dogmatikon Theotokion, Tone 6:* Who blesses thee not, O most holy Virgin? Or who sings not of thy pure childbirth? For the timeless only-begotten Son, Who shone forth from the Father, the Same came forth from thee, O pure one, having ineffably become incarnate: being God by nature, and having become man by nature for our sake; not divided into two Persons, but known in two natures without confusion: Him entreat, O pure and most blessed one, that our souls may have mercy!

*At the Aposticha, Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Thy Resurrection, O Christ Savior, the Angels hymn in the heavens: make us also worthy upon earth with pure heart to hymn and glorify Thee!

*Verse: The Lord is King, He is robed in majesty.*

*Sticheron 2:* The gates of Hades Thou didst shatter, O Lord, and the bars of Hades...

...having shattered [them] as God all-powerful, the human race that was fallen[^152] Thou hast raised up by Thy Resurrection. Therefore we, singing, cry aloud: O Thou Who didst rise from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!

*Sticheron 3:* Desiring to correct the corruption of ancient ruin, Christ was nailed upon the Cross and laid in the tomb. Him the myrrh-bearing women sought with tears, and weeping they said: "Woe to us, O Savior, King of all! How didst Thou deign to dwell in a tomb? And having dwelt there of Thine Own will, how wast Thou stolen away? What manner of place hid Thy life-bearing body? Yea, appear, even as Thou didst promise us, appear unto us, and stay our tearful weeping!" But while they wept, an Angel cried aloud unto them: "Cease from weeping! Go, tell the Apostles that Christ is risen, granting resurrection and great mercy unto the world!"

*Theotokion:* My Creator and Deliverer, O pure one, Christ the Lord, coming forth from thy womb, having clothed Himself in me, freed Adam from the ancient curse. Therefore unto thee, O most pure one, as truly the Mother of God and a Virgin, we cry aloud unceasingly: Rejoice, as the Angel [said]! Rejoice, O most pure Protectress, and Shelter, and Salvation of our souls!

*Troparion, Tone 6:* The angelic hosts were upon Thy tomb, and the guards became as dead; and Mary stood at the tomb, seeking Thy most pure Body. Thou didst overthrow Hades, not being tempted of it; Thou didst meet the Virgin, granting life. O Thou Who didst rise from the dead, Lord, glory to Thee! *(Twice)*

*Glory... Both now...*

*Theotokion:* O Thou Who didst call Thy Mother blessed, Thou camest to Thy Passion by Thine Own will, ascending upon the Cross and shining forth upon the Cross, desiring to seek out Adam, saying unto the Angels: "Rejoice together with Me, for the lost drachma is found!" O Thou Who hast ordered all things in wisdom, Lord, glory to Thee!

## SUNDAY MIDNIGHT OFFICE (TONE 6)

*Canon to the Holy and Life-Originating Trinity, Tone 6. Ode 1. Heirmos:* As Israel walked on dry land...

*Troparion:* Let us hymn the thrice-hypostatic Godhead of the unified Deity, the unchangeable Image: O good God, Lover of mankind,[^153] grant unto us cleansing of our transgressions!

*Troparion:* O supersubstantial One, both Single and Thrice-radiant, being in unity of form: enlighten us who are unworthy [to partake] of Thy divine illumination![^154]

*Troparion:* Paul, who adorned the Church of the Gentiles as a bride, taught [her] to worship Thee, the one Thrice-hypostatic God, from Whom and through Whom and in Whom all things came to be.

*Theotokion:* From thy womb came forth the Noetic Sun, O Mother of God, and shone upon us the thrice-radiant ray of the Godhead; singing of Whom, we piously call thee blessed.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:* There is none holy like Thee, O Lord my God...

*Troparion:* O Thrice-radiant One, having adorned the heavenly ranks, Thou hast ordained that they sing to Thee with thrice-holy voices: together with them receive us also, as we sing of Thy goodness!

*Troparion:* Hymning the one immutable, equal Triune Unity, O Principle of Godhead, we fervently entreat Thee: send down upon us forgiveness of our many transgressions!

*Troparion:* Timeless Mind, O Father; co-equal divine Word; and Spirit divine, good, and right: as in faith we hymn Thy dominion, preserve us, as Thou art merciful!

*Theotokion:* Having [delivered our] pasture from corruption, and having become man by nature, O my God, Thou didst dwell within the womb of the most pure one, and didst deliver [us] from the ancient first condemnation of our forefather.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6.*

*Podoben: "The Angelic Powers" (А҆́гг҃льскія си́лы):* [^155]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* O pure Virgin, look down from heaven and behold the grievous wounds of our passions, and have compassion, O most pure one, and heal [our] hidden infirmity, distilling thy mercy upon thy servants: if thou art with us, none shall be against us!

*Ode 4. Heirmos:* Christ is my power...

*Troparion:* Elevating our thoughts, O Thrice-radiant Unity, quickly lift up the soul and heart of Thy singers, and make them worthy of Thine illumination and brightness!

*Troparion:* Change and transform [us] from the evil of the passions unto virtue, O one immutable and unchangeable Trinity, enlightening us with Thy rays!

*Troparion:* In Thy pre-eternal counsel Thou didst establish the angelic ranks as ministers of Thy goodness, O Thrice-hypostatic Unity: together with them receive also our praise!

*Theotokion:* The uncreated divine Nature, co-eternal, having assumed created human nature...

...in thy womb, O Mother of God, Ever-Virgin!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:* By Thy divine light, O Good One...

*Troparion:* Having recognized the Principle of Godhead to be by nature providential and saving for all, O Master, Thrice-radiant yet One: unto Thee do we rise early, asking forgiveness of transgressions!

*Troparion:* O Father, with the unoriginate Son, and co-eternal Son, [and] Holy Spirit: establish, O Monarchical Trinity, Thy singers [free] from every temptation and affliction!

*Troparion:* Ordering Thy beneficent providence, guiding us ever unto the pleasing of Thy Thrice-hypostatic Godhead, make us partakers of the heavenly Kingdom!

*Theotokion:* O unchangeable Word of God, Who upholdest and preservest all things by Thine almighty hand: preserve and protect us who magnify Thee, by the prayers of the Mother of God who bore Thee!

*Ode 6. Heirmos:* Beholding the sea of life...

*Troparion:* Grant wisdom and understanding unto Thy singers, O Thrice-radiant Principle of Godhead, and vouchsafe that the rays of the beauty and light-giving brightness of Thy goodness may illumine us all!

*Troparion:* O indivisible Light, thrice-radiant by nature, almighty and unapproachable: illumine the hearts of those who faithfully sing of Thy dominion, and give them wings unto divine love!

*Troparion:* One Light in three Persons, in Father, Son, and Holy Spirit: Three indivisibly, praised as three-in-one, and known as worshipped as One!

*Theotokion:* He Who dwelt within thee, O Ever-Virgin, as the Almighty...

...taught men to worship [the one Thrice-radiant Godhead]!

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 6.*

*Podoben: "Fulfilling the dispensation for our sake" (Е҆́же ѡ҆ на́съ):*

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* O Virgin, look upon us, O all-hymned Mother of God; enlighten with light our darkened hearts, preserve thy flock, O most pure one; for as much as thou wilt, thou art able, as being the Mother of thy Creator, for those who call upon thee and love thee: if thou art with us, none shall be against us!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:* The angel made the furnace produce dew...

*Troparion:* Grant me manly understanding, O Unity of thrice-radiant form, to keep and preserve Thy divine commandments, ever to sing unto Thee faithfully: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Troparion:* Praised as one by nature, and God unutterable by nature, yet bearing a threefold number in Persons: preserve us all from diverse temptations!

*Troparion:* Co-essential and co-eternal in glory, Thee the one God by nature... the Trinity of Hypostases, clearly unchangeable in form...[^156]

*Theotokion:* The co-eternal God, having assumed our composite nature immaculately within thy womb, O pure one, appeared in love for mankind, and taught all to sing: Blessed is the God of our fathers!

*Ode 8. Heirmos:* From the flame Thou didst pour forth dew for the venerable...

*Troparion:* Cleansing of transgressions quickly grant unto me, and from manifold passions...

...deliverance, O Trinity of one form, Thrice-hypostatic Unity, that I may glorify Thee unto the ages!

*Troparion:* As God hearing the prayer of Thy warriors, mercifully have mercy upon all, O Thrice-radiant Unity, all-good Trinity, as we hymn Thy majesty!

*Troparion:* Light co-eternal from the eternal Light, from the Father the begotten Word, with the Spirit proceeding as Light, praised and supremely exalted with faith!

*Theotokion:* Thou hast borne the Physician of mankind, O immaculate one, the almighty Word, Christ and Lord, Who heals the primal curse of our forefather: Whom we supremely exalt unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:* It is not possible for man to see God...

*Troparion:* Unable to behold the beauty of Thy glory, O Master, the cherubic ranks, covering themselves with wings, unceasingly cry out a threefold hymn to the Thrice-hypostatic [Godhead], and glorify the single dominion of Thy Godhead's Principle!

*Troparion:* By Thy brightness, O Never-setting Sun, grant unto Thy people: enlighten our hearts and souls, and deliver us from many transgressions, O One all-merciful and Thrice-hypostatic, and make us worthy of unending life!

*Troparion:* O Light unified and thrice-radiant, Giver of light: by Thy divinity illumine those who faithfully sing of Thee, and deliver them from gloomy wickedness; make them worthy of bright mansions, as Thou art all-good!

*Theotokion:* He Who by wisdom before the ages created all things, O Virgin, has through thee saved that which was corrupted, and has filled all things with the rays of divine light;...

...thee, O Mother of God, we truly glorify in faith!

*Troparia: "It is truly meet," and the rest as usual.*

## SUNDAY MATINS (TONE 6)

*On Sunday at Matins, at "God is the Lord": Troparion, and as at Vespers. After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymns, Tone 6:*[^157]

*Sessional Hymn 1:* The tomb having been opened and Hades lamenting, Mary cried unto the Apostles who had hidden themselves: "Come forth, ye laborers of the vineyard, and proclaim the word of the Resurrection: that the Lord is risen, granting great mercy unto the world!"

*Sessional Hymn 2:* O Lord, Mary Magdalene stood before Thy tomb, and weeping she cried aloud, supposing Thee to be the gardener, saying: "Where have ye laid the Eternal Life? Where have ye hidden Him Who sits upon the cherubic throne? For the guards thereof became as dead from fear. Either give me my Lord, or cry aloud with me: O Thou Who wast among the dead, and hast raised up the dead, Lord, glory to Thee!"

*Theotokion:* O Virgin Mother of God, entreat Thy Son, Who was nailed willingly to the Cross and is risen from the dead, Christ our God, that our souls may be saved!

*After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn 1:* By Thy voluntary death we have found immortal life, O all-powerful and only Savior of all; for by Thy precious Resurrection Thou hast called all, O merciful One, shattering the enemy's arrows and the victory of Hades and the sting of death!

*Sessional Hymn 2:* The myrrh-bearing women, having set aside grief, unto them the Angel spoke: [why seek ye the living among the dead?][^158] Christ is risen from the tomb, having great mercy!

*Theotokion:* Gideon prefigured the conception, and David declared thy childbirth, O all-hymned Mother of God: for the Word came down like dew upon the fleece into thy womb, and sprouted without seed, O holy Earth, the salvation of the world, Christ our God, O thou who art full of grace!

*After the Evlogitaria (The Blameless), Hypakoe:* By Thy voluntary and life-creating death, O Christ, having shattered the gates of Hades as God, Thou hast opened unto us the ancient Paradise; and having risen from the dead, Thou hast delivered our life from corruption.

*Gradual Antiphons, Tone 6. Antiphon 1:*

*Verse 1:* Unto heaven do I lift up mine eyes, unto Thee, O Word: have compassion on me, that I may live unto Thee!

*Verse 2:* Have mercy upon us who are set at naught, fashioning us as well-fitted vessels for Thee, O Word!

*Glory... Both now... In the Holy Spirit:* Every saving cause exists: if He blow upon anyone worthily, He quickly takes him from earthly things, elevates him, gives him wings, and sets him on high.

*Antiphon 2:*

*Verse 1:* If the Lord had not been among us, not one of us could have withstood the enemy's warfare to conquer it; for they that conquer are exalted hence.

*Verse 2:* Let not my soul be seized by their teeth like a bird, O Word: woe is me, how shall I escape the enemy, being a lover of sin?

*Glory... Both now... In the Holy Spirit:* Is deification unto all, good pleasure, understanding, peace, and blessing: for He is equal in operation to the Father and the Word.

*Antiphon 3:*

*Verse 1:* They that trust in the Lord are fearsome to enemies and marvelous to all: for they look on high.

*Verse 2:* Unto iniquities the lot of the righteous stretches not forth his hands, having Thee as Helper, O Savior.

*Glory... Both now... In the Holy Spirit:* Is dominion over all; Whom the armies on high worship,...

...together with every breath below.

*Prokeimenon: Lord, stir up Thy might, and come to save us (LXX Ps. 79:3).*

*Verse: Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel (LXX Ps. 79:2).*

*Let every breath. The Resurrection Gospel. Then: "Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ." Psalm 50: "Have mercy on me, O God." And the refrains, and the rest.*

*Resurrection Canon, Tone 6. Ode 1. Heirmos:* As Israel walked on dry land through the deep with their feet, beholding the pursuer Pharaoh drowned, they cried: Let us sing a song of victory unto God, for He is glorified!

*Troparion:* Stretching out Thy palms upon the Cross, Thou didst fulfill the Father's good pleasure, O Lord Jesus, [Creator] of all things: therefore let us all sing a song of victory unto Thee!

*Troparion:* In fear unto Thee, as a handmaid, death came commanded unto the Master of all, Who through her dispenses endless life and resurrection!

*Theotokion:* Having received Thine Own Creator, as He Himself willed, incarnate beyond understanding from thy seedless womb, thou hast truly appeared as Sovereign Lady of creation!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Heirmos:* By the wave of the sea...

*Troparion:* Thee, Who by the wood of the Cross didst abolish the ancient curse,[^159] pouring forth salvation unto men and healing of passions, having for our sake assumed human nature: Thee, our Savior, do we glorify!

*Troparion:* Thee, Who didst descend into the depth among the dead, Who givest life and didst cause the light to shine forth: we sing of Thy Resurrection, and worshipping we glorify Thee, O merciful King!

*Theotokion:* Inexhaustible treasure of virginity for mankind, having borne the Creator, Christ: Whom earthly beings having found, rejoicing, unceasingly glorify thee!

*Canon to the Theotokos. Ode 1. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Eve made me partaker of the ancient disobedience and by tasting brought in the curse; which thou, O most pure one, hast destroyed, having borne the Beginning of blessing, the Savior!

*Troparion:* Having borne the divine Flash of lightning, Christ God: drive away the mist of my passions and the gloom of sin, O pure one, by the light of thy brightness!

*Troparion:* The Expectation of the nations brought forth from thee, even as [the prophets] mystically foresaw with noetic eyes: God Who has delivered us through thy mediation!

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 3. Heirmos:* There is none holy like Thee, O Lord my God, Who hast exalted the horn of Thy faithful, O Good One, and established them upon the rock of Thy confession!

*Troparion:* Beholding God crucified in the flesh, all creation dissolved in fear; yet by the hand of Him Who holds all things, Who was crucified for our sake, it was firmly sustained!

*Troparion:* By death death was abolished, and lies breathless; for unable to endure the divine assault of Life, the strong one is slain, and [Christ] grants resurrection unto all!

*Theotokion:* The miracle of thy divine childbirth, O pure one, surpasses every order of nature: for beyond nature thou didst conceive God in thy womb, and having given birth, thou remainest ever a Virgin!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Ode 3. Heirmos: Thee, Who suspended [the earth] upon the waters...*

*Troparion:* Thou didst ascend upon the height of the Cross, and didst despoil all the power of the strong one; the Cross gave the gift to understand Thy mystery: and there is none holy besides Thee, O Lord![^160]

*Troparion:* Thine enemies slandered Thee, marvelling at the greatness of Thy power; unto whom Thou hast granted resurrection, freeing the captives who cry aloud: There is none holy besides Thee!

*Theotokion:* Hope of the human race, and protection of the faithful,[^161] O Mother of God, thou alone didst appear as Ever-Virgin: therefore thee, our immaculate Sovereign Lady, do we magnify!

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Ode 3. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Having assumed our corruptible flesh without pain and without seed beyond utterance,[^162] O most pure one, the Good One, having preserved thee intact, united [thee] unto Himself eternally.

*Troparion:* Beholding God incarnate from thee, O Virgin, the angelic ranks marvelled with fear, magnifying thee with unceasing hymns as the Mother of God.

*Troparion:* No earthly word nor tongue, O Virgin, can worthily praise thee: for from thee seedlessly Christ the Giver of life was pleased to be incarnate.

*Resurrection Canon, Tone 6. Ode 4. Heirmos:* Christ is my power, God and Lord: the venerable Church piously cries aloud, keeping festival in the Lord from a pure mind.

*Troparion:* The Tree has blossomed, O Christ, the true Life: for the Cross was set up, watered with water and blood from Thy incorruptible side, pouring forth life unto us.

*Troparion:* No longer does the serpent's deceitful temptation promise deification: for Christ, the Benefactor of human nature, has now prepared for us an unhindered path and length of life.

*Theotokion:* Unutterable and incomprehensible, O Mother of God, is thy truly divine childbirth: a mystery unto earthly, heavenly, and nether beings!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Ode 4. Heirmos: Wondered...*

*Troparion:* Seeking again ancient victory[^163] over the old malefactor upon the Cross, Thou Thyself, O Master, didst right the fall of the first transgression: for Thou hast stretched forth Thy hand unto him that was fallen, O Lord,...

...[stretching forth Thy hand unto him that was fallen, O Lord]!

*Troparion:* The angelic hosts marvelled, wondering at Thy tomb, at the divine deliverance; the souls of the righteous, being set free, blessed God, for Thou didst grant a fitting release.[^164]

*Theotokion:* In eternal [light] I have recognized Him Who was conceived of thee beyond the ancients: we who sit in sadness, O Mother of God, rejoicing, enjoying God's reward; therefore joy has been announced as good tidings unto all the world.

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Ode 4. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* We hymn thee, O most pure and immaculate one, for being saved through thee, singing faithfully we cry aloud: Blessed art thou, who hast given birth unto God as an Ever-Virgin!

*Troparion:* Thou hast given birth unto the Never-setting Light, O Virgin, for those that are in darkness, O pure one, shining in the flesh; and unto those that sing of thee, O Ever-Virgin, pour forth joy!

*Theotokion:* We became dead of old through the tasting [of the tree]; but the Tree of Life that appeared from thee, O most pure one, has raised us up and made us heirs of the delight of Paradise.

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 5. Heirmos:* By Thy divine light, O Good One, illumine with love, I pray, the souls of those that rise early unto Thee, that they may behold Thee, O Word of God, true God, Who deliverest from the gloom of sin.

*Troparion:* The Cherubim steps aside from me, and the sword, the flaming weapon, O Master, turns its back unto me, beholding Thee, O Word of God, true God, Who didst make a way into Paradise for the thief.

*Troparion:* No longer do I fear the return to the earth, O Master Christ: for from the earth Thou hast raised me up who was forgotten, for the sake of Thy great compassion, unto the height of incorruption by Thy Resurrection.

*Theotokion:* Confessing thee from our soul as Mother of God, O good Sovereign Lady of the world, we are saved:...

...for we have thee as an immaculate Protectress, truly the Mother of God.

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Ode 5. Heirmos: O Thou Who didst make light to shine forth unto all...*

*Troparion:* By Thine obedience even unto the Cross, O Christ, and by Thy death, Thou hast delivered us from our disobedience and taken away condemnation, and by Thy wound, O Good One, Thou hast healed our infirmity.

*Troparion:* Thou didst lay down Thy life for Thy servants, O Christ, and by Thy death Thou hast delivered us; from the nethermost Hades, [conquering] the enemy, as God Thou didst raise us up together with Thyself.

*Theotokion:* We hymn thy childbirth, O pure one, whereby enlightenment was given unto our souls, and we have enjoyed everlasting peace, O Mother of God.

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Ode 5. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* Shining brightly in purity, thou didst become the dwelling-place of God, O all-hymned Sovereign Lady: for thou alone didst appear as Mother unto the one God, and in thine arms as an infant thou didst bear Him.

*Troparion:* Thou bearest noetic beauty, all-comely in soul, having become the Mother of God, sealed with pure virginity, enlightening the world with the light of purity.

*Theotokion:* Let the assembly of the ungodly lament, who do not confess thee, O pure one, as Mother of God: for thou hast appeared unto us as the door of divine Light, driving away the gloom of sin.

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 6. Heirmos:* Beholding the sea of life surging with the tempest of temptations, fleeing to Thy calm haven I cry unto Thee: Lead up my life from corruption, O greatly Merciful!

*Troparion:* Being crucified, O Master, by the nails Thou didst nail the curse that was upon us; being pierced in Thy side by the spear, Thou didst tear up the handwriting against Adam, freeing the whole world by the Cross.

*Troparion:* Adam was brought down by deceit, deceived into the abyss of Hades; but being the merciful God, Thou didst descend to seek him out: and taking him upon Thy shoulders, Thou didst raise him up!

*Theotokion:* O most pure Sovereign Lady, who on earth didst give birth unto the Helmsman: stay the grievous evil assault of passions, and grant calm unto my heart!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Ode 6. Heirmos: [Unto the beast Jonah became an inmate]...*[^165]

*Troparion:* Thou didst offer unto the unoriginate Father, O Lord, Thy precious Blood, slain for us in the flesh, being nailed upon the wood of the Cross, sacrificed as a Lamb.

*Troparion:* Thou didst raise up, O Lord, those fallen through the ages, having humbled Thyself, O Christ, for the sake of us who are humble, that Thou mightest cancel our debts through Thy flesh.

*Theotokion:* Thy childbirth, O all-hymned one, we glorify unceasingly; we the faithful proclaim thee as Mother of God: for thou didst bear the Creator and Savior of our souls.

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Ode 6. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* The great Moses, who wrote among the prophets beforehand, prefigured thee as the Ark, the Table, the Candlestick, and the Jar, figuratively revealing the incarnation from thee of the Most High, O Virgin!

*Troparion:* Death was slain, and corruption was abolished, and the condemnation of Adam, by the fruit of thy womb, O pure one: for thou didst bring forth Life, delivering from corruption those that praise thee.

*Theotokion:* The legal [shadow] grew faint, and now there surpasses mind and thought that which has appeared by thy grace, O Virgin: thy childbirth, the blessing and Savior of our souls, O all-hymned one!

*Kontakion: "O Protectress..."*[^166]

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 7. Heirmos:* The angel made the furnace produce dew...

*Theotokion:* Through thee, O Virgin, [light has shone upon us], granting saving release:[^167] for thou hast given birth unto the Creator, God; Whom entreat, O most pure one, to send down great mercy upon us, the faithful!

*Theotokion:* From the Light, the Giver of light, thou didst conceive the Word, and beyond utterance gavest birth unto the Pre-eternal One: for the Spirit of God, O Virgin, dwelt within thee; therefore, O pure one, all of us call thee blessed!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Ode 8. Heirmos: The holy youths...*

*Troparion:* Clap your hands, and shout aloud, all ye nations, hearing the joyful praise of the voice of the Most High, when the evil enemy fell, and [the wealth of Hades was despoiled]:[^168] thus sing and supremely exalt [Him]!

*Troparion:* He Who by the Tree destroyed the ancient curse, the Fountain of sanctification, all-good Christ: Thee, O Savior, do we hymn and supremely exalt!

*Theotokion:* [Beyond the laws of nature],[^169] O all-hymned one, alone didst thou pass, having given birth unto the Master, the Creator of creation; and after childbirth, O immaculate one, appearing higher than all creation;...

...therefore we sing: All ye works of the Lord, hymn the Lord!

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Ode 8. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* In a vesture inwrought with gold as a Queen, illumined by the brightness of the Spirit, Thy Son set thee at His right hand, O most pure one: Whom we supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Troparion:* He Who by His will alone was framed, taking flesh from thy most pure womb, desiring to renew [man] from above: Whom we supremely exalt [unto all ages]!

*Theotokion:* The golden candlestick prefigured thee, who didst bear the unutterable and unapproachable Light, enlightening all creation with understanding: Whom we supremely exalt unto all ages!

*Resurrection Canon. Ode 9. Heirmos:* It is not possible for man to see God, upon Whom the ranks of Angels dare not gaze; but through thee, O most pure one, the Word incarnate has appeared unto us: Whom magnifying, together with the heavenly hosts we call thee blessed!

*Troparion:* Though being without part in passions, O Word of God, Thou didst become partaker of passions in the flesh; and looking upon impassioned man, Thou didst endure the Passion, O our Savior: for Thou alone art passionless and all-powerful!

*Troparion:* Thou didst deign to undergo death without corruption, preserving Thy body untasted of corruption; and Thy divine and life-creating soul, O Master, was not left in Hades: but Thou didst arise as from sleep, and didst raise us up together with Thyself!

*Theotokion:* The prophetic choirs prophesied concerning thee, O all-hymned one, that from thee the all-radiant Ray of the Father should shine forth, O Mother of God: with whom praising thee unceasingly, [we pray that] we may be delivered from every affliction through thy prayers!

*Another Canon, of the Cross. Ode 9. Heirmos: Weep not for Me, Mother...*

*Troparion:* Unable to endure [the fall of] Thy creation, O Master,[^170] by reason of the compassion of Thy tender mercies, Thou didst glorify me who was dishonored by the reproach of the Cross, O Riches of glory: Himself we worthily praise and magnify!

*Troparion:* How was Life numbered among the dead? How did He endure death? How did He descend into Hades, saving [the human race]? Desiring to bring all the dead to life, and to put death to death, and to release those in Hades: Him do we magnify in hymns!

*Theotokion:* Beholding Him upon the Cross, Whom from thy blood thou didst incarnate, O pure Maiden who gavest birth to God: "Why, O my Son, has an evil people dealt thus with Thee, putting Thee to death, Who art the Life and Resurrection of the faithful?"

*Another Canon, to the Mother of God. Ode 9. Heirmos: The same.*

*Troparion:* The Star of Jacob, shining with the rays of Godhead upon those held in the gloom of sleep, has now through thee, O most pure one, [shone forth as] Christ the Word incarnate: by Whom being enlightened, together with the heavenly hosts we magnify thee!

*Troparion:* Strengthened by thy power and grace, I bring unto thee a hymn from a helpless heart; which do thou accept, O pure Virgin, bestowing in return thy bright grace from incorruptible treasures, O divinely blessed one!

*Theotokion:* Resurrection has been given unto the dead by Thine unutterable incarnation from thee, O all-venerable Mother of God: for Life, clothed in flesh from thee, has shone forth unto all, and has utterly destroyed mortal darkness!

*Holy is the Lord our God. The Evangelical Exapostilarion as appointed. On "Praise [the Lord]": Resurrection Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* Thy Cross, O Lord, is life and protection unto Thy people, and trusting therein, we sing of Thee, our risen God: Have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 2:* Thy burial, O Master, opened Paradise unto the human race, and being delivered from corruption, we sing of Thee, our risen God: Have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 3:* With the Father and the Holy Spirit[^171] let us praise Christ, Who is risen from the dead, and let us cry aloud unto Him: Thou art our Life and Resurrection: Have mercy on us!

*Sticheron 4:* On the third day Thou didst rise, O Christ, from the tomb, even as it is written, raising with Thyself our forefather: therefore the human race glorifies Thee, and sings of Thy Resurrection!

*Stichera Anatolika:*

*Sticheron 5:* O Lord, great and all-glorious is the mystery of Thy Resurrection! For Thou camest forth from the tomb as a bridegroom from his bridal chamber, by death having destroyed death that Thou mightest grant freedom unto us:[^172] therefore in the heavens the Angels dance, and on earth men glorify Thy tender mercy towards us, O Lover of mankind!

*Sticheron 6:* The tomb being sealed, how was it stolen away, O Judeans, having set guards and affixed seals? The King came forth, the doors being shut: either present Him as dead, or worship Him as God, singing with us: Glory, O Lord, to Thy Cross and to Thy Resurrection!

*Verse: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be lifted up; forget not Thy poor unto the end.*

*Sticheron 7:* O lawless Judeans, where are the seals and the silver coins which ye gave to the soldiers? The Treasure was not stolen, but He is risen, mighty in power! But ye are put to shame, having rejected Christ, the Lord of glory, Who suffered and was buried and rose from the dead: Him let us worship!

*Verse: I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will speak of all Thy marvelous works.*

*Sticheron 8:* Unto Thy life-bearing tomb the myrrh-bearing women came weeping, O Lord, bearing myrrh, seeking to anoint Thy most pure Body; and they found a radiant Angel sitting upon the stone, and he spoke unto them: "Why weep ye for Him Who poured forth Life unto the world from His side? Why seek ye the Immortal One in the tomb as dead? Run rather, proclaim unto His disciples His glorious Resurrection and worldwide joy!" Through whom enlighten us also, O Savior, and grant cleansing and great mercy!

*Glory... Evangelical Sticheron. Both now... Most blessed art thou... At the Liturgy, the Beatitudes, Tone 6:*

*Refrain: Remember me, O God my Savior, when Thou comest into Thy Kingdom, and save me, as Thou alone art the Lover of mankind!*

*Troparion 1:* Through a tree Adam was deceived; but through the tree of the Cross, O Savior, the thief was saved, who cried aloud: Remember me, O Lord!

*Troparion 2:* Being nailed upon the tree of the Cross, Thou didst deliver the world from deceit: with one accord let us glorify Christ!

*Troparion 3:* Let us sing of Christ our God, Who willingly was crucified and buried, and rose from the tomb on the third day!

*Troparion 4:* Remember me, O Thou Who didst overthrow Hades by Thy burial, and by Thy Resurrection didst fill all things with joy, as Thou art compassionate!

*Troparion 5:* O Thou Who didst shatter the gates and bars of Hades, and by Thy Resurrection didst raise those that were in Hades, who cry aloud: O Savior, glory to Thy Resurrection!

*Glory...* We praise the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, saying: O Holy Trinity, save our souls!

*Both now...* Coming together in concord, let us the faithful sing of the Mother of God: for she is our acceptable Protectress!

*Epistle to the Hebrews [Heb. 7:26–8:2]:*

Brethren: Such a High Priest was fitting for us, Who is holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and made higher than the heavens; Who has no need daily, as the high priests, to offer up sacrifices, first for His own sins, and then for those of the people: for this He did once for all when He offered up Himself. For the law appoints men high priests having infirmity; but the word of the oath, which was after the law, appoints the Son, perfected unto ages of ages. Now of the things spoken this is the sum: We have such a High Priest, Who is seated on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens; a Minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.

*Alleluia. Verse 1: He that dwells in the help of the Most High (LXX Ps. 90:1). Verse 2: He shall say unto the Lord: Thou art my protector (LXX Ps. 90:2).*

*Gospel according to John [John 10:9–16]:*

The Lord said: "I am the door: by Me if anyone enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture. The thief comes not, but to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life. I am the Good Shepherd: the Good Shepherd lays down His life for the sheep. But he that is a hireling, and not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming, and leaves the sheep, and flees; and the wolf catches them,[^173] and scatters the sheep. The hireling flees, because he is a hireling, and cares not for the sheep. I am the Good Shepherd, and know Mine own, and am known of...

...Mine, even as the Father knows Me, and I know the Father; and I lay down My life for the sheep. And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear My voice; and there shall be one flock, and one Shepherd."

## SUNDAY EVENING VESPERS (TONE 6)

*On Sunday Evening, Penitential Stichera unto the Lord our God, Tone 6.*

*Podoben: "Having laid aside all earthly hope" (Всѐ ѿло́жше):*

*Sticheron 1:* I have sinned, O Lover of mankind; I confess unto Thee as a man, that I may seek forgiveness; but that which is beyond man, being above nature, and above the law of mercy,[^174] having acquired love for mankind beyond understanding: have mercy upon him that turns unto Thee!

*Sticheron 2:* Thou hast appointed repentance for sinners and not for the righteous, setting forth as an example the thief and the prodigal, Manasseh and the harlot, the persecutor and the publican, and the adulterer,[^175] that none may easily despair of Thy love for mankind, O Christ Savior, most good: turning back I pray unto Thee, being of good hope that Thou wilt receive me!

*Sticheron 3:* Grant me compunction, and rejection of evils, and perfect amendment, unto me who am sunken in bodily passions, and distanced from Thee, O God, in the storm of sins, having no hope: do Thou save me by the rich radiance of Thy goodness, O Jesus God, the salvation of our souls!

*Other Stichera of the Bodiless Powers.*

*Podoben: "The same Tone and Podoben" (Гла́съ и҆ подо́бенъ то́йже):*

*Sticheron 1:* Most radiant ranks, ineffable servants of the Godhead, illumined with the rays of that divine Light, standing around the ineffable Throne, bodiless ministers: Angels and Archangels, Cherubim and...

...Seraphim, six-winged living creatures, Dominions of the Throne, Powers, Principalities, and Authorities, the resting-place of God:[^176] with boldness pray ye for our souls!

*Sticheron 2:* Brightly adorned with all-radiant beams of the bodiless ranks, venerable Archistrategoi Michael and Gabriel, ministers of Christ's Throne, all-radiant heralds: by the most pure prayers of her that bare God,[^177] subdue the painful assaults of our visible and invisible enemies; having boldness unto God, pray ye fervently for our souls!

*Sticheron 3:* Adorned together with ineffable forms, illumined with the brightness of the thrice-radiant Godhead, Thrones, Dominions, Powers, Principalities, Authorities, Cherubim and Seraphim, Angels and Archangels: fervently do I, the wretched one, pray unto you, deliver me from affliction; for I have you as invincible protectors and good intercessors for our souls!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* In archangelic fashion, ye faithful, let us praise the heavenly bridal chamber and the sealed door: Rejoice, for whose sake Christ, the Giver of life and our God, sprang forth and saved the whole human race, casting down the tormentor and our godless enemies by Thy spotless hand, O Hope of the faithful!

*At the Aposticha, Penitential Stichera, Tone 6:*

*Sticheron 1:* At Thy dread coming, O Christ, let me not hear: "I know you not";[^178] for I have set my hope of salvation upon Thee, though I have not kept Thy commandments because of our negligence; but spare our souls, we pray Thee!

*Sticheron 2:* Having found me stripped of good...

...deeds, the enemy wounded me with the arrow of sin; but do Thou, as the Physician of souls and bodies, heal the wounds of my soul, and have mercy on me!

*Martyrikon:* Thy Martyrs, O Lord, denied Thee not, nor departed from Thy commandments: by their prayers have mercy on us!

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:* Having come to know the image of Him Who was incarnate of thee,[^179] O Virgin: pray Thou unto Him for the salvation of our souls!
