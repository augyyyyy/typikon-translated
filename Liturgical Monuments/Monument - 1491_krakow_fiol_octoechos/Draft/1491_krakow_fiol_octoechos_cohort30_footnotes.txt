[^329]: Saturday Vespers (Friday evening) in Tone 8: completion of the Stichera to the Martyrs (*Martyrs of the Lord*); followed by the idiomelon Doxasticon for the departed by St. John of Damascus (*Плачу и рыдаю...* / *I weep and I wail when I contemplate death*), reflecting on Gen. 2:7, 3:19; Theotokion of spiritual healing; and the opening of Saturday Matins at *God is the Lord*, with the common Troparia in Tone 2 (*Апостоли, мученицы, пророцы...* and *Помяни, Господи, яко благ, рабы Твоя...*).

[^330]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of the troparion for the departed (*...save Thee alone, Who art able to grant rest...*); Theotokion of the Ineffable Light; followed by the Sessional Hymns at the 1st Stichologia (*Свѣтила мысленая явистеся...* and *Тесному и прискорбному пути...* with Theotokion *Небесная поют Тя...*); and Sessional Hymns at the 2nd Stichologia (*Что вас наречем, святии?...* echoing the classical Martyricon of Tone 8, and *Велицы подвизастеся, святии...*).

[^331]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of the Sessional Hymn to the Martyrs; followed by the Kontakion of the Martyrs in Tone 8 (*Яко начатки естества...* / Greek: *Ὡς ἀπαρχὰς τῆς φύσεως*); the Sessional Hymn automelon *Having received the secret command* (*Повеленное тайно прием в разуме...* / Greek: *Τὸ προσταχθὲν μυστικῶς*); and Ode 1 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Воду прошед яко сушу...* / Greek: *Ὑγρὰν διοδεύσας*, troparia and Theotokion), followed by Ode 1 of the second Canon for the departed.

[^332]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of Ode 1 of the Canon for the departed; followed by Ode 3 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Небеснаго круга Верхотворче, Господи...* / Greek: *Οὐρανίας ἁψῖδος ὀροφοποιὲ Κύριε*, troparia, and Theotokion); and Ode 3 of the second Canon for the departed (troparia of the martyrs and the reposed, meditating on Christ's voluntary burial and descent into hades).

[^333]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of Ode 3 of the Canon for the departed; followed by Ode 4 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Ты моя крепость, Господи...* / Hab. 3:2, troparia, and Theotokion); and Ode 4 of the second Canon for the departed, invoking the heavenly mansions (John 14:2) prepared for those reposed in faith.

[^334]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of Ode 4 of the Canon for the departed and its Theotokion; followed by Ode 5 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Вскую мя отринул еси...* / Greek: *Ἵνα τί με ἀπώσω*, troparia, and Theotokion); and Ode 5 of the second Canon for the departed, praying for the translation of the faithful into the bosom of Abraham (Luke 16:22).

[^335]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of Ode 5 of the Canon for the departed; followed by Ode 6 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Очисти мя, Спасе...* / Jonah 2:1–10, troparia of penitence and intercession, and Theotokion); and Ode 6 of the second Canon for the departed, meditating on the martyrs' love and Christ's descent to harrow the belly of hades.

[^336]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of Ode 6 of the Canon for the departed with its Stavrotheotokion; followed by the classical Kontakion of the Departed in Tone 8 (*Со святыми упокой, Христе, души раб Твоих...*); Ode 7 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Отроцы еврейстии в пещи...* / Dan. 3:52, troparia, and Theotokion); and the rubric for Ode 7 of the second Canon for the departed.

[^337]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: Ode 7 of the Canon for the departed (troparia of the martyrs and the reposed, and Theotokion); followed by Ode 8 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Царя Небеснаго...* / Dan. 3:57–88, troparia including a poignant personal lament on human frailty and judgment, and Theotokion); and the opening of Ode 8 of the second Canon for the departed.

[^338]: Saturday Matins in Tone 8: conclusion of Ode 8 of the Canon for the departed; Katavasia of Ode 8 (*Мусикийским органом согласующим...* / Dan. 3:1–30, the golden image set up on the plain of Dura); followed by Ode 9 of the Canon to the Prophets, Apostles, Martyrs, and Venerable Ones (Heirmos *Устрашися всяк слух...* / Greek: *Ἔφριξε πᾶσα ἀκοή*, troparia of the prophets, martyrs, and soul's repentance, and opening of the Theotokion).
