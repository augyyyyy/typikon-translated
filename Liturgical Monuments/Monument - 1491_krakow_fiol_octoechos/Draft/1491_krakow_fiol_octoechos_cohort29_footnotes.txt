[^320]: Friday Matins in Tone 6: conclusion of Ode 5 Katavasia; Ode 6 of the Canon to the Cross, Heirmos (*Ятъ бысть, но не удержанъ...* / Jonah 2:1–10, with Fiol's typesetting variants *понесꙋдръжанъ* and *тконообразносѧ*), troparia of the brazen serpent (Num. 21:8–9) and the cross-shaped hands, and Martyricon; followed by the second Canon to the Theotokos, Ode 6 Heirmos (*Бездна послѣдняя грѣховъ...*) and troparion of the Mosaic types (Ark, Table, Lampstand, and Vessel of Manna).

[^321]: Friday Matins in Tone 6: second Canon to the Theotokos, Ode 6 troparia and Katavasia (*Бездна послѣдняя грѣховъ обниде мя...*); followed by the Kontakion of the Cross in Tone 4 (*Вознесыйся на Крестъ волею...*); and Ode 7 of the Canon to the Cross, Heirmos (*Неизреченное чудо...* / Holy Saturday Ode 7) and troparia of the Crucifixion and the mocking of Christ as King.

[^322]: Friday Matins in Tone 6: conclusion of Ode 7 of the Canon to the Cross, Martyricon; second Canon to the Theotokos, Ode 7 troparia of the Cherubic throne and judgment, and Katavasia (*Святыхъ отрокъ пѣснь услышавый...*); followed by Ode 8 of the Canon to the Cross: Heirmos (*Ужаснися и бойся, небо...* / Holy Saturday Ode 8) and troparion of the cup of gall and the piercing of Christ's side.

[^323]: Friday Matins in Tone 6: conclusion of Ode 8 of the Canon to the Cross, Martyricon; second Canon to the Theotokos, Ode 8 troparia of the golden lampstand and vesture of repentance, and Katavasia (*Егоже воинства небесная славятъ...*); followed by Ode 9 of the Canon to the Cross, opening incipit of the Heirmos (*Не рыдай Мене, Мати...* / Holy Saturday Ode 9).

[^324]: Friday Matins in Tone 6: conclusion of Ode 9 of the Canon to the Cross (*Не рыдай Мене, Мати...* / Holy Saturday Ode 9), troparia on the cup of gall and crown of thorns, and Martyricon; followed by the second Canon to the Theotokos, Ode 9 troparia invoking the Star of Jacob (Num. 24:17) and intercession for soul-cleansing adornment.

[^325]: Friday Matins in Tone 6: completion of Ode 9 of the second Canon to the Theotokos; Exapostilarion of the Cross, Podoben (*Жены услышите...* / *Give ear, ye women*), and Stavrotheotokion; followed by the Aposticha Stichera of the Cross in Tone 6 (*О Крестѣ упованїе имѣя, Христе...*), Martyricon, and Theotokion (*Обрадованная Мати Христа Бога...*).

[^326]: Friday Liturgy in Tone 6: the Beatitudes (*Блаженны*), meditating on the Cross, the martyrs (note Fiol's corrupted typesetting *иранскыѧ* for *райскыя*), the Holy Trinity, and the Stavrotheotokion; followed by the Friday Prokeimenon of the Cross in Tone 6 (*Спаси, Господи, люди Твоя...* / LXX Ps. 27:9, 1); and the Friday Epistle to the Corinthians (1 Cor. 1:18–24), proclaiming Christ crucified as the power and wisdom of God.

[^327]: Friday Liturgy in Tone 6: Alleluia (LXX Ps. 73:2, 24:1); Friday Gospel of John (John 3:13–17), the lifting up of the Son of Man as Moses lifted up the serpent; Kinonikon (LXX Ps. 4:7); followed by Friday evening (Saturday Vespers) in Tone 8 at *Lord, I have cried*, Stichera to the Prophets and Martyrs to the automelon (*Podoben*) *O most marvelous wonder* (*О преславное чудо* / Greek: *Ὢ τοῦ παραδόξου θαύματος*), and the opening of *With what crowns of praise...* (*Кыими похвальными вѣнцы...*).

[^328]: Saturday Vespers (Friday evening) in Tone 8: conclusion of the Stichera at *Lord, I have cried* (Sticheron to the Prophets, Hierarchs, and Venerable Fathers; followed by Stichera to the Apostles, Teachers, and Holy Fathers of the Councils against heresies); followed by the rubric for *Glory, and now* prescribing the greater Doxasticon of the oncoming Tone (*наставшаго гласа*), with the incipit of the Martyricon automelon in Tone 8 (*Мученицы Господни, всяко мѣсто освящаете...* / *Martyrs of the Lord, every place ye sanctify...*).
