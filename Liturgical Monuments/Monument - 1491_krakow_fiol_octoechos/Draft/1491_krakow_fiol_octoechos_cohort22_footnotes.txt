Note Fiol's epigraphic witness in the second troparion: *ѡбразы іра въ селеніе*, an archaic abbreviated rendering corresponding to standard Church Slavonic *еже по образу, райское селение* ("that which was in His image unto the habitation of Paradise").

[^246]: Sunday Liturgy Prokeimenon, Tone 8. Fiol's printed rubric presents an enigmatic textual corruption: `пр҃оо въщи инте̄гвинда · і҃ · з҃ наємь въ іоудєнѣхъ`, designating the 8th Tone Sunday Liturgy Prokeimenon (LXX Ps. 75:12, *Помолитеся и воздадите Господеви Богу нашему*) with verse from LXX Ps. 75:2 (*Вѣдомъ въ Іудеи Богъ / въ Іудеехъ*). The corrupt rubric appears to incorporate garbled Polish/German or Latin liturgical markers (*intervallum* / *Sonntag nach Trinitatis*) or misread manuscript numerals for the 17th cycle / 8th Sunday lectionary designation.
