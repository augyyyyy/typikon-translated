[^291]: Wednesday Matins Canons in Tone 3: Ode 7 Theotokia and Katavasia (the budding rod of Aaron and the dewy furnace, Num. 17:8; Dan. 3:49–50), followed by Ode 8 Heirmos (*Непостоянный огнь...*) and troparia of the Cross meditating on Christ stretching forth His palms upon the Cross to heal Adam's unrestrained reach to the tree.

[^292]: Wednesday Matins Canons in Tone 3: Ode 8 Theotokia, Katavasia of the Three Holy Children in Babylon, and Ode 9 Heirmos (*Въ законѣ сѣни и писаніихъ...* / Luke 2:23; Ex. 13:2) with troparion of the Cross praising Christ Who established the earth upon nothing (Job 26:7).

[^294]: Wednesday Matins Aposticha Stichera of the Cross in Tone 3 (*Дрѣво преслушанія...* contrasting the tree of the Fall with the Tree of the Cross, and *Дрѣвомъ разумѣхомъ от смерти...*), Martyricon, and Theotokion; followed by the opening of Wednesday Liturgy Beatitudes in Tone 3 (*Овергшюся, Христе, заповѣди Твоея...*).

[^295]: Wednesday Liturgy: Beatitudes concluded, followed by the Prokeimenon in Tone 3 (LXX Luke 1:46, 48), and the Liturgy Epistle reading from Galatians 4:22–31 (the allegory of the two covenants: Hagar the bondwoman and Sarah the freewoman, the earthly and the heavenly Jerusalem).

[^296]: Wednesday Liturgy: Galatians Epistle conclusion (Gal. 4:30–31), Alleluia with psalm verses (LXX Ps. 44:11, 13), and Gospel according to Luke 10:38–42; 11:27–28 (Martha and Mary, and the woman from the multitude blessing the Mother of Christ). Concludes with the rubrics for Wednesday evening (Thursday Vespers) for the Holy Apostles and Saint Nicholas in Tone 4, and opening of Sticheron 1 to the Apostle Peter to the automelon (*Podoben*) *Called from on high* (*Званный свыше* / Greek: *Ὁ ἐξ ὑψίστου κληθείς*).

[^297]: Thursday Vespers: Stichera to the Holy Apostles (St. Peter) concluded, meditating on Christ giving Peter the keys of the Kingdom to bind and loose (Matt. 16:18–19) and healing by his shadow (Acts 5:15); followed by Stichera in Tone 4 to Saint Nicholas of Myra, recounting the deliverance of the three innocent men from execution through the saint's apparition to Emperor Constantine.

[^298]: Thursday Vespers: Stichera to Saint Nicholas concluded, Theotokion, Wednesday evening Prokeimenon in Tone 4 (LXX Ps. 53:1–2), Aposticha Stichera to the Apostles in Tone 4, Dismissal Troparion of the Holy Apostles in Tone 3 (*Апостоли святіи...*), and Dismissal Troparion of Saint Nicholas in Tone 4 (*Правило вѣрѣ и образъ кротости...* / *A rule of faith and a model of meekness*).
