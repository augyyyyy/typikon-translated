## Common Service to a Single Hieromartyr[^292]

*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried", Stichera, Tone 1.*  
*Tone 1, Podoben: "O all-praised martyrs" (Всехва́льніи мꙋ́нцы):*[^293]

*Sticheron 1:*  
O divinely wise and blessed [Name], with the crimson of thy blood thou didst adorn thy sacred and divine vesture; for piously thou didst pass from strength to strength, and from glory to glory: and now pray that peace and great mercy be granted unto our souls.

*Sticheron 2:*  
First offering unto God an unbloody sacrifice, as a most lawful priest, as a whole burnt offering and acceptable sacrifice...

...and afterwards, as a most true martyr, thou didst offer thyself in blood unto Christ, O divinely speaking and all-honorable one; pray Him for those who sing praise unto thee.

*Sticheron 3:*  
Thou didst bring armies of martyrs unto Christ, O father, through thy teachings and instructions, teaching and instructing them, and manifestly offering thyself as an example, O divinely wise one; with them pray that peace and great mercy be granted unto our souls.

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*  
Storm-tossed by a sea of transgressions, fleeing unto the calm haven of thy most pure prayer, O Mother of God, I cry unto thee: Save me, stretching out thy sovereign right hand unto thy servant, O all-immaculate one.

*Cross-Theotokion:*[^294]  
Standing by the Cross of thy Son and God, and beholding His long-suffering, the pure Mother wept, saying: Woe is me, sweetest Child! Why dost Thou suffer these things unjustly, O Word of God, that Thou mayest save mankind?

*Glory... Tone 6:*

Therefore, having boldness toward Him, fervently pray for those who celebrate thy most honorable memory with faith and love, and for those who honor it, that they may be delivered from temptations, and from all tribulations and distresses.

*Sticheron 1:*  
Having dyed thy sacred vesture with blood, thou didst enter into the Holy of Holies, having become holy, O glorious [Name]; and there, ever enjoying deification, adorned also with holy martyrdom, thou wast shown to be more honorable than the angels by thy communions: therefore we honor thee, and celebrate in love thy most sacred festival, O divinely blessed one.

*Verse: The righteous shall flourish like a palm tree, and shall be multiplied like a cedar in Lebanon (Ps. 91:12).*

*Glory... Tone 4:*
