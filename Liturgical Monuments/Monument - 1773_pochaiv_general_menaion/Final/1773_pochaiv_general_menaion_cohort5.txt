*Troparion 2:*  
Having living water abiding in thy soul, O holy one, thou didst dry up muddy streams of malice, O all-glorious one, strengthened by the grace of the Savior, O divinely blessed one.

*Troparion 3:*  
Strengthened by the power of God, O divinely blessed one, thou wast able to shatter the haughtiness of the enemy; and thou wast an eminent victor, and a citizen of the city on high.

*Theotokion:*  
Show thy tender mercy, O all-pure Lady, upon me, the wretched one, fervently praying unto thy Son, O all-holy one, that by thy prayers we may escape eternal fire.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*[^238]  
Thou Who by Thy command didst fix the earth upon nothing, and didst suspend its unsupportable weight, establish Thy Church upon the immovable rock of Thy commandments, O Christ, Who alone art good and the Lover of mankind.

*Troparion 1:*  
Departing from the mire of passions with wisdom, and drawing near unto God by divine purities, thou didst receive divine illumination, showing thee forth, O worthy of glory, as a son of light, working the work of light.

*Troparion 2:*  
Earthly warfare and great glory thou didst count as nothing, O divinely blessed one: therefore thou didst enlist under Christ, and wast numbered among the ranks of martyrs, rejoicing, O blessed God-bearing martyr [Name].

*Troparion 3:*  
The paths of thy journey, O worthy of wonder, were directed unto a peaceful way, entering into the Kingdom of God, into the breadth of paradise, and unto eternal rest, O glorious one.

*Theotokion:*
