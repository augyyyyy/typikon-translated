# 1773 Pochaiv Lavra General Menaion / Mineia Zahalna (Мінея Загальна, Почаїв: Друкарня Почаївської Лаври, 1773) <a id="1773-pochaiv-lavra-general-menaion-mineia-zahalna"></a>

*(Binding — Upper outer front cover: Worn brown calfskin binding over pasteboard with diagonal blind tooling and scuffed edges; no printed or manuscript text present).*

*FRONT MATTER, TITLE PAGE, AND BLESSING:*

*(Inside Front Cover — Flyleaf / Paste-down: Archival library notations, nineteenth-century shelfmarks, inventory classifications, and typographical stamp from Przemyśl)*[^1]

**GENERAL MENAION**  
that is:  
**General Services to single and multiple Saints,**  
**sung according to the Typikon.**  
**PRINTED**  
**By the blessing of His Excellency, the Most Reverend Kyr**  
**SYLVESTER LUBIENIECKI-RUDNICKI,**  
**Exarch of all Rus’, Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh,**[^2]  
**In the Holy Miracle-Working Lavra of Pochaiv,**  
**of the Order of Saint Basil the Great.**[^3]  
**In the year from the creation of the world according to the Greek chronographers, 7281;**  
**and from the Nativity of Christ, 1773.**[^4]

### Notes

[^1]: The paste-down on the inside of the front board contains assorted nineteenth-century archival inscriptions, inventory numbers, and a typographical imprint of M. Dzikowski in Przemyśl (1870), indicating provenance in the library of the Greek Catholic Eparchy of Przemyśl.
[^2]: Sylvester Lubieniecki-Rudnytsky (Sylwester Lubieniecki-Rudnicki, c. 1708–1777), Basilian monk and Greek Catholic Bishop of Lutsk and Ostroh (1752–1777), was appointed Exarch of all Rus' by the Holy See.
[^3]: The initials "Ч. С. В. В." stand for the Order of Saint Basil the Great (*Чина Святаго Василїя Великаго*, Latin *Ordo Sancti Basilii Magni*, OSBM). The Pochaiv printing press was operated by the Basilian order from 1730 until 1831.
[^4]: The year of creation 7281 according to the Byzantine-Greek calculation (5508 BC) corresponds precisely to AD 1773.
[^5]: In accordance with Ruthenian printing tradition, the Table of Contents (*Оглавленїе*) provides the folio references for each category of the Common Services, followed by the appendices of Theotokia.
