## Theotokion Canons Sung According to the Typikon, on Memories of Celebrated Saints, Before Their Canons
*First Canon*

*Refrain: Most holy Mother of God, save us!*
*Troparion 1:*
The immaterial ladder of old, and the strangely congealed pathway of the sea, revealed thy childbearing, O pure one, which we all sing: for He is greatly glorified!
*Troparion 2:*

having become incarnate of thee, O all-pure one, conversed with men: for He is greatly glorified!
*Troparion 3:*
The Sun of Righteousness passed through the impassable, sealed door of thy womb, O pure one, and shone forth upon the world: for He is greatly glorified!
*Ode 3. Heirmos:*
The bow of the mighty is broken by Thy dominion, O Christ, and the weak are girded with strength.[^678]
*Troparion 1:*
He Who is higher than every time, as the Creator of the times, was voluntarily formed as an infant from thee, O Virgin.
*Troparion 2:*
Let us hymn the womb more spacious than the heavens, through which Adam, rejoicing, lives in the heavens.
*Ode 4. Heirmos:*
I heard, O Lord, Thy glorious dispensation, and I glorified Thine incomprehensible power, O Lover of mankind.[^679]
*Troparion 1:*
Behold, the divine mountain of the house of the Lord is exalted higher than the powers, most manifestly as the Mother of God.
*Troparion 2:*
Apart from the laws of nature, having alone given birth unto the Master of creation, O Virgin, thou wast accounted worthy of the divine calling.
*Ode 5. Heirmos:*
The coal revealed to Isaiah, the Sun, shone forth from the virginal womb, granting the enlightenment of the knowledge of God unto those who had wandered in darkness.[^680]
*Troparion 1:*
Ye clouds, drop down the sweetness of gladness upon those on earth: for a young Child is given, Who exists before the ages, our God incarnate of the Virgin.
*Troparion 2:*
A light shone forth upon my life and flesh, and destroyed the gloom of sin, the Most High having become incarnate in the last times of the Virgin without seed.
*Ode 6. Heirmos:*
Having heard the voice of supplicatory words from a suffering soul, O Master, deliver me from grievous things: for Thou alone art the Author of our salvation.[^681]

*Theotokion Canons*
*Troparion 1:*
Human nature, in bondage to sin, O pure Sovereign Lady, obtained freedom through thee: for thy Son, as a lamb, is slain for all.
*Troparion 2:*
We all cry out unto thee, true Mother of God: deliver thy servants who have provoked anger, for thou alone hast boldness before thy Son.
*Ode 7. Heirmos:*

*Troparion 1:*
Jacob indeed in the night beheld God incarnate as in an enigma; but from thee He appeared visibly unto us who sing: The supremely divine God of our fathers, and supremely glorified!
*Troparion 2:*
Manifesting the signs of the ineffable condescension in thee, He wrestled with Jacob, by which He was voluntarily united unto men, O pure one: the supremely divine God of our fathers, and supremely glorified!
*Troparion 3:*
Abominable is he who proclaims not Thee, the only Son of the Virgin, of the all-hymned Trinity, with unhesitating faith and with his tongue crying aloud: The supremely divine God of our fathers, and supremely glorified!
*Ode 8. Heirmos:*
Disregarding the golden likeness, the thrice-blessed youths, seeing the immutable and living image of God, sang in the midst of the fire: Let all united creation sing the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages![^683]
*Troparion 1:*
He was seen on earth through thee, and lived with men, Who is incomparable in goodness and in power, unto Whom singing we all the faithful cry: Let all united creation sing the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!
*Troparion 2:*
Truly proclaiming thee as pure, we glorify thee as the Mother of God: for thou didst bear One of the Trinity incarnate, unto Whom with the Father and the Spirit we all sing: Let all creation sing the Lord, and supremely exalt Him unto all ages!

*Theotokion Canons*
*Ode 9. Heirmos:*
Thou art all desire, Thou art all sweetness, O Word of God, Son of the Virgin, God of gods, O Lord of the holy, ever-existing: therefore we all magnify Thee together with her who gave Thee birth.[^684]
*Troparion 1:*

*Troparion 2:*
Whom Thou didst choose, O Master, accept graciously as an intercessor, Thy Mother on our behalf, and let all things be filled with Thy goodness: that we all may magnify Thee as Benefactor.

## Resurrectional Theotokia of the Eight Tones, in the Evening at "Lord, I Have Cried" and at the Aposticha
*Tone 1. At "Lord, I Have Cried", Theotokion:*
Let us hymn the Virgin Mary, the glory of all the world, who budded forth from men and bare the Master, the gate of heaven, the song of the bodiless hosts, and the adornment of the faithful! For she was shown forth as heaven and the temple of the Godhead: she, having broken down the barrier of enmity, ushered in peace and opened the Kingdom! Having her therefore as the firm foundation of faith, we have the Lord Who was born of her as our Defender! Take courage therefore, take courage, O people of God, for He will conquer the enemies, as He is all-powerful![^693]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Behold, the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled: for as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and after giving birth thou didst remain as thou wast before giving birth; for God was the One born, therefore He renewed the course of nature. But O Mother of God, despise not the supplications of thy servants offered unto thee in thy temple: but as carrying the Compassionate One in thine arms, have mercy upon thy servants, and pray that our souls may be saved![^694]
*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
The shadow of the Law passed away when grace arrived: for as the bush was not consumed when wrapped in flame, so as a Virgin thou didst give birth, and a Virgin thou didst remain. Instead of a pillar of fire, the righteous Sun shone forth; instead of Moses, Christ the salvation of our souls![^695]

*Resurrectional Theotokia of the Eight Tones*
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O new wonder of all ancient wonders! For who hath known a Mother who gave birth without a husband, and carried in her arms Him Who upholds all creation? The One born is the counsel of God! Whom as an infant having carried in thine arms, O all-pure one, and possessing maternal boldness toward Him, cease not to pray for those who honor thee, that He may have compassion and save our souls![^696]
*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
How shall we not marvel at thy God-man childbearing, O most honorable one? For having received no temptation of a man, O all-immaculate one, thou didst give birth in the flesh without a father unto the Son, Who was born of the Father without a mother before the ages, Who suffered no change, nor mingling, nor division, but preserved entire the property of both natures. Therefore, O Mother, Virgin, Sovereign Lady, pray Him to save the souls of those who in orthodox manner confess thee as the Mother of God![^697]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Without seed from the Holy Spirit, but by the will of the Father, thou didst conceive the Son of God, Who exists before the ages from the Father without a mother, and Who for our sake became of thee without a father; thou didst give birth unto Him in the flesh and didst nurse Him as an infant with milk. Therefore cease not to pray that our souls may be delivered from dangers![^698]
*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
David the Prophet, the forefather of God, melodiously proclaimed beforehand concerning thee unto Him Who hath done great things for thee: "The Queen stood at Thy right hand." For God, Who was pleased to become man of thee without a father, revealed thee as the Mother and mediatress of life, that He might renew His own image corrupted by passions; and having found the lost sheep wandered upon the mountains, taking it upon His shoulder, He might bring it unto the Father, and according to His own will *Resurrectional Theotokia of the Eight Tones*
unite it with the heavenly hosts, and Christ, Who hath great and rich mercy, may save the world, O Mother of God![^699]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Look down upon the entreaties of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, calming the grievous uprisings against us, freeing us from every sorrow: for thee alone do we have as a firm and certain foundation, and we have acquired thy protection, that we who call upon thee may not be put to shame, O Sovereign Lady! Hasten unto the supplication of those who faithfully cry unto thee: Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, help of all, joy, protection, and salvation of our souls![^700]
*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
In the Red Sea the image of the unwedded Bride was inscribed of old: there Moses was the divider of the water; but here Gabriel was the minister of the wonder. Then Israel traversed the deep without being wetted; but now the Virgin hath given birth unto Christ without seed. The sea remained impassable after the passage of Israel; the immaculate one remained incorrupt after the birth of Emmanuel. He Who is and Who was before, having been revealed as man, O God, have mercy on us![^701]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
Thou art the temple and the gate, the palace and the throne of the King, O all-honorable Virgin: through whom my Deliverer, Christ the Lord, shone forth upon those that slept in darkness, being the Sun of Righteousness, wishing to enlighten those whom He created according to His own image with His own hand. Therefore, O all-hymned one, as having acquired maternal boldness toward Him, unceasingly pray that our souls may be saved![^702]
*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Who will not bless thee, O most holy Virgin? Who will not hymn thy most pure childbearing? For the only-begotten Son, Who shone timelessly from the Father, *Resurrectional Theotokia of the Eight Tones*
the Same came forth from thee, O pure one, having become ineffably incarnate: being God by nature, and having become man by nature for our sake, not divided into two persons, but known in two natures without confusion. Pray Him, O pure and all-blessed one, that our souls may be shown mercy![^703]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
My Maker and Redeemer, Christ the Lord, having come forth from thy womb, O all-pure one, and clothed Himself in me, delivered Adam from the former curse. Therefore unto thee, O all-pure one, as truly the Mother of God and Virgin, we cry unceasingly with the angel: Rejoice! Rejoice, O Sovereign Lady, protection, shelter, and salvation of our souls![^704]
*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
Thou wast known as a Mother above nature, O Mother of God, yet didst remain a Virgin, above word and understanding; and the wonder of thy childbearing no tongue can declare. For the conception being most glorious, O pure one, the manner of the birth is incomprehensible: for where God so wills, the order of nature is overcome. Therefore knowing thee to be the Mother of God, we earnestly pray thee: Entreat that our souls may be saved![^705]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
All us earthborn fleeing under thy protection, O Sovereign Lady, cry aloud unto thee: O Mother of God, our hope, deliver us from immeasurable transgressions, and save our souls![^706]
*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
The King of heaven, through love for mankind, was revealed upon the earth and lived with men: for having taken flesh from the pure Virgin, and having come forth from her with the assumption, He is one Son, twofold in nature, but not in hypostasis. Therefore, truly proclaiming Him as perfect *Resurrectional Theotokia of the Eight Tones*
God and perfect man, we confess Christ our God. Whom entreat, O unwedded Mother, that our souls may be shown mercy![^707]
*At the Aposticha, Theotokion:*
O unwedded Virgin, who didst ineffably conceive God in the flesh, Mother of the Most High God: accept the supplications of thy servants, O all-immaculate one, granting unto all purification of transgressions! Receiving now our entreaties, pray that we all may be saved![^708]

## Resurrectional Dismissal Theotokia of the Eight Tones[^798]
*Tone 1. Theotokion:*
When Gabriel proclaimed unto thee, O Virgin: "Rejoice!", with that voice the Master of all was taking flesh in thee the holy ark, as the righteous David said: thou wast shown forth more spacious than the heavens, having borne thy Creator. Glory to Him Who dwelt within thee!

Glory to Him Who came forth from thee! Glory to Him Who freed us through thy childbearing![^799]
*Tone 2. Theotokion:*
All above understanding, all most glorious are thy mysteries, O Mother of God: with purity sealed and virginity preserved, thou wast known as a true Mother, having given birth unto the true God; pray Him that our souls may be saved![^800]
*Tone 3. Theotokion:*
Thee, who didst intercede for the salvation of our race, we hymn, O Virgin Mother of God: for having through the flesh received from thee accepted the Passion upon the Cross, thy Son and our God delivered us from corruption, as He is the Lover of mankind.[^801]
*Tone 4. Theotokion:*
The mystery hidden from the ages and unknown unto angels was through thee, O Mother of God, revealed unto those upon earth: God incarnate in unconfused union, and willingly accepting the Cross for our sake, whereby having resurrected the first-formed, He saved our souls from death.[^802]
*Tone 5. Theotokion:*
Rejoice, impassable door of the Lord! Rejoice, wall and protection of those who flee unto thee! Rejoice, untempest-tossed haven, and thou that knewest not wedlock, who didst bear in the flesh thy Creator and God: fail not in praying for those who hymn and worship thy childbearing![^803]
*Tone 6. Theotokion:*
Calling Thy Mother blessed, Thou didst come unto the Passion by voluntary counsel, shining forth upon the Cross, wishing to seek out Adam, saying unto the angels: Rejoice with Me, for the lost drachma is found! O our God Who hast wisely ordered all things, glory to Thee![^804]
*Tone 7. Theotokion:*
As the treasury of our resurrection, lead up from the pit and depth of transgressions those who hope in thee, O all-hymned one: for thou didst save those guilty of sin, having given birth unto our Salvation; who before childbearing wast a Virgin, and in childbearing a Virgin, and after childbearing again dost remain a Virgin.[^805]
*Tone 8. Theotokion:*
Thou Who for our sake wast born of a Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, O Good One, Who didst overthrow death by death, and reveal resurrection as God: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thine own hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O merciful One; receive the Mother of God who bare Thee, who intercedes for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people![^806]
