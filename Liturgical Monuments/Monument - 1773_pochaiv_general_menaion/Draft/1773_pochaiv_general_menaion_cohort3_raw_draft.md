*Sticheron 1:*

*Sticheron 2:*  
Having enlightened the inhabited earth with spiritual splendors, ye appeared as light-giving suns; as ye extend rays of divine commands unto all ends, ye enlighten the hearts of the faithful, O most blessed God-bearers, driving away the darkness of heresies by the power of Him Who dawned from the Virgin.

*Reading from the Proverbs (Prov. 10:7, 3:13–16, composite):*[^125]  
The memory of the righteous is with praises, and the blessing of the Lord is upon his head. Blessed is the man that finds wisdom, and the mortal that knows prudence. For it is better to buy her than treasures of gold and silver. And she is more precious than costly stones; and everything precious is not worthy of her. For length of days and years of life are in her right hand, and in her left hand are riches and glory. Out of her mouth proceeds righteousness, and law and mercy she carries upon her tongue. Give ear therefore unto me, O children, for I will speak honorable things; and blessed is the man that will keep my ways: for my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and desire is prepared from the Lord. For this cause I entreat you, and put forth my voice unto the sons of men: for I, wisdom, have built; counsel, knowledge, and understanding have I called upon. Mine is counsel and confirmation; mine is understanding, and mine is strength. I love them that love me, and they that seek me shall find grace. Understand craftiness therefore, ye simple ones, and apply your hearts, ye uninstructed. Give ear unto me again, for I will speak honorable things; and I will open right things from my lips, for my throat shall meditate on truth, and lying lips are an abomination before me. All the words of my mouth are with righteousness; there is nothing crooked nor perverse in them. All things are right unto them that understand, and plain to them that find knowledge. For I will teach you truth, that your hope may be in the Lord, and ye may be filled with the Spirit.

*Reading from the Proverbs (Prov. 10:31–32, 11:1–12):*[^126]  
The mouth of the righteous drops wisdom, but the tongue of the unrighteous shall perish. The lips of righteous men drop graces, but the mouth of the impious is perverse. False balances are an abomination before the Lord, but a just weight is acceptable unto Him. Wheresoever insolence enters, there also is dishonor; but the mouth of the humble meditates on wisdom. The perfection of the upright shall guide them, but the overthrow of the rebellious shall despoil them. Possessions shall not profit in the day of wrath, but righteousness shall deliver from death. When the righteous man dies, he leaves regret; but easy and laughed to scorn is the destruction of the impious. The righteousness of the blameless directs paths, but unrighteousness falls into impiety. The righteousness of upright men shall deliver them, but the lawless are taken in their own foolish counsel. When a righteous man dies, his hope does not perish; but the boast of the impious perishes. The righteous man escapes from the snare, and in his stead the impious is delivered up. In the mouth of the impious is a snare to citizens, but the perception of the righteous is prosperous. In the good prosperity of the righteous a city is set right, and at the destruction of the impious there is rejoicing. By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but by the mouth of the impious it is overthrown. A man void of understanding scoffs at citizens, but a wise man keeps silence.
