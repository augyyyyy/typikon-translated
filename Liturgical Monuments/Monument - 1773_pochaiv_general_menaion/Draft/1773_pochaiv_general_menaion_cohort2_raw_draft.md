*Tone 4. Ode 1. Heirmos:*[^71]  
Having traversed the deep of the Red Sea on foot with dryshod steps, ancient Israel of old, by Moses' hands stretched out in the form of a cross, vanquished the power of Amalek in the wilderness.

*Troparion 1:*  
As I desire to hymn the apostolic choir, O Christ, by their prayers, as God, grant me the radiance of the All-Holy Spirit and the light of Thy wisdom.

*Troparion 2:*  
Strengthened by Thy power and grace, O Christ, Thine honorable apostles destroyed the power of the adversary enemies, having become beholders of God by blameless beckoning toward Thee.

*Troparion 3:*  
Working healings in Thy name, O Master, the glorious apostles caught the gatherings of the gentiles into the knowledge of Thee, and enlightened them with Thy light.

*Troparion 4:*  
Taught heavenly wisdom, O most glorious apostles of Christ, truly wise, ye manifestly turned the wordiness of the impious into foolishness by the success of your preaching.

*Theotokion:*  
Thou didst bear, O all-pure one, One of the most divine Trinity, Who appeared in the flesh from thee according to our nature, by the good pleasure of Him Who begot Him, and by the operation of the All-Holy Spirit, O Virgin Mother.

*Ode 3. Heirmos:*[^72]  
Thy Church rejoices in Thee, O Christ, crying aloud: Thou art my strength, O Lord, and my refuge and my foundation!

*Troparion 1:*  
Speaking heavenly and honorable teachings on earth, preaching God, confirmed with fiery tongues, ye delivered them, O preachers of Christ.

*Troparion 2:*  
Thou didst show forth Thy disciples, O Master, as rational heavens declaring Thy glory unto all the ends of the earth.

*Troparion 3:*  
Being written in the heavens, and having appeared as habitations of Christ: preserve now, O all-wise ones, them that honor you with love.

*Theotokion:*  
He that dwells in the heights took up His abode among us, O all-pure one: for having taken flesh from thee without seed, He appeared.

*Sessional Hymn, Tone 8.*  
*Tone 8, Podoben: "Of Wisdom" (Премꙋ́дрости):*[^73]  
Having caught rational fish with the net of divine words, ye brought these as first-fruits unto our God; and desiring to be clothed in the wounds of Christ, ye were shown forth as imitators of His passions: therefore coming together, as is due we honor, O glorious apostles, your all-festive memory, and with one accord cry aloud unto you: Entreat Christ God, that He grant remission of sins unto them that with love honor your holy memory.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:*  
As a Virgin and the only one among women who without seed didst bear God in the flesh, all generations of men call thee blessed: for the Fire of the Godhead dwelt in thee, and thou sucklest the Creator and Lord as an infant: therefore the angelic and human race worthily glorify thy all-holy childbearing, and with one accord cry aloud unto thee: Entreat Christ God, that He grant remission of sins unto them that in faith worship thy most holy Offspring.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^75]  
Beholding Thee lifted up upon the Cross, O Sun of Righteousness, the Church stood in her place, worthily crying aloud: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!

*Troparion 1:*  
The sound of the divine words of the apostles went forth into all the universe like fire, burning up the material of delusion, and enlightening the congregations of the pious with grace.

*Troparion 2:*  
The world, blackened with the gloom of ungodliness, the disciples of the Lord enlightened as God-shining luminaries with the rays of grace, having appeared with the radiance of preaching.

*Troparion 3:*  
Enlightened with the sacred rays of the Noetic Sun, ye shine like the sun in the world, O all-praised ones, driving away the mist of delusion with divine light.

*Troparion 4:*  
Having Thy Cross as a rod of strength, Thine eyewitnesses of glorious life, O Word, cut through the sea, like horses troubling the waters of polytheism.

*Theotokion:*  
Adorned with diverse effusions of light like an animate heaven, bearing Thee, O Christ, King of kings, the all-pure Maiden is now glorified as the Birthgiver of God.

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^76]  
Thou, O my Lord, didst come into the world as a light, a holy light turning from the dark gloom of ignorance them that in faith hymn Thee.

*Troparion 1:*  
Thy divine and all-wise ministers Thou didst show forth as light in the world, proclaiming Thee unto all, the Unsetting Light.

*Troparion 2:*  
Having manifestly traversed every virtue, O apostles, destroy the tangled webs of diverse demonic malice.

*Troparion 3:*  
The apostles revealed unto us the Trinitarian brilliance in the unity of the Godhead, speaking with fiery tongues.

*Theotokion:*  
Thee do we set forth as an unconquerable weapon against our enemies; thee have we acquired as our foundation and the hope of our salvation, O Bride of God.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^77]  
I will sacrifice unto Thee with the voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries aloud unto Thee, having been cleansed from the blood of demons by the blood that for mercy's sake flowed from Thy side.

*Troparion 1:*  
Having established Thy disciples with wisdom and miracles, O Savior, Thou didst make them mightier than Hellenic babbling, and they destroyed its deceitful dictates.

*Troparion 2:*  
Divine rivers of wisdom filled all the church valleys with saving waters, enriching them with streams from the saving springs.

*Troparion 3:*  
Ye were shown forth as living stars, O all-praised ones; by radiant flashings scatter all dark delusion, having made the light of the knowledge of God to dawn.

*Theotokion:*  
He saw thee as a dove perfect and all-comely, finding a radiant lily and flower of the valleys, O Mother of God: the Noetic Bridegroom dwelt in thee.
