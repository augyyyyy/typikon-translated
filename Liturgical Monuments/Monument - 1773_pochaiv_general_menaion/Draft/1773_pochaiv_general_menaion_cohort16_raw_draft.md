*On Wednesday Evening, and on Thursday at "God is the Lord":*  
O noetic gate of life, all-pure Mother of God: deliver from perils those who flee unto thee with faith, that we may glorify thy most holy childbearing, unto the salvation of our souls.[^902]

*At the End of Matins:*  
O all-pure Virgin, save us: (*see above*).[^903]

*On Thursday Evening, and on Friday at "God is the Lord":*  
Beholding upon the Cross the Lamb, and Shepherd, and Savior of the world: (*see above*).[^904]

*At the End of Matins:*  
Rejoice, gate of the King of Glory: (*see above*).[^905]

*On Friday Evening, and on Saturday at "God is the Lord":*  
Who for our sake wast born of the Virgin, and didst endure crucifixion, O Good One, Who by death didst overthrow death, and reveal resurrection as God: despise not those whom Thou didst create with Thine own hand; show forth Thy love for mankind, O Merciful One; receive the Mother of God who bare Thee, who intercedes for us, and save, O our Savior, a despairing people![^906]

*At the End of Matins:*  
O all-pure Virgin, save us by thy prayers, stirring maternal compassion toward thy Son and our God.[^907]
