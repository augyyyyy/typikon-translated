## Common Service to Two and Many Female Martyrs

*At Vespers, at Lord, I have cried, Stichera, Tone 4.*  
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Thou hast given a sign" (Да́лъ є҆сѝ зна́менїе):*[^457]  
*Sticheron 1:*  
The virgin maidens, bound by the law of nature and certainly strengthened by love for their Creator, loosed the bond of bodily things by faith; and the powerless enemy they manfully crushed with their feet, and were radiantly adorned with victorious honor, and joyfully made their abode in the noetic bridal chamber.  
*Sticheron 2:*  
Enduring fire and manifold torments and death, O most honorable ones, possessing by faith the Bridegroom's most fair beauty, and adorned with diverse wounds, they were joined unto Him: therefore Jesus, the Lover of mankind and Savior of our souls, crowned them with many gifts!  
*Sticheron 3:*  
Incorruptible virginity, most marvelous virginity ye brought unto Christ, O good virgins, having cast down the insolence of the deception of godlessness by the power of the Cross with a manly mind: therefore all the churches of Christ celebrate your holy memory, radiant and most glorious!  
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*  
Having unsleeping prayer and abiding intercession unto the Lord, O all-pure one, lull misfortunes to sleep, calm the waves of my wretched soul, and comfort my heart which is in affliction, O Maiden, I pray thee, and bestow grace upon my mind, that I may worthily glorify thee!  
*Cross-Theotokion:*  
When the Ewe-lamb that gave Him birth beheld Thee, the Lamb and Shepherd, upon the Tree, she lamented and maternally spake unto Thee: O my most desired Son, how art Thou suspended upon the Wood of the Cross, O longsuffering One? How are Thy hands and Thy feet nailed by the lawless, O Word, and Thou hast poured forth Thy blood, O Master?  
*If thou celebratest with Polyeleos: At Lord, I have cried, Stichera on 6.*  
*Glory... Tone 8:*[^458]  
Let every tongue be moved to the praise of the all-praiseworthy female martyrs, every generation and age; let us, all young men and virgins, crown the great-martyrs of Christ with praises! For having lawfully shown courage and cast off female weakness, they cast down the enemy tormentor: adorned on account of their martyric labors with heavenly divine crowns, they ask of their Bridegroom and God to grant us great mercy!
