*Tone 3, Podoben: "Of divine faith" (Бж҃е́ственныѧ вѣ́ры):*[^352]  
Having been enriched with the chrism of anointing, ye were shown forth as pastors unto God-wise people; and as pure lambs ye sacrificed and offered yourselves unto the Word Who was first sacrificed as a sheep, O all-praised martyrs and universal luminaries: therefore with love we all celebrate your divine memory.  
*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*  
Our refuge and strength art thou, O Mother of God, sovereign help of the world; by thy prayers shelter thy servants from all necessity, O only blessed one.  
*Cross-Theotokion:*[^353]  
Having acquired a rod of power, the Cross of thy Son, O Mother of God, thereby do we cast down the insolence of enemies, unceasingly magnifying thee in love.

*Ode 4. Heirmos:*[^354]  
Beholding Thee, the Sun of righteousness, lifted up upon the Cross, the Church stood in its place, crying out as is meet: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!  
*Troparion 1:*  
The choir of sacred martyrs sings praise unto Thee, adorned with the grace of the hierarchal office and with martyrdom, crying aloud in gladness: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!  
*Troparion 2:*  
Being instructed in divine mysteries, O God-bearing fathers, ye pastured the people in holiness and righteousness, and received a divine end through martyrdom.  
*Troparion 3:*  
Adorned with apostolic and prophetic divine gifts, the divine choir of hieromartyrs cries aloud in gladness: Glory to Thy power, O Lord!  
*Theotokion:*  
Catechized by God-proclaiming utterances, O Mother of God, beholding the fulfillment of what was spoken of thee, we cry aloud: Rejoice, O all-immaculate Virgin, who by thy childbearing hast brought us deliverance!

*Ode 5. Heirmos:*[^355]  
Thou, my Lord, camest as a light into the world, a holy light turning those from dark ignorance who sing praise unto Thee in faith.  
*Troparion 1:*  
With the divine rays of most precious preaching, ye shone forth a grace-filled light upon those who sit in the darkness of deceit.  
*Troparion 2:*  
Now beholding Christ face to face, ye enjoy ineffable Light: pray Him for us!  
*Troparion 3:*  
The divine river of Christ's teachings was filled in you; stop up the streams of deceit, and firmly nourish the minds of the faithful.  
*Theotokion:*  
The Lord chose thee alone out of all generations, O Mother of God; incarnate of thee, He deified humanity.

*Ode 6. Heirmos:*[^356]  
I will sacrifice unto Thee with a voice of praise, O Lord, the Church cries unto Thee, cleansed from the gore of demons by the blood that for mercy's sake flowed from Thy ribs.  
*Troparion 1:*  
Having lived in courage and wisdom, O Lord, the choir of Thy saints now rejoices in Thee, in righteousness, prayer, and chastity.  
*Troparion 2:*  
Strengthened by the hope of eternal life, the valiant ones rejoiced as they endured bodily tearings, sacred zealots of the divine prophets!  
*Troparion 3:*  
Nurtured by spiritual laws, O most wise ones, ye offered saving laws unto the lawless, and brought them unto the light of the right faith.  
*Theotokion:*  
Adam was stripped of life unto deadness in paradise; but through thy childbearing, O pure Bride of God, he was made immortal by the life-creating One.

*If there is no Typikon, speak this Kontakion, Tone 3:*[^358]  
*Tone 3, Podoben: "Today the Virgin" (Дѣ́ва дне́сь):*[^359]  
As never-setting luminaries of the Spiritual Sun, coming together today let us praise you in songs; for ye shone forth unto those who were in the darkness of ignorance, summoning all unto the height of piety, O hieromartyrs. Therefore we cry unto you: Rejoice, foundation of all ascetics!  
*Ikos:*  
Shine upon me, O Jesus, Giver of life, Who in the beginning didst form divine beauty, that I may hymn Thy saints who struggled for Thee even unto death, and cast down the strength of the enemy, and refuting abominable godlessness, were established by the power of the Cross, O Christ, and worthily received glory from Thee: therefore we cry unto them: Rejoice, foundation of all ascetics!

*Ode 7. Heirmos:*[^360]  
In the Persian furnace the children of Abraham, burned rather by love of piety than by the flame, cried aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!  
*Troparion 1:*  
Enlightened with the chrism of the hierarchy, O God-wise ones, and made divinely comely on both sides with martyric blood, ye cry aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!  
*Troparion 2:*  
Directing earthly thoughts as in the twinkling of an eye, as in a ship of all-pure glory, O all-praised ones, being elevated, ye cry aloud: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!  
*Troparion 3:*  
Without knowing the opposite intention, ye put to shame every tormenting deceitful knowledge, O glorious hieromartyrs, singing: Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy glory, O Lord!  
*Theotokion:*  
He Who covered the earth with mist and the heaven with clouds, O Lord, clothed Himself in mortal flesh from thy blood, O Virgin, and clothed men in the garment of the glory of immortality.

*Ode 8. Heirmos:*[^361]  
Daniel, stretching out his hands, stopped the gaping mouths of lions in the pit; and the youths, zealots of piety, girded with virtue, quenched the power of the fire, crying aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!  
*Troparion 1:*  
Beaten with sinews, and with fleshly wounds, ye obtained incorruptible life, O hieromartyrs, and delivered all who were serving dead idols; who, being saved, cry aloud with you unceasingly: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!  
*Troparion 2:*  
As clusters of the spiritual vineyard, O hieromartyrs, ye now pour forth the wine of healing, and gladden the hearts of all who praise you in faith, and cry aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!  
*Troparion 3:*  
The tombs of the hieromartyrs poured forth wonders upon the universe, crushing the deceitful apparitions of the evil demon, and granting abundant healings unto those who cry aloud: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!  
*Theotokion:*  
He Who was seen as a man, yet was before all being, was numbered with double essence, O all-pure one; without alteration of actions or wills, He was made like us in His will, and appeared: Whom we sing in faith: Bless the Lord, all ye works of the Lord!

*Ode 9. Heirmos:*[^362]  
A cornerstone was cut without hands from thee, O Virgin, from that unhewn mountain, even Christ, Who joined together the separated natures: therefore rejoicing, we magnify thee, O Mother of God!  
*Troparion 1:*  
Celebrating the sacred memory of the holy hieromartyrs, come, let us all honor them with loud voice in sacred songs, as they pray for us unto our all-merciful God.
