## Common Service to a Single Prophet

*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," stichera, Tone 4.*  
*Tone 4, Podoben: "Called from on high" (Зва́нный свы́ше):*[^6]

*Sticheron 1:*  
Having received the ray of the most divine radiance through purity of mind, and having become a herald and signifier of the divine words and a divine prophet: thou didst appear as the God-moved mouth of the Spirit, proclaiming and making known unto all the nations the salvation that is given and the coming Kingdom of Christ, O [Name];[^7] Him do thou entreat, to save and enlighten our souls.

*Sticheron 2:*  
Radiant worthily with the vision of God and prophetic preeminence, and honored with grace, O God-inspired [Name], and accounted worthy of divine nearness: now in thy boldness toward the All-Good One and sustained by His mercy, pray unceasingly for those who in faith praise thee and honor thee as one who speaks of God, honorable and God-pleasing, that we may be delivered from tribulations and that our souls may be saved.

*Sticheron 3:*  
Thou hast shown Thy prophet, O [Name], as a living cloud, O Immortal One, pouring forth water unto life that is truly eternal, having sent him richly, and having granted the All-Holy Spirit, Who is consubstantial with Thee, the Father Almighty, and with Thy Son Who shone forth from Thy essence: through Whom he foretold the saving...

=== LEAF p4 ===

...the saving advent of Christ our God, and to proclaim salvation unto all nations.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:*  
For I have fallen into the depth of many transgressions from my laziness, wretched and despairing because of despondency and ignorance, and now I am held fast by despair. Be unto me help and cleansing and salvation, O most pure one, granting me timely consolation. I entreat thee, and unto thee, O beloved Virgin, do I fall down, crying unto thee with faith, lest I become entirely the prey and rejoice the deceiver.

*Cross-Theotokion:*[^8]  
Weep not for Me, O Mother, beholding upon the Tree hanging Thy Son and God, Who without support hung the earth upon the waters and created all creation; for I will arise and be glorified, and will crush the kingdoms of Hades with power and destroy his might, and deliver those who are bound from his malice, as I am tender-hearted; and I will bring them unto My Father, as I am the Lover of mankind.

*If thou celebratest with Polyeleos: On "Lord, I have cried," stichera on 6.*  
*Glory... Tone 6:*  
O prophet and herald of Christ, thou never departest from the Throne of majesty, and thou ever standest before each one who calls upon thee; serving in the highest, thou blessest the universe, being glorified everywhere: ask cleansing for our souls.

*Now and ever... Theotokion:*  
Who will not call thee blessed, O most holy Virgin?

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day, and the Readings:*  
*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Wisdom 3:1–9):*[^9]  
The souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and no torment shall touch them. In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to die, and their departure was taken for misery, and their going from us to be utter destruction; but they are in peace. For though they be punished in the sight of men, yet is their hope full of immortality. And having been a little chastened, they shall be greatly rewarded; for God proved them, and found them worthy for Himself. As gold in the furnace has He tried them, and received them as a burnt offering sacrifice. And in the time of their visitation they shall shine, and run to and fro like sparks among the stubble. They shall judge the nations, and have dominion over the people, and their Lord shall reign forever. They that put their trust in Him shall understand the truth; and such as be faithful in love shall abide with Him; for grace and mercy is to His saints, and He has care for His elect.

*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Wisdom 5:15–6:3):*[^10]  
The righteous live unto ages, and their reward is in the Lord, and the care of them is with the Most High. Therefore shall they receive a kingdom of glory, and a crown of beauty from the hand of the Lord; for with His right hand shall He cover them, and with His arm shall He protect them. He shall take to Him His jealousy for complete armor, and make the creature His weapon for the revenge of His enemies. He shall put on righteousness as a breastplate, and true judgment instead of a helmet. He shall take holiness for an invincible shield. His severe wrath shall He sharpen for a sword, and the world shall fight with Him against the unwise. Then shall the right-aiming thunderbolts go abroad, and from the clouds, as from a well-drawn bow, shall they fly to the mark. And hailstones full of wrath shall be cast as out of a stone bow, and the water of the sea shall rage against them, and the floods shall cruelly drown them. Yea, a mighty wind shall stand up against them, and like a storm shall blow them away: thus iniquity shall lay waste the whole earth, and ill dealing shall overthrow the thrones of the mighty. Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth. Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations. For power is given you from the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest.

*Reading from the Wisdom of Solomon (Wisdom 4:7–15):*[^11]  
Though the righteous man happen to die early, he shall be in rest. For honorable age is not that which stands in length of time, nor that is measured by number of years. But wisdom is the gray hair unto men, and an unspotted life is old age. He pleased God, and was beloved of Him; so that living among sinners he was translated. Yea, speedily was he taken away, lest wickedness should alter his understanding, or deceit beguile his soul. For the bewitching of naughtiness obscures things that are honest, and the wandering of concupiscence undermines the simple mind. He, being made perfect in a short time, fulfilled a long time; for his soul pleased the Lord: therefore He hastened to take him away from among the wicked. This the people saw, and understood it not, neither laid they up this in their minds, that His grace and mercy is with His saints, and that He has respect unto His chosen.

## Common Service to a Single Apostle

*At Vespers, on "Lord, I have cried," stichera, Tone 8.*  
*Tone 8, Podoben: "What shall we call you" (Что́ вы нарече́мъ):*[^28]

*Sticheron 1:*  
What shall we call thee, O apostle? Heaven, for thou hast declared the glory of God; a river, for thou mystically waterest creation; a star, illuminating the Church; a chalice, pouring forth holy drink; a closest friend of Christ; an equal citizen with the bodiless hosts: pray for the salvation of our souls.

*Sticheron 2:*  
O glorious apostle and beholder of God, thy feet were made beautiful, walking well in the path of preaching, and confining all enemy paths by the breadth of divine knowledge, through the Word Who appeared in the grossness of flesh and chose thee as a most glorious disciple, O blessed one: Him do thou entreat, that our souls may be saved.

*Sticheron 3:*  
Truly, O apostle and speaker of God, thou wast sent forth as a radiant arrow from Christ, wounding enemies, but manifestly granting healing unto wounded souls: therefore as is due we call thee blessed, and accomplish thy holy celebration today: pray that our souls may be saved.

*Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion:*  
Countless times, O most pure one, have I promised to make repentance for my sins, but the habitual love of my vices does not release me. Therefore I cry unto thee and fall down praying: do thou, O sovereign Lady, deliver me from such torment, guiding me unto better things that are close unto salvation.

*Cross-Theotokion:*[^29]

*Entrance. Prokeimenon of the day. And the Readings:*  
*Reading from the General Epistle of John (1 John 3:21–4:6):*[^30]  
Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, then have we confidence toward God. And whatsoever we ask, we receive of Him, because we keep His commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in His sight. And this is His commandment, That we should believe on the name of His Son Jesus Christ, and love one another, as He gave us commandment. And he that keeps His commandments dwells in Him, and He in him. And hereby we know that He abides in us, by the Spirit which He has given us. Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world. Hereby know ye the Spirit of God: Every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God: and every spirit that confesses not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is not of God: and this is that spirit of antichrist, whereof ye have heard that it should come; and even now already is it in the world. Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is He that is in you, than he that is in the world. They are of the world: therefore speak they of the world, and the world hears them. We are of God: he that knows God hears us; he that is not of God hears us not.
