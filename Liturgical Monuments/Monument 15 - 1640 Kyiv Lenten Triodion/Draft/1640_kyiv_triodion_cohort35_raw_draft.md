## The Conciliar Decree of the Holy Seventh Ecumenical Council. Bless, Father.[^918]

*The due annual thanksgiving unto God, on the day wherein we received the Church of God, with the exposition of the dogmas of piety, and the shaming of the malice of the impious.*

Following the prophetic utterances, and conforming ourselves unto the apostolic teachings, and being instructed by the evangelical narratives, we celebrate the Day of Dedication.[^919] For Isaiah said: *Keep dedication toward God, ye islands* (Isa. 41:1), enigmatically signifying the churches from among the Gentiles. Now the churches were not simply the structures and embellishments of temples, but the fulness of those who practice piety within them, and wherewith they serve the Godhead with hymns and doxologies.

And the Apostle, teaching this very thing, commands us to walk in newness of life (Rom. 6:4); and if anyone be a new creature in Christ, to be renewed (2 Cor. 5:17).

And the words of the Lord also indicate the prophetic intention: *It was,* He says, *the Feast of Dedication in Jerusalem, and it was winter* (John 10:22). Either a spiritual winter, wherein the Jewish race bound the sickle of foul murder and turmoil against the common Savior; or that which grieves the bodily senses by the change of the air unto coldness. And there was indeed upon us also a winter, not accidental, but truly a great winter of malice, pouring forth cruelty. But there hath blossomed forth for us by the grace of God an eternal spring, wherein we have come together to produce a thankful harvest in good deeds unto God!

So that we may say the words of the Psalm: *Summer and spring, Thou hast formed them; remember this* (Ps. 73[74]:17–18). For in truth the enemies who reproached the Lord, and who dishonored His holy veneration in the holy Icons, who were haughty and exalted in impiety, God cast them down with wonders, and trod underfoot upon the earth the insolence of their apostasy!

Nor did He despise the voice of those who cry aloud unto Him: *Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Thy servants, which I have held in my bosom, of many nations, wherewith Thine enemies have reproached, O Lord, wherewith they have reproached the footsteps of Thine Anointed* (Ps. 88[89]:51–52).[^920] Now the recompense of Christ was those who were redeemed by His death and who believed in Him, both by the word of the Preaching and by the depiction of the Icons, whereby the great work of the Dispensation...

=== LEAF p347 ===
...is made known unto those who are redeemed: by the Cross, and before the Cross, and after the Cross, and by His Passions and miracles. From which also the imitation of His Passions passes over unto the Apostles, and thence unto the Martyrs, and through them it extends even unto the Confessors and Ascetics.

Remembering this reproach, therefore, wherewith the enemies of the Lord reproached, wherewith they reproached the recompense of His Christ, our God, being appeased by His compassion and inclined by the prayers of His Mother, and furthermore by those of the Apostles and of all the Saints who were likewise insulted together with Him and dishonored in the Icons; that just as they suffered in the flesh, so also, as was fitting, they who shared in the insults against the venerable Icons should be judged at last; when He willed it, He hath accomplished today a second time that which He performed at first!

For at first indeed, after a certain time of many years of humiliation and dishonor of the holy Icons, He restored them unto His piety;[^921] and now, which is the second time, after nearly thirty years of affliction, He hath wrought for us unworthy ones a change from evil days, and deliverance for those in sorrow, and the proclamation of piety, and the confirmation of the veneration of Icons, and a festival bearing unto us all things conducive unto salvation![^922]

For upon the Icons we behold the Master's Passions for our sake: the Cross, the Tomb, hades slain and destroyed, the contests of the Martyrs, their crowns, and salvation itself, which our first Contest-master, Contest-giver, and Crowner wrought in the midst of the earth!
