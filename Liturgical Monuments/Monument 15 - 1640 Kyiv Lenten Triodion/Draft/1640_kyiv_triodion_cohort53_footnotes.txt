[^1379]: The liturgical rubric prescribes the reading of the Studite Catechesis of St. Theodore the Studite (*Оглаше́нїе студи́тово*) in the narthex following the First Hour, a customary feature of Kyivan Lavra monastic practice during Great Lent.
[^1380]: Markian Chapter 81 (Ма́ркова глава̀ па҃) governs the liturgical concurrence when the Feast of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos (March 25) coincides with the Third Sunday of Great Lent (Sunday of the Veneration of the Holy Cross).
