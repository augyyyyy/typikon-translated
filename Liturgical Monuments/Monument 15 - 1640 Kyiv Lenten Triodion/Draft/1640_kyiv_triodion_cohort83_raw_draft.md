## ON THE SAME WEDNESDAY OF THE SIXTH WEEK, AT VESPERS[^2852]

*On "Lord, I have cried," Stichera Idiomela, Tone 5:*
Being rich in passions, I am clothed with the deceitful garment of hypocrisy, taking delight in the evils of intemperance, and showing forth boundless unmercifulness, despising my mind which lies cast down before the doors of repentance, hungering after every good thing, and ailing through my neglectfulness. But do Thou, O Lord, make me a Lazarus poor in sins, lest desiring, I fail to receive a dipped fingertip to cool my tongue which suffers in unquenchable fire; but settle me in the bosom of the Patriarch Abraham, as the Lover of mankind![^2853]

*Martyrikon, Tone 5:*
With insatiable love of soul, ye denied not Christ, O holy martyrs; having endured diverse wounds of sufferings, ye cast down the cruelty of the torturers; having preserved unswerving and unharmed faith, ye were translated unto heaven: therefore, having attained boldness before Him, pray ye that great mercy may be granted unto us![^2854]

=== LEAF p823 ===
