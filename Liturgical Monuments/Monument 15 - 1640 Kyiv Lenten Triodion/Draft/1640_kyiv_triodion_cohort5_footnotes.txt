[^128]: Narrative woodcut illustration on leaf p44: Commemoration of all departed Orthodox Christians. [^129]: Meatfare Saturday (Saturday of the Souls / Ancestral Saturday): Dedicated to the universal commemoration of all Orthodox Christians who have fallen asleep throughout all ages in piety, particularly those who met unexpected, violent, or unrecorded deaths without the customary funeral rites.
