## On Monday of the Fourth Week of the Holy Fast

*At Matins, after the Six Psalms, Alleluia in the Tone of the Oktoechos; then the Trinity Hymns. After the 1st Kathisma, Sessional Hymns in the Oktoechos. After the 2nd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 8. By Kyr Joseph. Podoben: "Thou art risen from the tomb" (Воскре́слъ є҆сѝ):*[^1424]
*Sessional Hymn:* Let us the faithful bow down in worship before the all-honored Tree whereon the Creator of all was lifted up! It lies before us, sanctifying those who draw near with soul and body, and cleansing the defilement of sin from those who fast faithfully and ever hymn Christ, the only Benefactor!

*Glory: the same. Both now: Theotokion:*
The Word of the Father descended upon the earth, and the radiant Angel cried aloud unto the Theotokos: "Rejoice, O blessed one, who alone hast preserved the bridal chamber, having received the conception of the pre-eternal God and Lord, that as God He may save the human race from deception!"

*After the 3rd Kathisma, Sessional Hymn, Tone 3, Podoben: "Beholding the beauty of thy virginity" (Красотѣ̀ дѣ́вства):*[^1425]
*Sessional Hymn:* Having reached the midpoint of abstinence today, and having anticipated the veneration of Thy life-bearing Cross, O Christ, falling down before Thee we cry aloud: Great art Thou, O Lover of mankind, and great are Thy works, for Thou hast revealed Thy Holy Cross, before which we bow down in worship with fear, crying aloud: Glory, O Christ, to Thine extreme tender-heartedness!

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
As an uncultivated vineyard, O Virgin, thou hast budded forth the most beautiful cluster, pouring forth unto us the wine of salvation, gladdening the souls and bodies of all! Therefore, blessing thee as the cause of good things, we ever cry aloud unto thee with the Angel: Rejoice, thou who art full of grace!

=== LEAF p542 ===
*Triodion [Triōdion], by Kyr Joseph. Tone 8. Ode 1. Heirmos: "The armored Pharaoh" (Вѡрꙋже́нна Фараѡ́на):*[^1426]
*Troparion:* The radiant veneration of the Cross hath appeared unto all, sending forth the sunlike dawn of salvation, illuminating all who are held fast by the darkening of passions, as we hasten purely to embrace it!

*Troparion:* Cleansed by the grace of the fast, with a pure mind let us come and cry aloud unto the only Pure One with a voice of thanksgiving: Thou, O Word, hast poured forth Thy Blood for our sake, and by Thy Cross Thou cleansest us!

*Troparion:* Tripped up by the assaults of the serpent, I ever lie in a terrible fall! O Savior, Who by Thy Passion and Cross didst restore the fall of the first-created, restore me and guide me unto the fulfillment of Thy desires!

*Theotokion [Stavrotheotokion]:* Standing before Thy Cross, O Lord, she that knew not wedlock, beholding Thy wounds, O Master, wounded in soul, cried aloud: "Woe is me, my Child! Having escaped pangs in Thy birth-giving, now I am grievously wounded!"

*Another Canon, by Kyr Theodore. Tone 3. Heirmos: "Let us sing unto the Lord" (Пои́мъ Гдⷭ҇еви):*[^1427]
*Troparion:* This holy and light-bearing week sets forth the all-honored Cross before the world! Come, let us bow down in worship, having enlightened our souls with fear and love, glorifying in songs Christ Who was nailed thereon!

*Troparion:* Before Thy Cross we all bow down in worship, O Christ, celebrating the passage unto Thy Resurrection; we sing praises unto Thy Nails, and we revere Thy Spear, with lips and eyes, being cleansed by them in soul and body!

*Glory: Triadikon:*
O Trinity, praised as one Essence in Unity,...

=== LEAF p543 ===
...Father, and Son, with the Holy Spirit: unoriginate, co-eternal,[^1428] uncreated Godhead, Wellspring of light and life, before Thee all we of the earth bow down in worship, unceasingly magnifying Thee!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
We hymn thee, O Virgin pure Theotokos, chariot of the Cherubim, from whom God was born! For thou alone hast been a fountain of incorruption, pouring forth life unto all, drawing from which we receive healing!

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*
*Troparion:* Reaching the midpoint of the forty-day stadium of the fast, come, with love let us go with Christ unto the Divine Passion, that being crucified together with Him, we may become partakers of His Resurrection!

*Katavasia: Heirmos:*
Let us sing unto the Lord Who wrought marvelous wonders in the Red Sea: for He covered the adversaries with the deep, and saved Israel (Exod. 14:28–30; 15:1); unto Him alone let us sing, for He hath been glorified!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: "Sevenfold the furnace" (Седмь седми́цею):*[^1429]
*Troparion:* Having my soul and mind wounded by poisonous passions, O Lord, have mercy, show compassion, protect me by Thy Cross, and heal me with the drops poured forth from Thy ribs (John 19:34), that I may fervently sing: "Ye Priests, bless; ye people, hymn and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!"

*Troparion:* Bent down by passions, taken captive by enemies, dragged away by evil custom: show compassion, O Merciful One, protect me with Thy Cross, that with an unwavering heart I may sing: "Ye Children, bless; ye Priests, hymn and supremely exalt Him!"

*Troparion:* Of old on the mountain Moses prefigured thee, O all-honored Cross, lifting up his hands and conquering Amalek (Exod. 17:11–12);[^1430] bowing down before which today, we conquer demonic legions and sing in faith: "Ye Priests, bless!"

*Both now: Theotokion [Stavrotheotokion]:*

=== LEAF p544 ===
...Groaning in anguish, crying out mother-like, and unable to endure the maternal yearning of thine inner parts, thou didst look upon the Cross at Him Who was born of thy womb, crying aloud: "Child, what sight is this! How sufferest Thou, Who art impassible by nature, desiring by all means to deliver the human race from corruption?"

*Another Heirmos [Tone 3, Ode 8]: "Unceasingly by Angels" (А҆́гг҃лы немо́лчнѡ):*[^1431]
*Troparion:* Beholding the Cross of Christ set forth in the church, with fear and faith, brethren, drawing near let us bow down in worship, crying aloud unto it: "Thou hast blossomed forth life unto men, O radiant one!"

*Troparion:* Victorious in battles, heaven hath revealed thee as life-creating unto us, O Cross, an invincible weapon for sovereigns, casting down of enemies, horn of the Church, and salvation for the faithful!

*Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord.*
*Triadikon:* I glorify the Holy Father, and I revere the Holy Son, and I hymn the Holy Spirit: the consubstantial,[^1432] simple Trinity, each of Them God, lights and Light, proceeding as from a single sun!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
Rejoice, O impassable Gate (Ezek. 44:2); rejoice, O unburnt Bush (Exod. 3:2); rejoice, O Golden Urn (Heb. 9:4); rejoice, O Unquarried Mountain (Dan. 2:34, 45);[^1433] rejoice, O Theotokos, indestructible hope and rampart of them that put their trust in thee!

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*
*Troparion:* Having reached the midpoint of the fast, let us take courage in spirit for what is to come with youthful vigor, running well with God, brethren, that we may joyfully behold also the Pascha of Christ, Who is risen!

*We praise, we bless, and we worship the Lord.*
*Katavasia: Heirmos:*
God, Who is unceasingly glorified on high by the Angels: ye heavens of heavens, earth and mountains, and hills, and the abyss, and all the human race, with hymns bless, sing praises, and supremely exalt Him as Creator and Deliverer unto the ages!

=== LEAF p545 ===
*Ode 9. Heirmos: "Heaven was astonished" (ᲊ҆страши́сѧ):*[^1434]
*Troparion:* Blessing his grandchildren of old, Jacob clearly prefigured the Precious Cross (Gen. 48:13–14);[^1435] whereby having shown compassion, bless us, granting sanctifying grace unto them that bow down in worship before it, and who glorify Thee as the Deliverer of all!

*Troparion:* Cleansed by the water of fasting in both soul and mind, ye faithful, let us embrace the Life-giving and Divine Tree! For it manifestly lies before all, pouring forth Divine forgiveness, and heavenly light, and life, and true rejoicing!

*Troparion:* I know of the awesome judgment seat coming in the glory of God, when Thou, O Lord, art about to expose the hidden secrets of all! Therefore I cry aloud unto Thee, O Good One: Forgive me who have sinned, and expose not my many and grievous transgressions!

*Theotokion [Stavrotheotokion]:* The Ewe-lamb of old, beholding the Lamb nailed upon the Cross, and the sun's radiance darkened, weeping cried aloud: "O never-setting Sun, my Son, Thou settest, being put to death! But shine forth the light of the Resurrection unto them that love Thee!"

*Another Heirmos [Tone 3, Ode 9]: "On Mount Sinai" (На Сїна́итѣй горѣ̀):*[^1436]
*Troparion:* In the place where Thy feet stood, O Master and God (Ps. 131:7 LXX),[^1437] let us all bow down in worship today before the all-honored Cross, even as David the most God-inspired cries aloud: for He Who holds all things in His palm was lifted up thereon!

*Troparion:* Ye that dwell upon earth, draw near with pure fasting, and beholding the all-honored Cross lying before you, bow down in worship with fear, rejoice in faith, and draw sanctification for your souls with joy!

*Glory: Triadikon:*
A simple, indivisible Monad in essence is the all-divine Trinity,...

=== LEAF p546 ===
...Trinity united in essence, yet divided properly in Persons: undivided yet severed, consubstantial yet revered in Trinity; This is Father, and Son, and the Living Spirit, Who preserve all things!

*Both now: Theotokion:*
"Who hath ever heard of a virgin bearing a Child, and a Mother without a husband? Mary, thou workest a wonder! But tell me, how?" "Inquire not into the depths of my God-bearing; for this is truly beyond the comprehension of the human mind!"

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*
*Troparion:* Let all of us people offer a hymn of thanksgiving unto God, as beholding now the Precious Cross, we embrace it! Understand therefore and submit yourselves, all ye nations;[^1438] understand, ye barbarian nations, and submit yourselves, for on account of Him God is with us (Isa. 8:9–10 LXX)!

*Katavasia: Heirmos:*
Moses, having seen thee on Mount Sinai in the bush, receiving unburnt the Fire of the Godhead in thy womb; and Daniel saw thee as the Unquarried Mountain; and Isaiah cried aloud: "A Rod budded forth from the root of David!"[^1439]

*Exapostilarion in the Oktoechos.*

*At the Aposticha: Stichera Idiomela, Tone 6:*
As an exalted path of exaltation, O Christ, Thou hast shown humility, having emptied Thyself and taken the form of a servant (Phil. 2:7);[^1440] not giving ear unto the boastful prayers of the Pharisee, but receiving on high the contrite groaning of the Publican as a spotless sacrifice (Luke 18:10–14)! Therefore unto us also who cry aloud: "Cleanse me, O God, cleanse me! O my Savior, save me!"

*Verse: We were filled in the morning with Thy mercy, O Lord, and we rejoiced and were glad (Ps. 89:14 LXX):*
*And: And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us (Ps. 89:17 LXX):*
*Martyrikon:*
O Lord, at the memory of Thy Saints all creation keeps feast: the heavens rejoice with the Angels, and the earth makes glad with men! By their prayers, have mercy on us!

*Glory, both now: Theotokion:*
Thou hast received the Archangel's word, and hast appeared as the Cherubic Throne,...

=== LEAF p547 ===
...thou hast appeared, and in thine arms thou hast borne, O Theotokos, the Hope of our souls!

*Then: "It is good to give thanks unto the Lord" (Ps. 91:2 LXX). Trisagion through "Our Father." Troparion of the Day; Litanies, and Three Great Prostrations. First Hour, and complete Dismissal.*

*At the First Hour, we perform the veneration of the Precious Cross, even as on Sunday, according to the prescribed pattern: singing instead of "Order my steps": "Before Thy Cross we bow down in worship, O Master" (see fol. 506).[^1441] In like manner also on Wednesday and Friday, these four days. And on Friday we take back the Cross, even as it shall be set forth there (see fol. 562).[^1442]*

*On Monday of the Fourth Week of Lent, at the Sixth Hour. Troparion of the Prophecy, Tone 7:*
O Lord, rebuke not in Thine anger the people who have sinned, nor punish us in Thy wrath (Ps. 6:2 LXX),[^1443] for Thou art good; for all the earth glorifies Thee, and we pray Thee: Spare us, O Holy One!

*Glory... the end: "and we pray Thee: Spare us, O Holy One!" Both now... the whole troparion.*

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 61, Tone 6: In God is my salvation and my glory (Ps. 61:8 LXX):*
*Verse: Shall not my soul be subjected unto God? For from Him is my salvation (Ps. 61:2 LXX):*

*Reading from the Prophecy of Isaiah (Isa. 14:24–32):*
Thus says the Lord of hosts: As I have spoken, so shall it be; and as I have purposed, so shall it stand: to destroy the Assyrians upon My land, and upon My mountains; and they shall be trodden under foot, and their yoke shall be taken away from them, and their dominion shall be wiped away from their shoulders. This is the purpose which the Lord hath purposed against the whole world; and this is the hand that is lifted high over all the nations. For what the Holy God hath purposed, who shall bring to naught? And His hand that is lifted high, who shall turn it away? In the year that King Ahaz died, this word came to pass: Rejoice not, all ye strangers, because

And the poor shall be pastured by Him, and needy men shall rest in peace; but He shall destroy thy seed with famine, and shall slay thy remnant. Wail, ye gates of cities! ## On the Same Monday Evening, at "Lord, I Have Cried"

*Stichera, Tone 8, Podoben: "What shall we call you, O holy ones" (Что́ ва́съ нарече́мъ):*[^1444]
*Sticheron:* Passing today through the middle days of abstinence, by the power of the Cross we give glory unto the Savior and God Who was lifted up thereon in the midst of all the earth (Ps. 73:12 LXX),[^1445] crying aloud: Reveal us as beholders of Thy Passion, O Master, and of Thy Precious Resurrection, granting us cleansing and great mercy!

*Sticheron:* Let us subdue the carnal passions by setting aside meats and turning away from pleasures, and let us faithfully embrace the Tree of the Cross; for it is manifestly seen being worshipped, sanctifying all by Divine grace; and unto the Lord let us cry aloud: We thank Thee, O Master, Who by it savest our souls!

*Another Sticheron, Tone 8, Podoben: "The Mother of God" (Бг҃оро́дицꙋ):*[^1446]
*Sticheron:* Now bowing down in worship before the Cross, let us all cry aloud: Rejoice, O Tree of life! Rejoice, O holy scepter of Christ! Rejoice,...

=== LEAF p549 ===
...O heavenly glory of earthly men! Rejoice, O boast of sovereigns! Rejoice, O dominion of the faith! Rejoice, O invincible weapon! Rejoice, O banishment of enemies! Rejoice, O radiant illumination, salvation of the world! Rejoice, O great glory of Martyrs! Rejoice, O confirmation of the Venerable! Rejoice, O brightness of Angels! Rejoice, O all-honored one!

*And from the Menaion, 3 stichera. Glory, both now: Theotokion. Entrance. "O Gladsome Light."*

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 63, Tone 6: Hear, O God, my prayer, when I make supplication unto Thee (Ps. 63:2 LXX):*
*Verse: Deliver my soul from fear of the enemy (Ps. 63:2b LXX):*

*Reading from the Book of Genesis (Gen. 8:21–9:4):*
God said: I have considered in My heart, and I will not again curse the earth because of the works of men, for the imagination of man is inclined toward evil from youth; neither will I again smite all living flesh, as I have done. While all the days of the earth remain, seed-time and harvest, cold and heat, summer and spring, day and night, shall not cease. And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them: Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and have dominion over it. And the dread and fear of you shall be upon all the beasts of the earth, and upon all the birds of the heaven, and upon all that move upon the earth, and upon all the fishes of the sea; under your hands have I delivered them. And every creeping thing which is alive shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things. But flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof, shall ye not eat.

=== LEAF p550 ===
...For surely your blood, the life of your souls, will I require at the hand of all beasts; and from the hand of man, of a brother, will I require it. Whoever sheds man's blood, in place of his blood shall his blood be shed; for in the image of God have I made man. But as for you, increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and have dominion over it!

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 64, Tone 6: Hear us, O God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth (Ps. 64:6 LXX):*
*Verse: Unto Thee, O God, praise is meet in Zion, and unto Thee shall a vow be performed in Jerusalem (Ps. 64:2 LXX):*

*Reading from the Proverbs (Prov. 11:19–28):*[^1447]
A righteous son is born unto life; but the pursuit of the ungodly is unto death. Crooked ways are an abomination before the Lord; but all that are blameless in their way are acceptable unto Him. He that lays hands unjustly hand to hand shall not go unpunished; but he that sows righteousness shall receive a faithful reward. As an earring of gold in the snout of a swine, so is beauty unto an evil-minded woman. All the desire of the righteous is good; but the hope of the ungodly shall perish. There are some who, while scattering their own, make it more; and there are some who, while gathering what is another's, suffer lack. Every simple soul is a blessed soul; but a wrathful man is unsightly. He that hoards grain, let him leave it also unto the nations; he that sells grain greedily is cursed of the people; but blessing is upon the head of him that gives. He that does good seeks good favor; but as for him that seeks evil, it shall overtake him. He that trusts in his riches, the same shall fall; but he that upholds the righteous, the same shall arise.
