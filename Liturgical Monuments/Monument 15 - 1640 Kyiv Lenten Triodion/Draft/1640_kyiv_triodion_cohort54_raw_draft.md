=== LEAF p531 ===
...to do so; easing also our labors by setting before our eyes the afflicted suffering of Christ, and by the remembrance and hope of that glory and majesty which is given because of the Cross.[^1394] For as our Savior, having mounted the Cross, was glorified because of the unseemly degradation and mockery, and very great bitterness and torment: so it behooves us also to do, that we may be glorified together with Him, even though we suffer ungratefully and without any sweetness. And explaining this yet in another way:
As those who travel a harsh and long road, being wearied by that labor, if anywhere they find a leafy and broad-spreading tree making a great shade, sit down a little and rest, and being as it were refreshed from rest, finish the remainder of the journey: so now also in this time of the fast, on this arduous and laborious road, in this solemn course, the Life-bearing Cross hath been planted and set in the midst by the Holy Fathers, to grant us some relief and breathing space, or rather strength, and to equip those wearied for further labor with steadfastness and lightness. Or as is customary at the time of the arrival of a King, his coats of arms and scepters are carried before him, and then after them he himself arrives, rejoicing and triumphing over victory, and gladness rejoices together with all: so also our Lord Jesus Christ, desiring to show forth so great a triumph from the victory over death, and to go forth with glory on the day of His Resurrection from the dead, sent beforehand His Scepter, the Royal Sign, the Life-creating Cross, filling us with great joy and strengthening, and preparing us to be ready to receive and praise the King Himself Who comes after it, Who is to triumph gloriously. And that the Precious Cross is set forth in the middle week of the Holy Forty Days is also because the Holy Forty-Day Fast is like the spring at Marah (cf. Exod. 15:23–25),[^1395] by reason of the contrition, bitterness, and hardship that is found in us from the fast. As therefore in the midst of that spring Divine Moses placed a tree, and made it sweet: so also God, leading us through the noetic Red Sea, and delivering us from the invisible Pharaoh, by the Life-giving Tree of the Precious and Life-creating Cross sweetens the bitterness that proceeds from the Forty-Day Fast, and reminding us

=== LEAF p532 ===
and granting us consolation, as if having watered those who dwell there, which is to lead us by His Resurrection from the dead even unto the noetic Jerusalem. Or since the Cross is called the Tree of Life, and that Tree of Life was planted in the midst of the Garden of Eden (Gen. 2:9):[^1396] fittingly and appropriately have the Most Divine Fathers planted the Tree of the Cross in the midst of the Holy Forty Days, both that they might bring to our remembrance the place of Adam's brightness, and that through this present tree they might depict for us the blotting out and fall of that former one; for tasting of this Tree of the Cross, we do not die from that time forth, but shall be alive. By its power, O Christ God, preserve us also from the malice of temptation and assaults, and make us worthy to render worship unto Thy Divine Sufferings and Life-bearing Resurrection from the dead; and vouchsafe to grant strength unto the joyous completion of the Holy Forty-Day Fast, and have mercy on us, as the only good and man-loving God, unto Whom belong honor, glory, and dominion, now and always, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
