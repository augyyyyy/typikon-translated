[^689]: Elevation without lifting (*не воздвизаетъ*): At the Presanctified Liturgy, the priest touches the Holy Bread beneath the veil without elevating It high, since the sacrificial offering took place when It was consecrated on Sunday.

[^691]: Fasting rubric: Strict xerophagy (*сухояденіе*) is appointed on Wednesday evening after the Presanctified Liturgy, consisting of nuts, figs, dates or dried fruit (*аниды*), and olives, with warm water and honey (*укропъ с медомъ*).
