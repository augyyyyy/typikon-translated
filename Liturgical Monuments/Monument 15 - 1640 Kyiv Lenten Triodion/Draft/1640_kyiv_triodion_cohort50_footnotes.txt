[^1323]: Gen. 7:11 LXX: The Septuagint and Church Slavonic witness records the onset of the Deluge "in the six hundred and first year of the life of Noah, in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month" (LXX: ἐν τῷ ἑξακοσιοστῷ καὶ πρώτῳ ἔτει ἐν τῇ ζωῇ τοῦ Νῶε, τοῦ δευτέρου μηνός, ἑβδόμῃ καὶ εἰκάδι τοῦ μηνός), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("in the six hundredth year... in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month").
[^1324]: Prov. 10:5a LXX: The Septuagint and Church Slavonic tradition contains an additional proverb (Prov. 10:5a) absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text: "A man of understanding is saved from scorching heat, but a lawless son is smitten by the wind in harvest" (διεσώθη ἀπὸ καύματος ἀνὴρ νοήμων, ἀνεμοφόρητος δὲ γίνεται ἐν ἀμήτῳ υἱὸς παράνομος).
[^1325]: Prov. 10:10 LXX: The second hemistich in the Septuagint and Church Slavonic reads "but he that reproveth with boldness maketh peace" (ὁ δὲ ἐλέγχων μετὰ παρρησίας εἰρηνοποιεῖ), contrasting with the Masoretic Text's repetition of "a prating fool shall fall".
