On this Third Sunday of the Fasts, we celebrate the veneration of the Precious and Life-giving Cross, for the following reason:[^1393] Inasmuch as through the forty-day fast, in a certain manner, we also are crucified, being mortified by passions, and having already the weariness of bitterness and hardship, living intemperately, and falling down: the Precious and Life-giving Cross is set forth, as it were giving heart, strengthening, and invigorating us; likewise bringing to our remembrance the Sufferings of our Lord Jesus Christ, and granting us consolation: if our God was crucified for us, how much more does it behoove us for His sake to do so!
