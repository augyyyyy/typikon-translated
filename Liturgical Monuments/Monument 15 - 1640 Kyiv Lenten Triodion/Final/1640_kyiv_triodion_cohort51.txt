*If the Apodosis of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos fall on the Saturday, then here on Thursday at Compline we sing the Four-Ode Canon in advance. Thus do we when it falls on the Fourth Saturday, on March 26, or on the Fifth.*[^1328]

## ON FRIDAY OF THE THIRD WEEK OF THE HOLY FAST

*At Matins, the Triadica of the Tone. After the 1st Stichology, the Sessional Hymn in the Octoechos. After the 2nd Stichology, Sessional Hymn, of Kyr Joseph. Tone 5, Podoben: "The Word co-unoriginate" (Собезнача́лное):*[^1329]

We who died of old through the eating from the tree have been quickened by Thy Cross, O compassionate One! By its power, O good One, strengthen us to pass through the season of abstinence in compunction, doing Thy will, and to behold the day of Thy radiant Resurrection! *(Twice)*

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion:*

Beholding Thee upon the Cross, O Christ, suspended voluntarily in the midst of thieves, Thy Mother, beating her maternal womb, cried aloud: "O sinless Son, how art Thou unjustly lifted up upon the Cross as an evildoer, desiring as Thou art compassionate to quicken the human race?"

*After the 3rd Stichology, Sessional Hymn, of Kyr Theodore. Tone 4, Podoben: "Thy tomb, O Savior" (Гро́бъ тво́й):*[^1330]

Having cleansed our flesh through abstinence, and having enlightened our souls in prayers, O Lord, vouchsafe us to behold Thy most honorable and divine Cross, and to venerate it with fear, singing in hymns and saying: "Glory to the life-bearing Cross, glory to Thy divine Spear, whereby we have been quickened, O Thou Who alone art the Lover of mankind!" *(Twice)*

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion, the same Podoben:*

=== LEAF p502 ===
Beholding the Lamb suspended upon the Tree with thieves, O longsuffering Word, and pierced in the ribs with a Spear, the Ewe-lamb wailed, crying aloud maternally: "What is this strange and terrible mystery, O my Jesus? And how is the uncircumscribed God covered by a tomb? Ineffable is that which is wrought! Leave me not alone who bare Thee, O my sweetest Jesus!"

*Triode, of Kyr Joseph. Tone 5.*[^1331]
*Ode 5. Heirmos: "Thou Who coverest Thyself with light" (Ѡдѣ́ѧйсѧ свѣ́томъ):*[^1332]

*Troparion:* Heal me, O Lord, and let my soul be healed by Thy wound, having cleansed my wounds, O Christ Who in the flesh didst receive the Passions!

*Troparion:* When the sun beheld Thee suspended in the flesh upon the Tree, O Christ, it turned its light into darkness, and the earth was shaken, and the rocks were split asunder! (Matt. 27:45, 51).

*Troparion:* Having cleansed my soul that is overgrown with thorns and made desolate with the forms of material passions, O Lord Who wast crowned with thorns, grant me Thine exceeding grace!

*Theotokion:* In a new manner hast thou borne as an Infant my Creator, O pure Lady; pray Him, O Sovereign Lady, to renew me who have grown old in manifold wickedness!

*Another Heirmos, of Kyr Theodore. Tone 4.*
*Heirmos: "Rising early from the night" (Ѿ но́щи ѹ҆́треннююще):*[^1333]

*Troparion:* In the Place of the Skull, the Jews crucified Thee, O Christ, shaking their heads, mocking and scoffing; but Thou didst endure, desiring to save us! (Matt. 27:33, 39).

*Troparion:* Having written threefold upon the title of Thy Cross (John 19:19–20), Pilate revealed Thee as One of the Trinity Who didst suffer voluntarily, O Christ, ordaining salvation!

=== LEAF p503 ===
*Triadikon:* Let us, the faithful, hymn the three-hypostatic Unity of the all-radiant Trinity, honoring the Light of the Father, glorifying the Light of the Son, and proclaiming the Light of the Spirit!

*Theotokion:* He Who is higher than angels was revealed as born of thee, O pure Lady; for Him Whom they tremble to behold as God, thou barest upon thine arms as Thine own Son, O pure Maiden!

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*

*Troparion of the Cross:* O all-honorable Cross, saving guardian of the world, preserve me as I fast, and show me worthy of thine honorable veneration!

*Katavasia Heirmos:* Rising early from the night, we hymn Thee, O Christ God, Who for our sake didst become poor (2 Cor. 8:9), and didst endure the Cross and death in Thy flesh!

*Ode 8. Heirmos: "Thee, the Creator of all" (Тебѐ вседѣ́телю):*[^1334]

*Troparion:* Thou didst stretch out Thy palms upon the Cross, O Christ, abolishing the incontinence of the forefather's hands; and by the Tree Thou didst heal the curse of the tree; therefore we hymn Thee unto all ages!

*Troparion:* Unto Thee, O Word, Who willest salvation, I fall down; do Thou swiftly remove all-wicked desires from my heart, O Thou Who didst voluntarily endure crucifixion and passions!

*Troparion:* Having desired a prodigal life and a defiled conduct, I, the wretched one, have been darkened; shine upon me the ray of conversion, O Word, Who didst darken the shining of the sun in Thy Passion!

*Theotokion:* Put an end to my manifold-painful, all-heartfelt malice, O Mary, Mother of Christ; crush the arrows and bows of the demons who wage war against my humble soul!

*Another Heirmos. Tone 4, Heirmos: "The hymn-singers in the furnace" (Пѣсносло́вца въ пе́щи):*[^1335]

*Troparion:* What shall we, the wretched ones, offer unto Thee Who didst suffer, O Master of all? For for our sake Thou didst accept the Cross, O Christ; we hymn the goodness of Thy boundless mercy!

=== LEAF p504 ===
*Troparion:* Having slain Thee through envy upon the accursed tree (Gal. 3:13), O Christ, the lawless Jews did not diminish the dominion of Thy glory, for Thou art the Master of all Who didst suffer voluntarily!

*We bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord:*

*Triadikon:* I honor the three Persons of the super-essential Godhead: the unoriginate Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit; I glorify the Essence unknown by nature unto the ages!

*Theotokion:* Higher than the highest, and more glorious than the Cherubim, the holy-first and undefiled Maiden, the Mother of the God of all, we hymn and supremely exalt unto the ages!

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*

*Troparion of the Cross:* O most blessed Cross of Christ, uncircumscribed victory, that hast sprouted forth from the earth for us: show us all worthy of thy veneration, to behold and to venerate thee!

*We praise, we bless, we worship the Lord:*

*Katavasia Heirmos:* Singing praise unto Christ God, Who saved the children in the furnace, and changed the burning furnace into dew: hymn and supremely exalt Him unto the ages!

*Ode 9. Heirmos: "O Isaiah, dance!" (И҆са́їа ликꙋ́й):*[^1336]

*Troparion:* Soften the petrified hardness of my soul, O Christ, and grant me a fountain of divine compunction, O good One, Who from Thy ribs didst pour forth life for me, consuming the torrents of my lawlessness!

*Troparion:* Having cast myself into the depth of sins, having abandoned the height of virtues: yet draw me and save me, O Lord Who didst ascend upon the Cross and didst draw mankind unto Thyself! (John 12:32).

*Troparion:* Being Sweetness and delight, as Creator Thou didst taste gall, O Christ, correcting that falling-away from sweetness which came from Adam; therefore, having been saved by Thy Passions, we hymn Thee!

*Theotokion:*

=== LEAF p505 ===
O all-immaculate Sovereign Lady, my hope and refuge: heal the wounds of my soul, bring peace unto my mind, that I also, rejoicing, may hymn thy great deeds, O Theotokos, Ever-Virgin!

*Another Heirmos. Tone 4, Heirmos: "Thee Whom Moses beheld" (Тѧ̀ ю́же ви́дѣ):*[^1337]

*Troparion:* Thee Whom the lawless, O Christ, mockingly clothed in purple and a crown, mocking Thee as King, striking Thy head with a reed, crucifying Thee out of envy, and giving Thee gall to drink: with hymns all we the faithful magnify! (Matt. 27:28–30, 34).

*Troparion:* Thee Whom the sun, seeing Thee suffering upon the Cross, hid its light, while all creation together bowed down at the Master's outrage, and the rocks split asunder: with hymns, O Christ the Savior, we magnify!

*Triadikon:* Light and Life and Creator, the three-hypostatic Unity, God and Lord, shining with one light, flashing forth with three characters in one Godhead: let all we the faithful worship Him!

*Theotokion:* Thee whom David the psalmist proclaimed as Zion (Ps. 131:13 LXX [132:13]), wherein He dwelt Who cannot be contained by the heavens, and fashioned from thy womb a cleansing for the world: with hymns, O pure Mother, we magnify!

*Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee.*

*Troparion of the Cross:* Vouchsafe us, O Lord, who run by abstinence, to anticipate, kiss, and venerate Thy saving Cross, the enlightener of the faithful, unto our sanctification, that for its sake we may magnify Thee!

*Katavasia Heirmos:* Thee Whom Moses beheld as the unburnt Bush (Ex. 3:2), and the animate Ladder which Jacob saw (Gen. 28:12), and the impassable Gate through which Christ passed (Ezek. 44:2): with hymns, O pure Mother, we magnify!

*It is truly meet:*

*Exaposteilarion, Tone 4:*[^1338]

*At the Aposticha: Idiomelon Sticheron, Tone 6:*

I have squandered the wealth of the Father's gift; in prodigality have I spent my life; by wicked thoughts I was bound in my sin, and taking pleasure in the sweetness of passion, I became like the senseless beasts through the transgression of the saving commandments! But O Thou Who wast well-pleased to be suspended upon the Cross for my sake, O Christ God, cast me not away, Thy servant, but receive me as I turn back, even as the Prodigal Son, and save me! *(Twice)*

*The verses, (see fol. 74)[^1339]:*

*Martyrikon:* O Lord, in the memory of Thy saints, all creation keeps festival; the heavens rejoice with the angels, and the earth makes glad with men; through their prayers have mercy on us!

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion, Tone 6, Podoben: "Having risen on the third day" (Тре́тїй дн҃ь):*[^1340]

When the all-immaculate Virgin beheld Thee suspended upon the Cross, weeping she cried aloud maternally: "O my Son and my God, my sweetest Child, how endurest Thou shameful suffering?"

*On the same Friday of the Third Week: At the Sixth Hour, Troparion of the Prophecy, Tone 8:*

By Thy word Thou hast ordered all things by Thy power, O God, and brought us from non-being into being; deliver us not up unto our iniquities, O sinless Lord!

*Glory... the end: Both now... the same.*

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 57, Tone 6:* The righteous man shall be glad when he sees the vengeance: *Verse:* If ye indeed speak righteousness (Ps. 57:11, 2 LXX [58:10, 1]).

*Reading of the Prophecy of Isaiah (Isa. 13:2):*

Thus says the Lord: Upon a level mountain lift up a standard, raise the voice, fear not; summon with the hand, open the gates, ye princes! (Isa. 13:2).

I command, and I lead them: they are sanctified, and giants come to execute Mine anger, rejoicing together and reviling (Isa. 13:3 LXX).[^1341] A voice of many nations upon the mountains, like the voice of many nations; a voice of kings and nations gathered together: the Lord of Sabaoth hath commanded an armed nation (Isa. 13:4) to come from a far land, from the utmost foundations of heaven, the Lord and His warriors, to destroy all the world (Isa. 13:5). Wail ye, for the day of the Lord is at hand, and destruction from God shall arrive (Isa. 13:6). Therefore shall every hand be enfeebled, and every soul of man shall be dismayed (Isa. 13:7). The elders shall be troubled, and their pangs shall come upon them, even as of a woman in travail; and they shall weep one to another and be amazed, and their countenance shall they change like flame (Isa. 13:8 LXX).[^1342] For behold, the day of the Lord comes, with incurable wrath and anger, to lay the whole world desolate, and to destroy sinners from it (Isa. 13:9). For the stars of heaven, and Orion, and all the host of heaven shall not give their light and shall be darkened when the sun rises, and the moon shall not give her light (Isa. 13:10 LXX).[^1343] And I will command evils for all the world, and their sins upon the ungodly, and I will destroy the insolence of the lawless, and will humble the insolence of the proud (Isa. 13:11). And they that are left shall be more precious than refined gold, and a man shall be more honored than a stone from Ophir (Isa. 13:12 LXX).[^1344] For the heaven shall be incensed, and the earth shall be shaken from its foundations, because of the wrath of the anger of the Lord of Sabaoth, in the day whereinsoever His wrath shall come (Isa. 13:13 LXX)![^1345]

As the prodigal I departed from Thy goodness, O Lord, and squandered the wealth of grace; unto Thee have I fled, O compassionate One, crying aloud unto Thee: "O God, I have sinned, have mercy on me!" *(Twice)*

*Reading of Genesis (Gen. 8:4–9):*

The ark rested in the seventh month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat (Gen. 8:4 LXX).[^1347] And the water was continually decreasing until the tenth month. And in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains were seen (Gen. 8:5). And it came to pass after forty days, Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made (Gen. 8:6). And he sent forth a raven to see if the water had abated; and it went forth and did not return until the water was dried up from the earth (Gen. 8:7). And he sent forth a dove after it to see if the water had abated from the face of the earth (Gen. 8:8). And the dove, not having found rest for her feet, returned unto him into the ark, for water was upon all the face of all the earth; and he stretched forth his hand, took her, and brought her in unto himself into the ark (Gen. 8:9).

=== LEAF p509 ===
And having waited yet other seven days, he sent forth again the dove from the ark (Gen. 8:10). And the dove returned unto him toward evening, and she had a leafy olive twig in her mouth; and Noah perceived that the water had abated from the earth (Gen. 8:11). And having waited yet other seven days, he sent forth again the dove; and she did not resume returning unto him anymore (Gen. 8:12). And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year of the life of Noah, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the water had failed from the face of the earth; and Noah uncovered the covering of the ark which he had made, and saw that the water had failed from the face of the earth (Gen. 8:13). And in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, the earth was dried (Gen. 8:14). On the twenty-seventh day of the month, he opened the ark (Gen. 8:15 LXX). And the Lord God spoke unto Noah, saying (Gen. 8:15): "Come forth out of the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and the wives of thy sons with thee (Gen. 8:16); and all the wild beasts that are with thee, and all flesh, from birds even unto cattle, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth, lead forth with thee, and increase ye and multiply upon the earth" (Gen. 8:17). And Noah came forth, and his wife, and his sons, and the wives of his sons with him (Gen. 8:18). And all the wild beasts, and all the cattle, and every bird, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth, according to their kind, came forth out of the ark, on the first day of the third month (Gen. 8:19 LXX).[^1348] And Noah built an altar unto the Lord, and took of all clean cattle, and of all clean birds, and offered whole-burnt-offerings upon the altar (Gen. 8:20). And the Lord God smelled a sweet-smelling savor (Gen. 8:21a).

*Prokeimenon, Psalm 60, Tone 7:* Hear my supplication, O God, attend unto my prayer: *Verse:* From the ends of the earth have I cried unto Thee (Ps. 60:2, 3 LXX [61:1, 2]).

*Reading of Proverbs (Prov. 10:31–32; 11:1–12):*

The mouth of the righteous drips wisdom, but the tongue of the unjust shall perish (Prov. 10:31). The lips of righteous men drip graces, but the mouth of the ungodly is perverse (Prov. 10:32).

False balances are an abomination before the Lord, but a just weight is acceptable unto Him (Prov. 11:1). Wheresoever insolence enters, there also is dishonor; but the mouth of the humble meditates on wisdom (Prov. 11:2). The integrity of the upright shall guide them, but the stumbling of the rebellious shall despoil them (Prov. 11:3). Possessions shall not profit in the day of wrath, but righteousness shall deliver from death; when a righteous man dies, he leaves repentance, but the destruction of the ungodly is sudden and derisive (Prov. 11:4 LXX).[^1349] The righteousness of the blameless directs ways, but ungodliness falls into injustice (Prov. 11:5). The righteousness of upright men shall deliver them, but the lawless are caught in their own thoughtlessness (Prov. 11:6). When a righteous man dies, his hope does not perish; but the boast of the ungodly perishes (Prov. 11:7 LXX).[^1350] The righteous man escapes from the snare, and in his stead the ungodly man is delivered up (Prov. 11:8). In the mouth of the ungodly is a snare to citizens, but the perception of the righteous is prosperous (Prov. 11:9). In the good things of the righteous a city is set right, and at the destruction of the ungodly there is rejoicing (Prov. 11:10). By the blessing of the upright a city shall be exalted, but by the mouth of the ungodly it is overturned (Prov. 11:11). A man void of understanding scoffs at his fellow-citizens, but a prudent man keeps silence (Prov. 11:12).
