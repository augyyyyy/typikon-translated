# PART III: APPENDICES: HYMNS TO THE TRINITY, SESSIONAL HYMNS, AND PHOTAGOGIKA OF THE EIGHT TONES

## HYMNS TO THE TRINITY IN THE EIGHT TONES[^3032]

*(These are sung in the Holy Great Fast, and in the other Fasts, when we do not sing: "God is the Lord," nor the Troparion, but "Alleluia" with its stichoi: "From night my spirit wakes at dawn unto Thee, O God..." and so forth. Look on Friday of the 1st Week of the Fast, (see fol. 173)[^3033], in the current Tone of the Oktoechos.)*

*Hymns to the Trinity, Tone 1:*
Through bodily representations of the Bodiless Powers being led up in mind unto the immaterial and noetic, and receiving the tri-radiant illumination of the three-hypostatic Godhead through thrice-holy singing, with Cherubim we cry aloud unto the One God: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3034]

*[And it is meet to say at the end of each Troparion in each Tone thus:]*
*On Monday:* Through the intercessions of Thy Bodiless Powers, have mercy on us.
*On Tuesday:* By the prayers of Thy Forerunner, have mercy on us.
*On Wednesday:* By the prayers of the Mother of God, have mercy on us; *or also on Friday.*
*On Thursday:* By the prayers of Thine Apostles, and of Thy Hierarch Nicholas.
*On Friday:* By the power of Thy Cross preserve us; *(when it is not Wednesday).*[^3035]

*Glory...*
With all the heavenly powers, with Cherubim we cry aloud unto Him Who is in the highest, sending up thrice-holy praise: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3036]

*Unto Saturday:* *And on the middle [Troparion] always:* By the prayers of all Thy Saints, have mercy on us.

*Both now...*
Having risen from sleep we fall down before Thee, O Good One, and we cry aloud unto Thee the angelic hymn, O Mighty One: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God. *[And on the third always:]* For the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3037]

*Hymns to the Trinity, Tone 2:*
Imitating the Powers on high: upon earth we offer the victory song unto Thee, O Good One: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3038]

*Glory...*
O uncreated Nature, Creator of all, open our lips, that we may proclaim Thy praise, crying aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3039]

*Both now...*
From the bed and sleep Thou hast raised me up, O Lord: enlighten my mind and my heart, and open my lips, that I may hymn Thee, O Holy Trinity: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3040]

*Tone 3:*
O Trinity consubstantial and undivided, Unity in three hypostases and co-everlasting: unto Thee as God we cry aloud the angelic hymn: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3041]

*Glory...*
The Father without beginning, the co-unoriginate Son, and the co-everlasting Spirit, one Godhead, daring to glorify with Cherubim we say: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: by the prayers of all Thy Saints![^3042]

*Both now...*
Suddenly the Judge shall come, and each man's deeds shall be laid bare; but with fear let us cry aloud at midnight: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3043]

*Tone 4:*
Daring to offer the prayerful hymn of Thy noetic servants, we say: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3044]

*Glory...*
As the Angelic ranks now in heaven, and with fear the assemblies of men upon earth, we offer the victory song unto Thee, O Good One: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: by the prayers of all Thy Saints![^3045]

*Both now...*
The Father without beginning, and Thee, O Christ God, and Thy Most Holy Spirit...[^3046]

...and Thy Most Holy Spirit, daring to glorify with Cherubim we say: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3047]

*Tone 5:*
It is the time of singing, and the hour of prayer; fervently let us cry aloud unto the One God: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3048]

*Glory...*
Daring to represent Thine intelligible hosts, O unoriginate Trinity, with unworthy lips we cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: by the prayers of all the Saints![^3049]

*Both now...*
Contained within the Virgin's womb, yet not departing from the Father's bosom: receive also us with the Angels, O Christ God, as we cry aloud unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy![^3050]

*Tone 6:*
The Cherubim standing before Thee with fear, and the Seraphim in awe with trembling, offer the thrice-holy song with unceasing voice; with them also we sinners cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God![^3051]

*Glory...*
With bodiless lips, with unceasing glorifications, the six-winged beings cry aloud the thrice-holy song unto Thee, our God; and we upon earth with unworthy lips send up praise unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: by the prayers of all the Saints![^3052]

*Both now...*
The Trinity Unity Godhead we glorify in unconfused union, and we cry aloud the angelic hymn: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3053]

*Tone 7:*
Hymned with the power on high in the manner of the Cherubim, and worshipped with divine glory in the manner of the Angels: receive also us upon earth, sending up a song unto Thee with unworthy lips: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God![^3054]

*Glory...*
Having cast aside slothfulness like sleep, O soul, show forth an amendment unto singing to the Judge, and cry aloud with fear: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: by the prayers of all Thy Saints![^3055]

*Both now...*
Sending up Seraphic thrice-holy praise unto the unapproachable Godhead, the Trinity in Unity, with fear we cry aloud: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3056]

*Tone 8:*
Having our hearts toward Heaven, let us be likened unto the Angelic rank, and with fear let us fall down before the Judge, sending up the victory praise: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3057]

*Glory...*
Not daring to gaze upon Thee, the Cherubim flying cry aloud with acclamation the divine song of the thrice-holy voice; with them also we cry aloud unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, O God: by the prayers of all the Saints![^3058]

*Both now...*
Bowed down by the multitude of our transgressions, and not daring to look up to the height of heaven, having inclined our souls with bodies, with Angels we cry aloud the hymn unto Thee: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God: for the sake of the Mother of God, have mercy on us![^3059]

*Sessional Hymns from the Oktoechos, which we sing after the first Stichology during the Holy Forty Days:*[^3060]

*([Whichever Tone of the Oktoechos occurs on Cheesefare Sunday, that one we begin on Monday of the 1st Week of the Fast, in weekly order.])*[^3061]

*On Friday at Matins, after the 1st Stichology:*

*Penitential Sessional Hymn, Tone 1:*
In iniquities was I conceived, prodigal that I am, and I dare not look up to the height of heaven; but daring upon Thy love of mankind, I cry: O God, cleanse me and save me![^3062]

*Stichos: O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger, neither chasten me in Thy wrath. The same verse. Both now... We say...*[^3063]

*(We say [it] simply without chanting, in whichever Tone on Monday; to it the verse:)*

*Troparion:*
If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall I appear, a sinner, who have not borne the burden and heat of the day?[^3064] But with the hirelings of the eleventh hour, number me, O God, and save me![^3065]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
A wonder of wonders, O thou full of grace! Seeing thee, creation rejoices; for thou didst conceive without seed, and didst bear ineffably Him Whom the ranks of Angels cannot gaze upon: pray Him, O Mother of God, that our souls may be saved![^3066]

*(At Ode 6, the Martyrikon is said, if the Menaion does not have a Kontakion; but if it has [one], this Martyrikon is said together with the Sessional hymns, before the stichos:[^3067] "Marvelous is God in His saints, the God of Israel."[^3068] After this, the Theotokion. And thus we do throughout the whole Fast.)*

*Martyrikon, Tone 1:*
Clothed with the boast of sufferings and with the honour of the crown by Thee, O Lord, the glorious Passion-bearers, by the endurance of wounds, overcame the lawless, and by divine power received victory from heaven: by their prayers grant us, O God, Thy great mercy![^3069]

*On Tuesday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 1:*

*Troparion:*
Hasten to open unto me Thy Fatherly embrace! I have prodigally spent my life, gazing upon the inexhaustible wealth of Thy compassions, O Savior; despise not now my heart that has become impoverished, for unto Thee, O Lord, with compunction I cry: I have sinned against heaven and before Thee![^3070]

*Troparion:*
Thy judgment seat is awesome, and the judgment is righteous, but my deeds are grievous; yet do Thou Thyself, O Merciful One, anticipate and save me, and delivering me from torment, rescue me, O Master, from the parts on the left, and vouchsafe me to stand at Thy right hand, O most righteous Judge![^3071]

*Theotokion:*
O unwedded, pure Virgin Mother of God, the only protectress and shelter of the faithful: deliver from afflictions, and sorrows, and grievous distresses all that place their hope in thee, O Maiden; and save our souls by thy divine prayers![^3072]

*At Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
As noble soldiers, with one mind having believed, ye feared not the threats of the tormentors, O Saints; ye came eagerly unto Christ, having taken up the precious Cross; and having finished your course, ye received victory from heaven: glory to Him that strengthened you; glory to Him that crowned you; glory to Him that through you works healings for all![^3073]

*On Wednesday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 1:*

*Troparion:*
When Thou wast crucified, O Christ, the tyranny perished; the might of the enemy was trampled down; for neither Angel nor man, but Thou Thyself, O Lord, hast saved us: glory to Thee![^3074]

*Stichos: Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship the footstool of His feet, for it is holy.[^3075] We say the same verse.*

*Troparion:*
We worship the wood of Thy Cross, O Lover of mankind, for upon it Thou didst nail the Life of all; Thou didst open paradise, O Savior, unto the thief who came to Thee with faith, and didst vouchsafe Him delight when he confessed unto Thee: Remember me, O Lord! Receive us also as Him, as we cry aloud: We all have sinned; in Thy tender mercy despise us not![^3076]

*Stavrotheotokion:*
The Ewe-lamb, seeing upon the Wood the Lamb, Thee, O Word, patiently crucified with two thieves, and pierced in the side with a spear, cried out, exclaiming as a mother: What is this strange and awesome mystery, O my Son?[^3077]

...how the tomb covers the uncircumscribed God: ineffable is that which is accomplished! Leave not me that gave Thee birth, O my sweetest Son![^3078]

*At Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
By the wounds of the Saints which they suffered for Thy sake, be Thou implored, O Lord, and heal all our sicknesses, O Lover of mankind, we pray Thee![^3079]

*On Thursday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Apostles, Tone 1:*

*Troparion:*
Having torn asunder the rhetorical embroideries with the spiritual net of the Cross, the simple fishermen enlightened the Gentiles piously to glorify Thee, the true God; therefore we also cry aloud a song unto Thee that didst strengthen them: Glory to the Father and to the Son; glory to the consubstantial Spirit; glory to Him that for their sake enlightened the world![^3080]

*Stichos: Their sound has gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.[^3081]*

*Troparion:*
All-wise fishermen of the universe, having received mercy from God, pray now also for us who cry aloud: O Lord, save the King and Thy City, and for the sake of the Apostles deliver our souls from the evils that encompass us![^3082]

*Theotokion:*
Without a mother in heaven, beyond understanding and hearing, on earth without a father thou didst give birth to Him: pray Him, O Mother of God, for our souls![^3083]

*At Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
We all pray the Martyrs of Christ, for they pray for our salvation; and let us all come unto them with faith: for they pour forth the grace of healings; they drive away the demonic legions, as guardians of the faith![^3084]

*On Friday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 1:*

*Troparion:*
The weapon of the Cross was shown forth of old in battles unto the pious Emperor Constantine, an aid unto victory...[^3085]

...against the enemies, for the sake of faith. At this the powers of Hades tremble; this has become both salvation for the faithful, and the boast of Paul![^3086]

*Troparion:*
Thou Who of old didst fashion Adam from the earth, wast struck with a blow by a clay hand, O Compassionate One, and didst endure crucifixion, mockings, and wounds. O wonder, O Thy great longsuffering! Glory, O Lord, to Thy life-bearing Passion, whereby Thou hast saved us![^3087]

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion:*
Seeing Thee, O Christ, stretched out dead upon the Cross, the all-immaculate Mother cried aloud: O my Son, Who art co-unoriginate with the Father and the Spirit, what is this Thine ineffable dispensation, whereby with Thy pure hand Thou hast saved Thy creation, O Master?[^3088]

*At Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
*By the boast of sufferings... Seek [it] on Monday of this Tone.*[^3089]

*(And as we have indicated in this First Tone: thus it is meet to do in each Tone throughout the whole holy Forty Days.)*[^3090]

*On Monday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 2:*

*Troparion:*
Like the waves of the sea, my transgressions have risen up against me; like a ship in the deep, I alone am tempest-tossed by many trespasses; but guide me, O Lord, into the calm haven of repentance, and save me![^3091]

*Troparion:*
I am an unfruitful tree, O Lord, not bringing forth at all the fruit of repentance; and I fear cutting down, and I dread that unquenchable fire; therefore I pray Thee, before that calamity, turn me back and save me![^3092]

*Theotokion:*
Being a fountain of tender mercy, vouchsafe us mercy, O Mother of God; look upon the people that have sinned; show forth as...[^3093]

...ever thy power, for hoping in thee, we cry aloud unto thee: Rejoice! as once Gabriel, Archistrategos of the Bodiless Powers![^3094]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon: The same on Monday of this Tone:*
Having Thee, Who clothest the heaven with clouds, as their clothing in the world, the Saints endured torments from the lawless, and abolished the deception of idols: by their prayers deliver us also from the invisible enemy, O Savior, and save us![^3095]

*On Tuesday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 2:*

*Troparion:*
Have mercy on me, said David; and I cry unto Thee: I have sinned, O Savior; having cleansed my sins, have mercy on me![^3096]

*Troparion:*
Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me! For two sins David wept bitterly, but from myriad transgressions I cry unto Thee: he drenched his couch with tears, but I have not acquired even a single tear; having despaired, I pray: Have mercy on me, O God, according to Thy great mercy![^3097]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
We became partakers of the divine nature through thee, O Ever-Virgin Mother of God; for thou didst bear God incarnate for us; therefore as is due we all piously magnify thee![^3098]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Passion-bearers of the Lord, blessed is the earth that drank in your blood, and holy the shrines that received your bodies! For at the judgment seat ye convicted the enemy, and with boldness proclaimed Christ: pray Him as God, we pray, that our souls may be saved![^3099]

*On Wednesday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 2:*

*Troparion:*
The life-creating Cross of Thy goodness, which Thou hast granted unto us who are unworthy, O Lord, we offer unto Thee in prayer: save the King and Thy City who pray, for the sake of the Mother of God, O Thou Who alone art the Lover of mankind![^3100]

*Troparion:*
Thou Didst work salvation in the midst of the earth, O Christ God, having stretched out Thy pure hands upon the Cross, gathering all nations who cry aloud: O Lord, glory to Thee![^3101]

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion:*
We magnify thee, O Mother of God, crying aloud: Rejoice, Rod from which without seed thou didst bud forth God, Who by the Wood destroyed death![^3102]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Thou Who didst enlighten Thy Saints more than gold, and didst glorify Thy Venerable Ones as Good: being implored by them, O Christ God, pacify our life, as Lover of mankind, and direct our prayer as incense, O Thou Who alone restest in the Saints![^3103]

*On Thursday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Apostles, Tone 2:*

*Troparion:*
Having made the fishermen wiser than rhetoricians, and sent them forth as preachers unto all the earth by Thine ineffable love of mankind, O Christ God: for their sake establish Thy Church, and send down Thy blessedness upon the faithful, O Thou Who alone restest in the Saints![^3104]

*Troparion:*
The fishermen, having caught the nations and taught the ends of the earth to worship Thee, by Thine ineffable love of mankind, O Christ God: for their sake establish Thy Church, and send down Thy blessing upon the faithful, O Thou Who alone art most merciful and Lover of mankind![^3105]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
We magnify thee, O Mother of God, crying aloud: Rejoice, Cloud of the unwaning Light! For thou didst bear Him in thy bosom, the glory of the Lord![^3106]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Apostles, Prophets, and Martyrs, Hierarchs, Venerable, and Righteous Ones, who have fought the good fight well, and kept the faith...[^3107]

...having boldness toward the Savior on our behalf: pray Him as Good, we pray, that our souls may be saved![^3108]

*On Friday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 2:*

*Troparion:*
Thou Who didst enlighten the earthly by the Cross, and called sinners unto repentance: separate me not from Thy flock, O Good Shepherd, but seek me, O Master, who have strayed, and number me with Thy holy flock, O Lover of mankind![^3109]

*Troparion:*
We worship Thine immaculate Icon, O Good One, asking forgiveness of our trespasses, O Christ God; for of Thine own will Thou wast pleased to ascend the Cross, that Thou mightest deliver them whom Thou hadst fashioned from the bondage of the enemy; therefore with thanksgiving we cry aloud unto Thee: Thou hast filled all things with joy, O our Savior, Who didst come to save the world![^3110]

*Stavrotheotokion:*
The Virgin and Thy Mother, O Christ, seeing Thee stretched out dead upon the Wood, weeping bitterly, said: O my Son, what is this strange mystery? Thou Who grantest eternal life unto all, how dost Thou die of Thine own will upon the Cross by a reproachful death?[^3111]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
*Thou Who clothest [the heaven with clouds]... Look on Monday of this Tone.*[^3112]

*On Monday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:*
Living upon the earth, O my soul, repent! For the dust in the tomb sings not, nor does it deliver from transgressions; but cry aloud unto Christ God: O Knower of hearts, I have sinned; before Thou condemnest me, have mercy on me![^3113]

*Troparion:*
How long, O my soul, dost thou abide in transgressions? How long dost thou receive the neglect of repentance? Receive in thy mind the coming Judgment; cry aloud unto the Lord: I have sinned, O sinless Lord, save me![^3114]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*...[^3115]

*Theotokion:*
Marveling at the beauty of thy virginity and the surpassing radiant purity of thine, Gabriel cried aloud unto thee, O Mother of God: What worthy praise shall I bring unto thee? And what shall I call thee? I am in doubt and astonished! Therefore, as I was commanded, I cry unto thee: Rejoice, O thou full of grace![^3116]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
By the magnanimity of your endurance, ye overcame the wiles of the evil-originating enemy, O all-praised Passion-bearers, and therefore were vouchsafed eternal blessedness; but pray unto the Lord to save the flock of Christ-loving people, as ye are martyrs in truth![^3117]

*On Tuesday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:*
At the awesome judgment seat, being convicted without accusers, and condemned without witnesses; for the books of conscience are unfolded, and hidden deeds are uncovered; before on that universal amphitheater Thou wilt examine the deeds done by me, O God, cleanse me and save me![^3118]

*Troparion:*
The awesome judgment seat awaits me, O Lord; for while I keep silence, my deeds cry aloud; but look past me who have evilly wandered; turn me back, O Christ God, and save me![^3119]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*
Our refuge and strength, O Mother of God, mighty helper unto the world, by thy prayers save thy servants from every affliction, O thou who alone art blessed![^3120]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Ye shine in the faith as most radiant luminaries, O Saints, physicians of piety, most praised Passion-bearers; for ye feared not the wounds of the tormentors, and cast down the impiety of idols, having as invincible victory the true Cross![^3121]

*On Wednesday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:*
The Cross was set up on the earth, and touched the heavens; not that the wood had attained such height, but because Thou upon it fillest all things: glory to Thee![^3122]

*Troparion:*
Upon the cypress, the pine, and the cedar Thou wast lifted up, O Lamb of God, that Thou mightest save them that worship with faith Thy voluntary crucifixion, O Christ God: glory to Thee![^3123]

*Stavrotheotokion:*
Having acquired as a rod of strength the Cross of thy Son, O Mother of God, thereby we cast down the haughtiness of the enemy, and unceasingly magnify thee![^3124]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
The memory of Thy Passion-bearers, O Lord, Thou hast made radiant as Almighty, for Thou didst strengthen them to be conformed unto Thy Passion; for they manfully overcame the power of Beliar, therefore they also received gifts of healings: by their prayers, O Lover of mankind, grant peace unto our souls![^3125]

*On Thursday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Apostles, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:*
Come, all ye faithful, let us praise the Apostles as helmsmen; for they drove away the deception of idols, and led the people up unto the light of life, and taught them to glorify the Trinity; therefore today, celebrating their precious memory, let us glorify the Savior![^3126]

*Troparion:*
All the Apostles watered the earth with the Word as with divine dew, and caused the ear of grain of faith to grow; and they filled the ends of the world with Thy grace, O Lord, and cut down all the tares; therefore they brought all unto Christ God, King of all, baptizing them into the uncreated Trinity; therefore, all of us, celebrating their all-precious memory, glorify them in orthodox manner![^3127]

*Glory... Both now... Theotokion:*

*Theotokion:*
Prophets proclaimed, Apostles taught, and Martyrs confessed, and we have believed, truly to magnify thee as Mother of God in orthodox manner![^3128]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Armed with the help of Christ, and clad in the armor of faith, like archers ye cast down the encampments of the enemy; for eagerly in hope of life ye endured all things, the threats and the wounds of the tormentors; therefore ye also received the crown, O Martyrs of Christ, longsuffering in soul![^3129]

*On Friday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 3:*

*Troparion:*
Having willed to suffer the Cross and death, Thou didst plant it in the midst of creation. When Thou wast pleased, O Savior, to have Thy body nailed, then also the sun hid its rays. Seeing these things, the thief sang of Thee as God, crying aloud unto Thee: Remember me, O Lord! and having believed, He received Paradise![^3130]

*Troparion:*
At Thine uncircumscribed power and Thy voluntary crucifixion, the angelic hosts beholding were astonished: how He Who is invisible received wounds in the flesh, desiring to deliver mankind from corruption! Therefore as unto the Benefactor we cry aloud unto Thee: Glory, O Christ, to Thy tender compassion![^3131]

*Glory... Both now... Stavrotheotokion:*
A reproachful death, O Compassionate One, and crucifixion Thou didst endure of Thine own will. Seeing this, she that bare Thee, O Christ, was wounded; by her prayers, in the tender compassion of Thy mercy, O Lord Who alone art all-good and Lover of mankind: have compassion and save the world, O Thou that takest away the sins of the world![^3132]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
*Magnanimity of endurance... Look on Monday of this Tone.*[^3133]

*On Monday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 4:*[^3134]

*Troparion:*
Visit my humble soul, O Lord, which has spent all its life in sins; like the harlot receive me also, and save me![^3135]

*Troparion:*
Voyaging over the abyss of this life, I contemplate the deep of mine many evils; having no helmsman through my thoughts, I utter unto Thee Peter's cry: Save me, O Christ; save me, O God, for Thou art the Lover of mankind![^3136]

*Theotokion:*
Thou art an invincible rampart unto us Christians, O Virgin Mother of God; for fleeing unto thee we remain unharmed, and though we sin again, we have thee as an intercessor; therefore with thanksgiving we cry unto thee: Rejoice, O full of grace, the Lord is with thee![^3137]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Today the angelic hosts have come at the memory of the Passion-bearers, to enlighten the minds of the faithful, and to illumine the universe with grace; being implored by them, O God, grant us great mercy![^3138]

*On Tuesday, after the 1st Stichology, Penitential Sessional Hymns, Tone 4:*

*Troparion:*
Quickly let us enter into the bridal chamber of Christ, that we all may hear the blessed voice of that God of ours: Come, ye that love heavenly glory, being partakers with the wise virgins, let us make bright our lamps by faith![^3139]

*Troparion:*
By the multitude of my transgressions I am condemned, by the fear of torment I am consumed, O Christ our God. Tears of repentance from the heart I offer unto Thee Who hast power over life and death, and I cry unto Thee with compunction: I have sinned, O Lord, save me![^3140]

*Theotokion:*
The Word of the Father, Christ our God, Who became incarnate of thee, we have come to know, O Virgin Mother of God, who alone art pure...[^3141]

...who alone art blessed; therefore unceasingly, chanting hymns to thee, we magnify thee![^3142]

*After Ode 6, Martyrikon:*
Armed with the Cross, Thy Passion-bearers overcame the wiles of the evil-originating enemy, O Christ our God; they shone forth as luminaries instructing men; they grant healings unto them that ask with faith: by their prayers save our souls![^3143]

*On Wednesday, after the 1st Stichology, Sessional Hymns to the Cross, Tone 4:*

*Troparion:*
Thou hast redeemed us from the curse of the law by Thy precious Blood; having been nailed to the Cross and pierced with a spear, Thou didst pour forth immortality unto men: O our Savior, glory to Thee![^3144]

*Troparion:*

*Stavrotheotokion:*
