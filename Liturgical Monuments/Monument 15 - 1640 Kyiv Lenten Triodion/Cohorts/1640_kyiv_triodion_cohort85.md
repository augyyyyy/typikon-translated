Through bodily representations of the Bodiless Powers being led up in mind unto the immaterial and noetic, and receiving the tri-radiant illumination of the three-hypostatic Godhead through thrice-holy singing, with Cherubim we cry aloud unto the One God: Holy, Holy, Holy art Thou, our God![^3034]
