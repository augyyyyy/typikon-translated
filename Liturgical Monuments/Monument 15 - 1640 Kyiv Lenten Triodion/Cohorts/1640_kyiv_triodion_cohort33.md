*George Kedrenos, book 3.*[^871] When Leo the Isaurian, from being a donkey-driver and from the manual trade by which he had gained his sustenance, obtained the scepter of the Empire [following the voluntary abdication of Theodosius the Evil], by divine permission: Saint Germanus, who at that time held the supreme governance and administration of the Church, was immediately summoned by him, and heard him say: "It seems to me, Master, that holy Icons are in no way different from idols. After him, Theophilus his son became heir to the Empire, and to madness against the images, surpassing others therein.[^878] This Theophilus, though he delivered many holy Fathers unto diverse punishments and torments, namely for the sake of the venerable images, nevertheless claimed civil justice most especially unto himself and championed it for the wronged; so that even if anyone sought out and inquired whether there was anyone in the city who caused grievance unto another, for many days, as they say, he found not one such person.

And when he had been Monarch for twelve years, being seized with the disease of dysentery, that is, the bloody flux, so that his life was about to be undone, his mouth opened excessively wide, so that even the inner parts of his entrails were exposed.[^879] But Empress Theodora, being grieved at such a misfortune, thereupon fell asleep; and in a dream she saw the All-pure Theotokos holding in her bosom the pre-eternal Child, and round about her stood all-radiant Angels, who were striking Theophilus her husband and mocking him. When therefore her sleep left her, and Theophilus, having revived a little, cried out: "Woe, woe unto me, a wretched man! Because of the holy Images they beat me!"

Straightway the Empress placed upon him an Image of the All-pure Theotokos, praying unto her with tears. Thus Theophilus, wandering in mind, saw one from among those who stood before him carrying an Icon; and seizing it, he began to kiss it. And forthwith his mouth, which had gnashed against the Images, and his shameless, gaping throat, were restored unto their former nature, and became just as they had been before; and from the injuries and torment wherewith he had been held, he grew calm and fell asleep, confessing that it is a good thing to hold the holy Images in reverence and esteem. Then the Empress, bringing forth from her coffers the venerable and holy Images, brought it to pass that Theophilus her husband kissed those Images and honored them with all his soul.
