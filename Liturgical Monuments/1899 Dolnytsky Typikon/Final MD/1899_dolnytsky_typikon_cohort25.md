Christ", on Sundays indeed thrice, but on Weekdays once.[^643]

6. **At the Hours:** after **"Blessed is our God"** (*Благослове́нъ Бо́гъ на́шъ*), we say **"Christ is risen"** (*Христо́съ воскре́се*) thrice; and after the Dismissal, **"Christ is risen"** thrice, with the addition: **"And unto us He hath granted eternal life"**, and the rest.

*Note.* Since we do not say **"Blessed is our God"** when we join an Hour with Matins, or with the preceding [Hour], and likewise since we do not say the Dismissal when we join an Hour with the following [Hour], on this account neither do we say **"Christ is risen"** at that time.

7. **At the Liturgy:** we say **"Christ is risen"** in the same places as on the Resurrection of Christ, here on pages 468, 469.

The first two Antiphons are Resurrectional, but the third is the weekday one with the Resurrectional Refrain; likewise also at **"Come, let us worship"** (*Прїидѣ́те поклони́мся*), both on Sundays and on weekdays.

We conclude the Troparia with the Kontakion of Pascha,[^644] except in Thomas Week and [during the Mid-Feast of] Pentecost, for then we conclude them with their Kontakion, as the Triodion has it; although in Thomas Week also the Troparia may be concluded with the Kontakion of Pascha, as our Sluzhebnyks, and our manuscript Typikon, and the *Tserkovne Oko* do.

In place of **"It is truly meet"** (*Досто́йно є҆́сть*), we sing **"The Angel cried"** (*А҆́нгелъ вопїя́ше*) and **"Shine, shine"** (*Свѣти́ся, свѣти́ся*), except during the Octave of the Mid-Feast, which has its own Heirmos.

We say the Dismissal of Pascha, except in Thomas Week, which has its own Dismissal. And we say the Dismissal with

[Leaf p482 / Page 480]

the Cross only on Sundays,[^645] and on the Feast of the Mid-Feast,[^646] and at the Apodosis of Pascha.[^647] We deem that on certain other days also, when there is a sufficient number of people, it will be well to say the Dismissal with the Cross, yet not on all days, as some do, having for this neither Typikon nor general custom.

8. The Service of a saint without Polyeleos occurring on these Sundays, and the rest even unto Pentecost, and on the Feast of the Mid-Feast, and at the Apodosis of Pascha, we sing at Compline.

## Proper Typika
### From Thomas Sunday to the Ascension

### Thomas Sunday
**2nd from Pascha. Tone 1.**

We celebrate an All-Night Vigil.

We sing all things according to the General Typikon of a First-Class Feast of the Lord, yet at Great Vespers without Readings, and with **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (*Воскресе́нїе Христо́во*) thrice (according to others, once) at Matins, and without proper Antiphons at the Liturgy; and the Feast has its Apodosis on the following Saturday.

#### Weekdays of Thomas Week

On weekdays even unto the Sunday of the Myrrh-bearers, we sing all things according to the General Typikon inside the Feast

[Leaf p483 / Page 481]

5. Dismissal of Pascha, without the Cross.

### Weekdays of the Week of the Myrrh-Bearers

All things according to the General Typikon of a Saint inside a Feast; only at the Aposticha of Vespers, and at the Sessional Hymns which follow the 1st Kathisma-reading of Matins, and at the Praises (for there are Stichera both at the Praises and afterwards at the Aposticha) there are certain Stichera or Troparia, taken from the Weekday Service of the Octoechos of the Tone of the week, differing according to the difference of the days of the week, except on Saturday, which has all things festal. Specifically: at Vespers at the conclusion: Troparion: **"The noble Joseph"** (*Благообра́зный*); **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*): to the Saint; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*): to the Myrrh-bearers; and the Troparia **"The noble Joseph"** and **"When Thou didst descend"** (*Є҆гда̀ снизше́лъ є҆сѝ*) we say in the evening, alternating them daily.[^651]

#### At Compline

After the Trisagion, the one Kontakion of the Sunday: "Thus we say even unto the Sunday of All Saints" (*Triodion*).

#### At Matins

At **"The Lord is God"** (*Бо́гъ Госпо́дь*), Troparion **"The noble Joseph"** (*Благообра́зный*) once, and **"When Thou didst descend"** (*Є҆гда̀ снизше́лъ є҆сѝ*) once; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*): to the Saint; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*): to the Myrrh-bearers. But when there are two Saints with

---

[Leaf p486 / Page 484]

their own Troparia, then we deem that the Resurrectional one is to be omitted.

At the Sessional Hymns and at the Praises, there are two Troparia or two Weekday Stichera of the Octoechos of the Tone of the week, with the customary refrains; and before Psalm 50, **"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ"** (*Воскресе́нїе Христо́во*) once; and at the conclusion of Matins, the Troparia as at **"The Lord is God"** (*Бо́гъ Госпо́дь*).

#### At the Hours

Since not a single Typikon makes this clear, on this account we deem that it is to be: at the 1st [Hour], Troparion: **"The noble Joseph"** (*Благообра́зный*); at the 3rd, Resurrectional, and **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*): to the Saint; at the 6th: to the Myrrh-bearers; at the 9th: again **"The noble Joseph"**, and **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*): to the Saint. Kontakia: at the 1st and 6th, to the Myrrh-bearers; at the 3rd and 9th, to the Saint. If two Saints occur, then at the 3rd after **"Glory"** there is the Troparion to the first, and at the 9th to the second Saint. Likewise also the Kontakia: at the 3rd to the first, and at the 9th to the second Saint.[^652]

#### At the Liturgy

Troparia: **"The noble Joseph"** (*Благообра́зный*), and Resurrectional, and to the Myrrh-bearers; **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*), Kontakion to the Myrrh-bearers; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Kontakion of Pascha;[^653] or **"Glory... Both now..."**, Kontakion of the Myrrh-

[Leaf p487 / Page 485]

bearers, without Pascha.[^654] If the Service of a Saint is also taken, or of some of the various Petitions, then after the three Troparia of the Sunday, we take also the Troparion to the Saint, or to the Petition; then Kontakion to the Myrrh-bearers, **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*), Kontakion to the Saint, or to the Petition; **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Kontakion of Pascha; or **"Glory"** (*Сла́ва*), Kontakion to the Saint, or to the Petition, and **"Both now"** (*И҆ ны́нѣ*), Kontakion to the Myrrh-bearers. Epistle and Gospel of the day; and all the rest of the Sunday of the Myrrh-bearers, or also of the Saint, or of the Petition, if there be one.

## Sunday of Pentecost

1. The Service of this Feast is celebrated over seven days, for it has its Apodosis on the coming Saturday. It has this proper feature, that on Sunday evening at Vespers toward Monday the Kneeling Prayers are read; and on the morrow, that is, on Monday, the entire Service of the Feast is sung without the Menaion, and at the Liturgy also the Service of the Holy Trinity, if desired,[^658] as we indicate immediately.

---

[Leaf p500 / Page 498]

All the rest, from the Feast even unto its Apodosis, is performed according to the General Rule of a Feast and Inside a Feast of the Lord. At the Liturgy, **"As many of you as have been baptized into Christ"** (*Є҆ли́цы*), only on the Feast.

2. Here they have the custom on the day of Pentecost itself at the beginning of the Liturgy after **"Blessed is the Kingdom"** (*Благослове́нно Ца́рство*) (better before **"Blessed is the Kingdom"**), and afterwards at the beginning of Vespers after **"Blessed is our God"** (*Благослове́нъ Бо́гъ на́шъ*), to sing with kneeling **"O Heavenly King"** (*Царю̀ небе́сный*).

3. We sing Vespers on the day of the Feast itself immediately after the conclusion of the Liturgy, for the sake of the Kneeling Prayers, so that all the people may have a part in them.[^659]

4. The Service of a Saint occurring on Sunday and Monday, we sing on another day at Compline, at the discretion of the Superior (*Tserkvenachálnyk*).

5. Throughout this entire week the Canons of the Most Holy Theotokos are omitted at Compline, and we are dispensed to meat-eating (*Miasoyástiye*).

### Monday of the Holy Spirit
#### On Sunday Evening

*Note.* The Kneeling Prayers take place in three places at Vespers: after the Great Prokeimenon; after **"Let us all say"** (*Рцѣ́мъ всѝ*); and after **"Vouchsafe, O Lord"** (*Сподо́би, Го́споди*). At each of these three Stations of Prayers, the Little Ektenia is said with each time its proper Exclamation, yet thus: **"Again and again, having bended our knees, let us pray to the Lord"** (*Па́ки и҆ па́ки прекло́ньше колѣ́нѣ Го́споду помоли́мся*); and the Choir: **"Lord, have mercy"** (*Го́споди поми́луй*) thrice; then the Priest [reads] one Station of the Prayers with kneeling; and at the end of the Station the Deacon, or the Priest, follows with the remainder of the Little Ektenia, that is: **"Help us, save us"**, **"Remembering our most holy, most pure"**; and the Exclamation. Thus it takes place at each Station.
