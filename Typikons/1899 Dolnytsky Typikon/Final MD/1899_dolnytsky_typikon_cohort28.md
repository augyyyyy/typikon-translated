### Litany at the Exaltation of the Precious Cross[^699]

**1.** Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy, we pray Thee, hear and have mercy: Let us all say.

**2.** Furthermore we pray for the most holy, ecumenical High Priest (Name), the Pope of Rome, and for our right-believing and God-preserved Emperor (Name), for their dominion, victory, endurance, peace, health, salvation, and remission of their sins, and that the Lord our God may especially prosper and help them in all things, and subdue beneath their feet every enemy and adversary: Let us all say.

**3.** Furthermore we pray for our most reverend Archbishop, Metropolitan Kyr (Name), and for our God-loving Bishop Kyr (Name), and for our spiritual fathers, and for all our brethren in Christ, for their health, and salvation, and remission of their sins: Let us all say.

**4.** Furthermore we pray for every Christian soul: for their health, and salvation, and remission of their sins: Let us all say.

**5.** Furthermore we pray for all who serve and have served in this holy temple: for their health, and salvation, and remission of their sins: Let us all say.

## Directory of Services of the Entire Year

### Notes

**1.** When any Service is transferred from a weekday to a Sunday, then the Service of the Saint occurring on that Sunday is transferred to the weekday of the Service transferred to Sunday.

**2.** In the Menologion, we give the names only of the Saints having a Service; the others we omit, since we do not need to commemorate them, but only the former.

**3.** The celebrant does not omit Services written in cinnabar letters; and he does not combine Services for the Dead with Festal Services having a cinnabar sign in the margin, but takes only the Festal Service with the intention for the dead.[^700]

**4.** Of the Services of the entire year, some are Immovable, that is, of the Menaion, and others are Movable, that is, of both Triodia and the Octoechos. We provide first the Directory of Immovable Services, then of the Movable.[^701]

**5.** Since the Services of the Menaion are of various classes, on this account, that the reader may understand what class of Service he is to undertake, we attach in the margin beside each Service one of the various signs defining the class of the Service, as follows:

- **`⊕`** Sign of a Feast of the Lord with an Afterfeast.
- **`⊕`** Sign of a Feast of the Mother of God with an Afterfeast.
- **`⊕`** Sign of a Feast with a Vigil without an Afterfeast.
- **`+`** Sign of a Polyeleos Feast.
- **`(:·`** Sign of a Feast for 6 with Great Doxology.
- **`(:`** Sign of a Feast for 6 with Small Doxology.
- **`(·`** Sign of a Feast for 4 with Epistle and Gospel at Liturgy.
- **`(`** Sign of a Feast for 4 with Troparion.
- **`(`** Sign of a Feast for 4 without Troparion.[^702]

November: . . . . . . . . 21.

June: . . . . . . . . 24, 29.
