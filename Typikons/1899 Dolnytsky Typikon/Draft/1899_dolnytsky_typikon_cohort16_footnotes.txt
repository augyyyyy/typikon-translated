[^425]: Concerning the Katavasia in the Odes where the Triode does not enter, the Typika are silent. Even if according to the general rule of the Apodosis of Feasts the Katavasia ought to be of the Feast, and that after every Ode, nevertheless we deem that the Katavasia of the Feast in this case has no greater force than during the Afterfeast, because the Festal Service in this Apodosis has little. We deem therefore that the Katavasia in this case is to be only after the 3rd, 6th, 8th, and 9th Odes, and that is the Heirmos of the last Canon, that is, after the 3rd, 8th, and 9th the Heirmos of the Triode, and after the 6th of the Cross.
