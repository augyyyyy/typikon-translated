[^416]: Since here there are 4 Kathismata, that is, 3 regular ones and the Polyeleos, it is asked which Ektenias ought to be said here; for the general Typikon has only that. Furthermore, since the Feast has three Sessional Hymns, but here only two are appointed, which two of the three are to be sung here? Concerning the Ektenias: since our Typikon and the Russian ones mention an Ektenia after the 3rd Kathisma, it follows thence that after the 1st and 2nd Kathismata there are no Ektenias, which the Pochaiv Triodion also excludes after the 1st Kathisma before the Sessional Hymn of the Triodion for March 25; and under such a supposition two Little Ektenias ought to be said: one before the Sessional Hymn after the Polyeleos, with the exclamation: "For Holy art Thou," and the other before the Sessional Hymn of the 3rd Kathisma, with the exclamation: "For Thou art good," or "For Thine is the dominion," according to preference. However, since not saying Little Ektenias at Matins on weekdays is found only in the Russian and Romanian Typika, but is not observed among us, on this account we deem that here only after the 1st Kathisma an Ektenia is not to be said, but after the 2nd it is to be said with the exclamation: "For Thine is the dominion," and after the 3rd with the exclamation: "For Thou art good." Of the two Sessional Hymns of the Feast, let the one after the Polyeleos be taken, and another from the first two, according to preference.
