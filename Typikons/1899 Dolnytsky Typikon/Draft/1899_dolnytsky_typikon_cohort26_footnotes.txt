[^662]: Since the last prayer, "O God, great and eternal", is the eighth of the prayers said by the Priest during the Introductory Psalm, on this account it is to be omitted either there or here. The number of these prayers varies according to the difference of editions of the Triodion. Our Pochaiv Triodion appoints seven prayers, as does also the Greek Triodion of the Propaganda. The manuscript Slavonic Triodion which we possess appoints seven prayers, yet it does not appoint the prayer "O God, great and eternal", but another: "O Master Almighty". The *Tserkovne Oko* appoints only four prayers: the 1st: "O Most Pure", after the Prokeimenon; the 2nd after "Let us all say": "O Lord our God"; the 3rd and 4th after "Vouchsafe, O Lord": "O Ever-flowing", and "O Heavenly King". The *Moscow Typikon* appoints 5: "O Most Pure", "O Lord Jesus Christ", and after "Vouchsafe, O Lord": "O Ever-flowing", and the other two which are in the Triodion. The Typikon of the University of Lviv and another manuscript which is among us mention only three Prayers in general.
