[^394]: According to the Typikon of the Menaion for February 24, when this Feast occurs on one of the fast days of the Forty Days, Vespers with the Presanctified ought to be celebrated both on the Eve of the Feast and on the day of the Feast itself; on the Eve with the Readings, and on the Feast itself with the Apostle and Gospel of the Forerunner. However, among us now the custom is maintained to celebrate the Service of the Presanctified only on Wednesdays and Fridays, and on the other three fast days the Liturgy of Chrysostom, after which in Cathedral Churches the three last Hours with the Typika are sung as well, and Vespers at the 11th hour. And where on Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday the Liturgy of Chrysostom is not used, nor is there a Liturgy of the Presanctified, then on the Eve of the Feast Vespers takes place according to the Typikon previously indicated; on the day of the Feast itself, according to the Direction of the *Tserkovne Oko* (in Mark's Chapter 62) and of the manuscript Typikon which we possess (February 24), there must be a Liturgy of the Presanctified, even if it is neither Wednesday nor Friday, and at it after *Let my prayer be set forth* the Prokimenon, Alleluia, and Apostle-Gospel of the Forerunner are to be taken, and the Communion Hymn *Taste and see* and of the Forerunner. If however out of necessity a Liturgy cannot be celebrated, then we deem that after the Readings of the Forerunner there ought to be the Prokimenon and Apostle-Gospel of the Forerunner, just as the Typikon has concerning the Annunciation.
