## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] Kathisma Omission
- **Issue:** Sometimes Small Vespers drafts mistakenly leave in a placeholder for the Kathisma (Blessed is the man).
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §271] At Small Vespers, the Kathisma is not sung. Immediately after the great litany, the choir sings 'Lord, I have cried' with only 4 stichera."*

### [FAIL] The Entrance & Prokeimenon
- **Issue:** Confusion over whether there is an Entrance at Small Vespers.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L34] There is no Entrance at Small Vespers. After 'O Gladsome Light' is sung, the Great Prokeimenon is replaced by the standard Prokeimenon of the day."*

## Verdict & Next Steps
Small Vespers is a highly abbreviated service used almost exclusively before an All-Night Vigil. The structural omissions (No Kathisma, No Entrance) must be strictly enforced.
