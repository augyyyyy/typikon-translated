**Canonical Baselines:** *Ordo Celebrationis 1996*, *Dolnytsky Typikon*

## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] Symbol of Faith Placement
- **Issue:** The placement of the Nicene Creed is occasionally confused or omitted.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §289] At Small Compline, following the reading of Psalms 50, 69, and 142, and the Great Doxology (which is read, not sung), the Symbol of Faith ('I believe in one God...') is recited immediately before the Trisagion."*

### [FAIL] Troparion / Kontakion Rotation
- **Issue:** The rubric regarding the Troparion after the Trisagion does not account for the standard weekday rotation.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §289] After the Trisagion and Our Father, the Troparion of the Day or the Saint is said. If there is a feast, the Kontakion of the feast is said."*

## Verdict & Next Steps
Small Compline is the standard evening prayer outside of Lent. It must have the Creed explicitly placed before the Trisagion.
