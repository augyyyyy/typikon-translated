# Report: Stamford vs Royal Doors Translation Evolution (2017 - 2026)

This report tracks how the translations of the **Stamford Divine Office (2014) Troparia & Kontakia** compare against the **Royal Doors Daily Propers** across a 10-year span (2017–2026).

Stamford translations have remained static, while Royal Doors has undergone revision cycles over the years. Below, we map out each revision version and show the exact pointing diffs between them.

## Summary Statistics

* **Total Stamford Keys Analyzed:** 188
* **Keys with Royal Doors Matches (Any Year):** 159 (84.6%)
  * **With Translation Evolution/Shifts:** 150 (79.8%)
  * **Verbatim Match Across All Years:** 9 (4.8%)
* **Unmatched Keys:** 29 (15.4%)
