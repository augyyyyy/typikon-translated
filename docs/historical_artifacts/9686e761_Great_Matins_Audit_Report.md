## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] The Hexapsalmos (Six Psalms) Posture & Logistics
- **Issue:** The rubric during the reading of the Six Psalms does not enforce the strict stillness required by the Typikon.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §99] The priest remains inside the altar during the first three psalms, and reads the prayers before the Holy Table; he goes out during the small doxology [Glory... Now and ever...] and stands before the royal doors to read the rest of the prayers."* 

### [FAIL] The Polyeleos Censing Protocol
- **Issue:** The choreography for censing during the Polyeleos and Megalynarion (or Evlogitaria) is often vaguely written.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L122] During the singing of the Polyeleos, the Priest and Deacon perform a full censing of the entire church, beginning at the Holy Table. If the Evlogitaria of the Resurrection are sung, the censing must be timed to conclude as they finish."*

### [FAIL] The Gospel Reading Logistics
- **Issue:** The document assumes the Gospel is read from the Ambo.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L133] The priest, standing before the Holy Table and facing East, reads the Holy Gospel... But on feasts of the Lord, the Mother of God, and Saints with a Polyeleos, he reads it from the ambo or an analogion."*
