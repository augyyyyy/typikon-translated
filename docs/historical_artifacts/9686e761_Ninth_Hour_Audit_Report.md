## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] Troparion & Kontakion Insertion Logistics
- **Issue:** The raw draft does not clearly specify where the Troparia and Kontakia are inserted, nor which Kontakion to use when there are two.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §323] After the Trisagion and Our Father, the Kontakion of the Day or Feast is said. If there are two Kontakia, they alternate between the Hours. At the First and Sixth Hours, the first Kontakion is read; at the Third and Ninth Hours, the second Kontakion is read."*

### [FAIL] The Vespers Transition Rule
- **Issue:** The document assumes a full dismissal is always taken at the end of the Ninth Hour.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §270] When Vespers immediately follows the Ninth Hour, the Reader concludes the Ninth Hour with the Prayer of St. Basil, omitting the typical dismissal. The Priest immediately vests in his phelonion, stands before the Holy Table, and intones 'Blessed is our God...' to begin Vespers."*

## Verdict & Next Steps
The Ninth Hour serves as the critical bridge to Vespers. The draft must explicitly document the transition rule while maintaining the rotational logic for the Kontakia.
