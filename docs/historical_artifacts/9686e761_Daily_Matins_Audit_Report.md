## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] Polyeleos & Gospel Intrusion
- **Issue:** Occasionally, raw drafts of Daily Matins mistakenly leave in structural placeholders for the Polyeleos or the Matins Gospel. 
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L108] At standard Daily Matins, there is no Polyeleos, no Megalynarion, and no Gospel reading. After the Sessional Hymns (Kathismata) from the Psalter are read, the reader immediately begins Psalm 50."*

### [FAIL] The Doxology Protocol
- **Issue:** The rubric leading into the Doxology fails to distinguish between the Great (sung) and Small (read) Doxology.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §320] On ordinary days, the Small Doxology is read instead (the Small Doxology is longer than the Great Doxology). It is immediately preceded by the Priest saying: 'Glory to You who have shown us the light!'"*

### [FAIL] The Aposticha & Conclusion
- **Issue:** The ending sequence often confuses the transition from the Aposticha to the concluding prayers.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L179] After the Aposticha are sung, the Reader immediately intones: 'It is good to give thanks to the Lord...' (Psalm 91:2-3). This is followed directly by the Trisagion ('Holy God...')."*
