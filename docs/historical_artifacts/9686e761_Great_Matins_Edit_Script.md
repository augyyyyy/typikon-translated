## Edit 1: The Hexapsalmos Rule
**Location:** Immediately before the reading of the Six Psalms.
**Action:** Based on Ordo §99, insert the strict posture rubric:
> **[Rubric]:** During the Six Psalms, absolute silence and stillness are required. The Priest remains inside the altar during the first three psalms, reading the morning prayers before the Holy Table. He goes out during the small doxology (the midpoint) and stands before the closed Royal Doors to read the rest of the prayers bareheaded.

## Edit 2: The Polyeleos Censing
**Location:** The singing of the Polyeleos (Psalms 134 & 135).
**Action:** Based on Dolnytsky L122, insert the choreography for the clergy:
> **[Rubric]:** During the singing of the Polyeleos, the Priest performs a full censing of the entire church, beginning at the Holy Table. If the Evlogitaria of the Resurrection are sung, the censing is timed to conclude as they finish.

## Edit 3: The Gospel Location
**Location:** The reading of the Matins Gospel.
**Action:** Based on Dolnytsky L133, insert the constraint regarding the reading location:
> **[Rubric]:** On Sundays, the Matins Gospel is read by the Priest from the Holy Table, facing East. On Feasts with a Polyeleos, it is read from the Ambo (or analogion) standing amidst the people.
