- **Issue:** The raw draft often mistakenly copies the Daily Midnight Office layout (using the 17th Kathisma).
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §292] On Saturday, the Midnight Office differs from the Daily Office. Instead of the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118), the 9th Kathisma (Psalms 65-69) is read. The rest of the service proceeds as usual."*

- **Issue:** The second part of the Midnight Office is sometimes missing.

After the Priest gives the blessing concluding the first part, the reader immediately begins 'Come, let us bow in worship...' followed by Psalms 120 and 133, the Trisagion, and the Troparia for the departed ('Remember, O Lord, the souls of Your servants...')."*
