## Edit 1: The Opening Censing Protocol
**Location:** Opening Rubrics (Psalm 103)
**Action:** Based on Ordo §272, insert this explicit rubric regarding multiple deacons:
> **[Rubric]:** If serving with two Deacons, the first stands to the right and the second to the left. The Priest places incense into both censers. They cense the Holy Table together, then the first goes to the right and the second to the left to cense the entire church simultaneously, returning together to the solea.

## Edit 2: The Entrance Protocol
**Location:** The Entrance with the Censer (O Gladsome Light)
**Action:** Based on Dolnytsky L30, insert the procession order constraint:
> **[Rubric]:** If there are two Deacons, all three exit the sanctuary from the North Door. The second deacon processes first, followed by the first deacon carrying the censer, followed by the Priest.

## Edit 3: The Litiya and Blessing of Loaves
**Location:** The Procession for Litiya
**Action:** Based on Ordo footnote [98], add the logistical rubric for the Vigil Vessel:
> **[Rubric]:** The deacon (or an acolyte) must carry the vigil vessel (litiinyk) with the five loaves, wheat, wine, and oil out to the tetrapod during the procession, placing it there before the Priest arrives.
