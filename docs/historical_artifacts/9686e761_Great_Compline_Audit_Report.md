**Canonical Baselines:** *Ordo Celebrationis 1996*, *Dolnytsky Typikon*

## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] "God is with us" Choreography
- **Issue:** The rubric during the singing of "God is with us" does not specify the posture of the clergy or if the Royal Doors are opened.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L69] When 'God is with us' is sung, the Royal Doors are opened. The Priest, standing before the Holy Table in his epitrachelion, censes the Holy Table and the entire church. If a Deacon is present, he performs the censing."*

### [FAIL] Great Doxology vs. Lenten Variations
- **Issue:** The draft fails to clearly demarcate the difference between a Festal Great Compline (Eve of Nativity/Theophany) and a Lenten Great Compline.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §285] At Great Compline served on the Eve of the Nativity, Theophany, and Annunciation, the Great Doxology is sung. However, when Great Compline is served during the weekdays of Great Lent, the Doxology is read, and the Prayer of St. Ephrem with its prostrations is taken at the end."*

## Verdict & Next Steps
Great Compline is highly contextual (Festal vs. Lenten). The rubrics must clearly gate the Lenten prostrations and the singing of the Doxology.
