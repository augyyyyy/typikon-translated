## 2. Exhaustive Catalog of All 32 Cited Delta Points

### DOMAIN I: All-Night Vigil & General Office Structure

#### Delta Point 1: Vigil Frequency (Weekly vs. Festal Only)
* **Citation:** *Ordo Celebrationis*, Chapter III, Footnote [86] ([Line 2787](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L2787)); *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.2.1 ([Line 362](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L362)) & §1.2.2 ([Line 450](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L450)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Celebrated every Saturday evening for Sunday, and before every Great Feast or Vigil-ranked saint (Rank 2).
* **Parish Usage:** Celebrated only on major feasts or patronal titles; ordinary Sundays have Great Vespers without Vigil.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Ordo [86]:* `"In normal parish use, the 'All-Night' Vigil lasts about two hours; in monasteries it can last three or four hours, or even longer. The service is usually a combination of Vespers, Orthros, and First Hour."`

#### Delta Point 2: Vigil Duration and Composition
* **Citation:** *Ordo Celebrationis*, Chapter III, Footnote [86] ([Lines 2787–2792](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L2787-L2792)).
* **Monastic Rule:** 3 to 4+ hours, with full unshortened psalmody, multiple patristic readings (e.g. from the St. Theodore Studite catecheses or Menology), and First Hour appended.
* **Parish Usage:** Condensed to ~1.5 to 2 hours; patristic readings and the First Hour are omitted; psalmody is selected.

#### Delta Point 3: Suppression of Small Compline and Midnight Office
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part II, Footnote [161] ([Line 9367](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9367)).
* **Monastic Rule:** An All-Night Vigil connects Great Vespers and Matins without interruption; Small Compline and Midnight Office are eliminated entirely.
* **Parish Usage:** When Vespers and Matins are served at distinct hours of the day (e.g., Saturday afternoon and Sunday morning), Small Compline and Midnight Office remain standalone.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky [^161]:* `"The Greek Typikon does not give anything, for the All-Night Vigil is implied, which excludes Compline."`

#### Delta Point 4: Small Vespers before the Vigil
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.2.4 ([Lines 505–510](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L505-L510)); Part II, Footnote [160] ([Line 9366](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9366)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Celebrated on Saturday afternoon before sunset and before the communal refectory meal.
* **Parish Usage:** Completely omitted in secular parishes.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky [^160]:* `"According to the ancient typikon, on Sunday, in view of the All-Night Vigil, everything was taken on Small Vespers only of the Sunday, as usual."`

#### Delta Point 5: Reverence and Bows to the Superior
* **Citation:** *Ordo Celebrationis*, Chapter III, Footnote [96] ([Line 2828](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L2828)).
* **Monastic Rule:** The priest or deacon performs ceremonial bows and censes the Monastic Superior (Hegumen/Archimandrite) in his presiding stall.
* **Parish Usage:** Directed to the Parish Rector, or to a presiding Bishop if present.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Ordo [96]:* `"The reference is to a monastic superior, or to a bishop present but not serving."`

### DOMAIN II: Vespers Choreography & Psalmody

#### Delta Point 6: Kathisma 1 at Great Vespers (*"Blessed is the man"*)
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part II, §2.1.1.1 ([Line 805](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L805)) & §2.6.1.1 ([Line 992](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L992)).
* **Monastic Rule:** The entire First Kathisma (all three stases: Psalms 1–3, 4–6, 7–8) is sung in full.
* **Parish Usage:** Only selected verses are chanted from the *Heirmologion* / parish hymnal.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §2.1.1.1:* `"Kathisma 1 'Blessed is the man' - whole, according to the Typikon. By our custom today, only certain selected verses of it are sung, which are contained in the church hymnal."`

#### Delta Point 7: Entrance at Daily Vespers in Provincial Parishes
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.2.3.3, Note 1 ([Line 502](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L502)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Strictly forbidden; at Daily Vespers the Holy Doors remain closed, no entrance is made, and the priest serves in the epitrachelion only.
* **Parish Usage:** Parishes in the province maintain a local custom of making an Entrance at Daily Vespers on Sunday evenings or feasts with Vigil.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §1.2.3.3.1:* `"On Sunday itself or on another Feast with Vigil in parish churches in the province, the general custom remains to make an Entrance."`

#### Delta Point 8: Litya Temple Sticheron under Paradigm 6 & 7
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part II, §2.6.1.4 ([Line 995](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L995)), §2.7.1.3 ([Line 1015](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L1015)); Footnote [143] ([Line 9349](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9349)).
* **Monastic Rule:** The 1st sticheron of Litya is appointed to the Temple Patron (1 Temple + 3 Saint).
* **Parish Usage:** The Temple sticheron is suppressed; parishes take only the stichera of the saint.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §2.6.1.4:* `"On the Lytia: according to the Slavonic Typikon, first sticheron - of the temple (which today we usually do not take), also stichera to the saint."*  
  > *Dolnytsky [^143]:* `"Moscow Typikon, chapter 3... and Mark: 'At the Lytia -- sticheron of the Saint of the Monastery, Glory: to the Saint, Both now: Theotokion... (chapter 78)'."`

#### Delta Point 9: Standing and Sitting Rubrics during Vespers
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.2.2.3 ([Line 473](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L473)) & §1.2.3.3, Note 2 ([Line 503](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L503)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Monks sit only during prescribed Kathisma readings and patristic lessons; standing is strictly observed throughout all litanies, hymns, and prayers.
* **Parish Usage:** Congregational standing and sitting adapts to parish seating and local usage.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §1.2.3.3.2:* `"It is not proper to sit at this Vespers either, when it is not permitted to sit at Great Vespers without Vigil."`

### DOMAIN III: Compline & Midnight Office (Nocturns)

#### Delta Point 10: Troparion of St. Basil the Great at Small Compline
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.3.1.1, ¶ 5 ([Line 527](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L527)); Footnote [20] ([Line 9226](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9226)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Monks insert the Troparion of St. Basil before *"O God of our Fathers"* on weekdays (producing 8 troparia on Thursdays).
* **Parish Usage:** Omitted; secular parishes take only the standard Horologion troparia (7 troparia on Thursdays).
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §1.3.1.1:* `"...monks before 'O God of our Fathers' recite also the troparion to St. Basil. On Thursday, because the service is double and has two troparia, there will be seven troparia, and in monasteries -- eight, with the troparion of St. Basil."`

#### Delta Point 11: Intercessory Petitions at Compline & Midnight Office
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.3.1.1, Note 2 ([Line 532](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L532)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Full communal intercessory petitions and mutual forgiveness formulas are recited aloud in choir prior to the concluding litany.
* **Parish Usage:** Omitted; replaced in parish/private practice by the prayer *"Remit, pardon"* (*Oslaby, ostavy*).
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §1.3.1.1.2:* `"At the end -- the dismissal, and after it -- the litany, as in the Horologion at the end of Daily Midnight Office (the petitions given before the litany are recited only in monasteries). Instead of this litany, we say today in private 'Remit, pardon'."`

#### Delta Point 12: Daily Celebration of Midnight Office
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.4 ([Line 547](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L547)); *Ordo Celebrationis*, Section I, §5 ([Line 115](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L115)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Celebrated communally in the church at midnight or early morning before Matins.
* **Parish Usage:** Rarely celebrated in parish churches; recited privately by clergy or omitted.

### DOMAIN IV: Matins / Orthros Architecture

#### Delta Point 13: The Royal Office (*Tsarske Nachalo*)
* **Citation:** *Ordo Celebrationis*, Chapter IV, Footnote [109] ([Line 2868](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L2868)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Chanted at the beginning of Matins (Psalms 19 & 20, Troparia of the Cross, and Ektene) as intercession for the monastery's royal founder.
* **Parish Usage:** Very seldom celebrated in parishes; Matins begins directly at the Six Psalms after the initial exclamation.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Ordo [109]:* `"This preliminary sequence of psalms and prayers for the King is called the 'Royal Office', and is a spiritual recompense for the King or Emperor who founded a given monastery. In modern practice, it is very seldom done in parish churches, although it is still maintained in monasteries."`

#### Delta Point 14: Psalter Kathismata Division & Abbreviation
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.5.1.3 ([Line 595](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L595)); Footnote [77] ([Line 9283](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9283)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Two or three full Kathismata read daily in rotation from the Psalter.
* **Parish Usage:** Abbreviated to a few verses, or omitted entirely in favor of hymns.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §1.5.1.3:* `"After the troparia the Reader reads the current Kathisma, given according to the content in the Psalter (according to the current local custom, only a few verses)."`

#### Delta Point 15: Small Litanies after Kathismata (Audible vs. Silent)
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.5.1.3 ([Line 598](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L598)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Chanted aloud by the deacon from the solea with full choral responses after each Kathisma.
* **Parish Usage:** The priest recites the Small Litany quietly while sitting at his place in the sanctuary, while the choir chants the responses.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §1.5.1.3:* `"...which according to local custom the Priest says quietly, sitting at his place, while the Choir sings its responses aloud; then follows the Sessional Hymn..."`

#### Delta Point 16: Intercalation of Biblical Canticles with Canons
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part IV, §4.1.5 ([Line 5476](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L5476)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Canons are chanted intercalated with the **9 Biblical Canticles** (Odes of Moses, Hannah, Habakkuk, Isaiah, Jonah, Three Holy Children, Magnificat, Benedictus) to reach 14, 12, or 8 troparia per ode.
* **Parish Usage:** Biblical Canticles are omitted; only the troparia of the canon are read/sung with brief refrains.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §4.1.5:* `"A more precise example of the combination of the Odes of the Psalter with the Odes of the Canon is given at the 1st Ode in the Moscow Typikon..."`

#### Delta Point 17: Katavasia Choreography (*"The Descent"*)
* **Citation:** *Ordo Celebrationis*, Glossary ([Line 2001](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L2001)).
* **Monastic Rule:** The two monastic choirs literally descend (*katabaino*) from their stalls and meet in the center of the nave to chant the concluding Irmos together.
* **Parish Usage:** Chanted in place from the choir loft, cantor stand, or kliros.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Ordo Glossary:* `"Katavasia — literally 'descent'... In monasteries the two monastic choirs 'descend' from their positions and meet together in the center of the nave to chant this concluding Irmos."`

#### Delta Point 18: Polyeleos vs. Full Vigil
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part III ([Line 5059](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L5059)); Footnote [279] ([Line 9485](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9485)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Polyeleos is an integral part of the All-Night Vigil.
* **Parish Usage:** Following the **Synod of Zamość (1720)**, Polyeleos was established as a standalone festal Matins rank in secular parishes without Vigil.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky Part III:* `"...and by the decision and decree of the Synod of Zamość, even with All-Night Vigil (although with us there is none, but only Polyeleos)..."`

### DOMAIN V: The Little Hours (1st, 3rd, 6th, 9th Hours)

#### Delta Point 19: Omission of the Little Hours in Parishes
* **Citation:** *Ordo Celebrationis*, Chapter V, Footnote [268] ([Line 3372](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Ordo/Ordo_Celebrationis_1996_CLEAN.md#L3372)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Strictly read in choir at their appointed hours (1st Hour after Matins; 3rd & 6th before Liturgy; 9th before Vespers).
* **Parish Usage:** Frequently omitted entirely before the Divine Liturgy.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Ordo [268]:* `"In parishes the Little Hours are often omitted."`

#### Delta Point 20: Deaconal Litanies at the Hours in Monasteries
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part II, Footnote [311] ([Line 8919](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L8919) & [Line 9517](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9517)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Ancient Russian monastic typikons (*Tserkovne Oko*) prescribe three litanies sung by the deacon at each Hour.
* **Parish Usage:** Never practiced; Hours are read simply by a solitary reader without litanies.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky [^311]:* `"Typikons of some Russian monasteries, given by the Tserkovne Oko, prescribe for the Deacon at each Hour to sing three litanies, that is 'In peace let us pray to the Lord'..."`

#### Delta Point 21: Inter-Hours (*Mesoria*)
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, §1.6 ([Line 751](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L751)); Horologion rubrics.
* **Monastic Rule:** Read during fasting seasons between the major Hours in monasteries.
* **Parish Usage:** Suppressed completely in secular parish usage.

### DOMAIN VI: Divine Liturgy & Proskomedia

#### Delta Point 22: Number of Prosphora (5 vs. 1)
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Appendix §98 ([Line 7831](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L7831)); Footnote [767] ([Line 9981](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9981)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Strict canonical use of five separate prosphora (Lamb, Theotokos, Nine Ranks, Living, Dead).
* **Parish Usage:** Pastoral adaptation permits taking the Lamb and all particles from one single prosphora.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §98:* `"Also, it is not necessary to always use five prosphora, if the Lamb and all particles can be taken from one and the same prosphora. The custom of using five prosphora may be kept, if one pleases, when celebrating more solemn liturgies."`

#### Delta Point 23: Repetition of Liturgy on the Same Holy Table
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Appendix §97 ([Line 7830](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L7830)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Strict ancient canonical rule: only one Divine Liturgy per day may be celebrated on the same Holy Table.
* **Parish Usage:** Pastoral dispensation permits repeating the Liturgy for the pastoral benefit of the faithful in parishes, preferring side chapels where possible.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §97:* `"According to the ancient custom of the Eastern Church, only one Holy Liturgy may be celebrated at the same holy table. However, one may depart from this rule if the Divine Liturgy must be repeated for the benefit of the faithful. If there is one or several secondary churches in the parish, then the more solemn Liturgy is celebrated in the main sanctuary, and other liturgies should be celebrated rather in the side sanctuaries."`

#### Delta Point 24: Athonite Curtain Movement after Proskomedia

#### Delta Point 25: Concelebration and Visiting Parish Priests

#### Delta Point 26: Large Presanctified Communion Volume in Parishes

### DOMAIN VII: Great Lent, Prostrations & Fasts

#### Delta Point 27: Earthly Prostrations (*Zemnye Poklony*)
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part I, Footnote [13] ([Line 9219](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L9219)).

* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky [^13]:* `"In some parishes, they still bend their knees and heads to the ground, as presented in the Greek Typikon, in which: 'And while we have bowed and bent down to the ground' (Chapter 2)."`

* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §3.1.8.2 Note:* `"The Service of the falling saint, let it be sung before that, on Friday at Compline, as noted..."`

### DOMAIN VIII: Temple Feasts & Parochial Usages (Dolnytsky Part V)

#### Delta Point 30: Outdoor Procession & Water Blessing on the Patronal Feast
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part V, §5.2.1.3 ([Line 6376](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L6376)).
* **Monastic Rule:** Blessing of water takes place within the monastery phiale / courtyard.
* **Parish Usage:** Procession outside the church with water blessing, culminating in a solemn Polychronion for all parish members.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §5.2.1.3:* `"On the very day of the feast, after the Ambo [prayer], in our lands, a Blessing of Water usually takes place outside the church, to where a procession goes: in front - the cross-bearer, then - the censer-bearer, the Deacon with the Gospel, and the Priest with a hand cross... After the dismissal of the Liturgy, the Polychronion is sung for the Ecumenical Pontiff, the Emperor, the Metropolitan, one's own Bishop, all present sacred ministers, and all people of the parish."`

#### Delta Point 31: Parish Memorial Liturgy on the Morrow of the Feast
* **Citation:** *Dolnytsky Typikon Master*, Part V, §5.2.1.4 ([Line 6377](file:///c:/Users/augus/OneDrive/Documents/Google%20Antigravity/Projects/Typikon%20Coded/Data/Service%20Books/Typikon/Dolnytsky_Typikon_Master.md#L6377)).
* **Monastic Rule:** The day after a feast is celebrated as the Synaxis of the sacred person or an Afterfeast.
* **Parish Usage:** Dedicated to a parochial requiem Liturgy and Parastas for all deceased parishioners.
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Dolnytsky §5.2.1.4:* `"On the day following the feast, according to local custom, a Liturgy for the deceased of the parish with a Parastas is sung, at which it is not fitting to take anything of the feast, but everything only - for the deceased."`
