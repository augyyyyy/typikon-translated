## Edit 1: "God is with us" Choreography
**Location:** Immediately before the choir sings "God is with us" (Isaiah 8).
**Action:** Based on Dolnytsky L69, insert the following rubric:
> **[Rubric]:** The Royal Doors are opened. During the singing of "God is with us," the Priest (or the Deacon, if serving) performs a full censing of the Holy Table, the sanctuary, the iconostasis, and the entire church. 

## Edit 2: The Festal/Lenten Pivot
**Location:** Immediately preceding the Doxology.
**Action:** Based on Ordo §285, insert the conditional gating rubric:
> **[Rubric]:** Note: If Great Compline is served on the Eve of a Great Feast (Nativity, Theophany, Annunciation), the Holy Doors are opened and the Great Doxology is SUNG. The Prayer of St. Ephrem is omitted. However, if served during Great Lent, the Doxology is READ, and the Prayer of St. Ephrem (with its prostrations) is taken at the conclusion of the service.
