## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] 17th Kathisma Placement
- **Issue:** The rubric regarding the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118) is often formatted poorly, making it unclear what follows Psalm 50.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §292] On weekdays (Monday through Friday), the Midnight Office features the reading of the 17th Kathisma immediately following Psalm 50. After the Kathisma, the Symbol of Faith (The Creed) is read."*

### [FAIL] The Second Part Logistics
- **Issue:** The second part of the Midnight Office (Prayers for the Departed) is sometimes erroneously omitted or blended into the first part.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Dolnytsky Part 1, L78] The Daily Midnight Office consists of two parts. After the Priest gives the blessing concluding the first part, the reader immediately begins 'Come, let us bow in worship...' followed by Psalms 120 and 133, the Trisagion, and the Troparia for the departed ('Remember, O Lord, the souls of Your servants...')."*

## Verdict & Next Steps
The Daily Midnight Office is a two-part service characterized by the massive 17th Kathisma. The structural break between Part 1 and Part 2 must be clarified.
