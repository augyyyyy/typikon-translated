## Edit 1: Eradicating the Deacon Prohibition
**Location:** Opening Rubrics
**Action:** Delete any text that states Deacons do not serve. Based on Dolnytsky L54, insert the following:
> **[Rubric]:** If a Deacon is serving at Daily Vespers: He remains in his vestments. During the opening Psalm, he does not cense the whole church, but only the Holy Table and the Iconostasis. There is no Entrance. The Deacon intones the Ektenias as usual from the Ambo.

## Edit 2: The Aposticha and Theotokion Rule
**Location:** Before the Aposticha
**Action:** Based on Ordo §278, insert the following explicit rubric for the Cantor:
> **[Rubric]:** At the Aposticha, the stichera are taken from the Octoechos. Glory... Now and ever... Theotokion. Note: On Tuesday and Thursday evenings, a Stavrotheotokion is always sung in place of the regular Theotokion.

## Edit 3: The Dismissal Protocol
**Location:** The Final Blessing
**Action:** Based on Dolnytsky L58, insert this constraint for the Priest:
> **[Rubric]:** At the dismissal of Daily Vespers, because there was no Entrance, the Priest gives the final blessing ("May Christ our true God...") from the Ambo or the Holy Doors, but does not bless with the hand-cross.
