## 1. Structural Diagnostics
### [FAIL] The Opening Censing Protocol (Missing 2-Deacon Rule)
- **Issue:** The rubric for the opening censing of the church assumes only one priest and one deacon. It fails to account for concelebrating deacons.
- **Canonical Proof:** *"[Ordo §272] If there are two deacons, the first stands to the priest's right and the second to the priest's left. During the opening Psalm, if there are two deacons, both receive incense and cense the Holy Table together, then the first goes to the right and the second to the left to cense the entire church."*

### [FAIL] The Entrance Protocol (Incomplete Procession)
