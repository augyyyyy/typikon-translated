## 2. Statutory Mandates 1–13 (`data/templates/work_order_template.md`)

1. **Physical Ink Witness**: Transcribe 100% directly from 300 DPI page images in `Source Text/images/`. Distinguish red cinnabar rubrics (render in italics `*...*`) from black text (render in standard Roman).
2. **Register Enforcement**: Concise, imperative, Active Present Indicative for rubrical movements (`Juridical`, `Rubrical`, `Hymnographic`, or `Scholarly Critical`).
3. **Canonical Realia & Loanwords**: `Tetrapod`, `Klepalo`, `Aer`, `Kolyvo`, `Plashchanytsia`, `Sluzhebnik`, `Trebnik`, `Antimension`.
4. **Father Paul Doxology & Anti-Slop Standard**: Ban *"for ever and ever"* (use *"unto ages of ages"* or *"unto the ages of ages"*). Ban pseudo-archaic slop (`wherefore` → `therefore`; `wondrous` → `marvelous`; `verily` → `truly`; `hearken` → `give ear`; `resplendent`/`effulgent` → `radiant`) and all 3rd-person `-eth` verbs (`singeth`, `proclaimeth`, `censeth`, `entereth`, `maketh`, `readeth`, `beginneth`, `taketh`, `vesteth`, `boweth`, `kisseth`, `turneth`, `standeth`, `cometh`, `sayeth`, `giveth`, `believeth`, `forgiveth`, `partaketh`, `rebuketh`).
5. **Hieratic Deity Pronouns**: 100% mandatory capitalization for Holy Trinity pronouns (*He, Him, His, Thou, Thee, Thy, Thine*). Keep human/Marian/adversary pronouns lowercase (*thee/thy/thou* when addressing the *Birthgiver of God / Mother of God / Theotokos*, and *him/his* for clergy, saints, or the *serpent/enemy*).
6. **Scripture & Psalter**: Septuagint (LXX) versification mandatory in body text; Masoretic (MT) references in footnotes `[^N]` only.
7. **Footnote Monotonic Bijectivity**: Every marker `[^N]` in body text MUST have an exact corresponding entry `[^N]:` in the footnotes file. Never attach `[^N]` directly to a `**[Left/Right Page — Fol. Xr]**` 2-up spread milestone or `=== LEAF pN ===` banner; always attach `[^N]` to a surviving heading or content line.
8. **Strict Epigraphic Markdown Discipline**: Never invent or interpolate modern liturgical office or structural headings (`### At Vespers`, `### At Matins`, `### Canon...`, `#### Ode...`). Render liturgical rubrics, genre indicators, ode numbers, troparion labels, kathismata, and prosomoia strictly as inline italicized text (`*Ode 1, Tone 2. Heirmos:*`, `*Troparion:*`).
9. **Canonical 3-Layer Melodic Model (`Podoben`) Law**: Render all melodic model headings (`Подо́бенъ` / `Самогла́сенъ`) using the canonical English incipit from `data/liturgical_primitives_registry.json` followed by the parenthetical Slavonic witness in inline italics: `*Tone N, Podoben: "<Canonical English Incipit>" (<Slavonic Witness>):*`.
10. **Structured Liturgical Stanzas & Dialogue Typography**: Format each Heirmos, Troparion, Theotokion, and Sticheron as a distinct paragraph preceded by its inline italicized role tag. Preserve literal colometric breathing-pause asterisks (`*`) in `Source Text/` and convert them into natural English punctuation (commas, semicolons, colons) in `Draft/`.
11. **Diachronic Drift Invariance & Recensional Fidelity**: Translate identical original-language formulas identically via the shared lexicon; translate genuine recensional divergences strictly as inked and document variants in `[^N]`. Render internal folio cross-references in body prose as `(see fol. N)[^M]` (never bare `[Page N]` or `[Leaf pN]` brackets, which fail Gate 1).
12. **Front-Matter Paratext, Vacat Folios & Parenthetical Cyrillic**: Provide symmetric diplomatic descriptions in both `Source Text/` and `Draft/` on binding/engraving/frontispiece leaves so `SGC-02` length ratios remain within `[0.80, 1.50]`. Represent blank folios and flyleaves strictly as `*(Vacat — Fol. Xv: Unnumbered blank verso)*` or `*(Flyleaf — Archival binding sheet)*` (never `[Blank leaf...]`). Render all inline Slavonic/Cyrillic script witnesses or table numerals in **parentheses** `(*Слава...*)`, **never in square brackets** `[Слава...]`.
13. **Patristic Hagiography, Synaxarion Prose Diction & Incremental 2–3 Leaf Disk Flush**: Maintain elevated classical patristic English prose across narrative *Synaxaria*, Patristic Homilies, and Martyrological Passions with zero `-eth` verbs or pseudo-archaic particles, and flush transcribed/translated leaves to disk incrementally every **2 to 3 physical leaves** (`write_file` then `write_file(Append=True)` / `edit_file`).
