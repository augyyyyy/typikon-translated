# Master Translation Standard (MTS-1): Genre Registers, LXX Psalter, Realia & Titulature

## 1. The Four Stratified Genre Registers (`MTS-1` Pillar II)

| Register | Target Monuments | Mandatory Stylistic & Grammatical Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Juridical / Canonical** | *Synodal Acts (1720 Zamość, 1891 Lviv), Basilian Chapters, Nomocanons* | Strict formal equivalence; preserve verbatim statutory numbering (`§ 1.`, Roman numerals); reserve `"shall"` for binding canonical decrees (*"The parish priest shall be bound..."*). |
| **2. Ceremonial / Rubrical** | *Typika (1899 Dolnytsky, 1901 Mikita, 1852 Doskovsky, 2010 Lviv), Horologia, Liturgika* | Concise, imperative **Active Present Indicative** for all ceremonial actions (*"The priest enters the sanctuary, kisses the Holy Table, and begins..."*). Gratuitous legalistic `"shall"`-bombing in bodily rubrics is banned. |
| **3. Poetic / Hymnographic** | *Octoechos, Triodion, Pentecostarion, Menaia, Anthologia, Irmologia, Akathistniks* | Elevated liturgical diction with hieratic Deity pronouns (*Thou, Thee, Thy, Thine*); preserve colometric asterisks (`*`) in `Source Text/` and convert to natural English punctuation in `Draft/`. |
| **4. Critical Scholarly** | *1910 Skaballanovich, 1895/1917 Dmitrievsky, 1798 Strilbytsky, Lexicons* | Academic prose preserving original pagination markers, parenthetical Cyrillic/Greek lemmas `(*...*)`, and comprehensive footnote apparatus `[^N]`. |

---

## 2. Scripture & Psalter Standard (`MTS-1` Pillar III — Septuagint LXX)
- **Septuagint (LXX) Primacy**: All Psalm numbers and biblical references in body text and rubrics must strictly use Septuagint numbering (e.g., `Psalm 50`, `Psalm 90`, `Psalm 103`, `Psalm 140`).
- **Footnote Cross-Referencing**: When providing a scriptural citation in `[^N]:`, cite the LXX reference first, followed by the Masoretic (MT) chapter/verse in parentheses or brackets if divergent (e.g., `Psalm 50:3 (Psalm 51:1 MT)`).

## 4. Hagiographical & Marian Titulature (`MTS-1` Pillar VI)
- **`Hierarch`** (*Святитель*) — holy bishop or Church Father (*"Our Father among the Saints, the Holy Hierarch Nicholas"*).
- **`Venerable`** (*Преподобный*) — monastic saint (*"Our Venerable Father Anthony of the Caves"*).
- **`Hieromartyr`** (*Священномученикъ*) — priest or bishop martyr.
- **`Great-Martyr`** (*Великомученикъ*) — high-ranking martyr (St. George, St. Demetrius).
- **`Venerable-Martyr`** (*Преподобномученикъ*) — monastic martyr.
- **`Unmercenary`** (*Безсребреникъ*) — physician saint (Cosmas and Damian, Panteleimon).
- **`Mother of God`** / **`Theotokos`** / **`Birthgiver of God`** (*Богородиця*) — always keep second-person pronouns addressing the Theotokos lowercase (*thou, thee, thy, thine*).
